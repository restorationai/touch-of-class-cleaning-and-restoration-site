## Why Clute Properties See Water Damage Issues

Clute sits on the flat Gulf Coastal Plain of Brazoria County, where heavy clay soil swells when saturated and shrinks hard during dry stretches. That cycle stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them, which is why so many of the water calls we take in this area start underneath the house rather than above it. Add in a humid subtropical climate with a long storm season and a water table that sits close to the surface, and you get conditions where standing water under flooring or inside wall cavities doesn't evaporate on its own the way it might in a drier region. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Clute

The first step is always extraction, pulling standing water out before it has a chance to wick further into drywall, baseboards, and subfloor.
