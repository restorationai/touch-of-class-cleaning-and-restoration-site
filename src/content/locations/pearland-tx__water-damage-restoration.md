## Why Pearland Properties See Water Damage Issues

Pearland sits on flat Gulf Coast terrain with heavy clay soil that expands and contracts with moisture. That movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing lines running beneath them, which is a common source of slow leaks that go unnoticed until flooring starts to cup or baseboards swell. The region's humid subtropical climate adds a second layer: once water gets into wall cavities or under cabinetry, the ambient humidity here slows natural evaporation, giving mold a head start if drying isn't handled quickly and correctly. Add in the hurricane and heavy-rain season that runs through the summer and fall, when Clear Creek and the surrounding drainage network can back up faster than storm drains can clear, and you have a city where both sudden flooding and gradual plumbing failures show up regularly.
