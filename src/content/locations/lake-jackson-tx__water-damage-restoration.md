## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Lake Jackson

The first call is almost always about getting water out before it spreads further into walls and flooring, so extraction comes first, whether that's standing water from a supply line failure or intrusion from outside grading. From there we moisture-map the affected rooms with meters, because in slab homes the surface can read dry while moisture is still migrating under baseboards and into the wall cavity above. Containment goes up where needed to protect unaffected rooms, and structural drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space, stays in place and is monitored daily until materials are dried per the IICRC S500 standard rather than pulled on a guess. Throughout, we document moisture readings and photos, which matters when a claim involves both the water event and any pre-existing foundation movement an adjuster will want to rule out.
