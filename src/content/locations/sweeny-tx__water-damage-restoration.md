## Why Sweeny Properties See Water Damage Issues

Sweeny sits in flat, low-lying Brazoria County terrain where clay-heavy soil doesn't drain quickly after a heavy rain event. That slows runoff and keeps ground moisture pressed against slab foundations and crawlspace framing longer than homeowners expect. Pair that with the area's humid subtropical climate, where summer dew points stay high for weeks at a stretch, and even a minor roof leak or supply line failure can turn into hidden moisture that doesn't show up on drywall for days. Older homes near the center of town, many built with original plumbing runs, are especially prone to slow pinhole leaks behind walls that go unnoticed until a baseboard starts to bow or flooring cups.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sweeny

The first step is always stopping the source and extracting standing water, whether that's from a burst supply line, an overflowing fixture, or storm intrusion through a roof or window. From there we assess how far moisture has traveled into subfloor, insulation, and framing, since Gulf Coast humidity means surface-dry materials can still hold water deep inside. We set up air movers and dehumidification equipment calibrated to the structure and monitor drying progress daily, working toward moisture levels consistent with the IICRC S500 drying standard rather than guessing based on how a wall feels. Carpet, pad, and baseboards that can't be salvaged are removed early to stop secondary damage and odor from setting in.
