## Why Manvel Properties See Water Damage Issues

A lot of Manvel's housing stock is newer construction built on slab foundations, which is standard across this part of Texas but comes with its own vulnerability: expansive clay soil underneath those slabs swells when it's saturated and shrinks hard during dry stretches. That shrink-swell cycle puts ongoing stress on foundations and the plumbing lines running through them, and a hairline crack can leak slowly for weeks before anyone notices. Add in the Gulf Coast's long hurricane season and the heavy, fast rain events that come with it, plus roadside and bar ditches that aren't always equipped to carry a sudden downpour away fast enough, and you get a city where water intrusion shows up from more directions than just a broken appliance hose.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Manvel

We start with a moisture assessment that goes beyond the visible wet spot, since slab-related leaks in this area often travel before they surface.
