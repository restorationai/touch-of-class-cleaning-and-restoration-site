## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Iowa Colony

The first step on any call is stopping the source and getting standing water out, usually with truck-mount or portable extraction units depending on how much square footage is affected. From there we map moisture, not just where the floor looks wet, but where it's migrated: under laminate and engineered wood flooring common in newer Iowa Colony builds, behind baseboards, and up into drywall through capillary action. Clay-heavy soil and high humidity mean drying has to be deliberate rather than rushed. We set air movers and dehumidifiers based on moisture readings and follow industry S500 drying guidelines, tracking progress daily rather than guessing at a timeline. In homes with engineered hardwood or vinyl plank, which dominate a lot of Iowa Colony's newer subdivisions, that often means pulling sections of flooring the manufacturer's warranty would otherwise void if left to cup and warp. We document every step with moisture logs and photos, which matters when a claim goes to an adjuster.
