## Why Carrollton Properties See Water Damage Issues

North Texas clay soil is part of the story. Add in sudden, high-volume thunderstorms that roll through the Metroplex in spring, and you get two very different causes of the same problem: slow pinhole leaks that go undetected for weeks, and fast overland flooding from storm runoff that overwhelms yard drainage and backs water up against exterior walls. We size up which one we're dealing with before we pull a single piece of equipment off the truck, because the drying plan is different for each.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Carrollton

We start with moisture mapping, not guesswork, using meters to read how far water has traveled into drywall, baseboard, and subfloor rather than assuming it stopped where it's visible. Standing water comes out with truck-mount or portable extraction depending on the volume, then we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's actual moisture load, following the IICRC S500 drying standard as our benchmark rather than a fixed number of days. For slab leaks specifically, we coordinate with a plumber for leak location and repair before we close up any opened wall or flooring, since drying around an active leak is just delaying the same damage. Daily moisture checks tell us when the structure is actually dry, not just when it looks dry.
