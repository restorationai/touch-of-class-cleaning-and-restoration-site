## Why Rowlett Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Rowlett was built out from the 1970s through the early 2000s, which means a lot of homes still run original copper supply lines through concrete slabs. North Texas blackland prairie clay expands when it's saturated and contracts hard during dry spells, and that movement stresses slab plumbing at the joints over time. Add in the occasional hard winter freeze that catches older pipe insulation off guard, and slab leaks and burst lines are a recurring call type across the city, not just near the lake.
