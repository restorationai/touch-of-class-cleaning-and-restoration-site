Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website or reaches out to us about a water, fire, mold, or biohazard cleanup project. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form or request an estimate, we collect what you provide directly: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or service you need. If you call or email us, we retain that correspondence so we can follow up and keep a record of the job. Our website also uses basic analytics tools that collect general, non-identifying information such as browser type, pages visited, and approximate location based on IP address. We do not use this data to build advertising profiles.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule site visits and estimates, prepare documentation for insurance claims, and communicate with you about the status of a project. If you ask us to coordinate directly with your insurance carrier or property manager, we use your contact and loss details for that purpose only.

## How We Share Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share details about your loss only with your authorization, typically to coordinate with an insurance adjuster, carrier, or another contractor working on the same property. We may also disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid subpoena.

## Retention

We keep contact form submissions, estimates, and job-related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to support the project, respond to insurance or warranty questions, and meet our own recordkeeping practices. We periodically remove records that are no longer needed for these purposes.

## Your Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request what personal information we hold about you and to request its deletion, subject to our legitimate business and legal recordkeeping needs. Because Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration operates exclusively in the United States and does not knowingly collect data from visitors in the European Union, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) generally does not apply to us, but we are glad to answer questions from any visitor about how their information is handled.

To make a request about your information, whether to review, correct, or delete it, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## Cookies and Analytics

Our site may use standard analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our pages and to improve load times and navigation. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so will not affect your ability to call or email us directly.
