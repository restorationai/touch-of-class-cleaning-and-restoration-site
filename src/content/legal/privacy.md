## Information We Collect

When you reach out to Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration through our website, we collect the information you choose to give us: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and any details you share about the damage or service you need. If you call or email us directly, we keep a record of that conversation for scheduling and documentation purposes. Like most websites, ours also uses basic analytics tools that log general visitor activity, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors found the site. This analytics data does not identify you personally.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, schedule estimates and job walkthroughs, and prepare documentation related to your restoration or cleaning project, including photos, notes, and scope of work. If your claim involves an insurance carrier, we may use your contact and property information to prepare the paperwork and communication needed to support that claim. Analytics data helps us understand how people use the site so we can keep it useful and easy to navigate.

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information with your insurance carrier or adjuster only when you've authorized us to coordinate directly with them on your claim. We may also share limited information with subcontractors or vendors directly involved in completing your project, such as a disposal service or equipment supplier, but only as needed to perform the work. We do not share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions, job records, and related correspondence for as long as reasonably necessary to support the service we provided, respond to insurance or warranty questions, and meet our recordkeeping obligations as a licensed contractor in Texas. If you'd like us to delete information we hold about you sooner, contact us and we'll do so unless we're required to keep it for legal or insurance-related reasons.

## Your Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we've collected about you, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of any sale of personal information, which we do not engage in. Because Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration operates exclusively in Texas and does not knowingly collect data from visitors in the European Union, the GDPR generally does not apply to us, but we're happy to address privacy questions from any visitor in good faith. To make a request regarding your information, reach out using the contact details below.
