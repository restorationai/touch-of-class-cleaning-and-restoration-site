## Information We Collect

When you reach out to Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration through our contact form, by phone, or by email, we collect what you give us voluntarily: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or service you're asking about. If you later work with us on a loss, we may also collect documentation tied to that job, such as photos of the affected areas or insurance claim details you choose to share. Our website also uses basic analytics tools that record general visit data, such as which pages were viewed and how visitors found the site. We don't use this analytics data to identify individual visitors.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule estimates and job walkthroughs, and prepare documentation for insurance claims when a loss is covered. If you ask us to coordinate directly with your insurance carrier or property manager, we use your contact and property details for that purpose only. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing campaigns or pass it along to third parties for their own promotional use.

## How We Share Information

We share your information only where it's necessary to do the work you've asked us to do. That typically means your insurance carrier or adjuster, but only with your authorization, and only the details relevant to your claim. We don't sell, rent, or trade personal information to data brokers or marketers. Outside of coordinating your service or claim, or where required by law, your information stays with us.

## Data Retention

We keep contact form submissions and project records for as long as reasonably needed to support the service relationship, respond to follow-up questions, and maintain records for insurance or accounting purposes. After that, information is deleted or archived in a way that limits ongoing access.

## Your Rights

If you're a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to request its deletion. Because we're a Texas-based contractor serving clients within the state, the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) generally does not apply to our operations, but we're glad to answer questions about how your data is handled regardless of where you're located. To make a request about your information, whether to review, correct, or delete it, contact us using the details below.
