**Water heater leaking or burst in Richwood?** Call now for emergency service before the water finds its way into your subfloor, drywall, and the closet or garage wall behind the tank. A failed water heater can push out dozens of gallons before anyone notices, and that water doesn't stay where it lands.

## What water heater flood cleanup actually involves

A water heater failure is rarely a clean, contained puddle. Whether the tank split at the seam, the pressure relief valve let go, or the bottom simply rusted through, water typically runs under the unit, soaks into the subfloor, and wicks into adjoining drywall and baseboards before it's ever noticed. In homes with the water heater in a closet or interior utility space, that moisture can sit behind the wall for hours unseen. In pier-and-beam homes common throughout older parts of Richwood, water can also drop straight through the floor into the crawlspace, where it's easy to miss entirely.

The work itself starts with stopping the source: shutting the water supply and, if it's gas, the gas valve, or de-energizing an electric unit before anyone steps into standing water. From there it's extraction, moisture mapping with a meter (not a guess), and targeted drying of the materials that actually got wet, not the whole room by default. Depending on the age of the tank and how long the leak ran before discovery, this can be a one-day job or a multi-day drying cycle with daily moisture checks.

## Our process

1. **Shut down the source.** Confirm water supply, gas, or electrical is off to the unit before any cleanup begins, and check for secondary hazards like a tripped breaker sitting in wet insulation.
2. **Extract standing water.** Pull free water from flooring, the base of cabinets, and any low spots before it has more time to spread or soak deeper into subfloor material.
3. **Open and inspect affected materials.** Baseboards, drywall, and flooring near the unit get checked with a moisture meter, not just a visual look, since water heater leaks often travel further than the visible wet mark suggests.
4. **Set equipment and map moisture daily.** Air movers and dehumidifiers go where the readings say they're needed, and we track drying progress day by day rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.
5. **Document for your insurance file.** Photos, moisture logs, and a written scope of the affected materials, which matters if subrogation against the tank manufacturer or installer ever comes up.

## What separates a good water heater flood response from a bad one

The most common mistake is treating a water heater leak like a simple mop-and-fan job. Operators who skip moisture mapping often miss water that's traveled under vinyl flooring or into the wall cavity behind the unit, which is exactly the kind of hidden moisture that turns into a mold problem within a day or two in a humid climate like the Gulf Coast. Adjusters reviewing these claims typically want to see the age and model of the failed tank, the duration of the leak if it's known, and documented moisture readings, not just a invoice for drying equipment. Materials that read dry on the surface but stay damp underneath, like the particleboard sometimes used under vinyl in older Richwood-area homes, are a frequent point where corners get cut.

## What does water heater flood cleanup cost?

Costs depend heavily on how long the water sat before discovery, what materials it touched, and whether the unit was gas or electric. Every loss is different, which is why Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration provides a written scope of work before starting anything. Richwood's humid Gulf Coast climate means any water a failed tank leaves behind has less time before conditions favor mold growth, typically within 24 to 48 hours on wet drywall paper.
