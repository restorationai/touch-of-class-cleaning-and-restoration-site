## Restoration emergencies common in Wylie

North Texas clay soil expands and contracts hard through Wylie's wet springs and dry summers, and that movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing lines running under them. A hairline slab crack or a shifted supply line can leak for weeks before a homeowner notices a soft spot in the flooring. Summer storms off the Blackland Prairie bring heavy, fast rainfall that overwhelms grading around newer subdivisions, while spring hail and high wind events common to this part of the state are a regular driver of roof damage and the water intrusion that follows. Humidity swings between seasons also mean a water event that isn't dried quickly has a real head start on mold colonization, especially in homes built tight for energy efficiency.
