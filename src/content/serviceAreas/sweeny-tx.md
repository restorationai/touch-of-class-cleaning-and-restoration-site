## Restoration emergencies common in Sweeny

Sweeny sits on the Texas Gulf Coast plain, which means two things work against homes and buildings here at once: heavy seasonal rainfall and humidity that rarely lets up. Slab foundations common to the area shift on expansive clay soil, opening hairline cracks that let groundwater seep in slowly, often unnoticed until flooring starts to cup or baseboards darken. Tropical storm systems moving off the Gulf can stall over Brazoria County and drop rain faster than yard drainage or municipal systems can carry it away, which is when crawlspaces and low-lying rooms take on water fast. Add in the region's year-round humidity, and any delay in drying out a wet wall cavity or subfloor gives mold a real head start, often within 48 to 72 hours of the initial water event.
