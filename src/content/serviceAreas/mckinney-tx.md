## Restoration emergencies common in McKinney

North Texas swings hard between drought and downpour, and McKinney's clay-heavy soil amplifies both. Long dry stretches shrink the ground around foundations, then a fast-moving spring or fall storm saturates it again, and that cycle stresses slab foundations and the plumbing running through them. Pipe breaks under slabs, roof and flashing failures after hail, and supply line splits following a hard freeze are the calls we see most. Summer humidity turns any unresolved leak, whether from a roof, a water heater, or a slow slab leak, into a mold problem within a few days if the wet materials aren't pulled out and the space isn't dried down properly.

## Services we provide in McKinney

Water damage restoration is the service McKinney calls us for most, and it's almost always tied to a slab leak, a failed supply line, or storm water finding its way past roofing or grading. We contain the area, remove affected materials, and address the moisture source, not just the visible growth.

Biohazard and trauma cleanup is handled with the same documentation and care as our other services, following accepted safety protocols for affected materials and disposal.
