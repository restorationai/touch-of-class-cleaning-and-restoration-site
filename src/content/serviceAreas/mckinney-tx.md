## Restoration emergencies common in McKinney

McKinney's growth has produced two very different building populations side by side: a historic core with homes built in the early-to-mid 1900s and a much larger ring of subdivisions built from the 1990s onward. Older homes bring aging plumbing and roof systems that fail quietly behind plaster or brick veneer. Newer construction brings PVC and PEX supply lines that generally hold up well, but also slab foundations poured on the area's expansive clay, which shifts with wet and dry cycles and stresses pipe joints and foundation penetrations over time. North Texas also delivers hard spring thunderstorms with hail and straight-line wind, plus the occasional deep winter freeze that splits exposed or poorly insulated pipes. Each of those events shows up on our service calls in a predictable way: water under flooring, water-stained ceilings after a hailstorm compromises roofing, or a frozen hose bib that floods a garage or utility room overnight.

## Services we provide in McKinney

**Water damage restoration.** Whether it's a slab leak pushing moisture up through carpet pad or a supply line failure in an upstairs bathroom, we extract standing water, map moisture with meters, and dry structural materials per the IICRC S500 drying standard rather than guessing by feel.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration.** Smoke residue behaves differently on brick, painted drywall, and the engineered wood trim common in newer McKinney builds. We clean and deodorize surface by surface instead of applying one blanket treatment.

**Mold remediation.** Humidity trapped behind a slow leak or a poorly vented bathroom will colonize drywall and framing within days in this climate. We contain the affected area, remove compromised material, and verify conditions before closing the job back up.

**Storm damage response.** After hail or wind events, we address water intrusion through compromised roofing and siding and coordinate the drying work that needs to happen before repairs are scheduled.

**Biohazard cleanup.** Trauma, sewage backup, and other biohazard situations get handled with the containment and disposal protocols the work requires, not treated as an extension of a standard cleaning job.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in McKinney

Much of McKinney's housing was built in two waves: an older core from the early-to-mid 20th century and a much larger volume of slab-on-grade construction from the 1990s through today. Older homes may still carry galvanized supply lines, older cast iron drain runs, or lead paint on original trim, all of which change how a restoration crew has to plan demolition and disposal. Newer homes are typically slab-on-grade with PEX or copper supply lines, but they sit on the Blackland Prairie clay common across Collin County, soil that expands and contracts significantly with moisture swings and puts ongoing stress on foundations and slab penetrations. That movement is a quiet contributor to the slow leaks that turn into larger mold or structural problems if they go unnoticed. Structural repair and rebuild work typically requires a permit through the City of McKinney's building department, and many of the newer subdivisions carry HOA architectural requirements on top of that, both of which affect how quickly repairs can move forward after the drying and remediation work is done.
