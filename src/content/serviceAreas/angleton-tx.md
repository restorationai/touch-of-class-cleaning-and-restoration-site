Angleton sits in the Gulf coastal plain of Brazoria County, which means the restoration calls here follow a predictable pattern. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Angleton

Much of Angleton's housing stock dates from the mid-20th century through the 1980s, with a mix of slab-on-grade ranch homes and older pier-and-beam construction closer to downtown. Pier-and-beam crawlspaces trap humidity and are prone to wood rot and mold after any water intrusion, while slab homes hide plumbing leaks until flooring or baseboards show damage. Plumbing from that era often means galvanized supply lines or early copper, both of which are past or approaching the point where leaks become more frequent. Brazoria County's clay-heavy soil expands and contracts with moisture swings, which stresses foundations and slab joints over time and contributes to the cracks that let water find its way in. Structural repair or rebuild work in Angleton requires a permit through the city's building department, and flood-zone considerations are worth checking given the area's proximity to Gulf coast drainage patterns before any major reconstruction begins.
