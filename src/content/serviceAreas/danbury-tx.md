## Restoration emergencies common in Danbury

Danbury sits on the flat Gulf Coastal plain of Brazoria County, which means standing water doesn't drain the way it would on sloped terrain further inland. Heavy rain events and tropical systems that move through the region during hurricane season can saturate yards and crawlspaces for days, and slab foundations sitting on expansive clay soil are prone to settling and cracking as that clay swells and shrinks with moisture. Combine that with the area's humid subtropical summers, and any water intrusion, whether from a roof leak, a burst supply line, or storm surge, has a short window before mold starts colonizing wet drywall and framing. Older homes in the area also carry aging plumbing that fails without much warning, which is a common driver of the water calls we see from this part of the county.
