## Restoration emergencies common in Pearland

Pearland sits on flat Gulf Coastal Plain terrain with heavy clay soil that drains slowly, which means standing water from a burst pipe, roof leak, or heavy rain event often has nowhere to go but into slab foundations and crawlspaces. The region's long, humid summers accelerate mold growth once materials stay wet, sometimes within 24 to 48 hours. Tropical storm season brings wind-driven rain that finds its way through roof flashing and window seals, and the area's history of major rainfall events means homeowners are often dealing with both standing water and the anxiety of whether it will happen again. Slab-on-grade construction, common throughout the city, complicates water intrusion because moisture can wick up through concrete and sit unseen under flooring for days.
