Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration is based in Richardson, TX, and schedules jobs across the surrounding North Texas communities. Call (979) 418-6099 to describe what's going on at the property, and we'll let you know directly whether your address falls inside our standard coverage or if we need to work out logistics for a farther site. Either way, a phone call gets you a straight answer faster than guessing from a map.

## Where we work

Our crews run out of Richardson and cover the surrounding Dallas-Fort Worth metro, with regular jobs across Collin, Dallas, and Denton counties. That footprint includes dense older neighborhoods with slab foundations and aging plumbing, newer suburban builds still under original warranty, and everything in between. Property type and construction era change how a loss behaves, which is part of why we like to talk through the specifics before showing up.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Richardson, places like Garland, Plano, and the rest of the near-metro, get scheduled first simply because travel time is shortest. From there, coverage extends out across the wider DFW metroplex, where arrival order depends on current job load and how far the crew has to drive through metro traffic. At the outer edge of our service area, we still take calls, but we're upfront that scheduling may take a bit longer to line up given the distance. We'd rather tell you that plainly than promise a window we can't hit.

## Don't see your city?

If your address isn't listed among our service-area pages, that doesn't automatically mean we can't help. Larger losses, especially water and fire damage that's spreading or getting worse by the hour, are often worth a phone call regardless of exact location. Tell us where you are and what you're dealing with, and we'll tell you honestly whether we can get a crew out or point you toward someone who can.
