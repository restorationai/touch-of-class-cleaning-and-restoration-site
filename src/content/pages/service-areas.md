## Where we work

Our home base in Richwood sits in Brazoria County, on the flat coastal plain south of Houston. From there we reach the cluster of cities that share the same drainage, soil, and housing stock, places like Lake Jackson, Clute, Freeport, and Angleton, along with the smaller communities that sit between them. The region's humid Gulf Coast climate and heavy clay soil mean water intrusion and moisture problems behave similarly from one town to the next, which is part of why a single local crew can cover this stretch efficiently.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Richwood get first position in our scheduling because there's less drive time between jobs. As you move outward toward the edges of Brazoria County and into neighboring areas, we still take the work, but plan for a slightly longer wait between the call and the truck pulling up, especially if Highway 288 or FM 523 traffic is heavy at the time. For locations well outside our usual radius, we're upfront on the phone about where you'll fall in the queue rather than guessing at a number we can't back up.
