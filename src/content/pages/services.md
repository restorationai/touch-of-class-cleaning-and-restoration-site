Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration handles the full arc of property damage, from the first call after a pipe bursts through the last coat of paint on a rebuilt wall. The team works with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial building owners across Richardson and the surrounding North Texas communities. Below is a quick map of what falls under each category so you can find the right page, or just call and describe what's happening.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage from a burst pipe, failed appliance hose, or roof leak needs fast extraction before it travels into subfloors, drywall, and framing. Fire and smoke damage bring their own urgency: soot is acidic and keeps etching into finishes the longer it sits, and smoke odor works its way into porous materials like drywall and insulation. Biohazard situations, whether from an unattended death, sewage backup, or other contamination, require careful containment and disposal handled with the right protective equipment. Storm damage across North Texas often means wind-driven rain, hail impact, or downed limbs that compromise a roof and let water in behind it. Call during business hours to get a crew scheduled for any of these.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once the damaged materials are out and the structure is dry, the rebuild starts: framing, drywall, flooring, paint, trim, and cabinetry brought back to pre-loss condition. Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration also takes on general contracting work tied to a restoration project, from permitting coordination to finish selections, so the same team that stabilized the damage is the one that closes it out. Keeping mitigation and reconstruction under one roof cuts down on the handoffs and delays that happen when separate contractors have to coordinate schedules and scope.

## Specialty services

Not every call starts with visible damage. Mold inspection matters when a home has had any history of moisture, a musty smell that won't go away, or visible growth on drywall, baseboards, or in a crawlspace. Odor removal addresses smoke, pet, or mildew smells that linger after the obvious damage is gone, using equipment suited to the source rather than just masking it. Emergency board-up and tarping services secure a property against weather and intrusion right after a storm, fire, or break-in, buying time until permanent repairs can begin.

## One call, start to finish

The reason mitigation and reconstruction live under one company is simple: a property moving from an emergency call to a finished repair shouldn't require juggling multiple contractors, multiple estimates, and multiple schedules. Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration manages that handoff internally, so the documentation from the mitigation phase carries straight into the rebuild.
