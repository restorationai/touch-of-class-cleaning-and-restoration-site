Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration handles the full span of property damage work in Richwood and the surrounding Brazoria County communities, from the first hour of water extraction through the final coat of paint after rebuild. Homeowners, property managers, and commercial building owners call for the same reason: a problem that started small is spreading, and they need one team that can see it through start to finish rather than juggling separate contractors for cleanup, repairs, and insurance paperwork.

## Emergency restoration services

When a pipe bursts behind a wall, a kitchen fire leaves soot on every surface, or a storm tears through roofing and siding, timing matters more than anything else. Our emergency restoration services cover water damage and flood extraction, fire and smoke damage cleanup, biohazard and trauma scene cleanup, and storm damage response including emergency tarping and board-up. Standing water left under flooring or carpet pad can support mold growth within days, and smoke residue sets deeper into porous materials the longer it sits, so call as soon as it's safe to do so and we'll get a crew scheduled.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction brings the property back to a livable or workable condition. Once materials are dried, cleaned, or removed, our reconstruction and general contracting crews handle framing, drywall, flooring, painting, and the trade coordination needed to close out permits and inspections. Because the same company oversees both phases, there's no gap where a homeowner has to find a second contractor to interpret someone else's demolition work, and nothing gets rebuilt until the moisture or contamination issue underneath it is actually resolved.

## Specialty services

Not every call is a flooded basement or a fire scene. We also handle mold inspection and remediation, odor removal after smoke or biohazard events, and emergency board-up or tarping to secure a property between the damage and the repair. These jobs often get missed by general contractors who aren't set up to assess mold growth or seal a structure against weather and trespassers, but they matter just as much to the long-term condition of the building.

## One call, one company

Property damage rarely stays in one lane. Water damage leads to mold. Fire damage leads to reconstruction. Storm damage can lead to both. Working with one company across mitigation and rebuild means one point of contact, one set of documentation for your insurance carrier, and a crew that already knows the property's history when the next phase starts.
