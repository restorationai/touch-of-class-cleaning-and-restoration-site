Mold Solutionz 24/7 was founded in 2017 and is based out of Cerritos, California. The company was built around a single, unglamorous reality: mold and water damage don't wait for business hours, and most homeowners have no idea what to do when they find a wall that's been wet for longer than it should have been. Since opening, the focus has stayed narrow on purpose — moisture intrusion, mold assessment and remediation, and the water damage that almost always precedes it.

## What we do

On a typical day, that means answering calls from homeowners who've just pulled back a bathroom vanity and found black growth behind the drywall, or from property managers dealing with a slow leak that went unnoticed for weeks. It means walking through a house with a moisture meter before anyone touches a wall, documenting conditions for an insurance adjuster, and explaining to a family why their belongings need to be moved out of a room before containment barriers go up. The work is methodical and detail-driven — the difference between a remediation that holds and one that doesn't usually comes down to what happens in the first few hours on-site. Because the company operates around the clock, those first hours don't have to stretch into the next morning.

A significant portion of the work runs through homeowners' insurance. That means preparing photo documentation, moisture readings, and scope-of-loss reports that adjusters can actually use — not just handing a homeowner a bill and wishing them luck with their carrier.

## Our certifications and what they mean

The lead technician holds IICRC Water Restoration Technician certification (WRT #70145645). The IICRC — Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — is the body that sets the technical standards the restoration industry runs on. The S500 standard for water damage and the S520 standard for mold remediation aren't suggestions; they're the benchmarks that define what proper drying protocols, containment procedures, and clearance testing look like. Holding WRT certification means the work is performed against those documented standards, which matters when an insurance adjuster or a future home inspector is reviewing what was done.

## Where we work

The primary service area runs through Los Angeles and Orange counties — Cerritos, Lakewood, Long Beach, Downey, Norwalk, Bellflower, Cypress, Buena Park, Anaheim, and the surrounding communities. This stretch of Southern California sits in a semi-arid climate, but the housing stock tells a different story: older slab-on-grade construction, stucco exteriors that can trap moisture, and irrigation systems that run year-round. Plumbing failures, slow slab leaks, and condensation issues inside poorly ventilated crawl spaces are common calls. The team knows the building types in this region and the conditions that tend to produce recurring moisture problems.
