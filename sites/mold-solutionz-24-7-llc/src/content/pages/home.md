## Services we handle

**Mold Inspection** — A trained technician walks the property, identifies visible growth, checks moisture readings behind walls and under flooring, and maps out exactly where the problem starts and stops — so you're not guessing.

**Mold Testing** — Air and surface samples go to an accredited third-party lab. Results tell you which species are present and at what concentration, giving you documentation that matters for insurance claims and real-estate transactions.

**Mold Remediation** — Affected materials are contained, removed, and treated following established industry protocols. We don't mask odors or paint over staining — we remove the source.

**Post-Remediation Verification** — After the work is done, clearance testing confirms spore counts are back within normal range. You get a written report you can hand to your insurer, landlord, or buyer.
