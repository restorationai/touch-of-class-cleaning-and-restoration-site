Mold doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we. Mold Solutionz 24/7 dispatches from Cerritos around the clock — whether you're calling at noon on a Tuesday or 3 a.m. on a Sunday, a live team member picks up and gets a crew moving. Coverage spans a wide band of Los Angeles and Orange County communities, and the cities listed on this page each have their own dedicated page with local detail.

## Where we work

Our base in Cerritos sits near the LA/Orange County line, which puts us within a practical drive of dozens of cities across the South Bay, the Gateway Cities corridor, north Orange County, and the San Gabriel Valley. That central position means we're rarely far from a job — whether the address is in a mid-century ranch home in Lakewood, a condo complex in Anaheim, or a commercial property in Long Beach. Southern California's marine layer and periodic heavy rain events create the kind of persistent moisture that feeds mold growth inside walls and under flooring, so we stay familiar with the building stock and climate patterns across the whole region.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Cerritos — Norwalk, Downey, Bellflower, Lakewood, La Palma, Artesia, Hawaiian Gardens — are typically our fastest arrivals. From there, coverage fans out to Long Beach, Torrance, and Carson to the west; Whittier, La Mirada, and Buena Park to the east; and Anaheim, Fullerton, and Garden Grove deeper into Orange County. The outer edges of our regular service area include communities in the San Gabriel Valley and parts of the South Bay, where freeway conditions on the 605, 91, and 710 corridors can affect arrival order during peak traffic windows. We're straightforward about that — if you're calling during a rush-hour window, we'll give you an honest estimate rather than a number we can't keep.

## Don't see your city?

The list on this page covers our most frequently served locations, but it isn't a hard boundary. For significant mold losses — large square footage, commercial properties, multi-unit buildings — we regularly dispatch to cities outside the standard list. Call (714) 409-4758 and describe the situation. If we can get there responsibly, we will.
