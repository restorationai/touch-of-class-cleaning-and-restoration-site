Mold doesn't wait for business hours, and neither does the dispatch line. Mold Solutionz 24/7 takes calls around the clock and schedules crews from its Cerritos base to address active mold problems, moisture intrusion, and post-remediation testing across the greater Los Angeles and Orange County region. Whether you're calling at 2 a.m. or Sunday afternoon, a real person picks up and gets the process moving.

## Where we work

The team is headquartered in Cerritos, which sits at the crossroads of Los Angeles and Orange counties — a location that puts a large stretch of Southern California within practical reach. Coverage runs west toward the South Bay beach cities, north into the San Gabriel Valley and Whittier area, east into the Inland Empire corridor, and south through Orange County into communities along the 405 and 5 corridors. The region's mix of older stucco homes, mid-century ranch houses, and newer construction each carries its own moisture vulnerability — from flat roofs that pond water during winter rains to slab foundations that wick groundwater in low-lying areas near the Los Angeles River basin.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Cerritos — Norwalk, Lakewood, Bellflower, Downey, Artesia, Hawaiian Gardens, Long Beach, and Cypress — receive the fastest crew arrivals under normal traffic conditions. The next ring out includes Torrance, Carson, Compton, Whittier, La Mirada, Buena Park, Anaheim, and Fullerton. Further out, the team regularly serves Pasadena, El Monte, Pomona, Santa Ana, Irvine, and the broader Orange County interior.

Southern California traffic is a real variable. A job in Irvine at 10 a.m. on a weekday takes longer to reach than the same address at 7 a.m. Dispatch accounts for current conditions when giving you an honest arrival estimate — you'll get a realistic window, not an optimistic one.

## Don't see your city?

The listed service areas cover the most common dispatch zones, but they aren't a hard boundary. For larger losses — significant mold colonization after a slow leak, post-flood remediation, or commercial properties — the crew will travel beyond the standard footprint. Call **(714) 409-4758** and describe the situation. If it's within reach, the answer is almost always yes.
