From a burst pipe flooding a Cerritos living room at 2 a.m. to a commercial kitchen fire in a Long Beach strip mall, Mold Solutionz 24/7 handles the full arc of property damage — emergency mitigation, structural drying, mold remediation, and complete reconstruction. We work with single-family homes, rental properties, apartment complexes, HOAs, and commercial buildings throughout the greater Los Angeles and Orange County area.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, every hour matters. Water intrusion can saturate wall cavities and subfloor assemblies within hours; mold colonization can begin on wet drywall in as little as 24 to 48 hours; smoke residue bonds to porous surfaces and keeps off-gassing long after the flames are out. The emergency side of the business covers **water damage restoration** (extraction, structural drying, moisture mapping), **fire and smoke damage restoration** (soot removal, content pack-out, deodorization), **mold remediation** (containment, removal, and post-clearance testing coordination), **biohazard and trauma cleanup** (handled with full personal protective protocols and proper waste disposal), and **storm and wind damage response** (debris removal, emergency tarping, water intrusion control). Our IICRC WRT-certified team is available around the clock — call (714) 409-4758 and a technician can be dispatched any hour of the day or night.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure has passed drying verification, the rebuild phase begins — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, paint, and finish work. Handling both phases under one contractor means no gap between the remediation crew leaving and a separate GC arriving weeks later, no finger-pointing over scope, and a single point of contact for your insurance adjuster. We also take on general contracting projects that are not damage-related: bathroom and kitchen remodels, additions, and deferred maintenance work that homeowners often bundle with an insurance repair.

## Specialty services

Some situations do not fit neatly into an emergency or a rebuild. **Mold inspections and air quality assessments** give property owners a documented baseline before a purchase, after a leak, or when occupants are reporting musty odors without a visible source. **Odor removal and hydroxyl treatment** address persistent smells from smoke, pets, or long-term moisture that survive standard cleaning. **Board-up and property securing** protects a structure immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism until full restoration can begin. **Contents cleaning and pack-out** moves salvageable belongings off-site for cleaning and climate-controlled storage during active remediation.

The advantage of working with a single company across all of these phases is straightforward: the same team that extracts standing water from your subfloor writes the scope for replacing it. There is no handoff delay, no version conflict between the mitigation report and the rebuild estimate, and no second contractor who was not there to see the original damage. If you are not sure which service applies to your situation, call (714) 409-4758 — we can walk through it with you and get the right crew scheduled.
