From a burst pipe flooding a Cerritos condo at 2 a.m. to a commercial kitchen fire in Long Beach that shuts down operations mid-week, property damage rarely waits for a convenient moment. Mold Solutionz 24/7 handles the full arc of that damage — emergency mitigation, structural drying, mold remediation, and complete reconstruction — for homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial tenants across the greater Los Angeles and Orange County area.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still settling, or a hazardous material is still present, the clock matters. Water damage response focuses on stopping the source, extracting standing water, and getting structural materials dry before microbial growth can take hold — mold can begin colonizing wet drywall and subfloor within 24 to 48 hours in Southern California's climate. Fire and smoke damage work goes beyond the char: smoke odor penetrates wall cavities, HVAC ductwork, and soft goods in ways that require chemical treatment and controlled ventilation, not just surface cleaning. Biohazard cleanup — sewage intrusion, trauma scenes, hoarding situations — follows strict containment and disposal protocols to protect occupants and technicians alike. Storm damage response addresses wind-driven water intrusion, roof breaches, and debris impact, which in this region often means sudden heavy rain overwhelming older drainage systems in neighborhoods built before modern grading codes.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction gives you your property back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure has passed clearance, the rebuild phase begins — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Handling both phases under one roof means the crew that documented what came out knows exactly what needs to go back in, which eliminates the coordination gap that typically delays timelines and inflates costs when a homeowner has to hire a separate general contractor. Reconstruction services are available for single-family homes, multi-unit residential buildings, and commercial spaces.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an active emergency. Mold inspections and air quality assessments are available for buyers, sellers, and landlords who need documentation before a transaction closes or a tenant moves in. Odor removal — from smoke, pets, mildew, or other embedded sources — uses hydroxyl and ozone treatment rather than masking agents. Board-up and tarping services secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism damage, preventing secondary loss while the scope of work is being assessed.
