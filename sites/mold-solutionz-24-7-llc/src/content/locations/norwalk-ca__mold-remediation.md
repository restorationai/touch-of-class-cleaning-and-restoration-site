Norwalk sits in a low-lying stretch of southeastern Los Angeles County where marine layer moisture from the coast lingers well into the morning hours for much of the year. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Norwalk

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping — we use thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to trace the moisture plume from its source outward, because what's visible on a wall surface is rarely the full picture. Once the scope is defined, we establish negative-air containment barriers using 6-mil poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running at negative pressure, which prevents disturbed spores from migrating to unaffected rooms. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood sheathing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per California Department of Public Health guidelines for mold-contaminated debris. Structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, wire-brushed where needed, and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial encapsulant. Drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient conditions — runs until moisture readings return to the baseline range for the specific material type, per the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. We document every reading and every step with photographs, which matters both for your peace of mind and for the insurance claim file.
