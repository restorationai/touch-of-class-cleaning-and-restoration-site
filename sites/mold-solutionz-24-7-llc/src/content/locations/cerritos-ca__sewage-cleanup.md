## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Cerritos

Every sewage cleanup job follows a defined sequence because the order of operations matters — sanitizing before extraction traps contamination rather than removing it.

**Step 1 — Containment and safety assessment.** The crew establishes a containment perimeter, cuts power to affected circuits if standing water is present, and identifies the source. If the lateral is still active, we coordinate with the homeowner to get the line snaked or capped before extraction begins.

**Step 2 — Extraction and gross debris removal.** Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing Category 3 water and solid waste from the structure. Hard flooring, baseboards, and any saturated drywall below the flood line are documented and removed — porous materials that have contacted raw sewage cannot be dried in place.

**Step 3 — EPA-registered disinfection.** After extraction, all affected surfaces receive a hospital-grade, EPA-registered disinfectant application. We allow full dwell time per the product label before any drying equipment is placed — a step that is sometimes skipped by crews rushing to look productive.

**Step 4 — Structural drying.** Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned per the IICRC S500 standard. Moisture readings are logged at each visit until the structure reaches documented dry standard.

**Step 5 — Post-remediation verification.** A final inspection with a moisture meter and ATP surface testing confirms contamination levels are within acceptable range before equipment is removed.
