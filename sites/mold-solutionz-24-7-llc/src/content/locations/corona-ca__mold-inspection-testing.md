## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Corona

Every inspection starts with a moisture survey using a calibrated pin-type and non-invasive moisture meter to map wet zones behind walls, under flooring, and in ceiling cavities — no guesswork, no tearing out drywall before we know where the problem actually is. From there, we collect air samples in each affected room and establish an outdoor baseline control sample, because indoor spore counts only mean something when compared against what's naturally present in Corona's air on that specific day. Surface swab or tape-lift samples are taken from any visible growth or staining to identify the mold genus — important information if the property involves a sensitive occupant, an insurance claim, or a pending real estate transaction.

All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory, and we provide a written report with spore counts, species identification, and moisture readings. That documentation is formatted to meet the requirements most insurance carriers and real estate escrow officers request, so you are not chasing paperwork after the fact.
