## Why Cerritos Properties Are Prone to Hidden Mold

Cerritos was largely developed between 1956 and 1980, and a significant portion of the housing stock in the 90703 ZIP code reflects the construction norms of that era: fiberglass batt insulation tucked against exterior walls, paper-faced drywall that wicks moisture readily, and HVAC systems that recirculate air through the whole house. When a supply line fails under a kitchen sink or a slow roof leak goes unnoticed through a wet winter, those materials become ideal substrates for Cladosporium, Penicillium, and Aspergillus — the mold genera most commonly identified in Southern California indoor air quality testing.

The city's proximity to the Los Cerritos Wetlands and the low-lying drainage corridors that run toward the San Gabriel River means groundwater can intrude through slab cracks during extended rain periods, something that catches homeowners off guard in a region where rain is infrequent enough to feel like a non-issue. Crawl spaces and utility rooms in older Cerritos homes often show elevated relative humidity readings even in dry months, simply because the soil beneath retains moisture year-round.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Cerritos

A thorough mold assessment starts before any equipment comes out of the case. The inspection begins with a visual walkthrough of the property — attic, crawl space, bathrooms, laundry area, and any room with a documented or suspected water history. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras map the extent of elevated readings behind surfaces that look dry to the eye.

When visual findings or elevated moisture readings warrant it, air sampling and surface sampling are collected and sent to an accredited third-party laboratory. Air cassette samples capture the concentration and species of mold spores present indoors compared to an outdoor baseline — a critical comparison that helps distinguish a normal ambient spore count from an active amplification site inside the structure. Surface swab or tape-lift samples identify the specific mold genus on a stained ceiling tile or discolored wall cavity. Results typically return within 24 to 48 hours, and the written report documents findings, lab data, and recommended next steps in plain language.
