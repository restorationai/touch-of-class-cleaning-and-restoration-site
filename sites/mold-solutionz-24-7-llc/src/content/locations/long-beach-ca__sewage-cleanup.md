## Why Long Beach Properties See Sewage Backup More Often

The City of Long Beach maintains a combined sewer system in many older corridors, meaning stormwater and sanitary waste share the same underground pipes. During heavy El Niño rain events — the kind that hit the South Bay hard in 2023 and 2024 — those shared mains can surcharge and push sewage backward through floor drains and toilets in lower-lying homes. Neighborhoods closer to the Los Angeles River flood control channel, as well as blocks near the Port of Long Beach where soil liquefaction and ground settlement are ongoing concerns, see accelerated pipe joint separation over time. That slow cracking lets root intrusion and sediment buildup choke lines that were already undersized for today's household water use.

Older bungalows throughout Long Beach — particularly those built before 1955 — frequently still have original Orangeburg or cast-iron drain lines. Orangeburg pipe, made from compressed wood pulp and pitch, softens and collapses over decades, and no amount of snaking fixes a pipe that has lost its round cross-section. When those lines fail, the backup is sudden and the contamination is severe.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Long Beach

Every job follows the IICRC S520 and S500 standards for contaminated water removal. Here is what happens from the moment we arrive:

**1. Source confirmation and containment.** We verify the backup is stopped — either by your plumber or by our team coordinating with one — before extraction begins. Pumping sewage out of a room while the line is still pressurized just moves the problem.

**2. Category 3 extraction.** Truck-mounted extraction units remove standing sewage and saturated materials. Porous flooring — hardwood, carpet, laminate — is almost always non-salvageable under Category 3 guidelines and is removed and bagged for proper disposal.

**3. Structural demo as needed.** Drywall wicking lines are cut, subfloor sections are assessed, and any material that absorbed raw sewage is removed. In Long Beach's older homes, this sometimes means dealing with original tongue-and-groove subfloor planks rather than modern plywood, which absorb contamination differently and require more conservative removal margins.

**4. EPA-registered disinfection.** We apply hospital-grade disinfectants to all affected framing, concrete, and hard surfaces. Multiple applications with dwell-time monitoring — not a single spray-and-wipe — are required to bring bacterial counts down to safe levels.

**5. Drying and documentation.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in walls and subfloor reach dry standard. Every reading is logged and photographed for your insurance file.
