## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Norwalk

Every inspection begins with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using calibrated meters that read moisture content in drywall, wood framing, and concrete without cutting into surfaces. We note areas of discoloration, efflorescence on block walls, and any musty or earthy odors — the latter is often the first sign of Cladosporium or Penicillium activity inside wall cavities.

From there, we collect air samples using calibrated spore-trap cassettes in affected rooms and an outdoor baseline sample. The baseline matters: if outdoor spore counts are elevated on a high-wind day, that context changes how we interpret indoor readings. Samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results typically return within 24–48 hours. Depending on what the visual inspection reveals, we may also collect surface swab or bulk material samples to identify species and estimate colony density.

The final deliverable is a written assessment report — not just a lab printout — that documents moisture readings by room, sample locations, laboratory results, and a clear explanation of what the findings mean for your health, your property, and your next steps. That report is formatted to be legible to insurance adjusters and, when relevant, to real estate agents handling disclosure requirements under California law.
