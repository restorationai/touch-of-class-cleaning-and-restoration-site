## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Mold Solutionz 24/7 LLC is committed to making its website accessible to all visitors, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification tools. We believe everyone deserves clear, usable information — whether they're dealing with a mold emergency at 2 a.m. or researching remediation options from a mobile device.

This site is built with the goal of conforming to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** — the widely recognized benchmark for digital accessibility. That means we aim for sufficient color contrast, descriptive link text, properly labeled form fields, and meaningful image alt text throughout the site.

## What We've Done to Support Accessibility

Our site is structured with semantic HTML so that screen readers can navigate headings, landmarks, and content in a logical order. Contact forms include visible labels and error messages that assistive technologies can surface. Images that convey meaningful information include descriptive alt attributes; decorative images are marked to be skipped.

We also aim to ensure the site is fully operable by keyboard alone — no mouse required — so that visitors who cannot use a pointer device can still reach every page, form, and phone link.

## Known Limitations

We're a small remediation company, not a dedicated web development team. Despite our best efforts, some content — particularly third-party embeds, older PDFs, or recently added pages — may not yet meet every WCAG 2.1 AA criterion. We are actively working to identify and resolve these gaps.

If you encounter a barrier that prevents you from accessing information or contacting us, please let us know. We take these reports seriously and will work to address confirmed issues within a reasonable timeframe.

## Third-Party Content

Some pages may include content or tools provided by third parties — such as mapping services or review widgets — over which we have limited control. We encourage those vendors to meet the same accessibility standards we hold ourselves to, and we'll look for accessible alternatives when a third-party component creates a persistent barrier for our visitors.

## Ongoing Effort

Accessibility isn't a one-time checkbox. As we update the site, add new service pages, or change our layout, we review those changes against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria. We welcome feedback at any time — it's one of the most direct ways we can improve the experience for everyone who visits.
