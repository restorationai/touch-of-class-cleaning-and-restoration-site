## Our Commitment to Accessibility

MCC Restoration and Contracting Services is committed to making mccrestoration.com usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** — the widely recognized benchmark for accessible web content — and we treat accessibility as an ongoing responsibility rather than a one-time checkbox.

## What We've Done to Build an Accessible Site

The site is structured with semantic HTML so that screen readers can navigate headings, links, and form fields in a logical order. Images include descriptive alternative text. Color contrast ratios are chosen to remain readable for users with low vision or color-vision differences. Interactive elements — including the contact form and phone links — are reachable and operable by keyboard alone, without requiring a mouse.

We also aim to keep page language clear and plain. Restoration emergencies are stressful enough; the last thing anyone needs is a website that's hard to read or navigate when they're trying to reach us quickly.

## Known Limitations

We recognize that no site is perfectly accessible in every context, and third-party content or tools embedded on the site (such as mapping widgets or analytics scripts) may not fully meet the same standards we hold for our own content. We review these integrations when updates are made and work to minimize gaps where we can.

## How to Report an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter a barrier on this site — a page that doesn't work with your screen reader, a form field that's hard to use, or any other difficulty — please let us know. You can reach us by phone at **(682) 772-9123**. We take these reports seriously and will work to address confirmed issues within a reasonable timeframe, typically within 30 days of a verified report.

If you need information from the site in an alternative format in the meantime, call us directly and we'll provide it over the phone or by another accessible method.

## Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility standards and assistive technologies continue to evolve, and so does this site. We periodically review pages for accessibility as content is updated or new features are added. Our goal is a site that works for every visitor — whether you're a homeowner calling from a phone at 2 a.m. after a pipe burst, or a property manager reviewing our services from a desktop with specialized software.

Questions or feedback about accessibility on this site can be directed to us at **(682) 772-9123** or through the contact form on our website.
