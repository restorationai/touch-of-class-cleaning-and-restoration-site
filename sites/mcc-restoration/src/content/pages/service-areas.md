MCC Restoration and Contracting Services is based in Duncanville, TX, and handles water damage, fire damage, mold remediation, and reconstruction for homeowners and property managers across the greater Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex. To schedule service or ask whether your address falls within our coverage, call **(682) 772-9123** during business hours and a team member will confirm availability and get you on the schedule.

## Where we work

Our crew operates out of Duncanville, which sits near the geographic center of the southern DFW corridor — a practical staging point for reaching communities in Dallas County, Tarrant County, Ellis County, and Johnson County without fighting cross-metroplex traffic. Whether you're dealing with a burst pipe in a 1970s brick ranch in Cedar Hill, smoke damage in a newer build in Mansfield, or a slow roof leak that's turned into a mold problem in a Grand Prairie duplex, the drive from our shop to your door is manageable. The region's clay-heavy soil, aging cast-iron drain lines in older neighborhoods, and the freeze-thaw cycles that catch DFW homeowners off guard every few winters all factor into the kind of damage we see regularly.

## Coverage by distance

The cities closest to Duncanville — Cedar Hill, DeSoto, Lancaster, Glenn Heights, Midlothian, and Mansfield — are typically our fastest arrivals. From there, our team regularly works throughout Dallas, Grand Prairie, Irving, Arlington, Burleson, Waxahachie, and Ennis. For properties further out along the I-20, I-35E, or US-67 corridors, scheduling works the same way — we dispatch based on crew availability and job queue, so the further you are from Duncanville, the more lead time it helps to build in. We don't make clock-time promises we can't keep; what we can tell you is that distance jobs are routed efficiently and you'll know your arrival window before we leave the shop.
