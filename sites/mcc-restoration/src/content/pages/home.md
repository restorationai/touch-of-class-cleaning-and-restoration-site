When water is soaking into your subfloor, smoke smell is embedded in your walls, or you've spotted black mold behind the drywall, you need someone who can assess the damage fast and start fixing it — not schedule you two weeks out. MCC Restoration and Contracting Services handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, storm damage, biohazard cleanup, and full reconstruction for homeowners and property managers in Duncanville and the surrounding DFW area.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in January, a slow leak under the kitchen sink that finally gave out, or flooding from a backed-up drain, standing water starts damaging structural materials within hours and creates conditions for mold growth within 24 to 48. We extract, dry, and document.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Visible char is only part of the problem. Smoke residue travels through HVAC systems and settles into porous materials far from the fire's origin. Restoration means addressing both the structural damage and the odor at its source, not masking it.

**Mold remediation** — North Texas humidity and older housing stock are a reliable combination for hidden mold. We locate the moisture source, contain the affected area, and remove contaminated materials following established industry protocols — because covering mold without fixing the source just delays the problem.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Hail, high winds, and the occasional tornado touch-down are part of life in the Dallas–Fort Worth corridor. From roof damage and broken windows to full interior rebuild after a tree comes through the ceiling, we carry the work from emergency tarping through finished reconstruction.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Restoration jobs go sideways when the company doing the drying hands off to a different contractor for repairs, and nobody owns the full picture. MCC Restoration and Contracting Services handles both sides — mitigation and rebuild — which means one point of contact, one consistent scope of work, and no finger-pointing between trades when something doesn't line up.

We work directly with homeowners' insurance carriers. That means we document damage thoroughly with photos and moisture readings, prepare the scope in a format adjusters recognize, and bill the carrier directly when your policy applies. You shouldn't have to front the full cost of an emergency you didn't cause.

Duncanville's housing stock runs heavily toward homes built in the 1970s through the 1990s — slab foundations, older cast-iron or galvanized supply lines, and HVAC systems that have been patched more than replaced. We've worked in enough of these homes to know where water hides, where mold is likely to colonize after a leak, and what a realistic repair scope looks like before we quote it.
