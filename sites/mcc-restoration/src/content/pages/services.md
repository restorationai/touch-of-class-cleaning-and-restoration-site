From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely arrives on a convenient schedule — and the decisions you make in the first hours matter. MCC Restoration and Contracting Services handles the full arc of damage recovery: emergency mitigation, structural drying, smoke and odor removal, mold assessment, biohazard cleanup, and complete reconstruction. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial tenants across Duncanville and the surrounding DFW communities.

## Emergency restoration services

When something goes wrong fast, the priority is stopping the damage from spreading. Water intrusion — whether from a failed water heater, a slab leak, or a roof breach during one of North Texas's severe thunderstorm seasons — can saturate wall cavities and subfloor assemblies within hours, setting the stage for secondary mold growth within days. Fire and smoke damage leave behind acidic soot that continues etching surfaces long after the flames are out; the smell that lingers in porous materials like drywall and insulation is a sign that residue is still active. Storm damage — hail, wind, fallen trees — often combines structural breaches with water intrusion, requiring both tarping and interior response at the same time. Biohazard situations, from sewage backups to trauma scenes, require careful containment and disposal handled according to applicable safety standards. MCC responds to all of these, with the equipment and process knowledge to stabilize a property before the damage compounds.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the bleeding; reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and clean, MCC handles the rebuild — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, painting, cabinetry, and finish work. Because the same company that documented the damage is doing the rebuild, there are no handoff gaps where scope gets lost between a remediation contractor and a separate GC. For commercial clients, that continuity matters for minimizing downtime. For homeowners, it means a single point of contact from the day of the loss through the final walkthrough.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an emergency, but some problems quietly get worse when ignored. Mold inspections and assessments help property owners understand the scope of a suspected issue before committing to remediation. Persistent odors — from smoke, pets, or moisture — that survive a standard cleaning often require targeted treatment of the source material rather than surface masking. Board-up and roof tarping services secure a property after a break-in, storm breach, or fire until permanent repairs can begin, protecting both the structure and its contents from further exposure.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction under one roof means the process moves in a straight line. There is no waiting for a second contractor to pick up where the first one left off, no duplicated assessments, and no finger-pointing when something in the scope falls between two separate contracts.
