## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Duncanville

The first thing we do on any fire job is a room-by-room scope before any cleaning starts. Soot isn't uniform — protein residues from a kitchen fire behave differently than the wet smoke residue left by a slow-burning upholstered couch fire, and the wrong cleaning chemistry makes permanent staining worse. We identify residue types, document every affected surface with photos, and produce a written scope that your insurance adjuster can work from directly.

Structural assessment comes next. In Duncanville's older ranch homes, we pay close attention to load-bearing walls that may have experienced prolonged heat exposure — wood can lose structural integrity at temperatures well below what causes visible charring. We coordinate with licensed structural engineers when the fire involved any wall or ceiling framing for more than a few minutes.

Odor elimination is where many contractors cut corners, and it's the step homeowners notice most. We use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to neutralize smoke odor molecules embedded in porous materials — not just surface sprays that mask the smell for a few weeks. Ductwork cleaning is included in our scope on every job, because soot-coated HVAC systems will re-contaminate a freshly restored home the first time the system runs.
