## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Duncanville

Every flood cleanup job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through slab, subfloor, wall cavities, and ceiling assemblies — because in a 1960s brick ranch, water rarely stays where it entered. We document everything photographically before any material is moved, which matters when you're filing a claim.

Extraction comes first: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from flooring, subfloor, and crawl spaces. Structural drying follows with industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual square footage and material types in the affected space. Drying logs are recorded daily so the process is defensible to your insurer. Affected materials — saturated drywall, buckled hardwood, soaked insulation — are removed only when moisture readings confirm they cannot be dried in place, keeping demolition targeted rather than wholesale.

Once the structure reads dry, we handle the rebuild: drywall, texture, flooring, trim, and paint matched to existing finishes. You work with one contractor from water on the floor to finished room, which simplifies scheduling and insurance documentation.
