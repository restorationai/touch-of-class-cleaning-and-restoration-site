## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy explains how Desert Valley Contracting Inc collects, uses, and protects information you share when you visit desertvalleycontracting.net or contact us directly. It applies to visitors from any location, including California residents whose rights are addressed below.

## Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form, request an estimate, or call us, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or service you need. We also collect basic, anonymized analytics data through standard website tools, such as which pages were visited and what device type was used. We do not collect payment card information through this website.

## How We Use Your Information

The information you share is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site estimate, coordinate the scope of work, and, where you have authorized it, communicate with your insurance carrier on your behalf. We may retain documentation related to a completed project for the period required by Nevada contractor regulations and standard business recordkeeping practices. We do not use your information for automated marketing campaigns or sell it to third parties under any circumstances.

## How We Share Your Information

We share your information only when you have specifically authorized us to do so, most commonly to coordinate directly with your homeowner's or commercial property insurance carrier during the claims process. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers, data brokers, or any other outside party.

## California Residents

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information we have collected about you, request that we delete it, and opt out of any sale of that information. Because we do not sell personal data, the opt-out right has no practical application here, but your right to access and deletion requests is honored. To exercise either right, contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.

## Data Retention

Contact and project records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which they were collected and to comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations. If you would like us to delete your information and no legal hold applies, reach out and we will process your request promptly.

## Your Rights and Choices

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate records, or request deletion at any time. We will respond to verified requests as quickly as practicable.

## Contact Us About Privacy

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described above, you can reach us by email at relliston@desertvalleyco.com, by phone at (702) 633-5033, or by mail at 3808 N Octagon Rd, North Las Vegas, NV 89030. We will do our best to respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the revised version will be posted on this page. Continued use of the site after any update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
