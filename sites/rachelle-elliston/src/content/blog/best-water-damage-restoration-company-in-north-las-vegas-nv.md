## What to Ask Any Water Damage Company Before You Hire

The right questions cut through marketing and get to what actually matters for your loss.

**Are you IICRC certified in WRT and ASD?** Water Damage Restoration Technician (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD) are the two credentials that demonstrate a technician understands how to dry a structure correctly - not just extract standing water. Ask for the firm's certification number if you want to verify.

**Do you carry a Nevada contractor license?** Restoration that involves removing and replacing drywall, flooring, or framing is construction work under Nevada law. A company without a contractor license cannot legally perform that phase. Confirm the license number and verify it at the Nevada State Contractors Board.

**Will you provide a written scope before work begins?** Any reputable company will document the damage, explain the proposed work, and give you a written scope. This document also becomes part of your insurance claim file.

**How do you document the drying process?** IICRC S500 requires moisture mapping at the start and end of every drying project, with daily readings logged. Those logs are what your adjuster needs to validate the claim. Ask if they use Drybook, Encircle, or a similar platform.

**Do you bill the insurance company directly?** Most restoration companies do. Confirm the billing process before work starts so there are no surprises on the back end.
