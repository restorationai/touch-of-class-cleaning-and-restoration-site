## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, A burst pipe or appliance leak can saturate wall cavities and subfloor within hours. We extract standing water, deploy drying equipment calibrated to the IICRC S500 standard, and monitor moisture levels until the structure reads dry throughout, not just at the surface.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, After a fire is extinguished, smoke residue and soot keep working. Acidic particles etch metal fixtures, discolor walls, and embed a persistent odor into insulation and framing. Our IICRC FSRT-certified process addresses both the visible char and the damage you cannot see.

**Mold remediation**, North Las Vegas homes that have experienced a slow leak or high indoor humidity are prime candidates for mold growth, often inside wall cavities where it goes unnoticed for weeks. Our IICRC AMRT-certified team contains the affected area, removes colonized materials safely, and addresses the moisture source so the problem does not return.

**Reconstruction**, Once mitigation is complete, we handle the rebuild so you are not coordinating a second contractor. From replacing drywall and flooring to restoring a room to its pre-loss condition, reconstruction is part of the same project.
