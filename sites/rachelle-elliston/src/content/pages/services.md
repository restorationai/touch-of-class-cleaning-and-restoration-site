From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, Desert Valley Contracting Inc. handles the full arc of property damage: emergency mitigation, structural drying, mold remediation, reconstruction, and general contracting. We work with homeowners, commercial property owners, landlords, and multi-family operators across North Las Vegas and the greater Las Vegas Valley.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, the clock matters. Water left standing warps subfloors and feeds mold within days; smoke residue bonds to surfaces and etches finishes within hours; storm debris left exposed invites secondary damage. Desert Valley Contracting Inc. responds around the clock to water damage, fire and smoke damage, biohazard cleanup, and storm damage. Our IICRC-certified crews arrive equipped to extract, stabilize, and document from the first visit, giving your insurance adjuster a clear, organized loss file from day one.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once the damage is mitigated and the structure is dry and safe, the rebuild begins. Desert Valley Contracting Inc. handles the full scope of post-damage reconstruction: framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish carpentry. Because we are also a licensed general contractor, we can take on renovation and improvement work beyond what the insurance claim covers, so you are not managing a separate contractor for upgrades while we handle repairs. One project manager, one schedule, one point of contact through completion.

## Specialty services

Some jobs do not fit neatly into emergency or rebuild categories. Our specialty services include mold inspection and remediation (handled by our IICRC AMRT-certified technicians), odor removal for smoke, sewage, and other persistent sources, and board-up and tarping to secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism. We are also EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which matters in older Las Vegas Valley housing stock where lead-based paint can be disturbed during any demolition or repair work.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction under one roof shortens the timeline considerably. There is no gap between the remediation crew finishing and the rebuild crew starting, no duplicated site visits, and no finger-pointing between separate contractors about what was left in what condition. If your property has been damaged, one call to (702) 633-5033 starts the entire process.
