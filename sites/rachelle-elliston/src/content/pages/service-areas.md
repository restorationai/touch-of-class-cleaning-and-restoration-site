Desert Valley Contracting Inc. dispatches from North Las Vegas around the clock, seven days a week. Whether a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or a weekend storm pushes water into a crawl space, the crew is reachable and ready to schedule. Call (702) 633-5033 and a real person will confirm your location, assess urgency, and get a team moving.

## Where we work

The Las Vegas Valley sits in a basin ringed by desert ranges, and that geography shapes how water damage, fire damage, and mold behave here. Flash floods funnel through drainage channels faster than most homeowners expect. Extreme summer heat accelerates mold colonization after a slow leak. Dry air can mask moisture trapped behind stucco or drywall until the damage is well advanced. The team works across this basin daily, from the master-planned communities of the northwest valley to the older block-construction homes closer to downtown, and out to the suburban corridors that have grown along the I-15 and US-95 corridors.

## Coverage by distance

Jobs closest to North Las Vegas get the fastest arrival, which matters when standing water is still spreading or smoke odor is still fresh. From there, coverage extends across Henderson, Boulder City, and the unincorporated communities of Clark County. The team also reaches into the outlying areas along the valley's edges, including communities near Lake Mead and the Spring Mountains foothills.

Distance does affect order of arrival. A loss in a nearby neighborhood will see a crew sooner than a call from the far edge of the service area, and that is worth knowing when you call. Traffic on the I-215 beltway and US-95 during peak hours can add time, so dispatchers account for current conditions when giving you an honest estimate of when to expect the crew.

For large commercial losses or catastrophic residential events at the outer edge of the region, the team will discuss logistics on the call and give you a straight answer about timing.
