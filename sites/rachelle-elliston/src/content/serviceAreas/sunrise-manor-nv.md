## Services we provide in Sunrise Manor

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Sunrise Manor frequently starts small and reads worse on the second inspection. Desert-built slab-on-grade homes have no crawlspace buffer, so a slow leak under a kitchen or bathroom can saturate the concrete substrate and wick into adjacent wall framing before the surface shows any visible staining. Desert Valley Contracting uses moisture mapping and thermal imaging to locate saturation that a visual check misses, then extracts standing water, sets industrial drying equipment, and monitors daily until the structure reaches target moisture levels.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a fire, smoke residue does not stay where the flames were. The negative pressure created by a house fire pulls soot into HVAC returns, closets, and wall cavities throughout the structure. Restoration here means more than wiping surfaces; it means cleaning ductwork, treating porous materials, and neutralizing odor at the source rather than masking it. Desert Valley Contracting documents the full extent of smoke migration for insurance purposes before any cleaning begins.

### Mold remediation

Mold can begin colonizing wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. In Sunrise Manor, where many homes have original drywall and paper-faced insulation from decades past, a slow leak behind a vanity or under a refrigerator line can produce a significant mold colony before the homeowner notices discoloration or odor. Remediation involves containing the affected area, removing compromised materials, treating structural surfaces, and verifying clearance before reconstruction begins.
