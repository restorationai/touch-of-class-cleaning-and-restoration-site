## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Sunrise Manor

Every job starts with a systematic inspection of the exterior envelope: roof field, ridge caps, flashing, fascia, and soffit. Wind-driven rain in the valley frequently enters through compromised flashing before it ever touches the interior, so identifying those entry points early shapes the entire drying plan. From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

- **Moisture mapping**: Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters locate hidden saturation behind walls and under flooring before any material is removed.
- **Emergency tarping and board-up**: Roof penetrations and broken windows are secured to stop ongoing water intrusion while the full scope is assessed.
- **Water extraction and structural drying**: Commercial-grade extractors remove standing water, and industrial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run continuously until readings meet the IICRC S500 drying standard for the material class involved.
- **Debris and tree-damage removal**: Limbs and root-ball debris are cleared from structures and staging areas before reconstruction begins.
- **Documentation for your insurance carrier**: Photos, moisture logs, and a written scope are compiled in a format most major carriers accept for direct billing.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with credentials in Water Restoration, Structural Drying, and Fire and Smoke Restoration, the team brings the right equipment and methodology for each damage type rather than applying a single template to every job.
