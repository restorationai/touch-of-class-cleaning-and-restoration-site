## Local Note: Caliche and Sub-Slab Moisture in Summerlin Homes

One condition that catches out-of-market contractors in Summerlin is the caliche layer common in the Mojave soil profile beneath residential slabs. Caliche is a calcium carbonate hardpan that restricts downward drainage, which means sewage that wicks beneath a slab has nowhere to go and pools against the underside of the concrete. Moisture meters reading the slab surface can show acceptable numbers while the caliche layer traps contaminated moisture below. Experienced crews in this market probe multiple depths and use thermal imaging alongside standard moisture mapping to confirm the full extent of contamination before signing off on drying completion.
