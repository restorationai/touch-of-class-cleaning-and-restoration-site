## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Sunrise Manor

Every job begins with a controlled-access assessment. The crew establishes a containment perimeter before any materials are moved, limiting cross-contamination to the rest of the structure. Personal protective equipment, negative-air machines, and HEPA filtration are deployed based on the scope of the situation, not a one-size checklist.

Affected porous materials, including carpet, padding, drywall, and in some cases concrete sealer, are removed, double-bagged in regulated medical waste packaging, and transported off-site. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, and contact times are observed per label instructions rather than rushed. A final ATP or luminometer reading gives an objective, documented baseline that the space is clean before the crew leaves. That documentation matters for insurance claims and, in some cases, for Clark County Health District records.

Because Sunrise Manor homes often feature original terrazzo or ceramic tile in bathrooms and kitchens, the crew pays particular attention to grout lines and expansion joints where biological fluids can travel by capillary action well beyond the visible boundary of the incident.
