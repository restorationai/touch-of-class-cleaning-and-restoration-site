## Why Sunrise Manor Properties See Mold Issues

Most of Sunrise Manor's residential housing stock was built between the late 1950s and the mid-1980s, a period when vapor barriers were minimal and wall insulation was thin. Those construction practices made sense for a dry desert climate, but they leave homes vulnerable when a slow supply-line leak or a monsoon-season roof intrusion goes undetected. Water behind original plaster or early-generation drywall can read completely dry at the surface while the underlying framing stays damp for weeks, long enough for mold colonies to establish well before the stain appears.

The area's clay-heavy soil also contributes. After a heavy rain, water pools against slab foundations and block-wall perimeters rather than draining quickly, and that sustained contact drives moisture into lower wall assemblies. Evaporative coolers, still common on Sunrise Manor rooftops, add another variable: when a cooler pad clogs or a float valve sticks, water overflows into the ductwork or the ceiling cavity beneath the unit, creating a hidden wet zone that can stay damp for days in a sealed attic space.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Sunrise Manor

Every remediation starts with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected area. Because older Sunrise Manor homes often have original texture-coat walls, surface readings alone are unreliable. The crew uses penetrating moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate the actual wet boundary before any containment is set.

Once the extent of the contamination is confirmed, containment barriers and negative air pressure are established to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms. Affected materials are removed carefully, with HEPA-filtered equipment running throughout. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously during the removal phase and for a set period afterward, filtering the air inside the containment zone before it is released.

After removal, exposed framing and structural surfaces are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Structural drying equipment is staged to bring the remaining materials to target moisture levels before any rebuild work begins. Desert Valley Contracting Inc. holds IICRC AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician) certification, and the work follows established IICRC S520 protocols throughout.
