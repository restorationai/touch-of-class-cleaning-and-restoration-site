## Why Paradise Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Paradise is an unincorporated community in Clark County, which means its infrastructure and permitting landscape differs from the City of Las Vegas proper. Many residential and commercial properties here were built during the rapid development boom of the late 1960s through the 1980s, and the clay-and-cast-iron sewer laterals installed during that era are now well past their expected service life. Root intrusion from desert landscaping, grease accumulation in older drain lines, and the ground movement that follows the Mojave's freeze-thaw cycles during winter nights all contribute to line collapses and backups.

The Strip corridor and the dense residential blocks surrounding it generate enormous volumes of wastewater daily. When a main sewer line surcharges during peak demand or after a monsoon event pushes debris into the system, the pressure finds the weakest point, which is often a cracked lateral under a residential property. The result is sewage backing up through floor drains, toilets, and tub drains simultaneously.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paradise

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water loss, the most contaminated category recognized by the IICRC. That classification drives every decision on a job site.

The process begins with containment. Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. All standing sewage and solid waste is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment rated for Category 3 material. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage, including carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, and insulation, are removed and bagged for proper disposal per Clark County waste handling requirements.

Once the structure is stripped to cleanable surfaces, the sanitization phase begins. Hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces, including subfloor, framing, and concrete slab where applicable. In Paradise's climate, where ambient temperatures can exceed 110°F in summer, bacterial colonies in sewage-contaminated material can double in population within hours. That urgency is why extraction and disinfection happen on the same visit, not sequentially across days.

Structural drying follows using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the low ambient humidity typical of the Las Vegas Valley. Our team holds IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD), and drying is monitored with moisture meters and thermal imaging until readings confirm the structure meets dry standard.

## Local Note

Properties in Paradise built on or near the alluvial fan deposits that spread east from the Spring Mountains can have slab foundations that sit on compacted gravel and sand rather than poured footings. When sewage saturates that material beneath a slab, it wicks laterally much farther than the visible damage suggests. A floor drain backup in a utility room can mean contamination has traveled several feet under the slab in any direction. We use moisture mapping and, when warranted, slab penetration testing to confirm the full extent before closing out the sanitization scope.
