## Why Spring Valley Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Spring Valley's housing stock skews toward single-story ranch and two-story tract homes built from the late 1980s through the early 2000s, with a significant share of newer construction pushing toward the western edge of the community. Most of these homes use engineered wood products, composite cabinetry, and laminate flooring that react badly to prolonged moisture exposure. Unlike older plaster-and-lath construction, these materials can delaminate or swell within 24 to 48 hours of a water event, which means the contents sitting on or near those surfaces face secondary damage even after the primary water source is stopped.

The desert heat compounds the problem in a counterintuitive way. During summer months, interior temperatures in an unventilated room can spike rapidly after HVAC systems are shut down for remediation, accelerating mold colonization on porous contents like upholstered furniture, mattresses, and cardboard storage boxes. Items that might survive a week of moderate humidity in a cooler climate can cross the threshold into microbial growth territory much faster here. That urgency shapes how we prioritize pack-out decisions on every job.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Spring Valley

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything leaves the property. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was removed and in what condition.

Items that can be restored are transported to our climate-controlled facility for cleaning and treatment. Depending on what caused the damage, that process may involve ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor, or specialized drying chambers for documents and artwork. Items that are structurally sound but need time away from the job site while remediation proceeds are stored securely until your home is ready to receive them.

When contents are returned, we work from the inventory to place items back in their original locations. Nothing is returned to a space that has not been cleared for occupancy, and we coordinate that timing directly with the remediation crew so there is no gap in the chain of custody.

Most homeowner policies in Nevada cover sudden and accidental water or fire damage to personal property, but the documentation requirements vary by carrier.
