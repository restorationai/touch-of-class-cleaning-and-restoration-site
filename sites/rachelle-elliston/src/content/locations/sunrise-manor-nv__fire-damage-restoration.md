## Why Sunrise Manor Properties See Distinctive Fire Damage Patterns

The residential neighborhoods that make up Sunrise Manor were built across several decades, with a significant portion of the housing stock dating from the 1960s through the 1980s. Homes from that era commonly used aluminum wiring, single-pane windows, and original ductwork, all factors that influence how a fire starts, how fast it spreads, and how deeply smoke infiltrates the structure. Aluminum wiring connections can loosen over time and arc, and when that happens inside a wall cavity, smoke has a direct path through the framing before any visible flame appears at a surface.

The desert climate adds another layer. Relative humidity in this part of Clark County regularly drops below 20 percent during summer afternoons and again in late fall. At that moisture level, char residue dries and bonds to drywall, cabinetry, and ceiling texture almost immediately after a fire is suppressed. That bonded soot is harder to remove than fresh residue, which is one reason prompt response matters as much here as it does anywhere in the valley.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Sunrise Manor

Every job begins with a thorough scope assessment before any cleaning or demolition starts. Technicians use thermal imaging and air sampling to map where smoke has traveled, including inside wall cavities and above drop ceilings. In older Sunrise Manor homes, this step often reveals that smoke has moved further than the visible char line suggests.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Emergency board-up and tarping** to secure the structure and prevent secondary weather intrusion
- **Controlled demolition** of materials that cannot be restored, including charred framing, saturated insulation, and smoke-saturated drywall
- **HEPA vacuuming and dry-chemical sponge cleaning** of structural surfaces, working from the top of each room down
- **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment** to neutralize odor compounds embedded in framing and subfloor
- **Air scrubbing** with commercial-grade HEPA filtration to clear particulate from the living space
- **Moisture monitoring** throughout, because fire suppression water left in a Mojave-climate home can still develop mold within days if not properly dried

We document every phase with photographs and written scope notes, which your insurance adjuster will need for the claim.
