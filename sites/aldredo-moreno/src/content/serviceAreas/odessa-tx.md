## Restoration emergencies common in Odessa

Odessa sits in the Permian Basin where the climate runs to extremes. Summer thunderstorms can drop inches of rain in under an hour onto ground that is too dry and compacted to absorb it quickly, pushing water through garage doors, under exterior doors, and into finished spaces before a homeowner can react. Winter is the other pressure point: when temperatures drop sharply, supply lines in exterior walls and under-insulated crawl spaces freeze and burst, and the damage is often hidden behind drywall until a ceiling starts to sag or a floor starts to buckle. The housing stock in many Odessa neighborhoods was built during the mid-century oil booms, meaning galvanized and early copper plumbing is still common, and those systems fail at joints and fittings under the stress of a hard freeze. Flat and low-slope roofs on commercial and industrial properties throughout the area are also a recurring source of interior water intrusion after heavy rain.

## Services we provide in Odessa

### Water damage restoration and cleanup

Water damage in Odessa frequently involves slab-on-grade construction, which means moisture migrates laterally under flooring rather than draining away. Engineered wood, laminate, and tile set over concrete can trap water for days while the surface looks dry. ACS uses moisture meters and thermal imaging to find what the eye misses, then extracts standing water and sets drying equipment calibrated to the West Texas climate.

### Sewage cleanup

Older sewer laterals in established Odessa neighborhoods can back up during heavy rain events when the municipal system is overwhelmed, or they fail outright as cast-iron pipe corrodes over decades. Sewage backup is a Category 3 loss that requires proper containment, removal of contaminated materials, and thorough disinfection before any space is safe to reoccupy.

### Storm damage cleanup

Permian Basin storms arrive fast and leave debris across roofs, yards, and driveways. ACS handles the cleanup side: removing debris, extracting water that entered through a compromised roof or broken window, and drying the structure. For any structural repair or rebuild work, ACS can point you to the right contractor, as reconstruction is outside the company's scope.

### Carpet cleaning

Odessa's dust and caliche grit work deep into carpet fibers, and a water loss accelerates that problem by wicking soil upward as the carpet dries. Hot-water extraction removes both the residual moisture and the embedded grit before mold has a chance to take hold in the backing.

### Air duct cleaning

After a water event, HVAC systems can circulate odors and particulates through the whole structure. Duct cleaning removes the debris that accumulates in the system and helps restore air quality after a loss.

### Tile and grout cleaning and junk and debris removal

Grout lines in kitchens and bathrooms absorb discoloration after water intrusion. ACS cleans and restores tile surfaces as part of the post-loss cleanup. Junk and debris removal handles the contents and materials that need to leave the property before drying and restoration can begin.
