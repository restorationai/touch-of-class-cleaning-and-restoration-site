## Restoration emergencies common in Greenwood

Greenwood sits in the Permian Basin, where the climate swings hard between dry heat and sudden, intense rainfall. When a thunderstorm drops an inch of rain in under an hour on ground that has baked hard through a long summer, the soil sheds water rather than absorbing it. That runoff finds its way into garages, under doors, and through any gap in a foundation. The region also sees periodic hard freezes that can catch older supply lines off guard, particularly in homes where pipes run through exterior walls or unconditioned spaces. When a freeze thaws fast, the break often goes unnoticed until a ceiling shows a stain or flooring starts to buckle. Sewage backups are another recurring problem in the area, especially when heavy rain overwhelms drainage systems. Each of these events shares one feature: the longer water sits, the deeper it travels into building materials, and the more disruptive the cleanup becomes.
