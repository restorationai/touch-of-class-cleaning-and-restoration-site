## Restoration emergencies common in Southwest Sandhill

Southwest Sandhill sits in the Permian Basin, where the climate swings hard. Summer thunderstorms roll through fast and drop more water than the caliche and clay soil can absorb quickly, sending runoff toward foundations and garage slabs before drainage infrastructure can carry it away. In winter, brief but sharp freezes catch homeowners off guard: supply lines routed through exterior walls or unconditioned spaces burst when overnight temperatures drop into the teens, and the damage often goes unnoticed until a ceiling starts to sag or a wall cavity smells of standing water. The region also sees dust storms and high winds that drive rain horizontally through window frames and roof penetrations. These are not rare events here. They are the seasonal rhythm that makes water damage one of the most common property problems in the area.

## Services we provide in Southwest Sandhill

### Water damage restoration and cleanup

Water in a Permian Basin home moves fast and hides well. It wicks up drywall, travels along slab seams, and sits inside wall cavities long after the surface feels dry. Permian Basin dust finds its way into HVAC systems year-round. Duct cleaning removes accumulated debris that reduces airflow and circulates particulates through the home every time the system runs.
