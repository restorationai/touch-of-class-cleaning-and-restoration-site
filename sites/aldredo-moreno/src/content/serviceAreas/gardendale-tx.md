## Restoration emergencies common in Gardendale

Gardendale sits in Ector County just north of Midland, sharing the same high-desert climate that makes West Texas hard on buildings. Summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F, baking roofing materials and caulk until they crack and allow water intrusion the next time a thunderstorm rolls through. Winter brings periodic hard freezes, the kind that arrive fast overnight, and supply lines running through exterior walls or uninsulated garages are the first to burst. Because much of the area's housing stock was built during the oil-field boom decades, aging plumbing and older mechanical systems are a recurring factor in water losses. The region also sits in a high-wind corridor; blowing dust and debris during spring storms can compromise roofing, siding, and window seals, and a single hail event can trigger dozens of simultaneous claims across a neighborhood.

Soil conditions compound the risk. The caliche and clay-heavy soils common across Ector County expand when wet and contract in drought, a cycle that stresses slab foundations and the plumbing embedded in them. A slab that has shifted even slightly can shear supply or drain lines, and the leak may go undetected for weeks before moisture wicks up through flooring.

## Services we provide in Gardendale

### Water damage restoration

Water losses in the Gardendale area frequently trace back to slab-embedded plumbing failures, failed water heaters in garage installations, or roof penetrations after high-wind events. Water that enters a slab-on-grade home has nowhere to drain downward, it spreads laterally under flooring and into wall cavities. ACS Enterprise uses moisture mapping and thermal imaging to locate saturation that reads dry at the surface, then extracts standing water and places drying equipment sized to the actual wet volume rather than the room square footage.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration
