## Restoration emergencies common in Stanton

Stanton sits in the heart of the Permian Basin, where the climate swings hard between extremes. Winter freezes arrive fast and can be severe enough to split supply lines in uninsulated exterior walls, a common pattern in the area's older ranch-style and single-story homes where plumbing runs close to the exterior. Summer thunderstorms push water through aging roof assemblies and around door thresholds faster than interior drains can handle. The region's caliche-heavy, low-permeability soil doesn't absorb surface water quickly, which means a hard rain can pond against foundation slabs and find every existing crack or gap. Wind events, including the occasional high-plains dust storm, drive debris into roofing and siding, opening pathways for moisture long after the storm has passed. These are the conditions that put Stanton property owners on the phone with a restoration crew.

## Services we provide in Stanton

### Water damage restoration

Water in a Stanton slab-on-grade home behaves differently than water in a crawlspace structure. When a supply line fails beneath the slab or a water heater lets go in a utility closet, moisture wicks laterally through concrete and into wall cavities before it ever appears at the surface. ACS Enterprise uses moisture mapping and thermal imaging to locate saturation that isn't visible, then extracts standing water, places drying equipment, and monitors daily until the structure reads dry, reducing the window during which mold can establish.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue from a kitchen or electrical fire doesn't stay in the room where the fire started. It travels through HVAC returns, settles into insulation, and bonds to cool surfaces throughout the home. Restoration here involves controlled demolition of charred material, dry and wet cleaning of affected surfaces, odor neutralization, and rebuild, not just a coat of paint over the damage.

### Mold remediation

In a climate that cycles between dry heat and sudden moisture events, mold can colonize porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion. Remediation work in Stanton homes typically involves containment of the affected area, removal of compromised drywall or insulation, HEPA filtration, and treatment of framing before any rebuild begins. Remediation work is scoped to address the source, not just the visible growth.

### Reconstruction

Once mitigation is complete, ACS Enterprise carries the project through structural and finish repairs, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and painting, so property owners aren't left coordinating a separate contractor after the restoration crew leaves.
