## Restoration emergencies common in Stanton

Stanton sits in the heart of the Permian Basin, where the climate swings hard between extremes. Summer thunderstorms roll across Martin County with little warning, dropping concentrated rainfall onto soil that drains slowly when it is saturated. That combination pushes water through foundation gaps, window wells, and low-lying doorways faster than most homeowners expect. Winter brings a different risk: brief but sharp freezes that catch older supply lines off guard, particularly in homes where pipes run through exterior walls or uninsulated crawl spaces. When a line bursts at 2 a.m. in January, the damage spreads quickly through drywall and subfloor before anyone notices. Sewage backups are also a recurring issue in parts of Stanton where aging lateral lines have not kept pace with the wear of decades of use.
