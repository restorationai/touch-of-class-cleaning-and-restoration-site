## Services we provide in Garden City

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Garden City often starts quietly. A slow drip behind drywall or a wicking slab leak can go unnoticed until flooring starts to buckle or a musty odor appears. ACS Enterprise uses moisture meters and thermal imaging to find where water has actually traveled, not just where it is visible, then extracts standing water and sets drying equipment calibrated to the low-humidity West Texas air.

### Sewage cleanup

A sewage backup is a category-three water loss, meaning the contamination level requires more than extraction and drying. We remove affected materials, treat surfaces, and document the scope for your insurance carrier. Proper containment and disposal matter here, and we handle the process from initial extraction through cleanup.

### Storm damage cleanup

After a hail event or a wind-driven rain, the immediate priority is stopping additional water from entering the structure. We remove debris, extract water that has entered through roof damage or breached windows, and dry the affected areas. We handle the water and cleanup side of the job. ### Air duct cleaning

HVAC systems in this region pull in a lot of particulate. After a water loss, ducts can also carry odor and residue through the whole house. We clean and inspect duct systems so the air moving through your home is not spreading the problem to rooms that were never affected.
