## Why Stanton Properties See Distinctive Storm Damage

Martin County sits squarely in the southern end of the Llano Estacado, where the flat, open terrain gives severe weather systems almost nothing to slow them down. Hailstorms that build over the Permian Basin can reach Stanton with full intensity, and the same wind patterns that drive dust storms through the region can push rain horizontally under rooflines, around window seals, and through any gap that a calm-weather inspection would never catch.

The housing stock in Stanton skews older, and older construction in this part of Texas often means wood-framed roofs with original decking, single-pane windows, and exterior stucco or painted masonry that can look intact after a storm while hiding moisture intrusion behind the surface. A hailstrike on aging composition shingles doesn't always punch through immediately, it bruises the mat, and the next rain event does the real damage. That delayed failure pattern means storm damage in Stanton sometimes doesn't become visible inside a home until weeks after the original event.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Stanton

Every job starts with a full exterior and interior assessment, roof decking, fascia, gutters, window frames, exterior walls, and any attached structures like carports or covered patios that are common on Stanton properties. We document everything with photographs and written notes calibrated for insurance review.

From there, the sequence depends on what the storm left behind:

- **Roof and envelope stabilization**: If the roofline is open to weather, we install temporary tarping or board-up before any interior work begins. Leaving a compromised roof exposed while addressing interior water damage is working backwards.
- **Water extraction and drying**: Storm-driven water intrusion is treated with the same urgency as a burst pipe. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate water that has tracked behind walls or under flooring, areas that feel dry underfoot but are actively feeding mold conditions.
- **Debris and tree damage cleanup**: Wind-thrown branches and downed trees require careful removal to avoid additional structural damage during extraction. We stage equipment to protect driveways and landscaping where possible.
- **Structural assessment and repair coordination**: Once the structure is dry and stabilized, we document repair scope for the insurance adjuster and can coordinate or perform the reconstruction work directly, reducing the number of contractors a homeowner has to manage.
