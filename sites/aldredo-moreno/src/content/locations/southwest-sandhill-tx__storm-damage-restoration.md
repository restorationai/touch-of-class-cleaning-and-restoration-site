## Why Southwest Sandhill Properties See Storm Damage Differently

The Permian Basin climate is not gentle. Summers bring intense heat that dries out roofing materials and caulking, leaving gaps that are invisible until a fast-moving thunderstorm drives rain sideways into them. Caliche-heavy soil common across this part of Ector and Midland counties sheds water instead of absorbing it, which means runoff during a heavy storm event can accumulate against foundations and push into crawl spaces or garage slabs faster than most homeowners expect. Flat and low-slope rooflines, common on ranch-style homes built across Southwest Sandhill from the 1960s through the 1980s, are especially vulnerable to pooling when gutters are overwhelmed or downspouts are blocked by debris. Once water finds a seam, it moves fast through insulation and into wall cavities before the storm has even passed.

Hail is a separate concern entirely. West Texas hail events can leave HVAC units, roof decking, and exterior siding compromised in ways that are not visible from the ground. A roof that looks intact after a storm may be allowing moisture intrusion at puncture points that only show up during a careful inspection.

## Our Storm Damage Cleanup Process in Southwest Sandhill

The first step after any storm event is a thorough walkthrough to understand what the storm actually did to the structure, not just what is visible from the curb. ACS Enterprise documents damage with photographs and written notes before any work begins, which matters when an insurance claim is involved.

From there, the sequence follows the damage:

- **Debris removal and exterior stabilization**: fallen branches, displaced roofing material, and broken fencing are cleared so the structure can be properly assessed and so no additional damage occurs from shifting debris.
- **Water extraction and interior drying**: if the storm drove water inside through a compromised roof, broken window, or overwhelmed drainage, standing water is extracted and industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned to begin drying wall cavities, flooring, and insulation.
- **Moisture mapping**: readings are taken at multiple points across affected areas to confirm drying is progressing and that no hidden pockets of saturation are being missed behind drywall or under flooring.
- **Junk and debris removal**: damaged materials that cannot be dried and restored are removed from the property cleanly and efficiently.

ACS does not handle rebuild or reconstruction work. Once the cleanup and drying are complete, we can point you toward the right contractor for repairs.
