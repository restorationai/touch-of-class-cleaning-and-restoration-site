## Why Garden City Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Glasscock County's rural character means most properties in and around Garden City rely on private septic systems rather than municipal sewer connections. That distinction matters enormously when something goes wrong. A municipal sewer backup is typically a one-source problem. A failing septic tank or a compromised drain field can release contaminated effluent across a wider footprint, sometimes surfacing in unexpected locations like laundry rooms, low-lying yard areas adjacent to the foundation, or beneath pier-and-beam structures.

The region's clay-dominant caliche soil compacts well but drains poorly. After any significant rainfall, saturated ground reduces a septic system's ability to disperse effluent, which pushes backpressure into the home's plumbing. West Texas doesn't get frequent rain, but when it does arrive it often comes in volume. That combination of infrequent but intense precipitation and slow-draining soil is a recurring setup for septic overflow events in this area.

Older ranch-style homes common throughout Glasscock County were often built with drain fields sized for smaller household loads. Decades of use, root intrusion from mesquite and other native shrubs, and shifting soils can compromise those systems well before any visible warning sign appears inside the house.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Garden City

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification in the restoration industry. That means the response protocol is more involved than a standard water loss. The process begins with containing the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or surfaces, followed by removing all standing sewage and saturated porous materials that cannot be safely restored.

After extraction, affected surfaces receive a thorough application of EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for pathogen elimination, including bacteria, viruses, and other biological contaminants present in raw sewage. Structural drying follows using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers, with moisture readings taken at the subfloor, wall cavities, and slab level to confirm drying is complete rather than just surface-dry. Documentation of pre- and post-treatment conditions is prepared throughout, which supports insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was done.
