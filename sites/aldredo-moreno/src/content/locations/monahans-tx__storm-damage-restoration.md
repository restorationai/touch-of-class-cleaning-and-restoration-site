## Why Monahans Properties Are Vulnerable to Storm Damage

The Ward County climate is unforgiving in a specific way: low annual rainfall means the caliche-heavy soil stays hard and compact most of the year, so when a storm drops two inches of rain in forty minutes, the ground simply cannot absorb it fast enough. Water sheets across driveways and yards and finds the lowest point of entry into a structure, which is often a garage threshold, a foundation crack, or a soffit damaged by hail.

The housing stock in Monahans skews older, with a significant share of homes built during the oil-boom decades of the mid-twentieth century. Those structures often used construction materials and framing techniques that performed well in dry conditions but were not engineered for the kind of wind-driven rain a modern Permian Basin storm can generate. Brick veneer that has lost its weep-hole drainage, original single-pane windows, and aging flat or low-slope roofs are all common points of failure when severe weather arrives. Hail damage to those roofs may not produce an immediate interior leak, but granule loss and cracked substrate accelerate deterioration quickly under the intense UV exposure Monahans sees the rest of the year.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Monahans

The first priority after any storm event is stabilizing the structure to stop ongoing damage. That means tarping breached roof sections, boarding compromised windows or doors, and identifying any standing water before it migrates further into wall assemblies or subfloor materials.

Once the structure is secured, the assessment phase documents every affected surface, from hail strikes on exterior cladding to moisture readings inside wall cavities. Documentation matters here not just for the repair scope but for the insurance claim process, and thorough photo and moisture-log records from the first visit prevent disputes later.

Drying and structural drying equipment is deployed based on what the moisture mapping shows, not a one-size schedule. Older homes with plaster or adobe-style construction common in this part of Texas release moisture more slowly than modern drywall assemblies, so drying timelines are set accordingly. Once materials reach acceptable moisture content, repair and reconstruction work begins, coordinated to match existing materials and finishes as closely as possible.
