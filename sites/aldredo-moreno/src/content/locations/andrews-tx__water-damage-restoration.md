## Why Andrews Properties See Water Damage Issues

The Permian Basin's climate creates a specific set of conditions that homeowners elsewhere don't deal with. Andrews gets very little annual rainfall, which means drainage infrastructure and residential grading are rarely tested, until a localized storm drops an inch of rain in an hour and water finds every gap in a foundation or window seal it has never been forced to find before. Caliche-heavy soils common throughout this part of West Texas shed water rather than absorbing it, so runoff moves fast and can pool against slab foundations before it has anywhere to go.

The housing stock in Andrews skews toward mid-century slab-on-grade construction. Those slabs don't have the crawl space buffer that homes in wetter climates often have, which means a plumbing failure under or near the slab sends water directly across finished flooring with no warning. Older galvanized supply lines, still present in a portion of the area's pre-1980 homes, are prone to pinhole failures that go undetected for weeks behind walls before the damage becomes visible.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Andrews

When we arrive at a property, the first priority is stopping the source if it hasn't been stopped already, then mapping the moisture. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the visible wet zone. In slab-on-grade homes common in Andrews, water frequently travels under flooring and surfaces several feet from the actual leak point. Finding the full extent of saturation before pulling materials saves unnecessary demolition.

Once the moisture map is complete, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then place industrial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in a calculated pattern to drive evaporation. Drying in West Texas has one genuine advantage: the ambient air is naturally low in humidity for much of the year, which means outdoor air can sometimes assist the drying process when conditions allow. We monitor moisture readings daily and adjust equipment placement until structural materials reach target drying goals.

Documentation runs parallel to every step, photos, moisture logs, and equipment placement records, formatted to support an insurance claim from day one.
