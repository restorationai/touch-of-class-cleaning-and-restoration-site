## Our Water Cleanup Process in Andrews

The first step on any water loss is locating all the water, not just the water you can see. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help map saturation behind drywall, under flooring, and inside wall cavities, critical in slab-on-grade construction where water can wick laterally under tile or LVP before surfacing. Once the scope is mapped, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment sized to the job.

After extraction, the drying phase begins. In Andrews, the low relative humidity is actually an asset here, ambient conditions support aggressive evaporation when the structure is set up correctly with commercial air movers and dehumidifiers. Readings are logged daily so the drying curve can be tracked and documented. That documentation matters for two reasons: it tells you the structure is genuinely dry (not just dry at the surface), and it gives your insurance adjuster the data they need to process the claim.

Materials that cannot be dried in place, saturated insulation, compromised subflooring, drywall that has wicked water above the flood line, are removed and disposed of properly before reconstruction begins. Leaving wet material inside a wall cavity in any climate is a mold risk; in a home that stays warm year-round, that timeline compresses.
