## Why Stanton Properties See Carpet Wear Faster Than You'd Expect

The Permian Basin's alkaline caliche soil is the defining carpet challenge in this part of Texas. When it tracks indoors, from driveways, job sites, or the open lots common throughout Stanton, it doesn't behave like ordinary dirt. Caliche particles are angular and mineral-dense, acting like fine sandpaper against carpet fibers with every footstep. Over months, that friction dulls color and weakens the pile structure, making carpets look years older than they are.

The region's low annual humidity compounds the problem differently than most homeowners expect. Dry air means static charge builds easily in synthetic carpet fibers, which actually attracts and holds airborne dust rather than letting it settle and be swept away. Add in the occasional high-wind event that drives fine particulate through window seals and door gaps, and carpets in Stanton homes accumulate a layer of embedded grit that standard cleaning equipment, the kind rented from a grocery store, simply cannot extract from the base of the pile.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Stanton

The process starts before any water or solution touches the floor.
