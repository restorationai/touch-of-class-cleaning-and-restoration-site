## Why Big Lake Properties Accumulate Junk Differently

Reagan County's oil and gas economy shapes the way properties are used and left behind. ## Our Junk and Debris Removal Process in Big Lake

The job starts with a walkthrough. Before anything is loaded, the crew assesses what's there, separates items that can be donated or recycled from those that need to go straight to disposal, and identifies anything that requires special handling, such as old appliances with refrigerants or containers with residual chemicals from agricultural or oilfield use.

From there, the process is straightforward:

- **Load and sort on-site.** Items are carried out and sorted at the truck, keeping recyclables and donation-eligible goods separate from general waste.
- **Full haul-away.** Everything that leaves your property is transported by the crew. You don't manage drop-off or disposal.
- **Sweep out.** After the main load is gone, the crew does a final pass to clear smaller debris, dust, and residual items so the space is ready for its next use.

For larger cleanouts, such as a full house, a detached shop, or a property that has sat vacant for years, the job may take more than one truck run. That's planned for upfront, not discovered at the end.
