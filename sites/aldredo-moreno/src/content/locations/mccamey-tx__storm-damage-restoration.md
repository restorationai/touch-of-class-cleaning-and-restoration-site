## Why McCamey Properties See Serious Storm Damage

The landscape around McCamey offers almost no natural windbreak. Storms rolling in from the southwest accelerate across flat, semi-arid rangeland before reaching town, and by the time they arrive, wind speeds can be severe enough to peel metal roofing, shatter windows, and drive rain sideways into gaps that would normally stay dry. Hail is a recurring concern here too. The same atmospheric conditions that produce the region's frequent thunderstorm cells also generate large hail that dents gutters, cracks vent covers, and punches through older roofing materials.

McCamey's housing stock includes a significant number of older single-story homes built during the oil boom decades, when construction standards and materials differed from what is used today. Flat or low-pitch roofs common in that era are more susceptible to ponding water after heavy rain, and older exterior cladding can allow moisture to wick into wall framing quickly. Once water is inside the structure, the dry West Texas heat does not always help as much as people expect. Rapid surface evaporation can mask moisture that remains trapped in framing, insulation, and subfloor materials for days.

## Our Storm Damage Cleanup Process in McCamey

When a storm tears through this part of Upton County, the first priority is stopping additional water from entering the structure. That means tarping compromised roofing, boarding damaged openings, and clearing debris that is blocking drainage before it redirects runoff into the building. ACS Enterprise handles that protective work as part of the cleanup scope, not as a separate add-on.

Once the structure is stabilized, the team moves to water extraction and drying. Standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment, and industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are deployed to address moisture in materials that look dry at the surface but are still holding water underneath. In McCamey's climate, low ambient humidity can create a false sense of progress. Moisture meters and thermal imaging give a more accurate picture of what is actually happening inside walls and under flooring.

Debris removal is coordinated alongside drying so the space is clear and accessible throughout the process. ACS also handles sewage cleanup when storm surges or drain backups are part of the loss, which is not uncommon when heavy rain overwhelms older municipal infrastructure.

One thing that catches property owners off guard in McCamey is how quickly the soil sheds water during a heavy storm event.
