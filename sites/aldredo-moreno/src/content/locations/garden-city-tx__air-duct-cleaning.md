## Why Garden City Properties See Duct Issues

Glasscock County's climate is the primary driver. Summers push well past 100°F for weeks at a stretch, which means HVAC systems in Garden City run long cycles with little rest. Extended run time pulls more air, and more air means more dust, pollen, and agricultural particulate deposited on duct walls and inside air handlers. The region's soil is predominantly sandy loam with a high caliche content, and when that material dries out in drought conditions, it becomes fine enough to pass through standard filter media and settle inside the duct system itself.

Housing stock in this part of West Texas also tends toward single-story ranch construction, often with ducts routed through unconditioned attic space. Attic temperatures in a Garden City summer can exceed 140°F, which accelerates the breakdown of older flex duct liner and makes any moisture intrusion from a roof leak or condensate issue significantly worse. Older metal ductwork in the area sometimes has unsealed joints that draw in attic air, bypassing filtration entirely.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Garden City

The process starts with a visual inspection of the supply and return registers, the air handler cabinet, and accessible duct runs. In homes with attic-routed flex duct, we check for kinks, disconnected sections, or liner deterioration before any cleaning begins, because cleaning a compromised duct system without addressing structural issues first just redistributes the problem.

Once the system is mapped, we use negative-pressure equipment to place the duct system under vacuum, then work each branch with agitation tools that break loose compacted debris from duct walls. All dislodged material is captured in a HEPA-filtered collection unit rather than blown into the living space. Supply registers, return grilles, and the air handler blower compartment are cleaned as part of the same visit. If the inspection reveals microbial growth on duct surfaces, an EPA-registered sanitizing agent can be applied after mechanical cleaning.

Dryer vent cleaning follows a similar approach and is worth scheduling at the same time. Garden City's dusty conditions mean lint accumulates faster in dryer exhaust lines, and a partially blocked vent forces the dryer to run longer cycles, increasing both energy use and fire risk.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically in this part of Glasscock County: homes that were built or expanded during oil-field boom periods sometimes have additions with ductwork that was connected to the original system without proper sizing calculations. The result is a branch that is either undersized for the square footage it serves or oversized in a way that reduces velocity and allows dust to settle inside the duct rather than staying airborne long enough to reach the filter.
