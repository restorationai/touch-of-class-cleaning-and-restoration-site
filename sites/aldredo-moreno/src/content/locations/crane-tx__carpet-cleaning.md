The Permian Basin's soil is a particular problem for carpet. ## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Crane

Every job starts with a dry inspection pass. Before any water or cleaning agent touches the floor, a technician evaluates fiber type, soiling level, and any pre-existing damage.
