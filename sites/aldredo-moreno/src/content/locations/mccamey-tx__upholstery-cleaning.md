## Why McCamey Homes See Upholstery Wear Differently

McCamey sits in oil and gas country, and a lot of households here have at least one family member coming home in work clothes that carry crude residue, grease, or frac sand. That transfers to armchairs and truck-seat-adjacent furniture pieces long before it's visible to the eye. Add the near-constant wind common to this stretch of Upton County and unpaved driveways and lots throughout town, and upholstery fibers end up trapping fine dust at a rate that outpaces cleaning schedules built for wetter, calmer climates. Direct sun through west-facing windows, common in the area's older frame houses, also fades and dries out fabric and leather faster, which makes regular conditioning more important here than in shadier, humid regions.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in McCamey

We start with a dry pass, vacuuming with an upholstery attachment to lift the loose grit and sand that a wet cleaning step would otherwise just push deeper into the weave. From there we pretreat any grease or oil-based stains, which show up often in households connected to fieldwork, using a solution matched to the fabric type. Fabric sofas and chairs typically get hot water extraction, which flushes soil and cleaning agents out of the cushion rather than leaving them to dry back into the fibers. Leather pieces, common in ranch-style McCamey homes, get a gentler wipe-down and conditioning treatment instead, since heat and heavy moisture can crack or dull leather over time. We finish by grooming the fabric nap and setting up air movement to speed drying, though McCamey's low humidity already works in our favor there.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

Which method we use depends on the piece and the fabric. Cotton and linen-blend sofas generally hold up well to hot water extraction, sometimes called furniture steam cleaning, because the fabric can handle the moisture and the heat helps break down ground-in oilfield soil. Delicate or older upholstery, along with anything with a wood frame that's already dried and contracted in this climate, does better with a low-moisture encapsulation method that cleans without oversaturating the cushion or stressing the frame joints. Leather upholstery gets cleaned and conditioned separately, since West Texas's dry air pulls moisture out of leather quickly and it needs a conditioner worked in after cleaning to prevent cracking.
