## Why Andrews Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The geology here matters more than most people expect. Caliche is nearly impermeable when compacted, which means groundwater and sewage effluent tend to travel laterally rather than percolating down. After a heavy rain, rare but intense in this part of West Texas, municipal sewer systems can experience surges that push raw sewage back through floor drains and low-lying fixtures. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, which make up a significant portion of Andrews's residential stock, often have original cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals that have corroded or deformed over decades. Those aging pipes are far more susceptible to root intrusion and collapse than the PVC lines used in newer construction.

Septic systems are also common on the outskirts of Andrews, particularly on larger lots closer to the county line. A saturated drain field, whether from a prolonged wet spell or a system that has simply reached the end of its service life, can force untreated effluent to the surface or back into the home. That's a Category 3 contamination event, and it requires a different response than a simple water loss.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Andrews

Raw sewage carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that don't disappear when the visible water is removed. The cleanup process has to address what you can see and what you can't.

When a crew arrives, the first priority is containment, stopping additional contamination from spreading to unaffected rooms. Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units capable of handling solids and debris, not just water. Once the bulk material is removed, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 losses. Porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, subflooring, that have been in direct contact with sewage are typically removed rather than cleaned in place, because pathogens penetrate those materials in ways that surface disinfection cannot fully address.

After demolition and disinfection, the space is dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. In Andrews's dry climate, ambient conditions can actually help accelerate drying, but that same low humidity can mask elevated moisture readings in dense materials like caliche-based stucco or older plaster. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters is essential before any reconstruction begins.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically in Andrews: homes with slab-on-grade foundations built over caliche tend to show deceptively dry surface readings shortly after a sewage event because the caliche layer beneath the slab holds moisture separately from the concrete above. A surface that reads within normal range on a standard moisture meter can still have saturated soil pressing against the underside of the slab, which creates ongoing humidity and odor problems weeks after cleanup. Thermal imaging and extended monitoring periods are the only reliable way to confirm the slab assembly is genuinely dry before closing out the job.
