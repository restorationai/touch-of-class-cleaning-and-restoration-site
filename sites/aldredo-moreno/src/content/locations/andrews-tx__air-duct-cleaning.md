## Why Andrews Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than Most

The West Texas climate around Andrews is hard on HVAC equipment in ways that aren't obvious until you pull a duct panel. Summer heat drives systems to run long cycles, sometimes eight or more hours a day, which means more air volume moving through the ductwork and more opportunity for dust, pollen, and soil particles to accumulate on duct walls and coil surfaces. The region's frequent dry winds lift fine caliche and silica from unpaved lots and open rangeland, and that material finds its way indoors through gaps around windows, doors, and even through the air handler itself during filter changes.

Older ranch-style homes common in Andrews, many built during the oil-boom decades of the mid-twentieth century, often have original sheet-metal ductwork that has never been professionally cleaned. Joints sealed with now-brittle mastic or dried foil tape can allow unfiltered attic air to enter the supply system. When we inspect ductwork in homes of that era, it's common to find accumulated debris that has compacted over years into a dense layer along the bottom of horizontal runs.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Andrews

Every job starts with a visual inspection, we use a camera or inspection mirror to assess what's actually in the duct system before any equipment runs. That step matters because the right approach for a 1960s galvanized steel system is different from what a modern flex-duct installation requires. Aggressive agitation on older rigid ductwork can dislodge joints; flex duct needs controlled negative pressure to avoid collapsing the liner.

Once we understand the system, we establish negative pressure using a high-powered truck-mounted or portable vacuum collection unit, then work section by section with rotary brushes and compressed-air tools to break debris loose and pull it into the collection system rather than into your living space. Supply and return registers are removed, cleaned individually, and reinstalled. If dryer vent cleaning is on the scope, and in Andrews, where lint buildup in long horizontal dryer runs is common in single-story homes, that's handled as a separate line item with its own rotary brush system sized for the vent diameter.

For properties where dust or microbial odor is a concern, we offer air duct sanitization using an EPA-registered disinfectant applied as a fine mist after cleaning, not as a substitute for mechanical cleaning, but as a finishing step.
