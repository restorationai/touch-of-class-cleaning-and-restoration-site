## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Monahans is how quickly a water loss can affect the HVAC system in a slab-on-grade home. Many homes here run ductwork through the attic rather than the floor, but the air handler unit itself often sits in a closet at or near floor level. When standing water reaches that closet, it can saturate the base of the air handler cabinet and wick into the blower compartment. Running the system before that component is dried out circulates humid air through the entire duct system, which dramatically extends total drying time and raises the risk of secondary moisture problems in rooms that were never directly flooded. Shutting the HVAC off is one of the first things to do after a water loss, and it is worth confirming with the crew before the system is turned back on.
