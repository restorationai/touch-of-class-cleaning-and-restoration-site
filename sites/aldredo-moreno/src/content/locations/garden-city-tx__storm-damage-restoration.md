## Why Garden City Properties See Storm Damage Differently

Glasscock County's flat, treeless terrain is both an asset and a liability during severe weather. Without natural windbreaks, gusts that accompany thunderstorms or the occasional tornado warning can sustain speeds that would be partially absorbed in more wooded or urban areas. That exposure means roof decking and ridge caps take a beating that homeowners in more sheltered regions rarely experience at the same frequency.

The housing stock in Garden City tends toward single-story ranch-style construction, common across rural West Texas. These homes often feature older asphalt shingle roofs and wood-framed walls that, once breached by wind or hail, allow rainwater to travel horizontally into wall cavities before it ever reaches the floor. That lateral water movement is easy to miss on a surface inspection, and it is exactly the kind of moisture that creates long-term structural and air-quality problems if the drying process does not account for it.

The region also experiences hail events that can be severe enough to compromise both roofing materials and exterior HVAC units in a single storm. Damage to a condenser or ductwork during a hail event can compound interior moisture problems when the cooling system is no longer functioning properly in the days following the storm.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Garden City

The first priority after any storm loss is stopping ongoing water intrusion. Before any drying equipment is staged, the crew assesses the roof and exterior envelope for openings and installs temporary tarping or board-up protection where needed. This step matters especially in Garden City, where a second storm system can follow within 24 to 48 hours of the first during active weather seasons.

Once the building is secured against further intrusion, moisture mapping begins. Technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through wall assemblies, insulation, and subfloor materials. In ranch-style homes with slab foundations, which are common throughout this part of Glasscock County, water that enters through a compromised exterior wall has nowhere to drain downward and instead saturates the base plate and lower wall framing.

Structural drying follows, using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers positioned based on the moisture map rather than placed by guesswork. Daily readings track the drying curve until materials reach acceptable moisture content. Throughout this process, ACS Enterprise documents conditions with photographs and moisture logs, which become part of the insurance claim file.
