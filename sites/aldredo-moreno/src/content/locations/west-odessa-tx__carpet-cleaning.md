## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in West Odessa

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment before any water or solution touches the carpet. West Odessa homes frequently have nylon or polyester cut-pile carpet installed over concrete slab foundations, which changes how moisture behaves during cleaning. Without a basement or crawl space beneath, heat from the slab can slow drying in summer and accelerate it unpredictably in winter. We account for that when setting dwell times and extraction passes.

The process runs in sequence: dry soil removal first, then targeted pre-treatment for stains and high-traffic lanes, followed by hot water extraction at pressures and temperatures matched to the fiber type. Petroleum-based staining from work boots or tracked-in residue gets a dedicated solvent pre-spray before the main extraction pass. Post-cleaning, we apply a carpet sanitization treatment where requested, which matters in homes with pets or young children spending time on the floor. We don't rush the drying phase either. In West Odessa's low-humidity environment, carpets often dry faster than they would in a humid climate, but we verify airflow and surface temperature before we leave rather than assuming the job is done.
