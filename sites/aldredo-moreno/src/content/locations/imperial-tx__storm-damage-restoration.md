## Why Imperial Properties See Significant Storm Damage

The Permian Basin's climate creates a specific combination that punishes residential and commercial structures. Summers bring intense heat that dries out wood framing and caulk seals, leaving rooflines and window perimeters brittle heading into late-summer storm season. When hail strikes a roof that has been baking at 100-plus degrees for months, granule loss is more severe and water infiltration happens faster than it would on a cooler, more humid climate's roofing stock.

The region also sits within a corridor that sees periodic tornado watches and warnings, particularly in spring and early fall. Even a brief touchdown or a near-miss with straight-line wind can shear off fascia, collapse carports, and drive water through any gap in the building envelope. Because Imperial is a smaller community, the gap between when damage occurs and when a crew arrives matters enormously. Water that sits inside wall cavities in this dry climate can behave counterintuitively: the surface dries quickly while moisture stays trapped in insulation and framing, creating conditions that go undetected without moisture meters.

## Our Storm Damage Cleanup Process in Imperial

Every storm job starts with a thorough inspection before any equipment is placed. That means walking the exterior to document hail strikes, wind-lifted flashing, and broken seals, then moving inside to identify water intrusion points with thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. In Imperial, where flat or low-slope rooflines are common on ranch-style construction, water can pool and wick laterally across a ceiling before it ever drips, so the visible stain on drywall is rarely the outer edge of the wet zone.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves in a clear sequence:

- **Water extraction**: Standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment before any drying begins.
- **Debris and tree material removal**: Downed branches, blown insulation, and wind-carried debris are cleared and hauled away.
- **Structural drying**: Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Readings are logged each visit so drying progress is documented.
- **Content handling**: Furniture, stored items, and flooring materials affected by water intrusion are assessed and moved or protected.

ACS does not handle mold remediation or structural rebuilding. If the inspection reveals mold growth from a prior unaddressed leak, or if framing and roofing need reconstruction after the storm, we will point you to qualified specialists for that work and focus on the water damage and cleanup side.
