## Why McCamey Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The geology under McCamey is not forgiving to underground pipe systems. Caliche layers shift with temperature swings and periodic moisture, and older cast-iron or clay sewer laterals that were common in mid-century construction crack under that pressure over time. When a lateral fails, sewage does not just back up into a single drain. It can surface at floor drains, bathtubs, and washing machine connections simultaneously.

Septic systems are also common on properties that sit at the edges of McCamey's service area, and a septic overflow carries the same pathogens and cleanup requirements as a municipal sewer backup. The dry climate means spilled effluent evaporates at the surface quickly, but that evaporation does not eliminate contamination. It concentrates it into flooring, subfloor materials, and wall cavities at the base of walls.

Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s, which represent a significant portion of McCamey's housing stock, often have original cast-iron drain lines that have never been replaced. Those lines corrode from the inside out, and the first sign of failure is frequently a backup rather than a visible leak.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in McCamey

The first priority on any sewage call is containment. Raw sewage is a Category 3 water loss, meaning it carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that require full personal protective equipment and controlled removal procedures. ACS begins by identifying the source and stopping additional sewage from entering the affected space, then establishes a containment boundary to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Extraction comes next. Standing sewage water is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment, and porous materials that have absorbed contaminated water, including carpet, pad, drywall, and insulation at the base of walls, are removed and bagged for disposal. In McCamey's older homes, this often means removing original hardwood or vinyl composition tile that cannot be adequately sanitized once saturated with Category 3 water.

After extraction and material removal, all affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This is not a single pass. Hard surfaces receive multiple applications with appropriate dwell times, and the space is assessed before any drying equipment is staged. Dehumidifiers and air movers then address residual moisture in the structural materials that remain.

ACS handles the water damage and cleanup side of the work. If the backup has caused damage that requires reconstruction, such as subfloor replacement or drywall rebuild, we can point you toward the right contractors for that scope.

## McCamey Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage varies significantly by policy. Standard homeowners insurance in Texas frequently excludes sewer backup unless the homeowner added a specific rider, and many McCamey properties have policies that were written years ago without that endorsement. Before assuming your carrier will cover the loss, pull your declarations page and look for language about "sewer backup" or "water backup" as a named coverage.
