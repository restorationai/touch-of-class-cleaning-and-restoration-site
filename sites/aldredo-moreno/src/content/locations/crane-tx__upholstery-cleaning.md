Crane County sits in an arid stretch of the Permian Basin, and that dryness cuts both ways for upholstery. ## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Crane

We start every job with a fiber and dye test, since a lot of the fabric sectionals and leather recliners we see in Crane are older pieces that have already seen years of sun exposure through west-facing windows. From there we vacuum with a upholstery-rated HEPA attachment to pull out the embedded caliche dust before any moisture touches the fabric, because wetting gritty fibers first just grinds the dust deeper. Leather gets cleaned with a pH-balanced solution and finished with a conditioner suited to the piece, addressing the cracking that's common in furniture that's sat near a sunny window for a few West Texas summers.
