## Why McCamey Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than Most

The geography here works against clean ductwork in ways that homeowners in more temperate climates don't deal with. Upton County's soil is heavy in fine particulate, caliche, silica, and windblown sand, and during the dry months, that material circulates freely. When a home's return-air grilles pull in air near floor level, they're also pulling in whatever has settled on the slab or carpet. Older ranch-style homes common to this part of West Texas were often built with oversized duct runs and minimal filtration, which means more surface area for dust to cling to and fewer barriers stopping it.

Oil-field activity in the surrounding area also contributes. Diesel exhaust particulate and combustion byproducts can drift into residential areas, and once they enter the HVAC system, they bond to duct walls differently than ordinary household dust, they're stickier and harder to dislodge with a standard filter change alone.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in McCamey

The process starts with an inspection of the full duct system before any equipment is connected. Supply and return runs are mapped, and any sections showing signs of damage, disconnection, or pest intrusion are flagged before cleaning begins. In West Texas homes, it's not unusual to find duct joints that have dried out and separated over years of extreme temperature cycling, a detail worth knowing before pressurized air is introduced.

Cleaning uses negative-pressure equipment: a high-powered vacuum collection unit is connected to the main trunk line, creating continuous suction throughout the system. Technicians work each branch run with agitation tools, rotary brushes and compressed-air whips, to break debris loose from duct walls and move it toward the collection unit rather than back into the living space. Supply registers, return grilles, and the air handler cabinet are all addressed as part of the same service visit.

Dryer vent cleaning is available as an add-on during the same appointment. In McCamey's dry climate, lint accumulation in dryer vents is a genuine fire risk, and the short drive time from Midland makes combining both services on one visit practical.

## Local Note

Homes in McCamey that were built during the oil boom decades of the mid-twentieth century frequently used sheet-metal ductwork with fibrous interior lining, a material that was standard at the time but that degrades over decades of extreme heat cycling. When that lining begins to break down, it sheds fibers into the airstream in addition to ordinary dust. If your home is older and you've never had the ducts inspected, it's worth asking the technician to check liner condition during the cleaning visit. Replacing deteriorated lined duct sections is outside the scope of a cleaning service, but knowing the condition of your system before it becomes an air quality problem is genuinely useful information.
