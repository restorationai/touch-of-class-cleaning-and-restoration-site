## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process for West Odessa Homes

We start every job with a fabric and fiber check, since West Odessa households mix everything from budget microfiber sectionals to inherited leather recliners, and each needs a different approach. Dust-heavy fabric pieces get a dry vacuum pass first to pull out loose grit before any moisture touches the material, which keeps hard-water minerals from binding to soil during extraction. For fabric upholstery we use hot water extraction with a pH-balanced cleaning solution, agitating stains rather than saturating the whole cushion, then extracting thoroughly so drying time stays short despite the dry climate helping on the back end. Leather upholstery gets cleaned and conditioned separately, since West Texas air pulls moisture out of hides faster than more humid regions, and unconditioned leather here cracks noticeably sooner. We finish with a low-moisture drying pass using air movers, since low ambient humidity in West Odessa actually works in our favor for fast cushion recovery.
