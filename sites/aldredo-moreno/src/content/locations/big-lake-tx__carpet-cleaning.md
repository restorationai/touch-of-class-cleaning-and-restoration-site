## Why Big Lake Carpets Take a Beating

Reagan County's climate is dry most of the year, but that dryness is deceptive. When rain does arrive, it tends to come fast, and tracked-in mud from caliche-heavy soil sets quickly once it dries. Homes and rental properties near the oil field corridors also see heavy boot traffic from workers, and petroleum-based residues from work boots bond to synthetic carpet fibers in ways that consumer-grade spot treatments rarely lift. The result is carpet that looks aged well before its time.

Older ranch-style homes common to this part of West Texas often have carpet laid directly over concrete slab, which means there is no subfloor air gap to help moisture escape. ## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Big Lake is how quickly caliche dust reappears after a cleaning if the entryways are not addressed. Caliche is a calcium carbonate-rich soil that is essentially powdered concrete when dry, and it tracks in on shoes and pet paws from unpaved driveways and yards. We often suggest placing commercial-grade entry mats at exterior doors and vacuuming them separately at least twice a week.
