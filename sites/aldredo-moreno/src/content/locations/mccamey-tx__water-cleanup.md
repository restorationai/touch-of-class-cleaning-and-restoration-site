## Why McCamey Properties See Emergency Water Cleanup Issues

Most of McCamey's residential housing stock was built during the oil boom decades of the mid-twentieth century. That era of construction means copper and galvanized steel supply lines that have been under pressure for sixty or seventy years, cast-iron drain stacks that corrode from the inside out, and water heaters tucked into closets with no secondary containment pan. When any of those components fail, water moves fast through the original hardwood or vinyl composition tile that covers many of these older slab floors.

The climate adds pressure of its own. McCamey averages very low annual rainfall, but when storms do arrive, they tend to arrive hard. The caliche and clay soil profile across Upton County does not absorb heavy rain quickly, so runoff collects against foundation perimeters and finds its way through aging sill plates and door thresholds. Evaporative coolers, still common on older homes in the area, introduce another failure point: the water supply lines and distribution pads that feed those units can leak slowly for weeks before anyone notices the damage spreading into the attic or ceiling cavity below.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in McCamey

The first thing we do on any water cleanup call is locate the source and confirm it is controlled. If a supply line is still running, nothing else matters until the water is off. From there, the process moves in a specific sequence calibrated to the conditions here.

**Assessment and moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to read what the walls and floors are actually holding, not just what looks wet. In McCamey's older homes, original plaster and lath construction can read dry at the surface while the lath itself stays saturated. We map the full moisture boundary before any equipment is placed.

**Standing water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet. On slab foundations, water that has migrated under flooring material requires targeted injection extraction to avoid leaving a hidden wet zone.

**Structural drying.** High-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just placed near the visible damage. In McCamey's low-humidity climate, ambient conditions can actually help accelerate drying, but only if the structure is properly set up to exhaust moisture rather than recirculate it.

**Monitoring and documentation.** We log moisture readings at each visit so you, and your insurance adjuster, have a clear record of the drying progression. Documentation matters when a claim is open.
