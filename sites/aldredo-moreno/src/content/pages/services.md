When a pipe bursts, a fire tears through a kitchen, or a storm peels back part of a roof, the last thing you want is to coordinate three different contractors. ACS Enterprise handles the full arc of property damage, from the first hours of emergency response through the final coat of paint, for homeowners, commercial property owners, and multi-family operators across Midland and the surrounding Permian Basin region.

## Emergency restoration services

Some damage compounds by the hour. Standing water soaks into subfloor and wall cavities fast; smoke residue bonds to surfaces within days; a compromised roof edge invites the next weather event before you've filed a claim. ACS Enterprise responds to the urgent end of the spectrum: **water damage** extraction and structural drying, **fire and smoke damage** cleanup and odor control, **biohazard and trauma scene** remediation handled with proper containment and disposal protocols, and **storm damage** response including emergency securing of the structure. Each of these services has its own process and its own timeline, the linked pages go deeper on what to expect for each.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, ACS Enterprise transitions directly into the rebuild phase, replacing drywall, framing, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and finishes to pre-loss condition or better. Because the same company that documented the damage is handling the rebuild, nothing gets lost in the handoff between a remediation crew and a separate general contractor. ACS Enterprise also takes on renovation and improvement projects outside of insurance claims, so if a loss event revealed older materials worth upgrading, that conversation can happen in the same scope.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an emergency, and not every problem is visible. ACS Enterprise also provides **mold inspection and remediation** for properties where moisture has lingered long enough to colonize building materials, a common concern in West Texas structures that have experienced slow leaks or inadequate vapor management. **Odor removal** services address smoke, pet, and biological odors that survive surface cleaning. **Board-up and tarping** secures a structure after a break-in, fire, or storm until permanent repairs can begin. These services are often the piece that gets overlooked until a buyer's inspection or a tenant complaint forces the issue.
