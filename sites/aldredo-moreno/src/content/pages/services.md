ACS Enterprise handles the water damage and cleanup work that follows a loss at your home, rental property, or commercial building. From standing water after a pipe failure to sewage backups, storm flooding, and the deep cleaning your property needs once the damage is contained, the services below cover what most Midland-area property owners need when something goes wrong. If your situation calls for mold remediation, fire restoration, or structural rebuild work, we will point you to the right specialist.

## Emergency water and storm services

Water damage moves fast. Within the first day, saturated drywall begins to soften, wood subfloors start to swell, and the window for saving flooring and cabinetry narrows. ACS Enterprise responds to water damage and water cleanup calls across Midland and the surrounding Permian Basin, extracting standing water, drying structural materials, and documenting the loss for your insurance carrier. Storm damage cleanup covers the debris, mud, and water intrusion that West Texas weather events leave behind. When a sewer line backs up or a drain overflows, sewage cleanup requires a different level of containment and sanitation than a clean-water loss, and that work is handled separately from standard water extraction.

## Cleaning services

Not every call is an emergency, but the results still matter. Carpet cleaning removes the ground-in dirt and residue that West Texas dust and caliche soil track into homes and offices over time. Tile and grout cleaning restores surfaces that routine mopping cannot reach. Air duct cleaning clears out the accumulated dust, debris, and allergens that circulate every time your HVAC system runs, which is most of the year in a climate that swings between summer heat and dry winter cold. These services are available for residential homes, commercial spaces, and multi-family properties.

## Junk and debris removal

After a water loss, a storm, or a renovation, the leftover material has to go somewhere. ACS Enterprise provides junk and debris removal so property owners are not left coordinating a separate hauling crew on top of everything else. This service is available as a standalone job or as part of a broader cleanup scope.

## One call, one company

Dealing with property damage means juggling a lot at once: insurance adjusters, contractors, timelines, and decisions about what can be saved versus what needs to go. ACS Enterprise handles the water damage mitigation and cleanup side of that process from start to finish, keeping the scope clear and the documentation organized. When the work moves into territory outside our services, such as mold remediation or structural reconstruction, we will tell you plainly and help you find who handles that next step.
