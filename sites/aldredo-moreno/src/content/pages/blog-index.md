West Texas properties face a specific set of problems, caliche soil that drains poorly after a hard rain, aging slab foundations that shift with the clay, and the kind of summer heat that turns a slow roof leak into a mold problem faster than most homeowners expect. The posts here are written to help Midland property owners understand what's actually happening inside their walls, under their floors, and above their ceilings when something goes wrong.

You'll find emergency response guides that walk you through the first hours after a pipe bursts or a storm pushes water under your door, plain-language explainers on how insurance claims work for water, fire, and mold losses, and seasonal pieces on the hazards that show up most often in this part of the Permian Basin. The goal is to give you enough information to make good decisions, whether that means calling a restoration contractor immediately, waiting to see if a problem resolves, or knowing which questions to ask your adjuster. Read whatever is useful to you. The posts are written to be helpful regardless of who ends up doing the work.
