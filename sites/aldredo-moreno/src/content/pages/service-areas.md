ACS Enterprise is based in Midland, TX and serves property owners across the Permian Basin and surrounding West Texas communities. When you call during business hours, our team works to get you scheduled and on the calendar as quickly as possible. Whether you are dealing with standing water in a Midland home or a sewage backup at a commercial property further out, the process starts with a single phone call to (432) 847-4704.

## Where we work

Our team operates out of Midland and covers a wide stretch of West Texas, including the greater Midland-Odessa metro and the smaller cities and communities that ring the basin. This part of Texas is defined by flat, caliche-heavy soil that sheds water quickly on the surface but gives it nowhere to go during heavy rains, pushing it toward foundations, window wells, and low-lying slabs. Older homes in established Midland neighborhoods often have pier-and-beam foundations or aging supply lines that are more vulnerable to sudden failures. We know the region's housing stock and work accordingly.

## Coverage by distance

The communities closest to our Midland base, including Odessa, Gardendale, and Greenwood, are typically our fastest to reach. From there we extend outward to communities like Andrews, Stanton, Big Spring, and Pecos. Further out, we serve areas across the broader Permian Basin corridor, including parts of Ector, Martin, Howard, and Reeves counties.

West Texas distances are real. A job in a remote area outside the metro will take longer to reach than one a few miles from our shop, and we will be straightforward with you about arrival windows when you call. We do not make clock promises we cannot keep. What we can promise is that your call goes to a real person during business hours and scheduling moves the same day.

## Don't see your city?

The city links on this page cover our most active service areas, but the list is not a hard boundary. For significant water losses, sewage events, or storm damage, we are willing to discuss dispatch to locations outside the listed cities. Call us at (432) 847-4704 and describe the situation. If we can help, we will tell you so. If the location is too far for us to serve you well, we will say that too.
