When water backs up through a drain, smoke settles into drywall, or a storm tears through your roof, the damage doesn't wait, and neither should your call for help. ACS Enterprise handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, storm damage, mold remediation, and reconstruction work for homeowners and property managers across Midland, TX and the surrounding Permian Basin.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, Whether it's a burst pipe, an appliance leak, or a sewage backup, standing water can saturate subflooring and wall cavities faster than the surface suggests. We extract, dry, and document the full scope of the loss.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, Smoke residue is acidic and keeps corroding surfaces long after the flames are out. We remove char, neutralize odor, and clean materials that are worth saving before moving to reconstruction.

**Storm damage restoration**, West Texas hail and high-wind events can compromise roofing, siding, and structural sheathing in ways that aren't obvious from the ground. We assess the full envelope and coordinate repairs so the structure is weathertight again.

**Mold remediation**, In a climate that swings between dry heat and sudden humidity, hidden moisture, inside walls, under slabs, above ceiling tiles, can support mold growth within days of a water event. We locate the source, contain the affected area, and remediate to industry standards.

**Reconstruction**, Restoration work often ends with damaged materials removed and a structure that needs to be rebuilt. Rather than handing you off to a separate contractor, we carry the project through finish work so you're dealing with one point of contact.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

ACS Enterprise has been operating in Midland since 2019. That's enough time to understand how the local housing stock behaves, the pier-and-beam foundations common in older Midland neighborhoods that can trap moisture underneath, the flat or low-slope roofs on mid-century commercial properties that pool water after a hard rain, the caliche soil that sheds water quickly and pushes it toward foundations when it can't absorb fast enough.

We work directly with insurance carriers. That means we photograph affected materials, write detailed documentation of the loss, and submit to your adjuster, so you're not managing paperwork while also managing a damaged home. Most major carriers are familiar with this process; if you haven't filed a property damage claim before, we can walk you through what to expect.

Every job gets a written scope before work begins. You'll know what we're doing, why, and in what order, not because we hand you a brochure, but because a homeowner who understands the drying or remediation process makes better decisions and has fewer surprises when the final invoice reaches the adjuster.
