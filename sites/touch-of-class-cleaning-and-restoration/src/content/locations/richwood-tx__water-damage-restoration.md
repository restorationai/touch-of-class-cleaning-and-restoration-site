## Why Richwood Properties See Water Damage Issues

Richwood sits in the flat, low-lying stretch of Brazoria County where drainage depends almost entirely on ditches and culverts keeping pace with Gulf Coast downpours. When a heavy rain event outruns that drainage, water backs up against foundations faster than it would in a hillier market. Add in the clay-heavy soil common across this part of the county, which expands and contracts with moisture swings, and you get slab movement that opens hairline cracks plumbing lines sit on top of. Most of the housing stock here is slab-on-grade construction from the subdivision boom of the 1970s through 1990s, which means a supply line leak or water heater failure often spreads laterally under flooring before anyone notices a wet spot, rather than dripping down through a basement ceiling the way it might further north.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Richwood

We start by identifying the water category and tracking how far it has migrated, which matters more in slab homes because moisture meters have to check under vinyl, tile, and carpet pad separately rather than relying on one visible stain. Extraction comes first, pulling standing water off hard flooring and out of saturated carpet and pad. From there we set containment and begin structural drying, using air movers and dehumidification calibrated for the ambient humidity typical of this stretch of the Gulf Coast, because a dehumidifier sized for a drier climate simply can't keep up here. We monitor moisture content in framing, subfloor, and drywall daily and document readings as materials return to a dry standard, which also gives you a clear record if you're filing an insurance claim.

## Richwood Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Richwood water losses we see start with either a plumbing failure or wind-driven rain during a tropical system, and carriers generally want the same basic documentation either way: photos of the source, moisture readings, and a drying log. We put that packet together as we go so you're not scrambling to reconstruct the timeline later. If your property sits in a subdivision with an HOA, we're glad to coordinate on anything tied to exterior repairs or dumpster placement during the job, since Brazoria County subdivisions vary in what they require for temporary equipment staged in a driveway or yard.
