Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration handles the full arc of property damage, from the first call after a pipe bursts to the final coat of paint once the space is rebuilt. Homeowners, property managers, and commercial owners across Richardson and the surrounding DFW area rely on one company to carry a loss through mitigation, cleanup, and repair, rather than juggling separate contractors for each phase. Below is a quick guide to what we cover and where to go for more detail on your specific situation.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage from a burst pipe, supply line failure, or roof leak needs attention fast. Water migrates into subfloors, wall cavities, and baseboards within hours, and once it sits, drywall swells and mold risk climbs. Fire and smoke damage leave behind more than visible char. Soot works into fabric, grout lines, and HVAC ductwork, and the acidic residue continues etching surfaces until it's professionally neutralized. Storm damage in North Texas often arrives as wind-driven rain finding its way through a compromised roofline or window seal, and the resulting interior damage can outpace the visible exterior harm. Biohazard situations, including sewage backups and trauma-related contamination, require careful containment and disposal because they carry health risks beyond a standard cleanup. Call now for emergency service if you're dealing with any of these; our team is ready to assess the loss and start stabilizing the property.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once the water is extracted, the smoke odor is treated, or the damaged materials are removed, the property still needs to be put back together. Our reconstruction work covers drywall and framing repair, flooring replacement, painting, trim, and the general contracting tasks that follow a mitigation job, whether that's a single bathroom after a slow leak or multiple units in a multi-family building after a storm. Because the same company handles both phases, there's no gap where a homeowner is left managing two separate crews, two separate timelines, and two separate invoices.

## Specialty services

Not every call starts with standing water or visible fire damage. Mold inspection addresses the musty smell or discoloration that shows up weeks after a slow leak, often in a crawlspace, attic, or behind a shower wall. Odor removal tackles smoke, pet, or moisture odors that linger after the visible damage is gone. Board-up and tarping services secure a property immediately after a break-in, storm impact, or fire, protecting the structure and contents while repairs are scheduled.
