Touch of Class Cleaning and Restoration works from our Richardson office out across North Texas, and the areas we serve are listed on this page. When you call, our team asks a few quick questions about the loss and your address so we can get the right crew and equipment routed to you with as little back and forth as possible.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Richardson and responds across the surrounding Dallas-Fort Worth communities, with the heaviest concentration of our work in Collin and Dallas counties. From there, coverage extends outward into the neighboring suburbs and smaller towns that sit along the main highway corridors into the metro.

## Coverage by distance

Richardson and the cities that border it get the fastest turnaround simply because the drive is short. As you move further out into the broader metro, arrival still happens the same day in most cases, but it depends on where our crews are already working and what the traffic looks like on 75, 635, or 190 at that hour. At the outer edge of our coverage area, we still take the call, we just ask for a little more patience on scheduling and we're upfront about where your job falls in the queue.

## Don't see your city?

If your address isn't listed on one of our service area pages, call us before assuming we can't help. Larger losses, like a flooded basement or a fire-damaged structure, are often worth the extra drive, and we'd rather have that conversation on the phone than have you rule us out. Tell us the city and the type of damage, and we'll give you a straight answer about whether we can get there and roughly when.
