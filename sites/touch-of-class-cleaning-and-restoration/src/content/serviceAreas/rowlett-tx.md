## Restoration emergencies common in Rowlett

Rowlett sits along the shoreline of Lake Ray Hubbard, split between Dallas and Rockwall counties, and that geography shapes the damage we see. North Texas clay soil expands and contracts with the area's wet-dry cycles, which stresses slab foundations and the plumbing lines running through and under them, a common precursor to slow leaks that go unnoticed until flooring or drywall is already compromised. Humid summers mean any uncontrolled moisture, whether from a roof leak, a failed water heater, or a slab plumbing break, has a short runway before mold colonization becomes a second problem layered on top of the first.
