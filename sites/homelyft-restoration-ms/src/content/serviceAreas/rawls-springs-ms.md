## Restoration emergencies common in Rawls Springs

Rawls Springs sits in Forrest County, where the Gulf Coast's humid subtropical climate reaches inland with enough force to make moisture management a year-round concern. In Rawls Springs, where older wood-frame construction is common, smoke odor can be particularly stubborn. We use thermal fogging, hydroxyl treatment, and content pack-out when needed to address odor at the source rather than masking it.
