## Restoration emergencies common in Henderson Point

Henderson Point sits on a narrow strip of land between the Bay of St. Louis and the Mississippi Sound, which means the water table is never far below the surface. During heavy Gulf Coast rain events — and Mississippi sees well over 60 inches of rainfall annually — low-lying slabs and crawlspaces collect water faster than standard drainage can handle it. The peninsula's older cottages and elevated beach houses were built under codes that predate current wind and flood standards, and many have original wood framing, tongue-and-groove interior walls, and single-pane windows that give storm-driven rain easy entry points.

Salt air is a compounding factor that most homeowners underestimate. Moisture-laden air off the Sound accelerates rust on HVAC coils, corrodes fasteners inside wall cavities, and keeps relative humidity elevated even on days that feel dry. That sustained humidity is why mold can begin colonizing wet drywall within 24 to 48 hours after a leak — and why a slow drip behind a kitchen cabinet can produce a significant mold problem before anyone notices the smell. Henderson Point's seasonal rental market adds another layer: properties that sit vacant between summer bookings may not catch a slow leak for weeks.

## Services we provide in Henderson Point

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Henderson Point frequently involves more than a single wet room. Storm surge, failed sump systems, and roof penetrations during squalls can saturate wall cavities, subfloor sheathing, and insulation simultaneously. Our crews use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map hidden saturation before any drying equipment is placed, then establish a drying system calibrated to the ambient humidity conditions typical of coastal Harrison County. We document moisture readings throughout the process — documentation that matters when you're filing a claim with your flood or homeowner's carrier.

### Mold remediation

The combination of heat, humidity, and frequent water intrusion makes mold a persistent problem for Henderson Point structures. We follow established remediation protocols to contain affected areas, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces before reconstruction begins. We do not make health claims about air quality outcomes, but we do provide clearance documentation that satisfies most insurance adjusters and real estate transaction requirements.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Grass fires, kitchen fires, and electrical fires in older wiring are all patterns we see along the peninsula. Smoke residue in a coastal home behaves differently than in an inland structure — salt-laden air causes residues to bond more aggressively to porous surfaces like wood paneling and fabric. Our technicians use dry-chemical and wet-chemical cleaning methods matched to the specific residue type, and we address odor at the source rather than masking it.

### Storm and wind damage

After named storms and severe thunderstorms, roof tarping and board-up services protect Henderson Point properties from secondary water intrusion while permanent repairs are planned. We coordinate with your insurance adjuster and can assist with scope documentation to support your claim.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Henderson Point

Much of Henderson Point was rebuilt after Katrina, so the housing splits into two very different groups. Post-2005 construction tends to sit elevated on treated pilings with PEX or CPVC supply lines and PVC drains, which means water usually runs down through the framing and out rather than pooling — but wind-driven rain still soaks the sheathing and batt insulation behind it. The surviving older cottages are the harder scope: galvanized or copper supply, cast iron drains, and materials from an era where asbestos floor tile, mastic, and lead paint are all realistic finds, so testing comes before demolition.

The soil here is sandy coastal fill and deposits over a shallow water table, which drains fast but keeps crawlspaces and ground-level slabs damp for weeks. Henderson Point is unincorporated, so structural repair and rebuild permits — and floodplain elevation review — go through Harrison County rather than a city hall.
