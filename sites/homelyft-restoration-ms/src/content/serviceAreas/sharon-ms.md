## Restoration emergencies common in Sharon

Sharon sits in central Mississippi, where the climate creates a near-perfect environment for moisture-related damage. Summers are long, humid, and punctuated by fast-moving thunderstorms that can drop two or three inches of rain in under an hour. That kind of volume overwhelms gutters, saturates soil, and finds every gap in a foundation or roofline. Winters are mild but not dry — freeze-thaw cycles are infrequent enough that older plumbing in Sharon's housing stock often lacks adequate insulation, making a rare hard freeze a reliable source of burst-pipe calls.

Much of Sharon's residential construction dates to mid-twentieth-century builds and earlier, meaning pier-and-beam foundations and crawl spaces are common. Those crawl spaces hold humidity, and when a slow plumbing leak or a grading problem lets ground moisture accumulate, mold can begin colonizing wood framing within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. Madison County's clay-heavy soil also drains slowly, which keeps hydrostatic pressure against foundation walls long after a rain event ends.

## Services we provide in Sharon

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Sharon frequently starts in crawl spaces or attics — two areas homeowners don't inspect until there's a visible stain on a ceiling or a soft spot in a floor. HomeLyft crews use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map how far water has traveled through subfloor materials and wall cavities, then deploy commercial drying equipment sized to the actual affected area. We document every reading for your insurance claim.

### Mold remediation

Sharon's humidity means mold isn't always the result of a dramatic flood — a slow drip behind a vanity or a poorly vented bathroom can produce visible growth within weeks. We contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, treat structural surfaces, and verify clearance before reconstruction begins. We follow established industry protocols for containment and air filtration throughout the process.

### Fire and smoke damage cleanup

After a kitchen fire or an electrical fault, the visible char is often less damaging than the smoke residue that settles into insulation, ductwork, and porous surfaces throughout the house. Smoke odor in Sharon homes can intensify in summer heat if remediation is delayed. HomeLyft removes unsalvageable materials, cleans and deodorizes affected surfaces, and seals or replaces what's needed before reconstruction.

### Reconstruction

Once mitigation is complete, the same company that dried your floors or cleaned your structure can rebuild — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and more. Keeping mitigation and reconstruction under one contractor simplifies the insurance process and eliminates the gap where a home can sit exposed between trades.
