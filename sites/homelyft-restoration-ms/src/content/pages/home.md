When water soaks through your drywall, smoke odor settles into every surface, or mold starts spreading behind a wall you can't see, the clock matters more than anything else. HomeLyft Restoration MS handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, and storm damage for homeowners, businesses, and property managers across the Mississippi Gulf Coast and surrounding area — with the certifications and equipment to do it right the first time.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether a pipe burst overnight or a slow leak finally gave out under the sink, standing water can reach the subfloor and wall cavities within hours. We extract, dry, and document everything to IICRC S500 standards.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue is acidic and keeps corroding surfaces long after the flames are out. We address soot, odor, and structural char — not just what's visibly burned.

**Mold remediation** — In South Mississippi's humidity, mold can begin colonizing a wet surface in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Our IICRC-certified technicians contain, remove, and treat affected materials while protecting the rest of your home from cross-contamination.

**Storm damage restoration** — Gulf Coast storms can drive water through rooflines, windows, and siding in ways that don't show up until days later. We're Fortified Building Roofing MS Windstorm Certified, which matters when you're dealing with insurers after a named storm.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Certifications aren't just wall decorations — they tell you the technician walking into your home has been trained to a documented standard. HomeLyft holds IICRC certification as a firm, with individual technicians credentialed in Water Restoration (WRT), Structural Drying (ASD), Fire and Smoke Restoration (FSRT), and Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT). The team is also EPA Lead-Safe Certified and OSHA trained, which is relevant any time work touches older construction — a common reality in Gulfport's housing stock.

License number RO 2728 is on file with the state of Mississippi, and we work directly with most major insurance carriers. That means we document the loss with photos and written scope, communicate with your adjuster, and bill the carrier directly when possible — so you're not managing a paperwork crisis on top of a property crisis.

What you won't get here is a crew that shows up, pulls a few wet baseboards, drops some fans, and disappears. Structural drying is a process with daily moisture readings, equipment adjustments, and a documented drying log — the kind of record your insurance company will ask for and that protects you if secondary damage shows up later.
