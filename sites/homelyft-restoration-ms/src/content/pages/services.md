From a burst pipe flooding a Gulfport living room at midnight to a commercial kitchen gutted by fire, HomeLyft Restoration MS handles the full arc of property damage — mitigation, drying, cleanup, and rebuild. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and business owners across the Mississippi Gulf Coast, handling both residential and commercial losses under one roof.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, every hour matters. Water intrusion can saturate wall cavities and sub-flooring within hours; smoke residue begins bonding to surfaces almost immediately after a fire; storm debris left unprotected invites secondary water damage that compounds the original loss. HomeLyft Restoration MS responds to water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, biohazard and trauma cleanup, and storm damage. Each service draws on our IICRC-certified technicians — credentialed in Water Restoration (WRT), Structural Drying (ASD), Fire and Smoke Restoration (FSRT), and Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT) — so the right expertise is matched to your specific loss from the first call. Our team is also EPA Lead-Safe Certified and OSHA trained, which matters in the older housing stock common throughout Biloxi, Gulfport, and the surrounding coastal communities.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts your property back together. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and stable, our crews handle the rebuild — framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and finish work. Because we hold a Fortified Building Roofing MS Windstorm Certification, we can reconstruct roofing systems to the Insurance Institute for Business & Home Safety's FORTIFIED standard, which is increasingly relevant for Gulf Coast properties facing hurricane-season wind and rain exposure. Working with a single contractor from demo through final paint means no handoff delays, no finger-pointing between a mitigation company and a separate GC, and a cleaner paper trail for your insurance adjuster.

## Specialty services

Not every job fits neatly into an emergency category. HomeLyft Restoration MS also provides mold inspection and testing, professional odor removal (including persistent smoke and sewage odors that survive basic cleaning), emergency board-up and tarping to secure a property after a break-in or storm breach, and contents pack-out when personal belongings need to be moved and stored during a restoration. These services often run alongside a primary mitigation project, but they're available as standalone work when that's what the situation calls for.
