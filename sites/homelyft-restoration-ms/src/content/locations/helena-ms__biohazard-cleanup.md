## Why Helena Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

The Delta climate is not forgiving. Helena's summers are long, hot, and humid, with moisture levels that accelerate the breakdown of organic materials and the growth of secondary contamination in ways that cooler, drier climates simply don't see. When a biohazard situation goes unaddressed for even a short period in this environment, the affected area can deteriorate significantly — not just the visible surface, but subflooring, wall cavities, and HVAC pathways that circulate air throughout the structure.

Helena's residential building stock skews older. Many homes in and around the city were constructed before modern building codes standardized subfloor materials and vapor barriers. Older pine subfloors and plaster walls are porous in ways that modern OSB and drywall are not — they absorb biological fluids more deeply and hold them longer, which means surface cleaning alone is never sufficient. Proper remediation requires assessing what's beneath the visible layer and, in many cases, removing and disposing of affected structural materials according to regulated waste protocols.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Helena

Every biohazard situation is different, but the process follows a disciplined sequence regardless of the specific circumstances.
