## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Sandersville

We start with a fabric and fiber test on a hidden section of the piece, since leather, microfiber, cotton blends, and older farmhouse-style upholstery all respond differently to heat and moisture. From there we vacuum and agitate to lift dry soil before any liquid touches the fabric, because wet-cleaning over embedded clay dust just turns it to paste. Steam extraction follows at a temperature and pressure matched to the material, with a low-moisture approach on pieces in rooms that already run humid so we're not adding drying time on top of what the Mississippi air is already doing. We finish with targeted air movement so cushions and frames dry evenly instead of staying damp in the center, which is where mildew odor usually starts.

## Leather vs. Fabric: Method Differences for Local Homes

Leather sofas and recliners are common in Sandersville living rooms and dens, and they need conditioning after cleaning or the material can dry out and crack, especially with wood stoves or space heaters running through the cooler months. Fabric pieces, particularly anything with a lighter weave, tend to show soil transfer from denim and work clothes more visibly, so we adjust agitation and extraction passes based on how the fabric is holding the stain rather than using one method for every job.
