## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Hattiesburg

Every project starts with a walk-through to categorize debris types and identify surfaces that need specialized treatment — grout haze on bathroom tile is handled differently than adhesive residue on hardwood, and both are different from the fine silica dust that coats every horizontal surface after framing and drywall work. The cleaning sequence matters: rough debris removal first, then high-dusting from ceiling fans and light fixtures down to baseboards, followed by detail work on windows, cabinetry interiors, and fixtures.
