**Upholstery holding onto a musty smell that won't quit, even after you've aired the room out?** In Ellisville's thick, humid stretches of the year, fabric and leather furniture pick up moisture from the air itself, not just spills, and that moisture feeds odor and staining long before anyone notices a wet spot. A proper cleaning pulls that trapped humidity and soil out of the cushions and padding, not just the surface fibers.

## Why Ellisville Homes See Upholstery Issues

Jones County's long, humid summers mean upholstered furniture rarely gets a real chance to dry out between uses. Sofas and chairs positioned against exterior walls or near windows pick up ambient moisture that settles into foam and batting, and that moisture combines with everyday body oils, pet dander, and the fine red clay dust common to this part of south Mississippi to create the dull, gray cast that shows up on light-colored fabric first. Homes built before central air was standard, and many older houses around the county seat still carry that layout, often have upholstered pieces placed in rooms with limited airflow, which slows natural drying even further. Add pollen season, which runs hard here, and fabric fibers end up holding onto allergens long after the windows are shut again.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Ellisville

We start with a fiber and dye test on an inconspicuous spot, because a lot of the furniture we see in this area is older or was reupholstered at some point, and not every fabric responds the same way to heat or moisture. From there, we vacuum thoroughly to lift dry soil before it turns to mud under steam, then apply a pretreatment matched to the fiber type, whether that's a synthetic blend, natural cotton, or leather. Fabric pieces get hot water extraction calibrated to Mississippi's ambient humidity, pulling out soil and moisture together rather than just pushing more water in. Leather gets a different approach entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner followed by conditioner, since leather in this climate dries out between humid stretches and cracks if it's not reconditioned after cleaning.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

Given how much moisture is already in the air most of the year here, we lean on low-moisture extraction equipment for upholstery rather than soaking methods that take a day or two to dry in a humid house. Portable truck-mount and hot water extraction units let us control water volume precisely, which matters more in Jones County than in drier climates because over-wetting a cushion here can mean it never fully dries before mildew sets in. For leather and delicate fabrics we use separate tools and solutions entirely, so nothing cross-contaminates between a leather recliner and a microfiber sectional in the same living room.

## Local note

One thing that catches people off guard: furniture stored in an Ellisville garage or a detached structure, even for a short stretch during a move or renovation, often needs a heavier pretreatment than a piece that's been indoors the whole time. Unconditioned outbuildings here trap heat and humidity worse than the house itself, and fabric left in that environment for even a few weeks can develop a mildew smell that regular cleaning solutions won't fully lift without a targeted enzyme treatment first.
