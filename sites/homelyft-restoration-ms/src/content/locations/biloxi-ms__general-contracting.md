## Why Biloxi Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining variable in almost every remodel here. Moisture vapor moves through wall assemblies differently when ambient humidity sits above 70% for months at a time, and materials that perform fine in drier climates — certain adhesives, engineered flooring substrates, even standard drywall compound — can fail prematurely if a contractor doesn't account for it during installation. Older homes in Biloxi's established residential corridors often contain lead-based paint in trim and window sashes, a reality that shapes demolition sequencing and disposal requirements. Biloxi also sits in one of the most wind-exposed counties in Mississippi.
