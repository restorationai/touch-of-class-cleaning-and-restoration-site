## Why Lumberton Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After Storms

Fire damage adds another layer. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lumberton

When a call comes in from a Lumberton address, the first thing the crew assesses remotely is the type of opening and the current weather forecast for the Hattiesburg–Lumberton corridor. That forecast drives material selection: a calm, dry stretch allows a standard 6-mil poly tarp anchored with dimensional lumber; an approaching front means we move to reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for higher wind loads, secured with screw-down battens rather than staples or simple weights.

On arrival, the crew photographs every opening before touching anything — documentation that matters enormously once the insurance adjuster reviews the claim. Window board-ups use ¾-inch plywood cut to overlap the frame by at least two inches on each side, fastened with structural screws rather than nails so the board can be removed cleanly when permanent repairs begin. Roof tarps are run from the peak down and over the eave, never just laid flat over the damaged section, so wind can't catch an edge and peel the cover back during the next storm. For fire-damaged structures, we assess load-bearing integrity before crew members access the roof — a step that's non-negotiable regardless of how straightforward the tarp job looks from the ground.
