## Why Pearlington Properties See Persistent Mold Problems

Handcock County's humidity rarely dips below 70% for most of the year, and in Pearlington's river-delta setting it often runs higher. That baseline moisture level means building materials never fully dry between rain events. Wood framing, OSB sheathing, and paper-faced drywall — the standard components in homes rebuilt after Katrina — can sustain mold colonization at relative humidity levels above 60%, which is simply normal outdoor air here for eight or nine months of the year.

Homes rebuilt in the mid-2000s post-Katrina are now approaching the age where original vapor barriers, HVAC duct seals, and crawlspace encapsulation are degrading. When those systems fail, the warm humid air that flows under and through a Pearlington home creates the dark, damp conditions where mold establishes in 24 to 48 hours. Older structures that survived the storm — some dating to the mid-twentieth century — often have pier-and-beam foundations with open, unencapsulated crawlspaces that act as mold incubators during the wet season.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Pearlington

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We identify not just visible growth but the hidden moisture reservoir feeding it — because treating surface mold without eliminating the moisture source produces a temporary result.

Once the scope is established, we build negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent spore migration into clean areas of the home during work. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, contaminated wood — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Mississippi DEQ guidelines. Structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins. In Pearlington's climate, we pay particular attention to drying verification: we do not close walls until readings are stable across multiple days, because the ambient humidity here can mask incomplete drying that would be obvious in a drier region.

Because HomeLyft is an EPA Lead-Safe Certified firm, homes built before 1978 — and there are a number of them in this area — receive lead-safe work practices during any material removal, at no additional process step for the homeowner.
