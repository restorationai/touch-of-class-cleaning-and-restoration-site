## Why Leakesville Homes See Elevated Contents Damage

Greene County's housing stock skews older, and many residences in and around Leakesville were built before modern vapor barriers and synthetic materials became standard. A kitchen fire that might leave only surface soot on laminate cabinets in a newer build can penetrate deeply into solid-wood cabinetry and hand-me-down furniture in an older Leakesville home.

The region's climate compounds the challenge. Summers push heat and humidity into ranges where untreated contents can warp, swell, or grow mold within days. ## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Leakesville

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory before a single item leaves the property.
