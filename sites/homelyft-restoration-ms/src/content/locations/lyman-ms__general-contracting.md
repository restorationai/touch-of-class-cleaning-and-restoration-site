## Why Lyman Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Pearl River County's combination of high annual rainfall, clay-heavy soils, and proximity to the Gulf means homes in and around Lyman deal with conditions that general contractors from drier regions often underestimate. Expansive clay soil shifts seasonally — it swells when saturated and contracts in dry spells — which puts ongoing stress on slab foundations and pier-and-beam structures alike. Older homes in the area frequently show evidence of this movement in door frames that no longer square up, tile floors that crack along grout lines, and exterior brick that develops stair-step mortar fractures over time.

The region also sits in a wind-exposure zone that affects how renovation work should be permitted and built. ## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in the Lyman area: homes built on pier-and-beam foundations — common in older parts of Pearl River County — often have crawl spaces that have never had proper vapor barriers installed or have barriers that have degraded over decades.
