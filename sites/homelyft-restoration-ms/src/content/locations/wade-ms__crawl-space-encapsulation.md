## Why Wade Properties See Crawl Space Moisture Problems

Forrest County's climate doesn't give crawl spaces much of a break. Even in winter, ground temperatures stay warm enough to keep relative humidity elevated in unprotected crawl spaces. When warm, moist air from the soil meets cooler structural wood above it, condensation forms — and within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture contact, mold colonies can begin establishing on wood surfaces.

Wade's residential areas include a mix of homes built from the 1950s through the 1980s, many of which were constructed with traditional vented crawl spaces that were standard practice at the time. Building science has since reversed that guidance: in humid climates like south Mississippi, vented crawl spaces actively pull in outdoor humidity rather than expelling it. Homes in this region that still rely on those original vents are essentially inviting the outside air — and everything it carries — into the structural core of the house.

The soil composition in this part of Forrest County also matters. Clay soils drain slowly, which means water from a heavy rain or a plumbing drip can pool beneath a home for days. That standing moisture evaporates upward, and without a sealed barrier in place, it has nowhere to go but into the wood and insulation above.

## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Wade

Encapsulation is not a single product — it's a system, and every component has to work together for the barrier to hold over time. The process begins with a thorough inspection of the crawl space: we check for existing moisture intrusion, assess the condition of floor joists and insulation, look for signs of mold or pest activity, and measure humidity levels with a calibrated meter.

If standing water or active moisture damage is present, that's addressed before any barrier goes down. Attempting to seal over a wet crawl space traps the problem rather than solving it.

Once the space is dry and structurally sound, we install a heavy-duty polyethylene vapor barrier — typically 20-mil reinforced material — across the entire crawl space floor and up the foundation walls, sealed at all seams with moisture-resistant tape. Existing vents are closed and sealed as part of converting the space to a conditioned or semi-conditioned environment. Depending on the size and configuration of the crawl space, a dehumidifier sized for the specific square footage may be installed to maintain target humidity levels year-round.

All work is performed by an IICRC-certified firm, and the process follows established moisture control standards so the encapsulation functions as a long-term system rather than a temporary fix.
