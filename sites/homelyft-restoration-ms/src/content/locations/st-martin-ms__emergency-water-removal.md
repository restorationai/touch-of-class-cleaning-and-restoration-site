## Why St. Martin Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of St. Martin sits on flat, low-lying ground typical of the Gulf Coast, which means yards and foundations drain slowly after heavy rain. A lot of the housing stock here was built on concrete slabs during the area's growth decades, and slab foundations hide water migration well: a pipe leak under the slab can soak carpet pad and baseboards for days before anyone notices the smell. Older sections of the community still have pier-and-beam construction with crawlspaces, which trap humidity differently and tend to develop musty odors faster once water gets in. Add in Harrison County's long, humid storm season, and any standing water left untreated for more than a day or two has a real shot at becoming a mold problem rather than just a water problem.

## Our Water Extraction Process in St. Martin

We start by pulling standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, prioritizing rooms where water is pooling against baseboards or wicking into cabinetry. From there we check moisture levels in the subfloor, drywall, and framing with meters, not guesswork, because a floor that looks dry on top can still be saturated underneath, especially with slab construction where water has nowhere else to go. Our technicians work under IICRC water damage and structural drying protocols, which means air movers and dehumidifiers get positioned based on the actual moisture readings in your home rather than a one-size-fits-all setup. We log those readings daily until materials hit dry standard, then document everything for your insurance file.

## St. Martin Insurance & Flood Considerations

A good share of water losses we see in this part of Harrison County involve a mix of homeowner's policy coverage and, in some cases, separate flood insurance, since parts of the Gulf Coast fall within mapped flood zones. We photograph and document affected materials as we remove them so your adjuster has a clear record of what was saturated and what had to come out, and we can work directly with most major carriers so you're not paying out of pocket while a claim is processed. If your loss involves rising water from outside the home rather than a plumbing failure, we'll flag that early since it can change which policy applies.

## Local note

Homes in St. Martin built with vinyl or sheet flooring directly over a slab often look fine on the surface within a day of a leak, but the adhesive layer underneath can stay wet for a week or longer, which is why we don't call a slab-floor job finished just because the surface reads dry. We've learned to pull a test section of flooring in those cases rather than relying on a single moisture reading, because Gulf Coast humidity slows the last stage of drying more than most homeowners expect.
