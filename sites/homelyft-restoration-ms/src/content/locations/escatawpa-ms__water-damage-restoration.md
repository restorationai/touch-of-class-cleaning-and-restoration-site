## Why Escatawpa Properties See Water Damage Issues

The geography along the MS-613 and US-90 corridors in this part of Jackson County creates a specific set of conditions that accelerate water intrusion. Clay-dominant soil expands when wet and contracts in dry spells, and that repeated movement gradually opens gaps around pipe penetrations and foundation perimeters. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s — a common vintage in this area — often have galvanized supply lines that have reached the end of their service life, making pinhole leaks and sudden bursts more likely. Pier-and-beam construction, still common in lower-lying parcels closer to the river, traps moisture underneath the floor deck where it can feed wood rot and mold colonies within 24 to 48 hours of a leak event.

The regional humidity compounds every stage of drying. On a typical August afternoon in Escatawpa, outdoor relative humidity can sit above 85%, which means opening windows to "air things out" after a flood actually slows evaporation rather than speeding it. Effective structural drying here requires sealed, dehumidified drying chambers — not passive ventilation.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Escatawpa

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We document readings at the affected walls, subfloor, and ceiling cavities before any equipment is placed — this baseline matters for both the drying log and your insurance claim.

**Water extraction** comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from flooring assemblies, including from under hardwood and laminate where it pools between the subfloor and finish layer. In slab-on-grade homes common in this area, we pay close attention to the gap between the slab and bottom plate, where water wicks into wall cavities without visible surface flooding.

**Structural drying** follows the IICRC S500 standard. We calculate the drying system load based on the affected square footage, material types, and the ambient conditions outside — then place LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in a configuration that drives moisture out of building materials and into the air stream for the dehumidifiers to capture. Daily moisture readings track progress and let us adjust equipment placement as the structure dries.

**Contents handling** runs parallel. Salvageable furniture and personal items are moved, documented, and either dried on-site or packed out to our facility. Items that cannot be restored are photographed for the insurance record before disposal.
