## Why DeLisle Properties See Mold Issues

Harrison County's subtropical climate doesn't give mold spores much of an off-season. Summer heat drives moisture into wall assemblies through vapor drive, and the shoulder months — April through June and September through November — bring enough rain to keep soil saturation high around foundations. Crawl-space homes common throughout the DeLisle area are especially vulnerable: when ground moisture evaporates upward and meets unconditioned air beneath the floor deck, mold can establish itself on floor joists and subfloor panels long before a homeowner notices any interior sign.

Older wood-framed construction in this part of Harrison County often used dimensional lumber with higher moisture content than modern kiln-dried material, which means the substrate itself is more hospitable to fungal growth once humidity climbs. Air conditioning systems that cycle off during extended absences — vacation, storm evacuation — can allow interior humidity to spike above 60% within hours, creating ideal conditions for rapid colonization.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in DeLisle

Every job begins with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters. We identify the moisture source first — because remediating mold without correcting the underlying water intrusion is a temporary fix at best. Once the source is addressed, we establish negative-pressure containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet padding — that cannot be cleaned to clearance are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines. Hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution. Structural lumber in crawl spaces and attics, which we see frequently on DeLisle properties, is wire-brushed, HEPA-vacuumed, and treated before encapsulation where appropriate. Drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers depending on ambient conditions — runs until we confirm moisture readings are back within normal range. We document every step with photographs and moisture logs, which your insurance adjuster will need.
