## Why Helena Properties Face Elevated Contents Damage Risk

Phillips County's climate is relentless in ways that accelerate secondary damage to personal belongings. Summer relative humidity regularly sits above 70 percent, which means smoke odor doesn't just sit on surfaces after a fire — it bonds with fabric fibers and wood grain faster than it would in a drier climate. After a water loss, mold can begin colonizing porous materials like upholstered sofas, cardboard boxes, and wood furniture in as little as 24 to 48 hours when ambient humidity is already elevated before the loss even happens.

Helena's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in the area were built in the mid-twentieth century, with solid-wood furniture, plaster-and-lathe interior walls, and older construction that tends to hold moisture longer than modern builds. When a pipe bursts or a roof is breached during a storm, the water doesn't just pool — it wicks into wood floors and the furniture sitting on them, making a prompt pack-out of contents one of the most important early decisions in any restoration project.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Helena

The first step is a detailed room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and assessed before anything leaves the property. This documentation matters enormously for insurance claims, and it gives homeowners a clear record of what was removed and where it's going.

Salvageable items are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility while structural work proceeds at the home. In Helena's humid environment, that climate control isn't a luxury — it's what prevents mold from taking hold on furniture and textiles during the weeks a home may be under active remediation or reconstruction.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Ultrasonic cleaning works well for hard goods, collectibles, and non-porous items. Ozone and hydroxyl treatments address smoke and odor in textiles and soft furnishings. Electronics and documents require a different approach entirely — specialized drying and cleaning protocols that differ from what you'd use on a wool rug or a wooden dining set. The IICRC-certified team at HomeLyft Restoration MS follows documented standards for each material category rather than applying a single method across the board.

Once the home is cleared and restored, items are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in their original rooms. The goal is to hand the home back to the family looking as close to pre-loss condition as possible.
