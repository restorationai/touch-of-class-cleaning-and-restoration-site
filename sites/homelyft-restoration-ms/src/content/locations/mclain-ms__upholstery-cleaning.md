## Why McLain Furniture Wears Differently

McLain sits well inland from the coast, in pine-forest country where pollen season runs long and homes often rely on wood stoves or window units rather than whole-house HVAC. That combination means upholstery here collects a specific mix of problems: fine soot particles that settle into fabric weave, pollen that triggers allergies when it's stirred up by a cushion, and ambient humidity that keeps fibers from ever fully drying between cleanings. Older site-built homes in the area, many without central air, also run more window-open hours in spring and fall, which lets outdoor dust and insects work their way into upholstered seams faster than in a sealed, air-conditioned house.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start with a fabric or leather identification test, since a hunting cabin's leather recliner and a living room's cotton-blend sofa need completely different chemistry. For fabric pieces, we use hot water extraction with a pH-balanced solution, agitating gently to lift embedded soil and soot before the extraction pass pulls both the cleaner and the loosened residue back out. Leather gets a separate conditioning step to keep it from cracking, since Mississippi's humidity swings can dry out unconditioned leather faster than people expect. Because ambient moisture is already high in Greene County most of the year, we pay close attention to drying time and often run air movers for a few hours after extraction so the piece doesn't stay damp longer than it should, which is exactly the kind of environment that invites mildew odor to set back in.

## Local note

One thing that catches people off guard: furniture stored in a detached garage or hunting camp outbuilding in this area often has a different moisture profile than furniture kept in the main house, even after it's brought back inside. Unconditioned outbuildings in Greene County run humid nearly year-round, and fabric or leather that's sat out there for a season can carry moisture deep in the padding that isn't obvious from the surface. We check for that before cleaning, because running standard extraction on already-damp padding can trap moisture instead of releasing it.
