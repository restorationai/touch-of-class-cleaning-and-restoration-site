## Why Nicholson Properties See Crawl Space Moisture Problems

Pearl River County's climate is classified as humid subtropical, which means even a dry week still leaves relative humidity levels in an unprotected crawl space well above the 60% threshold where mold begins to thrive. The soil composition in this part of Mississippi compounds the issue. Clay-dominant ground doesn't drain quickly — it absorbs rainfall and then releases it slowly, creating a persistent vapor source directly beneath your floor joists.

Many homes in the Nicholson area were built during a period when vented crawl spaces were considered best practice. Building science has since reversed that position. Vents that were meant to carry moisture out often pull humid outdoor air in, especially during Mississippi summers when outdoor dew points routinely exceed indoor temperatures. The result is condensation forming on cold water lines, subfloor sheathing, and the underside of your flooring — damage that's invisible until a floor starts to feel soft underfoot or a musty smell reaches the living space above.

## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Nicholson

Every encapsulation project starts with a thorough crawl space inspection — measuring moisture content in the wood framing, checking for existing mold growth, assessing insulation condition, and identifying any standing water or drainage issues. In Nicholson's climate, we frequently find that fiberglass batt insulation installed between floor joists has absorbed enough moisture to lose most of its R-value and is holding fungal growth against the wood it was meant to protect. That material comes out before encapsulation begins.

The encapsulation itself involves sealing the crawl space from the ground up. We install a reinforced polyethylene vapor barrier — typically 20-mil thickness — across the entire crawl space floor and up the foundation walls, overlapping seams and taping them airtight. Pier columns and any penetrations get wrapped and sealed individually. Depending on the home's configuration, we then address ventilation: either conditioning the crawl space with a supply of dehumidified air from the HVAC system or installing a dedicated crawl space dehumidifier sized to the square footage. Vents are sealed to prevent the humid outdoor air exchange that caused the problem in the first place. The team holds IICRC AMRT (mold) and IICRC ASD (structural drying) certifications, so the moisture readings that guide these decisions are calibrated, not guesswork.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Crawl Space Encapsulation

The vapor barrier material matters more than most homeowners realize. Thin poly sheeting tears during routine HVAC maintenance visits and degrades faster under the alkaline conditions that concrete foundations create. The reinforced 20-mil barrier we use is rated for long-term ground contact and resists puncture from gravel or debris on the crawl space floor.
