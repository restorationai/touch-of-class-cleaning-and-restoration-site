## Why Picayune Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Pearl River County's clay-heavy soils expand and contract dramatically with Mississippi's wet-dry seasonal swings. That ground movement puts lateral stress on older clay tile and cast iron sewer lines — the type of pipe still found under many Picayune homes built before the 1980s. When those lines crack or belly, roots infiltrate and blockages form. Combine that with the area's tendency toward heavy rainfall events that overwhelm municipal sewer capacity, and the result is backflow that pushes sewage up through floor drains, toilets, and tub drains simultaneously.

Septic system overflows are equally common in the rural and semi-rural pockets surrounding Picayune, where lot sizes are large and systems sometimes date back decades. A saturated drain field after a prolonged rain event can send effluent back toward the home just as surely as a blocked municipal line.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Picayune

Every sewage job starts with containment. Before any extraction begins, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination from spreading to unaffected living spaces — a step that matters especially in Picayune's older ranch-style and pier-and-beam homes where open floor plans and crawl spaces can channel contaminated water across a wide footprint.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Extraction and removal** — Standing sewage water is removed with truck-mounted extraction units. Saturated porous materials (carpet, pad, drywall, insulation) that cannot be decontaminated to safe levels are bagged and disposed of per Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines.
- **Surface cleaning and disinfection** — All affected hard surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Category 3 (black water) contamination requires multiple treatment passes, not a single spray-and-wipe.
- **Structural drying** — Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned using psychrometric calculations to dry framing, subfloor sheathing, and concrete slab areas. Moisture readings are logged daily.
- **Post-sanitization verification** — Before equipment is removed, surface moisture and air quality readings confirm the structure has returned to acceptable levels. Documentation is provided for insurance and for any future disclosure requirements.
