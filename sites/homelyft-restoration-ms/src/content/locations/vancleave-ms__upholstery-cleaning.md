## Why Vancleave Furniture Ages Differently

Vancleave sits far enough inland to avoid direct storm surge, but the humidity off the Gulf doesn't stop at the coastline. Homes here run air conditioning most of the year, and that constant cycle of cool indoor air against warm, moist air infiltrating through doors and mudroom entries leaves upholstery in a near-constant state of slow moisture absorption. Add in the rural and semi-rural lots common around Jackson County, where pets, hunting gear, and farm dust track onto fabric furniture more than in denser subdivisions, and you get soiling patterns that respond poorly to store-bought spot cleaners. Leather furniture fares differently but no better: the same humidity that swells fabric fibers dries out leather's natural oils when the AC runs hard, leading to cracking that homeowners often mistake for normal wear.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process for Vancleave Homes

We start every job with a fabric and fill assessment, because a microfiber sectional and a cotton-blend armchair need different chemistry and different dwell times. For fabric upholstery, we use low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded soil and allergens without oversaturating cushion foam, a step that matters more here than in drier regions since foam that stays damp too long in a humid house is exactly how mildew odor starts. Leather pieces get a separate conditioning-based process: gentle cleaning followed by a pH-balanced leather conditioner to replace the oils that coastal-humidity air conditioning strips out over time. Drying is the part most DIY jobs get wrong in this climate. We use targeted airflow and, when needed, dehumidification equipment to bring cushions back to a safe moisture level within hours rather than letting a damp sofa sit in a closed-up room overnight.

## Local note

One thing we watch for specifically on Jackson County service calls: homes on larger rural lots around Vancleave often have well water rather than municipal service, and well water with higher iron or mineral content can leave rust-colored tidelines on light-colored fabric upholstery if furniture has ever been near a leaking planter, pet water bowl, or humidifier. Standard upholstery shampoo doesn't touch mineral staining, it actually can set it, so we test for mineral versus organic staining before choosing a cleaning solution on any fabric piece with unexplained discoloration.
