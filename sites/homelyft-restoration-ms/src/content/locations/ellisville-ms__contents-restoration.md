## Why Ellisville Homes Face Elevated Contents Damage Risk

Jones County averages well over 50 inches of rainfall annually, and Ellisville's older residential neighborhoods — many with homes built in the mid-twentieth century — tend to feature wood-framed construction with pier-and-beam foundations that can shift and settle over decades. That settling creates gaps where moisture migrates, and when a plumbing failure or roof breach finally happens, water doesn't just pool on the floor — it wicks into baseboards, travels under hardwood, and saturates furniture sitting directly on affected surfaces.

Smoke damage compounds the problem. Older homes in Ellisville often have original wood paneling, plaster walls, and porous ceiling tiles that absorb odor deeply. Textiles, upholstered pieces, and paper documents left inside a smoke-affected room for even 48 to 72 hours can cross the threshold from restorable to unsalvageable. Getting contents out of the affected environment fast is not a luxury — it's what determines what you get to keep.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Ellisville

Every pack-out starts with a room-by-room inventory. Each item is photographed, catalogued, and given a condition rating before it leaves your property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was removed and in what state.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning tanks for hard goods, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for textiles and upholstered furniture with embedded smoke odor, and HEPA-filtered air scrubbing for documents and electronics staged in a controlled area.

Once your property has been restored and cleared, contents are returned and placed back in their original rooms based on the inventory photos. Nothing is guessed at.
