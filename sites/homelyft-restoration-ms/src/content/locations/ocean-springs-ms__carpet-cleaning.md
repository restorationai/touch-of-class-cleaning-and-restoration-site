## Why Ocean Springs Carpets Take a Harder Hit

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining factor. Ocean Springs averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and relative humidity regularly climbs above 80% during summer months. That persistent moisture doesn't just sit on the surface — it penetrates carpet backing and padding, creating conditions where dust mites, mold spores, and bacteria thrive well below the pile. Combine that with the fine, silica-rich sand common along the Mississippi Sound shoreline, and you have an abrasive particulate that works its way deep into carpet fibers with every footstep, dulling color and accelerating wear.

Older homes in Ocean Springs — and the city has a genuinely rich stock of them, from Victorian-era cottages near Washington Avenue to mid-century bungalows a few blocks off Government Street — often have carpet laid over original hardwood or concrete slab. Slab foundations in particular are prone to moisture wicking upward, which keeps carpet backing damp even when the surface looks dry. That hidden moisture is where odor problems start.

## Our Deep Carpet Cleaning Process, Calibrated for Coastal Conditions

Before any cleaning begins, a technician walks the space and assesses moisture levels in the backing and subfloor — a step that matters more here than it would in a drier inland climate. If elevated moisture is present, we address that first; cleaning a wet carpet only compounds the problem.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Pre-treatment and agitation** — A pH-balanced pre-spray loosens embedded soil and breaks down the oily residue that salt air deposits on fibers over time. A counter-rotating brush works the solution into the pile rather than just coating the surface.
- **Hot-water extraction** — Truck-mounted equipment delivers heated water at controlled pressure and recovers it along with suspended soils in a single pass. The heat is particularly effective against the biological load — dust mites, pet dander, mold spores — that Gulf Coast humidity encourages.
- **Stain treatment** — Tannin stains from sweet tea and coffee, red clay tracked in from Jackson County's interior, and the occasional storm-water intrusion stain each respond to different chemistry. Spot treatment is matched to the stain type rather than applied as a blanket solution.
- **Deodorization and sanitization** — A sanitizing rinse reduces bacterial counts in the fiber and backing. For homes with pets or any history of flooding, an enzyme-based deodorizer is applied to the backing before the final extraction pass.
- **Controlled drying** — High-velocity air movers are positioned to bring drying time down to two to four hours in most cases. In peak summer humidity, we may extend drying time or recommend the homeowner run the HVAC to assist.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Ocean Springs homes built in the 1960s and 1970s is carpet installed directly over concrete slab with a thin foam pad — a common construction choice in coastal Mississippi at the time. That foam degrades with age and moisture exposure, and when it breaks down it releases a fine particulate that discolors carpet from the backing outward. No amount of surface cleaning resolves it; the pad has to be replaced. If a home in that era shows persistent gray or brown shadowing at the edges of rooms or along baseboards, that's almost always the culprit, not surface soil. It's worth flagging before a cleaning appointment so expectations are set correctly.
