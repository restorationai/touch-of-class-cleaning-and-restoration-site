## Why Helena's Building Stock Shapes Every Renovation

Phillips County's older homes — many built before World War II — carry construction details that modern contractors sometimes underestimate. Balloon-frame walls, original knob-and-tube wiring runs, and plaster-over-lath interiors are common in Helena's established residential blocks. Opening a wall for a kitchen remodel or bathroom update can surface surprises: galvanized supply lines that have narrowed to a trickle, subfloor joists notched for old cast-iron drains, or load-bearing walls that don't appear on any original plan.

The Delta's climate compounds this. Summers in Helena push heat indices well above 100°F, and the combination of high humidity and aging envelope details — single-pane windows, minimal attic insulation, gaps in vapor barriers — drives moisture into wall cavities year-round. A bathroom remodel that skips proper vapor management in this climate is a mold problem waiting to happen. Every scope we write for Helena homes accounts for the existing envelope conditions, not just the finish work a homeowner can see.
