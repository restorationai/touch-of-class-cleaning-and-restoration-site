## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Helena

When HomeLyft Restoration MS dispatches to a Helena property, the crew arrives with a pre-loaded vehicle carrying plywood panels in standard and oversized dimensions, OSB sheathing, heavy-gauge polyethylene tarping rated for wind uplift, roofing nails, screws, batten strips, and the ladders necessary to access steep-pitch roofs safely. The process moves in a defined sequence:

**Assessment first.** The crew walks the perimeter and photographs every opening — broken windows, missing doors, compromised roof sections, damaged soffit — before touching anything. This documentation is critical for your insurance adjuster and establishes a clear before/after record.

**Roof tarping.** For partial roof losses, tarping is installed using batten strips secured through the tarp and into the roof deck, not just stapled at the edges. In Helena's wind environment, a tarp that is only perimeter-fastened becomes a sail in the next storm. Battened installation holds through sustained winds and buys the time needed for a proper roofing contractor to assess structural damage.

**Window and door board-up.** Openings are measured and cut to fit, then secured with exterior-grade fasteners into the surrounding framing — not just the window trim. For historic or older homes common in Helena, the crew takes care not to damage original millwork unnecessarily, since that material is often irreplaceable.

**Re-inspection and documentation.** Before leaving, the crew photographs the completed work and provides a written scope summary you can hand directly to your insurance carrier.
