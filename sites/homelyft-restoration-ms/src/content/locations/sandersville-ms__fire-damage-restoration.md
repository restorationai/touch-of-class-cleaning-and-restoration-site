## Why Sandersville Properties Face Particular Fire Damage Challenges

Sandersville sits in a part of Mississippi where older wood-frame construction is common — the kind of home where balloon framing allows smoke and heat to travel vertically through wall cavities with almost no resistance. When fire moves through that type of framing, soot deposits inside wall voids long before visible damage appears on the surface. Homeowners who attempt a surface-level cleanup often discover weeks later that odor is still radiating from inside the walls.

The region's humidity compounds the problem. Firefighting water — typically hundreds of gallons — combines with Mississippi's ambient moisture to create conditions where mold can begin colonizing wet framing within 24 to 48 hours of the fire. That overlap between fire damage and water damage is one of the most misunderstood aspects of post-fire restoration, and it's why a team certified in both fire and smoke restoration (IICRC FSRT) and structural drying (IICRC ASD) matters more here than in a drier climate.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Sandersville

Every fire job starts with a thorough inspection — not just of the burned area, but of the entire structure. Smoke follows air pathways, so a kitchen fire can deposit fine soot particles in a bedroom on the opposite end of the house. We document every affected surface with photographs and moisture readings before any cleaning begins, which also creates the paper trail your insurance adjuster will need.

From there, the process moves through these concrete phases:

- **Board-up and tarping** to secure the structure and prevent weather intrusion through compromised rooflines or windows
- **Debris removal and controlled demolition** of materials that cannot be restored — charred framing, melted insulation, compromised drywall
- **Dry ice blasting or chemical sponge cleaning** of salvageable surfaces, calibrated to the material (wood, brick, metal, and drywall each respond differently)
- **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment** to neutralize smoke odor molecules embedded in porous materials
- **Structural drying** to address firefighting water before it becomes a mold problem
- **Reconstruction coordination** to return the home to pre-loss condition

For homes with older construction common in this part of Jones County, we pay particular attention to attic spaces and crawlspaces, where smoke and heat damage is frequently underestimated during initial walk-throughs.
