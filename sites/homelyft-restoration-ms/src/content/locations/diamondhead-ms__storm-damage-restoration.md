## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Diamondhead

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional water from entering the structure. That means inspecting the roof, siding, windows, and any penetrations created by fallen debris, then installing temporary tarps or plywood board-up where needed. We document every damaged surface photographically before anything is moved or covered — that documentation becomes the backbone of your insurance claim.

Once the envelope is secured, the focus shifts to moisture mapping. Even a few hours of rain intrusion through a compromised roof can saturate attic insulation, run down interior wall cavities, and pool under flooring. Using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, the team identifies wet materials that aren't visible to the eye. Structural drying follows IICRC S500 and S520 protocols, with commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers placed according to a calculated drying plan — not guesswork. Affected insulation, drywall, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed and documented for replacement.

Tree and debris removal is coordinated in parallel, with particular attention to root damage at the foundation line. After debris is cleared, the structural repair phase begins: roof decking, framing, sheathing, and exterior finishes are rebuilt to current Mississippi wind-load code, with upgrades to FORTIFIED standards available for homeowners who want stronger long-term protection.
