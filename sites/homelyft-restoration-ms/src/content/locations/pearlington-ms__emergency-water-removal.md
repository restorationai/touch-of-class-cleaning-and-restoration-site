## Why Pearlington Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Pearlington was rebuilt after Katrina with homes raised on piers or block foundations, which helps during storm surge but creates a different problem once water does get in: it pools underneath the structure, in the crawlspace, against ductwork and subfloor insulation where nobody sees it until the smell shows up or the floor starts to feel soft. The Pearl River floodplain also means the water table here sits close to the surface most of the year, so soil around foundations drains slowly after heavy rain. A sewer backup, a failed sump pump, or a supply line break doesn't just flood a room, it saturates ground that was already wet to begin with, which slows natural drying and raises the odds of wood rot and mold colonizing framing within 24 to 48 hours.

## Our Water Extraction Process in Pearlington

We start with a moisture assessment using penetrating meters and thermal imaging to find water that's migrated past what's visible, including under pier-and-beam crawlspaces common throughout the area. Extraction comes next with truck-mount or portable pumps depending on access, since a lot of Pearlington properties have gravel driveways or long setbacks from the road that affect how we stage equipment. Once standing water is out, we map moisture in flooring, drywall, and framing, then set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the IICRC S500 drying standard. Our technicians carry WRT and ASD training, so the drying plan accounts for how slowly moisture releases from older framing lumber and crawlspace insulation batts rather than just running equipment until a meter reads dry on the surface.

## Pearlington Insurance & Flood Claim Coordination

A large share of Pearlington sits in a FEMA-mapped flood zone, so many homeowners carry NFIP flood policies in addition to standard homeowners coverage, and the two handle water claims differently. We document the source of the water, photograph affected materials before removal, and separate storm-surge or river-flood damage from plumbing-source damage in our reporting, since that distinction often determines which policy responds to the claim. We bill most major carriers directly so you're not covering the full cost upfront while the claim is reviewed.
