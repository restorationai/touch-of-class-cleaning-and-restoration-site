When a pipe bursts, a kitchen fire smolders, or a storm pushes water under your doors, the belongings inside your home absorb damage faster than most people expect. ## Why DeLisle Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Harrison County's subtropical humidity creates a specific problem for contents after any water or fire event: secondary damage sets in quickly. Smoke odor binds to fabric and porous wood within hours in warm, moist air. Mold can begin colonizing soft goods — upholstered sofas, mattresses, area rugs — in as little as 24 to 48 hours when ambient humidity stays above 60%, which is routine in DeLisle from April through October.

Many homes in this part of the county were built during the post-Katrina reconstruction boom, using materials and layouts that prioritized speed of rebuild over long-term moisture resistance. That means particleboard furniture components, laminate flooring, and insulation batts that swell and delaminate quickly when wet. Knowing which items are worth the investment of professional cleaning versus which are a total loss is a judgment call that requires hands-on experience with this specific regional housing stock — not a generic checklist.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in DeLisle

The first step is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a priority — salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with specialized treatment, or non-salvageable. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear picture before anything leaves your home.

Salvageable contents are carefully packed out and transported to a climate-controlled facility where temperature and humidity are actively managed — critical for preventing secondary mold growth on items that are already stressed by moisture or smoke exposure. Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics components, dry-soot sponging followed by ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke-affected textiles, and hand-cleaning for fragile or antique items.

Once your home has been dried and restored, belongings are returned in the same documented order they left. Nothing is returned to a structure that still has elevated moisture readings — a step that matters especially in DeLisle's climate, where re-introducing cleaned contents into a still-damp home would undo the work within days.

Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration) and IICRC AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation) certifications, which directly inform how we handle smoke-damaged fabrics and mold-risk items during the pack-out process.

## DeLisle Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents claims are often the most contested part of a property insurance file.
