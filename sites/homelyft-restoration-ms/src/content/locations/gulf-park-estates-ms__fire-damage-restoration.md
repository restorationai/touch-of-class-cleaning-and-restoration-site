## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Gulf Park Estates

Every job starts with a structured assessment — not a walkthrough, but a room-by-room documentation of char depth, smoke penetration zones, and suppression water intrusion. We photograph and log everything before a single item is moved, which protects your insurance claim and gives the adjuster a clear record.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Emergency board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against Gulf weather, which can shift quickly and introduce additional moisture into an already compromised building.
- **Contents pack-out** — salvageable items are catalogued, packed, and transported to a controlled environment for cleaning and deodorization, rather than attempting restoration inside a smoke-saturated space.
- **Structural cleaning** using dry-chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and where needed, media blasting on exposed framing to remove char and embedded soot.
- **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation** for odor neutralization — particularly important in Gulf Park Estates homes where the open floor plans and high ceilings common in coastal construction allow smoke to travel widely.
- **Moisture extraction and structural drying**, run concurrently with smoke remediation, using calibrated drying equipment and monitored with psychrometric readings until materials reach target moisture content.
- **Reconstruction coordination** — our team holds Mississippi RO license #RO 2728, so we can move from mitigation directly into rebuild without the homeowner managing a second contractor handoff.
