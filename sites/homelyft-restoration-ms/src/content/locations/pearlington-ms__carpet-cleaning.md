## Why Pearlington Carpets Face Harder Challenges Than Most

The Gulf Coast's subtropical climate is the defining factor for carpet care in this part of Hancock County. Relative humidity regularly climbs above 80 percent for weeks at a stretch, which means carpets that aren't dried thoroughly after any wet cleaning become a breeding ground for mildew within 24 to 48 hours. That's not a worst-case scenario here — it's a routine risk that shapes every decision we make about dwell time, extraction pressure, and airflow during a cleaning job.

Pearlington's proximity to the Pearl River also means that many homes have dealt with flood intrusion at some point, either from named storms or from the river backing up during heavy inland rain events. Even after a home is restored, carpets that were replaced or re-laid post-flood can trap residual sediment and organic matter in the pad and subfloor boundary — material that works its way back up over time and shows up as persistent discoloration or a musty smell that returns every summer.

Older homes in the area, many of which were rebuilt or substantially renovated after Hurricane Katrina, often have slab-on-grade construction with minimal vapor barrier beneath the flooring system. That means ground moisture migrates upward more readily than it would in a raised foundation home, keeping the carpet pad slightly damp even in dry spells and accelerating the breakdown of adhesives and backing materials.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process, Calibrated for This Climate

Before any cleaning begins, we assess moisture conditions in the carpet, pad, and subfloor using calibrated meters. In Hancock County's climate, starting a hot-water extraction cycle on a carpet that's already holding elevated moisture can push water deeper into the pad rather than pulling it out — a mistake that leads to longer dry times and potential mold growth beneath the surface.

Once we confirm baseline conditions, the process moves through pre-treatment, agitation, and truck-mounted hot-water extraction. The extraction step is where equipment capacity matters most in this region: our truck-mounted units generate consistent heat and vacuum pressure regardless of ambient humidity, which is critical when outdoor air is already saturated and can't accept much additional moisture. We don't use portable units on full-room jobs here for that reason.

After extraction, we set directional air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's square footage and the current dew point. Dry time targets in Pearlington's climate run longer than national averages — typically six to ten hours for a standard room versus the four to six hours you'd see in a drier climate. We communicate that timeline clearly before we leave so nothing is moved back onto the carpet prematurely.

For stain removal, we identify fiber type and stain chemistry before applying any treatment. The silty, iron-rich soil common near the Pearl River delta responds differently to spotting agents than typical clay-based dirt — alkaline pre-sprays can set some mineral stains rather than lift them, so we test in an inconspicuous area first on every job.
