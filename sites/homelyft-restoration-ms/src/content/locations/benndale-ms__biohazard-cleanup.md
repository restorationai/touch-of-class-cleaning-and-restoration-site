## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Benndale

Every job starts with a thorough assessment — identifying affected surfaces, porous materials, and any secondary contamination pathways like HVAC returns or subfloor gaps. We treat each situation as its own scope of work, not a template.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment**: Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or the home's air supply.
- **Removal of contaminated materials**: Porous materials — carpet, padding, sections of subfloor — that cannot be decontaminated are carefully removed and disposed of in accordance with Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) regulations for biohazardous waste.
- **Disinfection and treatment**: Hard surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants. Our OSHA-trained technicians follow bloodborne pathogen protocols throughout.
- **Odor neutralization**: Biological situations often leave persistent odor compounds embedded in structural materials. We use hydroxyl or thermal fogging treatment calibrated to the specific materials present.
- **Clearance verification**: Before we close out, affected areas are inspected to confirm contamination has been fully addressed.

Because we hold IICRC AMRT certification, we're also equipped to identify and address any secondary mold growth that may have begun — a real concern in George County's climate.
