The Gulf Coast's humidity rarely drops below uncomfortable for long. ## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Pass Christian

Every job starts with a walkthrough to assess fiber type, staining patterns, and — critically in this climate — any signs of moisture intrusion from below. We check pad condition before we commit to cleaning, because deep-cleaning a carpet over a saturated pad just reactivates the problem.
