## Why Pass Christian Properties See Water Damage Differently

The Mississippi Gulf Coast averages more than 60 inches of rainfall annually, and Pass Christian's position on the western edge of Harrison County puts it directly in the path of moisture-laden air moving off the Gulf. That sustained humidity — often 80% or higher in summer — means structural drying here is a fundamentally different challenge than it would be in a drier inland climate.

The housing stock adds another layer. Many homes along the beachfront and in the older residential blocks closer to downtown were built decades before modern moisture barriers and vapor retarders became standard. Crawl space foundations are common, and those crawl spaces can hold standing water for days after a heavy rain event without any visible sign inside the living area. By the time a homeowner notices a musty smell or soft spot in the floor, moisture has often already begun degrading the subfloor decking and floor joists beneath.

Sanitary sewer backups are also a recurring problem here. When Gulf-driven storm systems overwhelm the area's drainage infrastructure, sewage can push back through floor drains and toilets — a category of water damage that requires a different response than a clean supply-line break, both in terms of protective equipment and the materials that can safely be dried versus those that must be removed.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Pass Christian

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. In Pass Christian homes with crawl spaces, that inspection extends below the floor deck — because what reads as a dry surface can conceal saturated framing just inches below.

Once we understand the full scope, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. For homes with pier-and-beam construction, we access the crawl space directly to remove pooled water and begin airflow. Structural drying follows, using industrial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers along with high-velocity air movers positioned to dry wall cavities and subfloor assemblies — not just surface materials. Drying progress is documented daily with moisture readings so there's a clear record for your insurance claim.

All drying is performed to the IICRC S500 standard, and our team holds IICRC WRT and ASD certifications, meaning the people running equipment on your job understand the science of psychrometrics, not just the mechanics of placing fans.

For homes built before 1978 — and there are many in Pass Christian — our EPA Lead-Safe certification matters. Disturbing painted surfaces during water damage work in older homes requires lead-safe work practices, and we follow them without needing a reminder.

## Pass Christian Insurance Coordination

Water damage claims on the Gulf Coast carry some nuances that homeowners inland don't typically encounter. Flood damage — water that enters from outside the structure due to rising surface water — is typically covered under a separate NFIP or private flood policy, not a standard homeowner's policy. Interior water damage from a burst pipe, appliance failure, or roof leak is usually a homeowner's policy claim. The distinction matters because it affects which adjuster handles your file and what documentation they need.

We photograph affected materials, document moisture readings, and prepare the kind of detailed scope that adjusters and insurance carriers expect. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster to keep the claim moving.
