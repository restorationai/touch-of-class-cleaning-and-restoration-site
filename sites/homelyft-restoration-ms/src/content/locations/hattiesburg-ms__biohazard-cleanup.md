## Why Hattiesburg Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Hattiesburg sits in Forrest County, where summer relative humidity regularly exceeds 80 percent. That ambient moisture accelerates the breakdown of biological materials and creates secondary concerns — odor penetration into porous surfaces, potential cross-contamination into HVAC systems, and faster deterioration of subflooring and wall cavities. Many homes in the city's older residential corridors were built in the mid-twentieth century with materials that are more porous and harder to fully decontaminate than modern construction: tongue-and-groove hardwood floors, plaster-over-lath walls, and crawl-space foundations that allow air to circulate beneath living areas. Each of these factors changes how a biohazard cleanup must be approached — containment barriers need to account for HVAC return paths, and decontamination protocols must reach further into the building envelope than a simple surface clean.

Mississippi state regulations require that biological waste be handled, packaged, and transported by personnel following OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Hattiesburg

Every job begins with a site assessment before any cleaning starts. The technician identifies the full extent of contamination — visible and non-visible — and establishes a containment zone so that unaffected areas of the property stay unaffected. In Hattiesburg's climate, this step also includes checking whether the HVAC system has been running, because conditioned air can distribute particulates through ductwork before anyone realizes the scope of the problem.

Once containment is established, affected materials are removed following regulated medical waste disposal protocols. Surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological agents present. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated — sections of subfloor, drywall, carpet padding — are bagged, labeled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. After cleaning, the team conducts a post-remediation verification check before removing containment, and full documentation is prepared for insurance or legal purposes.

Throughout the process, the team works with a level of discretion that matters in residential neighborhoods. Unmarked vehicles, plain protective equipment rather than conspicuous hazmat suits when the situation permits, and direct communication only with the authorized point of contact are standard practice.
