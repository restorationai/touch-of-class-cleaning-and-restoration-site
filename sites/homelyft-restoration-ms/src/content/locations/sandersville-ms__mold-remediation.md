## Why Sandersville Properties See Mold Issues

Mississippi's Gulf Coast climate pushes inland further than most homeowners expect. Sandersville and the surrounding Jones County area regularly see summer humidity levels above 80%, and that moisture doesn't stay outside. It seeps through crawl space vents, condenses on cold water pipes, and saturates insulation in attics that weren't designed with today's vapor loads in mind. Older homes — particularly those built before the 1980s with pier-and-beam foundations — often have minimal vapor barriers beneath the subfloor, if any at all. Groundwater wicks upward through soil, evaporates into the crawl space, and feeds mold growth on floor joists and the underside of subfloor sheathing long before a homeowner notices anything from inside the house.

Leaking roofs after the severe thunderstorm seasons that roll through central Mississippi are another common trigger. Water that enters the attic and sits in blown-in insulation can sustain active mold growth for months without producing a visible stain on the ceiling below.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Sandersville

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not a visual scan, but a moisture mapping exercise using thermal imaging and pin-type meters to locate hidden saturation behind walls and under flooring. That step matters here because wood-framed construction common in this region can hold moisture in framing members even after surface materials feel dry to the touch.

Once the scope is defined, containment goes up before any disturbance begins. Negative air pressure machines with HEPA filtration keep spores from migrating into unaffected rooms while work is underway. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines. Structural surfaces are then treated and dried to verified moisture content levels consistent with the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation. Our team holds the IICRC AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician) credential, and the firm is an IICRC Certified Firm, so the process follows a defined protocol rather than a judgment call made on the fly.

Drying in Sandersville's climate takes longer than the national average. When outdoor dew points are above 65°F — which is routine here from May through September — simply opening windows does nothing. We run refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space and monitor daily until readings confirm the structure is dry, not just surface-dry.
