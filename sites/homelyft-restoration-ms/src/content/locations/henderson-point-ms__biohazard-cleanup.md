## Why Henderson Point Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

The Gulf Coast climate is not forgiving. Temperatures in Henderson Point regularly push into the upper 80s and 90s from May through September, and that heat accelerates the biological degradation of any organic material left unaddressed — meaning the window between an incident and a containable situation is shorter here than in cooler climates. High ambient humidity also means porous surfaces like subflooring, older wood-frame walls, and unsealed concrete absorb contaminants more readily than they would in a drier region.

Many of the residential structures along the Henderson Point peninsula were built in the mid-twentieth century as fishing camps and beach retreats that later became permanent homes. These properties often feature pier-and-beam construction with open crawl spaces, which can allow contaminants to migrate downward and become trapped in areas that are difficult to access and ventilate. That kind of structural detail changes how a biohazard remediation team has to approach containment and disposal — it is not a one-size approach.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Henderson Point

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. A certified technician evaluates the scope of contamination, identifies all affected surfaces and materials, and establishes a containment perimeter before any cleaning begins. This step protects unaffected areas of the home and limits the movement of potentially infectious material.

Once cleaning and disinfection are complete, the space is deodorized and clearance testing is conducted to confirm the area is safe for re-occupancy. Documentation is provided for insurance and, when applicable, for property management records.
