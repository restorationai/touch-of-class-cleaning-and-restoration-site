## Why Nicholson Properties Need a Dedicated Post-Construction Clean

Nicholson sits in a stretch of Pearl River County where the soil is sandy and loamy — great for drainage, but a constant source of tracked-in silica dust on active job sites. That fine particulate doesn't stay on the floor. It migrates into HVAC returns, settles behind newly hung cabinet doors, and films over recessed lighting fixtures. Standard janitorial crews aren't equipped to address it systematically; they move debris around rather than removing it.

The region's high relative humidity compounds the problem. Grout haze and drywall compound dust that might brush off cleanly in a drier climate can bond stubbornly to tile and trim here when moisture in the air reactivates the alkaline residue. Homes in this part of Mississippi also tend to use fiber-cement siding and pressure-treated wood in exterior builds — both of which leave behind sawdust that, if left on surfaces, can trap moisture and accelerate surface staining. Getting ahead of that residue before a homeowner moves in isn't optional; it's the difference between a clean finish and a callback.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Nicholson

Every post-construction clean follows a structured sequence rather than a room-by-room sweep. The first phase is rough-out: removing all construction debris, tape scraps, packaging materials, and loose particulate before any detail cleaning begins. Vacuuming with HEPA-filtered equipment comes next — this captures the fine silica and drywall dust that a standard shop vac recirculates into the air.

After the rough-out, the detail phase addresses surfaces in order from ceiling to floor. Light fixtures and ceiling fans are wiped of compound dust; window frames and sills are cleaned of adhesive residue from protective film; cabinet interiors are vacuumed and wiped; and hard-surface flooring gets a pH-appropriate cleaning agent matched to the material — different chemistry for luxury vinyl plank versus ceramic tile versus hardwood. The final phase is a polish pass: streak-free glass cleaning, hardware polishing, and a walk-through inspection against a checklist before we call the space done.

For larger commercial buildouts or phased renovations, we can coordinate a rough clean at the end of each trade phase so debris doesn't accumulate across the full project timeline.
