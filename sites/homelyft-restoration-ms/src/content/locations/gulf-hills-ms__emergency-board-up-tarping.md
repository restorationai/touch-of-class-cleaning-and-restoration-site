## Why Gulf Hills Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After Damage

Jackson County's coastal corridor — including Gulf Hills — experiences a climate that is unforgiving to any breach in a building envelope. Summer dewpoints regularly sit above 75°F, meaning that an unprotected opening left overnight can allow enough moisture intrusion to initiate mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. The housing stock in Gulf Hills skews toward mid-century ranch and cottage construction, many of which predate modern hurricane strapping requirements. Roof decks on these homes are often 1×6 skip-sheathing or early-generation OSB — materials that absorb water rapidly once exposed and can delaminate or rot within days if left open to Gulf Coast rainfall.

Beyond moisture, the proximity to the Gulf means post-storm wind events don't simply stop when the named system passes. Outer bands, tropical moisture surges, and fast-moving afternoon thunderstorms can follow a major weather event by 24 to 72 hours. A tarp installed without proper ballasting and edge-sealing in these conditions is a liability, not a solution.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Gulf Hills

When HomeLyft Restoration MS responds to a board-up or tarping call in Gulf Hills, the process starts with a rapid structural assessment before any material goes up. We identify load-bearing points on the roof, check for sagging decking that could compromise anchor placement, and document all damage with photographs — both for your insurance carrier and to establish a baseline for reconstruction.

For roof tarping, we use minimum 6-mil polyethylene tarps on smaller openings, stepping up to reinforced woven poly tarps rated for high-wind conditions on larger or more exposed sections. Tarps are anchored with 2×4 battens screwed into solid decking — not simply draped and weighted — because Gulf Coast wind gusts can strip an improperly secured tarp in minutes. On fire-damaged structures, we prioritize boarding windows and door frames with 5/8" plywood before tarping the roof, since open window frames create interior wind pressure that can lift even a well-anchored tarp.

For broken window board-up after fire or storm damage, plywood panels are cut to fit flush with the exterior frame, secured with structural screws rather than nails, and marked with our contact information and the date of installation — a detail that matters when adjusters and inspectors are cycling through the property over the following days.

Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration) certification and is a FORTIFIED Building Roofing MS Windstorm Certified contractor, which means the methods we use for temporary roof protection align with the same wind-resistance standards used in permanent roofing upgrades — not just a tarp thrown over a hole.
