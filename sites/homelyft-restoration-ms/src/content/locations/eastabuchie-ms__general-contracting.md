## Why Eastabuchie Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Jones County's climate is not gentle on residential structures. Summers are long, hot, and relentlessly humid — average relative humidity regularly exceeds 75% — and that moisture works its way into wood framing, subfloors, and wall cavities over time. Homes built in the 1960s through 1980s, which make up a significant portion of Eastabuchie's housing stock, often used materials that are no longer code-compliant: single-pane windows, aluminum wiring in some cases, and insulation that has long since compressed and lost its R-value.

Clay-rich soil in this part of Mississippi is also a real factor. It expands when wet and contracts in dry spells, which means slabs and pier-and-beam foundations both shift over time. Before any renovation begins, we assess whether existing framing or flooring has been compromised by that movement — because a beautiful new kitchen installed over a soft subfloor is a problem waiting to happen. Our team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which matters on any pre-1978 home in the area where disturbing painted surfaces is part of the scope.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Eastabuchie

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough — not a quick glance, but a methodical review of structural condition, existing utilities, and any signs of prior water intrusion or mold that could affect the renovation scope. In Jones County's climate, it is common to open a wall for a bathroom remodel and find moisture damage that was never addressed after a past plumbing leak. We document everything before work begins so there are no surprises mid-project.

From there, the process moves through permitting (Jones County building permits are required for most structural, electrical, and plumbing work), material sourcing, and phased construction. For post-damage rebuilds, our background in IICRC-certified restoration means we understand how to sequence drying, remediation, and reconstruction so that new materials go in only after the structure is confirmed dry and clean.

Timelines vary by scope: a bathroom remodel typically runs two to four weeks, a kitchen remodel four to eight weeks depending on cabinet lead times, and a full post-damage rebuild is scoped individually after the damage assessment.

## Local Note: Clay Soil and Subfloor Surprises

One thing that comes up repeatedly on Eastabuchie renovation projects is what we find under the flooring. Jones County's clay soil movement means that pier-and-beam homes — and there are plenty of them in this part of the county — often have piers that have shifted or wood sills that have absorbed ground moisture over decades. When a homeowner schedules a flooring replacement and we pull up the old material, it is not unusual to find subfloor sections that need sistering or full replacement before new flooring can go down. We build a contingency review into every flooring and kitchen project in this area specifically because of how common this is.
