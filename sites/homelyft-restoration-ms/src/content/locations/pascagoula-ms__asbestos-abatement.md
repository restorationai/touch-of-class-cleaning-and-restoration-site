## Why Pascagoula Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Pascagoula grew rapidly during and after World War II to support the Ingalls Shipbuilding complex, and that construction boom produced thousands of residential and light-commercial buildings that relied heavily on asbestos for fireproofing, insulation, and acoustical finishes. Popcorn ceilings, floor tiles, pipe wrap, duct insulation, and roofing mastics from that era routinely tested positive for chrysotile or amosite asbestos fibers. The problem does not stay dormant forever. Jackson County's subtropical climate — summer heat that pushes past 95°F combined with humidity that rarely drops below 70% — accelerates the deterioration of older building materials. When asbestos-containing materials become friable, meaning they crumble or release dust easily, the risk of fiber exposure rises significantly. That combination of aging industrial-era construction and relentless coastal humidity makes Pascagoula one of the more asbestos-active markets along the Mississippi Gulf Coast.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Pascagoula

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials — floor tiles, ceiling texture, pipe insulation, joint compound, roofing felt, and any transite siding or soffit panels. Samples are submitted to an accredited laboratory, and we review results before recommending a scope of work. Nothing is assumed; everything is tested.

Once a positive identification is confirmed, the work area is isolated using negative-air containment with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running continuously to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent spaces. Wet methods are used throughout removal to suppress airborne fibers. All regulated waste is double-bagged in labeled 6-mil poly bags, sealed, and transported to a licensed disposal facility in accordance with Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) requirements and EPA National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants (NESHAP) regulations. Final air clearance testing is conducted before containment is removed, giving you documented proof that fiber counts meet clearance criteria — a record that matters if you are selling the property or pulling a renovation permit in Jackson County.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, we work room by room so occupants of multi-unit or larger properties can maintain partial use of the building during the project wherever the containment layout allows.
