## Why DeLisle Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Harrison County's building boom of the 1950s through the 1970s left a legacy of construction materials that were considered state-of-the-art at the time. Asbestos was added to floor tile adhesive, ceiling texture spray, roofing felt, HVAC duct insulation, and even some exterior siding products because it was cheap, fire-resistant, and durable. In DeLisle specifically, the combination of salt-laden Gulf air and high annual humidity causes these materials to degrade faster than in drier inland climates. Brittle floor tiles crack. Popcorn ceiling texture flakes. Pipe wrap crumbles. Each of those failure modes can release fibers into living spaces — which is exactly the condition that makes professional asbestos testing and remediation necessary rather than optional.

The area also saw significant repair and rebuild activity after Hurricane Katrina in 2005. Some of that post-storm work involved disturbing pre-existing asbestos materials without proper abatement, meaning a structure that looks recently renovated may still contain encapsulated or partially disturbed asbestos in wall cavities, attic spaces, or under new flooring layers.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in DeLisle

Every project begins with a formal asbestos inspection — bulk sampling of suspect materials by our IICRC-certified team, followed by laboratory analysis. We do not guess. Until lab results confirm the presence and fiber type, we treat suspect materials as if they are hazardous.

Once asbestos is confirmed, the abatement sequence follows a defined protocol:

1. **Containment** — Negative air pressure enclosures are constructed around the work area using 6-mil poly sheeting and sealed with tape. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms.
2. **Wet removal** — Asbestos-containing materials are wetted with amended water before removal to suppress fiber release. Dry removal is not permitted under Mississippi DEQ regulations.
3. **Waste packaging** — All removed material is double-bagged in labeled, leak-tight containers and transported to a licensed disposal facility. We handle the manifest paperwork.
4. **Air clearance** — Post-abatement air sampling is conducted before containment is removed. Fiber counts must fall below regulatory thresholds before we clear the space for re-occupancy.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal — one of the most common requests we receive from DeLisle homeowners updating older properties — the wet-removal method is especially important because ceiling texture is friable by nature and releases fibers easily when dry-scraped.

## DeLisle Insurance Coordination

Homeowner's insurance coverage for asbestos abatement varies significantly by policy. Some carriers cover abatement when asbestos is disturbed by a covered peril — a roof failure during a storm, for example, that damages asbestos-containing roofing felt. Others treat asbestos as a pre-existing condition excluded from coverage.
