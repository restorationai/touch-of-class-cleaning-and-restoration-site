## Why Rawls Springs Properties Present Specific Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Forrest County's climate creates a compounding problem for post-construction sites. High ambient humidity means fine drywall particulate doesn't simply settle and stay put — it absorbs moisture and forms a thin, chalky film on window glass, cabinet interiors, HVAC registers, and light fixtures. In older homes throughout the Rawls Springs area, many of which were built with pier-and-beam foundations and have crawl spaces open to ground moisture, construction activity stirs up decades of accumulated dust and debris from below the subfloor. That material migrates upward during renovation and ends up coating surfaces inside the living space.

Additionally, Rawls Springs properties that undergo renovation while occupied — or that sit unfinished through a Mississippi summer — often develop early-stage mold on porous surfaces before the post-construction clean even begins. Our IICRC-certified team is trained to identify and flag those conditions rather than clean over them, which protects both the property and the people who will occupy it.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Rawls Springs

Post-construction cleanup is a sequenced process, not a single sweep. Skipping steps — or doing them out of order — means fine particulate gets redistributed rather than removed.

**Phase 1 — Rough Clean:** We remove all visible debris, scrap material, packaging, and construction waste. This phase happens before any detail cleaning so we're not dragging abrasive material across finished surfaces.

**Phase 2 — Detail Clean:** HEPA-filtered vacuums run across every surface — walls, ceilings, window tracks, cabinet boxes, and HVAC returns — before any wet cleaning begins. In Rawls Springs's humid conditions, wet-cleaning drywall dust before it's fully vacuumed creates a paste that's significantly harder to remove from trim and flooring.

**Phase 3 — Final Clean:** Window glass, mirrors, fixtures, and hard-surface floors get a final pass with appropriate cleaning agents. Grout lines, caulk joints, and threshold transitions — the spots that always get missed — are cleaned by hand.

**Phase 4 — Air Quality Pass:** Construction activity loads the air with fine particulate. We run air scrubbers equipped with HEPA filtration to pull suspended dust down before the space is handed off. This step matters especially in Rawls Springs homes with older ductwork that can re-circulate particulate after the clean is complete.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

The difference between a post-construction clean and a standard deep clean is largely equipment. We use commercial-grade HEPA vacuums rated to capture particles down to 0.3 microns — the size range where drywall dust and silica particles live. For hard-surface floors, we use low-moisture cleaning methods that won't raise the grain on new hardwood or compromise fresh grout before it's fully cured.

For specialty cleaning needs — paint overspray on glass, construction adhesive on tile, sticker residue on fixtures — we carry a range of solvent and non-solvent cleaning agents selected based on the substrate. We do not use one-size-fits-all chemical approaches on finished surfaces, particularly in renovated homes where original millwork or period hardware may be present.

Our EPA Lead-Safe certification is relevant on any Rawls Springs property built before 1978. Renovation activity in those homes disturbs lead-containing paint, and our cleaning protocols follow EPA RRP guidelines to contain and properly dispose of that material rather than spread it.
