## Why D'Iberville Properties See Asbestos Risks

Harrison County's building boom of the 1960s and 1970s left a legacy of asbestos-containing materials throughout the D'Iberville area. The city's position between Interstate 10 and the Back Bay of Biloxi meant it absorbed significant commercial and light-industrial development during that era — warehouses, strip retail, and modest residential subdivisions built fast and built with the materials of the time. Popcorn ceilings, 9×9 vinyl floor tiles, duct insulation, and transite pipe were all common. Post-Katrina rebuilding added another layer of complexity: some repairs made in 2005–2008 used salvaged or improperly sourced materials, and the remediation work itself sometimes disturbed previously undisturbed asbestos without proper containment.

The Gulf Coast climate compounds the issue. High humidity and seasonal flooding cause the adhesives and binders in older floor tiles and ceiling textures to degrade, releasing fibers that would otherwise remain stable. A slow roof leak or a single flood event can turn a dormant asbestos hazard into an active one almost overnight.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in D'Iberville

Every project starts with a thorough inspection — visual assessment followed by bulk material sampling sent to an accredited laboratory. We do not guess. If lab results confirm asbestos-containing material, we build a site-specific abatement plan that accounts for the scope of work, the condition of the material (friable vs. non-friable), and the occupancy status of the property.

Containment comes next. We seal the work area with poly sheeting and establish negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers so that disturbed fibers cannot migrate into adjacent rooms or HVAC systems. Removal follows strict Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) protocols and EPA NESHAP standards — materials are wetted to suppress fiber release, bagged in labeled, double-sealed containers, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. Final air clearance sampling is conducted before containment is removed. Nothing is signed off on a visual pass alone.
