When something difficult happens, the last thing a family or property owner should have to manage alone is the cleanup.

## Why Ocean Springs Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

The housing stock along the coast here skews older. Many homes in Ocean Springs were built in the mid-twentieth century or earlier, and they share a common trait: porous materials — hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath walls, uncoated concrete block — that absorb biological contamination more readily than modern construction. Unlike newer vinyl plank or sealed concrete, these surfaces require more careful assessment to determine what can be cleaned and what must be removed entirely.

The Gulf Coast climate compounds this. Sustained heat and humidity accelerate the breakdown of biological material and can allow secondary contamination to set in faster than in drier inland climates. A situation that might allow a 24-hour window in, say, central Mississippi may require faster action here simply because of what the air does to organic matter in an unventilated space.

Local regulatory requirements also shape the work. Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) guidelines govern the packaging, transport, and disposal of infectious waste. Every job we handle follows those protocols, and waste is transported to a licensed medical waste disposal facility — not a standard landfill.
