## Why Glendale Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Mississippi's climate is not gentle to buildings. Summers push heat indexes well past 100°F, and the humidity that hangs over the region for months at a time works its way into framing, subfloors, and wall cavities in ways that only become visible when you open up a wall during a remodel. Older homes in Glendale — many built in the mid-20th century — frequently have original pier-and-beam foundations that have shifted over decades of wet-dry soil cycling. That movement shows up as out-of-plumb door frames, cracked tile, and gaps in trim that look cosmetic but often signal something structural underneath.

Because HomeLyft holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, pre-renovation testing and containment for lead paint is handled in-house on older homes — a step that's legally required in Mississippi for disturbance of painted surfaces in pre-1978 structures and one that out-of-state or uncertified contractors sometimes skip, leaving homeowners exposed to liability.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Glendale

Every project starts with a scope walkthrough — not a sales visit. We document existing conditions, identify any moisture or structural concerns before demolition begins, and produce a written scope that ties labor, materials, and timeline to specific line items. That documentation matters especially when the remodel follows a water or fire loss, because it supports the insurance claim and prevents disputes over what was pre-existing versus storm- or water-related.

From there, the sequence follows a logic that protects the investment: structural and mechanical rough-in before drywall, moisture testing before flooring goes down, and a final walkthrough against the original scope before we close out. For kitchen and bathroom remodels — the two rooms where plumbing, electrical, and finish work intersect — we coordinate subcontractors on a schedule that avoids the costly delays that come from trades arriving out of order.

For post-damage rebuilds specifically, our IICRC certifications in water damage and structural drying mean the rebuild phase starts on a verified dry substrate, not an assumed one. That distinction matters in Mississippi's climate, where residual moisture in framing can feed mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours if the structure isn't properly cleared before reconstruction begins.
