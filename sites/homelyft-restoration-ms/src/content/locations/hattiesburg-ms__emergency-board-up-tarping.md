## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hattiesburg

When a call comes in from the Hattiesburg area, the first thing the crew does is a rapid exterior assessment — roof pitch, breach size, access points, and any active hazards like downed utility lines or unstable chimneys. That assessment drives the material choice: heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for wind uplift on roofs, and OSB or plywood panels cut to fit window and door openings for fire-damaged or vandalized structures.

For roof tarping, the crew installs 2x4 anchor boards along the ridge and eaves so the tarp is secured to wood rather than relying on nails through the membrane alone — a method that holds significantly better under the kind of wind-driven rain Hattiesburg sees in a typical storm season. For board-up work after a fire or break-in, panels are cut, fitted, and screwed into the structural framing rather than just the trim, which matters for insurance documentation and for keeping the opening secure until permanent repairs begin.

Photographic documentation happens at every stage. Insurers want to see the pre-board condition, the materials used, and the finished installation. That documentation is organized and ready to hand off to your adjuster.
