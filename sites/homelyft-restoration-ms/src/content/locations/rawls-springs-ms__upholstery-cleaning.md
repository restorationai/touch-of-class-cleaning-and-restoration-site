## Why Rawls Springs Homes See Upholstery Wear Fast

Rawls Springs sits in that stretch of south Mississippi where humidity rarely drops below uncomfortable, even in the cooler months. Leather furniture fares differently: the humidity keeps hides supple longer, but without regular conditioning it cracks faster once air conditioning cycles dry the room out during a Mississippi summer.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process for Local Homes

We start by identifying the fiber and dye type, since a cotton-poly blend sofa and an aniline leather chair need entirely different chemistry.
