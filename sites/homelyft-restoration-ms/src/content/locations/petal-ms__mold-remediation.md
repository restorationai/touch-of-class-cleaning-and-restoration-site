## Why Petal Properties See Mold Issues

Petal's climate is the primary driver. The city averages more than 55 inches of rainfall annually, and the heavy clay soils common throughout Forrest County drain slowly, keeping foundation perimeters damp for days after a significant rain event. Homes built on pier-and-beam foundations — a construction style common in older Petal neighborhoods — are especially vulnerable because the crawlspace beneath the floor system can trap humid air with no cross-ventilation to dry it out. Once relative humidity inside a crawlspace climbs above 60 percent consistently, wood framing and insulation become a food source for Cladosporium, Penicillium, and the Stachybotrys strains most people call black mold.

Air conditioning systems also play a role that surprises many homeowners. In a Mississippi summer, the temperature differential between a cool interior and a hot attic or wall cavity creates condensation on any surface where vapor can migrate. Ductwork with small gaps or disconnected joints pulls humid attic air directly into living spaces, distributing spores before anyone notices a musty smell.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Petal

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We identify not just where mold is visible but where elevated moisture readings suggest hidden growth behind drywall or beneath flooring. This step matters more in Petal than in drier climates because the source of moisture is often ongoing — a slow plumbing leak or chronic condensation — and remediation without fixing the source is temporary at best.

Once the scope is clear, the affected area is sealed off with negative air pressure containment using 6-mil poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to clean rooms during removal. Contaminated porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood — are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent, and dried to industry-standard moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We follow the IICRC S520 Standard for Professional Mold Remediation throughout, which gives your insurance adjuster a defensible, documented protocol.
