## Why Pascagoula Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

The Gulf South climate is not forgiving. Pascagoula's average relative humidity hovers well above 70% for most of the year, and summer heat accelerates the breakdown of biological material in ways that compress the window for safe, effective remediation. What might take days to become a serious contamination risk in a drier climate can become a health concern within hours here.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many Pascagoula homes were built during the post-WWII shipbuilding boom — solid construction, but with materials like original pine subfloors, plaster-and-lath interior walls, and crawl space foundations that were never designed with contamination containment in mind. Biological material migrates into these porous surfaces faster than it does into modern concrete board or engineered flooring. That means surface-level cleaning is rarely sufficient; affected materials often need to be removed and the substrate treated before the space is genuinely safe.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Pascagoula

Every situation is different, and we approach each one without assumptions. When we arrive, the first priority is assessing the full scope of contamination — not just what's visible, but what may have migrated into adjacent materials. We use ATP testing and other field-validated methods to identify affected surfaces that aren't apparent to the eye.

Containment is established before any removal begins. This protects unaffected areas of the property and ensures that cross-contamination doesn't extend the remediation footprint. All surfaces are then treated with hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants, and the space is cleared before we close out the job.

Our team holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, and we carry the documentation that insurance carriers and property managers require. We handle the paperwork trail so that the remediation record is complete and defensible.
