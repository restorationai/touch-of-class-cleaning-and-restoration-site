## Why Ellisville Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Jones County's climate is the first variable that changes how post-construction cleaning has to be done here. Summer humidity regularly climbs past 80%, which means fine construction particulate — silica dust from concrete cutting, joint compound powder, fiberglass insulation fibers — stays airborne longer and settles into HVAC systems, window tracks, and subflooring gaps more aggressively than it would in a drier climate. If that dust isn't captured before the HVAC is turned on, it recirculates through the ductwork for weeks.

Ellisville also has a meaningful share of older residential housing stock — ranch-style homes and pier-and-beam construction that were built during the area's mid-century growth period. Renovations on these properties often disturb decades of accumulated debris in crawl spaces and wall cavities. Commercial construction in the corridor along US-11 and near the Jones County Airport area also tends to involve phased occupancy — meaning one wing or floor may be occupied while another is still under construction. ## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Ellisville

Post-construction cleaning isn't one pass with a mop — it's a sequenced process that moves from the roughest debris down to the finest particulate.

**Rough clean:** We remove all construction debris — lumber scraps, drywall cutoffs, packaging, adhesive containers — and sweep or vacuum all horizontal surfaces before any wet cleaning begins. In Ellisville's humidity, wet-mopping over construction dust creates a paste that's far harder to remove than dry debris.

**Detail clean:** This is where most cleaning crews stop too early. We clean inside cabinet boxes, wipe down window frames and sills, remove paint overspray and caulk smears from glass, clean light fixture housings, and vacuum HVAC registers before the system runs. Sticker residue from new appliances and fixtures gets removed with appropriate solvents that won't damage finishes.

**Final polish:** Hard floors are cleaned and inspected under raking light to catch dried grout haze, adhesive spots, and scratches that need to be documented before the client accepts the space. We photograph the completed work so there's a clear record of condition at turnover.

For larger commercial projects, we can coordinate a multi-day schedule and provide a written scope of work that satisfies general contractor punch-list requirements.
