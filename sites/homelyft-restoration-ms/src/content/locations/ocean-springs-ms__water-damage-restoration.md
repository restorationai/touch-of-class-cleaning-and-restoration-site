## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Ocean Springs

The first step on every job is a thorough moisture assessment — not a visual walkthrough, but calibrated readings with thermal imaging cameras and penetrating moisture meters that map exactly where water has traveled. In Ocean Springs homes with pier-and-beam construction, water commonly migrates 8 to 12 feet from the visible source before it shows up on a surface, so we scan the full affected zone before any equipment is staged.

Once we understand the scope, water extraction begins using truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of pulling standing water from hardwood floors, carpeting, and crawl space soil. Structural drying follows immediately — high-capacity LGR dehumidifiers and axial air movers are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard, with placement adjusted for the specific wall and floor assemblies in your home. Drying logs are recorded daily so you, your adjuster, and your contractor all have a documented moisture history from start to finish.

For homes with plaster walls or original hardwood floors, we use low-grain refrigerant dehumidification rather than aggressive heat drying, which can cause hardwood to cup or crack. Drying timelines in these structures typically run three to five days rather than the two-day average for modern drywall construction — we communicate that upfront so there are no surprises.
