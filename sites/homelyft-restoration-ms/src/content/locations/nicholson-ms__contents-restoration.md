## Why Nicholson Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Pearl River County's climate sits in a humid subtropical zone that accelerates secondary damage to contents after any loss event. After a pipe bursts or a roof leak lets rainwater in, mold can begin colonizing porous materials — upholstery, cardboard boxes, wood furniture joints — within 24 to 48 hours. That timeline is not theoretical here; the combination of warm indoor temperatures and ambient outdoor humidity means wet belongings left in place while structural repairs are scheduled are often unrecoverable by the time a crew arrives.

Homes throughout the Nicholson area also tend to reflect the rural residential building patterns common across Pearl River County — wood-framed construction, pier-and-beam foundations in older properties, and crawl spaces that trap humidity after flood events. Contents stored in lower-level rooms or in contact with flooring are especially vulnerable after any water intrusion. Fire losses compound the problem: smoke and soot from a structure fire travel through HVAC systems and settle on contents in rooms that never saw flames, meaning belongings two or three rooms away from the origin point often need professional cleaning.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Nicholson

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued by room, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was packed out and when.

From there, contents move to a controlled storage facility where temperature and humidity are managed, protecting items from the same environmental conditions that caused or worsened the damage in the first place. Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard surfaces and small objects, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing soft goods, dry-cleaning processes for textiles, and hand-cleaning for antiques or fragile items where machine contact would cause more harm than good. Electronics are evaluated separately — circuit boards and internal components need assessment before power is restored.

Once structural repairs at your Nicholson property are complete, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in their original rooms based on the inventory photos. Nothing gets lost in the shuffle, and you're not left guessing what came back.
