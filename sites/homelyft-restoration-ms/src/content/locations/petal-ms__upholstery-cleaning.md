## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Petal

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, cotton blends, synthetic microfiber, and leather all respond differently to heat and moisture, and using the wrong method can shrink fabric or dry out hide. For most fabric pieces we use hot water extraction, sometimes called steam cleaning, which forces heated solution into the weave and pulls it back out along with embedded soil, dust mite waste, and pollen residue. Because of the ambient humidity typical here, we pay close attention to drying time and often bring air movers to speed evaporation so cushions aren't sitting damp for hours afterward, which is exactly the kind of extended wet-dwell time that lets odor and mildew set back in before the piece ever fully dries.

## Leather vs Fabric: Matching the Method

Leather upholstery needs a different approach entirely. We clean with pH-balanced leather cleaners and condition afterward to replace oils that Mississippi's heat and UV exposure pull out over time, especially on furniture near south- or west-facing windows. Fabric pieces get tested for colorfastness before any solution touches the whole cushion, and we adjust dwell time and water temperature based on whether we're working with a tight weave like canvas or a looser blend that absorbs faster. For households with pets or young kids, we also offer a protective treatment after cleaning that helps the next spill bead up instead of soaking straight into the fibers.
