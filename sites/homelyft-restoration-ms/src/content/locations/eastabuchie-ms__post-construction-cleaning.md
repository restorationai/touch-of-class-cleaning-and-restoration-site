## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Eastabuchie

Post-construction cleaning isn't a single pass — it's a sequenced, three-phase process calibrated to what the trades left behind.

**Phase 1 — Rough Clean:** Bulk debris removal, disposal of scrap materials, and HEPA vacuuming of all framed cavities and subfloor surfaces before finish materials go in. If the general contractor calls us in at this stage, we can prevent construction dust from being sealed behind baseboards and under flooring.

**Phase 2 — Detail Clean:** This is where most of the labor lives. Every window track, cabinet interior, light fixture housing, and HVAC grille gets addressed. We use low-residue cleaning agents on new countertops and flooring to avoid voiding manufacturer warranties — a detail that matters when a Jones County homeowner has just invested in hardwood or luxury vinyl that carries a finish warranty.

**Phase 3 — Final Polish:** Touch-up wipe-downs, glass polishing, and a room-by-room walk with the project owner or general contractor. We document the condition of surfaces before and after so there's no ambiguity about what the cleaning crew did versus what arrived scratched from the job site.
