## Why Rawls Springs Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The housing stock in and around Rawls Springs skews older, and older construction in this part of Mississippi comes with a specific set of variables. Pier-and-beam foundations are common, and the clay-heavy soils in Forrest County expand and contract with seasonal moisture swings — which means a bathroom remodel that seems straightforward can reveal subfloor deflection or joist damage that needs to be corrected before a single tile goes down. Vapor barriers in crawl spaces are frequently absent or deteriorated, and that persistent ground moisture accelerates wood rot in ways that aren't obvious until demo begins.

Because HomeLyft holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, pre-1978 homes — and there are plenty of them in this part of the county — can be renovated without the liability exposure that comes from hiring a contractor who isn't trained to handle lead-based paint disturbance. That's not a minor detail when you're pulling out old trim, windows, or kitchen cabinetry in a house built before the Carter administration.
