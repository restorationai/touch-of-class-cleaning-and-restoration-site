George County's climate is no secret to anyone who has spent a summer here. ## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Lucedale

Every post-construction clean begins with a structured walk-through to document what trades left behind: overspray on windows, grout haze on tile, silicone smears on fixtures, and the inevitable scatter of fasteners and scrap material. From there, the work moves in a deliberate top-down sequence.

**Rough clean first.** Bulk debris — scrap lumber, packaging, drywall cutoffs — comes out before any surface cleaning begins. Skipping this step and going straight to detail cleaning is one of the most common ways a post-construction clean gets done twice.

**HEPA vacuuming of all surfaces.** Walls, ceilings, window sills, cabinet interiors, and light fixtures are vacuumed with HEPA-filtered equipment before any wet cleaning starts. This prevents construction dust from turning into muddy streaks when a damp cloth hits it.

**Surface-by-surface detail cleaning.** Windows get scraped and polished. Tile receives grout haze treatment appropriate to the tile type — a process that differs between ceramic, porcelain, and natural stone. Cabinetry is wiped inside and out, including hinges and drawer slides where dust packs in and causes premature wear. Fixtures are cleaned and polished without abrasives that would scratch new finishes.

**Final air quality pass.** In Lucedale's humid conditions, airborne particulate can stay suspended longer than in drier climates. Air scrubbers run through the final stage to pull residual dust from the air column before the space is handed back.
