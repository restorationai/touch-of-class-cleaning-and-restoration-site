## Why Long Beach Properties See Water Damage and Flooding Issues

Harrison County's sandy, low-lying soil holds a high water table most of the year, which means even a moderate rain event or a failed sump pump can leave crawlspaces and slab perimeters saturated for days. Many homes in Long Beach were rebuilt or elevated after Hurricane Katrina, so you'll find a mix of raised pier-and-beam construction alongside newer slab-on-grade builds, each of which traps and releases water differently. Humidity along the Gulf stays high for most of the year, which slows natural evaporation and gives mold a head start if standing water isn't pulled fast. ## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Long Beach

We start by locating the water source and shutting it off if it's still active, then move straight into extraction with truck-mount and portable pumps rated for standing water, not just damp carpet. Once the bulk water is out, we check subfloor and wall cavities with moisture meters because Gulf Coast humidity means surfaces can read dry to the touch while the material underneath is still saturated. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed based on room size, humidity load, and how the structure is built, raised homes dry differently than slab homes, and we calibrate equipment accordingly. We document every step with photos and moisture readings as we go, which matters later when you're filing a claim.
