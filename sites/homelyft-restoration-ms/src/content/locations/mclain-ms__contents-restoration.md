## Why McLain Properties See Elevated Contents Damage

Greene County's combination of high annual humidity, sandy loam soils, and an older rural housing stock creates conditions that accelerate secondary damage to personal property. Many homes in the McLain area were built in the mid-20th century, when construction relied heavily on solid wood furniture, wool textiles, and paper-backed insulation — materials that absorb smoke odor and moisture far more aggressively than modern composites. When a pipe fails or a kitchen fire sends soot through an HVAC system, those older materials act like sponges. Smoke particles bond to natural fibers within minutes; mold can begin colonizing damp fabric and cardboard in as little as 24 to 48 hours in south Mississippi's heat. Waiting even a day to address saturated or smoke-affected contents is rarely a neutral decision here.

Greene County also sees a meaningful number of homes with pier-and-beam foundations, which means floodwater and humidity can migrate upward into living spaces and storage areas that sit close to grade. Documents, photographs, and stored clothing in lower rooms or under-house storage are especially vulnerable after heavy rainfall events.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in McLain

The process begins with a detailed room-by-room inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing is misplaced during transport.

From McLain, contents travel to a secure, climate-controlled storage facility where temperature and relative humidity are actively managed. That environmental control is not optional in south Mississippi; without it, items that survived the initial event can develop mold or warp during the weeks a home is being repaired.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, ceramics, and metal items — high-frequency sound waves remove soot and residue from crevices that hand-cleaning misses.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods and upholstered furniture carrying smoke odor.
- **Freeze-drying referral** for documents and photographs that are wet but structurally intact.
- **Hand cleaning and dry-sponge techniques** for delicate antiques, artwork, and items with fragile finishes.

Once your home passes clearance, items are returned in the same organized sequence they were packed — room by room, with the original inventory as a checklist.
