## Why Laurel Homes Face Distinct Contents Challenges

Jones County's housing stock skews older, and Laurel proper has a significant number of homes built between the 1930s and 1970s — many of them craftsman bungalows and wood-frame construction with original hardwood floors, plaster walls, and furniture that has been in families for generations. That's not a liability; it's a reason to be precise. Antique sideboards, upholstered settees, and hand-stitched quilts don't respond to aggressive industrial cleaning the way a mass-produced pressboard shelf does. When fire or water damage reaches these items, the cleaning chemistry, drying temperatures, and handling protocols all have to be dialed back and carefully monitored.

Laurel also sits in a region where summer relative humidity regularly exceeds 80 percent. Any contents that are packed out after a loss and moved into an uncontrolled environment — a garage, a storage unit without climate control — can sustain secondary damage that rivals the original event. That's why the storage component of this service matters as much as the cleaning itself.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves the property. Every piece — from the dining room table down to the framed photos on the hallway wall — is photographed, tagged, and logged. That documentation serves two purposes: it protects your insurance claim, and it ensures every item is returned to its original location when the home is ready.

From there, items are sorted by material type and damage category. Smoke-affected textiles go through ultrasonic cleaning or ozone treatment depending on fiber sensitivity. Hard surfaces — wood furniture, ceramics, metal fixtures — are cleaned with pH-balanced solutions calibrated to the finish. Electronics are assessed for soot infiltration before any power is applied. Belongings that are structurally sound but odor-affected go through deodorization chambers rather than being written off.

All restored contents are stored in a climate-controlled facility while the home undergoes structural repairs. Temperature and humidity are actively managed — not just passively monitored — so that a leather sofa cleaned on day one doesn't re-absorb ambient moisture by week three.

## Coordinating with Insurance in Laurel

Most homeowner policies in Mississippi cover contents as part of a broader property loss claim, but the documentation requirements vary by carrier. Adjusters working Jones County losses will typically want a line-item contents inventory with pre-loss value estimates, photographs of damage before cleaning, and receipts or records for high-value items. The inventory process built into the pack-out stage is designed to produce exactly that output — a claim-ready document rather than a handwritten list.

If your carrier requires a separate contents rider or has specific depreciation schedules for furniture and electronics, that information is worth pulling from your policy before the pack-out begins. The team can work alongside your adjuster during the initial walk-through if scheduling allows, which tends to shorten the supplement cycle on larger losses.
