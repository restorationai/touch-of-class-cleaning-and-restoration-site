## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Saucier

Sewage is classified as Category 3 — "black water" — under the IICRC S500 standard, meaning every affected material is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise. The process here starts with containment: we establish negative air pressure in the affected area using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to clean areas of the home while extraction is underway.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable wet-vac units pull standing sewage and saturated material from hard floors, subfloor assemblies, and crawl spaces. In Saucier's crawl-space homes — common on the older rural parcels throughout Harrison County — technicians work in full PPE to remove contaminated insulation, treat the vapor barrier, and apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to joists and pier blocks. Any porous material that absorbed Category 3 water — drywall, insulation, carpet, pad — is removed and disposed of per Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines rather than dried in place.

After extraction and demo, the space is treated with hospital-grade disinfectants, dried to IICRC moisture targets using desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, and cleared with moisture readings documented for your insurance adjuster. We photograph every phase so there's no ambiguity about what was affected and what was remediated.
