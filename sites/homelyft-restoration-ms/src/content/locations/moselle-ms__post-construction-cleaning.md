## Why Moselle Construction Projects Need a Thorough Final Clean

Jones County's climate sits in a sweet spot that builders love and cleaners respect: warm enough to pour concrete and hang drywall in February, humid enough that fine construction dust absorbs ambient moisture within days of settling. That dust — a blend of drywall compound, joint compound, wood particles, and insulation fibers — doesn't just sit on surfaces. It migrates into HVAC returns, coats window tracks, and films over newly installed cabinetry. Left in place, it can cause HVAC filters to clog within weeks of a system's first run, and it creates a haze on hard flooring that looks like a scratch but is actually embedded particulate.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Moselle

Post-construction cleaning isn't one pass — it's a sequenced process that matches the condition of the space after trades have cleared out.

**Rough clean first.** Large debris — cut lumber ends, packaging, drywall scraps, tape rolls — gets removed and sorted before any detail work begins. Trying to detail-clean a space that still has construction debris on the floor wastes time and grinds particulate deeper into surfaces.

**Dust before you wipe.** HEPA-filtered vacuums pull fine drywall dust off surfaces before damp wiping begins. Wiping first smears the dust into a paste that's harder to remove and can streak finished surfaces. Every horizontal surface, light fixture, window sill, and cabinet interior gets vacuumed before a microfiber cloth touches it.

**Surface-specific cleaning.** New LVP flooring gets different treatment than freshly stained hardwood. Tile grout lines get a separate scrub pass. Stainless appliances get wiped with the grain. Glass gets a two-step clean to remove the haze that construction adhesive off-gassing leaves on interior windows.

**Final inspection pass.** Before the crew leaves, a walkthrough with the property owner or general contractor confirms that outlets, switch plates, door hardware, and any specialty surfaces — concrete countertops, custom tile, exposed brick — meet the agreed standard.
