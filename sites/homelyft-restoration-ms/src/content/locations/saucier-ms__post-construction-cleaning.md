## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Saucier

A final clean after construction isn't one pass — it's a sequenced process that works from the ceiling down and from the inside out.

**Rough clean phase:** Bulk debris removal, scraping adhesive labels off windows and fixtures, sweeping subfloors, and clearing construction waste from every room and exterior threshold. In Saucier builds on wooded lots, this phase often includes removing tracked-in pine straw and clay from garage slabs and covered porches before it can be ground into finished surfaces.

**Detail clean phase:** HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all surfaces — window sills, door frames, cabinet interiors, light fixture housings, and exposed ductwork openings. Grout lines on tile floors get scrubbed before grout haze sets permanently. Hardware and plumbing fixtures are wiped down to remove installer fingerprints and protective film residue.

**Final inspection clean:** A white-glove pass on glass, mirrors, and stainless surfaces. HVAC filter replacement or cleaning (if within scope), and a walkthrough against the contractor's punch list to confirm every surface is ready for the property owner or tenant.
