## Why DeLisle Properties Face Compounded Fire Damage Challenges

Harrison County's coastal humidity doesn't pause for a fire loss. Within 24 to 48 hours of a structure fire, suppression water trapped inside wall cavities begins feeding mold colonies — and DeLisle's warm baseline temperatures accelerate that timeline significantly compared to inland markets. Pier-and-beam construction common in this part of the county means smoke and water can migrate beneath the subfloor, reaching areas that look undamaged from above but are actively deteriorating.

Older homes in the DeLisle area also frequently contain materials that trigger EPA Lead-Safe protocols during demolition and debris removal. Our team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, so when charred drywall or original flooring has to come out, we follow the containment and disposal procedures required by federal regulation — not as an upsell, but because the law and your family's safety require it.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in DeLisle

Every job starts with a structured assessment before a single piece of debris moves. We document the loss with photographs and moisture readings, then map the smoke migration path — because in a ranch-style home with open attic space, soot travels far beyond the room of origin. That documentation also becomes the foundation of your insurance claim file.

From there, the process moves through board-up and tarping to secure the structure, controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, HEPA-filtered air scrubbing to capture fine smoke particulate, and thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize odor molecules embedded in framing and insulation. Our IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) certification shapes every step of that sequence — it's the industry standard for deciding what gets cleaned versus what gets replaced, which directly affects both your outcome and your claim value.

Structural drying runs concurrently with smoke remediation on any job where suppression water is involved. We use calibrated desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the structure, not a one-size fleet, because over-drying can warp original hardwood floors that are otherwise salvageable.
