## Why Diamondhead Properties Are Especially Vulnerable

The Gulf Coast's climate doesn't give crawl spaces much of a break. Diamondhead averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and the region's sandy-clay soil mix means water doesn't drain quickly away from foundations — it sits, evaporates slowly upward, and saturates the air trapped under your floor joists. Vapor barriers alone — the thin plastic sheeting many older homes have — degrade in this environment within a few years, leaving exposed wood and insulation to absorb moisture continuously.

Homes built in Diamondhead during the community's development boom in the 1970s and 1980s often have vented crawl spaces that were standard practice at the time but are now understood to make moisture problems worse in humid Southern climates. Outside air vented in during summer brings in far more moisture than it removes. Encapsulation — sealing the crawl space entirely with a heavy-duty barrier and conditioning the air inside — is the approach that actually works here.

## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Diamondhead

Before any barrier goes down, we assess the full crawl space: standing water or active drainage issues, existing vapor barrier condition, wood moisture readings on joists and subfloor, and whether any mold growth has already taken hold. Our team holds IICRC AMRT (Mold) certification, so if we find active mold during the inspection, we can remediate it before encapsulation seals it in — a step that's easy to skip and costly to fix later.

The encapsulation itself involves:

- **Clearing and prepping** the crawl space floor and walls, removing old deteriorated sheeting
- **Installing a reinforced polyethylene barrier** (typically 20-mil) across the floor and up the foundation walls, sealed at all seams and penetrations
- **Closing foundation vents** and sealing the rim joist area to prevent humid outside air from bypassing the barrier
- **Installing a crawl space dehumidifier** sized to the cubic footage of your specific space, with a drain line routed to daylight or a condensate pump
- **Final moisture readings** to confirm the space is performing within target range before we close out the job

In Diamondhead's climate, skipping the dehumidifier step is a common shortcut that leads to callbacks. A sealed but unconditioned crawl space can still accumulate moisture from concrete off-gassing and minor ground seepage. We don't leave that to chance.
