## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Hattiesburg

The process starts with a structured pack-out. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code before it leaves the property — a step that matters both for your insurance claim and for making sure nothing is misplaced during transport. We use padded moving containers and climate-controlled vehicles, because moving smoke- or water-damaged belongings in the back of an uncontrolled trailer on a 90-degree Hattiesburg afternoon can accelerate deterioration rather than stop it.

Once items reach our secure storage facility, cleaning begins based on material type. Hard surfaces — furniture frames, cabinetry hardware, decorative items — go through ultrasonic cleaning or hand-cleaning with appropriate solvents. Throughout the process, items are stored in a controlled environment until your home is ready to receive them, at which point we coordinate delivery and placement room by room.
