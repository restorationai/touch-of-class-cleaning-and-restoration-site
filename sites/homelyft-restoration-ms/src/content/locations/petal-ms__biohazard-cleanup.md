## Why Petal Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Petal's climate is not forgiving. Humidity regularly climbs above 80% through the warmer months, and that moisture doesn't just sit in the air — it works into subfloor assemblies, wall cavities, and the porous surfaces common in the area's older ranch-style and brick-veneer homes. When biological material contacts an already-humid substrate, microbial activity accelerates quickly. What might be a contained surface issue in a drier climate can penetrate into flooring underlayment or drywall paper within hours here.

Many homes in and around Petal were built in the 1960s through 1980s, a period when subfloor materials, adhesives, and insulation choices were very different from current standards. These materials are more porous and harder to decontaminate than modern equivalents, which means a thorough biohazard remediation job here often requires more careful assessment of what can be cleaned in place versus what needs to be removed and properly disposed of under Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines for regulated medical and biohazardous waste.
