## Why D'Iberville Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The Gulf Coast's rainfall patterns are not gentle. D'Iberville regularly absorbs heavy tropical moisture from named storms and unnamed squalls that push through Harrison County, and that volume of water overwhelms both municipal sewer mains and private septic systems. When ground saturation is high, septic drain fields stop accepting effluent — the liquid has nowhere to go but back up through the lowest fixture in the house.

Older residential construction in the D'Iberville area also contributes. Cast-iron and Orangeburg sewer laterals installed in the mid-twentieth century have long passed their service life. These pipes corrode from the inside, collapse under root intrusion, and develop bellies where solids accumulate — all conditions that set up a backup. Newer slab-on-grade construction common in D'Iberville's post-Katrina rebuilds presents a different challenge: when sewage seeps under a concrete slab, extraction requires careful moisture mapping before any drying begins, because the contamination can wick into the slab edge and wall framing before it's visible at the surface.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in D'Iberville

Every sewage job starts with containment, not extraction. Before anything is removed, the affected area is isolated to keep cross-contamination from spreading to unaffected rooms — a step that matters especially in D'Iberville's smaller ranch-style and split-entry homes where the laundry room or hallway bath sits close to living areas.

Once contained, we extract standing sewage using truck-mounted and portable units capable of handling solid-laden water. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — that have contacted raw sewage are removed. There is no safe way to sanitize porous material that has absorbed Category 3 water; restoration industry standards are clear on this point. Structural cavities are then treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, and the space is dried to pre-loss moisture levels using industrial dehumidification and air movement equipment. Our team holds IICRC AMRT and WRT credentials, and all work is documented with moisture readings and photographs at each stage — documentation your insurance adjuster will expect.
