## Why Diamondhead Upholstery Ages Differently

Diamondhead was built out as a planned golf course and canal community, and a lot of that original housing stock is decades old now, with sunrooms, lanais, and open floor plans that connect living rooms directly to humid outdoor air for much of the year. Fabric upholstery in those rooms absorbs ambient moisture even when nothing has spilled, which lets mildew and must set in quietly. Salt-tinged air off the Mississippi Sound also affects leather differently than it would inland, drying out the finish and making it prone to cracking if it isn't conditioned after cleaning. We see more of this slow humidity damage in Diamondhead than the kind of single-event staining that dominates drier climates.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Diamondhead

We start with a fiber and finish check, fabric, leather, or a blend, since the wrong method on the wrong material can set a stain permanently or damage a leather finish outright. For fabric sofas and chairs, we pre-treat soiled areas, then use hot water extraction calibrated to pull out embedded moisture along with the dirt, which matters more here than in drier regions because Gulf humidity slows natural evaporation. Leather pieces get cleaned with pH-balanced solutions and a conditioning step to replace oils that coastal air strips away over time. We finish with controlled airflow drying rather than letting the piece sit in a humid room to dry on its own, which is where a lot of at-home cleaning attempts go wrong on the Coast.
