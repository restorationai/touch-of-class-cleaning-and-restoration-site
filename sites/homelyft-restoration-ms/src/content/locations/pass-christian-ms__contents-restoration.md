## Why Pass Christian Properties See Elevated Contents Damage Risks

Pass Christian's position along the Gulf Coast makes it one of the most weather-exposed communities on the Mississippi shoreline. Homes here regularly face tropical moisture intrusion, hurricane-force wind events, and the slow creep of humidity that never fully leaves between storms. That persistent humidity — often sitting above 70% relative humidity even on calm days — means that smoke residue from a kitchen fire doesn't just sit on surfaces; it bonds with moisture already present in wood furniture, fabric upholstery, and paper documents, making it harder to reverse the longer it waits.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Pass Christian's residential properties are older coastal cottages and raised pier-and-beam homes built to take advantage of Gulf breezes — beautiful construction, but often featuring heart pine floors, antique millwork, and heirloom furnishings that require careful, material-specific treatment rather than a one-size-fits-all cleaning approach. These aren't items you can simply replace; they're part of what makes a Pass Christian home worth restoring.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Pass Christian

When we arrive at a Pass Christian property, the first priority is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. This documentation step protects you during the insurance claim process and ensures nothing is lost or misattributed during transport.

From there, affected belongings are carefully packed out and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility. For items damaged by fire or smoke, we use a combination of ultrasonic cleaning — which uses high-frequency sound waves in a cleaning solution to lift soot and residue from intricate surfaces like carved wood, ceramics, and electronics — along with ozone treatment and thermal fogging for odor neutralization. Smoke odor that has bonded with fabric in a humid coastal environment requires more aggressive deodorization cycles than the same damage in a dry inland climate, and we calibrate our approach accordingly.

Water-damaged contents from flooding or pipe failures go through a separate drying and sanitation protocol. Upholstered furniture is assessed for salvageability; items that can be saved are dried, cleaned, and treated with antimicrobial agents before storage. Throughout the process, you have access to an itemized inventory so you know exactly where each belonging is and what stage of restoration it's in.
