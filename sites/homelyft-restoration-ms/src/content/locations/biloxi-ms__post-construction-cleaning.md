## Why Biloxi Construction Sites Present Unique Cleaning Challenges

Building and renovating along the Mississippi Gulf Coast means contending with conditions that don't exist inland. Biloxi's proximity to the Gulf means ambient relative humidity regularly climbs above 80 percent, even in winter. Construction dust — drywall compound, concrete particulate, wood fiber — absorbs that moisture and becomes a fine paste that clings to surfaces rather than sweeping away cleanly. Silica-laden dust from cut concrete or tile is particularly stubborn on the textured stucco exteriors common in Biloxi's beachside developments and on the older plaster walls found in some of the city's mid-century residential stock.

Beyond moisture, the region's post-Katrina rebuilding era produced a wave of construction between 2006 and 2015 that used a wide mix of materials — some of which, in older structures that were partially renovated rather than fully replaced, may include lead-containing paint layers. HomeLyft holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, which means when a specialty cleaning scope touches disturbed painted surfaces in a pre-1978 structure, we follow proper containment and disposal protocols rather than simply wiping and moving on.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Biloxi

A final clean after construction isn't a single pass with a mop. The sequence matters, especially in Biloxi's climate.

**Rough clean first.** We remove bulk debris — scrap lumber, packaging, drywall offcuts — before any fine cleaning begins. Leaving debris in place while attempting surface cleaning just redistributes dust.

**Top-down surface cleaning.** Ceiling fans, light fixtures, cabinet tops, window sills, and door frames are cleaned before floors are touched. In Biloxi's humid air, dust that falls to the floor during overhead cleaning can re-adhere to surfaces if the sequence is reversed.

**HVAC and vent cleaning.** Construction dust in return air vents is one of the most overlooked post-build problems. We vacuum and wipe register covers and accessible duct openings so the system doesn't redistribute debris the moment it cycles on — a particular concern given how continuously HVAC systems run in South Mississippi's heat.

**Detail and specialty surfaces.** Window tracks, grout lines, hardware, and appliance interiors get individual attention. For commercial properties — a renovated restaurant near the casino corridor, a medical office, a retail buildout — we can document the clean with before-and-after photography to satisfy landlord or general contractor punch-list requirements.

**Final floor care.** Hard floors are cleaned last: vacuumed, mopped, and inspected under raking light for residual haze. Carpet, if present, is vacuumed with HEPA-filtered equipment.

## Local Note: Salt Air and Window Seals

One thing that surprises property owners doing their first post-construction walkthrough in Biloxi: the fine white haze on newly installed windows isn't always construction dust. Salt aerosol from Gulf breezes can deposit on glass and frames within days of installation, especially on properties east of downtown toward the Back Bay. It looks like calcium or silica residue and responds to the same acidic cleaning approach — but if a crew treats it as ordinary dust and uses an alkaline all-purpose cleaner, they'll smear it rather than lift it.
