## Why Leakesville Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Leakesville sits in the heart of Greene County, where the Chickasawhay River basin influences soil conditions and where a significant portion of the residential housing stock dates to the mid-twentieth century or earlier. That means renovation crews regularly encounter pier-and-beam foundations that have shifted over decades, original hardwood subfloors hiding beneath layers of vinyl, and wall cavities with little to no insulation by modern standards. Homes built before 1978 — and there are many in this part of Mississippi — also carry the risk of lead-based paint, which is why our EPA Lead-Safe Certified status matters on any project that involves cutting, sanding, or demolishing older painted surfaces. Skipping that step isn't just a code issue; it's a health issue for your family and our crew.

Mississippi's humidity also accelerates the deterioration of caulk lines, wood trim, and exterior siding in ways that homeowners in drier climates simply don't face. A bathroom remodel here isn't just about aesthetics — it's about vapor barriers, proper exhaust ventilation, and tile backer board choices that hold up when summer humidity sits above 80 percent for months at a time.
