## Why Henderson Point Homes Are Hard on Belongings

The peninsula's geography creates conditions that accelerate damage to personal property in ways that inland Mississippi towns rarely see. Salt air is corrosive — it oxidizes metal hardware on furniture, degrades leather, and leaves a fine residue on electronics that continues causing damage long after the initial event. Homes in Henderson Point also tend to be older coastal construction, many built before modern vapor barriers were standard, which means crawl spaces and subfloors trap humidity that migrates upward into living areas and the items stored there.

When a weather event or plumbing failure hits, that baseline humidity means textiles, wood furniture, and paper documents are already at a disadvantage. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials in as little as 24 to 48 hours under these conditions — faster when windows and doors stay closed after a loss event. Getting belongings out of the affected environment quickly is not just helpful; on the Gulf Coast, it is often the difference between cleaning an item and replacing it.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

Every contents job starts with a room-by-room inventory before a single item moves. Our crew photographs, barcodes, and logs each piece so you have a complete record — one that also supports your insurance claim documentation. That inventory matters especially here, where high-value items like fishing and boating equipment, outdoor furniture, and coastal artwork are common and often underappreciated on standard homeowner policies.

From there, items are sorted by material type and damage category. Upholstered furniture that has absorbed smoke odor goes through ozone treatment and thermal fogging in a controlled environment. Hard surfaces — wood, metal, glass — are cleaned using methods calibrated to the specific contaminant, whether that is soot, mold, or floodwater sediment. Electronics are assessed by a qualified technician before any cleaning attempt, because salt-air corrosion changes the protocol compared to a standard inland water loss.

All items are then moved to our climate-controlled storage facility, where temperature and relative humidity are actively managed. For Henderson Point homeowners whose repairs may stretch across a full Gulf Coast storm season, that storage environment is not a luxury — it is what keeps restored items from needing restoration a second time.
