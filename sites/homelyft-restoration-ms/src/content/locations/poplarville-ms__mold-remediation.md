## Why Poplarville Properties See Mold Issues

Pearl River County averages well over 60 inches of rainfall per year, and Poplarville's position inland doesn't insulate it from the humidity that rolls up from the Gulf. Summers here regularly push heat-index readings past 105°F, and when that heat meets cooler conditioned air inside a home, condensation collects in crawl spaces, attics, and wall cavities without anyone noticing. Pier-and-beam homes — common throughout Pearl River County — are especially vulnerable because the crawl space beneath the floor acts as a humidity reservoir. Vapor barriers degrade, cross-ventilation is often inadequate, and wood floor joists stay damp for extended periods after heavy rain events.

Older homes in Poplarville sometimes have kraft-faced insulation in exterior walls that was never designed for this level of sustained humidity. When that facing gets wet, it holds moisture against the wood framing rather than allowing it to dry, and mold colonizes the back side of drywall long before a homeowner sees discoloration on the surface. By the time a musty smell is noticeable in a living room or bedroom, the growth behind the wall may already be substantial.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Poplarville

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection — not a visual scan, but a systematic assessment using moisture meters and thermal imaging to map where water has traveled and where it is still sitting. Mold follows moisture, so understanding the moisture map is the only way to set accurate containment boundaries.

Once the scope is defined, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment using heavy-gauge poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the removal process. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and wood framing are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines. Remaining structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent, and dried to verified moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

Because Poplarville's climate means ambient humidity is rarely low, we bring industrial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the containment zone — not undersized rental units. Drying documentation is logged throughout so there is a clear record for your insurance adjuster and, if required, for any post-remediation verification testing.
