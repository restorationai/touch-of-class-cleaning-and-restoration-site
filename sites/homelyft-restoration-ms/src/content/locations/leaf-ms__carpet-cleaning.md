## Why Leaf Properties See Persistent Carpet Problems

Jones County's climate is not kind to flooring. Average annual rainfall in this part of Mississippi regularly exceeds 55 inches, and the combination of high humidity and warm temperatures creates conditions where organic material — pet dander, pollen, mold spores — settles into carpet pile and stays there. Homes in Leaf that rely on older HVAC systems or have crawl spaces with inadequate vapor barriers often see moisture migration from below, which means the carpet pad can hold moisture long after the surface appears dry. That trapped moisture is exactly the environment where microbial growth accelerates.

The local soil is another factor. The reddish-brown clay and loam typical of this region stains differently than coastal sand or urban grime. It binds to synthetic fibers and, once dried, becomes abrasive — slowly cutting carpet strands from the inside out. Addressing it requires pre-treatment with the right pH-balanced agents before hot-water extraction, not just a pass with a steam wand.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Leaf

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment — not every carpet in Leaf is the same, and the cleaning chemistry that works on a nylon loop pile in one home can damage a wool blend in another. Once we identify the fiber type and the nature of the soiling, we pre-treat high-traffic lanes and stained areas with targeted spotting agents calibrated to the red-clay and organic staining patterns common here.

Hot-water extraction follows, using truck-mounted equipment that generates consistent heat and suction regardless of outdoor temperature. This matters in Leaf's summer months, when ambient humidity can slow drying significantly. We adjust dwell time and extraction speed accordingly, and when conditions call for it, we deploy air movers and dehumidifiers to bring drying time down — typically to four to six hours rather than the twelve-plus hours a consumer rental machine might leave behind. For commercial properties, we can section the work to keep parts of the space accessible while adjacent areas dry.

Carpet sanitization is available for homes with pets, allergy concerns, or any history of moisture intrusion. The sanitizing step uses EPA-registered solutions applied after extraction, targeting the microbial load that humidity and organic debris build up over time.
