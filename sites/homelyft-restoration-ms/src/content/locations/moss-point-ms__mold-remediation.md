## Why Moss Point Properties See Mold Issues

The Jackson County Gulf Coast climate is among the most mold-permissive in the continental United States. Average annual rainfall exceeds 60 inches, and the months between May and October bring sustained humidity that rarely drops below the threshold where mold growth slows. Moss Point's position near the confluence of two river systems means that even properties that haven't flooded can pull ambient moisture through crawl spaces and slab joints — a process that accelerates in older pier-and-beam construction, which is common throughout the city's established residential areas.

Many homes in Moss Point were built in the mid-20th century, before vapor barriers and modern HVAC design became standard. Attic ventilation in these structures is often inadequate for the local climate, creating warm, still air pockets where condensation accumulates on roof sheathing — a pattern that produces black mold colonies that homeowners never see until a roofer or inspector opens the attic. Post-hurricane moisture intrusion compounds the problem: water that enters through damaged roofing or windows can travel along wall cavities and settle in floor assemblies well below the visible water line.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Moss Point
