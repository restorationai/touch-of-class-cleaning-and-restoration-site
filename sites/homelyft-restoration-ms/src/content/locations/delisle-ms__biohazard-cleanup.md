## Why DeLisle Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Harrison County's coastal climate means that even a brief delay in biohazard cleanup can accelerate secondary contamination. Ambient temperatures along this stretch of Mississippi regularly climb into the upper 80s and 90s from late spring through early fall, and interior humidity in homes without strong climate control can exceed 70%. Those conditions — warmth and moisture together — dramatically shorten the window before infectious material begins to interact with porous surfaces like subflooring, drywall, and insulation.

Many homes in the DeLisle area were built in the mid-20th century, and that construction era typically used hardwood flooring over pier-and-beam foundations rather than concrete slab. Contaminants can migrate through seams and gaps into the subfloor cavity below, which is not visible from the living space. Standard surface cleaning misses this entirely. Proper remediation here means testing beneath the visible surface, not just treating what can be seen.
