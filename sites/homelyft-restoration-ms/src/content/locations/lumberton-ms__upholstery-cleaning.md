## Why Lumberton Homes See Upholstery Wear

Most of the housing stock around Lumberton is ranch-style brick built from the 1960s through the 1980s, along with a good number of manufactured and modular homes on larger rural lots. Central air conditioning helps, but a lot of these homes still run window units in back bedrooms or sunrooms, which means uneven humidity control from room to room. Fabric upholstery in a room without steady airflow can hold moisture for days after a spill or a humid stretch, and that's exactly the window mildew needs to take hold in the padding and frame, not just the visible fabric. Add in a rural lifestyle that often includes hunting dogs, farm dust, and mud tracked in from the yard, and upholstery in this area tends to carry embedded soil that a store-bought spot cleaner won't touch.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Lumberton

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, since a wrong cleaning method can set a stain permanently or damage a hide. Leather upholstery gets conditioned and cleaned with pH-balanced products that won't crack or dry out the surface; fabric upholstery gets pre-treated for the specific stain type before we move to hot water extraction or low-moisture steam cleaning, depending on the material. Because of the ambient humidity common to this part of the state, we pay close attention to drying time after extraction. We use targeted airflow and, when needed, dehumidification equipment to pull moisture out of the padding and frame, not just the fabric surface, so cushions don't stay damp longer than they should in a Lamar County summer.

## Local Note

A lot of homes in and around Lumberton rely on well water rather than municipal supply, and hard water minerals can leave a faint yellow-brown ring on light-colored fabric upholstery after a spill dries, especially near baseboards where furniture sits close to exterior walls.
