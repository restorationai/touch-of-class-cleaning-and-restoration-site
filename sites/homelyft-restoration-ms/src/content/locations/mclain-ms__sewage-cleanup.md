## Why McLain Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Greene County's rural character means a large share of McLain homes rely on private septic systems rather than municipal sewer connections. Septic systems have finite lifespans, and when drain fields become saturated — especially after the heavy Gulf-influenced rainfall events that roll through southern Mississippi from late spring through fall — effluent has nowhere to go but back up through the lowest fixture in the house. Older homes in the area, many built in the mid-20th century with cast-iron drain lines, are also prone to root intrusion and pipe corrosion that triggers sewer line backups independent of the septic tank itself.

Crawl space construction is the dominant foundation type across much of McLain, which matters enormously for sewage events. When a toilet or floor drain backs up, contaminated water travels the path of least resistance — often straight into the crawl space, where it contacts untreated wood joists, insulation batts, and bare soil. That environment is warm, humid, and dark: mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours, and bacterial contamination in soil under a crawl space doesn't resolve on its own.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in McLain

Every sewage job starts with containment and category assessment. Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and that classification drives every decision about what gets cleaned versus what gets removed. Porous materials like carpet padding, saturated insulation, and compromised drywall below the flood line are typically removed rather than dried in place, because pathogens embed in porous substrates in ways that surface sanitization cannot fully address.

Once the source is confirmed stopped, our crew extracts standing water and liquid waste using truck-mounted extraction equipment. In crawl space situations common to McLain homes, we deploy portable extractors and submersible pumps to pull contaminated water from low-clearance areas that truck mounts can't reach directly. After bulk removal, affected surfaces — concrete, wood framing, soil — are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for Category 3 contamination. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, monitored with moisture meters until readings return to regional baseline levels. Because HomeLyft holds IICRC AMRT certification, we can also assess and address any secondary mold growth that developed before the call was made.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically in Greene County crawl space jobs: the soil under older McLain homes often contains a high clay fraction just a few inches below the surface layer. Clay holds moisture and holds contamination differently than sandy soil — it doesn't drain, so pooled sewage sits against the vapor barrier or bare earth for longer than it would in a sandier substrate. During remediation, we test soil moisture at multiple depths rather than just surface readings, and in cases of significant contamination we may recommend removing and replacing the top layer of crawl space soil before laying new vapor barrier. It's an extra step that isn't always discussed upfront by every contractor, but it's the difference between a job that's documented clean and one that smells fine for six months and then doesn't.
