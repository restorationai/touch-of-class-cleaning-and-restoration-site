## Why Bond Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Much of the residential building stock in and around Bond reflects construction methods common to rural Mississippi from the mid-twentieth century — pier-and-beam foundations, tongue-and-groove sheathing, and in older structures, lead-based paint on trim and windows. That last point matters practically: any renovation that disturbs painted surfaces in a pre-1978 home requires EPA Lead-Safe protocols, and HomeLyft Restoration MS carries that certification. Skipping that step isn't just a code issue — it's a health risk to your family during the project.

Beyond the age of the housing stock, the climate does real work on these homes. The combination of high summer humidity and seasonal heavy rain means moisture infiltrates wall cavities, crawl spaces, and attic decking in ways that aren't always visible until demolition begins. A kitchen remodel that looks straightforward on paper can reveal blackened floor joists or soft sheathing the moment the cabinets come down. Planning for discovery is part of how we scope every project in this region.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Bond

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough — not a sales visit, but a working inspection.
