## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Wade

Every job begins with a full assessment before any material is disturbed. A technician walks the affected area, identifies the scope of contamination, and determines which surfaces are porous, semi-porous, or non-porous — because each category calls for a different protocol.

Containment comes next. Plastic sheeting, negative-air machines, and HEPA filtration isolate the work zone so that cross-contamination to unaffected rooms does not occur. After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, and odor-neutralizing agents are applied to any remaining porous material.
