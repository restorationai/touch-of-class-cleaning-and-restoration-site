## Why Soso Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Jones County's housing stock includes a significant number of older rural homes — many built before modern subfloor and ventilation standards — where porous materials like untreated wood, aged drywall, and unfinished concrete can absorb biological contaminants deeply. Unlike tile or sealed surfaces, these materials cannot simply be wiped down. Proper remediation often means controlled removal of affected material down to a structurally sound layer, followed by treatment and verification.

The region's humidity also accelerates the risk of secondary contamination. Even after visible material is removed, residual moisture in a warm, unventilated space can create conditions where pathogens persist longer than expected. That's a factor our OSHA-trained, IICRC-certified team accounts for at every job in this area — not just the visible cleanup, but the environmental conditions around it.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Soso

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. We arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested, and we work with the property owner or authorized representative — not bystanders — to understand the scope before anyone enters the affected area.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — Isolating the affected space to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC pathways, which in older Soso homes may be less compartmentalized than in newer construction.
- **Removal of affected materials** — Porous items that cannot be decontaminated are bagged, labeled, and transported in compliance with Mississippi State Department of Health regulations for biohazardous waste disposal.
- **Surface treatment** — EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected and adjacent surfaces. In homes with exposed wood framing or subfloor, treatment goes beyond the surface layer.
- **Odor neutralization** — Biological incidents leave behind compounds that standard cleaning cannot address. We use hydroxyl or ozone treatment depending on the space and materials present.
- **Clearance verification** — Before we close out, affected surfaces are tested to confirm contamination levels are within safe thresholds. You receive documentation of the completed work.
