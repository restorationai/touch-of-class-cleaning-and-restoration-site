## Why Poplarville Properties See Carpet Problems

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Poplarville

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment before a drop of solution touches the carpet. We apply the pre-spray, allow dwell time calibrated to the soil load, then agitate with a counter-rotating brush machine that lifts clay particles from the base of the fiber rather than just the surface.

Extraction uses truck-mounted hot-water equipment capable of reaching temperatures that sanitize at the fiber level — relevant in Poplarville's climate because heat is the most reliable way to neutralize the mold spores and dust mite populations that thrive in high-humidity carpet environments. After extraction, we measure moisture content in both the carpet and the pad. If pad saturation is above threshold, we address it before packing up — leaving wet pad under clean carpet is one of the most common reasons carpets smell again within a week of cleaning.
