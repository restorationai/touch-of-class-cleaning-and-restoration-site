## Why Leakesville Carpets Take a Harder Hit Than Most

Greene County's climate is the main culprit. Leakesville averages well over 60 inches of rainfall a year, and the region's sandy-loam and red-clay soils track indoors constantly — especially during wet winters and the long, muddy shoulder seasons. Clay particles are abrasive; they work down into carpet pile and cut fibers over time, dulling color and accelerating wear. Add the region's persistent humidity and you create conditions where carpet backing stays damp long enough for mildew to colonize between cleanings.

Older homes in this part of Greene County — many built in the 1960s through 1980s — often have wall-to-wall carpet over concrete slab foundations. Slabs in humid climates like this one can wick ground moisture upward, which means carpet padding can hold latent dampness even when the surface feels dry. That's a pattern we see regularly in homes throughout the Leakesville area, and it's one reason a surface-only cleaning often isn't enough.

## Our Deep Carpet Cleaning Process in Leakesville

We use truck-mounted hot-water extraction equipment — the same category of system the IICRC points to as the most effective method for deep fiber cleaning. The process starts with a dry pre-vacuum pass to pull out loose soil before it turns to mud during extraction. We then apply a pH-balanced pre-treatment to break down the organic compounds in stains — pet accidents, food spills, the tannins in red clay — and let it dwell long enough to actually work.

Hot-water extraction follows: water heated to the right temperature for the carpet fiber type is injected under pressure and immediately vacuumed back out, pulling emulsified soil, residue, and allergens with it. In Leakesville's climate, we pay close attention to moisture levels left in the carpet after extraction. Leaving too much water in high-humidity conditions extends dry time significantly and raises the risk of secondary mildew growth. We use moisture meters throughout the job and, when needed, position air movers to accelerate drying before we leave the property.

For carpet stain removal on stubborn spots — rust from iron-rich well water, tannin stains, or set-in pet odor — we apply targeted enzyme treatments or specialty spotters calibrated to the stain chemistry rather than a one-size product.
