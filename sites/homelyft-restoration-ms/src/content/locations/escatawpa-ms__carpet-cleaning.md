## Why Escatawpa Properties See Carpet Problems Faster Than You'd Expect

Jackson County's climate sits at a humidity crossroads. Carpets that look surface-clean can harbor microbial growth in the underlayer — something that becomes obvious when a room starts smelling musty even after vacuuming.

The soil composition around Escatawpa compounds the problem.
