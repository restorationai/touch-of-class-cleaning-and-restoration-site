## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Rawls Springs

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment. Not all carpet responds the same way to hot-water extraction — some older cut-pile styles common in homes built in the 1980s and 1990s can shrink or distort if water temperature or dwell time isn't calibrated correctly. We identify the fiber type before we set up equipment.

From there the process runs in sequence:

1. **Pre-treatment** — a pH-appropriate conditioner is applied to loosen clay-based soils and break down organic staining from pets, food, or tracked-in debris.
2. **Agitation** — a counter-rotating brush works the pre-treatment into the pile, lifting compacted fibers so extraction reaches the base of the carpet rather than just the face.
3. **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted units deliver heated water at controlled pressure and recover it immediately, pulling suspended soil, allergens, and residual detergent out in a single pass. Truck-mount power is meaningfully stronger than portable units, which matters on heavily soiled carpet.
4. **Spot treatment** — any stains that survive the main extraction pass get targeted individually. Tannin stains from sweet tea and coffee, pet urine salts, and red clay each respond to different chemistry.
5. **Drying and airflow** — in Rawls Springs's humid conditions, we position air movers to accelerate surface drying and reduce the window during which re-soiling or microbial activity can start. We check moisture readings before packing up.
