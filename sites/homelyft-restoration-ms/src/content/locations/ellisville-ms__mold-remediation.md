## Why Ellisville Properties See Mold Issues

Jones County's clay-heavy soil doesn't drain quickly. After heavy rain, water pools against foundation slabs and crawl spaces longer than it would in sandier coastal terrain. Many homes in and around Ellisville were built in the 1960s through 1980s, when vapor barriers in crawl spaces were minimal or absent entirely. Without adequate vapor control beneath the floor system, ground moisture migrates upward into subfloor sheathing and floor joists — exactly the kind of dark, damp, poorly ventilated space where *Cladosporium*, *Penicillium*, and *Stachybotrys* (commonly called black mold) establish colonies before a homeowner notices any visible growth.

HVAC systems are another frequent culprit. Central air units in Mississippi homes work hard from April through October. When a duct develops a slow leak or an evaporator coil drain line clogs, condensation accumulates inside walls and ceilings for weeks before anyone sees a stain. By then, the mold count in that cavity is already elevated.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Ellisville

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — visual assessment plus moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters to find hidden saturation that doesn't show on the surface. We don't guess at the boundary of contamination; we measure it.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

1. **Containment** — Negative-air containment barriers isolate the affected area so spores don't migrate to clean rooms during removal. In Ellisville's older homes, where open floor plans are less common and interior walls are often load-bearing, we plan containment carefully to avoid disrupting structural elements.
2. **HEPA air scrubbing** — Industrial HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the project, capturing airborne spores down to 0.3 microns.
3. **Controlled demolition and removal** — Affected drywall, insulation, or wood materials are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines. Salvageable structural lumber is treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent.
4. **Structural drying** — If the underlying moisture source was a plumbing leak or intrusion event, we dry the structure to IICRC S520 standard before any rebuilding begins. Skipping this step is the single most common reason mold returns.
5. **Post-remediation verification** — We recommend independent clearance testing by a third-party industrial hygienist before the area is closed in. We can coordinate that referral.

## Ellisville Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Mississippi are handled differently depending on the cause. If the mold resulted from a sudden, accidental water event — a burst pipe, an appliance failure — most homeowner policies will cover remediation. If the cause is long-term seepage or deferred maintenance, coverage is often denied. We document the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs, written scope of damage, and cause-of-loss narrative.
