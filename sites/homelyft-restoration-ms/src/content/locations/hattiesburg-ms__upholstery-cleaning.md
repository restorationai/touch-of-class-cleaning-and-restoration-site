## Why Hattiesburg Homes See Upholstery Issues

The Pine Belt region sits at a humidity level that rarely gives fabric furniture a chance to fully dry out between uses. ## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Hattiesburg

We start with a fabric and dye test on an inconspicuous spot, because a surprising number of upholstery pieces we see in this area are older or big-box pieces without a legible cleaning code tag. ## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

Hot water extraction is our default for most fabric upholstery because it flushes soil and cleaning solution out of the padding rather than just the surface, which matters most for the deep-set odor issues that humidity causes here. For delicate or dry-clean-only fabrics, we switch to a low-moisture encapsulation method that cleans without oversaturating cushions that already struggle to dry fully in this climate. Leather gets cleaned and conditioned as a separate process entirely, using products formulated to replace the oils that Hattiesburg's humidity swings strip out over time.
