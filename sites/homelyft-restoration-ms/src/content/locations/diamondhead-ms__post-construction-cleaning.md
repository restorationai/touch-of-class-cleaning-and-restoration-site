## Why Diamondhead Properties Accumulate More Post-Construction Residue

Harrison County's coastal climate creates a specific challenge for post-construction cleanup that contractors from inland markets don't always anticipate. Drywall compound dust, grout haze, and overspray are hygroscopic — they pull moisture from the air. In Diamondhead, where relative humidity routinely climbs above 80 percent during summer months, these residues can set into a film on windows, cabinetry, and light fixtures within days of a crew packing up. Left unaddressed, that film traps allergens and makes surfaces look perpetually dingy even after a homeowner's first wipe-down attempt.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Diamondhead

Every job starts with a walk-through before a single cloth is unfolded. We document existing conditions — scratched glass, pre-existing scuffs, anything the construction crew left that isn't our responsibility — so there's no dispute at the end. That documentation also helps when a general contractor or property owner needs to make a punch-list claim against a subcontractor.

From there, the work moves top to bottom, rough to fine:

- **Rough clean**: removal of construction debris, leftover materials, and bulk dust from floors, sills, and exposed framing cavities.
- **Detail clean**: HEPA-filtered vacuuming of every duct register, cabinet interior, and window track. Construction dust in HVAC systems is a particular concern in Diamondhead homes — fine silica and drywall particulate pulled into ductwork during a renovation will recirculate every time the air handler runs.
- **Final clean**: surface-specific treatment for glass, hardware, tile, and countertops. Grout haze removal, stainless steel polishing, and fixture detailing happen here.
- **Inspection pass**: a second walk-through under raking light to catch streaks, residue, and missed areas before we call the job complete.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor neutralization on a renovated space, deep cleaning after a tenant vacancy, or cleaning ahead of a certificate-of-occupancy inspection — the process adapts to the specific scope. Our team holds IICRC certifications in fire and smoke restoration and mold remediation, so when a "cleaning" job reveals something more serious beneath the surface, we can address it without bringing in a second contractor.
