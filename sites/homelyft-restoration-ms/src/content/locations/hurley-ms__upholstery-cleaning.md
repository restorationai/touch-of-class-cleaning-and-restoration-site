## Why Hurley Homes See Upholstery Wear Faster

Hurley sits along Highway 63 near the Pascagoula River, and that river humidity doesn't stay outside. Fabric upholstery in un-air-conditioned porches, dens, and hunting camps absorbs moisture straight out of the air, and once that happens, dust mites and mildew spores get a head start. Add in well water that runs a little harder than city water in some parts of the county, and you get mineral buildup in fabric fibers that plain vacuuming never touches. Leather furniture fares differently but not better: the same humidity that swells fabric dries out leather's natural oils faster between rainy stretches, leaving cushions and armrests cracked at the seams long before they should be.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Hurley

We start with a dry vacuum pass using an upholstery attachment to lift loose soil, pet hair, and grit before any moisture touches the piece, because working wet soil into fabric just grinds it deeper. Fabric sofas and chairs get a hot water extraction pass, calibrated lower than a carpet job so the padding underneath doesn't stay damp longer than the fabric shell. Leather pieces get a separate conditioning step: a pH-balanced cleaner followed by a leather conditioner that replaces the oils Gulf Coast humidity swings tend to strip out. We finish with fans positioned to move air through and under the piece, not just across the surface, since a cushion that feels dry on top can still be wet in the foam core.

## Local note

A lot of Hurley living rooms have at least one piece of furniture that's spent time near a wood stove or propane heater through the winter. Fabric near a heat source dries out and gets brittle faster than fabric across the room, so those cushions often need a gentler extraction pass and a longer air-dry time to avoid over-drying fibers that are already stressed. We check heat-adjacent pieces separately rather than running the whole set through the same settings.
