## Why Long Beach Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

The housing stock along the Long Beach waterfront skews older — many homes were rebuilt or heavily renovated after Katrina, which means you'll find a mix of original mid-century construction methods alongside newer materials installed under post-2005 Mississippi building codes. That combination creates layered cleaning problems. Older concrete block walls hold fine silica dust in their surface texture differently than smooth modern drywall. Spray-foam insulation, now common in Gulf-region rebuilds for energy efficiency, off-gasses during the first weeks after application and leaves a residue film on glass and cabinetry that standard wipe-downs won't fully remove.

The coastal climate compounds all of this. Relative humidity in Harrison County regularly runs above 70 percent even in mild months. Construction dust that would stay powder-dry in a drier climate becomes a paste here — it bonds to tile grout, window tracks, and HVAC fins. If that residue isn't removed before the HVAC system runs at full capacity, it circulates through the ductwork and undoes the clean before the client ever moves in.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Long Beach

A proper final clean after construction or renovation moves in a specific sequence. Skipping steps — or doing them out of order — means rework.

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work, bulk debris comes out: drywall chunks, fasteners, cut lumber scraps, packaging materials. We bag and stage debris according to Long Beach's municipal waste guidelines, which matter especially on properties close to the beach where runoff into storm drains is regulated.

**HEPA vacuuming of all surfaces.** Walls, ceilings, window sills, cabinetry interiors, and light fixtures get HEPA-filtered vacuuming before any wet cleaning begins. This is the step most general cleaning crews skip, and it's the reason you see streaks on windows after a standard wipe-down — they're pushing dust through moisture rather than removing it.

**Surface-by-surface detail clean.** Tile and grout lines, hardware, plumbing fixtures, appliance interiors, and glass all get cleaned with products appropriate to each material. Newly installed surfaces are particularly vulnerable — we use pH-neutral solutions on stone countertops and avoid abrasives on brushed-metal finishes.

**Final air quality pass.** We run air scrubbers with HEPA filtration to capture any remaining airborne particulate before the space is handed back. In Long Beach's humid conditions, this step also helps stabilize interior moisture before the HVAC takes over.

Post-construction cleanup is the most common call, but specialty cleaning covers a broader range of situations that Long Beach property owners encounter.
