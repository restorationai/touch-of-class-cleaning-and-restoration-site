## Why Kiln Properties See Elevated Contents Damage

Hancock County's combination of high annual humidity, recurring tropical weather systems, and older rural housing stock creates conditions that accelerate damage to personal property. Many homes in the Kiln area were built in the 1970s and 1980s with crawl-space foundations, which means groundwater intrusion and vapor migration can quietly saturate lower-level belongings — furniture legs, cardboard storage boxes, rugs — before a visible water event even occurs. When a named storm pushes through and a roof lets in rain, or a pipe freezes during a rare January cold snap and bursts, the contents inside absorb moisture rapidly in an environment that already has limited air circulation.

Smoke damage compounds this. Kiln's older homes often have wood-paneled interiors and natural-fiber furnishings that hold smoke odor tenaciously. Soot particles settle into fabric weaves, wooden grain, and porous ceramics within hours of a fire, and the longer they sit, the deeper the staining and the harder the odor is to neutralize. Prompt pack-out is not optional — it's the difference between a restorable sofa and a total loss.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Kiln

Every contents job begins with a detailed room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves your home. Each piece is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a disposition — restore, clean and store, or document as a total loss for your insurance claim. From there, the process moves through several calibrated stages:

- **Pack-out**: Items are wrapped, crated, and transported in enclosed vehicles to prevent additional exposure during transit. For Kiln addresses, that means a controlled drive to our Gulfport facility rather than leaving contents in a staging tent on a property that may still have active humidity issues.
- **Cleaning and restoration**: Depending on the damage type, we use ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, dry-cleaning and ozone treatment for textiles, and thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation for smoke-affected items. IICRC FSRT protocols guide fire and smoke work; IICRC WRT standards govern water-damaged contents.
- **Climate-controlled storage**: Restored and in-progress items are held in a temperature- and humidity-regulated facility. This is especially important for Kiln customers whose homes may require weeks of structural drying before contents can safely return.
- **Coordinated return**: We schedule the return delivery around your contractor's completion timeline and walk through each item with you on delivery.
