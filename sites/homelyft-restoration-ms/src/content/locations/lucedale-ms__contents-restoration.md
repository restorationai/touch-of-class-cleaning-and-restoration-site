## Why Lucedale Homes Face Elevated Contents Damage Risk

George County's housing stock skews older, and many Lucedale-area homes were built with wood-framed construction and pier-and-beam foundations that allow air — and moisture — to move freely beneath the floor. When a water loss happens, that movement pulls humid air up through subflooring and into living spaces, accelerating the rate at which upholstered furniture, wood cabinetry, and stored paper goods absorb moisture. The region's pine-heavy timber framing also means that smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire travels through wall cavities faster than it does in tighter modern construction, embedding odor into contents rooms away from the fire's origin point.

Lucedale also sits in a tornado and severe-storm corridor. When a storm tears a section of roof open, rain can saturate an attic and flow down into closets and bedrooms before anyone realizes the structure is compromised. By the time the damage is visible on the ceiling, textiles and stored items below may already be at risk of mold colonization — which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in George County's warm, wet conditions.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Lucedale

The first step is a detailed contents inventory. Every item in the affected area is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before anything is moved. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

From there, salvageable contents are carefully packed out and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. In a climate like Lucedale's, storage environment matters enormously — items moved from a water-damaged home into a hot, unventilated space can develop mold within days. Our storage conditions are monitored for temperature and relative humidity so that the pack-out itself doesn't create a second loss.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Hard surfaces — furniture frames, lamps, décor — are cleaned with appropriate solutions and dried thoroughly. Soft goods including upholstery, rugs, and drapes go through specialized cleaning processes calibrated to the type of soiling (smoke residue, water staining, or microbial contamination). Documents and photographs with sentimental or legal value are handled separately, with freeze-drying or air-drying techniques used when warranted. The team holds IICRC FSRT and IICRC AMRT credentials, which directly inform how fire-and-smoke-affected and mold-affected contents are treated.

Once the structure is restored and cleared, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed back in their original locations where possible.
