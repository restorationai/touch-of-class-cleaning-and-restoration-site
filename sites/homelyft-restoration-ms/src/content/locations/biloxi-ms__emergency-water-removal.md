## Why Biloxi Properties See Water Damage

Biloxi sits on the Mississippi Sound, which means two separate threats: tropical systems pushing storm surge and wind-driven rain inland, and ordinary plumbing failures that behave differently here than they would further from the coast. Humidity along the coast regularly sits high enough that wet materials don't just sit wet, they actively resist drying unless air movement and dehumidification are aggressive from hour one. A lot of the city's housing stock was rebuilt or substantially renovated after 2005 to updated flood-elevation requirements, which means a mix of raised foundations and slab-on-grade construction on the same block. Slab homes in particular trap moisture against the concrete and the base of interior walls, so a leak that looks minor on the surface can be feeding moisture into framing for days before anyone notices a smell.

## Our Water Removal Process in Biloxi

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable extractors depending on how much volume we're dealing with and whether the structure still has power. For slab foundations, we pay close attention to baseboards and the bottom few inches of drywall, since that's where coastal slab moisture tends to wick upward longest after the visible water is gone.
