## Why Nicholson Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Pearl River County's housing stock skews older, and many Nicholson properties were built during an era when vapor barriers were minimal or nonexistent. That matters enormously when you open up a wall for a bathroom remodel or kitchen expansion — you frequently find substandard insulation, knob-and-tube remnants, or framing that has absorbed decades of seasonal humidity cycling. The clay-heavy soils common to this part of Mississippi also shift more than sandy coastal soils, which means slab foundations and pier-and-beam structures alike can show settling that affects door frames, tile work, and load-bearing walls before a single cosmetic renovation begins.

Adding to that, the area's proximity to the Gulf means any renovation that touches the roofline or exterior envelope needs to meet Mississippi's FORTIFIED building standards — a requirement that HomeLyft is certified to address as a FORTIFIED Building/Roofing/Windstorm certified contractor. That certification isn't just a credential; it changes how we specify materials and sequence work on projects where insurance discounts or lender requirements hinge on windstorm compliance.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Nicholson

Every project starts with a thorough walk-through that goes beyond cosmetic assessment. Before we quote a kitchen remodel or a post-damage rebuild, we check subfloor moisture levels, inspect the attic for ventilation issues that could undermine new finishes, and confirm whether any pre-1978 materials require EPA Lead-Safe protocols — a real consideration in Pearl River County homes of that vintage.
