## Insurance Coordination After a Storm

Most homeowners' policies in Mississippi cover wind and wind-driven rain as a named peril, but flood damage from storm surge or rising water typically requires a separate NFIP or private flood policy. That distinction matters when we write the scope of loss — misclassifying a water source can delay or reduce your claim. We document the damage origin (roof breach vs. ground-level intrusion) with photographs and moisture mapping so your adjuster has the evidence needed to apply the correct coverage. We bill carriers directly and communicate with adjusters throughout the project so you are not managing paperwork while also managing a damaged home.
