**Hidden water leak in your Long Beach home?** A slow leak behind a wall or under a slab doesn't always announce itself with a puddle. It shows up as a water bill that's crept up for no reason, a warped baseboard, or a patch of tile that stays cool even in August humidity. ## Why Long Beach Properties See Leak Detection Issues

Long Beach sits on the Mississippi Gulf Coast, where sandy, shifting soil and a high water table are part of daily life. Slab foundations are common here, and that soil movement, combined with seasonal saturation from Gulf storms, puts steady stress on the copper and PEX lines running beneath them. Add in the salt-humid air that works on older galvanized fittings and supply lines, and you get a coastline where slab leaks and pinhole leaks in copper pipe show up earlier than they would further inland. Homes built before the 1990s, especially those with original plumbing, are the ones we get called to most.

## Our Water Leak Detection Process in Long Beach

We start by isolating which zone of the plumbing system is losing pressure, then move to acoustic listening equipment that picks up the sound of water escaping under a slab or inside a wall cavity, something that's easy to describe but genuinely hard to hear without the right sensors tuned to concrete transmission. From there we bring in infrared thermal imaging to map temperature differentials along the floor or wall, since an active leak almost always reads cooler (or occasionally warmer, with hot water lines) than the surrounding material. Moisture meters confirm the read before anyone cuts into drywall or tile. The goal on every job is to locate the leak with the smallest possible opening, not guess-and-chase.

## Long Beach Insurance & HOA Coordination

Slab leaks and hidden wall leaks often fall into a gray area with insurance: carriers typically cover the resulting water damage but not the plumbing repair itself, so documentation matters. We photograph and log the detection findings (thermal images, moisture readings, acoustic data) so your adjuster has a clear record of what was found and where, which speeds up approval on the drying and repair side of the claim.
