## Why Big Point Properties Face Elevated Contents Risk

Jackson County's coastal proximity means that even storms that don't make direct landfall can push enough wind-driven rain through window seals, roof penetrations, and crawl space vents to saturate interior spaces. Homes in the Big Point area frequently feature older wood-framed construction with pier-and-beam foundations — a style that allows air movement underneath but also lets humidity migrate upward into flooring, cabinetry, and stored items in lower rooms. That combination of storm exposure and building style means contents don't just get wet from above; moisture wicks in from multiple directions simultaneously.

Furniture with solid-wood frames and upholstered pieces absorb that humidity quickly. Smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire compounds the problem — soot particles settle into fabric fibers and porous wood within hours, and the longer they sit, the deeper the staining and the harder the odor is to neutralize. Acting within the first day or two makes a measurable difference in what can be saved versus what must be replaced.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Big Point

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves your home. Every piece — from a dining table to a box of children's books — is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition rating. That documentation matters enormously for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of exactly what left your property.

From there, items are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Jackson County's summer heat and humidity make on-site drying of contents impractical; a controlled environment lets us work on belongings without fighting ambient moisture. Depending on the type of damage, restoration may involve:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, ceramics, and metal items — a process that removes soot and residue from intricate surfaces without abrasion
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods and upholstered furniture to neutralize smoke odor at the molecular level rather than masking it
- **Document and photo drying** using specialized freeze-drying or air-drying techniques for irreplaceable paper items
- **Furniture refinishing and fabric cleaning** calibrated to the specific material — leather, microfiber, and antique wood each require different chemistry

Once restoration is complete and your home is ready, items are delivered back and placed according to your floor plan. Nothing is returned until it passes a final inspection.

## Insurance Coordination for Big Point Contents Claims

Contents losses are frequently the most contested part of a property insurance claim. Adjusters work from depreciated replacement value by default, which can significantly undervalue furniture, electronics, and collectibles. Our itemized inventory — with photographs, condition notes, and restoration cost estimates — gives your carrier the documentation needed to process a fair settlement.

We work directly with most major carriers and can communicate with your adjuster throughout the process. If your policy includes replacement cost value coverage for contents, we help you capture that full benefit rather than leaving money on the table.
