## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Latimer

The process begins with a room-by-room contents inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code — salvageable, needs treatment, or unsalvageable — so your insurance claim is supported by documentation from the first hour on-site. Items that can be treated on location are handled there; everything else goes through a structured pack-out.

Pack-out from a Latimer home means wrapping and boxing contents under controlled conditions, then transporting them to a climate-controlled facility where temperature and humidity are maintained at levels that halt further deterioration. At the storage facility, contents undergo ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke-affected soft goods, and specialized document drying for paper records. Electronics are evaluated by a certified technician before any power is applied — a step that matters because Gulf Coast humidity can cause corrosion inside devices that looks minor but creates a short-circuit risk.

Once the structure is cleared and dried to IICRC S500 standards, contents are returned in the same documented sequence they left, making reinstallation straightforward and reducing the chance that items are misplaced during the chaos of a loss event.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Latimer and the surrounding unincorporated Harrison County areas: pier-and-beam homes with wood subfloors tend to trap moisture beneath area rugs and furniture pads in ways that aren't visible from above. After a water loss, a sofa or bookcase that looks dry on the surface may be sitting on a subfloor section that reads 25 to 30 percent moisture content on a pin meter. Leaving furniture in place during structural drying without first moving it to a staging area — or off-site storage — means the contents themselves become a moisture reservoir that slows the overall drying timeline and can lead to mold growth under the piece. Moving contents out early isn't just about protecting the belongings; it's often what makes the structural drying job achievable within a standard claim timeline.
