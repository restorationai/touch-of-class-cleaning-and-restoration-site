## Why Sandersville Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Jones County's climate averages over 55 inches of rainfall per year, and summer humidity regularly pushes above 80 percent. That combination means that belongings left inside a water- or fire-damaged home don't just sit — they actively absorb moisture from the surrounding air even after the source event is resolved. Older homes in and around Sandersville frequently feature pier-and-beam foundations, which allow humidity to migrate upward through flooring and affect furniture legs, area rugs, and stored boxes in ways that slab-foundation homes typically don't. Wood furniture swells, fabric develops musty odors, and paper documents begin to degrade within 24 to 48 hours under these conditions. The local building stock also includes a meaningful share of homes with original hardwood floors and plaster-and-lath interior walls — materials that hold onto smoke particulates and odor compounds more stubbornly than modern drywall, meaning contents stored inside those rooms need more thorough deodorization before they can safely return.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Sandersville

The process begins with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued by category, and assessed for damage type — soot, water saturation, mold growth, or odor absorption. Fragile or high-value pieces are wrapped and packed separately. From Sandersville, contents are transported to our climate-controlled storage facility where temperature and humidity are actively managed, removing the ongoing threat that Jones County's outdoor air poses to recovering belongings.

Once in the facility, each category of item goes through the appropriate cleaning method. Hard surfaces — furniture frames, lamps, decorative items — are cleaned with ultrasonic equipment or hand-wiped with appropriate solutions depending on the material. Soft goods like upholstery, drapery, and clothing are assessed for laundering, dry-cleaning referral, or ozone and hydroxyl deodorization treatment. Documents and photographs receive specialized drying and stabilization. Throughout the process, the inventory log is updated so you and your insurance adjuster have a clear, itemized record of what was received, what was restored, and what could not be salvaged. When your home is ready, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in their rooms.

Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration) and IICRC WRT (Water Restoration) certifications, and the company is an EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm — relevant in Sandersville's older housing stock where lead paint on furniture or trim is a real consideration during contents handling.
