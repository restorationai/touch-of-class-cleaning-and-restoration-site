Lyman sits in Harrison County where the Gulf Coast's humidity doesn't take a break — not even after your renovation crew packs up and leaves. That lingering moisture, combined with the fine construction dust that settles into every gap in a freshly remodeled home, means a post-construction cleanup here is a different animal than a quick sweep and mop. HomeLyft Restoration MS handles that gap between the last subcontractor walking out the door and the moment a space is actually ready to live or work in — removing drywall dust, adhesive residue, caulk smears, and the kind of grime that only a full-scale specialty clean can address.

## Why Lyman Properties Face Particular Post-Construction Challenges

Harrison County's climate is the first complication. Average relative humidity along this stretch of the Mississippi Gulf Coast frequently exceeds 70 percent, and construction dust — especially the fine particulate left by drywall sanding — absorbs ambient moisture and bonds to surfaces more aggressively than it would in a drier climate. What looks like a thin film on window sills and cabinet interiors can harden into a paste that ordinary cleaning products won't lift without the right chemistry and dwell time.

Lyman's housing stock adds another layer. The area includes a mix of newer builds put up after Hurricane Katrina's rebuilding wave and older structures that predate modern vapor barrier standards. In older homes, construction activity can disturb settled dust in wall cavities and attic spaces, pushing particulate into living areas through gaps that weren't sealed during the renovation. A thorough post-construction clean has to account for that migration — not just the visible debris on the floor, but the fine dust that has traveled.

Finally, the soil here is sandy and tracks easily. Construction crews moving in and out leave a trail of fine silica-rich material on subfloors and finished surfaces that can scratch hardwood and tile if it isn't removed with the right technique before foot traffic begins.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Lyman

Every job starts with a walk-through to categorize the work: rough clean, final clean, or specialty clean. Most Lyman renovation projects need at least a final clean, and many need both phases.

**Rough clean** removes bulk debris — cut lumber scraps, packaging, excess caulk, and paint drips — and prepares the space for the detail work.

**Final clean** is systematic and room-by-room. Surfaces are cleaned top to bottom so that dislodged dust doesn't re-contaminate areas already finished. That means light fixtures and ceiling fans before countertops, countertops before floors. We clean inside cabinets and drawers, wipe down every window frame and sill, remove construction stickers from glass, and detail baseboards and door casings where joint compound tends to collect.

**Specialty cleaning** covers items outside a standard final clean: HVAC duct purging after dusty trades, pressure washing of exterior concrete and brick, cleaning of appliances left on-site during construction, and odor neutralization when solvents or adhesives have off-gassed into porous surfaces. For projects in homes built before 1978, our team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters when renovation work has disturbed painted surfaces — cleanup protocols are stricter and documentation is required.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

Industrial HEPA vacuums are the backbone of construction dust removal. Standard shop vacuums exhaust fine particulate back into the air; HEPA-filtered units capture particles down to 0.3 microns, which is where drywall dust and silica live. We run HEPA vacuums on every surface before any wet cleaning begins.

For adhesive and caulk residue, we use appropriate solvents matched to the substrate — what works on concrete doesn't belong on luxury vinyl plank. Window glass gets razor-blade scraping followed by a streak-free detail clean. Grout lines in tile work get a separate pass with a low-speed rotary tool and appropriate brush heads before sealing if that's in scope.

Where construction activity has introduced odors — spray foam, oil-based primers, epoxy coatings — we use hydroxyl generators rather than masking agents. Hydroxyls break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level, which matters in Lyman's climate where a sealed-up house can concentrate those smells quickly.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up repeatedly on Lyman jobs: HVAC systems that were left running during construction to control humidity end up packed with drywall dust in the return air filter and, more problematically, in the ductwork itself. Harrison County's humidity means contractors often can't shut the system off entirely without risking moisture problems in the new work — but running it pulls dust through. We flag this on every walk-through and offer duct cleaning as an add-on before the system is run in normal operation. It's a small step that prevents that new-construction smell from turning into a dusty, irritating air quality problem a week after move-in.
