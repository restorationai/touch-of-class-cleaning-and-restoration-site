## Why Kiln Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Hancock County's climate is the defining variable. Average annual humidity hovers well above 70%, and during summer months the dew point regularly climbs high enough that construction dust absorbs ambient moisture before a final clean even begins. That moisture-laden dust sticks to freshly painted walls, grout lines, and light fixtures differently than the dry particulate you'd find on a job site in a drier climate. Kiln also has a significant inventory of older wood-frame homes — many built in the 1960s and 1970s with tongue-and-groove subflooring — where construction vibration works fine debris into floor gaps that require vacuum extraction with HEPA filtration rather than sweeping, which simply redistributes the problem.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Kiln

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through to map the scope: which trades were on site, what materials were cut or sanded, whether spray texture or joint compound was applied, and whether the HVAC system was running during construction (a common source of duct contamination). From there the sequence is consistent:

**Phase 1 — Rough clean:** Removal of construction debris, scrap lumber, packaging, and bulk dust accumulation from floors, sills, and rough openings.

**Phase 2 — Detail clean:** HEPA-vacuum of all horizontal surfaces including the tops of door frames, light cans, and cabinet shelving. Window tracks, sliding door channels, and grout joints get hand-tool attention because power equipment misses them.

**Phase 3 — Final clean:** Streak-free glass cleaning, fixture polishing, floor mopping with appropriate chemistry for the finished surface (LVP, tile, hardwood, and concrete each require different approaches), and a final HVAC filter swap if the system ran during construction.

**Phase 4 — Specialty services as needed:** If the project involved fire damage repair, smoke odor from torch work, or any mold remediation prior to reconstruction, the IICRC-certified team applies the appropriate odor counteractant or antimicrobial treatment before sign-off.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

The equipment list for a post-construction clean in a high-humidity environment like Kiln's differs from what you'd bring to a climate-controlled metro project. HEPA-filtered commercial vacuums are non-negotiable — standard shop vacs recirculate fine particulate. Microfiber systems replace cotton mops for final floor passes because they trap rather than smear. On jobs where the HVAC ran during construction, a duct inspection determines whether the returns need cleaning before the system is handed back to the owner.
