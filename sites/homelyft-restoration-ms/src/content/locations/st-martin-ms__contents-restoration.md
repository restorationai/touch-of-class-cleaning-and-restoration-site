## Why St. Martin Properties See Elevated Contents Damage

Jackson County's subtropical climate creates conditions that are genuinely punishing for household contents after any water or fire event. Average relative humidity in the St. Martin area hovers above 70 percent for much of the year, which means wet belongings — soaked upholstery, waterlogged books, damp clothing — don't simply air-dry on their own. They colonize with mold within two to three days if left in place during structural repairs.

The housing stock in this part of the county adds another layer of complexity. Many St. Martin homes were built in the post-Camille and post-Katrina rebuild eras, meaning a mix of older pier-and-beam structures and newer slab-on-grade construction. Pier-and-beam homes in particular allow floodwater and moisture to wick upward through subfloor assemblies, saturating the lowest shelves, furniture legs, and stored items before a homeowner even realizes the extent of the intrusion. Coastal salt air also accelerates tarnishing and corrosion on metals, electronics, and artwork — damage that compounds quickly if contents aren't removed and stabilized promptly.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in St. Martin

Every contents job begins with a detailed room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves your home. Each piece is photographed, logged by category and condition, and assigned a barcode so you can track it through the entire process. This documentation also serves as the foundation for your insurance claim.

Once inventoried, items are packed out using materials rated for the specific hazard — fire-damaged contents require sealed containers to prevent cross-contamination of smoke odor; water-damaged textiles are bagged separately to avoid mold spread during transport. From St. Martin, contents travel to our climate-controlled facility in Gulfport, where temperature and humidity are actively managed — a critical step given that moving belongings from a humid coastal environment into an uncontrolled warehouse would simply continue the damage.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material and the damage type. Ultrasonic cleaning tanks handle hard goods, tools, and collectibles with precision that hand-wiping can't match. Ozone and hydroxyl generators address smoke and mold odor in soft goods and documents. Electronics are assessed by a certified technician before any cleaning attempt, since improper handling of salt-air-corroded circuit boards can cause permanent failure. Items that can be restored are returned to you at project completion; those that cannot are documented for your adjuster.
