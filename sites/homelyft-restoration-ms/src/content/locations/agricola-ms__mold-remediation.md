## Why Agricola Properties See Mold Issues

George County's combination of sandy loam soil and shallow water tables creates conditions where moisture migrates upward into pier-and-beam foundations and crawl spaces year-round. Many homes in the Agricola area were built in construction eras that predated modern vapor barrier requirements, leaving crawl spaces open to ground evaporation. When outside humidity climbs — and along the Mississippi Gulf Coast corridor it climbs often — that vapor condenses on floor joists, subflooring, and HVAC ductwork running beneath the house. Mold colonies can establish on wood surfaces in as little as 24 to 48 hours under those conditions.

Roof systems are another recurring entry point. The region sees frequent afternoon thunderstorms from late spring through early fall, and even minor storm damage — a lifted shingle, a compromised ridge cap — can allow water intrusion that saturates insulation and wall cavities without ever producing a visible stain on the ceiling below. By the time discoloration appears, the mold growth behind it may already be weeks old.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Agricola

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We identify not just where mold is visible but where moisture readings are elevated — because active moisture means active or imminent growth. Affected areas are then isolated with negative-air containment barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the work.

Physical remediation follows IICRC S520 protocols. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood — that cannot be brought to an acceptable contamination level are removed and bagged for proper disposal. Structural surfaces that can be retained are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, and dried to target moisture content. We run commercial-grade HEPA air scrubbers throughout the process to capture airborne spores. Because our team is also EPA Lead-Safe Certified, homes with older painted surfaces don't require a separate contractor for lead-safe work practices during material removal — a detail that matters in George County's older housing stock.

Once remediation is complete, we conduct a post-remediation verification using clearance moisture readings and visual inspection. For jobs where an independent industrial hygienist has conducted pre-remediation air sampling, we coordinate the final clearance test with that same third party before closing out the project.
