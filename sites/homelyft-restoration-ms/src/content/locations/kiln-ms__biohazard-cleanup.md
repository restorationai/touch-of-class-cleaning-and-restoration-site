Much of Kiln's housing stock consists of older wood-frame construction — pier-and-beam homes and crawl-space foundations that were common throughout Hancock County before modern slab building became standard. What looks contained often isn't. Remediation in these homes requires careful assessment beneath finished surfaces, not just treatment of what's visible.

The county's climate compounds this. Kiln averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and interior humidity rarely drops to the levels where biological breakdown slows meaningfully. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Kiln

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. Unmarked vehicles and plain-clothed technicians are standard practice — neighbors don't need to know what's happening inside. After removal, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, and the area is tested to confirm contamination levels are within safe thresholds before containment is lifted.
