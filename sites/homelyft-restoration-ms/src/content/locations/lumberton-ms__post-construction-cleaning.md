Homes built in Lumberton's older residential corridors — many dating to the mid-20th century — often have original hardwood subfloors and plaster-blend wall finishes that trap fine particulates differently than modern drywall. Construction work forces dust into ductwork, and in Lumberton's climate, that dust-laden air supply becomes a humidity delivery system that can accelerate surface soiling within days of occupancy. A thorough post-construction clean addresses the duct registers, the filter housing, and the return air pathways — not just the visible floors and countertops.
