## Why Gulf Hills Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

The Gulf Coast's subtropical humidity — routinely above 80% in summer — accelerates the breakdown of biological material and the growth of secondary contamination like mold and bacteria. In Gulf Hills, many homes feature crawl spaces and pier-and-beam foundations common to coastal Mississippi construction, which means fluids and infectious material can migrate below the visible floor surface before anyone realizes the extent of the affected area. That sub-floor zone is dark, warm, and poorly ventilated: conditions that compound the hazard significantly.

Gulf Hills is also part of a broader Jackson County coastal corridor where a meaningful portion of the housing stock dates to the 1970s and 1980s. Homes from that era often have original hardwood subfloors, older HVAC ductwork, and insulation types that absorb biological material rather than repel it. Our IICRC-certified team accounts for these structural variables before any remediation work begins — because the cleanup plan for a slab-foundation home looks very different from one with a vented crawl space beneath it.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Gulf Hills

Every job starts with a contained, private assessment. We arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested — neighbors do not need to know what is happening inside your home. After evaluating the full scope of the affected area, including any sub-surface or HVAC migration, we establish containment barriers and work in full personal protective equipment in compliance with OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards.

All biological material — including blood, bodily fluids, sharps, needles, and infectious waste — is collected, packaged, and transported as regulated medical waste through licensed disposal channels. Nothing is placed in standard trash. After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants and ATP-tested to verify that contamination levels meet public health standards before containment is lifted. Our EPA Lead-Safe certification also matters here: in older Gulf Hills homes where lead paint is a possibility, we follow lead-safe work practices throughout the remediation to avoid creating a secondary hazard.

We provide written documentation of every step — the scope, the methods, the disposal manifests — which your insurance carrier and, if needed, a future property buyer can review.
