## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Sharon and the surrounding Madison County area: older homes with pier-and-beam foundations often have crawl spaces that have never been properly encapsulated. When a kitchen or bathroom remodel opens up the subfloor, it's not unusual to find joists with long-term moisture exposure, occasional wood rot, and in some cases evidence of past pest activity. Addressing the crawl space as part of the renovation — vapor barrier, ventilation correction, joist sistering where needed — adds to the upfront cost but prevents the same problems from recurring under the new finish work. We flag this early so there are no surprises mid-project.
