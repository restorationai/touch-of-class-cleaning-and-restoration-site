## Why Biloxi Roofs Wear Differently

Biloxi's proximity to the Mississippi Sound means roofing materials face a steady cycle of salt air, UV exposure, and high humidity that accelerates granule loss on asphalt shingles and corrosion on exposed fasteners. Add in the wind events that move through the coast every hurricane season, and a roof that might last three decades inland can show seam separation, lifted shingles, or flashing failure well ahead of schedule here. Older Biloxi homes, many built before coastal wind codes were tightened after Katrina, often carry roofing systems that were never rated for the uplift pressures the current code requires, which is one reason a straightforward-looking repair sometimes turns into a full replacement once a contractor gets on the deck.
