## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Gautier

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. Containment barriers go up before any cleaning begins, preventing cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home or building.

Cleaning and disinfection follow a strict sequence: removal of affected porous materials that cannot be safely decontaminated, application of EPA-registered disinfectants at appropriate dwell times, and verification testing to confirm pathogen reduction.
