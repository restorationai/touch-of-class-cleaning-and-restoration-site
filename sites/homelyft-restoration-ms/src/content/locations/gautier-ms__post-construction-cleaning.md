## Why Gautier's Climate Makes Post-Construction Cleanup More Demanding

The Jackson County coastline averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and Gautier's position between the Pascagoula River and Back Bay means relative humidity routinely climbs above 80 percent — even in winter. When a renovation wraps in those conditions, construction dust doesn't behave the way it does in a drier climate. Fine particulate from cutting cement board, sanding drywall compound, or grinding tile grout becomes a paste-like film on glass, stainless fixtures, and painted trim. Left more than a week, that film etches into surfaces and requires abrasive intervention rather than simple wiping.

Gulf Coast construction also leans heavily on fiber cement siding, pressure-treated lumber, and spray foam insulation — all of which shed debris that standard janitorial crews aren't equipped to handle. Silica dust from fiber cement cutting is a genuine respiratory concern, and our OSHA-trained technicians treat it accordingly, using HEPA-filtered vacuums and negative air pressure containment before any surface cleaning begins.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Gautier

Every post-construction clean follows a top-to-bottom, inside-to-outside sequence calibrated to what Gulf Coast builds actually leave behind.

**Rough clean first.** We remove bulk debris — cut lumber, packaging foam, wire scraps, caulk tubes — and bag it for contractor disposal or haul it ourselves depending on the scope. HVAC vents are masked or cleaned at this stage to prevent recirculating dust once the system runs.

**Detail clean second.** Windows get a two-pass treatment: a razor scrape for paint overspray and adhesive labels, then a streak-free wash. Cabinets are wiped inside and out, drawer slides are cleared of sawdust, and hardware is polished. Tile grout haze — common after any ceramic or porcelain installation — is treated with appropriate pH-balanced cleaners rather than vinegar solutions that can dull grout sealers.

**Final clean and air quality pass.** We run HEPA air scrubbers through occupied areas to capture residual airborne particulate before the final surface wipe. This step matters especially in Gautier's humidity, where fine dust that settles on surfaces can re-suspend when the first occupant opens a window and lets in a Gulf breeze.

**Specialty services on request.** Pressure washing of driveways and exterior flatwork, concrete sealer removal, construction-grade window film removal, and post-renovation carpet cleaning are available as add-ons for full-scope projects.
