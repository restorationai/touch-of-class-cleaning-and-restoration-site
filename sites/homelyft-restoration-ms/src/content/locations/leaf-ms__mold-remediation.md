## Why Leaf Properties See Mold Problems

Jones County's climate creates near-ideal conditions for mold growth: warm temperatures, high relative humidity, and frequent heavy rain events that can overwhelm older drainage systems. Homes in and around Leaf often feature pier-and-beam or block foundations with ventilated crawl spaces — a construction style common across rural south Mississippi that exposes floor joists and subfloor sheathing directly to ground moisture. When vapor barriers deteriorate or were never installed, that moisture wicks upward. Combine that with the region's clay-heavy soil, which drains poorly and holds water against foundation walls long after a storm passes, and you have a recipe for persistent mold pressure from below.

Older housing stock in the area — much of it built before modern building codes required continuous vapor retarders or conditioned crawl spaces — is particularly vulnerable. HVAC systems that weren't sized correctly for the square footage also contribute: an oversized unit short-cycles, never running long enough to pull adequate humidity out of the air, and condensation accumulates on supply ducts and inside wall assemblies.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Leaf

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to locate hidden wet zones behind drywall, inside wall cavities, and under flooring — because in Leaf's housing stock, the visible mold patch on a baseboard is rarely the full picture.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish containment using 6-mil poly sheeting and negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sections — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Mississippi DEQ guidelines. Structural wood surfaces are then HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture thresholds before any reconstruction begins.

For crawl space remediation specifically, we encapsulate after remediation when the existing vapor barrier is compromised, and we address ventilation to reduce the humidity load that feeds future growth. We are EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which matters in older Leaf homes where disturbing building materials can expose lead paint — a step many contractors skip.
