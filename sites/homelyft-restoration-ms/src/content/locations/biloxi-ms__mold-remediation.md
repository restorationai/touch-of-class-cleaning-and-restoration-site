## Why Biloxi Properties See Mold Problems

The Gulf Coast climate doesn't give mold much of an off-season. Biloxi averages relative humidity above 70% for most of the year, and indoor moisture levels can spike fast after a plumbing failure, roof intrusion, or even a window left cracked during a summer storm. What makes Biloxi specifically challenging is the combination of that ambient humidity with the construction patterns that followed Hurricane Katrina. A significant portion of the city's residential stock was rebuilt quickly between 2006 and 2012, often with engineered wood framing and OSB sheathing — materials that absorb moisture readily and can harbor mold colonies within 24 to 48 hours of a water event if drying isn't started immediately.

Crawl-space construction, still common in older Biloxi neighborhoods closer to the water, creates a secondary risk: ground moisture wicks upward, and without adequate vapor barriers, the underside of subfloors becomes a reliable mold environment that homeowners rarely see until the damage is extensive. Pier-and-beam homes along the Back Bay corridor are particularly susceptible after heavy rain events push groundwater closer to the surface.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Biloxi

Every project starts with a visual inspection and moisture mapping — we use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find what the eye misses, including hidden saturation behind drywall or under flooring. Once the affected area is identified, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the removal process.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sections — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces are then HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Because Biloxi's outdoor air is rarely dry enough to assist passive drying, we rely on commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers to drive interior relative humidity down to levels that prevent recolonization before reconstruction begins. The project isn't closed out until clearance moisture readings confirm the structure is dry and stable.

For homes built before 1978 — a category that includes a meaningful portion of Biloxi's older residential areas — our EPA Lead-Safe certification governs how we handle disturbed building materials, which matters when mold remediation requires cutting into painted surfaces.
