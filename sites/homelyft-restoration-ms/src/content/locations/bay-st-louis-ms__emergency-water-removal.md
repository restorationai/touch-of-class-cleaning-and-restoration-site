## Why Bay St. Louis Properties See Water Damage Issues

A lot of the housing stock here falls into two categories: older raised or pier-and-beam homes near Old Town and the bayfront, built to let storm surge pass underneath, and newer slab-on-grade construction from the post-Katrina rebuild years. Both have their own water problems. Pier-and-beam crawlspaces trap humidity and standing water after heavy rain, and that moisture works its way up into subfloors before anyone notices a smell. Slab homes, on the other hand, hide plumbing leaks under the concrete itself, so by the time carpet or tile grout shows staining, water has often been sitting for days. Add in Gulf Coast humidity most of the year and afternoon thunderstorms that can dump an inch of rain in under an hour, and you get a region where water intrusion rarely stays contained to one room.

## Our Water Extraction Process in Bay St. Louis

We start by identifying the water category and shutting off or capping the source, then move straight into extraction with truck-mounted and portable pumps depending on how much standing water we're dealing with and whether the affected area is a crawlspace, slab, or finished living space. Because so many homes here sit on sandy or filled coastal soil, we pay close attention to what's happening below the visible surface: moisture meters and infrared imaging tell us whether water has tracked into wall cavities or under cabinetry before we commit to a drying plan. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the humidity load, which in Hancock County during summer months runs noticeably higher than drying calculations built for drier climates.

## Local note

One thing that trips up a lot of out-of-town restoration crews: homes near the Bay of St. Louis often have crawlspace vents that were sealed or modified after Katrina-era rebuilds to meet updated flood venting requirements, which changes how air moves (or doesn't move) under the house. Before we set up drying equipment in a crawlspace, we check whether those vents are functioning the way they were designed to, because a sealed or blocked vent can trap moisture indefinitely even after the standing water is gone.
