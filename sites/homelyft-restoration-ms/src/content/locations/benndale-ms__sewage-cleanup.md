## Why Benndale Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Benndale sits in a part of south Mississippi where the soil composition creates a particular challenge for both aging septic systems and older sewer laterals. Clay-dominant ground doesn't absorb excess water quickly, so during extended rain events — common from late spring through early fall — saturated soil puts hydrostatic pressure on underground pipes. That pressure can force sewage backward through the lowest drain in a home, typically a floor drain, toilet, or washing machine connection in the lowest level of the structure.

Many homes in the Benndale area were built in eras when cast-iron or Orangeburg pipe was standard. Orangeburg — a compressed wood-fiber pipe used widely from the 1940s through the 1970s — is notorious for deforming under soil pressure and eventually collapsing entirely. A collapsed lateral means sewage has nowhere to go except back into the home. Identifying pipe material is one of the first things our team considers when assessing a backup, because it affects both the remediation scope and the likelihood of recurrence.

Septic system overflows are also a distinct concern in the more rural pockets of the area. When a drain field becomes saturated or a tank reaches capacity without a recent pump-out, the overflow doesn't stay contained to the yard — it can migrate toward the foundation and enter the home through cracks in the slab or crawl space vents.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Benndale

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification under the IICRC S500 standard — which means the removal and sanitization protocol is more involved than a standard water damage job. Our process follows these steps:

**Containment first.** Before extraction begins, we isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. HVAC vents in the zone are sealed, and negative air pressure may be established depending on the extent of the backup.

**Extraction and solid waste removal.** Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing sewage from floors and cavities. Any solid waste is bagged and disposed of in accordance with Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines.

**Controlled demolition of contaminated materials.** Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and subflooring — that absorbed Category 3 water cannot be dried and saved. They are removed, bagged, and documented for your insurance claim. In Benndale homes with pier-and-beam construction, we pay close attention to floor joists and the underside of the subfloor, where sewage can wick upward and remain hidden.

**Hospital-grade disinfection.** After extraction and demo, all affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. We apply, dwell, and re-apply — a single pass is not sufficient for Category 3 events.

**Structural drying.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to bring structural moisture readings back to pre-loss levels. Daily moisture mapping tracks progress and provides documentation for your adjuster.
