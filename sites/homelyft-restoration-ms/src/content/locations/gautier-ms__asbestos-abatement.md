Jackson County's residential building boom ran hard through the 1950s, '60s, and into the mid-1970s — right through the era when asbestos was a standard additive in floor tile mastics, pipe insulation, roofing felt, textured ceiling coatings, and joint compound. ## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Gautier

Every project starts with asbestos testing — bulk sampling of suspect materials collected by a trained technician, sent to an accredited third-party laboratory. We don't skip this step even when a material "looks like" asbestos-containing tile or insulation, because misidentification in either direction creates problems: unnecessary abatement costs money, and missed identification creates liability.

Once results confirm the presence of regulated materials, we establish a containment zone using 6-mil polyethylene sheeting and negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. Work areas are sealed from the rest of the occupied structure. Our technicians wear full personal protective equipment and follow Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) notification and disposal requirements, which include advance notice for projects above the regulated threshold and proper packaging for transport to a licensed disposal facility.

After removal, the containment area is wet-wiped and HEPA-vacuumed before clearance air sampling is conducted. We coordinate with an independent industrial hygienist for that final clearance test — a step that protects you and satisfies most lender and insurance documentation requirements if you're selling or refinancing.
