## Why Soso-Area Properties Need a Thorough Post-Construction Clean

Jones County's climate sits in a humidity band that rarely drops below 60% relative humidity, even in winter. That means fine construction dust — the kind that settles into HVAC returns, coats window tracks, and films over every horizontal surface — picks up moisture and becomes tacky rather than simply blowing away. Drywall compound dust, in particular, can harden into a thin crust on flooring and countertops if it isn't removed within the first few days after a project closes out.

The local building stock adds another layer. Much of the residential construction in the Soso area includes pier-and-beam foundations, which are common across rural Jones County. Renovation work on these homes often disturbs crawl space debris, and that material migrates upward through gaps around plumbing penetrations and subfloor seams. A proper final clean addresses not just the visible surfaces but the particulate that has settled into those transitional zones between old structure and new finish work.

Commercial builds in the county — metal buildings and pre-engineered structures that house light manufacturing and agricultural supply operations — present their own challenge: concrete dust from saw-cut slabs and grinding operations is alkaline and abrasive, and it works into expansion joints and floor coatings in ways that standard janitorial sweeping won't resolve.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Soso

We approach every post-construction clean in three distinct passes, because a single-pass wipe-down misses the particulate that resettles after the first disturbance.

**Rough clean:** We remove all construction debris — tape scraps, cut material ends, packaging, overspray — and HEPA-vacuum every surface before any wet cleaning begins. Skipping this step drags abrasive grit across new flooring and finishes.

**Detail clean:** Every window frame, sill, and track is wiped by hand. Cabinet interiors, drawer boxes, and door hardware are cleaned individually. Grout lines are scrubbed before sealer is applied by others. Light fixtures and electrical covers are wiped free of drywall dust before power is restored.

**Final clean:** This is the inspection-ready pass. Floors are cleaned with equipment and chemistry appropriate to the finish — no alkaline cleaners on new hardwood, no abrasive pads on polished concrete. Glass is cleaned streak-free. HVAC registers are vacuumed and wiped so the first time the system runs, it isn't circulating construction particulate through the new ductwork.

Because our team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified and IICRC certified, we can handle post-construction cleans on older Jones County homes where renovation work may have disturbed lead-based paint — a real consideration in pre-1978 housing stock that still makes up a meaningful portion of the rural residential inventory in this part of Mississippi.
