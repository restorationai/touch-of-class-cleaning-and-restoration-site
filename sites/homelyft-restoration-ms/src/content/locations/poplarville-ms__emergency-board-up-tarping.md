## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Poplarville

When we arrive on site, the first step is a rapid perimeter assessment — documenting every opening, evaluating roof slope and decking condition, and identifying any structural instability that would affect safe access. Photographs are taken before any material goes up, which matters when you're working with an insurance adjuster later.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure, secured with 2×4 battens screwed into sound decking rather than stapled or weighted at the edges. On older Poplarville homes with narrow rafter spacing or deteriorated decking, we probe for solid substrate before committing a fastener — a tarp that pulls loose in the next storm does more harm than good. We overlap tarp seams by at least 18 inches and run the material past the ridge when possible to prevent wind-driven water from finding the edge.

For fire damage board-up and broken window board-up, we cut plywood to fit each opening, use exterior-grade screws into framing members (not just siding), and install door barricades where entry points are compromised. Every opening gets labeled in our documentation so the adjuster and any subsequent contractor know exactly what was secured and when.
