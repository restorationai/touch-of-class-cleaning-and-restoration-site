## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Ovett

A proper post-construction clean happens in phases, not one sweeping pass. Here's how we approach it on Ovett job sites:

**Phase 1 — Rough Clean.** We remove bulk debris: drywall scraps, cut lumber, packaging materials, and adhesive backing. This phase often reveals damage that the construction crew missed — a gouged subfloor, a cracked tile, a window seal broken by a dropped tool. We document and photograph everything.

**Phase 2 — Detail Clean.** This is where specialty cleaning separates itself from a standard janitorial visit. We clean inside cabinets and drawers, wipe down every door frame and baseboard, purge HVAC registers of construction dust, clean light fixture interiors, and remove paint overspray from glass and hardware using appropriate solvents. Grout lines get scrubbed before sealer is applied by the tile contractor.

**Phase 3 — Final Polish and Air Quality.** We run HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to pull fine particulate out of the air column — the dust you can't see but will breathe for months if it's left behind. Final surfaces are wiped with microfiber to a streak-free finish. For commercial renovations, we can provide a written cleaning log for occupancy inspections.
