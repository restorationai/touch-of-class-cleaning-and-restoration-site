## Why Hurley Properties Face Distinct Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Jackson County's climate creates a specific set of problems that contractors from drier regions often underestimate. Relative humidity in this part of Mississippi regularly climbs above 80 percent during spring and summer, which means construction dust doesn't simply settle — it bonds to surfaces as humidity fluctuates. Silica-heavy concrete dust from slab work, adhesive overspray from flooring installation, and caulk residue all behave differently here than they would in a climate-controlled environment.

Hurley's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. The area includes a mix of older rural homes that have been expanded or renovated over decades and newer construction on larger lots. Older structures frequently have crawl spaces and pier-and-beam foundations where construction debris — insulation scraps, broken fasteners, sawdust — collects and can trap moisture. Cleaning those spaces thoroughly matters not just for aesthetics but for long-term air quality, since damp organic debris in a crawl space is exactly the substrate mold needs to establish itself within 48 to 72 hours.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Hurley

Every post-construction clean begins with a structured walk-through to categorize debris type, surface materials, and any areas where construction activity may have affected HVAC ducting or returns. Dust that enters ductwork during a renovation circulates through the finished space every time the system runs — so we assess whether duct cleaning or at minimum filter replacement is warranted before we begin surface work.

The cleaning sequence moves rough to fine: construction debris removal and rough sweep first, then HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all horizontal and vertical surfaces, followed by detail cleaning of windows, fixtures, cabinetry interiors, and hardware. On tile and hard flooring, we use pH-appropriate cleaners calibrated to the specific material — grout haze on new ceramic tile requires a different chemistry than adhesive residue on luxury vinyl plank. Final inspection under raking light catches the silica haze and fingerprint smudges that standard lighting misses.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans on kitchens or bathrooms, pressure washing of exterior flatwork, or cleaning after a specific trade like spray foam insulation — we scope each task individually rather than applying a flat package. Spray foam overspray, for example, requires mechanical removal before any chemical treatment; skipping that step just spreads the residue.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

HEPA filtration is non-negotiable on every interior post-construction clean. Standard shop vacuums recirculate fine particulate — silica dust, fiberglass strands, drywall compound particles — back into the air. Our HEPA-filtered equipment captures particles down to 0.3 microns, which matters especially in Hurley homes where windows may stay closed during humid stretches and ventilation is limited.

For exterior flatwork and driveways, hot-water pressure washing removes concrete splatter and paint overspray without damaging new surface finishes when used at the correct pressure and standoff distance. On interior hard surfaces, microfiber flat-mop systems pick up fine dust without redistributing it the way traditional string mops do. Where a renovation involved lead-containing paint in an older structure, our EPA Lead-Safe certified approach governs containment and cleanup — a credential that matters in Jackson County's older rural housing stock where pre-1978 paint is common.
