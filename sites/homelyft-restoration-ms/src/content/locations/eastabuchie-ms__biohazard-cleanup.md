## Why Eastabuchie Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Jones County's housing stock skews older, and many homes in and around Eastabuchie were built in the mid-twentieth century with materials that complicate remediation. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Eastabuchie

Every job begins with a discreet arrival — unmarked vehicles are available on request — and a thorough assessment of the affected area before any work begins.
