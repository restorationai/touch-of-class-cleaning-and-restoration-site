## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Pearlington

Every job starts with a rapid exterior assessment: we photograph and document every breach point before touching anything, because that documentation becomes part of your insurance claim file. For window and door openings, we cut OSB panels to fit the rough opening, fasten them with structural screws into the framing (not just the trim), and seal the perimeter with foam backer rod and weatherproof tape to block wind-driven rain. For fire-damaged structures where wall framing may be compromised, we check load-bearing integrity before committing weight to any panel attachment point.

Roof tarping in this region requires heavier material than the blue poly tarps sold at hardware stores. We use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure, secured with screw-down battens or sandbag ballast systems depending on the roof pitch and surface condition. On steep-pitch metal roofs — common in coastal Mississippi because of their wind and hail resistance — we use non-penetrating anchor systems that hold the tarp without creating new leak points. On low-slope or flat sections, we lap the tarp at least four feet past the damaged area on all sides and seal the leading edges.
