## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in D'Iberville

When a call comes in from a D'Iberville address, the first step is a rapid site assessment to identify every breach point — not just the obvious one. We document all openings with photographs before any material goes up, which serves both the structural protection plan and your insurance claim.

For window and door board-up, we use 5/8-inch plywood secured with structural screws into framing — not just into trim or siding — so the panels hold against wind pressure rather than simply covering the opening.
