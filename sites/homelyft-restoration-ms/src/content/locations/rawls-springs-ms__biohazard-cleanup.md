## Why Rawls Springs Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Forrest County's climate is the defining factor. Average summer humidity in this part of Mississippi routinely exceeds 80%, and older homes in the Rawls Springs area — many built with pier-and-beam foundations and original hardwood subfloors — absorb biological contamination differently than newer slab-on-grade construction. Hardwood and older vinyl composite flooring can wick fluids beneath the surface layer, meaning what appears to be a contained area on inspection is often larger once materials are lifted. Crawl spaces under pier-and-beam homes can also trap odor and harbor secondary contamination that isn't visible from the living area.

Beyond the housing stock, properties in this part of Forrest County are often on private well and septic systems rather than municipal utilities. That affects how wastewater from the remediation process is handled — our team accounts for those site-specific conditions during the initial assessment rather than discovering them mid-job.

## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Rawls Springs

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. We use unmarked vehicles when requested, and our crew does not discuss the nature of the work with neighbors or bystanders. The process follows a structured sequence:

**Assessment and containment** — we identify the full extent of affected surfaces, establish containment zones using negative air pressure when the structure permits, and document everything for your insurance carrier before a single item is removed.

**Removal of affected materials** — biohazardous waste is packaged and transported in compliance with Mississippi State Department of Health regulations and applicable OSHA standards. Our team is OSHA trained and operates as an IICRC Certified Firm, which means the handling protocols meet nationally recognized industry standards.

**Surface treatment and deodorization** — hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all confirmed contact surfaces. In older Rawls Springs homes with exposed wood framing or original plaster, we test surface porosity before selecting the appropriate treatment method, because a product that works on sealed drywall may not achieve adequate penetration on unfinished wood.

**Clearance verification** — we do not close a job based on visual inspection alone. ATP testing or third-party verification is available and, in some cases, recommended before a space is reoccupied.
