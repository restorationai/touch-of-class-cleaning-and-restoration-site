## Why Poplarville Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Pearl River County's housing stock skews older in many pockets of Poplarville, and that matters the moment you open a wall. Homes built before 1978 frequently contain lead-based paint in trim, window casings, and exterior siding — our team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, so we follow the required containment and disposal protocols without pushing that burden onto the homeowner. Older framing in this area also tends to be true-dimension lumber, which is thicker than modern dimensional lumber and behaves differently when you're tying in new framing or matching existing profiles.
