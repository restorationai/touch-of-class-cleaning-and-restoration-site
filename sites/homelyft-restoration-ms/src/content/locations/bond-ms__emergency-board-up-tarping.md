## Why Bond Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Much of the residential housing stock in and around Bond reflects construction patterns common to rural Lamar County — wood-frame homes, often with pier-and-beam foundations, that were built to a different standard than today's wind-rated construction. Older rooflines with minimal overhangs and aging shingle systems are more vulnerable to uplift during high-wind events, which means partial roof losses are common here after strong storms. When a section of decking is exposed, even a brief rain event can push water into attic insulation and down into ceiling assemblies before a homeowner realizes how far the damage has traveled.

Fire damage board-up is equally time-sensitive in this area. A structure fire that burns through a wall or collapses a window frame leaves the interior open to weather, wildlife, and unauthorized entry. Mississippi's humidity means that smoke-saturated materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing — begin absorbing ambient moisture almost immediately after the fire is out, which compounds restoration costs if the opening isn't sealed quickly.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Bond

When HomeLyft Restoration MS responds to a call in Bond, the first priority on arrival is a rapid exterior assessment: we identify every compromised opening, map the affected roof area, and photograph everything before touching a single surface. That documentation matters for your insurance claim, and it shapes the sequence of work.

For broken window board-up and door openings, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to the rough opening, secured with structural screws into the surrounding framing — not just surface-nailed into trim. For roof tarping, we use 6-mil or heavier poly tarps with weighted or screwed-down perimeter boards so the tarp stays put through subsequent wind events rather than peeling back the first time the weather returns. On steep or complex rooflines, we use fall-protection equipment to work safely without causing additional damage to undamaged sections of the roof.

If the structure has already been through a fire, we coordinate with the fire marshal's office or the responding department to confirm the scene is cleared before any crew goes onto the roof or into the structure. That step protects our team and keeps the chain of evidence intact for your insurance adjuster.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically in rural Lamar County properties: older homes in this area were frequently built with wide roof overhangs and exposed rafter tails — a regional style that looks good but creates a specific vulnerability during high-wind events.
