## Why Lucedale Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Lucedale sits in a region where the combination of heavy seasonal rainfall, shallow water tables, and a significant share of older housing stock creates recurring sewage problems that aren't common in higher-elevation parts of the state. Many homes in and around the city were built in the 1960s through 1980s on clay-heavy lots where septic drain fields struggle to percolate after prolonged wet periods. When the soil is already saturated — a pattern that repeats every spring and after Gulf-driven storm systems — septic tanks can back up into the lowest fixtures in the house before a homeowner realizes anything is wrong.

Municipal sewer connections in older sections of Lucedale also contend with root intrusion and pipe joint deterioration in lines that have been in the ground for decades. A slow-developing blockage can become a full overflow event quickly, especially when a heavy-use weekend coincides with a rain-swollen water table. The result is the same regardless of cause: standing sewage in living spaces that requires professional extraction, not a mop and a bottle of bleach.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lucedale

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification under IICRC S500 standards — which means the process is more involved than a standard water loss. Our IICRC-certified team begins with containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. We then extract all standing liquid using truck-mounted extraction units, followed by the removal of any porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that cannot be safely restored once saturated with sewage.

After physical removal, every hard surface in the affected zone is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 losses. This isn't a single-pass wipe-down; surfaces are treated, allowed to dwell, and treated again. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the ambient conditions — in Lucedale's humid subtropical climate, ambient relative humidity is often already high, which means drying equipment has to work harder and run longer than it would in an arid region. Our IICRC ASD-certified technicians monitor moisture readings daily until the structure reaches documented dry standard before any reconstruction begins.
