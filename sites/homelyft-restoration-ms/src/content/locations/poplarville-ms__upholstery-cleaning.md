## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Poplarville

We start with a fabric and fiber assessment, because a hot-water extraction method that's safe on a cotton-blend sofa can water-stain silk or certain leather finishes. Leather pieces get a pH-balanced cleaning and conditioning pass instead of steam, protecting the finish while still lifting body oils and surface grime.
