When a burst pipe, kitchen fire, or roof failure sends water or smoke through a Petal home, the contents inside — the heirlooms, the electronics, the everyday items that make a house feel lived-in — need specialized attention that goes far beyond wiping surfaces down and hoping for the best.

## Why Petal Properties Face Elevated Contents Damage Risks

The Petal area's housing stock skews toward ranch-style and split-level homes built between the 1960s and 1990s, many of which feature open floor plans that allow smoke and soot to travel quickly from room to room. Unlike tightly compartmentalized older construction, that open layout means a contained kitchen fire can coat furniture and textiles throughout the entire living area within minutes. Forrest County's clay-heavy soil also contributes: during heavy rain events, poor drainage around slab foundations allows water to intrude at floor level, saturating carpets, padding, and the lower sections of wood furniture before homeowners realize the extent of the problem. The combination of high ambient humidity and slow-draining soil creates conditions where mold colonization on porous contents can begin in as little as 24 to 48 hours — a narrow window that makes prompt pack-out critical.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Petal

The first step on any Petal job is a room-by-room contents inventory conducted before a single item leaves the home. Every piece — from living room sectionals to kitchen appliances to children's toys — is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code. That documentation becomes the foundation of your insurance claim and ensures nothing gets lost in the shuffle.

Items that can be safely restored are transported to a climate-controlled facility where they undergo the appropriate cleaning method for their material type. Textiles and upholstery go through thermal fogging or ozone treatment to neutralize smoke odor at the molecular level, not just mask it. Hard surfaces — wood furniture, cabinetry, decorative items — are cleaned using ultrasonic technology or hand-cleaning with pH-balanced solutions depending on finish sensitivity. Electronics are assessed by a technician before any cleaning begins, because improper handling of circuit boards after smoke or water exposure can cause permanent damage.

Items that cannot be restored immediately, or that need to stay out of the home during reconstruction, move into secure, monitored storage. Petal homeowners dealing with extended repairs — roof replacement after a storm, full bathroom reconstruction after a pipe failure — often need storage that stretches weeks or months, and the facility stays climate-controlled throughout to prevent secondary damage from humidity.
