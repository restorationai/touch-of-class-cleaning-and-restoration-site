## Why Diamondhead Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Average annual humidity in the Diamondhead area hovers well above 70 percent for much of the year, which means moisture infiltration isn't a hypothetical — it's a baseline condition. Older homes in the community frequently show signs of wood rot at sill plates, subfloor deterioration near bathrooms and laundry rooms, and HVAC ductwork that has never been properly sealed against humid attic air. Any honest renovation scope in this area has to account for what's behind the walls before a single tile goes down or a cabinet goes up.

The planned-development origins of Diamondhead also mean that deed restrictions and community covenants still govern a meaningful portion of the housing stock.
