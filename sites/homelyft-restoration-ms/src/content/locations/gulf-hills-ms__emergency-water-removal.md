## Why Gulf Hills Properties See Water Removal Issues

Coastal Mississippi humidity rarely drops below the point where materials can air-dry on their own, which means a supply line failure, water heater leak, or storm-driven intrusion in Gulf Hills tends to linger instead of evaporating out. Homes built on slab foundations trap water against the concrete, where it wicks into baseboards and drywall for days if it isn't pulled out mechanically. Crawlspace homes closer to the bayou-fed drainage common to this part of the Gulf Coast face a different problem: standing water under the floor joists that you can't see or smell until the subfloor starts to soften.
