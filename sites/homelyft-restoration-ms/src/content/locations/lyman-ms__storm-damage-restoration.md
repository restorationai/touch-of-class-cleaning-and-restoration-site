## Why Lyman Properties Are Vulnerable to Storm Damage

Lyman's position in southern Harrison County places it in a high-wind-risk corridor that insurance adjusters know well. Homes here face a combination of threats that don't always show up together in other parts of Mississippi. Gulf-origin tropical systems — including the remnants of named storms — push sustained winds and rain horizontally, forcing water into soffit vents, around window frames, and under roof decking in ways that a straight-down rainstorm never would.

The local housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Older homes may have original roof sheathing that's already borderline; one severe storm can push it past the point of no return. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Lyman

Every job starts with a systematic damage assessment — not a quick walk-around, but a documented inspection that covers the roof deck, attic cavity, exterior walls, windows, and any below-grade spaces. Once the scope is established, the work moves in a logical sequence:
