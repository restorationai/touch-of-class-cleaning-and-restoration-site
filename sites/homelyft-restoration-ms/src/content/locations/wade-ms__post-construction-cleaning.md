## Why Wade Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Forrest County's climate is unforgiving to freshly built or renovated spaces. Average relative humidity sits above 70% for much of the year, which means construction dust doesn't simply dry out and vacuum away — it bonds to surfaces. Silica-laden drywall dust mixed with humidity can create a film on windows, light fixtures, and cabinetry that requires more than a standard wipe-down. On older homes in the Wade area that have been through partial renovations, there's an additional layer of complexity: lead-based paint disturbance during demo work can leave micro-particulate contamination that standard cleaning crews aren't equipped to handle. HomeLyft Restoration MS holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, which matters when the renovation touched pre-1978 painted surfaces.

The region's soil composition also plays a role. The reddish-brown silty clay common to this part of Mississippi is notoriously difficult to remove from grout lines, concrete subfloors, and textured surfaces. A builder's broom isn't enough — it takes HEPA-filtered vacuuming, controlled wet mopping, and surface-specific cleaning agents to get new floors and counters to the standard a homeowner or property manager expects at handoff.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Wade

Every post-construction or specialty clean follows a structured, phase-based approach rather than a single sweep through the space.

**Phase 1 — Rough Clean:** We remove large debris, scrap materials, and packaging. HEPA vacuums run on all horizontal surfaces, window sills, and inside cabinet boxes before any wet work begins. This prevents debris from becoming slurry.

**Phase 2 — Detail Clean:** Windows are cleaned inside and out, including frames and tracks. Light fixtures are wiped down and bulbs cleaned. Cabinetry interiors, drawer slides, and door hardware get individual attention. Grout lines and tile surfaces are scrubbed with appropriate pH-balanced cleaners — not general-purpose sprays that can dull new finishes.

**Phase 3 — Final Clean and Inspection:** Hard floors are mopped with surface-specific solutions. Carpet areas are HEPA-vacuumed in multiple directions. A final walkthrough is conducted room by room against a checklist, and any touch-up items are addressed before the space is signed off. For specialty cleans — post-fire odor cleaning, post-flood renovation cleaning, or deep sanitation after a commercial buildout — additional steps are layered in based on what the space requires.
