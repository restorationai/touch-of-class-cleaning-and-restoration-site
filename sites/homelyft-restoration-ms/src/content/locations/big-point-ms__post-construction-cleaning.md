## Why Big Point Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Jackson County's proximity to the Mississippi Sound means relative humidity routinely climbs above 80 percent, even in months that feel mild. After a renovation, that moisture accelerates the way fine construction dust — joint compound, tile grout haze, plaster dust — cures onto surfaces. Left 48 hours, a light film of drywall dust on hardwood flooring can become a micro-abrasive layer that scratches the finish the moment foot traffic begins.

The local building stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in the Big Point area were built in the post-Katrina reconstruction wave of 2006–2012, when contractors were working fast and material availability was inconsistent. That era produced a mix of engineered lumber, fiber-cement siding, and spray-foam insulation in the same structure — each surface type responds differently to cleaning chemistry. Older structures that predate the storm can have original wood-plank subfloors that swell when over-wetted during cleanup, so moisture control during the cleaning process is as important here as it is during water damage restoration.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Big Point

A final clean after construction isn't a single pass — it's a sequenced workflow, and the sequence matters.

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work, we remove bulk debris: cut lumber scraps, packaging, drywall offcuts, spent caulk tubes. We coordinate with your GC on dumpster access and staging so we're not creating a secondary mess in a driveway or on a lawn.

**HEPA vacuuming before any wet work.** Every horizontal and vertical surface — window tracks, door frames, light fixture boxes, cabinet interiors — gets a HEPA-filtered vacuum pass before we introduce any liquid. This is the step most one-person cleaning crews skip, and it's the reason grout lines and caulk seams still look dusty a week after a "final clean."

**Surface-specific chemical selection.** Tile haze on ceramic or porcelain requires a mild acid wash; the same product on natural stone causes etching. Painted trim needs a pH-neutral cleaner; aluminum window frames need something different still. We identify every surface material before we open a bottle.

**Detail and glass work.** Construction adhesive overspray on glass, paint specks on hardware, grout smears on fixtures — these come off with razor scrapers and appropriate solvents, not pressure. We protect surrounding surfaces while we work.

**Final walk-through documentation.** We photograph the completed space room by room. If a contractor or property owner spots something missed, we come back — that's part of the job, not an extra charge.

## Equipment and Methods for Coastal-Climate Final Cleans

Because Big Point's humidity makes airborne construction dust behave differently than it does in drier climates, we run commercial air scrubbers with HEPA filtration throughout the cleaning process — not just during demolition phases. This pulls suspended particulates out of the air column while we work, so dust we dislodge from one surface doesn't simply resettle on a surface we already cleaned.

For specialty cleaning scopes — post-fire odor removal from a renovation that involved smoke-damaged materials, or biohazard-adjacent cleanup after a contractor found unexpected mold behind a wall — we carry the equipment and hold the IICRC certifications (including AMRT for mold and FSRT for fire and smoke) to handle those situations without subcontracting. For renovation projects where lead paint is a possibility — common in structures built before 1978, which exist throughout Jackson County — our EPA Lead-Safe Certified status means we can handle post-renovation cleaning under RRP protocols without requiring the GC to bring in a separate firm.

One thing contractors and property managers in Big Point learn quickly: schedule the final clean for morning, not afternoon.
