## Why Bond Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Construction in this part of Mississippi happens against a backdrop of high relative humidity for much of the year. That matters for post-construction cleanup in ways that aren't obvious until you're on-site: joint compound dust absorbs ambient moisture and re-adheres to surfaces, making dry-sweep methods ineffective. Silica-bearing construction dust that settles into HVAC returns can carry that grit through a system the moment a homeowner flips the thermostat — and in Bond's warmer months, that thermostat gets flipped early.

Older homes in the Bond area — many built with the kind of wood-framed construction common to rural Lawrence County — also tend to have crawl spaces and subfloor gaps that collect construction debris in ways that slab-on-grade builds don't. Sawdust, insulation scraps, and fasteners can accumulate below the finished floor and become a moisture trap or a pest harborage point if not addressed during the final clean. A surface-only wipe-down misses this entirely.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Bond

Every post-construction clean begins with a structured walk-through of the site before a single cloth is lifted. The goal is to sequence the work correctly — rough debris removal before any detail cleaning, HVAC protection before dust is disturbed, and window cleaning last so it isn't re-contaminated by overhead work.

The process typically follows this order:

- **Construction debris removal** — bulk scraps, packaging, fasteners, and leftover materials are cleared and staged for disposal.
- **HVAC return protection and duct inspection** — registers are checked and cleaned or masked depending on the system's condition, preventing construction particulate from circulating once the system is activated.
- **Surface decontamination** — walls, ceilings, trim, and cabinetry are cleaned using appropriate methods for the finish type. Flat paint requires different handling than semi-gloss; new tile grout requires a different approach than sealed hardwood.
- **Detail and fixture cleaning** — light fixtures, outlets, switch plates, plumbing fixtures, and hardware are wiped free of compound dust, adhesive residue, and fingerprints from the installation crews.
- **Floor care** — construction grit is highly abrasive to new flooring finishes. We use the correct sequence of vacuuming and damp-mopping to avoid scratching surfaces during the final clean.
- **Window and glass cleaning** — paint overspray, caulk haze, and label adhesive are removed without damaging seals or frames.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation odor neutralization, pressure washing of exterior flatwork, or deep cleaning of a space that has been closed up during construction — those are scoped and priced during the initial walk-through.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

Post-construction cleaning requires more than mops and microfiber. The team uses HEPA-filtered vacuums rated for fine construction particulate — standard shop vacs recirculate a significant portion of what they pick up, which defeats the purpose of a final clean. For adhesive residue and paint haze on glass and hard surfaces, appropriate solvents are selected based on the substrate so finishes aren't dulled or damaged. Where new flooring is involved, floor pads and cleaning solutions are matched to the manufacturer's recommendations to avoid voiding warranties on materials that haven't seen a single day of normal use yet.
