## Why Saucier Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

Saucier sits in a part of Harrison County where the housing stock skews older and the lots are heavily wooded. That combination creates two compounding problems after a fire. First, mature pine and hardwood surrounding a home act as a heat sink during the event itself — radiant heat from burning trees can extend fire damage to exterior siding and eaves well beyond the room of origin. Second, older construction in this area frequently used wood-panel interior walls and tongue-and-groove sheathing that absorb smoke odor at a molecular level. Standard surface cleaning won't reach it.

The Gulf Coast's humidity is a year-round factor here, too. Saucier averages significant moisture in the air even in cooler months, and that ambient humidity accelerates the way soot and smoke residue bond to porous materials. Wet smoke — the dense, sticky residue produced when a fire smolders rather than burns hot — is particularly common in homes where a fire was partially suppressed before full ignition, and it's the hardest type to remediate. Our IICRC FSRT-certified technicians identify residue type on the first walk-through, because the cleaning chemistry and dwell times differ substantially between dry soot and wet smoke.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Saucier

Every job starts with a structured assessment — not a sales walk, but a technical evaluation. We photograph and document every affected surface, cavity, and system for your insurance file. From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

**Stabilization and board-up.** If the fire compromised windows, doors, or the roof line, we secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry before any cleaning begins. In Saucier's rural setting, an unsecured structure on a wooded lot is also a wildlife-entry risk.

**Controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials.** Char-damaged drywall, insulation, and flooring come out. We separate materials that can be cleaned and restored from those that cannot — a distinction that matters both for cost and for odor elimination. Leaving compromised material in place is one of the most common reasons smoke odor returns months later.

**Structural cleaning and deodorization.** We use hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging alongside EPA-registered cleaning agents to neutralize odor at the source rather than masking it. Wood framing that tested positive for smoke penetration is sealed with an encapsulant rated for fire restoration.

**Reconstruction coordination.** HomeLyft holds Mississippi RO license #RO 2728, which means we can carry a fire job from initial cleanup through finished reconstruction — drywall, paint, flooring, cabinetry — without handing the project to a second contractor.
