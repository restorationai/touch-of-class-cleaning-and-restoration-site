## Why Kiln Properties See Asbestos Risks

Hancock County's building boom ran from the post-WWII era through the mid-1970s, and a significant share of the housing stock in and around Kiln reflects that timeline. Ranch-style homes and pier-and-beam construction from that period routinely used chrysotile asbestos in vinyl floor tiles, duct wrap, attic insulation, and the spray-applied "popcorn" texture on ceilings. The Gulf Coast's subtropical climate is hard on these materials: repeated expansion and contraction from summer heat, combined with the moisture intrusion that follows even minor storm events, causes bonded asbestos products to friable — meaning they crumble and release fibers into living spaces.

Hurricane damage compounds the picture. Properties that sustained roof or wall damage during past storm seasons may have had repairs layered over original asbestos-containing materials rather than removing them. That kind of patchwork construction is common in older Hancock County homes and is exactly the scenario where a professional asbestos inspection — before any renovation or insurance repair work — can prevent a serious exposure event.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Kiln

Every project begins with a systematic asbestos inspection and bulk material sampling. Suspect materials — ceiling texture, floor tile and mastic, pipe insulation, roofing felt, joint compound — are collected by our EPA Lead-Safe Certified team and sent to an accredited laboratory. Results typically return within a few business days, and we walk you through the findings before any abatement work is scheduled.

When confirmed asbestos-containing materials are present, containment comes first. We seal the work area with poly sheeting and establish negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers so that fibers cannot migrate into adjacent rooms. Removal follows state-regulated wet methods that suppress dust throughout the process. All collected material is sealed in labeled, double-bagged containers and transported to a licensed disposal facility — Mississippi DEQ regulations govern both the handling and the disposal chain, and our documentation covers every step for your records and for any insurance or permit requirements.

Final air clearance sampling confirms that fiber counts meet the clearance criteria before containment is removed and the space is returned to you. That third-party verifiable clearance is what separates a properly closed abatement project from one that leaves liability on the table.
