## Why Wade Properties See Stubborn Carpet Problems

Forrest County averages over 60 inches of rainfall a year — well above the national average — and Wade's position in the region means that red clay and sandy loam soil gets tracked indoors constantly. That reddish-brown Mississippi clay is particularly aggressive: it bonds to carpet fibers at a microscopic level, and the tannins in the soil can permanently discolor light-colored carpet if it sits too long. Humidity that routinely climbs above 80% in summer also slows the natural drying process, which means any moisture left in carpet after a spill or cleaning can linger long enough to encourage mold growth in the padding beneath.

Older homes in this area — many built in the 1960s and 1970s with thicker, denser carpet styles — tend to hold onto particulate matter more stubbornly than modern low-pile carpets. Pet dander, pollen from the surrounding pine forests, and fine road dust from nearby rural routes all accumulate deep in the pile where standard vacuuming never reaches.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Wade

Every job starts with a walkthrough to identify fiber type, soiling level, and any areas of concern — staining from tannin-rich well water (common in parts of rural Forrest County), pet urine that has wicked into the pad, or high-traffic lanes worn flat by years of use. From there, a pH-balanced pre-conditioner is applied and given dwell time to break the bond between soil particles and carpet fibers before any agitation begins.

We use truck-mounted hot water extraction equipment — the same method the IICRC identifies as the most effective for deep fiber cleaning — which generates water temperatures and suction levels that portable rental units simply can't match. The high-heat extraction also sanitizes the carpet surface, which matters in homes where children or pets spend time on the floor. After extraction, a neutralizing rinse is applied to prevent residue buildup, which is one of the main reasons carpets re-soil quickly after a DIY cleaning.

For commercial properties — offices, rental units, or any business in the Wade area — we offer low-moisture encapsulation cleaning as an option when faster dry times are a priority.

## Carpet Stain Removal and Sanitization

Not all stains respond to the same chemistry. Red clay tracked in from outside needs an oxidizing agent; protein-based stains from food or pet accidents need an enzyme treatment; tannin stains from coffee or tea respond best to an alkaline spotter. Identifying the stain type before applying any chemical is what separates a professional result from a stain that gets pushed deeper into the fiber.

For homes where odor is the primary complaint — often the case after a pet has repeatedly used the same area — we apply a topical enzyme treatment followed by a sub-surface injection to reach the padding. Masking the odor isn't the goal; neutralizing the source is.

All products used are safe for re-occupancy after drying, though we'll give you a realistic dry-time estimate based on the specific conditions in your home that day, including indoor humidity and airflow.
