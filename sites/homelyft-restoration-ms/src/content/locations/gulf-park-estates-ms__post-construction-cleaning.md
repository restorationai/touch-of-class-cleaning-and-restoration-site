## Why Gulf Park Estates Construction Sites Need More Than a Broom

Harrison County's coastal climate creates conditions that amplify the mess left behind after any construction or renovation job. The combination of 70%-plus average relative humidity and salt-laden air means drywall dust doesn't just settle — it bonds. Sawdust on window sills absorbs ambient moisture overnight and becomes a paste. Paint overspray on tile or hardwood can haze in ways that standard wiping won't touch. Insulation fibers work their way into HVAC vents and, if left uncleaned before the system runs, get distributed through every room in the house.

Gulf Park Estates also has a meaningful share of older coastal cottages and mid-century homes that have been partially renovated over the decades — additions tacked on, bathrooms updated, kitchens opened up. When those projects finish, the dust and debris don't stay neatly in the new section. Fine particulates migrate through gaps in older subflooring, settle into original hardwood grooves, and accumulate in crawl spaces that weren't part of the scope. Cleaning those homes requires a different level of attention than a clean-slab new build.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Gulf Park Estates

A proper final clean after construction moves in a specific sequence — skipping steps or reversing the order means you're cleaning surfaces twice.

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work, the crew removes bulk debris: lumber scraps, drywall offcuts, packaging material, and anything the trades left behind. This stage protects finished floors from being scratched by debris dragged across them during the fine clean.

**Top-down detail clean.** Ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the tops of door casings get wiped before floors are touched. In Gulf Park Estates homes near the water, this step often reveals a fine salt-dust film on interior surfaces that came through open windows or doors during the build — it requires a damp wipe rather than a dry pass to fully remove.

**Window and glass cleaning.** Construction leaves a specific film on glass — a combination of paint mist, silicone residue from caulking, and concrete splatter. We use appropriate solvents matched to the glass type and frame material, which matters especially on any impact-rated windows that are common in coastal Harrison County builds.

**Floor care.** Hardwood, tile grout lines, and LVP edges all need different approaches. Grout haze from fresh tile work is time-sensitive — the longer it sits, the harder it bonds. We identify and address grout haze as part of the final clean rather than leaving it for the homeowner to discover.

**HVAC vent wipe-down.** Every supply and return vent cover is removed, wiped, and replaced. If the system has already been run before the clean, we note it for the client — that's a conversation about duct cleaning, which is a separate scope.
