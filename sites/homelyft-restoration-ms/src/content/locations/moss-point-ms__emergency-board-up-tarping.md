## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Moss Point

When a call comes in from Moss Point, the first priority is a rapid damage assessment to determine whether the opening is a broken window, a compromised door frame, a partial roof collapse, or a combination. That assessment drives material selection — plywood thickness, tarp weight rating, and anchor method all vary depending on what the structure can support and what the next 72-hour weather forecast looks like.

For roof tarping, the team uses reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for sustained wind exposure, secured with batten boards rather than simple rope ties. Batten-board attachment distributes load across the tarp surface and dramatically reduces the risk of the tarp lifting in a subsequent wind event — a critical detail in a coastal Mississippi climate where a second storm can arrive before repairs begin. For window and door board-ups, OSB panels are cut to fit and fastened into surrounding framing rather than just the window casing, which matters on older wood-frame homes where the casing itself may be deteriorated.

The team is IICRC FSRT certified for fire and smoke response, which means the board-up work is coordinated with an awareness of what comes next — smoke residue documentation, content protection, and insurance scope — so nothing done during emergency securing creates complications for the remediation phase.
