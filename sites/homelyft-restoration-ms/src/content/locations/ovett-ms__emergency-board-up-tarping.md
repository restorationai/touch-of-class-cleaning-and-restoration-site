## Why Ovett Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Jones County averages more than 60 thunderstorm days per year, and the dense longleaf and loblolly pines that define the landscape around Ovett become projectiles in straight-line winds. Older wood-frame homes — many built decades ago on pier-and-beam foundations — have roof decks and exterior walls that can be more vulnerable to impact than newer engineered construction. A single large pine limb doesn't just crack shingles; it can open a three- or four-foot hole that lets in driven rain within minutes.

Fire damage compounds the problem. A kitchen fire that scorches one room can blow out windows on the opposite wall from pressure and heat, leaving multiple openings simultaneously. In rural Jones County, where volunteer fire response is often the first line of defense, the time between ignition and suppression can be longer than in urban areas — meaning more thermal damage and more openings to secure before the adjuster ever arrives.

## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Ovett

When we reach a property, the first step is a quick perimeter walk to identify every breach — not just the obvious hole in the roof, but cracked window frames, compromised soffit vents, and any door that no longer latches. We document everything with photos before touching a single board, because that documentation becomes part of the insurance file.

For roof openings, we use 6-mil reinforced polyethylene tarps anchored with dimensional lumber and screwed into the decking — not stapled, not draped loosely. The tarp is lapped over the ridge when possible so wind can't lift the leading edge. For windows and doors, we cut OSB panels to fit each opening precisely, fasten them with structural screws into the framing (not just the trim), and seal the perimeter with butyl tape to block wind-driven rain. On fire-damaged structures, we also assess whether the floor system is safe before sending anyone inside — a precaution that matters especially in older pier-and-beam homes where fire can travel through the crawlspace.
