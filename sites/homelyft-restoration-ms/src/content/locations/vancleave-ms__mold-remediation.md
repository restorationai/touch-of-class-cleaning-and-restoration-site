## Our Mold Remediation Process in Vancleave

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not a visual scan, but a moisture mapping session using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate saturation behind walls and under floors that would otherwise go undetected. Visible mold is often the smaller part of the problem.
