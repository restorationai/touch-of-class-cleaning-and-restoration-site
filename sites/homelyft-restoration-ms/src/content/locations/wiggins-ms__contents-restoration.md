## Why Wiggins Properties Face Elevated Contents Damage Risk

Stone County's climate is the defining factor. Wiggins averages over 60 inches of rainfall per year, and the region sits in a corridor that funnels tropical moisture from the Gulf straight through the Pine Belt. That combination means roof leaks, plumbing failures, and flood intrusions are not rare events — they're recurring realities for homeowners here. When water gets into a house, it doesn't just wet the floor; it wicks into upholstered furniture, saturates mattresses, and soaks into the cardboard backing of framed artwork within hours.

Fire events compound the problem differently. Smoke residue from pine-heavy construction — common in older Wiggins-area homes built with local timber — carries a sticky, oily character that bonds to fabric, wood finishes, and electronics more aggressively than smoke from synthetic materials. That residue continues to etch and discolor surfaces long after the flames are out, which is why prompt pack-out matters even when the fire itself seemed minor.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Wiggins

When we arrive at a Wiggins property, the first step is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and tagged before anything moves — that documentation becomes the foundation of your insurance claim. Items are then sorted by restoration category: textiles go to ultrasonic or ozone treatment, hard surfaces get cleaned with pH-appropriate solutions, electronics are triaged by a qualified technician, and documents or photographs are stabilized immediately to prevent further ink migration or mold growth.

Packed contents are transported to a secure, climate-controlled storage facility where temperature and relative humidity are actively managed. This matters specifically in the Wiggins area because bringing smoke- or water-damaged goods into an uncontrolled environment — even a dry garage — can allow secondary mold growth or continued off-gassing of smoke odors. Contents stay in storage until your home passes structural drying benchmarks and is ready to receive them, at which point we coordinate delivery and placement.

Throughout the process, you have access to a digital inventory so you can identify any items you need immediately — a work laptop, medications, children's school materials — and we can arrange priority release of those specific pieces.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier on Contents Claims

Contents claims are frequently the most disputed portion of a property loss — adjusters want itemized documentation, replacement cost values, and evidence that restoration was the appropriate choice over replacement. Our inventory process is built around that standard. We photograph every item before and after treatment, document cleaning methods used, and provide a written scope that aligns with how carriers evaluate personal property losses.

IICRC-certified firms are recognized by most major insurance carriers as qualified to perform contents restoration work, and our certifications — including IICRC FSRT for fire and smoke and IICRC WRT for water damage — are on file and available to share with your adjuster. We can communicate directly with your carrier's field adjuster to clarify scope and support your claim, which reduces the back-and-forth you'd otherwise have to manage yourself during an already stressful period.
