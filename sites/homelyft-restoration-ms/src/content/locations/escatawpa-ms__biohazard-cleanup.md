## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Escatawpa

Every biohazard remediation job begins with a thorough assessment of the affected space before any materials are disturbed. Our IICRC-certified technicians establish a contained work zone using engineering controls — physical barriers and negative air pressure where appropriate — to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home or building.

From there, the process moves through removal of affected porous materials, treatment of non-porous surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants, and verification testing to confirm the space meets established safety benchmarks before containment is removed. All regulated biological waste is packaged, labeled, and transported according to Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality requirements and applicable federal standards — nothing is left for the homeowner or property manager to handle.

For sharps and needle cleanup situations, which do arise in Jackson County properties — sometimes discovered during estate cleanouts or property turnovers — our team uses puncture-resistant collection protocols and proper sharps disposal containers. We do not treat these as minor items; they receive the same chain-of-custody documentation as any other regulated waste stream.
