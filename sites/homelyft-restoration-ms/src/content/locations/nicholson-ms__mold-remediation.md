## Why Nicholson Properties See Mold Issues

Pearl River County's climate is the underlying driver. Nicholson averages high relative humidity through spring, summer, and fall, and the dense tree canopy common to rural Pearl River properties slows surface drying after rain. Homes built on pier-and-beam foundations — a common construction style throughout this part of Mississippi — leave crawl spaces exposed to ground moisture year-round. When vapor barriers degrade or are absent entirely, that moisture migrates upward into subfloor sheathing and floor joists, creating exactly the dark, damp, poorly ventilated environment where mold thrives.

Older wood-framed homes in the Nicholson area also tend to have less airtight building envelopes than newer construction, meaning humid outdoor air cycles through wall cavities more freely. A small roof penetration around a vent boot or a slow drip under a bathroom fixture can saturate insulation for weeks before a homeowner notices discoloration on a ceiling or catches that distinctive musty odor — often described as wet cardboard or earth — that signals active mold growth.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Nicholson

Every job starts with a thorough inspection rather than a guess. We assess visible growth, probe moisture readings in walls and subfloor assemblies, and identify the moisture source — because mold remediation that skips source correction is a temporary fix at best.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — Poly sheeting and negative air pressure isolate the affected area so spores don't migrate to clean parts of the home during removal.
- **HEPA filtration** — Air scrubbers run continuously throughout the job, capturing airborne spores down to 0.3 microns.
- **Removal of affected materials** — Porous materials like drywall, insulation, and compromised wood that cannot be cleaned to clearance standards are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — Remaining structural surfaces are treated and allowed to dry to target moisture levels before any encapsulation or rebuild begins.
- **Moisture correction** — We address the source — whether that's a vapor barrier replacement in a crawl space, a plumbing repair referral, or improved attic ventilation — so the conditions that fed the mold don't return.

Because many Nicholson properties have pier-and-beam crawl spaces, we frequently extend containment and treatment below the subfloor, not just inside the living space — a step that matters enormously in this climate and is sometimes skipped by crews less familiar with Gulf South construction.
