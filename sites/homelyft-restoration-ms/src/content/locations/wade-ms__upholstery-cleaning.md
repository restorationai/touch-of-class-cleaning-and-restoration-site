## Why Wade Homes See Upholstery Issues

Wade sits along the Highway 57 corridor in Jackson County, in a stretch of coastal Mississippi where summer humidity regularly sits well above what most fabric manufacturers design for. Sofas and chairs positioned against exterior walls, a common layout in the ranch-style and ranch-adjacent homes common throughout this part of the county, tend to trap moisture behind the cushions where airflow is weakest. Add in pollen from the surrounding pine and hardwood tree cover each spring, and pet hair from homes with yard space for dogs, and fabric upholstery in this area collects a mix of allergens and organic material faster than upholstery in drier inland climates. Leather pieces face a different problem: the same humidity that swells fabric fibers dries out leather conditioning oils unevenly, leading to cracking at seams and armrests sooner than you'd expect from the piece's age.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Wade

We start every job with a fabric and fill identification step, because coastal humidity changes how a cushion should be treated. Fabric blends that read as dry on the surface can still hold moisture in the foam or batting underneath, so we test absorbency before choosing a method. For most fabric upholstery, we use low-moisture hot water extraction, which lifts embedded soil and allergens without oversaturating fill material that's already carrying ambient humidity. Leather pieces get a pH-balanced cleaning solution followed by a conditioning treatment matched to the leather's finish, since re-oiling too aggressively after a humid stretch can leave a tacky residue. We finish with targeted air movement across the piece, usually 2 to 4 hours depending on fill density, so cushions aren't left holding moisture in a house that's already fighting humidity from the outside.
