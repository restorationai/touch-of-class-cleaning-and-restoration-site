## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Sharon

Every job starts with a fast visual assessment of the breach — size, location, structural condition of the surrounding framing, and what's directly below the opening inside the structure. For roof damage, we measure the affected area and select a reinforced polyethylene tarp rated for the load, then secure it with weighted anchor boards rather than just staples or rope, which pull free in wind. For broken windows and door openings, we use exterior-grade plywood cut to fit and secured with structural screws into the framing — not just the trim — so the panel holds against both wind pressure and opportunistic entry.

Fire-damaged openings get an extra step: we assess whether smoke is still migrating through the structure before sealing, because trapping residual smoke inside an unventilated space can intensify odor penetration into porous materials. Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration) certification, which informs how we sequence board-up work after a fire loss specifically. After securing the structure, we document every opening with photographs and written measurements — that documentation goes directly into the insurance claim file.
