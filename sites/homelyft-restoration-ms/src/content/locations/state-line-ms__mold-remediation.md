## Why State Line Properties See Mold Issues

The Greene County side of State Line is heavily forested and sits in a region that averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually — one of the wettest corners of Mississippi. That moisture doesn't just fall on roofs; it saturates soil, raises groundwater tables, and pushes humidity into crawl spaces and pier-and-beam foundations that are common in older homes throughout the area. When a crawl space lacks adequate vapor barrier or ventilation, ground moisture wicks upward into floor joists and subfloor sheathing year-round, not just after a storm.

Many homes in this part of the state were built in the mid-20th century with materials and construction methods that predate modern moisture management standards. Older wood framing, minimal insulation, and single-pane windows create condensation patterns that newer construction largely avoids. Add in the long, hot summers — where air conditioning runs constantly and the temperature differential between a cool interior and a humid exterior wall can produce condensation inside wall cavities — and you have a recipe for hidden mold growth that's easy to miss until the colony is well established.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in State Line

We start every job with a visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. In State Line homes with crawl spaces, that means getting under the structure to check joist moisture content and look for active vapor intrusion before we ever open a wall. This step matters because treating visible mold without addressing the moisture source guarantees the problem returns.

Once we've identified the scope, we establish negative-air containment — sealing off the affected area with poly sheeting and running HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Mold-damaged materials that can't be cleaned to a safe standard are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Remaining structural surfaces are treated and dried to IICRC S520 protocol. We document moisture readings before, during, and after remediation, which also creates the paper trail your insurance adjuster will need.

Because many State Line properties have pier-and-beam construction, we frequently install or upgrade crawl space vapor barriers as part of the remediation scope — otherwise the mold remediation addresses the symptom but leaves the cause wide open.
