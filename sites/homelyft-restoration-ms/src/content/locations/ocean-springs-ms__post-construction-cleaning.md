## Why Ocean Springs Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

The coastal climate in Ocean Springs is the defining factor in post-construction cleanup work. ## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Ocean Springs

A thorough final clean on a construction or renovation project moves in a deliberate sequence — top to bottom, rough to detail, dry to wet. Skipping steps or rushing the sequence is how cleaning crews leave grout haze on tile or drywall dust inside cabinet interiors.

The process starts with a rough clean: removing all construction debris, plastic sheeting, tape residue, and bulk material from every room. HVAC vents and returns are covered or cleaned first to prevent recirculation of fine particulate through a newly installed system. From there, the team works ceiling to floor — light fixtures, ceiling fans, window frames, door casings, baseboards — before addressing floor surfaces.

For specialty cleaning phases, which often follow the rough clean by 24 to 48 hours, the focus shifts to detail work: cleaning inside cabinets and drawers, polishing fixtures, removing construction film from glass, and addressing any adhesive or caulk overspray on finished surfaces. Final walk-throughs are conducted with the contractor or homeowner present so that any missed areas are caught before the crew leaves the site.

In Ocean Springs, we build extra time into the schedule for humidity-related issues. If drywall mud or joint compound wasn't fully cured before painting — a common shortcut when contractors are racing a deadline — the cleaning process can disturb the surface. We flag these conditions rather than clean over them.
