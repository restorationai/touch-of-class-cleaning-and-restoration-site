Lyman sits in Harrison County where the Gulf Coast's humidity doesn't just hang in the summer air — it seeps into the ground, travels through soil, and rises into crawl spaces year-round. Homes here deal with a moisture load that's genuinely different from what you'd find inland: coastal clay soils hold water long after a rain event, and the region's mild winters mean there's rarely a dry season that gives a crawl space a chance to recover on its own. If you've noticed musty odors drifting up through your floors, soft spots in hardwood, or condensation on floor joists, the crawl space beneath your home is likely the source.

## Why Lyman Homes Are Particularly Vulnerable to Crawl Space Moisture

Harrison County's combination of high annual rainfall, shallow water tables, and dense clay-loam soils creates a persistent vapor pressure problem for any home built over a vented crawl space. When warm, humid outside air enters those foundation vents — the way older building codes intended — it meets the cooler surfaces of floor joists and subfloor sheathing and condenses. That condensation is enough to raise wood moisture content above the 19% threshold where wood rot and mold colonization begin.

Many homes in the Lyman area were built during the post-war and mid-century boom, when pier-and-beam construction was standard and vapor barriers, if installed at all, were thin polyethylene sheeting that has long since torn, shifted, or degraded. A 6-mil poly sheet stapled loosely to dirt in 1975 is not doing meaningful work in 2024. The result is that a large portion of the local housing stock is sitting on crawl spaces that have been slowly accumulating moisture damage for decades — often without visible signs inside the home until the damage is significant.

## What Crawl Space Encapsulation Actually Involves in Lyman

Encapsulation in this climate is not simply rolling out a thicker plastic sheet. The process starts with a full crawl space inspection: measuring moisture levels in the wood framing, checking for existing mold or fungal growth (which our IICRC AMRT-certified team can identify and remediate before encapsulation seals anything in), and assessing drainage conditions around the foundation.

Once the space is cleared and any remediation is complete, we install a heavy-duty reinforced vapor barrier — typically 20-mil — across the entire floor and up the foundation walls, sealed at all seams and penetrations. In Lyman's climate, we also evaluate whether a crawl space dehumidifier is warranted. Given the region's average relative humidity, a sealed crawl space without active dehumidification can still accumulate moisture through diffusion; adding a dedicated unit with a drain line keeps the space below 55% RH year-round. Existing foundation vents are sealed as part of the system, converting the crawl space from a vented to a conditioned or semi-conditioned zone.

The entire process typically runs two to three days depending on crawl space size and the extent of any pre-existing damage. Our team carries EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters in older Lyman homes where lead-based paint on foundation walls or floor joists can be disturbed during prep work.

## Coordinating with Insurance for Encapsulation Work in Lyman

Pure encapsulation — installing a vapor barrier in a dry, undamaged crawl space as a preventive measure — is generally not covered by homeowner's insurance. However, when moisture intrusion has caused identifiable damage (rotted joists, mold growth, damaged insulation, subfloor deterioration), the remediation and structural drying portions of the project often do qualify under a standard HO-3 policy, depending on the cause of loss.

We photograph and document conditions thoroughly before any work begins, which gives your adjuster a clear picture of the damage versus the preventive work. We work directly with most major carriers and can help you understand what's likely to be claimable before you file. Harrison County policies vary in how they handle "gradual damage" exclusions, so getting documentation in place early matters.

One thing that comes up specifically in Lyman and the surrounding Harrison County communities: homes on larger rural lots often have crawl spaces that were partially backfilled or graded at odd angles over the years — sometimes to manage surface drainage, sometimes just as part of landscaping projects.
