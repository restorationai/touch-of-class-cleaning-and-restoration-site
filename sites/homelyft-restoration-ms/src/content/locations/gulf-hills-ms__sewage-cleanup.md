## Why Gulf Hills Properties Experience Sewage Backups

The combination of coastal soil composition and the area's mix of older residential construction creates conditions that amplify sewage backup risk. Many homes in Gulf Hills were built on lots with high clay-sand content that drains poorly after heavy rainfall — the kind of rainfall that follows Gulf storm systems from June through November. When the ground is already saturated, municipal sewer laterals and private septic drain fields lose their capacity to accept effluent, and pressure builds backward into the home.

Older cast-iron and Orangeburg drain lines — common in Gulf Coast homes built before the 1980s — are also prone to root intrusion and collapse. Once a line fails, sewage doesn't trickle; it surges. The coastal humidity means that any organic contamination left standing for more than 24 to 48 hours begins supporting secondary microbial growth, compounding the cleanup scope significantly.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Gulf Hills

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most hazardous category under the IICRC S500 standard — and the process reflects that. Our IICRC-certified team arrives with full PPE, containment barriers, and commercial-grade extraction equipment. Here's how the work typically unfolds:

**Containment and safety assessment** — We isolate the affected area immediately to prevent cross-contamination to clean zones. In Gulf Hills homes with open floor plans or central hallways, this step is critical before any equipment is staged.

**Solid and liquid waste extraction** — Standing sewage and saturated materials are removed using truck-mounted extraction units. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are bagged and disposed of per Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines for biohazardous waste.

**Structural cleaning and EPA-registered disinfection** — Every surface that contacted sewage, including subfloor and wall framing, is cleaned and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Our team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters in Gulf Hills's older housing stock where disturbing contaminated materials can expose lead-containing paint.

**Drying and post-sanitization verification** — After disinfection, structural drying begins using industrial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged daily until materials reach target dry standard, protecting against mold colonization in the coastal humidity.
