## Why Hattiesburg Properties See Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Hattiesburg

The process begins with a structured assessment: documenting the burn pattern, identifying affected materials by category (char, smoke-coated, water-damaged), and establishing a drying baseline before any cleaning begins. Smoke residue falls into distinct types — wet smoke from slow, smoldering fires leaves a sticky, pungent film that requires chemical cleaning agents; dry smoke from fast, high-heat fires produces a powdery soot that can be mechanically removed but spreads easily if disturbed incorrectly. In Hattiesburg's older residential neighborhoods, many homes were built with heart pine framing — an exceptionally dense, resinous wood that was standard in Mississippi construction through much of the early twentieth century.
