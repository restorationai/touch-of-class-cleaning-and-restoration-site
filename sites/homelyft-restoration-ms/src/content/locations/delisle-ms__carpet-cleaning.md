## Why DeLisle Properties See Persistent Carpet Problems

Harrison County's average relative humidity hovers above 75% for much of the year. That sustained moisture creates a slow-motion problem inside carpet systems: dust mites thrive, organic debris stays damp rather than drying out, and odors set into backing material that would stay dry in a less humid climate. ## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in DeLisle

Every job starts with a fiber and backing assessment. Not all carpet is cleaned the same way — nylon pile handles hot-water extraction well, while older olefin or wool blends common in 1980s construction need lower-temperature methods to avoid shrinkage or backing delamination. We identify what you have before any equipment touches the floor.

From there, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Pre-treatment and agitation** — a pH-balanced pre-spray loosens soil and breaks down the oily residue that Gulf Coast humidity helps cement into fiber. A counter-rotating brush machine works the solution into the pile rather than just coating the surface.
2. **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted equipment operating at high temperature and pressure flushes suspended soil out of the carpet system. Truck-mount units generate consistent heat regardless of ambient humidity, which matters in DeLisle's climate where portable units can struggle to maintain temperature.
3. **Targeted stain treatment** — red clay, pet waste, mold-adjacent discoloration, and food stains each require a different chemistry. We match the treatment to the stain type rather than applying a single universal product.
4. **Controlled drying** — in Harrison County's humidity, carpets left to air-dry can take 12–24 hours and may develop musty odors in the process. We deploy air movers and dehumidifiers to bring drying time down significantly, which also reduces the window for microbial re-colonization.
