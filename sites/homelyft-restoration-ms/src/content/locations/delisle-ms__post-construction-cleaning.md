## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in DeLisle

A final clean that actually passes a builder's walkthrough moves in phases, not in one pass. The sequence matters because disturbing settled dust before surfaces are wiped creates a second layer of contamination.

**Phase 1 — Rough clean.** Bulk debris removal: broken tile, scrap lumber, packaging, drywall offcuts. Everything bagged and staged for haul-off before any detail work begins.

**Phase 2 — Detail clean.** HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all horizontal surfaces, window tracks, cabinet interiors, and HVAC registers. Grout haze removal from tile using appropriate pH-adjusted cleaners — a step that's easy to skip and expensive to correct once it cures fully in the heat. Window glass polished to remove construction film and silicone smears.

**Phase 3 — Final inspection clean.** Surfaces wiped in a consistent pattern so nothing is double-touched or missed. Light fixtures cleared of dust and debris. Floors cleaned last, after all overhead and wall work is complete, so they aren't re-contaminated.

For projects where EPA Lead-Safe protocols apply — common in DeLisle homes built before 1978 that are undergoing renovation — the team follows certified containment and cleaning procedures throughout, not just during demolition.
