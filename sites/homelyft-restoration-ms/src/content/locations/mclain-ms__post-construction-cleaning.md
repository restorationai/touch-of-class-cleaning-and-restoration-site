## Why McLain Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

McLain sits in Greene County, where summer humidity regularly pushes past 90 percent and even mild rain events can push moisture into an unfinished or freshly finished structure. That matters for post-construction cleanup because fine particulate — joint compound dust, silica from cut concrete block, fiberglass strands from batt insulation — doesn't just settle on surfaces here. It clumps. It sticks to window tracks, HVAC registers, and rough-sawn lumber edges in ways that a standard sweep-and-mop approach misses entirely.

Older homes in the McLain area that have undergone renovation rather than new construction present an additional layer of complexity. Many structures in this part of Greene County were built during an era when lead-based paint was standard. Our team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which means renovation cleanup on pre-1978 housing follows containment and disposal protocols that protect your family and satisfy Mississippi DEQ guidance — not just a general wipe-down.

Local contractors in this region also tend to work with materials common to the Gulf South: fiber cement siding, pressure-treated lumber, and spray foam insulation. Each leaves a different residue profile. Fiber cement cutting dust is particularly fine and travels far; spray foam overspray hardens quickly and bonds to glass and metal if not addressed within the first 24 to 48 hours of cure.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in McLain

Every post-construction clean begins with a structured walk-through to document the scope — room by room, surface by surface. We photograph the condition before we touch anything, which matters if there are punch-list disputes between the homeowner and the general contractor.

From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Rough clean first.** Large debris, scrap lumber, excess caulk, and packaging material come out before any fine-surface work begins. Attempting to detail-clean while bulk debris is still present just redistributes dust.

**HEPA filtration throughout.** We run commercial-grade HEPA air scrubbers during the entire cleaning process, capturing airborne particulate down to 0.3 microns. In a humid climate like McLain's, airborne dust that settles on damp surfaces becomes a surface contamination problem — filtration prevents that cycle.

**Detail surfaces in order: high to low.** Ceiling fans, light fixtures, cabinet interiors, window sills, baseboards, and finally floors. Skipping this sequence means you clean floors twice.

**Final inspection under raking light.** We use directional lighting to catch adhesive haze on glass, grout film on tile, and paint overspray on hardware — the details that fail a builder's final walkthrough.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated space, deep cleaning after a mold remediation project, or sanitizing a property before occupancy — we layer in the appropriate IICRC-certified protocols from our fire and smoke (FSRT) or mold (AMRT) training.
