**Sofa smelling musty even though it looks clean?** Along the Mississippi Gulf Coast, salt-laden humidity works its way into upholstery fibers faster than most homeowners expect, and Henderson Point's proximity to the water means fabric and leather furniture here carry moisture loads that inland Harrison County homes rarely deal with. If your couch feels damp to the touch after a stretch of gulf breeze or smells faintly of mildew even after airing out, that's usually trapped humidity in the padding, not just surface dirt.

## Why Henderson Point Furniture Needs Different Care

Henderson Point sits close enough to the Mississippi Sound that salt air and high ambient humidity are a near-constant factor, especially from late spring through early fall. That combination is hard on upholstery in two ways: fabric fibers hold moisture longer than they would in a drier climate, and metal frame components, springs, and staples inside sofas and recliners are more prone to corrosion. Many homes in this part of Pass Christian were rebuilt or substantially renovated after Hurricane Katrina, which means a lot of furniture in the area is newer than the houses themselves, but it's still exposed to the same coastal air every day. We also see more mildew-related odor complaints here than we do working further inland, simply because the moisture never fully clears between cleanings.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start with a fabric or leather assessment to confirm what the piece can handle. Cotton blends and natural fibers common in slipcovers react differently to hot water extraction than synthetic microfiber, and leather needs a conditioning step that steam alone won't provide. For fabric sofas and chairs, we use low-moisture hot water extraction that lifts embedded dirt and allergens without oversaturating the padding, which matters more here than in drier climates since waterlogged cushions in a humid house can take a day or more to fully dry. Leather pieces get cleaned with pH-balanced solutions and conditioned afterward to prevent the cracking that salt air accelerates. Every piece is inspected for hidden mildew at the base and along seams before we call the job finished.

## Local note

One thing that trips up a lot of Henderson Point homeowners: furniture positioned near windows or sunrooms facing the water tends to collect a fine layer of salt residue over time, even when it looks visually clean. That residue attracts moisture from the air, which is part of why upholstery near coastal-facing windows can feel perpetually damp no matter how often it's vacuumed. We treat those pieces a little differently, with an extra rinse pass to pull the salt out before extraction, rather than just cleaning over it.
