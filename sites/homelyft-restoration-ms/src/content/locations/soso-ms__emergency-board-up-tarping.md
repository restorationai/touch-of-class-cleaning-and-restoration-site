## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Soso

When a call comes in from the Soso area, the crew loads the service vehicle with dimensional lumber, OSB and plywood panels, heavy-mil polyethylene tarping, fasteners rated for high-wind exposure, and the laddering equipment needed to reach steep-pitch roofs common to the area. The process on-site follows a consistent sequence:

1. **Rapid damage assessment** — walking the perimeter and interior to identify every breach: broken windows, kicked-in doors, missing shingles, open ridge lines, and any compromised load-bearing elements that affect safe access.
2. **Roof tarping** — tarps are anchored with lumber battens screwed into solid decking, not just draped and weighted. In Jones County's wind environment, a tarp that isn't mechanically fastened is a tarp that blows off at 2 a.m.
3. **Window and door board-up** — plywood panels are cut to fit and secured with exterior-grade fasteners. For fire-damaged frames where the surrounding wall is soft, we anchor into adjacent solid framing rather than the compromised surround.
4. **Documentation** — every opening is photographed before and after securing, with measurements logged. This documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster and eliminates disputes about pre-existing conditions.
5. **Handoff** — we walk the property with you or your representative before leaving, confirm all access points are secured, and provide a written summary of what was done.
