## Why Moselle Homes See Contents Damage Differently

Jones County's climate is genuinely punishing on personal property. Average annual humidity in this part of Mississippi regularly sits above 75%, which means contents that might air out in a drier climate will instead absorb ambient moisture and continue to degrade even after the primary water source is stopped. Wood furniture joints loosen, particle-board cabinetry swells and delaminates, and fabric items — upholstery, rugs, clothing, drapery — can develop musty odors that are nearly impossible to eliminate without professional ozone or thermal fogging treatment.

Moselle's housing stock skews toward ranch-style and split-level homes built between the 1970s and early 2000s. Many of these properties have crawl spaces rather than full basements, and that crawl-space air circulates directly into the living area. When a pipe fails under the floor or a washing machine line bursts, the moisture doesn't just pool — it rises. Furniture sitting on hardwood or laminate floors in those homes often sustains damage from below before the homeowner even notices the leak above the surface.

Fire damage compounds the problem in a specific way in this region. Wood-burning fireplaces are common in Jones County homes, and when a chimney fire or kitchen fire produces smoke, the pine-resin soot that coats contents is particularly oily and penetrating. Standard dry-sponge cleaning alone rarely removes it from porous surfaces like unfinished wood, leather, or natural-fiber textiles.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Moselle

Every contents job begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item is moved. We photograph and log each piece — furniture, electronics, artwork, clothing, documents, collectibles — so you have a complete record that supports your insurance claim and gives you confidence that nothing is lost in transit.

Once inventoried, items are packed using materials rated for the specific damage type. Smoke-affected textiles go into sealed poly bags to prevent cross-contamination during transport. Water-damaged furniture is padded and loaded in a way that allows airflow rather than trapping residual moisture. Fragile or high-value items are crated individually.

At our secure, climate-controlled storage facility, each category of item receives the appropriate treatment:

- **Water-damaged contents**: Structural drying principles from the IICRC S500 standard guide how we handle saturated materials. Wood pieces are dried slowly to prevent checking and cracking.
- **Smoke and fire-affected contents**: Ultrasonic cleaning tanks handle hard surfaces and non-porous items. Ozone chambers and thermal fogging address odor in soft goods. Items that cannot be restored to pre-loss condition are documented for replacement claims.
- **Mold-affected contents**: HEPA vacuuming, antimicrobial treatment, and controlled drying address active growth. We do not make guarantees about future mold recurrence — that depends on conditions in the restored home — but we follow established remediation protocols.

Return delivery is scheduled in coordination with your contractor's completion timeline so your belongings arrive back in a home that is fully dry, repaired, and ready.
