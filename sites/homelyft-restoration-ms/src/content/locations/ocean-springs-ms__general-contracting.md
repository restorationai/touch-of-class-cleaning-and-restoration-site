## Why Ocean Springs Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining variable in any Ocean Springs renovation. High humidity — routinely above 70% for much of the year — means that materials acclimate differently here than they would inland. Hardwood flooring installed without adequate moisture equalization can cup or gap within a single season. Exterior trim painted during a humid stretch may blister before the next summer arrives. These aren't hypothetical risks; they're patterns contractors who only occasionally work the Coast tend to underestimate.

The city's older housing stock adds another layer. Homes built before the 1970s frequently contain original plaster walls, knob-and-tube wiring routed through cavities that modern renovation plans need to account for, and pier-and-beam foundations that respond to wet winters and dry summers by moving — sometimes enough to rack door frames or crack tile. Any remodel that touches flooring, walls, or load paths needs to start with a realistic assessment of what's already there. Our EPA Lead-Safe Certified status matters here too: pre-1978 homes in Ocean Springs are common, and disturbing painted surfaces without proper containment protocols isn't just a code issue — it's a health one.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Ocean Springs

Every project begins with a scoped walkthrough, not a sales pitch. We measure, photograph, and document existing conditions — including moisture readings in walls and subfloors — before a single line item goes on paper. That baseline matters because Ocean Springs inspectors and the Jackson County permitting office both expect documentation that reflects actual field conditions, not a generic scope imported from another job.

From there, the process moves in a logical sequence:

- **Design and material selection** calibrated to coastal durability — fiber-cement siding over wood where exposure is a factor, moisture-resistant cabinet boxes in bathrooms and kitchens, and corrosion-resistant hardware throughout.
- **Permitting and scheduling** coordinated with the City of Ocean Springs Building Department. Timelines here can run longer than in inland municipalities, especially for projects in flood zones, and we build that buffer into the project schedule rather than promising dates we can't keep.
- **Phased construction** that minimizes disruption. Many Ocean Springs homes are occupied during renovation; we sequence work to keep livable space functional and contain dust and debris tightly.
- **Final inspection and punch-list** with the homeowner present, not handed off to a subcontractor.

For post-damage rebuilds — whether from hurricane wind, flooding, or fire — our IICRC certifications and FORTIFIED Building/Roofing windstorm certification allow us to bridge the gap between remediation and reconstruction without handing the project off to a second company.
