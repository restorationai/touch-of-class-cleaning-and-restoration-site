## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fabric and fill assessment, because a linen sectional and a leather recliner need different chemistry and different drying times. For fabric upholstery we use hot water extraction calibrated to the fiber type, pulling soil and moisture out rather than just pushing cleaner in, which matters in a humid climate where over-wetting furniture can extend drying time and invite odor. Leather gets a separate conditioning step after cleaning since Gulf Coast humidity dries out leather finishes faster than a drier climate would. We finish with controlled air movement to push drying time down, because in St. Martin's average humidity, cushions left to air dry on their own can stay damp at the core for a day or more, which is long enough for odor to set in.
