## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Nicholson

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and it requires a structured removal and sanitization sequence, not just a shop-vac and a mop. Our process in Nicholson follows the IICRC S500 standard adapted to the specific conditions we encounter here:

**Containment and PPE setup.** Before anything is touched, technicians establish containment barriers to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms. In homes with open crawl spaces — common in Pearl River County construction — we extend containment below the subfloor if effluent has migrated down.

**Extraction and solid waste removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units. Solid waste and contaminated porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are bagged, documented, and disposed of per Mississippi solid waste regulations.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** All affected hard surfaces receive EPA-registered antimicrobial application. We pay particular attention to subfloor sheathing and floor joists in pier-and-beam homes, where sewage can pool in the cavity and remain hidden.

**Structural drying.** After sanitization, drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — runs until moisture readings in framing and subfloor materials return to regional equilibrium levels. Pearl River County's ambient humidity means this step cannot be rushed.

**Post-remediation verification.** We document moisture readings at closeout so your insurance adjuster and any future buyer's inspector have a clear record that the structure was properly dried.
