## Why Escatawpa Properties See Contents Damage Differently

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining factor here. Escatawpa's proximity to the Pascagoula River basin means ambient humidity regularly sits above 70% even on clear days. After a water or fire loss, that baseline moisture accelerates secondary damage to everything inside the home — wooden furniture begins to warp within 48 hours, fabric items absorb smoke and mold spores simultaneously, and paper documents or family photographs can become permanently fused if not addressed quickly.

The local housing stock adds another layer. Much of Escatawpa's residential development includes older wood-frame construction where crawl spaces trap moisture and transfer it upward into flooring and cabinetry. When a loss event occurs in one of these homes, contents on lower levels are often affected not just by the primary damage source but by the ambient moisture that has been building in the structure for years. That combination — acute event plus chronic humidity — means belongings here need more aggressive drying and deodorization than comparable items in drier climates.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Escatawpa

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory before a single item leaves your home. We photograph, catalog, and condition-grade each piece — furniture, electronics, clothing, artwork, documents, and personal keepsakes — so you have a complete record for your insurance claim and so nothing is misplaced during the pack-out.

From there, the process breaks into three tracks depending on what each item needs:

- **Structural and upholstered furniture** goes through ultrasonic cleaning or dry-soot sponge treatment depending on the damage type, followed by thermal deodorization in an ozone or hydroxyl environment to neutralize smoke compounds that have penetrated the fibers.
- **Hard-surface items** — ceramics, metals, plastics, appliances — are cleaned using pH-appropriate solutions matched to the material, then inspected for heat warping or corrosion before being cleared for return.
- **Documents, photographs, and media** are stabilized first (freeze-drying for wet paper items when needed) and then assessed for restoration feasibility. We're honest when an item is beyond recovery.

All items are stored in a climate-controlled facility while your home is being dried and repaired. You receive a secure inventory log so you know exactly where each piece is at every stage.
