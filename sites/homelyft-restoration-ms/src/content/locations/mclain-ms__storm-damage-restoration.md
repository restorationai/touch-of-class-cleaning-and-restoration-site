## Why McLain Properties See Severe Storm Damage

McLain sits in a corridor of Greene County that experiences some of the most direct exposure to Gulf moisture systems tracking northeast from the coast. Afternoon thunderstorms here don't behave the way they do in more urbanized areas — there's less impervious surface to redirect runoff, and the dense longleaf pine and hardwood canopy means falling trees and large limbs are a primary damage source, not a secondary one. Rooflines take direct strikes, gutters get crushed, and fascia boards get torn free before the wind even peaks.

The housing stock in and around McLain skews older, with a meaningful share of homes built in the 1960s through 1980s using construction methods that predate current Mississippi windstorm standards. That means roof decking is often nailed on wider spacing, ridge vents are less robust, and attic insulation — once soaked — retains moisture for weeks if not properly extracted. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in McLain

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional damage. That means getting eyes on the roof and exterior fast, installing temporary tarps or board-ups where the envelope is breached, and documenting every affected surface photographically before anything is touched — a step that matters enormously when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim later.

Once the structure is stabilized, the work shifts to moisture mapping. In McLain's climate, a storm that passes on a Tuesday afternoon can leave attic sheathing and wall cavities at elevated moisture levels by Wednesday morning — and mold colonization in Mississippi's heat and humidity can begin within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. Our IICRC-certified technicians use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to trace water migration behind drywall and under flooring before any drying equipment is placed.

Drying is staged using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, calibrated to the specific volume and material composition of each affected space. Structural drying logs are maintained throughout and turned over to the homeowner and insurer at job close. Debris removal — downed limbs, damaged roofing materials, compromised insulation — runs concurrently so the property is safe to occupy or re-enter as quickly as conditions allow.

Where structural repairs are needed — framing, sheathing, roofing, siding — HomeLyft Restoration MS performs those under the same license and scope, so homeowners aren't coordinating between a restoration company and a separate contractor mid-claim.
