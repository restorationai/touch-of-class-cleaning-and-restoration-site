## Why Ovett Homes Face Particular Contents Risks

Ovett sits in a part of Jones County where older timber-frame construction is common, and many homes were built with heart pine flooring and solid-wood cabinetry that respond to moisture very differently than engineered materials. When a roof leak, burst pipe, or fire suppression system activation occurs, that dense wood absorbs water slowly but holds it for a long time — and the contents inside absorb ambient humidity right along with the structure. Smoke from a fire in a wood-heavy home also penetrates deeper into fabric fibers and porous finishes than in newer builds with synthetic materials, which means surface cleaning alone rarely removes odor.

The region also experiences significant storm activity, particularly during late spring and summer. Wind-driven rain finds its way through compromised soffits and roof decking, and by the time a homeowner notices water stains on the ceiling, furniture and stored items below may already be saturated. Jones County's clay-heavy soil can also contribute to slow drainage around foundations, increasing the chance that a crawl-space moisture event will affect belongings stored at floor level.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a tracking number — this documentation matters both for your peace of mind and for the insurance claim. Items are then carefully packed using appropriate materials: artwork is wrapped in acid-free paper, electronics are boxed with anti-static protection, and upholstered furniture is wrapped to prevent cross-contamination during transport.

Once contents reach our facility, the cleaning method is matched to the material. Ultrasonic cleaning tanks remove smoke and soot from hard goods — ceramics, metals, small appliances — without abrasive contact. Ozone and hydroxyl treatment addresses odor in fabrics and soft furnishings. Documents and photos that have been wet can sometimes be stabilized through controlled drying techniques, though results depend on how long they were saturated before recovery. Wood furniture is assessed for structural integrity, refinished where needed, and stored in a climate-controlled environment until your home is ready to receive it.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier on Contents Claims

Contents losses are often the most contested part of a property insurance claim because adjusters and policyholders sometimes disagree on whether an item can be restored versus replaced. Our itemized inventory, condition photographs, and cleaning records give your adjuster a clear, defensible paper trail. We document pre-cleaning condition, the methods applied, and post-cleaning results so there is no ambiguity about what was salvageable. For items that cannot be restored to pre-loss condition, the documentation supports a replacement cost claim. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster to keep the process moving.

Something worth knowing if your home is one of the older properties in the Ovett area: solid heart pine — common in Jones County homes built before the mid-20th century — is extremely dense and does not release absorbed moisture quickly. When smoke or water damage affects furniture or flooring made from this material, standard drying timelines are often not long enough.
