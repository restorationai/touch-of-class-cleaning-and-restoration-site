## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in D'Iberville

Every job starts with a walk-through to identify surface types, coating sensitivities, and any areas where construction activity left behind hazards — stray screws, broken glass, or uncured adhesives. From there, the work moves in a deliberate top-down sequence:

**Rough clean first.** Bulk debris — lumber scraps, packaging, excess caulk, and drywall chunks — is removed and disposed of before any fine cleaning begins. Skipping this step and going straight to detail cleaning is a common shortcut that grinds debris into finished surfaces.

**HVAC protection and vent cleaning.** Construction dust loads HVAC systems heavily. We seal or clean return air vents before running any equipment that circulates air, then clean or replace filters so the system doesn't redistribute particulate the moment the owner turns it on.

**Detail and specialty surfaces.** Window tracks, door hardware, light fixtures, cabinet interiors, and grout lines all get individual attention. For polished concrete or epoxy-coated floors — common in D'Iberville's newer commercial builds — we use pH-neutral cleaners and non-abrasive pads that won't dull the finish.

**Final inspection pass.** A second walk-through under raking light catches streaks on glass, haze on floors, and dust on top of door casings that's easy to miss on the first pass.

For projects involving older materials or pre-renovation work, our EPA Lead-Safe Certified team handles any lead-containing dust according to federal RRP protocols — relevant for any D'Iberville property where earlier structures were incorporated into a renovation.
