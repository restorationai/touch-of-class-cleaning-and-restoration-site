## Why Big Point Properties See Mold Issues

Jackson County's climate is the primary driver. Average annual humidity along this stretch of coastal Mississippi routinely stays above 70%, and Big Point's proximity to the Back Bay of Biloxi and surrounding tidal wetlands means that even homes with no plumbing failures can accumulate moisture through vapor intrusion alone. Slab-on-grade construction — common throughout this part of the county — gives mold spores a direct path through concrete micro-cracks into flooring assemblies and lower wall sections.

Older wood-frame homes in the area often have minimal vapor barriers under the structure, and the combination of warm soil temperatures and high outdoor dew points creates a near-constant condensation cycle in unconditioned crawl spaces. Post-Katrina rebuilds added a second layer of complexity: some homes were reconstructed quickly with materials that were not fully dried before enclosure, leaving latent moisture trapped inside wall assemblies that can feed mold growth years later. When a new leak activates that dormant moisture, remediation has to account for both the fresh intrusion and the pre-existing conditions inside the wall.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Big Point

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water pathways behind surfaces — because in Jackson County's climate, the visible mold is rarely the full extent of the problem. Containment barriers go up before any material is disturbed, using negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Contaminated porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing where spore penetration is confirmed — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality guidelines. Affected structural surfaces are then treated and dried to IICRC S520 standard before any rebuild begins. Because many Big Point homes have pier-and-beam or older slab foundations with limited under-floor access, our crew carries compact equipment sized to work in tight crawl spaces without cutting additional access panels unless structurally necessary.

Final clearance documentation is provided at job close. If your lender, insurer, or a prospective buyer's inspector requires independent air quality testing, we can coordinate with a third-party industrial hygienist — a step that's increasingly common on real estate transactions in Jackson County.
