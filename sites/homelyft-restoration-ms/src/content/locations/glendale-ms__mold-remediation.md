## Why Glendale Properties See Mold Issues

The Mississippi Gulf Coast corridor, including Glendale and the surrounding Harrison County area, sits in one of the most persistently humid climate zones in the country. Average relative humidity rarely dips below 70% even on clear days, and after a heavy rain event the ground stays saturated for days. That moisture migrates — through slab foundations, through crawl space vents, through the rim joists of pier-and-beam homes — and once it finds an organic surface like wood framing, paper-faced drywall, or insulation batting, mold colonization begins fast.

Older construction in this part of Mississippi compounds the challenge. Homes built before modern vapor barrier standards often lack adequate crawl space encapsulation, meaning ground moisture wicks upward continuously rather than seasonally. HVAC systems that run hard through a seven-month cooling season can develop condensation issues inside air handlers and ductwork — a hidden mold source that homeowners rarely suspect until they notice a musty odor that follows them from room to room.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Glendale

Every remediation starts with a thorough assessment — not a quick visual walk-through, but a systematic inspection using moisture meters, thermal imaging, and air sampling to map where contamination actually exists versus where it's merely suspected. Mold hides behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, and beneath flooring; visible surface growth is often the smallest part of the problem.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment. This keeps spores from migrating to clean areas of the home during removal — a step that matters especially in Glendale's open-floor-plan homes where HVAC return vents can spread disturbed spores quickly if containment is skipped. Contaminated materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of per EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces are then treated and dried to the moisture targets defined by the IICRC S520 mold remediation standard. Our IICRC AMRT-certified technicians oversee the process from start to finish.

Drying in Glendale's climate requires more aggressive equipment than you'd need in an arid region. We deploy commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient conditions, and we monitor daily until readings confirm the structure has reached equilibrium — not just surface dryness, but moisture content through the depth of the material.

## Glendale Insurance Coordination

Mold remediation is covered under many homeowner policies when the growth is tied to a sudden and accidental water loss — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, storm-driven intrusion. Coverage is less consistent when the source is long-term seepage or deferred maintenance, which is why thorough documentation of the origin matters from the first day on-site.

HomeLyft works with most major insurance carriers. We photograph the affected areas, document moisture readings, and prepare a detailed scope of work that adjusters can review. We can communicate directly with your adjuster to answer technical questions about the remediation process, which often speeds up the approval timeline. If your policy includes an Additional Living Expenses provision and the mold makes part of your home uninhabitable during remediation, we can help document that as well.
