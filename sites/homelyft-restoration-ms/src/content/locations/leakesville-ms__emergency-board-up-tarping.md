## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Leakesville

When you call, we start with a few quick questions about the type of opening — roof breach, broken window, compromised door frame, or structural wall damage — so we load the right materials before leaving. For roof tarping, we use minimum 6-mil polyethylene tarps secured with 2×4 battens screwed through the tarp and into solid rafter or truss members, not just stapled to the surface. That method holds through a second storm, which matters in a county where follow-on weather is common.

Window and door board-ups use ¾-inch CDX plywood cut to fit the rough opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails so the panels can be removed cleanly when the permanent repair begins. We photograph every opening before and after installation — dimensions, existing damage, fastener placement — because that documentation becomes part of your insurance claim file. Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke) certification, which means fire-damage board-ups get the same systematic approach: we assess smoke spread and structural stability before anyone goes onto a compromised roof.

One thing we've learned working in rural Mississippi counties like Greene: older homes here were often built with heart pine framing — dense, resinous wood that resists rot better than modern lumber but is also heavier and less forgiving when a rafter is partially compromised.
