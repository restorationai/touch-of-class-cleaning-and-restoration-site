## Why Poplarville Properties Take a Harder Hit from Severe Weather

Pearl River County averages more than 60 inches of rainfall a year, and the clay-heavy soil beneath much of Poplarville reaches saturation quickly during sustained storms. Once the ground is full, water has nowhere to go but toward foundations, crawl spaces, and low-lying slab entries. That hydrostatic pressure accelerates after a hurricane remnant stalls overhead — a pattern Gulf Coast residents know well from systems that lose wind speed but not rainfall as they push north from the coast.

The housing stock adds another layer of vulnerability. A significant portion of Poplarville's residential properties were built in the mid-20th century, when roof sheathing was thinner and attic ventilation standards were less stringent. When a tornado-producing supercell or a fast-moving line of severe thunderstorms strips shingles or lifts soffit panels, the exposed decking absorbs rain within minutes. By the time the storm passes, moisture has often already wicked into ceiling joists and insulation before a homeowner can get a tarp in place.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Poplarville

Every job starts with a structured damage assessment — roof, exterior walls, windows, and then interior moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. In Poplarville's older homes, we pay particular attention to tongue-and-groove subfloors and plaster ceilings, both of which hold water longer than modern materials and can mask elevated moisture readings at the surface.

Once the scope is documented, the sequence is deliberate: emergency tarping and board-up to stop active intrusion, water extraction from affected rooms and crawl spaces, and then structural drying using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage — not a one-size estimate. Drying is monitored against the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged and shared with your insurance adjuster. Tree limb removal and debris clearing happen in parallel so the property is safe for interior crews to work.

For storm jobs that involve roof decking replacement or structural framing repairs, our team holds a FORTIFIED Building/Roofing/MS Windstorm certification — a designation that matters in Mississippi because it aligns with the Insurance Institute for Business and Home Safety's wind-resistance protocols and can support premium adjustments with some carriers after repairs are complete.
