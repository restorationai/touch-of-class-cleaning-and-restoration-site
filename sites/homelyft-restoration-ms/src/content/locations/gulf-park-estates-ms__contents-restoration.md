## Why Gulf Park Estates Properties See Contents Damage Differently

The Gulf Park Estates area experiences some of the highest average relative humidity readings in Mississippi, routinely hovering above 70% even in mild months. That baseline moisture level means that after a water loss — whether from a roof breach during a tropical system or a slow plumbing failure — contents begin absorbing ambient humidity almost immediately, even after the standing water is gone. Upholstered furniture swells. Wood veneer on cabinets and bookshelves delaminates. Paper documents and photographs start to cockle and stick together within 24 to 48 hours.
