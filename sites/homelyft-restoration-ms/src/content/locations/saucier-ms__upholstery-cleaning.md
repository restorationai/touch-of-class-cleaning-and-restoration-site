## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric type and testing for colorfastness before any solution touches the piece, since a lot of Saucier households have a mix of synthetic blends and natural fiber upholstery that react differently to heat and moisture. From there we pre-treat soiled areas, agitate embedded soil out of the weave, and use hot water extraction for fabric sofas and sectionals, calibrated to a lower moisture setting than we'd use in a drier climate so cushions aren't left carrying extra humidity into an already humid house. Leather upholstery gets a separate conditioning step to replace oils lost to heat and sun exposure. We finish with a controlled drying pass so pieces aren't sitting damp in a room where the air itself is already working against them.
