## Why Pearlington Upholstery Ages Differently

Hancock County sits low and close to the Pearl River, and Pearlington in particular is a humid, often waterlogged stretch of coastline. Furniture in homes without strong HVAC dehumidification tends to hold ambient moisture in the padding and frame long after the fabric surface feels dry to the touch. That's how mildew odor and soft mold spotting show up on the underside of cushions even when nobody spilled anything. Many homes in this part of the county were rebuilt or elevated after Hurricane Katrina, and a lot of that newer construction still runs on window units or older central systems that don't pull humidity down as aggressively as a modern system would. Fabric sofas and chairs in those houses are working harder just sitting in the living room.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fabric and fill identification step, since Pearlington households run the gamut from older cotton-blend sectionals to newer microfiber and leather pieces picked up after storm replacements. Leather gets a pH-balanced conditioning clean rather than a standard hot-water extraction, because heat and moisture can dry out and crack leather that's already been through a humid summer or two. Fabric upholstery gets low-moisture steam cleaning with controlled extraction, pulling as much water back out as we put in, which matters more here than in drier climates because leftover dampness in the padding is exactly what invites mildew given the local air. We finish with targeted air movement on the piece itself, not just the room, so the frame and internal padding actually dry instead of just the visible fabric surface.
