## Why Agricola Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Agricola sits in the heart of George County, where the combination of high ambient humidity, frequent summer thunderstorms, and a housing stock that includes many older wood-frame homes creates conditions that accelerate secondary damage to personal property. Unlike a drier inland climate, a soaked upholstered sofa or a stack of family photos left in a closed room here doesn't simply dry on its own — moisture wicks deeper into wood grain, fabric fibers, and paper before the air can pull it back out.

Fire events compound the problem in a different way. Smoke from a structure fire in a wood-frame home produces a sticky, oily residue that bonds to porous surfaces quickly. In the humid George County air, that residue can absorb ambient moisture and become harder to remove with every passing hour. Getting contents out of the structure and into a controlled environment is not optional — it's the step that determines whether a piece of furniture or a set of family heirlooms can be saved at all.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Agricola

Every contents job starts with a detailed inventory before a single item leaves the property. Our team photographs, barcodes, and catalogs each piece — furniture, electronics, clothing, artwork, documents — so you have a complete record for your insurance claim and so nothing gets lost in transit.

From there, items move to our climate-controlled storage facility, where temperature and humidity are actively managed. This matters especially for pieces coming out of Agricola homes after a summer storm or a plumbing failure: wood furniture that arrives wet needs a controlled drying environment, not a warehouse that swings with outdoor humidity. Textiles go through ultrasonic or immersion cleaning as appropriate. Smoke-damaged hard goods are treated with dry-ice blasting or chemical sponging depending on the surface. Documents and photographs are assessed for freeze-drying if water saturation is severe.

Restored items are returned only after the structure itself has passed drying verification — we coordinate that timeline directly with our structural drying crews so the two processes finish together.

## Insurance Coordination for Agricola Contents Claims

Contents restoration is one of the most documentation-intensive parts of a property claim, and Mississippi insurers vary in how they handle personal property line items versus structural damage. Our team produces a room-by-room inventory with condition notes and photographs that aligns with standard adjuster review formats. We can communicate directly with your assigned adjuster to clarify scope, and we bill carriers directly on most major policies so you are not fronting the full cost of pack-out and storage while the claim processes.

One thing that comes up repeatedly in George County homes — including properties in the Agricola area — is the presence of solid-wood antique furniture that has been in families for generations.
