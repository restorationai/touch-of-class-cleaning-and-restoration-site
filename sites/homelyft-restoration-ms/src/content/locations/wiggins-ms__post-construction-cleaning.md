## Why Wiggins Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Stone County's climate sits in a humid subtropical band that makes construction dust behave differently than it does in drier inland markets. Fine drywall dust and sawdust absorb ambient moisture quickly, turning from a dry powder into a tacky residue that bonds to window tracks, HVAC registers, and cabinet interiors. If a project ran through the summer months — when Wiggins regularly sees heat indexes above 100°F and relative humidity that rarely drops below 60% — that residue has had time to cure onto surfaces.

The local housing stock adds another layer. Many homes in the Wiggins area were built during the post-WWII expansion of Stone County and feature original hardwood floors, plaster-over-wood-lath walls, and older millwork that requires a different touch than the vinyl plank and drywall common in newer builds. Aggressive scrubbing or the wrong cleaning chemistry on aged heart pine floors can do more damage than the construction crew did. Knowing what's underneath the dust matters as much as removing it.

Wiggins also sits close enough to the Bowie Creek watershed that slab-on-grade homes sometimes see elevated ground moisture during and after heavy rain events — a common occurrence during active construction when the envelope isn't fully sealed. That moisture can migrate under fresh flooring and into wall cavities before the crew even finishes, meaning a post-construction clean may need to include moisture mapping before surfaces are declared ready for occupancy.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

Every post-construction clean starts with a structured walk-through — not a visual glance, but a systematic room-by-room assessment that documents what trades were active, what materials are present, and whether any moisture intrusion occurred during the build. From there the work moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Rough clean first.** Large debris, fasteners, scrap material, and bulk dust are removed before any fine cleaning begins. Attempting to detail-clean over construction debris wastes time and risks scratching finished surfaces.

**HVAC and duct protection.** Construction dust infiltrates ductwork fast. We verify that duct openings are sealed or cleaned before the system is run, because the first time an HVAC unit cycles in a freshly built space, it can redistribute fine particulate across every surface you just cleaned.

**Surface-specific detail work.** Window tracks, door hardware, cabinet interiors, light fixtures, grout lines, and appliance surfaces each get appropriate chemistry and method. Paint overspray on glass gets a different treatment than adhesive residue on tile.

**Final inspection pass.** We walk the space with the client or general contractor before signing off. If a surface doesn't meet the standard for occupancy or final inspection, we address it before leaving.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleaning of older homes, odor neutralization after finish work, or cleaning following lead-safe renovation work — the process is adjusted accordingly. HomeLyft holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification and IICRC credentials, which matters when the project involved disturbing pre-1978 painted surfaces, a common situation in Stone County's older residential stock.
