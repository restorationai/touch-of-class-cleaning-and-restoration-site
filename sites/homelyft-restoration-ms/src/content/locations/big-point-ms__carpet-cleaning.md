## Why Big Point Properties See Stubborn Carpet Problems

Jackson County's climate is the main culprit. Relative humidity along the Gulf Coast routinely climbs above 80 percent for weeks at a stretch, which slows the natural evaporation that would otherwise help carpets recover after a wet event. When a drink spills or a pet has an accident, moisture lingers in the pad beneath the carpet — and within 24 to 48 hours, that damp environment is ideal for microbial growth and odor-causing bacteria.

The soil composition adds another layer of difficulty. The sandy clay that makes up much of the ground around Big Point and the broader Jackson County area tracks indoors easily, especially during the rainy season. Once ground into carpet fibers, that fine clay-based sediment bonds to the fiber's texture and resists surface cleaning. Standard rental machines rarely generate enough suction pressure or water temperature to break that bond loose.

Older homes in the area — many built in the 1970s and 1980s with lower-pile carpet over foam padding — present a specific challenge: the foam degrades over time, crumbling under the carpet and releasing a chalky residue that works its way up through the pile. Deep extraction pulls that residue out rather than pushing it around.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Big Point

Every job starts with a fiber assessment. Nylon, polyester, and olefin each respond differently to heat and cleaning agents, and the wrong combination can set stains permanently or shrink a carpet's backing. We identify the fiber type before selecting a cleaning solution.

Pre-treatment comes next. A pH-balanced conditioner is applied to loosen soil and break down the fatty acids left by cooking grease, pet dander, and tracked-in clay. We let it dwell — typically 10 to 15 minutes — before any extraction begins.

Hot-water extraction (often called steam cleaning, though the water is near-boiling rather than steam) follows. Our truck-mounted units generate significantly higher water temperature and vacuum pressure than portable rental equipment, which matters in a humid climate: the hotter the rinse water, the less residue it leaves behind, and the faster the carpet dries — critical when ambient humidity is already working against you.

After extraction, we apply a post-treatment rinse to neutralize the pH and prevent rapid re-soiling. On commercial jobs or high-traffic residential areas, we can apply a fiber protectant that extends the time between deep cleanings.

Drying is the final step and the one most people underestimate in coastal Mississippi. We deploy air movers and, when conditions call for it, dehumidification equipment to bring drying time down to a range that prevents the secondary moisture problems that Gulf Coast humidity invites.

One thing that catches homeowners in the Big Point area off guard: carpet that feels dry to the touch after cleaning can still hold significant moisture in the pad beneath it, especially during summer months when outdoor humidity is near-saturation.
