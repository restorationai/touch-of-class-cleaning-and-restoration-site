## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Nicholson

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We document readings room by room before a single piece of equipment is placed — that documentation matters for your insurance claim and for making sure no wet pocket is missed behind a wall or under flooring.

Once the scope is mapped, water extraction comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and saturated material out of carpet, pad, and subfloor assemblies. In crawl-space homes common to this part of Pearl River County, we also address moisture intrusion below the living space — a step that gets skipped by crews unfamiliar with the region's construction patterns and leads to callbacks months later when mold appears.

Structural drying follows extraction. We calculate the drying system using the IICRC S500 standard, placing commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in a configuration matched to the square footage and material types involved. Daily moisture readings track progress and let us adjust equipment placement rather than guessing. We don't pull equipment until readings confirm the structure has returned to acceptable moisture content — not on a fixed calendar schedule.
