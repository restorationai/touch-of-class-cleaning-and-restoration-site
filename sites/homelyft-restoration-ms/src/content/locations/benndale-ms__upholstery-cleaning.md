## Why Benndale Homes See Different Upholstery Issues

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fiber and dye test, since a lot of Benndale homes mix older hand-me-down furniture with newer pieces, and not every fabric responds the same way to hot water extraction. For most fabric sofas and sectionals we use low-moisture steam cleaning, which lifts embedded soil without oversaturating the padding underneath, a real concern in a climate that's slow to dry anything out fully. Leather upholstery gets a pH-balanced cleaner followed by a conditioner matched to the leather type, since the wrong product can leave a tacky residue that actually attracts more dust. We finish with a grooming pass on the pile to even out the nap and speed drying time, since we'd rather a customer wait an extra hour for the cushions to fully set than deal with a sofa that stays damp for two days in the summer humidity.
