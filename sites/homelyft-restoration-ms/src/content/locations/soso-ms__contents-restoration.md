## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Soso

The first step when we arrive at a Soso property is a detailed contents inventory — every item photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything moves. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives us a baseline for measuring what can be restored versus what must be replaced.

From there, salvageable belongings are carefully packed out and transported to our climate-controlled facility. Items go through the appropriate cleaning track based on what they are and how they were damaged:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, collectibles, tools, and electronics components — sound waves dislodge smoke residue and soot from crevices that hand-cleaning can't reach.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods, clothing, and upholstered furniture carrying smoke or mold odor.
- **Dry-cleaning and wet-cleaning protocols** for textiles, drapes, and area rugs, selected based on fiber content and dye stability.
- **Document and photo recovery** — freeze-drying and air-drying techniques for paper items, which are especially vulnerable in Soso's humid summers.

Once restored and cleared, contents are stored in our secure, climate-controlled facility until your home is ready to receive them. We coordinate the return delivery and placement so you're not managing a moving project on top of an already stressful restoration.
