## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Latimer

Every job begins with a walk-through assessment — no assumptions, no shortcuts. A project lead identifies the full scope of affected materials before any removal begins. Personal protective equipment is staged appropriately for the specific type of incident, and the work area is contained to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or HVAC pathways.

Affected porous materials — carpet, padding, drywall, wood framing — are carefully removed and packaged according to regulated waste protocols. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants applied at appropriate dwell times, not wiped down quickly and moved on. Where odor-producing compounds have penetrated structural materials, we address the source rather than masking it. All biohazardous waste is transported and disposed of through licensed channels — nothing is placed in standard residential waste streams.

Documentation is produced throughout: photographs, moisture readings, scope-of-work records. That documentation becomes part of the insurance claim file and gives you a clear record of what was done and why.
