Sewage backing up into a home is one of the most urgent — and genuinely hazardous — situations a property owner faces, and in Glendale, Mississippi, the combination of aging municipal sewer infrastructure and the region's heavy seasonal rainfall creates conditions where backups can escalate from a slow drain to several inches of standing black water in under an hour. That standing water carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that begin contaminating porous surfaces almost immediately, which is why the clock starts the moment you notice the problem.

## Why Glendale Properties Experience Sewage Backups

Glendale sits in an area of Mississippi where clay-heavy soils shift noticeably with wet and dry cycles. That ground movement puts lateral sewer lines — the pipes running from your home to the municipal main — under repeated stress. Over years, those lines develop root intrusions, low spots, and small cracks that go unnoticed until a heavy rain event saturates the ground and overwhelms the system. When the municipal main surges, sewage has nowhere to go but back through the path of least resistance: your floor drains, toilets, and tub drains.

Older homes in the area compound the problem. Properties built before the 1980s frequently have cast-iron or clay tile drain lines that have been patching themselves with mineral deposits for decades. Those deposits restrict flow, and once a backup event occurs, the line is often too compromised to simply snake clean — the underlying pipe condition needs to be addressed alongside the cleanup.

Mississippi's humid subtropical climate also means that any sewage-saturated material left in place for more than 24 to 48 hours will begin supporting secondary mold growth. Category 3 water — the classification that covers raw sewage — requires a more aggressive removal and sanitization protocol than a clean-water pipe burst, and that protocol has to account for the region's ambient humidity, which slows drying and keeps surfaces hospitable to microbial growth longer than in drier climates.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Glendale

When HomeLyft Restoration MS responds to a sewage backup in Glendale, the first priority is containment. We establish a clear boundary between affected and unaffected areas before any equipment moves through the space, because foot traffic is one of the fastest ways to spread contamination into rooms that were otherwise clean.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Extraction** — Standing sewage is removed using truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of handling solids-laden water. We do not use standard wet-vacs on Category 3 losses.
- **Removal of unsalvageable materials** — Saturated drywall, insulation, flooring, and cabinetry below the contamination line are removed. These materials cannot be dried to a safe standard once sewage-saturated; attempting to dry them in place traps pathogens inside wall cavities.
- **EPA-registered disinfection** — All structural surfaces in the affected area are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials rated for sewage contamination. This is not a general-purpose cleaner — the chemistry is selected specifically for the pathogen load associated with raw sewage.
- **Structural drying** — Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers are positioned per IICRC S500 drying science. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches documented drying goals.
- **Post-remediation verification** — Before any reconstruction begins, surface samples or ATP testing confirm that contamination levels are within acceptable limits.

Our team holds IICRC AMRT and WRT certifications, which means the technicians making decisions on your job have formal training in microbial remediation and water damage — not just general labor experience.
