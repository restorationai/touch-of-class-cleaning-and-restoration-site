## Why St. Martin Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Jackson County's coastal climate creates conditions that accelerate the breakdown of biological material. High ambient humidity — routinely above 70% for much of the year — means that infectious material left unaddressed can begin affecting surrounding surfaces and air quality within hours. Homes in the St. Martin area frequently feature carpet over concrete slab, which holds moisture and organic matter in ways that standard cleaning cannot reach. Manufactured and modular homes, common in this part of the county, often have subfloor cavities and wall assemblies that are more porous than site-built construction, requiring careful containment to prevent cross-contamination during cleanup.

Sharps and needle cleanup presents a particular concern in any community, and St. Martin is no exception. Discarded needles require handling under strict OSHA protocols — puncture-resistant containers, documented disposal, and personal protective equipment at every stage. This is not a job for general cleaning services.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in St. Martin

Every biohazard remediation begins with a discreet arrival. Unmarked vehicles and plain-clothed crews are available on request — in a community where neighbors know each other, privacy matters. Before any work begins, the affected area is assessed and contained using physical barriers and negative air pressure where appropriate, preventing the spread of airborne particles to unaffected rooms.

Our IICRC-certified team removes and packages all biological material and contaminated porous items — flooring, bedding, drywall — following EPA and OSHA guidelines for infectious waste. Hard surfaces are then treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants. Because St. Martin's humidity can cause residual moisture to linger in slab-on-grade floors and low-clearance crawl spaces, we verify surface dryness before closing out containment. Final air quality checks confirm the space is safe for re-occupancy. Throughout the process, documentation is maintained for insurance purposes and, if required, for coordination with Jackson County health or law enforcement contacts.
