A slow roof leak, a sweating crawl space, or a single plumbing failure can seed a colony inside wall cavities within 24 to 72 hours. ## Why Hurley Properties See Mold Issues

Jackson County's proximity to the Pascagoula River watershed means the soil stays saturated through much of the year, and homes built on pier-and-beam foundations — common throughout the rural stretches of Hurley — are particularly exposed. Crawl spaces in these older structures often lack adequate vapor barriers, allowing ground moisture to migrate upward into floor joists and subfloor sheathing. Once organic wood material stays damp long enough, Cladosporium, Penicillium, and the strains commonly grouped under the label "black mold" find exactly what they need to establish.

The area also sees regular tropical weather events that push water into structures through soffit gaps, ridge vents, and aging window seals. After a storm, attic insulation saturated with rainwater can harbor hidden growth for weeks before any visible sign appears at the ceiling level below. By the time a homeowner notices a stain or a musty smell, remediation is almost always more involved than a simple wipe-down.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hurley

Every job starts with a moisture investigation, not just a visual scan. The team uses thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to map where water is actively present or has recently been — because mold follows moisture, and treating only what's visible misses the problem. Once the affected zone is identified, physical containment goes up: negative air pressure barriers isolate the work area so spores don't migrate into clean rooms during removal.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces are then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent and dried to IICRC AMRT standards before any reconstruction begins. The IICRC AMRT certification the HomeLyft team carries is specifically focused on applied microbial remediation, which means the technicians on your job have been trained in the science of fungal growth, not just the mechanics of demo and cleanup.

In Hurley's older pier-and-beam homes, the process often includes crawl space encapsulation as a final step — sealing the ground with a reinforced vapor barrier so the remediated structure doesn't simply re-absorb moisture from below and restart the cycle.

## Hurley Insurance & HOA Coordination

Mold claims in Mississippi are handled differently depending on the cause of loss. If the mold traces back to a sudden, covered water event — a burst pipe, storm-driven roof damage — most homeowner policies will engage. If the source is long-term seepage or deferred maintenance, coverage is often limited or excluded.
