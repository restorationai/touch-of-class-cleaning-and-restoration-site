## Why Ovett Homes See Upholstery Problems Sooner

Jones County's humid subtropical climate keeps relative humidity high for most of the year, and a lot of homes around Ovett are set back from paved roads on gravel or dirt drives. That means dust, pollen, and fine clay particulate work into fabric weave faster than they would in a denser town setting. Add in wood-burning stoves and central air units that cycle on and off instead of running steady, and fabric upholstery in particular ends up trapping moisture between cleanings. Leather pieces fare differently, they tend to dry out and crack under the same humidity swings once the finish starts to break down, especially in rooms that get direct afternoon sun.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Ovett

We start every job with a fabric and fill identification step, because a farmhouse-style sofa with a cotton-blend cover needs a different approach than a leather recliner or a microfiber sectional. For fabric pieces we typically use low-moisture hot water extraction, pulling embedded soil and allergens out of the weave without oversaturating the padding underneath, since over-wetting in a humid climate like this one is exactly what invites mildew growth. Leather gets cleaned with a pH-balanced solution and conditioned afterward to replace oils lost to sun exposure and dry indoor heat in winter. We also pretest colorfastness on an inconspicuous spot before applying any product, which matters more here than in drier climates because fabric that's already been through a few humid summers can be more sensitive to cleaning chemicals than it looks.
