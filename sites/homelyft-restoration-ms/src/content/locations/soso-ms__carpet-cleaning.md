## Why Soso Properties See Persistent Carpet Problems

Jones County's climate sits in a humidity band that keeps indoor moisture levels elevated for most of the year. Carpets act like sponges in these conditions — fibers hold onto airborne humidity, and that retained moisture creates an ideal environment for dust mites, mildew odor, and accelerated soiling. The red clay and sandy loam soils common to the Soso area are particularly stubborn; clay particles are fine enough to work deep into carpet pile where a household vacuum cannot reach, and they carry a reddish tint that stains light-colored fibers over time.

Many homes in this part of Jones County were built in the 1970s and 1980s, when wall-to-wall carpet was standard in every room including hallways and bedrooms. Older carpet installations often have a denser, lower-pile construction that traps soil at the base of the fiber rather than the surface — which means the carpet can look relatively clean while holding a significant load of embedded debris and allergens below the visible layer.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Soso

Before any water or cleaning agent touches the fiber, we perform a thorough dry soil extraction pass. In Soso's climate, skipping this step and going straight to hot-water extraction drives clay particles deeper into the pile and can create a muddy residue that prolongs drying time in already-humid air. Our process follows the IICRC S100 standard for carpet cleaning and includes:

- **Pre-inspection and fiber identification** — different fiber types (nylon, polyester, wool blends) respond differently to heat and pH, and we adjust chemistry accordingly.
- **Pre-treatment and dwell time** — targeted spotting agents are applied to stains and high-traffic lanes and allowed to break down soils before extraction begins.
- **Truck-mounted hot-water extraction** — our truck-mounted units generate consistent heat and vacuum pressure regardless of how far the hose run extends into the home, which matters on larger rural properties.
- **Post-cleaning grooming and speed-dry passes** — in Jones County's humidity, airflow management during drying is critical. We use axial air movers positioned to maximize evaporation and reduce the window during which damp carpet could develop a musty smell.
