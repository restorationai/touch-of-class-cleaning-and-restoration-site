## Why Long Beach Homes Are Hard on Belongings After a Loss

The Gulf Coast climate is the underlying variable in almost every contents claim we see in Long Beach. Salt air accelerates corrosion on metal fixtures and electronics. High ambient humidity — regularly above 80% during summer months — means that upholstered furniture, wood cabinetry, and paper documents can begin absorbing airborne moisture within hours of a water intrusion event, not days. Mold colonization can begin in as little as 24–48 hours under those conditions.

Long Beach's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in the city were built in the decades following Hurricane Camille's 1969 rebuilding boom, using construction materials and furniture finishes that respond differently to water and smoke than modern composites do. Older lacquered wood furniture, for example, can cloud or crack if dried too aggressively, while vintage upholstery may require solvent-based cleaning rather than water-based extraction. Knowing the difference before you start is what separates careful contents restoration from accidental damage.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Long Beach

Every contents job starts with a detailed inventory. Before a single item leaves your home, our IICRC-certified technicians photograph, catalog, and condition-note each piece — furniture, clothing, electronics, artwork, documents, and personal items. That record protects you during the insurance claim process and gives us a baseline for measuring what cleaning and restoration actually accomplished.

From there, items are sorted into three streams:

- **Restorable contents** go to our climate-controlled facility for professional cleaning, deodorization, and drying. Ultrasonic cleaning tanks handle hard goods and small items with precision that hand-wiping cannot match. Ozone and hydroxyl treatment cycles address smoke and mildew odor in soft goods without the chemical residue that aerosol deodorizers leave behind.
- **Questionable items** — those with heavy contamination or structural compromise — are flagged for a secondary evaluation with you before any decision is made. Nothing is discarded without your sign-off.
- **Items you need immediately** (medications, documents, a child's essentials) are identified on day one and either cleaned on-site or fast-tracked so you have them back within 24 hours when possible.

Storage remains in our secured, climate-controlled facility until your home passes structural drying and any reconstruction milestones. We coordinate the return delivery directly with your contractor's schedule so your belongings aren't sitting in a finished room while adjacent work is still ongoing.

## Coordinating with Insurance on Long Beach Contents Claims

Contents losses are frequently the most contested part of a homeowner's insurance claim because adjusters and policyholders rarely agree on replacement versus restoration value. Our documentation process — photo inventory, condition notes, cleaning logs, and before-and-after records — is structured to support your adjuster's review rather than complicate it. We work with most major carriers and can provide itemized line-item estimates in the format adjusters expect.

If your policy includes Additional Living Expense (ALE) coverage, storage costs during restoration are typically a covered line item. We can provide the documentation your carrier needs to process that portion of the claim. Questions about what your specific policy covers should go to your agent, but we're available to speak with adjusters directly when that helps move a claim forward.
