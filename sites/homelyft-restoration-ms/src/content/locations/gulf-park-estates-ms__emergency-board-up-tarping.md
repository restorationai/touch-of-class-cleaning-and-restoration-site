## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Gulf Park Estates

When a call comes in from Gulf Park Estates, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment — photographing every breach point before anything is touched. That documentation matters immediately for insurance purposes, and it also tells the crew exactly what materials to pull from the truck: what thickness of OSB or plywood, how many linear feet of tarp, which anchor systems will hold against coastal wind loads without damaging the existing roof structure further.

For roof tarping, the tarp is never simply draped and weighted. On coastal Mississippi properties, a tarp secured only with sandbags or loose battens will fail in the next wind event. The crew runs strapping over the ridge and down both sides, anchoring to structural members where possible. On fire-damaged properties, window and door openings are boarded with screwed plywood rather than nailed — nailing into fire-weakened framing can split compromised wood, and screws allow cleaner removal during reconstruction without additional structural damage.
