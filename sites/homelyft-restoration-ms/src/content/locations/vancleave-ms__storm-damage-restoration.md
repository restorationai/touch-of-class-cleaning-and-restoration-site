## Why Vancleave Properties See Elevated Storm Damage

Vancleave's geography puts it in a uniquely vulnerable position. The community sits inland enough to avoid direct tidal surge in most storms, but close enough to the Gulf that tropical systems retain serious wind energy as they push north through Jackson County. That combination — sustained winds plus heavy rainfall — is particularly hard on the region's housing stock, which includes a significant number of pier-and-beam homes and older wood-frame structures built before modern windstorm code upgrades became standard in Mississippi.

The dense longleaf and loblolly pine canopy that makes Vancleave feel rural is also one of its biggest storm liabilities. Large pines have shallow root systems relative to their height, and saturated soil after prolonged rain makes them dramatically more likely to uproot or snap at the trunk. Tree-on-structure impacts are among the most common calls HomeLyft receives after a named storm or severe squall line moves through the area. When a tree punches through a roof deck, it opens the interior to rain intrusion within minutes — and mold colonization can begin on wet framing in as little as 24 to 48 hours in Mississippi's humidity.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Vancleave

Every job starts with a thorough damage assessment — roof, exterior walls, windows, and any below-grade spaces where water may have entered. In Vancleave, that often means checking crawl spaces carefully, because pier-and-beam construction allows wind-driven rain to infiltrate from the sides as well as from above.

After documentation (photographed and logged for your insurance carrier), the immediate priority is stopping further intrusion. That means emergency tarping over breached roof sections, boarding compromised windows, and extracting any standing water before it migrates into subfloor assemblies. Our team is IICRC certified in water damage restoration and structural drying, so drying protocols follow the IICRC S500 standard — equipment placement is based on moisture mapping, not guesswork.

Once the structure is stabilized and dried to target moisture content, reconstruction begins: roof decking, framing repairs, insulation replacement, drywall, and exterior finishes. Because HomeLyft holds Mississippi RO license #RO 2728 and carries the FORTIFIED Building — Roofing windstorm certification recognized by the Mississippi Windstorm Underwriting Association, we can complete roof replacements to FORTIFIED standards — which can meaningfully reduce future insurance premiums for Vancleave homeowners who qualify.
