## Why Vancleave Properties See Water Damage Issues

Jackson County's geography puts Vancleave in a complicated spot. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Vancleave

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We don't guess at the boundary of the wet zone — we trace it through walls, under flooring, and into ceiling assemblies if needed. That initial map drives everything: how many drying systems we deploy, where we set negative air pressure, and what materials can be dried in place versus what needs to come out.

Extraction comes next. For standing water on hard surfaces, truck-mounted extraction pulls volume fast. For water that has wicked into carpet padding, subfloor OSB, or wall cavities, we use specialty tools including weighted extraction heads and injectidry systems that drive airflow directly into enclosed spaces. Once the bulk water is out, we set industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the room volume and material types — drying is a science, not just running fans.

We monitor daily. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so you and your insurance adjuster have a documented drying record. We follow IICRC S500 drying standards throughout, and because our team holds IICRC WRT and ASD certifications, the protocol isn't improvised — it's structured and defensible if your claim gets scrutinized.
