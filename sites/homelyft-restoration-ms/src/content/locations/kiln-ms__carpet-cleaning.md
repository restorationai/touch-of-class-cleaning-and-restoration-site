## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Kiln

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment before a drop of water touches the floor. In coastal Hancock County, we routinely encounter salt-laden grit near exterior rooms and elevated moisture readings at the carpet backing — both of which change how we set up equipment and how long we allow drying time.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

1. **Pre-treatment** — A pH-balanced pre-spray is applied and allowed to dwell, loosening the bond between soil and fiber. For pet stains or biological odors, an enzyme treatment is applied first and given additional contact time.
2. **Hot water extraction** — We use truck-mounted extraction equipment that heats water to 200–230°F and recovers it at high vacuum. The heat kills dust mites and bacteria on contact; the vacuum pull removes the emulsified soil rather than pushing it deeper.
3. **Spot treatment** — Residual stains — rust from metal furniture legs, tannin from tea or coffee, or the red-clay tracking common around Kiln — are treated individually with targeted chemistry matched to the stain type.
4. **Grooming and speed-dry** — Carpet is raked to restore pile direction, and high-velocity air movers are positioned to bring drying time down. In Kiln's humidity, we do not skip this step; leaving carpet wet for more than a few hours in this climate creates conditions for mold colonization at the backing.

All drying protocols follow IICRC standards, and HomeLyft Restoration MS holds IICRC AMRT certification — relevant here because the line between a slow-drying carpet and an early-stage mold problem is shorter in coastal Mississippi than most homeowners realize.
