## Why Wiggins Properties Are Vulnerable to Sewage Backup

Stone County's soil profile is a significant factor that sets Wiggins apart from coastal Mississippi communities. The area sits on a mix of sandy loam and clay-dominant subsoils that drain unevenly — during heavy rainfall events, saturated ground puts hydrostatic pressure on aging lateral sewer lines and septic drain fields alike. When that pressure exceeds what older pipes can handle, sewage finds the path of least resistance: back through floor drains, toilets, and washing machine standpipes.

Wiggins also has a meaningful share of homes built in the 1960s through 1980s, many of which still rely on original cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Wiggins

Sewage cleanup is a multi-phase process, and cutting corners on any step creates lingering odor, hidden contamination, and potential health exposure. Here is how the work unfolds on a typical Wiggins job:

**Containment and safety assessment** — Before extraction begins, technicians identify the contamination boundary, establish negative air pressure where needed, and don appropriate PPE. In homes with crawl spaces — common in this part of Stone County — the crawl is inspected immediately, because Category 3 sewage migrates through subfloor gaps quickly.

**Extraction and removal** — Truck-mounted extraction units remove standing sewage and saturated materials. Porous materials like carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, and insulation are bagged and removed; they cannot be effectively sanitized once saturated with raw sewage.

**Cleaning and disinfection** — All affected hard surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered disinfectants. This step is methodical — walls, framing, concrete slab, and HVAC registers that may have drawn contaminated air are all addressed.

**Structural drying** — Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run for a measured drying cycle calibrated to Stone County's ambient humidity levels, which run high compared to inland states. Drying logs are maintained and moisture readings are taken daily.

**Post-remediation verification** — Before any reconstruction begins, surfaces are re-tested to confirm contamination levels are within safe thresholds. Documentation from this step supports your insurance claim.

## Local Note: Crawl Spaces and Clay Soil in Stone County

One thing that comes up repeatedly on Wiggins sewage jobs is the interaction between clay subsoil and pier-and-beam construction.
