## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Petal

Every job starts with a documented exterior assessment — photographs of every breached opening, measurements of affected roof sections, and a note of any structural instability that would affect how we anchor materials. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for sequencing the work safely.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind conditions, secured with furring strips screwed into the roof deck rather than simply weighted at the edges. In Petal's wind environment, a tarp that's only edge-weighted can become a sail in the next storm system. We extend coverage at least two feet past the damaged area on all sides to account for wind-driven rain.

Board-up work on windows and doors uses 5/8-inch plywood cut to fit and secured with case-hardened screws into the surrounding framing — not just the window casing. On older homes with deteriorated wood framing around window openings, we assess the anchor points before committing to a fastening pattern. Where framing is compromised, we use a through-bolt and backer-board method that distributes load across a wider area. Our team holds IICRC FSRT certification for fire and smoke work, which means board-up after a house fire gets treated as part of a larger loss-containment strategy, not just a carpentry task.
