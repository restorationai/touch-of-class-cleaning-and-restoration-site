## Why Latimer Homes See Upholstery Wear and Odor

Latimer sits close enough to the coast that indoor humidity rarely drops below what fabric fibers prefer, even with air conditioning running most of the year. That moisture gets trapped in cushion foam and upholstery backing, where it feeds mildew growth that no amount of surface cleaning will touch. Homes with pets or younger kids see it worse, since spills and accidents soak into that same damp foam layer and never fully dry between cleanings. Older homes in the area, many built before central HVAC was standard, also tend to have less consistent airflow through living rooms, which lets that trapped moisture linger longer in couches pushed up against exterior walls.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric type, since cotton blends, microfiber, and leather all need different chemistry and won't tolerate the same water temperature or agitation. Fabric pieces get a pre-treatment to break down oils and protein stains, followed by hot water extraction that pulls soil and moisture out of the cushion core, not just the surface weave. Leather pieces are cleaned with a pH-balanced solution and conditioned afterward so the material doesn't dry out and crack, which is a common problem once Gulf Coast humidity swings from a wet summer into a drier fall. We finish every job with controlled drying, using air movers to push moisture out of the foam and backing faster than the region's ambient humidity would allow on its own.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

We use truck-mounted and portable hot water extraction units depending on the size of the job and how far we need to run hoses into the house. For leather, we carry separate tools and cleaning agents entirely, since using a fabric-rated cleaner on leather can strip its finish. Cushion covers that are removable get cleaned separately from the frame and foam, which lets us hit stains that have worked into seams without over-wetting the rest of the piece.

## Local note

One thing we watch for in Latimer specifically: sofas and chairs positioned against exterior walls in older, pre-HVAC-retrofit homes often test damper on the back panel than the cushions look like they should be.
