## Why Moss Point Properties See Water Damage Issues

Moss Point sits low along the Pascagoula River and its tributary creeks, which means a lot of the city's older housing stock, especially homes built on pier-and-beam foundations before slab construction became standard, has crawl spaces that sit close to the water table. During heavy Gulf rain events or storm surge pushes, those crawl spaces flood before a homeowner ever sees water inside. Add in cast-iron and galvanized plumbing common in homes from the mid-20th century, and you get a city where both sudden pipe failures and slow-building crawl space saturation are regular calls. The region's year-round humidity also means materials that stay wet even a day or two start supporting mold growth faster than they would in a drier climate.

## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Moss Point

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted pumps before it has a chance to wick further into drywall, subfloor, and insulation. From there we moisture-map the structure with meters and thermal imaging so we know exactly which materials are still holding water even when the surface looks dry, which matters in homes with plaster or older lath construction where moisture hides longer than it does in modern drywall. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized for Moss Point's ambient humidity rather than a one-size-fits-all setup, because pulling moisture out of the air matters as much as pulling it out of the materials when the outdoor dew point barely drops at night. If the home predates 1978 and we need to disturb painted surfaces during drying or removal, our EPA Lead-Safe certified crew follows lead-safe work practices rather than treating it like a standard demo.

Most water losses we see in Moss Point run through homeowners insurance rather than flood policies, unless the water came from rising river or surge conditions, in which case NFIP flood coverage often applies instead and has its own documentation requirements. We photograph and log the loss as we work, moisture readings, affected materials, equipment placement, so your adjuster has what they need whether you're filing under a standard HO policy or a separate flood claim. A detail that catches a lot of Moss Point homeowners off guard: pier-and-beam homes near the river can look completely dry upstairs after a flood event while the crawl space underneath stays saturated for days, because the ventilation gaps that keep these foundations cool in summer also slow natural air movement after a flood.
