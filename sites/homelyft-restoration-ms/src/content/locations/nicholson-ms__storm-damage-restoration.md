## Why Nicholson Properties See Serious Storm Damage

Pearl River County's geography puts Nicholson in a complicated position when severe weather arrives. Tornado watches are a regular feature of spring and early fall, and the region's red-clay soil, while firm when dry, sheds water poorly once saturated, pushing moisture toward foundations and under slabs.

The housing stock here skews older and rural. Pier-and-beam construction — common throughout Pearl River County — leaves crawl spaces exposed to wind-driven debris and pooling water. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Nicholson

Every storm job starts with a thorough damage assessment — roof, exterior walls, windows, attic, and crawl space — before any work begins. Documenting everything with photos and written notes is essential for insurance claims, and it also reveals secondary damage that isn't obvious from the street: soaked insulation batts, compromised vapor barriers, or water that has tracked along a rafter and pooled in a wall cavity far from the original breach.

Once the scope is clear, the priority is stopping additional water intrusion. That typically means emergency tarping of damaged roofing or boarding compromised windows before Mississippi weather delivers another round of rain. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers, calibrated to the specific materials involved — plywood decking dries differently than tongue-and-groove pine subfloor, and both behave differently from modern OSB. Our team holds IICRC Water Damage Restoration and Applied Structural Drying certifications, so drying protocols are based on documented moisture readings, not guesswork. Where lead-based paint is a concern in older Pearl River County homes, our EPA Lead-Safe certification governs how we handle disturbed materials.

Reconstruction — replacing damaged sheathing, re-roofing, repairing soffits and fascia, restoring interior finishes — follows only after moisture readings confirm materials have reached acceptable drying targets.
