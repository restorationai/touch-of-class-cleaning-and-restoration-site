## Why Gautier Properties See Water Damage Issues

Jackson County's humid subtropical climate keeps indoor moisture elevated most of the year, which slows natural evaporation after any water event. Add in a hurricane season that regularly pushes storm surge and heavy rain bands through the area, and Gautier homes deal with water intrusion from more directions than most inland cities: roof leaks during tropical systems, yard flooding that backs up against slab foundations, and sewer line surcharge when municipal systems run at capacity. Many homes built from the 1960s through the 1980s sit on slab foundations with minimal vapor barriers underneath, so moisture that gets under the slab or along the perimeter can wick upward into drywall and flooring long after the original water is gone. Older sections of the city still have pier-and-beam or crawlspace construction, where standing water pools under the house and goes unnoticed until the smell of damp wood shows up inside.

## Our Water Removal Process in Gautier

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how much water is involved and where it collected. From there we check moisture levels in flooring, drywall, and framing with meters, because water that looks gone to the eye is often still saturating the material underneath. Air movers and dehumidifiers go in next, sized to the room and set up to dry structural materials per the IICRC S500 standard rather than just drying the surface. We recheck moisture daily until readings return to a normal range, then document the whole process with photos and readings for your records or your adjuster.

## Gautier Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most of the water claims we see locally fall into two categories: sudden events like a burst pipe or failed water heater, and storm-related intrusion from wind-driven rain or surge during tropical weather. Carriers treat these differently, and a storm-surge claim may route through flood coverage rather than a standard homeowners policy, so we document the source and timeline of the water carefully from the first visit. If your property sits in an HOA-governed subdivision, we're used to providing the kind of detailed photo and moisture log documentation some associations request before approving exterior repairs or dumpster placement during the cleanup.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Water Extraction

Standing water over an inch or two usually calls for pump extraction before air movers do any good, otherwise you're just blowing air across a pond. For water that has worked into carpet pad, hardwood, or engineered flooring, we use low-profile extraction tools that pull moisture without tearing up the material unnecessarily. Where water has reached wall cavities, we may need to drill small weep holes near the baseboard to let trapped water and air movement reach the inside of the wall, a step that saves most homeowners from a full drywall tear-out.

## Local note

Homes near the lower-lying areas close to the Pascagoula River and the bayous feeding into it tend to have a higher water table, which means crawlspace and slab-edge moisture takes longer to fully dry even after extraction looks complete on the surface.
