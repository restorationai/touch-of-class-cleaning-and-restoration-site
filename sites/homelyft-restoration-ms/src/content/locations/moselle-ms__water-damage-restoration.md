## Why Moselle Properties See Water Damage Issues

Jones County averages roughly 55 inches of rainfall per year — well above the national average — and Moselle's position along the lower Leaf River watershed means that heavy rain events can saturate ground faster than drainage infrastructure handles it. When soil reaches saturation, hydrostatic pressure pushes groundwater against crawl-space walls and through slab cracks in ways that a single wet season can accelerate noticeably.

The housing stock here skews toward ranch-style and older craftsman builds, many with pier-and-beam construction that creates an open crawl space beneath the living area. That design is practical for the region's soil movement, but it also means a slow plumbing leak or a backed-up condensate drain can go undetected for weeks — long enough for mold to colonize framing members. Mold can begin forming on wet organic material in as little as 24 to 48 hours under Moselle's warm, humid conditions, which is why the window between water intrusion and professional extraction matters so much.

Older homes in the area may also contain lead-based paint in window trim and door casings. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Moselle

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the obvious wet zone — because in pier-and-beam homes common to this area, water that enters at a crawl-space vent can travel laterally along floor joists well past the point of visible damage.

**Step 1 — Extraction:** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from flooring, carpet, and subfloor assemblies. In homes with hardwood over a crawl space, we pay close attention to the underside of the subfloor, which often retains moisture even after the surface feels dry.

**Step 2 — Structural drying:** We deploy commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for psychrometric drying. Drying logs are recorded daily so you and your insurance adjuster have a documented drying timeline.

**Step 3 — Monitoring and clearance:** Moisture readings are taken at each check until materials reach target moisture content for the region's ambient conditions. We don't pull equipment early to save a day — we pull it when the numbers confirm the structure is dry.
