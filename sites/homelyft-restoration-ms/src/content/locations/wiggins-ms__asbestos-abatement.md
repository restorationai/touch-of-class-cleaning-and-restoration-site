## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Wiggins

Every abatement project starts with a thorough inspection and, where indicated, bulk sample collection sent to an accredited laboratory. We do not guess at what materials contain asbestos — confirmed testing drives every decision. Once results are back, we build a scope of work that accounts for the specific materials involved, the size of the affected area, and how the building is currently being used.

Containment comes next. We establish negative air pressure zones using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers, seal off HVAC systems to prevent fiber migration, and post the required regulatory signage. Removal is performed wet — wetting asbestos-containing materials before handling them is a proven method for suppressing fiber release, and it's required under EPA and Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality (MDEQ) guidelines. All waste is double-bagged in approved containers, labeled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. The job isn't closed until air clearance testing confirms fiber counts are within safe limits.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically — a common request in Wiggins homes from the 1960s and 70s — we take extra care with the prep work. Those ceilings can release fibers rapidly when disturbed, so containment and wetting protocols are especially critical before any scraping begins.
