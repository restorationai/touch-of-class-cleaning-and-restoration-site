## Why Ocean Springs Properties See Elevated Storm Damage

The combination of coastal exposure, mature tree canopy, and a significant share of pre-1980 wood-frame construction makes Ocean Springs one of the more demanding storm-damage environments on the Mississippi Gulf Coast. Live oaks and longleaf pines that give the city its character also become projectiles in a strong thunderstorm — root systems in the sandy, moisture-retentive soil here don't anchor the way they do in harder inland ground, so trees topple at lower wind speeds than homeowners expect. When a trunk or major limb punches through a roof, the breach rarely stays dry for long: Gulf humidity means that even a few hours of exposure can begin the moisture intrusion cycle that leads to mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours.

Older homes in the city — particularly the craftsman and cottage-style houses that define much of Ocean Springs's residential character — often have original tongue-and-groove sheathing under asphalt shingles rather than modern OSB. That sheathing can absorb and hold water in ways that modern panels don't, making thorough moisture mapping essential before any drywall or insulation work begins. Homes built before 1978 also require our EPA Lead-Safe certified protocols any time storm damage exposes painted surfaces during debris removal or reconstruction.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Ocean Springs

Every job starts with a full structural and moisture assessment. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map water intrusion behind walls and under flooring — areas that look dry to the eye but are already harboring elevated moisture content. That data drives the drying plan and gives your insurance adjuster documented evidence of the actual scope of loss.

Once the assessment is complete, the sequence typically runs: emergency tarping and board-up to stop ongoing weather intrusion, debris removal (including tree limbs and displaced roofing materials), structural drying using industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, and then controlled demolition of materials that cannot be dried in place. Our team holds IICRC Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD) certifications, and all drying is conducted per the IICRC S500 standard — which matters when your adjuster is reviewing the claim file.

For properties with roof damage, our FORTIFIED Building Roofing – MS Windstorm certification means we understand the specific construction standards that can qualify a home for improved insurance ratings under Mississippi's FORTIFIED program. If your roof needs replacement anyway, it's worth a conversation about whether building back to FORTIFIED Gold or Silver standards makes sense for your property.

## Insurance Coordination for Ocean Springs Storm Claims

Mississippi Gulf Coast storm claims can be complicated by the overlap between wind coverage (typically homeowners) and flood coverage (typically a separate NFIP or private flood policy). We photograph and document damage in a format that separates wind-driven water intrusion from ground-level flooding where possible — a distinction that matters significantly when two different adjusters are reviewing the same loss. We work directly with most major carriers and can provide the moisture logs, thermal images, and scope-of-work documentation that adjusters need to process claims efficiently. We do not determine coverage — that's your adjuster's role — but we make sure the evidence supports an accurate assessment.
