Gulf Coast humidity does something to fabric furniture that inland homes rarely deal with: even with the AC running, indoor relative humidity in Pascagoula households often sits high enough that upholstery never fully dries between spills, pet accidents, or everyday use. ## Why Pascagoula Homes See More Upholstery Wear

Jackson County's climate is hot and humid for most of the year, with salt air drifting in off the Gulf and Mississippi Sound. That salt air is corrosive to more than metal; it works into fabric fibers and leather finishes, leaving a slightly tacky residue that attracts dust and grime faster than furniture would collect it further inland. Many Pascagoula households also deal with sand, sunscreen, and boat or dock residue tracked in from routine coastal living, plus the heavier soil load that comes with maritime and shipyard work clothes brushing against sofas and recliners after a shift. Add in hurricane season, when windows stay shut and AC units run overtime, and you get stretches where fabric furniture barely gets fresh air at all. The result is upholstery that looks fine on the surface but smells musty or feels damp to the touch, especially in ground-level dens and Florida rooms that sit closer to outdoor humidity.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Pascagoula

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, since a wrong cleaning method on the wrong material can set a stain permanently or damage leather finishes. Cotton and linen blends get a low-moisture hot water extraction that lifts embedded soil without oversaturating the cushion fill, which matters in a climate where excess moisture doesn't evaporate quickly. Leather upholstery gets a pH-balanced cleaner and conditioner pass instead, since leather in coastal humidity tends to dry out between the salt exposure and AC cycling, leading to cracking if it's not conditioned regularly. For fabric pieces we suspect are holding moisture from past spills or humidity intrusion, we run a moisture reading before we start so we know whether we're dealing with a surface clean or something that needs deeper extraction and forced air drying. Every piece gets a final pass with fans or air movers set to account for the ambient humidity in the room, because in Pascagoula, letting furniture air-dry on its own can take twice as long as it would in a drier climate.
