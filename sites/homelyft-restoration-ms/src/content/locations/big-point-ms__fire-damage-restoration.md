## Why Big Point Properties Face Particular Fire Damage Challenges

Big Point sits in a stretch of Jackson County where the housing stock leans toward older wood-frame construction and manufactured homes — building types that respond to fire and heat very differently than modern stick-built houses. Wood-frame homes built decades ago often used heart pine framing, which is dense and durable but chars deeply and retains smoke compounds in its grain long after the visible damage is cleaned. Manufactured homes, common throughout this part of the county, can experience rapid structural compromise during a fire because their lightweight engineered components lose integrity quickly under heat.

The coastal humidity compounds every step of the restoration. Soot that might stay powdery and removable in a drier climate becomes sticky and corrosive here within 24 to 48 hours, etching into glass, metal fixtures, and painted surfaces. Firefighting water — sometimes thousands of gallons — soaks into subfloors and wall cavities and, in Jackson County's heat and humidity, can begin supporting mold colonization in as little as 48 to 72 hours if not aggressively dried. Fire damage and water damage become one problem, not two.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Big Point

Every fire loss is assessed from the outside in. The first step on-site is a structural safety evaluation — confirming that the roof deck, load-bearing walls, and floor systems are stable enough for the crew to work safely. In older Big Point homes with pier-and-beam foundations, this matters: fire can compromise the sill plates and floor joists that sit closest to the ground, and those failures aren't always visible from the doorway.

Once the structure is confirmed safe, the process moves through these stages:

- **Emergency board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry
- **Contents inventory and pack-out** — salvageable belongings are documented, packed, and transported to a climate-controlled facility for cleaning and deodorization
- **Dry-out and structural drying** using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers, calibrated to Jackson County's ambient humidity levels
- **Soot and smoke residue removal** — dry chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and wet cleaning methods matched to each surface type (painted drywall, wood trim, masonry, metal)
- **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment** for deep odor neutralization in wall cavities and HVAC systems
- **Structural repairs and reconstruction** up to full pre-loss condition

The team holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, which matters in Big Point's older housing stock — any fire restoration that disturbs painted surfaces in a pre-1978 home requires lead-safe work practices by federal law.
