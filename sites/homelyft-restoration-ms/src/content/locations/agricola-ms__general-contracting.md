## Why Agricola Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Stone County's climate is unforgiving to building materials. Average annual humidity in this part of Mississippi hovers well above 70 percent for much of the year, and the region sits within a wind-exposure zone that FORTIFIED building standards were specifically designed to address. Many homes in and around Agricola were built in eras when vapor barriers were an afterthought and roof decking was nailed rather than ring-shanked — details that matter enormously when you're opening up walls for a remodel or adding square footage.

Beyond moisture, the soil profile in Stone County includes expansive clay layers that shift seasonally. That movement telegraphs into slab foundations and pier-and-beam systems alike, meaning a kitchen remodel that ignores a slightly out-of-level floor can result in cabinet gaps and tile cracks within a year. A general contractor working in this area needs to read the structure before drawing up a scope of work, not after.
