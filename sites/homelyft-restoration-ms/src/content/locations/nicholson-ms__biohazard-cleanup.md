## Why Nicholson Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Pearl River County's housing stock includes a significant share of older pier-and-beam and wood-frame construction — the kind of home where subflooring, crawl spaces, and wall cavities readily absorb biological material that may not be visible at the surface. Porous concrete block foundations common in the area can also wick and retain biological fluids in ways that solid poured concrete does not, requiring additional treatment steps that a standard surface wipe-down would miss entirely.

The regional climate adds another layer: Pearl River County averages over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and high relative humidity persists through much of the year.
