## Why DeLisle Properties Experience Sewage Backup Problems

Harrison County's coastal geography creates conditions that stress both municipal sewer connections and private septic systems. DeLisle's soil profile — a mix of sandy loam and clay pockets — can shift after heavy Gulf rain events, putting lateral sewer lines under pressure they weren't designed to handle. When those lines crack or root intrusion creates a partial blockage, a single heavy-use morning is enough to push sewage back through floor drains, toilets, or washing machine standpipes.

Many homes in the DeLisle and Pass Christian area were built in the 1970s and 1980s on slab foundations or with shallow crawl spaces. Cast-iron drain lines from that era are now at or past their expected service life, and corroded pipe walls collapse inward without warning. Septic systems on larger lots — common in the more rural stretches of DeLisle — can overflow when drain fields become saturated after prolonged rainfall, sending effluent into yard areas and occasionally back toward the structure itself.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in DeLisle

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 "black water" contamination under the IICRC S500 standard — the most hazardous category, carrying bacteria, viruses, and parasites. The process we follow reflects that classification.

**Assessment and containment** come first. We identify the source, confirm the sewer line or septic issue has been addressed by a plumber, and establish containment barriers to keep contaminated air from migrating into unaffected rooms. In slab-foundation homes common to this area, we probe for moisture migration beneath the slab using thermal imaging and moisture meters before any removal begins.

**Extraction and controlled demolition** follow. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and saturated subfloor sections — that have contacted black water cannot be dried and kept; they are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Mississippi DEQ guidelines. We document every removed material with photographs for your insurance file.

**Sanitization** uses EPA-registered disinfectants applied to all affected structural surfaces. We treat the area in passes, allowing appropriate dwell time, rather than a single spray-and-wipe approach. Odor control — including hydroxyl or thermal fogging where the contamination spread into wall cavities — is part of this phase, not an afterthought.

**Structural drying** closes the job. Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings return to regional baseline levels. In DeLisle's climate, ambient humidity is high enough year-round that residential fans and open windows are not an adequate substitute.

## DeLisle Insurance Coordination for Sewage Losses

Sewage backup claims have specific documentation requirements that differ from standard water damage claims.
