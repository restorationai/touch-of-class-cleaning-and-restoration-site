## Why Biloxi Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining factor. Biloxi sits in a subtropical zone where summer temperatures regularly push past 90°F and indoor humidity can spike quickly if HVAC systems are undersized or lose power — both common after storm events. In those conditions, infectious material and biological residue can begin to affect porous surfaces within hours, not days. Older construction found throughout the city, including wood-framed homes built before modern vapor barriers were standard, absorbs contaminants into subfloor assemblies and wall cavities in ways that aren't visible from the surface. That hidden contamination is exactly what makes professional biohazard remediation — not a surface wipe-down — the appropriate response.

Casino-corridor properties and short-term rentals along the beach-adjacent blocks also create a distinct pattern: high occupant turnover, deferred maintenance, and situations that may go unnoticed for longer than they would in owner-occupied homes. Property managers in those segments often need discreet, fast-turnaround service to meet health code requirements before the next occupancy.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Biloxi

Every job begins with a contained assessment — no unnecessary personnel, no open doors. Our OSHA-trained technicians identify the full scope of affected materials before any removal begins. This matters in Biloxi's older housing stock, where contamination can wick into original hardwood flooring, plaster substrates, or crawl-space framing that sits close to grade and retains moisture.

Affected porous materials — flooring, drywall, insulation — are bagged, sealed, and transported in compliance with Mississippi Department of Environmental Quality and EPA guidelines for regulated biological waste. Non-porous surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens and infectious material. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the process to address airborne particulates. Once remediation is complete, the space is documented photographically for insurance and, where required, for property management records.
