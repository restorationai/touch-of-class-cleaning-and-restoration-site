## Why Wiggins Properties See Mold Issues

Wiggins sits in a climate zone that combines high annual rainfall — Stone County averages well over 60 inches per year — with warm temperatures that persist well into autumn. That combination creates a long mold season that effectively never fully closes. Unlike coastal cities where salt air is the dominant moisture driver, Wiggins properties deal with inland humidity that saturates soil around foundations, keeps crawl spaces damp for weeks after a rain event, and traps moisture inside wall cavities when air conditioning cycles off at night.

The housing stock compounds the problem. Much of Wiggins was built during the mid-20th century, when vapor barriers under crawl spaces were either thin or absent entirely, and ventilation in attic spaces was minimal by modern standards. In homes like these, mold often establishes in the floor joists and subfloor long before a homeowner notices a musty smell in the living area above. By the time visible black or green growth appears on a surface, the hidden colony behind it is frequently weeks or months older.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Wiggins

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not a quick visual scan, but a systematic check of moisture readings in walls, floors, and ceiling assemblies using calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging. In Wiggins homes with pier-and-beam construction, that inspection always includes the crawl space, because that's where the source is most commonly found.

Once the scope is mapped, containment goes up before any removal begins. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed and bagged per IICRC AMRT standards. Structural surfaces that can be salvaged are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and dried to verified moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which matters when you're filing an insurance claim or need to demonstrate clearance to a buyer during a real estate transaction.
