## Why Ocean Springs Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

The Gulf Coast climate is the defining factor here. Ocean Springs receives well over 60 inches of rainfall annually, and the humidity alone accelerates deterioration once a structure is breached. A broken window or a lifted roof section that might stay relatively dry for 48 hours in a drier climate can admit enough moisture in a single afternoon storm to soak insulation, warp sheathing, and begin the 24–48 hour clock on mold colonization.

The city's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Ocean Springs has a meaningful concentration of older craftsman bungalows and mid-century wood-frame homes — many of them in the historic core near Washington Avenue and the downtown arts district — where roof decking and window framing have decades of weathering behind them. Aged fascia boards and deteriorated glazing compound hold less securely than modern construction, which affects how anchoring points are selected during a board-up. A fastener pattern that works cleanly on OSB sheathing may need to be adapted on heart-pine boards that have hardened over 70 or 80 years.

Fire damage calls in Ocean Springs also present a specific challenge: post-fire structural openings often need to be secured before the fire marshal clears the scene, and the combination of smoke-compromised framing and water from suppression efforts means the crew has to assess load-bearing integrity before installing any board or tarp anchor.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Ocean Springs

Every job starts with a rapid perimeter assessment — cataloging every breach point before a single sheet of plywood is cut. That inventory matters for two reasons: it drives the material list so the crew isn't making return trips, and it produces the photographic documentation your insurance adjuster will need to establish the scope of loss.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind conditions, secured with lumber battens screwed into undamaged decking rather than simply weighted at the edges. Along the Gulf Coast, a tarp that isn't mechanically fastened is a liability — wind gusts during a passing storm system can pull a weighted tarp free and turn it into a projectile or leave the roof re-exposed overnight.

Board-up work on windows and doors uses 5/8-inch plywood cut to fit and secured with structural screws rather than nails, which back out under repeated wind pressure. For fire-damaged openings where the frame itself is compromised, we install temporary framing before the panel goes in. Every opening is sealed at the perimeter with foam backer to reduce wind-driven rain infiltration at the edges — a step that makes a measurable difference in how dry the interior stays while the property awaits full restoration.

We are an IICRC Certified Firm with IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration) credentials, which means the team assessing your fire-damaged opening understands what's happening inside the structure, not just at the surface.
