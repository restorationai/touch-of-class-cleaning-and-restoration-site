From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen fire that leaves smoke embedded in every surface, property damage rarely follows a convenient schedule or a tidy scope. Heritage Restoration LLC handles the full range of residential, commercial, and multi-family restoration work across central Minnesota, mitigation, structural drying, mold remediation, reconstruction, and the specialty services that fall between those categories. One company, one point of contact, from the first call through the finished repair.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still bonding to surfaces, or a storm has left a structure exposed, the clock matters. Water damage response focuses on stopping the source, extracting standing water, and drying the structure to IICRC S500 standards before secondary damage, swelling framing, buckled flooring, mold colonization, takes hold. Fire and smoke restoration addresses not just charred material but the soot that travels far beyond the flame, the odor that embeds in insulation and HVAC systems, and the corrosive residue that continues damaging surfaces if left untreated. Storm damage work covers wind-driven water intrusion, hail damage to roofing and siding, and debris impact. Biohazard cleanup is handled with the containment protocols and disposal compliance that the situation requires. Each of these services has its own page with process detail, equipment, and what to expect on-site.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back. After the drying equipment comes out and the damaged material is removed, Heritage Restoration LLC handles the rebuild, framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because the same company that documented the loss is doing the repair, there are no handoff gaps, no disputes between a mitigation contractor and a separate GC over what was removed, and no second mobilization cost. Reconstruction services are also available for properties that need renovation work independent of a prior loss event.

## Specialty services

Some problems don't fit neatly into a single category. Mold inspection and remediation, backed by IICRC AMRT certification, addresses both the visible growth and the moisture condition driving it. Odor removal goes beyond masking: thermal fogging, hydroxyl treatment, and encapsulation reach the materials where odor compounds actually live. Board-up and emergency tarping protect a structure immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism so that secondary damage doesn't compound an already difficult situation. EPA Lead-Safe certification covers properties where older building materials are disturbed during remediation or reconstruction.

The advantage of working with a single restoration contractor from mitigation through final walkthrough is straightforward: the scope doesn't get lost between companies, the documentation stays consistent for your insurance carrier, and you're not managing two separate timelines.
