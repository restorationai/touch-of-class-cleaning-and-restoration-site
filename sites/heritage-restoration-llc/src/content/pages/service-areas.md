Heritage Restoration LLC serves homeowners and property managers across a wide stretch of central Minnesota. Scheduling is handled during business hours, call **(320) 733-8868** and a team member will confirm whether your address falls within our coverage and get a visit on the calendar as quickly as possible.

## Where we work

Our crew is based in Little Falls, which puts us near the geographic center of Morrison County and within practical driving distance of a large portion of the central Minnesota lake country. The region is a mix of older in-town housing stock, rural acreages, and lake-adjacent cabins, each with its own set of moisture, drainage, and structural considerations. We work across this varied landscape regularly, from riverfront properties along the Mississippi to agricultural-area homes where basement water intrusion and crawl space humidity are seasonal facts of life.

## Coverage by distance

The cities and townships closest to Little Falls, including Royalton, Pierz, Swanville, and Randall, are our most routine service stops. From there, coverage extends outward to communities like Brainerd and Baxter to the north, St. Cloud and Sartell to the south, and Staples and Long Prairie to the west. These areas represent the core of our dispatch range, where we can typically reach a property within the same business day a call comes in.

For locations at the edge of that range, farther corners of Morrison, Todd, Crow Wing, and Stearns counties, scheduling may shift to the next available day depending on current workload and the nature of the job. Large or complex losses (significant water intrusion, fire damage affecting multiple rooms, or mold remediation requiring containment) may justify a longer drive; we evaluate those on a case-by-case basis.

Traffic is rarely the limiting factor in this part of Minnesota, but road conditions in winter, particularly on county roads and lake-access routes, can affect arrival windows. We factor that in when scheduling during freeze-thaw season.
