When water soaks through your subfloor, smoke odor settles into your drywall, or you spot the dark bloom of mold behind a baseboard, Heritage Restoration LLC is the team Little Falls homeowners call to get the damage assessed, contained, and reversed. We handle water damage mitigation, fire and smoke restoration, mold remediation, and structural drying, and we work directly with insurance carriers to keep the process from becoming a second emergency.

## Services we handle

**Water damage mitigation**, Whether it's a burst pipe in January or a sump pump that quit during a heavy rain, standing water starts degrading flooring, framing, and drywall within hours. We extract, dry, and document everything your adjuster will need.

**Fire and smoke restoration**, Smoke residue doesn't stay where the fire was. Soot travels through ductwork and embeds in surfaces rooms away from the source. We trace it, treat it, and remove the odor rather than masking it.

**Mold remediation**, Minnesota's freeze-thaw cycles and humid summers create ideal conditions for mold to colonize after any moisture event. If you can see it or smell it, that musty, earthy odor in a closed room, it's worth having it properly assessed and removed.

**Structural drying**, Water behind plaster or under hardwood can read dry at the surface while the framing beneath stays saturated for weeks. Our structural drying process uses calibrated equipment and moisture mapping to confirm the building is actually dry, not just dry to the touch.
