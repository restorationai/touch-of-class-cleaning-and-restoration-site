## For urgent situations

Call **(320) 733-8868** during business hours and describe what you're seeing, standing water, smoke odor, a sewage backup, whatever the situation is. The more detail you can give on that first call, the faster we can arrive with the right equipment. If you reach us outside of business hours, leave a voicemail with your address and a callback number and we'll get back to you as soon as we're available to schedule.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If you're planning ahead, a mold inspection, a post-storm assessment, a reconstruction estimate after an older loss, email us at **support@heritagermn.com** or use the contact form on this page. For most non-emergency requests, expect a response within one business day. Having photos of the affected area ready when you reach out helps us give you a more accurate initial assessment before we ever set foot on the property.
