## Why Randall Properties See Significant Storm Damage

Morrison County's geography puts Randall directly in the path of convective storm tracks that build over the prairie and intensify as they approach the central Minnesota lakes region. Hail events here can shift from pea-sized to golf-ball-sized within minutes, punching through asphalt shingles and leaving roof decking exposed to whatever rain follows. Wind gusts during severe thunderstorm warnings routinely exceed 60 mph in open stretches along the county roads surrounding town, and the mature tree canopy that shades older Randall homes becomes a liability when those trees fail.

The housing stock in and around Randall leans toward mid-century construction with wood-framed walls, older window flashing, and in many cases, original fascia and soffit that have been painted over rather than replaced. When a storm drives water horizontally, those older assemblies absorb moisture quickly and release it slowly. A roof breach that looks minor from the street can mean wet insulation, saturated ceiling joists, and the beginning of mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours if the interior isn't dried promptly.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Randall

After a storm, the sequence matters as much as the speed. When we arrive, the first priority is a safe inspection of the structure: checking for compromised load-bearing elements, exposed electrical, and any gas line concerns before crews enter damaged areas. Once the structure is confirmed safe, we photograph and document every affected surface for the insurance claim before a single piece of debris is moved.

Temporary weatherproofing comes next. Roof tarps, board-up panels for broken windows, and door-frame bracing stop the weather from continuing to enter while permanent repairs are planned. From there, we move to interior moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find water that has migrated behind walls or under flooring. Structural drying equipment, including industrial air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers, runs until readings confirm the framing and subfloor have returned to acceptable moisture levels. Every reading is logged so the insurance adjuster has a complete drying record, not just a verbal summary.

## Randall Insurance Coordination

Storm claims in Minnesota are governed by state statutes that give carriers specific timelines for acknowledgment and adjustment, and most standard homeowner policies in Morrison County cover sudden storm damage to the structure. Where claims get complicated is in the documentation: adjusters want to see the damage before it is disturbed, moisture readings taken at multiple depths, and a clear line between storm-caused damage and pre-existing wear.

## Local Note

One pattern we see in Morrison County storm work: older homes near the river corridor often have crawl spaces with minimal vapor barriers, and when a storm drives significant rain against the foundation or overwhelms gutters, that crawl space can accumulate moisture that never shows up on a first-floor inspection. If your Randall home has a crawl space and you've had a significant storm event, it's worth asking any restoration contractor to check below the floor deck, not just the visible ceiling and walls. Moisture trapped in a crawl space will eventually wick upward into the subfloor and framing, and it tends to be discovered months later as a mold problem rather than immediately as a water problem.
