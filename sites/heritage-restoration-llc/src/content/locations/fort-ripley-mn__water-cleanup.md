## Why Fort Ripley Properties See Water Damage Differently

Morrison County's geology works against homeowners during wet seasons. The soils in this part of central Minnesota shift between sandy river-bottom deposits near the Mississippi and denser clay-loam farther inland, and clay-heavy ground doesn't drain quickly. When snowmelt accelerates in March and April, or when a heavy summer storm drops several inches overnight, that water has nowhere to go fast. Basement walls in older Fort Ripley homes, many of them built with poured concrete or concrete block rather than modern waterproofed systems, absorb hydrostatic pressure and develop seepage that can go unnoticed until flooring begins to buckle or a musty smell takes hold.

The region's cold winters add another layer of risk. Pipes routed through uninsulated crawl spaces or exterior walls, common in homes built before modern energy codes, are vulnerable to freezing and sudden bursts when temperatures drop hard in January and February. A burst pipe in a crawl space can deposit hundreds of gallons under a home before anyone notices the pressure drop.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Fort Ripley

Every water cleanup job begins with moisture mapping. Before a single piece of furniture moves, technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled, including behind wall cavities and under subfloor assemblies. Water follows the path of least resistance, and in older construction it often travels farther than the visible stain suggests.

Once the scope is documented, truck-mounted extraction equipment removes standing water and pulls moisture from carpet pads, hardwood subfloors, and concrete slabs. Structural drying follows using industrial air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage, not residential units from a hardware store. Drying is monitored against the IICRC S500 standard, with readings logged daily so you and your insurance adjuster have a clear record of progress. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, saturated insulation, swollen OSB, compromised drywall, are documented before removal so the scope is defensible for your claim.
