## Why Pierz Properties See Roofing Wear Faster Than Expected

Morrison County sits in a climatic band where annual snowfall accumulation is heavy enough to stress roof decking, but temperatures fluctuate enough that the snow melts and refreezes repeatedly rather than staying as a stable pack. That cycle is harder on roofing systems than either a consistently cold climate or a mild one. Asphalt shingles lose granules faster when they are repeatedly wetted, frozen, and dried.
