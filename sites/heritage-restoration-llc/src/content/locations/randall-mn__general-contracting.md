## What Randall's Housing Stock and Climate Mean for Renovation Work

Morrison County's rural communities, including Randall, have a significant share of homes built between the 1940s and the 1980s. That era of construction brings specific challenges: knob-and-tube or early aluminum wiring that must be evaluated before walls are opened, plaster-and-lath interiors that respond differently to moisture than modern drywall, and foundation systems sized for the loads of a different generation of appliances and living patterns.

Minnesota's freeze-thaw cycle is the other constant. Frost heave affects slabs, garage floors, and exterior steps in ways that compound over time. A kitchen remodel that seems straightforward can reveal a subfloor that has shifted, or a bathroom renovation can uncover a crawl space with inadequate vapor barrier, issues that are far more common in central Minnesota than in milder climates. Addressing them properly during a remodel costs less than discovering them after the new tile is set.

Heritage Restoration LLC holds an EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters directly here: homes built before 1978 require lead-safe work practices during any renovation that disturbs painted surfaces. In a community like Randall where pre-1978 housing is common, that credential is not a formality, it is a legal and safety requirement that every contractor working on older homes must meet.
