## Why Royalton Properties See Water Damage Issues

Morrison County's clay-heavy soils don't drain quickly. When snowmelt or heavy rain saturates the ground faster than it can absorb, that hydrostatic pressure finds the path of least resistance, usually a foundation crack, a window well, or a floor drain that backs up. Homes built in and around Royalton span several decades, and older construction often relied on drainage tile systems that were never designed for today's rainfall intensity. A tile system that worked fine for thirty years can fail quietly, leaving water to migrate under a concrete slab or behind block foundation walls long before a homeowner notices soft flooring or a musty smell.

Central Minnesota winters add another layer. Pipes in uninsulated exterior walls or crawl spaces are vulnerable when temperatures drop sharply overnight, and a burst pipe can release dozens of gallons before the pressure drop is noticed. The damage from that kind of event isn't just wet carpet, water travels along joists, wicks into insulation, and pools in cavities that standard visual inspection won't catch.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Royalton

When we arrive at a Royalton property, the first step is a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not a visual walkthrough. Water hides. It moves horizontally through subfloor assemblies and vertically through wall cavities, and what reads dry at the surface is often still saturated an inch deeper. We map the full extent of the loss before any equipment is placed.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete. Once bulk water is removed, we set drying equipment, commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create the airflow patterns the IICRC S500 standard calls for. Drying is monitored daily with documented moisture readings so you and your insurance adjuster can see the progress in writing.

If building materials are too saturated to dry in place, saturated insulation, swollen OSB, compromised drywall, we remove only what cannot be saved. Selective demolition, not wholesale gut jobs, is the goal. Heritage Restoration LLC holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, which matters in older Royalton homes where disturbing building materials requires proper containment and testing protocols.

## Royalton Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water losses, burst pipes, appliance failures, storm-driven water intrusion that enters through a compromised opening. What they typically exclude is long-term seepage or maintenance-related deterioration. We document losses thoroughly: photographs, moisture logs, equipment placement records, and scope notes that give your adjuster a clear picture of what happened and what it takes to fix it. We work with major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster to keep the claim moving. If your policy has a question about cause of loss, thorough documentation is what resolves it.
