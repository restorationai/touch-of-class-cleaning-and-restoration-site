## Why Royalton Properties See Roofing Problems

Morrison County's winters regularly push well below zero, and the warming cycles in late winter create the ice-dam conditions that shorten roof lifespans across this part of Minnesota. Royalton's housing stock includes a mix of older farmhouse-style homes and more recent construction along the county roads feeding into town. Older homes often have lower roof pitches and minimal attic insulation by today's standards, which accelerates ice dam formation: warm air escapes through the attic, melts snow at the ridge, and that meltwater refreezes at the cold eaves. Over several seasons, that cycle lifts shingles, compromises flashing, and eventually finds its way into the wall cavity.

Beyond ice, the region's heavy spring snowmelt can stress gutters and fascia that were already weakened by winter, and summer hail events, which track through central Minnesota with some regularity, can bruise asphalt shingles in ways that are not obvious from the ground but dramatically reduce their remaining service life.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Royalton

Every project starts with a detailed inspection that goes beyond counting missing shingles. We assess decking condition, attic ventilation, flashing at chimneys and valleys, and the state of the drip edge, because replacing shingles over compromised decking or inadequate ventilation just restarts the clock on the same problems.

For full replacements, we strip to the deck, inspect and replace any soft or delaminated sheathing, and install an ice-and-water shield along the eaves and in valleys before any felt or synthetic underlayment goes down. In Royalton's climate, that eave protection is not optional. We then install the chosen shingle system, re-flash all penetrations, and replace or upgrade gutters and fascia where needed. Material selection is discussed with the homeowner before any work begins: heavier architectural shingles with a strong wind rating hold up better in Morrison County than lighter three-tab products, and we walk through the tradeoffs plainly.

For new construction or additions, we coordinate with the general contractor and schedule around Morrison County's permit process. Royalton falls under Morrison County jurisdiction for building permits, and we handle the documentation on our end.
