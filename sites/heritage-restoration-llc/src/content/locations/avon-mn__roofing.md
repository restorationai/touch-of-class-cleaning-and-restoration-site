## Why Avon Properties See Roofing Deterioration

Central Minnesota's climate does not ease up on roofing systems. Avon experiences genuine four-season stress: heavy snow loads in January and February, ice damming along eaves when attic heat escapes unevenly, and then the UV exposure and summer humidity that accelerate granule loss on asphalt shingles. Homes built in the mid-twentieth century, common throughout Stearns County, often have lower roof pitches that were designed for the materials and ventilation standards of their era. Those pitches can trap snow longer and allow standing water to form at the eaves when drainage is slow.

Beyond the weather, Avon's proximity to agricultural land means airborne dust and debris are a consistent factor. Fine particulate settling into shingle surfaces over years can accelerate moisture retention and moss or algae growth, especially on north-facing roof planes that see limited direct sun. A roof that looks structurally sound from the street may have underlayment or decking that has been quietly absorbing moisture for a season or two.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Avon

Every project begins with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system, not just the surface layer. We check decking for soft spots, examine flashing at chimneys, valleys, and penetrations, and assess ventilation to make sure a new roof will perform the way it should over the long term. Skipping that step and simply laying new shingles over compromised decking is one of the most common reasons a replacement roof underperforms in Minnesota's climate.

Once the scope is clear, we strip the existing material down to the deck, replace any damaged sheathing, and install a code-compliant underlayment system before a single shingle goes down. For Avon homes, we pay particular attention to ice-and-water shield installation along the eaves and in valleys, since those are the zones most vulnerable to ice dam infiltration during the freeze-thaw cycles that run through late winter and early spring. Final installation follows manufacturer specifications so that material warranties remain intact.
