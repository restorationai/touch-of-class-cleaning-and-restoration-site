## Why Brainerd Properties See Accelerated Roof Wear

The Brainerd Lakes area sits in a climate zone where annual snowfall accumulation can load a roof with significant weight for weeks at a time, and where ice dams form reliably on homes that lack adequate attic insulation and ventilation. Ice dams are not just a cosmetic problem. When meltwater backs up behind a dam and sits against the roof deck, it finds every nail hole, every dried-out sealant joint, and every lifted shingle tab. Older homes in Brainerd, many built in the mid-twentieth century with shallower roof pitches common to that era, are especially vulnerable because low-slope designs shed snow more slowly and give standing water more time to work.

The region also experiences hail seasons that can granulate asphalt shingles in a single storm, stripping the UV-protective coating and cutting years off a roof's functional life. A roof that looks intact from the ground after a hail event may already be on a shortened timeline.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Brainerd

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof deck, not just the visible shingle layer. In central Minnesota, it is common to pull back damaged shingles and find rotted OSB or plank sheathing underneath, particularly on homes where ice dam damage went unaddressed for several seasons. We document the deck condition before any materials are ordered.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence: remove existing roofing down to the deck, replace any compromised sheathing, install a high-quality ice-and-water shield along the eaves and in valleys (this step is especially important in Brainerd's climate, where eave ice dams are nearly inevitable without it), apply synthetic underlayment across the field of the roof, and then install the finish roofing system. We work with asphalt architectural shingles as the most common residential choice in this region, though we can discuss material options based on your home's pitch, exposure, and budget.

Ventilation is part of every installation we do. A new roof installed over an under-ventilated attic will develop ice dam problems and premature shingle aging regardless of material quality. We assess ridge and soffit ventilation as part of the scope.

One pattern worth knowing in Brainerd: many homes near the Mississippi River corridor were built on lots where mature trees have grown significantly over the decades.
