## Why Baxter Homes and Properties Call for Thoughtful Renovation Planning

Crow Wing County's climate is not gentle on buildings. Baxter's growth along the Highway 371 corridor brought a wave of construction in the late 1990s and 2000s that used engineered lumber and OSB sheathing extensively.
