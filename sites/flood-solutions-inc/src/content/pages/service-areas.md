Flood Solutions Inc. is based in Macomb and serves homeowners and property managers across Macomb County and the surrounding communities in southeast Michigan. Scheduling is handled during business hours, call **(586) 580-0197** to confirm availability and get a crew dispatched to your address. The layout below links to every city page we maintain, so you can find local detail for your specific area.

## Where we work

Our team operates out of Macomb, which puts us squarely in the middle of one of Michigan's most densely populated suburban corridors. From here we reach north into the Thumb-area townships, south toward the Detroit metro, west into Oakland County, and east toward the Lake St. Clair shoreline communities. Southeast Michigan's mix of mid-century ranch homes, brick colonials, and slab-on-grade construction from the postwar boom means water intrusion, basement flooding, and storm-related losses are common calls, especially during the spring thaw and the heavy rain events that push the region's clay-heavy soils past their drainage capacity.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Macomb, Sterling Heights, Shelby Township, Clinton Township, and Utica, are our most frequent service stops, and crews can typically reach them well ahead of those in outlying areas. Moving outward, we regularly work in Warren, Roseville, St. Clair Shores, and into the Grosse Pointe communities along the lake. To the north, we cover Romeo, Washington Township, and the rural stretches of northern Macomb County. West into Oakland County, we serve communities like Troy, Rochester, and Auburn Hills.

Traffic on I-94, M-59, and Van Dyke Avenue can affect arrival during peak hours, so callers in the farther reaches of our coverage area should expect a longer lead time than those closer to Macomb. We'll give you an honest estimate when you call, not a number we can't stand behind.
