## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, Whether it's a burst pipe in January or a sump pump that gave out during a heavy rain, standing water and hidden moisture need to be addressed before secondary damage sets in. We locate saturated materials that look dry at the surface and dry the structure down to safe levels.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, Smoke residue travels far beyond the room where a fire starts, embedding in porous surfaces and leaving odors that linger for months if not treated correctly. We handle soot removal, odor neutralization, and the cleaning of affected contents and structural materials.

**Mold remediation**, Michigan's humidity and the region's older housing stock create conditions where mold can colonize within days of a moisture event. We contain affected areas, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces to address the source, not just the visible growth.

**Storm damage restoration**, High winds, ice dams, and the flooding that follows a heavy storm can compromise a roof, walls, and foundation in a matter of hours. We assess the full scope of storm-related damage and coordinate the restoration work needed to make the structure sound again.
