When water soaks through drywall at 2 a.m. when smoke has settled into every surface after a kitchen fire, or when a storm peels back part of a roof, the last thing you need is to coordinate three different contractors. Flood Solutions Inc handles the full arc of property damage mitigation: emergency response, structural drying, and cleanup. We work with homeowners, landlords, commercial property owners, and property managers throughout Macomb and the surrounding communities.

## Emergency restoration services

Some damage keeps spreading until someone stops it. Water migrates through wall cavities and under flooring faster than it looks; smoke residue bonds to surfaces within hours; storm openings let weather in with every passing hour. Flood Solutions Inc responds to water damage, fire and smoke damage, sewage backups, and storm damage, the kinds of losses where the clock is already running when you call. Each service has its own process and equipment, but the goal is the same: limit what's lost, document what happened, and stabilize the property so the recovery can begin.

## Reconstruction handoff

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction puts the property back. Flood Solutions Inc focuses on the mitigation, water extraction, structural drying, cleanup, and remediation, that stabilizes your property. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and safe, your reconstruction contractor handles the rebuild: drywall, flooring, cabinetry, trim, insulation, and structural framing as needed. We document exactly what was removed and why and hand that scope over cleanly, so your builder isn't starting from a report.

## Specialty services

Not every job fits neatly into water or fire. Mold inspection and remediation often follow a water loss that wasn't caught quickly, Macomb County's humid summers and the region's older housing stock mean moisture problems can develop in crawl spaces, attics, and wall cavities before they're visible. We also provide odor removal for smoke, mold, and other persistent sources, as well as board-up and tarping services to secure a property after a break-in, fire, or storm event. These services can stand alone or run alongside a larger restoration project.
