## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Flood Solutions Inc. is committed to making this website usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We believe access to information about emergency restoration services should never be a barrier, especially when someone is dealing with a stressful situation at home or at work.

We aim to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** as our baseline standard. These guidelines cover a broad range of recommendations for making web content more perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for users with disabilities.

## What We've Done to Support Accessible Use

This site has been built with accessibility in mind from the ground up. Pages are structured with semantic HTML so that screen readers can navigate headings, landmarks, and content in a logical order. Images include descriptive alternative text. Color contrast ratios are maintained at levels that support users with low vision or color-vision differences. Interactive elements, including phone number links and contact forms, are reachable and operable by keyboard alone.

We also avoid auto-playing media and flashing content that could cause difficulty for users with vestibular or photosensitive conditions.

## Known Limitations

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time checklist. Some third-party content or embedded tools on this site may not yet meet the same standard as our own pages. We are actively working to identify and address those gaps. If you encounter something that prevents you from getting the information you need, we want to hear about it.
