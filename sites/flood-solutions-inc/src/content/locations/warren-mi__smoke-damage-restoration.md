## Why Warren Homes See Stubborn Smoke Residue

A large share of Warren's housing stock was built between the late 1950s and the mid-1970s, when open-return HVAC systems and minimal attic insulation baffles were standard. That construction pattern means smoke from even a contained fire gets pulled into the ductwork quickly, coating the interior of supply and return lines with a thin, oily film. Protein smoke from a kitchen fire is the worst offender, it's nearly invisible on walls but produces a persistent, rancid odor that gets stronger with heat and humidity. Michigan summers deliver both. Without targeted cleaning of the duct system and affected cavities, the smell returns every time the furnace kicks on.

Warren's brick exteriors hold up well against fire spread, but they create a different problem after the event: brick and mortar are porous and absorb odor compounds at the surface level. Standard wiping doesn't reach deep enough. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment is often necessary to neutralize what's trapped in masonry, especially in older homes where the mortar joints have weathered and opened slightly over decades.

## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Warren

The work starts with a full scope walk before any cleaning begins. Every surface, walls, ceilings, contents, inside cabinets, inside HVAC registers, gets assessed for soot type and deposit depth. Dry smoke from fast-burning fires responds to dry-sponge wiping. Wet smoke from slow, smoldering fires leaves a sticky, smearing residue that requires chemical sponges and specific degreasers. Using the wrong method on the wrong soot type makes the staining worse, not better.

After surface cleaning, the duct system is inspected and cleaned if smoke has entered it. Affected drywall and insulation that cannot be cleaned to an acceptable standard are removed and documented for the insurance claim. Odor neutralization follows, this is not masking with fragrance, but chemical treatment that breaks down the odor compounds at a molecular level. A final air quality check confirms the space is ready before any reconstruction begins.
