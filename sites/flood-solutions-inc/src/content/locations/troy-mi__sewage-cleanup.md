## Why Troy Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Troy's residential neighborhoods were largely built in waves: modest ranch homes and split-levels from the late 1950s through the 1970s, then larger colonials and brick-front subdivisions through the 1980s and 1990s. The earlier homes often have original clay or cast-iron lateral lines running from the house to the street main. Clay tile joints shift over decades as Oakland County's clay-heavy soil expands and contracts through freeze-thaw cycles, and root intrusion from mature trees is common in established neighborhoods. When those laterals crack or collapse, municipal backpressure during heavy rain events pushes sewage upstream into the home rather than downstream to the treatment plant.

Troy also sits on relatively flat terrain, which means storm and sanitary systems can be taxed simultaneously during the intense summer thunderstorms common to southeast Michigan. A backed-up sanitary main during a cloudburst is not unusual, and homes with basement floor drains connected directly to the sanitary line are the first to see overflow.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Troy

The first priority on any sewage call is containment. Standing sewage water is isolated, and any HVAC returns or floor vents in the affected zone are sealed to prevent aerosolized contamination from spreading to clean areas of the home. Porous materials that have direct contact with raw sewage, including carpet, pad, drywall, and insulation, are removed. There is no safe way to dry and restore those materials once they have been saturated with Category 3 water.

After physical removal, the affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for sewage contamination. This is not a single-pass wipe-down. The process involves application, dwell time, and a second round of treatment on concrete, block, and wood framing to address pathogens that penetrate porous surfaces. Structural drying equipment, including dehumidifiers and air movers, runs until moisture readings confirm the substrate has returned to normal levels. Documentation of those readings is provided throughout, which matters when you file a claim.

## Troy Insurance Coordination for Sewage Claims

Sewage backup coverage is one of the more misunderstood endorsements in homeowner policies. Standard HO-3 policies typically exclude sewer backup damage unless the homeowner purchased a separate rider, and that rider is often capped at a lower limit than the actual loss.
