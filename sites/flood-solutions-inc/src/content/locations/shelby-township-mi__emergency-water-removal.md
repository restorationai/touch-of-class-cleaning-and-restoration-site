## Why Shelby Township Properties See Water Damage Differently

Macomb County's glacial clay layer is the defining factor in how water behaves under and around Shelby Township foundations. Clay compacts rather than drains, so water that enters a basement or crawl space through a crack, a failed sump pump, or a backed-up floor drain has nowhere to go on its own. Sump pump failures are one of the most common calls we handle in this area, particularly during the spring thaw when the water table rises and pumps that have been idle all winter are asked to work hard overnight.

Shelby Township also developed rapidly through the 1980s and 1990s, and a significant portion of the housing stock from that era used vinyl-faced fiberglass batt insulation in basement walls. That material wicks and holds moisture invisibly. A basement that looks dry to the eye can have saturated insulation behind the drywall, creating exactly the warm, dark, damp environment where mold takes hold within 24 to 48 hours of a water event.

## Our Water Removal Process in Shelby Township

When we arrive, the first step is containment assessment, not just extraction.
