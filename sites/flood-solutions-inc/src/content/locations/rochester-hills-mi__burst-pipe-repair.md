## Why Rochester Hills Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

The geology under Rochester Hills is largely glacial till, which drains unevenly and can shift during hard freezes. Homes with crawl spaces or slab-on-grade foundations are particularly vulnerable when exterior temperatures drop sharply, because uninsulated supply lines near exterior walls lose heat faster than the thermostat suggests. Older split-level and ranch-style homes common throughout the city often have plumbing routed through unheated garage walls or exterior chases, locations that were code-compliant at the time but are now the first to fail during a polar vortex stretch.

Beyond the freeze-thaw cycle, Rochester Hills has seen significant growth in finished basement living space. A water line break on the first floor can saturate oriented strand board subfloor, migrate down into insulation batts, and pool on a concrete slab before it ever appears on the surface. By the time a homeowner notices a wet carpet seam or a musty smell, the moisture has often been present long enough to begin affecting wall framing.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup Process in Rochester Hills

The first priority on arrival is source control. If the supply line is still pressurized, we locate the shutoff, whether that is a main valve, a zone valve, or the municipal curb stop. Oakland County Water Resources Commissioner infrastructure serves most of Rochester Hills, and knowing how local meter pits and curb stops are configured helps move that step quickly.

Once the water source is secured, we use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation, not just the visible wet area. Water behind plaster or inside a stud cavity can read dry at the surface while the framing stays saturated inches deeper. That hidden moisture is what drives mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in a warm, enclosed space.

Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted or portable units depending on access. Structural drying follows with commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern through affected materials. We monitor moisture readings daily and adjust equipment placement as materials dry, rather than setting equipment and returning at the end of a fixed window.
