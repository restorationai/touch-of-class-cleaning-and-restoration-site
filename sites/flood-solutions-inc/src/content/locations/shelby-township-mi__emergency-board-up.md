## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Shelby Township

We start by assessing the full opening, not just the visible damage, because a cracked frame or a bowed door track often means the surrounding material is compromised too. Plywood panels are cut to the exact opening and fastened into the structural framing rather than just the trim, which matters on older homes where trim boards have already loosened from years of expansion and contraction. On garage doors and larger commercial storefronts we use additional bracing to handle wind load, since an under-secured board can fail in the same storm system that caused the original damage. Every board up is documented with photos before and after, which becomes part of the paperwork your insurance adjuster will want to see.
