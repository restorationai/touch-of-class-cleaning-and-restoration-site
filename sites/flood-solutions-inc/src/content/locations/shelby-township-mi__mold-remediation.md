## Why Shelby Township Properties See Mold Issues

The dominant soil type across much of Macomb County is dense glacial clay, which sheds surface water slowly and keeps it near foundations longer than sandier soils would. Homes built during the suburban expansion of the 1970s through the 1990s, which make up a large share of Shelby Township's housing stock, were often constructed with block or poured concrete basements that rely on aging drain tile systems. When those systems back up or fail, water intrusion is rarely a single dramatic event. It tends to be gradual and invisible behind finished drywall, inside wall cavities, and beneath carpet padding.

Mold thrives in exactly those hidden, humid spaces. Fiberglass batt insulation against a wet block wall stays damp long after the visible surface appears dry. Wood framing behind vinyl-wrapped drywall can sustain moisture levels that support mold growth for weeks without any visible sign at the surface. By the time a musty odor is noticeable, colonies are typically well established.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Shelby Township

Every remediation starts with a thorough moisture assessment, not just a visual inspection. Technicians use penetrating moisture meters and thermal imaging to map where water has traveled inside wall assemblies and under flooring, because the visible mold is rarely the full picture.

Once the scope is defined, containment is established to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home. Negative air pressure, HEPA-filtered air scrubbers, and poly barriers isolate the work zone before any disturbing of materials begins. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, and wood framing that cannot be adequately cleaned, are removed and bagged for disposal. Remaining structural surfaces are treated and dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

In Shelby Township's finished basements, this process often involves careful sequencing around mechanicals, egress windows, and HVAC systems that are common in these homes. Ductwork that passes through a contaminated space gets inspected and, when indicated, cleaned separately to avoid redistributing spores through the air system once the home is reoccupied.

## Shelby Township Insurance Coordination

Mold remediation claims can be among the more contested in residential property insurance, because coverage often depends on the underlying cause. A sudden pipe burst that led to mold growth is typically treated differently than long-term seepage that the carrier may argue was a maintenance issue. Flood Solutions Inc. documents the loss thoroughly, including moisture readings, photographic evidence of affected materials, and a written scope, so that the claim file reflects what was actually found. That documentation supports your adjuster's review and reduces the back-and-forth that delays project approval.

If your Shelby Township property is part of a homeowners association, check whether the HOA master policy covers any shared structural elements, particularly in attached or cluster-style homes, before assuming the full scope falls on your individual policy.

## Local Note

Homes in Shelby Township built during the 1980s and early 1990s frequently used kraft-faced fiberglass insulation in basement walls, stapled directly to wood framing before the drywall was hung. That paper facing is a cellulose food source for mold, and it holds moisture against the framing even after the wall cavity has otherwise dried. Remediation crews who are not familiar with this construction pattern sometimes miss it during scoping. It is worth confirming that any remediation plan accounts for the full wall assembly, not just the drywall face, before work begins.
