When a finished basement floods in Shelby Township, the water damage to drywall and flooring is often the smaller problem. The bigger loss sits in the boxes, furniture, electronics, and family photos that were stored below grade and never had a chance to dry before mold set in. Content recovery in Shelby Township means moving fast to pull salvageable items out, document what's lost, and get everything else cleaned and stabilized before it becomes unrecoverable.

## Why Shelby Township Properties See Content Recovery Needs

Macomb County's clay-heavy soil doesn't drain quickly, and a lot of Shelby Township's housing stock, ranch and split-level homes built through the 1960s, 70s, and 80s, has finished or partially finished basements that were never designed with flood risk in mind. When spring thaw or a heavy summer storm overwhelms a sump pump, water sits against foundation walls under hydrostatic pressure and finds its way in through cove joints and cracks. The contents stored down there, holiday decorations, furniture, home offices, kids' belongings, take on water and start developing mold within 24 to 48 hours if nobody's home to catch it. We also see contents damage from sewer or septic backups on older properties, which changes how we're legally allowed to handle and clean affected items.

## Our Content Recovery Process in Shelby Township

We start with an inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item gets photographed and logged, both for the insurance claim and so nothing gets lost in the shuffle between pack-out and return. Soft goods, upholstery, and clothing go through wet cleaning or dry cleaning depending on the contamination category. Electronics and documents get separated immediately since they need specialized handling, ultrasonic cleaning for circuit boards, freeze-drying for water-logged paperwork, rather than standard extraction. Furniture gets assessed piece by piece: solid wood often survives with proper drying, while particleboard and MDF, common in furniture from newer Shelby Township builds, usually doesn't. Salvageable contents move to climate-controlled storage while the structural drying and repairs happen at the property, then we coordinate the move-back once the space passes a moisture check.

## Shelby Township Insurance Coordination

Most Shelby Township homeowners we work with are filing a claim through their homeowner's policy, and the contents portion of that claim depends heavily on documentation. We photograph and catalog items in their damaged state before cleaning begins, which gives your adjuster something concrete to work from instead of a verbal description of what used to be in the basement. For sewer backup losses, which fall under a separate rider on many Michigan policies, we flag that distinction early since it affects both coverage and how the contents need to be handled from a contamination standpoint.

## Local note

A lot of Shelby Township basements were finished by a previous owner or a contractor without a permit, which means the wall assemblies and flooring underneath don't match what shows on the county's records. That matters for content recovery because carpet pad, subfloor, and wall insulation hidden behind unpermitted finishing work can hold moisture long after the visible surfaces look dry, and any contents that were pushed against those walls need closer inspection before they get cleared for storage or return.
