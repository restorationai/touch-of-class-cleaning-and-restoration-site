## Why Rochester Hills Properties See Fire Damage Differently

The housing stock across Rochester Hills spans several decades of construction, and that variety matters when fire damage restoration begins. Homes built in the 1960s and early 1970s often used wood-paneled interiors, real hardwood trim, and plaster-over-lath wall systems. When fire or intense heat moves through those materials, the char goes deeper than it would in a home with modern drywall. Smoke also penetrates plaster differently, soaking into the substrate rather than staying at the surface, which means odor removal requires more than a surface wipe-down.

Oakland County winters are another factor. Cold temperatures after a fire event can cause secondary damage quickly: water used to suppress the fire freezes inside wall cavities, burst pipes follow, and the structural drying timeline extends. Coordinating fire cleanup and water mitigation at the same time is not unusual in a Rochester Hills winter loss.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Rochester Hills

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a clear path. The first priority after the fire marshal releases the property is a thorough assessment of structural integrity and the full spread of smoke and soot. Soot is not uniform: dry soot from fast-burning fires brushes off surfaces more readily, while wet, oily soot from slow smoldering fires bonds to paint, cabinetry, and HVAC components and requires chemical treatment.

Once the scope is documented, the work moves in this order:

- **Board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry
- **Debris removal** of unsalvageable materials, separated for insurance documentation
- **Soot and smoke cleaning** of walls, ceilings, structural framing, and contents using appropriate agents for each surface type
- **Odor neutralization** through thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment, reaching areas that surface cleaning cannot
- **Duct cleaning** to remove smoke particulate before any HVAC system is operated again
- **Structural drying** if firefighting water is present
- **Reconstruction handoff**: a documented scope of the damaged framing, drywall, insulation, and finishes for your rebuild contractor

Documentation runs throughout every phase, producing the photo and scope records your insurance adjuster will need.
