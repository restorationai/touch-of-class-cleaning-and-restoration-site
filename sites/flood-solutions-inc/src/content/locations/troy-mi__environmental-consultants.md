Flood Solutions inc has worked around southeast Michigan's building stock and soil conditions since 1996, and that background shapes how we scope environmental work in Troy specifically.

## Why Troy Properties See Environmental Consultants Called In

Troy's mix of mid-century residential neighborhoods and dense corporate office parks along the I-75 corridor creates two very different sets of environmental questions. Homes built through the 1960s and 70s commonly have original plaster, vinyl floor tile, and pipe insulation that may contain asbestos, and older paint layers that predate lead-based paint restrictions. On the commercial side, office and light-industrial parcels that have changed hands multiple times sometimes carry legacy underground storage tanks or unclear prior land use, which is exactly what a Phase I Environmental Site Assessment is designed to uncover before a transaction closes. Southeast Michigan's clay-heavy soil also holds moisture against foundations through freeze-thaw cycles, and where that moisture has been sitting against block or slab for years, it often brings microbial growth questions along with it.

## Our Environmental Consultants Process in Troy

We start with a records and use-history review, since knowing what stood on a Troy parcel decades ago, whether it was agricultural, residential, or light industrial, tells us where to focus sampling. For residential jobs, that means targeted material sampling in areas most likely to have original construction: basements, older additions, and anything behind a wall about to be opened for renovation. For commercial Phase I work, it means a site walk, an interview with current and past owners where possible, and a review of regulatory databases maintained by Michigan's EGLE and Oakland County records. If sampling points to a Phase II, we coordinate soil borings or indoor air quality testing and get results back to the client with a clear explanation of what the numbers mean for their timeline, not just a lab printout.

## Troy Insurance & Regulatory Coordination

Most environmental consulting work in Troy isn't insurance-driven, it's transaction-driven or renovation-driven, so our reporting is built to satisfy the party that actually needs it: a lender's due diligence checklist, a building department's pre-renovation requirement, or an attorney's closing conditions. When mold or moisture findings do tie back to a covered water loss, we document the material damage and testing results in a format that carriers can use alongside a restoration claim, so the environmental piece doesn't become a separate, disconnected process from the repair.

A detail that catches out-of-town inspectors off guard: several of Troy's commercial corridors were built out in phases over multiple decades, so a single office park can have one building from the 1970s sitting next to a 2000s addition with completely different construction materials and mechanical systems. We treat each building on a site separately rather than assuming uniform construction, which matters when deciding where asbestos or lead sampling actually needs to happen.
