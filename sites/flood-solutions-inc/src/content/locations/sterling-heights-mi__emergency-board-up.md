A shattered patio door after a Macomb County hailstorm or a kicked-in entry door from an overnight break-in leaves a Sterling Heights home exposed to weather, animals, and a second round of damage before repairs even start. Emergency board up work is about closing that gap fast, with materials that hold through wind and freeze-thaw swings until permanent repairs or a full restoration scope can begin.

## Why Sterling Heights Properties Need Emergency Board Up

Much of Sterling Heights was built out during the subdivision boom of the 1960s through 1980s, which means a large share of the city's housing stock is now 40 to 60 years old. Original aluminum-frame windows, older sliding patio doors, and garage service doors from that era don't flex the way modern vinyl and composite units do, so a hard impact, wind-driven debris, or a forced entry tends to crack the frame along with the glass rather than just the pane. Macomb County's severe summer storm line brings straight-line wind and hail most years, and those systems can take out multiple windows on the same exposure of a house in one pass. Winter freeze-thaw cycling adds a second pattern: door frames and window sashes that were already loose from age can split or pull away from the jamb once temperatures swing below freezing, leaving gaps that let in wind, rain, and pests until the opening is secured.

## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Sterling Heights

The first step on arrival is a walk-around to identify every compromised opening, not just the one that prompted the call, since storm and impact damage often affects more than one window or door. Broken glass and loose frame material get cleared before we measure and cut exterior-grade plywood sized to the actual opening rather than a generic sheet, which matters on older Sterling Heights homes where window openings were often built to now-nonstandard dimensions. Panels are fastened through the frame into solid structural members, not just the trim, so the board holds under wind load rather than working loose in the next storm. Before we leave, we photograph every secured opening and document pre-existing conditions for the insurance file, since a board up is almost always the first line item in a larger claim.

## Sterling Heights Insurance Coordination

Board up costs are typically covered under the same claim as the storm, fire, or break-in damage that caused the need for it, and most carriers expect to see mitigation completed quickly to avoid a secondary damage dispute. We document the condition of each opening with photos and notes before boarding, which gives the adjuster a clear record of what was damaged before any temporary materials went up. For Sterling Heights homeowners working with carriers based outside Michigan, having that documentation ready at the first adjuster call tends to move the claim along faster than waiting for an in-person inspection.

## Local Note

A number of the ranch and colonial homes built across Sterling Heights in the 1970s used a narrower standard door slab than what's sold today, which means a board cut to "standard" pre-hung dimensions can leave a gap along one edge of the frame. On these older openings we measure the actual jamb rather than assuming a modern door width, which keeps the seal tight against wind and rain until a full-size replacement door can be installed.
