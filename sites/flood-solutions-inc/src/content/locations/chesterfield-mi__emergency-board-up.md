When a storm off Lake St. Clair peels back a section of roofing or drives a tree limb through a picture window, the opening left behind is as much a security problem as a weather one. Emergency board up in Chesterfield isn't just about covering a broken pane, it's about closing a house or business back up fast enough that wind, rain, and anyone passing by can't get further inside before repairs start.

## Why Chesterfield Properties Need Emergency Board Up

Chesterfield Township sits along the Lake St. Clair shoreline, and that proximity means weather moves through differently than it does further inland in Macomb County. Lake-effect gusts can snap tree limbs and peel loose siding or shingles with little warning, and older cottages closer to the water often have single-pane windows and wood-frame construction that gives way faster than newer builds. Further from the shoreline, the township's newer subdivisions, many built out over the last two decades on former farmland, bring their own version of the problem: vinyl siding and larger picture windows that a fallen branch or a vehicle leaving I-94 or the Gratiot corridor can breach just as easily. Vacant homes mid-renovation or mid-sale are also common across the township right now given the pace of new construction, and an open door frame or missing window on an unoccupied property is an invitation for trespassing until it's sealed.

## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Chesterfield

We start by assessing what's structurally sound around the opening, since boarding over damaged framing without checking it first just delays the real repair. From there we measure and cut plywood or OSB panels on site rather than guessing dimensions in advance, because storm damage rarely leaves a clean rectangle to work with. Panels get fastened into solid framing, not just the drywall or siding around the hole, and edges are sealed against wind-driven rain, which matters more on lakefront-facing walls than on interior-facing ones. Before we leave, we photograph the damage and the completed board up for your insurance file, since that documentation is often what determines how quickly a claim moves.

Whether the call comes from a lakefront property or one of the newer developments set back from the water, we're working the same general stretch of Macomb County we've covered since 1996. ## Chesterfield Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most of Chesterfield's newer subdivisions have an HOA, and many require prompt temporary repair of any exterior damage to keep a property from looking vacant or unmaintained, even before a full insurance claim is settled. We document the board up with dated photos specifically so that paperwork exists for both the carrier and, if needed, the association. For older lakefront properties without an HOA, the priority is usually just getting the opening weathertight before the next system moves off the lake.

## Local note

Wind off Lake St. Clair tends to shift direction faster than storms further inland, which means a board up that only protects against the initial wind direction can leave a structure exposed once the system rotates. On lakefront-facing openings we typically brace panels to hold against gusts from more than one direction rather than assuming the wind will keep blowing the way it started.
