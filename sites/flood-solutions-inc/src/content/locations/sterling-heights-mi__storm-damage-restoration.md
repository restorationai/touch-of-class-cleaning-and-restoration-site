Sterling Heights sits in the heart of Macomb County, where late-spring and summer storm systems rolling off Lake St. Clair can drop several inches of rain in under an hour, send wind gusts well past 60 mph, and leave behind a landscape of downed trees, stripped siding, and flooded basements before the sirens have stopped. ## Why Sterling Heights Properties Take a Hard Hit in Severe Weather

Macomb County's flat topography gives storms very little to slow them down before they reach residential neighborhoods. Sterling Heights is heavily developed with subdivisions built across several decades, and that mix of construction eras matters when a storm hits. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s often have original roof decking that has absorbed decades of freeze-thaw cycling, when a storm tears away shingles, that aged wood soaks up rainwater quickly and can begin to delaminate within hours. Newer construction in the city's eastern corridors tends to use oriented strand board decking, which is vulnerable in a different way: once the protective surface is breached, OSB swells and loses structural integrity faster than plank lumber.

The city's clay-heavy soil compounds the problem. During a heavy storm event, saturated clay sheds water rather than absorbing it, pushing runoff toward foundations and window wells. Basement flooding after a severe storm is common here, and it often arrives through window wells and floor drains simultaneously rather than through a single obvious entry point.

## What Storm Damage Restoration Looks Like in Sterling Heights

Every storm loss has a sequence, and skipping steps creates bigger problems later. The process starts with a full exterior and interior assessment, roof, siding, soffit, windows, and any point where wind or water found an opening. Temporary protective measures come next: roof tarping, board-up of broken windows or doors, and any shoring needed to stabilize a compromised structure. These steps are not cosmetic; they stop an active loss from growing while the permanent repair plan is built.

Once the structure is stabilized, water intrusion is addressed directly. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters identifies where water has traveled behind walls and under flooring, places that look dry at the surface but are still saturated underneath. Structural drying with commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers runs until materials reach documented drying goals, not just until things feel dry to the touch. Only after moisture readings confirm the structure is dry does reconstruction begin: replacing damaged decking, framing, insulation, drywall, and exterior cladding.

## Insurance Coordination for Storm Claims in Sterling Heights

Storm damage claims in Michigan move through homeowners' insurance under the wind and hail peril, which is handled separately from flood coverage. That distinction matters in Sterling Heights, where a single storm event can cause both wind-driven roof damage (covered under standard homeowners' policies) and basement flooding from surface water intrusion (which typically requires separate flood coverage). Flood Solutions inc documents both types of damage with photographs, moisture readings, and written scope so your adjuster has a complete picture of what the storm actually did to your property. Keeping the two damage categories clearly separated in the documentation prevents claim disputes and speeds up the approval process.

## Local Note

Sterling Heights has a significant number of homes with attached garages that were converted to living space in the 1980s and 1990s, a common modification in Macomb County subdivisions from that era. These converted spaces often lack the original garage's drainage slope, and their insulation and vapor barrier details were not always brought up to residential code at the time of conversion. In a storm event, these rooms flood and retain moisture in ways that standard room layouts do not. If your home has one of these spaces and it took on water, plan for a more involved drying process than a comparable above-grade room would require.
