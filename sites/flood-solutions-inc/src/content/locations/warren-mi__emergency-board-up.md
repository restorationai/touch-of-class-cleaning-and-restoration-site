When a storm knocks a tree limb through a picture window or a break-in leaves a door frame splintered, the gap in your home's envelope becomes the most urgent problem on the property, not the damage that caused it. Warren's mix of mid-century ranch homes and light industrial and commercial buildings along Van Dyke and Mound Road means openings come in every shape: aluminum-frame windows original to 1950s and 60s construction, storefront glass on strip retail, garage doors torn off their tracks. Emergency board up closes that gap fast, before weather, animals, or opportunists get a second look.

## Why Warren Properties Need Emergency Board Up

Macomb County winters bring the freeze-thaw cycles that stress old window seals and siding, and a hard freeze after a break can turn a cracked pane into a fully blown-out frame overnight. Warren's housing stock, much of it built during the postwar auto industry boom, often still carries original wood window frames or single-pane glass that doesn't hold up to impact the way newer construction does. On the commercial side, the city's strip malls and light-industrial buildings tend to have large storefront glass or roll-up doors that, once breached, leave an entire bay exposed to the street. Add in southeast Michigan's summer storm line, straight-line winds and hail are common from late spring through early fall, and it's easy to see why board up calls spike after a front moves through.

## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Warren

The work starts with measuring and securing the opening, not just nailing a sheet of plywood over it. We assess whether the frame itself is compromised, since a lot of Warren's older homes have wood-framed openings that have swelled or rotted at the sill, and that changes how the board needs to be anchored. Plywood is cut to size, treated when the job calls for weatherproofing, and fastened through the frame into solid structural material rather than just the trim, so it holds against wind load. For commercial storefronts, we match the board to the glazing system, sometimes framing out a temporary stud wall behind the opening when the original frame is too damaged to anchor into directly. Every board up is documented with photos before and after, which matters later when the claim gets reviewed.

We schedule based on the urgency of the opening, not the type of property, since an exposed home and an exposed business carry the same risk of secondary damage and loss.

## Warren Insurance Coordination

Most carriers treat emergency board up as mitigation, meaning the cost of securing the property is generally covered separately from the repair of whatever caused the damage, provided it's done promptly and documented. We photograph the opening, the board up materials used, and the surrounding area before starting work, then again once the board is set, so the paperwork supports the claim rather than leaving gaps an adjuster has to question. If the damage stems from a covered peril, storm, fire, vandalism, that documentation tends to move the claim along faster.

## Local note

A detail that trips up a lot of out-of-town crews: Warren's older homes, particularly the ranch-style builds common through the city, often have window openings that are slightly out of square after decades of settling. A standard-cut board that looks right on paper can leave a gap at one corner once it's up against the actual frame. We measure each opening on site rather than pre-cutting off a standard template, which adds a few minutes but avoids a board that needs to be pulled and re-cut in the middle of a job.
