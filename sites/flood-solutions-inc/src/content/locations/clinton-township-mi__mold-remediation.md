## Why Clinton Township Properties See Mold Problems

Macomb County's clay-heavy soil does not drain quickly. After a heavy rain, water that cannot percolate through the ground finds the path of least resistance: foundation walls, window wells, and crawl space vents. Homes built in the postwar expansion of the township, many of them slab-on-grade or with shallow block foundations, were not designed with today's understanding of vapor barriers and drainage planes. That combination of soil type and construction era creates conditions where moisture migrates into living spaces slowly and quietly, often going undetected until mold growth is already established on floor joists, insulation batts, or the back side of drywall.

Central air conditioning systems common in the township's ranch-style homes also contribute. When a system is slightly undersized or a condensate drain clogs, relative humidity inside the home can creep above 60 percent for days at a time, enough for mold spores, which are always present in indoor air, to germinate and begin spreading.

## The Mold Remediation Process in Clinton Township

Effective mold abatement starts with containment, not cleaning. Before any material is disturbed, affected areas are isolated with poly sheeting and negative air pressure is established using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from traveling to unaffected rooms during removal.

The process moves through these stages:

- **Source identification**: Moisture readings are taken with calibrated meters on walls, floors, and ceilings. Thermal imaging helps locate moisture that has migrated beyond the visible damage area.
- **Containment setup**: Affected zones are sealed off. HVAC registers in the work area are covered to prevent cross-contamination through the duct system.
- **Controlled removal**: Porous materials that cannot be cleaned to clearance standards, insulation, heavily affected drywall, saturated subfloor sections, are bagged and removed. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned using EPA-registered antimicrobial agents.
- **Drying and dehumidification**: After removal, structural cavities are dried to manufacturer and industry standard moisture levels before any rebuild begins.
- **Post-remediation verification**: Air and surface samples confirm that spore counts have returned to normal background levels before containment is removed.

## Local Note

One pattern that shows up repeatedly in Clinton Township's ranch-style homes is mold growth inside the soffit cavities above kitchen and bathroom cabinets. These spaces sit directly below the roof deck, and when attic ventilation is inadequate, condensation forms on the underside of the sheathing and wicks down into the soffit framing. Because the cavity is enclosed and rarely inspected, growth can be extensive by the time a homeowner notices staining on the ceiling. A thorough mold inspection in these homes should always include attic moisture readings and a check of ridge and soffit vent clearance, not just the visible wall and floor surfaces.
