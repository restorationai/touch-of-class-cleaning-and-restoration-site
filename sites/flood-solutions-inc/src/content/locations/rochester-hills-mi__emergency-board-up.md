A tree limb through a picture window during a spring thunderstorm, a shattered patio door after a break-in, a garage wall crushed by a vehicle backing out wrong: these are the calls that turn into emergency board up jobs, and Rochester Hills sees its share, especially in the wooded, tree-lined subdivisions that give the city its name. When a structure is open to the weather or to anyone walking by, the priority shifts from \"what happened\" to \"how fast can this opening be closed.\"

## Why Rochester Hills Properties See Board-Up Emergencies

Rochester Hills was built out largely with mature oak and maple canopy left standing between subdivisions, which is part of what makes the city attractive and part of what causes storm damage. High wind events and the ice storms that move through Oakland County most winters put real stress on those older trees, and a large limb coming down on a roofline or window wall doesn't wait for business hours to happen. Add in the Clinton River corridor that runs through the eastern part of the city, where saturated ground during spring thaw makes tree failure more likely, and you have a steady, weather-driven source of board-up calls that has nothing to do with age of the house. Break-ins and vehicle-into-structure accidents round out the rest, particularly around commercial strips near major roads where storefront glass is the point of entry.

## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Rochester Hills

The work starts with securing the opening, not admiring the damage. We measure and cut construction-grade plywood on-site rather than arriving with pre-cut panels, because Rochester Hills housing stock ranges from newer two-story colonials with standard window openings to older ranch-style homes with irregular framing, and a panel that doesn't seat tight against the studs defeats the purpose. Openings are sheathed and fastened directly into structural framing wherever possible, not just into trim, so wind and freeze-thaw movement don't work the board loose overnight. In winter months we pay particular attention to sealing gaps around the board's edges, since Michigan's freeze-thaw cycle can drive moisture into a poorly sealed seam and turn a simple board-up into a hidden water and mold problem by the time repairs start. Once the structure is secured, we photograph the damage and the boarding work itself, documentation that becomes the backbone of the insurance claim that typically follows.

## Reaching Rochester Hills from Macomb

We've worked out of Macomb since 1996, and the run into Rochester Hills is a familiar one, generally north on I-75 and west on M-59 into Oakland County, with local routing adjusted depending on which side of the city the damage is on. We don't publish a fixed response-time promise because traffic on M-59 during rush hour is its own variable, but we schedule board-up calls as the priority they are and keep the crew moving rather than stacking jobs ahead of an active opening.

## Local note

A number of Rochester Hills subdivisions carry HOA design standards that technically apply to exterior appearance, including temporary repairs. We haven't run into an HOA that objects to a board-up done to protect a home, but we do use unpainted, uniform plywood rather than mismatched scrap when a job is likely to sit boarded for more than a few days, simply because it draws less attention from a neighbor or a board member driving past and keeps the homeowner from getting an unnecessary letter on top of an already bad week.
