## Why Rochester Hills Homes See Water Heater Failures More Often Than You'd Expect

The housing stock across Rochester Hills spans several decades and construction styles. Subdivisions built through the 1970s and 1980s often placed water heaters in finished basement utility rooms with low clearance and limited drainage, meaning a slow tank weep can saturate drywall, insulation, and the subfloor assembly for days before anyone notices the musty smell. Newer construction along the city's northern corridors tends to use tankless or power-vent units, but the older tank-style heaters in established neighborhoods are frequently past their eight-to-twelve-year service window.

Oakland County's water supply, drawn from the Detroit water system, carries moderate mineral hardness. Over years, that hardness accelerates sediment buildup at the tank bottom, which insulates the heating element, drives up internal temperatures, and corrodes the tank from the inside out. A tank that looks fine on the outside can be months from failure. When it goes, the water it releases is often close to 120 degrees Fahrenheit, which means it penetrates porous concrete, engineered wood flooring, and wall cavities faster than a cold-water loss would.

## What the Cleanup Process Actually Involves

A water heater flood is not a simple mop-and-dry situation, even when the volume looks manageable. Hot water drives moisture into materials quickly, and by the time the floor surface feels dry underfoot, the subfloor and lower wall cavities may still be holding significant moisture content.

The process Flood Solutions inc follows on these losses:

- **Moisture mapping before anything is moved.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters establish exactly where water traveled, including inside wall cavities and under adjacent flooring runs.
- **Extraction of standing water.** Truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment pulls bulk water from the affected area before drying begins.
- **Controlled drying with industrial air movers and dehumidifiers.** Equipment placement is calculated based on the room geometry and the materials involved, not a one-size setup.
- **Daily monitoring.** Moisture readings are logged each day so drying progress is documented and the equipment isn't pulled before materials reach acceptable levels.
- **Evaluation of affected materials.** Drywall, insulation, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are identified early so decisions about removal happen before mold has time to colonize. Mold can begin developing in wet organic materials within 24 to 48 hours under the right temperature conditions.

## Local Note

Rochester Hills has a notable number of homes built in the late 1980s and early 1990s with finished basements that used oriented strand board (OSB) subflooring rather than traditional plywood. OSB holds moisture longer than plywood and swells at the edges when saturated, which can cause the finished flooring above it to buckle even after surface drying appears complete. On these losses, we extend the drying timeline and take moisture readings at the subfloor level specifically, not just at the surface, before calling the structure dry. It is a detail that matters a great deal for the final floor repair and for preventing a callback months later.
