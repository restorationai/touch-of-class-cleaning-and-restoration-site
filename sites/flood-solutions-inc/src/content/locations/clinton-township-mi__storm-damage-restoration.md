## Why Clinton Township Properties See Significant Storm Damage

Macomb County's position along the Lake St. Clair shoreline corridor creates a specific weather pattern: lake-enhanced thunderstorms that intensify quickly and produce localized wind gusts capable of snapping mature trees. Clinton Township's residential neighborhoods are heavily treed, and many of the subdivision lots developed through the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s feature large silver maples and cottonwoods that have reached full maturity. Those species are notorious for shallow root systems and brittle upper canopies, a 60 mph gust is often enough to bring a major limb or an entire trunk down onto a roof or through a fence line.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Ranch-style and split-level homes built during the postwar suburban expansion of Macomb County frequently have low-slope or flat roof sections over attached garages and rear additions. Those sections drain poorly under heavy rainfall rates and are the first to show interior water infiltration after storm damage compromises flashing or shingles.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Clinton Township

The first priority after any storm event is emergency stabilization, not paperwork, not estimates. That means tarping compromised roof sections, boarding openings left by broken windows or displaced doors, and extracting any standing water before it migrates into subfloor assemblies. Wet insulation in a crawl space or attic loses its thermal value immediately and becomes a moisture reservoir that feeds mold long after the storm passes.

Once the structure is stabilized, the assessment phase begins. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and calibrated meters identifies water that has traveled beyond the obvious damage zone, water follows wall cavities, runs along top plates, and pools in low spots that aren't visible from the surface. That mapping drives the drying plan, which typically involves structural drying equipment positioned to create directed airflow through affected assemblies rather than just circulating room air.

Debris removal, tree sections, damaged roofing material, compromised siding, is coordinated alongside the structural work so the property is secured and accessible for insurance documentation at the same time.
