## Why Rochester Hills Properties See Mold Problems

The geology under Rochester Hills is largely glacial till with a high clay content. Clay drains slowly, which means water that pools against a foundation during a heavy rain event lingers far longer than it would in sandier soil. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a large portion of the housing stock in this area, were often constructed with fiberglass batt insulation in rim joist bays and minimal vapor barriers. When interior humidity climbs in July and August, that combination creates a reliable condensation zone where mold can colonize within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.

Finished basements compound the problem. Drywall installed directly against a concrete block wall, a common shortcut in mid-century construction, traps moisture between the paper facing and the masonry. The surface can read dry to the touch while the cavity behind it is sustaining active growth. Thermal imaging and moisture meters calibrated for masonry assemblies are the only reliable way to map the true extent of the problem.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Rochester Hills

Every remediation starts with a thorough assessment before any material is disturbed. We identify the moisture source first, because removing mold without correcting the underlying condition is a temporary fix. Once the source is addressed, we establish containment using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, a step that matters especially in open-plan layouts common in newer Rochester Hills construction.

Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, and wood framing showing active growth, are removed and bagged for disposal according to Michigan environmental guidelines. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned using EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the project to capture airborne spores. Structural drying equipment stays on-site until moisture readings return to baseline for the specific material type, not just until the air feels dry.

For homes with finished basements, we document every step photographically. That documentation supports your insurance claim and gives a future buyer or inspector a clear record of what was done and when.
