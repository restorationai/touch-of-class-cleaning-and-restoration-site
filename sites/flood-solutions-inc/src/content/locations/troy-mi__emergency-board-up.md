When a storm tears a limb through a bedroom window or a break-in leaves a shattered patio door on a Troy property, the opening itself becomes the emergency: rain, snow, wind, and anyone walking by all have a straight path inside until it's sealed. Emergency board up in Troy means getting plywood, fasteners, and weather barrier on that opening fast, before a single Michigan cold front or a second intrusion attempt turns a repair into a much bigger loss.

## Why Troy Properties See Emergency Board Up Needs

Troy's mix of established residential subdivisions and heavy commercial and office corridor development along routes like I-75 means board-up calls come from very different directions. Homeowners in older ranch-style subdivisions often need us after a tree limb or wind event punches through a window or a garage panel during a fast-moving Michigan storm. Commercial and office park properties, many sitting empty overnight or on weekends, are more prone to break-ins or vandalism that leave storefront glass, service doors, or loading bay openings exposed. Oakland County's freeze-thaw winters add urgency to both: an unsecured opening left overnight in January isn't just a security risk, it's a direct path for pipes to freeze once the heated envelope of the building is broken.

## Our Emergency Board Up Process in Troy

We start by measuring and photographing the opening for the insurance file, then cut and fit exterior-grade plywood or OSB sized to the actual frame rather than a generic panel that leaves gaps. On commercial buildings we pay close attention to how the board-up interacts with any existing alarm contacts or door hardware, since Troy's mix of office parks and retail strips often has systems that need to stay functional even with an opening temporarily sealed. Fasteners go through framing, not just trim, and on ground-floor commercial glass we typically add anti-pry bracing given how often storefront break-ins target the same weak point twice. Where the opening is on a sloped roof line or a bay window, we add flashing tape or poly sheeting underneath the board to stop wind-driven rain from working its way behind the panel during the next system that rolls through.

## Troy Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Troy homeowners we work with are filing a claim tied to the same event that caused the opening, whether that's wind, a fallen tree, or a break-in, so we document the board-up itself as mitigation work separate from the repair estimate. Insurers generally expect to see that a property owner took reasonable steps to prevent further damage once an opening exists, and a dated, photographed board-up is exactly that evidence. For properties inside a homeowners association or a managed commercial complex, we also try to get temporary signage or fencing up where the HOA or property management company has rules about how a secured but damaged unit has to look from the street while repairs are pending.

## Local note

One thing that catches people off guard in Troy: a board-up that looks solid in October can loosen by January once the wood shrinks and swells through a few freeze-thaw cycles. We check fastener tightness as part of any follow-up visit rather than assuming a board installed during a fall storm is still snug three months later, because in Michigan winters, a gap that opens up around a board-up panel is often how ice and meltwater find their way in long after the original storm has passed.
