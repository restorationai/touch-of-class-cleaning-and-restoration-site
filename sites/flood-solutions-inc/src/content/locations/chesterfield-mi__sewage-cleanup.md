When a sewer line backs up into a basement or a septic system overflows after a hard rain, the contamination spreads fast and the cleanup window is short. ## Why Chesterfield Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Much of Chesterfield's residential development happened in two distinct waves: older ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and 1970s on the western side of the township, and newer subdivisions that pushed east toward Lake St. Clair through the 1990s and 2000s. The older homes often have original cast-iron or clay-tile drain lines that have had decades to crack, root-intrude, or partially collapse. When those lines fail, the backup doesn't stay in the floor drain. It spreads across concrete slab floors, wicks into drywall, and soaks into any porous material at grade level.

The clay-heavy soils throughout Macomb County drain slowly. After a significant rain event, the ground becomes saturated and puts lateral pressure on older sewer laterals, compressing already-compromised joints. Septic systems in the less-sewered pockets of the township face a similar problem: a saturated drain field can't accept effluent, and the system backs up into the home. Either way, the result is a Category 3 contamination event that requires more than a mop and bleach.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Chesterfield

The first priority on any raw sewage call is containment. Sewage carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that cross-contaminate surfaces quickly, so the affected area is isolated before extraction begins. Standing waste water is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment, and any porous materials that absorbed sewage, including drywall, carpet, and insulation, are removed and bagged for proper disposal.

Once the space is cleared down to hard surfaces, the sanitization phase begins. Every affected surface receives an EPA-registered disinfectant application, not a single pass but a dwell-time treatment that actually kills pathogens rather than masking them. After disinfection, industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to acceptable levels. In Chesterfield's climate, where humid summers slow evaporation and cold winters can trap moisture in below-grade spaces, drying timelines require monitoring rather than guesswork. Readings are logged at each visit so there is a documented record of the drying progression.

## Chesterfield Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is often a separate rider on a standard homeowner's policy, and many Chesterfield homeowners discover at the worst moment that their base policy doesn't automatically cover sewer or septic overflow. Flood Solutions Inc. documents the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials before removal, moisture readings, a written scope of work, and a complete inventory of what was removed and why. That documentation package is what an adjuster needs to process a claim, and having it organized from the start prevents the back-and-forth that delays settlements.

If your policy does include sewage backup coverage, the documentation also supports the full scope of the loss, including structural drying and sanitization, not just the visible debris removal.
