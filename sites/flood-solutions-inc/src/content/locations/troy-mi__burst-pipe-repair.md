Troy sits in one of Michigan's coldest suburban corridors, where January wind chills routinely drive pipe-wall temperatures below freezing in uninsulated exterior walls and garage-adjacent supply lines. When a pipe lets go, water moves fast through the open floor plans and finished basements that define so much of Troy's housing stock, and the damage clock starts the moment the break happens. Flood Solutions inc has been responding to pipe break water damage across Macomb County and the surrounding communities since 1996, and the pattern we see in Troy has its own distinct character.

## Why Troy Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Troy's residential development happened in waves, with significant construction through the 1960s, 1970s, and 1980s. Homes from those decades often have copper supply lines that have now accumulated decades of mineral cycling from Michigan's moderately hard water. Repeated freeze-thaw stress on those lines, particularly in crawl spaces, unheated garage walls, and the exterior-facing runs beneath kitchen sinks, eventually leads to pinhole failures or full splits. Newer subdivisions built in the 1990s and early 2000s introduced CPVC and early PEX, materials that handle cold better but are not immune to a sustained freeze when a furnace fails or a home sits vacant over a holiday weekend.

Troy's relatively flat topography means standing water from a water line break has nowhere to drain on its own. Finished lower levels, common throughout the city's ranch and colonial-style homes, can accumulate several inches before anyone notices. By the time a homeowner finds the problem, water has typically already wicked into drywall, insulation, and subflooring.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup Process in Troy

The first step on any pipe break water damage call is stopping the source. If the homeowner has not already shut the main, our crew locates the shutoff and confirms the flow is stopped before any extraction begins. We then use thermal imaging cameras alongside moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled, including behind finished walls and beneath engineered hardwood or tile, where visible water is only part of the story.

Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted and portable units calibrated for the volume typical in Troy's mid-century and late-century finished basements. Once standing water is removed, we place commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in a pattern matched to the room geometry and material types. Drying logs are recorded throughout the process so there is a documented record for your insurance carrier.

If the pipe break has compromised drywall, insulation, or cabinetry beyond drying, our team handles the controlled demolition and coordinates the rebuild phase so you are not managing two separate contractors.

## Troy Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage from a burst pipe, though the specific coverage for pipe repair versus water cleanup varies by carrier and policy language. We document the loss thoroughly, including photos, moisture readings, and a written scope, so your adjuster has what they need to process the claim accurately. Troy homeowners with older homes sometimes discover that deferred maintenance language in their policy complicates a claim; thorough documentation of the sudden nature of the break helps address that. We bill major carriers directly and can walk you through the process if this is your first time navigating a property damage claim.

## Local Note

Troy has a notable number of homes built on concrete slab foundations, particularly in subdivisions developed through the 1970s along the city's western corridors. When a supply line breaks inside a slab-on-grade home, water migrates laterally under the concrete before it ever surfaces, and moisture meters at the floor level can read deceptively low while the material beneath the slab stays saturated for weeks. We account for this by extending monitoring periods on slab homes and using readings at multiple points across the floor plane rather than relying on a single spot check near the visible water.
