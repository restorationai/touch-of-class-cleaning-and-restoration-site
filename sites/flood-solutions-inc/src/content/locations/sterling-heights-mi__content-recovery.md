## Our Content Recovery Process in Sterling Heights

We start by photographing and inventorying everything in place before a single item moves, which matters for the claim and for knowing what's salvageable versus what isn't. Soft goods, upholstered furniture, and paper documents get triaged first since they hold moisture fastest, while electronics and photographs go through separate drying protocols. Items that can be treated on-site stay on-site to limit handling; anything needing ozone treatment, ultrasonic cleaning, or specialized drying for electronics or textiles gets packed out, logged, and transported for processing. Everything is returned with a documented chain of custody so homeowners and adjusters both know what happened to each item along the way.
