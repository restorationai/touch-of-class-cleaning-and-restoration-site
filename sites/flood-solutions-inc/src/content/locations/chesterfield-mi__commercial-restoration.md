## Why Chesterfield Commercial Properties Face Distinct Restoration Challenges

Macomb County clay does not drain. When snowmelt or a heavy spring rain saturates the ground around a commercial foundation, hydrostatic pressure builds against basement walls and slab edges in ways that masonry-block construction, common in Chesterfield's older retail and industrial buildings, handles poorly. A slow seep behind a block wall can go unnoticed for weeks, feeding mold colonies inside insulated cavities while the interior looks perfectly dry. Fire losses carry their own local wrinkle: commercial kitchens and manufacturing spaces along the township's industrial corridors often have spray-foam insulation and metal-deck roofing that retain smoke and char odor differently than standard drywall construction, requiring longer deodorization cycles and more aggressive HEPA filtration.

Winter adds another layer. Frozen sprinkler heads in unheated storage bays, ice dams on low-slope commercial roofs, and burst copper supply lines in older suites are all seasonal patterns Flood Solutions Inc encounters regularly in this part of the county. Recognizing those patterns early, before a secondary loss compounds the first, is a core part of how commercial restoration in Chesterfield differs from a straightforward residential job.

## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Chesterfield

Every commercial loss starts with a scope that protects your ability to operate, not just your building. The first priority is separating affected areas from active business space so your employees and customers are not breathing particulates or walking through contaminated zones. From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

**Loss assessment and moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters identify hidden saturation behind drywall, under flooring systems, and inside mechanical chases. In Chesterfield's slab-on-grade commercial buildings, this step often reveals that water has wicked further under the concrete than the visible damage suggests.

**Extraction and controlled drying.** Industrial-grade truck-mount extractors remove standing water; desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the square footage and material types involved. Drying targets are set to industry standards and documented daily.

**Structural and content restoration.** Affected drywall, insulation, and flooring are removed to verified dry substrate. Contents, equipment, and documents are inventoried before any disposal, which matters when you are filing a commercial property claim.

**Deodorization and clearance.** Smoke and microbial odor require thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment depending on material sensitivity. We do not close out a commercial job until odor readings and moisture readings confirm the space is ready for reconstruction.
