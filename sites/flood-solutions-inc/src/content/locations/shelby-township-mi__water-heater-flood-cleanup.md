## Our Water Heater Flood Cleanup Process in Shelby Township

The first step on any water heater loss is containment and documentation. We photograph the failed unit, the water line, and all affected surfaces before extraction begins. This matters for your insurance claim, and it also tells us which direction the water traveled.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted equipment pulls standing water from concrete and carpet quickly. After extraction, we use penetrating moisture meters and thermal imaging to map how far water has moved into walls, under flooring, and into any adjacent framing. In finished basements, which are common in Shelby Township homes built during the 1990s buildout, drywall and insulation behind the water heater often need to come out. Wet fiberglass batt insulation holds moisture indefinitely and cannot be dried in place.

Drying equipment, including high-capacity dehumidifiers and directional air movers, is placed based on the moisture map, not just around the visible wet area. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until all structural readings return to acceptable dry standards. The process typically runs several days depending on how long the water was present before the call.
