## Our Commitment to Accessibility

ProCraft Exteriors is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe that every visitor — whether you're a homeowner dealing with storm damage, a property manager coordinating repairs, or anyone in between — deserves a clear, usable experience on our site.

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**, the internationally recognized standard for web accessibility. These guidelines describe how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.

## What We've Done to Support Accessibility

Our site has been built with accessibility in mind from the ground up. This includes efforts to ensure compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies, meaningful alt text on images, sufficient color contrast throughout the interface, keyboard-navigable menus and interactive elements, and clearly structured headings that help users move through content efficiently.

We also aim to avoid content that flashes or strobes in ways known to cause issues for visitors with photosensitive conditions, and we work to keep our language plain and our layouts uncluttered — which benefits all visitors, not just those using assistive technology.

## Known Limitations

While we strive to meet WCAG 2.1 AA across the entire site, some third-party content or tools embedded in our pages — such as mapping widgets or scheduling integrations — may not fully conform to these standards. We do not control the accessibility of third-party services, but we try to choose partners who take accessibility seriously and we flag known gaps as we identify them.

If you encounter a barrier on any part of our site, we want to hear about it.

## Contact Us About an Accessibility Issue

If you experience difficulty accessing any content or functionality on this website, please reach out to us directly. We are committed to addressing reported accessibility issues within a reasonable timeframe — typically within five business days of receiving a clear description of the problem.

## Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a one-time checkbox — it requires ongoing attention as our site evolves. We periodically review our pages and update our practices as standards develop. If you have a suggestion for how we can improve, we welcome that feedback just as much as a report of a specific barrier.
