## Restoration emergencies common in Florissant

Florissant sits in north St. Louis County, where the Missouri River floodplain transitions into rolling upland neighborhoods built largely between the 1950s and 1980s. That construction era means full basements are the norm, and those basements are surrounded by the heavy, expansive clay soils that define much of the St. Louis metro. When spring rains saturate the ground faster than it can drain, hydrostatic pressure builds against foundation walls and water finds every crack, cold joint, and window well it can. Summer brings the other extreme: intense convective storms capable of producing straight-line winds that strip shingles, push water under flashing, and send tree limbs through roofs. In winter, freeze-thaw cycles stress gutters and masonry, and interior pipes in poorly insulated exterior walls — common in homes of this era — can freeze and burst overnight. The combination of an aging housing stock, clay-heavy drainage, and a genuinely variable St. Louis climate keeps restoration demand steady across Florissant's neighborhoods year-round.

## Services we provide in Florissant

### Water damage restoration
Water damage in Florissant frequently starts in the basement — a sump pump that fails during a heavy rain event, a water heater that lets go on a concrete floor, or groundwater seeping through a poured-concrete or block foundation wall. Because these homes were built before modern waterproofing standards, moisture can travel behind paneling or under carpet for days before it's obvious at the surface. ### Fire and smoke damage restoration
Ranch-style and split-level homes common throughout Florissant have open floor plans that allow smoke to travel quickly through HVAC systems and into every room, even when the fire itself is contained to a kitchen or utility area. Smoke residue in insulation, ductwork, and wall cavities requires more than surface cleaning. We assess the full path of smoke migration and scope the restoration accordingly.

### Mold remediation
Mold can colonize wet drywall and wood framing within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion. Florissant's humid summers accelerate that timeline in crawlspaces, attic decks, and finished basements that don't dry quickly on their own. We identify the moisture source, contain the affected area, and remove compromised materials — addressing the condition rather than masking it.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Florissant

The majority of Florissant's residential housing was constructed between roughly 1950 and 1985, when the area grew rapidly as families moved north from St. Louis city.
