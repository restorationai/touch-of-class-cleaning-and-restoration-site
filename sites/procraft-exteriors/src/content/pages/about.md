ProCraft Exteriors has been working on homes and commercial properties in the St. Louis area since 2005. The company was built around a straightforward idea: exterior work done correctly the first time saves homeowners from the kind of compounding damage — rot behind siding, water intrusion through a failed roof, mold behind a wall — that turns a manageable repair into a major loss. Nearly two decades later, that focus on getting the details right is still what drives how the company operates.

## What we do

Most days, the work looks like this: a homeowner calls after a storm has lifted shingles or driven hail through a soffit. A property manager reaches out because water has been finding its way behind a facade for longer than anyone realized. The crew shows up, assesses what's actually happening — not just the visible damage but what's underneath it — and maps out a repair plan that addresses the source, not just the symptom. A significant share of that work runs through insurance claims, which means the team is familiar with documentation requirements, adjuster timelines, and what carriers typically need to approve a scope of work. That experience matters when a homeowner is already stressed and doesn't want to navigate the claims process alone.

## Where we work

The company is based in Chesterfield, in west St. Louis County, and serves the broader metro area on both sides of the Missouri River. The region's housing stock spans a wide range of construction eras — from postwar ranch homes in inner-ring suburbs to newer construction in fast-growing communities like Wildwood, O'Fallon, and St. Charles. That variety matters because older homes carry different risks: aging flashing details, original wood substrates, and materials that weren't designed to meet current wind or moisture standards. The St. Louis area also sees genuine weather extremes — ice storms in winter, severe thunderstorm and hail seasons in spring, and stretches of heat and humidity in summer that accelerate wear on exterior materials. ProCraft Exteriors works across this geography regularly and understands what the climate asks of a roof, a wall system, or a gutter installation here specifically.
