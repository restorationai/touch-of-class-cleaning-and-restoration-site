ProCraft Exteriors handles the full range of property damage work — from the first hours after a loss through the final coat of paint. Residential homeowners, commercial property managers, and multi-family building owners across the Chesterfield area and greater St. Louis region call us when water, fire, storm, or contamination has made a space unlivable or unusable. One company manages the process from emergency response through finished reconstruction, so nothing falls through the gap between contractors.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active or a property needs to be secured quickly, the priority is stopping the loss from getting worse. Water intrusion — whether from a burst pipe, appliance failure, sewer backup, or storm flooding — spreads fast; moisture behind drywall and under flooring can begin supporting mold growth within days if extraction and drying aren't started promptly. Fire and smoke damage require a different kind of urgency: soot is acidic and continues to etch surfaces, embed into porous materials, and push odor deeper the longer it sits. Biohazard situations — sewage contamination, trauma scenes, hoarding remediation — call for careful containment and disposal handled to regulatory standards. Storm damage, from hail-punctured roofing to wind-driven debris, often needs immediate board-up or tarping to keep weather and intruders out while the full scope of damage is assessed. ProCraft Exteriors responds to each of these situations and can move directly into the next phase of work without handing the project off to a separate contractor.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once mitigation is complete and affected materials have been removed, the rebuild begins. ProCraft Exteriors handles structural repairs, framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and exterior work — the same scope a general contractor would cover, but with the context of exactly what was damaged and why. That matters: a rebuild crew that wasn't part of the mitigation phase can miss a detail that affects the finished product. We also take on renovation and improvement work outside of insurance claims, so if a loss creates an opportunity to update a kitchen layout or upgrade materials, that conversation can happen in the same project.

## Specialty services

Some situations don't fit neatly into emergency response or reconstruction. Mold inspection and remediation — including post-remediation verification — addresses contamination that may have developed long before a visible loss event. Odor removal for persistent smoke, pet, or biological odors often requires equipment and chemistry beyond standard cleaning. Board-up and temporary weatherproofing protect properties between the damage event and the start of active work. These services are available as standalone scopes or as part of a larger project.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction under one roof means the documentation, scheduling, and communication stay consistent from day one through project completion.
