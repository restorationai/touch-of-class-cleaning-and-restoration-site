ProCraft Exteriors is based in Chesterfield and schedules exterior work across the greater St. Louis metro. Coverage questions are best answered with a quick phone call to [(314) 965-2353](tel:3149652353) — the team can confirm availability for your address and get a visit on the calendar during business hours.

## Where we work

Chesterfield sits near the center of St. Louis County, which puts the ProCraft crew within a reasonable drive of communities stretching from the Missouri River bluffs in the north down through the older inner-ring suburbs and out toward the newer developments along the I-44 and I-64 corridors. The region mixes mid-century ranch homes, brick colonial builds from the postwar boom, and more recent vinyl-sided construction — each with its own exterior quirks when it comes to siding, roofing, gutters, and trim.

## Coverage by distance

**Closest in:** Communities immediately surrounding Chesterfield — Ballwin, Ellisville, Wildwood, Town and Country, Creve Coeur, and Maryland Heights — are the most straightforward to schedule. Crews can typically reach these areas without fighting significant cross-county traffic.

**Mid-range:** St. Louis County's eastern and southern neighborhoods, along with municipalities like St. Peters, O'Fallon, and Lake Saint Louis in St. Charles County, fall comfortably within the regular service footprint. Crossing the county line adds drive time, but it's a routine part of the schedule.

**Outer edge:** Communities farther out — toward Jefferson County to the south or Lincoln County to the north — may involve longer lead times depending on project load and crew positioning. It's worth a call before assuming the answer is no.

Traffic on I-270, I-64, and Highway 40 can shift arrival windows meaningfully during peak hours, so the team accounts for that when booking morning versus afternoon visits.
