## What to Look for in a Storm Damage Restoration Company

Choosing the right company after a storm is not just about who answers the phone first. Here is what actually matters when you are comparing your options in Chesterfield.

**Local presence matters more than brand recognition.** A company headquartered in Chesterfield knows the local building codes, the permit process through St. Louis County, and the specific materials used in the area's housing stock. They are also more accountable, because their reputation lives and dies in the same community you do.

**Insurance documentation is a core skill, not a bonus.** Missouri homeowners' policies cover most sudden storm damage, including wind, hail, and falling trees, but the claim outcome depends heavily on the quality of the damage documentation. A restoration company that produces a thorough, itemized scope of work protects your claim from the start. Ask any company you are considering how they handle adjuster coordination.

**Verify licensing and insurance before anyone gets on your roof.** Missouri requires roofing and exterior contractors to carry general liability and workers' compensation insurance. A licensed and insured contractor protects you from liability if someone is injured on your property. Ask for certificates before work begins.

**Storm chasers are a real risk in Chesterfield.** After any significant hail or wind event in the St. Louis metro, out-of-state contractors flood the area offering fast, cheap repairs. Many disappear before the work is complete or before warranty issues surface. Stick with companies that have a verifiable local address and a track record in the area.

**Tarping and board-up are time-sensitive.** Missouri summers bring fast-moving storms, and a second storm can follow within days. Emergency tarping and board-up are not optional if your roof or windows are compromised. Ask whether the company you call handles emergency stabilization before permanent repairs begin.

## What Storm Damage Restoration in Chesterfield Actually Involves

Storm damage restoration is not just replacing shingles. A complete restoration scope for a Chesterfield home typically covers several phases.

First is the damage assessment. A qualified contractor walks the full property, documents every point of impact, and produces a written scope that matches what the insurance adjuster will look for. Photographs, measurements, and material specifications all matter here.

Second is emergency stabilization. If the roof is open, windows are broken, or structural elements are exposed, tarping and board-up happen before anything else. This prevents secondary water damage and keeps the claim from expanding.

Third is the actual repair work: roofing, siding, windows, gutters, fascia, and any interior damage caused by water intrusion. In Chesterfield, where many homes have brick exteriors and architectural shingles, material matching is important for both aesthetics and insurance compliance.

Fourth is the insurance close-out. A good restoration company does not hand you a bill and disappear. They work with your adjuster through the supplement process if additional damage is found during repairs, and they provide the documentation you need for final claim settlement.
