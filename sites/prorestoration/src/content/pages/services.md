From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen fire that's left soot in every room, ProRestoration Services handles the full arc of property damage — emergency mitigation, structural drying, demolition, rebuild, and everything in between. Serving homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial operators across Bakersfield and the surrounding San Joaquin Valley, the team works on single-family homes, apartment complexes, retail spaces, and light industrial properties. One company, one point of contact, from the first call through the final inspection.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, every hour matters. Water intrusion — whether from a failed water heater, a slab leak, or a broken supply line — can saturate drywall and subfloor within hours and create conditions for mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. The emergency response side of the business covers **water damage restoration**, **fire and smoke damage restoration**, **biohazard and trauma cleanup**, and **storm damage response**. Fire jobs often involve more than charred material; smoke residue penetrates HVAC systems, insulation, and wall cavities, leaving an acrid smell that persists long after visible soot is gone. Biohazard work — sewage backups, unattended incidents, hoarding situations — is handled by a trained crew following OSHA protocols and proper disposal procedures. Storm response in Kern County typically means wind-driven rain intrusion, roof damage, and debris removal after the Valley's periodic high-wind events. All emergency services are available around the clock.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and stable, ProRestoration Services transitions directly into the rebuild phase — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, painting, cabinetry, and finish work. Because the same company that documented the damage is handling the rebuild, there's no handoff gap where scope gets lost or timelines slip. The reconstruction team also takes on general contracting projects for clients who need renovation work unrelated to an insurance claim.

## Specialty services

Some jobs don't fit neatly into emergency or rebuild categories. **Mold inspection and remediation** addresses visible growth and hidden colonies behind walls, under flooring, and inside crawl spaces — a persistent concern in properties that have had any moisture intrusion. **Odor removal** uses hydroxyl and ozone treatment to neutralize smoke, pet, and biological odors at the molecular level rather than masking them. **Board-up and tarping** services secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism damage, protecting it from weather and unauthorized entry while the restoration plan is finalized. **Contents pack-out and storage** moves salvageable belongings off-site for cleaning and safe storage during the restoration process.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction under one roof means the documentation, scope of work, and timeline stay consistent from start to finish — which matters both for your peace of mind and for a smoother insurance claim.
