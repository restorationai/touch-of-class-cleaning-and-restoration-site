ProRestoration Services dispatches from Bakersfield around the clock — water doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we. When you call, you reach a live person who can schedule a crew, walk you through immediate steps to limit damage, and confirm whether your address falls within our service area. Most calls are answered and a response is coordinated the same day, often within hours of your first contact.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Bakersfield and covers a wide swath of Kern County and the surrounding region of California's Central Valley. The valley's geography works in our favor: the flat terrain between the Sierra Nevada foothills to the east and the Temblor Range to the west means our crews can move efficiently across a large footprint without the mountain-pass delays that complicate response in other parts of the state. Whether you're dealing with a burst pipe in a tract home in southwest Bakersfield or a commercial flood in an agricultural facility further out in the county, our dispatch team routes crews to minimize drive time.

## Coverage by distance

The cities and communities closest to our Bakersfield headquarters see the fastest arrivals — crews are typically loading equipment and heading out while you're still on the phone. Communities in the mid-range of our coverage area, including outlying Kern County towns and nearby valley cities, are well within our regular service rotation. For locations at the edge of our territory, we're still dispatching — arrival simply takes longer, and we'll give you an honest estimate when you call rather than a number we can't stand behind. One practical note: Highway 99 and I-5 corridor traffic can affect timing during peak hours, so early-morning calls on weekdays sometimes move faster than mid-afternoon ones.

## Don't see your city?

The list of service-area pages on this site covers our most frequent destinations, but it isn't a hard boundary. For significant losses — large commercial properties, multi-unit buildings, or situations involving extensive structural damage — we regularly dispatch beyond our standard footprint. Call (661) 393-9306 and describe the situation. If we can get a crew to you, we will. If the location is genuinely outside our range, we'll tell you straight and point you toward a qualified option.
