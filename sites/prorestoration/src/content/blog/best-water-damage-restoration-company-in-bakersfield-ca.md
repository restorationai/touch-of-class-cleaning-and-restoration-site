## What To Look for When Choosing a Water Damage Company in Bakersfield

The right company for your loss depends on a few specific factors. Here is what to ask before you commit.

**IICRC certification.** Ask specifically for the company's IICRC firm number, not just a claim of certification. The IICRC maintains a public directory at iicrc.org where you can verify any firm.

**California CSLB license.** If your job will involve any reconstruction (drywall, flooring, cabinetry), the company needs a California contractor's license. You can verify any license at the [CSLB license check](https://www.cslb.ca.gov). Unlicensed reconstruction work can create problems with your insurance claim and with future home sales.

**Written scope before work begins.** A reputable company provides a written scope of work with line-item pricing before any mitigation begins. Verbal estimates that balloon after the fact are one of the most common complaints in this industry. Read [How To Choose a Restoration Company in Bakersfield](/blog/choosing-a-restoration-company/) for a full breakdown of what to watch for.

**Insurance direct billing.** If you are filing a claim, ask whether the company bills your insurer directly or requires you to pay upfront and seek reimbursement. Most established restoration companies handle direct billing, but confirm it.

**Lead-safe certification for older homes.** If your home was built before 1978 and walls or ceilings will be disturbed, ask for the company's EPA Lead-Safe Firm certification number. This is not optional under federal law for covered renovations.

Bakersfield's slab-on-grade construction also means that slab leaks are a distinct category of loss here. A slab leak can saturate the concrete and the flooring above it before you notice any visible water. The drying process for a slab leak is different from a burst pipe above grade: it typically requires desiccant dehumidifiers and extended monitoring, not just air movers. Ask any company you are considering whether they have experience with slab leak drying specifically.
