## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rosedale

When you call (661) 393-9306, a crew is dispatched immediately — around the clock — with truck-mounted extraction units, moisture meters, and drying equipment loaded and ready. The process follows a clear sequence calibrated to the homes we see most often in this part of Kern County:

**1. Rapid water extraction.** Standing water is removed first using high-capacity extractors. In the wide, open floor plans common to Westdale and Rosedale Ranch, water can travel 30 or 40 feet from the source before it finds a wall — we map the full migration before pulling equipment.

**2. Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters identify water that has wicked into wall cavities, under baseboards, and beneath flooring. Engineered hardwood and LVP — both common in these homes — trap moisture underneath in ways that aren't visible to the eye.

**3. Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and LGR dehumidifiers are placed to dry the structure, not just the surface. Drying targets are set to IICRC S500 standard and monitored daily with written moisture logs — documentation your insurance adjuster will require.

**4. Controlled demolition when needed.** Saturated drywall and insulation cannot be dried in place. We remove only what is necessary, photograph everything, and catalog materials so your contractor can match finishes — a detail that matters in a neighborhood where homes carry significant equity and owners expect restoration to pre-loss condition.

**5. Clearance and reconstruction handoff.** Once the structure reaches dry standard, we provide a final moisture report and coordinate directly with your insurance carrier and, if applicable, your general contractor for reconstruction.
