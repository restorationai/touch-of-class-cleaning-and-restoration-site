## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Arvin

A mold assessment isn't a single swab and a report. The process starts with a thorough visual inspection of the areas most likely to harbor hidden growth: under kitchen and bathroom sinks, inside HVAC air handlers, along the base of exterior walls, and in any room that has a history of plumbing repairs or water intrusion. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help locate wet zones behind surfaces that look dry to the eye.

Once high-risk areas are identified, we collect air samples and, where surface growth is visible, tape-lift or bulk samples. Those samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory for analysis. The lab report identifies the genera and relative concentration of spores present — information that matters because elevated Stachybotrys or Chaetomium counts in a living space carry different remediation implications than a background level of Cladosporium. You receive a written report with findings, sample results, and a clear explanation of what the numbers mean for your property and the people living in it.
