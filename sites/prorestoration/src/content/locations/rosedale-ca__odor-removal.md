## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Rosedale

Effective deodorization isn't a single step — it's a sequence, and skipping any part of it is why DIY attempts and surface sprays fail.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization method eliminates an odor that still has an active source. We locate and remove or clean the contaminated material before any treatment begins — whether that's charred drywall, saturated insulation, or pet-affected carpet padding.

**Thermal fogging.** A petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed to penetrate the same porous surfaces that smoke or odor particles reached. This is particularly effective in the vaulted great rooms common in Rosedale's newer builds, where odor-laden air stratifies near the ceiling.

**Ozone or hydroxyl treatment.** For severe smoke odor or biologically sourced smells, we deploy ozone generators or hydroxyl machines depending on occupancy status. Ozone treatment requires an unoccupied structure; hydroxyl generators are safe for occupied spaces and are often the right call when a homeowner needs to remain on-site. Both methods oxidize odor molecules rather than masking them.

**HVAC deodorization.** In tight modern construction, the duct system is a primary odor distribution pathway. We treat supply and return ducts directly — a step that's easy to skip but critical in the single-story and two-story open-plan homes typical of this area.

**Final air quality verification.** We don't close a job on smell alone. Post-treatment readings confirm that VOC levels have returned to baseline.
