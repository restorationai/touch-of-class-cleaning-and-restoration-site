## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Wasco

Every job starts with a fiber and soiling assessment — not every carpet in a 1960s Wasco ranch home is the same cut pile, and pre-treatment chemistry that works on nylon performs differently on older wool-blend or polyester fiber.
