## Our Carpet Cleaning Process, Calibrated to Rosedale Conditions

Professional carpet cleaning here is not a one-size-fits-all hot-water extraction pass. Before any cleaning begins, technicians assess moisture levels with calibrated meters — a step that matters especially in Rosedale homes where a past appliance event may have left residual dampness in the pad. If the carpet is dry and the issue is soiling and odor, the process moves through pre-treatment with a pH-appropriate emulsifier, agitation to break the bond between soil particles and carpet fibers, truck-mounted hot-water extraction that pulls suspended debris and cleaning solution back out, and a grooming pass that sets the pile for even drying.

For stain removal, the chemistry is matched to the stain type rather than applied generically — tannin stains from spilled coffee or wine respond to an acidic treatment, while protein-based stains from pets or food need an enzyme-based approach.
