## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Arvin

When we arrive, the first priority is source control — confirming the supply valve is fully closed and the appliance is isolated. Then we assess the spread with moisture meters and thermal imaging, mapping where water has traveled under flooring and behind walls rather than guessing by what's visible on the surface.

Extraction comes next. Standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, followed by targeted drying using commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create the airflow patterns the IICRC S500 standard calls for. In Arvin's climate, ambient outdoor humidity is often low, which helps drying times — but that same dry air can mask moisture trapped in dense materials like tile mortar beds or original hardwood subfloors, so we don't rely on feel. We log moisture readings daily until affected materials reach documented dry standards.

If the leak has been running long enough to raise mold concerns — typically 48–72 hours of wet conditions — we flag that during the initial assessment and scope the remediation separately so nothing is missed.
