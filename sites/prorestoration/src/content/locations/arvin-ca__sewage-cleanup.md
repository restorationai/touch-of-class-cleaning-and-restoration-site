## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Arvin

Every sewage job starts with containment. Before a single piece of flooring comes up, the crew establishes a contamination boundary using poly sheeting and negative-air pressure — this keeps aerosolized pathogens from migrating into living areas or HVAC ductwork. From there, the process moves through five concrete phases:

1. **Extraction and solid removal** — standing sewage and solid waste are removed with truck-mounted extractors and HEPA-filtered vacuums.
2. **Tear-out of unsalvageable material** — porous materials (drywall, insulation, saturated subfloor) that absorbed Category 3 water are removed and bagged for disposal per California health and waste codes.
3. **EPA-registered disinfection** — structural surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants rated for sewage pathogens; dwell times are observed, not rushed.
4. **Drying and dehumidification** — commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry throughout, not just on the surface.
5. **Post-sanitization verification** — surfaces are tested before containment comes down, and documentation is prepared for your insurance adjuster.
