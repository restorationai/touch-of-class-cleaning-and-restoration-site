## Why Wasco Properties Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock along the Palm Avenue corridor and throughout Westside Wasco tells the story clearly: homes built in the 1950s and 1960s were constructed with original plaster walls, hardwood floors laid over concrete slabs, and minimal vapor barriers. These materials are highly absorbent. When a smoke event, sewage spill, or water loss occurs, odor-causing compounds — volatile organic compounds from smoke, hydrogen sulfide from sewer gas, microbial off-gassing from mold — penetrate deeply into the substrate rather than sitting on the surface.

The Valley's climate compounds the problem. ## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Wasco

Effective odor elimination is a sequence, not a single treatment. The process begins with a thorough source identification — because applying ozone or hydroxyl technology to a space that still contains wet materials or active contamination is wasted effort. We locate and remove or dry every odor-generating source first.

Once the source is addressed, we select the deodorization method matched to the specific odor type and the structure:

- **Thermal fogging** — a heated solvent-based fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, reaching inside wall cavities, under flooring, and into HVAC returns. Particularly effective after structure fires and for homes where smoke has deeply penetrated original plaster.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — safe for occupied or partially occupied spaces, hydroxyls break down odor molecules through oxidation without the re-entry restrictions that ozone requires. Useful for ongoing mold odor or sewage odor in homes where full evacuation is not practical.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is one of the most powerful tools for severe smoke odor removal, but it requires complete evacuation of people, pets, and plants. We schedule, seal, and monitor ozone treatments carefully, then ventilate fully before clearance.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct deodorization** — in Wasco's older homes, the HVAC system is often the last reservoir of odor. We treat ductwork directly so the air handler is not redistributing contaminated air after the rest of the job is complete.

All work is documented with pre- and post-treatment air quality readings so you have a record for insurance purposes.
