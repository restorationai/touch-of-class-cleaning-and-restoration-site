## Why Taft Properties See Mold Issues

The West Side's dry heat is often mistaken for protection against mold. In open air, that logic holds — Taft's low annual rainfall and low relative humidity do slow surface evaporation. The problem is what happens inside wall cavities and under flooring. Galvanized pipes that have reached end-of-life corrode from the inside out, producing pinhole leaks that wet subfloor sheathing and wall framing for months before the water bill or a soft spot in the floor gives it away. Old wall furnaces — still common in pre-1960 cottages throughout South Taft — create convective air loops that pull humid air from crawl spaces up into wall assemblies, then cool it against exterior sheathing. That cycle feeds mold even in dry years.

The desert-edge climate does offer one real advantage: structural drying times in Taft are shorter than in coastal or Central Valley markets, because ambient vapor pressure is low. That shortens the remediation window, but only after containment is established and the moisture source is stopped.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Taft

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and pin-type meters across walls, ceilings, and subfloor — before any demolition begins. Once the scope is defined, we establish negative-pressure containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers.
