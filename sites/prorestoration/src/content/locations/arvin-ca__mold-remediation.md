## Why Arvin Properties See Mold Issues

Arvin's housing stock skews older and densely occupied, which creates specific conditions that feed mold growth. Many homes in the 93203 ZIP code were built during the mid-20th-century agricultural expansion of the San Joaquin Valley, and their crawl spaces, single-pane windows, and original plumbing fittings were not designed with moisture management in mind. Evaporative coolers — still common in this part of Kern County — introduce significant humidity into living spaces when not properly maintained or when door and window seals have degraded.

Caliente Creek and the broader Arvin-Lamont basin drainage pattern also matter here. During heavy storm years, shallow groundwater levels rise and moisture migrates through slab foundations, particularly in low-lying areas near Di Giorgio Park and the Highway 223 corridor. Homeowners sometimes discover mold not after a visible flood, but after a wet winter quietly saturated the soil beneath their slab for months.

Agricultural dust is a secondary factor worth noting: fine particulate matter that settles into HVAC filters and ductwork can trap moisture and organic material, giving mold spores an additional surface to colonize beyond the obvious wet areas.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Arvin

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not just a visual check, but thermal imaging and moisture meter readings taken at the wall cavity level. In Arvin's older stucco and wood-frame homes, mold frequently travels behind wall surfaces well beyond the visible damage zone, so the inspection determines the true scope before any containment is set up.

Once the affected area is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating into unaffected rooms during the removal process. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and framing are removed, bagged, and disposed of per California Department of Public Health guidelines. We do not simply encapsulate and walk away — materials that cannot be cleaned to a verified standard are removed.

After physical removal, affected structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings confirm the structure has returned to acceptable levels. A final visual and air-quality check closes out the remediation phase before any reconstruction begins. The entire process follows IICRC S520 standard protocols.

In Arvin's older wood-frame homes — particularly those built before 1970 in the blocks surrounding Downtown Arvin — interior walls often use a lath-and-plaster construction rather than modern drywall.
