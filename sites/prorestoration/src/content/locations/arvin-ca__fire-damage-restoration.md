## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Arvin

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is a room-by-room scope of the fire, smoke, and water damage — documented with photographs and moisture readings that support your insurance claim from the first hour. We board up or tarp any compromised openings to secure the structure before work begins.

Demolition is limited to what is genuinely unsalvageable. Because many Arvin homes have original hardwood floors, plaster ceilings, and built-in cabinetry that would be expensive to replicate, we take a conservative approach: test before you tear out. Soot and smoke residue on salvageable surfaces are treated with dry-chemical sponges first, then HEPA-vacuumed, then cleaned with appropriate chemical agents matched to the surface type — alkaline cleaners for protein-based smoke from cooking fires, different chemistry for synthetic-material smoke from electrical fires.

Odor elimination is not masking. We use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generators to break down odor-causing molecules in wall cavities and HVAC ductwork — the pathways smoke travels that a surface wipe-down never reaches. Structural drying runs concurrently using commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers, with daily moisture readings until the structure meets IICRC drying standards.
