## Our Reconstruction Process in Taft

Reconstruction begins the moment mitigation is complete and the structure is dry and stable. The sequence matters:

**Structural assessment and scope documentation** — Before any framing or drywall goes up, we photograph and measure every affected cavity, identify any pre-existing code deficiencies that the loss exposed, and produce a line-item scope that your insurance adjuster can review directly. In Taft's older homes, this step frequently uncovers knob-and-tube wiring or un-permitted additions that need to be addressed before the rebuild can proceed.

**Permitting through Kern County** — Most structural reconstruction in the 93268 ZIP code falls under Kern County Building and Safety jurisdiction. We pull the required permits, schedule inspections, and keep the timeline moving so you aren't waiting on approvals while the job sits open.

**Framing, mechanical rough-in, and systems upgrades** — Where galvanized supply lines or undersized drains contributed to the original loss, we replace them with PEX or ABS during the rebuild rather than patching around them. Wall furnace alcoves are reframed to current clearance standards or converted to central HVAC rough-in if the homeowner chooses to upgrade.

**Finish work and final inspection** — Drywall, texture, paint, flooring, and trim are matched as closely as possible to the existing home. We do not consider the job complete until the county signs off and you've walked the finished space.

## Insurance Coordination for Taft Reconstruction Claims

Post-disaster reconstruction claims on older Taft properties frequently involve coverage disputes around code-upgrade costs — the portion of the rebuild that brings the structure into compliance with current California building code rather than simply restoring it to its pre-loss condition. Many standard homeowners' policies include an "ordinance or law" endorsement that covers this gap, but adjusters don't always apply it automatically.

We document code-upgrade line items separately in our scope so your adjuster can match them against your policy's ordinance-or-law coverage. We also communicate directly with carriers throughout the project, reducing the back-and-forth that stalls rebuilds for weeks at a time.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors working in Ford City and South Taft: the desert-edge climate here dries structural framing faster than coastal California, which looks like good news until you realize that wind-driven dust infiltrates open wall cavities aggressively during reconstruction. If a house is open to the exterior for more than a day or two — common during framing or window replacement — fine particulate settles into new insulation batts and HVAC rough-in.
