## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Taft

Effective odor elimination in an older Taft home requires matching the method to what the structure has actually absorbed — not running a single machine and calling it done.

The process typically starts with a thorough source assessment. We identify whether the odor originates from fire and smoke residue, microbial growth, sewage, or a combination, because each requires a different chemical and mechanical approach. From there, we select from a range of proven methods:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules bonded to porous surfaces inside wall cavities and under flooring.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — hydroxyl generators produce UV-catalyzed oxidizing radicals that break down odor-causing compounds at a molecular level. Unlike ozone, hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner near Taft College needs to remain on-site.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is one of the most effective tools for severe smoke odor in vacant structures. We use it in unoccupied homes where full evacuation is possible, following established protocols for off-gassing and re-entry.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct cleaning** — in homes with forced-air systems, odor recirculates through ductwork. We address the air handler and ducts as part of the scope, not as an add-on.
