## Our Mold Remediation Process in Rosedale

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across all affected surfaces, not just the visible damage. In Rosedale's slab-on-grade homes, that means checking the perimeter of the slab, the bottom plates of framed walls, and any cabinetry that sits directly on the floor. We identify the moisture source first, because remediating mold without eliminating the water intrusion is a temporary fix at best.

Once the scope is clear, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms — critical in open-concept floor plans where a kitchen, dining area, and living room share continuous air space. Affected drywall, insulation, and porous materials are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines. Structural framing that can be dried and treated in place is cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and dried to verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which your insurance adjuster will need.
