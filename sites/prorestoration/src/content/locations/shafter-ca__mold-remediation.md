## Our Mold Remediation Process in Shafter

Every job begins with a thorough moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a visual walkthrough. We identify the moisture source, document affected materials, and establish containment before any demolition starts. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised framing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per California Department of Public Health guidelines.
