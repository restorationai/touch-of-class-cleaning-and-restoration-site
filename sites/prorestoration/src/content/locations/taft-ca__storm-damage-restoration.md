## Why Taft Properties See Disproportionate Storm Damage

The housing stock is the first thing anyone working in this area has to reckon with. The cottages that spread through Ford City and South Taft during the oil-boom decades were built quickly and practically — single-wall framing in some cases, shallow roof pitches, and eave overhangs that were sized for shade, not for deflecting wind-driven rain. The soil compounds the problem. Caliche — the calcium-carbonate hardpan common throughout Kern County — sits just below the surface in much of Taft.
