## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Wasco

When a call comes in from the Westside Wasco area or anywhere else in the 93280 ZIP, the process starts with a rapid assessment of the source — confirming the appliance is isolated and water supply is shut off before any drying equipment goes in. Standing water is extracted first using truck-mounted units capable of pulling water from beneath vinyl plank, tile, and original hardwood. Moisture mapping follows immediately: thermal imaging and calibrated meters identify how far water has wicked into wall cavities, toe-kick spaces, and subfloor panels that aren't visibly wet.

Drying is structured around the IICRC S500 standard, which means equipment placement is calculated, not guessed. Air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are staged to create a drying chamber around the affected zone. Daily moisture readings track progress and determine when structural materials have returned to acceptable levels — typically three to five days for a contained appliance leak in a single-story home, though older plank subfloors common in Wasco can retain moisture longer than modern OSB and may require an additional drying day or two.

Affected cabinetry, drywall, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed and documented photographically before disposal — a step that matters significantly for insurance claims.

Sudden and accidental appliance leaks — a burst ice maker line, a washing machine hose failure, a dishwasher pump seal that gives out — are generally covered under standard homeowners policies as sudden and accidental water damage.
