## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Lamont

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence we follow is deliberate and calibrated to what we actually find on-site in communities like Lamont.

**Board-up and structural stabilization** comes first. If a fire has compromised a roof section or exterior wall, we secure the opening before smoke odor and soot spread further — and before opportunistic entry becomes a concern in a neighborhood where properties sometimes sit vacant between tenants.

**Soot and smoke mapping** follows. Smoke doesn't stay in the room where the fire started. It migrates through ductwork, settles into closets two rooms away, and penetrates porous surfaces like the unpainted drywall common in older Lamont rentals. We use thermal imaging and air sampling to trace exactly how far contamination has traveled before we start cleaning.

**Structural drying and odor neutralization** run in parallel. Even a fire that was extinguished quickly leaves behind water from suppression — and in the Valley's dry heat, that moisture can begin supporting mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours. We deploy commercial dehumidification and HEPA air scrubbers to pull both moisture and particulate from the structure simultaneously.

**Debris removal and content evaluation** separate salvageable items from those that need to be documented and discarded. We photograph everything for your insurance claim.

**Reconstruction** closes the job — framing, drywall, insulation, painting, and finish work handled under our CSLB license (#960566) so you're not coordinating a separate contractor.
