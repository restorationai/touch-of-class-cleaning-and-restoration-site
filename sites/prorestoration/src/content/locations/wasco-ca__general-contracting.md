## Local Note: What Wasco Contractors Often Miss on Ranch-Home Remodels

One pattern that comes up repeatedly on Wasco ranch-home renovations: the original slab was poured without a vapor barrier, or with a barrier that has long since degraded. When homeowners replace vinyl or carpet with tile or hardwood, moisture migrating up through the slab can cause adhesive failure or cupping within the first year — especially in rooms on the shaded north side of the house where the slab stays cooler and condensation is more likely. Before any flooring goes down, we test slab moisture content with a calcium chloride or in-situ probe test. It adds a day to the schedule and is worth every hour.
