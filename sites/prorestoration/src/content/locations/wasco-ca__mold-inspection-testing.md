## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Wasco

A mold assessment is not a visual walk-through with a flashlight. The inspection process begins with a structured moisture survey using calibrated thermal imaging cameras and non-invasive moisture meters to map wet areas behind walls and under flooring without cutting into surfaces unnecessarily. In Wasco's older ranch homes, where original lath-and-plaster construction is still common in pre-1960 properties, moisture readings behave differently than in modern drywall — plaster holds water longer and releases it slowly, so elevated readings can persist weeks after the source is corrected.

Once moisture pathways are mapped, air sampling is collected from affected rooms and from outside the structure to establish a baseline spore count. Surface samples — tape lifts or swabs — are taken from visible growth to identify the genus and concentration of mold present. All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results typically return within 24 to 48 hours. The written assessment report identifies the moisture source, the affected materials, the mold species and concentration, and a recommended scope of remediation. That documentation is formatted to meet insurance carrier requirements and California contractor licensing standards.
