## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Shafter

The first priority on any appliance leak call is stopping the source. If a homeowner hasn't already shut off the supply valve, the crew locates the nearest isolation point — sometimes the main shutoff near the meter — before any equipment is staged. From there, the process follows a calibrated sequence:

- **Moisture mapping**: Thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters trace where water has migrated under flooring, into wall cavities, and beneath cabinetry. In Gossamer Grove homes with engineered hardwood over concrete slab, water frequently travels farther than it appears on the surface.
- **Extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and water trapped in flooring layers. Refrigerator ice maker leaks that have run slowly for days often saturate the subfloor well beyond the visible wet zone.
- **Structural drying**: Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned to dry the structure to pre-loss moisture content, documented against IICRC S500 drying standards. In Kern County's dry summer heat, ambient conditions can accelerate drying — but that same low humidity can mask residual moisture in wall cavities if readings aren't taken at depth.
- **Documentation**: Every affected material, moisture reading, and equipment placement is photographed and logged for the insurance claim file.
