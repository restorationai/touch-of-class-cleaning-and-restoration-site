## Why Delano Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Mold

Kern County's climate averages fewer than 6 inches of rainfall annually, but that figure masks the real risk. When rain does arrive — typically between November and March — older homes in Downtown Delano absorb it through aging roof flashings, single-pane windows, and foundation gaps that were never designed to handle modern irrigation runoff patterns. The result is localized moisture intrusion that dries on the surface while staying wet inside wall cavities and under subfloors.

West Delano's 2000s-era tract homes face a different problem. Meanwhile, properties near the Highway 99 commercial strip — including cold-storage and ag-processing facilities — deal with high-humidity interior environments that create persistent mold pressure in HVAC systems and wall assemblies.

Older bungalows also commonly contain original plaster walls and cellulose-based insulation, both of which hold moisture longer than modern materials and provide excellent organic substrate for mold growth. That combination of building stock and climate makes a thorough mold assessment more than a precaution — it's often the only way to know what's actually happening behind the walls.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Delano

A mold inspection isn't a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. The process begins with a detailed moisture mapping of the affected areas using calibrated thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters, which lets us identify wet zones behind drywall and under flooring without unnecessary demolition.

From there, we collect air samples from inside the structure and compare them against outdoor baseline samples — a step that's particularly important in Delano, where high ambient particulate counts from agricultural activity can complicate raw spore counts if you don't account for outdoor conditions. Surface swab or tape-lift samples are collected from any visible growth or discoloration. All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory for analysis, and results typically return within 24 to 48 hours.

The final deliverable is a written mold assessment report that documents sample locations, lab results, moisture readings, and recommended next steps. That report is formatted to be usable with insurance carriers and, when remediation is needed, serves as the scope-of-work foundation for the remediation contractor.
