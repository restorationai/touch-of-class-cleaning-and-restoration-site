## Why Lamont Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Hidden Mold

The housing stock in Lamont proper and the surrounding unincorporated community skews older, with many homes built in the 1950s through 1970s. ## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Lamont

A thorough mold assessment here isn't a quick visual walkthrough. The process starts with a moisture mapping survey using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate elevated readings inside walls, under flooring, and in crawl spaces — areas where mold grows long before it becomes visible. This step is especially important in Lamont's older homes, where original construction often lacks the vapor barriers and ventilation gaps that would otherwise slow moisture migration.

Once suspect zones are identified, air samples and surface swab samples are collected and sent to an accredited third-party laboratory. Air sampling captures spore counts and species data for both the interior and a baseline outdoor sample — that comparison is what tells you whether indoor levels are genuinely elevated relative to the ambient outdoor environment, not just whether spores are present (they always are, to some degree). Surface samples from visible growth confirm species identity and help determine whether conditions indicate active colonization or dormant residual staining.

Results typically return within two to five business days. The written report details findings by room, identifies the likely moisture source driving growth, and outlines remediation scope if action is warranted. That documentation is also structured to support insurance claims — a practical consideration given how frequently flood-related moisture events affect properties in this area.
