## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Rosedale

Every sewage call follows a structured sequence designed for Category 3 contamination — there are no shortcuts that meet the IICRC S500 standard, and we don't take them.

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area with physical barriers and establish negative air pressure where needed to prevent aerosolized contaminants from spreading through HVAC systems — a real concern in the tightly sealed, energy-efficient homes common in Rosedale's newer subdivisions.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted units. Saturated materials that cannot be decontaminated — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Kern County hazardous waste protocols. We photograph and document everything before demolition for your insurance file.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials rated for Category 3 contamination. We don't consider this step complete until surface ATP testing confirms pathogen levels are within safe thresholds — not just until the smell is gone.

**Drying and clearance.** Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in framing, subfloor, and wall cavities return to baseline. In Rosedale's well-insulated newer homes, that baseline is typically lower than in older construction, which means we're monitoring tightly to avoid over-drying engineered lumber components.
