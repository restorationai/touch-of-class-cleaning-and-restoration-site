## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Shafter

The first priority on any pipe-burst call is stopping the water. If the homeowner hasn't already located the main shutoff, our crew identifies and closes it on arrival. From there the process follows a documented sequence:

**Damage mapping and moisture readings.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water traveled — under flooring, inside wall cavities, into subfloor assemblies. Water moves along framing and vapor barriers in ways that aren't visible to the eye, and skipping this step leads to hidden pockets of moisture that grow mold within 24 to 48 hours.

**Extraction and controlled demolition.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard and soft surfaces. Saturated drywall, insulation, or flooring that can't be dried in place is removed — cleanly and documented — to expose the structural materials beneath.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to dry the affected assembly from the inside out. Drying logs are recorded daily and shared with your insurance adjuster.

**Pipe repair and restoration.** Once the structure is dry, our CSLB-licensed team (license #960566) completes the pipe repair and any required drywall, flooring, or finish work, coordinating inspections where required by Kern County Building and Safety.
