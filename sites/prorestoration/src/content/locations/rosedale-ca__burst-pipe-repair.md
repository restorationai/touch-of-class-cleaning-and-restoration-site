## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Rosedale

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the loss. If the homeowner hasn't already shut the main, we locate and close it immediately — in most Rosedale tract homes, the main shutoff is at the meter box near the street or at the side of the garage. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**1. Rapid water extraction.** Truck-mounted extractors pull standing water from hardwood, tile, and carpet before it migrates further. In two-story homes, we work top-down to prevent water from wicking into structural framing.

**2. Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters identify hidden saturation behind drywall, inside cabinets, and beneath flooring — areas that look dry but aren't.

**3. Controlled drying.** Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are placed according to the IICRC S500 standard. Rosedale's dry ambient air actually helps — we often see faster structural drying here than in coastal climates — but we still monitor readings daily until materials reach target moisture levels.
