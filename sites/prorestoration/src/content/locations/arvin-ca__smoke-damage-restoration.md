## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Arvin

Every job starts with a structured scope assessment, not a quick visual walk-through. Smoke migrates — it follows air pressure, not the path of the fire — so our technicians use thermal imaging and air sampling to map where soot and odor compounds have traveled before any cleaning begins.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

1. **Content protection and pack-out** — salvageable belongings are documented, inventoried, and moved to a clean staging area before any wet or chemical cleaning starts.
2. **Dry soot removal** — HEPA-filtered vacuuming and dry chemical sponges lift loose soot from walls, ceilings, and structural surfaces without smearing it deeper into the substrate.
3. **Surface cleaning** — appropriate alkaline or acidic cleaners are matched to the surface type. Older painted plaster requires a gentler approach than modern painted drywall to avoid lifting paint or saturating the substrate.
4. **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation run simultaneously in enclosed spaces. In homes with whole-house HVAC systems, duct cleaning is included rather than treated as an add-on.
5. **Final air quality verification** — clearance readings confirm that particulate and VOC levels have returned to acceptable ranges before the job is closed.

Work is performed under IICRC certification standards and California CSLB license #960566. When lead paint is a concern — likely in any pre-1978 home — EPA Lead-Safe protocols govern all disturbance and disposal.
