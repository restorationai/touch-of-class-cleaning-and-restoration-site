## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Delano

When we arrive at a Delano property, the first priority is stopping the source — shutting off the supply valve to the failed appliance or, if the valve is seized (common in older homes near the Randolph Street corridor), closing the main at the meter. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the visible wet area. In slab-on-grade homes, water travels along the path of least resistance — often under baseboards and into adjacent rooms before it surfaces.

**Controlled demolition when necessary.** Saturated cabinet toe-kicks, drywall below the flood line, and waterlogged flooring underlayment are removed rather than dried in place. Attempting to dry materials that have exceeded their absorption threshold extends drying time and raises mold risk.

Concrete slab drying in Delano's climate requires specific dwell times — we monitor daily with moisture readings and don't pull equipment until readings confirm the structure has returned to baseline.
