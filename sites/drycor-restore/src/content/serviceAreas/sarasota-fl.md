## Restoration emergencies common in Sarasota

Sarasota sits on a narrow coastal peninsula where the Gulf of Mexico shapes nearly every weather pattern. Summer brings daily afternoon thunderstorms, and named storms can push storm surge across low-lying barrier islands and into bayside neighborhoods well before wind damage becomes the headline. The region's sandy, low-elevation soil drains poorly once it is saturated, which means water from a single heavy rain event can pool against slab foundations and find its way through any gap in a stem wall or expansion joint. Humidity rarely drops below uncomfortable levels, so building cavities that stay wet for even 24 to 48 hours after a plumbing failure or roof breach can begin supporting mold growth faster than in drier climates. Add the prevalence of stucco exteriors and flat or low-slope roofs on mid-century and early condominium construction, and you have a housing stock that rewards fast, thorough drying over surface-only repairs.
