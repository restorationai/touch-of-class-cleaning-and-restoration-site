## Restoration emergencies common in Nokomis

Nokomis and the surrounding Sarasota County coastline sit squarely in Florida's hurricane corridor. Named storms and unnamed tropical systems push storm surge inland from the Gulf, and even a modest surge event can flood slab-on-grade homes that have no elevated foundation to buy time. Between June and November, afternoon thunderstorms drop intense, localized rainfall that overwhelmed gutters and flat or low-pitched roofs funnel directly into attic assemblies and wall cavities. The area's sandy, high-permeability soil drains quickly in some spots but offers little resistance to lateral water intrusion along stem walls and garage slabs when the water table rises after sustained rain. Older homes with original single-pane jalousie windows and aging roof-to-wall connections are particularly vulnerable to wind-driven rain penetration. Because many properties here sit close to tidal waterways and the Intracoastal, saltwater intrusion adds a corrosion dimension to flood losses that freshwater events do not.

## Services we provide in Nokomis

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in coastal Sarasota County often involves more than a visible puddle. Storm-driven moisture wicks into concrete block walls, saturates the fiberglass batt insulation common in 1970s and 1980s construction, and pools beneath tile flooring where it can stay hidden for days. DRYCOR RESTORE uses thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation before any drying equipment is placed, so hidden pockets do not get missed.

### Mold remediation

Nokomis's year-round humidity means mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion event. Crawlspace voids, enclosed soffits, and the cavity behind stucco exteriors are common growth sites that standard visual inspections miss. Remediation scopes here routinely include containment, HEPA filtration, and controlled demolition of affected materials, followed by clearance testing before reconstruction begins.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Fire losses in Nokomis's older wood-frame and concrete block neighborhoods often leave smoke residue in HVAC ductwork and inside wall cavities long after the visible char is removed. Oily, protein-based smoke from kitchen fires and synthetic-material smoke from structure fires each require different cleaning chemistry. Restoration work here also accounts for the salt air environment, which can accelerate corrosion on metal surfaces that smoke has already etched.
