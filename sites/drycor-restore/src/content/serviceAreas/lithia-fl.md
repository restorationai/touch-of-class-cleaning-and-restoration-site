## Restoration emergencies common in Lithia

Lithia sits in eastern Hillsborough County where the combination of a high water table, heavy clay-rich soils, and Florida's intense summer storm season creates conditions that push water into homes in ways that are not always obvious at first. Afternoon thunderstorms between June and September can drop several inches of rain in under an hour. When saturated ground has nowhere to drain, water finds its way through slab penetrations, garage thresholds, and low-lying doorways. The area's proximity to the Alafia River and its tributary creeks also means that prolonged rain events can raise local water levels quickly, affecting properties that have never flooded before.

Beyond storm-driven intrusion, the housing stock here produces its own failure points. Supply line failures, water heater ruptures, and HVAC condensate overflows are among the most common calls in established residential communities. Because many Lithia homes were built on slab foundations, a slow leak under a slab or inside a wall cavity can stay hidden for weeks, feeding mold growth well before a stain appears on the ceiling or baseboard.

## Services we provide in Lithia

### Water damage restoration

Water in a Lithia home moves fast and hides well. Clay-dense soil around a slab can hold moisture against the foundation long after the surface appears dry, and drywall in a Florida home can reach mold-colonization thresholds within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. DRYCOR RESTORE deploys moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the actual wet boundary, not just the visible stain, then extracts standing water and sets drying equipment calibrated to the ambient humidity levels typical of a Hillsborough County summer.

### Mold remediation

High year-round humidity makes Lithia a challenging environment once mold takes hold. Remediation here involves more than surface cleaning: containment, HEPA air filtration, controlled demolition of compromised materials, and treatment of the substrate before any rebuild begins. Mold findings during a restoration scope may also require disclosure under Florida real estate rules, which is worth discussing with your agent or attorney.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue behaves differently depending on what burned and how hot the fire ran. Protein smoke from a kitchen fire leaves an almost invisible film that smells long after the visible soot is wiped away. Structural fires produce heavier char and acidic soot that etches surfaces within hours. DRYCOR RESTORE scopes the full affected area, not just the room of origin, and coordinates board-up and tarping to secure the structure before cleaning and reconstruction begin.

### Biohazard and specialty cleanup

Sewage backups, which occur in Lithia homes when heavy rain overwhelms drain fields or municipal lines, require Category 3 water protocols: full PPE, antimicrobial treatment, and disposal of porous materials that absorbed contaminated water. We follow established industry and regulatory guidelines for containment and disposal.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Lithia

The majority of Lithia's residential construction dates from the 1990s through the 2010s, a period when PVC and CPVC supply lines were standard and slab-on-grade foundations were nearly universal in this part of Hillsborough County. Homes from the earlier end of that range may contain polybutylene supply piping, a material known for stress fractures that can fail without warning inside walls or under slabs. Asbestos-containing materials are less common in this construction era than in pre-1980 housing, but textured ceilings and some floor mastics from the early 1990s may still warrant testing before any demolition.

The soils across eastern Hillsborough County include expansive clays that shift with moisture content, which can stress slab edges and plumbing penetrations over time. The water table in low-lying areas near the Alafia River corridor sits close to the surface, limiting how quickly saturated ground releases moisture. Structural repair and any work that affects the building envelope in unincorporated Hillsborough County falls under the jurisdiction of Hillsborough County's Building Services department, and permits are typically required for work beyond cosmetic replacement.
