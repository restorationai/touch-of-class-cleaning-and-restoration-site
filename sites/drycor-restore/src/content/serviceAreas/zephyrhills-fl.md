## Restoration emergencies common in Zephyrhills

Zephyrhills sits in a part of west-central Florida where the water table is shallow, the rainy season runs hard from June through September, and afternoon thunderstorms can drop several inches in under an hour. That combination means stormwater intrusion and plumbing backups are perennial problems, not once-in-a-decade events. The city's housing stock leans heavily toward slab-on-grade construction, which limits where water can drain once it gets inside. On a slab, a slow leak under a refrigerator or a failed supply line behind a washing machine can saturate the concrete and wick into adjacent wall framing before the surface ever feels wet to the touch.

Fire risk in Zephyrhills follows the same seasonal rhythm.
