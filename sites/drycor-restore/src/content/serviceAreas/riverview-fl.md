## Restoration emergencies common in Riverview

Riverview sits in one of the wettest corridors of Hillsborough County. The combination of a high water table, flat terrain, and heavy subtropical rainfall, particularly during the June-through-October hurricane season, means water has few places to go when it arrives fast. Afternoon thunderstorms that drop two or three inches in an hour regularly overwhelm gutters, window seals, and garage door thresholds on the area's slab-on-grade homes. Tropical systems push storm surge and sheet flow into low-lying streets, and even a near-miss can leave standing water against foundations for days.

Beyond weather, the rapid residential build-out that reshaped Riverview from the late 1990s through the 2010s left a large share of the housing stock in the 15-to-25-year range, old enough for original plumbing fittings, water heater tanks, and HVAC condensate lines to start failing, but recent enough that polybutylene pipe occasionally still shows up in older sections. A slow supply-line leak inside a wall cavity can go undetected for weeks in Florida's humidity before mold colonizes the framing.

## Services we provide in Riverview

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Riverview often begins at the slab perimeter or at sliding glass doors and window frames that have lost their seal after years of UV exposure and thermal cycling. Once water is inside a slab-on-grade home, it moves quickly under luxury vinyl plank or tile and into the base of interior walls before it shows on the surface. DRYCOR RESTORE uses thermal imaging and moisture meters to map hidden saturation, then extracts standing water and sets drying equipment calibrated to Riverview's ambient humidity, conditions that make passive air-drying nearly useless.

### Mold remediation

Florida's heat and humidity mean mold can begin colonizing wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion. Remediation in Riverview homes frequently involves wall cavities, attic sheathing after roof leaks, and the interior of HVAC air handlers. Work is contained and conducted following established industry protocols to limit cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home.

Odor control and structural cleaning have to follow the smoke's actual travel path, not just the room where the fire started.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Riverview

The majority of Riverview's residential construction dates from the late 1990s through the mid-2010s, built predominantly as slab-on-grade wood-frame homes with stucco or fiber-cement exteriors and engineered roof trusses. There are no basements and very few crawlspaces, so water intrusion stays at slab level, but it migrates laterally under flooring and into wall bottom plates quickly. Plumbing in homes built before roughly 2000 may include polybutylene supply lines, which are prone to fitting failures; copper and PEX are more common in later construction. Homes built before 1980 are uncommon in Riverview proper but can carry asbestos-containing texture coatings or floor mastics that must be identified before any demo work begins.

The sandy, low-lying soils typical of this part of Hillsborough County drain poorly and keep the water table close to the surface, which means prolonged saturation events can affect slab moisture from below as well as above. Structural repair and significant reconstruction in unincorporated Riverview falls under Hillsborough County permitting jurisdiction. Work that alters structural elements, electrical, or plumbing generally requires a county permit, and some HOA-governed communities add their own approval layer on top of county requirements.
