DRYCOR RESTORE is available around the clock, if water is spreading across your floor at 2 a.m. or smoke odor is still hanging in your hallway on a Sunday, you can call (813) 829-1091 and reach a live dispatcher who can get a crew moving. Scheduling works the same way at any hour: we assess the situation over the phone, confirm your address, and dispatch the closest available team.

## Where we work

Our home base is Thonotosassa, which sits at a practical crossroads of Hillsborough County, close enough to Tampa's urban core to reach dense residential neighborhoods quickly, and close enough to the county's eastern and northern edges to serve the communities where newer subdivisions and rural properties sit on clay-heavy soils that drain poorly after heavy rain. From that position, we cover a wide arc of the greater Tampa Bay region: communities along the I-4 and I-75 corridors, the suburban neighborhoods ringing Tampa proper, and the areas stretching toward the Pasco and Polk county lines.

Florida's subtropical climate means the calls we get aren't seasonal the way they are in colder states. Roof leaks and storm intrusion follow hurricane season, but slab leaks, AC condensate overflows, and mold growth behind drywall happen year-round, and they happen across every part of this region, from older concrete-block homes built in the 1960s to newer frame construction in master-planned communities.

## Coverage by distance

The cities and communities closest to Thonotosassa, including areas of eastern Tampa, Temple Terrace, and the communities along the US-301 and SR-582 corridors, typically see our crews arrive first. From there, coverage extends outward to the broader Hillsborough County area, then into neighboring Pasco County to the north and toward the Plant City and Brandon corridors to the east and southeast.

Traffic on I-275, I-4, and the Veterans Expressway can affect arrival order during peak hours, particularly for calls that come in during the morning or evening commute window. We're transparent about that on the phone, we'd rather give you an honest estimate than a number we can't hit.

## Full service territory

DRYCOR RESTORE serves all of Hillsborough, Pinellas, Pasco, Polk, and Manatee counties. Communities we serve include:

**Hillsborough County:** Thonotosassa, Tampa, Brandon, Plant City, Temple Terrace, Seffner, Dover, Riverview, Valrico, Apollo Beach, Ruskin, and Lutz.

**Pinellas County:** St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo, Pinellas Park, Palm Harbor, and Dunedin.

**Pasco County:** Wesley Chapel, Land O' Lakes, New Port Richey, and Zephyrhills.

**Polk County:** Lakeland, Winter Haven, and Auburndale.

**Manatee County:** Bradenton and Palmetto.

If your community isn't named here, that doesn't mean we don't serve it. These five counties are our territory, including every city, town, and unincorporated area in between.
