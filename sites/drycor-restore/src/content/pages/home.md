## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, Whether it's a burst pipe, appliance failure, or storm flooding, standing water and hidden moisture can compromise structural materials within hours. Extraction, drying, and moisture mapping happen in the right sequence so nothing is left saturated behind walls or under flooring.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces long after the flames are out. Restoration work addresses soot on walls and ceilings, odor locked into porous materials, and any structural repairs needed before the space is livable again.

**Mold remediation**, Florida's humidity gives mold a short window to colonize, often 24 to 48 hours after a moisture event. Remediation follows containment and removal protocols aligned with industry standards, and affected materials are addressed at the source rather than covered over.

**Reconstruction**, Once mitigation is complete, the rebuild starts. Drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and structural framing are restored to pre-loss condition under contractor license #CBC1253966, so homeowners aren't left coordinating separate trades.
