From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely follows a schedule or a neat category. DRYCOR RESTORE handles the full range of residential, commercial, and multi-family restoration work across the greater Tampa Bay area, emergency mitigation, structural reconstruction, and the specialty services that fall between. One call connects you to a certified crew that can take a loss from soaking wet or smoke-blackened all the way to move-in ready.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still bonding to surfaces, or a sewage backup makes a space unsafe to occupy, the clock matters. Water damage response focuses on stopping the source, extracting standing water, and drying structural materials before secondary damage, swelling, delamination, mold colonization, sets in. Fire and smoke restoration addresses not just charred material but the soot and odor that travel far beyond the burn zone, embedding in HVAC systems, wall cavities, and soft goods. Sewage cleanup requires containment, proper PPE, and disposal that meets regulatory standards, it is not a job for a standard cleaning crew. Storm damage response covers wind-driven water intrusion, impact damage, and the board-up and tarping that protects a property from further exposure while the full scope of work is assessed.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction puts the structure back. Once affected materials have been removed and the environment is dry and stable, DRYCOR RESTORE transitions directly into rebuild, framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because the same company that documented the loss is doing the rebuild, scope gaps and change-order surprises are far less common than when a homeowner has to hire a separate contractor mid-claim. This phase also covers permitted work where local code requires it, so inspections and sign-offs are part of the process, not an afterthought.

## Specialty services

Some jobs don't fit neatly into emergency or rebuild. Mold inspection and remediation, conducted under NAERMC guidelines, addresses hidden growth behind drywall, under flooring, and inside air-handling systems, with containment protocols that prevent cross-contamination during removal. Odor removal goes beyond masking: thermal fogging, hydroxyl treatment, and ozone application reach the spaces that surface cleaning cannot. Emergency board-up and roof tarping secure a property immediately after impact or intrusion, reducing liability and preventing weather from compounding an already costly loss.
