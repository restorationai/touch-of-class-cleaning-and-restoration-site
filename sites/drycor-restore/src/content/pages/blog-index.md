Florida's humidity, storm season, and aging housing stock create a steady drumbeat of water intrusion, mold growth, and storm damage, and most homeowners don't know what to do in the first hour after something goes wrong. The posts here are written to change that. You'll find step-by-step guides for the moments right after a pipe bursts or a roof takes on water, plain-language walkthroughs of how insurance claims actually move from first notice of loss to final payment, and honest explainers about what restoration work involves, the equipment, the timeline, the decisions you'll be asked to make.

We also write about the regional patterns that matter in and around Thonotosassa: what summer thunderstorms do to crawl spaces and block foundations, how the wet season shifts mold risk, and why a slow leak behind a wall can look like nothing on the surface for weeks before the damage becomes obvious. Some posts are practical checklists; others go deeper into the science of drying or the biology of mold colonization. All of it is written to be useful whether you're in the middle of a loss right now, preparing before storm season, or just trying to understand what a contractor told you. You don't need to be a DRYCOR RESTORE customer to get value from any of it, read whatever helps.
