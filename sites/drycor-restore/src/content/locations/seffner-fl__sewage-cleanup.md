## Why Seffner Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Much of Seffner developed in the mid-to-late 20th century, and a significant portion of residential lots still rely on private septic systems rather than Hillsborough County's municipal sewer network. Those systems age, tree roots infiltrate lateral lines, and Florida's wet season, which can drop several inches of rain in a single afternoon, overwhelms drain fields that are already working near capacity. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Seffner

Every sewage call starts with containment, not extraction. Before a pump or wet-vac touches standing effluent, the crew establishes a contamination perimeter, shuts down HVAC to prevent aerosolized pathogens from spreading through ductwork, and photographs the full affected area for insurance documentation.

Extraction follows, standing sewage is removed using truck-mounted units capable of handling solid-laden waste. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to IICRC standards (carpet, pad, saturated drywall below the flood line) are removed and bagged on-site. What remains is treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents applied at dwell times sufficient to address Category 3 contamination, the classification that covers raw sewage and septic overflow.

Drying comes next. In Seffner's climate, ambient humidity slows evaporative drying significantly, so the crew deploys commercial dehumidifiers calibrated to the grain-per-pound readings in the structure rather than relying on ambient airflow. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin meters continues through the drying cycle to confirm that subfloor assemblies and wall cavities reach target moisture content before any reconstruction begins. The process follows the IICRC S500 standard throughout.
