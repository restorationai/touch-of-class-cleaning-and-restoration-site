## Why Holiday Properties See Water Damage So Often

Pasco County's coastal flatlands give water almost nowhere to drain quickly. Holiday's neighborhoods sit on sandy, low-permeability soils that can become saturated during the rainy season, and many lots have minimal elevation above surrounding grade. That combination means a slow roof leak or a weeping irrigation line at the foundation can migrate inward rather than drain away. Older concrete-block homes, which make up a large share of Holiday's housing stock, present a specific challenge: the hollow cores of CMU walls act like channels, carrying water vertically and horizontally well beyond the original entry point. A wet spot on the interior drywall may be the last place the water arrived, not the first.

The region's humidity compounds the problem. Even after visible water is removed, ambient moisture in the 80-to-90-percent range common during Florida summers slows evaporation dramatically. Without active dehumidification calibrated to those conditions, materials that test dry at the surface can remain saturated at the core, and mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of initial wetting.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Holiday

Every job starts with a full moisture survey using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not just a visual walkthrough. Because block construction and slab-on-grade foundations are so common in Holiday, the survey maps moisture readings at multiple depths: surface drywall, the underlying block or concrete, and the slab itself when readings suggest migration below the floor covering.

Once the affected zone is mapped, water removal comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet, and specialized floor mat systems draw residual moisture out of concrete and tile adhesive layers. After extraction, the crew sets a calculated drying system: commercial-grade dehumidifiers sized to the cubic footage of the affected space, air movers positioned to maximize surface evaporation, and, where necessary, injectidry panels that force conditioned air directly into wall cavities without requiring full demolition.

Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented, useful both for confirming the structure is genuinely dry and for supporting an insurance claim with objective data.
