## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Oldsmar

When a call comes in from an Oldsmar address, the first step is a rapid assessment of the opening type and exposure. Not every breach is the same: a fire-damaged door frame needs a different anchoring approach than a wind-lifted roof section over a living space.

For roof tarping, the tarp is anchored using a cap-sheet and batten method along the ridge and raked edges, not simply weighted with sandbags.
