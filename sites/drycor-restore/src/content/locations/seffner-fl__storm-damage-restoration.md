## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Seffner

When a crew arrives at a Seffner property, the first priority is life-safety and structure stabilization, tarping breached roof sections, boarding broken windows, and identifying any electrical hazards from water intrusion near panels or outlets. Once the structure is secured, the assessment shifts to moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find water that has migrated behind drywall or under tile without surfacing visibly.

From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

- **Debris removal and documentation**, storm debris is catalogued and photographed before removal for insurance purposes, not just cleared to the curb.
