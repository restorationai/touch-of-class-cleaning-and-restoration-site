## Why Valrico Properties See Mold Issues

Florida's Gulf Coast climate is the backdrop, but Valrico has its own wrinkles. Much of the residential development here occurred rapidly during the 1990s and 2000s, which means a significant portion of the housing stock uses paper-faced drywall, a material that mold consumes readily once moisture penetrates the paper facing. Unlike the concrete block cores that older Florida homes relied on, interior partition walls in these subdivisions are almost entirely organic material sandwiched between two gypsum boards. Once moisture gets behind a baseboard or under a bathroom tile, the paper facing becomes a food source before the surface ever looks wet.

Valrico's terrain also plays a role. The area's relatively flat topography and clay-heavy soils slow surface drainage after heavy rain events, and slab foundations with inadequate vapor barriers can allow ground moisture to wick upward into flooring assemblies, particularly in rooms that sit close to grade. Air conditioning systems running nearly year-round create a second vulnerability: when a unit is oversized for the space or a duct is leaking into an unconditioned attic, condensation accumulates in places a homeowner never sees until the smell arrives.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Valrico

Remediation is not the same as mold removal, the distinction matters. Spraying a surface with a biocide and wiping it down leaves dead spores in place, and dead spores still trigger allergic responses and can be disturbed back into the air. A proper remediation process addresses the source of moisture, physically removes contaminated material, and verifies the result.

The process begins with a thorough inspection using moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate hidden saturation, behind walls, under flooring, inside cabinet toe-kicks. Affected areas are isolated with poly sheeting and negative air pressure so that spores disturbed during removal do not migrate to clean areas of the home. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and porous materials are bagged and removed following IICRC S520 guidelines. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously during the work. Structural surfaces that can be cleaned rather than removed are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial and allowed to dry to acceptable moisture levels before any encapsulation or rebuild begins. The DRYCOR RESTORE team holds NAERMC mold certification and operates as an IICRC Certified Firm, so the process follows recognized industry standards rather than a proprietary shortcut.

## Local Note

Valrico's block-and-stucco exterior walls can mask a slow roof-to-wall leak for months. Water enters at a failed flashing or a cracked stucco joint, travels down the inside of the block cavity, and pools at the base of an interior wall, sometimes 10 or 15 feet from the actual entry point.
