## Why Winter Haven Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Florida's interior lake country doesn't get the same headlines as coastal communities during hurricane season, but Winter Haven absorbs its share of punishment. The city sits in a corridor where fast-moving squall lines regularly produce localized wind damage, and the chain of lakes that defines the area, nearly 50 of them, creates its own microclimate of afternoon convective storms that can generate straight-line winds capable of peeling back roofing sections or driving debris through windows.

The housing stock adds complexity. Winter Haven's older neighborhoods include homes built in the 1940s through the 1960s with wood-frame construction, original single-pane jalousie windows, and aging roof decks that were never designed to meet current Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. When a storm or fire compromises one of these structures, the exposed framing absorbs moisture rapidly, in Central Florida's humidity, mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of an opening forming in the envelope. Getting a watertight tarp or plywood panel in place is not cosmetic; it is the difference between a manageable repair and a gut renovation.

Fire damage in Winter Haven also drives a significant share of board-up calls. After a structure fire, open windows, doors, and compromised walls invite both weather and unauthorized entry. Prompt board-up protects the property while the insurance adjuster schedules their inspection.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Winter Haven

Every call starts with a rapid damage assessment when the crew arrives on site. We identify every breach in the building envelope, not just the obvious ones. Wind events in particular can lift flashing, crack ridge caps, or shift soffit panels in ways that aren't visible from the ground but will funnel water directly into the attic cavity.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps secured with furring strips and screws driven into sound decking, not bungee cords or rope, which fail in the next wind event. The tarp is sized to overlap the ridge and extend well past the damaged section on all sides, creating a true watershed rather than just covering the hole.

For window and door board-up, we cut plywood panels to fit each opening and fasten them to the surrounding framing. On older wood-frame homes common in Winter Haven's established neighborhoods, we take care to locate studs before fastening to avoid splitting deteriorated exterior trim that would compound the repair cost.

Where fire damage has compromised structural elements, door frames, porch columns, sections of exterior wall, we assess load-bearing conditions before boarding and flag anything that requires a structural engineer's review before full restoration begins.
