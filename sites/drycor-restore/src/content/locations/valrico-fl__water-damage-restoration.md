## Why Valrico Properties See Water Damage Issues

Valrico's residential growth exploded through the 1990s and 2000s, which means a large share of the housing stock uses slab-on-grade construction with PVC supply lines that have now been in service for two to three decades. The area's clay-heavy soils compound the problem. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Valrico

Every water loss is different, but the sequence follows a disciplined framework. When we arrive, the first step is containment and safety, shutting off the water source if it's still active and assessing electrical hazards before anyone enters a flooded space.

From there, we move to extraction. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from carpet, tile, and hard flooring quickly. Speed here is not cosmetic, the longer water sits, the deeper it wicks into subfloor plywood, wall cavities, and insulation batts.

Once bulk water is removed, we map the moisture with thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. In Valrico's slab-on-grade homes, this step is especially important: concrete slabs can hold significant moisture that reads dry at the surface while the flooring adhesive and the bottom course of drywall remain saturated. We don't guess at dry, we measure it.

Structural drying follows, using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the affected area. Drying timelines in Hillsborough County's climate require equipment that can overcome ambient humidity levels that regularly exceed 70 percent outdoors. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until affected materials reach acceptable moisture content per the IICRC S500 standard.
