Dunedin sits on a narrow peninsula jutting into St. Joseph Sound, which means Gulf storm systems don't have to travel far before they're raking rooflines and punching out windows here. When a fast-moving squall or a named storm leaves your property open to the elements, roof decking exposed, a window frame shattered, a door blown off its hinges, the next rain event can begin inside your walls within hours. DRYCOR RESTORE responds around the clock to board up openings and tarp damaged roofs throughout Dunedin before secondary water intrusion turns a structural problem into a mold problem.

## Why Dunedin Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After a Storm or Fire

Dunedin's housing stock skews older. Much of the city's residential fabric was built in the mid-20th century, when roof sheathing was thinner and window openings were framed without the impact-rated assemblies now common in newer Pinellas County construction. A storm that barely registers in a newer development can compromise the flashing, fascia, or glazing on a 1950s or 1960s bungalow.

The coastal exposure compounds the problem. Salt air accelerates corrosion on metal roof components and window hardware, meaning that what looks like a minor crack or gap after a storm may already have compromised fasteners working in its favor. Fire damage adds another layer: smoke and heat warp older wood-framed openings in ways that make them difficult to seal without custom-cut boards rather than stock panels.

Dunedin also lies within Pinellas County's hurricane evacuation zones, so when a mandatory evacuation lifts and residents return, they're often finding damage for the first time and racing against the next weather window. That urgency is real, and it's exactly when a property left unsecured is most at risk of both weather intrusion and opportunistic entry.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Dunedin

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first question is always: what is open to the sky or to the street right now? That determines whether we route a crew to you immediately or coordinate a staged arrival with any active emergency services still on scene.

On arrival, we assess every breach, not just the obvious ones. A fire that vents through a soffit may leave the attic open in three places while the front door looks intact. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up, both for your insurance file and to establish a baseline for the restoration work that typically follows.

For window and door openings, we cut and install plywood to fit the actual rough opening, fastened into the structural framing rather than just the trim. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps secured with weighted battens or screwed-down lumber caps, not just draped and tied, which fails in the next wind event. On older Dunedin homes with thinner or deteriorating fascia, we anchor into the decking itself when the fascia won't hold a load.

All work is documented with timestamped photos. If your carrier requires a scope before authorizing emergency services, we can walk you through that conversation, we bill most major carriers directly and are familiar with the documentation standards adjusters expect in Pinellas County claims.

## Dunedin Insurance and HOA Coordination

Many of Dunedin's older neighborhoods have active civic associations, and some of the newer townhome developments along the waterfront fall under HOA governance with specific rules about exterior materials and appearance, including what temporary protective coverings may look like from the street. We've worked in communities where an HOA will flag unpainted plywood within 48 hours of installation. If that's a concern for your property, let us know when you call: we can discuss tarp color options and board finishes that are less likely to draw a violation notice while the permanent repair is being permitted.

On the insurance side, emergency board-up and tarping is typically covered under the "protection from further damage" provision of a standard homeowners policy. We document the scope in a format that supports that claim and can communicate directly with your adjuster if needed.

## Local Note

Dunedin's proximity to the water means that even a small roof breach can introduce not just rain but wind-driven salt spray into an attic space. Salt deposits on wood framing and insulation don't dry out the way fresh water does, they're hygroscopic, meaning they continue to pull ambient moisture from the air long after the storm passes. On coastal-facing properties, we pay particular attention to sealing ridge and hip intersections where wind-driven spray tends to concentrate, and we flag any salt contamination we observe for the restoration team so it's addressed before drywall goes back up.
