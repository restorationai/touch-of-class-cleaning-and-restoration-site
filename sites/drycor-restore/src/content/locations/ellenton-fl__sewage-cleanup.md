## Why Ellenton Properties Are Vulnerable to Sewage Backups

Maratee County's flat coastal plain gives stormwater and groundwater nowhere to drain quickly. During the wet season, which runs roughly June through September, saturated soil puts hydrostatic pressure on older sewer laterals and septic drain fields alike. That pressure can force sewage backward through floor drains, toilets, and utility sink connections before a homeowner realizes anything is wrong.

Ellenton's housing stock includes a meaningful share of homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, when cast-iron and Orangeburg sewer pipes were still common. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out over decades; Orangeburg, a compressed wood-fiber pipe used as a wartime substitute for metal, deteriorates into a soft, collapsible tube. Either material can fail without warning, sending raw sewage into a home's lowest level. Homes on private septic systems face a separate but related risk: drain fields that were sized for a smaller household or that have been compacted by vehicle traffic over the years lose their absorption capacity, and the tank backs up into the house.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Ellenton

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water under the IICRC S500 standard, the most hazardous category, because it contains bacteria, viruses, and parasites that survive on porous surfaces long after visible water is gone. The process is not simply wet-vacuuming the floor.

The crew begins with containment: sealing the affected area from the rest of the living space to prevent cross-contamination. All standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted equipment, then affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, pad, are removed and bagged for disposal per Manatee County solid waste guidelines. Hard surfaces are cleaned, disinfected with EPA-registered antimicrobials, and dried to measured moisture targets using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. A final sanitization pass addresses residual pathogens and odor-causing compounds. Moisture readings are documented throughout so there is a clear record for your insurance carrier.

Because Ellenton's humidity stays high even indoors during summer months, drying timelines here often run longer than in drier climates. Equipment is monitored and adjusted daily rather than set and left.
