Lithia sits in one of Hillsborough County's most humid inland corridors, where summer afternoons routinely push relative humidity past 90 percent and afternoon thunderstorms can dump two inches of rain in under an hour. That moisture doesn't just stay outside. It works into attic decking, behind vinyl-wrapped exterior walls, and underneath the slab edges of the newer construction that defines much of this community. When it does, mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event, often well before a homeowner notices a musty odor or a visible stain.

## Why Lithia Properties See Mold Issues

The Lithia area developed rapidly through the 2000s and 2010s, producing large volumes of tract-built homes with open floor plans, vaulted ceilings, and HVAC systems that circulate air across a lot of square footage. These designs are comfortable but they create conditions where a single slow leak behind a shower wall or a condensation problem on an air handler can spread spores through multiple rooms before the source is identified.

Florida's climate adds another layer. Unlike northern states where mold season is roughly seasonal, Hillsborough County properties face elevated mold pressure year-round. The combination of warm temperatures, high humidity, and frequent rain means that any building envelope failure, whether a compromised roof flashing, a cracked stucco section, or a slow drip under a kitchen sink, can escalate quickly. Crawl spaces are less common here than in older Florida construction, but slab-on-grade foundations with improperly graded lots can direct water toward the structure rather than away from it, keeping subfloor and baseboard areas persistently damp.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lithia

Every remediation starts with a thorough moisture investigation, not just a visual scan. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters let the crew identify wet building materials that look and feel dry at the surface. This matters in Lithia homes where insulation inside exterior walls can hold moisture long after the drywall face reads normal on a pin meter.

Once the affected area is mapped, containment goes up before any disturbing work begins. Negative air pressure and poly barriers keep spores from migrating into clean living spaces while the remediation is underway. Affected drywall, insulation, and other porous materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of following EPA guidelines. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process.

After physical removal, the crew treats structural surfaces with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment stays in place until moisture readings confirm the structure is within acceptable parameters. The IICRC NAERMC mold remediation certification that guides this work sets specific clearance criteria, and the job isn't considered complete until those criteria are met. DRYCOR RESTORE holds both IICRC certification and NAERMC mold certification, along with EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters on any Lithia home where older building components may be present.

## Local Note

Lithia's newer construction often uses engineered wood products, including LVL beams, OSB sheathing, and laminated flooring, rather than the solid lumber found in older Florida homes. Engineered wood is significantly more sensitive to moisture than solid wood: OSB in particular begins to swell and delaminate at moisture levels that solid pine would tolerate. This means that in a Lithia home, a roof leak or plumbing failure that might be a contained repair in an older structure can require more extensive material removal if the moisture has reached engineered components. Identifying those materials early in the assessment changes the remediation scope and the drying protocol.
