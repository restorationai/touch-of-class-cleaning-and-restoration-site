## Why Largo's Housing Stock Shapes Fire Damage Differently

Most of Largo's residential neighborhoods were built during Florida's postwar growth surge, and that era left a specific fingerprint: concrete block walls, terrazzo or vinyl tile floors, jalousie windows, and attic spaces framed with older-growth lumber. When fire reaches that attic framing, the dense wood chars slowly but holds heat long after suppression, thermal imaging during our assessment often reveals hot pockets that aren't visible to the eye. Smoke from these fires tends to carry a heavier, oilier residue than fires in modern OSB-framed homes, partly because older insulation materials and original cabinetry finishes off-gas differently under heat.

The region's humidity compounds the problem. Pinellas County's coastal air, rarely dry even in winter, means soot and smoke residue begin bonding to surfaces faster than in drier climates. Within 24 to 48 hours of a fire, smoke proteins can etch into porous block walls and ceiling texture in ways that make later cleaning significantly more difficult. That window matters.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Largo

Every fire loss is assessed before any cleaning begins. We document the full scope with photos and moisture readings, fire suppression water introduces its own damage, and in Largo's climate, mold colonization can begin in as little as 48 to 72 hours in water-saturated materials. Our IICRC-certified team separates salvageable materials from those that need removal, then sequences the work so structural drying and smoke remediation happen in the right order rather than in conflict.

Smoke odor in concrete block construction is one of the more stubborn problems in this market. Masonry is porous, and standard surface wiping doesn't reach the smoke particles that have migrated into the block itself. We use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to neutralize odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Affected drywall, insulation, and cabinetry are removed and documented for your insurance claim before any reconstruction begins.

For homes with original terrazzo floors, common throughout Largo's older neighborhoods, fire and suppression water can cause surface crazing that looks minor but signals deeper thermal stress. We flag those areas during assessment so your adjuster has a complete picture.
