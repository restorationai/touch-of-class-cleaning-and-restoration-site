## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Holiday

Category 3 water, which is what any sewage backup is classified as under IICRC standards, requires a different response than a clean-water pipe burst. The first priority on arrival is containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms and establishing negative air pressure where necessary. From there, the process moves through these stages:

- **Extraction and removal**: Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units. Porous materials that have absorbed contaminated water, including drywall, insulation, and carpet, are removed and bagged for proper disposal.
- **Antimicrobial treatment**: All affected hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination. On slab-on-grade homes common throughout Holiday, this includes the concrete subfloor, which is porous enough to harbor pathogens if not treated thoroughly.
- **Drying and monitoring**: After sanitization, the structure is dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches acceptable drying goals.
- **Clearance documentation**: Written documentation of the scope, treatment, and final moisture readings is provided for insurance claims and for any required Pasco County permitting.
