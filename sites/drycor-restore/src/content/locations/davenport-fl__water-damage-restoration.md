## Why Davenport Properties See Water Damage Issues

Davenport's growth over the past two decades has produced a wide mix of housing stock: sprawling vacation-rental communities with shared amenity buildings, newer single-family subdivisions, and older concrete-block homes built when the area was far more rural. Each of those construction types behaves differently under water intrusion.

Concrete-block construction, common in Polk County homes built before the 1990s, can wick moisture into the block cavities long after surface water is removed. Moisture meters read the face of the wall as dry while the interior of the block stays saturated, which is exactly the condition that sets up mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. Newer vinyl-wrapped framing in the resort-style communities around Davenport is faster to dry but more vulnerable to delamination if water sits under LVP flooring for more than a day or two.

The region's high water table, especially during the June-through-September rainy season, means slab-on-grade foundations have almost no margin when ground saturation rises. A slow leak under a slab can go undetected for weeks, surfacing only as a warm spot on tile or a spike in the water bill.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Davenport

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment, not just a visual inspection. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map where water has traveled behind walls, under flooring, and into ceiling cavities, because in Florida's climate, hidden moisture is the rule rather than the exception.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Water extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extraction units remove standing water and pull moisture from carpet padding, subfloor materials, and grout lines.
- **Structural drying**: High-capacity dehumidifiers and axial air movers are positioned according to the IICRC S500 drying standard, creating the airflow and vapor-pressure differential that pulls moisture out of building materials rather than just off the surface.
- **Daily monitoring**: Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented, which matters both for insurance claims and for confirming that materials have reached safe levels before any reconstruction begins.
- **Antimicrobial treatment**: Applied to affected surfaces to slow microbial growth during the drying window, particularly important in Davenport's year-round warmth.

DRYCOR RESTORE is an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding the Water Restoration Technician (WRT) credential, and the team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which becomes relevant in older Davenport homes where disturbing painted surfaces is part of the scope.
