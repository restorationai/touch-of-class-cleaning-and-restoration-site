## Why Holmes Beach Properties See Distinctive Storm Damage

The geography here works against a quick recovery. Holmes Beach is flanked by the Gulf of Mexico to the west and Tampa Bay to the east, which means surge and wind can push water from two directions during a strong tropical system. Sandy barrier island soil drains quickly under normal conditions, but once storm surge or heavy rainfall overwhelms the ground, water has nowhere to go except into crawl spaces, garages, and first-floor living areas.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Holmes Beach homes were built in the 1960s and 1970s, when construction standards for wind resistance were far less stringent than today's Florida Building Code requirements. Older roof decking, single-pane jalousie windows, and wood-frame construction can mean that what looks like minor exterior damage has allowed wind-driven rain to penetrate wall cavities, attic insulation, and subfloor assemblies. Salt air accelerates corrosion on fasteners and metal flashing, so even a "small" roof breach can hide water intrusion that has been wicking into framing for hours before anyone notices.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Holmes Beach

When a storm moves through Anna Maria Island, the first priority is stopping additional damage. That means tarping compromised roofs, boarding breached windows, and extracting any standing water before it migrates into wall assemblies or beneath tile and hardwood floors.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 and S520 standards that govern professional drying and restoration:

- **Moisture mapping**: Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has traveled beyond the visible damage zone. In Holmes Beach's older concrete block and stucco homes, water can track along block cavities well past the point of entry.
- **Structural drying**: Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers run until readings confirm materials have returned to acceptable moisture levels. Coastal humidity makes this step take longer than it would in a drier climate, and equipment is sized accordingly.
- **Debris and material removal**: Storm-damaged drywall, insulation, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed and disposed of properly. If the home was built before 1978, our EPA Lead-Safe certified process governs how disturbed materials are handled.
- **Reconstruction coordination**: Once the structure is dry and documented, DRYCOR RESTORE holds a contractor license (#CBC1253966) to carry repairs through to completion, so you are not handing off to a second company mid-project.

Storm damage claims on barrier island properties in Florida carry their own documentation requirements. ## Local Note

Holmes Beach homes built on slab foundations in the 1960s and early 1970s often have terrazzo flooring, which is dense and slow to release moisture absorbed from below.
