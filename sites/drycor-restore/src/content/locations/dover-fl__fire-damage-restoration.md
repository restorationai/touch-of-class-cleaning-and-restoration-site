## Why Dover Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

Much of Dover's residential housing stock was built during Florida's postwar and mid-century growth periods, when wood-frame construction with plank sheathing was standard. That framing absorbs smoke particulate and odor compounds differently than modern OSB or engineered lumber, the porous grain draws in soot and volatile organic compounds deeply, which means odor can return weeks after a superficial cleanup. Older electrical systems in these homes, some still carrying aluminum wiring or undersized panels, are also a more frequent ignition source than in newer construction.

Hillsborough County's humidity compounds the problem. After a fire is extinguished, the water used to suppress it, combined with ambient moisture, accelerates secondary damage: mold can begin colonizing wet, char-adjacent materials within 24 to 48 hours. Addressing fire damage here means working on two fronts simultaneously: the burn and smoke damage itself, and the water intrusion left behind by suppression efforts.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Dover

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence we follow is deliberate and documented at each stage.

**Emergency stabilization** comes first, board-up and roof tarping to prevent weather intrusion, which matters in Dover's rain-heavy climate where an exposed structure can accumulate additional water damage overnight.

**Scope and documentation** follows. We photograph and catalog every affected surface, structural component, and content item before anything is moved or removed. This documentation becomes the foundation of your insurance claim.

**Demolition of unsalvageable materials** is done selectively. In older Dover homes, this sometimes means removing original tongue-and-groove flooring or plaster that has absorbed smoke beyond recovery, a judgment call that requires experience, not guesswork.

**Smoke and soot cleaning** uses HEPA filtration, dry chemical sponges for delicate surfaces, and thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation for odor neutralization. Ductwork is inspected and cleaned separately, because smoke-laden HVAC systems will re-contaminate a restored space if overlooked.

**Structural drying** addresses suppression water. We use calibrated drying equipment and monitor moisture readings daily until materials reach documented dry standards.

**Reconstruction** closes the job, framing, drywall, insulation, painting, and finish work handled under our general contractor license (#CBC1253966).

Dover's older wood-frame homes frequently have interior walls finished with a thin skim coat over drywall or, in some cases, original lath-and-plaster construction.
