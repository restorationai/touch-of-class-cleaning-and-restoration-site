Hot, humid summers mean that the soot and smoke residue left behind after a fire does not just sit on surfaces, it absorbs into drywall, insulation, and HVAC ductwork within hours, and the Florida heat accelerates that penetration. ## Why Trinity Properties Face Particular Challenges After a Fire

Most of Trinity's housing stock dates from the late 1990s through the 2010s, an era when builders in this area relied heavily on engineered lumber, OSB sheathing, and spray-foam insulation. These materials behave differently in a fire than older solid-wood framing does. OSB loses structural integrity faster under heat exposure, and spray foam can off-gas toxic compounds that embed deeply into wall cavities. After the flames are out, those cavities hold odor and residue that surface cleaning alone will not reach.

Trinity also sits in a high-humidity zone where smoke odor reactivates on warm, wet days. A home that smells clean in December can smell like a fire again the following August if the restoration did not address the molecular odor compounds inside wall assemblies and ductwork. That is not a cosmetic issue, it signals incomplete remediation.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Trinity

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a disciplined path. The first priority is a structural safety assessment and board-up or tarping to secure the property. From there, the process moves through debris removal, dry and wet cleaning of salvageable surfaces, thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment for odor, and HVAC decontamination, because smoke travels through return-air systems and deposits residue throughout the home, not just in the rooms that burned.

For Trinity homes with tile roofs, which are common throughout the area's planned communities, post-fire inspections need to include the attic space beneath the tile. Heat rises, and attic insulation often holds smoke compounds long after the living areas have been cleaned. The DRYCOR RESTORE team is IICRC certified and EPA Lead-Safe certified, which matters in any Trinity home built before 2000 where older painted surfaces may be disturbed during fire debris removal.
