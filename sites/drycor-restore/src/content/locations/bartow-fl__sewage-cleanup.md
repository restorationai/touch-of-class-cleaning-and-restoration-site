## Why Bartow Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Bartow's residential areas include a substantial number of homes built during the mid-twentieth century, when cast-iron and clay-tile sewer laterals were standard. Those materials corrode, crack, and shift over decades, especially in Polk County's sandy, expansive soils that move with seasonal moisture swings. When a lateral collapses or root intrusion blocks flow, the backup has nowhere to go except back into the lowest drain in the house, typically a floor drain, tub, or toilet on the ground level.

The city's flat topography compounds the problem. Unlike hillside communities where gravity helps flush lines aggressively, Bartow's gentle grades mean sewer lines rely on precise slope to maintain velocity. Any settling or joint separation can create a low spot where solids accumulate and eventually block the line entirely. Heavy rainfall events, which are frequent from June through September, can also overwhelm older combined or undersized sewer mains, pushing effluent back through private laterals into homes.

Septic systems are common on properties outside the city's sewer service boundary, and Polk County's high water table limits the drain field's ability to absorb effluent after extended wet periods. A saturated drain field causes sewage to surface or back up into the home just as a blocked municipal line would.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bartow

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water, meaning it carries bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that require a structured remediation protocol, not just a mop and bleach. The process DRYCOR RESTORE follows is built around IICRC standards for contaminated water losses.

The first step on every job is containment. Technicians establish physical barriers to prevent cross-contamination from the affected area to clean spaces, and they assess how far effluent has migrated, including beneath flooring and inside wall cavities, using moisture meters and thermal imaging.

Material decisions come next. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage, including carpet, pad, drywall, and certain types of insulation, typically cannot be adequately sanitized and must be removed. In Bartow's older homes, this sometimes means encountering subfloor assemblies with tongue-and-groove pine boards rather than plywood, which can absorb contamination deeply along the grain. Technicians document all removed materials for insurance purposes before disposal.

After extraction and demo, affected structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants applied at the correct dwell times to address the pathogen load. Drying equipment, including air movers and dehumidifiers, runs until moisture readings in framing and subfloor materials return to acceptable baselines. A final post-remediation assessment confirms the space is ready for reconstruction.

## Bartow Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup losses are often covered under a homeowner's policy, but the coverage depends on whether the backup originated from the municipal main or from the property's own lateral, and whether the policy includes a sewer backup rider. These distinctions matter when the adjuster reviews the claim.

DRYCOR RESTORE documents the loss thoroughly, photographing affected materials before removal, recording moisture readings, and preparing scope-of-work reports in the format most carriers expect. That documentation reduces disputes over what was removed and why.
