## The Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Seminole

The first priority on any water loss is stopping active intrusion and identifying the full moisture boundary, which is almost always larger than it looks. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map the actual wet zone, including areas behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, and beneath flooring systems. Once the boundary is established, truck-mounted extraction equipment removes standing water from affected rooms. ## Local Note: What Older Seminole Homes Do to Drying Timelines

Homes built in Seminole during the 1960s and early 1970s frequently used concrete block construction with interior plaster walls rather than the paper-faced drywall that became standard later.
