## Why Lithia Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

That era of construction brought engineered roof trusses and OSB sheathing that perform well under normal conditions but are highly vulnerable once a breach forms. ## The Board-Up and Tarping Process, Calibrated to Lithia Conditions

Every board-up and tarping response starts with a rapid site assessment. Crews identify all breach points, including secondary openings that aren't obvious from the street, such as soffit separations or gable vents that storm pressure has displaced. In Lithia's newer construction, those gable vents are often a weak point during high-wind events.

For roof tarping, the crew measures the full breach area and selects reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for the load, then anchors them using a cap-and-batten system rather than simple tie-downs. Battens distribute tension across the tarp edge, preventing the tearing and lifting that flat-tied tarps suffer in the afternoon convective storms that roll through this part of Hillsborough County almost daily during summer months. Tarps are lapped well beyond the breach perimeter so that wind-driven rain cannot find the edge.

Window and door board-up uses plywood cut and fitted to the opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails, which hold better against repeated wind pressure and are easier to remove cleanly once permanent repairs begin. Crews photograph every covered opening before and after installation, creating a documented record that supports both the insurance claim and any HOA communication.
