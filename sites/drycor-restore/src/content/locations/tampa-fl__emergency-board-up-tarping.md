## Why Tampa Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After a Loss

Florida's Gulf Coast geography puts Tampa in a position that few metro areas share. The region sits within a well-documented lightning corridor, sees consistent tropical storm and hurricane activity from June through November, and experiences afternoon convective storms intense enough to strip shingles from roofs that were perfectly sound that morning. That weather pattern means board-up and tarping calls aren't seasonal, they happen year-round.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Older neighborhoods like Seminole Heights and Ybor City contain wood-frame homes built in the early twentieth century, many with original tongue-and-groove sheathing and single-wythe masonry that doesn't respond well to prolonged water intrusion. When a window breaks or a soffit tears away, moisture finds pathways through those older assemblies quickly. Newer construction in South Tampa and the waterfront corridors often features impact-resistant windows and tile roofing, but tile systems can still shift or crack under significant wind load, leaving the underlayment exposed.

Fire damage is a separate driver. A kitchen fire that's extinguished within minutes can still leave multiple window openings compromised by firefighter ventilation cuts and heat-warped frames. Those openings need to be boarded before the structure can be safely left unattended.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Tampa

When you call (813) 829-1091, the process starts immediately, not after a site visit is scheduled.

**Assessment and documentation** come first. The crew photographs every compromised opening, damaged roofline, and structural breach before touching anything. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and establishes a baseline for the restoration scope that follows.

**Structural openings**, broken windows, kicked-in doors, fire-vented walls, are covered with exterior-grade plywood cut and fastened to the structural framing, not just the trim. In Tampa's humidity, a board that's only surface-fastened will work loose within days. Fasteners go into framing members.

**Roof tarping** uses reinforced polyethylene sheeting weighted and secured at the ridge and eaves. On tile roofs, which are common across Tampa's older neighborhoods, the crew takes care not to walk patterns that crack additional tiles, a detail that affects both the tarping job and the eventual roofing repair estimate.

**Final walkthrough** confirms that every opening is weather-tight and that the property is secure against unauthorized entry before the crew clears the site.

## Tampa Insurance and HOA Coordination

Most standard homeowner policies in Florida cover emergency protective measures, board-up and tarping are typically categorized as mitigation costs, not repairs, which means they're usually part of the same claim as the underlying loss. DRYCOR RESTORE documents the work with photographs, material quantities, and labor records in a format that aligns with how carriers expect to receive that information.

HOA requirements are worth flagging for Tampa's planned communities and historic districts. Ybor City's historic overlay, for example, has design standards that can affect what materials are acceptable even for temporary protective work. If your property falls under an HOA or a historic district review, mention it when you call, the crew can work within those constraints and flag anything that needs a variance before work begins.
