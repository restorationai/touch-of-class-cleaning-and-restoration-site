Apollo Beach's waterfront geography creates conditions that make open structures unusually risky. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Apollo Beach

When you call (813) 829-1091, the process starts immediately, not after paperwork. Here's what happens on a typical Apollo Beach job:

**Assessment on arrival.** The crew evaluates every breach point: roof decking, skylights, windows, doors, and any soffits or fascia that have been compromised. In coastal properties, soffits are a frequent secondary entry point that gets missed.

**Tarping.** Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are secured with weighted edges and mechanical fasteners anchored into intact decking, not just draped and weighted with sandbags, which can shift in the next wind event. For roofs where decking is partially missing, plywood decking is installed before the tarp layer.

**Board-up.** Windows, doors, and other openings are covered with plywood cut and fastened to the structural frame, not just the trim. This matters in an HOA community where a board that pulls loose overnight creates a second call.

**Documentation.** Every breach point is photographed before and after closure. That documentation goes directly to your insurance carrier and is formatted to support your claim from the first submission.
