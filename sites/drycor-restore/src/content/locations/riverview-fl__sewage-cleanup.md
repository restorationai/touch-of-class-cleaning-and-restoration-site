## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Riverview

Category 3 water, which includes raw sewage, sewer backups, and septic overflow, is treated as a biohazard from the moment the crew arrives. The process isn't simply mopping and drying; it requires containment, controlled demolition of saturated porous materials, antimicrobial treatment, and verification before any reconstruction begins.

The typical sequence on a Riverview job looks like this:

**Containment and PPE setup**, Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces. Crew members work in appropriate respiratory and skin protection throughout.

**Extraction and removal**, Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units. Saturated materials that cannot be effectively sanitized, certain flooring assemblies, lower sections of drywall, insulation, are removed and bagged for disposal per county guidelines.

**Antimicrobial application**, All affected surfaces receive EPA-registered disinfectants. The goal is pathogen reduction on every contact surface, not just the visibly soiled ones. Sewage contamination travels with water, and water moves into places you can't see.

**Structural drying**, Once sanitized, remaining structural components are dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged throughout to document drying progress, important for insurance documentation and for confirming no residual moisture remains to feed mold growth.

**Clearance documentation**, The job isn't complete when it looks clean. It's complete when moisture readings confirm structural dryness and the sanitization log is ready for the insurance adjuster or property manager.

## Local Note: Slab Foundations and Hidden Sewage Migration

A large share of Riverview's single-family homes, particularly those built during the construction booms of the late 1990s through the 2010s, sit on concrete slab foundations. When a sewer line beneath the slab fails or backs up, contaminated water doesn't just appear at the surface.
