## Why Dunedin Homes Face Particular Contents Risks

Dunedin's housing stock skews older. Many of the bungalows and block homes that line the streets near downtown and along the Pinellas Trail were built in the 1950s and 1960s, before modern moisture barriers and vapor retarders were standard. When a roof leak or plumbing failure soaks through these homes, the damage rarely stays contained to one room. Water wicks laterally through original terrazzo subfloors, climbs plaster walls, and saturates wood cabinetry that has absorbed decades of Gulf Coast humidity. By the time a homeowner notices the problem, the contents, furniture, artwork, electronics, clothing, documents, have often been sitting in elevated moisture conditions far longer than they realize.

The coastal proximity adds another layer. Salt-laden air accelerates oxidation on metal frames, hardware, and electronics. A television or antique lamp that looks fine on the surface can have corroded internal components after even a short exposure to a flooded room in this climate. That's why contents assessment here isn't a visual pass, it requires moisture readings, odor testing, and in some cases ultrasonic cleaning to reach contamination that surface wiping won't touch.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Dunedin

The process begins with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before anything leaves the home. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was packed, its pre-loss condition, and where it goes.

From there, contents move to a climate-controlled facility where temperature and relative humidity are actively managed, critical in a region where ambient outdoor humidity routinely sits above 70 percent for much of the year. Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic tanks for hard goods and metal items, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing soft goods, freeze-drying protocols for water-damaged documents and photographs, and hand-cleaning for fragile antiques and artwork.

Storage continues until your home passes structural drying milestones, we coordinate with the restoration crew on-site so the return delivery happens when conditions are genuinely ready, not just when the calendar says it's time. Items are returned room by room, placed according to your original floor plan documentation.

## Local Note

Dunedin's older concrete block homes, common throughout the city's mid-century neighborhoods, often have jalousie windows that were never fully sealed against wind-driven rain. During a named storm or even a fast-moving afternoon thunderstorm, water can enter horizontally through those louvered panes and soak interior contents along an entire wall before the homeowner realizes the windows were left cracked. If your home has jalousie windows and you've had any water intrusion, it's worth checking the contents along those exterior walls even if the floor looks dry, moisture can pool inside upholstered furniture and behind bookshelves without leaving a visible puddle.
