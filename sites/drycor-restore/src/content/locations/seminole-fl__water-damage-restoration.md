Pinellas County's flat coastal topography gives standing water very little incentive to move. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Seminole

Every job begins with a thorough assessment using moisture meters and infrared cameras. In Seminole's older concrete block construction, water migrates through the block cores and into interior wall cavities without showing visible staining for days. We map the full moisture boundary before any equipment is placed.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing water from carpet, tile grout lines, and subfloor assemblies. For slab-on-grade homes common throughout Seminole, we use drying mats and specialty equipment designed to draw moisture upward through concrete rather than waiting for passive evaporation.

Structural drying follows IICRC S500 standards. We set industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient humidity levels typical of coastal Pinellas County. Drying logs are recorded daily so your insurance adjuster has a documented drying timeline, not just a final invoice.

Once materials reach target moisture content, we conduct a final inspection and provide written documentation of the completed drying scope.
