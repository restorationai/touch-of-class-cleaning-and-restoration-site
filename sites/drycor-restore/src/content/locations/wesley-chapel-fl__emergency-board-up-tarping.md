## Local Note

Wesley Chapel's newer master-planned communities, many built with tile roofs rather than shingle, present a specific tarping challenge that crews encounter regularly in this area. Concrete and clay tile is heavy and fragile, and walking a damaged tile field to reach an interior breach can cause secondary breakage that expands the loss. The approach here is to work from the eaves up using roof jacks and staging boards to distribute weight, protecting undamaged tile sections while still getting the tarp anchored properly. It takes longer than tarping a shingle roof, but cutting corners on a tile field turns a localized repair into a full re-roof conversation.
