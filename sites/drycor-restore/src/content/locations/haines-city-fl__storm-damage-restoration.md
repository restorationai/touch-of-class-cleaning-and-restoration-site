## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Haines City

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional loss. ## Local Note

Haines City's older neighborhoods contain a notable number of concrete block homes built in the 1970s and early 1980s. CBS construction handles wind pressure well, but it creates a specific challenge after storm-driven rain: water that enters through a compromised window frame or roof penetration can wick laterally through the block cores and appear as moisture in a wall on the opposite side of the room from the actual entry point. Technicians who are not familiar with this pattern sometimes miss the source entirely. Thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters used at multiple heights along the block courses are the reliable way to trace the actual migration path and avoid incomplete drying.
