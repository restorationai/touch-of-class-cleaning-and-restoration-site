## Our Water Cleanup Process in Seffner

Every call starts with a moisture assessment, not a visual inspection. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map where water has traveled, behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, beneath flooring, because what's visible on the surface is rarely the full picture. From there, the process moves through these stages:

**Extraction**, Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water and surface moisture from carpet, hardwood, tile, and concrete. In slab-on-grade homes common throughout Seffner, we pay particular attention to the perimeter where water migrates toward the stem wall.

**Structural drying**, Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are placed according to the IICRC S500 standard, creating a controlled drying environment. Equipment placement and readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented, useful when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

**Monitoring and clearance**, We don't pull equipment based on a calendar. Drying is complete when moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines for the specific material type. Concrete block walls, common in older Seffner construction, hold moisture longer than wood framing and require extended monitoring.

One thing that surprises homeowners in Seffner's older neighborhoods: concrete block construction, CBS (concrete block structure) homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, absorbs and holds moisture in a way that reads very differently on a moisture meter than wood-framed walls do.
