## Why Plant City's Building Stock Shapes Every Renovation

A significant portion of Plant City's residential housing was built during the postwar boom through the 1970s, when construction standards, insulation materials, and plumbing choices looked very different from today's code. Galvanized steel supply lines, single-pane jalousie windows, and slab-on-grade foundations without modern vapor barriers are common finds once walls come open. That matters because a remodel that looks straightforward on paper, say, a bathroom gut and retile, can reveal corroded drain lines or subfloor rot that has been wicking moisture for years without any visible surface sign.

Hillsborough County's permitting process applies to most structural, electrical, mechanical, and plumbing work, and Plant City falls under that umbrella. Pulling the right permits isn't optional, and work done without them can complicate a future sale or insurance claim. As an IICRC Certified Firm with an active state contractor license, DRYCOR RESTORE manages permitting coordination as part of the project, not an afterthought.
