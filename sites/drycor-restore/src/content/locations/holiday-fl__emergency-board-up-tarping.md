## Why Holiday Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

Pasco County's Gulf Coast fringe gets a particular brand of weather stress. Holiday's housing stock leans heavily toward ranch-style and block-construction homes built in the 1960s through 1980s, a generation of construction that predates current Florida Building Code wind-load requirements. Original single-pane jalousie windows, aging fascia boards, and low-pitch roofs common to that era are more vulnerable to hurricane-season gusts and even strong pop-up thunderstorms than newer builds.

Fire damage is the other major driver. When a kitchen fire or electrical fault breaches a wall or blows out a window, the structure is immediately exposed to Florida's humidity. Unprotected openings in this climate can allow moisture intrusion that begins affecting interior materials within hours, especially during the June-through-September wet season when outdoor humidity regularly sits above 80 percent. Board-up and tarping is not just about security; here, it is the first line of defense against a mold clock that starts ticking the moment the structure is open to the air.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Holiday

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first question we ask is what type of opening we are dealing with: a broken window, a compromised door frame, a section of roof decking exposed by storm damage, or a combination after a fire. That determines what we load on the truck.

## Local Note

One thing that catches some crews off guard in Holiday's older block homes is the way stucco exteriors respond to fastener placement. The finish coat over concrete block can be brittle after decades of Florida sun and thermal cycling, and driving a screw in the wrong spot can crack a larger section of stucco than the original damage affected.
