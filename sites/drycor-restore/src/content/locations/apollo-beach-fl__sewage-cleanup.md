## Why Apollo Beach Properties See Sewage Backup More Often Than Inland Communities

The same waterfront access that makes Apollo Beach desirable creates real plumbing vulnerability. Much of the community was developed in the 1970s and 1980s on fill material dredged from the bay to create the canal lots. That engineered soil compresses and shifts over decades, and older clay or cast-iron sewer laterals running through it tend to crack, root-intrude, and belly, creating low spots where solids accumulate until flow stops entirely.

Apollo Beach also sits within Hillsborough County's sewer service area, but a meaningful portion of older homes still operate on private septic systems. When the water table rises after heavy summer rain, and in a coastal community this flat, it rises quickly, septic drain fields lose their capacity to percolate. The result is sewage surfacing in yards, backing up into floor drains, or seeping through slab penetrations into living spaces. During active storm seasons, this isn't a rare event; it's a predictable one.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Apollo Beach

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification in the IICRC S500 standard, which means the response protocol is more involved than a typical water loss.

Here's how a job typically unfolds:

**Containment first.** Technicians establish a contamination perimeter immediately. In canal-adjacent homes, this matters especially because sewage-saturated materials near slab edges can wick toward exterior soil and reach the waterway if not isolated quickly.

**Extraction and removal.** Truck-mounted extraction units remove standing sewage. Porous materials, carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation, that have contacted Category 3 water are removed rather than dried in place. There is no safe way to dry and retain them.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** All affected hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. This step is repeated, not done once. Bacterial and viral load in raw sewage requires dwell time and a second application to reach safe surface counts.

**Drying and documentation.** After sanitization, the structural drying phase begins using commercial desiccants and air movers. Moisture readings are logged daily and provided to your insurance carrier as part of the claim file.

**Clearance verification.** Before any reconstruction begins, surfaces are tested to confirm contamination levels are within acceptable limits. We do not skip this step to move faster.
