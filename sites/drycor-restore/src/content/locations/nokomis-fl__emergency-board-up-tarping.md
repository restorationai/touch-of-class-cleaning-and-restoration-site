## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Nokomis

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first thing we do is ask about the type of opening, whether it is a broken window, a compromised door frame, a fire-damaged wall section, or a roof breach, because the approach differs for each. A broken window board-up uses exterior-grade plywood cut and secured to the framing, not just taped across the opening. A roof tarp job requires us to assess the deck condition before walking it, anchor the tarp to solid structure rather than damaged decking, and weight or strap the edges so Gulf wind cannot get underneath and peel it back.

For fire-damaged properties, we coordinate with the fire marshal's release before entering, then board openings in a sequence that preserves evidence documentation for your insurance adjuster. Our IICRC-certified team photographs every opening before and after boarding so your carrier has a clear record of the scope.
