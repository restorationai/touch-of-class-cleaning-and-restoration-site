## Why Dover Properties See Water Damage Issues

The same geography that makes this part of Hillsborough County agricultural and affordable also makes it vulnerable. Dover's soils are predominantly sandy with pockets of clay that limit lateral drainage, meaning water from a slab leak or overflowing appliance pools rather than disperses. Homes built on slab foundations, common throughout the area, can trap moisture under flooring for days before a surface reading suggests anything is wrong. Older ranch-style construction, which makes up a significant share of Dover's housing stock, often features original galvanized or polybutylene plumbing that is statistically more prone to pinhole leaks and joint failures than modern PEX or copper. Add the subtropical humidity that keeps indoor relative humidity elevated even when no active leak is present, and drying timelines here routinely run longer than national averages suggest.

Hillsborough County's wet season, roughly June through September, concentrates the risk. Roof intrusions during storm events, garage flooding from sheet flow across flat lots, and HVAC condensate overflows are the calls that spike during these months. The region's high ambient moisture also means that even a modest water intrusion, left unaddressed for 48 to 72 hours, creates conditions favorable for mold colonization inside wall assemblies.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Dover

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not a visual walk-through. In slab-on-grade homes common to this area, that means probing floor coverings, baseboards, and lower wall sections to map the full extent of saturation before any equipment is placed. What reads dry at the surface can be saturated two inches in, especially under vinyl plank or ceramic tile that seals moisture against the slab.

Once the moisture map is complete, the extraction phase removes standing and absorbed water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Structural drying follows with industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage and ambient conditions, not a generic equipment drop. Readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented and the job is closed only when materials reach target moisture levels per the IICRC S500 standard.
