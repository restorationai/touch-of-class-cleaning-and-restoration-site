## Why Bartow Properties Face Particular Water Damage Risks

Bartow's housing stock spans more than a century. Homes built in the early and mid-1900s near the historic downtown core often feature original hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath interior walls, and pier-and-beam or shallow slab foundations that were not engineered with modern moisture loads in mind. When water gets under those floors or behind plaster walls, it does not behave the way it does in a 2005 vinyl-and-drywall construction. The plaster face can feel dry to the touch within a day while the lath and framing behind it hold moisture for a week or more, creating ideal conditions for mold colonization well before visible signs appear.

The local climate adds pressure. Polk County averages well over 50 inches of rain annually, much of it concentrated in intense summer storms that can overwhelm roof drainage, window seals, and older clay-tile sewer laterals in a matter of minutes. A slow roof leak during dry season becomes an emergency the first time a heavy storm follows it.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Bartow

When you call (813) 829-1091, a technician walks you through immediate safety steps before the crew arrives. On-site, the first priority is stopping the water source if it has not already been addressed. From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters establish exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and beneath flooring. In Bartow's older homes, this step is critical because water migrates along original wood framing in ways that are not obvious from surface inspection.

**Extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and water trapped in carpet, pad, and subfloor material. For homes with original hardwood floors, the extraction approach is adjusted to minimize cupping and warping.

**Drying and monitoring.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are placed according to the drying plan. Readings are logged at each visit so you and your insurance adjuster have a documented drying record, not just a verbal estimate.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** Applied to affected surfaces before containment is removed, this step addresses microbial growth that can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion event.
