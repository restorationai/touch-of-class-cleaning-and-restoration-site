## Why Lake Wales Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Polk County's climate is not kind to building materials over time. Lake Wales sits at a slightly elevated ridge compared to much of central Florida, but the lake-effect humidity and the region's frequent afternoon thunderstorms mean wood framing, drywall, and insulation in older homes absorb moisture seasonally. Many homes in the Lake Wales area were built during the mid-century Florida land boom, and those structures often feature original terrazzo floors, jalousie windows, and plaster-over-block construction that behaves very differently from modern stick-frame homes during a remodel.

Plaster-over-concrete block, common in homes built from the 1950s through the 1970s, does not accept standard drywall anchors the way wood-framed walls do. Electrical runs, plumbing rough-ins, and HVAC duct modifications all require a different approach when the substrate is masonry. Skipping that assessment at the estimating stage is how budgets collapse mid-project.

For homes that experienced water intrusion before the renovation, the situation is more layered still. Elevated moisture readings inside block walls or beneath terrazzo can persist long after the visible damage is gone. Because DRYCOR RESTORE is also an IICRC Certified Firm with IICRC WRT and NAERMC mold certifications, we can assess and address those conditions before the finish work begins, rather than discovering them after cabinets are already installed.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Lake Wales

Every project starts with a detailed walkthrough of the space as it exists today, not as it appears in the listing photos or the previous owner's memory. For post-damage rebuilds, that walkthrough includes moisture mapping and a structural review of any framing or sheathing that was affected. For elective remodels, it means identifying what is behind the walls before the demolition schedule is set.

From there, the process moves through permitting with Polk County Building Services, material selection, subcontractor scheduling, and phased construction. We coordinate directly with the county on inspections so homeowners are not managing that communication themselves. For kitchen and bathroom remodels, we handle plumbing and electrical coordination through licensed subcontractors, keeping the project under a single contract rather than leaving the homeowner to manage multiple trades.

Finish work, whether that is tile, cabinetry, flooring, or drywall, is scheduled after rough-in inspections pass, not before. That sequence protects the homeowner from having to tear out finished surfaces to satisfy an inspector.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors working in Lake Wales is the prevalence of post-tension slab construction in homes built from the 1980s onward. These slabs contain tensioned cables that absolutely cannot be cut during a bathroom remodel or any project that requires penetrating the floor. Cutting a post-tension cable causes immediate structural failure and is not repairable in the conventional sense. Before any core drilling or saw-cutting in a Lake Wales slab-on-grade home, we verify the construction type and, when necessary, use ground-penetrating radar to locate cable positions. It is a step that adds a day to the planning phase and prevents a catastrophic mistake.
