Lithia sits in one of Hillsborough County's fastest-growing corridors, and the combination of Florida's punishing humidity, frequent afternoon thunderstorms, and the area's newer construction boom means that when a water loss, fire, or storm event hits a home here, it rarely stops at the walls. Furniture, clothing, documents, electronics, and irreplaceable keepsakes absorb smoke odor and moisture quickly in this climate, and without prompt, professional contents restoration, the damage compounds fast. DRYCOR RESTORE handles the full contents picture, from pack-out and cleaning to climate-controlled storage and return delivery, so your belongings get the same focused attention as the structure itself.

## Why Lithia Homes Face Elevated Contents Damage Risk

The residential build-out in Lithia has accelerated significantly over the past two decades, and the dominant housing stock reflects it: large open-plan layouts with vaulted ceilings, engineered hardwood, and open shelving that maximizes living space but also maximizes exposure when a pipe fails or a kitchen fire produces soot. Unlike older concrete-block construction common elsewhere in Hillsborough County, many Lithia homes feature wood-frame interiors where smoke and moisture travel quickly through wall cavities and settle on soft goods throughout the house, not just in the room of origin.

Hillsborough County's subtropical climate adds another layer of urgency. Relative humidity in this region regularly climbs above 80 percent during summer months, which accelerates mold colonization on porous materials like upholstered furniture, mattresses, and cardboard boxes within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. Contents that sit in a humid, unventilated home while structural repairs are underway are at serious risk of secondary damage even after the visible water is gone.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Lithia

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory. Before anything leaves your home, our crew photographs and logs each item, creating a documented chain of custody that your insurance adjuster can reference directly. This step protects you and keeps the claim moving.

From there, the process follows the condition of the items:

- **Pack-out and transport**: Items are carefully wrapped, boxed, and moved to our climate-controlled storage facility. For Lithia homeowners whose structural repairs may take weeks, having belongings stored in a stable environment rather than a damp or smoke-affected house makes a measurable difference in what can be saved.
- **Cleaning and deodorization**: Depending on the loss type, we use ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for smoke odor on soft furnishings, and specialized laundering for textiles. Soot from kitchen fires behaves differently than smoke residue from a structural fire, and the cleaning method is matched to the residue type.
- **Drying and evaluation**: Moisture-affected items go through controlled drying before any restoration work begins. Items that cannot be restored to pre-loss condition are documented for your adjuster rather than discarded without a record.
- **Return and placement**: Once your home is cleared for reoccupancy, we coordinate delivery and can assist with room placement so the return feels like a return, not a moving day.

One thing that comes up repeatedly in Lithia's newer planned communities is the volume of finished garage space used as secondary living or storage areas.
