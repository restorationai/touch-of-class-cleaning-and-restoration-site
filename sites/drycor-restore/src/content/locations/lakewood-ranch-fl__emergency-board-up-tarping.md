## Why Lakewood Ranch Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Lakewood Ranch is a master-planned community developed largely from the late 1990s onward, which means the housing stock skews toward concrete block construction with tile roofs. Tile roofs are durable under normal conditions, but hurricane-force winds can lift and shatter individual tiles, leaving the underlayment exposed. Once that membrane is breached, even a brief afternoon thunderstorm, which is a near-daily occurrence in Manatee and Sarasota counties from June through September, can drive several inches of water into an attic space before a homeowner realizes the damage has happened.

The community's design also includes a dense network of preserve buffers and retention ponds. Properties that back up to those areas tend to experience higher sustained wind speeds during storms because there is little in the way of natural windbreak. That same open exposure accelerates drying on the surface of a damaged roof, which can make the damage look less severe from the ground than it actually is underneath the tile field.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lakewood Ranch

Every call starts with a rapid damage assessment. When a crew arrives, they identify all breach points, including not just the obvious ones but secondary openings like attic vents, soffit gaps, and broken skylights that are easy to miss after a fire or impact event. From there, the process moves in a specific order:

- **Roof tarping**: Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are secured with wood battens and fastened to undamaged decking, not just draped over the surface. A tarp that shifts in the next storm does nothing.
- **Window and door board-up**: Plywood panels are cut to fit and secured with appropriate fasteners for the substrate. Concrete block walls require a different anchor approach than wood-frame construction, and Lakewood Ranch's newer homes are predominantly block.
- **Debris clearing at the opening**: Loose material around a breach point can work its way inside and create additional damage or block drainage. Crews clear the immediate area before sealing.
- **Photo documentation**: Every opening is photographed before and after boarding, which matters significantly when an insurance claim is filed.
