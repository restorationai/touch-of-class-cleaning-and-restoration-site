## Why Winter Haven Properties See Water Cleanup Issues

Polk County's sandy, low-permeability soils drain poorly after heavy rain, and Winter Haven's position among more than two dozen interconnected lakes means the local water table can rise quickly during the wet season, roughly June through September. When that happens, water doesn't need a dramatic event like a hurricane to enter a home. Slow seepage through block foundation walls, overwhelmed French drains, and backed-up floor drains are common calls during a sustained rainy stretch.

The city's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Winter Haven's residential neighborhoods were built in the 1950s through the 1970s, when CBS (concrete block structure) construction was the regional standard. Block walls wick moisture differently than wood-frame construction, the surface can feel dry to the touch while the block itself holds significant saturation. Standard moisture meters calibrated for drywall will underread these walls, which is why DRYCOR technicians use thermal imaging alongside pin and pinless meters to map the actual wet boundary.

Appliance failures, dishwashers, water heaters, refrigerator ice-maker lines, are a year-round source of calls. Florida's hard water accelerates corrosion in supply fittings, and older homes on the city's original plumbing grid can have galvanized lines that fail without warning.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Winter Haven

Every job starts with a scope assessment, not an assumption. Technicians document affected materials, measure moisture content at multiple depths, and photograph the loss before any equipment is placed. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and it matters for getting the drying plan right.

Extraction comes first, truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from flooring, subfloor cavities, and low-lying areas. In slab-on-grade homes common throughout Winter Haven, there is no crawl space buffer; water that reaches the slab edge migrates under flooring quickly, so extraction is paired with immediate floor covering removal when readings confirm saturation beneath.

Structural drying follows IICRC S500 standards. Refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers are staged to the affected square footage, and high-velocity air movers create directed airflow across wet assemblies. Moisture readings are logged daily so the drying curve is visible, not guessed. Antimicrobial treatment is applied to affected surfaces where microbial growth is a documented risk, which in Florida's humidity is essentially any porous material that has been wet for more than 24 to 48 hours.

When structural materials need to come out, wet drywall, saturated insulation, damaged flooring, our EPA Lead-Safe certification matters in pre-1978 homes, which includes a substantial share of Winter Haven's older neighborhoods. Disturbing lead-containing paint without proper containment and testing protocols is a liability most homeowners don't think about until someone raises it.
