## Why Pinellas Park Properties See Water Damage Differently

Concrete-block construction is the dominant building type across Pinellas Park's residential neighborhoods, and it creates a specific challenge: CMU walls wick moisture laterally through the block cells long after the visible water is gone. A slab-on-grade floor with no crawl space means there is no underfloor airflow to help the assembly dry, moisture that migrates into the slab edge or the bottom course of block can stay elevated for weeks if drying equipment is not positioned correctly.

Pinellas Park also sits in one of the most active lightning and afternoon-storm corridors in the country. Summer convective storms can dump several inches of rain in under an hour, overwhelming older stucco window seals and flat or low-slope rooflines common on mid-century ranch homes. When that water gets behind the stucco cladding, it is often invisible at the surface, moisture meters and thermal imaging are the only reliable way to find it before mold colonization begins, which in Florida's humidity can start within 24 to 48 hours of a saturation event.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Pinellas Park

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source, coordinating with Pinellas County Utilities if a main-line issue is involved, or isolating the supply at the meter. From there, the process follows a structured sequence calibrated to what we actually find in Pinellas Park homes:

**Inspection and moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map the full extent of saturation, including block walls, slab edges, and any wood framing around windows or interior partitions. We document everything photographically before any material is moved.

**Water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from tile, carpet, and slab surfaces. In concrete-block homes, extraction is faster than in wood-framed construction, but that speed is deceptive, the block itself retains moisture that extraction alone cannot remove.

**Structural drying.** High-capacity dehumidifiers and directional air movers are placed per the IICRC S500 standard, with drying logs recorded daily. In Pinellas Park's ambient humidity, ambient air drying is not an option, equipment runs continuously until readings reach dry standard.

**Content and material assessment.** Flooring, baseboards, and drywall sections that have crossed the threshold for effective drying are documented for removal. Decisions are made on moisture data, not appearance.

## Local Note

One pattern that shows up consistently in Pinellas Park's mid-century concrete-block homes: the terrazzo flooring that was standard in homes built between roughly 1950 and 1975 is nearly impermeable, which sounds like an advantage in a flood, but it means water that gets under the terrazzo has nowhere to evaporate upward. It migrates to the slab perimeter and into the bottom block courses instead. Drying equipment placed only in the room center will show improving readings at the surface while the perimeter stays saturated. Positioning dehumidifiers and air movers along the wall line, not the center of the room, is the adjustment that actually moves the numbers on the moisture meter.
