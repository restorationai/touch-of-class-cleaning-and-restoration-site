## Why Sarasota Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

Sarasota sits in one of Florida's most active storm corridors. Even a tropical storm that weakens before landfall can push wind-driven rain horizontally through a compromised soffit or a cracked window pane. The region's older residential neighborhoods include a significant number of homes built in the 1950s through 1970s with single-wythe concrete block construction. That building method is durable in many ways, but the mortar joints and older stucco finishes can crack under sudden pressure changes, and once the envelope is open, interior moisture intrusion begins almost immediately in Sarasota's high-humidity air. Homes closer to the bay and barrier islands also deal with salt-laden wind, which accelerates corrosion on any exposed metal fasteners used in a temporary repair if the wrong materials are chosen.

Fire damage creates a separate board-up need that is common year-round. A kitchen fire that burns through a wall or a roof section leaves the structure open to afternoon thunderstorms, which arrive almost daily in Sarasota from late spring through early fall. Securing those openings within hours, not days, is the difference between a contained fire loss and a combined fire-and-water claim.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Sarasota

When a call comes in, the crew assesses the scope of the opening before selecting materials. For window and door openings, we cut plywood to fit, fasten it with structural screws into the surrounding framing or block, and seal the perimeter to limit wind infiltration. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarping secured with weighted batten boards and mechanical fasteners rather than relying on adhesive alone, because Sarasota's afternoon gusts can strip a loosely anchored tarp in minutes.

On properties with active fire damage, we coordinate with the fire marshal's release before entering. Florida requires that a structure be cleared by the responding fire department before any contractor begins interior work, and we follow that protocol on every job. Photographs of all openings, all installed materials, and all fastening points are taken before and after installation to support your insurance documentation.

## Local Note

One detail that matters specifically in Sarasota's coastal zones: many homes built on or near the barrier islands use impact-resistant window systems installed as part of post-2004 hurricane code upgrades. When one of those impact windows cracks or the frame separates from the rough opening, the board-up approach has to account for the existing frame geometry, which is often thicker and more complex than a standard single-pane installation. Forcing a standard plywood cut into that opening without measuring the frame depth can damage the remaining window hardware and complicate the eventual replacement. We measure before we cut.
