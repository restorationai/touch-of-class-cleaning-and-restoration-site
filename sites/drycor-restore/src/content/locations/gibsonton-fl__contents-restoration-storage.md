## Why Gibsonton Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Gibsonton's position between the Alafia River floodplain and Tampa Bay means storm surge, heavy rainfall, and poor lot drainage regularly combine to push water indoors. Many homes in this part of Hillsborough County were built during the mid-century Florida construction boom, when building materials, insulation choices, and floor assemblies were not designed with repeated moisture exposure in mind. Older subflooring and wall cavities in those homes absorb and hold moisture longer than newer construction, which means contents sitting on or near affected surfaces have more time to absorb odors, smoke residue, or mold spores before the structure is even dry.

Beyond flooding, Gibsonton's proximity to the water means salt-laden air contributes to corrosion on metal surfaces, electronics, and hardware on furniture. A fire loss in this climate compounds quickly: smoke and soot residue bonds to porous materials faster in high humidity, and without prompt pack-out, fabrics and upholstery can begin to show secondary mold growth within 48 to 72 hours of the initial event.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Gibsonton

The first step is a systematic pack-out of salvageable items from the affected structure. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code before it leaves the property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was recovered and what its pre-loss condition was.

From there, contents move to a controlled environment for cleaning and restoration. Depending on the material and the type of damage, that can mean ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics, ozone or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize smoke and odor, dry-cleaning and wet-cleaning for textiles, or freeze-drying for documents and photographs. Sensitive items, including artwork and antiques, are handled separately with appropriate stabilization methods.

Secure storage keeps your belongings protected while the structure is dried, repaired, and rebuilt. Items are returned in documented condition once the property is ready, with a final walkthrough to confirm everything is accounted for.
