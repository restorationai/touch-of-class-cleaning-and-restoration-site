## Why Largo Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Largo's housing stock tells a specific story. A large share of the city's residential neighborhoods were developed through the 1950s, 1960s, and 1970s, an era when concrete block construction dominated Pinellas County. CBS (concrete block and stucco) homes handle wind differently than wood-frame construction: the walls hold, but the roof-to-wall connections and older single-pane jalousie or aluminum-frame windows are frequent failure points during named storms and even moderate wind events. When a jalousie window shatters or a soffit blows away, the opening channels wind-driven rain directly into the attic or wall cavity.

Largo also sits at low elevation across much of the city, with limited natural drainage relief. After a roof breach, standing water accumulates faster than it drains, and mold colonization in Florida's heat can begin within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure. Securing the opening quickly is not just about preventing more rain from entering, it's about compressing the timeline before microbial growth takes hold.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Largo

When you call (813) 829-1091, the process starts immediately, not when a crew happens to be in the area. Here's what happens on site:

**Assessment first.** Before any material goes up, a technician walks the perimeter and documents every breach, roof openings, broken windows, compromised doors, and any structural gaps created by fire or impact. Photographs are taken for your insurance carrier from the start.

**Plywood board-up for windows and doors.** Openings are measured and cut on site. Boards are secured to the frame or surrounding masonry using appropriate fasteners for CBS construction, a detail that matters when you're attaching to stucco over block rather than to wood framing.

**Documentation package.** Before leaving, we provide a written scope of secured openings, materials used, and photo documentation, the format most Pinellas County insurance adjusters expect to see with an emergency services claim.
