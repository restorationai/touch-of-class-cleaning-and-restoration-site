## Why Lake Wales Properties Face Elevated Water Damage Risk

Lake Wales occupies a ridge-and-lake landscape that sounds picturesque until a storm overwhelms a drainage swale or a water main surges. The city's older residential blocks include homes built in the 1940s through 1960s, many with original cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out. A pinhole leak behind a plaster wall can saturate the wall cavity for days before anyone notices a soft spot or a musty odor. Slab-on-grade construction, common throughout Polk County, means there is no crawl space to buffer a plumbing failure: water spreads laterally under flooring and into adjacent rooms with little resistance.

The area's sandy, well-draining soil helps in some respects but creates a false sense of security outdoors. When a roof drain backs up or a sliding glass door seal fails during a heavy squall, interior moisture has nowhere to go. Central Florida's average annual rainfall exceeds 50 inches, concentrated in a summer wet season that runs roughly June through September, meaning water intrusion events cluster in the same months when indoor humidity is already at its highest.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Lake Wales

When a call comes in, the first priority is stopping the source if it is still active, whether that means coordinating with a plumber or advising a homeowner on shutting off the main. Once the source is controlled, the work follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

The process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Moisture mapping:** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has migrated behind walls, under tile, or beneath baseboards. In older Lake Wales homes with plaster-and-lath construction, water can travel several feet from the visible wet area before stopping.
- **Standing water extraction:** Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove bulk water from floors and subfloor cavities. Carpet and pad are assessed for salvageability based on contamination category and saturation depth.
- **Structural drying:** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned according to a drying plan calibrated to the room's square footage, material types, and ambient humidity. Florida's outdoor dew point during summer often exceeds 70°F, which means simply opening windows is counterproductive.
- **Daily monitoring:** Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented for both the homeowner and any insurance adjuster reviewing the claim.
