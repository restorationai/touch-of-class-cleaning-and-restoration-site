## Why Lakewood Ranch Properties Experience Sewage Backup Events

Lakewood Ranch is a master-planned community built in phases across Manatee and Sarasota counties, and that phased development created a patchwork of infrastructure ages. Older sections of the Ranch carry sewer laterals that have had years to accumulate root intrusion and scale buildup, while newer villages are still settling into their drainage basins. Florida's wet season, which runs roughly June through September, pushes the regional water table high enough that even a partial sewer blockage can result in a full backup when storm volume overwhelms the system.

The community's extensive network of retention ponds and interconnected lakes is a design feature, but it also means that surface water and groundwater interact closely beneath finished slabs. A sewage overflow that reaches a garage floor or a first-floor bathroom is not just a surface problem. Moisture carrying biological contaminants can track under tile grout lines and into the air gap beneath floating floors before the visible mess is even cleaned up. Catching that migration early is the difference between a one-day remediation and a week-long structural dry-out.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lakewood Ranch

Every sewage call starts with containment. Before extraction begins, the crew isolates the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean zones of the home or commercial space. Sewage is a Category 3 biohazard, which means all standing water, saturated materials, and surface residue are treated as infectious regardless of how the backup originated.

The process follows IICRC S500 and S520 standards and moves through these stages:

- **Extraction and removal**: Truck-mounted extraction units pull contaminated water and semi-solids. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, including saturated drywall, carpet padding, and certain flooring substrates, are bagged and removed.
- **Surface cleaning and disinfection**: All hard surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered, hospital-grade disinfectants. In Lakewood Ranch's concrete block construction, this includes block cores and mortar joints in any exposed wall cavities.
- **Drying and monitoring**: Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in walls, subfloors, and slab edges return to baseline. Florida's ambient humidity means drying equipment runs longer here than in drier climates. Daily moisture mapping documents progress.
- **Odor control**: Thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation addresses odor compounds that survive surface cleaning. Sewage odor embedded in HVAC ductwork is addressed separately if the backup reached air handler returns.
- **Clearance documentation**: Final readings and photographs are compiled for the insurance claim file and, where required, for HOA compliance review.

Lakewood Ranch's newer construction relies heavily on open-cell spray foam insulation in interior walls, particularly in homes built after 2015.
