## Why Valrico Properties See Water Damage Differently

The soils throughout this part of eastern Hillsborough County are a mix of fine sand and clay-heavy pockets that drain unevenly. During a heavy afternoon thunderstorm, the kind that drops two inches in forty minutes from June through September, surface water can sheet toward foundations before the ground has any chance to absorb it. Slab foundations, which are nearly universal in Valrico's subdivisions, don't have a basement buffer. Water finds the path of least resistance: the gap around a conduit penetration, a hairline crack in the slab perimeter, or a weeping sliding-glass-door threshold.

Valrico also sits close enough to the Brandon and Fish Hawk growth corridors that its newer developments were built quickly, sometimes with spray foam and OSB sheathing that holds moisture longer than older wood-frame construction. When water gets behind that wall assembly, the exterior can feel dry to the touch while the interior cavity stays saturated for days.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Valrico

The first step on any job is containment and assessment, not extraction. Pulling water out of carpet before you know where it came from and how far it has traveled is how secondary damage gets missed. The IICRC S500 standard guides the drying protocol: moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters establishes a baseline, then extraction equipment removes bulk water from flooring, wall cavities, and subfloor assemblies.

In Valrico's stucco-clad homes, the exterior cladding system can trap moisture between the scratch coat and the framing. That's a condition that won't show up on a surface reading. The crew checks those assemblies specifically in homes where water has been present for more than a few hours, because mold colonization in a concealed wall cavity can begin within 24 to 48 hours in Florida's humidity. Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run until every monitored reading returns to the dry standard, not until the floor feels dry underfoot.
