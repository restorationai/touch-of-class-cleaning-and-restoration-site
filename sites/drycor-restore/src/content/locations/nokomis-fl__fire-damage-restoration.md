## Why Nokomis Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

The housing stock in coastal Sarasota County spans several eras, and each era carries its own fire damage profile. Older concrete block homes common throughout the Nokomis area absorb smoke odor into their porous masonry in ways that standard surface cleaning cannot address. The Gulf Coast climate adds another layer. High relative humidity means that soot, which is hygroscopic, pulls moisture from the air and becomes increasingly corrosive the longer it sits on metal fixtures, appliances, and wiring. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Nokomis

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of decisions follows a disciplined framework. The first step on-site is a full structural assessment: which walls, ceilings, and floor assemblies are load-bearing, which are compromised, and where the fire's heat created hidden damage behind intact-looking surfaces. Thermal imaging helps locate heat pockets in wall cavities before any demolition begins.

Content pack-out comes next for salvageable belongings. Items that can be restored are inventoried, transported, and cleaned off-site using ultrasonic cleaning and ozone treatment for odor. Meanwhile, the structure itself is stabilized: board-up and roof tarping if the fire breached the envelope, followed by controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials.

Smoke and soot removal is the phase most homeowners underestimate. Dry sponge cleaning, HEPA vacuuming, and chemical sponge treatment address different soot types. Protein-based smoke from kitchen fires behaves differently than the thick, oily residue from a structural fire involving synthetic materials. After surface cleaning, thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation neutralize odor molecules that have penetrated into framing, concrete block, and ductwork. Reconstruction follows once the structure is documented, cleaned, and cleared.
