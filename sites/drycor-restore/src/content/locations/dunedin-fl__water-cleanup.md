## Why Dunedin Properties See Water Damage Differently

Dunedin's housing stock tells part of the story. Much of the city developed in the mid-20th century, and older concrete block construction, common throughout Pinellas County from the 1950s through the 1970s, behaves differently under a water event than modern wood-frame builds. Block walls wick moisture laterally, and the interior furring strips and plaster or drywall applied to them can stay saturated long after the surface reads dry on a basic moisture meter. A leak that looks contained can be feeding moisture into wall cavities that won't show visible damage for days.

The local water table compounds this. In many parts of Dunedin, the water table sits close enough to grade that a sustained rain event or a failed sump in a low-lying yard can push groundwater toward slab edges. Older homes with original terrazzo or tile floors may not show obvious buckling the way laminate would, but the subfloor and any organic material beneath can begin to support mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of sustained saturation, faster in Florida's heat and humidity than in most of the country.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Dunedin

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not just a visual walk-through. Water follows the path of least resistance, and in Dunedin's older homes that often means it has traveled farther than the obvious wet spot suggests, behind baseboards, under tile adhesive, into the bottom courses of block walls.

Once we've mapped the moisture, extraction comes first. Industrial truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and surface saturation before drying equipment goes in. Then we establish a drying system calibrated to the structure: commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers positioned to create directed airflow through cavities, and daily moisture readings to track the drying curve. We dry to IICRC S500 standard, not to a visual estimate. Documentation of moisture readings at each check supports your insurance claim and gives you a clear record that the structure was returned to an acceptable moisture baseline.

For homes with original plaster or older drywall over block, drying timelines are typically longer than a newer wood-frame home of the same square footage. We set realistic expectations on day one.

## Dunedin Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners' policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge, a burst supply line, a failed water heater, an appliance leak, but coverage for groundwater intrusion or long-term seepage is typically excluded. ## Local Note

Dunedin's older CBS (concrete block stucco) homes often have original jalousie windows, the louvered glass style common in Florida construction from the 1950s and 1960s. During a wind-driven rain event, these windows leak at the frame seals in ways that are easy to miss until water has already tracked down the interior wall and pooled at the base. If your home has jalousie windows and you've had a storm, check the interior wall below every window before assuming the only source was a roof or plumbing issue. That secondary infiltration point can extend the affected area significantly and change the scope of drying needed.
