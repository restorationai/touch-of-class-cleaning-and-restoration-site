## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Dundee

## Local Note

Dundee's older block homes near the lake corridors often have terrazzo floors over concrete slabs, a finish common throughout Polk County construction from the 1950s through the 1970s. Terrazzo is dense and largely impermeable, which sounds like good news during a water loss, but it means water that gets underneath it has nowhere to go. Moisture becomes trapped between the slab and the terrazzo, and standard drying equipment placed on top of the floor does almost nothing for what is happening below. Drying a terrazzo floor correctly requires drilling small relief holes or using specialty drying mats that draw moisture up through the surface. Skipping that step and pulling the equipment too early is one of the most common reasons water damage jobs in this part of Polk County result in mold callbacks weeks later.
