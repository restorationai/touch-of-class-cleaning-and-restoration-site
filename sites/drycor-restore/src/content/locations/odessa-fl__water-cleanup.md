## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Odessa

The first step on any water call is containment and documentation. The crew identifies the water source, shuts it off if it is still active, and photographs the affected area before moving anything. This documentation matters for insurance claims and is something adjusters specifically look for when reviewing restoration invoices.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from carpet, tile grout, and hard flooring. After visible water is removed, the work shifts to what is behind and beneath: thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters map where water has migrated into walls, subfloor, and cabinetry. In Odessa homes with tile over concrete slab, that mapping step is critical because tile can feel dry at the surface while the concrete beneath holds significant moisture.

Drying is structured around the IICRC S500 standard, using a calculated combination of air movers and commercial dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage and material types. Daily moisture readings track progress and determine when materials have reached drying goals, so nothing is closed up prematurely.
