## Why Sun City Center Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Sun City Center is a large active-adult community, and much of its housing stock was built in the 1960s through the 1980s, an era when construction standards for wind uplift were considerably less stringent than what Florida's current building code demands. Roof-to-wall connections on homes from that period often rely on toe-nailing rather than modern hurricane straps, which means a strong storm can peel back roofing material or lift fascia sections that newer construction would hold. Older single-pane jalousie and aluminum slider windows, still common throughout the community, are also more vulnerable to windborne debris than impact-rated glass.

The region's flat terrain and high water table mean that once an opening exists, rainwater has nowhere to drain quickly. Standing water under flooring or inside wall cavities begins supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours in Florida's warm, humid climate. Sealing the opening fast is not cosmetic work; it is the first line of defense against a water and mold loss that dwarfs the cost of the board-up itself.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Sun City Center

When we arrive on site, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment to identify every compromised opening, not just the obvious one.
