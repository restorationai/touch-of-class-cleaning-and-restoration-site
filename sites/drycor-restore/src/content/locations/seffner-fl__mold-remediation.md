## Why Seffner Properties See Mold Issues

The geography here works against homeowners in a few specific ways. Seffner's low-lying terrain and clay-heavy soils slow drainage significantly after heavy rain, meaning crawl spaces and slab perimeters stay wet long after the storm passes. Concrete block construction, common in homes built throughout the 1960s through the 1990s in this part of Hillsborough County, is durable, but the hollow cores and mortar joints can wick moisture inward for days after a roof leak or plumbing failure. Insulation packed into those cavities holds that moisture against the block, creating a dark, warm, humid environment that mold thrives in.

Central air conditioning systems, which run almost year-round in this climate, add another layer of risk. When an AC unit is undersized, poorly maintained, or leaking condensate, it raises indoor humidity rather than lowering it, and high indoor humidity alone, without any visible water event, is enough to sustain mold growth behind baseboards and inside ductwork.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Seffner

Every job starts with a thorough inspection, not just the visible surface, but the wall cavities, subfloor, and HVAC system where mold hides longest. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help identify wet zones that look dry to the eye, which matters especially in Seffner's concrete block homes where surface readings can be misleading.

Once the scope is mapped, the affected area is sealed off with negative air pressure containment to prevent spores from migrating to clean parts of the home during removal. Contaminated materials, drywall, insulation, flooring, are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Florida Department of Environmental Protection guidelines. Remaining structural surfaces are treated and cleaned using EPA-registered antimicrobials. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process.

After remediation, the space is dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture thresholds before any reconstruction begins. Skipping that step, or rushing it, is how mold comes back.

DRYCOR RESTORE is an IICRC Certified Firm with NAERMC mold certification and EPA Lead-Safe credentials, which matters in older Seffner homes where lead-based paint can be present on surfaces that need to be disturbed during remediation.
