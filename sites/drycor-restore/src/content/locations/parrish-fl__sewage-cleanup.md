## Why Parrish Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Parrish

Raw sewage removal is not the same process as a clean-water extraction. Every sewage job begins with containment: technicians establish negative air pressure in the affected area and suit up in full PPE before any material is moved. This matters especially in Parrish's newer open-plan homes, where an uncontained sewage event in a first-floor laundry room can spread aerosolized contaminants into an adjacent great room or kitchen.

Once the standing waste is extracted, the real work begins. Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 water, including drywall, insulation, carpet, and in some cases engineered hardwood, are removed and bagged for disposal per EPA guidelines. The structural materials that remain are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, not general-purpose cleaners. After application, dwell times are observed before surfaces are wiped and air scrubbers are run to clear the space. Moisture readings are logged throughout the drying phase to confirm that subfloor assemblies and wall cavities return to acceptable levels before any reconstruction begins.
