## Why Osprey Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Osprey's geography puts it directly in the path of Gulf-fed thunderstorms that can intensify rapidly as they cross the water and make landfall along this stretch of the coast. The community also sits close to the Intracoastal Waterway, and properties near that corridor experience consistent salt-air exposure that accelerates wear on roofing materials, window frames, and exterior cladding. Older ranch-style homes common to this part of unincorporated Sarasota County often have original single-pane jalousie or casement windows that are far more vulnerable to wind pressure than modern impact-rated glass. When those windows fail, or when aging roof decking lifts, the interior becomes exposed to humidity levels that can push mold colonization to begin within 24 to 48 hours of the first water intrusion. Securing the opening quickly is not cosmetic work; it is the first line of defense against a much larger restoration bill.

Fire damage in this area creates its own board-up demands. DRYCOR RESTORE's IICRC-certified crews treat board-up after fire as part of the restoration sequence, not a separate task.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Osprey

Every call starts with a rapid site assessment. The crew identifies all breach points, including roof openings, broken windows, compromised doors, and any structural gaps created by impact or fire. Measurements are taken before any material is cut, because a board-up done to the wrong dimension wastes time and leaves edges that wind can catch.

For roof tarping, crews use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps secured with furring strips and screws driven into sound decking, not just weighted at the edges. In Osprey's wind environment, a tarp that is only draped over a peak will not survive the next afternoon storm. Proper mechanical fastening is the difference between a tarp that holds through a week of weather and one that peels away overnight.

For window and door board-up, plywood panels are cut to overlap the frame by several inches on each side and fastened into the structural framing, not just the trim. On homes with stucco exteriors, the crew accounts for the substrate when selecting fastener length to ensure the panel is anchored to something solid.

Documentation runs parallel to every step: photographs of the breach, the surrounding damage, and the completed closure are compiled for the insurance claim file before the crew leaves the property.

## Local Note

Osprey's coastal ranch homes from the 1960s and 1970s were frequently built with wood-frame construction and low-slope rooflines that drain slowly.
