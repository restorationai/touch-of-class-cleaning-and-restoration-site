## Local Note

Homes built along Auburndale's lakefront corridors in the 1960s and 1970s frequently used concrete block construction with interior furring strips and drywall applied directly over the block. When water infiltrates through the block wall, which happens during sustained rain events when the water table rises, the furring cavity traps moisture against the block face and the back of the drywall simultaneously. Surface readings can be misleading because the drywall face dries faster than the block behind it. Experienced crews account for this by taking readings at multiple depths and extending the drying phase longer than a standard frame-wall loss would require.
