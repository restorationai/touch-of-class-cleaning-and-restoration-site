## Why Valrico Properties See Significant Storm Damage

Valrico's housing stock spans several decades and construction styles, and that variety matters when a storm hits. Newer subdivisions built during the 2000s boom sometimes have lighter-gauge roofing components that perform adequately in code-minimum conditions but show vulnerability in a direct thunderstorm cell.

Hillsborough County's clay-heavy soils compound the problem. Mature tree canopy throughout Valrico's established communities adds another layer of risk: large live oaks and slash pines under stress from prior drought cycles are prone to catastrophic limb failure or full uprooting during high-wind events.
