Lakewood Ranch sits in one of Florida's most active storm corridors, and when a burst pipe, roof leak, or hurricane-driven flood damages a home here, the contents inside face a second threat that moves faster than most homeowners expect: the region's year-round heat and humidity can push mold colonization on porous materials within 48 to 72 hours. DRYCOR RESTORE provides contents restoration and storage for Lakewood Ranch properties, removing, treating, and safeguarding your belongings while the structure itself is being dried and repaired, so nothing gets left behind in a compromised environment.

## Why Lakewood Ranch Homes Face Distinct Contents Damage Risks

Lakewood Ranch is a master-planned community built largely on former ranchland in eastern Manatee and Sarasota counties. The construction here skews newer, with most homes built after the mid-1990s using engineered wood products, open floor plans, and high ceilings. Those features look great but behave differently in a loss event. Engineered wood subfloors and cabinetry absorb moisture quickly and swell or delaminate before surface readings show a problem. Furniture placed on slab foundations with no crawl space has nowhere for vapor to escape, so upholstered pieces and wood furniture sitting on affected flooring can begin warping within hours.

The community's location also matters. Afternoon thunderstorms from June through September regularly produce wind-driven rain that finds its way into window frames, sliding glass doors, and roof penetrations common in the villa and coach-home building styles popular throughout Lakewood Ranch. When that water reaches interior contents, the high ambient humidity slows evaporation and accelerates microbial activity on textiles, paper goods, and wood furnishings.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Lakewood Ranch

When we arrive at a Lakewood Ranch property, the first priority is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, cataloged, and assessed before anything is moved. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what condition.

Items that can be restored on-site, such as hard-surface furniture and non-porous decor, are cleaned and treated in place using HEPA-filtered equipment and appropriate antimicrobial solutions. Soft contents, including clothing, bedding, draperies, and upholstered furniture, are packed and transported to our climate-controlled facility in Thonotosassa, where they are dried, deodorized, and stored securely until your home is ready to receive them. Electronics are handled separately, assessed for corrosion and moisture intrusion before any power-on attempt.

For items with smoke or soot involvement, we use thermal fogging and ozone treatment in controlled conditions. Lakewood Ranch's newer construction often includes open-concept layouts where smoke from even a contained kitchen fire can travel through the entire living area and settle into soft goods in multiple rooms simultaneously. Our process accounts for that spread rather than treating only the room of origin.

Lakewood Ranch's various community development districts each have their own governing documents, and HOA rules in many villages specify how and when restoration equipment can be staged on driveways or common areas. We communicate directly with property managers and HOA contacts when access or staging requirements need to be confirmed before work begins. On the insurance side, we document the contents inventory in a format that aligns with standard carrier claim requirements, which reduces the back-and-forth between you and your adjuster during an already stressful process.

## Local Note

Lakewood Ranch's newer homes are frequently furnished with large sectional sofas, media consoles, and custom cabinetry that were ordered to fit specific rooms.
