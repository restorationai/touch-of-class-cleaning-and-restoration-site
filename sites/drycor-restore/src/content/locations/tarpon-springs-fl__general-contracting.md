## Why Tarpon Springs Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The Gulf Coast climate is the first variable any general contractor here has to respect. Tarpon Springs averages more than 50 inches of rain annually, and the combination of high humidity and salt-laden air accelerates corrosion on fasteners, framing connectors, and any exposed metal. In older homes, that means a remodel often uncovers surprises: rusted lally columns, deteriorated sill plates, or galvanized plumbing that has scaled shut over decades.

Homes built before 1978 are common throughout Tarpon Springs, and that matters for renovation planning. Florida's statewide building code sets the baseline, but Tarpon Springs properties near the coast or within certain flood zones carry additional overlay requirements.
