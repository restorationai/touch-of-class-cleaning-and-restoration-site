## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Sun City Center

The first priority on any water loss is stopping the spread. Once the crew confirms the source is controlled, moisture mapping begins using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across every surface the water could have reached, including under baseboards, inside wall cavities, and beneath flooring. In slab-on-grade homes, which make up the majority of Sun City Center's residential stock, that means paying particular attention to the slab interface, where water can travel laterally well beyond the visible wet zone.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard floors and carpet, followed by the placement of industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space. Drying targets are set to the IICRC S500 standard, and readings are logged at each visit so the documentation trail is complete when the insurance adjuster reviews the file.

Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, saturated drywall, swollen baseboards, compromised flooring, are removed with care and documented before disposal. Nothing is torn out without a record.

## Local Note

Sun City Center's predominant slab construction means there is no crawl space to act as a buffer, but it also means water that gets under a floor covering has only one direction to go: into the slab itself. Concrete is porous, and a slab that has absorbed significant moisture will continue releasing that moisture upward for days after surface extraction is complete. Crews working here routinely extend drying cycles and recheck slab moisture readings before signing off, because a reading that looks acceptable at the surface can mask residual saturation an inch deeper.
