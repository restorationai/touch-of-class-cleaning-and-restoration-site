## Why Anna Maria Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Anna Maria Island sits within Manatee County's coastal high-hazard zones, which means nearly every significant renovation or remodel triggers a review against current Florida Building Code wind and flood provisions. Homes built before the major code updates that followed the 2004 hurricane season may have roof-to-wall connections, window ratings, and sheathing details that no longer meet current standards. When a remodel opens up walls or touches the roof line, those deficiencies often have to be addressed as part of the permitted scope, not left for another day.

Beyond code, the island's soil and water table conditions affect foundation work and any project that disturbs the ground. Older concrete block homes common to the island can harbor moisture intrusion in the block cavities themselves, and that moisture doesn't always show at the surface. A kitchen remodel that pulls cabinets off an exterior wall sometimes reveals block that has been wicking salt-laden groundwater for decades. Knowing to look for it, and knowing what to do when you find it, is the difference between a renovation that holds and one that starts failing within a few seasons.

## Local Note: What Island Contractors Learn About Older Beach Cottages

Many of the smaller cottages on Anna Maria were built in the 1950s and 1960s using construction methods that made sense for the era but create specific challenges today. Roof overhangs were often minimal, jalousie windows were standard, and interior finishes like terrazzo floors and plaster ceilings were common. Terrazzo is durable and increasingly valued, but it complicates plumbing rough-ins and any work that requires cutting the slab. Plaster ceilings in these homes often contain multiple layers applied over decades, and disturbing them without testing first is a step no responsible contractor should skip. Because DRYCOR RESTORE is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, pre-renovation testing and proper handling of older materials is part of the standard workflow, not an add-on.
