## Why Land O' Lakes Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Pasco County's soil is notoriously expansive in spots and soft in others, a combination of sandy fill and clay-heavy pockets that can shift foundations and cause tile floors to crack, doors to bind, and framing to rack over time. Homes built in the late 1980s and through the 1990s, a heavy construction era in this part of the county, often used materials that are now at or past their service life: polybutylene plumbing, single-pane windows, and aluminum wiring in some cases. When a renovation opens up walls, these legacy systems frequently surface, and a general contractor who isn't ready to redirect scope mid-project creates delays and cost overruns.

The Florida Building Code also carries real weight here. Pasco County enforces energy efficiency requirements, wind-load standards, and impact-resistant glazing provisions that affect window replacements, room additions, and roof tie-ins. Permit pulls, inspections, and code compliance aren't optional steps, they're the difference between a finished project and one that stalls at resale.
