## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Palm Harbor

When you call (813) 829-1091, dispatch is immediate. A crew arrives with pre-cut plywood panels, structural screws, and OSHA-compliant equipment to work safely at height, no improvised ladders on uneven ground. The process follows a consistent sequence:

1. **Rapid site assessment**, we identify every breach point, not just the obvious ones. Wind events routinely lift soffit sections that allow water into wall cavities even when windows appear intact.
2. **Window and door board-up**, 3/4-inch plywood is cut, fitted, and mechanically fastened to the structural framing, not just the casing. This matters for both security and wind resistance.
3. **Roof tarping**, heavy-duty polyethylene tarps (6 mil minimum) are secured with batten boards and screwed through to the roof deck, not just weighted at the edges. Edge-weighted tarps fail in the next afternoon storm; mechanically fastened tarps hold.
4. **Documentation**, every breach point is photographed before and after securing. That photo set becomes part of your insurance file.
