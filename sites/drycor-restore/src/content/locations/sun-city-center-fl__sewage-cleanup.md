## Why Sun City Center Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Much of Sun City Center was developed in the 1960s and 1970s as a planned retirement community, which means a significant share of the housing stock is now carrying plumbing that is 50 or more years old. Cast-iron drain lines from that era are prone to internal corrosion and root intrusion, and when they fail, the backup often surfaces at the lowest drain in the house: a floor drain, a ground-floor toilet, or a laundry utility sink.

The region also relies heavily on septic systems rather than centralized municipal sewer connections. Hillsborough County's sandy, high-permeability soils drain quickly under normal conditions, but during Florida's rainy season, a saturated drain field can cause a septic system to reverse flow into the home. That scenario is especially common on properties where the drain field has not been serviced in several years. Compounding this, the flat grade throughout the area means there is very little natural slope to carry wastewater away from a structure once it has surfaced.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Sun City Center

Every sewage job starts with containment. The crew isolates the affected zone to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms, then extracts standing sewage using truck-mounted and portable extraction units rated for Category 3 water. Category 3 is the classification for raw sewage and any water that has contacted it: it carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that require full PPE and EPA-registered disinfectants, not just a standard water-damage protocol.

After extraction, porous materials that cannot be adequately sanitized are removed. In Sun City Center's older ranch-style homes, that typically means baseboards, sections of drywall up to the flood cut line, and occasionally subfloor materials if sewage has migrated beneath vinyl or tile. Hard surfaces are then cleaned in two stages: a detergent wash followed by an EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectant applied at the dwell time specified by the manufacturer. Antimicrobial treatment follows, and the space is dried using commercial air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to Hillsborough County's ambient humidity levels. Moisture readings are logged throughout the drying cycle to document that structural materials have returned to acceptable moisture content before any reconstruction begins.
