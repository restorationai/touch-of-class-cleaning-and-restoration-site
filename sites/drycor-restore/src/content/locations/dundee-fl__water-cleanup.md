## Why Dundee Properties See Water Damage Differently

Dundee's position along the Lake Hamilton chain and its proximity to Lake Maud means the water table in many residential areas sits closer to grade than homeowners expect. During a sustained rain event, that high water table limits how effectively a sump or French drain can discharge, and water that has nowhere to drain laterally tends to find its way into slab edges, block foundation walls, and low-lying utility penetrations.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Dundee's single-family homes were built during the Florida building booms of the 1960s through the 1980s, when concrete block construction was the regional standard. Block walls wick moisture differently than wood-frame construction: the surface can feel dry to the touch while the interior of the block remains saturated for days. Standard moisture readings taken at the surface will undercount the actual moisture load, which is why thermal imaging and deep-wall probes are part of every assessment we run here.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Dundee

The first priority on any Dundee call is stopping the source if it hasn't been stopped already. A burst supply line under a slab, a failed water heater in a utility closet, or a roof penetration opened by a storm all require different immediate actions, and the crew confirms the source before extraction begins.

Once the water source is controlled, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable units calibrated for the volume and category of water present. Category 2 and Category 3 losses, which include water from appliance overflows or any intrusion that has contacted soil or exterior drainage, require containment protocols that differ from a clean-water pipe break. After bulk extraction, we map the moisture migration using thermal imaging and calibrated meters, then place drying equipment, including commercial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, according to the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying.

Drying timelines in Dundee's climate require attention. High ambient humidity, common through most of the year in central Florida, slows evaporative drying significantly. Equipment placement and settings are adjusted daily based on psychrometric readings, not left on a fixed schedule and checked at the end of the job.
