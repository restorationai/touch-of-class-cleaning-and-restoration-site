## Why Nokomis Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Sarasota County's coastal geography is a direct contributor to the sewage problems Nokomis homeowners face. The region's sandy, low-permeability soils drain poorly during the June-through-September rainy season, and when the water table rises, it puts hydrostatic pressure on septic tanks and drain fields that were installed decades ago. Many homes in Nokomis were built in the 1970s and 1980s, when septic systems were sized for smaller households and lighter daily use. As families grow and fixtures are added, those systems get overtaxed.

Municipal sewer connections in parts of Nokomis run through aging clay or cast-iron laterals that crack, root-intrude, and collapse. Heavy rain events can overwhelm lift stations, sending sewage back up through the lowest fixture in a home, typically a ground-floor toilet or laundry drain. Because many Nokomis properties sit on slab foundations with minimal crawl space, a backup can spread across a large floor area before a homeowner even notices the smell.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Nokomis

Category 3 water, which is what raw sewage and septic overflow are classified as under the IICRC S500 standard, requires a different response than a clean-water pipe burst. Every surface the contaminated water touched is treated as a biohazard until testing confirms otherwise.

Here is how the process works on a Nokomis property:

**Containment and PPE staging** come first. Technicians set up containment barriers to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms before any equipment is moved in.

**Extraction and solid removal** follow. Truck-mounted extractors pull standing sewage and contaminated water from flooring, and any porous materials, including carpet, pad, and saturated drywall below the flood line, are bagged and removed per Sarasota County disposal guidelines.

**Antimicrobial treatment** is applied to all hard surfaces, subfloor, and framing that had contact with sewage. On Nokomis slab homes, this step is especially thorough because concrete is porous and can harbor bacteria if not treated correctly.

**Drying and monitoring** use commercial air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the high ambient humidity typical of coastal Sarasota County. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches acceptable levels.

**Post-remediation verification** documents that surfaces are clean and dry before reconstruction begins.
