## Why Parrish Properties See Mold Issues

Parrish's growth has been explosive, and much of the housing stock consists of production-built homes constructed within the last 15 to 20 years. These homes rely heavily on engineered lumber, OSB sheathing, and spray foam insulation, all of which can trap moisture rather than breathe it away. Unlike older masonry construction, OSB is particularly vulnerable to mold colonization once moisture content climbs above roughly 19 percent, and Florida's ambient humidity keeps conditions close to that threshold for much of the year.

The area's relatively flat topography and clay-heavy soils also slow drainage after heavy rain events. Slab-on-grade foundations, standard throughout most Parrish subdivisions, leave little buffer between the ground and interior flooring. A slow roof leak, a failed irrigation line running too close to the foundation, or a condensate drain backing up inside an air handler closet can all introduce enough moisture to sustain mold growth for weeks before a homeowner notices discoloration or a musty odor behind drywall.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Parrish

Every remediation begins with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Because so many Parrish homes use spray foam in attic spaces, our technicians pay close attention to the roof deck and top plates where condensation can accumulate when the thermal boundary is improperly installed.

Once the affected zone is identified, we establish negative air pressure containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents cross-contamination to unaffected rooms during removal. Mold-damaged materials are removed following IICRC S520 guidelines, bagged and sealed on-site, and disposed of properly. After removal, affected surfaces are treated and the area is dried to manufacturer and IICRC standards before any reconstruction begins. We coordinate independent post-remediation clearance testing when required by a homeowner's insurance carrier or HOA, so you are not left managing multiple contractors.
