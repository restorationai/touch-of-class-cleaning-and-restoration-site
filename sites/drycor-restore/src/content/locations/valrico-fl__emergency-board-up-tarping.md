## Why Valrico Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Valrico's housing stock spans several decades and construction styles, and that variety shapes how emergency board-up and tarping work actually plays out on the ground. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, common throughout the community's established subdivisions, often feature truss roof systems that are efficient to build but highly vulnerable once even a small section of sheathing is breached. A missing patch of decking in one corner can allow wind-driven rain to travel laterally under the felt and saturate a much wider area than the visible damage suggests.

The region's sandy, well-draining soil means standing water clears relatively quickly after a storm, but that same drainage profile does little to protect attic insulation and ceiling assemblies once water has entered through a roof breach. Hillsborough County's subtropical climate, high humidity even on clear days following a storm, means an unprotected opening doesn't need active rainfall to keep driving moisture into wall cavities and framing.

Fire damage board-up adds another layer of complexity here. Many Valrico homes have vinyl siding and soffit systems that warp and pull away from the structure after fire exposure, creating gaps that standard plywood panels must bridge carefully to maintain a weathertight seal until full reconstruction begins.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Valrico

When we arrive on site, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment, identifying every compromised opening, not just the obvious ones. Wind events in particular create secondary breaches: a broken window on the windward side, a lifted ridge cap on the opposite slope, a garage door frame racked out of square. We document each opening photographically before any material goes up, which matters later when your insurance adjuster reviews the scope.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps secured with wood battens screwed into sound decking rather than weighted down at the edges, a method that holds through the afternoon squalls that roll through this part of Hillsborough County with little warning. Tarps are sized to overlap the ridge and extend well past the damaged area so that wind-driven rain at low angles doesn't find the seam.

Board-up on windows, doors, and other wall openings uses 5/8-inch plywood cut and fitted to the opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails, so panels can be removed cleanly when the rebuild phase begins. We label each panel with the opening location and date installed, a small detail that speeds up the contractor handoff and keeps the insurance file organized.

## Local Note

Valrico's newer planned communities, built largely from the late 1990s through the 2000s, frequently include concrete tile roofing, which is durable under normal conditions but creates a specific challenge after storm damage. Cracked or displaced concrete tiles leave the underlying felt and decking exposed, but the tile field around the breach is fragile and can crack further under foot traffic. Our crews are trained to work from roof jacks and ladders positioned to avoid loading the undamaged tile field, protecting the surrounding roof system while the tarp goes over the affected area. It's a slower setup than working on shingle roofs, but it prevents a repair scope from expanding because of how we accessed the damage.
