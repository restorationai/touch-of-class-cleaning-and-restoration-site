## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Lakewood Ranch

When a call comes in, the first priority is stopping the source if it's still active, whether that's a supply line behind a vanity, an overflowing retention area, or roof intrusion after a storm. From there, the process follows a structured sequence calibrated to Florida's humid climate:

**Extraction first.** Standing water removal happens before any drying equipment is staged. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull water from carpet, pad, and hard flooring. In slab-on-grade construction common throughout Lakewood Ranch, water that migrates under tile or luxury vinyl plank requires targeted extraction along grout lines and seams.

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters document exactly where water has traveled. In Florida's climate, water moves faster laterally through wall cavities than it does in drier states, so mapping covers a wider perimeter than the visible wet zone.

**Drying system placement.** Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just the wet spots visible to the eye. In Lakewood Ranch's high-ambient-humidity environment, undersized drying equipment stalls out quickly. DRYCOR RESTORE uses commercial-grade low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage and humidity load.

**Daily monitoring.** Moisture readings are logged each day until materials reach dry standard per the IICRC S500. Drying timelines in Florida's summer humidity typically run longer than national averages, and documentation matters for insurance claims.
