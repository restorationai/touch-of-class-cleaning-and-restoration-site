## Why Lithia Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Storm Damage

Much of the residential development in Lithia expanded rapidly during the early-to-mid 2000s, which means a large share of the housing stock is now approaching the age when roofing materials, soffits, and fascia boards begin to show fatigue.
