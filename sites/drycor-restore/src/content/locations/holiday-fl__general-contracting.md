## Why Holiday's Housing Stock Shapes Every Renovation

Most of the residential neighborhoods in Holiday were developed during Florida's mid-century growth boom. Concrete block walls, terrazzo floors, jalousie windows, and single-story slab foundations are the norm rather than the exception. These materials are durable, but they present specific challenges during renovation. Cutting into a CBS (concrete block structure) wall for a new doorway or electrical chase requires masonry tools and a different structural approach than framing lumber. Terrazzo restoration or removal adds time and cost that a contractor unfamiliar with the local housing stock might not budget for.

Beyond construction type, the persistent humidity accelerates paint failure, promotes mildew behind wall cavities, and causes wood components like door frames, cabinet boxes, and subflooring to swell and warp faster than they would in a drier region. Any remodel that opens up walls needs to account for what moisture may have already done inside them, even when the surface looks fine.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Holiday

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough that looks at the home as a system, not just the room being remodeled. One thing that catches out-of-area contractors in Holiday is the prevalence of older aluminum wiring in homes built before the mid-1970s.
