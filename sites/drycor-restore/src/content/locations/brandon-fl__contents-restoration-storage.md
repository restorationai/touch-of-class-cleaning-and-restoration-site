## Why Brandon Homes Face Elevated Risk to Personal Contents

Hillsborough County's wet season runs roughly June through September, and Brandon sits in a zone where afternoon thunderstorms can drop several inches in under an hour. Flat yards and older stormwater infrastructure in established parts of the community mean water finds its way inside through garage slabs, sliding door thresholds, and low-lying windows before a homeowner can react. Once inside, relative humidity levels that already hover in the 70–80% range outdoors accelerate mold colonization, IICRC guidelines note that mold can begin to establish within 24 to 48 hours on porous materials in warm, humid conditions. That window is shorter here than in drier climates.

Brandon also has a mix of housing eras. Subdivisions built in the 1980s and 1990s, common throughout the area, often feature wood-framed construction with particleboard furniture and laminate flooring that swell and delaminate quickly when wet. Homes built more recently may have tile throughout, which protects the floor but does nothing to protect the contents sitting on it. Fire damage adds another layer: smoke residue in Florida's humid air becomes sticky and acidic faster than in low-humidity regions, bonding to upholstery and electronics in ways that require specialized cleaning rather than simple wiping.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Brandon

The process begins with a complete room-by-room inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating, salvageable, restorable with treatment, or beyond recovery. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

Salvageable contents are packed and transported to a climate-controlled facility where they stay protected from Brandon's ambient humidity while your property is dried, repaired, or rebuilt. Items requiring active treatment go through the appropriate process: ultrasonic cleaning for hard surfaces and non-porous items, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-saturated soft goods, freeze-drying for water-damaged documents and photographs, and dry-cleaning coordination for textiles. Electronics are evaluated by technicians before any attempt at restoration, corrosion from Florida's salt-laden air can complicate recovery on items that sat in a humid environment even briefly.

Once your home is cleared for move-back, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed according to your original room layout. Nothing is marked as complete until you've walked through and confirmed the inventory matches.
