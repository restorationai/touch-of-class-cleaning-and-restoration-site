## Why Valrico Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The housing stock in Valrico spans several distinct eras. Subdivisions that developed through the 1970s and 1980s often feature concrete block construction, CBS homes, with terrazzo or original tile floors, single-pane jalousie windows, and electrical panels that predate arc-fault requirements. Updating these homes isn't simply cosmetic; a kitchen remodel frequently uncovers aluminum branch wiring or undersized service panels that need to be brought up to current Florida Building Code before any finish work can proceed.

Newer communities built from the late 1990s onward tend to use wood-frame construction on slab foundations, which responds differently to Florida's humidity cycles. Seasonal expansion and contraction in the soil can telegraph into tile grout lines, door frames, and drywall seams, damage that looks like settling but is often moisture-driven. Renovating these homes well means accounting for vapor management from the start, not as an afterthought.

For post-damage rebuilds specifically, the timing matters. Florida's humidity means that framing exposed after a roof event or plumbing failure can begin to support mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. Getting a licensed general contractor on-site quickly, to assess structural integrity and begin coordinated reconstruction, limits secondary damage and keeps the insurance claim scope from expanding.
