## Our Water Cleanup Process in Tampa

Every water cleanup job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Crews use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls and under flooring, because in Tampa's humidity, what looks dry at the surface is often still saturated an inch in. The process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

1. **Containment and safety check**, identify electrical hazards, document affected areas, photograph materials for the insurance file.
2. **Standing water extraction**, truck-mounted extraction units pull free water from flooring, carpet, and low-lying areas before structural drying begins.
3. **Structural drying**, industrial dehumidifiers and air movers are placed in a calculated pattern based on the class and category of the loss. In Tampa's ambient humidity, drying equipment works harder than it would in a drier climate; equipment placement and dwell time are adjusted accordingly.
4. **Monitoring**, moisture readings are logged at each visit until materials reach acceptable drying goals. Nothing is closed up until the numbers confirm it.
5. **Documentation**, a complete drying log is produced for your insurance carrier, including daily readings, equipment placement, and photo evidence.
