## Why Lithia's Climate and Housing Stock Shape Every Renovation

Hillsborough County's humidity is not a background detail. It is an active force on every building material from framing lumber to cabinet substrates. Lithia's position inland from Tampa Bay doesn't spare it from the afternoon storm cycles that push moisture into attic spaces, wall cavities, and crawl spaces throughout the summer months. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, which make up a significant portion of Lithia's older neighborhoods, often used building practices that predate current moisture-management standards. That means a kitchen remodel or bathroom renovation can surface hidden problems: soft subfloor edges near shower pans, staining on wall framing behind tile, or insulation that has been holding moisture against exterior sheathing for years.

Identifying those conditions before the finish work begins is not optional. Addressing them mid-project after cabinets are ordered and countertops are templated is expensive and disruptive. The DRYCOR RESTORE approach is to assess the structural condition of the space before any design decisions lock in, so the scope of work reflects what the home actually needs rather than what it looks like it needs from the surface.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Lithia

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough of the space being renovated and the systems that connect to it. ## Local Note: What Lithia's Soil Conditions Mean for Foundation-Adjacent Work

Lithia and the broader eastern Hillsborough County area sit over soil profiles that include areas of sandy fill and clay-heavy subsoil. That combination affects drainage behavior around foundations and slabs, particularly during the wet season. Homeowners planning additions, garage conversions, or any work that involves breaking a slab or altering drainage grades should expect a soil assessment to be part of the pre-construction evaluation. Grading changes that look minor on paper can redirect surface water toward a foundation rather than away from it, and in Lithia's rainfall environment, that kind of unintended consequence shows up quickly. DRYCOR RESTORE factors drainage impact into the design conversation before work begins, not after the first heavy rain.
