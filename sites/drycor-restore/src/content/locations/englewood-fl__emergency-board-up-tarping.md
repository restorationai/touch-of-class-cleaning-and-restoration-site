## Why Englewood Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Englewood's coastal position on Charlotte County's southern edge means it sits squarely in the cone for storms that track up the Gulf coast. The housing stock here reflects decades of growth: older concrete block homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, wood-frame cottages closer to the waterfront, and newer construction that often carries a tile roof. Each building type presents a different vulnerability. Tile roofs shed individual sections cleanly in high winds, leaving large open decking areas that must be tarped quickly to prevent the roof sheathing from swelling and delaminating. Older wood-frame structures near the water can have window framing that is already softened by years of salt air exposure, meaning a broken window is not just a security gap but a structural concern once rain begins driving through it.

Fire damage in Englewood also creates board-up needs that go beyond the obvious. A kitchen fire that chars a wall can leave adjacent windows warped and unsealed. Insurance adjusters expect the property to be secured before they arrive to document the loss, and a properly boarded structure signals that the owner acted to limit further damage, which matters during the claims process.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Englewood

When you call (813) 829-1091, a crew is dispatched immediately. On arrival, the team assesses every compromised opening: doors blown off hinges, windows shattered by debris, garage doors buckled inward, and any section of roof where the covering has lifted or torn away.

For window and door board-up, we use structural-grade plywood cut and secured with exterior fasteners rated for the application. Openings are measured precisely so panels seat flush, reducing the chance of wind getting beneath the edge and prying them loose in a follow-on storm. For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and anchored with batten boards and strapping rather than simply draped and tied, which is the method that fails in the next gust. The goal is a secured envelope that holds through subsequent weather while the underlying repair or reconstruction is planned.

All work is documented with timestamped photographs before, during, and after installation. That documentation goes directly to your insurance carrier and supports your claim from the first contact.
