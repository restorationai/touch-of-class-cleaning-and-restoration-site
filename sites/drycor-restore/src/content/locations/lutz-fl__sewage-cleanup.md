## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lutz

Sewage cleanup is not a wet-vac-and-fan job. Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the highest contamination level, and every step of the process has to account for that.

The crew begins with containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces, and establishing a decontamination zone for equipment and personnel. Affected materials are assessed for salvageability, porous materials like carpet, pad, and certain types of drywall that have been saturated with sewage are typically removed rather than dried in place, because no amount of drying eliminates the biological hazard embedded in the material.

After extraction and removal, the structural surfaces, concrete slab, subfloor, wall framing, are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for Category 3 contamination. This is not a single-pass wipe-down; it involves dwell time, repeat application, and verification. Drying equipment is then deployed to bring structural moisture levels back to baseline. The process follows IICRC standards for sewage remediation, and the crew is OSHA trained for the handling and disposal of biohazardous waste.
