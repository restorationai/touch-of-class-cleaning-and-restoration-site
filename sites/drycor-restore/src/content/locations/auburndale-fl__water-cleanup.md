## Why Auburndale Properties See Water Damage So Frequently

Polk County's subtropical climate means Auburndale averages well over 50 inches of rainfall a year, with the heaviest events concentrated between June and September. The city's position between multiple lakes, including Lake Ariana and Lake Juliana, creates a high water table that limits how quickly saturated soil drains after a heavy storm. That slow drainage puts persistent hydrostatic pressure on slab foundations and crawl-space enclosures, especially in neighborhoods developed in the 1960s and 1970s when vapor barriers were minimal or absent entirely.

Older construction in Auburndale also means galvanized steel supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks that have been corroding quietly for decades. A pinhole leak behind a bathroom wall can saturate the subfloor for weeks before a homeowner notices a soft spot underfoot. By then, the moisture has often wicked into the wall cavity and the framing below, a much larger remediation scope than the original leak would have required.

Florida's humidity compounds everything. Even after standing water is removed, ambient moisture in the 80–90% relative humidity range that Auburndale sees through summer can re-wet materials that appear dry on the surface. Without active dehumidification, a cleaned-up room can begin supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Auburndale

Every water cleanup job starts with a full moisture survey, thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, floors, and ceilings, before a single piece of furniture moves. That map tells us where water traveled, not just where it pooled. In Auburndale's slab-on-grade homes, water routinely migrates under tile grout lines and into the concrete itself, showing no visible surface sign until the readings confirm it.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Extraction**, truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and surface saturation from carpet, hardwood, and tile.
- **Controlled demolition**, if moisture readings confirm wet drywall, insulation, or subfloor, affected materials are removed to the dry line rather than dried in place, which is slower and less reliable in Florida's humidity.
- **Drying system placement**, high-velocity air movers and commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not a standard room count.
- **Daily monitoring**, readings are logged each day until all structural materials reach the target moisture content for the region's equilibrium humidity.
- **Documentation**, every reading, photo, and equipment log is packaged for your insurance carrier.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with IICRC WRT (Water) certification, EPA Lead-Safe certification, and OSHA training, the crew handling your job is qualified to work safely in homes of any age, including those with lead paint in window trim and door casings, which is common in Auburndale's pre-1978 housing stock.
