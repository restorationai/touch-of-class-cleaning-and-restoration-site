## Why Dundee Homes Require a Different Renovation Approach

Florida's Central Ridge climate means contractors here deal with conditions that don't apply in most of the country. Dundee's position between several lakes creates localized humidity pockets that accelerate wood rot in older framing, cause adhesive failures in flooring, and shorten the life of exterior finishes that would last a decade elsewhere. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, which make up a meaningful share of Dundee's residential stock, often used concrete block construction with minimal interior insulation. Renovating those structures means working around thermal bridging, addressing moisture migration through block walls, and sometimes discovering that previous owners patched rather than replaced aging plumbing or electrical systems.

Post-damage rebuilds add another layer of complexity. When water intrusion or fire damage triggers a renovation, Polk County typically requires permits that bring the affected portions of the structure up to current Florida Building Code, even if the original work predates those standards. That means a straightforward bathroom rebuild can involve updated ventilation requirements, GFCI placement, and sometimes load calculations if the layout changes. DRYCOR RESTORE holds a Florida Certified Building Contractor license (#CBC1253966) and coordinates directly with the county building department so permit timelines don't stall your project.

## Our General Contracting Process in Dundee

## Dundee Insurance and Permit Coordination

When a renovation follows a covered loss, the general contracting phase is where insurance claims either stay on track or fall apart. Carriers want itemized documentation, not ballpark figures, and they want to see that work is permitted and inspected. We produce the line-item scopes adjusters need, photograph existing conditions before demolition, and flag any code-upgrade costs that may qualify for additional coverage under your policy's ordinance-or-law provision. Not every homeowner knows that provision exists, and in Dundee's older housing stock, it can cover a meaningful portion of the rebuild cost.
