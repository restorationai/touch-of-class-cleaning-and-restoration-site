## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Seffner

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory before a single item is moved. Every piece is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code, this documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what existed before the loss.

From there, salvageable contents are packed out to a climate-controlled facility where the real work happens away from the damaged structure. Textiles go through ozone treatment or thermal fogging to neutralize smoke and microbial odors that open-air airing will not reach. Hard surfaces, furniture, cabinetry components, decorative items, are cleaned using methods matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for small valuables and electronics components, hand-cleaning for antiques and heirlooms where abrasion risk is real. Documents and photographs receive freeze-drying or air-drying protocols depending on saturation level.

Once the structure is dried and cleared, items are returned in the same documented order, placed back in the rooms they came from. The inventory created at pack-out becomes the delivery receipt at pack-back.
