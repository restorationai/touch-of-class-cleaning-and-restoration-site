## Why Seffner's Climate and Building Stock Shape Every Renovation

Hillsborough County's subtropical climate isn't just a comfort issue, it's a construction variable. Seffner's high annual rainfall and persistent humidity mean that moisture management has to be built into a renovation from the design phase, not addressed as an afterthought when drywall starts bubbling. Homes here that were built in the 1970s and 1980s, a common vintage in this part of the county, frequently used single-pane windows, minimal vapor barriers, and duct configurations that were never designed for today's efficiency standards. When a remodel opens up those walls, what's behind them often tells a different story than what was visible from the living room.

Slab-on-grade construction is the norm across most of Seffner, which affects how plumbing renovations get planned and permitted.
