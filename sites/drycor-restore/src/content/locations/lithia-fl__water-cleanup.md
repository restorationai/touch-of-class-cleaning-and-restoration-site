## Why Lithia Properties See Water Damage Differently Than Other Hillsborough Communities

The housing stock in Lithia skews newer, but newer does not mean immune. Planned communities like FishHawk Ranch and Starling were built on former ranchland with clay-heavy soils that drain slowly. During heavy rain events, that poor percolation means water tables rise quickly and storm runoff has nowhere to go fast. Slab-on-grade construction, which dominates this area, has no crawl space to absorb overflow, so water that enters a garage, a laundry room, or a back door goes directly onto the concrete slab and migrates under flooring within minutes.

Many Lithia homes also use luxury vinyl plank or engineered hardwood over that slab. Both materials can appear undamaged at the surface while moisture wicks laterally under the click-lock seams and begins saturating the subfloor adhesive or the concrete itself. A moisture meter reading at the surface can look acceptable while readings two inches deeper are still elevated. That hidden saturation is exactly what drives mold growth behind baseboards weeks after a homeowner thought the problem was resolved.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Lithia

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected area using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. This step matters especially in Lithia's newer construction because spray-foam insulation in exterior walls, common in post-2010 builds throughout the FishHawk corridor, traps moisture against the wall cavity rather than letting it evaporate outward. Knowing where the moisture actually is determines where the drying equipment goes.

From there, the process moves through these stages:

- **Standing water extraction**: truck-mounted and portable extractors pull bulk water from flooring, carpet pad, and low-lying areas.
- **Controlled demolition if needed**: saturated drywall, baseboards, or flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed to expose wet framing or concrete.
- **Structural drying**: industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned according to a drying plan calibrated to the room volume and material types.
- **Daily monitoring**: moisture readings are logged each day until all materials reach their dry standard per the IICRC S500 protocol.
- **Documentation**: every reading, photograph, and equipment placement is recorded for your insurance carrier.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding the Water Restoration Technician credential, the team follows a structured drying protocol, not a rough estimate of when things feel dry.

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Lithia's newer construction: the transition zone between a home's conditioned interior and an attached garage is often sealed with spray foam and a single layer of drywall on the garage side.
