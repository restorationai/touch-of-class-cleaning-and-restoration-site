Upholstered furniture, family photos, electronics, and clothing can begin breaking down within hours in Pasco County's warm, saturated air. ## Why Holiday Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Holiday's housing stock skews toward concrete block construction built through the 1960s and 1970s, a style common throughout unincorporated Pasco County. That construction era predates modern vapor barriers and interior insulation standards, which means moisture migrates through walls and into living spaces more readily than in newer builds. When a pipe bursts or a roof leak goes undetected after a summer storm, the contents of a room can be sitting in elevated humidity for days before a homeowner realizes the scope of the problem.

The Gulf influence also matters. Salt-laden air accelerates corrosion on metal-framed furniture, electronics, and appliances even before a loss event. After a flood or fire, that corrosion window shortens dramatically. Contents that might be salvageable in a drier inland climate can cross the threshold to total loss within 48 to 72 hours here if they are not moved to a climate-controlled environment.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Holiday

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition code. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was removed.

From there, items are transported to a secure, climate-controlled facility. Textiles go through ozone treatment or thermal fogging to remove smoke and odor compounds. Hard surfaces are cleaned with appropriate chemistry based on the material, whether that is finished wood, upholstery, ceramics, or electronics. Electronics and documents with restoration potential are separated and handled by specialists.

Items that cannot be restored are documented with the same detail as salvageable ones, so your adjuster has the evidence needed to process a replacement claim. Nothing is discarded without your sign-off.

Our team holds IICRC certification and is EPA Lead-Safe certified, which matters in Holiday's older concrete block homes where lead-based paint on window frames and interior trim can become a handling concern once water or fire disturbs those surfaces.
