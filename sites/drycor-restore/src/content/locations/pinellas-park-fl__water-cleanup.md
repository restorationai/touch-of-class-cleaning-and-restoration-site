## Local Note: What the Water Table Means for Drying Timelines

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Pinellas Park is how the local water table affects slab drying. When the water table is elevated after a heavy rain event, which happens regularly in the low-lying areas of Pinellas County, moisture can migrate upward through a concrete slab by capillary action even after the interior source has been eliminated.
