## Why Mulberry Properties Face Elevated Water Damage Risk

The geology beneath Mulberry is not like the sandy, well-draining soil found closer to the Gulf coast. Decades of phosphate mining have left portions of Polk County with compacted, clay-heavy fill and subsidence-prone ground that resists drainage. During the wet season, which runs roughly June through September, rainfall totals routinely exceed what storm drains and yard grading can handle quickly. That means water that should sheet off a property instead pools against foundations, finds its way through crawl space vents, or backs up through floor drains.

Older homes in and around Mulberry also carry plumbing systems installed in eras when galvanized steel was standard. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, and the failure mode is rarely a slow drip. It tends to be a sudden split, often at a joint, that can push dozens of gallons per minute into a wall or ceiling before anyone notices. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s are especially susceptible, and Mulberry has a meaningful share of that construction vintage.

## What Emergency Water Cleanup Looks Like in Mulberry

The process starts with containment, not equipment. Before a single pump runs, the crew locates the water source, confirms it is isolated, and documents the affected area with moisture readings and photographs. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for establishing a drying baseline.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard floors, carpet, and the cavities beneath them. In Mulberry homes with tile-over-concrete slabs, water migrates under the tile and collects in grout lines and adhesive voids. Thermal imaging helps locate water that has tracked beyond the visible wet zone, which is common when a supply line fails inside a wall.

Once bulk water is out, the drying phase begins. Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the room volume, material types, and ambient humidity. Polk County's summer humidity levels make this phase longer than it would be in a drier climate. The crew returns daily to take psychrometric readings and adjust equipment placement until materials reach their dry standard per IICRC S500 protocol.

DRYCOR RESTORE holds IICRC certification for water damage restoration and carries EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters in older Mulberry homes where disturbing painted surfaces during drying is sometimes unavoidable.

## Local Note

In Mulberry and the surrounding Polk County communities built on or near former phosphate processing areas, concrete slab construction is nearly universal, and those slabs are often thicker than standard residential pours. That extra mass holds moisture longer than a typical slab, and it means drying timelines that look complete on the surface can still show elevated readings at depth. Crews working in this area check slab moisture at multiple depths before signing off on a dry structure, not just at the surface, because a slab that reads acceptable at the top can still be releasing vapor upward into flooring adhesive and baseboards weeks later.
