## Why Dundee Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After a Storm or Fire

Dundee is a small lake-town community, and that geography matters when it comes to structural exposure. Properties near Lake Maud and the other water bodies that ring the city face wind that travels across open water with little to slow it down before it reaches a roofline or window. That kind of lateral wind load is exactly what lifts ridge-cap shingles, forces water under flashing, and drives rain horizontally through any gap a fire or impact has already created.

The housing stock in Dundee also skews older. Many homes were built in construction eras when roof decking was thinner and fastener patterns were less robust than current Florida Building Code requires. An older roof that has already lost a few shingles to a previous storm is far more likely to see a full section fail in the next one. When that happens, the window for getting a tarp in place before interior materials are soaked is measured in hours, not days.

Florida's humidity accelerates everything. Wet insulation and wet ceiling drywall begin to support mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. A proper emergency tarp, installed tight and weighted correctly, is not just cosmetic protection; it is the first line of defense against a secondary loss that can dwarf the original damage.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Dundee

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first questions are practical: what type of opening, how large, what is the current weather window, and is the structure safe to access. That intake shapes how the crew stages equipment before arriving on site.

For roof tarping, the process starts with a safe inspection of the damaged area. Crews secure a heavy-duty poly tarp using furring strips or cap boards anchored to undamaged decking, not just draped and weighted. A tarp that lifts in the next wind event provides no protection and can cause additional damage as it flaps against the structure. The goal is a tarp installation that will hold through the next rain event while permanent repairs are arranged.

For board-up after fire damage or a broken window, plywood panels are cut to fit the opening, sealed at the edges, and fastened in a way that deters both weather intrusion and unauthorized entry. On older Dundee homes with non-standard window dimensions, that often means custom cuts on site rather than pre-cut panels. The crew documents every opening, every panel dimension, and every photo of pre-existing damage before any material goes up, which matters significantly when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

## Dundee Insurance Coordination

Emergency board-up and tarping is typically covered under the "mitigation" provision of a homeowner's or commercial property policy, meaning the cost of protecting the structure from further damage is separate from the repair cost and is generally reimbursable. DRYCOR RESTORE documents the loss thoroughly: date-stamped photographs of every opening before and after boarding, written scope of work, and material records that match what an adjuster needs to process the claim.

If your carrier requires a preferred vendor or a specific documentation format, let the crew know at the time of the call. The team has worked with the range of carriers that cover Polk County properties and can adapt documentation accordingly.
