## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Pinellas Park

Every board-up and tarping job starts with a fast structural read, identifying which openings are load-bearing concerns, which are weather-entry risks, and what sequence of work keeps the structure stable while crews are on it. For roof tarping, we use reinforced poly tarps weighted and anchored at the deck level rather than draped loosely over ridge lines, which is the approach that actually holds when afternoon thunderstorms roll in off Tampa Bay with little warning.

For window and door board-up, we cut OSB panels to fit the rough opening rather than nailing oversized sheets that leave gaps at the edges. On masonry block homes, a construction type you'll find throughout Pinellas Park's older residential streets, we use tapcon anchors into the block rather than relying on wood-frame fastening methods that don't translate. The goal is a secured opening that keeps weather and unauthorized entry out until permanent repairs can be scheduled.

We photograph every opening before and after securing it, document the materials used, and provide a written scope that matches the format most carriers and public adjusters expect to see when a claim is filed.
