## Why Auburndale Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Much of Auburndale's residential stock was built between the 1950s and 1980s using concrete-block construction, a method that handles Florida's wind loads well but creates specific challenges during renovation. CBS (concrete block and stucco) walls don't accept standard framing anchors the way wood-frame construction does, and running new electrical or plumbing through them requires core drilling and careful waterproofing of any penetrations. Older homes in this era also frequently used single-pane jalousie windows, asbestos-containing floor tiles, and galvanized supply lines, materials that require proper identification and handling before any remodel can move forward.

Polk County's clay-heavy soils shift seasonally, and that movement shows up as diagonal cracks at door corners, sticking doors, and uneven tile floors, cosmetic symptoms that can mask underlying slab or foundation issues. A kitchen or bathroom remodel that ignores those cracks risks finishing over a problem that will reopen within a year. Part of responsible general contracting in Auburndale is knowing when to call for a slab evaluation before the tile order is placed.

## Kitchen and Bathroom Remodels in Auburndale's Climate

Kitchen and bathroom remodels in Central Florida require moisture management decisions that don't come up the same way in drier climates. Auburndale's year-round humidity means that tile backer selection, grout sealing, and exhaust fan sizing aren't cosmetic choices, they're the difference between a bathroom that holds up for fifteen years and one that shows mold staining at the grout lines within two. We specify cement board or equivalent moisture-resistant backer on all wet-area tile installations and recommend ENERGY STAR–rated exhaust fans sized to the actual cubic footage of the space.

For kitchen remodels, the age of the home often determines what's possible without a full electrical panel upgrade. Homes built before the mid-1980s frequently have 100-amp service panels that can't support a modern kitchen's load, induction cooktops, double ovens, and dishwashers running simultaneously. Identifying that early keeps the project from stalling when the electrician arrives.

## Local Note

Auburndale's position between two lakes, Lake Ariana and Lake Juliana, means that properties on the lower-lying streets near the water table can see slab moisture vapor transmission that surprises homeowners who've never lived near a Florida lake system. If you're planning to install hardwood or luxury vinyl plank over a slab in one of those lakeside neighborhoods, a moisture vapor emission rate (MVER) test before flooring installation is worth the time. Skipping it is the most common reason a beautiful new floor starts cupping or delaminating within the first year.
