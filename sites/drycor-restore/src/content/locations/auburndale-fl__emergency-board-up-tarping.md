## Why Auburndale Properties Are Vulnerable After Storm Events

Auburndale's position between Lake Ariana and Lake Juliana means the city sits in a low-lying corridor where wind funnels and storm surge from nearby water bodies can amplify localized damage. Older residential construction, much of it built during Auburndale's mid-century growth, frequently features wood-frame walls and single-pane windows that are more susceptible to wind pressure than modern impact-rated glazing. When a fast-moving storm cell clips the area, it's common to see multiple simultaneous failures: a soffit blown loose, a window cracked by debris, and a section of roofing peeled back all on the same property.

Fire damage adds another layer of urgency. After a structure fire, Auburndale Fire Rescue and Polk County inspectors will red-tag a property and prohibit re-entry until it is secured. That means open doorways, broken windows, and exposed roof decking must be boarded and tarped before the homeowner can even begin the insurance process. Waiting until morning is not a neutral choice, it is an invitation for rain intrusion, opportunistic theft, and accelerating structural deterioration.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Auburndale

When you call (813) 829-1091, a crew is dispatched immediately, day or night. The process follows a deliberate sequence designed to stop the loss from growing:

**Assessment first.** The crew walks the perimeter and photographs every opening, every compromised roofline, and every point where weather or intruders could enter. This documentation is shared directly with your insurance adjuster.

**Structural openings.** Windows and doors are measured and covered with plywood cut to fit, fastened to the surrounding framing rather than the finish material. For masonry block construction, common in Auburndale homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, anchoring methods are adjusted so fasteners don't crack the block face.

**Roof tarping.** Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are secured with lumber battens and screwed into the decking, not just draped over the ridge. In Central Florida's afternoon convective storms, a tarp held down only by weight will fail within minutes. Battened installation holds through sustained winds and keeps the interior dry while permanent repairs are scheduled.

**Re-inspection.** Before leaving, the crew documents the completed work with timestamped photos and provides a written scope summary for your insurance carrier.

## Local Note

Auburndale's older wood-frame homes, particularly those constructed before Florida adopted its current wind-load requirements, often have roof sheathing fastened with smooth-shank nails rather than ring-shank or screws.
