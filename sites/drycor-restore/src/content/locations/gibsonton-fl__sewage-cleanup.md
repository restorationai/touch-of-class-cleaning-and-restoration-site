## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Gibsonton

Category 3 water, the classification for raw sewage, requires a different approach than a clean-water pipe burst. The crew arrives with personal protective equipment, containment materials, and industrial extractors rated for solid-laden waste. The process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted, and porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, including saturated drywall, insulation, and certain flooring substrates, are removed and bagged for proper disposal.
