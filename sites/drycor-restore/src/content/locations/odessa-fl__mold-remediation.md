## Why Odessa Properties See Mold Issues

Odessa's geography puts it at a particular disadvantage when it comes to indoor moisture control. The community developed rapidly through the 1990s and 2000s across land that was previously wetland or low-lying pasture, and many neighborhoods sit on fill soil with drainage characteristics that push groundwater close to the surface after heavy rain. That means moisture does not just come from above during a storm; it can wick upward through a slab, especially in homes where the vapor barrier beneath the concrete has aged or was never installed to current standards.

The housing stock compounds the issue. Homes built in the late 1980s and through the 2000s in this part of Hillsborough County frequently used paper-faced drywall throughout, including in bathrooms and laundry rooms where humidity is highest. Paper facing is a preferred food source for mold colonies, and once spore counts elevate inside a wall cavity, surface cleaning alone does not resolve the problem. The mold is feeding on the substrate, not just resting on it.

Odessa's long cooling season also means air conditioning systems run for eight or nine months of the year. When a duct connection loosens or an air handler drip pan overflows, the resulting moisture sits in a warm, enclosed space, exactly the conditions mold needs to spread from one stud bay to the next before anyone notices a musty smell.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Odessa

Every remediation begins with a thorough assessment rather than an assumption.
