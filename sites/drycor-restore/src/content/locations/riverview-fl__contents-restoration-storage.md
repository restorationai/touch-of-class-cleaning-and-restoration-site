When that happens, whether it's a smoke-damaged sofa, a warped bookcase full of family photos, or electronics sitting in an inch of standing water, the contents of your home often need as much attention as the structure itself. DRYCOR RESTORE handles contents restoration and storage for Riverview properties as a dedicated service, not an afterthought bolted onto structural drying.

## Why Riverview Homes See More Contents Damage Than You'd Expect

Riverview's growth has been explosive, and the housing stock reflects it: a mix of newer master-planned subdivisions with tile roofs and stucco exteriors alongside older ranch-style homes built in the 1970s and 1980s with original windows and aging HVAC ductwork. Both types carry real contents risk, just for different reasons.

Newer homes in Riverview's larger communities often have open floor plans where a single water intrusion event spreads quickly across a wide square footage before it's noticed. Older ranch homes, meanwhile, tend to have lower ceilings, less air circulation, and original flooring that traps moisture underneath, meaning the furniture and belongings sitting on that floor absorb damage longer before a homeowner realizes there's a problem. Florida's sustained high relative humidity (often above 70% even on dry days) accelerates mold colonization on porous contents like upholstered furniture, mattresses, and cardboard storage boxes. IICRC guidelines note that mold can begin developing on wet organic materials within 24 to 48 hours, in Riverview's climate, that window is not theoretical.

Fire and smoke events compound the problem differently. Stucco-and-block construction common throughout Riverview holds heat longer than wood-frame homes, which means smoke odor embeds more deeply into soft furnishings, closet contents, and cabinetry before temperatures drop enough to begin restoration work.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Riverview

The first step is a detailed contents inventory, every item photographed, catalogued, and categorized by material type and damage level before anything is moved. This documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing is misplaced during the restoration process.

Salvageable items are packed out and transported to a climate-controlled facility where they undergo the appropriate restoration method: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and non-porous items, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor, freeze-drying for water-damaged documents and photographs, and controlled drying for textiles and upholstery. Items that cannot be restored are documented thoroughly so your adjuster has the evidence needed to process a replacement claim.

Storage in a climate-controlled environment is critical in Hillsborough County's heat and humidity. Leaving contents in a standard warehouse or a homeowner's garage while structural repairs proceed is a reliable way to turn a salvageable item into a total loss. Items are returned to your Riverview home only after the structure has been cleared for re-occupancy and the environment is stable.

## Riverview Insurance & Contents Claims

Contents claims are frequently the most disputed portion of a homeowner's property insurance settlement. Adjusters may challenge replacement values, question whether an item was pre-existing damage, or push back on restoration costs versus replacement costs. The detailed inventory and photographic documentation DRYCOR RESTORE produces at the point of pack-out is specifically structured to support your claim, not just to track items internally.
