Seffner's position along the I-4 and US-92 corridor places it squarely in Central Florida's lightning and severe-weather belt. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Seffner

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first thing we do is ask about the type and location of the breach, roof, window, door, or wall, so we load the right materials before we leave. Roof tarps for Florida conditions need to be heavy-duty polyethylene rated for UV exposure; a standard blue tarp stapled at the edges won't survive the next afternoon storm. We use reinforced tarps secured with lumber battens and screws driven into undamaged decking, not just weighted at the corners.

For window and door board-up, we cut plywood to fit each opening, securing it with exterior-grade screws into the surrounding framing rather than tacking it to the siding. On concrete-block construction, common throughout Seffner, we use masonry anchors where the framing doesn't extend to the block, ensuring the board actually holds against wind pressure rather than just covering the gap visually.

Every opening gets photographed before and after installation. That documentation goes directly into your claim file and is formatted for submission to your carrier.
