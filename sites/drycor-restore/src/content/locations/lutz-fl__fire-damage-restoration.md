## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Lutz

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a disciplined order that keeps secondary damage from compounding the original loss.

**Stabilization and safety assessment** comes first. Before any cleaning begins, the structure is evaluated for compromised load-bearing elements, open roof sections, and electrical hazards. Temporary board-up and tarping protect the interior from Florida's afternoon rain while the full scope is being documented.

**Soot and smoke removal** is where the technical detail matters most. Dry soot from fast-burning fires is loose and powdery; wet soot from slow, smoldering fires is sticky and penetrating. The cleaning chemistry and method, dry sponges, HEPA vacuuming, alkaline cleaners, or thermal fogging, depends on which type of combustion produced the residue. Our IICRC-certified team identifies the residue type before selecting the approach, not after.

**Structural drying** addresses suppression water. Moisture meters and thermal imaging map saturation in wall cavities, subfloors, and ceiling assemblies. Drying equipment is placed to pull moisture out of the structure systematically, and readings are logged daily until materials reach acceptable moisture content.

**Odor neutralization** is the final technical step before reconstruction begins. In Lutz's climate, residual smoke odor can intensify on humid days if volatile compounds are not fully neutralized. Hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging reach into wall cavities and HVAC pathways where surface cleaning alone cannot.
