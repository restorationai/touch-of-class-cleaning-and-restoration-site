## Why Ruskin's Building Stock and Climate Shape Every Renovation

A significant portion of Ruskin's residential inventory was built between the 1960s and the early 1990s, when concrete block construction dominated South Hillsborough County. Block walls are durable, but they present specific challenges during remodels: running new electrical or plumbing requires core drilling or surface-mounted conduit, and older homes in this era often pre-date modern vapor barrier requirements. When humidity pushes through an unreinforced block wall, a common occurrence during Florida's June-through-September rainy season, moisture can accumulate inside wall cavities long before it's visible on the surface.

Soil movement is the other factor that shapes project planning here. The clay-heavy soils common throughout coastal Hillsborough County expand when saturated and contract during dry stretches, which means slab foundations in Ruskin can develop differential settlement over time. Before any floor tile replacement or addition framing begins, a honest assessment of the slab's current condition isn't optional, it's the difference between a renovation that holds and one that cracks within a few years.
