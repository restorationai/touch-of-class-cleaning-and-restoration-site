## Local Note

Lutz has a notable concentration of homes built on concrete slab foundations with clay-tile roofs, a combination that looks durable but creates a specific vulnerability during high-wind events. Clay tiles can lift or crack without visibly detaching, leaving gaps that aren't obvious from the ground but allow water to reach the underlayment and decking beneath. If your tile roof has taken wind damage, the visible surface may look intact while the layer underneath is already wet.
