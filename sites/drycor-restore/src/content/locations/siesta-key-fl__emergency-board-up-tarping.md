Siesta Key sits on a narrow barrier island where Gulf storms can strip roofing, shatter windows, and push storm surge through ground-floor openings in the span of a single afternoon. When that happens, every hour a structure stays open to wind-driven rain accelerates the damage, and on a barrier island with limited bridge access, getting the right crew there fast matters as much as the work itself. DRYCOR RESTORE responds 24/7 to board-up and tarping calls throughout Siesta Key, securing properties until permanent repairs can begin.

## Why Siesta Key Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Barrier island geography shapes almost every aspect of storm damage here. Siesta Key's position along the Gulf of Mexico means it sits in the direct path of both named hurricanes and the fast-moving squalls that develop offshore and make landfall with little warning. Wind speeds on the island routinely exceed those recorded a few miles inland, and salt-laden air accelerates the deterioration of window seals, fascia boards, and roofing underlayment, making structures more vulnerable to breach even in moderate storms.

The island's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant share of Siesta Key's residential properties were built during the post-war and mid-century construction boom, when building codes were less prescriptive about wind resistance and impact glazing. Older jalousie windows and single-pane sliding glass doors, common in properties from that era, are among the first components to fail under storm pressure. Once a window or door fails, interior damage escalates quickly: rain soaks drywall, flooring, and cabinetry within minutes, and in the Gulf Coast humidity, mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Siesta Key

When you call, the first thing we establish is the nature and extent of the opening: a single broken window, a compromised entry door, or a section of missing roof decking each requires a different response. For window and door board-up, we cut plywood to fit and fasten it with structural screws rather than nails, which hold better against sustained wind pressure and are easier to remove cleanly when the contractor arrives for permanent repairs. For roof damage, we use heavy-gauge polyethylene tarps secured with battens and screws driven into solid decking, not draped loosely and weighted with sandbags, which can shift and fail in the next wind event.

We photograph every opening before and after securing it, document the visible damage, and provide a written scope summary. That documentation is formatted to support an insurance claim from the start, so your adjuster has what they need without requiring a return visit.

## Reaching Siesta Key from Thonotosassa

Siesta Key is accessible via the Stickney Point Road bridge to the south and the Siesta Drive bridge to the north, and during and immediately after a storm, both can experience traffic delays or, in severe events, temporary closures. Our dispatch team monitors bridge and road conditions in real time and routes crews accordingly. Because we operate around the clock, we can often stage for island calls before a storm clears, positioning to respond as soon as access is restored rather than starting the clock from zero when conditions improve.

Properties closer to the southern end of the island, near Stickney Point, are typically reached via the southern bridge, while properties along the northern stretches near Siesta Drive are accessed from the north. Knowing which approach is open matters when every minute counts.

## Siesta Key Insurance and HOA Coordination

Many Siesta Key properties are managed as vacation rentals or are part of condominium associations with their own insurance requirements and vendor approval processes. We are familiar with the documentation standards that carriers operating in coastal Florida typically require for emergency protective measures, including itemized material lists, pre- and post-photos, and signed authorization records. If your property is managed by an HOA or a rental management company, we can coordinate directly with them on access and scope so you are not caught in the middle of multiple phone calls during an already stressful situation.

Emergency board-up and tarping is generally covered under the protective measures provision of a standard homeowners or commercial property policy. We provide the documentation; your adjuster determines coverage.

## Local Note

One thing that catches property owners off guard on Siesta Key is how quickly salt air works on exposed structural materials after a breach. Once a roof or wall opening exposes raw wood framing or OSB sheathing to the Gulf environment, the combination of humidity, salt, and heat begins degrading those materials faster than it would in an inland setting. A tarp installed loosely or with gaps at the edges is not just a water problem; it is an accelerated decay problem. We overlap tarp edges and seal perimeters specifically because of this, not as a general best practice but as a necessary adjustment to the coastal exposure Siesta Key properties face year-round.
