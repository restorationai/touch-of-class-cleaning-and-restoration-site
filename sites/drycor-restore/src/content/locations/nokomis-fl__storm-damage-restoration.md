## Why Nokomis Properties See Elevated Storm Damage Risk

Nokomis occupies a narrow land mass between the Intracoastal Waterway and the Gulf, which compresses storm surge and wind funneling in ways that inland Sarasota County communities do not experience at the same intensity. The housing stock here skews older, with a significant share of homes built in the 1960s through the 1980s using construction methods that predate Florida's post-Hurricane Andrew building code reforms. That means original single-pane jalousie windows, wood-framed roof trusses without modern hurricane straps, and roofing materials that have cycled through multiple re-roofs over the decades.

When wind peels back a section of roof or pushes water past an aging window seal, the damage moves fast. Insulation becomes saturated within hours. Wood framing and OSB sheathing begin to swell. If the home has a concrete block exterior, the interior side of those walls can wick moisture inward without showing visible surface damage, which is one reason a post-storm inspection needs moisture meters and thermal imaging, not just a visual walkthrough.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Nokomis

Every storm job starts with a documented assessment before any material is moved or removed. We photograph and log affected areas, identify the moisture boundary using calibrated meters, and note any structural concerns before crews begin work. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for any permitting the county requires.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence: emergency tarping or board-up to stop ongoing water intrusion, controlled extraction of standing water, structural drying using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers, and then debris removal and reconstruction staging. For tree-impact jobs, which are common in Nokomis after any storm with sustained winds above tropical-storm strength, we coordinate safe limb and trunk removal from the structure before assessing the roof and wall penetrations beneath.

Because Sarasota County can experience permit backlogs following a widespread storm event, we document scope thoroughly upfront so that reconstruction permitting moves without unnecessary back-and-forth with the building department.

## Nokomis Insurance Coordination After a Storm

Most homeowner policies in Florida include wind and water damage coverage, but the documentation requirements for a storm claim are specific. Carriers want proof of the storm event, a clear separation between pre-existing conditions and storm-caused damage, and a scope of loss that matches the adjuster's inspection. We photograph every affected surface, log moisture readings with timestamps, and prepare a written scope that aligns with the format adjusters use.
