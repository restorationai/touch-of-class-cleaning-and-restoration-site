## Why Port Richey Properties Face Repeated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

The Gulf-facing position of Port Richey makes it one of the more storm-exposed communities in Pasco County. Beyond storms, the area's housing stock includes a significant number of concrete block homes built in the 1960s and 1970s. Fire damage is another common driver of emergency board-up calls in Port Richey. A kitchen fire or electrical fire that stays contained to one room still typically blows out windows from heat pressure and leaves openings that invite theft, weather intrusion, and additional structural compromise. Boarding those openings the same night is not optional, it is the difference between a recoverable loss and a total.
