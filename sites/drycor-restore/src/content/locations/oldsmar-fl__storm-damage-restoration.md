## Why Oldsmar Properties See Elevated Storm Damage Risk

Oldsmar's position on a narrow peninsula between Old Tampa Bay to the south and Lake Tarpon to the north means storm surge and sheet flooding can approach from more than one direction during a significant weather event. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Oldsmar

The first priority after any storm is stopping the water that is still entering the structure. That means emergency tarping of breached roof sections, board-up of broken windows or doors, and a rapid interior assessment to locate where water has migrated. Water travels quickly through wall cavities, across subfloor assemblies, and into lower levels, and in Oldsmar's older slab-on-grade homes it can wick into block walls before it becomes visible on interior surfaces.

Once the structure is secured, the drying phase begins. Our IICRC-certified technicians use thermal imaging cameras to map moisture behind drywall and under flooring without unnecessary demolition. Industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, along with high-velocity air movers, are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage drying. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, including saturated insulation, compromised roof sheathing, or swollen cabinetry, are documented, removed, and catalogued for the insurance claim before disposal.

Tree impact damage requires a separate scope: structural assessment of the roof framing, documentation of the point of penetration, and coordination with a licensed contractor for any repairs that require a Pinellas County building permit.
