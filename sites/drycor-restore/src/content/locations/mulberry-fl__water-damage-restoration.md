## Why Mulberry Properties See Water Damage Issues

The geology beneath Mulberry is not typical Florida flatland. Decades of phosphate mining have altered drainage patterns across Polk County, and in some areas the soil compaction and subsidence left behind can redirect groundwater in ways that surprise even longtime residents. Homes built on lots near former mining corridors sometimes experience moisture intrusion through foundation walls or slabs during heavy rain events, not because of a plumbing failure, but because the ground itself is saturated and has nowhere to route the water.

Mulberry's housing stock skews older, with a significant portion of the residential inventory built in the mid-20th century. Homes from that era typically used cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines, both of which corrode from the inside out over decades. A pinhole leak behind a wall can run for weeks before the surface shows any sign of damage, and by then the framing, insulation, and subfloor are already compromised. Florida's humidity keeps materials from drying naturally, which means secondary mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a water event if drying equipment is not in place quickly.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Mulberry

Every job starts with a moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated meters, not a visual inspection alone. Water hides in wall cavities, under tile, and inside structural framing, and what reads dry at the surface can still be saturated an inch deeper. Once the full extent of the intrusion is mapped, we move to extraction: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from flooring, subfloor, and crawl spaces before drying equipment goes in.

Structural drying in Mulberry requires accounting for the ambient humidity that Polk County carries almost year-round. Our drying systems are sized and placed based on the specific moisture load of each structure, not a generic formula. Refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers work alongside high-velocity air movers to drive moisture out of materials and capture it before it recirculates into the air. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until every affected material reaches its target drying goal, documented at each visit.

Because many Mulberry homes were built before lead paint regulations, our EPA Lead-Safe Certified status matters when demolition of water-damaged materials is necessary. Any cutting, sanding, or removal of pre-1978 building materials follows containment and disposal protocols that protect your family and satisfy Florida contractor requirements.
