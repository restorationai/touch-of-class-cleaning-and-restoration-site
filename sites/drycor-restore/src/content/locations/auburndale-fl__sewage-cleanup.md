## Why Auburndale Properties Experience Sewage Backups

Polk County's sandy, high-permeability soils drain quickly after light rain but can become saturated during the prolonged downpours that define a Central Florida summer. When the ground is already holding water, septic drain fields lose their absorption capacity and sewage can surface in yards or push back through floor drains. Auburndale's mix of mid-century block homes and more recent construction adds another layer of complexity: older cast-iron or clay sewer laterals are prone to root intrusion and joint separation, while some newer subdivisions built on former citrus groves may have fill soil that settles unevenly over time, stressing buried lines. Municipal sewer connections along the US-92 corridor have seen increased load as the city has grown, and lift station failures during power outages, common after afternoon thunderstorms, can send sewage back through the lowest fixtures in a home.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Auburndale

Raw sewage removal is not a job for a shop vac and a mop. The process starts with containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms, then extracting standing sewage with truck-mounted or portable extraction units capable of handling solids. Once the bulk material is removed, affected flooring, drywall, and cabinetry are assessed. In Auburndale's block construction homes, concrete block walls are non-porous but the mortar joints and interior drywall furring are not, materials that cannot be adequately sanitized are removed and documented for your insurance claim.

After demolition of unsalvageable materials, every affected surface is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 water contamination. As an IICRC Certified Firm, our crews follow the IICRC S500 and S520 standards for contaminated water losses. Drying equipment, desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, runs until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry, not just surface-dry. A final antimicrobial application closes out the sanitization phase before any reconstruction begins.
