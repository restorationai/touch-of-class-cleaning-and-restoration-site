## Why Bartow Properties See Mold Issues

Polk County's subtropical climate means Bartow rarely gets a sustained dry stretch long enough to naturally reset indoor humidity. Average summer relative humidity routinely climbs past 80 percent, and the rainy season stretches from late spring well into fall. That sustained moisture load is hard on any building envelope, but it is especially hard on older Bartow homes.

Many properties in Bartow were built before modern vapor barriers and pressure-treated framing were standard practice. Crawl spaces under pier-and-beam homes are common in the older residential corridors, and those spaces trap ground moisture year-round. Concrete block construction, which became the dominant building method in central Florida during the postwar decades, is durable but porous. When block walls wick moisture inward during heavy rain events, the interior face can stay damp long after the storm passes, giving mold a foothold behind baseboards and inside wall cavities where it is invisible until the smell gives it away.

Flatwood soils in this part of Polk County have low permeability, meaning water moves slowly through the ground after heavy rain. That keeps soil moisture high around foundations for extended periods and increases the risk of intrusion through slab edges and block-wall footings.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Bartow

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection, including moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters. We do not guess at the boundary of a mold problem based on what is visible. Mold that reads dry at the surface of a plaster or block wall can be actively colonizing the material behind it.

Once we establish the scope, we set up physical containment using negative air pressure and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Affected porous materials, including drywall, insulation, and wood framing that cannot be cleaned to a standard consistent with IICRC and NAERMC guidelines, are removed and bagged for disposal.

Surfaces that can be remediated in place are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. We then run HEPA air scrubbers through a full clearance cycle before any reconstruction begins. For homes with crawl spaces, which are common throughout Bartow's older neighborhoods, we address the crawl space as part of the same scope rather than treating it as a separate project, because a remediated living space will re-contaminate if the crawl space underneath remains a mold reservoir.

## Local Note

Bartow's older residential areas include a significant number of homes with original tongue-and-groove pine subfloors and interior wood paneling, materials that were standard in central Florida construction from the 1920s through the 1950s. That dense, resinous old-growth pine is actually more resistant to mold than modern engineered wood products, but it also holds moisture unevenly and can show surface mold while remaining structurally sound. The practical implication is that experienced moisture mapping matters more here than in a neighborhood of newer construction: a reading that would indicate replacement in a home with OSB subfloor may indicate a cleanable surface in a home with original pine. Getting that call wrong in either direction costs the homeowner money.
