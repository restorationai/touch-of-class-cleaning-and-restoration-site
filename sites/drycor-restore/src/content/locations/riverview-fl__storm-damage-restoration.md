## Why Riverview Properties Take Storm Hits Hard

Hillsborough County's flat topography gives water almost nowhere to go after a heavy storm. Riverview's newer subdivisions, many built on former wetland and agricultural land, sit on clay-heavy soils that shed water slowly, meaning standing water lingers against foundations and under slabs long after the rain stops. That moisture migrates. It wicks into block walls, seeps beneath vinyl plank flooring, and pools inside wall cavities before a homeowner notices anything more than a damp smell.

The area's tree canopy is a separate hazard. Mature oaks and laurel cherries common throughout Riverview's residential neighborhoods shed large limbs under tropical-force gusts, and full tree strikes on roofs are a regular occurrence during named storms. A compromised roof envelope turns a wind event into a water intrusion event within hours, sometimes minutes if a storm stalls overhead.

Riverview also sits close enough to Tampa Bay that storm surge is a real variable during major hurricane tracks. Homes in lower-elevation sections near the Alafia River corridor can see water intrusion from two directions simultaneously: rainfall from above and rising water from below.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Riverview

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence matters and it doesn't change. When we arrive, the first priority is making the structure safe and stopping active intrusion, that means emergency tarping of breached roofs, boarding compromised windows and doors, and identifying any structural concerns before crews move inside.

Once the envelope is secured, we shift to moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. In Riverview's stucco-over-frame construction, water behind cladding can read dry at the surface while the sheathing underneath is already saturating. Missing that pocket means mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours.

Debris removal, tree limbs, insulation, damaged roofing materials, runs parallel to drying setup. We place commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, calibrated to the affected square footage and material types. Readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented for your insurance adjuster, not estimated.

Reconstruction coordination follows once the structure meets dry standard. As a licensed contractor (CBC1253966), we handle the rebuild phase directly, no handoff to a second company, no gap in accountability.
