## Why Dade City Properties Experience Sewage Backups

Pasco County's clay-heavy soils drain poorly, and Dade City's older residential areas include homes built on lots where the original drain field sizing no longer matches the household load or current usage patterns. When heavy summer rains saturate the ground, septic systems that function normally in dry conditions can back up quickly because the drain field simply cannot absorb fast enough. Municipal sewer customers in the older parts of town are not immune: aging cast-iron and clay-tile lateral lines crack, root into, and collapse, especially in blocks where mature oaks and camphor trees have had decades to work into the joints. A single blocked lateral can push raw sewage back through the lowest fixture in the house, which in a slab-on-grade home is often a floor drain, a toilet, or a washing machine standpipe.

The region's wet season runs roughly June through September, and that window is when DRYCOR RESTORE sees the highest volume of septic overflow calls from the Dade City area. Homes on larger rural lots outside the city's utility service boundary are especially vulnerable because a failing drain field has nowhere to divert flow except back into the structure or onto the surface of the yard.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Dade City

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification recognized by the IICRC S500 standard, and it requires a different protocol than a clean-water pipe burst. The process begins with containment: affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. All standing sewage and solid waste are extracted using truck-mounted and portable units, then bagged and disposed of in compliance with applicable regulations.

After extraction, every surface that contacted the sewage, including flooring, baseboards, wall cavities where water wicked upward, and subfloor material, is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. Porous materials that cannot be adequately sanitized, such as saturated drywall, carpet padding, and certain hardwood flooring, are removed and documented before disposal. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, with moisture readings logged throughout to confirm the building envelope returns to acceptable levels. Because Dade City's ambient humidity is high for much of the year, the drying phase typically requires more aggressive equipment placement than the same job would in a drier climate.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with EPA Lead-Safe Certification, DRYCOR RESTORE follows established remediation protocols for homes of any age, including the older bungalows and block construction homes common in Dade City's historic residential areas where lead-based paint in wall cavities is a real consideration during demolition.
