## Why Pinellas Park Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The housing stock across Pinellas Park leans heavily on construction methods that were standard in the postwar boom, concrete block exteriors, terrazzo floors, jalousie windows, and flat or low-slope rooflines that were designed for a different era's energy codes and storm standards. Updating these homes isn't simply cosmetic. Removing a load-bearing wall in a CBS (concrete block structure) home requires engineering review that a wood-frame renovation might not. Terrazzo restoration or removal under a kitchen remodel often uncovers moisture intrusion at the slab edge that has been wicking quietly for years.

Pinellas County's proximity to the Gulf also means that any exterior work, siding, windows, roofing, or additions, must meet Florida Building Code wind-load requirements calibrated for a coastal high-velocity hurricane zone. Permits pulled through the City of Pinellas Park Building Department carry those requirements, and inspections are staged: framing, rough mechanical, and final.
