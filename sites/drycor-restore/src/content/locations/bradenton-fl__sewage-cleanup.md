## Why Bradenton Properties Are Prone to Sewage Backups

Several factors converge in Bradenton that make sewage events more common here than in drier, higher-elevation markets. Much of the city's residential plumbing was installed during the post-war boom of the 1950s through 1970s, when cast-iron and Orangeburg pipe were standard materials. Both degrade over decades, cast iron corrodes from the inside out, and Orangeburg, made from compressed tar paper, softens and collapses under sustained moisture. When those pipes fail, the failure is rarely clean: root intrusion, partial collapses, and grease buildup all narrow the line before it fully blocks.

Bradenton's flat topography compounds the problem. Without meaningful elevation change, gravity drainage is slow, and low points in older neighborhoods tend to collect both stormwater and sewer surcharge during heavy rain. Properties on lots that sit even slightly below street grade are especially vulnerable. Septic systems in unincorporated Manatee County present a different but related risk, drain fields in the region's sandy, high-water-table soil can saturate quickly during wet season, causing septic overflow at the surface or back into the structure.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bradenton

Sewage cleanup is not the same process as clean-water flood cleanup, and the distinction matters. Raw sewage, classified as Category 3 water by the IICRC standard, requires full personal protective equipment on entry, immediate containment of the affected zone, and disposal protocols that differ from standard water damage work.

The process begins with a site assessment to identify the source and confirm the backup is controlled before any extraction starts. Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units depending on access, then affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor, are removed and bagged for disposal. Hard surfaces are cleaned in multiple passes using EPA-registered disinfectants, not general-purpose cleaners. After cleaning, the space is treated with antimicrobial agents and dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are taken at structural cavities, not just surfaces, because sewage-saturated framing can read dry at the face while staying wet behind the wall.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding WRT credentials, DRYCOR RESTORE follows the S500 and S520 standards for contaminated water and microbial remediation. The team is also OSHA trained for bloodborne pathogen and hazardous material protocols, relevant on any Category 3 loss.

Most homeowners' policies cover sudden and accidental sewage backups, but the coverage often sits in a rider or endorsement rather than the base policy, and some carriers require that the backup originated from a covered peril. ## Local Note

In Bradenton's older concrete block homes, a construction style that was dominant here from the 1950s through the 1980s, sewage that reaches the slab often wicks laterally under the flooring before it becomes visible. Tile and vinyl plank can look intact while the concrete beneath holds contaminated moisture. Thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters are the only reliable way to map the true boundary of the loss in these structures. Skipping that step and cleaning only the visible area leaves contaminated material in place, which creates both a health risk and a documentation gap if the claim is later audited.
