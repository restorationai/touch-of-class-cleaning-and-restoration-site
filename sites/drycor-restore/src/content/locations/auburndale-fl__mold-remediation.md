## Why Auburndale Properties See Mold Issues

Central Florida's humidity doesn't behave the same way year-round, it concentrates. Auburndale's position between Lake Ariana and the broader chain of lakes that defines this part of Polk County means ambient moisture levels stay elevated even between rain events. Homes built in the 1960s through the 1980s, which make up a significant portion of Auburndale's housing stock, were often constructed with block-and-stucco exteriors that can trap moisture inside wall assemblies rather than letting it dry outward. Older HVAC systems that are undersized or poorly sealed compound the problem: when conditioned air can't keep interior relative humidity below 60%, mold doesn't need a visible leak to grow, it feeds on condensation alone.

Crawl spaces and slab-on-grade foundations each present their own challenges here. Slabs are common in Auburndale's mid-century neighborhoods, and when a supply line or drain sweats or weeps under the slab, the moisture wicks upward through the concrete and into flooring and baseboards before anyone notices a stain. By the time the smell registers, that damp, earthy odor that settles into a room, the colony is typically well-established and not visible from the surface.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Auburndale

Every remediation starts with a thorough assessment, not a guess. The crew identifies moisture sources using thermal imaging and moisture meters before any containment goes up, because cutting out affected drywall without first stopping the water source guarantees the mold returns.

Once the source is confirmed, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment using 6-mil poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, wood framing where necessary, are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces that can be saved are treated and HEPA-vacuumed. The space is then dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture thresholds before any reconstruction begins.

Post-remediation clearance testing, conducted by an independent party, not by the remediation crew, confirms that spore counts have returned to acceptable levels before the area is closed up. This step matters: it's the only objective confirmation that the work is complete.
