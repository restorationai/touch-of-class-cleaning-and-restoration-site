## Why Land O' Lakes Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After a Storm or Fire

The communities spread across this part of Pasco County represent several decades of residential construction, from the older ranch-style homes on larger lots near the lakes to the densely packed planned developments that expanded rapidly through the 2000s and 2010s. That mix matters for board-up and tarping work. Older wood-frame homes often have soffit and fascia that separate more readily in high winds, leaving attic spaces exposed. Newer construction in master-planned communities frequently features tile roofing, which looks intact from the street but can have cracked or displaced tiles that allow water infiltration invisible until drywall is already saturated.

Florida's subtropical climate adds another layer. Once a roof deck or exterior wall is breached, the combination of heat and humidity means interior moisture levels climb fast, mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours in an unventilated space. Securing the opening quickly is not cosmetic; it is the first line of defense against a secondary loss that can dwarf the original damage.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Land O' Lakes

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first questions are about the type and size of the breach, a broken sliding glass door is staged differently than a 400-square-foot roof section missing after a tree strike. That information lets the crew load the right materials before leaving Thonotosassa.

On arrival, the team assesses structural stability before anyone goes onto a roof or into a compromised room. For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps secured with lumber battens screwed into the decking, not bungee cords or sandbags, which fail in the next wind event. Tarps are lapped and sealed at ridges and valleys to redirect water away from the breach rather than pooling at it.

For window and door board-up, we use plywood cut to fit the rough opening and secured with construction screws into the framing, not just the trim. On fire-damaged structures where framing integrity is uncertain, we probe before we fasten. Every opening is documented photographically before and after, which feeds directly into the insurance claim file.
