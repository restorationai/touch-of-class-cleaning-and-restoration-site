## Why Osprey Properties See Water Damage Issues

Sarasota County's wet season runs roughly June through September, and Osprey absorbs the full force of afternoon thunderstorms that can drop two to three inches of rain in under an hour. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Osprey

Every water damage job starts with moisture mapping, not just a visual check. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters let the crew identify exactly where water has traveled behind walls, under flooring, and into insulation cavities before a single piece of equipment is placed. In Osprey's concrete block homes, moisture can track laterally through block cores in ways that are invisible from the surface, so this step is not optional.

Once the extent of saturation is confirmed, water extraction begins using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, followed by the placement of commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers. Drying is monitored daily against psychrometric targets aligned with the IICRC S500 standard, and no equipment is pulled until readings confirm the structure has reached acceptable moisture levels. Documentation of every reading is kept in the project file, which matters when the job involves an insurance claim.

## Local Note

Osprey's older concrete block homes, particularly those built in the 1960s through early 1980s, frequently have terrazzo floors over a concrete slab. Terrazzo is dense and non-absorbent, but the grout lines and any cracks in the slab beneath it can hold water that wicks upward into wall framing.
