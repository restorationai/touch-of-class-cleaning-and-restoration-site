## Why Ruskin Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After Storm or Fire Damage

Ruskin sits low. Much of the area drains toward Tampa Bay and the Little Manatee River, and the water table in this part of South Hillsborough County sits close enough to the surface that even a modest storm can leave standing water in yards within hours. That geography matters for board-up and tarping work because an unsecured roof opening during a rain event here isn't just a roofing problem, it becomes a water intrusion problem almost immediately, and water intrusion at this humidity level can begin supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Ruskin has a mix of older concrete-block construction from the mid-twentieth century, newer stucco-over-frame homes in the communities that grew up along US-41 and Sun City Center Boulevard, and manufactured housing on properties closer to the bay. Concrete-block homes can develop hairline cracks at window and door frames under impact stress, cracks that aren't obvious until you look for them, and that let moisture wick in even after a tarp is in place. Our crews inspect the full envelope, not just the obvious breach.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Ruskin

When you call (813) 829-1091, the first question we ask is the nature of the opening, fire-damaged wall, blown soffit, shattered sliding glass door, or missing roof decking. That shapes what we load before we leave.

On arrival, we assess the structural integrity of the area surrounding the breach before anyone goes on a roof or lifts a sheet of plywood. In Ruskin's storm context, that means checking for saturated decking, compromised fascia, and any live electrical concern, especially relevant in older homes where the service entrance or meter base may be near the damaged area.

For roof tarping, we use 6-mil or heavier polyethylene sheeting secured with wood battens and screwed-down perimeter boards rather than sandbag weighting alone. In Gulf Coast wind conditions, sandbags shift. Mechanical fastening holds. For board-up, we cut OSB or plywood to fit each opening, frame it into the existing rough opening where possible, and fasten it in a way that a window or door contractor can remove cleanly without additional damage to the frame.

We document every opening with photographs before and after, dimensions, materials used, fastener placement, because your insurance adjuster will need that record.
