## Local Note

Many of Wesley Chapel's newer subdivisions were built with spray foam insulation in the attic and closed-cell applications in exterior walls, a genuine energy efficiency upgrade, but one that changes how water behaves after a roof leak or HVAC condensate overflow. Closed-cell foam doesn't absorb moisture the way fiberglass batts do, so water tends to travel along the foam's surface and collect at the lowest point rather than saturating in place. That means the visible wet spot on a ceiling may be several feet away from where the water actually entered, and standard moisture meters calibrated for drywall can give misleading readings against foam-backed assemblies. Knowing to look further and use thermal imaging first is the difference between finding the source and chasing the symptom.
