Salt-laden air, high year-round humidity, and the kind of slow, ground-level moisture that comes with a water table just inches below the surface mean that a slow roof leak or a weeping AC drain pan can seed a mold colony inside wall cavities within days, not weeks. ## Why Nokomis Properties See Mold Problems

Nokomis and the surrounding Sarasota County coastline experience a rainy season that runs roughly June through September, with afternoon storms that can push several inches of rain in a single event. That moisture doesn't just fall on roofs; it saturates the sandy soil, raises the water table, and wicks into slab-on-grade foundations common throughout the area. Older concrete block construction, which makes up a significant portion of the housing stock in coastal Sarasota County, is particularly vulnerable because the block itself is porous. When interior humidity isn't actively managed, condensation forms inside wall assemblies and mold follows.

Air conditioning systems are another recurring factor. In a climate where AC runs nearly twelve months a year, a clogged condensate line or an undersized unit that short-cycles can push indoor relative humidity above 60 percent, the threshold at which most mold species colonize aggressively. Attic spaces are especially problematic in homes with soffit venting that faces the prevailing southwest breeze off the Gulf, because that breeze carries moisture-laden air directly into the attic cavity.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Nokomis

Every project begins with a thorough assessment rather than a visual scan. Mold in coastal construction often hides behind stucco exteriors or inside the cores of concrete block walls, where it reads dry at the surface while the interior stays damp. We use moisture mapping and thermal imaging to locate hidden saturation before containment goes up.

Once we understand the scope, we establish negative-pressure containment using heavy-gauge poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Affected materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Florida Department of Environmental Protection guidelines. After physical removal, we apply an EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to structural surfaces and run HEPA air scrubbers until post-remediation air quality readings confirm the work area meets clearance standards.

Our team holds NAERMC mold certification and the firm is IICRC certified, which matters when your insurance carrier or a prospective buyer's inspector asks for documentation of the remediation method. We are also EPA Lead-Safe certified, which is relevant for any Nokomis home built before 1978 where disturbing painted surfaces is part of the scope.
