## Why Lithia Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Lithia's growth over the past two decades brought large planned communities alongside older rural parcels, and those two housing types carry very different plumbing risks. Newer subdivisions in the area are served by Hillsborough County's public sewer system, but a meaningful share of Lithia properties still rely on private septic systems, particularly on larger lots along Fish Hawk and Boyette Road corridors. Septic tanks that haven't been pumped on schedule, combined with Lithia's periodic heavy rainfall events, are a reliable recipe for septic overflow into yards, garages, and home interiors.

Even homes connected to county sewer are not immune. Root intrusion from the area's mature oak canopy is a consistent cause of sewer line blockages in established neighborhoods. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lithia

Raw sewage removal is not a job for a shop vac and bleach. The IICRC classifies sewage as Category 3 water, meaning it requires containment, full extraction, controlled demolition of saturated porous materials, and EPA-registered disinfectants applied to all affected surfaces before any drying begins.

The process on a Lithia job typically follows this sequence:

1. **Containment and PPE staging**, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces.
2. **Extraction**, truck-mounted or portable extraction units remove standing sewage and saturated material.
3. **Controlled demolition**, flooring, baseboard, and drywall that have absorbed Category 3 water cannot be dried in place; they are removed and properly disposed of.
4. **Disinfection**, EPA-registered antimicrobials are applied to all structural surfaces, not just visible contamination zones.
5. **Drying and monitoring**, commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings confirm the structure has returned to acceptable levels.
6. **Documentation**, moisture logs, photos, and scope notes are compiled for your insurance carrier.

On properties with septic systems, we also note the point of overflow and can coordinate with a licensed septic contractor to address the source before the structure is closed up.
