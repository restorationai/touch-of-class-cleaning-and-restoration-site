## Why Polk City Properties See Mold Issues

The geography here works against homeowners in ways that are easy to underestimate. Polk City is positioned between several lakes and low-lying wetland areas, and the water table in parts of the city rises noticeably during the wet season. Slab foundations common to homes built in the 1980s and 1990s can wick ground moisture upward through concrete, especially where vapor barriers have degraded or were never installed to current standards. That slow, invisible moisture migration feeds mold colonies behind baseboards and inside closets without any obvious leak event to trace it back to.

Older CBS (concrete block structure) homes in the area present a different challenge: block walls absorb and hold humidity, and when air conditioning is cycled off for extended periods, the interior surface temperature drops below the dew point and condensation forms on the block face. Insulation retrofitted into these walls later can trap that moisture rather than allowing it to dry outward. The result is mold that reads dry at the surface while the block and any interior framing remain saturated.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Polk City

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection, not an estimate. We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map where moisture is actually present, because visible mold is rarely the full picture. Containment barriers and negative air pressure are established before any disturbed materials are removed, which prevents spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during work.

Demolition is limited to what is genuinely unsalvageable. Materials that test within acceptable moisture thresholds and show no active growth are dried in place using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the specific volume of the space. Affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Because DRYCOR RESTORE holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, homes built before 1978 receive the additional protocols required when painted surfaces or older building materials are disturbed, which is a real consideration in parts of Polk City with housing stock from that era.

Once remediation is complete, a post-clearance air quality test is conducted by an independent party to confirm spore counts have returned to acceptable levels before containment is removed. We do not self-certify clearance.
