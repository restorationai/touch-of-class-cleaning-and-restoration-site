## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Drycor Restore is committed to making drycor.com usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We believe access to clear, accurate information about restoration services should not depend on a visitor's abilities or the device they're using.

This site is built with the goal of meeting the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA**, the widely recognized benchmark for accessible web content. Those guidelines address things like sufficient color contrast, descriptive link text, image alt attributes, logical heading structure, and keyboard-navigable menus.

## What We've Done to Support Accessible Use

The pages on this site are structured with semantic HTML so that screen readers can interpret headings, lists, and form labels in a meaningful order. Images that convey information include descriptive alt text. Contact forms are labeled so that assistive technologies can identify each field. We avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning, and we aim to keep text legible at standard browser zoom levels without content breaking or overlapping.

We also test across common screen reader and browser combinations during development, though we recognize that no automated process catches everything, which is why we take user-reported issues seriously.

## Known Limitations and Ongoing Work

Accessibility is not a one-time checkbox. Third-party content, such as embedded maps or externally hosted review widgets, may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA, and those elements are outside our direct control. We flag these gaps when we find them and work to replace or supplement inaccessible third-party components where possible.

If you encounter a barrier on any page of this site, we want to know about it.

## Contact Us About an Accessibility Issue

If a page is difficult to read, a form is hard to complete, or any feature of the site is not working as expected with your assistive technology, please reach out directly:

If the fix requires a development change, we will let you know the expected timeline.

## Alternative Access

If any content on this site is inaccessible to you and you need the information it contains, service details, estimate scheduling, or anything else, please call or email us directly. A member of our team can provide that information in an alternative format or help you complete a request by phone.
