## Why Turlock Properties See Water Damage Differently Than Coastal Cities

The San Joaquin Valley's climate creates a deceptive damage pattern. Low ambient humidity in summer means surface materials dry quickly, which can make moisture readings at the wall face look acceptable while the framing cavity behind stays saturated for weeks. That hidden saturation is where mold colonization begins, typically within 24 to 48 hours of initial wetting. Turlock's housing stock adds another layer of complexity: the city grew rapidly through the 1970s and 1980s, and homes from that era frequently used galvanized steel supply lines that are now at or past their service life. A slow pinhole leak in galvanized pipe can go undetected for months, silently wicking into the bottom plates of interior walls. Older commercial properties near downtown and the agricultural processing corridors carry similar risks, with cast-iron drain lines that corrode from the inside out.

The region's clay soils also matter. Expansive clay contracts sharply during dry spells and swells after winter rains, and that movement can open gaps at pipe penetrations through concrete slabs. Slab leaks in Turlock are not rare, and they present differently than above-grade failures: the first sign is often a warm spot on a tile floor or an unexplained spike on a Turlock Irrigation District water bill rather than visible pooling.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Turlock

Every job begins with a moisture survey before any material is moved or removed. We use thermal imaging alongside calibrated moisture meters to map how far water has traveled, because water follows framing cavities and subfloor channels in directions that are not always intuitive. That survey drives the scope: we do not pull drywall that is genuinely dry, and we do not leave drywall that reads wet just because it looks intact.

Once the affected area is defined, extraction and structural drying run simultaneously. High-capacity desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers are sized to the cubic footage of the space, not to a one-size number. Air movers are positioned to create a directed airflow pattern through wall cavities rather than just across surfaces. Drying logs are recorded daily and shared with your insurance adjuster as documentation.

After materials reach target moisture content, the rebuild phase begins. This is where TDI USA's 40-plus years of construction experience matters: we do not subcontract the finish work to a separate crew. Drywall, insulation, texture matching, painting, and flooring replacement are handled under the same project, which compresses the timeline and keeps accountability in one place.
