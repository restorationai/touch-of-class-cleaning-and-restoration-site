## Local Note

One pattern we see consistently in Citrus Heights homes built before 1975: the original bathroom tile was often set directly over a mud-bed mortar layer rather than cement board or modern backer. When that mortar bed has absorbed decades of moisture, it can weigh significantly more than a standard tile substrate and may have developed soft spots that aren't visible until demolition begins. Homeowners planning a bathroom remodel should budget for the possibility of full substrate replacement rather than a tile-over approach — in this housing stock, the mud bed rarely passes inspection for a tile-over permit, and trying to work around it usually costs more in the long run than replacing it cleanly from the start.
