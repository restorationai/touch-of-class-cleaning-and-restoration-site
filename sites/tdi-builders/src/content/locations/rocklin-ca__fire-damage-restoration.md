## Why Rocklin Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Deep Fire Damage

Rocklin's residential growth has happened in distinct waves. Older neighborhoods closer to the historic downtown core tend to feature wood-frame construction with original plaster walls and attic spaces that lack modern fire-blocking. Newer master-planned communities built during the 1990s and 2000s boom — many of them HOA-governed — often use open-truss roof systems and engineered lumber that, while code-compliant at the time of construction, can behave unpredictably under heat stress. Both building types present their own challenges after a fire.

The region's climate adds another layer. Rocklin's hot, dry summers mean interior humidity is often very low when a fire occurs, which causes porous materials like drywall, wood framing, and insulation to absorb smoke particles more readily than they would in a more humid environment. That deep absorption is part of why post-fire odor in Rocklin homes can persist long after surfaces appear clean.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Rocklin

Every fire loss starts with a documented scope — not an estimate based on visible damage, but a systematic inspection that includes the attic, crawl space, wall cavities, and HVAC distribution system. Smoke and soot follow air pressure, not fire paths, so the affected area is almost always larger than what's immediately visible.

From there, the process moves through several concrete phases:

- **Emergency board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry — critical in Rocklin where afternoon winds can push rain or debris into an open structure.
- **Structural assessment** to identify load-bearing members, roof decking, and framing that require replacement versus cleaning and sealing.
- **Soot and smoke residue removal** using dry chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and wet cleaning methods matched to the surface type. Plaster, drywall, wood, and masonry each require a different approach.
- **Deodorization** using thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to neutralize odor molecules that have penetrated porous materials — not just surface masking.
- **Reconstruction** handled in-house, which matters when you're dealing with Placer County permit requirements and local building inspections.

Because TDI Builders operates as both a restoration and a general contracting firm, the handoff between mitigation and rebuild doesn't require a second contractor. That continuity shortens the overall timeline and keeps documentation consistent for insurance purposes.
