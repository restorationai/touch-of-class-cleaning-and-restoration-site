## Why Loomis Properties See Water Damage Differently

Loomis is a small, largely residential community with a housing stock that spans several decades — ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and 1970s sit alongside newer construction on larger parcels. The older homes in particular were built before modern moisture barriers and vapor retarders were standard practice, which means water that finds its way into a crawl space or wall cavity has more pathways to travel and more porous materials to saturate. Plumbing in homes from that era often includes galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out; a pinhole leak can drip undetected for months before the damage surfaces at a baseboard or ceiling.

The foothill climate adds another layer of complexity. Loomis sits at an elevation where winter storms can be intense but temperatures rarely drop far enough to freeze pipes for extended periods — yet a sharp overnight freeze following a warm spell is exactly the condition that causes supply lines near exterior walls or in uninsulated crawl spaces to split. Spring thaw can compound the problem when saturated soil presses against foundation walls.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Loomis

Water cleanup is a sequence, not a single event. When a crew arrives at a Loomis property, the first step is moisture mapping — using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. Water behind plaster or beneath hardwood flooring can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated. Skipping this step leads to incomplete drying and, within 24 to 48 hours of a loss, conditions that allow mold to begin colonizing.

Once the scope is established, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Affected materials are assessed: salvageable hardwood may be dried in place with desiccant dehumidifiers and floor-drying mats; compromised drywall or insulation is removed to allow air movement to reach the framing. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the square footage and material types present. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches its dry standard, documented per the IICRC S500 framework. IICRC-certified technicians manage the process from extraction through final clearance.

## Loomis Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water losses — a burst pipe, an appliance supply line failure, or storm-driven intrusion through a compromised roof or foundation opening. What carriers scrutinize is whether the damage was gradual and therefore preventable. TDI Builders documents the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials, moisture readings at each measurement point, and a written scope of work. That documentation package is submitted directly to your adjuster, which keeps the claim moving and reduces the back-and-forth that delays mitigation approvals.
