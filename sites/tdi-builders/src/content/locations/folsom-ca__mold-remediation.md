## Why Folsom Properties See Mold Issues

Folsom sits at the edge of the Sierra Nevada foothills, where the transition from valley floor to higher elevation creates microclimates that shift quickly between dry heat and concentrated winter rainfall. That rainfall does not always drain away cleanly. Older neighborhoods developed before modern drainage codes were tightened often have grading that directs runoff toward foundations rather than away from them. When water finds its way into a crawl space or behind stucco cladding — common in Folsom's ranch-style and split-level homes built during the 1970s and 1980s — the conditions for mold colonization can develop within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.

Folsom Lake's proximity also matters. Properties closer to the lake corridor experience higher ambient humidity during certain seasons, and homes with evaporative coolers — still common in the area — can introduce additional moisture into attic spaces if ductwork seals have degraded. What looks like a simple HVAC maintenance issue can, over time, become a mold problem embedded in insulation and roof sheathing.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Folsom

Every remediation project starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate saturation that does not show at the surface — because in Folsom's stucco-clad homes, the exterior wall can read bone-dry while the wood framing behind it is still holding significant moisture content. Once the scope is clear, we establish containment barriers and negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Affected materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sections — are removed, bagged, and disposed of in compliance with California Department of Public Health guidelines. Structural framing that can be retained is treated and dried to documented moisture levels. Because TDI Builders is a licensed and insured general contractor with over 40 years of construction and rebuilding experience, we do not hand the project off after remediation: we rebuild the affected areas ourselves, from framing and insulation through drywall, texture, and paint. One contractor, one timeline, one point of contact.

## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing in Folsom: many homes built during the rapid expansion of the 1980s and 1990s used OSB (oriented strand board) sheathing rather than plywood. OSB is more moisture-sensitive than plywood — it swells, delaminates, and holds water longer once it becomes saturated. In a remediation context, this means that sheathing which appears structurally intact at first inspection may need replacement once moisture readings are taken at depth. Homeowners who have had a slow roof leak or a plumbing drip inside a wall for any length of time should expect that sheathing assessment to be part of the scope, not an afterthought.
