## Why Folsom Properties See Mold Issues

Folsom sits at the edge of the Sierra Nevada foothills, where clay-heavy soils in many residential areas slow drainage and hold ground moisture against foundations longer than homeowners expect. Homes built during the rapid growth periods of the 1990s and early 2000s — the subdivisions that filled in around the American River corridor and the newer master-planned communities — often feature stucco exteriors over wood-frame construction. When that stucco develops even hairline cracks, winter rain drives moisture into the wall assembly. The interior stays warm and the cavity stays damp, which is the exact combination mold needs to colonize within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.

Older homes closer to Historic Folsom and the original downtown core present a different challenge: some date to the Gold Rush era or early twentieth century and may have original plaster walls, pier-and-beam foundations with limited ventilation, and plumbing that has been patched and re-routed multiple times. Leaks in those systems often go undetected for weeks because the building materials absorb moisture slowly and show staining only after saturation is well advanced.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Folsom

Remediation starts with containment — not cleaning. Before any physical removal begins, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms. This step matters especially in Folsom's open-plan homes, where HVAC systems can distribute airborne spores through the entire house if work begins without proper containment in place.

From there, the process moves through moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters, controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, HEPA-filtered air scrubbing, and treatment of structural surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Final clearance is confirmed through post-remediation verification — air and surface sampling that documents the space is within acceptable spore-count thresholds before reconstruction begins. Because TDI Builders is both a licensed remediation contractor and a full construction firm, the rebuild phase can follow directly without the homeowner coordinating a second contractor.

## Local Note

Folsom properties built in the 1990s boom years frequently used OSB (oriented strand board) sheathing behind stucco rather than plywood. OSB is more sensitive to repeated wetting cycles — it swells, delaminates, and holds mold more aggressively than plywood once moisture intrudes. When we open a wall cavity in these homes and find compromised OSB, the remediation scope typically expands beyond what a surface inspection suggested. Homeowners in these subdivisions should budget for the possibility that what looks like a contained mold patch on drywall involves sheathing replacement behind it. We identify this during the assessment phase, before any work is authorized, so there are no mid-project surprises.
