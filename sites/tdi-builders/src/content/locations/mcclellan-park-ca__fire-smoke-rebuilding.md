## Why McClellan Park Properties See Fire and Smoke Damage

Sacramento Valley summers run hot and dry for months at a stretch, and that stretch overlaps with regional wildfire season. Smoke from fires burning well outside city limits still settles into attics, duct systems, and wall cavities on calm, hazy days, leaving a resinous residue on framing and insulation long before anyone smells it indoors. Add in McClellan Park's older industrial and warehouse-style construction alongside newer residential buildouts, and you get two very different substrates, metal, masonry, and drywall, that each hold smoke odor and soot differently and need separate treatment approaches.

## Our Fire and Smoke Damage Rebuilding Process in McClellan Park

We start with a structural assessment to separate what can be cleaned and restored from what has to be demolished and rebuilt, since charred framing and compromised roof decking are common after any fire that burned long enough to need suppression. From there we document everything for the claim, remove damaged materials, and treat remaining structure and contents for smoke odor before any new framing, drywall, or finish work goes in. On commercial and industrial buildings in the business park, that often means coordinating with building management on access and occupancy timelines in addition to the rebuild itself, which residential-only crews aren't set up to handle.

## McClellan Park Insurance & Permitting Coordination

Fire rebuilds almost always require permits through Sacramento County, and the scope of those permits depends heavily on whether the structure is residential, commercial, or industrial, each with different inspection triggers. We handle the permit submissions and inspection scheduling as part of the rebuild so the property owner isn't managing that process on top of a displaced household or an idled business. On the insurance side, we itemize the damage by material and trade so the adjuster has a clear scope to work from, which tends to move the claim faster than a generic repair estimate.

## Local note

Industrial and warehouse buildings in and around McClellan Park often run centralized HVAC systems that serve multiple units or tenants, which means smoke from a fire in one bay can travel through shared ductwork into spaces that never saw flame. On those jobs we push for duct inspection and cleaning as part of the scope even when the fire itself was contained to one area, because skipping that step is how odor complaints show up weeks after a rebuild is signed off.
