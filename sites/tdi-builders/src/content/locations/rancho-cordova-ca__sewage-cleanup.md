## Why Rancho Cordova Properties Experience Sewage Backups

The Sacramento region's wet winters and baked-dry summers put unusual stress on underground sewer laterals. Clay soils expand when saturated and contract in the heat, and that movement works on older clay-tile or cast-iron pipe joints over decades. Many of the residential neighborhoods in Rancho Cordova developed rapidly during the postwar boom, and the original sewer laterals in those areas are now well past their typical service life. Root intrusion from mature trees — common in established residential corridors — compounds the problem, gradually choking lines until a heavy rain event or a grease buildup tips a partial blockage into a full backup.

Septic systems add another layer of risk. Rancho Cordova and its surrounding unincorporated pockets include properties that were developed before full municipal sewer connections were extended, and some of those lots still rely on aging septic infrastructure. When a drain field fails or a tank overflows, the contamination can saturate soil and reach interior spaces quickly, especially in homes with slab-on-grade construction where there is no crawl space buffer.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Rancho Cordova

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most hazardous category under the IICRC S500 standard — which means the cleanup protocol is more involved than a standard water loss. The process begins with containment: affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, and the crew establishes negative air pressure where necessary. All porous materials that have absorbed sewage — drywall, insulation, subfloor sheathing, carpet — are removed and bagged for proper disposal rather than dried in place. Attempting to dry contaminated porous materials is not a safe shortcut.

Once the contaminated materials are out, every surface in the affected area is treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. In Rancho Cordova's warmer months, this step is particularly critical: residual organic matter in a hot, enclosed space can support rapid microbial growth even after visible contamination is removed. After sanitization, the space is dried to IICRC standards using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers before any reconstruction begins. Documentation of moisture readings, material removal, and treatment is compiled throughout — both for your records and for insurance purposes.

## Local Note

Rancho Cordova's older residential areas include a significant number of homes built on concrete slab foundations with minimal under-floor access. When a sewer line backs up through a floor drain or toilet in a slab-on-grade home, contaminated water has nowhere to go but across the surface — and it migrates under baseboards and into wall cavities faster than it would in a home with a raised foundation. Crews working these properties scope the wall cavities with moisture meters before assuming the damage is contained to the visible floor area, because in slab construction, the visible wet zone is almost always smaller than the actual affected zone.
