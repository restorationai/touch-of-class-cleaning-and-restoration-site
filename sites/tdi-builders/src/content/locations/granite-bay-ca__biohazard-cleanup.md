## Why Granite Bay Properties Face Distinct Biohazard Considerations

Granite Bay's housing stock skews toward larger square footage, finished basements, detached garages, and guest structures — all of which can complicate containment and cleanup. Older ranch homes built in the 1970s and early 1980s, common throughout the unincorporated Placer County portions of Granite Bay, often have original HVAC ductwork that can distribute airborne particulates quickly if a scene is not properly contained from the start. The region's warm, dry summers and mild but damp winters also mean that biological material left unaddressed — even briefly — can begin to create secondary concerns in crawl spaces or under flooring where moisture from winter rains lingers longer than it appears to at the surface.

The area's semi-rural character also means properties are sometimes discovered after a delay, which changes the scope of work. Porous materials — hardwood subfloors, concrete slabs, drywall — may require more aggressive treatment when exposure time has extended beyond the first 24 hours.

## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Granite Bay

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. Vehicles are unmarked, and the crew communicates directly with whoever is coordinating access — a family member, property manager, or estate representative — without drawing attention to the property. Containment barriers go up before any work begins, isolating the affected area from the rest of the structure.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Assessment and documentation** — affected surfaces and materials are identified and photographed for insurance purposes before anything is disturbed.
- **Removal of biohazardous material** — blood, bodily fluids, sharps, infectious material, and contaminated soft goods are collected and packaged according to California Department of Public Health and Cal/OSHA handling requirements.
- **Surface treatment** — EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces, including subfloor and wall cavity areas where penetration may have occurred.
- **Air quality control** — HEPA filtration units run throughout the process to capture airborne particulates.
- **Clearance verification** — the area is tested before containment is removed, not after.

TDI Builders holds IICRC-certified technicians on staff, and the company is licensed and insured for this category of work in California.
