## Why Loomis Properties Present Unique Biohazard Cleanup Considerations

Loomis is not a dense urban grid. That variety matters in biohazard remediation because access, containment, and disposal logistics all change depending on the structure type.

The foothill climate adds another layer. Loomis experiences hot, dry summers and cool, wet winters. In warmer months, biological material degrades rapidly, which compresses the window for safe remediation and makes a prompt response critical. In older homes — many built in the mid-twentieth century with wood-framed construction, original subflooring, and minimal vapor barriers — porous materials can absorb and retain contamination in ways that are not visible at the surface. A thorough remediation here means going beyond what the eye can see.

## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Loomis

Every job begins with a protected assessment. Technicians arrive in personal protective equipment, evaluate the full scope of contamination, and establish containment before any removal begins. The goal is to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the property — a step that matters especially in homes with open floor plans or shared HVAC systems.

Contaminated materials are removed, packaged, and transported in compliance with California Department of Public Health regulations governing biohazardous waste. This is not a process where general contractors or cleaning services can fill in — it requires trained technicians, regulated disposal channels, and documentation. TDI Builders holds the certifications and licensure to handle this work correctly.

After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants and verified clean using ATP testing or other appropriate methods before containment is lifted. The property is left in a condition that is safe for re-occupancy, and documentation is provided for insurance or legal purposes if needed.
