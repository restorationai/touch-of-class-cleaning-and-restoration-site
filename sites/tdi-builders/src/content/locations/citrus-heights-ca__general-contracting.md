## Why Citrus Heights Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Much of the residential building stock in Citrus Heights was constructed in the 1960s through the 1980s, a period when tract development moved fast and material choices reflected the era. A thorough pre-renovation assessment of the existing structure isn't optional here — it's the difference between a remodel that holds and one that comes back to haunt you.
