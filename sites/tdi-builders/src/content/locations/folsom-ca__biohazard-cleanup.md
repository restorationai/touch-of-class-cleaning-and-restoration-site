## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Folsom

Every situation is different, but the professional framework is consistent. Our IICRC-certified technicians arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested, assess the affected area, and establish a containment perimeter before any remediation begins. Personal protective equipment, regulated waste containers, and EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants are standard on every job — not optional upgrades.

After physical remediation, affected surfaces are treated and tested to confirm pathogen levels meet safe thresholds. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated — sections of subfloor, drywall, carpet padding — are removed, packaged as regulated biological waste, and transported to a licensed disposal facility in compliance with California Department of Public Health guidelines. We document every step, which matters significantly when an insurance claim or a property transfer is involved.

For properties in Historic Folsom with original plaster or older wood framing, we adjust our approach: penetrating disinfectants and extended dwell times are often necessary because these materials hold biological contamination deeper than modern composites do.
