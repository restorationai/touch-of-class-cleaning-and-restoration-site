Roseville sits at the edge of the Sacramento Valley where summer temperatures regularly climb past 100°F, meaning air conditioning runs almost continuously from late May through September. ## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Roseville

The process begins with a camera inspection of the main trunk lines and a representative sample of branch runs. This step matters in Roseville homes because attic-routed flex duct can develop kinks or partial collapses that trap debris and reduce airflow — problems that aren't visible from the register opening. Once the system is mapped, the crew connects a high-powered negative-air vacuum to the main trunk, creating negative pressure throughout the network. Technicians then work register by register with rotary brush agitation tools, driving loosened debris toward the collection unit rather than back into the living space.

For systems showing microbial growth — common after a roof leak or in homes where the air handler sits in a humid garage — an EPA-registered duct sanitizer is applied as a final step. Air duct sanitization is not a substitute for addressing the moisture source, and the team will flag any active moisture conditions they observe during the inspection. The work is performed by IICRC certified technicians, and TDI Builders is licensed and insured.
