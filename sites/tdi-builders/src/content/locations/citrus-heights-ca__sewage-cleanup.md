## Why Citrus Heights Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The Sacramento Valley's clay-heavy soils are notoriously unforgiving to underground pipe systems. Clay expands when wet and contracts when dry, and in Citrus Heights that cycle happens hard — wet winters followed by long, dry summers. Over time, that movement cracks older vitrified clay sewer laterals, allows root intrusion from the mature trees lining many residential streets, and causes low spots where solids accumulate. Many homes in Citrus Heights were built in the 1950s through 1970s, when cast iron and clay were standard for drain lines. Those materials are now well past their expected service life, and a single heavy rain event or grease buildup can be enough to trigger a full backup into a bathroom, laundry room, or garage.

City sewer mains in Citrus Heights are maintained by Sacramento Area Sewer District (SASD), and when a main-line surcharge causes sewage to reverse into private laterals, the cleanup responsibility falls entirely on the homeowner — not the district. That distinction matters when you're standing in an inch of contaminated water at midnight trying to figure out who to call.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Citrus Heights

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification under the IICRC S500 standard — and it requires a structured response, not just a shop vac and bleach.

When our crew arrives, the first priority is stopping the source and establishing a containment perimeter to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected areas of the home. We extract standing water and solid waste using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then remove any porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — that have absorbed sewage and cannot be effectively sanitized. In the slab-on-grade homes common throughout Citrus Heights, that often means cutting back flooring to inspect for moisture migration beneath the concrete surface.

After extraction and demo, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to all affected surfaces, including framing, concrete, and HVAC components if sewage aerosols have reached ductwork. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings in structural materials return to pre-loss baselines. We document every reading and photograph every affected material for your insurance carrier.
