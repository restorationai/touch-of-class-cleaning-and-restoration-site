## Why Carmichael Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Storm Damage

Much of Carmichael's housing stock was built between the late 1940s and the early 1970s, an era when roof sheathing was often 1×6 skip sheathing rather than continuous plywood panels. That construction detail matters when a wind gust peels back a few shingles: water doesn't just wet the underlayment, it finds gaps between boards and migrates into the attic cavity before the storm has even passed. Combine that with the large valley oaks and sycamores that line many of Carmichael's older streets, and you have a recipe for puncture damage, gutter collapse, and debris-loaded downspouts that back up and overflow against siding and foundations.

The Sacramento Valley also experiences a pattern of strong Diablo-wind-adjacent events in fall, when dry, gusty northeast winds follow the end of fire season. These events are shorter than a full Pacific storm but can strip ridge caps and lift flashing on homes that weathered the wet season without issue. Siding damage, particularly on homes with original T1-11 or lap wood siding, is common after these wind events, and that damage is easy to underestimate from the ground.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Carmichael

When we arrive at a Carmichael property after a storm, the first priority is an honest damage assessment, not a quick tarp-and-invoice visit. We document the roof, siding, gutters, soffits, windows, and any structural framing that took a direct impact. Photos and measurements go into a written scope before any repair work begins, which matters both for your records and for the insurance claim process.

From there, emergency stabilization comes first: tarping compromised roof sections, boarding broken windows, and securing any loose siding panels that could cause further water intrusion. Once the property is stabilized, we move into the rebuild phase, which for a Carmichael home often means sourcing materials that match decades-old profiles. Matching existing wood lap siding, replacing skip-sheathed roof sections with code-compliant plywood decking, and blending new roofing into aged surrounding material all require experience with older residential construction, not just the ability to install new products.

Because we are licensed, insured, and handle both residential and commercial work, we can take a storm-damaged property from emergency board-up through permitted reconstruction without handing the job to a second contractor mid-stream.

Storm claims in Sacramento County tend to move faster when the documentation package is complete from the start.
