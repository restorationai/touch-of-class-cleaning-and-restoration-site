## Why Rancho Cordova's Building Stock Shapes Every Remodel

The older residential pockets of Rancho Cordova carry the hallmarks of mid-century California construction: slab-on-grade foundations, single-pane aluminum windows, galvanized or early copper plumbing, and wall framing that predates modern shear-wall requirements. When you open up a wall in a home from that era, you may find knob-and-tube wiring that hasn't been touched since Eisenhower was president, or insulation that was never installed at all. None of that is a dealbreaker, but it does mean your general contractor needs to scope the work honestly before the first demo cut is made, not after.

California's Title 24 energy standards also add a layer of planning that out-of-state homeowners sometimes don't anticipate. Window replacements, HVAC upgrades, and additions all trigger compliance reviews. Rancho Cordova falls under Sacramento County's permitting jurisdiction for most residential work, so pulling the right permits, and scheduling inspections correctly, is part of the job, not an afterthought.

Rancho Cordova's clay-heavy soils, common throughout the eastern Sacramento Valley, expand and contract significantly with seasonal moisture swings.
