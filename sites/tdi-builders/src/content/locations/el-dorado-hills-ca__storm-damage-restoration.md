## Why El Dorado Hills Properties See Distinctive Storm Damage

El Dorado Hills is not a flat valley community. The rolling terrain channels wind differently than Sacramento proper, accelerating gusts through ridge-line corridors and creating localized uplift that can strip ridge caps and fascia boards while a neighbor two streets over sees nothing. The community also spans a wide range of construction eras, from early tract homes built in the 1970s and 1980s to large custom builds from the 2000s and newer planned developments, and each generation of construction carries its own vulnerability profile.

Older homes in the area often used composition shingles that are now past their rated lifespan, meaning a single storm event can expose underlayment or decking that was already marginal. Newer tile-roof homes are durable under normal conditions but can suffer cracked or displaced tiles when large oak branches, common on foothill lots, come down during high-wind events. Either way, what looks like a surface problem from the ground frequently involves water infiltration that has already begun working into roof sheathing, attic insulation, or wall cavities by the time the storm clears.

## El Dorado Hills Insurance and HOA Coordination

Many of the planned communities in El Dorado Hills are governed by HOAs with architectural guidelines that specify approved roofing materials, siding colors, and fence styles. After a storm, replacing damaged components with non-conforming materials, even temporarily, can trigger HOA notices that complicate an already stressful situation. We review CC&Rs before ordering materials so that replacement selections stay within approved specifications, which also tends to smooth the insurance approval process since the carrier and the HOA are aligned on scope.

We document losses in the format most major carriers expect, which reduces back-and-forth and helps keep the claim moving. We do not bill on your behalf or act as a public adjuster, but we work alongside your adjuster and can meet on-site to walk the damage together.
