Arden-Arcade's commercial corridors are a mix of decades-old strip centers, converted office buildings, and small professional parks built during Sacramento's early suburban expansion. A tenant improvement here rarely means a blank slate: it usually means working around a building shell that predates current energy and accessibility codes while a new tenant wants to open on a tight timeline. TDI Builders handles both the construction and the code reconciliation so the buildout doesn't stall in plan check.

## Why Arden-Arcade Commercial Buildings Need Renovation Support

Much of the commercial stock in this part of Sacramento County went up between the 1960s and 1980s, when energy codes, ADA requirements, and modern fire-life-safety standards looked nothing like they do now. A retail space or office suite that's changed hands two or three times since often has layered-over electrical, undersized HVAC for today's occupancy loads, and restroom configurations that need upgrading before a certificate of occupancy will be issued for a new tenant. Add in Sacramento's long, hot summer stretch, which puts real strain on older rooftop units, and a tenant improvement project frequently turns into a mechanical and electrical upgrade as much as a cosmetic one.

## Our Commercial Construction and Tenant Improvements Process in Arden-Arcade

We start with a walkthrough of the existing shell against the new tenant's intended use, because occupancy classification changes (retail to medical, office to restaurant) trigger different code requirements before a single wall gets framed. From there we handle demolition, MEP rough-in, framing, and finishes as one coordinated build, with our own crews covering the trades that most affect schedule rather than subcontracting the whole job out and losing control of sequencing. Because much of Arden-Arcade is unincorporated, plan review and inspections run through Sacramento County rather than a city building department, and we factor that review cycle into the schedule we give tenants and landlords up front.

## Permitting Through Sacramento County

Unincorporated status changes the permitting path in ways a lot of out-of-area contractors miss. County plan check timelines, inspector availability, and submittal requirements don't always mirror what a tenant might expect from working with the City of Sacramento next door, and a project that assumes city process can lose weeks sorting that out mid-build. We manage the submittal package, respond to plan check comments, and schedule county inspections as part of the build, so the landlord and tenant aren't the ones chasing paperwork.

## Local note

One detail that catches tenants off guard: older Arden-Arcade commercial buildings on well or septic-era infrastructure from before full municipal sewer buildout sometimes still have undersized water lines feeding the suite, which only shows up once a new restaurant or medical tenant's fixture count is calculated. We check existing utility capacity early in the scope review specifically because retrofitting that mid-construction is far more disruptive than catching it during design.
