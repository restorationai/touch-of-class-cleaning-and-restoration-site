## Why Rancho Cordova Properties See Water Damage Issues

The city's development history plays a direct role in how water damage behaves here. Much of the residential stock east of Mather Field Road was built during the postwar aerospace boom of the 1950s and 1960s, when galvanized steel supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks were standard. Those materials are well past their design life. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing over decades until a pressure spike, a water hammer, a frozen-and-thawed section, a faulty pressure-reducing valve, causes a failure that can release hundreds of gallons before anyone notices.

On the commercial side, Rancho Cordova's Sunrise Boulevard and Gold Center Drive corridors are lined with flex-space and light-industrial buildings constructed in the 1980s and 1990s. Many use flat or low-slope roofs with interior drains. When those drains clog with debris during a November atmospheric river event, ponding water finds the path of least resistance, usually through aging roof membrane seams and into drop-ceiling cavities above occupied tenant spaces. The damage is often invisible until ceiling tiles collapse or a musty odor signals that the cavity has been wet for days.

The region's clay-heavy soils also contribute. Heavy rain saturates the ground faster than it can drain, and hydrostatic pressure against foundation walls or under slabs can push water into crawl spaces and below-grade areas even in buildings that have never had a plumbing failure.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rancho Cordova

Every project starts with moisture mapping, not a visual walk-through, but systematic readings with thermal imaging cameras and calibrated pin and pinless meters across every affected surface. Water behind plaster or stucco can read dry at the face while the substrate stays saturated; equipment doesn't lie the way a visual inspection can.

Once the full moisture boundary is documented, we contain the affected area, extract standing water, and place commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the square footage and material types involved. Drying targets follow IICRC S500 standards for structural drying, we're not pulling equipment because a wall feels dry to the touch; we're pulling it when the meter readings confirm equilibrium with the surrounding unaffected materials.

After drying is verified and any necessary remediation is complete, TDI Builders handles the rebuild in-house: framing, insulation, drywall, texture matching, painting, flooring, and finish carpentry. For commercial clients, that single-contractor model matters, it compresses the timeline between remediation sign-off and your tenants returning to the space.

## Local Note

Rancho Cordova's postwar residential neighborhoods often have original hardwood floors installed over a concrete slab rather than a subfloor, a construction method common in Sacramento Valley tract homes of that era. When a water loss saturates a slab-on-grade assembly, the hardwood cups and buckles quickly, but the slab itself retains moisture for weeks. Aggressive surface drying without addressing the slab moisture will cause a reinstalled floor to fail within months. We test slab moisture content with a calcium chloride or in-situ RH probe before any flooring reinstallation is scheduled, not as an upsell, but because skipping that step means doing the floor twice.
