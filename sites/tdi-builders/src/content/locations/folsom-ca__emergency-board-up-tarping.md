## Why Folsom Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Folsom sits at the edge of the Sierra Nevada foothills, and that geography shapes how damage happens here. The region's "Diablo wind" pattern — dry, fast-moving air that can gust through the foothills with little warning — is capable of lifting poorly secured roofing materials and driving embers during wildfire season. The Folsom area has seen its share of fire-related evacuations and structure damage tied to these conditions, and a structure left open after even a partial fire loss can deteriorate rapidly in triple-digit summer heat.

The city also has a wide range of housing stock. Newer master-planned communities sit alongside older ranch-style homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, and each presents different board-up challenges. Older wood-frame homes often have non-standard window openings and aging fascia that requires careful anchoring to avoid causing additional damage during the board-up process. Newer construction may have stucco exteriors or aluminum-framed windows where attachment points are less forgiving. Knowing the difference matters when you're working fast under pressure.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Folsom

When you call, the first thing we do is ask the right questions: What type of opening needs to be secured? Is there active fire or water intrusion? Are utilities still live? That intake shapes how the crew arrives staged and equipped.

On-site, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

1. **Hazard assessment** — We walk the perimeter before touching anything, checking for structural instability, downed lines, or active smoke.
2. **Measurement and material selection** — Openings are measured precisely. We use 5/8" OSB for most window and door board-ups, with framing anchored into structural members rather than just trim, so panels hold against wind load.
3. **Roof tarping** — Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps (typically 6-mil or heavier) are secured with battens and screwed into the decking, not just draped and weighted. In Folsom's wind conditions, a tarp that isn't mechanically fastened is a liability, not a solution.
4. **Documentation** — Every opening is photographed before and after securing. This documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster and speeds the claims process.
5. **Handoff** — We walk you through what was done, what's still at risk, and what the next steps in the restoration process look like.
