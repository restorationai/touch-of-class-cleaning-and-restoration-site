Arden-Arcade's mid-century housing stock, ranch-style homes built through the 1950s and 60s on quarter-acre lots, creates a different remodeling conversation than you'd have in a newer Sacramento subdivision. Original single-pane windows, galley kitchens sized for a smaller family, and additions bolted onto houses over decades mean a whole home remodel here usually starts with figuring out what's actually load-bearing before anyone talks finishes or fixtures.

## Why Arden-Arcade Homes Need a Different Remodeling Approach

Because Arden-Arcade is an unincorporated community governed by Sacramento County rather than a city planning department, permitting for structural changes, additions, and major electrical or plumbing work runs through the county. That matters for timeline: county plan review and inspection scheduling can move at a different pace than a municipal office, and a remodeling contractor who hasn't pulled permits in the county before can lose weeks guessing at requirements. The housing stock itself adds another layer. Many of these homes were built with galvanized plumbing and 100-amp electrical panels that were adequate in 1958 but undersized for a modern kitchen remodel with a double oven and an induction cooktop. We budget time up front to assess what's behind the walls rather than assuming code-era parity with newer construction.

## Our Home Remodeling Process in Arden-Arcade

We start with an on-site walkthrough that looks past the cosmetic scope: foundation type (many of these homes sit on raised perimeter foundations with crawlspaces, not slabs), existing electrical service, and whether prior additions were permitted or done without inspection. From there we scope the structural and mechanical work first, since that's what drives the county permit timeline, and sequence finish work, cabinetry, flooring, and fixtures around it. For whole home remodels we phase room-by-room where the floor plan allows, so a family isn't displaced for the entire project. Interior remodeling on a single kitchen or bathroom moves faster since it avoids the structural permitting that a room addition or load-bearing wall removal requires.

## Reaching Arden-Arcade from Our Sacramento Base

Arden-Arcade sits just northeast of downtown Sacramento, generally accessible via Watt Avenue or Fair Oaks Boulevard depending on which part of the community you're in. Being this close to our base means site visits for estimates, material deliveries, and inspector coordination don't require the lead time a more distant project would. We schedule crews and subcontractors with that proximity in mind, which keeps a multi-week remodel moving instead of stalling between trade visits.

## Local note

One detail that catches homeowners off guard: a lot of Arden-Arcade ranch homes have had a room or sunroom added on at some point without county permits pulled, often decades ago. When we open walls for a remodel and find that kind of undocumented addition, it has to get addressed as part of the current permit application rather than left alone, which can add a step to the process. We flag this during the initial walkthrough so it's priced and scheduled up front instead of surfacing as a surprise mid-project.
