## Why Lincoln Properties See Mold Issues

Lincoln's growth over the past two decades has been significant, and with it has come a wide range of construction vintages sitting side by side. Older homes — many built with minimal vapor barriers in the crawl space — absorb ground moisture differently than the newer tract developments that have expanded toward the foothills. When winter rains saturate the clay-heavy soils common across Placer County, that moisture migrates upward. Slab foundations in newer subdivisions can develop condensation issues when interior air conditioning runs against warm exterior walls in late spring, and that trapped moisture is exactly what mold spores need to colonize.

Beyond construction differences, Lincoln's proximity to seasonal creek corridors means that drainage events — even minor ones — can push water under homes or into garages. Mold doesn't wait for a dramatic flood. A slow roof leak after a series of winter storms, or a plumbing supply line that weeps behind a cabinet for two weeks, can produce visible growth within days and hidden colonization for months.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lincoln

Every remediation starts with a thorough assessment — not a visual scan, but a moisture-mapping inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate saturation behind surfaces that look dry. Mold often reads clean at the drywall face while the framing behind it is still holding moisture from a leak that happened weeks ago.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is physically contained using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet backing — that test positive for active growth are removed and bagged for disposal per California guidelines. Non-porous structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated, and dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

Because TDI Builders, Inc. is a full construction and restoration firm — not just a remediation-only crew — the same company that removes the damaged material can handle the rebuild. That matters when you're coordinating with an insurance adjuster and need a single point of accountability from demo through finished drywall.

## Local Note

Homes built in Lincoln's earlier residential areas — particularly those constructed before the city's more recent growth wave — frequently used paper-faced insulation batts in exterior walls without a continuous interior vapor barrier. In Placer County's climate, that assembly performs reasonably well in dry years, but after a wet winter with multiple atmospheric river events, those paper facings can hold moisture long after the source leak is repaired. During remediation in these homes, drying timelines run longer than they would in a newer home with closed-cell foam or faced fiberglass behind vapor-barrier drywall. Crews account for that when setting drying goals and scheduling reinspection — pulling containment too early is how a remediation turns into a second remediation.
