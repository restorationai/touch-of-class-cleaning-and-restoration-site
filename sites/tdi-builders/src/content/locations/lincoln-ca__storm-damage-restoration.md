## Why Lincoln Properties See Significant Storm Damage

Lincoln's rapid residential expansion over the past two decades brought a large volume of production-built homes, many featuring lightweight roof trusses, engineered wood siding, and stucco exteriors that perform well under normal conditions but can be vulnerable when sustained winds arrive from the northwest. Unlike older Sacramento neighborhoods with mature tree canopies that buffer gusts, many Lincoln subdivisions sit on open ground with limited natural windbreaks. That exposure accelerates wind-driven rain infiltration at roof edges, ridge caps, and around window flashing, areas where storm damage often hides until a second weather event makes it obvious.

The foothills transition zone also means Lincoln receives heavier hail events than communities closer to the valley floor. Composite shingles installed in the early 2000s construction boom are now aging into the range where hail impact causes granule loss that isn't always visible from the street but shortens remaining roof life significantly.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Lincoln

After a storm, the sequence matters. Water that enters through a compromised roof or broken window doesn't wait for a convenient inspection appointment, it moves into wall cavities, saturates insulation, and begins working on framing within hours.

The process starts with a thorough exterior inspection: roof field, ridge, valleys, flashing, gutters, siding panels, and any attached structures like covered patios or garages. We document damage with photographs calibrated for insurance submissions, which matters when you're working with adjusters who may be reviewing Lincoln claims remotely. From there, emergency stabilization comes first, tarping, board-up, or temporary siding protection, before any permanent repair work begins.

Permanent restoration follows the scope agreed upon with your carrier: roof decking replacement where needed, new shingles or tile matched to existing material, siding repair or full-panel replacement, and interior drywall and insulation work if water intrusion reached living spaces. Because TDI Builders handles both the exterior storm repair and the interior rebuild, you aren't coordinating between two separate contractors while your home sits open.

## Local Note

Lincoln's newer subdivisions were largely built under California's Title 24 energy standards, which means roofing assemblies often include radiant barrier sheathing.
