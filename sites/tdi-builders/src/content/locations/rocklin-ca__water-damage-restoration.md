## Why Rocklin Properties See Water Damage Differently

The expansive clay soils that underlie much of Rocklin and the broader western Placer County foothills shift with seasonal moisture. During the wet season, typically November through March, that soil swells against foundation walls and slab edges, increasing hydrostatic pressure and creating pathways for water to enter crawl spaces and slab perimeters. In summer, the same soil contracts, leaving gaps that can allow pressurized irrigation or plumbing failures to travel farther than they would in more stable ground.

Rocklin's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, a common vintage in established neighborhoods near the older downtown core, often have galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out, failing without warning. Newer developments, particularly those built during the construction boom of the late 1990s and 2000s, frequently used engineered lumber framing and OSB sheathing, both of which absorb moisture quickly and can lose structural integrity before surface materials show visible damage. Knowing which era of construction you're dealing with changes how we approach extraction and drying.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rocklin

Water damage restoration is not a single event, it's a sequence, and skipping steps is how secondary mold growth starts. When we arrive at a Rocklin property, the first priority is stopping the source if it hasn't been already, then mapping the moisture migration using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water follows framing cavities, HVAC chases, and subfloor channels in ways that look contained at the surface but aren't.

Once the affected area is fully mapped, we extract standing water and set commercial-grade drying equipment, dehumidifiers and air movers, positioned based on the specific layout and construction type of the structure. In slab-on-grade homes, which are common throughout Rocklin, we monitor slab moisture separately because concrete holds water long after the surface reads dry. Drying logs are maintained throughout the process, both for your records and for insurance documentation.

After the structure reaches target moisture levels, the rebuild phase begins: drywall replacement, ceiling repair, insulation, flooring, and finish work. Because TDI USA is a full-service construction firm, not just a mitigation company, the restoration and rebuild happen under one contractor, which simplifies scheduling and keeps the project moving.

## Rocklin Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner and commercial property policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an ice maker line that lets go. What they typically don't cover is damage that developed gradually, which is why documenting the timeline matters from the first call. We photograph affected materials before anything is removed, note moisture readings at each measurement point, and produce a scope of work that aligns with how carriers review residential and commercial claims in California.
