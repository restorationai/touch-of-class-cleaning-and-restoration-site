## Why Mather Properties See Renovation Challenges

Sacramento Valley clay soil expands and contracts with the region's wet winters and bone-dry summers, and that movement shows up in slab foundations as hairline cracks, sticking doors, and uneven flooring long before a homeowner notices anything else. Because a meaningful share of Mather's housing was built quickly during the base's redevelopment era, we also see consistent patterns in electrical panels, galvanized plumbing runs, and single-pane windows that were adequate for military housing standards decades ago but fall short of what a modern remodel needs.
