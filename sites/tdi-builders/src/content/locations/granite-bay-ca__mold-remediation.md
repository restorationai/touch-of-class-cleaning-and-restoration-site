## Why Granite Bay Properties See Mold Issues

Granite Bay's housing stock skews toward larger, custom-built homes constructed from the late 1980s through the 2000s — a build era that relied heavily on OSB sheathing, paper-faced drywall, and engineered wood products. Those materials are efficient and cost-effective, but they are also highly susceptible to mold colonization when moisture intrusion goes unaddressed. Unlike older plaster-and-lath construction, paper-faced drywall can harbor active mold growth behind a surface that still looks and feels dry to the touch.

The area's topography adds another layer of risk. Granite Bay's rolling terrain means some lots drain poorly toward foundation walls and under-slab areas. During the wet season — typically November through March in Placer County — saturated soil pushes moisture vapor upward through concrete slabs and into crawl spaces. Homes with inadequate vapor barriers or compromised crawl space venting are particularly vulnerable. That slow, seasonal moisture load is often what feeds a mold colony that doesn't announce itself until the smell becomes impossible to ignore.

HOA-governed communities, which are common throughout Granite Bay's planned developments, can also complicate timely remediation. Exterior repairs that are part of a mold source — a failing roof flashing, a cracked stucco facade — may require HOA approval before work begins. Understanding that approval layer matters for scoping a job correctly from day one.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Granite Bay

Every project starts with a thorough inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the extent of saturation — not just the visible surface damage. Mold remediation is not cleaning; it is controlled removal of contaminated materials, and the scope has to be defined by data, not by what's visible from a doorway.

Once the affected area is mapped, the crew establishes physical containment using negative air pressure and poly sheeting barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the removal phase. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and framing are bagged and removed per California Department of Public Health guidelines for mold-impacted materials.

After removal, affected structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. The area is then dried to target moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. TDI Builders is a licensed and insured general contractor, which means the same company that remediates the mold can handle the rebuild — no handoff gap where moisture conditions can change before repairs are made.

## Local Note

Granite Bay's newer planned communities — particularly those built in the 1990s and early 2000s — frequently used spray-applied house wrap and synthetic stucco (EIFS) systems on exterior walls. When EIFS is installed without proper drainage planes or flashing details, bulk water can become trapped behind the cladding for years before interior mold symptoms appear. By the time a homeowner notices a musty smell in a bedroom or sees discoloration at a baseboard, the moisture damage behind the wall assembly may extend well beyond the visible area. Thermal imaging during the initial assessment is especially important on these homes — a surface reading alone will underestimate the scope.
