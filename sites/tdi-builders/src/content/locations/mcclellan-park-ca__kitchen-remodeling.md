McClellan Park sits on land that spent decades as an active Air Force base before it was redeveloped into a mixed residential and business community, which means the kitchens here fall into two very different categories: newer builds from the redevelopment era with production-grade cabinets and builder-grade finishes, and older Sacramento County homes nearby with layouts that predate open-concept living entirely. A kitchen remodel here isn't one-size-fits-all, and the right approach depends on which kind of house you're standing in.

## Why McClellan Park Kitchens Need a Different Approach

Most of the housing stock built during and after the base's redevelopment uses slab-on-grade foundations, which is standard across this part of Sacramento County but changes how we handle plumbing relocation for a kitchen remodel. Moving a sink or gas line isn't a matter of ducking into a crawlspace, it means cutting and patching concrete, which adds time and cost that a lot of homeowners don't anticipate when they're budgeting a remodel. On top of that, Sacramento's long, hot summers and swings into damp winter months put real stress on cabinet boxes and countertop seams if the materials weren't rated for the Central Valley's humidity range. We see a fair number of kitchens where original cabinetry has started separating at the joints simply because it wasn't built for this climate.

## Our Kitchen Remodeling Process in McClellan Park

We start with a walkthrough that identifies whether you're dealing with slab plumbing, standard wood framing, or a layout that's already been altered by a previous owner, since that changes the whole sequencing of the job. From there we handle demo, rough-in for any plumbing or electrical moves, cabinet installation, countertop templating and setting, and finish work in a sequence built to minimize the number of days your kitchen is fully unusable. For slab-foundation homes we coordinate the concrete cutting and plumbing rough-in as one continuous phase rather than stopping and starting, because reopening a patched slab section twice adds cost nobody needs. Custom cabinet installation gets measured and ordered early in the process so the lead time doesn't stall everything else waiting on one delivery.
