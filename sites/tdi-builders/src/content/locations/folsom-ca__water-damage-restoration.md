## Why Folsom Properties See Water Damage Issues

Folsom's growth over the past three decades produced a wide range of construction vintages sitting side by side. Subdivisions built in the 1990s and early 2000s — many of them in the city's planned communities west of Iron Point Road — used polybutylene or early CPVC supply lines that are now reaching the end of their service life. When those lines fail inside a slab or behind drywall, water can travel surprisingly far before it surfaces. Meanwhile, Folsom's clay-heavy soils drain slowly after heavy rain, and homes with raised foundations or crawl spaces can accumulate ground moisture that wicks upward into floor framing over weeks without any visible leak. The American River's proximity also means that during high-runoff years, the water table in lower-elevation neighborhoods rises enough to push moisture through foundation walls and concrete slabs.

Rooflines are another common entry point. Folsom's summer heat causes roofing materials to expand and contract repeatedly, and by the time the rainy season arrives, small gaps around flashing, skylights, and chimney penetrations can channel water directly into attic insulation and ceiling assemblies.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Folsom

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment — not just a visual inspection. Our technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled inside wall cavities, under flooring, and into structural framing. In Folsom homes with engineered hardwood or laminate over a concrete slab (a very common finish in tract homes built here from the mid-1990s onward), water can pool between the slab and the flooring layer and read completely dry at the surface while the subfloor adhesive and any wood underlayment remain saturated.

Once the scope is mapped, we move to extraction — removing standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units — followed by targeted drying using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create a controlled drying chamber around the affected area. Drying progress is logged daily against the IICRC S500 standard so there is a documented record for your insurance carrier. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — saturated drywall, wet insulation, compromised flooring — are removed and properly disposed of before reconstruction begins. Because TDI Builders is a full-service construction and restoration firm, the same licensed and insured team that handles mitigation can carry the project through rebuild, which eliminates the handoff gap that often delays repairs.

## Local Note

Folsom's newer stucco-clad homes — a dominant exterior finish in subdivisions built after 2000 — present a specific drying challenge that is easy to underestimate. Stucco is a multi-layer system, and when water enters through a failed window flashing or a cracked coat, it can become trapped between the weather barrier and the framing for weeks.
