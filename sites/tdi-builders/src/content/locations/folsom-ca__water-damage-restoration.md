When a pipe lets go or a roof flashing fails during a January downpour, water moves fast through wall cavities and subfloor assemblies, and the clock on secondary damage starts immediately. ## Why Folsom Properties See Water Damage Issues

Folsom's building stock spans several distinct eras. The older neighborhoods near the historic district were built with construction methods and materials that behave very differently under moisture than the engineered lumber and OSB sheathing common in developments that expanded outward through the 1990s and 2000s. Homes built in the earlier decades often have cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines that are well past their service life, a slow pinhole leak inside a wall can saturate insulation and framing for weeks before it surfaces as a stain on the ceiling.

The region's clay-heavy soils also contribute. When the rainy season arrives after a long dry stretch, the ground swells and shifts, which can stress slab foundations and the plumbing penetrations through them. Crawl-space homes in hillside pockets around Folsom are particularly vulnerable: water that enters through a foundation vent or a cracked stem wall collects under the structure, and without prompt extraction and drying, wood rot and mold colonization follow within days.

Commercial properties, office parks, retail centers, and mixed-use buildings, face their own pattern. Flat or low-slope roofing common on commercial construction in the area is prone to ponding during sustained rainfall, and a compromised membrane can push water into ceiling tile systems and insulation before anyone notices.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Folsom

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment before a single piece of damaged material is removed. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. Water behind plaster or inside a double-wall assembly can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated, so we don't rely on visual inspection alone.

Once the scope is established, we extract standing water, place industrial drying equipment, and monitor moisture readings daily until structural materials reach acceptable drywall levels per the IICRC S500 standard. In Folsom's summer months, low ambient humidity actually accelerates drying, but in winter, when outdoor air is wet and cool, we adjust equipment placement and run dehumidifiers at higher capacity to compensate.

After drying is confirmed, the rebuild phase begins: framing repairs, insulation replacement, drywall, texture matching, and paint. Because TDI Builders holds a full general contractor license and carries insurance, we handle the entire scope, mitigation through finished reconstruction, without handing the project off to a second company.

Most water damage claims in Folsom run through standard homeowners or commercial property policies, and the documentation carriers require has become more detailed in recent years.
