## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Elk Grove

When we arrive at a fire-damaged property, the first priority is a room-by-room assessment that separates what can be cleaned from what must be removed. Smoke residue behaves differently depending on what burned: synthetic materials produce a wet, oily soot that smears if wiped incorrectly, while paper and wood fires leave a dry, powdery residue that responds better to dry-chemical sponging before any wet cleaning begins. Our technicians identify the residue type before touching a surface.

Once the structure is secured and utilities are isolated, we move into controlled demolition of materials that cannot be restored — charred framing, heat-warped drywall, melted insulation. We document every removed item with photographs and measurements, which feeds directly into the insurance estimate. Deodorization follows structural drying: thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach into wall cavities and ductwork where smoke odor embeds itself and can linger for months if not treated at the source. Reconstruction is handled in-house, so there's no handoff gap between the remediation crew and the rebuild team.
