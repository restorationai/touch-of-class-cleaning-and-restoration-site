## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Fair Oaks

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated class recognized by the IICRC S500 standard — and the cleanup protocol reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first priority is containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, and establishing negative air pressure where the layout allows. Standing sewage and solid waste are extracted using truck-mounted equipment, and affected porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are typically removed rather than dried in place, because Category 3 contamination cannot be reliably remediated by drying alone.

Once bulk removal is complete, all affected structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. In Fair Oaks ranch homes with concrete slab foundations, particular attention goes to the gap between the slab and any remaining wall framing, where sewage can pool invisibly and begin supporting microbial growth within 24 to 48 hours. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings in the structural assembly return to regionally appropriate baselines, accounting for the Sacramento Valley's ambient humidity levels. A final sanitization pass and documentation package close out the remediation phase before any reconstruction begins.
