## What Makes New Home Construction in Carmichael Different

Carmichael is an unincorporated community governed by Sacramento County rather than a city, which matters more than most people expect when you're pulling permits. Construction applications run through the County's Building Permits and Inspections division, and the review timelines, grading requirements, and setback rules reflect county code rather than any municipal overlay. For homeowners accustomed to city-level permitting in Sacramento proper, this can be a surprise — and for those who haven't built before, it's the kind of detail that quietly extends a project schedule when it isn't anticipated.

The soil profile across much of Carmichael also warrants attention during the design phase. Expansive clay soils are common in the Sacramento Valley, and they move seasonally — swelling when the wet season arrives and shrinking back during the long, dry summers. A foundation engineered without accounting for that movement can develop stress cracks within a few years. Every new home TDI Builders designs for this area includes a soils report and a foundation specification that reflects what's actually in the ground, not a generic regional assumption.

California's Title 24 energy standards add another layer. New residential construction must meet current insulation, window, HVAC efficiency, and solar-readiness requirements, and those standards have tightened in recent cycles. Getting the energy compliance documentation right before permit submittal — rather than treating it as an afterthought — keeps the project moving.

## Our New Home Construction Process in Carmichael

Every project starts with a site assessment: lot dimensions, utility connection points, drainage patterns, and any tree-protection requirements for the valley oaks that define so many Carmichael properties. Sacramento County has specific provisions around protected trees, and building near their drip lines requires coordination early — not after the excavator is already on-site.

From there, the process moves through design development, county plan check submission, and permit issuance before ground is broken. TDI Builders manages that coordination directly, including communication with Sacramento County Building Permits and Inspections and any required third-party reviews for structural or energy compliance. Each phase has a county inspection checkpoint, and scheduling those inspections efficiently is one of the places where local experience pays off in real time saved.

## Local Note

One thing that catches some builders off guard in Carmichael: the mature valley oak canopy that makes the neighborhood visually distinctive also creates real constraints during the design and grading phase. Sacramento County's tree ordinance requires protection measures for oaks above a certain trunk diameter, and those measures affect where you can place a foundation, run underground utilities, or stage heavy equipment. Homeowners who fall in love with a lot partly because of its trees sometimes discover that the trees themselves shape what can be built and where. Working through that early — during site assessment and design, before permit submittal — is far less costly than discovering it during grading.
