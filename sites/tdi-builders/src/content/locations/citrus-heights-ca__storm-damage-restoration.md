## Why Citrus Heights Properties Take Storm Damage Hard

The Sacramento region's Mediterranean climate means long dry summers followed by concentrated wet seasons, and that cycle is hard on building materials. Roofing that bakes under 100-degree heat for five months loses flexibility. When the first serious November or December storm arrives, those same shingles are brittle and more likely to crack, lift, or separate at the seams.

Citrus Heights also has a substantial inventory of homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, when wood-shingle and early composition roofing was standard. Many of those roofs have been re-covered once or twice rather than fully torn off and replaced, leaving multiple layers that hide deteriorated decking underneath. A storm that looks like minor surface damage on the outside can reveal saturated sheathing and compromised rafters once the work begins. Stucco exteriors common in this era are similarly deceptive, hairline cracks that have been painted over for years become entry points for wind-driven rain during a major storm event.

The clay soils across much of the city drain slowly, which means standing water around foundations and in crawl spaces is a secondary concern after any significant storm, not just a roofing problem.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Citrus Heights

Every job starts with a full exterior and interior assessment, roof, siding, soffits, gutters, windows, and any penetrations where water may have entered. TDI USA documents damage with photographs and written scope before any material is moved or covered, which matters when an insurance adjuster needs to verify the original condition.

Emergency tarping and board-up happen the same visit when the structure is exposed. This is not a temporary patch, it is a weather-tight barrier installed to stop the loss from growing while the full repair plan is finalized. From there, the process moves through structural drying if interior water intrusion has occurred, then material removal, framing repair or replacement, and finally exterior cladding and roofing restoration to current code.

For older Citrus Heights homes where the original roofing system predates current California Building Code requirements, the rebuild is permitted and inspected through Sacramento County. TDI USA is licensed and insured for commercial, industrial, and residential work, which means the paperwork and coordination with the county building department are handled as part of the job, not handed back to the property owner.
