Modesto's long, dry summers and damp valley winters create a cycle that's hard on everything inside a home — and when a kitchen fire, burst pipe, or sewage backup strikes, the belongings caught in the middle absorb smoke, moisture, and contaminants fast. Contents restoration and storage is the work of pulling those items out before secondary damage sets in, cleaning them to pre-loss condition, and keeping them safe while the structure is repaired. TDI Builders, Inc. has been doing exactly this since 1985, and our IICRC certified technicians are available around the clock to respond when it matters most.

## Why Modesto Homes See Significant Contents Damage

The Central Valley's climate is a particular challenge for personal property. Summer temperatures routinely push past 100°F, and when a fire occurs in that heat, smoke residue bakes into upholstery, cabinetry, and clothing within hours rather than days. In winter, the valley's tule fog season brings sustained humidity that accelerates mold colonization on porous materials — fabric, paper, wood furniture — that were already wetted by a water loss. Modesto's housing stock adds another layer of complexity: a large share of the city's residential neighborhoods were built during the postwar boom of the late 1940s through the 1960s, meaning many homes have original hardwood floors, plaster walls, and older cabinetry that require gentler, more deliberate cleaning methods than modern laminate or MDF construction would.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Modesto

The first step is a detailed inventory. Before a single box leaves your home, our crew photographs and logs every item — furniture, electronics, clothing, documents, collectibles — so you have a complete record for your insurance claim. Items are then packed out to a secure, climate-controlled facility where the real restoration work begins.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material and the type of loss:

- **Smoke and soot**: Dry-cleaning sponges remove loose carbon before any wet process touches the surface. Ultrasonic cleaning tanks handle hard goods — dishware, tools, decorative items — by using high-frequency sound waves to lift soot from crevices that hand-cleaning can't reach.
- **Water and mold**: Structural drying principles from the IICRC S500 standard guide how we handle saturated soft goods. Items that can be restored are dried, sanitized, and deodorized; items that cannot are documented for your adjuster.
- **Odor**: Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation treat odor at the molecular level rather than masking it, which matters especially for smoke losses in the valley heat where odor compounds penetrate deeply.

Once your home is ready, we coordinate return delivery and help you place items back room by room.

## Insurance Coordination for Modesto Contents Claims

Contents losses are often the most disputed line items in a restoration claim because adjusters want itemized documentation and replacement cost values for every affected piece. Our inventory process is built around that requirement — photographs, condition notes, and cleaning or replacement recommendations are formatted to work with most major carriers. We bill insurance directly where possible, reducing the out-of-pocket burden while your home is being repaired. If your policy includes additional living expenses (ALE) coverage, your adjuster may also cover the cost of temporary storage while your structure is under reconstruction.

Modesto's older residential neighborhoods — particularly those with homes built before the 1970s — frequently have detached garages and outbuildings that were used for storage long before climate control was standard. When a fire or water loss affects those structures, the contents inside have often been exposed to years of temperature swings and humidity fluctuation already, which means adhesives on furniture joints may be weakened, paper goods more brittle, and fabric more susceptible to dye transfer during cleaning. We assess outbuilding contents separately from main-structure contents because the baseline condition is different, and that distinction matters when we're documenting value for your carrier.
