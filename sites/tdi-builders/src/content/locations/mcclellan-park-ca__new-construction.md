New home construction in McClellan Park comes with a wrinkle most Sacramento-area builders never have to think about: the ground itself has a history. Large stretches of this community sit on the footprint of the former McClellan Air Force Base, which means ground-up construction here often starts with questions a typical residential lot never raises, long before the first stud goes up.

## Why McClellan Park Lots Need Extra Groundwork

Sacramento County's Central Valley clay soils expand and contract with the seasons, which is true across the region, but McClellan Park adds another layer: decades of prior industrial and military use on and around the base footprint. That history means some parcels carry environmental review requirements, old utility lines, or compacted fill that a custom home builder has to account for in the foundation design, not just the floor plan. We pull soil reports and check prior land-use records before we finalize a foundation type, because skipping that step on a McClellan Park lot can turn into a mid-build surprise instead of a pre-build decision.

## Our New Home Construction Process in McClellan Park

Ground-up construction here starts with the same due diligence we've used on commercial, industrial, and residential sites since 1985: survey and soil testing, then a permitting pass through Sacramento County that accounts for the parcel's history before plans go final. From there we sequence foundation work to match the soil report (slab, raised, or engineered fill depending on what the ground tells us), frame to the approved plan set, and bring in our own trade crews for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing rough-in rather than subbing out the coordination to a third party. Inspections happen at each milestone, not just at the end, which matters more on a former-base parcel where the county may ask for documentation a standard subdivision lot wouldn't need. We walk clients through each of those checkpoints so a slower permitting step on the front end doesn't feel like a mystery.

## Reaching McClellan Park from Our Sacramento Base

Our crews and project managers are based in Sacramento, which keeps McClellan Park well within normal working range for site visits, inspections, and the day-to-day supervision a custom build needs. Being close by also means we can respond quickly if a subcontractor schedule slips or a county inspector flags something that needs an on-site answer the same week, not the same month.

## Local note

One thing worth knowing if you're planning to build in McClellan Park: because parts of the area fall within the former base redevelopment zone, some lots require an environmental sign-off from the county or a prior-use disclosure before grading can start. It's not a universal rule for every address here, but it's common enough that we check it as a standard first step rather than waiting for the county to flag it mid-permit. Building elsewhere in greater Sacramento usually skips that check entirely.
