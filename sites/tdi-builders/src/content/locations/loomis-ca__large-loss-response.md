## Why Loomis Properties Are Vulnerable to Catastrophic Loss

The Loomis area straddles the transition zone between the Sacramento Valley floor and the Sierra Nevada foothills — a geography that creates specific risk patterns. Winter atmospheric river events can drop several inches of rain in 24 hours onto ground that's been baked hard through a six-month dry season, producing rapid surface runoff that overwhelms drainage systems and infiltrates structures from the foundation up. Properties on sloped lots, which are common throughout the Loomis bench and the surrounding rural parcels, face lateral water intrusion that standard perimeter drainage wasn't designed to handle at that volume.

Older ranch-style homes and equestrian properties — a defining feature of Loomis's character — often have crawl spaces, post-and-pier foundations, and wood-framed outbuildings that become saturated long before surface damage is visible. Fire risk is equally significant: the wildland-urban interface runs close to Loomis, and embers from a wind-driven fire can compromise multiple structures on a single parcel before suppression crews arrive. When any of these events reach catastrophic scale, the restoration scope typically involves structural drying, debris removal, structural repair, and rebuild — all running concurrently under a single coordinated plan.

## Our Large Loss and Catastrophic Response Process in Loomis

Large-loss work is fundamentally a project management discipline as much as a technical one. When TDI Builders responds to a catastrophic loss in the Loomis area, the first priority is a documented scope assessment — not a rough estimate, but a room-by-room, system-by-system inventory that satisfies both the property owner and the carrier's large-loss adjuster. For commercial properties, we maintain project documentation in formats that large-loss adjusters recognize and can move through their review process without back-and-forth. For residential clients, we walk through every phase in plain language so nothing is a surprise.
