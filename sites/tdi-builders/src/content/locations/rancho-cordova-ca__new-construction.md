## What Makes New Home Construction in Rancho Cordova Distinct

Rancho Cordova's growth story is unusual for the Sacramento region. Much of the city was built around defense and aerospace employment corridors, which means large swaths of the land base transitioned from commercial or light-industrial use to residential over the past two decades. That history matters when you're breaking ground. Soil conditions on former commercial parcels can vary significantly, compaction profiles, subsurface drainage, and even remnant utility infrastructure can affect foundation design in ways that a standard residential lot in an established subdivision wouldn't present.

Beyond the soil, the Sacramento Valley climate drives real decisions in new construction here. Summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F, which means proper attic ventilation, radiant barrier sheathing, and high-performance window specifications aren't upgrades, they're baseline necessities for a home that performs well and keeps energy costs manageable year-round. The valley's tule fog season also affects construction scheduling in winter months, compressing exterior work windows more than homeowners often anticipate.
