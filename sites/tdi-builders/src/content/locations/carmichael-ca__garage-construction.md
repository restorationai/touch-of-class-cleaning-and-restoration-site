## Why Carmichael's Housing Stock Shapes Every Garage Project

A large share of Carmichael's residential neighborhoods developed between the late 1940s and the early 1970s, which means the homes were designed around single-car garages, or no garage at all. Lots in these areas tend to be generous by Sacramento suburban standards, which is good news for detached garage construction, but the older foundations and utility layouts require careful evaluation before breaking ground. Gas, water, and sewer lines in neighborhoods developed before modern utility mapping are sometimes located where you wouldn't expect them, and a pre-construction locate is not optional, it's the first thing a responsible builder schedules.

Carmichael's climate adds another layer of planning. The region's hot, dry summers and wet winters mean concrete flatwork and exterior framing need to account for significant seasonal movement. Getting the foundation spec right the first time is far less expensive than addressing settlement or cracking after the structure is built.
