## Why Folsom Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

Folsom sits in a climate zone where summer relative humidity regularly drops below 20 percent. That dry air accelerates the oxidation process that follows a fire, meaning soot bonds more aggressively to porous surfaces — drywall, grout, insulation batts — than it would in a coastal or valley-fog environment. Many homes in Folsom's master-planned communities were built in the 1990s and 2000s with open floor plans and cathedral ceilings. Those designs are beautiful until a fire moves through them: the open volume acts like a chimney, pulling smoke into every room, every HVAC duct, and every closet cavity well beyond the room of origin.

The region's wildfire proximity is also a practical factor. Even homes that never see direct flame can suffer heavy smoke infiltration during nearby vegetation fires, coating attic insulation and HVAC systems with fine particulate that requires the same systematic cleaning as a structural fire. Post-fire restoration in Folsom often means addressing both the burn zone and the smoke-migration zone — which can be the entire house.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Folsom

The first priority on any fire loss is stabilization. That means boarding openings, tarping compromised rooflines, and securing the structure before any cleaning begins. From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

**Scope and documentation** — Every affected surface is photographed and catalogued. Structural members are probed for heat damage. This documentation drives both the repair plan and the insurance claim.

**Dry demolition of unsalvageable materials** — Charred framing, melted insulation, and fire-damaged drywall come out before any cleaning begins. Leaving compromised material in place traps odor and creates a substrate that cleaning chemistry can't fully address.

**Soot and smoke residue removal** — Dry chemical sponges handle loose soot on walls and ceilings before any wet cleaning is introduced. Wet-cleaning soot before dry-removing it smears the residue deeper into the substrate. IICRC-certified technicians on the crew follow the sequencing that the S700 standard describes for smoke and fire restoration.

**Odor neutralization** — Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are used to reach the same pathways smoke traveled. In Folsom's single-story and two-story tract homes, HVAC systems are a critical odor reservoir; duct cleaning is typically part of every full-structure fire job.

**Reconstruction** — TDI Builders, Inc. handles the rebuild side as well, which eliminates the handoff gap between mitigation contractor and general contractor that can delay a family's return home by weeks.
