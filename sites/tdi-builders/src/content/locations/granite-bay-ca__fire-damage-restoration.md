## Why Granite Bay Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

The housing stock throughout Granite Bay leans heavily toward custom and semi-custom homes built from the late 1980s through the 2000s — a construction era that used engineered lumber, composite materials, and spray foam insulation in ways that produce thick, oily smoke when they burn. That smoke penetrates HVAC ductwork, settles into wall cavities, and leaves a protein film on surfaces that is nearly invisible but carries a persistent odor. Older ranch-style homes on larger lots — the kind common in the western edges of the community near Auburn-Folsom Road — often have crawl spaces and attic runs that trap smoke particulates long after the visible damage has been cleaned.

The dry-summer Mediterranean climate also means that structural materials like framing lumber and OSB sheathing have very low moisture content at the time of a fire, which accelerates char depth. After suppression, the same dry conditions slow the off-gassing of smoke compounds, which can extend the timeline for odor neutralization if the work is rushed.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Granite Bay

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a proven structure. The first step on arrival is a systematic assessment — documenting char depth, smoke migration paths, and the condition of structural members before anything is moved or cleaned. Photographs and written notes from this phase are what support an insurance claim, and they also determine whether a wall can be cleaned versus must be removed.

Demolition of unsalvageable materials comes next: charred framing, smoke-saturated insulation, and compromised drywall are removed to clean structural lines. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are then used to address odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously during cleaning to capture fine particulates. Structural drying equipment manages any moisture introduced during fire suppression. Once surfaces pass a cleaning inspection, encapsulation or primer sealing locks in any residual smoke compounds before reconstruction begins — a step that matters especially in Granite Bay homes with high-end interior finishes where a single missed pocket of odor will be noticed.

TDI Builders, Inc. handles the full arc from emergency board-up and debris removal through framing, drywall, painting, and finish carpentry, which means the restoration doesn't stop at the point where a mitigation-only company would hand off the job.
