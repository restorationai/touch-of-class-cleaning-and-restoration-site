## Why Rocklin Properties See Distinct Storm Damage Patterns

Rocklin's position on the Placer County foothills means it catches storm systems that have already gathered moisture crossing the Central Valley but still carry enough energy to cause serious structural damage. Two factors make local losses different from what you'd see closer to Sacramento proper.

First, the housing stock. Rocklin experienced significant residential growth in the 1990s and 2000s, meaning a large share of homes carry composition shingle roofs now approaching or past their 20-to-25-year design life. A roof that looked fine through last summer can lose multiple squares of shingles in a single wind event, opening the decking to hours of rain before the storm even passes. Second, the area's clay-heavy soils drain slowly. Once the ground is saturated — which happens quickly during a multi-day storm — water that would normally percolate away instead migrates toward foundations and crawl spaces, compounding damage that started at the roofline.

The mature oaks and pines common throughout Rocklin add a third variable: falling limbs and whole trees that punch through roofs, soffits, and siding in ways that require structural assessment before any cosmetic repair begins.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Rocklin

Every job starts with a methodical damage assessment — not a quick visual walk — because storm damage in this climate hides. Wind-driven rain can travel six feet horizontally under lifted flashing before it ever shows as a ceiling stain. We document with photos and moisture readings before anything is touched, which matters both for your insurance claim and for making sure no wet material gets sealed behind new finishes.

From there, the sequence is:

- **Emergency stabilization** — tarping compromised roof sections, boarding breached openings, and addressing any active water intrusion to stop the loss from growing.
- **Structural assessment** — evaluating framing, sheathing, and load-bearing elements where impact damage or prolonged saturation may have compromised integrity.
- **Removal of damaged material** — wet insulation, ruined sheathing, and storm-damaged siding come out completely. Partial repairs over wet substrate are how mold problems start.
- **Rebuild and finish** — roofing, siding, windows, framing, and interior finishes restored to pre-loss condition, permitted and inspected where Placer County requires it.

Because we are licensed, insured, and have been doing this work for over 40 years, we can carry a job from the emergency tarp through the final inspection under one contract — no handoffs between a mitigation company and a separate GC.

## Rocklin Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most storm damage claims in Rocklin run through homeowners' or commercial property policies, and the documentation your adjuster needs is specific: date-stamped photos, moisture readings, a written scope of damage, and material specifications for what is being replaced. We build that file from the first day on site.

If your property sits within one of Rocklin's planned communities or HOA-governed neighborhoods, exterior repairs — roofing color, siding material, paint — typically require architectural committee approval before work begins.
