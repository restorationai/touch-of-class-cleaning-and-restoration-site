## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Rocklin

Every job begins with a scene assessment before any materials are disturbed. Technicians identify all affected surfaces — visible and non-visible — and establish a containment perimeter using negative air pressure where the structure allows. This step is especially important in Rocklin's older single-story homes, where HVAC systems can draw air from affected rooms into unaffected parts of the house if not isolated first.

Once containment is established, affected materials are removed, bagged, and transported in accordance with California Department of Public Health regulations governing biohazardous waste. This is regulated disposal, not curbside trash — every bag is tracked from the property to a licensed medical waste facility. After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants calibrated to the specific pathogen risk present. A final clearance check confirms the space is safe before containment is removed.

Throughout the process, the crew works in plain vehicles and unmarked packaging where possible. Discretion is a standard part of the job, not an add-on.
