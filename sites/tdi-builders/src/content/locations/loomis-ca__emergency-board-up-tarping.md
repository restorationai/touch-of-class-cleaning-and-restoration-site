## Why Loomis Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Placer County's foothill climate creates a specific pattern of property damage that makes rapid board-up and tarping more consequential here than in the flatlands to the west. Summer fire season is the obvious concern — dry brush along the Highway 193 corridor and the rural parcels east of town means wildfire smoke and ember damage can leave exterior walls and roof decking exposed to follow-on weather. But the transition seasons matter just as much. Late-fall atmospheric rivers can drop several inches of rain in 24 hours, and a roof opening left unprotected overnight will push water into attic insulation, wall cavities, and ceiling assemblies faster than most homeowners expect.

Loomis also has a meaningful stock of older ranch homes and custom builds on larger lots — properties where the roof pitch, material type, and structural quirks vary widely from parcel to parcel. A standard tarp deployment that works on a flat suburban roof may need to be rigged differently on a steeply pitched composition or wood-shake roof common to foothill construction. Getting that wrong means the tarp fails in the next gust, and the damage compounds.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Loomis

When you call, a crew is dispatched from our Sacramento base with materials sized for the job — plywood, OSB, anchor hardware, heavy-duty polyethylene tarps, and ballast systems for steep-pitch applications. The process on-site moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Assessment first.** Before a single sheet of plywood goes up, the crew walks the structure to identify every breach — not just the obvious one. A fire that breaks a front window often leaves heat-stressed frames elsewhere that will fail in the next temperature swing.

**Secure the structure.** Window and door openings are boarded with fastener patterns that meet the opening size and anticipated wind load. We do not nail into finished trim if it can be avoided; the goal is protection without making the eventual repair more expensive.

**Roof tarping.** Tarps are anchored with batten boards screwed through the tarp into the roof deck, not just weighted at the edges. On steeper pitches common to foothill custom homes, we use ridge-anchor rigging to keep the tarp from lifting in the Diablo-pattern winds that occasionally push through the Sacramento Valley into the foothills.

**Documentation.** Every opening is photographed before and after boarding. That documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster and becomes part of the claim file.

## Local Note

Loomis and the surrounding Placer County foothill area have a higher-than-average share of properties with wood-shake or aged composition roofs installed before current fire-resistance standards took effect. When tarping these roofs, a crew that does not account for the brittleness of older shake can cause additional damage by walking the surface without proper load distribution. Our crews use foam knee pads and crawl boards on fragile roof surfaces — a small procedural detail that prevents a protective measure from creating a new repair item on the claim.
