## Why El Dorado Hills Homes Require a Thoughtful Remodeling Approach

Much of El Dorado Hills developed rapidly through the 1990s and 2000s, meaning a large share of the housing stock is now 20 to 35 years old — old enough for original fixtures, cabinetry, and flooring to be at or past their useful life, but built under California codes that have since been substantially updated. Homes in this era often used builder-grade materials selected for speed and cost, not longevity. When you open up a wall for a kitchen remodel or gut a primary bathroom, it's common to find insulation that doesn't meet current Title 24 energy standards, plumbing that predates modern low-flow requirements, or electrical panels that weren't sized for today's appliance loads.

The foothill clay soil also matters indoors. Seasonal movement in the slab or foundation can telegraph into tile work, door frames, and even cabinetry alignment. A remodeling contractor who doesn't account for that movement when selecting grout types, flooring underlayment, and cabinet anchoring methods will leave you with callbacks. Proper material selection for this microclimate isn't optional — it's the baseline.
