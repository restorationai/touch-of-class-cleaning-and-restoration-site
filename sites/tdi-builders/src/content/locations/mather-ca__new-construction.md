Building a new home in Mather means building on ground with a history: much of the area was carved out of former Mather Air Force Base land and surrounding Sacramento County acreage, which means lot conditions vary more than a typical subdivision. Ground-up construction here has to account for that mixed land history before the first form board goes in, whether you're planning a custom home or a full residential build from the slab up.

## Why Mather Properties See New Construction Challenges

Sacramento Valley clay soil is the first thing any custom home builder has to plan around in Mather. The expansive clay common across this part of the county swells when wet and shrinks hard during the dry season, which puts real stress on a standard slab if the foundation design doesn't account for it. Add in the flat terrain typical of this stretch of the valley, where drainage has to be engineered into the grading plan rather than relying on natural slope, and you have two factors that shape nearly every ground-up build before framing starts. Summer heat in the triple digits is also a scheduling factor: concrete pours, stucco, and exterior finish work all get timed around the hottest stretches of the season to protect cure times and material performance.

## Our New Home Construction Process in Mather

We start with a soil and site evaluation specific to the parcel, since two lots a few blocks apart in Mather can carry different clay content and compaction history depending on how the land was used before residential development. Foundation work comes next, typically engineered to the specific soil report rather than a generic slab spec, followed by framing, rough mechanical and electrical, and the inspection sequence the county requires at each stage.
