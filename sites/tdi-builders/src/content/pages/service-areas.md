TDI Builders, Inc. serves homeowners and property managers across the greater Sacramento region — from the city core out through the surrounding valley communities and foothill towns. Dispatch runs around the clock, so whether a pipe bursts at noon on a Tuesday or a sump pump fails at 2 a.m. on a holiday weekend, you can reach a live person at (877) 688-0866 and get a crew moving.

## Where we work

The team is based in Sacramento and covers a wide band of Central Valley communities — the suburbs north and south along I-5 and Highway 99, the eastern foothill corridors toward El Dorado and Placer counties, and the western reaches toward Yolo and Solano counties. Sacramento's housing stock ranges from early-twentieth-century Craftsman bungalows in the midtown grid to postwar ranch homes in Rancho Cordova and newer construction in Elk Grove and Roseville — each era brings its own quirks when water, fire, or mold gets into the structure. Knowing the territory matters when a crew is pulling permits, coordinating with local utilities like SMUD or PG&E, or tracing a moisture path through a raised foundation versus a slab.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Sacramento — Citrus Heights, Rancho Cordova, West Sacramento, Elk Grove, and Folsom — are typically the first to see a crew arrive after a call. Communities a tier out, including Roseville, Rocklin, Lincoln, Davis, Woodland, and Galt, fall within the regular service footprint and are dispatched on the same priority basis. At the edges of the coverage area — places like Auburn, Placerville, Vacaville, and Lodi — arrival order depends on crew availability and active job load at the time of the call. Traffic on I-80, Highway 50, and Highway 99 is a real variable in this region, especially during commute windows, so the team routes dynamically rather than quoting a fixed clock time for distant locations.

## Don't see your city?

The linked pages below cover the communities TDI Builders serves most regularly, but the list is not a hard boundary. For significant losses — major water intrusion, fire damage, large mold remediation — it is always worth a call to (877) 688-0866. Dispatch can tell you immediately whether a crew can reach your property and give you an honest estimate of timing.
