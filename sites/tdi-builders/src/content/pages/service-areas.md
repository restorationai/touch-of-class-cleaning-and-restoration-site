TDI Builders, Inc. operates around the clock out of Sacramento, serving homeowners and property managers across a wide stretch of Northern California. Whether you're dealing with water damage at 2 a.m. or need a mold assessment scheduled for later in the week, the dispatch line is open 24/7 — call (877) 688-0866 and a real person will help you figure out next steps and get a crew moving.

## Where we work

The team is based in Sacramento and covers the surrounding region in every direction — the suburban corridors along I-80 toward the Sierra foothills, the Highway 50 and Highway 99 corridors, communities throughout the Sacramento Valley, and the cities and towns that ring the greater metro. The Central Valley's flat geography and the region's mix of older bungalows, mid-century ranch homes, and newer tract construction all show up regularly in restoration work — and the seasonal pattern of wet winters followed by long dry summers creates its own predictable damage cycle that the crew knows well.

## Coverage by distance

Cities close to Sacramento — those within the immediate metro and inner suburbs — typically see the fastest crew arrivals, since there's little standing between dispatch and your front door. As distance grows toward the outer edges of the Valley or up into the foothills, travel time increases, but the same crew and the same equipment make the trip. Traffic on I-80 and Highway 50 during peak hours can affect arrival windows for eastern locations; crews dispatched toward the Delta or into the Valley along Highway 99 generally have a cleaner run. The service area pages linked below lay out specific cities — use those to confirm your location falls within the coverage footprint.

## Don't see your city?

The listed service areas cover the core of where TDI Builders regularly dispatches, but they aren't always the hard edge of coverage. For significant losses — a burst pipe that's flooded multiple rooms, a fire that's affected a whole floor, a commercial property with a large mold problem — it's worth a phone call regardless of whether your city appears on the list. Call (877) 688-0866 and describe the situation; if the job warrants the drive, the answer is usually yes.
