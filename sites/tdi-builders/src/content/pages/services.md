From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, TDI Builders, Inc. handles the full arc of property damage — emergency mitigation, structural drying, demolition, and complete reconstruction. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial operators throughout the Sacramento region. Whether the loss is a single bathroom or an entire commercial floor, the goal is the same: stop the damage, document it thoroughly, and rebuild to code.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still settling, or a biohazard situation needs immediate containment, response speed determines how much of the structure can be saved. Water intrusion — from storms, slab leaks, appliance failures, or sewage backups — can saturate wall cavities and subfloors within hours, long before the surface feels wet. Fire and smoke damage leave behind acidic soot that continues etching surfaces and corroding metals after the flames are out. Biohazard and trauma scenes carry health risks that require proper containment, PPE, and disposal protocols before any other work begins. Storm damage — wind, hail, falling trees — often needs immediate board-up or tarping to prevent a second wave of water intrusion. TDI Builders, Inc. is available 24/7, and our IICRC certified technicians carry the equipment to begin stabilization on the first visit.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the loss; reconstruction restores the property. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because TDI Builders, Inc. is a licensed general contractor with more than 40 years of construction and restoration experience, the same company that performed the emergency work can carry the project through to final inspection. That continuity matters: a separate rebuild contractor has to re-learn the scope, re-document conditions, and coordinate with an adjuster who has already closed the mitigation file. Keeping it under one roof eliminates that friction. We also take on renovation and remodel projects outside of insurance claims for clients who want the same construction depth on planned work.

## Specialty services

Some damage doesn't announce itself with standing water or visible char. Mold colonies can establish behind drywall within days of an undetected leak, and the first sign is often an odor rather than a stain. TDI Builders, Inc. offers mold inspection and remediation, structural odor removal for persistent smoke or sewage smells, and emergency board-up and roof tarping to secure a property after sudden structural damage. These services are often the bridge between an insurance claim being filed and full reconstruction beginning.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction as a single engagement — one licensed contractor, one point of contact, one consistent scope of work — shortens the timeline and reduces the documentation gaps that slow insurance claims.
