Whether it's a ground-up build, a full remodel, or putting a property back together after a loss, the last thing you want is to manage a rotating cast of contractors. TDI USA, Inc. handles the full arc of construction and reconstruction for homeowners, property managers, commercial building owners, and multi-family operators across Sacramento and the surrounding region. One company, one point of contact, from the first call to the final walkthrough.

## Rebuilding after a loss

When a property has been through a fire, flood, or storm, the rebuild is where it becomes a home or a business again. TDI USA specializes in post-loss reconstruction: framing, drywall, roofing, flooring, cabinetry, and finishes, restored to pre-loss condition or better, with the documentation insurance carriers expect. We coordinate directly with your mitigation provider and your adjuster so the handoff from dry-out to rebuild is seamless, and the same licensed, insured crew carries the project from scope to final walkthrough.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Reconstruction puts the structure back. Once the drying equipment comes out and the damaged materials are removed, TDI USA takes the project through the full rebuild phase, framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, painting, and everything in between. One general contractor owning the rebuild means no handoff gaps, no finger-pointing between subs, and no delays waiting for a second crew to get up to speed on the scope. The same approach applies to renovation projects that aren't tied to an insurance claim, if a space needs updating alongside the repair work, that conversation is straightforward to have.

## Specialty services

Beyond full rebuilds, TDI USA handles the projects that make a property work better day to day: room additions, garage construction, kitchen and bathroom remodels, and commercial tenant improvements. Each carries its own permitting, scheduling, and inspection requirements, and each gets the same single-contractor accountability. Specialty work done right is often the difference between a project that closes cleanly and one that generates callbacks months later.
