When a pipe bursts at 2 a.m., a kitchen fire leaves smoke baked into every surface, or a storm tears through a roof, the last thing you want is to manage a rotating cast of contractors. TDI Builders, Inc. handles the full arc — emergency mitigation through finished reconstruction — for homeowners, property managers, commercial building owners, and multi-family operators across Sacramento and the surrounding region. One company, one point of contact, from the first call to the final walkthrough.

## Emergency restoration services

When a loss is active, the clock matters. Water left standing for more than 24 to 48 hours can push a straightforward extraction job into mold territory; smoke residue begins bonding to walls and contents within hours of a fire. TDI Builders responds around the clock to water damage and flooding, fire and smoke damage, biohazard and trauma scene cleanup, and storm damage — including wind, hail, and fallen debris. Each of these services has its own protocols, equipment, and documentation requirements; the service pages linked below go deep on each one. What connects them is the same licensed, insured crew arriving ready to stop the damage from spreading before it compounds.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the bleeding; reconstruction puts the structure back. After the drying equipment comes out and the damaged materials are removed, TDI Builders transitions directly into the rebuild phase — framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, painting, and everything in between. Because the same company handled the tear-out, there are no handoff gaps, no finger-pointing between a remediation sub and a general contractor, and no delays waiting for a second crew to get up to speed on what was removed and why. This integrated approach also applies to renovation projects that aren't tied to an insurance claim — if a space needs updating alongside the restoration work, that conversation is straightforward to have.

## Specialty services

Some damage isn't dramatic but still demands attention. Mold inspection and remediation, odor removal (including persistent smoke and sewage odors that survive surface cleaning), emergency board-up and tarping, and contents pack-out are all part of the TDI Builders service range. These aren't afterthoughts — odor that isn't fully addressed will resurface, and a property left unsecured after a loss is an invitation to further damage or theft. Specialty services are often the difference between a job that closes cleanly and one that generates callbacks months later.
