From the first phone call after a pipe bursts to the final coat of paint after a fire, TDI Builders, Inc. handles the full arc of property damage recovery — mitigation, reconstruction, and everything in between. We work with homeowners, commercial property owners, landlords, and multi-family operators across the Sacramento region and surrounding communities. Whatever the loss, the goal is the same: get the building back to the way it was, or better.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, every hour matters. Water intrusion spreads laterally through subfloor and wall cavities faster than most people expect; smoke residue begins bonding to surfaces within hours of a fire; a biohazard situation carries health risks that make the space unusable until it's properly remediated. TDI Builders responds around the clock to water damage emergencies — burst pipes, appliance failures, sewer backups, and storm flooding — as well as fire and smoke damage, biohazard and trauma scene cleanup, and wind or hail damage from severe weather. Our IICRC-certified technicians arrive with the extraction, drying, and containment equipment the job requires, not a wait-and-see approach.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction brings the building back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, TDI Builders transitions directly into the rebuild phase — framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because we hold our own contractor license and carry full insurance, there's no handoff to a separate GC and no gap in accountability between the remediation crew and the rebuild crew. We also take on renovation and improvement projects for property owners who want to upgrade while the walls are already open.

## Specialty services

Some situations don't fit neatly into emergency response or full reconstruction. TDI Builders also provides mold inspection and remediation for properties with moisture history or visible growth, professional odor removal for smoke, pet, and chemical odors that outlast the original damage event, and board-up and tarping services to secure a structure immediately after a fire, break-in, or storm. These services are available as standalone work or as part of a larger restoration scope.

## One company, start to finish

The most frustrating part of property damage recovery is often the handoff — a remediation company that disappears once the drying equipment is picked up, leaving the owner to find and coordinate a separate contractor for repairs. TDI Builders is structured to avoid that entirely. With more than 40 years of combined construction and restoration experience, we move from emergency response through final reconstruction under one project file, one point of contact, and one company accountable for the outcome.
