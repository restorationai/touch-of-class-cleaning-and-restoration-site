TDI Builders, Inc. has been doing restoration and construction work in the Sacramento area since 1985. That's four decades of showing up when a pipe bursts at midnight, when smoke has worked its way into every closet, when a slow leak has quietly grown mold behind drywall for months before anyone noticed. The company was built around the idea that restoration work demands a different kind of contractor — one who understands both the technical side of drying and remediation and the construction side of putting a home back together properly.

## What we do

Most of what we handle starts with a phone call from someone who is already stressed. Water is still coming in, or the smell of smoke won't leave, or a contractor just told them the subfloor has to come out. Our crews are available around the clock — nights, weekends, holidays — because damage doesn't wait for business hours. From the first assessment through insurance documentation, demolition, drying, and final reconstruction, we stay on the job rather than handing it off. Working directly with insurance carriers is a significant part of that: we document affected materials, photograph scope, and communicate with adjusters so homeowners aren't left translating between their contractor and their insurance company on their own.

## Our certifications and licensure

TDI Builders, Inc. carries IICRC-certified technicians on staff. The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — sets the technical standards the restoration industry runs on, including the S500 standard for water damage and the S520 for mold remediation. When a technician is IICRC-certified, it means they've been trained and tested against those standards, not just on-the-job trained by whoever hired them first. We are also licensed and insured in California, which matters when work involves structural repairs, plumbing tie-ins, or anything that requires a permit and inspection. With more than 40 years of combined construction and restoration experience behind the company, the credentials reflect work that's actually been done at scale.

## Where we work

Our base is Sacramento, and we serve the broader region that surrounds it — the communities of the Central Valley, the foothills to the east, and the suburban corridors stretching toward the Bay Area. Sacramento's climate creates predictable patterns of loss: wet winters with atmospheric river storms that push water through aging foundations and roof systems, dry summers that create fire risk, and a housing stock that spans from early-twentieth-century Craftsman bungalows in neighborhoods like Oak Park and Land Park to postwar tract homes and newer construction in the outlying suburbs. Older homes — anything built before 1978 — can involve lead paint and require handling under specific federal protocols. We work across that range regularly.
