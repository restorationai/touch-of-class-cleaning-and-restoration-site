TDI Builders, Inc. has been doing restoration and construction work in the Sacramento area since 1985. That's four decades of showing up when a pipe bursts at midnight, when a kitchen fire leaves soot in every corner of a house, or when a slow roof leak finally makes itself known through a ceiling stain that's been growing for months. The company was built around the idea that restoration work demands both construction knowledge and damage-science expertise — you can't do one well without the other.

## What we do

On any given day, the work looks like this: a crew arrives at a home where standing water is still sitting under the subfloor, reads the moisture map with calibrated meters, sets drying equipment, and then coordinates with the homeowner's insurance adjuster to document everything before a single damaged board gets pulled. Because we're available around the clock, that call can come in at 2 a.m. on a Tuesday and the response is the same as it would be on a weekday morning. Restoration isn't predictable work — water doesn't wait for business hours, and neither do we.

Working alongside insurance carriers is a significant part of what we do. We photograph affected materials, write detailed scope-of-loss documentation, and bill carriers directly where possible, so homeowners aren't left managing a claims process on top of an already stressful situation. When the remediation is complete and the structure is stable, our construction background means we can handle the rebuild as well — one contractor through the full cycle rather than a handoff mid-project.

## Our certifications and licensure

Our technicians are IICRC certified. The Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification sets the technical standards the restoration industry runs on — the S500 for water damage, the S520 for mold remediation, the S700 for fire and smoke. When a technician holds IICRC credentials, it means their drying protocols, containment procedures, and clearance standards are grounded in those published guidelines, not guesswork.

We are also licensed and insured in California. For homeowners, that matters practically: it means there's a license on the line if work is done improperly, and it means you're not exposed if something goes wrong on the job site.

With more than 40 years of combined construction and restoration experience behind the company, the credentials reflect work that's been done in the field, not just passed in a classroom.

## Where we work

Our home base is Sacramento, and most of our work stays in the greater Sacramento Valley — the city proper, the surrounding suburbs, and the communities that sit in the foothills and delta edges of the region. Sacramento's housing stock spans a wide range of construction eras, from early-twentieth-century Craftsman bungalows in midtown to post-war tract homes in the suburbs to newer construction in the outlying growth corridors. Each era comes with its own material profile and its own failure modes. The valley's hot, dry summers and wet winters create a seasonal rhythm to the damage we see — storm-related water intrusion in the rainy months, HVAC and plumbing failures year-round. If you're unsure whether your property falls within our service area, a quick call to (877) 688-0866 will get you a straight answer.
