## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in the middle of January or a slow leak that finally gave out, standing water and hidden moisture need to be extracted and dried before secondary damage sets in. We locate moisture behind walls and under flooring using professional detection equipment, not guesswork.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue penetrates further than the visible char. Soot settles into HVAC ducts, insulation, and wall cavities, and the odor can linger for months if the affected materials aren't properly cleaned or removed. We assess the full scope of the damage — not just what burned.

**Mold remediation** — Sacramento's hot, dry summers can mask the moisture problems that build up during the rainy season. Mold can begin colonizing a wet surface within 24 to 48 hours, and it doesn't stop at what you can see. Our IICRC-certified technicians contain, remove, and treat affected areas following established industry protocols.

**Reconstruction** — Once the damage is mitigated, the rebuild begins. From replacing drywall and flooring to full structural repairs, TDI Builders handles the construction phase in-house so you're not coordinating a separate contractor after an already stressful event.
