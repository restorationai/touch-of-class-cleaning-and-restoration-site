When water soaks through your subfloor, smoke odor settles into drywall, or mold starts spreading behind a wall, the clock is already running. ## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe at 2 a.m. or a slow leak that finally soaked through the ceiling, standing water needs to be extracted and structural materials dried before secondary damage sets in. Delays of even 24–48 hours can turn a contained loss into a mold problem.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Flames cause visible destruction, but smoke residue and soot penetrate porous materials — insulation, framing, HVAC ducts — long after the fire is out. Proper restoration means addressing both, not just what's charred.

**Mold remediation** — Sacramento's warm, dry summers can mask the moisture problems that develop after a plumbing leak or roof intrusion. Mold can begin colonizing wet materials within 24 to 48 hours; identifying the moisture source is as important as removing what's already grown.

**Reconstruction** — Once mitigation is complete, getting your home or building back to its pre-loss condition requires licensed construction work. Handling both phases under one contractor means fewer handoffs, cleaner documentation, and a faster path back to normal.
