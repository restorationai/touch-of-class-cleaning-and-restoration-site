## Restoration emergencies common in El Dorado Hills

El Dorado Hills sits at the western edge of the Sierra Nevada foothills, where the climate swings harder than most Sacramento-area communities expect. Summers are long, dry, and hot, conditions that stress roofing materials, crack caulk around windows and penetrations, and leave attic spaces vulnerable when the first fall rains arrive. Winter atmospheric rivers can drop several inches of rain in 24 hours on ground that has been baked hard all summer, producing fast runoff and overwhelmed gutters before the soil can absorb anything.

The community's rapid growth from the 1990s onward means a large share of the housing stock is tract construction from that era, homes where roofing underlayment, HVAC flashing, and window seals are now reaching the end of their designed lifespan at roughly the same time. When those systems fail during a storm, water finds its way into wall cavities and attic decking fast. The foothill wildfire risk adds a separate layer: smoke and ash infiltration after a nearby fire can leave odor and particulate contamination in HVAC systems and wall assemblies even when a home is otherwise undamaged.

## Services we provide in El Dorado Hills

**Water damage restoration**, Foothill homes often have complex roof lines and multiple roof-to-wall intersections where flashing fails. Water that enters at a ridge or valley can travel several feet inside a wall or ceiling cavity before it shows on a surface. Thermal imaging and moisture mapping locate the full extent of intrusion before any drying equipment is placed, so the scope of work reflects what actually got wet, not just what's visible.

**Fire and smoke restoration**, After a kitchen fire or a structure fire that affects adjacent rooms, smoke residue settles into porous materials in a way that standard cleaning cannot address. Soot chemistry varies by what burned, synthetic materials produce a different residue than wood framing, and the treatment has to match. Odor neutralization, content cleaning, and structural repair are coordinated so reconstruction doesn't begin over contaminated surfaces.

**Mold remediation**, In a climate where homes can go months without significant humidity and then absorb a week of rain, hidden moisture problems are easy to miss until mold is already colonizing behind drywall or under flooring. Mold can begin establishing within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. Remediation involves containing the affected area, removing compromised materials, and addressing the moisture source, not just treating the visible growth.

**Full reconstruction**, When mitigation is complete, the rebuild scope can range from replacing a single ceiling to full room or structural reconstruction. TDI USA carries both the remediation and reconstruction sides of the work, which means one point of contact for the insurance adjuster and a faster path from demo to finished space.
