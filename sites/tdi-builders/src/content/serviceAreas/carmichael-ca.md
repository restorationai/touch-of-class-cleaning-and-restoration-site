## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Carmichael

Carmichael is an unincorporated community in Sacramento County, which means building permits and inspections are handled through Sacramento County's Department of Community Development, not a city building department. Structural repairs, additions, and work that affects load-bearing elements typically require a permit; cosmetic repairs generally do not, but the line matters when an insurance scope includes framing or roof deck replacement.

The dominant housing stock dates from the postwar boom through the 1970s, with slab-on-grade foundations common in tract construction and raised foundations appearing in older or custom-built homes. Homes built before 1978 should be treated as presumptively containing lead paint; those built before the mid-1980s may contain asbestos-containing materials in floor tile, roofing, or pipe insulation — both require testing before any demolition scope begins.

Sacramento Valley soils in this area include expansive clay in many neighborhoods, which swells with moisture and shrinks in dry summers. That movement stresses slab foundations and can open gaps at plumbing penetrations over time, contributing to the slow-leak pattern common in older Carmichael homes.
