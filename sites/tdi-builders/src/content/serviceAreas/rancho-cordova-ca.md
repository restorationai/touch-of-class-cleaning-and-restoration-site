## Restoration emergencies common in Rancho Cordova

Rancho Cordova sits in the Sacramento Valley, where the climate swings between wet, cool winters and long, dry summers that regularly push past 100°F. That cycle creates predictable stress on buildings. Winter atmospheric rivers — the kind that drop several inches of rain in 48 hours — overwhelm gutters, saturate soil against foundations, and back water into crawlspaces and garages. The American River runs along the city's northern edge, and low-lying areas near the river corridor carry real flood exposure during high-water years.

Summer heat bakes roofing materials and causes supply lines and hose bibs to fail when the first cool-down arrives in fall. Wildfire smoke from the Sierra Nevada foothills drifts into the valley each fire season, leaving fine particulate and odor in HVAC systems and on porous surfaces — a less dramatic loss than a structure fire, but one that still requires professional cleaning to fully clear. Together, these seasonal patterns mean Rancho Cordova property owners face a fairly consistent cycle of water intrusion in winter and smoke or heat-related damage in summer and fall.

## Services we provide in Rancho Cordova

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Rancho Cordova often starts invisibly — a slow leak behind a vanity, a failing wax ring under a toilet, or condensation accumulating inside a wall cavity during a wet winter. By the time a stain appears on the ceiling or flooring starts to cup, moisture has usually been present long enough to begin affecting the structural assembly. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, not just the visible damage, then dry to the IICRC S500 standard before any repairs begin.

### Mold remediation

The warm, humid conditions that follow a water intrusion — especially in poorly ventilated crawlspaces common in parts of Rancho Cordova — can allow mold to colonize within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. Remediation involves containing the affected area, removing compromised materials, and treating surfaces before reconstruction. We do not offer medical advice about health effects, but we do follow established remediation protocols designed to return the space to a normal fungal ecology.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a structure fire, the visible char is rarely the full story. Smoke residue travels through HVAC ducts and settles on surfaces rooms away from the origin point. Protein smoke from kitchen fires leaves a nearly invisible film that produces a persistent odor. Our fire restoration scope includes structural assessment, odor neutralization, content cleaning, and full rebuild — handled under one contract so you are not coordinating between a mitigation company and a separate general contractor.

### Reconstruction

As a licensed general contractor, TDI Builders carries the work past the mitigation phase into finished reconstruction — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and structural framing — without handing the project off. That matters when your insurance adjuster needs a single point of contact and a single scope of work.

Much of Rancho Cordova's residential housing was built between the late 1950s and the 1980s, with significant tracts added through the 1990s and 2000s.
