### Water damage restoration

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue from a kitchen fire or a nearby wildfire event behaves differently depending on what burned. Protein smoke from cooking fires leaves a nearly invisible, foul-smelling film that bonds to painted surfaces. Wildfire smoke carries fine ash and volatile organic compounds that settle into ductwork and attic insulation. Restoration involves controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, HEPA air scrubbing, odor neutralization, and rebuilding to current California code.
