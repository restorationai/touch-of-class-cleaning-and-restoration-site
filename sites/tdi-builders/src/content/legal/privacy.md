## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy explains how TDI Builders, Inc. collects, uses, and protects information submitted through tdiusa.com. It applies to visitors who contact us through the site, request an estimate, or otherwise interact with our web presence. We are based in Sacramento, CA, and primarily serve California residents — so California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) rights are directly relevant here.

We do not believe privacy policies should require a law degree to understand, so we have written this one in plain language.

## Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact or estimate-request form, we receive the information you type: typically your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or project. We do not collect payment card numbers through the website.

We also use basic, anonymized analytics (such as page views and traffic sources) to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated — it is not tied to your name or contact details.

## How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site estimate, and — if you choose to move forward with services — coordinate documentation for insurance claims. We do not use your contact details for unrelated marketing, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

If your project involves an insurance claim, we may share relevant documentation (scope of work, photographs, moisture readings) with your carrier, but only with your written authorization. That coordination exists to help your claim move forward, not for any other purpose.

## Data Retention

We retain contact and project records for as long as is reasonably necessary to fulfill the services you requested and to meet our obligations under California contractor licensing and record-keeping requirements. If you would like your information removed from our records and there is no ongoing legal or contractual reason to retain it, you may request deletion by contacting us directly.

## Your Rights as a California Resident

Under the CCPA, California residents have the right to know what personal information we hold, request deletion of that information, and opt out of any sale of personal data. Because we do not sell personal data, the opt-out right is already satisfied by default. To exercise your right to know or request deletion, reach out using the contact information below.

GDPR (the European Union's data regulation) is unlikely to apply to most visitors of this site, as our services are limited to California. If you are visiting from the EU and have a question about how your data is handled, we are happy to address it directly.

## Contact for Privacy Questions

If you have a question about this policy, want to request access to your data, or need to report a concern, contact TDI Builders, Inc. by phone at (877) 688-0866 or by mail at 701 Del Paso Rd, Sacramento, CA 95834. We will respond to privacy-related requests within a reasonable timeframe.

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.
