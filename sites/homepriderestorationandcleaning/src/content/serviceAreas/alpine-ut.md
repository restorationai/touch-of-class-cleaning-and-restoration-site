## Restoration emergencies common in Alpine

Alpine sits at the base of the Wasatch Range, and that elevation brings heavier snow loads and a longer freeze season than much of Utah County. Roof and attic leaks from ice damming are common in late winter as snow melts and refreezes at the eaveline, and pipes in unheated crawlspaces or exterior walls are prone to freezing and bursting during extended cold snaps. Spring runoff and irrigation water from the surrounding foothills can also push groundwater against foundations on sloped lots, especially where properties back up to open space or horse pasture. In summer, wildfire smoke drifting down from canyon and mountain fires is a recurring air-quality and odor concern even when the fire itself is miles away.
