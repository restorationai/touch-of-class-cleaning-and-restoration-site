## Restoration emergencies common in Draper

Draper sits at the southern edge of Salt Lake County, tucked against the Wasatch Range where the valley floor climbs into bench and foothill terrain. That elevation change matters for water intrusion: homes built into the slope often deal with grading and runoff pressure against foundations during spring snowmelt, while flatter subdivisions closer to the valley floor see more standing water from heavy rain events pooling against slab foundations. Winter brings freeze-thaw cycles that stress exposed plumbing and can split a supply line overnight, and the dry summer months raise wildfire smoke concerns that drift into homes through HVAC systems even when the fire itself stays in the foothills. Basement finishes, common in this part of the valley, are especially vulnerable once water gets past a foundation wall, because carpet pad and drywall down low hold moisture far longer than anyone walking through the space would guess.

## Services we provide in Draper

Water damage restoration is our most frequent call in Draper, whether it is a slab leak under a finished basement, a washing machine line failure, or groundwater pushing in during a wet spring. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from framing and subfloor, and set up drying equipment calibrated to the structure rather than a one-size-fits-all fan placement.

Fire and smoke damage cleanup addresses both the structural burn and the smoke residue that migrates through ductwork into rooms the fire never touched. Soot behaves differently on painted drywall than on wood trim, which changes how we clean each surface.

Mold remediation follows any water event that went undetected for more than a day or two, particularly in basement framing or behind baseboards where airflow is poor. We contain the area, remove affected material, and verify the space before closing it back up.

Biohazard and trauma cleanup, along with storm and wind damage response for roofs and siding after a severe weather event, round out the emergency side of our work. We also handle the reconstruction that follows: framing, drywall, flooring, and paint, so the same crew that dried the structure can rebuild it.
