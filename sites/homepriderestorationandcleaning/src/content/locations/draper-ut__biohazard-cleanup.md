## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Draper

We start with a scene assessment to confirm the scope and identify any sharps, needles, or other infectious materials that need specialized disposal under OSHA bloodborne pathogen guidelines. From there we set containment barriers, remove and bag affected porous materials like carpet, padding, and in some cases subfloor sections, and treat non-porous surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants. Odor and contamination can travel into HVAC returns in homes with forced-air systems, which is common throughout Draper's newer developments, so we check ductwork before closing out a job. Every step is documented with photos for insurance and, where applicable, for the property manager or HOA board that needs confirmation the unit is safe to re-occupy.
