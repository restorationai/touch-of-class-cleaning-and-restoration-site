## Services we provide in Prospect

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Prospect

Much of Prospect's housing dates to the mid-20th century, with wood-frame construction, basement or crawlspace foundations, and plumbing that ranges from original galvanized pipe to later copper and PEX retrofits. Galvanized lines corrode from the inside and tend to fail at joints, which is where a lot of our water calls in older Butler County homes originate. Homes built before the 1980s may also carry asbestos-containing materials in flooring, siding, or pipe insulation, which has to be accounted for before any demolition begins.

Butler County's clay-heavy soil holds water and drains slowly, which puts real pressure on foundation walls and basement waterproofing after sustained rain. Structural repairs and rebuild work in Prospect go through the borough's permitting process under Pennsylvania's Uniform Construction Code, and any load-bearing or electrical work tied to a restoration project needs to clear that review before it's closed up.
