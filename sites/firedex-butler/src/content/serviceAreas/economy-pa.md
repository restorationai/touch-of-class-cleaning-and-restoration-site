## Restoration emergencies common in Economy

Economy sits in the Ohio River valley in Beaver County, where hillside lots, clay-heavy soil, and a mix of older farmhouses and mid-century ranch homes create a predictable set of problems. Heavy spring rain and fast snowmelt push groundwater against basement walls built decades before modern waterproofing was standard, and foundation seepage is one of the most common calls we get in this part of the county. Winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that crack older clay drain tile and stress galvanized supply lines, and ice damming along roof edges after a heavy snow is a recurring source of water intrusion in attics and upper-floor ceilings. Summer humidity turns any undetected leak into a mold concern within days rather than weeks, which is why fast extraction and drying matter as much as the initial cleanup.
