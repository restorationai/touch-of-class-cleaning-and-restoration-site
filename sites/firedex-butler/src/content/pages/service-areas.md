FireDEX Butler is based in Cranberry Township and dispatches around the clock — any time a pipe bursts, a fire tears through a kitchen, or a basement fills overnight, the team is reachable at (724) 452-7400. Coverage spans a wide corridor of western Pennsylvania, from the northern Pittsburgh suburbs through Butler County and into the surrounding communities. When you call, a dispatcher confirms your address and gets a crew moving; no answering service, no waiting until morning.

## Where we work

The crew operates out of Cranberry Township, which sits near the intersection of I-79 and PA-228 — a location that puts most of Butler County and the northern Allegheny County suburbs within a reasonable drive in either direction. The service area runs north toward the borough of Butler and beyond, south into the communities that border Pittsburgh's North Hills, east along the PA-8 corridor, and west into Lawrence County. Western Pennsylvania's mix of older housing stock — brick colonials, mid-century ranches, post-war cape cods — means the team regularly handles the kinds of losses those structures are prone to: ice-dam leaks, aging cast-iron drain failures, and smoke damage that works deep into plaster walls.

## Coverage by distance

Locations closest to Cranberry Township — Mars, Adams Township, Zelienople, Harmony, and the northern Pittsburgh suburbs like Wexford and Gibsonia — are typically reached first. From there, coverage extends to the borough of Butler and communities like Saxonburg, Evans City, and Slippery Rock to the north, and toward Allison Park and Hampton Township to the south. The outer edges of the coverage area include parts of Lawrence County to the west and Mercer County to the north. Travel times naturally stretch with distance, and during peak traffic on I-79 or PA-8, a crew heading toward Pittsburgh's northern edge will take longer than one rolling to a job two miles away. Dispatch will give you an honest arrival window when you call — not a number pulled from a brochure.

## Don't see your city?

The listed service areas cover the most common calls, but they aren't a hard boundary. For significant losses — a commercial building, a multi-unit property, or a large residential claim — it's worth a quick call to (724) 452-7400 regardless of where you are. Dispatch can tell you within minutes whether a crew can reach you and what to expect.
