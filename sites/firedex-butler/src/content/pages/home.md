## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in a Cranberry Township colonial or a flooded basement after a heavy Allegheny County rain event, standing water can reach wall cavities and subfloor within hours. We extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces long after the flames are out. We handle soot removal, odor neutralization, and structural repairs so the smell of that fire doesn't follow you for years.

**Mold remediation** — Mold can begin colonizing damp materials in as little as 24 to 48 hours. If you've had a recent leak or you're noticing that musty, earthy odor in a bathroom or crawl space, a prompt assessment matters.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Western Pennsylvania storms hit hard — wind, hail, ice dams in winter, heavy rain in spring. We board up, tarp, and stabilize immediately, then carry the project through full reconstruction so you're dealing with one company, not three.
