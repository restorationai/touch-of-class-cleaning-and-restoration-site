## Our Mold Remediation Process in Wexford

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — moisture meters, thermal imaging, and air sampling where warranted — so we know exactly where the mold colony ends before we cut a single piece of drywall. In Wexford's high-finish basements, that boundary matters enormously: unnecessary demolition means replacing custom millwork and matching rare tile, which drives costs up and extends your displacement.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using heavy-gauge poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating into the living areas above while remediation is underway. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, framing if necessary — are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to Pennsylvania DEP guidelines. Structural surfaces are then HEPA-vacuumed and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Drying equipment stays in place until moisture readings confirm the assembly has returned to normal levels, and we document every reading so your insurance adjuster has a complete picture of the work performed.
