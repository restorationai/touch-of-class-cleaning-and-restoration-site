## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Seven Fields

A thorough mold assessment is not a visual walk-through with a flashlight. The inspection begins with a moisture mapping pass using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to identify wet or previously wet building materials — including areas behind finished walls where no visible staining exists. That step matters especially in Seven Fields townhomes, where a loss in a shared-wall cavity may not produce obvious surface signs on either side.

Once moisture sources are identified, air sampling captures the spore load in affected rooms and compares it against an outdoor baseline collected at the same time. Surface sampling — tape lifts or swabs — can confirm species and concentration on specific materials. All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory; results typically return within 24 to 48 hours. The written report documents findings with photographs, moisture readings, sample locations, and lab results in a format that insurance adjusters and, where applicable, HOA property managers can act on directly.
