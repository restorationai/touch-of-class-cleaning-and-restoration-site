## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Beaver Falls

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification — and it requires a different response than a clean-water pipe burst. The moment our crew arrives, the priority is containment: stopping cross-contamination from spreading sewage-affected materials into unaffected areas of the home.

From there, the process moves through several concrete stages:

- **Extraction and removal** — standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted equipment, and porous materials that cannot be decontaminated (drywall, insulation, carpet) are removed and bagged for disposal per Pennsylvania DEP guidelines.
- **Hard-surface disinfection** — concrete floors, block walls, and framing lumber are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination. Stone foundation walls common in older Beaver Falls basements require multiple applications because the porous surface holds contaminants.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — industrial air movers and desiccant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. River-valley humidity means ambient moisture levels in Beaver Falls basements are already elevated, so this stage often runs longer than in drier climates.
- **Post-remediation verification** — surface samples and moisture readings confirm the space is clean and dry before any reconstruction begins.
