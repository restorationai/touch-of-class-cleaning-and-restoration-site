## Our Biohazard and Trauma Cleanup Process in Cranberry Township

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. Privacy is not an afterthought — it is built into how we schedule and execute the work.

Once on-site, our team assesses the full scope of affected materials: flooring, wall cavities, HVAC pathways, and any adjacent spaces where contamination may have migrated. We contain the work area using negative air pressure and physical barriers, then remove and properly package all regulated biological waste for transport to a licensed disposal facility in compliance with Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection requirements.
