## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Mars

The first priority when we arrive is stopping active water intrusion if it hasn't stopped on its own, then mapping the full extent of saturation — not just what's visible. Water migrates. In the older homes near Mars Borough, it travels through mortar joints and along floor joists in ways that don't show up until a moisture meter goes to work. From there, the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and content evaluation in a deliberate sequence:

- **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from floors, crawlspaces, and wall cavities.
- **Drying** — industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers combined with high-velocity air movers are positioned based on moisture mapping, not guesswork. Drying logs are kept for every job — documentation your insurance adjuster will ask for.

- **Material evaluation** — we identify what can be dried in place versus what needs removal. Saturated insulation, compromised subfloor, and wicking drywall sections are documented before any demolition begins.
