## Our Frozen Pipe Restoration Process in Mars

Restoration after a frozen pipe isn't just about drying — it starts with understanding exactly where the water went. When we arrive at a Mars address, we begin with thermal imaging and moisture mapping across every affected cavity. Water from a burst supply line in a finished basement doesn't stay put; it follows framing bays, migrates under LVP flooring, and wicks into drywall from the bottom up, sometimes traveling eight to ten feet from the actual break point.

Once we've mapped the moisture, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then place drying equipment — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — calibrated to the material types in your home. Older plaster walls in Mars Borough homes release moisture more slowly than modern drywall, so drying timelines in those structures typically run longer and require more frequent moisture checks before we can sign off. We document every reading throughout the process, which matters both for your insurance adjuster and for confirming the structure is genuinely dry before any rebuild begins.
