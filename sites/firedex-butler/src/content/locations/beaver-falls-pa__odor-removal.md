## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Beaver Falls

Effective odor removal isn't a single step — it's a sequence calibrated to what the structure absorbed and how long it's been sitting. Here's how we approach it in Beaver Falls properties:

In the older rowhomes on the numbered avenues, that sometimes means carefully extracting original tongue-and-groove subfloor that has absorbed sewage or smoke rather than trying to treat through it.

**Thermal fogging** penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled — into wall voids, behind baseboards, through HVAC ductwork. **Hydroxyl deodorization** runs continuously in occupied or semi-occupied spaces where ozone treatment isn't safe. **Ozone treatment** is reserved for severe cases — heavy smoke odors in vacant structures, or persistent musty odors in unoccupied basement spaces. Ozone is powerful and requires the space to be completely unoccupied during treatment and properly ventilated afterward.

**Post-treatment verification** closes the job. We don't sign off on a deodorization based on how the air smells to us after hours of working in it. We air out the space, return, and evaluate with fresh senses — and we document the process for insurance purposes.
