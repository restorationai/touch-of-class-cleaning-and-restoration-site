## Our Frozen Pipe Restoration Process in Beaver Falls

Frozen pipe restoration is not just a plumbing call — it is a water damage event the moment the pipe thaws or bursts. The sequence matters. First, we locate and document the break, assess water migration through walls, floors, and subfloor assemblies, and establish where moisture has traveled. In older plaster-and-lath construction common on College Hill, water wicks laterally through wall cavities in ways that modern drywall does not, which means the wet zone is almost always larger than it looks from the surface.

Once the source is controlled — coordinating with a licensed plumber if the line repair itself is needed — we extract standing water, then set drying equipment calibrated to the building's actual conditions.
