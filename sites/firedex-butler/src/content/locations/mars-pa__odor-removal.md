## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Mars

Effective odor elimination is a sequence, not a single treatment. We begin with a source assessment — locating the origin of the odor rather than masking it — because applying deodorization chemistry to an unresolved moisture problem only delays the inevitable. Once the source is confirmed and any damaged material is removed or dried, we select the appropriate technology for the structure.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective in older Mars Borough homes where odor molecules have penetrated plaster, original hardwood subfloors, and the mortar joints in stone foundation walls. The fog follows the same pathways smoke or sewer gas traveled, neutralizing odor at the molecular level rather than coating surfaces.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** is our preferred method for occupied or semi-occupied spaces and for newer construction in Adams Township where synthetic materials — carpet, engineered flooring, foam insulation — are more prevalent. Hydroxyl generators are safe to run while personal property remains in the home, which matters when a family can't relocate for multiple days.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with extreme odor loads — a fire-damaged garage, a long-vacant rental, or a vehicle. We never apply ozone in occupied structures, and we document clearance before re-entry.

For smoke odor removal specifically, we follow thermal fogging with a HEPA-filtered air scrubbing pass and treat HVAC returns and supply boots separately, because smoke residue inside ductwork will re-contaminate a cleaned space every time the system cycles.
