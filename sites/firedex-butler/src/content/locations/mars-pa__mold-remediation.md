## Why Mars Properties See Mold Issues

Mars sits at an interesting intersection of old and new construction, and that contrast shapes the mold risks here more than in many surrounding communities. In Mars Borough proper and along the Callery fringe, homes built before 1960 often have rubble-stone or poured-concrete foundations with no modern waterproofing membrane. When spring rains saturate the hillside soils above Breakneck Creek, hydrostatic pressure forces moisture through hairline cracks that have existed for decades. That seepage rarely announces itself dramatically — it shows up as a musty smell in the basement, a chalky white efflorescence on the block wall, or a soft spot in the carpet near the sump crock.

On the other side of the Route 228 corridor, the newer tract and semi-custom homes in Adams Ridge and Adams Township tell a different story. Builder-grade supply lines and appliance connections — refrigerator ice makers, dishwashers, washing machine hoses — are statistically most likely to fail between years three and eight of a home's life. When a slow leak goes undetected under a finished basement floor, the OSB subfloor and faced insulation behind the drywall can harbor active mold growth for months before the homeowner notices discoloration or odor.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Mars

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not just a visual scan. We use thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to trace water migration through walls and floors, because visible mold is almost always the trailing edge of a larger moisture plume. In the older homes near Mars Borough and Adams Township Community Park, that often means checking the rim joist cavity and the base of interior partition walls where cold-air infiltration meets warm interior humidity in winter.

Once the scope is defined, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected living spaces. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet padding — are removed, double-bagged, and disposed of per Pennsylvania DEP guidelines. Structural wood surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We don't close out a job until clearance readings confirm the remediated area is back within normal ambient ranges.

## Mars Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Butler County vary significantly depending on the source of moisture. Insurance carriers typically cover mold remediation when it's a direct result of a covered sudden-and-accidental water loss — a burst pipe, a failed water heater, storm-driven roof intrusion. They frequently dispute or exclude claims tied to long-term seepage, which is exactly the pattern common in the older foundation homes along Breakneck Creek. We document the loss thoroughly from day one: moisture readings, thermal images, written scope, and photo logs that give your adjuster a clear chain of evidence. We bill most major carriers directly and walk you through the supplement process if the initial estimate falls short.
