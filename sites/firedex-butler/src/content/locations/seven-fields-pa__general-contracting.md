## Why Seven Fields Homes Have a Specific Rebuild Profile

Almost every home in 16046 was built between the late 1980s and the 2000s, which means the housing stock shares a narrow band of construction methods: engineered floor systems, OSB sheathing, open-cell insulation in some later builds, and a heavy reliance on finished lower levels. That's a meaningful detail when you're scoping a renovation or a post-damage rebuild. Engineered lumber reacts to moisture differently than dimensional lumber — it can delaminate rather than simply warp — and OSB loses structural integrity faster when wet. A scope that looks like a cosmetic flooring replacement can turn into a subfloor and joist-repair project once the layers come up. We price and plan for what we find, not just what the surface shows.

Townhome and patio-home clusters near Brandywine Park add another layer of complexity. Shared-wall construction means a loss in one unit can migrate to an adjacent neighbor before anyone notices. When the rebuild phase begins, wall assemblies that cross property lines require careful coordination — sometimes with the neighboring owner, sometimes with the HOA — before framing and insulation work can close up.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Seven Fields

Every project starts with a detailed scope walkthrough.
