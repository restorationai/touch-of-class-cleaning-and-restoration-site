## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Wexford

The first priority on any sewage call is containment. Before any extraction begins, the source has to be confirmed stopped — whether that means coordinating with a plumber on a sewer line backup or isolating a failed septic connection. Once the flow is stopped, the work moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Extraction and removal**: Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units. Saturated materials — carpet, pad, drywall, insulation, cabinetry — are assessed and removed if they cannot be reliably sanitized. In Wexford's finished basements, that often means custom millwork and built-in cabinetry; we document everything photographically before any demolition for insurance purposes.
- **Sanitization**: All affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Category 3 water (raw sewage) requires a more aggressive sanitization protocol than a clean-water loss — there is no shortcut here, and the distinction matters when an adjuster reviews the scope.
- **Structural drying**: Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers are placed to dry the structural assembly, not just the surface. Concrete slab floors under finished basement spaces hold moisture longer than they appear to, and drying is monitored with moisture meters until readings return to baseline.
- **Clearance and documentation**: Before any rebuild begins, we provide a written drying log and photo documentation — the kind of file that supports a strong insurance claim and satisfies any contractor starting the reconstruction phase.
