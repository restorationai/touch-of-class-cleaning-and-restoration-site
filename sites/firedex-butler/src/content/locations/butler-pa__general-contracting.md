## Why Butler's Building Stock Shapes Every Renovation

The age of Butler's housing is not just a curiosity — it directly affects scope, budget, and timeline on almost every job. Plaster-and-lath walls are common on Institute Hill and throughout the older blocks near the Main Street business district, and they behave differently from modern drywall: they're heavier, they require different fastener patterns, and when water has touched them — from a roof leak, a failed supply line, or flooding off a Connoquenessing Creek tributary — they hold moisture far longer than drywall does.
