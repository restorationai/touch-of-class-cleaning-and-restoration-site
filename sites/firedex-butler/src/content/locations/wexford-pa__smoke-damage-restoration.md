## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Wexford

Smoke damage restoration is not the same as cleaning. Soot is chemically active — it continues to etch glass, corrode metal, and stain porous surfaces for days after a fire is out. The process has to move in a specific sequence to avoid spreading contamination further.

When we arrive, the first priority is containment: sealing HVAC registers and isolating affected zones so residue doesn't migrate to unaffected areas. We then conduct a full structural assessment — walls, ceilings, subflooring, and inside duct runs — before any cleaning begins. In the large estate homes common to Wexford, that assessment often turns up smoke penetration two or three rooms away from the fire origin.

Dry chemical sponge cleaning removes loose soot from walls and ceilings without smearing. Wet cleaning follows on non-porous surfaces. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation address odor molecules that have bonded to soft goods, insulation, and wood framing — the step that most distinguishes professional restoration from a surface wipe-down. Contents in home theaters and finished lower levels are inventoried, packed out if necessary, and cleaned off-site when on-site conditions aren't suitable. We document every step with photographs and written logs, which matters significantly for the thorough insurance claims that Wexford homeowners typically carry.
