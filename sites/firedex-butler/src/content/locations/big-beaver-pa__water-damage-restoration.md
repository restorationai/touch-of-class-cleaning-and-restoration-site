## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Big Beaver

The first visit is about finding where the water actually went, not just where it's visible. In a crawlspace home that means getting under the house with moisture meters and thermal imaging to check joists and subfloor, not just pulling baseboards in the living room. We extract standing water with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on access, then set up air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space, drying materials to the benchmarks laid out in the industry's S500 structural drying standard rather than guessing at a timeline. Homes with well water systems get an extra step: we check whether the pressure tank, pump, or line itself caused the loss, because restoring the structure without addressing the source just means a repeat call in a few months.
