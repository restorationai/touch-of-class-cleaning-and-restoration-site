## Why Mars Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The older blocks of Mars Borough sit on stone foundations with original cast-iron drain stacks that have been in the ground for 80 to 100 years. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out, and by the time a homeowner notices a slow drain, the pipe wall may already be partially collapsed or heavily scaled with mineral deposits. A single heavy rain event — especially when Breakneck Creek rises and the water table climbs — can push enough hydrostatic pressure into those aging laterals to force sewage backward through floor drains and basement fixtures.

The picture is different a few miles east in Adams Ridge and along the newer semi-custom developments off Three Degree Road. There, builder-era PVC plumbing is generally sound, but finished basements with full bathrooms sit directly below grade. When a supply line or appliance connection fails and water infiltrates a drain system already under stress, the finished space takes the full impact — carpet, drywall, insulation, and cabinetry all absorbing Category 3 contaminated water before the homeowner realizes what has happened.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Mars

Every sewage call starts the same way: we identify and isolate the source before anything else is touched. If the sewer line is still actively backing up, cleanup is temporary until the line is cleared — we coordinate with licensed plumbers when that step is needed so the remediation work is not undone.

Once the source is controlled, the process moves through these stages:

- **Extraction and removal** — Standing sewage water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable units. Saturated materials — carpet, pad, drywall, insulation — that cannot be adequately disinfected are removed and bagged for disposal per Pennsylvania DEP guidelines for Category 3 waste.
- **Disinfection and sanitization** — All affected hard surfaces, framing, and subfloor are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We pay particular attention to the porous stone foundation walls common in older Mars Borough homes, which can harbor contamination long after visible sewage is removed.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in walls and subfloor return to baseline. In stone-foundation homes, this phase routinely takes longer than in modern construction because masonry releases absorbed moisture slowly.
- **Documentation** — Moisture logs, photo documentation, and a written scope are produced throughout. This record is what your insurance adjuster needs and what protects you if questions arise later.
