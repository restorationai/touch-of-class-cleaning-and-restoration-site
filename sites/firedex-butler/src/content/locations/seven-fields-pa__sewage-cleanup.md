## Why Seven Fields Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Seven Fields is one of Pennsylvania's youngest boroughs, developed rapidly between Cranberry and Mars starting in the late 1980s. Washing-machine supply hoses, dishwasher drain lines, and water heater connections installed in the early 1990s are now approaching or past their expected service life. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Seven Fields

When we arrive at a Seven Fields address, the first priority is containment. Sewage-contaminated water is classified as Category 3 — the most hazardous water type — and it requires full personal protective equipment, physical containment barriers, and controlled airflow before any extraction begins.

Here's how the process unfolds on a typical Seven Fields job:

1. **Assessment and containment** — We identify the source, stop active flow if possible, and establish containment zones to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms.
2. **Extraction** — Truck-mounted extraction equipment removes standing water and saturated material. In finished basements common to Brandywine and Castlebrook homes, this often means pulling carpet, pad, and sometimes drywall that has absorbed contaminated water below the visible waterline.
3. **Removal of unsalvageable materials** — Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 water — drywall, insulation, carpet — are bagged, documented, and removed per Pennsylvania solid waste handling guidelines.
4. **Antimicrobial treatment** — All affected structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. This step is not optional or cosmetic; it's what separates a true sanitization from a surface clean.
5. **Drying and monitoring** — Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in walls, subfloor, and framing return to acceptable baselines. We monitor daily and document readings for your insurance file.
6. **Clearance documentation** — Before we close out, you receive a written scope, photo documentation, and moisture logs — the same package your adjuster will ask for.
