## Our Soot Removal Process in Seven Fields

When we arrive at a Seven Fields address, the first step is a room-by-room inspection to map how far smoke traveled — not just where the fire burned. In open-plan homes, soot often deposits heaviest in the coldest corners of rooms far from the fire origin, because smoke follows air currents and settles where temperatures drop. We photograph and document every affected surface before any cleaning begins, which also creates the record your insurance adjuster will need.

Surface cleaning is matched to material type. Flat latex paint on drywall — the standard finish throughout most Seven Fields construction — requires dry chemical sponges first to lift loose soot without smearing, followed by alkaline cleaning solutions that break down the oily binders in smoke residue. Cabinetry with factory finishes, common in the kitchens of homes near the Seven Fields Community Center neighborhood, gets a different protocol: gentler solvents that clean without lifting the clear coat. HVAC registers and ductwork are cleaned separately, because recirculating air through a contaminated system undoes surface work almost immediately.

Odor neutralization is the final and often most underestimated step. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment reaches into wall cavities, subflooring gaps, and the interior of ductwork where surface wiping cannot. We don't consider a job complete until the odor source is addressed at the molecular level — not masked.
