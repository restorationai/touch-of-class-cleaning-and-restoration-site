## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Gibsonia

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification — which means the work is sequenced carefully, not just mopped up.

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean portions of the home. In a finished Gibsonia basement, that typically means establishing a barrier at the stairwell before anything else moves.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage and saturated porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall, insulation — are removed. Category 3 losses do not allow for drying porous materials in place; contaminated drywall comes out to the flood cut, period.

**Disinfection and sanitization.** After bulk removal, all affected hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We pay particular attention to concrete block foundations common in Richland Township construction, which are porous and can harbor bacteria deeper than the surface.

**Drying and documentation.** Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry. Every step is photographed and logged for your insurance claim.
