## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Mars

The first thing we do on arrival is stop the loss. If the homeowner hasn't already shut the main, we locate it and close it — in older Mars Borough homes that sometimes means tracing a line back to a buried curb stop near the street, which takes a few extra minutes but prevents a bad situation from getting worse. Once the water source is controlled, we do a full moisture mapping of the affected area using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water migrates farther than it looks: a burst line in a first-floor wall can wick into subfloor framing, travel along joists, and show up as elevated moisture readings in a room two doors away.

Extraction comes next — truck-mounted or portable units depending on access — followed by the placement of commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the specific materials present. Plaster walls common in older borough homes release moisture more slowly than modern drywall and require longer dry cycles; we adjust drying targets accordingly rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. Structural repairs — framing, drywall, insulation, and the pipe itself — are coordinated once moisture readings confirm the structure is dry enough to close up without trapping residual humidity.
