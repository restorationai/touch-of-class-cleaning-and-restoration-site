## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Evans City

We arrive with personal protective equipment, containment supplies, and extraction equipment rated for Category 3 water — the classification that covers raw sewage, which carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that require a different protocol than a clean-water pipe burst.

The sequence on a typical Evans City job looks like this:

1. **Contain and assess.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into living spaces. In older homes with open-joist basements, sewage aerosols travel — containment barriers matter before extraction begins.
2. **Remove standing waste and saturated materials.** Porous materials that have contacted Category 3 water — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — are bagged and removed. There is no safe way to dry and retain them.
3. **Extract residual moisture.** Truck-mounted or portable extraction units pull moisture from concrete, block, and any salvageable structural wood.
4. **Apply EPA-registered disinfectants.** We treat all affected surfaces with disinfectants appropriate for biological contamination, with dwell times that actually match the pathogen kill requirements — not a quick spray-and-wipe.
5. **Dry and document.** Desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to acceptable baselines. Every reading is logged for your insurance file.
