## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Seven Fields

Effective odor elimination isn't a single step — it's a sequence calibrated to what the odor molecule is, where it has migrated, and what building materials are holding it. When we arrive at a Seven Fields address, we start with a thorough source assessment: locating char, residue, or contamination that is still off-gassing before any treatment begins. Masking an active source only delays the problem.

From there, the method depends on the odor type and the home's construction:

- **Thermal fogging** — a solvent-based fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, bonding with odor particles inside wall cavities, ductwork, and subfloor spaces. Particularly effective after kitchen and structural fires in the enclosed floor plans common to Seven Fields townhomes.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — safe for occupied or semi-occupied spaces, hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor compounds at the molecular level without requiring building evacuation. Useful when homeowners need to remain on-site or return quickly.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is one of the most effective tools for severe smoke odor in unoccupied structures, oxidizing residue embedded in porous materials. We follow strict re-entry protocols and air-out periods before clearance.
- **HVAC and duct deodorization** — because Seven Fields homes recirculate air tightly, treating the duct system is rarely optional after a smoke or sewage event. Odor left in ductwork will redistribute through the home every time the system runs.

We document conditions before and after treatment, which matters when you're coordinating with a homeowner's insurance carrier.
