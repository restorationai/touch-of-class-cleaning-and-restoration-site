## Why Butler Properties See Basement Flooding More Often

Butler is a pre-1950 city in almost every sense that matters for water damage. The steep hillside neighborhoods — Institute Hill especially — channel stormwater downhill toward lower-elevation foundations that were never designed to handle modern rainfall volumes. Many of those same homes still run galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside out, producing slow leaks that go unnoticed until a section finally lets go. Cast-iron drain stacks, common in Lyndora's older housing, can crack under root intrusion or freeze-thaw stress, turning a minor backup into a sewage event.
