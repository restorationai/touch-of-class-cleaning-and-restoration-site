## Our Mold Remediation Process in Gibsonia

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping — we use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the visible stain, because in a finished Richland Township basement, mold behind a framed wall cavity won't show on the surface until the colony is well established.

Once we know the scope, we build containment. Negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered exhaust units keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during demolition and cleaning. Affected drywall, insulation, and framing that can't be cleaned to bare substrate gets removed and bagged for disposal — there's no shortcut here that holds up over time.

Cleaning uses EPA-registered antimicrobial agents applied to all affected structural surfaces. After cleaning, we run commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers until moisture readings in the structural materials return to acceptable baselines. We document those readings before any reconstruction begins, so you have a paper trail if the insurance carrier or a future buyer asks for it.
