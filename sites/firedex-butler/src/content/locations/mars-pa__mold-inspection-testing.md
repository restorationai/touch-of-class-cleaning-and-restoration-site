## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Mars

A mold inspection here isn't a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. The process starts with a moisture mapping survey — we use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate elevated readings behind surfaces that look dry to the eye. In older Mars Borough homes, we pay particular attention to the sill plate zone where the wood framing meets the foundation, and to any area within 20 feet of a cast-iron drain stack.

Once moisture anomalies are mapped, we collect air samples from affected zones and an outdoor baseline sample for comparison. If surface growth is visible or suspected behind materials, we take tape-lift or bulk samples as well. All samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory — results typically return within 24 to 72 hours. The written report identifies the species present, the spore counts relative to outdoor baseline, and a recommended scope of work if remediation is warranted. We do not perform remediation on the same job as the inspection, which keeps our assessment objective.
