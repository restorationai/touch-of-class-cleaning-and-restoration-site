## Why Evans City Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The Breakneck Creek valley concentrates storm runoff in ways that catch homeowners off guard. Skipping that step and tiling over a wet slab or framing new walls against a damp foundation is a mistake that shows up two winters later.

Beyond flood exposure, the housing stock itself shapes every project. Most homes in Evans City Borough and the surrounding Forward Township predate 1950. ## Local Note: What Plaster Walls Mean for Your Remodel Timeline

One thing worth knowing before you start a renovation in an older Evans City Borough home: original plaster-and-lath walls hold moisture differently than modern drywall. If there's been any water intrusion — even a slow, long-term seep from a foundation crack or a roof leak that was patched years ago — the plaster can carry elevated moisture levels that aren't visible on the surface. We take readings before we demo and before we close up new walls. In homes near the lower blocks of the Breakneck Creek valley, we find elevated readings more often than not. Catching it before the drywall goes up costs almost nothing; catching it after costs a full redo.
