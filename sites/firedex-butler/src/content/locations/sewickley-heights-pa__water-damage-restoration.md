## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sewickley Heights

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted and portable units before it migrates further under flooring or into wall cavities. On stone and masonry foundations, we check moisture readings well past the visible wet line, because dense old masonry holds moisture and releases it slowly, which changes how long structural drying actually takes. Hardwood and original plaster get special attention too: plaster absorbs water quickly but gives it up gradually, so a room can look dry on the surface while the lath behind it is still saturated. We set up dehumidification and air movement calibrated to the materials in the house rather than a generic drying schedule, then verify dryness with moisture meters before we close the job out.
