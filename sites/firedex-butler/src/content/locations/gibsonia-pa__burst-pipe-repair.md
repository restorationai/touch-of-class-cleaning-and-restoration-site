## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Gibsonia

The first thing we do when we arrive is locate the shutoff — not always straightforward in homes on private wells where the main shutoff may be at the pressure tank, the wellhead, or both. Once water is controlled, we assess the full spread of the loss. Water from a burst pipe in a finished basement travels fast under luxury vinyl plank and laminate flooring, and it pools behind knee walls and inside stud cavities long before it's visible on the surface. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the actual wet boundary, not just the visible one.

From there, the work follows a structured drying protocol: extract standing water, remove saturated materials that won't dry in place (typically carpet pad, lower sections of drywall, and insulation), then deploy commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the cubic footage of the affected space. We monitor readings daily and don't close out a job until the structure is back to baseline moisture levels. Pipe repair or replacement — whether that's a section of copper, PEX, or a supply-line fitting — is handled as part of the same scope so you're not coordinating a second contractor.
