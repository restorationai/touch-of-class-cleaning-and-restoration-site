## Our Soot Removal Process in Beaver Falls

When a call comes in from a College Hill address or anywhere along the Route 18 corridor, the first thing our crew does on arrival is categorize the soot type — dry smoke, wet smoke, or protein residue from a kitchen fire — because each requires a different chemical approach. Wet smoke, far more common in the older Beaver Falls housing stock where fires tend to smolder in wall cavities or around aging insulation, requires alkaline-based cleaning agents applied in controlled passes to avoid driving residue deeper into plaster.
