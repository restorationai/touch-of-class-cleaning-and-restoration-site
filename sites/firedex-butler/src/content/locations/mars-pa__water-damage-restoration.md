## Why Mars Properties See Water Damage Issues

Mars sits at an interesting fault line between old and new construction. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Mars

When we arrive at a 16046 address, the first step is moisture mapping — not a visual inspection, but thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters that trace water migration through walls and subfloors. In the older borough homes, water often travels further than it appears: it follows the path of least resistance along floor joists, pooling in a corner of the basement while the source is a failed fitting two rooms away.

From there, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

- **Water extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard and soft surfaces before drying equipment is staged.
- **Structural drying** — industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the cubic footage and material types in the affected space. Stone foundation walls and plaster require different drying targets than OSB sheathing and drywall.
- **Monitoring** — we return on a set schedule to read moisture levels and adjust equipment until materials reach documented dry standard.
- **Controlled demolition if needed** — saturated drywall, wet insulation, or compromised flooring is removed before mold has a chance to establish. We document everything photographically, which matters when you're filing an insurance claim.
