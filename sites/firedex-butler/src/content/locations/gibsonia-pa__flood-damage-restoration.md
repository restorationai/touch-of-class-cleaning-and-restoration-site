## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Gibsonia

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected space — not just the visible wet area, but the subfloor cavity, wall assemblies, and any adjacent rooms that share framing bays. In a typical Treesdale-area home with a finished lower level, that means pulling back carpet and pad, probing the concrete slab for moisture migration, and checking the base of any framed partition walls before a single dehumidifier is placed.

Once the scope is documented, we extract standing water, set commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the room volume, and monitor drying progress on a structured schedule — not just a single check-in. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place (saturated insulation, swollen OSB subfloor panels, compromised drywall below the flood line) are removed and documented for your insurance file.
