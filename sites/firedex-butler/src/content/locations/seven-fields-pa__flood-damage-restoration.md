## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Seven Fields

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected structure. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly where water has traveled — behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, beneath flooring — before any equipment goes down. In Seven Fields homes with finished lower levels, that step routinely reveals saturation in areas that look dry to the eye.

Once we've mapped the loss, we extract standing water and begin strategic demolition of materials that cannot be dried in place: saturated drywall, soaked carpet and pad, compromised insulation. We set industrial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the volume of the affected space, then monitor moisture readings daily against drying targets. Nothing gets closed up until the structure reads dry.

For townhome and patio-home losses, we document the boundary of the water migration carefully — both for the insurance claim and to give the HOA or neighboring unit owner a clear picture of what was affected and what wasn't.
