## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Zelienople

Every job starts with a moisture inspection before any equipment is placed. In Zelienople's older homes, that means probing plaster walls, checking subfloor assemblies under original hardwood, and testing stone foundation walls for saturation depth — steps that matter more here than in a newer build with standard drywall and concrete block.

From there, the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and verification:

- **Water extraction** removes standing water from floors, crawl spaces, and basement slabs using truck-mounted and portable extraction units.
- **Structural drying** uses calibrated air movers and dehumidifiers positioned to pull moisture out of wall cavities, floor assemblies, and framing. In plaster-wall construction, drying cycles typically run longer than in modern drywall homes because dense plaster releases absorbed moisture more slowly.
- **Moisture mapping** tracks readings across multiple days so drying is confirmed, not assumed. We document readings throughout the job — useful for insurance claims and for your own peace of mind.
- **Content and materials assessment** identifies what can be dried in place, what needs to be moved, and what has to be removed to prevent secondary damage.

We work directly with most major insurance carriers, handling documentation and billing so you're not managing paperwork while your home is being dried out.
