## Our Mold Remediation Process in Cranberry Township

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration paths — especially important in finished basements where the actual wet zone often extends well beyond the visible staining.

Once we understand the scope, we establish containment using negative air pressure and physical barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the project. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Pennsylvania DEP guidelines. Structural surfaces are treated and dried to measured moisture thresholds before any rebuilding begins.

Documentation runs parallel to every step: photos, moisture readings, and written scope notes that your insurance adjuster can work from directly.
