## Why Evans City Homes Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock along Evans City Main Street and throughout Evans City Borough skews heavily toward 1900–1950s construction — balloon-frame walls with no vapor barrier, plaster-over-lath interiors, and original cast-iron drains that back up when Breakneck Creek swells during a summer cloudburst. ## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Evans City

Effective deodorization in a pre-1950 Evans City home requires matching the method to the material — there is no single-tool answer.

**Thermal fogging** is often the right starting point after a fire or smoke event. The fogging solution is heated into a dry vapor that penetrates the same pores and cavities smoke traveled through, chemically pairing with odor molecules rather than masking them. In balloon-frame construction, where smoke channels up through wall cavities with almost no resistance, thermal fogging reaches areas that surface wiping never could.

**Hydroxyl generation** runs continuously in occupied or semi-occupied spaces where re-entry is needed quickly. Hydroxyl generators produce the same oxidizing radicals the atmosphere uses to break down pollutants — no evacuation required, safe around electronics and fabrics, and effective over longer treatment windows.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe, embedded odors — a sealed basement after a sewage backup, or a room with heavy char residue. Ozone at treatment concentrations is not safe for occupancy, so we coordinate access timing carefully, particularly in attached homes or duplexes common along the Route 68 corridor.

After any treatment, we verify results with calibrated air sampling rather than a nose check. If the odor reading hasn't dropped to an acceptable threshold, we treat again before closing the job.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The equipment list matters because different odor problems require different physics:

- **Hydroxyl generators** — broad-spectrum, safe for occupied spaces, effective for mold and smoke odors over 24–72 hour treatment windows
- **Thermal foggers** — penetrating dry-vapor treatment for post-fire smoke in porous substrates and wall cavities
- **Ozone generators** — high-concentration treatment for unoccupied spaces with severe organic or char odors
- **Air scrubbers with HEPA and activated carbon filtration** — capture particulates and VOCs during and after treatment
- **Moisture meters and thermal imaging** — confirm that hidden moisture sources driving mold odor are identified before treatment begins

For Evans City's older homes, we often combine methods: thermal fogging the wall cavities, running hydroxyl generation in the living areas, and using ozone in a sealed crawlspace or basement — sequenced so each zone is treated appropriately without unnecessary re-entry delays.
