## Our Frozen Pipe Restoration Process in Seven Fields

When we arrive at a 16046 address, the first priority is confirming the water source is fully isolated — either at the main shutoff or at the fixture valve — before any drying equipment goes in. From there the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and material assessment:

- **Extraction**: Standing water comes out with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment. In finished basements with carpet over concrete, we pull carpet and pad to expose the slab and prevent secondary mold growth.
- **Structural drying**: High-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers are positioned according to the affected square footage. In shared-wall townhomes, we document moisture readings on the party wall so the neighboring unit's owner and their insurer have a clear record.
- **Material assessment**: Drywall, insulation, and cabinetry are evaluated for salvageability. Wet insulation in exterior wall cavities — a common finding after a pipe freeze — almost always needs to come out, because it won't dry in place and it accelerates mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Monitoring and sign-off**: We re-check moisture readings over the drying cycle and don't close out the job until materials reach acceptable levels.
