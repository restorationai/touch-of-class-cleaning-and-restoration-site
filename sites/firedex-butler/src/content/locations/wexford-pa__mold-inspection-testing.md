## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Wexford

A thorough mold assessment here isn't a single air sample and a handshake. The process starts with a visual inspection of the areas most likely to harbor growth: sump pits and utility rooms, the perimeter walls of walk-out basements, the framing cavities above garage ceilings, and any space that has experienced a prior water event. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help locate elevated readings behind finished surfaces without unnecessary demolition.

From there, we collect samples calibrated to what the inspection reveals. Surface samples (tape lifts or swabs) identify the genus and concentration of mold present at a specific location. Air samples — taken both inside the suspect area and outside as a baseline — measure airborne spore counts and compare them to ambient outdoor levels. In homes with home theaters or tightly sealed media rooms, that indoor-to-outdoor comparison is especially telling, because those spaces trap and concentrate spores that would otherwise disperse. All samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory, and you receive a written report with species identification, spore counts, and a clear summary of findings.
