## Restoration emergencies common in Nevada

Nevada sits in the Blackland Prairie belt of North Texas, where heavy clay soil expands when it's wet and shrinks hard when it's dry. That cycle stresses slab foundations and the plumbing lines running through them, which is why we see a steady run of slab leaks and foundation-related water intrusion here, especially after the area swings from a dry stretch into a hard spring rain. Severe weather season brings straight-line wind and hail that can strip shingles or punch through older composition roofs, and when a roof is compromised even briefly, water tracks into attic insulation and ceiling drywall fast. Homes on septic systems or older municipal lines also see backups after prolonged heavy rain saturates the ground and slows drainage.

## Services we provide in Nevada

Water damage restoration is our most frequent call in Nevada, and it often starts below the surface: a slab leak that's been feeding moisture into the foundation for weeks before anyone notices a warm spot on the floor or a rise in the water bill. We trace the source, extract standing water, and set drying equipment to pull moisture out of flooring and wall cavities before it reaches framing.

Fire and smoke damage work here frequently involves older wood-frame homes where fire travels through balloon-framed wall cavities faster than newer construction, and where smoke odor settles into porous materials like insulation, drywall, and fabric. We handle structural cleaning, odor neutralization, and coordination on the rebuild.

Mold remediation calls usually trace back to a water event that wasn't fully dried out, whether it's a roof leak that sat for a season or humidity trapped in a crawlspace. Given North Texas humidity, mold can colonize exposed wet drywall or framing within 24 to 48 hours, so containment and remediation move quickly once we're on site.

Storm damage and emergency board-up round out the work we do most in Nevada, covering tree limb impacts, wind-torn roofing, and securing a structure against further weather and intrusion until permanent repairs can start.
