## Restoration emergencies common in Greenville

Greenville sits in the Blackland Prairie belt, where expansive clay soils shift with every wet-dry cycle. That movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing embedded in them, making slab leaks and slow under-slab seepage more common here than in sandier parts of North Texas. The region also sits in a corridor that funnels severe thunderstorms up from the Gulf, and Hunt County sees its share of hail events, high-wind damage, and the occasional tornado watch that turns into something worse.

The housing stock adds another layer. Greenville has a meaningful number of homes built in the 1950s through 1970s — older galvanized supply lines, original cast-iron drain stacks, and in some cases insulation or tile materials that require careful handling during any demo work. Newer subdivisions on the city's growing edges carry their own risks: fast-built construction with tight wall cavities that trap moisture when a roof flashing fails or an upstairs supply line lets go. Both ends of the spectrum end up needing professional drying and documentation, just for different reasons.

## Services we provide in Greenville

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Greenville frequently starts small — a slow drip behind a cabinet, a washing machine hose that finally gives way — and gets discovered only after the drywall has already begun to wick. We extract standing water, set drying equipment calibrated to the humidity levels typical of East Texas spring and summer, and monitor moisture readings daily until the structure hits its dry standard. Every job is documented for your insurance carrier.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a fire, the visible char is rarely the whole story. Smoke residue travels through HVAC systems and settles into porous materials — drywall, wood framing, soft goods — leaving an odor that resurfaces for months if it isn't properly treated. We assess the full extent of smoke migration, remove unsalvageable materials, and use professional-grade odor neutralization rather than masking agents.

### Mold remediation

Greenville's humid summers give mold a short runway. Under the right conditions, mold colonies can establish within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. We locate the moisture source first — because remediation without fixing the source is temporary — then contain, remove, and treat affected areas following established industry protocols. We do not render medical opinions on health effects, but we do leave you with documentation of what was found and what was done.

### Reconstruction

Once mitigation is complete, many property owners don't want to manage a separate general contractor. We carry reconstruction services through the same job, rebuilding drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and structural elements to match pre-loss conditions where possible.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Greenville

Greenville's older core still has frame houses on pier-and-beam foundations with shiplap sheathing and, in the oldest ones, plaster over wood lath. Those crawlspaces hold humidity long after the leak is fixed, and plaster dries at a different rate than the drywall patched in beside it. Homes built before the late 1970s also warrant lead-safe practices during demo. Newer subdivisions sit on post-tensioned slabs with supply lines run through unconditioned attics — the assembly that fails during a hard North Texas freeze, dumping water down two floors at once.

Structural repair, reroofing, and electrical or plumbing replacement generally require a permit from the City of Greenville, and properties near creek bottoms may face floodplain review before rebuild. We plan that step into the scope rather than discovering it mid-reconstruction.
