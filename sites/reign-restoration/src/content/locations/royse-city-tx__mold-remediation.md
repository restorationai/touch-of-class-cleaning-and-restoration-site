## Why Royse City Properties See Mold Issues

The same expansive clay that gives Royse City its characteristic lawn cracking in August also funnels rainwater toward foundations when drainage is inadequate. Many of the subdivisions built along the State Highway 276 corridor in the early 2000s used slab-on-grade construction with minimal exterior waterproofing — a code-compliant choice at the time that still leaves interior wall bases vulnerable during the heavy spring storms that roll through Rockwall County. When gutters overflow or downspouts discharge too close to the foundation, moisture wicks through the slab edge and into bottom plates, where it hides behind baseboards for weeks.

Attic mold is the other pattern Reign Restoration sees repeatedly in Royse City homes. Production builders in this region often installed ridge vents without adequate soffit ventilation, creating stagnant attic air that stays humid through June, July, and August. Roof deck sheathing in those conditions can show active mold growth even when there has been no roof leak — just trapped humidity and summer heat cycling overnight.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Royse City

Every job starts with a thorough moisture investigation, not just a visual scan. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters identify wet building materials behind walls and under flooring before any demolition begins. That step matters here because Royse City's newer vinyl-plank flooring installations often trap moisture underneath without showing surface damage — the subfloor can be saturated while the floor above looks fine.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is sealed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers, preventing cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Mold-colonized materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing where warranted — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) guidelines. Structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which your insurance adjuster will need.
