## Our Mold Remediation Process in Plano

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We identify the moisture source first — because remediation without source correction is temporary — then establish containment using 6-mil polyethylene barriers and negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the removal process.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing — are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines. Hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial. We follow the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation throughout, which governs everything from containment protocols to post-remediation verification. Final clearance testing is available through a third-party industrial hygienist, and we can coordinate that scheduling so you're not managing two separate contractors.

For older Whiffletree or Hunters Glen homes with failing cast-iron drain lines, we flag the plumbing issue in our documentation so your plumber and insurance adjuster are working from the same picture.
