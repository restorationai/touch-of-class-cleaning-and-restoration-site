## Why Greenville Properties See Mold Problems

Hunt County averages over 40 inches of rainfall a year, and Greenville's clay-heavy soils don't drain quickly. That means water lingers against foundations, seeps into crawl spaces, and saturates the ground around slab-on-grade homes long after the rain stops. Older homes in Greenville — many built in the mid-20th century with pier-and-beam foundations — are especially vulnerable because the underfloor space is open to ground moisture and often has limited cross-ventilation. When humidity climbs into the 80–90% range in July and August, even a small vapor barrier gap or a dryer vent exhausting into a crawl space is enough to tip conditions toward mold growth.

Attic mold is another pattern that shows up regularly in Greenville. Inadequate soffit ventilation combined with summer heat causes condensation on roof sheathing, and by the time a homeowner notices discoloration on a ceiling, the colony has often spread across several rafters. Black mold remediation in these spaces requires careful containment so spores aren't pushed into living areas during removal.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Greenville

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — visual assessment plus moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters to find hidden saturation behind drywall or under flooring. We identify the moisture source first, because removing mold without fixing the water intrusion is a temporary fix at best.

Once the scope is clear, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment**: Polyethylene barriers and negative air pressure machines isolate the work area so spores can't migrate to clean parts of the home during mold abatement.
- **HEPA filtration**: Air scrubbers run continuously throughout the job, capturing airborne particulates down to 0.3 microns.
- **Controlled demolition**: Porous materials — drywall, insulation, subflooring — that are saturated beyond cleaning are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents.
- **Structural drying**: After mold cleanup, the underlying structure is dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture targets before any rebuild begins.
- **Documentation**: We photograph every stage, which matters when you're filing a claim or need to demonstrate clearance to a buyer or insurer.
