## Why Highland Park Properties See Mold Issues

Highland Park is a 2.2-square-mile enclave platted in 1907, and the housing stock reflects it. Tudor Revival and Spanish Colonial estates along Beverly Drive and Armstrong Parkway were built with plaster-over-lath walls, clay tile roofs, and cast-iron drain stacks — materials that perform beautifully until water intrudes. Plaster is hygroscopic: it absorbs and holds moisture far longer than modern drywall, giving mold a sustained food source even after the visible leak is fixed.

The Turtle Creek corridor through Lakeside Park and into Exall Lake sits in a mapped floodplain, meaning properties on the low side of that drainage can see elevated ambient humidity and periodic groundwater intrusion that pushes moisture into crawl spaces and true basements — both of which are more common here than in most of Dallas. North Texas summers compound the problem: outdoor humidity regularly climbs above 70%, and when a poorly sealed crawl space or a failing vapor barrier lets that air mix with cooled interior surfaces, condensation feeds hidden mold colonies without any pipe ever bursting.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Highland Park

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, subfloor, and ceiling cavities — because in homes with plaster construction, the wet zone is almost always larger than the visible stain suggests. We identify the moisture source first; remediating without fixing the source is work that will need to be redone.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered negative-pressure machines to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. In Highland Park's larger estate homes, that sometimes means multiple containment zones running simultaneously. Affected materials are removed following IICRC AMRT protocols, structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated, and drying equipment runs until moisture readings return to baseline — not just until the area looks dry. We document every phase with photographs and moisture logs, which matter both for insurance claims and for the independent clearance testing that many Highland Park homeowners commission before closing out a remediation.
