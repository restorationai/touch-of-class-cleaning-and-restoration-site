## Why Heath Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most homes in Heath were built from the 1990s onward on slab foundations, and North Texas blackland prairie clay expands when saturated and shrinks hard when it dries out. That cycle stresses plumbing runs embedded in or under the slab, and slow pinhole leaks can go undetected for weeks because the water migrates sideways through the slab rather than surfacing where the pipe actually failed. Add in the humidity swings common to this part of the Metroplex, and a leak that sits for even a few days starts feeding mold growth behind baseboards and inside cabinet toe-kicks before the homeowner ever sees a stain on the wall.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Heath

We start with moisture mapping, using meters to trace how far water has traveled under flooring and into wall cavities rather than just drying what's visible. On slab homes, that often means pulling baseboards and drilling small weep holes in drywall so trapped water and air can actually move instead of sitting behind an intact wall surface. Extraction comes first, pulling standing water from tile, engineered hardwood, or carpet pad, followed by structural drying equipment set and monitored daily until materials read dry per the IICRC S500 standard rather than just dry to the touch. Because many Heath homes have open floor plans with large tile runs, we size our drying equipment to the actual cubic footage of the space instead of a flat per-room estimate, which keeps the job from running longer than it needs to.

## Heath Insurance & HOA Coordination

A lot of the subdivisions built out in Heath over the past two decades are HOA-governed, which means exterior tarps, dumpster placement for water-damaged materials, and even driveway staging for our drying equipment sometimes need a quick heads-up to a property manager before work starts. We handle that coordination directly so homeowners aren't stuck relaying messages between their HOA and their insurance adjuster while water is actively sitting under their flooring. On the claims side, we document moisture readings, photograph affected materials, and bill most major carriers directly, which matters when a slab leak claim gets picked apart over whether the loss is sudden or long-term.
