That persistent moisture in the air — especially during the long stretch from late spring through early fall — means a slow leak behind a bathroom wall or a condensation problem in an attic crawl space can seed a mold colony in as little as 24 to 48 hours. ## Why Rowlett Properties See Mold Issues

Lake Ray Hubbard isn't just a scenic backdrop — it keeps relative humidity elevated across Rowlett in ways that neighborhoods farther west simply don't experience. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, which make up a large share of Rowlett's housing stock, frequently used paper-faced drywall and OSB sheathing that absorb and hold moisture readily. When an HVAC drain line clogs or a slab plumbing leak goes undetected for a few weeks, those materials become a near-ideal substrate for Cladosporium, Penicillium, and the Stachybotrys strains people commonly call black mold.

Rowlett also sits on expansive clay soils that shift seasonally. That movement stresses slab foundations and the plumbing embedded in them, making slow under-slab leaks more common here than in areas built on more stable ground. By the time a homeowner notices a musty odor or discoloration along a baseboard, the moisture event driving it may have been ongoing for weeks.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Rowlett

Every job starts with a visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a quick walk-through. Knowing exactly where elevated moisture readings exist tells us whether the mold visible in one room is the only colony or the visible tip of a larger problem behind walls or under flooring.

Once the scope is defined, the affected area is sealed off with negative-air containment barriers and a HEPA-filtered air scrubber is set running to capture airborne spores before they migrate. Contaminated drywall, insulation, or flooring is removed, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Commission on Environmental Quality guidelines. Structural framing and any salvageable surfaces are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — stays on-site until moisture readings return to normal baselines for the local climate, not just a generic national standard.

Because Rowlett's ambient humidity can slow the drying curve, we monitor readings daily rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule. A job that might dry in three days in a low-humidity market can take four or five here, and cutting that short is one of the most common reasons mold returns after a remediation.

## Rowlett Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Texas follow a specific path under the Texas Department of Insurance's mold remediation licensing framework. Reign Restoration holds a Texas Mold Remediation Contractor license (MRC2276), which is a hard requirement for any firm performing remediation work on Texas properties — not all companies operating in the DFW area carry it. That license matters when your adjuster reviews the claim file.

We document the loss with photographs, moisture logs, and a written scope of work formatted to meet carrier requirements. Most major homeowners' insurance policies in Texas cover mold remediation when it results from a covered water loss — a burst pipe, appliance failure, or storm intrusion — but not from long-term neglect. We can walk you through what your policy language typically covers and communicate directly with your adjuster so the process doesn't stall.

## Local Note

Rowlett homes built in the late 1980s and early 1990s — particularly those in subdivisions developed during the city's rapid growth period after Lake Ray Hubbard's recreational popularity took off — often have attic HVAC systems with flex duct runs that sag over time. A sagging flex duct traps condensation, and that condensation drips onto the attic deck or insulation below. Homeowners usually discover the mold during a roof inspection or attic insulation upgrade, not because they noticed symptoms inside the living space. If your inspector flags attic mold and your HVAC system is original to the home, have the ductwork evaluated at the same time — remediating the mold without correcting the condensation source means the problem comes back.
