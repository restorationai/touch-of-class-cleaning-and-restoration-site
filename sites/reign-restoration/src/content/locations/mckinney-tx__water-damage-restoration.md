## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in McKinney

Every job starts with a moisture mapping survey — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, floors, and ceilings — before any water removal begins. That survey shapes the drying plan and creates the documentation your insurance adjuster will need.

**Water extraction** comes next. For slab homes in Craig Ranch or Trinity Falls, that typically means truck-mounted extraction pulling standing water off hard surfaces, followed by weighted extraction tools pressed against carpet and pad. In pier-and-beam homes near Historic Downtown McKinney, the crawlspace itself often needs to be addressed simultaneously — a portable extractor and dehumidifier staged below the subfloor while the living area dries above.

**Structural drying** follows the IICRC S500 standard: industrial air movers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern, low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers pulling moisture out of the air column, and daily moisture readings logged until materials reach documented drying goals.
