## Why Greenville Properties See Water Damage Issues

The same expansive clay that makes Hunt County farmland productive creates real headaches for homeowners. When the soil swells after heavy rain, hydrostatic pressure pushes against foundation walls and slab edges. When it contracts in a dry summer, it pulls away from those same surfaces and leaves gaps. Greenville's housing stock includes a significant number of older pier-and-beam homes — construction styles common through the mid-twentieth century — where the crawl space beneath the floor is the first place standing water collects and the last place a homeowner thinks to check. By the time a musty smell reaches the living area, wood joists may already have been wet for days.

Greenville also sits in a corridor that sees intense convective thunderstorms, particularly in spring. A single storm event can overwhelm residential gutters, push water through window frames, and back up municipal storm drains in lower-lying neighborhoods. That combination of soil behavior, older housing, and storm intensity means water intrusion here tends to arrive fast and penetrate deep.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Greenville

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters identify wet materials behind walls and under flooring that look dry to the eye. That step matters especially in Greenville's older homes, where original hardwood floors and plaster-over-lathe walls hold moisture differently than modern OSB and drywall. Plaster in particular releases absorbed water slowly, which means drying timelines on a 1950s-era Greenville home can run longer than a comparable square footage in newer construction.

Once the scope is mapped, water extraction removes standing water and pulls moisture from carpet, pad, and subfloor. Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned to create a controlled drying environment calibrated to the ambient conditions — Texas summer humidity and a closed house after a storm can push indoor relative humidity well above 60%, which is the threshold above which mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours. Drying logs are recorded daily so the documentation is ready for your insurance adjuster. The process follows the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration, and Reign Restoration holds IICRC certification as a firm with technicians certified in Water Restoration Technology (WRT).

One pattern that comes up repeatedly on Greenville jobs: homes built in the post-war decades often have original cast-iron drain lines under the slab. When those lines crack or separate — which happens as the blackland clay moves beneath them — slow sewer intrusion can saturate the slab and the flooring above it for weeks before anyone notices.
