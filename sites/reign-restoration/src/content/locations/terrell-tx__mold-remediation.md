## Why Terrell Properties See Mold Issues

Kaufman County averages over 40 inches of rainfall annually, and Terrell's clay-heavy soils drain slowly. That means water from a single heavy storm can pool against a foundation for days, wicking moisture into sill plates and lower wall cavities before it ever shows up on a ceiling. Pier-and-beam homes — a common construction style in Terrell's older residential blocks — are especially vulnerable because the crawl space beneath them acts as a humidity reservoir. Poor cross-ventilation under the floor system lets relative humidity climb above 70%, which is all mold needs to establish a foothold on wood joists and subfloor sheathing.

Homes with original single-pane windows also create condensation problems in winter when warm interior air meets cold glass, feeding surface mold on window sills and the surrounding drywall. HVAC systems that were retrofitted into homes not originally designed for central air can create pressure imbalances that pull humid outside air into wall cavities — a pattern we see repeatedly in Terrell's mid-century housing stock.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Terrell

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not just a visual check, but thermal imaging and moisture meter readings across walls, ceilings, and subfloor assemblies. We identify the moisture source first, because remediating mold without correcting the underlying water intrusion is a temporary fix at best.

Once the source is addressed, we establish containment using negative air pressure and 6-mil poly barriers to prevent spores from spreading to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Department of State Health Services mold remediation guidelines. Our IICRC AMRT-certified technicians then apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatments to structural surfaces before the area is dried to industry standards. Final clearance documentation is available upon request for insurance claims or future property transactions.
