## Why Rowlett Homes See Elevated Contents Damage

The neighborhoods closest to the lake shoreline tend to feature larger, open-plan homes built in the 1990s and early 2000s — a construction era known for engineered wood cabinetry, laminate flooring, and composite furniture materials that swell, delaminate, and off-gas soot odors more aggressively than solid hardwood. When a kitchen fire or burst supply line hits one of these homes, the contents absorb damage quickly. Smoke particles settle into upholstery within hours; water wicks into particleboard furniture cores within minutes. The longer those items sit untreated in Rowlett's humid air, the narrower the salvage window becomes.

Rowlett also sits in a region where severe hail and tornado-producing storms roll through the Dallas–Fort Worth corridor with regularity. A roof breach during a spring storm can dump hundreds of gallons of water into a home before the rain stops — soaking stored items in attics, closets, and bedrooms that would otherwise never see moisture damage. That kind of sudden, large-volume event is exactly what a structured pack-out and off-site storage process is designed to handle.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Rowlett

When Reign Restoration arrives at a Rowlett property, the first priority is a room-by-room contents inventory — every item photographed, described, and categorized as salvageable, questionable, or total loss before anything leaves the home. That documentation matters enormously for insurance claims and gives homeowners a clear picture of what they're getting back.

Salvageable items are carefully packed, labeled, and transported to our secure climate-controlled storage facility. Off-site storage isn't just about clearing space for structural work — in Rowlett's humid summers, leaving smoke- or water-affected contents inside a partially remediated home accelerates cross-contamination. Odors migrate. Mold colonizes porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. Removing contents to a controlled environment stops that clock.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics components, dry-cleaning processes for delicate textiles, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor elimination in upholstered furniture and clothing, and hand-cleaning for artwork, documents, and irreplaceable items. Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration) and IICRC WRT (Water Restoration) certifications, so the cleaning protocols applied to your belongings follow the same industry standards used for the structure itself.

Once the home is cleared and restored, items are returned in the same documented order they left — inventoried on delivery so nothing is unaccounted for.
