Renovating a home in Heath often starts with a decision nobody plans for: a storm-damaged roof that needs more than a patch, a kitchen that's finally outgrown a family, or a bathroom with plumbing old enough to justify opening the walls anyway. Reign Restoration handles renovations, remodels and general contracting for Heath homeowners who want one licensed team managing the whole job, from the first permit application to the final trim work, rather than juggling separate contractors for each trade.

## Why Heath Properties See Renovation and Rebuild Demand

Heath is a city of large residential lots, custom-built homes, and a housing stock that skews newer than much of Rockwall County, but "newer" here often still means 1990s and early 2000s construction. Those homes are now old enough that original kitchens and bathrooms are due for a real update, not a cosmetic one, and builder-grade materials from that era (particle-board cabinet boxes, single-pane windows, older PEX runs) are common points of failure. Proximity to Lake Ray Hubbard also means Heath gets its share of straight-line wind and hail events that push roofs, siding, and sometimes whole additions past the point of repair and into full rebuild territory. When storm damage triggers a renovation, we handle the structural rebuild and the remodel scope as one continuous project instead of handing it off between a restoration crew and a separate contractor.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Heath

Every project starts with an on-site walkthrough where we document existing conditions, talk through what's structural versus cosmetic, and, for post-damage jobs, cross-reference what the insurance scope actually covers against what the homeowner wants done. From there we handle permitting through the City of Heath's building department, which for larger-lot custom homes can involve setback and easement checks that smaller-lot subdivisions elsewhere don't require. Design and material selection happen before demo starts, not during it, so homeowners aren't making cabinet decisions while their kitchen is a shell. Construction follows in phases (demo, rough-in, inspections, finish work), with the same project lead on site from start to walkthrough so nothing gets lost in a handoff.

## Heath Insurance & HOA Coordination

A meaningful share of Heath's subdivisions are HOA-governed with architectural review committees that have opinions about exterior materials, roof pitch, and even fence lines, which matters the moment a rebuild touches anything visible from the street. We submit the required plans to the ARC alongside the city permit set so approvals move in parallel instead of in sequence, which is usually where post-storm rebuilds lose weeks. On the insurance side, we document the loss and the proposed scope clearly enough that adjusters can see where restoration ends and discretionary remodeling begins, since those two budgets are usually tracked separately even on the same job.

## Local note

Many Heath properties sit on larger acreage lots with septic systems rather than city sewer, which changes how we sequence a bathroom or kitchen remodel. Relocating plumbing fixtures even a few feet can mean confirming septic line routing before we open a slab, and we check that early rather than discovering it mid-demo. It's a small detail, but it's the kind of thing that adds a week to a project when it's missed.
