## Our Mold Remediation Process in Allen

Every job starts with a thorough assessment — not a visual scan, but moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin-type meters to find wet material behind drywall and under flooring before demolition begins. In Allen's brick veneer homes, that step is especially important because the source of moisture is often in the wall cavity, not the interior surface where the staining is visible.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is sealed with physical containment barriers and placed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms — a real concern in open floor plans common in Allen's late-1990s construction. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and porous materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) guidelines.

After removal, surfaces are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent and dried to IICRC AMRT standards before any reconstruction begins. As an IICRC Certified Firm holding the Applied Microbial Remediation Technician (AMRT) credential, Reign Restoration follows a documented protocol — not a shortcut version — so the remediation holds up to scrutiny if your insurance adjuster or a future home inspector reviews the paperwork.
