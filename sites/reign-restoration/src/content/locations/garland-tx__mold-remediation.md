## Why Garland Properties See Mold Issues

Garland's housing stock skews heavily toward homes built between the 1960s and 1990s — a generation of construction that used paper-faced drywall, minimal vapor barriers, and HVAC ductwork routed through unconditioned attic spaces. When a slow plumbing leak, a failed roof flashing, or a backed-up condensate drain goes unnoticed for even 24 to 48 hours, mold can begin colonizing porous materials. The clay-heavy soil underlying much of Garland expands and contracts with seasonal moisture swings, which stresses slab foundations and the plumbing lines embedded in them. A pinhole slab leak under a bathroom or kitchen can saturate subfloor materials for months before a homeowner notices elevated humidity or discoloration on baseboards.

Garland also sits in a region that sees periodic severe weather — the kind of wind-driven rain events that expose every gap in a roofline or window seal. Attic mold following storm intrusion is a recurring pattern here, particularly in neighborhoods where the original roof decking was never replaced after hail damage was patched rather than fully remediated.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Garland

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — visual assessment, moisture mapping with a calibrated thermal camera, and air or surface sampling when the scope of contamination isn't visually clear. We document everything before a single piece of material is removed, which matters both for insurance purposes and for understanding how far the mold has traveled beyond the visible stain.

Containment is set up using negative air pressure and 6-mil poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing where necessary — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Department of State Health Services guidelines for mold-containing waste. Remaining structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. We then run commercial-grade air scrubbers with HEPA filtration until post-remediation air quality readings confirm the spore count has returned to an acceptable baseline. The moisture source is addressed before the job is closed — sealing a remediated cavity without fixing the underlying leak is a temporary fix, not a solution.

## Garland Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Texas are handled differently depending on the cause. If the mold traces back to a sudden and accidental water loss — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, storm-driven intrusion — most homeowner policies will cover at least a portion of remediation costs. Mold that developed from long-term neglect or a slow leak the insurer characterizes as a maintenance issue is frequently denied. Reign Restoration documents the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected materials, and a written scope of work that aligns with how adjusters evaluate these claims. That documentation doesn't guarantee approval, but it gives your claim the clearest possible foundation.
