## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in University Park

The first step on any University Park job is a room-by-room inventory before anything moves. After inventory, affected contents are carefully packed out and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Our technicians hold IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke) and IICRC WRT (Water) certifications, so the same team that assesses the damage understands both the chemistry of smoke residue and the moisture dynamics of water-damaged wood.

Once your home is dried, repaired, and cleared, contents are returned in the same catalogued order — nothing gets lost in the shuffle, and you're not unpacking mystery boxes.
