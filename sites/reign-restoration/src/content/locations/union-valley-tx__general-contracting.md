Renovations, remodels and general contracting in Union Valley, TX often start from a different place than they would in a denser DFW suburb: a lot of the work here follows storm damage, foundation movement, or a rebuild after a covered loss, rather than a simple style refresh. With Union Valley's mix of older rural homesteads and newer construction spreading out from Royse City, a kitchen remodel or whole-home rebuild has to account for the ground the house sits on just as much as the finishes going into it.

## Why Union Valley Properties Need Renovation and Rebuild Work

Union Valley sits on the Blackland Prairie belt that runs through Hunt County, and that soil is heavy in expansive clay. It swells when it's wet and pulls away from foundations when it dries out, which is most of the summer in this part of Texas. That cycle shows up as door frames that stop closing square, hairline cracks in drywall that reopen every year, and tile that pops loose along the same seam. On older homesteads built before slab-on-grade became standard, pier-and-beam foundations move differently than modern slabs, and a remodel on one of those homes has to start with an honest look at what the structure is actually doing before anyone picks a paint color. We also see a steady share of rebuild work tied to wind and hail: a roof or exterior wall gets compromised, water gets into the framing, and what started as storm damage turns into a full room or addition rebuild.

## Our Renovations, Remodels and General Contracting Process in Union Valley

We start with a walkthrough that separates cosmetic wear from structural movement, since in this part of Hunt County those two things get blamed on each other more often than they should. If the project follows a water or fire loss, our scope picks up where mitigation leaves off, so framing, subfloor, and insulation get rebuilt to the same standard the original structure was built to, not patched around. From there we handle design layout, material selection, and the permitting that runs through Hunt County's building office for anything touching electrical, plumbing, or structural framing. Kitchen and bathroom remodels get sequenced around the realities of a working household, and for larger rebuilds we keep a weekly check-in with the homeowner so nobody is guessing where the project stands. Final walkthroughs include a punch list the homeowner signs off on before we consider the job closed.

## Union Valley Insurance & Permit Coordination

A fair number of general contracting jobs out here begin with an insurance claim rather than a homeowner's own renovation budget, especially after hail season or a plumbing failure that soaked flooring and cabinetry. We document the original damage, write the rebuild scope against that documentation, and coordinate directly with the adjuster so the homeowner isn't the one relaying technical detail back and forth. Because much of Union Valley sits outside incorporated city limits, permitting for additions, structural repair, or septic-adjacent work typically runs through Hunt County rather than a municipal office, and we account for that in scheduling since county turnaround can run differently than what homeowners moving from Rockwall or Dallas County might expect.

## Local note

A good number of Union Valley properties are on acreage with well water and septic systems rather than city utilities, which changes how a kitchen or bathroom remodel gets planned. Moving a sink or adding a bathroom isn't just a plumbing question, it's a question of where the septic field runs and whether the well's pressure tank can support the new fixture load, so we check that before any wall comes down, not after.
