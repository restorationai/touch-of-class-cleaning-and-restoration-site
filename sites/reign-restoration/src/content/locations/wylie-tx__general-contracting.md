## Why Wylie Homes Are Ripe for Renovation Right Now

The bulk of Wylie's residential housing stock was built between the late 1990s and the mid-2010s, a period when tract builders leaned heavily on laminate countertops, hollow-core doors, and HVAC systems that are now approaching end-of-life. That era of construction also produced a lot of open-concept floor plans that looked great in a model home but created real acoustic and thermal problems once families moved in — thin interior walls with no insulation, kitchen islands that block natural light, and primary bathrooms that feel like afterthoughts.

North Texas clay soil compounds the issue. Wylie sits on expansive clay that swells with moisture and contracts in drought, and the region has seen both in recent years. Seasonal foundation movement can crack tile, shift door frames, and separate drywall joints. Before any cosmetic renovation begins, a good general contractor checks for active movement and addresses it — otherwise new finishes crack within a season. Reign Restoration scopes structural conditions before finalizing any remodel plan, so the work you pay for stays intact.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Wylie

Every project starts with an on-site walkthrough. For post-damage rebuilds, that means reviewing any adjuster documentation and aligning the scope with what the insurance carrier has approved. For elective remodels, it means listening to what the homeowner actually wants, then translating that into a realistic plan with a clear line-item estimate.

From there, the process runs in a defined sequence:

- **Permitting** — Wylie requires building permits for structural changes, electrical upgrades, plumbing relocations, and additions. We pull permits directly with the City of Wylie Building Inspections department and schedule inspections so the project doesn't stall.
- **Demolition and rough-in** — Selective demo where possible to preserve what's worth keeping. Rough-in work for plumbing and electrical is inspected before walls close.
- **Finish work** — Tile, cabinetry, countertops, paint, trim, and fixtures installed to the agreed spec. We use licensed subcontractors for trades that require it under Texas law.
- **Final walkthrough** — We walk the completed space with the homeowner before closing the job, and we don't consider it done until the punch-list is clear.

Wylie's rapid growth means that some of the city's older neighborhoods — areas developed in the early 1990s near the historic downtown core — contain homes built before Collin County adopted its current energy code requirements.
