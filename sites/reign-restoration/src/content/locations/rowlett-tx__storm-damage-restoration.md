## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Rowlett

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional water from entering the structure. Our crews perform emergency tarping and board-up on the same visit as the initial assessment — not as a separate appointment. We photograph every affected area before anything is moved or covered, creating a documented record that supports your insurance claim from the first hour on-site.

Once the structure is stabilized, the process moves through these stages:

**Damage assessment and moisture mapping.** Using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, we locate water that has migrated behind walls, under flooring, and into ceiling assemblies — areas that look dry but aren't.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage drying. In Rowlett's summer heat and humidity, drying timelines are monitored daily and adjusted based on actual psychrometric readings, not a fixed schedule.

**Debris removal and tree damage cleanup.** Storm-felled trees and limbs are removed from roofs, fences, and yards before reconstruction begins. We coordinate safe removal with attention to any utility lines that may have been compromised.

**Reconstruction.** Reign Restoration is licensed for reconstruction work (license #MRC2276), so the same company that mitigates the damage can also replace the roof decking, repair framing, reinstall drywall, and restore the property to pre-loss condition. You don't hand off to a second contractor mid-project.
