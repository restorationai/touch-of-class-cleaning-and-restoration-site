## Why Rowlett Properties Experience Water Damage Differently

Rowlett's housing stock spans several decades and building styles. Neighborhoods developed in the late 1980s and 1990s — which make up a large portion of the city's single-family homes — often feature slab-on-grade foundations with copper or early CPVC supply lines that are now reaching the end of their service life. When those lines fail, water migrates under the slab and wicks upward through flooring adhesive and baseboards before a homeowner ever notices a wet spot. By the time the floor feels soft underfoot, saturation has typically been ongoing for hours.

The clay-heavy soils common throughout Dallas County and into Rockwall County also shift seasonally. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Rowlett

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We map the full extent of saturation — not just the visible wet area — because water travels along the path of least resistance, often spreading far beyond the obvious damage zone before stopping.

From there, the process moves through these stages:

- **Water extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from flooring, carpet, and subfloor cavities. On slab foundations common in Rowlett, we pay close attention to tile grout lines and floating wood floors, which can trap water underneath even after the surface looks dry.
- **Structural drying**: Commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers combined with high-velocity air movers create the airflow conditions needed to drive moisture out of wall cavities, insulation, and framing. Drying is monitored daily against the IICRC S500 standard until materials reach acceptable moisture content.
- **Documentation**: We photograph and log all affected materials, moisture readings, and equipment placement — records your insurance adjuster will need to process the claim.
- **Controlled demolition (when necessary)**: Saturated drywall, insulation, and flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed to prevent hidden mold growth. We keep removal targeted and document everything removed for accurate scope-of-loss reporting.
