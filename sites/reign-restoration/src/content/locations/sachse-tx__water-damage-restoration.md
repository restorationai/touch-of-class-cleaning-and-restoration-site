## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sachse

We start with extraction, pulling standing water off hard flooring and out of carpet pad with truck-mount and portable extraction units, because every hour water sits is an hour closer to materials absorbing more than they can safely give back. From there we moisture-map the affected rooms with meters, not guesswork, checking subfloor, drywall, and baseboards to find out how far the water actually traveled versus how far it looks like it traveled on the surface. Structural drying follows with air movers and dehumidification set to the specific materials involved, since a slab home with ceramic tile over the moisture dries differently than a carpeted room with pad underneath. We document moisture readings daily until materials hit a dry standard consistent with IICRC S500 guidelines, not just until the surface feels dry to the touch.
