## Why Frisco Homes Present Specific Fire Damage Challenges

The same architectural choices that make Frisco properties feel spacious create real complications after a fire. Vaulted and two-story ceilings act as chimneys during a fire event, pulling smoke upward and depositing heavy soot layers on upper wall surfaces, ceiling joists, and the attic deck above. In homes where the air handler sits in a finished attic — a common configuration throughout the 75034 and 75035 ZIP codes — smoke and combustion byproducts can be drawn directly into the HVAC system and redistributed to every room that was never touched by flames.

Brick veneer construction, nearly universal in Frisco, holds up structurally during a fire better than wood siding, but the cavity between the veneer and the wood-framed wall behind it can trap smoke odor for months if it isn't addressed during remediation. ## Our Fire and Smoke Restoration Process in Frisco

The first hour on-site is spent on documentation and containment, not demolition. Every affected surface is photographed and catalogued before anything is removed — insurance adjusters and Frisco's building inspection process both require thorough pre-demo documentation, and skipping that step creates delays later.

Once the structure is secured and utilities are isolated, the process moves through four phases:

**1. Structural assessment and debris removal.** Char, collapsed drywall, and fire-damaged framing are removed to clean substrate. On slab-on-grade construction, the concrete itself is inspected for heat spalling, which can compromise the slab surface even if the fire originated above it.

**2. Smoke and soot cleaning.** Dry chemical sponges address loose soot on ceilings and walls before any wet cleaning begins — applying water or liquid cleaners to dry soot smears it deeper into porous surfaces. HEPA vacuuming clears ducts and the air handler cabinet before the HVAC system is tested or restarted.

**3. Odor neutralization.** Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are used in sequence. Thermal fog penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled — inside wall cavities, above drop ceilings, into attic insulation — rather than masking surface odor. Hydroxyl units run continuously during the process and are safe to operate in occupied adjacent spaces.

**4. Reconstruction.** Reign Restoration carries Texas Mold Remediation Contractor license #MRC2276 and handles structural repairs in-house, which means a single point of contact from emergency response through finished reconstruction rather than handing the job to a separate general contractor mid-project.
