## Why Nevada Properties See Mold Issues

Nevada sits on the blackland prairie clay that runs through Collin County, and that soil swells and shrinks hard with the weather. During wet stretches it pushes moisture up against slab foundations and perimeter beams, and during the dry months it pulls away and opens gaps that let humid air into crawlspaces and utility rooms. Combine that with North Texas summer humidity and a lot of homes in the area that rely on well water or septic systems, and you get the classic setup for mold: a slow, hidden moisture source that runs for weeks before anyone notices a stain on drywall or a musty closet. Homes near Lake Lavon and the surrounding rural tracts also tend to have detached garages, barns, or outbuildings with less ventilation than a typical subdivision house, which is where we often find the heaviest colonization when we're called out.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Nevada

We start with a moisture map, not a guess. Using moisture meters and sometimes a borescope, our IICRC-trained technicians trace the source first, because treating visible mold without stopping the water behind it just means a repeat call in a few months. Once the source is identified, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the HVAC system or adjoining rooms, which matters more in older Nevada homes where return air ducts weren't always sealed to current standards. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subfloor, are removed and bagged for disposal, while structural wood and framing are treated and dried in place per IICRC S520 guidance. We finish with HEPA air scrubbing and a final wipe-down before any rebuild work starts.

## Texas Mold Licensing & Insurance Coordination

Texas requires a Mold Remediation Contractor license for this kind of work, and our team operates under license #MRC2276. That matters most when you're filing an insurance claim, because carriers in this area increasingly ask for documentation showing the remediation was performed by a licensed contractor with a clear, itemized scope. We photograph the affected materials before removal, keep moisture readings on file, and provide the paperwork your adjuster needs to process the claim without back-and-forth delays.
