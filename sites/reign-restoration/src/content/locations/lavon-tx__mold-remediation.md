Mold remediation in Lavon comes with a wrinkle a lot of homeowners don't expect: the town sits right against Lake Lavon, and the humidity that rolls off that reservoir settles into attics, slab joints, and HVAC returns in new-construction homes just as easily as it ever did in older houses elsewhere in Collin County. If you're smelling a musty note near a closet, a bathroom vent, or the attic hatch, that's usually moisture that's been sitting longer than it looks.

## Why Lavon Properties See Mold Issues

Much of Lavon has been built out in the last decade and a half, which means slab foundations poured over the expansive clay soils common to this part of Collin County. That clay swells and contracts with rainfall and drought, and the hairline slab cracks it produces give moisture a path into wall cavities that looks fine from the baseboard but isn't. Add the lake's humidity load during the hot months, and a lot of newer Lavon homes end up with condensation forming inside attic trusses or around poorly sealed HVAC supply lines, exactly the dark, still air mold needs to colonize drywall paper and insulation paper backing within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lavon

We start with a moisture map, not a guess: a moisture meter and, where needed, a borescope to check inside wall cavities before we cut anything open. From there we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into the rest of the house while materials come out. Our crews carry IICRC AMRT training for the remediation itself, and on newer Lavon builds that usually means working around engineered trusses and blown-in insulation rather than the plaster and lath you'd find in an older Texas farmhouse, which changes how fast we can get to the source and how much material has to come out versus be treated in place. Once the affected material is removed and the area is treated, we run HEPA air scrubbers and dehumidification until the space reads dry, then do a final wipe-down before closing containment.

## Lavon Insurance & HOA Coordination

A fair number of Lavon neighborhoods were built by production builders within the last several years, and homes still under a builder warranty sometimes need documentation framed for the builder's claims process rather than a standard homeowner's insurance claim, especially if the mold traces back to a construction defect like an unsealed penetration or a missing vapor barrier. We document moisture readings, photos, and the remediation steps in a format that works for either path, and if your HOA or a buyer's lender is requiring third-party clearance testing before you list or close, we schedule our work around an independent hygienist's post-remediation verification rather than calling the job done ourselves.

## Local note

One thing that catches Lavon homeowners off guard: because so much of the housing stock here is new, people assume a musty smell means something is wrong with the build rather than normal settling. In reality, it's often just that the slab hasn't finished curing moisture out from underneath, and a vapor barrier gap or a poorly flashed window is letting that moisture migrate into the wall rather than venting outside. We check for that specific pattern on newer Lavon homes before assuming the mold source is plumbing or roof related, since treating the wrong source means the problem comes back in a season.
