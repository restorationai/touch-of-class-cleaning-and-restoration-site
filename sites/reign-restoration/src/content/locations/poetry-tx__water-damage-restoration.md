## Why Poetry Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most water losses we see out this way trace back to one of three things: aging supply lines in rural homes that have never been re-piped, well pump or pressure tank failures, and the shrink-swell cycle that clay soil goes through between dry summers and wet springs. That soil movement stresses slab foundations and underground plumbing joints over time, which is a quieter cause of water intrusion than a dramatic pipe burst but just as damaging if it goes unnoticed under a slab or inside a wall cavity. Properties on septic systems also face a different failure mode: a clogged or backed-up line can push gray water into a home's lowest floor, which changes both the cleanup category and the safety precautions we take.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Poetry

We start by identifying the water category and the foundation type, because those two facts drive almost every decision after that. On slab homes, we're watching for moisture trapped between the slab and finished flooring, which can read dry on the surface while concrete stays saturated for days. On pier-and-beam construction, we're working the crawlspace directly, which means checking insulation, subfloor, and any ductwork run underneath for saturation. Extraction comes first, using truck-mount or portable units depending on access, followed by placement of air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage. We track moisture content daily with meters and document drying progress against the IICRC S500 standard, which gives both the homeowner and the insurance adjuster a clear record of when the structure is genuinely dry, not just visually dry.
