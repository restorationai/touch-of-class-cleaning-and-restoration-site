## Local Note: What Dallas Contractors Know About Blackland Clay and Finish Timelines

One thing that catches homeowners off guard on Dallas renovation projects: tile and hardwood installations in homes built on Blackland clay should be scheduled during the drier months if possible, or after the foundation has been given time to stabilize following a wet spring. The clay's movement can introduce micro-gaps between a freshly poured self-leveling underlayment and the subfloor if the soil is still in a swelling phase. Experienced Dallas contractors build a short acclimatization window into flooring schedules — it adds a few days but prevents callbacks six months later when a grout line opens up or a plank lifts at the seam.

If your project is a post-damage rebuild following a White Rock Creek or Five Mile Creek flood event, that soil saturation window matters even more. The ground around the foundation needs adequate drying time before exterior concrete flatwork or interior flooring is finalized.
