## Why McKinney Properties See Contents Restoration Challenges

McKinney sits at an interesting split. East of US 75, the Historic Downtown corridor and surrounding blocks hold some of the oldest residential fabric in Collin County — pier-and-beam construction, deep covered porches, and original millwork that dates back to the 1890s and 1920s. When drain lines fail in those crawlspace homes, water wicks upward into wood framing and furniture legs before a homeowner even notices the smell. Antique sideboards, upholstered chairs, and heirloom rugs absorb moisture differently than the contents of a modern slab home.

West of the highway, master-planned communities like Craig Ranch and Trinity Falls are built on slab foundations with tightly packed interiors and shared walls in some sections. Fire and smoke travel differently through those floor plans — smoke residue settles into soft goods, cabinetry, and electronics faster than it can be ventilated. Meanwhile, Wilson Creek and its tributaries carry mapped floodplain risk through parts of the city, meaning flood-related contents losses are a recurring reality for some neighborhoods, not a once-in-a-generation event.

North Texas climate compounds all of this. The combination of high summer humidity and rapid temperature swings — McKinney regularly swings 40°F between a January night and an April afternoon — means that contents left in a damaged structure can develop secondary mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours of a water event.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in McKinney

The process begins with a documented pack-out. Every item that leaves your property is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition note before it's boxed or wrapped. That inventory matters for two reasons: your insurance adjuster needs it, and you need to know exactly what you own and where it is.

From there, contents go to a controlled storage and cleaning facility where the appropriate method is matched to the material. Ultrasonic cleaning works well for hard goods — ceramics, metals, tools, blinds — where high-frequency sound waves dislodge soot and debris without abrasion. Ozone and hydroxyl treatments address odor in soft goods and upholstered pieces. Electronics are assessed separately; smoke residue is corrosive to circuit boards and the evaluation window is short.

Our team holds IICRC certifications in fire and smoke restoration (FSRT) and water damage restoration (WRT), which means the cleaning protocols we apply are calibrated to the type of loss — not a one-size approach. Once your home is cleared for re-occupancy, contents are returned and placed according to your original room layout documentation.
