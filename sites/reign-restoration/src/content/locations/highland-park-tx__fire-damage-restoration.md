## Why Highland Park's Housing Stock Complicates Fire Damage

Highland Park's 2.2-square-mile footprint is almost entirely built out with pre-WWII construction — Spanish Colonial, Neoclassical, and Tudor Revival estates platted as early as 1907. That building stock creates specific challenges after a structural fire:

- **Plaster walls and ceilings** trap smoke particles in a way that standard drywall does not. Soot migrates into the plaster's porous matrix and can re-volatilize odor for weeks if the surface isn't treated with the right sealers before repainting.
- **Pier-and-beam foundations** mean smoke and water from firefighting efforts can pool in the crawl space beneath the floor system. That sub-floor environment needs to be assessed and dried separately from the living areas above.
- **Slate and clay tile roofs** are not replaceable with standard asphalt shingles — matching materials for fire-damaged roof sections often requires sourcing from specialty suppliers, and some repairs require approval from the Town of Highland Park's building department before work begins.
- **Cast-iron drain stacks** can warp or crack under extreme heat, a failure that isn't always visible from the surface. We inspect stack integrity as part of every post-fire structural assessment.

The Preston Road corridor and Old Highland Park sections near Exall Lake tend to have the oldest and most architecturally complex homes on the inventory. A fire in those neighborhoods is rarely a straightforward cleanup.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Highland Park

Fire damage restoration isn't a single task — it's a sequence, and the order matters. Here's how we work through it on Highland Park properties:

**1. Emergency stabilization.** Board-up, roof tarping, and temporary fencing happen first, before any cleaning begins. Highland Park's lot sizes and proximity to neighbors mean an unsecured structure can create liability quickly.

**2. Scope and documentation.** We photograph and document every affected surface before anything is moved or cleaned. This documentation becomes the foundation of your insurance claim and the Town of Highland Park permit application if structural repairs are required.

**3. Debris removal and structural assessment.** Charred materials are removed and segregated. We assess load-bearing elements — particularly the older balloon-frame or post-and-beam construction common in this ZIP code (75205) — for structural integrity before restoration crews enter.

**4. Smoke and soot remediation.** Dry-chemical sponges handle loose soot on plaster and masonry. HEPA vacuuming and thermal fogging address embedded particles and odor. In homes with original hardwood floors and millwork, we use low-moisture cleaning methods to avoid raising the grain or warping irreplaceable material.

**5. Odor neutralization.** Hydroxyl generators run continuously in occupied or semi-occupied structures. Ozone treatment is reserved for fully vacated spaces and is never used around pets, plants, or people.

**6. Reconstruction.** Our IICRC-certified firm handles the rebuild as well as the remediation, so there's no handoff gap between the cleanup crew and the contractor.
