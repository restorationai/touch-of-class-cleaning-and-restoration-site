## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Fate

When we arrive at a Fate property after a storm event, the first priority is a full exterior and interior assessment before any work begins. We document everything — roof penetrations, compromised siding, broken windows, water intrusion points — with photographs and moisture readings that become the foundation of your insurance claim.

From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Emergency stabilization**: tarping exposed roof sections, boarding broken windows, and removing standing water before it migrates into wall cavities or flooring systems.
- **Structural drying**: commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and axial air movers placed according to IICRC S500 drying science, not guesswork. Moisture readings are logged daily.
- **Debris and tree damage removal**: downed limbs and wind-driven debris cleared from rooflines, decks, and fence lines before reconstruction begins.
- **Reconstruction**: once the structure is dry and stable, framing, roofing, drywall, and finish work are coordinated to return the property to pre-loss condition.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Fire and Smoke Restoration (FSRT) credentials, the team brings documented methodology to every phase — which matters when an adjuster is reviewing your claim.
