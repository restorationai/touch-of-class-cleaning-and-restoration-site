When a pipe bursts at 2 a.m., a kitchen fire leaves smoke baked into every surface, or a storm tears through a roof, the last thing you need is to coordinate three different contractors. Reign Restoration handles the full arc of property damage — from the first hours of emergency response through the final coat of paint — for homeowners, commercial property owners, and multi-family operators across Royse City, Rockwall County, and the greater Dallas area.

## Emergency restoration services

Some damage compounds by the hour. Water left standing more than 24–48 hours begins feeding mold. Smoke residue from a structure fire bonds more deeply to walls and contents the longer it sits. Storm damage left open to the elements invites a second wave of water intrusion. Reign Restoration's emergency services exist to stop the clock on that progression. The team responds to **water damage** — burst pipes, appliance failures, sewage backups, and flooding — extracting standing water and beginning structural drying with commercial-grade desiccant and air-moving equipment. **Fire and smoke damage** response covers both the char and the invisible residue that travels far beyond the burn zone. **Storm damage** work addresses roof breaches, wind-driven rain, and structural compromise after severe weather. **Biohazard cleanup** handles situations that require more than a mop — sewage intrusion, trauma scenes, and other contaminated environments that demand proper containment and disposal protocols. Each of these services is handled by an IICRC-certified firm, so the methods and documentation meet the standards carriers and adjusters expect.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction gives you your property back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, Reign Restoration's reconstruction team takes over — reframing walls, replacing subfloor and hardwood, hanging and finishing drywall, restoring cabinetry, and matching paint to what was there before. Because the same company that documented the loss is doing the rebuild, there's no gap between what was torn out and what gets replaced. Reign also takes on general contracting work outside of insurance claims, so if a bathroom gut or a room addition is on your list, the crew and the process are already familiar.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an acute emergency. **Mold inspection and remediation** often follows a slow leak that went unnoticed — or turns up during a home sale inspection. Reign's IICRC AMRT-certified technicians assess the extent of growth, contain the affected area, and remove contaminated materials following established remediation protocols. **Odor removal** addresses smoke, pet, and musty odors that cleaning alone won't resolve, using hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging where appropriate. **Board-up and tarping** secures a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism — protecting it from weather and unauthorized entry while the claim and scope of work are sorted out.

From the first call through the final walkthrough, Reign Restoration keeps mitigation and reconstruction under one roof. That means one point of contact, one set of documentation, and no finger-pointing between a demo crew and a rebuild crew.
