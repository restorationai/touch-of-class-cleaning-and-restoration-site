If you're dealing with water damage, fire damage, or a biohazard situation right now, don't fill out a form — call **(214) 304-0621** and speak with someone directly.

## For urgent situations

Water doesn't wait, and neither should you. The longer standing water sits under flooring or inside wall cavities, the faster secondary damage sets in — mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. Call **(214) 304-0621** during business hours and we'll get you scheduled as fast as possible. When you call, have a rough sense of the affected area and, if it's a water loss, whether the source has been shut off — that helps us arrive prepared.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

Not every situation is a same-day emergency. If you're looking for a repair estimate, have a question about your insurance claim, or want to schedule a mold assessment at a convenient time, email **jerrott@reign-restoration.com** with a brief description of what you're dealing with and your preferred contact window. You can expect a response within one business day. For faster turnaround, a quick call is still the most direct route.
