Reign Restoration is based in Royse City, TX, and serves homeowners and property managers across the greater Dallas–Fort Worth metro and surrounding communities. When you call, a team member will confirm your location, walk you through immediate steps to limit damage, and get you scheduled as quickly as possible. Reach us at (214) 304-0621 during business hours to discuss your situation and arrange a visit.

## Where we work

Royse City sits along US-80 in Rockwall County, roughly 30 miles east of downtown Dallas — a position that puts our crew within practical reach of a wide band of communities. To the west, that means Dallas and the surrounding suburbs, along with the Rockwall–Rowlett–Sachse corridor. To the north, we cover Greenville, Caddo Mills, and the communities along SH-34 and SH-276. South and southeast, we regularly work in Terrell, Forney, and the growing Kaufman County communities that have expanded rapidly over the past decade. The older housing stock in some of these areas — pier-and-beam homes, aging cast-iron drain lines, crawl spaces that hold moisture after North Texas thunderstorms — tends to create the exact conditions that make water and mold losses more involved than they look on the surface.

## Coverage by distance

Jobs closest to Royse City are typically the easiest to schedule quickly, since our crew can mobilize without significant drive time. Communities in Rockwall County and the immediate Kaufman County area fall into this tier. A second ring covers Dallas and its eastern suburbs — Rowlett, Garland, Mesquite, Forney, and Terrell — where highway access is straightforward but peak-hour traffic on I-30 and US-80 can affect arrival windows. We're transparent about that: if you're calling during a weekday afternoon rush, we'll give you a realistic picture of when to expect us rather than a number that sounds good on the phone. For locations at the outer edge of our range — areas approaching Hunt County, Henderson County, or the far eastern suburbs — we evaluate each job individually. Large losses, commercial properties, and multi-unit situations often justify the extra drive.

## Don't see your city?

The list of named service areas on this page isn't a hard boundary. If your community isn't listed, call us anyway at (214) 304-0621. We regularly dispatch to locations outside our standard footprint for significant losses, and we'd rather tell you honestly whether we're the right fit than leave you searching when time matters.
