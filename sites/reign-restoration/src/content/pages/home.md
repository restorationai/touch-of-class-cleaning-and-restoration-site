## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe at 2 a.m., a washing machine overflow, or a slow leak that's been hiding inside your walls for weeks, standing moisture can compromise structural materials and invite mold growth within 24 to 48 hours. The crew extracts water, dries affected cavities with industrial air movers and dehumidifiers, and monitors moisture readings until the structure meets safe thresholds.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — The fire may be out, but the damage keeps spreading. Smoke residue is acidic and continues to etch surfaces, corrode metals, and embed odor into porous materials long after the flames are gone. Reign Restoration handles soot removal, odor neutralization, and content cleaning so that the smell of a fire doesn't become a permanent feature of your home.

**Mold remediation** — Mold colonies can establish themselves in as little as 48 hours after a moisture event, and they don't stop at the visible surface. The remediation process includes containment to prevent cross-contamination, removal of affected materials, and treatment of the underlying area — not just a coat of paint over the problem.
