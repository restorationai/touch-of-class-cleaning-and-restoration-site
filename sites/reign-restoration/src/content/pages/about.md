Reign Restoration was built around a straightforward idea: when water is soaking through your drywall at 11 p.m. or smoke residue is settling into every surface after a kitchen fire, the crew that shows up matters as much as the equipment they bring. Based in Royse City, TX, the company was founded with a focus on residential and commercial property restoration — not as a side service, but as the entire business.

## What we do

Most days look something like this: a homeowner calls after noticing a soft spot in the floor near the water heater, or a property manager needs a mold assessment before a tenant moves in, or a family is standing in a smoke-damaged living room trying to figure out what happens next. The work is part technical — moisture mapping, containment, structural drying, odor neutralization — and part coordination, helping clients navigate insurance claims, understand what's salvageable, and know what to expect at each stage of the process. Reign Restoration works directly with most major insurance carriers, documenting the loss thoroughly so homeowners aren't left guessing what their policy covers or fronting costs out of pocket.

## Our certifications and licensure

The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — sets the technical standards the restoration industry follows for water damage drying (the S500), mold remediation (the S520), and fire and smoke damage (the S700). Reign Restoration holds IICRC Certified Firm status, which means the company as a whole meets those standards, not just individual technicians. Beyond firm certification, the team carries specific IICRC credentials: WRT (Water Damage Restoration Technician), AMRT (Applied Microbial Remediation Technician), and FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician). These aren't marketing designations — they require coursework, hands-on training, and continuing education to maintain. When a technician shows up at your door, the credential behind their work is the same standard a claims adjuster or industrial hygienist would reference.

## Where we work

Royse City sits at the eastern edge of the Dallas–Fort Worth metro, where newer subdivisions built on expansive clay soils sit alongside older ranch-style homes that have seen decades of Texas weather — hail seasons, hard freezes, and the kind of humidity that makes a slow plumbing leak turn into a mold problem faster than most homeowners expect.
