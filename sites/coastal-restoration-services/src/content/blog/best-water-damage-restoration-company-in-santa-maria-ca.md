## What to Ask Any Water Damage Company Before You Hire

The right questions protect your claim and your home. Before you sign an authorization form with any company, ask these five things:

1. **Are your technicians IICRC-certified?** Specifically WRT and ASD for water losses. Ask to see the certification numbers.
2. **Will you provide a written drying log?** Daily moisture readings, equipment placement records, and psychrometric data. This is what your adjuster needs.
3. **What water category is this loss?** Category 1 (clean water from a supply line), Category 2 (gray water from an appliance or overflow), or Category 3 (black water from sewage or flooding) determines the remediation protocol under IICRC S500.
4. **Do you handle insurance documentation directly?** A good restoration company communicates with your adjuster and provides a written scope before work begins.
5. **Are you licensed in California?** Ask for the contractor license number and verify it at the California Contractors State License Board.

For more on navigating the insurance side of a water loss, see [What To Do in the First 24 Hours After Water Damage](/blog/what-to-do-first-24-hours-water-damage/).

The [EPA's guidance on mold and moisture](https://www.epa.gov/mold) is also worth reading if your loss has been sitting for more than 24-48 hours, secondary mold growth is a real risk in Central Coast homes where humidity stays elevated, and it changes the scope of the remediation.
