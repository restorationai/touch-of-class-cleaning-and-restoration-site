Coastal Restoration Services Inc handles water damage mitigation, fire and smoke cleanup, mold remediation, and structural drying for homes and rental properties throughout Oceano, dispatching from our Vandenberg Village headquarters just up the coast on Highway 1.

## Restoration emergencies common in Oceano

Oceano sits at the southern end of the Pismo Coast, where the marine layer rarely fully burns off and onshore wind pushes salt-laden moisture into every gap in a building envelope. That persistent humidity — combined with a water table that sits unusually close to the surface in the lower-lying blocks near the dunes — creates conditions where a slow leak under a kitchen sink or a failed wax ring can saturate subfloor sheathing within 48 to 72 hours and begin supporting mold colonization shortly after. The housing stock here skews older: a significant share of Oceano's single-family homes were built in the 1950s through 1970s, meaning galvanized supply lines, original cast-iron drains, and in some cases asbestos-containing floor tile or popcorn ceilings that require careful handling before any demolition begins. Seasonal storms off the Pacific can overwhelm gutters and push water through foundation stem walls, particularly on properties that back up to low-lying agricultural land on the east side of town. Fire risk is real year-round as well — the same dry Santa Ana winds that affect the broader Five Cities area can carry embers and accelerate kitchen or garage fires before a homeowner realizes the damage has moved into wall cavities.

## Services we provide in Oceano

### Water damage mitigation and structural drying

Water damage in Oceano often involves saturated subfloors and wall cavities that trap moisture long after the visible water is gone — especially in homes with original hardwood or tongue-and-groove subfloor over an unconditioned crawl space. We extract standing water, deploy desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated for coastal ambient humidity, and monitor moisture readings daily until structural materials reach acceptable drying targets. Every job is documented with moisture mapping so your insurance adjuster has a clear picture of the affected area.

### Mold remediation

The combination of Oceano's marine climate and older building stock means mold can establish itself quickly after any water intrusion — sometimes inside wall cavities where it isn't visible until demo begins. We contain affected areas, remove compromised materials following established remediation protocols, and treat structural surfaces before reconstruction begins. We do not perform air-quality testing (that's an independent industrial hygienist's role), but we can refer you to qualified local testers if a clearance report is needed for a real estate transaction or insurance claim.

### Fire and smoke damage cleanup

Smoke residue in Oceano homes behaves differently depending on what burned — synthetic materials leave oily, pungent soot that penetrates porous surfaces like drywall and wood framing, while protein fires from kitchen incidents leave a nearly invisible film that smells long after surfaces look clean. We perform controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, HEPA-vacuum and chemically neutralize smoke residue, and seal affected framing before reconstruction crews close up walls.

### Reconstruction and build-back

Once mitigation is complete, we coordinate or perform the repair work — replacing drywall, subfloor, insulation, and finish materials — so you're not managing two separate contractors through an already stressful claim.
