## Restoration emergencies common in Vandenberg AFB

Vandenberg AFB sits on the Central Coast where marine layer moisture is a near-constant presence. The fog that rolls in off the Pacific most mornings doesn't just affect visibility — it keeps relative humidity elevated year-round, which means building materials stay closer to their moisture threshold than they would inland. A slow plumbing leak that might dry on its own in Bakersfield can quietly feed mold colonization here within 48 to 72 hours of the initial intrusion.

The housing stock on and around the base includes a significant number of mid-century construction homes — slab-on-grade foundations, older galvanized or copper supply lines, and HVAC systems that were not designed with today's understanding of vapor management. Galvanized pipe corrosion and pinhole leaks are among the most common calls we receive from this area. When those leaks occur inside walls or under slabs, the damage is often more extensive than the visible water stain suggests.

Wildfires in Santa Barbara County have also put smoke and ash into structures well east of the burn perimeters in recent years. Even properties that never faced direct flame exposure can absorb enough particulate to require professional cleaning of HVAC systems, ductwork, and porous surfaces.

## Services we provide in Vandenberg AFB

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in the Vandenberg AFB area frequently involves slow, hidden moisture — a supply line weeping behind drywall, a shower pan that failed years ago, or condensation accumulating in an under-ventilated crawl space. We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation before any drying equipment is placed, so we're not just treating what's visible. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with daily monitoring until affected materials reach documented dry standard.

### Mold remediation

The coastal humidity that defines life near Vandenberg AFB creates favorable conditions for mold growth in attics, crawl spaces, and behind exterior-facing walls. We contain affected areas, remove compromised materials, treat structural surfaces, and clear the air with HEPA filtration before clearance testing. We do not provide medical advice, but we do follow established remediation protocols designed to return spaces to normal fungal ecology.

### Fire and smoke damage cleanup

Smoke residue from even a contained kitchen fire can migrate through a home's HVAC system and settle into insulation, cabinetry, and soft goods far from the origin point. We assess the full path of smoke travel, not just the charred area, and use appropriate cleaning chemistry for each surface type — alkaline cleaners for protein smoke, dry sponges for certain soot types. Odor neutralization is addressed at the source, not masked.

### Reconstruction and build-back

Once mitigation is complete, many property owners need someone to close up the walls, replace flooring, and restore the space to livable condition. We handle the build-back phase in-house, which eliminates the coordination gap between the mitigation contractor and a separate GC — a gap where projects often stall.
