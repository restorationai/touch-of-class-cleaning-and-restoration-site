## Services we provide in Mission Hills

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Mission Hills frequently starts small and reveals itself large. A slow supply-line failure under a bathroom vanity can saturate the subfloor, wick into wall cavities, and reach the adjacent bedroom before anyone notices the soft spot underfoot. Coastal Restoration Services Inc uses thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the actual extent of saturation — not just the visible wet area — then sets commercial drying equipment to bring structural materials to documented dry standards before any rebuild begins. ### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a kitchen fire or a brush-fire ember intrusion, the visible char is rarely the biggest problem. Smoke residue — particularly the oily, protein-based smoke from cooking fires — penetrates drywall, settles into HVAC ductwork, and leaves an odor that resurfaces every time the heat kicks on. Our fire restoration process in Mission Hills includes structural assessment, controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, HEPA air scrubbing, surface cleaning with appropriate chemical agents, and odor neutralization before reconstruction begins.

### Mold remediation

Mold can begin colonizing a wet surface within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.
