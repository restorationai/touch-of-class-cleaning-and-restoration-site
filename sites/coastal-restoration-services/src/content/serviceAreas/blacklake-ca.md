## Services we provide in Blacklake

### Water damage mitigation and structural drying

Water damage in Blacklake frequently starts small — a slow supply line drip under a kitchen island, condensation buildup inside a poorly ventilated crawl space, or a roof penetration that lets in just enough moisture to saturate insulation before anyone notices. By the time visible staining appears, the subfloor or wall cavity behind it has often been wet for days. Coastal Restoration Services Inc uses thermal imaging and moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation, then deploys commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the structure to documented standards before any repairs begin.

### Mold remediation

The combination of Blacklake's periodic humidity and the area's older housing stock creates real mold risk — particularly in crawl spaces, attic decking, and bathrooms that share walls with exterior framing. Remediation work is scoped to contain the affected area, remove compromised materials safely, and treat surfaces so the underlying moisture problem — not just the visible growth — is addressed. Clearance testing protocols can be coordinated with a third-party industrial hygienist if your insurance carrier or HOA requires documentation.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Wildfire smoke events in San Luis Obispo County leave a fine oily residue on interior surfaces that ordinary cleaning doesn't remove — it embeds in drywall texture, HVAC ductwork, and soft goods, and the odor returns with humidity. Coastal Restoration Services Inc uses dry-chemical sponges, thermal fogging, and hydroxyl treatment to address smoke penetration, not just surface discoloration. Structure fires in residential properties also require careful coordination with the local building department before reconstruction work can begin.

### Reconstruction and build-back

Once mitigation is complete and the structure is dry and cleared, Coastal Restoration Services Inc can carry the project through reconstruction — replacing drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and finish work so you're dealing with one point of contact from the emergency call through the final walk-through.
