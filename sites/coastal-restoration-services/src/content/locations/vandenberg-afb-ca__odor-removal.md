## Why Vandenberg AFB Properties Hold Odors Longer

The Santa Barbara County coastline around Vandenberg sits in a persistent marine influence zone. Humidity rarely drops below 60% overnight, and in the early morning hours it often climbs well above 80%. Odor molecules — particularly those from smoke, pet contamination, or sewage backup — bond to porous surfaces like drywall, insulation, carpet backing, and wood framing. When ambient humidity stays elevated, those surfaces never fully dry out, which means the odor compounds stay chemically active and continue off-gassing.

Base housing units and the civilian properties clustered just outside the installation share another common trait: many were built or last renovated during construction cycles that favored dense insulation and low air-exchange rates for energy efficiency. That's good for utility bills but it means odors recirculate rather than dilute. A single smoke event — a kitchen fire, a wildfire smoke intrusion during a Santa Ana wind reversal, or even a heavy tobacco situation in a unit being turned over — can saturate HVAC ductwork and wall cavities in ways that surface cleaning alone will never resolve.

## Our Deodorization Process for Vandenberg AFB Properties

Effective odor removal here starts with identifying where the odor source has migrated, not just where it's strongest. After an initial walkthrough and moisture mapping, the team determines which combination of methods fits the structure and contamination type.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective for smoke odor in enclosed spaces. A fogging agent is heated into a fine vapor that penetrates the same microscopic pathways smoke traveled — into wall cavities, behind baseboards, inside cabinetry — and neutralizes odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.

**Hydroxyl generation** is the preferred method when occupants or pets need to re-enter the space relatively quickly, or when ozone is not appropriate for the materials present. Hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing volatile organic compounds without the off-gassing concerns associated with high-concentration ozone.

**Ozone treatment** remains a powerful tool for heavily contaminated vacant spaces — fire-damaged rooms, long-term tobacco saturation, or units with severe pet odor. Ozone at treatment concentrations requires the space to be unoccupied, and we coordinate timing carefully with homeowners, property managers, or base housing offices to minimize disruption.

All work is performed by an IICRC Certified Firm team. The same technicians trained under WRT and ASD standards handle deodorization, which matters because moisture and odor problems are rarely separate issues — a musty smell is almost always a moisture story underneath.
