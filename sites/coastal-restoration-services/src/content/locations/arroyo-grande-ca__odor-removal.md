## Why Arroyo Grande Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same coastal air that makes Arroyo Grande one of the most livable spots on the Central Coast also makes odor remediation more technically demanding. Relative humidity along the Highway 1 corridor regularly climbs above 80% on summer mornings, and that moisture acts as a carrier — it drives odor compounds deeper into porous materials like drywall, subfloor sheathing, insulation batts, and the old-growth redwood framing common in homes built before the 1980s. Once those materials are saturated with smoke, mold, or biological odors, a surface spray or plug-in deodorizer does nothing but mask the problem for a few days.

Arroyo Grande also has a significant stock of older single-family homes — many with original hardwood floors, plaster-and-lath walls, and crawl spaces that vent directly under living areas. Crawl space odors from rodent intrusion, standing moisture, or a slow drain leak can migrate upward through gaps in the subfloor and become nearly impossible to localize without a systematic inspection. The combination of older construction and coastal humidity means odor problems here tend to be layered, not single-source.

## Our Deodorization Process in Arroyo Grande

Every job starts with a source assessment — we do not begin deodorization until we have identified and physically removed or cleaned the contaminated material. Deodorizing over an unaddressed source is a temporary fix that will fail within weeks.

Once the source is addressed, we select the appropriate technology for the property:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed through the structure, following the same pathways smoke or odor traveled. Particularly effective after fire and smoke events where residue has penetrated wall cavities.
- **Hydroxyl generation** — UV-light machines produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules at the chemical level. Safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a business on Traffic Way needs to stay partially operational during treatment.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is deployed in unoccupied spaces for a timed cycle. Highly effective on stubborn biological and smoke odors, but requires full evacuation of people, pets, and plants. We schedule ozone treatments carefully and ventilate thoroughly before re-occupancy.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and activated carbon filtration** — used in tandem with the above to capture particulates and VOCs during and after treatment.

For homes with crawl spaces — common throughout Arroyo Grande's older residential neighborhoods — we extend treatment below the subfloor and assess vapor barrier condition, since a deteriorated or missing vapor barrier is frequently the hidden source of persistent musty odors.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Not every odor problem calls for the same tool. Smoke odor from a kitchen fire behaves differently than cat urine that has soaked through hardwood into the subfloor, which behaves differently than a sewage backup that has wicked into drywall. We carry thermal foggers, commercial hydroxyl generators, ozone machines rated for large square footage, and truck-mounted HEPA filtration units. Technicians are trained to match the method to the odor chemistry — ozone, for example, is highly effective on smoke and biological odors but less effective on certain petroleum-based smells, where thermal fogging with a counteractant is the better choice.

All deodorization work is documented with before-and-after readings where applicable and written scope notes, which supports insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was done.
