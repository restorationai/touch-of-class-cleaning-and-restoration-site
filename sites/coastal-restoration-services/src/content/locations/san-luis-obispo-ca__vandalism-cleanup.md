## Why San Luis Obispo Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The building stock in San Luis Obispo skews older and more architecturally varied than many California cities its size. Downtown commercial blocks and residential properties near the Cal Poly campus frequently feature exposed brick, painted stucco, and decorative tile — all of which react to spray paint and solvent-based markers in ways that plain drywall or vinyl siding simply do not. Stucco, especially the sand-finish variety common on mid-century buildings throughout the city, is highly porous. Aerosol paint wicks into the surface texture within hours, meaning a cleanup crew that arrives the following morning is already working against deeper pigment penetration than one that arrives the same night.

San Luis Obispo's Mediterranean climate — warm, dry summers and mild, occasionally wet winters — also matters. UV intensity here is significant, and paint left on a sun-facing south or west wall can begin to cure and cross-link with the substrate surprisingly fast during summer months. Conversely, winter moisture from coastal fog and seasonal rain can cause water-soluble vandal markers to run and spread if not addressed before the next weather event.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in San Luis Obispo

Every job begins with a documented walkthrough. We photograph all affected surfaces before touching anything — this record is essential for insurance claims and, in cases involving repeat incidents, for law enforcement documentation. From there, the process is calibrated to the specific material:

- **Graffiti on stucco or masonry**: We test solvent compatibility on an inconspicuous area before applying any chemical remover. On older stucco, aggressive solvents can lift the finish coat along with the paint. We use a tiered approach — starting with the mildest effective agent and stepping up only as needed — followed by a low-pressure rinse to avoid driving residue deeper into the substrate.
- **Broken glass cleanup**: We clear visible glass first, then use fine-particle vacuums and adhesive rollers to capture shards from flooring, upholstery, and HVAC return vents. Glass migrates farther than it appears to, and we treat a radius well beyond the obvious impact zone.
- **Interior vandalism damage**: Paint on drywall, damaged fixtures, and forced-entry damage to door frames or window casings are assessed for repair versus replacement. We document scope thoroughly before any demolition so the insurance adjuster has a clear before-and-after record.
- **Odor and biohazard components**: Some vandalism incidents involve substances beyond paint — in those cases, we follow appropriate decontamination protocols and can coordinate with licensed specialists if the scope requires it.
