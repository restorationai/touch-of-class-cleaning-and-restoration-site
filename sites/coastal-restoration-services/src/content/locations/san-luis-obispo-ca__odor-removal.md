## Why San Luis Obispo Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same mild, marine-influenced air that makes San Luis Obispo one of the most livable cities on the Central Coast also slows the natural off-gassing that helps odors dissipate on their own. Relative humidity along the SLO corridor regularly sits in the 70–85% range on marine-layer mornings, and that moisture gives odor-causing molecules — particularly smoke particulates and microbial VOCs from mold — a medium to cling to.

Older housing stock compounds the problem. A significant portion of San Luis Obispo's residential neighborhoods feature homes built before 1980, many with original plaster walls, uninsulated crawl spaces, and attic cavities that weren't designed with vapor management in mind. Smoke, pet urine, and sewage odors migrate into these porous assemblies and re-release over weeks or months, even after surface cleaning. A standard deodorizing spray addresses what you can smell at the surface; it doesn't reach the odor reservoir that's built up inside wall cavities, subflooring, or HVAC ductwork.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in San Luis Obispo

Effective odor elimination starts with source identification, not equipment. Before any treatment begins, a technician walks the property to locate where the odor is originating — not just where it's strongest. That distinction matters: a smoke odor that seems to center in a living room may actually be driven by residue inside the return-air duct system, which will re-contaminate the space every time the HVAC runs.

Once the source is confirmed, treatment method is matched to the odor type and the building materials involved:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizer is vaporized and introduced into the space, mimicking the way smoke penetrated surfaces. The fog particles follow the same pathways the odor took, neutralizing residue inside wall voids and under flooring rather than just coating the surface.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing molecules at a chemical level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl machines operate safely in occupied or partially occupied spaces and are well-suited for ongoing deodorization during a multi-day restoration project.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is highly effective for severe smoke or biological odors in unoccupied structures. The space must be fully vacated during treatment and ventilated thoroughly before re-entry. We schedule ozone applications carefully given SLO's air quality management requirements under the San Luis Obispo County Air Pollution Control District.
- **Content and HVAC deodorization** — porous contents (furniture, clothing, documents) and ductwork often need separate treatment. Leaving contaminated ducts untreated is one of the most common reasons odor returns after a restoration job is otherwise complete.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The equipment matters as much as the chemistry. Industrial hydroxyl generators used in professional restoration produce a far higher concentration of reactive molecules than consumer-grade air purifiers. Thermal foggers calibrated for restoration work atomize deodorizer particles to a size that penetrates porous materials — drywall, wood framing, insulation — rather than sitting on the surface.

For smoke odor removal following a fire, we combine thermal fogging with HEPA air scrubbing to capture fine particulates while the deodorizer works. For biological odors — sewage backup, decomposition, or heavy pet contamination — enzymatic treatments are applied directly to affected materials before any fogging or ozone cycle begins. Enzymes break down the organic compounds that are the actual odor source; without that step, deodorizers mask rather than eliminate.

All methods are selected based on the specific odor type, the materials present, and whether the space will be occupied during treatment — a practical consideration in SLO's tight rental market, where a displaced tenant or a homeowner staying with family adds real urgency to the timeline.
