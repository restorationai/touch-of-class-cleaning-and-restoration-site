## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Arroyo Grande

When a call comes in, the first step is a rapid site assessment — identifying every breach point before a single sheet of material goes up. On roof tarping jobs, that means walking the entire deck, not just the visible damage, because wind events in the Edna Valley corridor frequently cause concealed lifting at ridge caps and flashing points that aren't obvious from the ground.

For broken window board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut to fit the rough opening and fastened with structural screws rather than nails, which matters on stucco-clad homes common throughout Arroyo Grande — nail guns can crack the surrounding stucco face and create new moisture entry points. Boards are sealed at the perimeter with foam backer and weatherproof tape to prevent wind-driven moisture infiltration during the days or weeks before permanent glazing is installed.

Roof tarping follows a similar precision approach: tarps are anchored with wood battens screwed into the decking rather than weighted with sandbags, which reduces the risk of secondary roof damage and holds far better in the offshore wind gusts that accelerate through the valley after a weather system passes.
