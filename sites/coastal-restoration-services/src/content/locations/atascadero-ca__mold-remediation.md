## Why Atascadero Properties See Mold Issues

San Luis Obispo County's inland valleys, including the Salinas River corridor that runs through Atascadero, receive enough winter rainfall to saturate the ground for weeks at a time. Homes built on slab foundations in the 1960s and 1970s — a common era in this part of the Central Coast — often lack the vapor barriers and modern drainage systems that newer construction requires. When groundwater wicks upward through a slab or a slow plumbing leak goes undetected in a bathroom wall, mold can colonize porous materials like drywall and insulation within 24 to 48 hours. Older ranch-style homes in Atascadero are particularly susceptible because their low-pitched rooflines and wide overhangs, while practical for the climate, can trap moisture against exterior sheathing after prolonged rain events.

The area's oak woodland landscaping adds another layer of risk. Decomposing leaf litter against foundation vents or wood siding holds moisture against the structure long after a storm passes, giving spores a foothold on the exterior that can migrate inward through gaps in older framing.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Atascadero

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a quick walkthrough. We identify the moisture source first, because removing visible mold without stopping the water intrusion is a temporary fix at best.

Once the source is confirmed, we establish physical containment using negative-air pressure and 6-mil poly barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the project. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sheathing — are removed and bagged for disposal. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions, then dried to verified moisture targets before any reconstruction begins.

In Atascadero's older homes, we frequently encounter original plaster-and-lath walls in addition to drywall additions from later remodels. Plaster releases absorbed moisture more slowly than standard drywall, so drying timelines in these spaces are extended and monitored with daily readings rather than a fixed schedule. Clearance air sampling is conducted after remediation to confirm spore counts have returned to acceptable levels before we close out the project.
