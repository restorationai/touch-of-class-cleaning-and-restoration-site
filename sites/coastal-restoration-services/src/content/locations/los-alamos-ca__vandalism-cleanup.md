## Why Los Alamos Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The building stock along Los Alamos's main corridor skews old — many commercial facades and residential structures date to the early- and mid-twentieth century, when lime-based stucco and soft adobe brick were standard. These materials are far more porous than modern cement board or painted concrete block. Spray paint and marker ink don't just coat the surface; they wick into the substrate within hours, especially during the warm, dry summers the Santa Ynez Valley is known for. By the time a property owner notices the damage the next morning, the pigment may already be bonded two or three millimeters deep.

That porosity also means that aggressive chemical stripping — the kind that works fine on a sealed concrete wall in a newer commercial district — can pull the face off a historic stucco facade in Los Alamos. Getting the cleanup right requires matching the solvent and agitation method to the specific substrate, not just reaching for the strongest product available.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Los Alamos

Every job starts with a documented assessment before anything is touched. We photograph all affected surfaces in natural and UV light, note the substrate type, and identify secondary damage — broken glass, forced entry points, displaced fixtures — that needs to be addressed alongside the cosmetic work.

For graffiti removal on the older stucco and painted wood common in Los Alamos, we typically work through a staged approach: a test patch with a pH-neutral solvent, followed by low-pressure agitation, followed by a neutralizing rinse. On surfaces where pigment has penetrated deeply, a stain-blocking primer seals residual color before the final finish coat is applied. For broken glass cleanup, we go beyond the visible shards — fine glass particulate settles into carpet fibers, soil, and HVAC returns, and we use HEPA-filtered vacuums and systematic surface wipes to clear it completely.

When structural elements are compromised — a kicked-in door frame, a damaged window sill — we handle the repair in-house rather than leaving the property exposed while a separate contractor is scheduled.
