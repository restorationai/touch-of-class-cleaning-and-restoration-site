## Why Oceano Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The combination of coastal climate and housing stock here creates a specific set of vulnerabilities. A significant portion of Oceano's residential properties were built in the 1960s and 1970s, when cast-iron and Orangeburg sewer laterals were standard. Both materials degrade over time — Orangeburg, a fiber-and-pitch composite, essentially softens and collapses — and neither handles the minor ground movement that comes with wet winters and dry summers on the Central Coast. When those laterals fail, the backup doesn't announce itself gently.

Oceano also has a notable number of properties on septic systems rather than the municipal sewer network. Septic tanks that haven't been pumped on schedule — or that sit in high-water-table zones close to the dunes — are prone to overflow during and after heavy rain events. The Pacific winds that keep Oceano cooler than inland communities also mean that exterior contamination can spread odor and aerosolized particles quickly across a property before cleanup begins.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Oceano

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the cleanup process reflects that. When we arrive at an Oceano property, the first step is containment: establishing physical barriers to prevent cross-contamination from the affected zone to clean areas of the home. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor sections — are typically removed rather than dried in place, because no amount of drying eliminates the pathogens that have already colonized those materials.

Extraction of standing water and solids comes next, followed by a thorough application of EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces. In crawl spaces, which are common in Oceano's older ranch-style homes, we use fogging equipment to reach areas that can't be accessed by hand. After sanitization, we run commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient humidity levels typical of coastal San Luis Obispo County — conditions that require more aggressive drying than you'd set for an inland property in the same square footage. Final clearance involves surface testing to confirm contamination levels are within safe limits before reconstruction begins.

## Oceano Insurance & HOA Coordination

Sewage backups are often covered under homeowners' insurance policies, but the coverage depends heavily on whether the loss originated from a sewer line backup (which typically requires a separate rider) or from an internal plumbing failure (which is more commonly covered under standard policies).
