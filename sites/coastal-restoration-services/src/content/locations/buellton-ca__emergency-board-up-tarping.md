## Why Buellton Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

The Santa Ynez Valley's climate is deceptively variable. Buellton's location along the Highway 246 corridor means properties are exposed to the Sundowner winds that funnel through the transverse ranges — gusts that can lift a poorly anchored tarp off a compromised roof in minutes and deposit it in a neighboring vineyard. Those same wind events are often the original cause of damage: broken skylights, displaced roof tiles on Spanish-style homes, and fence sections driven through sliding glass doors are common call types here.

The local building stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes and commercial buildings along the 246 corridor were built in the 1970s and 1980s with wood-frame construction and stucco exteriors. Once stucco is breached — by fire, impact, or a falling oak limb — moisture infiltrates the wood sheathing beneath faster than it would with modern weather-resistant barriers. A board-up that seals the visible opening but leaves gaps around the stucco crack line can still allow wind-driven rain to track behind the wall assembly. Getting the perimeter seal right matters more here than in drier inland markets.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Buellton

Every job starts with a rapid site assessment: we identify all breach points — not just the obvious broken window or fire-damaged wall, but secondary openings like attic vents, eave gaps, and HVAC penetrations that wind can exploit. That full-perimeter thinking is especially important on Buellton properties where Sundowner-driven rain arrives at sharp angles rather than falling straight down.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure, secured with weighted battens and mechanical fasteners anchored into undamaged decking — not just draped and tied. On fire damage board-up calls, we cut plywood panels to fit each opening precisely, fasten them with structural screws rather than nails (which back out under repeated wind pressure), and frame interior bracing on larger openings like garage doors and picture windows. For broken window board-up on occupied structures, we work to preserve the weather seal at the frame so the interior remains conditioned while repairs are scheduled.

Documentation runs parallel to the physical work: timestamped photographs of every opening before and after boarding, measurements of affected areas, and a written scope summary formatted to support an insurance claim.
