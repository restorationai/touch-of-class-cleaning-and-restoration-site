## Why Los Osos Properties See Elevated Mold Risk

The San Luis Obispo County coast averages relative humidity well above 70% for much of the year, and Los Osos sits at the low end of the Morro Bay estuary where that humidity is most concentrated. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s — a large share of the local housing stock — were framed with construction practices that didn't account for continuous vapor intrusion. Crawl spaces were often left unencapsulated, vapor barriers were thin or absent, and attic ventilation was minimal by current standards.

The result is that mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event and reach visible growth within a week. In homes with original fiberglass batt insulation, spores can travel through wall cavities before a homeowner ever sees surface staining. The clay soils common throughout Los Osos also retain groundwater longer than sandy coastal soils, keeping foundation-adjacent spaces damp well after rain has stopped.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Los Osos

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not just a visual scan. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to find wet building materials that look dry on the surface. In Los Osos homes with original plaster or paneling, this step matters more than it does in newer construction because older materials absorb and hold moisture unevenly.

Once affected areas are identified, containment goes up before any demolition begins. Negative air pressure with HEPA filtration keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, subflooring — that test above acceptable moisture thresholds are removed and bagged on-site. Structural wood is treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent and dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. Final clearance is based on post-remediation air sampling, not a visual check alone.
