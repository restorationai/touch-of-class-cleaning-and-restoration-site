## Why Post-Construction Cleaning at Vandenberg AFB Demands More Than a Broom

The coastal climate here is a complicating factor that most general cleaning crews underestimate. Marine layer humidity — especially during the June and July "June Gloom" stretch — causes fine construction dust to clump and adhere rather than stay loose. Drywall compound residue that would brush off a surface in an inland desert climate instead bonds lightly to window frames, cabinet interiors, and light fixtures when moisture levels stay elevated for days at a time. That same humidity can cause fresh caulk and grout haze to set unevenly if surfaces aren't cleaned within a tight post-cure window.

There's also the soil. The sandy, silica-rich soil common to this stretch of the Santa Barbara County coast tracks into construction sites constantly, embedding in subflooring gaps and grout lines in ways that require pressure-controlled extraction rather than standard mopping. A standard janitorial pass won't pull it out — it compacts it further.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

Every post-construction clean begins with a walk-through assessment before a single piece of equipment comes off the truck. We document the scope — room by room, surface by surface — so nothing gets missed and so the crew working the job understands what the general contractor or property owner considers the acceptance standard.

The sequence matters. We work top-down and inside-out:

- **HVAC and vent cleaning first.** Construction dust migrates into return air ducts faster than most contractors expect. Cleaning surfaces before clearing vents just redistributes particulate the moment the system runs.
- **Rough clean of debris and bulk material.** Scrap lumber, fasteners, packaging, and overspray masking are removed and disposed of properly.
- **Detail clean of all fixed surfaces.** Windows (interior glass, frames, and sills), cabinetry interiors, countertops, light fixtures, and hardware receive individual attention — not a wipe-down pass.
- **Floor care calibrated to the surface.** Hardwood, tile, polished concrete, and carpet each require different chemistry and equipment. We don't use a one-solution approach.
- **Final inspection pass.** We walk the space against the original assessment checklist before calling the job complete.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans, move-in/move-out cleans on properties that sat vacant during construction, or sanitizing after hazardous material abatement — the process is scoped individually.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

Post-construction cleaning isn't a mop-and-bucket operation. We bring HEPA-filtered vacuums capable of capturing fine particulate down to 0.3 microns — the size range where drywall dust and silica particles live. For floor surfaces, we use low-moisture scrubbing equipment that lifts embedded soil without over-wetting subfloors, which matters on coastal properties where subfloor drying can be slow. Window cleaning on new construction uses a two-stage process: a construction film remover followed by a streak-free detail pass, because standard glass cleaner won't cut through the adhesive residue left by protective film.

For specialty cleaning phases — sanitizing, odor neutralization after renovation work, or cleaning after lead or asbestos abatement by a licensed abatement contractor — we use EPA-registered products appropriate to the surface and situation.
