## Why Templeton Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

The rolling grasslands and oak woodlands surrounding Templeton are not just scenic — they are a fire-behavior factor. The local building stock adds another layer of complexity. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Templeton

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive at a Templeton property, the first priority is a safety and structural walk-through — confirming that the roof deck, floor joists, and load-bearing walls are stable before any crew member enters a compromised space. We document everything photographically before touching a surface, which matters enormously when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

Once the structure is cleared, we move through a defined process:

- **Board-up and tarping** to secure openings and prevent weather intrusion — critical in Templeton where afternoon winds can drive dust and debris into an already-damaged interior.
- **Dry demolition** of unsalvageable materials, separated and documented for the insurance scope.
- **HEPA vacuuming and dry-sponge cleaning** of soot from ceilings, walls, and structural cavities before any wet cleaning begins — reversing this order drives soot deeper into porous surfaces.
- **Odor neutralization** using thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation, reaching into wall cavities and HVAC systems where smoke odor hides long after visible residue is gone.
- **Structural drying and moisture control**, because firefighting water introduces a secondary damage layer that must be resolved before reconstruction begins.
- **Reconstruction coordination**, from framing and drywall to finish work, managed through a single point of contact.
