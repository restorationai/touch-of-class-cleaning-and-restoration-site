## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Grover Beach

Every job starts with a rapid assessment of the breach — window, door, roof section, or wall — and a documentation pass with photos before any material goes up. That documentation matters for your insurance claim, and it lets us size the repair correctly the first time rather than patching around the problem.

For window and door board-ups, we use exterior-grade plywood cut to fit the opening and secured with structural screws into the surrounding framing, not just the window trim. Trim-only fastening pulls out under wind load; frame-anchored boarding holds. For roof damage, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for UV exposure — important in a climate where even overcast days deliver meaningful UV — and we secure them with weighted battens and anchor lines tied to ridge points or structural elements rather than gutters, which can't carry that load.

When fire damage is involved, we coordinate the board-up with the smoke and soot documentation process so your adjuster has a clear record of the structure's condition at the time of securing. That sequencing matters: disturbing a fire-damaged opening before it's photographed can complicate coverage determinations.
