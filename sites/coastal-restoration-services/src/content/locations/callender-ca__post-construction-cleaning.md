## Why Callender Properties Face Distinct Post-Construction Cleanup Challenges

Callender sits in a region where daytime temperatures climb sharply and the air carries a fine mineral dust that is already present before a single saw blade spins. Add construction activity — concrete grinding, spray-texture application, spray foam insulation — and the particulate load inside a job site multiplies quickly. That dust does not stay put. It migrates through HVAC returns, settles into cabinet interiors, and coats plumbing fixtures before the plumber has even finished rough-in. Homes in the area also tend to use stucco exteriors, which means crews are cutting and patching a calcium-rich material that leaves a white chalky residue on every nearby surface if the site is not properly contained.

Older residential stock in the region often has original single-pane windows with aluminum frames — a combination that traps construction dust in the track channels and leaves visible streaking on the glass that standard window cleaning will not fully address. Specialty cleaning methods, including detail vacuuming with HEPA-filtered equipment and controlled solvent wipe-downs, are required to bring those surfaces to move-in condition.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Callender

Every job starts with a structured walk-through to document the scope before a single tool comes off the truck. We categorize surfaces by material type and contamination level — rough debris, construction dust, adhesive residue, paint overspray, and protective film removal each require a different approach and a different sequence.

**Phase 1 — Rough Clean:** Bulk debris removal, broom-down of all horizontal surfaces, and disposal of packaging material, scrap lumber, and leftover fasteners. We coordinate with the general contractor on any material they want to retain.

**Phase 2 — Detail Clean:** HEPA-vacuum of all surfaces including window tracks, cabinet interiors, light fixture housings, and HVAC registers. Adhesive residue is treated with appropriate solvents matched to the substrate — what works on tile grout does not belong on engineered hardwood.

**Phase 3 — Final Clean:** Streak-free glass cleaning on all windows and mirrors, polish of hardware, wipe-down of all painted millwork, and a final floor pass calibrated to the specific flooring type installed. On polished concrete — increasingly common in Callender-area new builds — we use a pH-neutral cleaner to avoid dulling the surface finish.

**Phase 4 — Verification Walk:** A documented punch-list review with the property owner or general contractor before we close out the job.
