## Why Cambria Properties See Mold Issues

Cambria's coastal climate is the primary driver. Relative humidity along this stretch of San Luis Obispo County regularly climbs above 80 percent overnight, even when the daytime feels dry. That persistent moisture seeps into wall cavities, subfloor assemblies, and attic sheathing — especially in the village's older craftsman bungalows and redwood-sided cottages, many of which were built before modern vapor barriers were standard practice.

Crawl spaces are a particular pressure point. The sloped terrain in and around Cambria means some homes sit close to grade on one side while the opposite end is fully elevated, creating uneven airflow beneath the structure. Standing moisture accumulates in low corners, and without adequate cross-ventilation, mold colonies establish themselves on the underside of subfloor joists before any visible sign appears inside the living space. Roof penetrations — skylights, chimney flashings, and older shake roofs — are another common entry point after the heavy rain events that arrive between November and March.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Cambria

Every project starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We identify the moisture source first — because mold remediation without source correction is temporary — then establish the full extent of colonization before any physical work begins.

Containment comes next. We seal affected areas with poly barriers and run negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. In Cambria's smaller cottages, where square footage is tight and rooms flow directly into each other, precise containment matters more than it does in a sprawling suburban floor plan.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed and bagged for disposal according to California's hazardous waste handling guidelines. Structural wood that can be salvaged is treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and dried to verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which becomes important when the work involves an insurance claim.
