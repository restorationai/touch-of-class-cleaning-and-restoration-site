## Why Vandenberg Village Properties Are Vulnerable After a Vandalism Event

The housing stock throughout Vandenberg Village leans heavily on exterior stucco — a durable finish in dry conditions, but one that absorbs aerosol paint differently than wood siding or fiber cement. Spray paint solvents wick into the porous texture quickly, and the mild but persistent marine humidity common to this part of Santa Barbara County slows the evaporation that would otherwise keep the pigment near the surface. The result: what looks like surface graffiti on day one can become a stain that requires grinding or skim-coat patching by day three.

Beyond graffiti, broken glass is one of the most common secondary hazards after a vandalism incident. Sliding patio doors and single-pane aluminum-frame windows — both standard in the tract homes built here during the late postwar decades — shatter into large, irregular shards rather than the safer tempered fragments found in newer construction. Cleanup requires more than a broom; it means systematic fragment mapping, careful extraction from door tracks and window channels, and inspection of adjacent flooring and cabinetry for embedded glass.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Vandenberg Village

When Coastal Restoration Services Inc responds to a vandalism call in Vandenberg Village, the first step is a structured site assessment — documenting every affected surface with photographs and written notes before anything is touched. That documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for scoping the correct cleaning method for each material.

For graffiti on stucco, we test the paint type (latex, enamel, or aerosol lacquer) before selecting a removal agent. Alkaline degreasers work well on fresh latex; older or oil-based marks often require a solvent gel that dwells on the surface to lift pigment without etching the stucco texture. On painted wood trim — common on the eaves and fascia of older Vandenberg Village homes — we use lower-pressure application to avoid grain-raising.

Broken glass cleanup follows a contained protocol: we establish a debris perimeter, remove large fragments by hand with cut-resistant gloves, then vacuum fine particles from flooring, upholstery, and HVAC intake areas. Board-up or temporary glazing is installed the same visit to secure the opening and protect the interior from the coastal evening air until permanent glass replacement is arranged.

Odor from smoke, aerosol propellants, or chemical agents left by vandals is addressed with HEPA air scrubbing and, where needed, hydroxyl treatment in enclosed spaces.
