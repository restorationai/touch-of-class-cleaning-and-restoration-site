## Why Lompoc Properties Hold Odors Longer

Lompoc's climate creates a specific challenge that sets it apart from drier inland communities in Santa Barbara County. The marine layer keeps relative humidity elevated through most of the morning, sometimes well into the afternoon during summer months. High ambient humidity means odor-causing compounds — volatile organic compounds from smoke residue, microbial off-gassing from mold, or ammonia from pet urine — stay suspended in the air and continue absorbing into soft materials like carpet padding, insulation, and wood framing rather than dissipating.

The city's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in Lompoc were built during the mid-20th century expansion tied to Vandenberg Space Force Base activity, and those properties often feature original hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath wall construction, and minimal vapor barriers under the slab. Plaster walls are denser than modern drywall and absorb odor compounds deeply — meaning a smoke or water event that might take two days to deodorize in a newer tract home can require an extra treatment cycle in an older Lompoc property.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Lompoc

Effective deodorization isn't a single product sprayed on a surface — it's a sequence of steps calibrated to the odor source, the affected materials, and the environmental conditions on the day of service.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization method works long-term if the contaminated material is still present. We locate and remove charred debris, saturated insulation, or compromised flooring before any treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging** penetrates porous surfaces — wood framing, subfloor boards, wall cavities — by converting a deodorizing solution into a fine fog that follows the same pathways smoke or moisture traveled. This is particularly effective in Lompoc's older homes where odor has migrated into wall cavities.

**Hydroxyl generation** uses UV-light-driven chemistry to break down odor molecules in the air and on surfaces without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators can run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner can't easily relocate.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for severe cases — heavy smoke damage, long-standing mold odor, or biohazard situations — where a high-concentration oxidizing agent is needed. Because ozone requires the space to be unoccupied during treatment, we coordinate timing carefully with property owners.

After treatment, we verify results with calibrated air sampling rather than relying on the technician's nose alone. Coastal conditions can temporarily mask odors, so objective measurement before we close out a job is standard practice.
