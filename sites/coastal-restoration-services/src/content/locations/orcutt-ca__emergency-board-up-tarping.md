## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Orcutt

Every job starts with a documented assessment — photographs and measurements that capture the scope of the breach before we touch anything. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and establishes a baseline if the adjuster disputes pre-existing conditions.

For window and door board-ups, we use ¾-inch plywood cut to fit and secured with exterior-grade fasteners at intervals that meet structural support requirements — not the thin OSB staple-gun approach that fails in the first wind event. For fire-damaged roofs, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and anchored with lumber battens rather than just bungee cords or sandbags, which shift under sustained wind load. When the roof structure itself is compromised, we assess load-bearing capacity before sending anyone onto the surface.
