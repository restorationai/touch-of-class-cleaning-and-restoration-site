## Why Oceano Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Oceano's geography does most of the explaining. The town sits directly downwind of the Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area, and the fine silica sand that drifts across the flats is abrasive enough to degrade roofing materials and window seals faster than in inland communities. Homes that might go five or six years without a roof inspection in a drier climate can develop cracked flashing or lifted shingles here in two or three. When a storm or fire finally breaches that already-stressed envelope, the exposure is immediate.

The housing stock adds another layer. Much of Oceano's residential inventory was built in the 1950s and 1960s, with wood-frame construction and original single-pane windows that are more vulnerable to impact, pressure differentials, and fire-related heat fracturing than modern assemblies. Older roof decks are often thinner sheathing over widely spaced rafters — a configuration that makes emergency tarping more technically demanding because anchor points need to be placed carefully to avoid compounding structural stress.

The marine layer also means that any opening in the building envelope — a broken window, a fire-damaged wall section, a wind-lifted roof panel — begins admitting high-humidity air almost immediately. Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours under those moisture conditions. Getting a proper board-up or tarp in place is not just about keeping rain out; it is about stopping that humidity clock.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Oceano

Every job starts with a rapid exterior assessment before any material goes up. We photograph and document the full scope of the breach — critical for insurance claims and for making sure the temporary protection actually covers the vulnerable area rather than just the most visible damage. In fire-loss situations, we coordinate with the fire department to confirm the structure has been cleared before we stage equipment.

For window and door board-ups, we use 5/8-inch plywood cut to fit and secured with exterior-grade fasteners. On older Oceano homes with wood siding, we take care with fastener placement to avoid splitting aged trim boards that would otherwise be salvageable. When a property has decorative or historically significant millwork — not uncommon in some of the older blocks near the town center — we use surface-protection barriers before any panel contacts the facade.

Roof tarping in a coastal environment requires heavier-duty material than a standard poly tarp. We use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure, weighted and strapped at the ridge and eaves rather than simply draped. In the wind conditions that accompany Central Coast winter storms, an improperly secured tarp becomes a liability — it can peel back and cause additional damage, or create a hazard for neighboring properties. We secure every tarp as if the next night will bring 40-mph gusts, because along this stretch of coast, it might.

## Oceano Insurance Coordination for Board-Up and Tarping

Temporary protective measures — board-up and tarping — are typically covered under the "additional living expenses" or "protection of property" provisions of a standard homeowner's policy, but the documentation requirements matter. Carriers want photographs of the pre-board condition, a written scope of the work performed, and itemized materials. We provide all three as a standard part of every job, formatted in a way that most adjusters can process without a supplemental request.

If your loss is fire-related, your adjuster may want to walk the property before permanent repairs begin. The board-up keeps the site stable and secure during that waiting period. We can also coordinate directly with your carrier's preferred vendor list if that is a requirement of your policy.
