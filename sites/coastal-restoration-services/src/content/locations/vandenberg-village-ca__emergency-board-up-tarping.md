## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Vandenberg Village

When we arrive on-site, the first step is a rapid exterior and interior assessment to identify every breach — not just the obvious ones. Wind events in this area frequently cause secondary damage: lifted ridge caps, cracked vent covers, and displaced soffit panels that aren't visible from the street. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up, which matters when your insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

For broken windows and door frames, we use 3/4-inch OSB panels cut to fit and secured with structural screws into the framing rather than surface-mounted staples. For roof damage, we deploy reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for UV exposure and wind uplift, anchored with weighted batten boards rather than simple rope ties — a method that holds through the kind of gusts that come off the Pacific in late fall and winter. On stucco exteriors common in this area, we use non-penetrating anchor points wherever the framing layout allows, to avoid creating additional repair work on the finish coat.
