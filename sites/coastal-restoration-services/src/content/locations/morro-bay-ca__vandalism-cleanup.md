## Why Morro Bay Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Morro Bay's working waterfront character means a mix of older commercial buildings, vacation rental cottages, and single-family homes with wood siding, redwood trim, and weathered masonry — all of which are more porous and chemically reactive than newer construction materials. Salt air accelerates oxidation on metal fixtures and window frames, so broken glass or pried hardware left unaddressed even briefly can show rust staining within days. Fog-heavy mornings keep surfaces damp, which means aerosol paint soaks in rather than sitting on top, and standard pressure washing alone rarely removes it cleanly without leaving ghost shadows in the substrate.

Graffiti on rough-textured stucco — common on older commercial blocks closer to the harbor — is especially stubborn because the pigment lodges in the texture peaks and valleys. Restoration here often requires a combination of chemical dwell time, low-pressure agitation, and a color-matched sealer coat to bring the surface back to a consistent appearance rather than just a scrubbed one.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Morro Bay

Every job starts with a documented assessment — photographs, surface material identification, and a scope of work before any cleaning begins. This documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for choosing the right chemistry: alkaline cleaners for latex-based spray paint, solvent-based products for oil or enamel, and specialty poultice compounds for paint that has cured into porous masonry or brick.

For broken glass and forced-entry damage, the priority is securing the opening first — boarding, temporary glazing, or hardware replacement — so the property is protected while surface restoration work continues. We remove all glass fragments including the fine shards that embed in door frames and thresholds, which are a common source of injury days after the initial incident. Structural damage to door frames, window casings, or siding is assessed and repaired or referred to the appropriate trade depending on scope.

For interior vandalism — paint on walls, damaged fixtures, or debris left behind — we follow a clean-then-restore sequence, treating affected surfaces before patching or repainting so contamination doesn't migrate into new materials.
