## Why Los Alamos Properties Face Persistent Odor Problems

The Santa Ynez Valley's climate is deceptively mild, but that mild coastal influence creates real challenges for odor control. Morning fog deposits fine moisture on porous surfaces — rough-sawn redwood siding, adobe-style stucco, and the tongue-and-groove interior paneling common in Los Alamos's late-19th and early-20th century commercial buildings along Bell Street. When smoke, pet dander, mold off-gassing, or sewage gases absorb into those surfaces during a humid morning, afternoon heat essentially bakes them in. Standard air fresheners and even consumer-grade ozone machines address airborne molecules; they do nothing for odor compounds that have polymerized into porous materials over repeated wet-dry cycles.

The agricultural character of the area adds another layer. Properties near vineyards or livestock operations can carry ambient organic odors that complicate post-loss deodorization — a fire-damaged home that also sits downwind of a working ranch needs a treatment plan that accounts for baseline odor load, not just the acute loss event.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Los Alamos

Every job starts with source identification. No deodorization method works if the odor-generating material is still present — charred framing, saturated insulation, or contaminated subfloor must be removed or encapsulated first. Once the source is addressed, we select the right technology for the structure and the odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum- or water-based deodorizing fog is heated to create a fine aerosol that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules on contact with surfaces inside wall cavities and ceiling voids. Particularly effective in the older balloon-frame construction found in parts of Los Alamos where smoke migrates vertically through open stud bays.
- **Hydroxyl generation** — hydroxyl radicals break down odor-causing volatile organic compounds (VOCs) at the molecular level without displacing occupants or harming plants and pets. Useful in occupied vacation rentals or wine-country bed-and-breakfast properties where ozone's evacuation requirement is impractical.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is deployed in unoccupied structures for set dwell periods, then fully purged before re-entry. Highly effective on smoke and biological odors in sealed spaces, but requires strict safety protocols and complete evacuation.
- **Counteractant application** — water-based counteractants are applied to porous surfaces (concrete slabs, subfloor sheathing, framing) to neutralize residual odor compounds that equipment alone cannot reach.

For most Los Alamos properties, we combine two or more of these methods in sequence, calibrated to the building's age, construction type, and the nature of the odor event.
