## Why Vandenberg AFB Properties Experience Sewage Backup Problems

The combination of coastal clay soils and the region's periodic heavy rain events — concentrated mostly in the November-through-March window — puts real stress on aging sewer laterals. Clay soils shift seasonally as they absorb and release moisture, and that movement can crack or offset pipe joints over time. On a military installation like Vandenberg AFB, housing stock ranges from mid-century construction with original cast-iron drain lines to more recent residential units, and older pipes are particularly vulnerable to root intrusion and joint separation. When those lines fail under a surge of stormwater infiltration, the backup doesn't just stop at the floor drain — it can migrate under slab foundations or into wall cavities before anyone notices the smell.

Septic systems serving properties on the outer edges of the installation face a related challenge: the slow-draining clay profile means a saturated drain field can cause a septic overflow even without a mechanical failure. That produces a different contamination scenario than a municipal sewer backup — the effluent is typically older and more concentrated, which affects both the extraction approach and the sanitization chemistry required.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated category — and the work sequence matters as much as the equipment. The process starts with containment: affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination into clean spaces, and anyone entering the work zone wears full PPE. Standing water and solid waste are removed first using truck-mounted extraction and manual removal of porous materials that cannot be effectively sanitized (drywall below the flood line, saturated insulation, carpet and pad).

Once the bulk removal is complete, all hard surfaces — concrete, tile, subfloor sheathing that passes a structural assessment — are treated with an EPA-registered disinfectant appropriate for Category 3 contamination. On Vandenberg AFB properties with slab-on-grade construction, particular attention goes to the seam where the slab meets the wall framing, because sewage wicks into that joint and the odor will return if it isn't addressed directly. After disinfection, the space is dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the ambient humidity levels typical of the Central Coast, which run higher than inland areas and extend drying times accordingly. Moisture readings are logged throughout so there's a documented record of the drying progression.
