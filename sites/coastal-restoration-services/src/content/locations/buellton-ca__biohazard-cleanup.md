## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Buellton

Every biohazard remediation job begins with a controlled, documented assessment. The affected area is isolated before any work begins — this protects unaffected rooms and gives the property's occupants or family members a clear boundary. We work in full personal protective equipment appropriate to the exposure category, whether the situation involves blood and bodily fluids, sharps and needle cleanup, infectious material, or unattended circumstances.

Materials that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are removed, containerized, and transported as regulated biological waste in compliance with California Department of Public Health and Santa Barbara County Environmental Health Services requirements. What remains is treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, and surfaces are tested before we consider a space cleared. We photograph the affected area before and after — documentation that matters both for your peace of mind and for any insurance claim that follows.

For sharps and needle cleanup specifically, every item is inventoried and disposed of through a licensed medical waste vendor. No sharps leave the site in standard refuse.
