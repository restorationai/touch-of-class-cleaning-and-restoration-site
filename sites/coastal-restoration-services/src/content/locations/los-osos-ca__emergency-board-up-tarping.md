## Why Los Osos Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

The geography here creates a specific set of vulnerabilities. Fog drip from the Irish Hills can saturate a roof overnight, and when that roof already has compromised flashing or aging asphalt shingles — common on the ranch-style and mid-century tract homes that make up much of Los Osos's housing stock — a single wind event during a Pacific storm can pull up entire sections. The same onshore winds that make the bay beautiful funnel through gaps in the coastal hills and hit properties broadside, driving rain horizontally under eaves and through any opening left unprotected.

Fire damage is a separate but equally urgent driver. Wildland-urban interface conditions on the hills above town mean that structure fires here sometimes involve ember cast, which can crack or shatter windows on the windward side of a house even when the main fire is contained. A broken window board-up in that scenario isn't just about security — it's about stopping smoke and soot infiltration from continuing after the flames are out.

Salt air also degrades the standard materials used in temporary board-up faster than in inland areas. Untreated plywood can begin delaminating in under a week in a marine environment. That's a detail worth knowing before choosing who does your emergency board-up and tarping in Los Osos.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Los Osos

When a call comes in for a Los Osos property, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment — identifying every breach point, not just the obvious one. A fire that blew out the front windows may have also compromised a soffit vent or loosened ridge cap shingles that won't hold through the next fog-heavy night.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for coastal wind loads, secured with weighted lumber battens and screwed-down perimeter boards rather than simple tie-downs that can fail in gusting onshore conditions. The tarp is lapped over the ridge when possible so water sheds away from the structure rather than pooling at the breach point.

For broken window board-up and door board-up, we cut plywood to fit the rough opening, apply a foam backer at the perimeter to block wind-driven moisture infiltration, and fasten with structural screws rather than nails — a detail that matters when the board-up needs to hold for several weeks while insurance claims are processed and contractors are scheduled. We photograph every opening before and after securing it, which feeds directly into the documentation your adjuster will need.

Fire damage board-up work gets an additional step: we assess whether smoke and soot are still migrating through the structure and seal interior-facing gaps in the board-up to slow further contamination of unaffected rooms.
