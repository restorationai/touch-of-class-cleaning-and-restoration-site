## Why Lompoc Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Lompoc's geography creates a specific set of vulnerabilities that aren't common in inland California cities. The Santa Ynez Mountains funnel marine air through the valley, and the area's proximity to Vandenberg Space Force Base means that fog and low-pressure systems can linger for days — keeping a compromised roof wet long after the initial event. Older residential neighborhoods in Lompoc contain a significant share of mid-century wood-frame construction with original single-pane windows, wood soffits, and composition shingles that are more fragile under impact and more susceptible to wind uplift than modern builds.

Fire damage board-up calls in Lompoc frequently involve homes where the original framing is older fir or redwood — dimensionally different from today's lumber. Securing openings in these structures requires custom-cut plywood rather than standard panel sizes, and fastening into older framing demands care to avoid splitting already heat-stressed wood. Broken window board-up after a vehicle impact or vandalism is also a recurring call type here, particularly along commercial corridors where storefronts sit close to the street.

## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lompoc

When you call (805) 345-7440, the first thing we do is gather information about the type of opening, the approximate size, and whether there is an active fire suppression or utility hazard on site. That allows us to load the right materials before leaving Vandenberg Village — heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for coastal wind exposure, pre-cut plywood stock, structural screws, and ridge-cap battens for roof tarping applications.

On arrival, the crew assesses the full perimeter before touching anything. For roof tarping, we anchor tarps with weighted battens and cap-nail the edges into solid decking — not just over damaged sheathing — so the tarp can withstand sustained coastal gusts without lifting at the corners. Board-up panels are cut to overlap the rough opening by a minimum of four inches on each side and are fastened into structural members, not just the window casing. Every opening gets photographed before and after installation, which matters when you're filing a claim with your homeowner's carrier.

For fire damage board-up specifically, we coordinate with the fire department's incident commander when crews are still on scene, and we wait for clearance before entering any area with structural compromise. Safety is non-negotiable on a fire loss.
