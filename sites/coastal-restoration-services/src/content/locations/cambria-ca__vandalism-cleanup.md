The town's proximity to Highway 1 also means that transient foot traffic passes through regularly, particularly during peak tourist season from late spring through fall. Storefronts, public-facing fences, and the exterior walls of short-term rental properties are the most common targets.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Cambria

Every job starts with a thorough walk-through to document all damage — photographs, written notes, and measurements — before any cleaning begins. That documentation matters for insurance claims and, in some cases, for law enforcement reports.

For graffiti on wood surfaces, which is the most common scenario in Cambria, we test the substrate first. Cedar and redwood respond differently to solvent-based removers than painted surfaces do, and applying the wrong product can raise the grain or bleach the wood unevenly. We use low-pressure application methods rather than high-pressure washing on older wood siding to avoid driving moisture into wall cavities — a particular concern in homes where the original building paper may be decades old.

Broken glass cleanup follows a strict containment protocol: interior glass is vacuumed with HEPA-filtered equipment, and exterior fragments are collected and bagged before any sweeping begins to prevent tracking. If a broken window or door has left an opening overnight, we assess whether interior contents have been exposed to moisture or unauthorized entry and document accordingly.

For etching on glass — a less common but more permanent form of vandalism — we evaluate whether the pane can be polished or needs full replacement, and we coordinate with local glaziers when replacement is the right call.
