## Why Atascadero Properties See Distinct Vandalism Challenges

Atascadero's building stock tells a story in two chapters. The residential side of the city skews toward stucco-clad homes on larger lots, many with wood-framed garages, fencing, and outbuildings that are common targets for tagging. ## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Atascadero

Every job starts with a surface-by-surface assessment before any chemical or equipment touches the property. Stucco, painted wood, glass, powder-coated metal, and raw concrete each respond to different removal approaches, and using the wrong solvent on the wrong substrate can leave a ghost image or cause surface damage that costs more to repair than the original vandalism.

For graffiti on exterior stucco — the most common call we receive in Atascadero — the process typically runs: chemical pre-treatment with an appropriate paint stripper, controlled dwell time calibrated to ambient temperature (hotter days require shorter dwell to avoid substrate bleaching), low-pressure rinse, and color-match repaint or anti-graffiti coating application. Broken glass cleanup follows a documented debris sweep, framing inspection for secondary damage, and temporary board-up or glazing depending on the scope. Interior vandalism — overturned fixtures, deliberate flooding, or contamination — is assessed under the same IICRC-certified framework we use for water and structural damage work, because the documentation and drying protocols overlap significantly.
