Oceano sits at the edge of the Pacific, where marine air rolls in off the dunes and settles into homes with a persistence that inland properties rarely see. That constant coastal humidity doesn't just encourage mold — it traps odors deep into porous materials like drywall, insulation, and upholstery, making DIY sprays and candles a temporary fix at best. Whether you're dealing with smoke residue after a kitchen fire, a pet odor that's soaked into subfloor padding, or the musty aftermath of a slow plumbing leak, professional-grade deodorization is the only way to fully neutralize what's embedded in the structure of your home.

## Why Oceano Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same marine layer that makes Oceano's coastline beautiful creates a humidity baseline that rarely drops low enough for odors to naturally off-gas. Homes here — particularly those within a few blocks of the dunes — cycle between damp and dry conditions with the tides and the season, which means odor-causing compounds get absorbed into building materials repeatedly over time. Older wood-framed homes common to this part of San Luis Obispo County tend to have less vapor-resistant wall assemblies than newer construction, so smoke, pet waste, and mold byproducts penetrate deeper and faster. Salt air also degrades some surface sealants, leaving materials more porous and receptive to odor molecules than they would be in a drier inland climate.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Oceano

Effective odor elimination isn't a single step — it's a sequence calibrated to the source, the affected materials, and the environment. When we arrive at a property in Oceano, we start with a thorough assessment to identify the odor source and the extent of penetration. From there, the process typically moves through these stages:

**Source removal and cleaning** — Any contaminated material that can be cleaned is treated with enzyme-based or oxidizing agents before deodorization begins. Skipping this step and jumping straight to ozone or hydroxyl treatment is a common mistake that leads to odor rebound.

**Thermal fogging** — For smoke odors especially, thermal fogging delivers a deodorizing agent in a fine mist that follows the same pathways smoke traveled through wall cavities, ductwork, and subfloor spaces. It's particularly effective in the older, less airtight homes common to coastal San Luis Obispo County.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** — Hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that chemically break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl units can run safely while occupants are present and are well-suited to Oceano's occupied rental properties and vacation homes.

**Ozone treatment** — For severe, deeply embedded odors in unoccupied spaces, ozone treatment is one of the most effective tools available. The property must be fully vacated during treatment and properly ventilated afterward — a step we take seriously and walk every client through before starting.

**Post-treatment verification** — We don't close out a job based on how the air smells to us in the moment. We verify results and walk through the space with you before we pack up equipment.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The right tool depends on the odor type, the material affected, and whether the space is occupied. Our IICRC-certified team carries hydroxyl generators, commercial-grade ozone equipment, thermal foggers, and air scrubbers with HEPA and activated-carbon filtration. For fire-damaged properties, we combine thermal fogging with surface cleaning using dry chemical sponges and odor-encapsulating sealants on framing before any reconstruction begins. For biological odors — sewage backup, decomposition, pet waste — enzyme treatments applied directly to the substrate are the first line before any atmospheric deodorization begins. No single machine handles every scenario, which is why we assess before we deploy.
