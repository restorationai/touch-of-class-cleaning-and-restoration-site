## Why Lompoc Properties See Mold Issues

The Santa Ynez Valley's coastal influence makes Lompoc's climate distinct from inland communities just 30 miles east. Morning fog regularly pushes relative humidity above 80 percent, and homes that lack adequate attic ventilation or vapor barriers trap that moisture inside the building envelope. Many homes in Lompoc were built during the mid-20th century housing expansion that accompanied the growth of Vandenberg Air Force Base, and construction methods from that era — including kraft-faced insulation, single-pane windows, and minimal crawlspace ventilation — are particularly susceptible to long-term moisture accumulation.

Crawlspace mold is especially common here. Ground moisture wicks upward through unencapsulated dirt crawlspaces, saturating floor joists and the underside of subfloor sheathing. Homeowners often don't discover it until a floor begins to feel soft or a musty smell migrates into the living space. Older plumbing in homes built before 1970 also contributes — slow pinhole leaks inside wall cavities can go undetected for months, giving mold a continuous moisture source long before visible staining appears.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lompoc

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to locate hidden moisture pockets behind drywall, under flooring, and inside ceiling cavities — areas that look dry on the surface but are actively feeding mold growth beneath.

Once the extent of contamination is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents mold spores from becoming airborne and migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing with active surface growth — are carefully removed, bagged, and disposed of per California Department of Public Health guidelines. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and physically scrubbed clean; mold cannot simply be sprayed away on wood framing.

Drying comes next. We deploy commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the ambient conditions in Lompoc — because coastal humidity means the drying environment itself is less forgiving than in a drier inland climate, equipment run times are often longer than industry averages. We monitor moisture readings daily until structural materials reach acceptable levels before any reconstruction begins.

## Lompoc Insurance Coordination

Mold remediation is covered under many standard homeowners policies when the growth is a direct result of a covered water loss — a burst pipe, appliance failure, or roof leak, for example. Gradual moisture intrusion from deferred maintenance is typically excluded, which is why thorough documentation at the start of a job matters. We photograph all affected areas, record moisture readings, and prepare written scope-of-work documentation that aligns with the format most Santa Barbara County insurance adjusters expect to receive.

If your carrier requires a third-party industrial hygienist report or post-remediation clearance testing — which some policies do — we can coordinate that step with an independent IH before the job is closed out, so you have a clean paper trail for your claim.
