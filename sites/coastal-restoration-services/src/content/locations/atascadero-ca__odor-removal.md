## Why Atascadero Properties Hold Odors Longer

Atascadero sits in a valley along Highway 101, and that geography matters for air quality. During fire season, smoke can settle into the valley and linger for days rather than dispersing quickly. Homes here — many of them built in the 1970s and 1980s with wood-paneled interiors, textured ceilings, and older HVAC systems — absorb particulates deeply. Popcorn ceilings, common in that era of construction, have an especially high surface area that traps smoke molecules and volatile organic compounds. Pet odors, cooking residue, and moisture-related smells from seasonal humidity also penetrate these materials in ways that over-the-counter sprays cannot reach.

Atascadero's climate adds another wrinkle: the diurnal temperature swings — warm afternoons, cool nights — cause building materials to expand and contract daily. That cycling pulls odor-bearing compounds deeper into drywall, insulation, and subflooring over time, which is why a smoke event from months ago can suddenly smell stronger when summer heat arrives.

## Our Deodorization Process in Atascadero

Every job starts with a source assessment. Odor removal fails when technicians skip this step and go straight to treatment — if the source material is still present (charred framing, saturated carpet pad, animal waste under a subfloor), no deodorizer will produce a lasting result. Once the source is addressed, we select from a range of proven methods based on the specific odor type, the materials involved, and the occupancy status of the property:

- **Thermal fogging** — a solvent-based fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, bonding with odor particles inside wall cavities, ductwork, and porous surfaces. Effective for post-fire and heavy smoke situations.
- **Hydroxyl generation** — produces hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules at the chemical level. Safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner can't fully vacate.
- **Ozone treatment** — highly effective for severe odors in unoccupied structures; ozone concentrations require the space to be cleared of people, pets, and plants for the treatment period and a safe re-entry window afterward.
- **Direct application of encapsulants and enzyme-based products** — used on hard surfaces, subflooring, and framing where odor compounds have bonded to the material itself.

For most Atascadero homes affected by wildfire smoke, we combine thermal fogging with hydroxyl generation and a duct cleaning step, since the HVAC system is often the primary re-distribution point for smoke odor long after the visible damage is cleaned up.
