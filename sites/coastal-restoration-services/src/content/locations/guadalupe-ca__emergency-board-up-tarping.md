## Why Guadalupe Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Guadalupe's housing stock reflects its history as an agricultural community — many homes along the older residential streets near downtown were built in the mid-20th century, with wood-frame construction, single-pane windows, and rooflines that were never designed to withstand the kind of wind-driven rain that can accompany a late-winter Pacific storm. When a fire scorches a wall or a vehicle impact shatters a window, those older assemblies are more vulnerable than modern construction: gaps in sheathing, aging fascia boards, and deteriorated flashing all create pathways for moisture intrusion that can begin within hours.

The region also sits in a wind corridor funneled between the Santa Maria River lowlands and the coastal dunes to the west. Roof tarps that would hold fine in a calmer inland climate can lift or shift here if they aren't properly weighted, strapped, and lapped — a detail that matters enormously when the next gust arrives before permanent repairs can be scheduled.

## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Guadalupe

When we arrive at a Guadalupe property, the first priority is a rapid exterior assessment — identifying every breach point, not just the obvious ones. Fire damage, for example, often compromises roof decking in areas that aren't visible from the street, and a board-up crew that only covers the broken front window can miss a six-foot section of open decking above the garage.

For window and door board-up, we use minimum ½-inch OSB cut to fit, secured with structural screws rather than nails, so panels can be removed cleanly by a contractor without additional damage to the surrounding frame. For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps — minimum 6-mil, typically 10-mil on steeper pitches — anchored with 2×4 battens screwed through the tarp into the roof deck, then lapped past the ridge or eave edge so wind can't get underneath and peel the cover back. On Guadalupe's older wood-shake or composition roofs, we probe for soft decking before we walk the surface to avoid secondary damage.

After securing the structure, we photograph every panel and tarp placement for your insurance documentation, noting dimensions, materials used, and the condition of surrounding surfaces at the time of installation.
