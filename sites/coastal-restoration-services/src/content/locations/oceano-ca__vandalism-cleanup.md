Spray paint soaks deeper into porous stucco and weathered wood siding than it would in an inland climate. ## Why Oceano Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Oceano's building stock skews older and closer to the ocean than most neighboring communities. Many residential and commercial properties along the Pier Avenue corridor and near the Oceano Dunes State Vehicular Recreation Area use exterior stucco finishes that were applied decades ago — finishes that have been slowly etched by salt-laden fog cycles. When spray paint lands on that surface, the aerosol binders bond into the micro-pores opened by years of weathering. Standard pressure washing alone strips the paint but leaves ghost shadows and can drive moisture into wall cavities that were never designed to handle it.

The dune-adjacent lots also deal with wind-driven sand abrasion, which strips protective sealants over time. A property that looks solid from the street may have exterior coatings that are already compromised before the vandal ever shows up. That context shapes how we approach every job — we assess the substrate condition before we choose a removal method, not after.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Oceano

When we arrive on-site, the first step is documentation — photographs of every affected surface from multiple angles, measurements of the impacted area, and notes on substrate type. That record matters both for your insurance claim and for selecting the right removal chemistry.

Graffiti removal in a coastal environment requires solvent systems that lift paint without driving moisture into the wall assembly. On painted wood siding, we typically use a gel-based remover that dwells on the surface rather than penetrating the substrate. On bare or lightly sealed masonry and stucco, we use a combination of chemical release agents and low-pressure hot water rinsing — not the high-pressure blasting that can pit soft coastal stucco.

Broken glass cleanup follows a strict containment protocol: visible shards are collected and bagged first, then the area is swept and vacuumed with a HEPA-filtered unit to capture fine fragments that embed in soil, gravel, or carpet. If the vandalism involved forced entry, we assess the door or window frame for structural integrity before boarding or temporary glazing goes in.

Final steps include surface sealing on any areas where the removal process disturbed the existing coating, and a written scope summary you can hand directly to your insurance adjuster.

## Oceano Insurance & HOA Coordination

Vandalism is a covered peril under most standard homeowners and commercial property policies, but the claim process moves faster when the documentation is thorough from the start. We photograph affected areas before any cleaning begins, itemize materials and labor in a format most major carriers recognize, and can communicate directly with your adjuster to answer scope questions.

If your property falls under an HOA or a property management company — common in some of the planned residential sections closer to the dunes — there may be exterior finish standards that govern what paint colors or sealant types can be used during restoration. We flag those situations early and work within the approved palette rather than creating a second problem after the first one is cleaned up.

## Local Note

One thing that catches property owners off guard in Oceano: the marine layer that rolls in off the Pacific most mornings keeps relative humidity elevated well into midday, even in summer. That matters for vandalism cleanup because many anti-graffiti sealants and exterior primers have minimum application humidity thresholds — apply them in a 90% RH marine layer and they cure slowly, trap moisture, or fail to bond properly. We schedule exterior sealing and coating work for the afternoon window after the fog burns off, which is a small adjustment that makes a real difference in how long the repair holds up against the next salt-air season.
