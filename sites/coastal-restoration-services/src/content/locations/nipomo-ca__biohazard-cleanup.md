Nipomo's housing stock is a mix of older ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and 1970s and newer construction in master-planned communities. Porous surfaces and aged caulking can harbor contaminants well below what's visible, which means surface cleaning alone is never sufficient. A thorough biohazard remediation job here requires controlled removal of affected porous materials and treatment of the substrate underneath.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Nipomo

Every job begins with a thorough assessment before any materials are touched.
