## Why Los Alamos Properties See Mold Issues

The Santa Ynez Valley's geography works against homeowners in a specific way: fog and marine moisture don't just sit outside. They infiltrate through crawl spaces, attic vents, and the gaps common in homes built before modern vapor barriers were standard. Many properties in Los Alamos were constructed in the mid-20th century, when building codes didn't require the moisture management details that newer construction includes. Crawl spaces in these homes often have inadequate ventilation and bare-earth floors, which allows ground moisture to migrate upward into floor joists and subfloor sheathing — exactly where mold establishes itself before you ever see it from inside the house.

Wine country also means irrigation. Properties near the agricultural edges of town can experience elevated soil moisture levels that affect slab foundations and perimeter walls. A slow seep along a foundation wall after winter rains, left unaddressed, can support active mold growth within 24 to 48 hours under the humidity conditions typical of this area.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Los Alamos

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just of the visible staining, but of adjacent materials, wall cavities, and the HVAC system, which can distribute spores through an entire structure if remediation isn't sequenced correctly. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of the affected area before any material is disturbed.

Containment comes next. Poly barriers and negative air pressure isolate the work zone so spores don't migrate to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing that has exceeded safe moisture thresholds — are removed and bagged following established abatement protocols. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions.

Drying is calibrated to the conditions. In Los Alamos, where ambient humidity can stay elevated through a marine-layer morning, we size our commercial dehumidifiers to the actual moisture load rather than relying on a standard formula. Readings are logged and checked until the structure reaches target moisture levels. We don't close a job until the numbers confirm the environment is no longer hospitable to regrowth.
