## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Templeton

Every vandalism job starts with a full damage assessment before any cleaning begins. We photograph all affected surfaces, document material types, and identify any secondary hazards — broken glass, forced-entry damage to doors or frames, or structural compromise from impact. This documentation serves both the restoration process and your insurance claim.

For graffiti removal, the method depends entirely on the substrate. Painted stucco and brick require chemical dwell treatments calibrated to the paint type and surface porosity, followed by low-pressure rinsing to avoid surface erosion. Smooth surfaces like metal roll-up doors or vinyl fencing respond better to solvent-based removers and light mechanical agitation. We test in an inconspicuous area first — a step that matters especially on older painted surfaces where aggressive treatment could strip original finish coatings.

Broken glass cleanup involves more than sweeping visible shards. We use fine-particle vacuums and systematic grid sweeps of surrounding areas, because glass fragments from a single broken pane can scatter 15 to 20 feet across hard flooring or gravel. If a door or window frame was compromised, we assess whether temporary boarding is needed to secure the property before permanent repair or replacement is scheduled.
