## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in El Paso de Robles

The first thing the crew does on arrival is locate the water source and confirm it is controlled, whether that means a shutoff was already turned or needs to happen before extraction begins. From there, the process follows a documented sequence calibrated to local conditions.

**Moisture mapping:** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters read the actual saturation levels behind walls and under flooring, not just the visible wet area. In Paso Robles homes with stucco exteriors and plaster-over-lath interior walls, moisture migrates laterally through wall assemblies in ways that drywall does not, so the affected zone is almost always larger than the surface stain suggests.

**Extraction:** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water and water embedded in carpet, pad, and subfloor. Speed here directly limits how far saturation spreads into adjacent materials.

**Structural drying:** Commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned to dry the structure to the IICRC S500 standard. Drying logs are recorded daily so there is a documented record for your insurance carrier.

**Monitoring and clearance:** Moisture readings are taken at each visit until all affected assemblies reach acceptable levels. Nothing is closed up until the numbers confirm it.
