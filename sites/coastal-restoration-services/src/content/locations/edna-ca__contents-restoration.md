## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Edna

The process starts with a detailed inventory — every item photographed, cataloged, and assessed before anything leaves your property. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was packed out and where it's going.

From there, items are transported to a climate-controlled facility where cleaning begins. The method depends on the material and the type of damage:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, ceramics, metal fixtures, and collectibles — a process that uses high-frequency sound waves in a cleaning solution to pull contaminants out of crevices without abrasion.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods and furniture with embedded smoke or mold odors.
- **Dry cleaning and wet cleaning** for textiles, depending on the fiber content and the type of soiling.
- **Document and photo drying** for paper items that have absorbed moisture.

Once items are cleaned and dried, they move into secure, climate-controlled storage — not a warehouse with fluctuating temperatures, but a monitored environment that won't introduce new humidity damage while your home is being repaired. Items are returned on a scheduled basis as rooms are completed, so you're not living around boxes.
