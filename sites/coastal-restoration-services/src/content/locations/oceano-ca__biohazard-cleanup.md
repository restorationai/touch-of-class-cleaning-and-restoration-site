## Why Oceano Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

The housing stock in Oceano skews older, with a significant share of single-family homes and mobile home park units built before modern ventilation and subflooring standards. That matters in biohazard situations because porous materials — older particleboard subflooring, unsealed concrete slabs, and fiber-backed vinyl — can absorb biological material more deeply than they appear to on the surface. A visual inspection alone won't tell the full story. Thorough remediation here means testing beneath surface layers, not just treating what's visible.

The coastal climate compounds this. Oceano's persistent marine layer keeps indoor humidity elevated for much of the year, which accelerates the growth of secondary biological hazards in affected areas. A situation that might stabilize slowly in a drier inland climate can escalate faster here. Prompt, professional response isn't just about comfort — in this environment, delays have measurable consequences for the structure and for the people who will return to the space.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Oceano

Every job begins with a contained assessment — we establish a perimeter, limit cross-contamination risk, and evaluate the full scope before any materials are disturbed.
