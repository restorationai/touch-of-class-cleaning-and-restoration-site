## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process

A final clean after renovation or new construction moves through distinct phases, and skipping any one of them leaves visible evidence. The sequence matters:

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work begins, bulk debris — broken tile, scrap lumber, excess caulk beads, packaging materials — is removed and disposed of properly. In Cambria, this includes coordinating with local waste haulers for construction debris, since standard residential bins don't accept mixed construction material.

**Surface-by-surface detail cleaning.** Windows get scraped of paint overspray and construction adhesive, then cleaned with appropriate glass-safe solutions. Tile and stone surfaces are treated for grout haze using pH-appropriate cleaners that won't etch the finish. Cabinetry interiors are wiped down before hardware is installed or protected. HVAC vents and returns are vacuumed and wiped — drywall dust in ductwork will circulate through a finished home for months if left behind.

**Final inspection pass.** Every surface is reviewed under raking light to catch the fine dust film that's invisible under flat overhead lighting but shows immediately once the homeowner moves in and opens the blinds. This step is where specialty cleaning diverges from a standard post-construction sweep — it's the difference between a home that looks clean and one that actually is.
