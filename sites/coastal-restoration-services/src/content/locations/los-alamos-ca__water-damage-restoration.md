## Why Los Alamos Properties See Water Damage Issues

The housing stock in and around Los Alamos skews older — many homes date to the mid-20th century or earlier, built during the town's ranching and oil-field era. Older galvanized supply lines are common, and they corrode from the inside out, often failing without warning. Crawl spaces are standard construction here, and in a valley that receives seasonal rainfall off the Pacific, those crawl spaces collect groundwater and condensation year-round. When a supply line lets go under a pier-and-beam floor, water spreads laterally through the crawl space before it ever shows up as a stain on the ceiling below — by then, the structural framing may already be wet.

The region also sits in a seismically active corridor. Minor ground movement over decades can shift drain lines just enough to create slow, hidden leaks at joints beneath slabs or inside walls. These slow leaks are often discovered only after a musty odor becomes impossible to ignore.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Los Alamos

Every job starts with a moisture survey — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map exactly where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. That matters in older Los Alamos construction because water follows the path of least resistance through original-era insulation, redwood framing, and plaster-over-lath wall assemblies.

Once the affected boundaries are confirmed, standing water is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create a directed airflow through wall cavities and subfloor spaces. Drying logs are recorded daily — moisture readings at each monitoring point — so there's a documented record of the drying progression. That documentation matters both for insurance adjusters and for confirming that materials have reached acceptable moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

If the loss involves a crawl space, vapor barrier assessment and replacement is included in the scope. A saturated crawl space that gets sealed back up without proper drying becomes a mold incubator within weeks.

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Los Alamos water damage jobs: the town's original homes were often built with redwood framing, which was abundant and affordable in mid-century California.
