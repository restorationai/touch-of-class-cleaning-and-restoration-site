## Why Los Alamos Properties Are Vulnerable to Storm Damage

The Santa Ynez Valley's topography concentrates storm energy in ways that flat coastal communities don't experience. Rainfall that spreads across a wide plain instead rushes down canyon drainages and collects around foundations. Los Alamos's building stock skews older — many homes and commercial buildings along Bell Street and the surrounding blocks were constructed in the mid-20th century, when drainage standards were less stringent and roof assemblies used materials that have now aged past their design life. Wood shake roofs, single-pane windows, and pier-and-beam foundations are common, and each becomes a liability when a strong atmospheric river event arrives.

Beyond the structures themselves, the mature tree canopy throughout town is both a visual asset and a storm hazard. A single large eucalyptus limb can punch through a roof deck, compromise rafters, and expose interior framing to hours of rainfall before a crew arrives. The longer that opening stays unprotected, the deeper moisture penetrates — and in a structure with older insulation and wood framing, mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of sustained wetting.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Los Alamos

The first priority on any storm call is stopping ongoing intrusion. That means emergency tarping of breached roofs, boarding compromised windows and doors, and — where flooding has entered the structure — deploying extraction equipment before standing water migrates under flooring or into wall cavities. We document everything photographically as we work, which matters enormously when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

Once the structure is stabilized, the assessment phase begins. We use moisture mapping to trace how far water has traveled beyond the visible damage zone. In older Los Alamos homes with plaster-over-lath wall construction, water wicks laterally through the lath before showing on the surface — the wet zone is almost always larger than it looks. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on that map, not just pointed at the obvious wet spot. Structural drying typically runs three to five days with daily moisture readings to confirm progress.

Tree damage cleanup — limb removal from rooflines, debris clearing from drainage paths — is coordinated alongside the structural work so the site is safe for restoration crews and accessible for any contractors handling roofing or carpentry repairs.
