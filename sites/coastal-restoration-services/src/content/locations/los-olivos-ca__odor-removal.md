## Why Los Olivos Properties Hold Odors Longer

The Santa Ynez Valley's climate is drier than the coast but still experiences significant temperature swings between day and night, especially in late fall and winter. Those swings cause building materials to expand and contract, which opens microscopic pathways in wood framing, plaster, and older insulation — pathways that trap odor molecules from smoke, pet dander, or sewage backups. Many homes and commercial properties in Los Olivos were built in the mid-twentieth century or earlier, featuring original hardwood floors, lath-and-plaster interior walls, and crawl spaces with minimal vapor barriers. These materials are far more porous than modern construction, meaning odors penetrate deeper and require more than a surface spray to eliminate. Wine country properties also face a specific challenge: the combination of organic material (grapes, must, barrel residue) and warm ambient temperatures can accelerate microbial growth that produces persistent musty odors, even in spaces that appear dry.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Los Olivos

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying the odor source — not guessing at it. When a crew arrives at a Los Olivos property, the first step is a structured walkthrough to locate primary contamination zones: char residue behind walls after a fireplace fire, subfloor saturation beneath a pet-soiled area, or HVAC ductwork carrying smoke particulate through the entire structure.

From there, the process moves through a sequence calibrated to the specific odor type and the building materials involved:

- **Thermal fogging** — a heated solvent-based fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules bonded to wood framing, insulation, and ceiling cavities. Particularly effective in older plaster-walled structures common to the area.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — UV-generated hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing compounds at a molecular level. Safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, which matters when a property owner needs to maintain partial access during treatment.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone applied in unoccupied spaces for deep penetration into porous materials. Requires full evacuation and a post-treatment airing period, but produces thorough results in heavily smoke-damaged rooms.
- **HVAC deodorization** — duct fogging and filter replacement to prevent the system from redistributing odors after surface treatment is complete.

The method selected — or the combination used — depends on the odor source, the materials present, and the timeline the property owner is working with.

## Local Note: What Working in Wine Country Properties Teaches You

Properties in and around Los Olivos often have detached structures — guest cottages, barrel rooms, equipment storage buildings — that share ventilation pathways or are connected by covered breezeways. A smoke or mold odor that appears confined to the main residence can migrate into these secondary structures through shared crawl spaces or open eave vents, and owners sometimes discover the problem only after the main building has been treated.
