## Why Morro Bay Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same oceanfront proximity that draws people to Morro Bay works against odor control in a few measurable ways. Relative humidity along the waterfront frequently sits above 80 percent, even on clear days. High ambient moisture slows the evaporation of odor-causing volatile organic compounds, meaning smoke, sewage, or biological odors that would off-gas and dissipate in a drier inland climate instead get trapped in porous building materials.

Morro Bay's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of the town's residential properties — particularly those closer to the Embarcadero and the older streets climbing toward Black Hill — were built in the 1950s through 1970s. Homes of that era commonly used plaster-and-lath wall systems, original hardwood subfloors, and minimal vapor barriers. Those materials absorb odor compounds deeply and release them slowly, which is why a homeowner might repaint and recarpet after a fire and still smell smoke six months later. Addressing the source requires getting treatment agents into wall cavities and subfloor voids, not just treating the air.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Morro Bay

Effective deodorization is a sequence, not a single treatment. The process starts with a thorough source assessment — locating and removing or encapsulating whatever material is generating the odor. Applying ozone or hydroxyl treatment before that step is done is like painting over rust: it delays the problem rather than solving it.

Once the source is addressed, the appropriate technology is matched to the structure and the odor type:

- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules at a chemical level. Because hydroxyl generators are safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, they're well-suited to Morro Bay's vacation rentals and short-term rental properties that need to turn over quickly.
- **Thermal fogging** forces a deodorizing agent in vapor form into the same porous pathways that smoke or soot traveled during a fire. It's particularly effective in older plaster-wall construction where odor has penetrated beyond the surface.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces where high-concentration ozone can be safely contained. It's aggressive and fast-acting, making it a strong option for severe smoke odor removal or post-sewage situations — but it requires proper aeration before re-occupancy.

All work is documented with before-and-after readings using calibrated air sampling equipment, giving you a record for insurance purposes.
