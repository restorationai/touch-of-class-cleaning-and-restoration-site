## Why Vandenberg AFB Properties Experience Water Damage Differently

The housing stock at and around Vandenberg AFB spans several construction eras, and that range matters when water gets into a structure. Older residential units — some dating to mid-century construction — often have galvanized supply lines that have been corroding quietly for decades. A small pinhole leak inside a wall cavity can saturate insulation and framing for weeks before a stain appears on drywall. Newer construction on the installation uses modern PEX plumbing, but slab-on-grade foundations common throughout the area mean that even a minor slab leak can wick moisture laterally across a wide floor area before it becomes visible.

The coastal climate adds another layer of complexity. Relative humidity along this stretch of the Central Coast regularly sits above 70 percent, which slows evaporative drying significantly. Equipment that would dry out a flooded room in three days in an inland desert climate can take five or six days here if the crew doesn't account for ambient moisture conditions. Ignoring that reality leads to incomplete drying reports and callbacks.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process at Vandenberg AFB

Every job starts with thermal imaging and moisture mapping — not just a visual inspection. Infrared cameras reveal wet pockets behind walls and under flooring that look dry to the eye. Once the full scope is documented, standing water is removed using truck-mounted extraction units capable of pulling hundreds of gallons per hour from carpet, pad, and subfloor assemblies.

After water removal, the structural drying phase begins. We deploy commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers positioned to create a drying vortex across wet surfaces. Moisture readings are logged at every visit — typically daily — so the drying curve is documented and defensible for insurance purposes. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place (saturated insulation, buckled hardwood, compromised subfloor panels) are removed and properly disposed of before reconstruction begins.
