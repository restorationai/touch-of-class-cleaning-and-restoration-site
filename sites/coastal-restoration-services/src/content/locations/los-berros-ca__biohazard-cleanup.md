## Why Los Berros Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

The rural character of the Los Berros area means many properties here are older agricultural or ranch-style structures — buildings with crawl spaces, unfinished outbuildings, detached garages, and porous concrete or wood-plank flooring that can absorb biological material far more readily than tile or sealed slab. When a biohazard event occurs in one of these spaces, the contamination doesn't stay on the surface. It migrates into substrate materials quickly, especially in the warmer months when San Luis Obispo County's inland temperatures push into the 90s and accelerate biological activity.

The region's semi-arid climate also means properties sometimes sit unoccupied for extended periods — a vacation parcel, a rental between tenants, a rural outbuilding that isn't checked weekly. By the time a biohazard situation is discovered in a space like that, the remediation scope is often broader than it would be in a frequently occupied urban home. We assess the full extent of affected materials before any work begins, so there are no surprises once the job is underway.
