## Why Santa Maria Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Santa Maria's building stock spans a wide range of eras and materials, and that mix matters when you're cleaning up vandalism. Older commercial buildings along the historic downtown corridor often have unpainted or lightly sealed concrete masonry unit (CMU) walls — highly porous surfaces that absorb aerosol paint deeply and resist standard pressure washing. Newer stucco-clad residential construction in the city's western and southern growth areas uses a three-coat finish that can be color-matched and patched, but only if the repair happens before UV exposure begins to fade the surrounding surface. Santa Maria also has a significant number of agricultural support facilities — packing sheds, equipment yards, and warehousing — where large metal roll-up doors and corrugated siding are frequent graffiti targets and require solvent-based removal methods that differ entirely from residential work.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Santa Maria

Every job begins with a surface-by-surface assessment before any cleaning agent touches the property. We identify the substrate — stucco, CMU block, painted wood siding, glass, metal, or composite — and test a small inconspicuous area to confirm the removal method won't cause secondary damage like etching or discoloration. For graffiti, we work through a tiered approach: chemical solvent application, dwell time calibrated to the paint type and substrate porosity, mechanical agitation where appropriate, and low-pressure rinse. We do not default to high-pressure washing on stucco or older painted surfaces because the water intrusion risk is real, particularly on properties where the exterior envelope hasn't been recently resealed.

Broken glass cleanup follows a strict containment protocol — interior glass fragments are mapped, vacuumed with HEPA-filtered equipment, and the affected area is inspected with raking light to catch secondary shard scatter. Boarding or temporary glazing is coordinated before we leave the site so the property is secured. Where structural elements have been damaged — door frames forced, locks defeated, interior surfaces defaced — we document everything photographically for your insurance claim before any material is removed or altered.
