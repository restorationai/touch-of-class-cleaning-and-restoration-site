## Why Lompoc Properties Face Distinct Vandalism Cleanup Challenges

Lompoc's building stock leans heavily on stucco exteriors and painted concrete block — materials that look straightforward but are actually among the trickiest surfaces for graffiti removal. Aerosol paint, permanent marker, and etching compounds penetrate stucco's porous surface in ways that a pressure washer alone will never fully address. Aggressive washing without the right chemical neutralizers can drive pigment deeper into the substrate and raise the pH of the surface, which accelerates future paint adhesion problems. Concrete block walls — common on commercial properties along the city's central corridors — present a similar challenge: the alkaline surface chemistry means certain solvent-based removers can leave a ghost stain even after the color appears gone.

Lompoc's climate adds another layer. The marine influence keeps relative humidity elevated through much of the morning, and vandalism events that happen overnight — broken windows, forced entries, spray paint — often sit in damp conditions for six to eight hours before they're discovered. That moisture window matters. Broken glass left on interior flooring can wick condensation into subfloor materials. Spray paint applied to a damp stucco surface bonds differently than it would in a dry inland environment, requiring adjusted dwell times for chemical removers.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Lompoc

Every vandalism cleanup job in Lompoc starts with a documented site assessment — photographs, surface identification, and a scope of work that your insurance adjuster can use directly. That documentation step is not optional; it protects you if a secondary claim surfaces weeks later.

For graffiti removal, the process is surface-specific. On stucco, we apply a penetrating graffiti remover calibrated to the porosity of the material, allow the appropriate dwell time based on ambient humidity that morning, and then use low-pressure rinsing rather than high-pressure blasting to avoid substrate damage. On painted wood or metal surfaces — common on older commercial facades — we test a small area first to confirm the existing finish won't lift with the paint. Where full removal isn't achievable without damaging the substrate, we match and apply a color-consistent anti-graffiti coating that makes future incidents far easier to clean.

For broken glass cleanup, we go beyond visible shards. Fine glass particulate settles into carpet fibers, HVAC return vents, and the gaps between floorboards. We use HEPA-filter vacuums and systematic surface wipe-downs to confirm clearance — not just a visual sweep.

Boarding and temporary security measures are coordinated the same day when needed, so the property is not left exposed overnight while restoration work continues.
