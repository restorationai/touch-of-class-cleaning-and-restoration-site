## Why Callender Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Rural and semi-rural properties in Tehama County face a specific combination of risk factors that urban areas don't. Long stretches of State Route 36 and the ranch roads branching off it mean that outbuildings, storage structures, and even main residences can go unmonitored for stretches of time — particularly on larger parcels. That window of opportunity is what vandals exploit most.

The building stock in this part of California also tends toward older wood-frame construction and metal agricultural buildings, both of which respond poorly to spray paint and impact damage. Porous wood siding absorbs aerosol paint quickly, and once it soaks past the surface layer, simple pressure washing won't recover it. Metal panel siding on barns and equipment sheds develops rust at any point where a rock or tool has scratched through the coating — damage that looks cosmetic at first but becomes a structural problem within a season or two if left untreated.

Winter rains in the Sacramento Valley foothills accelerate everything. A broken window left unboarded for even 48 hours during a rain event can allow enough moisture intrusion to begin warping interior framing and flooring.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Callender

Every vandalism job starts with a documented walk-through. We photograph all affected surfaces before touching anything — this record matters for insurance claims and, in cases involving law enforcement, for evidence documentation. We do not begin cleanup until the property owner has confirmed that any required police report has been filed.

For graffiti removal, the method depends entirely on the substrate. Painted concrete block takes a different approach than stained cedar siding or a powder-coated metal gate. We use a combination of chemical degreasers, low-pressure hot water extraction, and targeted sanding or wire brushing depending on what the surface can tolerate. On porous materials where full removal isn't achievable without damaging the substrate, we match and repaint rather than chase a perfect chemical strip.

Broken glass cleanup goes beyond sweeping visible shards. We treat the entire radius around a broken window or door as a contamination zone — glass fragments travel farther than most people expect, and small pieces embedded in flooring or soil create liability long after the obvious damage is gone. Interior glass events also get a fine-particle pass with a HEPA vacuum before we clear the area.

Boarding and temporary weatherproofing happen the same visit when needed, so the structure isn't left exposed while longer restoration work is scheduled.
