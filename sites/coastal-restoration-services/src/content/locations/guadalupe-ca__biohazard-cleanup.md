## Why Guadalupe Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Guadalupe's housing stock reflects its history as a working agricultural town. Many residences are older structures — some dating back several decades — with layouts and ventilation systems that weren't designed with rapid remediation in mind. Porous materials like older drywall, unfinished wood subflooring, and original tile grout can absorb biological contaminants more deeply than modern construction materials, which means surface cleaning alone is never sufficient. Proper remediation here requires systematic removal of affected porous materials, not just topical treatment.

The region's coastal climate also plays a role. Guadalupe sits close to the Pacific, and the persistent marine layer keeps humidity elevated for much of the year. Elevated ambient moisture accelerates the breakdown of biological materials and increases the risk of secondary contamination if a scene is not addressed promptly. That environmental reality shapes how we approach containment, drying, and final clearance on every job in this area.
