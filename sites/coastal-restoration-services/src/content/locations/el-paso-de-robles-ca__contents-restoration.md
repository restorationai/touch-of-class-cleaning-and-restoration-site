## Our Contents Pack-Out and Cleaning Process in El Paso de Robles

Every contents job begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves the property. Each piece is photographed, tagged, and logged — a record that serves both the restoration process and any insurance documentation your adjuster will need. Items are then packed using appropriate materials for their category: electronics are wrapped and padded separately from textiles, and fragile or high-value pieces are documented with condition notes before transport.

Cleaning methods are matched to the damage type. Smoke and soot residue from a kitchen fire requires dry-sponge techniques on delicate surfaces before any wet cleaning is attempted — skipping that step grinds carbon particles deeper into fabric fibers. Water-damaged items go through controlled drying before any cleaning begins, because cleaning wet materials often sets stains permanently. Furniture restoration after fire or water damage may involve wood refinishing, fabric cleaning or reupholstering referrals, and odor neutralization using hydroxyl or ozone treatment depending on the material.

Once cleaned, items are held in our secure, climate-controlled storage facility until your home or commercial property in El Paso de Robles is ready to receive them. Return delivery is coordinated with your restoration timeline, and the same inventory log used at pack-out is used to verify every item is returned.
