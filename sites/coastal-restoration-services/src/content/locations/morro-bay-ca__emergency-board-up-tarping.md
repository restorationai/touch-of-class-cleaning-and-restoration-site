## Why Morro Bay Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Risks

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Morro Bay

When a call comes in from the Morro Bay area, the first step is a rapid damage assessment to identify every breach in the building envelope: broken windows, kicked-in doors, fire-damaged walls, missing or lifted roofing sections. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up — that documentation becomes part of your insurance claim file.

For window and door openings, we use minimum ⅝-inch plywood cut to fit and secured with structural screws into the framing, not just the window casing. On older homes with stucco exteriors common throughout the area, we take care not to crack or spall the surrounding finish during installation. Roof tarping follows a similar principle: heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for UV and moisture resistance are run over the ridge where possible, then secured with 2×4 cap boards screwed through the tarp into the decking — a method that holds through coastal wind gusts far better than sandbags alone.

Every opening is logged with dimensions and materials used so that the remediation or reconstruction crew coming in behind us knows exactly what they are working with.

Board-up and tarping is almost universally covered under the "protection of property" provision of a standard homeowners policy — it is considered a mitigation expense, not a repair, which means it typically does not count against your deductible the same way structural repairs do.
