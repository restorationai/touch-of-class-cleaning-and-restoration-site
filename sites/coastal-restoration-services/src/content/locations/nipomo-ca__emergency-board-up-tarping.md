## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Nipomo

Every job starts with a rapid visual assessment of the full structure, not just the obvious breach. A broken window from a break-in on the street side of a home often means a second compromised opening around back that goes unnoticed until rain finds it. We photograph every affected area before touching anything — that documentation matters for your insurance claim.

For window and door openings, we use minimum ¾-inch OSB secured with structural screws into the framing, not just the trim. On fire-damaged openings where the surrounding framing is compromised, we sister in temporary support before mounting the board. For roof tarping, we use 6-mil or heavier poly tarping, secured with wood battens screwed through the tarp into the decking at the ridge and eaves — not bungee cords or sandbags, which fail in the kind of 30-plus mph wind gusts that move through the Nipomo Mesa corridor during late fall and winter.

We also coordinate with your insurance adjuster from the start. We can provide itemized material and labor documentation in the format most major carriers request, which speeds up the claim process on your end.
