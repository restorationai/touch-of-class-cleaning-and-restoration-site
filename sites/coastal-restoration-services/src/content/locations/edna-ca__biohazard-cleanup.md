Edna sits in a rural-agricultural stretch of San Luis Obispo County, and the local building stock reflects that history. Porous concrete floors, unfinished wood framing, and older HVAC configurations common in this part of the county can allow biological contamination to migrate into surfaces that look unaffected from the outside.

Identifying the true extent of contamination in these older structures takes more than a visual inspection, which is why thorough testing and containment are built into every job we handle in this area.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Edna

Every call begins with a discreet arrival — unmarked vehicles when requested, no unnecessary foot traffic, and a direct conversation with the property owner or authorized contact rather than a crew showing up unannounced. Remediation follows a structured sequence: removal of contaminated materials in sealed, regulated containers; treatment of affected surfaces with hospital-grade disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological hazard present; and verification testing to confirm the space has been returned to a safe condition.
