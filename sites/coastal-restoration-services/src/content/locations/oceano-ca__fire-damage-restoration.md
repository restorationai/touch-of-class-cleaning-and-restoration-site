## Why Oceano Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

Oceano's housing stock reflects decades of coastal California development — a mix of post-WWII bungalows, mid-century wood-frame construction, and more recent builds that sit close together on compact lots. Older homes in the area frequently have original wood-lath interiors, single-pane windows, and minimal attic insulation, all of which allow smoke to migrate and settle in ways that newer construction does not. The salt-laden marine air that defines life near the Oceano Dunes also means that metal fixtures, electrical components, and structural fasteners already carry a baseline level of corrosion — fire accelerates that process significantly, and post-fire inspection needs to account for it.

The proximity to the dunes themselves adds another layer: fine silica sand infiltrates structures through gaps that smoke exploits just as readily. After a fire, that combination of soot, moisture from suppression efforts, and windblown particulate can create a stubborn residue inside wall cavities and crawl spaces that standard cleanup misses.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Oceano

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a disciplined order that prevents secondary damage from compounding the original loss.

**Stabilization and assessment** come first. We board up openings, tarp compromised roofing, and document every affected surface with photos and moisture readings before any material is moved. This documentation is also what your insurance adjuster needs.

**Smoke and soot removal** is the most labor-intensive phase. Dry soot from fast-burning fires behaves differently than wet, oily smoke residue from smoldering materials — and Oceano homes with older wood framing and plaster-and-lath walls can trap both types in layers. We use dry-chemical sponges, thermal fogging, and hydroxyl generators calibrated to the specific residue profile, not a one-size approach.

**Structural drying** follows suppression water extraction. Coastal humidity in Oceano — regularly above 80% on marine-layer mornings — slows evaporative drying considerably. We deploy commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers rather than standard refrigerant units, which lose efficiency above 60% relative humidity.

**Odor neutralization and rebuild coordination** close out the process. We work directly with your insurance carrier to scope the structural repairs and can coordinate licensed subcontractors for framing, drywall, and finish work so you have a single point of contact through the full restoration.

Oceano's older wood-frame homes — particularly those built in the 1950s and 1960s on the blocks closest to the dunes — often have original redwood framing.
