## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

Every contents restoration project begins with a detailed inventory before a single item leaves your home. Each piece is photographed, catalogued by room, and assigned a condition rating so that nothing gets lost in the process and your insurance adjuster has a complete record from day one.

From there, items are transported to our secure climate-controlled storage facility — a critical step in this coastal environment, where leaving contents in a damaged structure exposes them to ongoing humidity swings that can cause mold to colonize soft goods within 48 to 72 hours of a water event. Once in our facility, items are sorted by cleaning method:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, ceramics, and metal items with intricate detail
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for textiles, upholstered furniture, and documents with embedded smoke odor
- **Dry-cleaning and hand-washing** for clothing, drapery, and delicate fabrics
- **Electronics triage** coordinated with certified electronics restoration specialists before any cleaning attempt

When restoration is complete and your home is cleared for re-occupancy, we coordinate a scheduled pack-back, returning items room by room according to the original inventory.
