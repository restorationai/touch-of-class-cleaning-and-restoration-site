Coastal Restoration Services Inc is based in Vandenberg Village and schedules service calls across the Central Coast and surrounding communities. When you call **[(805) 345-7440](tel:8053457440)**, you'll speak with someone who can confirm coverage for your address and get you on the schedule — no automated runaround. Reach out during business hours and we'll work to get a crew moving toward your property as quickly as the calendar allows.

## Where we work

Our team operates out of Vandenberg Village, which sits in the heart of Santa Barbara County along the Central Coast. That central position lets us reach communities stretching from the Lompoc Valley in the north down through Santa Barbara and into Ventura County to the south, as well as inland areas toward the Santa Ynez Valley. The region's coastal climate — marine layer moisture, seasonal rain events, and the occasional Santa Ana wind that carries embers — creates the exact conditions that drive water intrusion, mold growth, and fire damage calls. We know the housing stock here, from mid-century ranch homes in Lompoc to older craftsman bungalows closer to downtown Santa Barbara.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Vandenberg Village — including Lompoc, Santa Maria, and Orcutt — are our most frequently served communities, and crews can typically reach them well ahead of those farther out. From there, we regularly work in Santa Barbara, Goleta, Carpinteria, and Ventura. The Santa Ynez Valley communities of Solvang, Buellton, and Los Olivos fall within our standard coverage zone as well.

For properties at the outer edges of our range — think Oxnard, Camarillo, or points north toward San Luis Obispo County — arrival order depends on current crew location, traffic on the 101, and job load. Mountain passes and canyon roads can add meaningful time; we'll always give you an honest estimate when you call rather than a number we can't stand behind.
