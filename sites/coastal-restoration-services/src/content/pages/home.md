When water soaks through your subfloor, smoke odor settles into your drywall, or mold starts spreading behind a cabinet, you need someone who knows exactly what to do next. Coastal Restoration Services Inc handles residential and commercial property damage in Vandenberg Village and the surrounding Central Coast — from the first call through the final repair.

## Services we handle

**Water Damage Restoration** — A burst pipe or appliance leak can saturate structural materials faster than it looks from the surface. We locate hidden moisture, extract standing water, and dry everything down to prevent secondary damage like warping and mold growth.

**Fire & Smoke Damage Restoration** — Even after the flames are out, smoke residue keeps working — etching surfaces, corroding metal, and embedding odor into insulation and framing. We remove damaged material, clean affected surfaces, and address odor at the source rather than masking it.

**Mold Remediation** — Mold can begin colonizing damp materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. We identify the affected area, contain it to prevent cross-contamination, remove compromised material safely, and treat the underlying moisture problem so it doesn't return.

**Storm & Flood Damage** — The Central Coast sees its share of atmospheric rivers and wind-driven rain. When water intrudes through a roof breach, failed window seal, or overwhelmed drainage, we respond quickly to limit how far the damage spreads.
