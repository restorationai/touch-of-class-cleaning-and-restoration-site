From a burst pipe flooding a Lompoc rental unit to smoke damage working its way into the walls of a Santa Maria office building, property damage rarely announces itself at a convenient time. Coastal Restoration Services Inc handles the full range of residential, commercial, and multi-family restoration work along the Central Coast — water, fire, mold, biohazard, storm damage, and the reconstruction that follows. One company, one point of contact, from the moment the damage happens to the day you hand back the keys.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving through a structure, every hour matters — wet framing, saturated insulation, and soaked drywall can begin supporting mold growth within 24 to 48 hours. Water damage response focuses on stopping the source, extracting standing water, and drying the building cavity down to safe moisture levels before secondary damage sets in. Fire and smoke damage calls for a different kind of urgency: smoke residue is acidic and keeps corroding surfaces long after the flames are out, so content cleaning, structural deodorization, and soot removal begin as soon as the site is cleared. Biohazard cleanup — sewage intrusion, trauma scenes, hoarding situations — demands both technical containment and discretion. Storm damage, whether from the kind of atmospheric-river rain events that periodically saturate Santa Barbara County or wind-driven debris, often combines several of these problems at once. Each of these services is covered in detail on its own page.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction restores the property. Once affected materials have been properly removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins — replacing drywall, framing, flooring, cabinetry, roofing sections, and finishes to pre-loss condition or better. Coastal Restoration Services Inc handles this work directly rather than handing it off to a separate contractor, which means the crew that documented what came out knows exactly what needs to go back in. That continuity matters for insurance documentation, for timeline, and for the quality of the finished result. General contracting services are also available for renovation projects outside of an insurance claim context.

## Specialty services

Some situations don't fit neatly into an emergency category but still need professional attention. Mold inspection and remediation addresses hidden growth behind walls or under flooring — common in older coastal-area housing stock where moisture intrusion has gone undetected for a season or more. Persistent odor removal (pet, smoke, musty water damage) uses hydroxyl or thermal fogging treatment rather than masking agents. Board-up and tarping services secure a structure after a break-in, fire, or storm event before full restoration can begin.
