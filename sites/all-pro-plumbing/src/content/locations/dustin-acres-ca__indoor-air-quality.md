## Why Dustin Acres Properties See Air Quality Issues

The Central Valley's geography works against indoor air in ways that aren't obvious until you start measuring. Valley fever spores, fine agricultural particulates, and seasonal smoke from wildfire events to the north all find their way into ductwork through return-air intakes. Homes in the Dustin Acres area tend to run their air conditioning heavily from May through October, which means the same recirculated air passes through the system hundreds of times before a window gets cracked. Standard one-inch fiberglass filters, which is what most builder-grade systems ship with, capture large debris but let fine particles and biological contaminants pass right through.

Older homes in Kern County built before the energy-efficiency retrofits of the 1990s often have duct systems that were never sealed properly, pulling unconditioned attic air, insulation fibers, and whatever has accumulated in the crawl space directly into living areas. Newer construction is tighter, which is good for energy bills but means less natural dilution of indoor pollutants.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Dustin Acres

Every engagement starts with an assessment rather than a product recommendation. A technician inspects the existing filtration setup, checks static pressure across the air handler, and identifies whether the system can support an upgraded filter or whether the blower motor needs to be evaluated first. Pushing a MERV-13 filter into a system sized for MERV-8 can restrict airflow enough to cause coil freeze-up, which is a real concern during a Dustin Acres summer.

From there, the path forward depends on what the home actually needs:

- **HVAC filtration upgrades** replace builder-grade filters with media filtration rated to capture fine particulates, pet dander, and pollen without starving the system of airflow.
- **UV air purifiers for HVAC** mount inside the air handler and use ultraviolet light to neutralize biological contaminants on the evaporator coil, where moisture and darkness create ideal conditions for mold and bacteria growth.
- **Whole-house air purifiers** treat air actively as it circulates, targeting VOCs and ultrafine particles that even high-MERV filters miss.
- **Whole-home humidifier installation** addresses the flip side: while summers here are dry and hot, forced-air heating in winter can drop indoor humidity low enough to irritate airways and crack wood trim. A bypass or fan-powered humidifier tied into the HVAC system maintains a consistent level year-round.
- **Indoor air quality testing** gives you a baseline reading of particulate levels, VOC concentrations, and humidity so any improvements can be measured rather than assumed.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Air Quality

The Central Valley environment puts specific demands on equipment. UV lamp systems need to be sized for actual air handler dimensions, not just rated CFM, because coil geometry varies significantly between manufacturers. Whole-house humidifiers in this climate need bypass dampers that close completely in summer so the unit does not inadvertently add moisture during the cooling season. Media filter housings installed in Dustin Acres homes are selected to accommodate the higher dust loads common in agricultural areas, with larger surface area to extend service intervals.

For homes where duct leakage is contributing to air quality problems, duct sealing with mastic or aerosol-based sealant can be combined with filtration upgrades so the system is pulling air from the right places before the filtration investment goes in.
