## Why Weedpatch Properties See Ductwork Problems

The San Joaquin Valley's agricultural climate creates duct stress that homeowners in coastal cities rarely encounter. Daytime temperatures in Weedpatch can swing 40 degrees or more between dawn and mid-afternoon in summer, and that thermal expansion and contraction works on every duct connection in your attic. Flex duct, which is common in the tract and ranch-style homes built throughout this part of Kern County, develops small tears at the inner liner when it is repeatedly compressed or kinked, often where it bends around framing or rests on a joist.

Dust is the other factor. Valley wind events carry fine soil particles that work into return-side duct gaps, coating coil surfaces and shortening equipment life. Homes with older duct systems, particularly those installed before the current California Title 24 energy standards tightened duct-leakage requirements, are especially vulnerable. A system losing 20 to 30 percent of conditioned air through leaky ducts is not unusual in this region, and the financial cost shows up every month on a PG&E bill.

## Our Ductwork Repair and Installation Process in Weedpatch

Every job starts with a visual inspection and, where conditions call for it, a leaky duct testing procedure using a duct blower to measure how much air the system is losing and pinpoint where. That data shapes the repair scope rather than guessing.

For sealing work, we use mastic sealant on rigid duct connections and UL 181-rated tape on flex duct terminations. Aerosol-based duct sealing is available for systems where access is limited, injecting sealant particles that bond to leak points from the inside without requiring every section to be opened up. For replacement or new installation, we size the duct system to match the equipment and the layout of the home, which matters in Weedpatch where many homes have long duct runs serving rooms at the far end of a single-story floor plan. Undersized or poorly routed ducts make even a correctly sized air conditioner struggle on a 108-degree afternoon.

After repairs or installation, we retest to confirm the system meets California's duct-leakage performance targets before we close out the job.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Ductwork

Attic work in Weedpatch during summer requires equipment and scheduling awareness that indoor jobs do not.
