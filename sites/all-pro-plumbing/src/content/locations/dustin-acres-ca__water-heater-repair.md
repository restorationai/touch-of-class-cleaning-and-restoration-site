## Why Dustin Acres Properties See Water Heater Problems

The Bakersfield metro area, including Dustin Acres, draws water from sources with notably high hardness levels. Calcium and magnesium deposits settle at the bottom of tank-style water heaters over time, forming a thick layer of scale that forces the burner to work harder, drives up your gas or electric bill, and eventually causes the tank lining to crack. In homes built during the valley's postwar expansion, many of which use older galvanized supply lines, the problem compounds: mineral deposits from the pipes themselves add to what's accumulating inside the tank. If your hot water has started arriving with a slightly sulfurous or metallic smell, sediment buildup is often the first place to look.

The extreme summer heat also affects expansion and contraction cycles on the pressure relief valve and the tank itself. Units that might last 12 or more years in a milder climate often show stress signs earlier here, particularly if they're installed in a garage or outdoor utility space without shade, where ambient temperatures can exceed 120°F on a July afternoon.
