## Why Tarina Properties See Burst and Leaking Pipe Issues

Kern County's clay-heavy soils shift with the seasons. Wet winters expand the ground; dry summers contract it. That movement puts lateral stress on underground water lines and the connections where they enter a home's foundation. Homes built before the 1980s, which represent a meaningful share of the housing stock in this part of the Central Valley, frequently still have galvanized steel supply lines. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing over decades until a pressure spike or a cold snap causes a full rupture. Even copper lines in older construction can develop pinhole leaks at solder joints that were made with materials or techniques no longer in use.

Above-ground lines are equally vulnerable. Pipes that run through uninsulated garage walls, under mobile homes, or along exterior stud bays have little protection when a cold front drops temperatures into the low 30s. In the Central Valley, those nights are brief but real, and homeowners who have never experienced a frozen pipe often don't insulate until after the first one bursts.

## Our Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair Process in Tarina

The first step on any burst or leaking pipe call is locating the source. Surface water is almost never where the break is. We use pressure testing and, when the leak is suspected inside a wall or under a slab, acoustic detection equipment to pinpoint the failure without unnecessary demolition. In Kern County homes with stucco exteriors and concrete slab foundations, that precision matters, because opening the wrong section of wall or cutting the wrong section of slab adds cost and time.

Once the break is confirmed, we isolate the affected section, cut out the damaged pipe, and replace it with material appropriate to the line's function and location. For slab penetrations, we assess whether a reroute above the slab is more practical than a direct repair, particularly in older homes where the slab-embedded pipe may have additional corrosion beyond the visible failure point. Every repair is pressure-tested before we close any wall or trench. We also document the scope and affected materials thoroughly, which matters if you are filing a homeowner's insurance claim.

## Local Note

In parts of Kern County where agricultural irrigation infrastructure runs near residential parcels, the water table and soil moisture levels can vary significantly by season and by how recently nearby fields were irrigated. That moisture variation affects how quickly soil settles after a trench repair and can influence whether an underground line repair holds its grade over the first few months.
