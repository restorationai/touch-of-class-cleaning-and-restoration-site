## Why Valley Acres Properties See Burst and Leaking Pipe Issues

The San Joaquin Valley's clay soils are the hidden variable most homeowners never think about until something breaks. As the soil dries out in summer and swells again after winter rains, buried service lines shift laterally. Galvanized steel pipes common in homes built through the 1970s are especially vulnerable: they corrode from the inside out over decades, and the combination of internal rust and external soil movement can split a line with very little warning. Copper supply lines fare better against corrosion but can develop pinhole leaks at solder joints when the pipe flexes repeatedly over years of seasonal soil movement. In slab-on-grade construction, which is the dominant foundation type across this part of Kern County, a slab leak can run silently for weeks before a homeowner notices warm spots on the floor or an unexplained spike on a water bill.

## Our Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair Process in Valley Acres

The first priority when we arrive is locating the water shut-off and stopping active flow.
