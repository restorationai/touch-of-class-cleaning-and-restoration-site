## Why Tarina Properties See Sewer Line Problems

The underlying geology of the southern San Joaquin Valley is the starting point for almost every sewer failure in this area. Tarina sits on alluvial clay and silt deposits that swell when the water table rises after winter rain and shrink again through the dry summer months. That seasonal movement is relentless, and it works on buried pipe the way a slow vise works on a joint: a little pressure each cycle, year after year, until a clay-tile or older ABS section cracks or separates entirely.

Homes built during the postwar growth of Kern County often used Orangeburg pipe, a compressed tar-paper product that was standard through the 1970s. Orangeburg softens, deforms into an oval cross-section, and eventually collapses under soil load. If your home was built before 1980 and has never had a sewer camera inspection, there is a real chance the lower lateral still has original pipe. Tree roots, particularly from the large shade trees common in older Tarina neighborhoods, find every hairline crack and can fill a six-inch line within a season.

## Our Sewer Line Repair and Replacement Process in Tarina

Every job starts with a sewer camera inspection. A high-resolution camera travels the full length of the lateral from the cleanout to the city connection, and we record the footage so you can see exactly what we see: root intrusion, offset joints, belly sections holding standing water, or a full collapse. That footage also becomes documentation for your insurance claim if the loss qualifies.

Once we know what we are dealing with, we choose the right method:

- **Trenchless sewer repair (pipe lining or pipe bursting):** When the pipe retains enough structural integrity to serve as a host, we can install a cured-in-place liner that bonds to the interior and creates a smooth, root-resistant surface without digging up your yard or driveway. Pipe bursting is the trenchless option when the existing pipe needs to be replaced rather than rehabilitated: a bursting head fractures the old pipe outward while simultaneously pulling new HDPE pipe into position.
- **Open-cut replacement:** Collapsed sewer line sections, severe belly runs, or pipe that has shifted too far out of grade require excavation. We expose the failed section, remove it, and install properly graded pipe bedded in clean sand so it drains correctly from day one.

All work is coordinated with the local building department for permit and inspection requirements before backfill.
