## Why Tarina Properties See Emergency Plumbing Problems

Kern County's climate creates a specific freeze-thaw cycle that is easy to underestimate. During a cold snap, water inside uninsulated pipes in crawl spaces, garages, and exterior walls can freeze, expand, and split the pipe wall. The failure often doesn't show up until temperatures rise and the ice thaws, releasing water into wall cavities or subfloor framing. Homes built in the 1960s and 1970s, a common era in this part of the county, frequently have galvanized steel supply lines that have already narrowed from decades of mineral buildup. A freeze event in a galvanized system doesn't just crack the pipe; it can shear a joint that was already corroded thin.

Soil conditions in the region also matter. Expansive clay soils shift with moisture changes, and that ground movement stresses underground supply lines and sewer laterals over time. A slow lateral crack can become an active backup or a sinkhole in a yard with very little warning.
