## Why Dustin Acres Homes See Garbage Disposal Problems Early

Kern County's water supply is notoriously high in calcium and magnesium. Over time, those minerals build up inside the grinding chamber, on the impeller blades, and along the drain line connecting your disposal to the P-trap. The result is a unit that works harder, runs hotter, and wears out sooner. A disposal that hums but won't turn is often a seized grinding plate, sometimes caused by mineral scale locking the rotor rather than a foreign object. A unit that leaks at the bottom collar usually signals a cracked inner shell, accelerated by the thermal cycling that comes with Dustin Acres summers, where kitchen temperatures climb well above what most appliances are rated to handle continuously.

Older homes in the Dustin Acres area also tend to have narrower drain lines and shallower under-sink cabinets than newer construction, which limits which disposal models fit without modification to the drain configuration. Getting the right unit into the right space matters as much as the repair itself.

## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process

Every service call starts with a diagnostic, not a sales pitch. A technician will run the unit through its full range: power draw, rotation, leak points at the flange, the discharge elbow, and the dishwasher knockout if one is connected. If a jam is the issue, we clear it and test the reset button and motor before recommending anything further. Many disposals that appear dead just need the overload protector reset and the jam cleared with a hex wrench at the base.

When replacement makes more sense than repair, we size the new unit to your household's actual usage and your existing drain configuration. Installation includes:

- Removing and disposing of the old unit
- Inspecting the sink flange and mounting assembly for corrosion before setting the new unit
- Verifying the electrical connection and ground (disposals in California must be on a dedicated circuit or a properly rated shared circuit)
- Running a full leak check at every joint before closing out the call

We carry a range of continuous-feed models suited to the typical under-sink clearances found in Dustin Acres kitchens, so there is rarely a need for a return visit to swap out a unit that does not fit.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Kern County kitchens: homeowners replace a disposal themselves, use the old wiring connection without checking the gauge, and end up with a unit that trips the breaker under load. California's electrical code requires garbage disposal circuits to be properly rated for the motor size, and a mismatch is not always obvious until the new unit is running under a full load of vegetable scraps. If your recently installed disposal keeps tripping a breaker, the fix is often in the wiring, not the unit itself. It is worth having a licensed plumber confirm the full installation before assuming the disposal is defective.
