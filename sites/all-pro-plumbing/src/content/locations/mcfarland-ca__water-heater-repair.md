## Why McFarland Properties See Water Heater Problems

The water delivered to homes throughout McFarland and the surrounding Kern County agricultural corridor carries a high mineral load. Over time, calcium and magnesium carbonate settle to the bottom of a storage tank, forming a thick layer of sediment that forces the burner to work harder, raises energy costs, and produces the rumbling or popping sounds that many homeowners first notice during quiet evenings. Left unaddressed, that sediment layer can cause the tank lining to overheat and crack, turning a straightforward repair into a full replacement.

McFarland's housing stock includes a substantial number of homes built during the agricultural expansion decades of the mid-twentieth century. Many of those properties still have original gas lines sized for older appliances, and some have water heater installations tucked into utility closets or garages that were not designed with current clearance requirements in mind. A repair visit often reveals that the unit was never properly strapped for seismic compliance, which California code requires, or that the temperature and pressure relief valve has not been tested in years.

## Our Water Heater Repair Process in McFarland

When a call comes in from McFarland, the technician assigned to the job arrives with a fully stocked service vehicle so that common parts, including thermocouples, heating elements, anode rods, and T&P valves, do not require a second trip. The process follows a consistent sequence:

1. **Diagnosis**, The technician checks the pilot assembly, gas valve, thermostat, and burner on gas units, or the heating elements and thermostat on electric units, using calibrated meters rather than guesswork.
2. **Sediment assessment**, A flush test determines whether sediment has reached a level that affects efficiency or threatens the tank lining.

4. **Safety verification**, The T&P relief valve, seismic strapping, and venting are checked before the unit is returned to service.
5. **Operational test**, Hot water is confirmed at multiple fixtures before the technician leaves the property.
