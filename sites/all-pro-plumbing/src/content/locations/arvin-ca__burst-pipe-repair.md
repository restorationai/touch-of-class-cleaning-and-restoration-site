## Why Arvin Properties See Burst and Leaking Pipe Issues

The clay-heavy soil beneath much of Kern County shifts as it absorbs and releases moisture through the seasons. During dry stretches, that soil contracts and can pull slightly away from buried supply lines, creating micro-movement that fatigues pipe joints over time. During the wet season, the same soil swells. Older homes in Arvin, many built during the agricultural expansion of the mid-twentieth century, often have galvanized steel supply lines that have been working through this cycle for decades. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing the bore and weakening the walls until a pressure spike, a hard freeze, or simply time causes a failure.

Arvin also sits at an elevation where winter nights can drop well below freezing, particularly in January and February. Pipes running through uninsulated exterior walls or crawl spaces, common in the smaller ranch-style homes built for farmworker families in earlier decades, are genuinely vulnerable to freezing. A frozen pipe does not always burst immediately; the failure often happens as the ice thaws and pressure equalizes, which means the leak can appear hours after the cold snap passes.

## Our Burst and Leaking Pipe Repair Process in Arvin

When you call, the first priority is locating the source before more water moves into the structure. We use acoustic leak detection and thermal imaging to find breaks inside walls or beneath slabs without opening up every surface. In Arvin homes with stucco exteriors and interior plaster or drywall, this matters: unnecessary demolition adds cost and time to the repair.

Once the leak is isolated, we shut off the affected supply line, extract any standing water, and assess the pipe material and condition. If the failed section is galvanized, we typically recommend replacing the run with copper or PEX rather than patching, because a pinhole in old galvanized pipe usually signals that the surrounding length is equally compromised. After the repair, we pressure-test the line before closing any wall or floor access, and we document the work for your insurance claim.
