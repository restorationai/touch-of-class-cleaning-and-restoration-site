## Why Valley Acres Properties See Ductwork Problems

The Central Valley's climate is one part of the story. The other is the housing stock. A significant share of homes in the Valley Acres area were built during the post-war and mid-century construction booms, when flex duct was installed loosely and duct board connections were sealed with cloth tape that has since dried out, cracked, and separated. Attic temperatures in this region can exceed 150°F on a July afternoon, and that heat accelerates the breakdown of older duct materials faster than it would in a coastal climate.

Soil conditions also matter. The San Joaquin Valley's expansive clay soils shift seasonally as they absorb winter rain and dry out through the summer. In slab-on-grade homes, that movement can stress any ductwork running through or near the foundation, loosening joints and creating gaps that are invisible from the floor but measurable with a duct pressure test.

Finally, the region's agricultural dust and particulate load is genuinely higher than most California metros. Leaky return ducts pull that dust into the air handler and distribute it through the home, shortening equipment life and degrading indoor air quality.

## Our Ductwork Repair and Installation Process in Valley Acres

Every job starts with a leaky duct test before any repair work begins. Using a duct blower door, the system is pressurized and measured against design specs so we know exactly how much conditioned air is escaping and where the losses are concentrated. That data drives the repair scope rather than guesswork.

For duct sealing, we use mastic sealant on rigid connections and UL 181-rated tape on flex duct joints. Cloth tape is not used. Where duct board has delaminated or flex duct has kinked, collapsed, or been crushed by foot traffic in the attic, those sections are replaced rather than patched. Attic work in Valley Acres summers is done with proper heat precautions, and we schedule attic-intensive jobs for early morning start times when possible.

For full ductwork installation on new systems or gut-renovated homes, we size ducts to Manual D calculations matched to the load calculations for the specific home, not to whatever the previous system used. Undersized or oversized ducts are a common source of comfort complaints even when the equipment itself is correctly sized.

After repairs or installation, a post-test confirms the system is performing within acceptable leakage thresholds before the job is closed out.
