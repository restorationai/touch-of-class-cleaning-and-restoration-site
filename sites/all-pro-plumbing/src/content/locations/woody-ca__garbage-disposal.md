## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Woody

Every service call starts with a hands-on assessment rather than a phone diagnosis. A technician will test the reset button, check for a jammed flywheel using the hex wrench port, inspect the power supply and wiring connection under the cabinet, and run water through the unit to locate any leak at the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, or the drain outlet.

For repairs, the goal is always to identify the root cause rather than just restore function temporarily. A disposal that hums but won't turn has a different fix than one that leaks from the bottom, which is a different problem than one that trips the circuit repeatedly. Matching the solution to the actual failure keeps the unit running longer.

For replacements, the technician will measure the existing mounting assembly, confirm drain line compatibility, and recommend a unit sized appropriately for the household's usage and the drain configuration already in place. Installation includes securing the mounting ring, connecting the drain and optional dishwasher discharge line, testing for leaks at all connection points, and confirming the unit clears the reset cycle before the job is closed out.
