## Why Keene Properties See Emergency Plumbing Problems

The elevation at Keene hovers above 2,700 feet, and winter nights regularly push temperatures below freezing. That freeze-thaw cycle is the single biggest driver of burst pipes in this area. Homes along the Tehachapi corridor that were built in the mid-twentieth century often still carry their original galvanized steel supply lines, which corrode from the inside out.
