## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Buttonwillow

When a call comes in from Buttonwillow, the process starts with a straightforward diagnosis. A slow drain and a completely blocked drain call for different approaches, and using the wrong method wastes time and money. For partial blockages caused by grease, soap, or hair accumulation, a professional cable auger clears the immediate obstruction and lets us assess what the line looks like further downstream.

For more stubborn blockages, especially those caused by mineral scale or grease that has hardened along a longer section of pipe, hydro jetting is the appropriate method. A hydro jet delivers a high-pressure water stream that scours the interior pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the clog. In Buttonwillow's hard-water environment, that scouring action removes the mineral film that would otherwise catch the next round of debris within weeks. For lines where root intrusion is suspected, a camera inspection before and after cleaning confirms whether the root mass has been fully cleared and whether the pipe wall itself has been compromised.
