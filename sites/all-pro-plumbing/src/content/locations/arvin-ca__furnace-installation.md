## Why Arvin Homes Often Need Furnace Replacement Sooner Than Expected

The agricultural economy that built Arvin also shaped its housing stock. A significant portion of the city's residential neighborhoods were developed rapidly during the mid-20th century to house farmworkers and their families, which means ductwork and furnace closets were often designed to minimum standards of the era. Older homes in this area frequently have undersized flue passages, single-wall gas connectors that no longer meet current California code, and attic duct runs that were never properly sealed against the valley's heavy summer dust.

Kern County's air quality is another factor that shortens furnace life here. Fine particulate matter from agriculture, oil fields, and highway traffic loads up filters faster than in cleaner-air regions, and when filters go unchanged, heat exchangers overheat and crack. A cracked heat exchanger is not a repair situation, it is a replacement situation, and it is the single most common reason Arvin homeowners end up calling us for a full install rather than a tune-up.

## Our Furnace Installation and Replacement Process in Arvin

Every installation starts with a load calculation sized to the actual square footage and insulation level of your home, not a rule-of-thumb guess. Arvin's climate demands equipment that can handle both heating and cooling seasons efficiently, so we factor in the local design temperatures when selecting equipment capacity.

Once the right unit is selected, the process typically runs like this:

- **Existing equipment removal**: The old furnace is disconnected from gas, flue, and electrical, then hauled off the property.
- **Flue and venting inspection**: Older homes often need flue liner upgrades to meet current California mechanical code before a new appliance can be connected.
- **Equipment installation and gas connection**: New furnace is set, leveled, and connected by a licensed technician. All Pro holds California Contractors License #960566.
- **Duct integrity check**: We pressure-test the existing duct system. Valley dust and age cause duct leakage that can cut efficiency by 20 to 30 percent even on a brand-new furnace.
- **Commissioning and carbon monoxide verification**: The system is started, combustion is verified, and CO levels are checked at the register before we leave.

High-efficiency condensing furnaces (90-plus AFUE) require a secondary drain line for condensate, something older Arvin homes were never plumbed for. We handle that rough-in as part of the installation so there are no surprises after the permit is pulled.

## Permits and California Code Considerations in Arvin

Arvin is incorporated within Kern County's building jurisdiction for most purposes, and furnace replacements here require a mechanical permit. Some homeowners are surprised to learn that a permit is mandatory even for a straight like-for-like swap, but California's Title 24 energy standards have changed enough over the past two decades that a new furnace often triggers duct sealing requirements that did not apply to the old one.

We pull the permit, schedule the inspection, and coordinate directly with the inspector so you are not managing paperwork on top of being without heat. The permit is also what protects your homeowner's insurance coverage if a claim ever involves the heating system.
