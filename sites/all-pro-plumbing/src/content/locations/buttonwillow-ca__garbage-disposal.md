## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Buttonwillow

Every call starts with a diagnosis, not a parts order. A disposal that hums but won't spin usually has a jammed flywheel, which is often cleared without replacing the unit at all. When replacement is the right call, we size the motor to match actual household use.
