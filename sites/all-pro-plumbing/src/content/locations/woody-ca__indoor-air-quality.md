## Why Woody Properties See Air Quality Challenges

The San Joaquin Valley already carries one of the highest concentrations of ground-level ozone and agricultural dust in California. In Woody specifically, the elevation and proximity to working farmland mean that even on calm days, particulates settle into and around homes. Older single-story homes common to this part of Kern County were often built with minimal duct sealing and standard fiberglass filters rated for particle capture rather than allergen or VOC reduction. Those filters protect your blower motor; they do very little for the air your family breathes.

Dry winters compound the problem. When indoor humidity drops below 30 percent, mucous membranes dry out, making occupants more sensitive to airborne irritants. Dust mites and certain mold spores actually thrive at opposite ends of the humidity spectrum, so finding and holding the right indoor humidity range matters year-round in this climate.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Woody

Every engagement starts with an assessment of what your current system is actually doing. We look at your existing filter type and MERV rating, check for duct leakage that could be pulling unconditioned air from attic or crawl spaces, and evaluate whether your system's airflow can support a denser filtration media without starving the blower.

From there, the path forward depends on what the home needs:

- **HVAC filtration upgrades**: Swapping a standard filter rack for a media cabinet that holds a high-MERV or HEPA-style filter gives your existing system meaningful allergen and fine-particle capture without a full equipment replacement.
- **UV air purifier for HVAC**: A germicidal UV lamp installed in the air handler targets bacteria and mold spores at the coil, where moisture creates a hospitable surface. This is particularly relevant in homes where the evaporator coil runs long cooling cycles through the valley's extended summers.
- **Whole house air purifier**: Standalone or integrated electronic air cleaners handle particles that slip through mechanical filters, including the fine agricultural dust common to this area.
- **Whole home humidifier installation**: A bypass or fan-powered humidifier tied to your furnace maintains target humidity automatically, without the maintenance burden of portable units.

After installation, we verify airflow and system static pressure to confirm the new equipment isn't stressing your HVAC, then walk you through filter replacement schedules and any maintenance the new components require.
