That kind of sustained load exposes every weakness in a duct system, from pinhole leaks at flex-duct collars to fully collapsed sections in attic runs that have been baking in 140°F attic heat for years. ## Why Dustin Acres Properties See Duct Problems

The Central Valley's climate is the primary driver. Ductwork in this region cycles through extreme heat and cold more aggressively than in coastal California, and that thermal stress causes mastic sealant to crack, foil tape to peel, and flex duct to sag and kink between joists. Many homes in the Dustin Acres area were built during the rapid residential expansion of the 1970s through 1990s, when duct design standards were less rigorous and fiberglass duct board was common. That material degrades over time, especially in attics where heat and occasional pest activity accelerate the breakdown.

Slab-on-grade construction, which is typical throughout this part of Kern County, means most duct systems run through attic spaces rather than crawlspaces. That keeps them accessible, but it also means they spend their entire service life in one of the hottest environments in the building. A duct system that might last 25 years in a milder climate can show meaningful deterioration in 15 years here.

## Our Ductwork Repair and Installation Process

Every job starts with a duct leakage test before any repairs begin. We pressurize the system and measure how much air is escaping, which gives us a baseline and tells us where to focus. Thermal imaging can reveal cold spots along duct runs that point to insulation failures or gaps that a visual inspection would miss in a dark attic.

For duct sealing, we use mastic sealant on rigid connections and UL 181-rated tape on flex duct terminations. Foil tape alone fails in high-heat attic environments, so we do not rely on it as a primary seal. For air duct repair on damaged sections, we assess whether a repair is structurally sound or whether duct replacement is the better long-term answer. Patching a duct that has lost its internal liner or has a crushed inner core is a short-term fix that will need revisiting.

For full ductwork installation, we size the system to the actual load of the home using Manual D calculations rather than simply matching the old duct diameter. Undersized or oversized ducts are a common source of uneven temperatures and high static pressure, both of which shorten equipment life.

## Local Note

Attics in this part of Kern County get hot enough in summer that standard duct insulation rated at R-6 can fall short of current Title 24 energy code requirements, which call for R-8 on supply ducts in climate zones like this one. If your home's duct system predates the most recent California energy code updates, a full duct replacement is an opportunity to bring the insulation level up to current standards, which reduces heat gain into the supply air and meaningfully improves system efficiency. It is worth asking about during any leaky duct testing visit.

A typical duct replacement in a single-story Dustin Acres home takes one to two days depending on system size and attic access.
