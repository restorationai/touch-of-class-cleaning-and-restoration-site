## Why Woody Properties See Ductwork Problems

The San Joaquin Valley's extreme temperature swings, from scorching summer afternoons to cold winter nights, cause duct materials to expand and contract repeatedly over the years. Flex duct connections loosen, mastic sealant cracks, and older metal duct sections develop gaps at the joints. In Woody specifically, the agricultural dust that hangs in the air during harvest season works its way into unsealed duct runs, coating coils and reducing airflow. Homes built in the mid-20th century, which make up a significant share of the local housing stock in this part of Kern County, often have original ductwork that was sized for equipment with different airflow requirements than today's high-efficiency systems. That mismatch shows up as uneven temperatures, short cycling, and equipment that runs longer than it should.

Crawl spaces and attics in the Woody area also tend to reach extreme temperatures in summer, sometimes exceeding 150°F in an unventilated attic. Duct insulation that has thinned or separated in those spaces loses its ability to keep conditioned air cold before it reaches the registers, so the air handler works overtime to compensate.

## Our Ductwork Repair and Installation Process in Woody

Every job starts with a thorough inspection of the existing system before any repair or replacement work begins. We use leaky duct testing to measure how much conditioned air is escaping before it reaches your living space, which gives us a baseline and lets us show you exactly where the losses are happening rather than guessing.

From there, the scope depends on what we find:

- **Duct sealing**: For systems where the layout is sound but connections have opened up, we apply professional-grade mastic or foil-backed tape at every joint and boot connection. Aerosol-based sealing is also available for hard-to-reach runs.
- **Air duct repair**: Crushed, kinked, or torn flex duct sections get replaced rather than patched, because a patch in a high-heat attic rarely holds through a second summer.
- **Duct replacement**: When the system is undersized, improperly routed, or simply at the end of its service life, we design and install a new duct layout matched to your current equipment and the actual load of your home.
- **Ductwork installation for new systems**: If you are adding a room, converting from a wall-unit setup, or installing a new HVAC system, we size and route the ductwork from the start rather than adapting whatever was there before.

All work is coordinated with the local permit process through Kern County where required, so the installation is documented and your equipment warranty stays intact.
