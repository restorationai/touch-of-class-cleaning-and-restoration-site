## Why Tarina Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

The San Joaquin Valley's clay-dominant soil does two things that consistently trouble drain lines. First, it shifts seasonally: the wet winters swell it, the dry summers shrink it, and that repeated movement puts lateral stress on underground pipes that eventually cracks joints or collapses sections. Second, tree roots follow moisture, and in a region that sees long dry stretches, those roots aggressively seek out even the smallest pipe joint leak.

Inside the home, Kern County's water hardness is among the highest in California. That mineral content deposits inside drain pipes the same way it coats a showerhead, slowly, then suddenly. A drain that ran fine last season may be running at half capacity this one, not because of a single clog but because scale has been narrowing the interior diameter for months. Kitchen sink clogs in this area frequently combine grease with mineral deposits, creating a plug that a plunger won't touch.

Older commercial buildings in the area often still have cast-iron drain stacks that have corroded from the inside out, leaving rough interior surfaces that catch every food particle and soap film that passes through. Hydro jetting is often the only method that fully restores flow in those lines without requiring immediate repiping.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Tarina

Every service call starts with a drain inspection rather than a guess. Before we run a cable or a jetter, we confirm what we're dealing with: a soft blockage like grease and soap, a hard blockage like scale or a collapsed section, or root intrusion. That distinction changes the tool and the approach.

For soft blockages in kitchen sinks, shower drains, and floor drains, a motorized cable (rooter) clears the immediate obstruction quickly. For lines with scale buildup or root intrusion, hydro jetting delivers a pressurized water stream that scours the pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the clog. Hydro jetting is particularly effective in the older cast-iron and clay tile lines common in Kern County commercial properties, where cable-only service tends to leave debris clinging to rough pipe walls.

After clearing, we flow-test the line and, where access allows, run a camera to confirm the pipe interior is clean and intact. If we find a cracked joint or a section that's migrated out of alignment, we document it so you have a clear picture of what's a maintenance issue now versus what may become a repair later.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in the valley's agricultural and light-industrial corridors is grease trap and floor drain neglect in commercial kitchens and food-processing facilities. In Kern County's climate, fats congeal faster in cooler months and stay liquid longer in summer, which changes how quickly a grease blockage forms and how far down the line it travels before setting. Facilities that schedule drain cleaning on a fixed calendar often find themselves reacting to a backup instead of preventing one. A service interval based on actual usage and seasonal temperature rather than a fixed date tends to keep those lines clear year-round.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

The right tool depends on the pipe material, diameter, and the nature of the blockage. Our service vehicles carry both electric cable machines in multiple drum sizes and commercial-grade hydro jetters capable of handling mainline work. For kitchen sink clogs and shower drain clogs, a smaller cable or hand-held jetter attachment is usually sufficient. For mainline blockages, floor drains, and commercial lines, the high-pressure jetter is the more thorough option.

Where root intrusion is confirmed, we use a root-cutting head on the cable machine to clear the line, followed by jetting to flush debris. We do not apply chemical root killers as a standard practice; mechanical removal is more reliable and avoids introducing chemicals into the sewer system.
