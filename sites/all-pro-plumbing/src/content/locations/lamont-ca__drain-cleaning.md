## Why Lamont Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

The water supply throughout Kern County carries a high mineral load. Over time, calcium and magnesium deposits narrow pipe walls the same way arterial plaque narrows a vessel, and the effect compounds in older galvanized or cast-iron lines that are common in homes built before the 1980s. Add cooking grease from busy households and the fine clay-rich soil that washes into exterior drains after irrigation or rain, and you have a recipe for recurring clogs.

Lamont's agricultural surroundings also mean that root intrusion is a real and common problem. Mature trees planted along property lines and near older sewer laterals send roots toward any available moisture source. A hairline crack in a clay tile sewer line is all the invitation a root system needs, and once roots establish inside a pipe, a simple plunger or chemical drain cleaner will not solve the problem.

Commercial properties, including food service operations, face additional pressure from grease trap overflow and high-volume sink use. Blocked floor drains and backed-up prep sink lines can halt operations quickly, which is why fast, reliable service matters as much as technical skill.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Lamont

Every service call starts with a diagnosis, not a guess. For most clogs, a cable auger or rooter machine is the right first tool: it physically breaks up or retrieves the obstruction, whether that is a grease buildup, a root mass, or a foreign object. For lines that are heavily scaled or show recurring blockages, hydro jetting is the more thorough solution.

Hydro jetting uses pressurized water, typically in the range of 3,000 to 4,000 PSI, delivered through a specialized nozzle that scours pipe walls in all directions. It removes grease films, mineral deposits, and root remnants that a cable alone leaves behind. The result is a pipe that flows at or close to its original capacity rather than one that is merely open.

For sewer lateral problems, a camera inspection confirms what is happening inside the line before any work begins. This matters especially in Lamont, where older clay tile laterals can have multiple issues at once: root intrusion at one joint, offset sections at another, and scale buildup throughout. Knowing the full picture prevents unnecessary digging and helps homeowners understand exactly what they are paying for.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

The right tool depends on what is in the pipe and how old the pipe is. Older galvanized lines in Lamont homes require a careful touch: aggressive cabling on a corroded pipe can cause more damage than the clog itself. Hydro jetting pressure is calibrated down for pipes that show wear, and camera inspection before jetting is standard practice when pipe age or condition is uncertain.

For kitchen sink clogs, the combination of a cable to open the line and a targeted hot-water flush to clear residual grease is usually sufficient. Shower and tub drains typically accumulate hair and soap scum, which a cable retrieves cleanly. Floor drains in garages and commercial kitchens often need jetting to clear compacted sediment that has settled over years.

## Local Note

In Lamont and the surrounding unincorporated parts of Kern County, many residential properties were built on septic systems rather than connected to a municipal sewer. If your home is on septic and you are experiencing slow drains throughout the house rather than in a single fixture, the issue may be at the septic tank or drain field rather than inside your plumbing. A drain cleaning service will clear your interior lines, but it will not solve a saturated drain field. Knowing which system your property uses before calling helps the technician bring the right equipment and give you an accurate assessment on the first visit.
