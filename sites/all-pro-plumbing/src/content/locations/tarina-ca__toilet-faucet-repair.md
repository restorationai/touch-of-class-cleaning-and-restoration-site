## Why Tarina Properties See Fixture Problems

The Central Valley's water hardness is measurable in the scale that builds up on aerator screens and inside supply lines. Over time, that mineral accumulation restricts flow, causes faucet handles to stiffen, and forces toilet fill valves to work harder than they were designed to. The result is a running toilet that adds meaningfully to a monthly water bill, or a shower valve that delivers inconsistent temperature because the cartridge is partially seized.

Tarina's climate adds another layer. The region's dry summers and occasional hard freezes in winter create thermal cycling in supply lines and fixture bodies, particularly in older slab-on-grade construction common throughout this part of Kern County. Brass and chrome fittings expand and contract with that temperature swing, and over years that movement loosens compression fittings and stresses valve seats in ways that aren't obvious until a slow leak becomes visible.
