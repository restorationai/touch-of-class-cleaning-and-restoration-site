## Why McFarland Properties Experience Gas Line Problems

The San Joaquin Valley's expansive clay soils are the root cause of many buried gas line failures in this part of Kern County. When the ground dries out through a long, rainless summer and then absorbs moisture again in the winter months, it moves. That movement puts lateral stress on older steel and galvanized pipe, accelerating corrosion at joints and fittings. McFarland's housing stock includes a substantial number of homes built in the 1960s through 1980s that still have original black iron gas lines, and those lines are reaching the end of a serviceable lifespan. Homes in this era were also built before earthquake-rated flexible connectors and automatic shutoff valves became standard, which matters in a region that sits within reach of seismic activity along faults to the west.

Agricultural irrigation activity throughout the area can also affect soil moisture levels unevenly, which means ground movement isn't always uniform across a single property. A gas line that reads fine at the meter can be stressed at a mid-run joint twenty feet away.

## Our Gas Line Installation and Leak Repair Process in McFarland

When a call comes in, the first priority is confirming whether gas needs to be shut off at the meter before anyone enters the structure. For active leak calls, we coordinate with SoCalGas if the meter needs to be locked out, and we arrive with combustible gas detectors to map the concentration and source before any digging or disassembly begins.

For leak repairs, the process typically moves through these stages:

- **Detection:** Electronic gas detection equipment locates the leak zone without tearing open walls or trenching blindly.
- **Isolation:** The affected section is isolated so the rest of the home's gas service can often remain active during the repair.
- **Repair or replacement:** Corroded fittings, cracked flex connectors, and failed joints are replaced with code-compliant materials. Where a section of line has degraded beyond a point repair, we replace the run.
- **Pressure test:** The repaired system is pressure-tested before gas is restored, and we confirm all appliances relight correctly.

For new gas line installations, whether you're adding a gas range, outdoor grill connection, whole-house generator, or pool heater, we pull the required Kern County permit, trench to the correct depth, and use materials rated for the soil conditions common in this part of the valley.
