## Equipment and Methods for Gas Line Work

Electronic combustible gas detectors allow the crew to trace a leak to its source without opening every wall in the house. For buried lines, a pressure decay test isolates which section of the run is holding and which is not, narrowing the dig to the smallest possible area. New installations use corrugated stainless steel tubing where code allows, which handles minor ground movement better than rigid black iron and is faster to route through tight crawl spaces and wall cavities common in older Arvin homes.

Earthquake shutoff valves are installed at the meter and require no power or manual activation. They trigger on seismic motion above a set threshold and are a straightforward addition during any gas line service call.
