## Why Dustin Acres Properties See Sewer Line Problems

The San Joaquin Valley's clay soils behave almost like a slow-moving sponge. During winter rains and spring irrigation, the ground swells. By late summer, after months of 100-plus-degree heat, it shrinks back. Sewer pipes buried in that soil, especially older clay tile or Orangeburg pipe installed in mid-century tract homes, absorb that stress at every joint. A hairline crack at a joint lets in root intrusion from nearby trees; within a season or two, that crack becomes a partial collapse. Homes built before the 1980s in this area are particularly likely to have original clay pipe that has never been replaced. Even newer PVC lines can suffer if the bedding around them was not compacted properly during installation, which is a common shortcut in fast-build residential tracts.

## Our Sewer Line Repair and Replacement Process in Dustin Acres

Every job starts with a sewer camera inspection. A waterproof camera travels the full length of the line from the cleanout to the municipal connection, recording footage and flagging cracks, root intrusion, bellied sections, and offsets. That footage drives the repair decision, not guesswork.

For lines with localized damage, trenchless sewer repair is usually the right call. Pipe lining installs a resin-saturated liner inside the existing pipe, which cures in place and creates a smooth, joint-free interior surface. Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the original outward as it goes. Both methods avoid tearing up driveways, landscaping, and concrete flatwork that a traditional open-cut excavation would destroy.

When a pipe has collapsed completely, or when the grade has shifted so badly that the line no longer drains by gravity, open excavation and full sewer line replacement is the correct path. The crew exposes the failed section, removes it, and installs schedule 40 PVC bedded and backfilled to current standards. The work is permitted through the appropriate local authority, and the trench is restored before the crew leaves.
