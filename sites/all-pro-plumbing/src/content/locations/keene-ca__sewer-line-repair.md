## Why Keene Properties See Sewer Line Problems

The geology here does most of the damage. The Tehachapi foothills experience measurable soil expansion and contraction across the year, and pipe joints that were snug at installation gradually work loose as the ground moves. Older homes in the area, many built during the railroad-era development that shaped this community, were plumbed with clay tile or Orangeburg pipe that was never designed to flex. Once a joint opens even slightly, roots follow the moisture and begin filling the interior of the pipe. Left long enough, the root mass becomes a dam, and the first sign most homeowners notice is a toilet that drains slowly or a gurgling sound from the floor drain after a washing machine cycle.

Wind-driven debris from the canyon also accelerates grease and sediment buildup in lateral lines that run uphill toward the street connection, a layout more common here than in flat valley neighborhoods. Uphill runs trap solids that would otherwise flow freely, and the problem compounds faster than homeowners expect.

## Our Sewer Line Repair and Replacement Process in Keene

Every job starts with a sewer camera inspection. A waterproof camera travels the full length of your lateral line so we can see exactly what we are dealing with, whether that is a root intrusion, a belly in the pipe where ground settlement created a low spot, or a section that has collapsed under soil pressure. We record the footage and mark the depth and location of any defects before any ground is disturbed.

From there the repair method depends on what the camera shows. Where the pipe is structurally sound but blocked or cracked, trenchless sewer repair is usually the right call. Pipe lining installs a resin-saturated liner inside the existing pipe, which cures in place and essentially creates a new pipe within the old one without requiring a full excavation trench across your yard or driveway. Where a section has collapsed or the pipe has deteriorated beyond lining, pipe bursting pulls a new HDPE line through the old one, fracturing it outward. Both methods reduce the disruption to landscaping and hardscape that matters on canyon-area properties where re-grading after an open trench can be its own project.

When a full sewer line replacement is the only sound option, we excavate with care, document the existing pipe condition for your records, and backfill and compact to minimize settling.
