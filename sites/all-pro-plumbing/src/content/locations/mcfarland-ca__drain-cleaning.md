## Why McFarland Properties See Drain Problems More Often Than You Might Expect

The San Joaquin Valley's clay-heavy soils expand when wet and contract when dry. In McFarland, where agricultural irrigation is a fact of life for the surrounding land, that cycle happens more aggressively than in coastal or foothill communities. The result is that underground drain lines, especially older cast-iron or clay-tile runs, shift and crack over time. Roots from the mature trees common in McFarland's older residential blocks follow any moisture they can find, and a hairline crack in a drain line is an open invitation.

Hard water is the other persistent factor. The groundwater in this part of Kern County carries a high mineral load. Over years, calcium and magnesium deposits build up on the inside of drain pipes the same way they coat a water heater element, narrowing the effective diameter and turning a slow drain into a full blockage faster than homeowners expect.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in McFarland

Every job starts with a conversation about what you are seeing: which fixture is backing up, how long it has been slow, and whether there is any odor or gurgling from nearby drains. That information tells us where to start.

A hydro jet pushes water at high pressure through the pipe, stripping grease, scale, and soft root intrusion from the pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the clog.
