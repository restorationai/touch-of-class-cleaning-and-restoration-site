## Why Keene's Climate Makes AC Sizing and Selection Critical

The Tehachapi Pass region experiences some of the most demanding cooling loads in Kern County. Afternoon temperatures climb fast, shade is limited on many parcels, and the dry desert air accelerates heat transfer through rooflines and west-facing walls. An undersized system will short-cycle constantly, wear out its compressor early, and still leave rooms uncomfortable by mid-afternoon. An oversized unit cools too quickly without removing enough humidity, leaving the air feeling clammy and the equipment prone to premature failure.

Proper Manual J load calculations are not optional here. The canyon elevation, sun exposure, and the mix of older ranch-style homes and newer construction in the area all affect how much tonnage a property actually needs. Getting that number right before any equipment is ordered is the single most important step in a Keene installation.

## Our AC Installation and Replacement Process in Keene

Every installation starts with a site assessment, not a sales pitch. We look at the existing ductwork condition, attic insulation levels, electrical panel capacity, and where the outdoor condenser unit will sit relative to prevailing winds and afternoon sun. In canyon terrain, airflow around the condenser matters more than it does on a flat valley lot.

From there, equipment selection focuses on high SEER2 rated systems that meet California's current efficiency standards. California adopted updated SEER2 minimums that are stricter than federal baselines, so any new central air installation in Keene must meet state-level requirements. We handle the permitting through Kern County Building and Safety, which covers unincorporated communities like Keene, and we schedule the required inspection so the installation is fully documented and code-compliant.

Once equipment arrives, the installation sequence covers refrigerant line sets, electrical disconnect and whip, condensate drainage, thermostat wiring, and a full system commissioning that includes checking static pressure, supply and return temperatures, and refrigerant charge. We do not hand off a system until it is producing the right temperatures at the registers.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for AC Installation

For most Keene properties, a split-system central air conditioner paired with a high-efficiency air handler is the right configuration. Where existing ductwork is in poor shape, we assess whether sealing and repair makes sense or whether a ductless mini-split for specific zones is a better investment. Mini-splits also make sense for additions, converted spaces, or outbuildings where running new duct would be cost-prohibitive.
