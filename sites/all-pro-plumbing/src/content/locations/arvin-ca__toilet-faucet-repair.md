## Why Arvin Properties See Fixture Problems Earlier

The water delivered to Arvin comes from a system drawing heavily on groundwater sources in Kern County, and that water carries a high mineral load. Over time, calcium carbonate builds up inside faucet cartridges, around toilet fill valves, and inside shower valve bodies until flow drops, seals fail, or handles become stiff and hard to turn. In older ranch-style homes common to this part of the valley, galvanized supply lines that were standard through the 1970s compound the problem: rust and scale flake off the pipe interior and lodge in fixture seats, causing leaks that look minor at the faucet but signal a broader supply-line issue underneath.

Heat is the other factor. Exterior hose bibs and exposed supply lines in uninsulated wall cavities experience significant thermal cycling between cool winter nights and scorching summer afternoons. Rubber washers and o-rings harden and crack under that stress, turning a slow drip into a steady leak before the season changes.
