## Restoration emergencies common in Tarina

## Services we provide in Tarina

### Plumbing repair and leak detection

Water in the San Joaquin Valley carries high mineral content, which accelerates scale buildup inside supply lines and at fixture connections. Pinhole leaks in copper, corroded galvanized fittings, and failing shutoff valves are among the most frequent calls we receive from Tarina addresses. We locate leaks with non-invasive methods before opening walls or slabs, which limits the scope of any repair.

### Drain cleaning and sewer service

Clay soils and mature landscaping mean root intrusion into older clay or ABS sewer laterals is a recurring issue in established Tarina neighborhoods. Slow drains that back up seasonally are often a sign of partial root blockage rather than a simple clog. We clear lines mechanically and can camera-inspect the lateral to show you exactly what is happening underground before recommending a repair scope.

### Water heater repair and replacement

The valley's hard water is hard on water heater tanks and anode rods. Sediment accumulation shortens tank life and reduces efficiency noticeably. Whether your unit needs a flush and anode replacement or has reached the end of its service life, we service both tank and tankless systems and can advise on sizing for Tarina's water chemistry.

### Heating and air conditioning

Kern County summers are long and severe. An air conditioner that is low on refrigerant or has a failing capacitor in July is not an inconvenience; it is a health concern. We service and repair central HVAC systems, and in winter we respond to furnace and heat pump failures when nighttime temperatures in the valley drop into the low 30s or below.

### Emergency water damage response

Slab-on-grade construction common in valley homes means water has nowhere to drain and spreads laterally under flooring quickly, so early intervention limits the damage footprint.
