## Restoration emergencies common in Wasco

Wasco sits in the southern San Joaquin Valley, where summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F and winter nights can dip below freezing. That temperature swing is hard on plumbing. Meanwhile, the agricultural economy means many Wasco properties have irrigation infrastructure, outbuildings, and older service lines that see more wear than a typical suburban lot. The Valley's dry heat also stresses HVAC equipment.
