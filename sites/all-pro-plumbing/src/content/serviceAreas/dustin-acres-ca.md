## Restoration emergencies common in Dustin Acres

Dustin Acres sits in the southern San Joaquin Valley, where the combination of intense summer heat, hard water drawn from valley aquifers, and aging residential infrastructure creates a predictable set of plumbing and mechanical problems. Temperatures regularly push past 100°F from June through September, which accelerates corrosion in water heaters and puts stress on supply lines that were never designed for that kind of sustained thermal cycling. The valley's water is notoriously mineral-heavy, and scale buildup inside water heaters, dishwashers, and supply valves shortens their service life measurably. On the drainage side, the clay-heavy soils common to this part of Kern County shift seasonally, and that movement can crack or offset older sewer laterals over time. When a lateral fails, sewage backs up into the lowest drain in the house, typically a floor drain or tub, and the problem rarely announces itself until it is already a mess.

### Drain cleaning and sewer service

### Water heater repair and replacement

Hard valley water is hard on tank water heaters. Sediment accumulates at the bottom of the tank, insulates the burner from the water, and forces the unit to run longer and hotter to hit set temperature. That cycle shortens tank life and raises gas bills. We service both tank and tankless units and can advise on whether a repair or a replacement makes more sense given the unit's age and condition.
