## What Bakersfield Plumbing Emergencies Actually Look Like

Most emergency plumbing calls in Bakersfield fall into a handful of categories. Knowing which one you're dealing with helps you describe it clearly when you call, and helps the dispatcher send the right truck.

**Burst or leaking supply lines.** A pipe that lets go suddenly, usually from pressure, corrosion, or a fitting failure, needs the main shutoff closed immediately. If you're not sure where your main shutoff is, it's typically near the meter box at the street or at the perimeter of the slab. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then call. For older galvanized homes, a single burst section often signals that more failures are coming; a plumber worth their license will assess the full line condition while they're there. See our post on [what to do in the first 10 minutes after a burst pipe](/blog/what-to-do-burst-pipe/) for the full shutoff and damage-control sequence.

**Slab leaks.** A hot spot on the floor, the sound of running water when everything is off, or a water bill that jumped $80 for no reason, these are classic slab leak signals. Bakersfield's slab-on-grade construction makes this one of the most common emergency calls in the city. Proper diagnosis uses acoustic listening equipment and sometimes a thermal camera to pinpoint the leak before any concrete is touched. [Seven Warning Signs of a Slab Leak](/blog/signs-of-a-slab-leak/) covers the full symptom list.

**Sewer backups.** When sewage backs up into a tub or floor drain, the first step is to stop using all fixtures in the house. Don't flush, don't run the dishwasher. A sewer backup can mean a blockage in the lateral (often tree roots in older Bakersfield neighborhoods) or a failed line. A camera inspection confirms which before any repair decision is made.

**Water heater failures.** A water heater that's actively leaking from the tank body, not a fitting, not the TPR valve discharge pipe, but the tank itself, is not repairable. It needs to be replaced. Bakersfield's hard water shortens tank life; scale buildup on the lower heating element is the most common failure mode in electric units, and sediment accumulation accelerates corrosion in gas tanks. If the unit is over 10 years old and leaking from the tank, replacement is the right call.

**Gas line concerns.** If you smell gas, that rotten-egg odor added to natural gas as a safety odorant, leave the building immediately without flipping any light switches or creating any sparks. Call 911 or SoCalGas's 24-hour emergency line from outside the building. Only after the utility has confirmed the scene is safe should you call a licensed plumber for the repair or pressure test. All Pro handles gas line repairs and pressure testing, but the gas utility makes the first call in an active leak situation, that's not negotiable.
