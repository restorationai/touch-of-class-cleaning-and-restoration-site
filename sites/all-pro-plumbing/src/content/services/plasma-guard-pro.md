## Why we chose PlasmaGuard PRO

PlasmaGuard, LLC is an Ann Arbor, Michigan company built on 25 years of experience in the non-thermal cold plasma sector. Their PRO unit is the residential and commercial building version of a technology line that also serves industrial and marine applications, and it targets the full range of what makes indoor air feel and smell bad: pathogens, mold spores, allergens, volatile organic compounds, and odors.

For the Central Valley that matters more than most places. Between dust, agricultural activity, wildfire smoke season, and long stretches with the windows closed and the AC running, Bakersfield homes recirculate the same air all day. Treating that air continuously, instead of hoping a filter catches everything, is a meaningful upgrade for allergy sufferers, families with young kids, and anyone sensitive to odors.
