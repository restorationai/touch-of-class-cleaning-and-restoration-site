## For urgent situations

Water damage compounds fast. Mold can begin colonizing wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours, and smoke odor bonds to surfaces the longer it sits. If you're dealing with an active loss, a burst pipe, sewage backup, fire aftermath, or anything that can't wait, call **(877) 379-2767** during business hours and we'll get you scheduled as quickly as possible. Have the address and a brief description of what you're seeing ready; it helps us dispatch the right equipment on the first trip.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If your situation is stable and you're looking for an estimate, have a question about the restoration process, or want to understand how insurance billing works before committing, email us at **amin.mashouf@gmail.com**. Include your address, a short description of the damage, and any photos you've already taken, even phone snapshots help. We'll respond with next steps and available scheduling windows. Email is also the right channel if you're a property manager coordinating on behalf of a tenant or building owner and need documentation before work begins.
