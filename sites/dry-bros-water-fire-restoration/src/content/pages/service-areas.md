Dry Bros Water & Fire Restoration serves the Chicago metropolitan area and the surrounding region. Because the brand's current hours are not listed here, the best way to confirm availability and schedule a visit is to call **call us now**, a team member can tell you exactly when a crew can be on-site for your address.

## Where we work

The team is headquartered in Chicago and dispatches across the city's neighborhoods as well as the collar counties that ring Cook County, DuPage, Lake, Will, Kane, and McHenry among them. Chicago's housing stock spans everything from century-old two-flats and greystones with aging cast-iron plumbing to postwar ranch homes with slab foundations and newer construction in the suburbs, and the work reflects that variety. Whether the loss is in a high-rise condo on the North Side, a bungalow in the Southwest neighborhoods, or a commercial strip center in the suburbs, the region is the primary service footprint.

## Coverage by distance

Cities and communities closest to Chicago, Evanston, Oak Park, Cicero, Berwyn, Skokie, and the near south suburbs, are typically the fastest to reach. Moving outward, crews regularly serve communities throughout the collar counties: Naperville, Aurora, Joliet, Elgin, Waukegan, and the towns in between.

At the edge of the coverage area, travel time is a real factor. The Chicago metro's expressway network is efficient when traffic cooperates, but the Dan Ryan, the Eisenhower, and the Tri-State can add significant time during rush hours. For properties farther out, dispatch is sequenced by crew availability and distance, the team will give you an honest arrival estimate when you call, not a number pulled from a map.

Large commercial losses or catastrophic events sometimes warrant dispatch beyond the standard footprint. If you're unsure whether your address qualifies, the phone call costs nothing.
