## Why Berwyn's Housing Stock Makes Smoke Damage Harder to Clear

The construction era matters enormously in smoke restoration. Berwyn's residential fabric is dominated by pre-war bungalows, a building type Chicago's western suburbs perfected, and these homes share a few characteristics that complicate post-fire cleanup.

First, original plaster over wood lath is far more absorbent than modern drywall. Smoke particles, particularly the wet, protein-based smoke from kitchen fires and the dry, powdery smoke from fast-burning structural fires, penetrate plaster deeply. Surface wiping alone won't reach the contamination. Second, many Berwyn homes still have older forced-air duct systems with unlined sheet-metal runs. Smoke circulates through those ducts during and after a fire, depositing a thin film of residue that re-volatilizes every time the furnace kicks on, which is often in a Cook County winter. Third, the tight lot spacing typical of Berwyn's streetscapes means adjacent properties can absorb smoke from a neighbor's fire, even when the structure itself is unaffected.

These aren't abstract concerns. They shape the scope of work, the cleaning chemistry we select, and how long the process takes.

## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Berwyn

Every smoke job starts with a structured assessment, not a visual walkthrough, but a systematic evaluation of affected materials, smoke type, and migration pathways. The type of fire (electrical, cooking, synthetic materials, wood) produces chemically different residues that require different cleaning agents and techniques.

**Containment and ventilation** come first. We establish negative air pressure in affected zones to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, especially important in Berwyn's connected bungalow floor plans where open stairwells and shared ductwork can spread residue quickly.

**Surface decontamination** follows. We use dry chemical sponges on loose soot before any wet cleaning, applying liquid to dry soot smears it deeper into plaster or wood grain. Wet-smoke residue on walls requires alkaline cleaners; protein smoke from kitchen fires needs enzymatic treatment to break down the organic film.

**Duct cleaning and sealing** addresses the HVAC system. In Berwyn's older homes, this step is often what stands between a property that smells clean and one that keeps cycling odor through the house for months.

**Thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment** handles embedded odor in soft goods, subfloor cavities, and wall voids that surface cleaning can't reach.

Finally, we document every step with photographs and moisture readings, the kind of documentation your insurance adjuster needs to process a claim without delays.
