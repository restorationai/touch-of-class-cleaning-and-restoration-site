## Why Cicero Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Cicero's housing stock tells the story. The town's residential blocks, many built between the 1910s and 1940s, were constructed with plumbing systems that were never designed to last a century. Galvanized steel pipes corrode from the inside out over decades, narrowing the interior diameter and developing weak spots that a single hard freeze can exploit. When water inside those lines turns to ice and expands, the pipe doesn't bend, it cracks.

The problem compounds in Cicero's two-flats and courtyard buildings, where supply lines often run through shared wall cavities that see no heat from either unit. An unoccupied apartment on the north-facing side of a building is a particular risk: no thermostat running, no body heat, no awareness that a pipe is freezing until water is already moving through the ceiling of the unit below. Cook County's sustained cold snaps, the kind that push wind chills well below zero for 48 hours or more, are exactly the conditions that turn a vulnerable pipe into a water damage event.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Cicero

The first priority on any burst pipe call is stopping the water. If the main shutoff is accessible, that happens immediately. If it's buried in a finished basement or behind a utility panel that requires a plumber's key, we coordinate with a licensed plumber while our crew begins containing what's already on the floor.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence:

- **Water extraction**, truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hardwood floors, carpet, and subfloor before saturation spreads laterally.
- **Moisture mapping**, thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has wicked into wall cavities, under baseboards, and into ceiling assemblies below the break. In Cicero's older plaster-and-lath construction, water travels farther and faster than in modern drywall, so this step is not skipped.
- **Controlled demolition**, saturated materials that cannot be dried in place (sections of plaster, wet insulation, swollen subfloor panels) are removed to expose the structure for drying.
- **Drying system deployment**, commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork.
- **Monitoring and documentation**, moisture readings are logged daily. Drying is not called complete until readings return to pre-loss baselines, which matters for insurance documentation.

## Insurance Coordination for Cicero Homeowners and Landlords

Burst pipe losses are among the most commonly covered water damage claims under standard homeowner and landlord policies, but coverage depends on how the loss is documented. Insurers want to see moisture readings taken at multiple points, photographs of affected materials before removal, and a clear chain of evidence showing the water source and migration path.

Dry Bros provides that documentation as a standard part of every job. We photograph affected areas systematically, log moisture data, and prepare a detailed scope of loss that your adjuster can work from directly. For Cicero's two-flat and multi-unit owners, where a single pipe break can generate claims across multiple units or require coordination between a landlord's policy and a tenant's renter's insurance, organized documentation from the start prevents disputes later.

## Local Note

In Cicero's older bungalows and two-flats, plaster walls absorb water differently than modern drywall, the surface can feel and even read relatively dry on a pin-type meter while the underlying wood lath holds significant moisture. Crews working in this housing stock use non-invasive radio-frequency meters alongside standard pin meters to catch saturation that a surface reading would miss. Drying times in plaster construction routinely run longer than in comparable drywall structures, and the drying plan is adjusted accordingly rather than calling the job complete on a standard timeline.
