## Why Skokie Properties Hold Odors Longer

Most of Skokie was built between the late 1940s and the early 1970s, a construction era defined by plaster-and-lath walls, cast iron plumbing, and single-pane windows with minimal vapor barriers. That combination creates a building envelope that absorbs odor-causing compounds, soot particles, volatile organic compounds from smoke, microbial off-gassing from mold, and releases them slowly over weeks or months. Painting over smoke residue or running a box fan through the house won't reach the odor molecules embedded in plaster, subfloor boards, or the insulation packed into balloon-framed walls.

Skokie also sits in the Chicago metropolitan climate zone, where hot, humid summers push indoor humidity levels that accelerate microbial growth after any water intrusion. A slow roof leak or a basement seepage event, common in the village's older homes with aging clay tile drainage, can produce musty odors that seem to come from everywhere and nowhere at once. Identifying the source before treating the symptom is the only approach that holds.

## Our Deodorization Process in Skokie

Effective odor elimination starts with source removal, not masking. Before any deodorization equipment is deployed, affected materials, charred framing, saturated insulation, contaminated drywall, are documented and removed where necessary. Treating a surface that still carries the odor source is a short-term fix that fails within weeks.

Once the source is addressed, the method is matched to the property:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing agent as a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, into wall cavities, under flooring, behind cabinetry. It is particularly effective in Skokie's older homes where smoke has migrated through plaster cracks and unsealed penetrations.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules at the chemical level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators can operate in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a property manager needs to limit displacement time for tenants.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces where a high concentration of oxidizing gas can reach deeply embedded odors. Proper dwell time and thorough ventilation afterward are non-negotiable steps, not optional shortcuts.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs throughout the process to capture particulates that become airborne during treatment and cleaning.

The right protocol is often a combination of methods rather than a single pass with one piece of equipment.

## Local Note

Skokie's older housing stock frequently has what contractors call "balloon framing", a construction method where wall cavities run continuously from the basement sill plate to the roof rafters without the fire stops found in platform-framed homes built after the 1950s. In a fire or smoke event, this means odor-carrying soot and gases can travel vertically through the entire wall cavity, depositing residue at every floor level. Deodorization in a balloon-framed Skokie home requires treating the cavity from multiple access points, not just wiping down the visible wall surfaces. Missing this detail is the most common reason a smoke odor returns after a less thorough remediation.
