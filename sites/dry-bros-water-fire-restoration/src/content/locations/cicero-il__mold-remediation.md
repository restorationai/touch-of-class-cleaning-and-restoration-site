## Why Cicero Properties See Mold Issues

Cook County's flat topography means water has nowhere fast to go. During heavy rain events, increasingly common in the Chicago metro, storm and sanitary sewer systems can back up, pushing water into basements that were never designed to handle hydrostatic pressure from saturated clay soil. Cicero sits on that same dense clay that underlies most of the inner-ring suburbs, and it holds moisture against foundation walls long after the rain stops.

The town's building age compounds the problem. Homes constructed before 1960 frequently used cellulose-based insulation, wood lath behind plaster, and untreated lumber framing, all of which are highly porous and nutritionally ideal for mold. Older cast-iron drain lines can develop slow, hidden leaks at joints without any visible sign at the floor level, feeding moisture into wall cavities for months. By the time a musty smell is noticeable, the colonization is usually well established behind the surface.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Cicero

Mold remediation is not the same as mold removal. Wiping visible growth off a surface without addressing the moisture source and the spore load in the air accomplishes very little, and in an older Cicero home with plaster walls and wood framing, surface cleaning can actually disturb colonies and spread spores into previously unaffected areas.

The process starts with a thorough moisture assessment. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters let us read what's happening inside wall cavities and beneath flooring without unnecessary demolition. If active moisture is present, that source has to be resolved before remediation begins, otherwise the mold returns.

Containment comes next. Poly sheeting and negative air pressure isolate the affected area so that spore-laden air doesn't migrate to clean parts of the home during the work. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously throughout the project. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, sections of wood framing, are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Final clearance involves a post-remediation air quality check to confirm spore counts have returned to acceptable levels before containment comes down.

## Cicero Insurance Coordination

Mold claims are among the more contested in residential insurance, because coverage often hinges on whether the underlying moisture event was sudden and accidental (a burst pipe, typically covered) or gradual and maintenance-related (a slow leak over months, often excluded). We document the loss thoroughly, photographs, moisture readings, scope of affected materials, and provide the kind of organized documentation that adjusters need to process a claim efficiently. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster to reduce the back-and-forth on your end.
