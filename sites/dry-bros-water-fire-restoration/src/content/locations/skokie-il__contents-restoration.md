## Why Skokie Homes Present Specific Contents Challenges

The combination of older plaster interiors and Skokie's continental climate creates a particular set of conditions for contents losses. Winters here are genuinely cold and summers are humid, that swing in relative humidity means wood furniture, hardbound books, framed artwork, and textiles can begin warping or developing secondary mold growth within 48 to 72 hours of a water event, even after the visible standing water is gone. Plaster walls and original hardwood floors also hold ambient moisture longer than modern materials, which means belongings left inside a damaged home continue absorbing humidity from the structure itself long after the initial event.

Fire losses in mid-century Skokie homes carry an additional wrinkle: older plaster and lath construction tends to trap smoke particles in wall cavities, and that off-gassing can re-contaminate contents that were not removed promptly. Upholstered furniture and clothing left in a fire-affected home absorb a second wave of odor from the structure, which is one reason a professional pack-out matters as much as the cleaning itself.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Skokie

The process begins with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your home. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating, documentation that matters both for your own records and for the insurance claim process. From there, contents are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility where they are held securely while your home is being restored.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Porous items like upholstered furniture, books, and clothing require different approaches than hard surfaces such as wood, glass, or ceramics. Smoke-damaged items go through ultrasonic cleaning or ozone treatment depending on the material and severity. Water-damaged textiles are dried under controlled conditions rather than in ambient air, which prevents the secondary mildew that develops when items dry too slowly. Once your home is ready, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed, ideally in the same rooms and positions they came from.

## Skokie Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents losses are often the most contested part of a residential insurance claim because adjusters and policyholders frequently disagree on replacement versus restoration value. A thorough, itemized inventory completed before pack-out gives your carrier the documentation they need to process the claim accurately. Dry Bros photographs and logs every item, notes pre-existing condition, and provides a written scope, which reduces back-and-forth with adjusters and helps ensure restorable items are not written off as total losses when cleaning and treatment can bring them back.

If your policy includes additional living expense (ALE) coverage, professional contents storage during restoration may be a covered line item. It is worth confirming with your adjuster early in the process.
