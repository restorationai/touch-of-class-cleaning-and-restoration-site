Plaster walls, horsehair insulation, original hardwood subfloors, and decades of layered paint create a porous interior environment where smoke, fire residue, pet odors, and flood-related mustiness can penetrate far beyond the surface. When a smell lingers after a loss event in Berwyn, it usually means the odor compounds have bonded to the building materials themselves, and surface sprays won't reach them. ## Why Berwyn's Housing Stock Makes Odor Problems Harder to Resolve

Berwyn sits in Cook County's inner-ring belt, and the overwhelming majority of its residential properties predate the postwar housing boom. That matters for odor removal because older building assemblies behave differently than modern stick-frame construction. Plaster over wood lath is highly absorbent, smoke particulates and volatile organic compounds from a fire, or microbial off-gassing after a water intrusion, can wick into the substrate and continue releasing odor long after the visible damage is addressed.

The tight lot spacing common throughout Berwyn also means that odors from one unit can migrate into adjacent spaces through shared walls, common stairwells, and balloon-frame cavities that run floor-to-floor without modern fire-blocking. In a two-flat or courtyard building, what started as a localized problem in one unit can become a whole-building odor issue within days.

Berwyn's climate adds another layer. The Chicago metro's humidity swings, humid summers followed by dry, sealed-up winters, cause porous materials to expand and contract seasonally. Odor compounds trapped in plaster or wood during a humid period get released again when indoor heat drops the relative humidity in winter, which is why a smell that seemed to fade in October can come back in January.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Berwyn

Effective deodorization isn't a single step, it's a sequence calibrated to the odor type, the materials involved, and the structure's age and layout.

**Assessment first.** Before any equipment is deployed, we identify the odor source and the affected materials. In Berwyn's older homes, that often means checking inside wall cavities, under original hardwood floors, and in unfinished basement ceiling assemblies where odor-laden air settles.

**Thermal fogging** uses a petroleum- or water-based deodorizing agent heated into a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke or odor traveled, reaching into cracks, behind baseboards, and into porous plaster surfaces that a spray applicator can't access.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** generates hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules at the chemical level. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators can operate in occupied or partially occupied spaces without requiring evacuation, which matters in multi-unit Berwyn buildings where displacing every tenant isn't practical.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces where odor penetration is severe. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer and requires the space to be vacated and sealed during treatment, we coordinate timing carefully in attached or semi-detached Berwyn properties to avoid ozone migration into neighboring units.

**Air scrubbing and HEPA filtration** run throughout the process to capture particulates that have been dislodged, keeping the work area from spreading contamination to unaffected rooms.
