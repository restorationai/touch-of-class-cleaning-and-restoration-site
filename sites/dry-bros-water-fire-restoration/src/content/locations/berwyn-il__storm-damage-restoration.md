## Why Berwyn Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Storm Damage

Berwyn's housing stock tells the story. The city developed rapidly in the early twentieth century, and the vast majority of its homes were built before 1950, many with balloon-frame construction, original clay tile roofing, and plaster-over-lath interior walls. That building profile creates specific vulnerabilities during severe weather.

Balloon framing, common in Berwyn's bungalow belt, lacks the fire-stops and cavity breaks that modern platform framing uses. In a storm context, that matters because wind-driven water can travel vertically inside wall cavities from a breached soffit or damaged roof deck all the way to the basement slab, sometimes without leaving a visible wet spot on the interior walls until mold has already begun colonizing the hidden framing.

Berwyn also has a significant number of homes with unreinforced brick masonry, beautiful, durable under normal conditions, but susceptible to mortar erosion when hail and driving rain hit repeatedly. A storm that looks cosmetically minor on the surface can leave micro-fractures in mortar joints that become water pathways in the next heavy rain.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Berwyn

Storm damage restoration isn't a single service, it's a sequence that has to happen in the right order to prevent secondary damage from compounding the original loss.

**Emergency stabilization** comes first. That means tarping breached rooflines, boarding compromised windows, and removing or bracing hanging tree limbs before they fall through what's still intact. In Berwyn, where mature tree canopy is dense and lots are narrow, downed limbs frequently land across property lines, we document the damage boundary carefully from the start.

**Moisture mapping** follows immediately. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled inside the structure. In older Berwyn homes with plaster walls, the surface can read dry while the lath behind it holds significant moisture, equipment that only measures surface conditions will miss it entirely.

**Structural drying** is then set up based on the moisture map, not a generic formula. Desiccant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, and in-wall drying panels are deployed where the readings indicate, not just in the rooms that look wet.

**Debris removal and reconstruction** close out the process, from tree damage cleanup and shingle replacement to interior repairs that restore the home to pre-storm condition.

## Berwyn Insurance Coordination for Storm Claims

Storm damage claims in Illinois are governed by standard homeowner's policy language, but the documentation requirements are specific and the window for filing matters. Most carriers require prompt written notice of loss and will send an adjuster to inspect before authorizing repair work beyond emergency stabilization.

We photograph and document all affected areas before any materials are moved or removed, roof surfaces, interior water intrusion points, structural members, and personal property. ## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing in Berwyn: many of the city's bungalows and two-flats were built with shallow roof pitches designed for the original clay or concrete tile, materials with significant weight and interlocking profiles that shed water even at low slopes. When those roofs were re-covered decades later with asphalt shingles (a lighter, less interlocking material), the low pitch became a liability. After a severe storm, water can back up under low-slope asphalt shingles in ways it never would have under the original tile, pushing moisture into the roof deck and rafters before any interior ceiling stain appears. If your Berwyn home has a low-pitch roof and you've had a significant storm, it's worth having the deck inspected even if the ceiling looks fine.
