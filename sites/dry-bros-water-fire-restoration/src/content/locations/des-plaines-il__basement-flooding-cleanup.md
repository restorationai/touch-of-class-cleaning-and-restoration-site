## Why Des Plaines Properties See Basement Flooding Issues

The geology under Des Plaines works against homeowners in a specific way. The glacial lake clays that dominate Cook County's subsurface are nearly impermeable, rainwater that can't absorb into the ground has nowhere to go except toward the lowest point on the property, which is almost always the basement floor. This is compounded by the fact that a significant portion of Des Plaines' residential housing stock was built between the 1940s and 1970s, when basement waterproofing standards were minimal and drain tile systems were either absent or have since deteriorated. Poured-concrete foundations from that era develop hairline cracks over decades of freeze-thaw cycling; block foundations are even more susceptible to lateral water intrusion.

Des Plaines also operates on a combined sewer system in many of its older residential corridors, the same pipes that carry stormwater carry sanitary waste. During heavy rainfall, that system can surcharge, meaning sewage-contaminated water backs up through floor drains. That changes the cleanup category entirely: what looks like a simple flooded basement may be a Category 3 (black water) loss requiring full protective protocols, not just a wet-vac and a dehumidifier.

## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Des Plaines

The first thing we do on arrival is determine the water source and contamination category, because the extraction and drying plan depends entirely on what kind of water you're dealing with. A clean-water supply line failure is handled differently than a sewer backup, and both are handled differently than groundwater intrusion through a foundation wall.

Once the source is identified and, where possible, stopped, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment. In Des Plaines basements, many of which have concrete block walls, vinyl floor tile over concrete slabs, and older wood-framed utility areas, we pay close attention to materials that hold moisture invisibly. Concrete block is porous; it can read dry at the surface with a pin meter while the cores are still saturated. We use thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to map the actual wet zone before setting drying equipment, so we're not pulling gear too early.

Structural drying typically involves a combination of high-capacity desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and directional air movers positioned to create an engineered airflow pattern across affected surfaces. We monitor and log moisture readings daily until materials reach target dry standard, then document the final readings for your insurance file.

## Des Plaines Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in Illinois cover sudden and accidental water damage, a burst pipe, a washing machine supply line failure, an appliance leak. They typically do not cover gradual seepage or flooding from outside the home unless you carry a separate flood policy through the National Flood Insurance Program. Sewer backup coverage is usually an endorsement, not a base policy feature, and it's worth checking your declarations page before you assume it's included.

We document losses thoroughly: photographs of affected materials, moisture readings at each measurement point, a written scope of work, and itemized equipment logs. That documentation is what carriers need to process a claim, and we provide it in the format most adjusters expect.

## Local Note

In Des Plaines, homes built on slab-on-grade foundations, common in some of the postwar subdivisions developed through the 1950s and 1960s, present a specific drying challenge that flat-rate timelines don't account for. When water migrates under a concrete slab, standard surface drying equipment can't reach it. We use specialized drying mats and in-slab drying systems to draw moisture up through the concrete rather than waiting for it to evaporate on its own, which can take weeks and allows mold to establish in the meantime. If your basement has a slab floor and you've had standing water, ask us specifically about sub-slab moisture when we arrive, it's a step that gets skipped more often than it should.
