Evanston's combined sewer system, where storm runoff and sanitary waste share the same underground pipes, means that a heavy Lake Michigan storm can push raw sewage back through floor drains and basement fixtures with very little warning. When that happens, the contamination isn't just unpleasant; Category 3 "black water" carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that colonize porous surfaces within hours. Dry Bros Water & Fire Restoration responds to sewage backup calls throughout Evanston, handling extraction, disinfection, and structural drying so the property is safe to occupy again.

## Why Evanston Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Evanston sits on a narrow strip of land between Lake Michigan and the North Shore suburbs, and that geography shapes its drainage challenges in ways that differ from inland Chicago neighborhoods. The city's older residential blocks, many built between the 1890s and 1940s, were plumbed when combined sewers were standard practice. During significant rainfall events, the volume of stormwater entering the system can exceed its capacity, and the path of least resistance is often back up through the lowest fixture in a basement.

Foundation types compound the problem. Older homes in the blocks west of Ridge Avenue frequently have rubble-stone or brick foundations with clay-tile lateral lines that have cracked or root-infiltrated over decades. A slow lateral failure can go unnoticed until a backup makes it impossible to ignore. Newer construction closer to the lakefront tends to have better-isolated sanitary lines, but even those properties are not immune when the main sewer is overwhelmed.

The city has invested in sewer separation projects in certain corridors, but much of the residential grid still operates on the combined system. Homeowners who have not yet installed an overhead sewer or backwater valve are the most exposed.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Evanston

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage cleanup. The contamination classification changes everything about how materials are handled and what the finished standard looks like.

**Containment and PPE setup.** Before any extraction begins, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to living spaces. Technicians work in full personal protective equipment throughout the job.

**Solid waste and standing water removal.** Visible solids are removed first, followed by extraction of standing contaminated water using truck-mounted or portable units capable of handling debris-laden waste.

**Removal of unsalvageable materials.** Porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, and pad, that have direct sewage contact are removed and bagged for disposal per Illinois EPA guidelines for biohazardous waste. In Evanston's older homes, this sometimes includes original wood subfloor sections that have absorbed contamination deeply enough that surface cleaning is not sufficient.

**EPA-registered disinfection.** Hard surfaces, framing, concrete, and remaining structural elements are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 water events. Multiple application passes are standard; a single spray-and-wipe is not adequate for raw sewage contamination.

**Structural drying.** Once the space is sanitized, desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers are positioned to dry the structural assembly. Moisture readings are logged at each visit until the structure returns to baseline.

**Post-remediation verification.** Surface samples or ATP testing can be performed before reconstruction begins to confirm the space is clean.

## Evanston Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is often a separate endorsement on a standard homeowner's policy, it is not automatically included, and many Evanston homeowners discover that gap at the worst possible moment. Dry Bros documents the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials before removal, moisture readings, waste extraction logs, and an itemized scope of work. That documentation package is what carriers need to process a claim, and having it organized from the start reduces the back-and-forth that delays settlements.

If your policy does not include sewage backup coverage, the documentation still matters, it supports any negotiation with your carrier and establishes a clear record if the backup is traceable to a municipal sewer failure, which opens a separate claims path through the city.

Evanston's older homes, particularly the two- and three-flat buildings common in the blocks east of Chicago Avenue, often have finished basement units with below-grade bathrooms. When a sewer backup occurs in one of those units, the contamination footprint is frequently larger than it appears on first inspection because the sewage has traveled under the finished floor before surfacing. Scoping the extent before writing a cleanup plan saves time and prevents incomplete remediation.
