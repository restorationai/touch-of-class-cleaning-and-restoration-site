## Why Oak Park Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Issues

Oak Park sits just west of the Chicago city limits, and it shares the same brutal seasonal swings that make the region hard on older structures. Freeze-thaw cycles crack mortar joints in brick facades, loosen chimney caps, and lift roof flashing, all of which can turn a minor storm into an emergency opening. The village's housing stock skews heavily toward pre-1950 construction, which means you'll find original wood-framed windows, single-wythe brick walls, and slate or clay-tile roofs that don't respond to damage the same way a modern asphalt-shingle house does.

Fire damage in particular leaves these older structures exposed in ways that newer construction doesn't. A kitchen fire in a 1920s bungalow can burn through a plaster ceiling into an attic filled with knob-and-tube wiring and loose-fill insulation, and once that roof deck is compromised, rain and wind move in quickly. Board-up and tarping work in Oak Park has to account for the weight and fragility of historic roofing materials, and for the fact that many of these homes have irregular framing dimensions that don't match standard sheet goods.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Oak Park

When we arrive at a property, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment, identifying every breach point before we start staging materials. On a typical Oak Park fire-damage call, that means checking not just the obvious burn area but also soffit vents, attic access points, and any shared walls with an adjacent unit, which are common in the village's two-flat and courtyard-building stock.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps secured with lumber battens and cap nails driven into undamaged decking, not just draped over the ridge and weighted with sandbags, which fail in wind. For window and door openings, we cut OSB panels to fit the rough opening and fasten them through the exterior casing into the structural framing, which holds far better than fastening into brick veneer alone. Every opening gets photographed before and after installation to support your insurance claim documentation.

When structural damage is severe enough that ladders alone won't safely reach the breach, we coordinate lift equipment. Narrow parkways and mature street trees are common in Oak Park's older blocks, so we plan access routes before equipment arrives to avoid delays.

## Oak Park Insurance and HOA Coordination

Oak Park has a mix of single-family homes, condominiums, and multi-unit rental buildings, and the insurance coordination process differs across all three. Condo associations typically carry a master policy that covers the building envelope, meaning the board-up of a fire-damaged exterior wall or a tarped roof may fall under the association's claim rather than the individual unit owner's policy. We document the loss thoroughly so that whichever carrier is handling the claim has what they need: photos, measurements, material specs, and a written scope of the protective work performed.

For single-family homeowners, most standard HO-3 policies cover emergency protective measures as part of the covered loss, but the documentation has to be there. We provide an itemized invoice and photo package formatted for carrier review.

## Local Note

Oak Park's mature urban tree canopy, one of the densest in the western suburbs, is genuinely beautiful, but it creates a specific hazard after storms. Large silver maples and cottonwoods that overhang rooflines are common throughout the village, and when a limb comes down on a slate or clay-tile roof, the damage pattern is different from what you'd see on asphalt shingles. Tile roofs crack and displace rather than tear, leaving irregular gaps that a standard rectangular tarp won't seal cleanly. When we tarp a tile or slate roof in Oak Park, we take extra time to conform the tarp to the broken field and seal the edges carefully, a flat-laid tarp that bridges over displaced tiles still lets water channel underneath.
