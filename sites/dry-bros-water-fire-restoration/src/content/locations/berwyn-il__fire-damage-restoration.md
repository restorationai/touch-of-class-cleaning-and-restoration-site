## Why Berwyn's Building Stock Shapes Fire Damage Differently

Berwyn sits just west of Chicago's city limits along the Eisenhower Corridor, and its housing stock reflects that history. The majority of single-family homes and multi-unit buildings here were constructed before modern fire-blocking requirements, which means a kitchen fire that looks contained can have already pushed smoke through wall cavities into upper floors or the attic before the fire department clears the scene.

Brick exteriors give these homes a deceptive appearance of structural soundness after a fire. The masonry often holds, but the wood lath, plaster, and floor joists inside absorb smoke and combustion byproducts deeply. Porous plaster, far more common here than in post-1980 construction, holds odor compounds in a way that standard drywall does not. That means odor neutralization in a Berwyn bungalow typically requires thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment that penetrates the wall substrate, not just surface cleaning.

Gas service in older Berwyn homes also runs through aging iron lateral lines in some blocks. After a structural fire, the utility shutoff and inspection process through Nicor Gas can add time before interior work can safely begin, something to factor into your restoration timeline from day one.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Berwyn

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work is consistent and matters. Here's how the process typically unfolds on a Berwyn property:

**Initial assessment and securing the structure.** Before any cleaning or drying begins, we assess structural integrity, document all affected areas with photographs, and board or tarp any openings. In Berwyn's attached two-flat and coach-house configurations, this step also involves evaluating whether adjacent units have been affected by smoke migration through shared walls or stairwells.

**Soot and smoke residue removal.** Dry soot from fast-burning fires and wet, oily soot from smoldering fires require different cleaning chemistry. We identify the residue type in each room and apply the appropriate method, dry chemical sponges, alkaline cleaners, or encapsulants, rather than using a single approach throughout.

**Structural drying.** Suppression water from fire hoses or sprinklers saturates flooring, subfloor, and wall cavities. We deploy commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, and we monitor moisture readings in the structure until materials reach target drying goals. In plaster-walled Berwyn homes, this phase runs longer than in drywall construction, plaster releases trapped moisture slowly, and rushing it leads to hidden mold problems weeks later.

**Odor elimination.** Smoke odor that isn't fully neutralized at the source will return when temperatures rise. We use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generators to reach the same porous surfaces smoke penetrated during the fire.

**Controlled demolition and rebuild coordination.** When charred framing, flooring, or plaster must come out, we handle selective demolition and can coordinate the rebuild phase so you're working with one point of contact rather than managing separate contractors.

## Local Note

Berwyn's brick bungalows often have unfinished basements with exposed wood floor joists directly below the first floor, a construction detail that matters after a kitchen or first-floor fire. Suppression water pools in the basement and soaks those joists from below while the floor above dries from the top. Restoration crews who don't check the basement moisture readings after a first-floor fire routinely miss this, and the joists stay wet long enough to develop mold before anyone notices. On Berwyn properties, we treat the basement as part of the fire loss scope even when the fire itself never reached it.
