## Why Berwyn Properties See Mold Issues

Berwyn sits in a climate band that delivers cold, wet winters and humid summers, a combination that keeps indoor moisture levels elevated for much of the year. The city's older homes were built before modern building science understood vapor diffusion, so many have uninsulated rim joists, stone or poured-concrete basement walls with no drainage membrane, and original cast-iron or galvanized plumbing that corrodes slowly and weeps without announcing itself.

The soil throughout this part of Cook County is predominantly clay-heavy, which means it drains poorly after heavy rain events. Water that can't move away from a foundation tends to migrate through it instead. In a Berwyn two-flat or bungalow with an original mortar-and-brick foundation, that moisture finds its way into the basement or crawl space, and from there into wood framing and wall cavities. Mold doesn't need a flood, it needs sustained humidity above roughly 60 percent, and Berwyn basements routinely provide that from late spring through early fall without any visible leak at all.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Berwyn

Every remediation starts with containment and assessment, not demolition. Before any material is removed, the affected area is isolated with negative-air pressure barriers so that disturbing the colony doesn't spread spores to unaffected rooms. In Berwyn's older plaster-wall homes, this step matters more than it does in newer construction, plaster is dense and can hold mold at the surface while the wood lath behind it stays damp and colonized, meaning a surface wipe-down without proper containment just redistributes the problem.

Once containment is established, moisture mapping with thermal imaging and calibrated meters identifies exactly where the water source is and how far the saturation has traveled. In a 1920s Berwyn bungalow, that often means checking the rim joist, the bottom plates of exterior walls, and the subfloor above the basement, all areas that absorb and hold moisture long after the surface feels dry.

Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing, are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. After drying is complete and clearance testing confirms airborne spore counts are within acceptable limits, the space is ready for reconstruction.

## Berwyn Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in older Cook County homes can be complicated by how the loss is framed. Most homeowner policies cover mold remediation when it results from a sudden and accidental covered peril, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, a roof leak from a storm. They typically do not cover mold that developed from long-term seepage or deferred maintenance, which is exactly the pattern common in Berwyn's older housing stock.

The documentation you submit with your claim matters enormously. ## Local Note

In Berwyn's older bungalows and two-flats, plaster-and-lath walls behave very differently from modern drywall when it comes to mold assessment. Plaster can read at acceptable moisture levels on a surface pin meter while the wood lath directly behind it remains saturated, because plaster releases moisture slowly and unevenly. Crews that rely on surface readings alone in these homes routinely miss active growth behind the wall. Thermal imaging combined with deep-probe readings gives a more accurate picture, and it's worth asking any contractor you're evaluating whether they're accounting for this before they tell you the wall is clean.
