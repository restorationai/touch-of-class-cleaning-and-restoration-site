Cicero's aging municipal sewer infrastructure, much of it laid during the mid-20th century building boom that shaped the town's dense grid of brick two-flats and bungalows, creates conditions where a single heavy rain event can push raw sewage back through floor drains and basement toilets without warning. When that happens, the clock starts immediately: sewage carries bacteria, viruses, and pathogens that begin colonizing porous surfaces within hours. Dry Bros Water & Fire Restoration responds to sewage backup calls in Cicero with the equipment and protocols to contain the contamination, remove the waste, and sanitize the space down to the structural level.

## Why Cicero Properties Are Prone to Sewage Backups

Cicero sits on a combined sewer system, the same underground pipes carry both stormwater runoff and sanitary sewage. During heavy rainfall, that system can exceed its capacity, and the pressure finds the path of least resistance: your basement drain. The problem is compounded by the housing stock itself. A large share of Cicero's residential properties were built between the 1920s and 1950s, meaning the lateral sewer lines connecting individual homes to the main are often original clay or cast-iron pipe. These materials crack, root-intrude, and partially collapse over decades, narrowing the passage and making backups far more likely, even during moderate rain.

Basements in this part of Cook County tend to run shallow, with floor drains positioned at the lowest point of the home. That design was practical for its era, but it means sewage has a direct and easy route into finished and unfinished living space alike. Carpet, drywall, insulation, and stored belongings absorb contaminated water quickly, and what looks like a manageable puddle at the surface can hide saturation several inches into the subfloor.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Cicero

Every sewage job begins with containment. Before any removal starts, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected parts of the home. Personal protective equipment is non-negotiable, raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water (black water), the most hazardous class, and it requires handling protocols that go well beyond what a shop vac and bleach can address.

Once the area is secured, we extract standing sewage using truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of handling solid-laden waste. Porous materials, drywall, carpet, insulation, wood framing that has absorbed contaminated water, are assessed for salvageability. In most Category 3 losses, affected drywall below the flood line and saturated insulation are removed rather than dried in place, because drying contaminated porous material does not eliminate the pathogens embedded in it.

After physical removal, all hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for sewage contamination. Structural cavities are dried using commercial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers. We take moisture readings throughout the process to confirm the structure has reached drying targets before the area is closed up, skipping that step is how mold problems develop weeks after a cleanup appears finished.

## Cicero Insurance Coordination for Sewage Losses

Whether a sewage backup is covered depends heavily on the specific policy. Standard homeowners policies in Illinois frequently exclude sewer backup unless the homeowner has added a sewer backup rider, a relatively affordable endorsement that many Cicero homeowners overlook until they need it. If you have the rider, we document the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials before removal, moisture readings, and a written scope of work that aligns with what your adjuster will need to process the claim. If coverage is unclear, we can work with you while you sort out the policy question, the cleanup cannot wait for an adjuster's schedule.
