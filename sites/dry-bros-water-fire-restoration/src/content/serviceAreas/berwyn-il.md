## Restoration emergencies common in Berwyn

Berwyn sits in Cook County on Chicago's near west side, and the city's restoration profile follows directly from its age and geography. The housing stock is dense and largely pre-war, brick bungalows, two-flats, and coach houses packed onto narrow lots with finished basements that sit close to the water table. When the region's clay-heavy soil becomes saturated after heavy spring rains or a rapid snowmelt, that water has nowhere to drain quickly. Sump pumps run hard, and when they fail, or when a lateral sewer backs up, a finished basement can take on several inches of standing water before the homeowner realizes what's happening.

Winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that stress older masonry and the galvanized or cast-iron supply lines common in homes built before the 1960s. ## Services we provide in Berwyn

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Berwyn frequently involves basement flooding from sump failure or sewer backup, but it also shows up as slow leaks inside plaster walls and beneath hardwood floors, damage that reads dry at the surface while the underlying assembly stays saturated. Dry Bros uses moisture mapping and thermal imaging to find what a visual inspection misses, then dries the structure to IICRC S500 standards before any rebuild begins.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Berwyn's closely spaced housing means smoke from even a contained kitchen fire can migrate into adjacent units or neighboring structures through shared attic spaces and utility chases. Smoke residue is acidic and continues etching surfaces, metals, glass, finished wood, long after the flames are out. Restoration here means addressing odor at the molecular level, not masking it, and documenting every affected material for the insurance claim.

### Mold remediation

The combination of older plaster-and-lath construction, limited vapor barriers, and Berwyn's humid summers creates conditions where mold can establish in wall cavities and crawlspaces without obvious surface signs. Remediation scopes in these homes often require careful containment to avoid cross-contaminating living areas during work.

### Biohazard and specialty cleanup

For situations involving sewage intrusion, trauma scenes, or other biohazard conditions, Dry Bros handles safe removal, disinfection, and disposal in compliance with applicable health and safety standards, and coordinates documentation for insurance carriers.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Berwyn

Berwyn developed rapidly in the early twentieth century, and the vast majority of its residential buildings date from roughly 1900 through the 1950s. Brick bungalows and two-flats dominate, typically built on full basements with masonry foundations. Wall assemblies in this era are plaster over wood lath, a system that holds moisture differently than modern drywall and requires longer drying times and more aggressive monitoring. Supply lines in pre-1960s homes are commonly galvanized steel, which corrodes from the inside out and can fail without warning. Cast-iron drain lines are standard and generally durable but prone to root intrusion and joint separation in older runs. Any restoration scope in these homes must account for the potential presence of asbestos-containing materials in floor tile, pipe insulation, and plaster, as well as lead-based paint on pre-1978 surfaces. Cook County's clay-dominant soil drains slowly and exerts significant lateral pressure on masonry foundation walls. Structural repairs and significant reconstruction in Berwyn require permits through the city's Community Development department; work that alters structural elements, plumbing, or electrical systems will trigger a permit and inspection before the job can close.
