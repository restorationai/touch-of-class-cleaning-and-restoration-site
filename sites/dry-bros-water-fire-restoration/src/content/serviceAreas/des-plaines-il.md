## Services we provide in Des Plaines

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Des Plaines frequently starts below grade. Basements, common across the city's mid-century ranch and bungalow stock, collect water from foundation cracks, failed sump pumps, and sewer backups during heavy rain. Standing water under flooring reads dry at the surface while the subfloor and wall framing stay saturated for days. Dry Bros uses moisture meters and thermal imaging to find what the eye misses, then sets industrial air movers and dehumidifiers to dry the structure to IICRC S500 standards before mold has a chance to colonize.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue travels far beyond the room where a fire starts. In a ranch-style home, HVAC systems pull soot through ductwork and deposit it on surfaces in rooms that look untouched. Dry Bros scopes the full path of smoke migration, removes charred materials, applies appropriate cleaning agents for each surface type, dry-chemical sponge on flat paint, wet cleaning on hard surfaces, and addresses the embedded odor that lingers long after visible soot is gone.

### Mold remediation

Des Plaines homes with chronic basement moisture or slow roof leaks are susceptible to mold growth behind drywall and under flooring. Dry Bros contains affected areas, removes compromised materials, and treats structural surfaces according to EPA guidelines. Remediation scope and clearance testing requirements vary by the size and type of affected area, we walk property owners through what applies to their specific situation.
