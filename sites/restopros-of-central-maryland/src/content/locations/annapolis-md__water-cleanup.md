## Why Annapolis Properties See Water Damage

Annapolis sits where the Severn River meets the Chesapeake Bay, and that geography shapes how water gets into buildings here. Nuisance tidal flooding during full-moon high tides and nor'easters pushes water up storm drains and into low-lying streets long before a storm itself ever makes landfall. Downtown and waterfront properties see water intrude from the ground up through crawlspaces and slab penetrations, not just from roof leaks or burst pipes. Add in a historic housing stock, plenty of homes with brick foundations and plaster walls dating back generations, and you get a drying problem that behaves differently than a typical suburban drywall job. Plaster and old masonry hold moisture longer and release it slowly, which means a wall that reads dry on a moisture meter today can still be wicking water from the foundation a week later if drying isn't tracked properly.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Annapolis

We start with extraction, pulling standing water out of flooring, crawlspaces, and wall cavities before it has a chance to migrate further into framing. On older Annapolis homes we pay close attention to plaster and masonry walls, which absorb and release moisture on a different timeline than modern drywall, so our drying plan accounts for that rather than pulling equipment the moment surface readings look good. We set containment and air movers calibrated to the structure, monitor moisture daily, and document every reading for the insurance file. For properties near the water table or in flood-prone low areas, we also check subfloor and crawlspace conditions even when the visible damage looks contained, because water that enters from the ground doesn't always show itself at the surface first.
