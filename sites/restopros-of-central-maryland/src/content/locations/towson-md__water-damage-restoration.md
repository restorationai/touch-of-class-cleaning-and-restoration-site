## Why Towson Properties See Water Damage Issues

Baltimore County's Piedmont soil holds clay close to the surface, and Towson's rolling lots mean a lot of basements sit partially below grade. During a hard summer thunderstorm or the remnants of an Atlantic storm system working up the coast, that clay doesn't drain fast, so hydrostatic pressure builds against foundation walls and finds its way in through hairline cracks, old mortar joints, or sump pump failures. Add in a housing stock that ranges from early-to-mid-20th-century homes near downtown Towson with original plaster and cast iron plumbing to newer townhome communities further out, and you get two very different failure patterns: slow seepage through aging foundations on one end, and sudden supply-line or appliance failures on modern construction at the other. Winter brings its own version of the problem. A hard freeze followed by a quick thaw is a classic setup for a burst pipe in an uninsulated crawlspace or an exterior-wall run that wasn't updated when the home was renovated.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Towson

The first thing a technician does on arrival is separate what looks wet from what actually is wet. Older plaster walls common in Towson's established neighborhoods can look dry on the surface while the lath behind them stays saturated for days, so we use moisture meters and thermal imaging rather than guessing from appearance. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable extractors depending on how the property is accessed, followed by structural drying with air movers and dehumidifiers set to pull moisture out of framing, subfloor, and masonry at a rate the materials can actually release it. Maryland's humid summers work against us here; ambient humidity that's already high means we often run equipment longer than a drier climate would require, and we track that with daily moisture readings rather than a fixed timeline. Drying is documented and measured against the IICRC S500 standard so your insurance adjuster has a clear record of when materials actually reached dry standard, not just when equipment got pulled.
