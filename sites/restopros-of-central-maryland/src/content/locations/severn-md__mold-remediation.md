Mold doesn't wait for a convenient season in Anne Arundel County. Between the humid Chesapeake summers and the older crawl-space homes scattered through Severn, a slow leak under a bathroom vanity or a damp basement corner can turn into a colonized wall cavity in a matter of days. If you're smelling that musty, earthy odor or seeing dark speckling creep along a baseboard, the window to handle it as a simple cleanup rather than a larger remediation job is closing fast.

## Why Severn Properties See Mold Issues

Severn sits in the Severn Run watershed, and the water table across this stretch of Anne Arundel County stays high for much of the year. Combine that with the area's mix of 1960s-80s split-level and rambler-style homes, many with original crawl spaces or partially finished basements, and you get building stock that was never designed with vapor barriers or modern drainage in mind. Summer humidity pushes moisture into those low, unconditioned spaces, and without a dehumidifier running, relative humidity under the floor can sit well above the 60% threshold mold needs to establish itself. We also see it in newer builds where HVAC systems are oversized for the square footage, over-cooling the air without actually pulling out enough moisture, which leaves walls and closets perpetually damp to the touch.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Severn

Every job starts with identifying the moisture source, not just the visible growth, because treating the mold without fixing why it's there means it comes back. We isolate the affected area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into the rest of the house during removal, then remove and bag porous materials that can't be salvaged, typically drywall, insulation, and carpet padding that's absorbed moisture. Hard surfaces get HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an antimicrobial, and we follow the drying and verification approach outlined in the industry's S520 mold remediation guidelines before anything gets closed back up. For crawl space jobs common in Severn's older homes, we often recommend a vapor barrier or improved ventilation as part of the fix, since skipping that step just resets the clock on the same problem.

Homes with crawl spaces near the Severn Run tributary tend to hold ground moisture longer after a wet spring than homes on higher, better-drained lots elsewhere in the county.
