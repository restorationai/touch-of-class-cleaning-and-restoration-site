## Why Severn Properties See Storm Damage

A lot of Severn's housing stock was built before current wind-uplift codes, which means older asphalt shingle roofs can lose tabs in gusts that a newer roof would shrug off. Add in the area's dense tree cover, oaks and tulip poplars especially, and wind events regularly bring down limbs onto roofs, fences, and siding. Basements are common in this part of the county, and when gutters overflow or a downspout disconnects during a storm, that water finds its way through foundation cracks and window wells faster than most homeowners expect. We also see wind-driven rain intrusion around older vinyl and wood-frame windows that have shifted slightly over the decades, which lets water track into wall cavities long after the rain stops.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Severn

We start with a roof and exterior assessment to stop active water intrusion, tarping damaged roof sections and boarding broken windows so the interior doesn't keep absorbing moisture while repairs are scheduled. From there we extract standing water from basements and crawlspaces, check insulation and subfloor for saturation, and set up drying equipment calibrated to Maryland's summer humidity, which can slow evaporation compared to drier climates. Moisture readings get documented room by room, following IICRC drying guidelines, so we know when materials are actually dry rather than just surface-dry. Tree and debris removal from the roof or yard happens early in the process, since limbs left resting on shingles can cause secondary leaks even after the storm has passed.

## Local note

Severn's older basements, particularly in homes built before the 1980s, often have block foundations without a modern drainage board system behind them. When a storm saturates the surrounding soil, these walls can weep moisture for days after the rain stops even if there's no visible crack, which is why we recommend a follow-up moisture check a week or so after major storms rather than assuming the basement dried on its own.
