A downed tree limb through a roofline, wind-driven rain forcing its way under old window flashing, or a surge off the Chesapeake pushing water into a waterfront basement all call for the same thing: fast extraction before wood, plaster, and drywall start absorbing more than they can release on their own.

Annapolis sits where the Severn River meets the Chesapeake Bay, and that geography shapes how storms do damage here. Nor'easters and tropical remnants moving up the bay bring sustained wind and tidal surge at the same time, which means a single storm event can produce both wind damage to the roof and water intrusion at grade, sometimes on the same property. Homes closer to the waterfront deal with brackish water intrusion that behaves differently than plain rainwater, it carries salt and sediment that keep materials damp longer and accelerate corrosion on fasteners, HVAC components, and electrical boxes.

## Why Annapolis Properties See Storm Damage Issues

The city's building stock spans centuries, from colonial-era brick and timber-frame construction downtown to mid-century and newer construction further out toward the county line. Older masonry and wood-frame homes were not built with modern storm-sealing details, so wind-driven rain finds its way in through original window openings, chimney flashing, and roof penetrations that have been patched over decades rather than replaced. Mature tree canopy throughout the older residential streets adds another layer of risk: live oaks and poplars that shade a property beautifully in July become the biggest liability when a storm cell moves through with sustained gusts.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process

We start with a structural safety check, because storm damage often means compromised roof decking, cracked rafters, or electrical hazards from water contact before anyone goes further than the front door. Once the property is safe to work in, we extract standing water, remove saturated materials that won't dry in place, and set drying equipment calibrated to the specific assemblies involved, plaster and old-growth lumber release moisture more slowly than modern drywall and dimensional lumber, so drying timelines get adjusted accordingly rather than treated as one-size-fits-all. Our WRT-certified technicians document moisture readings throughout the process, which matters both for drying verification and for the insurance paperwork that follows. Tree limb removal, tarping of open roof sections, and board-up of broken windows happen early, generally before the interior drying phase begins, to stop ongoing intrusion.

## Annapolis Insurance & HOA Coordination

A lot of Annapolis's older neighborhoods and waterfront communities fall under historic district guidelines or HOA covenants that govern exterior repairs, from roofing material to fence replacement after storm damage. We photograph and document damage in a way that supports both the insurance claim and any HOA or historic review submission your property may need before exterior work can proceed. Storm claims in this area often involve a wind component and a water component on the same estimate, and carriers sometimes split those into separate line items, so clear documentation at the start saves back-and-forth later.
