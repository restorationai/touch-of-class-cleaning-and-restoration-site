## What we cover

RestoPros of Central Maryland handles the full arc of property damage, from the first call after a pipe bursts through the last coat of paint on a rebuilt wall. We work with homeowners, property managers, and commercial building owners across Central Maryland, including older rowhomes and farmhouses with plaster and lath, newer subdivisions with slab foundations, and multi-family buildings where one unit's flood becomes three units' problem. Below is a quick map of what we do and where to go for more detail.

## Emergency restoration services

Water intrusion from a burst pipe, failed sump pump, or storm backup needs attention before drywall and subfloor start absorbing moisture they won't give back easily. Our water damage team is IICRC WRT certified and documents moisture levels as part of the drying process. Fire and smoke damage calls for a different kind of urgency: soot etches into finishes fast, and smoke odor works its way into fabric, drywall, and HVAC ductwork the longer it sits untreated. Storm damage, from wind-driven rain to fallen limbs through a roof, often overlaps with water and structural concerns and needs the same fast assessment. Biohazard situations, whether a trauma scene or sewage backup, require careful containment and disposal, not a quick mop-up. We answer calls 24/7 for all of these.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once the damaged materials are out and the structure is dry, the rebuild starts: framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, paint, and trim work to bring the space back to pre-loss condition or better. We also take on general contracting projects tied to a loss, like reconfiguring a kitchen after a flood or replacing a section of roof after storm damage, so the same crew that managed drying and demo can carry the project through to a finished space. That continuity matters for insurance documentation and for not having to vet a second contractor mid-project.

## Specialty services

Not every call is a flood or a fire. Mold inspection and remediation address growth that's often a downstream effect of a water event, sometimes one that happened weeks before anyone noticed a musty smell. Odor removal tackles smoke, pet, or mildew odors that linger after visible damage is gone. Board-up services secure a property after a break-in, fire, or storm, protecting it from weather and unauthorized entry while repairs are arranged.

## One call, one company

The handoff between mitigation and reconstruction is usually where projects lose time, paperwork gets duplicated, and homeowners end up managing two contractors instead of one. We keep that process under one roof: the crew that extracts water or removes fire debris is the same company that rebuilds what was lost, with one point of contact and one set of records for your insurance carrier.
