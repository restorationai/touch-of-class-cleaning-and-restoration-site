A burst pipe in the middle of the night, a basement that won't stop seeping after a storm, smoke damage that lingers long after the fire trucks leave: these are the calls RestoPros of Central Maryland takes every day. We handle water damage restoration and extraction, fire and smoke damage cleanup, storm damage repair, and mold remediation for homeowners and property managers across Baldwin and the surrounding area, and we answer the phone 24/7 because disasters don't wait for business hours.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** covers everything from a failed sump pump to a slow leak behind drywall. Our technicians extract standing water, pull moisture from subfloors and framing, and dry the structure using monitoring equipment so nothing gets rebuilt over a damp cavity.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** addresses the soot, odor, and secondary water damage left behind after a fire, including the residue that settles into drywall, fabric, and HVAC systems long after the flames are out.

**Storm damage repair** deals with wind-driven rain, roof breaches, and the flooding that follows heavy Maryland downpours, with board-up and tarping to stop further intrusion while repairs are planned.

**Mold remediation** addresses the colonies that take hold when moisture sits in wall cavities or crawlspaces too long, with containment and removal handled according to industry drying and air-quality standards.
