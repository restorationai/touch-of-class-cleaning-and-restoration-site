**Need a restoration crew and not sure if we cover your address?** RestoPros of Central Maryland answers calls 24/7, and dispatch is built around one simple question: how fast can a crew get to your door. The service area below is organized by drive time from our Baldwin base, not by county lines, because a burst pipe in Towson and one in Bel Air don't wait for the same traffic.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Baldwin and responds across Baltimore County, Harford County, and Baltimore City, with regular coverage extending into northern Anne Arundel and Howard County along the I-95 and I-83 corridors. That footprint covers a mix of rowhomes and rain-heavy rural properties: older Baltimore City and Towson housing stock with plaster walls and cast-iron drain lines, newer subdivisions off Route 924 and Route 40, and the wooded, well-and-septic properties around Jacksonville, Phoenix, and White Hall that flood differently than a city block does.

## Coverage by distance

Crews reach Baldwin, Kingsville, Hydes, White Hall, Jacksonville, and Phoenix first, since those jobs are closest to home base. From there, coverage extends to Towson, Bel Air, Perry Hall, Fallston, and the rest of Baltimore City and Baltimore County, where dispatch order depends on what's already active and how the Beltway is running that hour. Jobs toward the outer edge, Columbia, Westminster, Annapolis, and northern Anne Arundel County, still get worked, but they're scheduled in after closer calls are stabilized, and two-lane routes through rural Harford and Baltimore County can add real time to an ETA, especially after a storm when fallen limbs and downed lines slow everything down.

## Don't see your city?

If your address isn't listed among our service-area pages, call before assuming we can't help. Coverage gets extended for larger losses, standing water across multiple rooms, a fire with significant structural damage, sewage backups, and we'd rather confirm scope over the phone than have you guess. A quick call to (240) 261-1639 gets you a straight answer on timing for your specific location.
