## Restoration emergencies common in Bethesda

Bethesda's mix of older established homes and higher-density condo and townhome development creates a few recurring patterns. Heavy regional thunderstorms and the occasional remnant tropical system push a lot of water at older roofs and grading systems that were never designed for today's storm volume, which shows up as basement seepage and window-well backups. Winter cold snaps followed by quick thaws are hard on aging copper and galvanized supply lines, especially in homes built before mid-century plumbing updates. In multi-unit buildings, a failed supply line or overflowing unit above can send water through several floors before anyone notices, turning a single apartment problem into a shared-wall mold risk within days.
