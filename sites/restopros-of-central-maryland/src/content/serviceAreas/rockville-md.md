## Restoration emergencies common in Rockville

Rockville's mix of mid-century single-family homes and denser townhome and condo development near the Metro corridor creates two very different damage patterns. Older homes built during the county's postwar growth often have aging plumbing, clay-heavy soil pressing against original foundations, and basements that were never fully waterproofed, so heavy rain or a failed sump pump shows up as seepage along the slab joint. Newer multi-unit buildings see more plumbing-stack failures and shared-wall water intrusion, where a leak in one unit becomes a drywall and mold problem two floors down before anyone notices. Add Maryland's humid summers and the occasional remnant tropical system moving up from the Chesapeake, and both building types end up on our call list for the same reason: trapped moisture that doesn't announce itself until it's already behind the wall.

## Services we provide in Rockville

Water damage restoration is our most frequent Rockville call, and it's rarely just the visible puddle. In older homes with crawlspaces or partial basements, water tracks along joists and insulation long after the surface looks dry, which is why we use moisture meters and thermal imaging rather than guessing from what's visible.

Fire and smoke damage cleanup in Rockville often involves not just the room of origin but smoke residue that's traveled through forced-air HVAC systems common in the area's 1960s and 70s ranch and split-level homes, settling into ductwork and soft materials throughout the house.

Mold remediation becomes necessary when any of the above goes unaddressed. Maryland's summer humidity means a water intrusion that sits for more than a day or two in an unventilated basement or crawlspace can support mold growth well before a homeowner notices a musty smell.

Biohazard and trauma cleanup rounds out what we handle locally, following OSHA and EPA guidelines for safe handling and disposal, work that requires training most general contractors don't carry.
