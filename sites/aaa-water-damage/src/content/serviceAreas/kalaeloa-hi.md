### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Kalaeloa frequently starts at the roof or at slab-level plumbing penetrations. ### Mold remediation

Kalaeloa's persistent coastal humidity creates conditions where mold can begin colonizing wet materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Kalaeloa

Kalaeloa's built environment reflects two distinct eras.
