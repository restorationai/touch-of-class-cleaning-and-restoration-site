## Restoration emergencies common in Mililani Mauka

Mililani Mauka sits at a higher elevation than the older Mililani Town below it, which means it catches more of the orographic rainfall that rolls off the Waianae and Koolau ranges. That consistent moisture load is the single biggest driver of water and mold calls in this community. Homes here are tightly spaced in a planned-community layout, and when a roof flashing fails or a supply line lets go inside a wall cavity, the humidity that already saturates the air gives mold a very short window to colonize wet framing, typically 24 to 48 hours after saturation begins. Trade winds offer some natural ventilation, but they also carry fine particulates during vog events from Kilauea activity on the Big Island, which can settle into HVAC systems and soft furnishings and complicate indoor air quality after any kind of structural opening. Flash flooding during heavy Kona storm events can push water into garages and lower-level entries faster than floor drains can handle.
