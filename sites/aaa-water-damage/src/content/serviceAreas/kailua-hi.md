## Restoration emergencies common in Kailua

Kailua sits on the windward side of the Ko'olau Range, and the geography works against property owners in ways that don't show up in mainland building guides. Trade-wind showers roll through almost daily, keeping ambient humidity high enough that even a slow drip behind a cabinet can feed mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. The water table around Kawainui Marsh and Enchanted Lake sits close enough to the surface that heavy rain events push groundwater up through slab foundations — a problem that surprises owners who assume their damage came from a roof or plumbing failure.

Kailua's classic single-wall construction is the other factor. Those post-war beach cottages — common throughout Kalama Tract and Coconut Grove — use a single layer of tongue-and-groove or board-and-batten siding with no vapor barrier between the exterior and the interior finish. Water wicks into the wall assembly within hours of a leak, and by the time a stain appears on the interior surface, the framing is already saturated. Salt air compounds the problem along the beach blocks near Lanikai Beach and Kailua Beach Park: corroded supply fittings and appliance connections fail quietly, and because Kailua carries one of Oahu's densest concentrations of vacation rentals and second homes, the owner is often off-island when it happens. Leaks that would be caught in a day on the mainland can run for a week before a neighbor notices.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

We clean structural surfaces, contents, and HVAC pathways and use hydroxyl or ozone treatment where odor has penetrated deeply.
