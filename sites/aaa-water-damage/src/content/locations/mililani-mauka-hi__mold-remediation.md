## Why Mililani Mauka Properties See Mold Issues

Mililani Mauka was developed primarily in the 1990s and early 2000s as a master-planned extension of Mililani Town. That construction era brought a particular building profile: concrete block or wood-frame homes with stucco exteriors, tile roofs, and central air conditioning systems that were sized for comfort but not always for the humidity swings the central plateau delivers. When AC systems are undersized or aging, indoor relative humidity can climb well above the 60 percent threshold where mold colonies begin establishing themselves on drywall paper, wood framing, and carpet backing.

The central Oahu plateau also experiences afternoon cloud cover and occasional heavy rain events that differ from coastal Honolulu patterns. Roof penetrations, sliding door tracks, and window frames that handle that rainfall without issue for years can develop slow leaks that go undetected inside wall assemblies. By the time a homeowner sees discoloration on a ceiling or baseboard, mold has often been active in the concealed space for weeks.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Mililani Mauka

Remediation here follows a sequence designed around the specific materials and humidity conditions of central Oahu homes. The process begins with containment: plastic sheeting and negative air pressure isolate the affected area so that disturbing mold-colonized material does not spread spores into clean living spaces. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously during the work.

Once containment is established, affected porous materials such as drywall, insulation, and saturated carpet pad are removed and bagged for proper disposal. Structural framing and concrete block that can be cleaned rather than removed is treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial. Because Mililani Mauka homes often have tile and concrete surfaces adjacent to affected areas, those surfaces are thoroughly cleaned and dried before any encapsulation or reconstruction begins.

Drying equipment, including commercial dehumidifiers calibrated for Hawaii's ambient humidity levels, runs until moisture readings in structural materials return to acceptable baselines. Clearance testing by an independent industrial hygienist is available and, depending on the scope of the loss, may be recommended before reconstruction closes up any opened wall cavities.
