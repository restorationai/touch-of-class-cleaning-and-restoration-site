## Why Hickam Housing Properties Face Elevated Mold Risk

The housing stock at Hickam was built in waves, many units dating to mid-century military construction designed for airflow rather than the sealed, air-conditioned interiors residents use today. When older buildings are retrofitted with central AC, condensation forms on surfaces that were never designed to manage it, particularly around duct boots, window casings, and concrete block walls. Concrete block, common throughout the Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam footprint, is porous in ways that modern framing lumber is not. Moisture wicks inward from the exterior, stays trapped behind paint or drywall, and feeds mold colonies that remain invisible until the problem is well established. Add the region's heavy rainfall patterns, O'ahu's Ko'olau Mountains push weather systems directly over the island's south shore during certain seasons, and the drainage challenges on low-lying lots near the harbor, and you have a set of conditions that make mold a recurring issue rather than a one-time event.

## Our Mold Remediation Process, Calibrated for This Environment

Mold remediation here is not the same job it is in a dry-climate city. Ambient humidity on O'ahu means that simply removing visible growth without controlling the moisture source and the air in the space often leads to recurrence. Our process starts with a thorough inspection to locate moisture intrusion points, including thermal imaging where surface readings alone would miss saturation behind concrete or plaster. We then establish containment with negative air pressure to prevent spores from spreading to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to proper protocols. Structural surfaces are treated and dried to verified moisture levels before any encapsulation or rebuild work begins. Throughout the process, HEPA air scrubbers run continuously to capture airborne spores, which is especially important in Hickam Housing where homes share walls or are positioned close together.

Concrete block construction, which is widespread in the older residential sections of the Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam area, behaves differently from wood-frame homes during mold remediation.
