## Why Royal Kunia Properties See Elevated Mold Risk

Central Oahu's interior climate is meaningfully different from beachside Honolulu. Royal Kunia receives less consistent trade wind ventilation than coastal areas, which means moisture that enters a home through a slow roof leak, a condensation issue around an AC unit, or a plumbing drip doesn't dry out as quickly. Homes here also tend to have slab-on-grade foundations with minimal crawl space, which shifts the moisture risk upward into wall cavities and ceiling assemblies rather than below the floor. Tightly constructed homes with spray foam or closed-cell insulation, common in newer builds, can trap elevated humidity in wall cavities where standard visual inspection won't reveal anything unusual. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event, and in a home where air circulation is limited, spore counts can climb without any obvious surface evidence.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Royal Kunia

A thorough mold assessment in this area starts with a visual walkthrough and moisture mapping using calibrated meters that read moisture content in drywall, wood framing, and concrete substrates. We look at areas that are statistically high-risk in central Oahu homes: bathroom exhaust paths, attic decking near ridge vents, the wall cavities adjacent to AC air handlers, and any area with a documented or suspected prior water intrusion.

Air sampling is collected using calibrated spore trap cassettes, typically at two or more interior locations plus an outdoor baseline sample. That baseline matters because Hawaii's outdoor air naturally carries a higher background concentration of certain mold genera than mainland climates. Without an outdoor reference point, an interior reading can look alarming when it's actually within the normal range for the island environment, or it can look acceptable when it's actually elevated relative to outdoor conditions. Samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory for analysis, and results are returned with a written report that identifies genus, concentration, and any species of concern.

If surface sampling is warranted based on visible growth or anomalous moisture readings, we collect tape lift or swab samples from suspected areas before any disturbance occurs.
