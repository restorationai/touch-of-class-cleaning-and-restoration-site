## Why Pearl City Properties See Elevated Water Intrusion Risk

Pearl City (ZIP 96782) sits on a series of ridges and benches above Pearl Harbor, and that topography shapes how water moves through the neighborhood. The hillside lots in Pacific Palisades and Momilani were graded in the 1960s and 1970s, and decades of settling have left many yards with negative drainage that directs runoff straight toward foundation walls and garage slabs. Flat lots closer to the harbor — in Waiau and along the Pearl City Peninsula — drain slowly because the water table sits high and the soil is dense fill over old wetland.

The housing stock compounds the problem. A large share of homes in the 96782 corridor were built with galvanized supply lines that are now at or past the end of their service life. When a galvanized fitting fails inside a wall, it often fails slowly — a pinhole that saturates insulation and framing for weeks before the ceiling stain appears. By the time the homeowner notices, the structural cavity is holding far more moisture than a single shop fan can address. Professional drying equipment — high-static-pressure air movers, refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers, and moisture meters to track progress — is what actually moves the needle.

Multigenerational households and ohana units are common throughout Pearl City, so a single water loss can displace several families at once. Dry-in-place drying, where equipment is staged inside the structure rather than requiring full gut-and-rebuild, keeps displacement time shorter and costs lower.

## Local Note: Hillside Homes and Airflow Dead Zones

Homes on the steeper terraces in Pacific Palisades were often built with split-level layouts to follow the grade — a lower garage or utility room sits partially below the main living floor, connected by a short interior stairwell. That configuration creates an airflow dead zone: air movers placed on the main level push dry air that never reaches the lower slab. When we stage equipment in those homes, we position at least one unit at the lower level pointing upward through the stairwell opening, and we seal the garage door gap with a threshold strip to keep humid outside air from counteracting the dehumidifiers. It's a small adjustment that can cut drying time by a full day.
