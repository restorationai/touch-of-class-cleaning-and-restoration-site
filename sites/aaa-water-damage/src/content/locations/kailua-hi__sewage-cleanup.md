## Why Kailua Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Kailua's charm comes partly from its geography, and that same geography creates real plumbing stress. The town sits in a natural bowl between the Koolau Range and the ocean, which means stormwater has limited places to drain. When Kona storms stall over the mountains and drop several inches of rain in a short window, municipal sewer lines in the 96734 ZIP code can surcharge — meaning the system fills faster than it can discharge, and pressure reverses into the lowest drain in a home. Ground-floor bathrooms, laundry rooms, and utility sinks are the first to show it.

Septic systems are still common on the edges of Kailua Town and in older Maunawili properties, and those tanks saturate quickly when the soil is already holding water from days of trade-wind showers. A tank that overflows during a wet stretch isn't just a plumbing issue — it's a Category 3 biohazard event that requires full containment and sanitization, not just a pump-out.

Salt air compounds the problem. Fittings and appliance supply lines corrode faster on the windward side of Oahu than almost anywhere else on the island. A braided steel supply line that might last fifteen years in a drier climate can fail in eight here, and a slow pinhole leak behind a toilet can go unnoticed for weeks — especially in the vacation rentals and second homes that make up a significant share of Kailua's housing inventory. Owners off-island often don't know there's a problem until a neighbor notices water at the door.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Kailua

Sewage cleanup isn't a single step — it's a sequence, and skipping any part of it leaves biological hazard behind.

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area immediately to prevent cross-contamination to clean parts of the home. In Kailua's open-plan beach cottages, that often means sealing doorways and shutting down HVAC airflow before any extraction begins.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage and saturated materials — flooring, baseboards, lower wall sections — are removed and bagged for proper disposal. We don't dry contaminated materials in place; in a Category 3 event, porous materials that contacted raw sewage are treated as waste.

**Disinfection and sanitization.** Structural surfaces that remain are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. Given Kailua's humidity, we pay particular attention to subfloor cavities and wall interiors where moisture and organic matter create ideal conditions for rapid mold colonization — on the windward side of Oahu, mold can establish a foothold in as little as 24 to 48 hours after a sewage event.

**Drying and monitoring.** Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in walls and subfloors return to baseline. In Kailua's climate, ambient humidity makes this phase take longer than it would in a drier region, and we monitor daily rather than setting equipment and walking away.
