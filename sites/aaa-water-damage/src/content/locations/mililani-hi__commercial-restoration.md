## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Mililani

Every commercial job starts with a documented scope — photographs, moisture readings, and a written assessment that satisfies both your insurance adjuster and, where applicable, your property management company or HOA. Mililani's strong association standards mean finishes need to match: paint sheens, carpet grades, and tile patterns are logged before demolition begins so reconstruction closes out without visible patchwork.

From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Extraction and containment** — standing water is removed with truck-mount or portable extraction units; affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination of unaffected inventory or workspaces.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned according to a drying plan, with daily moisture readings logged until materials reach target levels.
- **Demolition and debris removal** — saturated drywall, flooring, and insulation that cannot be dried in place are removed and disposed of properly.
- **Reconstruction** — framing, drywall, flooring, and finish work are completed to pre-loss condition, with materials matched to existing specs.

For fire and smoke jobs, odor neutralization and duct cleaning are built into the scope before reconstruction begins — not added as an afterthought.
