## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Pearl City

Every job starts with a dry inspection — we walk the space, check pile direction, test fiber content in a discreet corner, and identify any pre-existing moisture in the subfloor using a calibrated moisture meter. In Pearl City's older homes, we occasionally find that a previous water event soaked the pad without anyone realizing it; cleaning over a wet pad just reactivates odor and can accelerate mold colonization in the subfloor.

Once we confirm the substrate is dry, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Dry soil removal** — commercial-grade HEPA vacuuming to pull loose particulate before any liquid is introduced. Skipping this step turns dry soil into mud during hot-water extraction.
2. **Pre-treatment** — pH-balanced conditioning agents applied to high-traffic lanes and stained areas, with dwell time calibrated to the fiber type. Nylon and olefin — the two most common fibers in Pearl City's mid-century homes — respond differently and are treated accordingly.
3. **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted equipment generating water temperatures and vacuum lift that portable units can't match. This is the step that actually flushes contaminants out of the pile rather than redistributing them.
4. **Accelerated drying** — high-velocity air movers positioned to push drying time down. In Pearl City's humid climate, leaving carpet damp for hours invites the exact mildew problem most homeowners are trying to eliminate.

For commercial properties — office suites near Leeward Community College, retail spaces, and multi-unit buildings — we schedule around business hours and use low-moisture encapsulation methods where full dry-in-place speed is the priority.
