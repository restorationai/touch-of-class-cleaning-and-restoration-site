## Why Makakilo Properties See Mold Issues

Makakilo's position on the slopes above Kapolei means homes here catch more wind-driven rain than properties closer to the coast. That rain finds its way into soffits, behind stucco cladding, and under roofline overhangs that were not designed with Hawaii's microclimate variability in mind. Many homes in this area were built during the rapid residential expansion of the 1980s and 1990s, when certain construction details, particularly vapor management between interior drywall and exterior sheathing, were not held to the standards that came later. Drywall paper facing is one of the fastest substrates for mold growth when moisture is present, and in a home that has sat with a slow leak through a single wet season, the damage behind the wall can be far more extensive than the surface staining suggests.

The relative humidity inside Makakilo homes tends to climb overnight even without a visible leak, because warm daytime air cools against cooler interior surfaces after sunset. Bathrooms, laundry closets, and any room with inadequate exhaust ventilation are the most common starting points for mold growth in this part of Oahu.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Makakilo

Every remediation job starts with a thorough inspection, not a visual scan. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help locate saturation that has migrated away from the original source, which is common in Makakilo homes where water can travel along roof trusses or inside exterior walls before pooling somewhere unexpected. Once the affected area is mapped, containment goes up: heavy-gauge polyethylene barriers and negative air pressure keep spores from spreading to unaffected rooms during removal.

Affected drywall, insulation, and any porous building material that cannot be cleaned to a safe standard is removed and bagged for disposal. Structural framing that remains is treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution and dried to documented moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We do not close up a wall until readings confirm the substrate is within acceptable range, because sealing damp framing is how mold comes back within months.

Because we operate around the clock, a call at any hour connects you with a live response, not a voicemail. That matters when you have found visible mold growth and want to stop the spread before it reaches adjacent rooms.

## Local Note

Homes built on Makakilo's upper streets often have steeper lot grades, which means crawl spaces and sub-slab areas can collect groundwater that drains off the hillside during heavy rain. That moisture does not always announce itself with a puddle; it can wick into concrete block foundations and show up months later as mold on the interior face of a lower-level wall. If you are seeing mold at or near floor level on an exterior-facing wall, the source may be outside the house entirely, and addressing the mold without addressing the drainage pattern will result in recurrence. We flag that possibility during inspection so you have the full picture before deciding on a repair scope.
