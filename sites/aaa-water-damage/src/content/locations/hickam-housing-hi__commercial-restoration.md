## Why Commercial Properties in Hickam Housing Face Elevated Risk

The built environment around Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam reflects decades of layered construction. Older concrete masonry unit buildings common to this part of Oahu absorb and hold moisture in ways that framed drywall construction does not. Once water infiltrates a CMU wall cavity, it can migrate laterally across an entire floor plate before surfacing at a baseboard or ceiling tile. Salt air accelerates corrosion on HVAC coils, sprinkler heads, and electrical conduit, meaning a single water intrusion event often reveals pre-existing degradation that adjusters and property managers need to document carefully.

Hickam Housing's proximity to Pearl Harbor and the coastline also means that storm surge and wind-driven rain are not theoretical risks. A strong Kona storm tracking from the south can push water under commercial entry doors and through aging window seals in ways that a standard mainland weather event would not. Businesses that have never flooded before can find themselves with standing water on a Tuesday morning after a weather system that barely made the local news.

## Our Commercial Restoration Process, Calibrated to Island Conditions

Commercial restoration on Oahu is not simply a scaled-up version of residential work. The stakes are higher: every hour a retail space or office is closed represents lost revenue, disrupted employees, and potential lease complications. The process starts with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected area, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to locate water that has traveled beyond the visible damage boundary. On island, that boundary is almost always wider than it first appears.

From there, the workflow follows a structured sequence:

- **Extraction and containment**: Standing water is removed and affected zones are isolated to prevent cross-contamination, particularly important in food-service or medical-adjacent commercial spaces.
- **Structural drying**: Commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers are deployed at calculated density. In Hickam Housing's ambient humidity, standard residential dehumidifiers are undersized for the job.
- **Antimicrobial treatment**: Hawaii's warm baseline temperatures mean microbial growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. Treatment is applied to affected surfaces before drying is complete, not after.
- **Documentation for insurance**: Every affected material is photographed, measured, and logged in a format carriers can process without back-and-forth.
- **Reconstruction coordination**: Once materials are dry and cleared, rebuild work is scoped and scheduled to return the space to pre-loss condition.

Fire and smoke losses follow a parallel structure, with additional attention to odor neutralization. Smoke residue in a commercial kitchen or office HVAC system can make an entire floor uninhabitable even after visible soot is cleaned. Oahu's trade winds help with ventilation, but they also carry residue further into a building than crews expect.

## Local Note

Buildings in the Hickam Housing area that date to mid-century military construction frequently have terrazzo floors and concrete slab foundations with minimal vapor barrier by modern standards. When water infiltrates from above, it pools at the slab interface and wicks into partition walls from below, which is the opposite of what crews expect when they read a standard moisture map. Checking slab moisture with a pin meter at the base of every partition wall, not just at the obvious wet zone, is a step that saves significant rework on these properties.
