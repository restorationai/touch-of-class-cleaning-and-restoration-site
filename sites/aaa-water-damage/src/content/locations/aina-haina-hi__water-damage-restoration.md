## Why Aina Haina Properties See Water Damage Differently

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Aina Haina

Every job starts with a moisture assessment before a single piece of equipment is placed. In single-wall construction, that means using thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled inside the wall assembly — not just where it's visible on the surface. This step prevents the most common mistake in older Hawaii homes: drying the face of a wall while leaving saturated framing behind.

Once the moisture map is complete, we set up extraction first — truck-mounted or portable units depending on access — pulling standing water from flooring, subfloor cavities, and crawl spaces before structural drying begins. Drying in Aina Haina's valley microclimate requires attention to ambient humidity levels, which stay elevated even on clear days due to the valley's natural airflow patterns.
