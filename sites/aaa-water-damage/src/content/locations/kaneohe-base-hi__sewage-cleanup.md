## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Kaneohe Base

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated type recognized in the restoration industry. The cleanup process here is more involved than a standard water loss, and cutting corners creates health risks that linger long after the visible mess is gone.

The process begins with containment. We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean zones of the home or building. Standing sewage and saturated materials are extracted using truck-mounted and portable units capable of handling solid-laden wastewater. Any porous material that absorbed Category 3 contamination, including drywall, carpet, and insulation, is removed and bagged for proper disposal rather than dried in place.

Once the space is cleared, we apply EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces, including subfloor sheathing, concrete, and wall cavities. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers sized for the space. We monitor moisture readings throughout the drying cycle to confirm that framing and concrete have returned to acceptable levels before the area is closed up.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup requires a different equipment profile than a clean water loss. We use truck-mounted extraction units for high-volume removal, followed by portable extractors for tight spaces and subfloor cavities. HEPA-filtered negative air machines run during the removal phase to capture airborne particulates and odor compounds before they migrate to unaffected areas.

For disinfection, we use hospital-grade, EPA-registered antimicrobials applied by electrostatic sprayer where surface geometry makes standard application difficult. Thermal imaging cameras help us confirm that contamination hasn't wicked into wall cavities or under flooring beyond the visible damage boundary.
