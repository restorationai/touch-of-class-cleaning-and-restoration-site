## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Ewa Beach

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source — whether that means helping locate the main shutoff or coordinating with the homeowner to isolate the affected line. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Inspection and moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls and under flooring. In slab-on-grade homes, water travels laterally under vinyl plank and tile before it becomes visible — mapping tells us how far the loss has actually spread, not just how far it looks like it has.

**Water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard surfaces and saturated carpet. In Ewa Beach's newer homes, engineered hardwood is common — we assess whether it can be dried in place or needs to come up, which depends on how long water has been sitting and how much has migrated beneath it.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the room volume and measured humidity. Coastal Oahu's ambient humidity means a residential dehumidifier or open windows won't get the job done — professional equipment is what drives moisture readings down to pre-loss levels.

**Monitoring and documentation.** We check readings daily and log them. This documentation matters when you're filing a claim with your insurer — photograph the damage yourself before we begin, and keep any notes about when you first noticed the problem.
