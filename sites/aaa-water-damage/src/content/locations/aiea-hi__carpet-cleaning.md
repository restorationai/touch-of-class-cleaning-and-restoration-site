## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Aiea

- **Hot-water extraction** — Truck-mounted extraction equipment runs at temperatures and suction levels that portable units can't match. This step removes the pre-treatment solution along with the suspended soil, leaving significantly less residue in the fiber.
