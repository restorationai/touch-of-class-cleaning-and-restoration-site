## Why Kaneohe Base Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

The windward side of Oahu receives significantly more rainfall than Honolulu, and Kaneohe Base sits squarely in that wetter climate zone. Structures here deal with persistent moisture intrusion from the outside even before a plumbing failure adds to the load. Many of the residential and administrative buildings on and around the base were constructed during mid-century military expansion, using plumbing materials and installation methods that were standard at the time but are now well past their service life. Galvanized steel pipes corrode from the inside out, narrowing over decades until pressure spikes, a temperature drop, or simple age causes a rupture. Even copper lines soldered with older flux compounds can develop pinhole leaks that widen quickly under the water pressure common in Hawaii municipal supply systems. When a line does burst, the combination of high ambient humidity and warm temperatures means mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours if standing water and wet materials are not addressed promptly.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Kaneohe Base

The first step when we arrive is locating and confirming the source of the break, then coordinating with the property owner or base facilities personnel to shut off the affected supply line. From there, the work moves in a deliberate sequence: extract standing water, identify all wet materials using thermal imaging and moisture meters, establish a drying plan calibrated to the actual conditions in the space, and then monitor daily until readings return to baseline.

In concrete block or poured-slab construction, which is common throughout the Kaneohe area, water migrates differently than it does in wood-frame homes. It can travel along the slab and wick up into partition walls far from the visible break point. We map that migration before any materials are removed, so the scope of work reflects what is actually wet rather than what is visible at the surface. Structural drying in this climate requires equipment sized for the humidity load, not just the square footage of the affected room.

Concrete block construction, which is prevalent throughout Kaneohe and the surrounding windward communities, holds moisture in its cores long after the surface reads dry on a standard moisture meter.
