## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Kahala

Every flood job in Kahala starts with moisture mapping before a single piece of furniture moves. Extraction comes next. For slab-on-grade homes common throughout Kahala, we use truck-mounted extraction combined with desiccant dehumidification to pull moisture out of concrete subfloors efficiently.
