## Why Pearl City Properties See Sewage Backup More Often

The hillside terracing that gives Pacific Palisades and Momilani their views also creates drainage pressure that flat neighborhoods never face. Storm runoff races downslope through yards, overwhelms older clay-tile lateral lines, and forces wastewater backward into the lowest fixtures in the house — typically a basement bathroom, a utility sink, or a floor drain in a lower-level ohana unit. On the flat lots closer to Pearl Harbor, the problem is the opposite: slow-draining soil that holds groundwater high, leaving little margin before a blocked main line turns into a sewage event.

Galvanized steel drain pipes from that era corrode from the inside out. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Pearl City

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 — the most contaminated water type — which means the cleanup protocol is more involved than a clean-water pipe burst. Here is what the process looks like on a Pearl City job:

**1. Containment and safety assessment.** We establish work zones immediately to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. In homes with ohana units, this step protects the occupied portion of the structure while we work.

**2. Extraction.** Truck-mounted extraction equipment pulls standing sewage from flooring, carpet, and subfloor cavities. Porous materials — carpet padding, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed and bagged for disposal per Hawaii Department of Health guidelines for biohazardous waste.

**3. Antimicrobial treatment.** Every affected surface is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We pay particular attention to concrete slabs and tile grout, which are porous enough to harbor bacteria even after visible sewage is removed.

**4. Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in framing and subfloor return to baseline. In Pearl City's humid trade-wind climate, ambient humidity can slow drying if equipment is undersized — we calibrate the drying system to the actual conditions in the structure, not a generic formula.

**5. Clearance verification.** Before equipment comes out, we verify moisture readings across all affected materials. Documentation is provided for your records and for your insurer.
