## Our Mold Remediation Process in Kaimuki

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the visible surface, but the cavities behind walls, under flooring, and inside HVAC chases where mold often hides in older homes. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the extent of saturation before any physical work begins, because in single-wall construction the affected area is almost always larger than it looks.

Containment comes next. Negative air pressure barriers isolate the work zone so that spores disturbed during removal don't migrate into the rest of the home or an adjacent ohana unit. We use HEPA-filtered air scrubbers throughout the process. Affected materials — whether that's original wood sheathing, plaster, or modern drywall added in a renovation — are removed carefully and bagged for disposal per Hawaii Department of Health guidelines. After physical removal, antimicrobial treatment is applied to structural surfaces, and we run drying equipment until moisture readings confirm the area is within acceptable range before any reconstruction begins.
