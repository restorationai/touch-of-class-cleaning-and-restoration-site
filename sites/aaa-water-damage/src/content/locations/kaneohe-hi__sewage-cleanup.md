## Why Kaneohe Properties See Sewage Backup More Often Than You'd Expect

The 96744 ZIP code sits in one of the wettest residential corridors on the island. That rainfall is beautiful from the lanai, but it creates persistent hydraulic pressure on underground infrastructure. Neighborhoods like Kahaluu and Ahuimanu were developed along stream corridors in the 1960s and 1970s, and many of those homes still carry their original cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals — materials that crack, collapse, and root-intrude after decades of wet-soil movement. During flash-flood events, the same streams that make those lots scenic can surcharge the municipal sewer main, sending backpressure through every connected lateral on the street.

Septic systems on larger Haiku Village and Pikoiloa lots face a different version of the same problem: drain fields that were sized for drier mainland soil conditions struggle to percolate when the water table rises after a multi-day rain event. When the field is saturated, effluent has only one direction to travel — back toward the house.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Kaneohe

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the cleanup protocol reflects that. We don't treat it like an ordinary water loss.

**Containment first.** Before extraction begins, we establish a containment perimeter to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms. In Kaneohe's older ranch-style homes, open floor plans and louvered interior doors mean airborne pathogens can migrate quickly — containment stops that spread.

**Extraction and material removal.** Standing black water is extracted using truck-mounted units. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that have contacted raw sewage are removed. There is no safe way to dry and restore Category 3-saturated porous material; removal is the standard, not the exception.

**Disinfection and sanitization.** Structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. We pay particular attention to subfloor and crawl space areas, which in Kaneohe's chronically damp climate can harbor mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of a sewage event if not properly treated.

**Drying and clearance.** Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in walls and structural wood return to baseline. Given Kaneohe's ambient humidity, baseline here is not the same as a dry-climate baseline — we calibrate target readings to local conditions rather than applying a mainland standard.
