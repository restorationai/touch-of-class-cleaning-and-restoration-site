## Our Mold Remediation Process in Kaneohe

Every remediation starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping — not a quick walk-through, but a systematic check of wall cavities, subfloor assemblies, attic decking, and any crawl space accessible under the structure. In Kaneohe's older hillside homes, that crawl space inspection is often where the real story is: ground moisture wicking up through inadequate vapor barriers, combined with poor cross-ventilation, creates conditions where mold can cover floor joists and insulation batts without ever being visible from inside the living space.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish containment using negative air pressure and physical barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Affected porous materials — drywall sections, insulation, damaged wood — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Structural surfaces are then treated and dried to verified moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We use HEPA filtration throughout the process. In Kaneohe's climate, skipping the post-drying verification step is how mold comes back within a season; we don't close out a job until moisture readings confirm the environment won't support regrowth.
