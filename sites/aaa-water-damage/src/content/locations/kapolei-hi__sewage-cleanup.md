## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Kapolei

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 contaminated water, meaning it carries pathogens that require more than extraction and drying. The process here is methodical:

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean spaces — especially important in open-plan slab homes where a flooded bathroom sits just off a main living area with no physical barrier.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units. Saturated materials — flooring, baseboards, drywall — that cannot be adequately sanitized are removed and bagged for disposal per Oahu's solid waste guidelines.

**Sanitization.** We apply EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces, including subfloor concrete, wall framing, and any HVAC registers that may have drawn contaminated air. In Kapolei's heat, this step can't be skipped or shortened — warm, humid conditions accelerate bacterial growth on any surface that isn't fully treated.

**Drying and documentation.** Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. We document readings throughout so you have a clear record for your insurance adjuster.
