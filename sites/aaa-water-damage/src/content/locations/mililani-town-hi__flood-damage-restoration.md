## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Mililani Town

The first step on any flood call is a thorough moisture survey using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not a visual inspection alone. Water behind concrete-block walls or beneath vinyl plank flooring reads dry at the surface while the substrate underneath stays saturated. Once the full scope is mapped, extraction equipment removes standing water and then high-capacity drying equipment is positioned to address the specific materials affected.

In Mililani Town's slab-on-grade homes, drying strategy often focuses on the floor assembly. Concrete slabs absorb and hold moisture, and the flooring installed over them, whether tile, laminate, or carpet, can trap that moisture against the slab. Drying mats placed directly on the concrete surface accelerate evaporation from below, while dehumidifiers handle the vapor load in the air above. Progress is tracked with daily moisture readings so drying is completed to measurable standards, not estimated by feel.
