## Why East Honolulu Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

East Honolulu's topography plays a direct role in drainage and sewer stress. The neighborhoods that climb into the Ko'olau foothills sit on volcanic soil that drains quickly in some areas and becomes saturated in others, particularly during the heavy Kona storm systems that push moisture from the south. When those storms hit, municipal sewer lines serving the older residential corridors closer to the coast can receive more inflow than they were designed to handle, pushing wastewater back up through the lowest fixtures in a home.

Properties that rely on private cesspools or septic systems face a separate but related problem. Hawaii has been phasing out cesspools statewide, and many East Honolulu properties are in various stages of that transition. An aging cesspool that is nearing the end of its service life, or a septic tank whose drain field has been compromised by the island's clay-heavy subsoil layers, can overflow with very little warning, especially after a period of sustained rainfall.

Homes built in the post-statehood construction boom of the 1960s and 1970s are common throughout this part of Honolulu, and the original cast-iron or Orangeburg drain lines in those structures have had decades to corrode, collapse, or shift on the uneven volcanic substrate beneath the slab.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in East Honolulu

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated category recognized by the restoration industry, and the cleanup process reflects that. When we arrive, the first priority is containment: isolating the affected area so that foot traffic does not carry contaminated material into clean spaces.

From there, the process moves through several distinct phases:

- **Extraction and removal**: Standing sewage and saturated porous materials are removed. In East Honolulu homes with tile and concrete slab construction, that often means removing grout lines and testing whether the slab itself has absorbed contamination.
- **Antimicrobial treatment**: Hospital-grade disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces, including subfloor materials, wall cavities, and any structural elements that contacted the waste.
- **Drying and dehumidification**: Given the baseline humidity levels in East Honolulu, industrial dehumidifiers run longer here than they would in a drier climate. We monitor moisture readings until materials reach acceptable levels, not just until surfaces feel dry to the touch.
- **Air quality management**: HEPA filtration units run throughout the job to capture airborne particulates and odor compounds that become more volatile in warm conditions.
- **Final verification**: Before we close out, affected surfaces are tested to confirm contamination levels have been reduced to safe thresholds.

Sewage backup coverage is not automatically included in a standard homeowners policy.
