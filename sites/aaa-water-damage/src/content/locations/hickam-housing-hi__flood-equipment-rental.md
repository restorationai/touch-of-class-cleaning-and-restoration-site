## Why Hickam Housing Properties Face Unique Drying Challenges

The housing stock throughout Hickam was built largely in the mid-twentieth century to military construction standards that prioritized durability over vapor management. Concrete block walls and slab-on-grade foundations are common, and both materials behave differently than wood-frame construction when saturated. Concrete is slow to release absorbed moisture, which means a surface can feel dry to the touch while the slab beneath it still holds significant water content. Standard box fans from a hardware store cannot generate the directed airflow or the sustained low-grain humidity conditions needed to pull moisture out of dense masonry.

Oahu's climate compounds this. The island's trade winds bring consistent moisture from the northeast, and the south shore around Pearl Harbor can experience heavy localized rain events that overwhelm older drainage infrastructure. When standing water collects against a foundation or backs up through a floor drain, the window before secondary damage, including the kind of microbial growth that thrives in warm, humid environments, is shorter here than it would be on the mainland. Having the right equipment on-site within hours, not days, is what separates a contained drying project from a much larger remediation job.
