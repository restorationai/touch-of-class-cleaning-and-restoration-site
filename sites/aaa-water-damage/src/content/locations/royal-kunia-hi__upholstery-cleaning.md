## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Royal Kunia

Every job starts with a fiber identification check. Fabric codes on manufacturer tags (W, S, WS, or X) determine which cleaning agents are safe, and skipping that step on a dry-clean-only piece can shrink or distort the cover permanently. For fabric upholstery, we pre-treat high-contact zones like armrests and headrests with a low-moisture conditioning solution, then use hot-water extraction calibrated to the fabric weight. In Royal Kunia's humidity, we run airmovers and dehumidifiers during and after cleaning to pull moisture out before it can reset into the foam, targeting dry times that keep mold from getting a foothold.

Leather upholstery gets a separate protocol: pH-balanced cleaner applied by hand, followed by a conditioner that replaces the oils the humidity and UV exposure strip away. We avoid steam on leather because the heat opens the grain and can cause uneven darkening, particularly on lighter hides common in newer furniture.

For heavily soiled pieces, a pre-inspection lets us flag any structural issues, like broken springs or compromised stitching, that cleaning pressure could worsen. We flag those to you before we start, not after.
