## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Pearl City

A mold assessment here isn't a generic walkthrough. We calibrate the inspection to the specific construction patterns and moisture pathways common in Pearl City's housing stock.

**Visual inspection and moisture mapping** come first. Using calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging, we trace moisture gradients through walls, floors, and ceilings — paying close attention to lower-level spaces, plumbing chases, and any area where hillside runoff could be infiltrating. Older galvanized lines often corrode at fittings inside wall cavities, so we focus on those transition points.

**Air sampling** captures what's suspended in the indoor environment — spore types, concentrations, and how they compare to an outdoor baseline sample taken at the same time. This comparison is critical: Hawaii's outdoor air naturally carries higher ambient mold spore counts than most mainland climates, so a Pearl City indoor sample has to be read against a local outdoor reference, not a national average.

**Surface and bulk sampling** (tape lifts, swabs, or material samples) identify specific mold species on suspect surfaces. This matters for remediation planning: some species respond to standard HEPA and antimicrobial protocols; others require more aggressive containment.

All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory. You receive a written report with lab results, moisture readings, and a clear summary of findings — documentation that's useful for your own records, for your insurer, and for any contractor you hire to do remediation work.
