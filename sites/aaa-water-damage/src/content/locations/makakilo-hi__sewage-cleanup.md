Makakilo sits at a higher elevation than much of Oahu, but that altitude does not insulate its homes from one of the most urgent plumbing emergencies a property owner can face. When a sewer line backs up or a septic system overflows here, the combination of the island's clay-heavy soils, older lateral lines serving the community's hillside developments, and the year-round humidity means contaminated water spreads fast and bacteria multiply faster. AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care responds 24/7 to sewage backup and raw sewage removal calls throughout Makakilo, bringing the equipment and sanitization protocols the situation demands.

## Why Makakilo Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Makakilo's development history plays a direct role in its sewer vulnerability. Much of the community was built out in phases from the 1960s through the 1990s, which means a significant share of homes have lateral sewer lines that are now approaching or past the 40-to-50-year mark. Cast iron and Orangeburg-era piping corrodes, collapses, and roots infiltrate it from the ironwood and monkeypod trees common in the area's landscaping. When a lateral fails, raw sewage has nowhere to go but back through floor drains, toilets, and shower pans.

The soil composition on Makakilo's slopes compounds the problem. The area's volcanic clay soils drain slowly, so when a septic system experiences overflow or a sewer cleanout backs up during heavy Kona storm rainfall, the saturated ground has little capacity to absorb runoff. That keeps contaminated water sitting against foundations and pooling in crawl spaces longer than it would on more permeable terrain.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Makakilo

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated type, and the cleanup sequence reflects that. The first priority on arrival is containment: limiting cross-contamination to unaffected rooms before any extraction begins. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage, including drywall, carpet, and insulation, are removed and bagged for disposal according to Hawaii Department of Health guidelines for biohazardous waste. Nothing is dried in place if it cannot be properly sanitized.

Once affected materials are out, the structural surfaces that remain get a two-stage treatment: an EPA-registered antimicrobial disinfectant applied to all contacted surfaces, followed by a second application after the area has been mechanically dried to below acceptable moisture thresholds. In Makakilo's climate, where ambient humidity rarely drops below 60 percent even on dry days, achieving those thresholds requires commercial dehumidification, not just airflow. We use desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the space size and monitor readings daily until the structure is clear.

Many Makakilo properties, particularly in the planned residential sections of the community, fall under homeowners association oversight that can affect how and where work is staged and what documentation the association requires before you close out a claim. ## Local Note

Makakilo's hillside grading means that when a sewer lateral fails near the street connection, gravity can push effluent back toward the home rather than away from it, the opposite of what homeowners expect. Properties on the upper streets of the community are particularly susceptible to this dynamic because the municipal main runs below them at a lower grade. If your cleanout cap is at the low point of your yard and you see sewage surfacing there during a backup, that is a strong indicator the blockage is between your home and the main, not inside the house. Knowing that before we arrive helps us bring the right jetting equipment and coordinate with the county utility if the blockage turns out to be in the public main rather than your private lateral.
