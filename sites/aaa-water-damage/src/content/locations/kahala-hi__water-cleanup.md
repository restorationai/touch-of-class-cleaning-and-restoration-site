## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Kahala

When a call comes in from near Waialae Country Club or down toward Waialae Beach Park, the first priority on arrival is stopping ongoing water intrusion, whether that means shutting a main valve, capping a line, or coordinating with a plumber already on site. From there the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Moisture mapping**: Thermal imaging and non-invasive meters establish the full boundary of saturation before a single piece of flooring is touched. In slab construction, readings are taken at multiple heights to catch lateral migration.
- **Standing water extraction**: Truck-mounted extraction equipment pulls free water from hard surfaces and carpet simultaneously. Fine wool rugs and upholstered pieces are identified and protected or moved before equipment is staged.
- **Structural drying**: Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not a standard room layout. Drying logs are recorded at each visit so the documentation trail is complete for insurance purposes.
- **Content protection**: In homes with art, custom cabinetry, or high-end furnishings, plastic barriers and furniture tabs are standard, not an afterthought. Discretion matters in this neighborhood.
