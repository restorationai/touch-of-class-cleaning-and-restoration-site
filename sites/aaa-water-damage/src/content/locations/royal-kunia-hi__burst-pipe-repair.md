## Why Royal Kunia Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

Royal Kunia was developed largely as a planned residential community, which means many homes share similar construction vintages and plumbing configurations. Copper supply lines installed during the original build-out can develop pinhole leaks or joint failures as they age, particularly where the lines pass through concrete slabs or transition between materials. Oahu's water, while treated, carries mineral content that contributes to scale buildup inside older copper runs, narrowing the interior diameter and raising line pressure over time.

The community's position on the Ewa Plain also matters. The soil profile in this part of central Oahu tends toward compacted red laterite, which drains slowly. When a slab leak or exterior line break saturates the ground around a foundation, that moisture has fewer places to go, and it finds its way back toward the structure. ## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Royal Kunia

The first priority on any pipe burst call is stopping the water. If you have not already located your main shutoff, our crew can assist the moment they arrive. From there, the process moves through several concrete stages.

**Damage mapping.** Moisture does not stay where you can see it. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls, under flooring, and into ceiling cavities. In slab-on-grade homes common to Royal Kunia, we pay close attention to the floor-to-wall junction, where water traveling under a slab tends to surface.

**Water extraction.** Standing water comes out first with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment. Saturated carpet padding, if present, is typically removed at this stage because it holds water against the subfloor and extends drying time significantly.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the room volume and the materials involved. In Royal Kunia's humidity levels, dehumidification load is real: the outdoor air pulling through open windows or doors can reintroduce moisture faster than equipment can remove it, so we keep the structure closed during active drying cycles.

**Repair coordination.** Once materials are dry and documented, we coordinate the physical pipe repair and any necessary drywall, flooring, or cabinetry work so you are not managing multiple contractors.
