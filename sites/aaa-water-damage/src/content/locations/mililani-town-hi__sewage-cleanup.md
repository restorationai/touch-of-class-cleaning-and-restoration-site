## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Mililani Town

Raw sewage removal is not a job for a wet-vac and a bottle of bleach. Our crew arrives with personal protective equipment, industrial extraction equipment, and EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination. The process follows a structured sequence:

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. In Mililani Town's single-family homes, this often means sealing hallways between the laundry room or guest bath and the main living area.

**Bulk removal.** Standing sewage, saturated flooring materials, and any porous contents that cannot be safely restored are removed and bagged for proper disposal in accordance with Hawaii Department of Health guidelines for biohazardous waste.

**Surface extraction and cleaning.** Hard surfaces, subfloor decking, and wall cavities are extracted, scrubbed, and treated with disinfectant. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to confirm that contamination has not wicked into wall cavities or under tile.

**Drying and dehumidification.** Hawaii's ambient humidity, even at Mililani Town's inland elevation, means that air movers alone are not sufficient. We deploy commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to bring structural materials to acceptable moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

**Final sanitization.** A second round of EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment is applied to all affected surfaces before the space is cleared for re-occupancy or rebuild.
