## Why Royal Kunia Properties See Water Damage Issues

Royal Kunia was developed largely in the late 1980s and 1990s as a master-planned residential community, which means the majority of homes here share a similar construction profile: slab-on-grade foundations, stucco exteriors, and tract-home plumbing that is now approaching or past the 30-year mark. Polybutylene supply lines and early CPVC fittings from that era have a documented history of becoming brittle and failing without warning. When a supply line lets go inside a wall cavity or under a kitchen cabinet, water tracks along the slab and spreads far from the point of origin before it surfaces.

The community's position on the Ewa Plain also means soils here drain more slowly than volcanic soils closer to the mountains. During sustained rain events, groundwater can wick upward through slab cracks, leaving moisture readings elevated across a broad floor area even after the rain stops. Standard visual inspection misses this entirely; moisture mapping with calibrated meters is the only way to define the true boundary of the affected zone.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Royal Kunia

Every job starts with a thorough inspection using thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall assemblies and under tile or laminate flooring. That map drives every decision that follows.

Once the scope is clear, extraction comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water and trapped moisture from carpet padding, subfloor material, and low-lying cavities. In Royal Kunia's slab-on-grade homes, this step often reveals that water has migrated under tile grout lines and into the thin-set layer beneath, which stays wet long after the surface feels dry to the touch.

Structural drying follows, using commercial-grade air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage. Central Oahu's ambient humidity, even on a clear day, means residential dehumidifiers sold at hardware stores cannot maintain the vapor pressure differential needed to pull moisture out of concrete and stucco efficiently. We monitor drying progress with daily readings and adjust equipment placement until materials reach documented dry standards.

Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated drywall or swollen baseboards, are removed and disposed of properly before reconstruction begins. We photograph and document every step, which matters when an insurance claim is involved.
