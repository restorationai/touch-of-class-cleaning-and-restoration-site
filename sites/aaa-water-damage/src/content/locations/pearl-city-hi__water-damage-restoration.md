## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Pearl City

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. In Pearl City's older homes, water routinely migrates further than expected because 1960s construction used fewer vapor barriers and more open wall cavities than modern builds.

Once the scope is clear, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then set industrial drying equipment — high-velocity air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers — positioned specifically for the room geometry and material types involved. Concrete slab floors common in lower-level Pearl City rooms require mat drying systems rather than standard air movement alone; we carry that equipment on every truck.
