## Why Waiahole-Waikane Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Most properties along Kamehameha Highway in this corridor rely on private septic systems rather than a municipal sewer connection. Septic tanks and leach fields that were sized for smaller households decades ago are now under heavier daily loads, and the dense, clay-rich soils common throughout the Ko'olau foothills drain slowly even in dry weather. After a sustained rain event, those soils become fully saturated, and a leach field that was marginal on a dry Tuesday can fail entirely by Wednesday morning, pushing raw effluent back through floor drains, toilets, and laundry connections.

The region's trade-wind rainfall patterns compound the problem. The windward Ko'olau range wrings extraordinary moisture out of passing clouds, and Waiahole Valley in particular can receive several inches of rain in a matter of hours during a kona storm or a frontal passage. That volume of water overwhelms both septic absorption fields and older cast-iron or clay sewer laterals that have shifted or cracked over years of soil movement on the valley slopes.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Waiahole-Waikane

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated class of water loss, and the cleanup protocol reflects that. The process begins with containment: affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or outdoor soil that could reach garden beds or water features.

All standing sewage and saturated porous materials are removed first. In Waiahole-Waikane homes, that often includes wood subfloor sections and wall cavities at grade level, since many structures here are pier-and-beam or slab-on-grade construction built close to the valley floor. Once the bulk removal is complete, affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants formulated to address the bacteria, viruses, and parasites present in raw sewage. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated for Hawaii's ambient humidity levels, which are meaningfully higher than mainland baseline conditions. Clearance testing confirms contamination levels are within safe thresholds before any reconstruction begins.
