## Why Mililani Mauka Properties See Water Damage Issues

Homes here are typically built on concrete slab foundations, which limits below-slab seepage but creates a different problem: water that enters through a door threshold, a cracked slab joint, or a failed sliding-glass-door seal has nowhere to drain and spreads laterally across the entire floor plane.
