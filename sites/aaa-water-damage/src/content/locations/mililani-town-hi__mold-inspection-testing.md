## Why Mililani Town Properties See Elevated Mold Risk

Mililani Town's location on the Leilehua Plateau means it receives more rainfall and cloud cover than coastal Honolulu neighborhoods. Afternoon moisture regularly condenses on cooler interior surfaces, particularly in homes with original single-pane jalousie windows, which allow humid outside air to move freely through the louvers. Homes built during the earliest development phases of Mililani Town also tend to have slab-on-grade foundations with minimal vapor barriers beneath the flooring, which means ground moisture can wick upward into flooring adhesives and subflooring material without any visible leak ever occurring. Mold that establishes itself in those layers can go undetected for months because the surface flooring looks and feels dry.

Added to that, Oahu's termite treatment history means many homes have had structural lumber replaced or supplemented over the decades, and splice joints and patched framing are spots where moisture tends to collect and linger. A visual inspection alone will not reveal what is happening inside those assemblies.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process

A mold assessment here starts with a thorough visual survey of every area where moisture has a pathway: roof penetrations, plumbing chases, HVAC air handlers, and the perimeter of any room that shares a wall with the exterior. Moisture readings are taken at multiple depths using calibrated meters so that a wall reading dry at the surface can still be flagged if the reading spikes at the stud cavity.

From there, air sampling captures what is actually circulating through the living space. Spore trap cassettes are placed in rooms of concern and at a baseline outdoor location so the lab can compare indoor spore counts and species composition against what is naturally present outside. In Mililani Town, outdoor baseline counts often include Cladosporium and Basidiospores at elevated levels due to the surrounding vegetation, so a proper outdoor control sample is essential to interpreting the indoor results accurately rather than misreading normal tropical background counts as an indoor problem.

Surface sampling, either tape lift or swab, is added when there is visible growth that needs species identification. All samples are submitted to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results typically return within a few business days. The written report identifies affected areas, spore types, and relative concentration levels so that any remediation contractor or your insurance carrier has a documented baseline.
