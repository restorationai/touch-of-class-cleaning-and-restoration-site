## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Mililani Mauka

The process begins with a full moisture mapping of the affected area. Using thermal cameras and non-invasive moisture meters, the crew establishes exactly where water has traveled, including inside walls, under flooring, and along slab edges. This step matters more in slab construction than almost anywhere else because it determines whether drying can happen in place or whether flooring and baseboard need to come up to allow airflow to reach the slab surface.

Once the extent of the loss is documented, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Structural drying follows immediately: commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers are placed based on the room volume and the moisture load, and air movers are positioned to drive evaporation from wall cavities and flooring substrates. Readings are logged daily so the drying curve is documented for your insurance file.

Material removal, when necessary, is limited to what the moisture data actually supports. The goal is to dry in place wherever possible, removing only saturated material that cannot realistically be dried within the window that prevents microbial growth.
