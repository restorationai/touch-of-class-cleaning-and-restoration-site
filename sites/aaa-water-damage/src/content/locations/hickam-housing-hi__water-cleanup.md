## Why Water Damage Moves Differently in Hickam Housing

The housing stock here reflects decades of military construction, with many units built on post-tension concrete slabs that sit close to grade. Unlike mainland homes with crawl spaces that allow airflow underneath, slab-on-grade construction in this area traps moisture with nowhere to escape. Water that seeps under flooring, whether from a plumbing failure or storm intrusion, can travel laterally across a slab for several feet before it ever shows up as a wet spot on the surface.

O'ahu's climate compounds the problem. Relative humidity in this part of the island regularly runs above 70%, which means wet building materials don't dry on their own the way they might in a drier climate. Without active drying equipment, a water event that looks minor on day one can produce musty odors and material degradation within 48 to 72 hours. That window is shorter here than in most mainland markets.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Hickam Housing

The first step on any call is stopping the source of water if it hasn't been stopped already. From there, the process follows a specific sequence calibrated to this environment:

**Extraction first.** Standing water is removed using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. On slab-on-grade floors, this step is critical because water under vinyl plank or tile can spread well beyond the visible wet area.

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters identify exactly where water has traveled behind baseboards, under cabinets, and into wall cavities. In the older construction common to this community, wall assemblies sometimes include materials that absorb and hold moisture longer than modern drywall.

**Drying equipment placement.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. In Hickam Housing's humid baseline conditions, dehumidifier sizing matters more than in drier climates, undersized equipment simply can't overcome the ambient moisture load.

**Daily monitoring.** Moisture readings are logged each day until structural materials reach acceptable dry standards. The drying timeline here typically runs longer than the national average because the surrounding air is already carrying significant moisture.
