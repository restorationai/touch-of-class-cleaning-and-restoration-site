## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Mililani Mauka

Every cleaning starts with a dry inspection pass using a grooming rake and moisture meter. On Oahu, skipping the moisture check is a shortcut that leads to mold growth under the pad, so we establish a baseline before any water touches the carpet.
