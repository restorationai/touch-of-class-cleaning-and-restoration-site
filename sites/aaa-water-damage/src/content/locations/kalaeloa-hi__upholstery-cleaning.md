## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Kalaeloa

Every piece starts with a fiber identification check. Fabric type determines everything: the water temperature, the pH of the cleaning agent, the extraction pressure, and the drying method. Microfiber behaves differently from cotton-linen blends; genuine leather requires a different approach than bonded leather, which can delaminate if it encounters the wrong chemistry. Once we know what we're working with, we pre-treat high-contact zones like armrests and seat cushion fronts, where body oils and sunscreen residue concentrate. Hot-water extraction pulls the emulsified soil out of the fiber rather than pushing it deeper. On leather pieces, we follow extraction with a pH-balanced conditioner to restore suppleness that the salt air has drawn out. Cushion cores are moved to encourage airflow, and we use air movers calibrated for the ambient conditions on the day of the job, because drying times in Kalaeloa's western microclimate differ from what works in a Honolulu high-rise.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Upholstery

Portable low-moisture extraction units are the right tool for most residential upholstery work in Kalaeloa.
