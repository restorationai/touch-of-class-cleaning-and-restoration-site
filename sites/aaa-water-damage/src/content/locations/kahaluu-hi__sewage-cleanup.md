## Why Kahaluu Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

The windward Ko'olau foothills receive some of the highest annual rainfall on Oahu, and Kahaluu's valley floor absorbs a significant share of that runoff. Saturated ground puts constant hydrostatic pressure on underground pipes and septic tanks, which accelerates joint failures and seal degradation. Many homes in this corridor were built during the post-war residential expansion of the 1950s and 1960s, when cast-iron and clay-tile drain lines were standard. Those materials corrode and crack over decades, and root intrusion from the lush tropical vegetation common to windward Oahu makes the problem worse. A single heavy rain event can push a marginal septic system past its capacity, sending effluent back through the lowest fixture in the house.

Homes connected to the county sewer system are not immune. Stormwater infiltration into aging lateral lines during heavy rain can overwhelm the system and cause backflow into residential drains. The result is the same regardless of the source: standing sewage that carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites classified as Category 3 water, the most hazardous contamination category in the restoration industry.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Kahaluu

Sewage cleanup is not a job that benefits from waiting. The longer Category 3 contamination sits, the deeper it wicks into porous materials, and in Kahaluu's humid climate, secondary mold growth can begin on saturated surfaces within 24 to 48 hours.

When our crew arrives, the first step is containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces. We then extract all standing sewage using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment, followed by the removal of unsalvageable porous materials such as saturated drywall, flooring, and insulation. Concrete slabs and tile are cleaned and treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 losses. Structural cavities are dried using commercial air movers and dehumidifiers sized for the humidity levels typical on the windward side, where ambient moisture is consistently higher than the drier leeward areas of the island. Moisture readings are logged throughout the drying period so you have documentation for your insurance carrier.

Sanitization is not a single spray-and-walk-away step. We apply antimicrobial treatments to all affected surfaces, allow appropriate dwell time, and verify results before closing out the affected area.
