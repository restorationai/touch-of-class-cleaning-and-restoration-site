## Why Hickam Housing Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

The housing stock on and around Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam was largely constructed in the post-World War II decades, and much of it has never had a full replumb. Galvanized steel pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing over time until a pressure spike, a water hammer event, or even a routine valve shutoff triggers a catastrophic split. Copper lines fare better but are not immune: salt-laden humidity and the mineral content of Oahu's water supply can pit copper from the outside, especially where pipes pass through concrete or masonry.

Hawaii's climate adds a counterintuitive wrinkle. Because temperatures rarely drop low enough to freeze pipes, residents and property managers sometimes assume cold-weather pipe bursts are not a local concern. The real culprits here are corrosion, age, and pressure fluctuation, not frost. A pipe that fails in January on the mainland fails in Hickam Housing in August for entirely different reasons, and the response has to account for that.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Hickam Housing

The first priority when we arrive is stopping the water at its source. We locate the nearest shutoff, whether that is a unit valve, a building main, or a street-side curb stop, and confirm the flow is contained before any extraction begins. In slab-on-grade construction common throughout this area, water that escapes under the floor can travel surprising distances before surfacing, so we use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the actual extent of saturation rather than relying on what is visible.

Extraction comes next, pulling standing water from hard floors and any carpet that can be saved. Drying equipment, including high-velocity air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated for Hawaii's ambient humidity levels, goes into place immediately. High relative humidity outdoors means standard drying timelines need to be monitored closely; what dries in two days in a drier climate can take longer here if equipment is undersized or doors are left open to outside air. We track moisture readings daily and adjust equipment placement until structural readings return to acceptable levels.

Once the structure is dry, we coordinate the plumbing repair side of the scope, documenting damaged materials for your insurance carrier and providing a clear record of what was removed and why.
