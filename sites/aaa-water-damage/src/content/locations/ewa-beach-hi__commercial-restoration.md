## Local Note

One pattern we've seen repeatedly in Ewa Beach's newer slab-on-grade retail and office buildings: the vapor barrier installed between the concrete slab and the flooring system can actually work against you after a flood. Water that infiltrates from a plumbing failure or storm intrusion gets trapped between the barrier and the subfloor, creating a hidden reservoir that standard surface drying won't reach. In these cases, we use specialty drying mats that draw moisture directly through the concrete rather than relying solely on overhead air movement — a technique that cuts drying time significantly compared to a fans-only approach and prevents the secondary damage that shows up weeks later as buckled flooring or musty odors.
