## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Mililani

The first step on any job is containment, not paperwork. Technicians locate and shut off the water source, then use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map how far saturation has traveled through walls, flooring, and cabinetry. That map drives everything that follows.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water and trapped moisture from carpet padding, hardwood subfloor, and tile grout lines. Once bulk water is removed, high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers are positioned to drive evaporation at a rate the structure can handle without warping finishes or buckling flooring. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented rather than estimated.

Because Mililani's homeowner associations maintain strict standards for finishes and exterior appearances, we document affected materials with photographs before any removal and note product specifications where they're visible, giving you and your HOA a clear record of what was in place before the loss.

## Local Note

One pattern that shows up repeatedly in Mililani Town homes from the 1980s is tile-over-concrete slab construction with no vapor barrier between the slab and the tile adhesive.
