## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Kapolei

Every job starts with a fiber and soiling assessment — what works on a Berber loop in a Mehana townhome is different from the cut-pile carpet in a Ko Olina villa that's absorbed sunscreen, salt air, and poolside traffic. From there, the process follows these steps:

**Pre-inspection and pre-treatment.** We identify stains by type — pet protein, tannin-based (coffee, red dirt), or synthetic — and apply the appropriate pre-spray. Kapolei's laterite soil requires a low-pH pre-treatment to break the mineral bond before agitation.

**Hot-water extraction.** Truck-mounted equipment delivers water at controlled temperature and pressure, then recovers it along with loosened soil. The recovery step is critical in a humid climate — leaving excess moisture behind accelerates mildew growth in padding.

**Spot treatment and rinse.** Residual alkalinity left in fibers attracts re-soiling quickly in a dusty leeward environment. A pH-balanced rinse step keeps carpet cleaner longer between visits.

**Accelerated drying.** High-velocity air movers reduce dry time significantly — important for Ko Olina properties working against a guest-arrival window and for any Kapolei home where open windows invite the next round of dust before the carpet is fully dry.
