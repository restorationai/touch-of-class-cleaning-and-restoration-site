## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Kapolei

The first priority on any pipe break call is stopping the water. If you haven't already, locate your main shutoff — in most Kapolei slab-on-grade homes it's at the meter box near the street, often behind a small panel flush with the landscaping. Once flow stops, document the affected areas with photos before moving anything — useful for your own insurance claim records.

When the crew arrives, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

1. **Moisture mapping** — thermal imaging and pin meters trace where water traveled through walls, under flooring, and into ceiling cavities below the break point. In two-story plans, this step often reveals damage two rooms away from the visible wet spot.
2. **Extraction** — standing water and saturated carpet padding come out first. In Ko Olina villa units with tile-over-concrete floors, we check grout lines and expansion joints where water pools invisibly.
3. **Structural drying** — commercial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just placed near the obvious wet area. Leeward Oahu's outdoor humidity is lower than windward Oahu, which helps drying times, but interior AC systems can work against the dehumidifiers if left running — we'll advise you on settings.
4. **Monitoring** — moisture readings are logged each visit until materials reach dry standard. We don't pull equipment early because the floor feels dry to the touch.
5. **Repair coordination** — once the structure is confirmed dry, we coordinate or refer licensed plumbing repair so the replacement work happens on dry framing, not damp wood.
