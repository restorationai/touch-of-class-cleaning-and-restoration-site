## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Kaimuki

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment. In Kaimuki's older homes, that means going beyond the visible damage — we use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map how far water has traveled through single-wall framing, subfloors, and original hardwood. Once we understand the scope, we move to water extraction. Standing water comes out first with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment, depending on site access. Hillside lots on Wilhelmina Rise sometimes require portable units when a truck can't stage close enough to the affected area.
