## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Mililani Mauka

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated type recognized in the restoration industry, and it requires a different response than a clean-water pipe burst. The process here is not simply extraction and drying.

The first step is containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. In Mililani Mauka's typical single-family homes and townhome configurations, that often means sealing doorways and establishing a decontamination zone before any equipment moves through the house.

Next comes removal of all standing waste and saturated porous materials. Flooring, baseboards, and drywall that have contacted sewage are not salvageable and must come out. Once the space is cleared, the surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 contamination. This is not a surface wipe-down; it is a systematic application that accounts for contact time and material porosity.

After sanitization, the space is dried using commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated for Oahu's ambient humidity levels, which run higher than mainland averages year-round. Final clearance involves odor treatment, because even after visible waste is removed, volatile organic compounds from sewage can persist in wall cavities and subfloor materials.
