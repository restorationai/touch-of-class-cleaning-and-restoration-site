## What a Mold Inspection and Testing Visit Actually Involves

A thorough mold assessment starts before any samples are collected. The inspector walks the property with a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera, mapping areas of elevated moisture that aren't visible on the surface — inside closets, along the base of exterior walls, beneath bathroom fixtures, and around HVAC air handlers. In Mililani's older homes, the inspection often extends to attic spaces, where inadequate soffit ventilation traps humidity and creates ideal conditions for mold on roof sheathing.

Once suspect areas are identified, air samples and surface swab samples are collected and sent to an accredited third-party laboratory. Air sampling captures the concentration and species of mold spores actively circulating in the living space — a critical data point for indoor air quality assessment. Surface samples confirm what's growing on a specific material. The resulting lab report gives you documented, defensible evidence of what's present, at what levels, and whether remediation is warranted. That documentation matters especially in Mililani, where HOA standards and resale expectations mean owners need a clear paper trail.
