## Why Royal Kunia Properties Hold Moisture Longer Than You Might Expect

Much of Royal Kunia's residential stock was built during Oahu's planned-community expansion of the 1990s and early 2000s. Those homes typically feature concrete block or poured-concrete perimeter walls, tile floors over concrete subfloor, and minimal crawl space. Concrete is deceptive after a flood: the surface dries within hours, but moisture migrates laterally through the slab and wicks up into interior framing and drywall before a standard moisture meter catches it at the surface.

Central Oahu's average relative humidity regularly runs above 70 percent, which means a consumer-grade box fan accomplishes almost nothing. Drying to an acceptable moisture content requires equipment calibrated for high-ambient-humidity conditions: commercial-grade refrigerant dehumidifiers that can pull meaningful pints per day even when the room air is already saturated, and high-velocity air movers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern rather than just circulating warm, wet air.

## Our Flood and Drying Equipment Rental Process in Royal Kunia

Every rental starts with a site assessment, not a hand-off at the curb. A technician walks the affected area, reads moisture levels at the wall base, subfloor, and any adjacent cabinetry, and maps the drying zone before a single piece of equipment is placed. That map determines how many air movers are needed, where the dehumidifier discharge should be routed, and whether a flood pump is still necessary to remove pooled water before drying begins.

Equipment is staged to work as a system. Air movers are angled low to drive surface moisture into the air column; the dehumidifier captures that vapor and exhausts condensate through a drain line rather than a collection bucket that would need constant emptying. If flooring is involved, drying mats can be placed directly over wet tile or hardwood to pull moisture through the floor assembly from below without requiring demolition. Daily moisture readings confirm whether the equipment configuration is working or needs adjustment.

At the end of the rental period, a final moisture reading is documented. In Hawaii, insurance carriers and some HOA management companies require written drying logs before a loss file can be closed, and that documentation is part of what you receive.
