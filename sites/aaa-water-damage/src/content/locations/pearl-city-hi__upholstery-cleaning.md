Leather upholstery faces its own challenge here. Salt air accelerates the breakdown of leather conditioners, leaving hides dry and prone to cracking even on furniture that's only a few years old. That red tint can oxidize into the fibers if it isn't extracted promptly — and once it sets, surface cleaning won't lift it.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Pearl City

Every piece starts with a fiber identification check. Knowing whether you have a solution-dyed polyester, a natural linen blend, or a bonded leather determines which cleaning agents and how much moisture are safe to use — getting that wrong can shrink fabric, bleed dye, or delaminate bonded leather seams. We test a hidden panel before touching the main surfaces.

For fabric upholstery, we use hot-water extraction calibrated to the specific fabric weight. In Pearl City's ambient humidity, we keep dwell times shorter than we might in a drier climate and run air movers during the process so cushions aren't sitting damp in a humid room for hours after we leave. Odor treatment — particularly for pet dander and the mildew smell that builds up in homes where windows stay open — goes into the foam core, not just the surface fabric, because surface-only deodorizers off-gas within days and the smell returns.

Leather and faux-leather pieces get a pH-balanced deep clean followed by a conditioner appropriate for Hawaii's climate — one that resists the salt-air degradation that strips standard conditioners faster than the manufacturer's recommended reapplication schedule assumes.
