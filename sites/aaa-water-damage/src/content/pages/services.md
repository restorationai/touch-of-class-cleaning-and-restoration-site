From a burst pipe flooding a Kaimuki kitchen at 2 a.m. to smoke-blackened walls in a Pearl City commercial unit, AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care handles the full arc of property damage — emergency response, structural drying, rebuild, and the specialty work that falls in between. We serve single-family homes, condominiums, multi-family properties, and commercial spaces across Oahu.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, the clock matters. Water intrusion can saturate wall cavities and subflooring within hours; smoke residue begins bonding to surfaces almost immediately after a fire is extinguished; a biohazard situation carries health risks that compound the longer it sits. Our emergency services cover **water damage restoration** (extraction, structural drying, moisture mapping), **fire and smoke damage restoration** (soot removal, odor neutralization, content cleaning), **biohazard and trauma cleanup** (safe removal and disposal handled to regulatory standards), and **storm damage response** (wind-driven rain intrusion, roof leaks, flooding). Because we answer the phone around the clock, mitigation work can start the same day — often the same night — you call.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction brings the property back. Once drying and remediation are complete, our team handles the rebuild: drywall replacement, flooring installation, cabinetry, painting, and finish carpentry. Having one company carry the project from wet insulation to finished room means no handoff gaps, no finger-pointing between a remediation crew and a separate contractor, and a cleaner documentation trail for your insurance claim records. We work on partial-room repairs as well as larger gut-and-rebuild scopes across residential and commercial properties.

## Specialty services

Some damage problems don't fit neatly into an emergency or a rebuild. Our specialty services fill those gaps:

- **Mold inspection and remediation** — Oahu's humidity creates conditions where mold can begin colonizing a wet surface in as little as 24–48 hours. We locate hidden growth, contain it, and remove it safely.
- **Odor removal** — Persistent smoke, mildew, or sewage odors that survive surface cleaning often require hydroxyl or ozone treatment to neutralize at the molecular level.
- **Board-up and tarping** — Temporary weatherproofing after storm damage or fire to prevent further loss before full restoration begins.
- **Carpet care and cleaning** — Deep extraction cleaning for residential and commercial carpet, including post-water-damage sanitation.

## One call, start to finish

The most stressful part of recovering from property damage is often the coordination — figuring out who handles what, when each trade shows up, and whether the paperwork lines up. Calling AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care once puts mitigation and reconstruction under the same roof. We document conditions throughout the process, which also gives you a clear record to share with your insurer as you navigate your claim.
