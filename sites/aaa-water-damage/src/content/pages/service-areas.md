When water is soaking into your subfloor or smoke residue is settling into your walls, the last thing you need is to wonder whether help is on the way. AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care answers the phone around the clock and dispatches from Honolulu to communities across Oahu. Call anytime — days, nights, weekends, holidays — and a real person will pick up, confirm your location, and get a crew moving.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Honolulu and serves communities throughout Oahu — from the urban core of Honolulu and Waikiki to the suburban neighborhoods of the Ewa Plain, the windward communities along Kailua and Kaneohe, and the North Shore towns that sit at the far edge of the island. Oahu's compact geography means that even the most distant corners of the island are reachable in a single dispatch run, though traffic on H-1 and the Likelike and Pali highways can affect arrival windows during peak commute hours. If you're on a neighboring island, call us — we'll tell you honestly whether we can help or point you toward a qualified local resource.

## Coverage by distance

**Closest to our base:** Downtown Honolulu, Waikiki, Kaimuki, Manoa, and the surrounding East Honolulu neighborhoods see the fastest arrivals. Equipment can often be running before a slow leak has time to reach the second layer of drywall.

**Mid-island and leeward:** Pearl City, Aiea, Ewa Beach, Kapolei, and Mililani are well within our regular service footprint. The H-1 corridor connects us efficiently, though rush-hour congestion between downtown and the Ewa Plain is a real factor — we'll give you an honest estimate when you call.

**Windward and North Shore:** Kailua, Kaneohe, Haleiwa, and Wahiawa require a mountain crossing via the Likelike, Pali, or H-3. Travel time is longer, but for significant water intrusion, fire damage, or mold situations, we make the drive. A large loss doesn't get smaller while you wait for a closer crew that doesn't exist.

## Don't see your city?

The list of linked service areas on this page covers the communities we respond to most often, but it isn't a hard boundary. If your neighborhood isn't listed, call **(808) 349-3407** and describe the situation. For substantial losses — a burst pipe that's affected multiple rooms, a kitchen fire, a slow leak that's been feeding mold — we regularly dispatch to locations outside our standard footprint.
