When water is soaking into your subfloor, smoke has settled into every surface, or mold is spreading behind a wall, you need someone on the phone now — not a voicemail. AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care responds around the clock across Oahu, handling water damage, mold remediation, fire and smoke cleanup, storm damage, biohazard situations, and full reconstruction when a property needs to be rebuilt from the inside out.

## Services we handle

**Water damage & flood response** — Whether a pipe burst overnight in a Kaimuki bungalow or a storm surge pushed water under the doors of a Kapolei property, we extract standing water, pull moisture from walls and flooring, and run industrial drying equipment until readings confirm the structure is dry.

**Mold remediation** — Hawaii's humidity means mold can begin colonizing a wet surface within 24 to 48 hours of a leak. We locate hidden moisture sources, contain the affected area, and remove contaminated material so the problem doesn't migrate to the rest of the home.

**Fire & smoke damage cleanup** — After a fire, the visible char is only part of the damage. Smoke residue penetrates drywall, insulation, and HVAC systems, and the odor compounds over time if it isn't treated properly. We address both the structural damage and the persistent smell.

**Carpet & upholstery care** — Between restoration jobs, we offer professional carpet and upholstery cleaning — useful after a water event, after a tenant moves out, or simply when Oahu's salt air and red dirt have worked their way into the fibers.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

The phone is answered 24 hours a day, seven days a week — including holidays. That matters because water damage doesn't pause for the weekend, and every hour standing water sits in contact with wood framing or drywall is another hour of structural absorption and potential mold growth.

The crew that arrives is equipped for the job on the first visit: truck-mounted extraction units, commercial-grade air movers, refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers, and moisture meters that read behind walls without tearing them open unnecessarily. Drying progress is documented at each check so you have a clear record of what was found and what was done — useful when you're filing an insurance claim and need to show your adjuster the scope of work.

Because the company is based in Honolulu, response across the island doesn't require a long drive from the mainland or another island. That local presence also means familiarity with Oahu's specific building patterns — older plantation-style homes with pier foundations that trap moisture underneath, concrete block construction common in urban Honolulu neighborhoods, and the coastal conditions that accelerate corrosion and mold in ways that mainland restoration guides don't fully account for.
