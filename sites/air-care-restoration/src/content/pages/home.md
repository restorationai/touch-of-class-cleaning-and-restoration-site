When water soaks through your drywall, smoke smell won't leave a room, or mold shows up somewhere it shouldn't, Air Care Restoration is the team Abilene property owners call. We handle water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, and the reconstruction work that follows — available around the clock, every day of the year.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether a pipe burst overnight or a slow leak has been hiding behind your cabinets for weeks, standing water moves fast. It wicks into wall cavities, saturates subfloors, and creates the humidity mold needs within 24 to 48 hours. We extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke restoration** — Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces long after the flames are out. The soot you can see is only part of the problem; odor-carrying particles settle into HVAC systems, insulation, and soft materials throughout the structure. We work through the full scope — from initial board-up and debris removal to deodorization and finish repairs.

**Mold remediation** — West Texas humidity spikes during storm season, and older Abilene homes with pier-and-beam foundations or aging plumbing are particularly vulnerable to moisture intrusion that never fully dries. We locate the source, contain the affected area, and remove mold-compromised materials following established remediation protocols.

**Reconstruction** — Restoration doesn't end when the damaged material comes out. We handle the rebuild — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, painting — so you're working with one company from first call to finished room, not coordinating between a mitigation crew and a separate contractor.
