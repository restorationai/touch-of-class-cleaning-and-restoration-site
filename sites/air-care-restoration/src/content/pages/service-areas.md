Air Care Restoration is headquartered in Abilene and dispatched around the clock — every day of the year, including holidays. When a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or a storm pushes water through your roof on a Sunday, the same team that handles weekday calls is on duty. Coverage decisions are made from our Abilene base, so scheduling is straightforward: one call routes you to the crew closest to your address.

## Where we work

Abilene sits near the geographic center of West Texas, which puts us within practical driving range of a wide band of communities across the Big Country and the surrounding region. Our primary service footprint runs along the US-83 and US-84 corridors, extends east toward the Callahan County line, and reaches north and south through Taylor County and its neighbors. The terrain here is open and largely flat, which works in your favor — there are few mountain passes or congested urban cores to slow a crew down once a truck is rolling.

## Coverage by distance

Cities and communities closest to Abilene — including Wylie, Tye, Tuscola, Clyde, and Merkel — are our fastest-response territory. A call from one of these addresses typically puts a crew on the road with minimal lag.

Further out, communities like Sweetwater, Snyder, Stamford, Anson, and Coleman fall within our regular coverage zone. Drive times are longer, but the open highway between us and most of these towns keeps travel predictable. We arrive in the order the road allows — not on a clock promise we can't keep — but we communicate an estimated arrival when you call so you're never left guessing.

At the edge of our coverage area, we evaluate larger or more complex losses on a case-by-case basis. A significant water intrusion, fire, or mold situation in a community farther afield is often worth the extended drive for both sides.

## Don't see your city?

The list of linked service areas on this page covers our most frequently requested locations, but it isn't a hard boundary. If your address isn't listed, call us at (325) 339-8723 and describe the situation. For substantial losses — major flooding, fire damage, large-scale mold remediation — we regularly dispatch beyond our standard footprint. The worst outcome is a referral to someone who can help you faster; we'll tell you honestly either way.
