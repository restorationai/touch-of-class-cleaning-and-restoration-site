## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Baird

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the highest contamination level, and the response protocol reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first step is containment: identifying the source, stopping active flow if possible, and establishing a barrier between the affected zone and clean living areas. Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted and portable units capable of handling solid-laden waste, not just water.

Once bulk material is removed, affected porous materials, flooring, drywall, and insulation that cannot be fully decontaminated, are documented, bagged, and disposed of per Texas Commission on Environmental Quality guidelines for biohazardous waste. Hard surfaces are then treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants, applied in multiple passes. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, with moisture readings logged at each session to confirm the space is returning to normal levels. The IICRC-certified team follows the S500 standard for water damage and applies Category 3 protocols throughout, meaning no shortcuts on contact time for disinfectants and no skipping the post-treatment verification step.
