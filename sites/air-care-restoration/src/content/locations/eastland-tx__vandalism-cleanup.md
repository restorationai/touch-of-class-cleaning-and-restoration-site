## Why Eastland Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The same act of vandalism that would be a quick surface clean on a newer vinyl-sided building becomes a more involved project on Eastland's older masonry and painted brick. Porous limestone and soft mortar joints common in buildings constructed before World War II wick spray paint deep into the substrate. If the wrong solvent is used, it can bleach or etch the face of the stone permanently. West Texas heat compounds the problem: in summer, paint binders cure into masonry within hours of application, making early response critical.

Broken glass from vandalized windows or storefronts carries its own risk in this climate. Eastland's temperature swings between seasons mean that a compromised window opening left unboarded can expose interior materials to rapid moisture cycling, dust infiltration, and in winter, hard freezes that damage plumbing. Boarding and securing the structure is always the first step, not an afterthought.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Eastland

Every job starts with a documented walk-through. We photograph all affected surfaces before touching anything, which matters for insurance claims and, in some cases, for law enforcement documentation. From there, the process follows the material:

**Graffiti on masonry or brick:** We test a small, inconspicuous area with the appropriate remover before committing to the full surface. For older Eastland buildings, that often means a lower-pH cleaner applied with a soft brush rather than pressure washing, which can erode soft mortar joints.

**Graffiti on painted wood or metal:** Solvent-based removers lift most spray paint without damaging the substrate. We follow with a primer coat if the base paint was compromised, so the surface is ready for touch-up or full repaint.

**Broken glass and structural breach:** We remove all glass fragments, including those embedded in frames and surrounding surfaces, board or temporarily glaze openings, and document the scope for your insurance adjuster.

**Odor and interior contamination:** If vandals entered the structure, we assess for any biohazard concerns, spray residue on interior surfaces, or damage to HVAC intakes that could spread contaminants through the building.

## Local Note

Eastland's older downtown masonry was built with a softer, lime-based mortar that was standard practice through much of the mid-twentieth century. That mortar is more permeable than modern Portland cement mixes, which means graffiti ink and paint penetrate faster and deeper, especially in summer when surface temperatures on south-facing walls can exceed the ambient air temperature by a wide margin. Aggressive pressure washing, which might be fine on a 1990s concrete block building, can blow out the mortar joints on a 1930s brick storefront and create a repair bill that dwarfs the original cleanup.
