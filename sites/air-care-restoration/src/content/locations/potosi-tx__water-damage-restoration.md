## Why Potosi Properties Are Vulnerable to Water Damage

The geology under Taylor County is a major factor that separates water damage in this part of Texas from losses in other regions. Expansive clay soil swells when it absorbs moisture and contracts sharply during dry spells, which puts cyclical pressure on slab foundations, underground supply lines, and the joints where plumbing penetrates concrete. A slow pinhole leak under a slab can go undetected for weeks while the surrounding soil stays saturated, wicking moisture up through the concrete and into flooring adhesives, baseboards, and wall framing.

Potosi's rural and semi-rural character also means many homes rely on private well systems and septic infrastructure rather than municipal connections. Pressure fluctuations in private well systems can accelerate wear on older supply fittings, and a failed pressure tank or check valve can push water into a home at volume before the homeowner realizes anything is wrong. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a meaningful portion of the housing stock in this part of Taylor County, often have galvanized steel supply lines that have reached or exceeded their service life.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Potosi

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active source and then mapping exactly where water has traveled. Water follows the path of least resistance, and in a slab-on-grade home it often moves horizontally under flooring well beyond the visible wet zone. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full moisture boundary before any drying equipment is placed, because drying only the visible damage leaves hidden saturation behind.

Once the scope is confirmed, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then set a calculated array of commercial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers. Drying targets are set to the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, and moisture readings are logged at each monitoring visit so you and your insurance adjuster have a documented drying record. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated insulation or buckled subfloor panels, are removed and documented before disposal.

Because we hold an EPA Lead-Safe certification, we can handle pre-1978 construction properly if disturbed materials test positive for lead-based paint, which matters in older Taylor County homes where original paint layers are common.
