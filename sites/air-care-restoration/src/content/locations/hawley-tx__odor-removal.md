## Why Hawley Properties Hold Odors Longer

The Jones County climate is the first factor working against you. Extended dry heat causes wood framing, drywall, and insulation to become more porous over time, giving odor-causing compounds more surface area to cling to. Many homes in the Hawley area were built during the mid-twentieth-century agricultural boom, when construction relied on thicker wood framing and fewer vapor barriers than modern builds. That older material absorbs smoke and biological odors more aggressively than contemporary construction, and it releases them more slowly once a source is removed.

Hawley's proximity to working farms and ranch operations also means that animal-related odors, from livestock dander to rodent intrusion, are a more common complaint here than in suburban markets. Crawl spaces and pier-and-beam foundations common in older Hawley homes trap those smells underneath living areas, where they migrate upward through floor gaps and HVAC returns. Identifying the true source before any deodorization begins is what separates a lasting result from a temporary cover-up.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Hawley

Every job starts with a source inspection. Applying ozone or hydroxyl treatment to a room that still contains the contaminated material is a waste of time and money. Our technicians trace the odor to its origin, whether that's charred framing behind drywall, a saturated subfloor from a slow leak, or a decomposition event in a wall cavity, and remove or treat the source material before any deodorization equipment is deployed.

From there, the method we select depends on what the structure needs:

- **Thermal fogging** pushes a deodorizing fog into the same microscopic spaces that smoke and odor particles penetrated, neutralizing compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules in the air and on surfaces. It's safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a family can't fully vacate.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe smoke or biological odors. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer and requires proper clearance time before re-entry.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs throughout the process to capture particulates that carry odor compounds.

For smoke odor removal specifically, we follow IICRC FSRT guidelines, which means we don't skip the structural cleaning phase before fogging. Soot residue left on surfaces will keep re-releasing odor regardless of how much deodorizer is applied.
