## Why Ballinger Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same dry heat that defines West Texas summers is hard on building materials. Wood framing, plaster, and older insulation become brittle and porous over decades, creating microscopic channels where smoke particles, pet dander, mold off-gases, and cooking residues can settle well below the surface. When a fire or a long-standing pet odor problem occurs in a home built in the mid-twentieth century, the odor molecules are not just sitting on painted surfaces. They migrate into wall cavities, subfloor assemblies, and attic insulation where a simple airing-out or store-bought spray will not reach them.

Runnels County also sees periodic flooding from the Colorado River watershed, and water intrusion followed by slow drying in a sealed home is a reliable recipe for musty odor that becomes structural over time. Mold-related odor in particular tends to be underestimated until it has colonized wall cavities, at which point surface deodorization alone is not a solution.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Ballinger

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying the source, not masking it. When the crew arrives, the first step is a thorough inspection using moisture meters, thermal imaging where appropriate, and direct sensory assessment to locate every odor reservoir in the structure.

From there, the process is matched to the odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing solvent as a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than coating surfaces.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing volatile organic compounds. Hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner cannot easily relocate.
- **Ozone treatment** is effective for severe, pervasive odors in unoccupied spaces. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer and requires the structure to be vacated during treatment and properly ventilated afterward.
- **Encapsulant sealers** are applied to structural materials when odor has penetrated deeply into wood or concrete and cannot be fully neutralized through atmospheric treatment alone.

The right method, or combination of methods, depends on the odor source, the building materials involved, and how long the odor has been present. In older Ballinger homes with plaster walls and original hardwood floors, we typically plan for more than one treatment phase because those materials release absorbed compounds gradually.

## Equipment and Methods Calibrated to West Texas Conditions

Humidity levels in Ballinger regularly drop into the single digits during summer afternoons. That affects how deodorization equipment performs. Ozone generators, for example, produce ozone more efficiently at moderate humidity, so in extremely dry conditions we may introduce controlled moisture to optimize the treatment environment. For smoke odor removal after a structure fire, thermal fogging is particularly effective in the wood-framed construction common to Runnels County because the fog penetrates the same grain pathways the smoke used. The IICRC FSRT certification our technicians hold covers the science behind smoke behavior and residue types, which informs which deodorization method is applied to which surface.
