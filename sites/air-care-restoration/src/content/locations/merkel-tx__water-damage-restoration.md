## Why Merkel Properties See Water Damage Issues

Taylor County's soil composition is a significant factor that doesn't get enough attention. The caliche and clay-heavy ground common throughout the Merkel area expands when wet and contracts sharply during dry spells — a cycle that stresses foundation seals, shifts slab edges, and opens gaps where water intrudes during heavy rain events. Older homes in Merkel, many built during the mid-20th century boom that followed agricultural expansion in the region, frequently have galvanized supply lines that have reached the end of their service life. A pinhole leak inside a wall cavity can go undetected for weeks, and by the time a homeowner notices a soft spot in the floor or a musty smell near a baseboard, moisture has already migrated well beyond the visible damage.

Merkel's position in the Southern Plains also means it sits in the path of both spring severe weather and occasional winter freezes. Pipes in uninsulated exterior walls — common in homes built before modern energy codes — are vulnerable during the hard freezes that periodically push through Taylor County. When those pipes burst, the water release is sudden and high-volume, sometimes flooding multiple rooms before the main shutoff can be reached.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Merkel

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water that looks contained at the surface rarely is — it travels along subfloor joists, wicks into wall cavities, and pools in low spots under vinyl flooring without leaving an obvious trail. Mapping the full extent of saturation before any drying equipment is placed is what separates a genuine restoration from a surface dry that leaves hidden moisture behind.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is removed using truck-mounted extraction units capable of pulling hundreds of gallons quickly. After bulk extraction, the focus shifts to structural drying: commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers positioned per IICRC S500 drying science, and daily moisture readings to track progress. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor panels — are evaluated against drying targets, and anything that cannot be dried in place is removed to prevent mold colonization, which can begin in as little as 24 to 48 hours in warm, humid conditions.

Throughout the process the crew documents every reading, every photo, and every equipment placement log. That documentation matters directly for insurance claims.

## Local Note

Homes in the Merkel area built on pier-and-beam foundations — a common construction method for the region's older housing stock — present a specific drying challenge that slab homes don't share. When water enters the crawl space, it saturates the soil beneath the structure and the wood framing above it simultaneously. The crawl space itself acts as a humidity reservoir, continuously feeding moisture back into the subfloor even after surface water is removed. Effective drying in these homes requires crawl space ventilation management and sometimes temporary encapsulation to break that feedback loop. Treating only the interior without addressing what's happening under the floor is one of the most common reasons a water loss job has to be reopened weeks later.
