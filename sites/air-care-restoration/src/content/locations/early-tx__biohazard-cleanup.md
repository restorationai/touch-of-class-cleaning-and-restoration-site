## Why Early Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Brown County's climate runs hot and dry through much of the year, but Early's summers push interior temperatures high enough that unaddressed biological material can become a serious health concern within hours, not days. Older homes in the area — many built with pier-and-beam foundations common across this part of West Texas — can trap contaminants beneath flooring in ways that aren't visible from the surface. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Early

Every call is handled with full discretion. We arrive with privacy, compassion and professional discretion. The process follows a structured sequence:

- **Assessment and containment** — Affected areas are identified and isolated to prevent cross-contamination of unaffected rooms.
- **Personal protective equipment and regulatory compliance** — All technicians work under OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards. Sharps, needles, and infectious materials are collected and containerized according to Texas Department of State Health Services guidelines for regulated medical waste.
- **Surface decontamination and odor neutralization** — EPA-registered disinfectants rated for the specific biological hazard are applied to all affected surfaces, including porous materials that may require removal.
- **Waste transport and disposal** — Regulated waste is transported and disposed of through licensed channels — not in residential trash or standard dumpsters.
- **Documentation** — A written scope of work and completion report is provided, which most carriers require for insurance claims.

In Early's older housing stock, technicians pay particular attention to original baseboards, plaster-over-lath wall sections, and subfloor assemblies that can absorb fluids well beyond the visible stain line.
