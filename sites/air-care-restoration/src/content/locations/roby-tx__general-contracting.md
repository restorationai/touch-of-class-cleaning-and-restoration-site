## Why Roby Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Fisher County's expansive clay soils shift with moisture — swelling after a rare heavy rain and contracting during the long dry stretches that define this part of the Rolling Plains. That cycle puts stress on slab foundations, door frames, and load-bearing walls in ways that compound over decades. Older homes in Roby — many built during the mid-20th century agricultural boom — often carry original wood framing, single-pane windows, and plumbing that predates modern PEX or CPVC materials. Before a kitchen remodel or bathroom renovation can begin in earnest, a thorough assessment of what's behind the walls is essential. Galvanized steel supply lines corrode from the inside out and may look intact at the shutoff while failing silently at the joints.

The region's wind exposure also means that any exterior renovation — siding replacement, roofline work, window upgrades — needs materials and installation methods rated for sustained high-wind conditions. What works fine in a sheltered suburban neighborhood doesn't always hold up when there's nothing between your west wall and the New Mexico state line.

## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about renovation work in Roby and the surrounding Fisher County area: the region's caliche-heavy soil and the way it drains — or doesn't — after a heavy rain can affect exterior grading decisions made during a remodel.
