## Why Hawley Properties Face Asbestos Exposure Risks

Housing built across Jones County between roughly 1940 and 1980 routinely incorporated asbestos in ways that are easy to overlook. Popcorn ceiling texture, vinyl floor tiles, pipe insulation, duct wrap, and the mastic adhesive beneath linoleum were all common carriers. In Hawley specifically, many homes were constructed during the post-war decades when the town grew alongside agricultural expansion in the region, meaning the building materials of that era are well represented here.

The West Texas climate adds a layer of complexity. Extreme summer heat causes expansion and contraction in older building materials, and the dry, windy conditions that sweep through Jones County can accelerate the degradation of brittle asbestos-containing materials. Once those materials become friable, meaning they crumble or release dust easily, the risk of fiber release rises sharply. A ceiling texture that looks intact may already be releasing fibers into living spaces during seasonal temperature swings.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Hawley

Every project begins with a thorough inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis before any abatement work begins. This step is not optional, and it protects you legally and financially if questions arise later with a buyer, insurer, or contractor.

Once confirmed, our OSHA-trained crew establishes a regulated work area using negative air pressure containment. This prevents cross-contamination to adjacent rooms, which matters especially in older Hawley homes where HVAC ductwork may connect multiple zones. Asbestos-containing materials are wetted to suppress fiber release, carefully removed, double-bagged in labeled poly sheeting, and transported to a licensed disposal facility in compliance with Texas Commission on Environmental Quality disposal requirements.

Air clearance testing follows abatement to confirm fiber counts are within acceptable limits before containment is removed. We coordinate that clearance step so you are not left waiting on a third party to schedule independently.
