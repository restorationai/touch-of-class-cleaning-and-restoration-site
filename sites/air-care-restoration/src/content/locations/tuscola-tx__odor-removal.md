## Why Tuscola Properties Hold Odors Differently

The same climate that makes Taylor County's summers memorable also accelerates odor problems indoors. Extreme heat drives volatile organic compounds from smoke residue, pet waste, and mold deeper into porous materials, especially in homes with older fiberglass batt insulation or original wood-panel walls. When the HVAC system cycles on, those compounds circulate through every room.

Tuscola's housing stock leans toward single-story ranch-style homes built between the 1960s and 1990s, many with crawl space foundations or pier-and-beam construction. Both foundation types allow ground moisture and animal intrusion odors to migrate upward through subfloor gaps. The region's clay-heavy soils also retain moisture after rain, which can create persistent musty conditions beneath a home even during otherwise dry stretches of the year.

Fire and smoke odors present a particular challenge here. West Texas homes often have cedar trim, pine paneling, or OSB sheathing that absorbs smoke particles aggressively. Porous materials like these require more than one deodorization pass to reach the odor source rather than just masking the surface.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Tuscola

Effective deodorization starts with identifying where the odor is actually living, not just where it's strongest. The Air Care Restoration team begins with a thorough inspection, probing wall cavities, subfloor spaces, and HVAC returns where odor compounds accumulate and recirculate.

From there, the method is matched to the material and the source:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, reaching into wall voids, ceiling cavities, and upholstered surfaces that other methods can't contact.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules at a chemical level. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators can operate in occupied or semi-occupied structures, which matters when a homeowner needs to stay nearby.
- **Ozone treatment** is deployed in unoccupied spaces for stubborn odors, including heavy smoke damage or long-term pet contamination. The structure must be vacated and properly ventilated before re-entry.
- **HVAC deodorization** addresses duct systems that have been carrying odor-laden air throughout the home, a step that's easy to skip but critical for lasting results.

All work is performed by an IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) certified team, and the process follows IICRC standards for odor control.
