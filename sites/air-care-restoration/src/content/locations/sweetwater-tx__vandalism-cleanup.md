## Why Sweetwater Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The building stock across Sweetwater leans heavily on older masonry construction: brick exteriors, stucco facades, and concrete block that were common in mid-century commercial development. These materials are porous in ways that modern composite siding is not. Spray paint and marker pigment wick into the substrate quickly, especially during the warmer months when surface temperatures in West Texas can climb well above ambient air temperature. A tag applied on a 95-degree afternoon may penetrate twice as deep as the same paint applied in a cooler climate, which changes the chemistry of removal entirely.

The region's wind also carries fine caliche dust and particulate that settles into any freshly broken surface, including shattered glass frames and splintered wood. That contamination needs to be addressed alongside the visible damage, not after.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Sweetwater

Every job starts with a thorough damage assessment before anything is touched. We photograph and document all affected surfaces, which matters both for insurance purposes and for choosing the right removal method. Graffiti on brick calls for a different approach than graffiti on painted metal or plate glass, and using the wrong solvent on the wrong substrate can cause secondary damage that costs more to fix than the original vandalism.

For graffiti removal, we use professional-grade chemical strippers and pressure washing calibrated to the surface material. On older masonry common in Sweetwater's downtown commercial corridor, we work at lower pressures to avoid eroding mortar joints that may already show age. Broken glass cleanup includes full frame inspection, safe collection and disposal of shards, and temporary boarding or glazing so the property is secured before we leave the site. Interior damage from forced entry, including disturbed insulation, damaged drywall, or debris tracked inside, is addressed as part of the same scope rather than left for a second visit.

Because Air Care Restoration holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, our crews follow structured safety protocols during cleanup, including proper PPE when handling broken glass or chemically treated surfaces.
