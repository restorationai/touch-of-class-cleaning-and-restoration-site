## Why Paint Rock Properties Face Stubborn Odor Problems

The same West Texas climate that makes Paint Rock's landscape striking also creates conditions that trap and amplify odors inside homes and commercial buildings. Extreme daytime heat drives volatile organic compounds from smoke, mold, or animal waste deeper into wood framing, insulation batts, and soft furnishings. When temperatures drop at night, those materials contract and seal the odor molecules in. Older ranch-style homes common to this part of Concho County often have minimal mechanical ventilation, meaning odors recirculate rather than exhaust. Caliche-heavy soil also affects crawl space humidity in ways that encourage microbial growth, adding a secondary musty layer on top of whatever primary odor event triggered the call.

Smoke is a particularly persistent challenge here. Homes in rural Concho County frequently rely on wood-burning stoves or propane appliances, and a single flue backup or chimney fire can coat wall cavities with soot particles that off-gas for months. Because many of these structures use older, unsealed drywall or original plaster finishes, smoke penetrates well beyond the surface layer that a standard cleaning would reach.

## Our Deodorization Process in Paint Rock

Effective odor removal is a sequence, not a single treatment. The process starts with source identification, locating and physically removing the contaminated material whenever possible. No deodorization technology eliminates an odor that still has an active source.

Once the source is addressed, the method is matched to the structure and the odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing solvent as a fine fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, reaching inside wall voids, HVAC ducts, and subfloor cavities. It is particularly effective in the ranch-style and pier-and-beam homes common to this part of Concho County.
- **Hydroxyl generation** uses UV-light technology to break down odor molecules in the air and on surfaces without requiring the space to be vacated. This makes it practical for occupied commercial buildings or homes where complete evacuation isn't feasible.
- **Ozone treatment** delivers high-concentration oxidation for severe smoke or biological odors in unoccupied spaces. Given Paint Rock's heat, we time ozone cycles carefully, elevated ambient temperatures accelerate ozone's reaction rate, which affects both dwell time and post-treatment airing requirements.
- **Encapsulants and sealers** are applied to framing or subfloor materials after cleaning when odor has penetrated beyond what surface treatment can reach.

All work is performed under the IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) standard, which governs the sequencing and documentation of smoke and odor remediation.
