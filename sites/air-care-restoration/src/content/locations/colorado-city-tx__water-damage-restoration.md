## Why Colorado City Properties See Water Damage Issues

Mitchell County's soil profile leans toward expansive clay, the kind that swells when wet and shrinks when dry. That cycle puts constant lateral pressure on slab foundations and crawl-space footings, and it creates micro-gaps around utility penetrations where water finds its way in. When a plumbing failure or a hard rain event occurs, water doesn't just pool on the surface — it wicks into the slab edge, travels under flooring, and can reach interior wall cavities before a homeowner notices anything more than a faint musty smell.

Colorado City also sits in a region where severe weather arrives with little warning. Hail and high-wind events can compromise roof flashing and attic vapor barriers in a single afternoon, leaving insulation and ceiling assemblies saturated. Because the town's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built in the mid-twentieth century, you'll find original cast-iron or galvanized supply lines that have simply reached the end of their service life. A pinhole leak behind plaster or original hardwood flooring can run undetected for weeks, and by the time the floor buckles, the subfloor and bottom plate have been wet long enough to begin supporting mold colonization — typically within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Colorado City

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source — whether that means shutting the main at the meter or coordinating with a plumber you've already called. From there, the process follows a structured sequence calibrated to what we actually find in the structure, not a one-size checklist.

**Moisture mapping** comes before any equipment is placed. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to read what the surface can't show — wet lath behind plaster, saturated OSB under tile, elevated readings inside wall cavities. In older Colorado City homes with plaster-and-lath construction, this step is especially important: plaster can feel dry to the touch while the lath behind it holds moisture for days.

**Water extraction** removes the bulk of standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Structural materials — concrete, wood framing, insulation — are addressed separately with targeted drying equipment positioned according to the psychrometric conditions inside the structure that day.

**Structural drying** runs until daily moisture readings confirm the materials have returned to acceptable equilibrium moisture content, per IICRC S500 standards. We document every reading so you have a clear record for your insurance carrier.

**Content and materials assessment** happens throughout. Salvageable flooring, cabinetry, and personal property are separated from materials that need controlled demolition. We photograph everything before anything is moved.
