## Why Potosi Properties Carry a Higher Asbestos Risk

Taylor County's residential growth tracked closely with national construction trends that peaked in the 1950s through the 1970s, exactly the decades when asbestos use in building products was at its highest. Ranch-style homes, metal-roofed outbuildings, and slab-foundation houses common throughout the Potosi area frequently contain asbestos in places that are easy to overlook: the adhesive mastic under vinyl floor tiles, the insulation wrapped around duct work in crawl spaces, the joint compound on interior walls, and the spray-applied texture on ceilings.

West Texas's climate adds a layer of complexity. The region's extreme temperature swings, from scorching summers to hard freezes in winter, cause building materials to expand and contract repeatedly over decades. That thermal cycling can cause asbestos-containing materials to crack, crumble, or delaminate, moving them from a stable "non-friable" state to a friable one where fibers can become airborne. A ceiling texture that looked intact last summer may have shifted after a hard freeze in January.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Potosi

Every project begins with a thorough inspection and, where materials are suspect, bulk sampling sent to an accredited laboratory. We do not assume a material is safe because it looks undamaged. In Potosi's older homes, asbestos often appears in layers: a homeowner removes popcorn ceiling texture only to find a second layer of texture underneath, or pulls up linoleum to find two generations of tile and mastic below it.

Once the laboratory confirms the presence of asbestos-containing material, the abatement scope is defined and the work area is sealed with poly sheeting and negative air pressure equipment. Regulated waste is packaged in labeled, sealed containers and transported to a licensed disposal facility in accordance with Texas Commission on Environmental Quality requirements. After removal, air clearance sampling confirms that fiber levels have returned to acceptable levels before containment is removed. The process is methodical by necessity: cutting corners on containment in a tight ranch-style floor plan means fibers can migrate into adjacent rooms quickly.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Asbestos

Asbestos abatement is not a general contracting task. We use HEPA-filtered negative air machines to maintain pressure differentials inside containment zones, full-face respirators with P100 cartridges, and disposable Tyvek suits that are bagged inside the containment before removal. Wet methods, applying amended water to materials before disturbing them, keep fiber release to a minimum during the actual removal work. For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal, a common request in Potosi's mid-century homes, wet scraping followed by HEPA vacuuming of all surfaces is standard before final air clearance.

Our team holds OSHA training and the company carries EPA Lead-Safe Certification, which matters in older homes where lead paint and asbestos frequently coexist in the same structure.
