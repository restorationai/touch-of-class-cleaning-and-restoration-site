## Why Albany Properties See Water Cleanup Issues

The same clay-heavy soils that make Shackelford County farmland productive also create drainage problems around residential foundations. During a heavy rain event — and the Rolling Plains can receive intense, localized downpours even in otherwise dry stretches — water pools against exterior walls and finds its way through foundation cracks, window wells, or aging crawlspace vents. Albany's housing stock leans toward mid-century construction, meaning many homes were built before modern moisture barriers and vapor retarders were standard practice. Older galvanized supply lines in those homes are also well past their service life, and pinhole leaks or sudden failures are common. The result is water damage that often goes undetected longer than it would in a newer build.

West Texas summers accelerate the problem in a counterintuitive way: the soil desiccates and contracts during drought, then expands rapidly when rain arrives, placing cyclical stress on underground plumbing joints. A slow seep under a slab can run for weeks before it surfaces as a soft spot in flooring or a spike on a water bill.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Albany

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active source — whether that means coordinating a shutoff at the meter or isolating a failed appliance. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Assessment and moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters let us read what the eye cannot. Water behind plaster or beneath vinyl flooring often reads dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated. We document every affected area before extraction begins.

**Standing water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet. In Albany's slab-on-grade homes, we pay close attention to the perimeter of the slab and any interior walls running parallel to plumbing chases — those are the zones where moisture migrates farthest from the visible loss area.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, combined with high-velocity air movers, drive moisture out of framing, insulation, and finish materials. Drying timelines depend on material type and initial saturation levels; we monitor daily and adjust equipment placement based on readings, not guesswork.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** Any surface that held standing water or tested above acceptable moisture thresholds receives an EPA-registered antimicrobial application to inhibit microbial growth during the drying window.

## Albany Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners policies in Texas cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, a failed water heater, an appliance supply line failure. Gradual leaks and flood events (rising water from outside the structure) are treated differently, and flood losses typically require a separate NFIP or private flood policy. We document the loss thoroughly from the moment we arrive: photographs, moisture readings, affected material logs, and equipment placement records.
