## Why Eastland Properties See Water Damage Issues

Eastland's building stock skews older, and with age comes plumbing that was never designed for today's water pressure or the mineral content common in this part of Texas. Galvanized supply lines in homes built before the 1970s corrode from the inside out — the exterior of the pipe can look intact while the interior is nearly closed off, creating pressure spikes that split fittings at the worst possible time. Slab foundations, which are standard throughout much of Eastland, hide supply and drain lines beneath concrete, making a slow slab leak difficult to detect until flooring buckles or a water bill spikes.

The region's climate adds another layer. Eastland County can swing from prolonged drought — which causes expansive clay soils to shrink and shift — to sudden, heavy rainfall that overwhelms residential drainage in a matter of hours. When saturated soil has nowhere to go, water migrates toward the path of least resistance: crawlspaces, garage slabs, and poorly sealed foundation penetrations. Flood water cleanup after a storm event here often involves not just surface water removal but moisture that has wicked several inches up into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Eastland

Every water loss is different, but the sequence matters and it doesn't change. When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active source — whether that's shutting off the main supply, isolating a failed appliance line, or documenting a storm-entry point for your insurance carrier. From there:

**Extraction** comes before drying. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet, including water that has migrated under baseboards and into subfloor cavities. In Eastland homes with hardwood or engineered wood floors, early extraction is the difference between saving the floor and replacing it.

**Moisture mapping** uses thermal imaging and calibrated meters to locate water that isn't visible. Clay-rich soil against a slab foundation can keep concrete damp long after the surface appears dry — we read the structure, not just the surface.

**Drying and monitoring** follows a documented protocol aligned with the IICRC S500 standard. Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a calculated configuration based on the affected square footage and material types. We return to check readings and adjust equipment until every affected assembly reaches its dry standard.

**Clearance documentation** is provided at closeout — moisture readings, equipment logs, and photos that your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim cleanly.

One thing worth knowing about Eastland's older homes: many were built with wood-framed interior walls set directly on slab without a bottom plate moisture barrier — a common construction detail in this part of Texas before modern codes addressed it.
