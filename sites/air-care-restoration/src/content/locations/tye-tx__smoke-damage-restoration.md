## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Tye

We start by identifying what kind of smoke we're dealing with, because wet smoke from a slow-burning upholstery fire behaves differently on surfaces than the dry, powdery soot from a fast-moving grass or wildfire event. For wildfire-driven jobs common to this part of Taylor County, we typically find soot concentrated in attic spaces, window tracks, and HVAC filters before it's visible on walls. Our crews, trained to IICRC FSRT standards for fire and smoke restoration, vacuum and dry-sponge char-prone surfaces before any wet cleaning, since introducing moisture too early can smear soot into porous materials like drywall and grout. Ductwork gets inspected and, where contaminated, cleaned or sealed separately from the room-by-room cleaning so recirculated air doesn't reintroduce odor after we've finished. Where the property has manufactured-home construction, we pay closer attention to skirting vents and belly insulation, both common entry points for drifting wildfire smoke that a standard inspection checklist might miss.
