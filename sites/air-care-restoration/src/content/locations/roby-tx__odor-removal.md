## Why Roby Properties Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock across Roby and the broader Fisher County area skews older, with many homes built during the mid-twentieth century agricultural boom. Older construction typically means thicker wood framing, plaster or early-generation drywall, and less airtight building envelopes. Those characteristics are not flaws in themselves, but they create a specific odor challenge: porous, aged materials absorb smoke, pet waste, and moisture-related compounds more deeply than modern assemblies, and they release those compounds slowly over months. A window unit or swamp cooler cycling warm outside air through the structure during a Roby summer accelerates off-gassing, spreading contamination to rooms that were never directly affected by the original source.

The region's dust is also a factor. Fine caliche and red clay particulates common to this part of West Texas settle into HVAC filters, ductwork, and soft furnishings. When a fire or flood event happens, that existing dust layer becomes a secondary carrier for odor molecules, extending the deodorization scope beyond what the visible damage suggests.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Roby

Effective odor removal follows a sequence, and skipping steps is why DIY treatments fail. The process Air Care Restoration uses in Roby:

**Source removal first.** No deodorization chemistry works if the physical source remains. Charred material, saturated insulation, or contaminated subfloor gets removed before any treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging.** A petroleum-based or water-based fog is heated and dispersed to penetrate the same cavities smoke traveled through. Because fog particles are small enough to follow the same pathways as smoke, this step reaches behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, and into attic spaces that surface sprays cannot contact.

**Ozone or hydroxyl treatment.** Ozone generators produce a high concentration of O3 that oxidizes odor-causing molecules at the molecular level. In occupied or sensitive environments, hydroxyl generators achieve a similar result without requiring the space to be vacated for extended periods. The choice between them depends on the structure, occupancy status, and the nature of the odor source.

**HVAC and duct deodorization.** Given how much dust-laden air cycles through Roby homes, ductwork is treated as part of the scope, not an afterthought.

**Final verification.** The space is assessed before the job is closed, not just after equipment is running.
