The same relentless sun and seasonal temperature swings that crack foundation slabs and blister exterior paint are also the conditions your renovation has to be built to outlast. Air Care Restoration brings general contracting work to Tye with that climate reality baked into every material choice and construction sequence, whether the project is a kitchen overhaul, a bathroom remodel, or a post-damage rebuild after a fire or water loss.

## Why Tye Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Tye is a small community tucked just west of Abilene along I-20, and much of its housing stock reflects the construction patterns common to Taylor County from the mid-twentieth century onward — pier-and-beam foundations, older single-pane windows, and in many cases plumbing that predates modern PEX supply lines. Those details matter when you're planning a remodel. Opening a wall in a home built before the 1980s can surface galvanized steel pipe that's corroded from the inside, wiring that doesn't meet current NEC standards, or insulation that needs to be handled carefully under EPA Lead-Safe protocols.

The West Texas climate adds another layer. Extreme heat cycles cause wood framing to expand and contract more aggressively than in moderate climates, which means finish work — tile grout, caulk lines, trim joints — needs products rated for thermal movement. Drought conditions also affect expansive clay soils common to this part of Taylor County, causing foundation movement that can telegraph into interior finishes over time. A remodel that ignores the soil and climate context is one that will show wear ahead of schedule.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Tye

Every project starts with a thorough walk-through — not a quick estimate, but a structured assessment of existing conditions. In older Tye homes, that means checking for moisture intrusion behind exterior walls before any new finishes go in, verifying that the electrical panel can support updated kitchen or bathroom loads, and confirming that the subfloor is structurally sound before tile or hardwood is specified.

From there, the process moves through permitting with Taylor County and any applicable municipal requirements, material selection, subcontractor coordination, and phased construction. For post-damage rebuilds — a category where Air Care Restoration has specific expertise — the process integrates directly with the insurance documentation already completed during the mitigation phase, so the scope of repair is clearly defined before a single nail is pulled.

As an IICRC Certified Firm, and with our owner holding EPA Certified Renovator (Lead RRP) credentials, the team is equipped to handle the full range of conditions that turn up inside older structures without stopping the project mid-stream for a separate contractor for lead-related work.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors in this part of Taylor County: the clay-heavy soil around Tye shrinks noticeably during prolonged dry spells and swells again when rain returns. Homes that have been through several of these cycles sometimes show door frames that are slightly out of square and exterior brick veneer with step cracks at the mortar joints. Before committing to new interior doors or tile work in an affected area, it's worth checking whether the movement is ongoing or has stabilized — a detail that changes both the material spec and the warranty conversation significantly.
