## Why Clyde's Housing Stock and Climate Shape Every Remodel

Callahan County's expansive clay soils do more than cause foundation headaches — they affect how additions are framed, how drainage is graded, and how concrete flatwork is poured. A kitchen expansion or a bathroom addition that doesn't account for seasonal soil movement can develop cracks at the slab edge within a few years. West Texas also delivers temperature swings that punish under-insulated walls: summer attic temperatures regularly exceed 140°F, which means a remodel is an opportunity to upgrade insulation and ventilation in ways that cut utility bills for decades.

Many homes in Clyde were built with pier-and-beam or slab foundations during the 1950s through 1970s, and the original plumbing is often galvanized steel or early copper with compression fittings. When a renovation opens walls, it's common to find supply lines that are undersized by modern standards or drain lines with inadequate slope. Catching those conditions during a remodel — rather than after the drywall is closed — is one of the clearest advantages of working with a contractor who's also credentialed in damage assessment.

## Local Note

Homes in Clyde built during the 1960s and 1970s frequently used asbestos-containing materials in floor tile, mastic adhesive, and ceiling texture — products that were standard across West Texas construction of that era. Before any demolition begins on a home of that vintage, asbestos must be identified and addressed by a licensed abatement contractor. For lead paint, our owner Sarha Boyd holds an EPA Certified Renovator credential under the Lead RRP program, and regulated lead-containing materials are handled through that process before any walls or floors are opened. Skipping those steps doesn't just create a health risk; it can void a homeowner's insurance claim if a carrier determines that regulated materials were disturbed without proper handling. It's a step that adds a few days to the front end of a project and saves significant complications on the back end.
