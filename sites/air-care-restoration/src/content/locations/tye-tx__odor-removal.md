That climate does something specific to odors: it bakes them into porous surfaces, concentrates them in attic spaces, and cycles them back through HVAC systems every time the air kicks on. Whether the source is smoke from a kitchen fire, pet contamination in a slab-on-grade home, or musty residue left behind after a water intrusion, odors in Tye properties tend to set faster and penetrate deeper than homeowners expect. ## Why Tye Properties Hold Odors Differently

The Taylor County climate is not kind to building materials when odors are involved. Low humidity keeps surfaces from off-gassing naturally the way they might in more humid regions, which means smoke particulates and organic compounds bond tightly to drywall, insulation, and wood framing rather than dissipating over time. Homes built on concrete slab foundations, which are common throughout the Tye area, have no crawl space to ventilate, so odors that work their way into flooring assemblies have nowhere to migrate except upward into living spaces.

Wind is another factor. The prevailing southwest winds that sweep across this stretch of West Texas can push wildfire smoke into homes through attic vents and gaps in the building envelope, depositing fine particulates in insulation and ductwork long before a homeowner notices the smell inside. That kind of secondary smoke infiltration is easy to underestimate and harder to treat than a direct fire loss.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Tye

Effective odor removal is not a single-product job. The source of the odor, the materials it has penetrated, and the size of the affected space all determine which combination of methods will actually work.

The process typically moves through these stages:

- **Source removal**: No deodorization method is permanent if the contaminated material stays in place. Charred debris, saturated insulation, or soiled flooring gets removed before treatment begins.
- **Thermal fogging**: A petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed to penetrate the same porous surfaces that absorbed the original odor. This is especially effective in smoke-affected rooms where particulates have worked into wall cavities.
- **Hydroxyl generation**: Hydroxyl generators produce oxidizing molecules that break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level. Unlike ozone, hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner cannot fully vacate.
- **Ozone treatment**: For severe cases, ozone is deployed in unoccupied spaces for a controlled period. The high oxidant load neutralizes stubborn organic odors that other methods cannot reach. Re-occupancy timing is determined by air quality readings, not a fixed clock.
- **HVAC and duct treatment**: In Tye's climate, the air handler runs hard most of the year. Odors embedded in ductwork will recontaminate a treated space if the system is not addressed. We treat duct surfaces and replace filters as part of the close-out.

Air Care Restoration holds IICRC certification in Fire and Smoke Restoration (FSRT), which means the process follows documented industry standards rather than guesswork.
