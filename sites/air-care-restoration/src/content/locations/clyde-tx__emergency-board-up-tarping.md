## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Clyde

When we arrive on-site in Clyde, the first step is a rapid exterior assessment — identifying every breach point before any material goes up. That includes obvious openings like missing windows and roof holes, but also less visible gaps: soffit sections displaced by wind, garage door tracks bent by impact, or crawl space vents that have lost their covers. A board-up that misses a secondary entry point doesn't fully protect the structure.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps secured with lumber battens and cap nails driven into undamaged decking — not bungee cords or loose weighting that the next West Texas gust will rearrange. The tarp is lapped over the ridge when possible so water sheds away from the breach rather than pooling at its edge. For window and door board-ups, we use exterior-grade plywood cut to fit, fastened in a way that is secure but removable without additional structural damage when your contractor is ready to begin repairs.

Throughout the process we photograph every breach point before and after securing it. That documentation goes directly into the claim file — adjusters want to see the pre-mitigation condition, and having it on record protects you if there's any dispute about what was open and when.
