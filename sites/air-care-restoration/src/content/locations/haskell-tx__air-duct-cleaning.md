## Why Haskell Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Expect

Haskell County sits in the Rolling Plains, where cultivated fields surround the city on every side. During dry spells — which are frequent — fine soil particles become airborne and find their way into any gap in a home's envelope. Older homes in Haskell built during the post-war decades often have ductwork that was sized and sealed to the standards of that era: sheet metal connections secured with tape that dries out and gaps over time, and flex duct added during later renovations that can sag and trap debris. Even a small separation at a duct joint pulls unconditioned attic air — loaded with insulation fibers and dust — directly into the supply stream.

Cotton gin activity and grain handling in the surrounding county also contributes fine organic dust that doesn't behave like ordinary household debris. It packs tighter in duct bends and around register boots, and it holds moisture during the brief humid stretches that follow spring storms. That combination accelerates microbial growth in ways that homeowners don't notice until there's an odor or an unexplained spike in allergy symptoms.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Haskell

When an Air Care Restoration crew arrives at a Haskell property, the first step is a visual inspection of accessible ductwork, the air handler cabinet, and the return-air plenum. We use a camera scope on longer trunk lines to assess debris depth and identify any sections where the duct lining has deteriorated — a condition more common in systems that have run through multiple decades of West Texas summers.

Cleaning is performed with truck-mounted or portable negative-pressure equipment that places the entire duct system under continuous suction while agitation tools — rotary brushes and compressed-air whips — dislodge buildup from duct walls. All loosened material is captured in the vacuum stream rather than redistributed into living areas. After mechanical cleaning, we inspect the coil and blower compartment, because a clean duct system connected to a debris-coated evaporator coil will recontaminate quickly. Where microbial growth is present, EPA-registered sanitizing agents can be applied to duct interiors — we'll describe what we found and what we recommend before any additional treatment is performed.

Dryer vent cleaning is frequently requested alongside duct work in Haskell homes, particularly in older single-story ranch-style construction where the laundry room is interior and the vent run is long. A packed dryer vent is a fire risk, and the same crew can clear it during the same visit.
