## Why Roby Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Fisher County's climate is not gentle on exposed surfaces. Summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F, and the region's persistent winds carry fine caliche dust that works into fresh spray paint and etched glass almost immediately. Once that grit bonds with aerosol paint, simple scrubbing can grind pigment deeper into masonry or wood siding rather than lifting it. Winter brings a different problem: sudden freezes can cause broken window openings to admit enough cold air to burst interior pipes or warp door frames within a single night.

Roby's building stock tends toward older masonry commercial blocks and wood-frame agricultural structures. Masonry is porous, and paint penetrates the substrate rather than sitting on the surface, which means standard pressure washing alone rarely works. Wood siding, common on outbuildings and older homes in the area, can absorb solvent-based spray paints deeply if the coating is weathered. Knowing the substrate before choosing a cleaning method is not optional here; it is the difference between a clean surface and a bleached or abraded one.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Roby

When a call comes in from Roby, the first step after arrival is a systematic site assessment. The crew photographs every affected surface, documents the extent of damage for insurance purposes, and identifies substrate types before any cleaning begins. This documentation matters: insurance carriers want evidence of original conditions, and Fisher County properties often have multiple surface types on a single structure.

Graffiti removal is matched to the substrate. Painted block or brick gets a combination of chemical solvent application and low-pressure washing calibrated to avoid driving pigment deeper. Glass surfaces with etching or paint receive targeted film-removal solvents followed by inspection for structural compromise. Broken glass is collected and disposed of safely, with temporary boarding or polycarbonate sheeting installed to secure the opening until permanent glazing can be arranged. Interior spaces affected by forced entry, such as disturbed insulation, scattered debris, or tracked-in material, are cleaned and restored to a safe, habitable condition.

Where paint has penetrated deeply into masonry, anti-graffiti sealant application is offered as a final step. In Roby's dusty, high-UV environment, a sacrificial sealant coat makes future incidents significantly easier to address.
