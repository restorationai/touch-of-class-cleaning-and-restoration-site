## Why Hawley Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The housing and commercial stock in and around Hawley tends toward older construction: wood-framed homes with painted hardboard or lap siding, concrete block buildings, and metal agricultural structures that are common across Jones County. Each of those surfaces reacts differently to spray paint and chemical damage. Hardboard siding is especially unforgiving because the paint soaks into the substrate rather than sitting on a sealed surface, which means light sanding alone rarely resolves the stain. Concrete block and CMU walls are similarly porous, and in Hawley's low-humidity stretches the paint solvent flashes off quickly, locking pigment deep into the block face.

Broken glass from vandalized windows or doors is a separate concern. In a climate where overnight temperatures can swing dramatically from daytime highs, a broken window left unboarded creates an immediate interior humidity and dust intrusion problem on top of the security risk. Boarding and temporary weatherproofing are part of the cleanup scope, not an afterthought.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Hawley

Every job starts with a systematic damage assessment: photographing all affected surfaces, cataloging broken fixtures or entry points, and identifying the substrate type before selecting a removal method. That step matters here because applying the wrong chemical stripper to hardboard siding or a painted brick surface can cause secondary damage that costs more to fix than the original vandalism.

For graffiti removal, the approach is matched to the surface. Sealed concrete and metal typically respond to hot-water pressure washing combined with a biodegradable solvent. Unpainted masonry and wood require a lower-pressure chemical application followed by careful agitation to lift pigment without abrading the face. Where paint has penetrated too deeply to extract cleanly, the affected section is primed with a stain-blocking primer and repainted to match the surrounding surface.

Broken glass cleanup follows a contained protocol: visible shards are collected and bagged, then the area is swept and HEPA-vacuumed to capture fine fragments that embed in flooring or carpet. Temporary boarding is installed to secure the opening, and if the frame is damaged, we document it for the insurance scope before any permanent repair work begins.

Interior vandalism, including spray paint on drywall, damaged fixtures, or biohazard elements left behind after a break-in, is handled under the same structured assessment. Our team holds OSHA training and EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters in older Hawley-area structures where disturbing painted surfaces can create a lead exposure concern.
