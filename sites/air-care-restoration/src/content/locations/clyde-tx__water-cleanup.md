## Why Clyde Properties See Water Damage Differently Than Larger Cities

Clyde's housing stock leans toward older ranch-style construction — pier-and-beam foundations are common in the established residential streets closer to downtown, and those crawl spaces can pool water invisibly after a heavy rain event or a slow plumbing leak. Unlike slab-on-grade homes where moisture is largely confined to the surface, pier-and-beam structures allow water to migrate laterally under the floor deck, saturating wood joists and subfloor sheathing before a homeowner ever notices a soft spot underfoot.

Callahan County's clay-heavy soils compound the problem. ## Our Water Cleanup Process in Clyde

Every water cleanup job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. The crew uses thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to locate water that has traveled beyond the visible wet area — behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, beneath vinyl plank or tile. In Clyde's older ranch homes, water frequently wicks into wood framing faster than it appears on the surface, so skipping this step leads to mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of the initial loss.

Once the full extent of the moisture is mapped, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction units. Affected materials are assessed: some can be dried in place with commercial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers; others — saturated drywall below the flood line, soaked insulation, damaged subfloor sections — need to be removed to allow structural drying to complete properly. Drying is monitored daily against the IICRC S500 standard until affected assemblies reach their dry standard, and documentation is kept throughout for your insurance claim.
