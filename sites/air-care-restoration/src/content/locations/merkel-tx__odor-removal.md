## Why Merkel Properties Develop Stubborn Odors

Merkel sits in a semi-arid climate where temperature swings between seasons are dramatic. Homes heat up fast in summer and cool quickly in winter, and that thermal cycling pulls air through every gap in the building envelope. Odor molecules from a single fire, a slow gas leak, a deceased animal in a crawl space, or years of tobacco use get drawn into attic insulation, wall framing, and HVAC ductwork. Once odor compounds bond to porous materials, they off-gas slowly for months.

Older ranch-style homes in this part of Taylor County were frequently built with masonry veneer, wood-framed interior walls, and blown-in fiberglass insulation. All three materials have high surface area and absorb odor at a molecular level. Painting over smoke-stained drywall or replacing carpet without treating the subfloor and framing behind it will not eliminate the smell. It will return as soon as temperatures rise and the structure begins to breathe again.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Merkel

Effective deodorization is not a single product. It is a sequence of methods matched to the odor source, the affected materials, and the structure's ventilation characteristics.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization technology works if the source is still present. That means removing charred materials after a fire, extracting water-damaged contents that have begun to mold, or locating and removing a deceased animal. Skipping this step wastes every hour of treatment time.

**Thermal fogging.** A petroleum-based or water-based fogging agent is heated into a fine particulate cloud that penetrates the same pathways the original odor traveled. It is especially effective in wood-framed wall cavities and attic spaces where spray-on products cannot reach. In Merkel's dry climate, fog disperses quickly, which means technicians must seal the structure during treatment to allow full penetration.

**Hydroxyl generation.** Hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor compounds at the chemical level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. This method works continuously over hours or days and is well-suited to homes with pets, children, or occupants who cannot relocate for ozone treatment.

**Ozone treatment.** High-concentration ozone is one of the most effective tools for severe smoke odor and biological odors. Because ozone at treatment concentrations is harmful to occupants and pets, the structure must be vacated during the process. In Merkel's low-humidity environment, ozone disperses and reverts to oxygen relatively quickly after the generator is removed, which can shorten the re-entry waiting period compared to more humid climates.

**HVAC and duct deodorization.** In many Merkel homes, central HVAC systems run hard through summer. Odors that enter the return air path distribute throughout the entire duct network. Air Care Restoration treats duct interiors as part of a complete deodorization scope, not as an add-on.
