## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Anson

Every situation is different, and our technicians assess before they act. The general sequence follows a consistent, methodical approach:

**Scene assessment and containment** — The affected area is isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where appropriate, preventing cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. In Anson's older homes with open floor plans or shared HVAC returns, this step requires particular attention.

**Personal protective equipment and regulated waste protocols** — All materials removed from the site are handled as regulated biohazardous waste and transported in sealed, labeled containers per Texas Department of State Health Services requirements. Nothing is bagged and left curbside.

**Surface and substrate treatment** — Hospital-grade disinfectants and EPA-registered biocides are applied to all affected surfaces. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated — flooring, drywall, insulation — are removed and disposed of through licensed channels.

**Verification and documentation** — Before the space is returned to occupants, affected areas are inspected and documented. This documentation also supports insurance claims, which we help coordinate directly.
