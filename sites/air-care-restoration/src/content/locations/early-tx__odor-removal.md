## Why Early Properties Hold Odors Differently

The climate in Early and the surrounding Brown County area creates conditions that accelerate odor penetration. High summer temperatures drive volatile organic compounds deeper into porous materials: wood framing, drywall paper facing, ceiling tile, and the adhesives beneath vinyl flooring all absorb odor molecules faster when ambient heat is high. Then, when cooler weather arrives and windows stay closed, those same materials off-gas slowly into the living space.

Many homes in the Early area were built during the mid-twentieth century construction boom that followed the growth of the region, and older construction typically means less vapor barrier protection, pier-and-beam or early slab foundations with limited subfloor ventilation, and interior wall cavities that can trap smoke or moisture odors for months without the right equipment to reach them. That combination of climate and building vintage means a surface-level deodorizing spray rarely solves the problem.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Early

Every job starts with a source investigation. Odors that seem to come from a living room often originate in an adjacent wall cavity, an HVAC return, or the crawl space beneath the floor. We use moisture meters, thermal imaging where appropriate, and direct inspection to locate the actual point of contamination before any treatment begins.

Once the source is identified and any underlying damage is addressed, we select the deodorization method matched to the material and severity:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates porous surfaces the same way smoke did, neutralizing odor molecules bonded to wood, insulation, and fabric at a molecular level. It is particularly effective after fire and smoke losses where residue has migrated into wall cavities.
- **Hydroxyl generation** uses UV-light-driven chemistry to break down odor-causing compounds in the air and on surfaces without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods, which matters when a business in Early needs to resume operations quickly.
- **Ozone treatment** is deployed in unoccupied spaces where heavy biological or smoke odors require a high-concentration approach. Ozone is highly effective but requires strict re-entry protocols, and we manage that process carefully.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct deodorization** address airborne particulates and the HVAC system, which in Central Texas homes runs nearly year-round and can redistribute odors throughout the entire structure if left untreated.

All drying and deodorization work follows IICRC standards, and our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration) certification, which is directly relevant to smoke odor work.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The equipment we bring to an Early job is selected based on what the structure actually needs, not a one-size approach. Hydroxyl generators work well in occupied or semi-occupied spaces.
