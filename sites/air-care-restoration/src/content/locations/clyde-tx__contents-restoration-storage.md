## Why Clyde Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Clyde sits in a stretch of West Texas where weather can shift from dry and dusty to severe hail and thunderstorm conditions within the same afternoon. That combination creates a specific pattern of contents damage. A hail event that breaches a roof lets rain soak insulation and ceiling materials, which then drip onto furniture, electronics, and stored items below. Because the region also runs through extended dry periods, many homes rely on evaporative coolers rather than sealed HVAC systems, meaning airborne dust and smoke particulate from a fire loss can migrate further through interior spaces than it would in a tightly conditioned home.

The housing stock in and around Clyde includes a mix of older ranch-style construction and newer builds on the outskirts of town. Older homes often have open attic spaces and less vapor-resistant insulation, which means moisture from a roof breach or burst pipe can reach stored items in closets and bedrooms faster than owners expect. ## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Clyde

The process starts with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. Items that can be restored on-site are treated there; items that require deeper cleaning, deodorization, or drying are packed out using protective materials and transported to a climate-controlled facility.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material and the type of loss. Smoke and soot residue from a fire requires different chemistry and agitation than the musty odor left by standing water under flooring. Electronics, documents, artwork, and textiles each follow a separate protocol. Ultrasonic cleaning is used for hard surfaces and non-porous items; dry-cleaning and ozone or hydroxyl treatment address fabric and soft goods. Once items are cleaned and confirmed dry, they are stored in a controlled environment until your home or business is ready to receive them.

Throughout the process, the inventory log is updated and available to you and your insurance adjuster. Nothing is returned or discarded without your documented approval.
