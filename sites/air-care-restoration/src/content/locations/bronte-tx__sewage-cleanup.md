## Why Bronte Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Coke County's soil profile is a significant factor. Caliche layers beneath the surface drain poorly and can shift under frost-thaw cycles, gradually stressing buried sewer lines and septic drain fields. Many homes in and around Bronte were built in an era when cast-iron or Orangeburg pipe was standard — materials that corrode, collapse, or root-intrude over decades. When those lines fail, the backup doesn't just stop at the drain; sewage migrates under flooring, into wall cavities, and along slab seams before a homeowner notices the smell.

Septic systems are common here because municipal sewer hookups don't reach every property in Coke County. A septic tank that hasn't been pumped on schedule, or a drain field that's been saturated by an unusually wet season, can overflow in ways that contaminate a wide footprint of soil and structure simultaneously. That's a different cleanup challenge than a simple sewer line backup in a city with centralized infrastructure.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bronte

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated category under industry standards — and every step of the process reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first priority is stopping the source and establishing containment so contamination doesn't spread to unaffected rooms.

From there, the work follows a deliberate sequence:

- **Extraction and removal** — Standing sewage and saturated materials are removed using truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials like carpet, pad, and drywall that have contacted raw sewage are bagged and disposed of; they cannot be reliably sanitized.
- **Surface cleaning and disinfection** — Hard surfaces, subfloors, and framing are cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobials. We pay particular attention to concrete slabs and the gaps around plumbing penetrations, where sewage wicks into areas that look dry at the surface.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — After sanitization, the structure needs to reach normal moisture levels before any rebuilding begins. In Bronte's summer heat, ambient temperatures can accelerate surface drying while trapping moisture deeper in framing — equipment monitoring keeps the process honest.
- **Odor elimination** — Sewage odor isn't cosmetic. It signals residual organic matter. We use hydroxyl or thermal fogging as appropriate to address odor at the source rather than masking it.

Our team holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, and the process follows established Category 3 loss protocols throughout.
