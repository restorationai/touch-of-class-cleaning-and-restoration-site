## Why Eden Properties Hold Odors Longer

Concho County's low humidity is a double-edged factor. On one hand, surfaces dry quickly after a water event. On the other, porous materials like the caliche-based plaster common in older Eden homes and the unfinished wood framing found in mid-century ranch structures absorb odor-causing compounds deeply, then resist releasing them into the air where deodorization equipment can reach them. Smoke residue from a wood-burning stove, pet dander embedded in original hardwood subfloors, or the musty signature of a crawl space that flooded during a Concho River rise can all calcify into the building envelope over time.

Ranch-style homes built before the 1980s often lack the vapor barriers and mechanical ventilation that help modern construction breathe. That means odors from a single event, whether a sewage backup, a wildfire smoke intrusion, or a long-undetected animal carcass in an attic, can migrate through wall cavities and persist even after the visible source is removed.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Eden

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying exactly what compound is causing the problem, because the treatment method depends on the source. Smoke odors from structure fires require a different approach than biological odors from sewage or decomposition, and a technique that works on one can be ineffective or even counterproductive on the other.

Our IICRC-certified process typically moves through these stages:

- **Source removal**: Physical cleaning and removal of odor-saturated materials before any deodorization begins. Applying chemistry to a contaminated surface without removing the source only delays the problem.
- **Thermal fogging**: A heated fog penetrates porous surfaces, following the same pathways smoke or moisture used to get in. This is particularly effective in Eden's older wood-framed structures where odors have migrated deep into framing cavities.
- **Hydroxyl or ozone treatment**: Hydroxyl generators are safe to operate in occupied or semi-occupied spaces and work continuously over time. Ozone treatment is more aggressive and requires the space to be vacated, but can be appropriate for severe smoke or biological odor cases in unoccupied structures.
- **Encapsulation or sealant application**: When odor compounds have penetrated subfloor sheathing or ceiling drywall beyond what surface treatment can reach, an odor-blocking sealant applied to the affected substrate prevents off-gassing from continuing after the job is complete.

All work is documented with before-and-after readings using calibrated air quality meters, giving you a record for insurance purposes.
