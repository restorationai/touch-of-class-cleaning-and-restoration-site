## Why Merkel Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Storm Damage

Merkel sits on the Rolling Plains of West Texas, a landscape with minimal natural windbreaks and a climate that cycles through extremes — scorching summers, sudden cold fronts, and severe thunderstorm seasons that can produce large hail, damaging winds, and tornadoes in the same afternoon. The housing stock in and around Merkel leans toward older single-story construction, much of it built with wood-frame techniques common in mid-twentieth-century rural Texas. Those homes often have original roof decking, aging fascia boards, and soffit vents that weren't designed to handle the kind of sustained wind loads that modern building codes require. A single severe cell can compromise the roof envelope enough that the next rain event — even a modest one — drives water into the attic insulation, down into wall cavities, and across subfloor sheathing before the damage is visible from inside.

Flat or gently sloping terrain around Merkel also means storm runoff has nowhere to go quickly. After a heavy rain event, water pools against foundations and works into crawl spaces and slab perimeters, compounding the structural exposure that wind damage already created.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Merkel

Every storm job starts with a thorough exterior and interior assessment — not just a visual walk-around, but moisture readings taken at the wall cavities, attic decking, and any flooring adjacent to exterior walls. We document everything with photographs and written scope notes, which matters enormously when you're filing a claim with your carrier.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence:

- **Emergency tarping and board-up** to stop active weather intrusion at the roof, broken windows, or compromised siding
- **Water extraction and structural drying** using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient conditions — West Texas low humidity actually helps drying times, but only if equipment is placed correctly and monitored
- **Debris removal** — tree limbs, roofing material, insulation that has been saturated or displaced
- **Structural assessment and repair coordination** — we work alongside licensed contractors for framing, roofing, and siding replacement so the restoration and rebuild happen in sequence, not in conflict
- **Final moisture verification** before any new drywall, insulation, or finish materials go in

As an IICRC Certified Firm with OSHA-trained technicians, we dry to the IICRC S500 standard and document every reading so there's no question about whether the structure was properly dried before reconstruction began.
