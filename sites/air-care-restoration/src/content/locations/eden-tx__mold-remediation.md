## Why Eden Properties See Mold Issues

Concho County's caliche-heavy soil drains poorly after heavy rain events, and slab foundations common in Eden's housing stock can wick ground moisture upward when drainage grades shift over time. Homes built in the mid-twentieth century — a significant portion of Eden's residential inventory — often used kraft-faced insulation and vapor barriers that degrade, leaving wall cavities vulnerable once any moisture source is introduced. A slow drip behind a kitchen cabinet, a roof flashing failure after a spring hailstorm, or condensation around aging window frames can be enough. Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure, and in a climate that swings between high humidity and intense heat, the interior of a wall can stay damp long after the surface feels dry to the touch.

Small-town water infrastructure also plays a role. Eden's water lines, some of which date back several decades, can develop slow pinhole leaks at fittings inside walls — the kind that don't spike a water bill noticeably but saturate framing over weeks. By the time discoloration appears on drywall or a floor starts to feel soft underfoot, the mold colony is already established.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Eden

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — moisture meters, thermal imaging where warranted, and a visual assessment of all suspect areas including attic decking, crawl spaces, and HVAC air handler compartments. We identify the moisture source first, because remediation without source correction is temporary at best.

Once the scope is clear, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed and bagged for proper disposal. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and physically scrubbed; mold cannot be killed in place on a porous substrate, so removal is the standard, not the exception.

Drying and dehumidification follow to bring structural moisture content down to acceptable levels before any rebuild begins. In Eden's climate, where outdoor humidity can climb sharply during spring storm season, we monitor indoor conditions carefully and adjust equipment accordingly rather than relying on a fixed drying timeline.

Because the team is IICRC certified and EPA Lead-Safe certified, work in older Eden homes where lead paint may be present follows the required safe-work practices — relevant for any pre-1978 structure undergoing material removal.

## Eden Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in Texas can be complex. Coverage depends on whether the mold resulted from a sudden, covered peril — like a burst pipe — or from long-term seepage that a carrier may characterize as a maintenance issue. We document the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected materials, and a written scope of work that gives your adjuster the detail needed to process the claim. ## Local Note

One pattern that shows up in Eden-area homes specifically: the combination of metal roofing — popular in Concho County for its longevity in hail-prone weather — and minimal attic ventilation can create significant condensation on the underside of the decking during winter temperature swings. Homeowners often attribute the resulting attic mold to a roof leak and are surprised when a roofer finds no penetration. The source is condensation, not infiltration, and the fix involves both the mold remediation and correcting the ventilation before the next cold season. If your attic smells musty after winter, that is worth investigating before summer heat bakes the growth deeper into the decking.
