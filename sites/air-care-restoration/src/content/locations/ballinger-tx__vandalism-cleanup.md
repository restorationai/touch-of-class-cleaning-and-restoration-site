## Why Ballinger Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The building stock in Ballinger leans older, with a significant share of downtown commercial blocks and residential properties built from the mid-twentieth century or earlier. That era of construction favored painted brick, concrete block, and stucco exteriors, surfaces that look solid but are more porous than they appear. Aerosol paint penetrates the surface layer quickly, especially in summer when daytime temperatures regularly push past 100°F and the masonry is bone dry. By the time a property owner notices the damage the next morning, the paint has already begun to cure into the substrate.

Rural and semi-rural properties around Runnels County also tend to have longer response windows from local law enforcement, which means vandalism events sometimes compound overnight. A single broken window can become a point of entry, and what starts as graffiti cleanup can reveal interior disturbance that needs to be documented carefully before any cleaning begins.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Ballinger

Every vandalism cleanup job starts with a walk-through and documentation pass before anything is touched. Photographs, measurements, and written notes create a record that matters both for insurance claims and for any law enforcement follow-up. Only after that baseline is established do we move into active remediation.

For graffiti on masonry and stucco, the removal method depends on the paint type, the surface porosity, and how long the paint has been curing. Chemical desiccants and low-pressure washing work well on newer applications; older or heat-set paint on rough brick may require mechanical surface treatment followed by a sealer coat to prevent ghosting. We carry equipment suited to both approaches.

Broken glass cleanup follows a strict containment protocol. Shards are collected and bagged from the interior outward, frames are inspected for structural integrity, and openings are boarded with plywood to weather-code thickness before we leave the site. If the property has lead-based paint around older window frames, our EPA Lead-Safe certification covers the handling and disposal requirements that apply under Texas regulations.

Interior disturbance, when present, is assessed separately. Overturned fixtures, damaged drywall, and debris are documented, categorized, and removed in a sequence that preserves evidence and keeps the site safe for re-entry.
