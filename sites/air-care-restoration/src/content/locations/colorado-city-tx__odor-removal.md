The same dry heat that makes Mitchell County summers punishing also accelerates how deeply odor molecules penetrate porous building materials. ## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Colorado City

Every job starts with a source assessment. Masking an odor without identifying where it originates is a short-term fix that fails within weeks. The crew identifies the contaminated materials, documents the scope, and then selects the appropriate deodorization method for the situation.

For smoke odor removal after a fire or wildfire event, thermal fogging is often the right tool. A fogging agent is heated into a fine mist that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules at the molecular level rather than coating surfaces. For ongoing or biological odors, pet urine, mold, decomposition, hydroxyl deodorization runs continuously over a period of days, breaking down odor-causing compounds without requiring the space to be vacated the way ozone treatment does. Ozone treatment remains an effective option for unoccupied structures where deep penetration is the priority.

The team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) certification, which means the approach to smoke odor removal follows documented industry standards rather than guesswork. Where lead-containing materials are present in older Colorado City homes, the crew's EPA Lead-Safe certification governs how disturbed surfaces are handled during the process.
