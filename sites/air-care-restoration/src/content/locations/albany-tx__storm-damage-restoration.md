Shackelford County sits squarely in a corridor where dry-line thunderstorms fire regularly from late spring through early fall. ## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Albany

The first priority after any storm is stopping ongoing water intrusion. That means emergency tarping and board-up before a single piece of damaged material is removed. Covering a breached roof or broken window within the first few hours is the difference between a contained repair and a weeks-long drying project.

Once the structure is stabilized, the assessment phase begins. Using moisture meters and thermal imaging, the crew maps every wet zone — including areas that feel dry at the surface but are holding moisture behind plaster, inside wall cavities, or under flooring. Water that isn't found and extracted in the first 24 to 48 hours creates conditions for mold growth, which turns a storm claim into a more complicated and costly remediation job.

Structural drying follows extraction, using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers calibrated to the room volume and material type. As an IICRC Certified Firm, the drying process follows the S500 standard for water damage, which means documented moisture readings at each stage — data that also supports your insurance claim. Debris removal, structural repairs, and coordination with roofing and general contractors round out the process through to a finished, livable space.
