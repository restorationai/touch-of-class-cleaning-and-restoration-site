When a grass fire along the I-20 corridor jumps a fence line or a kitchen fire fills a farmhouse with soot, the smoke doesn't stay where the flames were. In Merkel, where many homes sit on larger rural lots with older HVAC systems and wood-frame construction, smoke residue travels through return air ducts and settles into insulation, fabric, and wood grain long before anyone calls for help. Getting ahead of that spread, before odor and acidic soot bond permanently to surfaces, is the difference between a cleanable loss and a full material replacement.

## Why Merkel Properties See Smoke Damage Issues

Merkel sits in Taylor County's dry, wind-exposed stretch of West Texas, where summer heat and periodic drought conditions raise wildfire risk across the grassland and mesquite acreage surrounding town. That regional fire pattern means smoke damage calls here aren't limited to kitchen fires and electrical shorts, wildfire smoke infiltration through attic vents, window units, and older weatherstripping is a real and recurring issue. Many Merkel homes were built decades ago with original ductwork and single-pane windows, both of which let smoke particulate migrate further into a structure than it would in a newer, tightly sealed build. Add in agricultural dust that's already present in barns, outbuildings, and older farmhouses, and soot tends to bind with existing residue faster, making surface cleaning alone ineffective.

## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Merkel

We start with a walkthrough to identify smoke type, wet smoke from a slow-burning fire behaves differently than the dry, wind-carried soot from a wildfire event, and that distinction changes which cleaning agents and equipment we bring in. Affected rooms get sealed off to stop cross-contamination while we run HEPA air scrubbers to pull particulate out of the air. From there, our crews clean hard surfaces, textiles, and HVAC components using methods matched to the material, thermal fogging and ozone or hydroxyl treatment where odor has penetrated porous surfaces like drywall and open-grain wood, common in Merkel's older housing stock. Content cleaning is handled on-site when possible; anything requiring off-site ultrasonic cleaning is documented and tracked separately for the insurance file.

## Merkel Insurance Coordination

A lot of the structures we see in and around Merkel are insured under farm and ranch policies rather than standard homeowner's policies, which sometimes carry different documentation requirements for outbuildings, equipment, and detached structures affected by smoke. We photograph and log damage room by room, including any outbuildings or storage structures caught in the smoke path, and provide that documentation directly to the carrier handling the claim. If a claim involves a volunteer fire department incident report, we request a copy early since it often speeds up adjuster approval on affected square footage.

## Local note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Merkel: window-unit air conditioners, still common in older homes and outbuildings here, recirculate smoke particulate every time they cycle, even after the visible haze clears from a room. If a unit ran during or shortly after a fire or nearby wildfire smoke event, we check and often clean or replace the filter and coil housing as part of the job, otherwise the unit keeps reintroducing odor into a room we've already cleaned.
