Abilene's climate is hard on homes — and even harder on everything inside them. When a West Texas dust storm, a summer hailstorm, or a burst pipe during one of the region's sharp winter cold snaps damages your property, the furniture, clothing, documents, and keepsakes inside take the hit right alongside the structure. Air Care Restoration handles the full cycle: careful pack-out, professional cleaning and restoration, climate-controlled storage, and coordinated return — so your belongings aren't an afterthought while the building gets fixed.

## Why Abilene Properties See Contents Damage

Abiline sits in a part of Texas where the weather swings hard in both directions. Summers push well past 100°F, winters drop below freezing with little warning, and the wind carries fine caliche dust through gaps that newer construction in more humid climates would never leave open. That combination creates a specific pattern of contents damage:

- **Fire and smoke losses** — dry conditions mean fires spread fast, and smoke residue from a structure fire in a single-story ranch home (a common housing type across Abilene) penetrates fabric, wood grain, and paper within hours.
- **Water intrusion after freeze events** — when temperatures drop suddenly, older copper and galvanized supply lines in mid-century homes fail at fittings. Standing water under flooring can read dry at the surface while the subfloor stays saturated, and anything stored at floor level absorbs that moisture quickly.
- **Dust and particulate contamination** — a windstorm that cracks a window or lifts a roof vent cap can deposit a fine layer of West Texas dust across an entire room's worth of upholstered furniture and electronics.

Contents restoration in this environment isn't a one-size approach. The cleaning chemistry, the drying protocol, and the storage conditions all need to account for what Abilene's climate actually does to materials.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process

Every contents job starts with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves the property. We photograph, tag, and log each piece — furniture, art, clothing, documents, electronics — so there's a verifiable chain of custody from pack-out to return.

**Pack-out**: Items are wrapped, boxed, and loaded with the same care used for a fine-art move. Fragile pieces get individual padding; upholstered furniture is wrapped to prevent cross-contamination from smoke or mold spores during transport.

**Cleaning and restoration**: Depending on the loss type, we use ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics components, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke odor in soft contents, and controlled drying for water-saturated documents and books. Smoke residue from a fast-moving grass or structure fire — the kind Abilene sees in dry spring conditions — often requires multiple cleaning passes because soot chemistry differs from the slow-smoldering smoke of a longer burn.

**Climate-controlled storage**: Restored items are held in a temperature- and humidity-controlled environment while structural repairs are completed. In Abilene's summer heat, uncontrolled storage can re-damage items that have already been cleaned — particularly wood furniture, which will warp or crack if cycled through extreme heat repeatedly.

**Return and placement**: When the structure is ready, we coordinate delivery and room placement, working from the original inventory so nothing ends up in the wrong room.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier in Abilene

Contents losses are often the most disputed part of a property claim. Carriers want documentation; adjusters want line-item detail. Our inventory process is built around that reality — every item is photographed, described, and assigned a cleaning or replacement cost before we begin work. We communicate directly with your adjuster and provide the documentation package they need to process the contents portion of your claim alongside the structural scope.

If your policy includes Additional Living Expense (ALE) coverage, storage costs during the repair period are typically a covered line item. We can provide the storage invoices in the format most carriers request.

## Local Note

Abiline's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built between the 1940s and 1970s, and those properties often have original hardwood floors, plaster walls, and built-in cabinetry that homeowners want to preserve rather than replace. Plaster, unlike modern drywall, holds odor compounds differently — smoke and moisture penetrate the surface layer but can linger in the substrate long after the surface appears clean. When we're packing out contents from one of these older homes, we flag items that have been in prolonged contact with plaster walls or original wood trim, because those pieces may need extended treatment cycles to fully clear the odor rather than a single-pass clean.
