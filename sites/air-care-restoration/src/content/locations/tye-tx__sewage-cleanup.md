## Why Tye Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Tye sits just west of Abilene along the US-83/84 corridor, and much of its residential development followed the post-war boom of the 1950s through the 1970s. Homes built during that era commonly used cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals — materials that corrode, collapse, or root-intrude over decades. When a lateral fails, the blockage doesn't just affect one fixture; the entire drain stack backs up, and Category 3 water (raw sewage) can surface through floor drains, toilets, and tub drains simultaneously.

The region's expansive clay soils compound the problem. During dry West Texas summers, the ground contracts and can shift pipe joints out of alignment. When heavy rain finally arrives — often in concentrated storms rather than steady rain — saturated soil can push groundwater and surface runoff into cracked sewer lines, overwhelming the system. Septic systems in the less-densely served areas of Tye face similar pressure: a saturated drain field after a hard rain has nowhere to send effluent, and overflow into the home or yard follows.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Tye

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 contaminated water, which means standard wet-vac extraction is only the first step — not the last. The process we follow is calibrated to the IICRC S500 standard and includes:

**Containment and safety assessment** — Before extraction begins, we identify the source and confirm it is controlled (line cleared or septic pumped). Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

**Bulk extraction and debris removal** — Standing sewage, saturated materials, and any porous contents that cannot be safely restored are removed. In older Tye homes with original hardwood or vinyl tile floors, we assess each material individually rather than applying a blanket removal policy.

**Structural drying** — After extraction, sub-floor assemblies and wall cavities are dried using desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers. In homes with pier-and-beam foundations — common in parts of the Tye area — the crawl space is treated as a separate drying zone, because moisture trapped beneath the subfloor will migrate back upward if ignored.

**EPA-registered disinfection and deodorization** — Every affected surface is treated with hospital-grade, EPA-registered disinfectants. Odor from sewage contamination is not cosmetic; it signals active bacterial presence. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment is used when odor has penetrated wall cavities or HVAC pathways.

**Post-remediation verification** — Moisture readings and, where indicated, surface testing confirm that affected areas are dry and sanitary before reconstruction begins.
