## Why Abilene Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After Storm or Fire Damage

The West Texas climate creates a specific set of risks that aren't common in more humid or coastal markets. Abilene sits in a zone where severe thunderstorms can produce large hail, damaging winds, and occasionally tornadoes in the same event — sometimes with little warning. The region's low average humidity means that after a roof breach, exposed structural wood dries unevenly: surface layers can feel stable while deeper framing members remain stressed from the initial impact and then crack further as temperatures swing between day and night. That thermal cycling is particularly hard on older masonry construction, which makes up a meaningful share of Abilene's housing stock — brick veneer homes built through the mid-twentieth century can develop secondary cracking at mortar joints when left exposed after a storm breach.

Fire damage introduces a different urgency. After a structure fire, charred wall cavities and compromised window frames are open invitations for wind-driven rain and opportunistic entry. The IICRC FSRT standard that guides our fire and smoke response recognizes that secondary water damage from weather intrusion after a fire is one of the most preventable sources of additional loss — which is exactly what board-up and tarping is designed to stop.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Abilene

When you call (325) 339-8723, the first question we ask is what type of opening we're dealing with — roof breach, broken window, compromised door frame, or structural wall damage — because the materials and anchoring method differ for each. A roof tarp installed over damaged decking in Abilene needs to be weighted and anchored differently than one in a low-wind market; the same storm that caused the breach can tear a poorly secured tarp loose within hours.

For window and door board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut to fit the opening and fastened in a way that meets the structural intent of the opening without causing additional damage to surrounding framing. For roof tarping, we anchor over the ridge where possible so the tarp sheds water rather than pooling it — pooled water on a compromised roof deck accelerates the very damage you're trying to stop. Every secured opening is photographed before and after, which feeds directly into the insurance documentation package.

## Working With Your Insurance Carrier After an Abilene Loss

Most homeowner policies in Texas include coverage for reasonable emergency protective measures — board-up and tarping typically falls under that language. We photograph all damage before we touch anything, document the scope of each opening we secure, and provide a written summary formatted for adjuster review. If your carrier sends an independent adjuster, the documentation we provide gives them a clear pre-mitigation baseline so there's no dispute about what the structure looked like when we arrived.

If your property is financed, your lender's insurance requirements may also specify that the structure be secured promptly after a covered loss — another reason not to wait on board-up when a storm or fire has left openings in the envelope.

## Local Note

Across Abilene's older residential areas, homes built with original single-wythe brick construction — common in neighborhoods developed from the 1940s through the 1960s — present a specific challenge for window board-up after impact damage. The brick surrounds on these windows are often load-bearing or partially load-bearing, and driving fasteners into mortar joints without care can widen existing cracks. Our crews are trained to use surface-mounted framing that distributes load across the opening rather than relying on the masonry itself, which matters both for preserving the structure and for keeping the adjuster's scope clean.
