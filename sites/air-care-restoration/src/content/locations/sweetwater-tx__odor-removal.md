## Why Sweetwater Properties Hold Odors Longer

The West Texas climate is the first factor to understand. Sweetwater's low humidity for much of the year means odor-causing molecules bind tightly to dry wood fibers, drywall, and insulation rather than being carried away by moisture movement. When a fire, a long-term pet situation, or a decomposition event occurs, those molecules embed themselves at a depth that surface sprays and candles cannot reach.

The region's housing stock adds another layer. A significant portion of Sweetwater's residential properties were built in the mid-twentieth century, when construction relied on plaster and lathe walls, real hardwood subfloors, and less vapor-controlled insulation. Those older materials are more absorbent than modern assemblies. Smoke and biological odors penetrate into wall cavities and floor voids that are genuinely difficult to access without the right equipment. Crawl spaces under pier-and-beam homes, common in older parts of the city, trap odors and recirculate them through gaps in the floor system every time the HVAC runs.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Sweetwater

Effective odor elimination is not a single step. The process starts with identifying every source, because masking one pocket of odor while another remains active produces a result that fails within weeks.

Once sources are confirmed, the approach is matched to the material and the type of odor:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates porous surfaces the same way smoke originally did, neutralizing odor molecules bonded deep in wood, drywall, and fabric. It is especially effective in rooms where fire or heavy smoke exposure occurred.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor compounds in the air and on surfaces. Hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner needs to stay nearby during treatment.
- **Ozone treatment** is used in unoccupied spaces where a high-concentration oxidizing agent can work without exposure risk. The space must be vacated during treatment and ventilated before re-entry.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs continuously during and after treatment to capture particulates that carry odor, including smoke soot and biological debris.

The team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) certification, which means the process follows a documented standard rather than a technician's best guess. After treatment, affected areas are re-evaluated before the job is closed out.
