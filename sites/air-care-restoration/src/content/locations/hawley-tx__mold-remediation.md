## Why Hawley Properties See Mold Problems

The housing stock in Hawley skews toward mid-century construction, which means pier-and-beam foundations, original wood subfloors, and wall cavities that were never designed with vapor barriers in mind. When a roof leak, plumbing drip, or HVAC condensate line failure pushes moisture into those cavities, the wood stays damp long after the surface feels dry to the touch. Mold colonies can begin forming within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure, and in a pier-and-beam home, the underside of the subfloor is often the first place growth takes hold because it is the last place anyone looks.

Jones County also sits in a transition zone where dry spells are broken by episodic heavy rainfall. When that rain drives against older exterior walls or backs up through deteriorating window seals, the moisture load on interior framing can be significant. Homes that have had previous water intrusion events, even ones that appeared to dry out on their own, are especially vulnerable because residual organic material in the wood gives mold a ready food source the next time conditions turn wet.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hawley

Every job starts with a thorough assessment before any material is touched. Technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of saturation, because visible mold growth is rarely the whole picture. In older Hawley homes with plaster or original drywall, moisture can wick laterally through wall cavities well beyond the stained area you can see.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment to prevent spores from migrating to clean parts of the home during removal. Contaminated porous materials, drywall, insulation, subfloor sections, are removed and bagged following IICRC S520 protocols. Structural framing that can be salvaged is treated and dried to documented moisture levels. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process. The work is performed by an OSHA-trained crew operating under Air Care Restoration's IICRC-certified firm standards, and the company's EPA Lead-Safe certification matters in Hawley's older housing stock where disturbing walls can mean disturbing lead-based paint at the same time.
