## Why San Angelo Properties See Water Damage Issues

The Concho River corridor and the low-lying areas around Lake Nasworthy are the most obvious flood-risk zones, but the majority of water damage calls in San Angelo come from inside the structure, not outside it. Older homes throughout the city — many built on post-tension slabs or pier-and-beam foundations common to this part of Tom Green County — develop supply-line failures and drain-line cracks as the expansive clay soil shifts seasonally. When the ground contracts in a drought and then rapidly absorbs a 2-inch rain event, that movement stresses every penetration point in the slab.

San Angelo also experiences hard freezes — sometimes with little warning — that can burst exposed supply lines in garages, under-insulated exterior walls, and irrigation systems. The city's older housing stock often has copper or galvanized plumbing that has been repaired and re-repaired over the decades, leaving joints that are one temperature swing away from failing.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in San Angelo

Every water damage job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not a visual walk-through. Our technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through walls, flooring, and subfloor assemblies before a single piece of equipment is placed. In a slab-on-grade home, water can travel 10 to 15 feet from the source along the vapor barrier before surfacing, which means the wet carpet you see is rarely where the problem ends.

Once the scope is documented, we move into water extraction using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, followed by a drying system engineered to the specific structure — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, and targeted heat when conditions call for it. Drying progress is logged daily against IICRC S500 drying goals so there is a defensible record for your insurance carrier and no guesswork about when materials have reached acceptable moisture content. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — saturated insulation, buckled hardwood, compromised drywall — are removed and documented before the structure is closed.

## San Angelo Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in Texas cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an ice-dam backup — but the documentation burden falls on the policyholder to prove the loss was sudden, not gradual. Our moisture mapping reports, photo documentation, and drying logs are structured to meet the evidentiary standard that Texas carriers and their third-party adjusters look for. We communicate directly with your adjuster throughout the job so scope disputes are resolved with data, not phone-tag.

If your property is a rental or a commercial building, the coordination process is similar but often involves a property manager as the primary contact. We can work with whoever holds the policy and whoever is on-site — the goal is a clean claim file and a dried, documented structure.

## Local Note

San Angelo's clay-heavy soils mean that after a significant water intrusion event, the ground immediately adjacent to a slab foundation can stay saturated for days even after interior drying is complete. That exterior moisture continues to push vapor through the slab, which can stall interior drying readings and make a structure appear dry when it isn't. On jobs in this area, we extend monitoring through the exterior drying cycle and sometimes recommend a perimeter drainage assessment before the final moisture check — a step that matters here in a way it simply doesn't in cities built on sandier soils.
