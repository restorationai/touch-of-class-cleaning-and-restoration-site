## Why Roby-Area Properties Carry Asbestos Risk

The housing stock in Roby and across Fisher County skews older. Ranch-style homes built between the 1940s and the early 1980s commonly contain asbestos in places that are easy to overlook: the backing on vinyl floor tiles, the joint compound between drywall sheets, the duct insulation on original HVAC systems, and the blown-in or sprayed ceiling texture that was popular through the 1970s. In a dry climate like West Texas, those materials can remain intact for decades, which sounds reassuring until a remodel, a roof repair, or a hail storm introduces mechanical disturbance. Once disturbed, asbestos fibers become airborne and the risk profile changes entirely.

The region's persistent wind is also a practical concern during abatement work. Containment that would hold under calm conditions in a more sheltered urban environment needs to be engineered more carefully here, where a stiff south wind can put negative-pressure enclosures under real stress. That is a site condition our crews account for before work begins, not after.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Roby

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and, where suspect materials are present, sample collection for laboratory analysis. We do not skip the testing phase to save time, because knowing exactly what you are dealing with determines every decision that follows.

Once results confirm the presence of asbestos-containing materials, the work sequence follows a structured protocol:

- **Containment setup**: Plastic sheeting, negative air pressure units with HEPA filtration, and regulated entry and exit points are established before any material is touched.
- **Wet removal**: Suspect materials are wetted to suppress fiber release before removal. Dry cutting or dry demolition is not part of our process.
- **Waste handling**: All removed material is double-bagged in approved containers, labeled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. Texas regulations govern every step of that chain.
- **Air clearance**: Post-abatement air sampling confirms fiber levels have returned to acceptable thresholds before containment is removed and the space is released.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, we test before any scraping begins. A ceiling that looks like standard texture from the 1970s may contain chrysotile asbestos, and the only way to know is a lab result.
