## Why Ballinger Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Runnels County's housing stock skews older. Many homes in and around Ballinger were built during the mid-20th century, when construction standards differed significantly from today. The West Texas heat compounds this. At summer temperatures common to the Ballinger area, biological material degrades rapidly, and the associated microbial risk escalates in proportion. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Ballinger

Every biohazard call is different, and the process is calibrated to what the property actually needs — not a checklist applied uniformly. In general terms, the work follows a consistent framework:

**Assessment and containment** come first. The affected area is evaluated to determine the full scope, including adjacent surfaces and HVAC pathways that may have been exposed. Containment barriers go up before any material is disturbed.

**Removal and decontamination** follow, using EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological hazard. All materials — including porous surfaces that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard — are removed, packaged, and disposed of in compliance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations for biohazardous waste.

**Verification** closes the process. We don't declare a space clean based on visual inspection alone. ATP testing and, where warranted, air sampling confirm that pathogen levels are within safe limits before the area is returned to use.

All crew members are OSHA trained, and Air Care Restoration holds IICRC certification — the same standards that govern water and fire restoration apply equally to our biohazard work.
