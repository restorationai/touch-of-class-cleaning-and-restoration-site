## Why Paint Rock Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Concho County's housing stock skews older. Many homes and ranch structures in and around Paint Rock were built during the postwar construction boom of the 1940s through the 1970s — the exact window when asbestos was used most heavily in residential and light commercial construction. Popcorn ceilings, floor tile adhesives, pipe insulation, duct wrap, and roofing felt from that era routinely contained asbestos fibers. In the dry, stable climate of this part of West Texas, those materials often remain physically intact decades later, which is both a blessing and a complication: intact asbestos is generally lower risk than friable material, but it still requires professional assessment before any disturbance.

Ranch properties and older agricultural outbuildings in the Paint Rock area sometimes contain corrugated asbestos-cement roofing panels, a material that was widely used for its durability in hot, low-humidity environments. Demolition or repair of those structures without abatement creates a fiber release risk that standard contractors aren't equipped to manage.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Paint Rock

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples go to an accredited laboratory — results guide the scope of work before any physical removal begins. This step is non-negotiable and protects both the occupants and the property owner from liability.

Once testing confirms asbestos-containing material, the abatement sequence follows Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS) requirements, which govern notification, containment, removal, and disposal for regulated asbestos projects in the state. The work area is isolated with poly sheeting and negative air pressure equipment to prevent fiber migration into unaffected spaces. Workers wear full protective equipment throughout, and all removed material is sealed in labeled, leak-tight containers for transport to an approved disposal facility.

After removal, air clearance sampling confirms fiber levels have returned to background before containment is broken. That final clearance step is what separates a properly closed abatement project from one that simply looks finished.

## Navigating Texas Asbestos Regulations for Paint Rock Projects

Texas requires that abatement work on regulated quantities of asbestos-containing material be performed by licensed asbestos abatement contractors and that projects above certain thresholds be reported to DSHS before work begins. For property owners in Paint Rock, this means that simply hiring a general contractor to demo an older popcorn ceiling or rip out floor tile isn't sufficient — the regulatory pathway requires licensed abatement professionals and proper disposal documentation. ## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically in Paint Rock and the broader Concho County area: older adobe-style and stone-and-mortar construction sometimes used asbestos-containing textured coatings on interior walls as a fire-resistant finish layer.
