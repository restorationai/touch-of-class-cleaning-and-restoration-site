## Why Albany Properties Hold Odors Differently

Albany sits in a part of West Texas where the climate does odors no favors. Low humidity in the summer months causes smoke and biological odors to concentrate in fibrous materials rather than dissipate, while the occasional wet winter can trap musty smells inside older homes that were never designed with vapor barriers in mind. Many residences in and around Albany were built in the mid-20th century using construction methods that included real plaster, solid wood millwork, and cellulose insulation, all of which absorb odor molecules deeply and release them slowly over weeks or months if not treated at the source.

Ranching and agricultural activity in the county also means that animal odors, whether from livestock tracked inside, a deceased animal under a pier-and-beam foundation, or a barn-adjacent property, are a more common call here than in suburban markets. That context shapes how we approach a job before we ever unload the truck.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Albany

Effective odor elimination isn't a single step. The process starts with identifying the odor source and the materials it has penetrated, because a thermal fog applied over an untreated substrate will mask the smell for a week and then lose the battle. Our IICRC FSRT-certified technicians assess the affected areas, remove or treat the source material where possible, and then select the right deodorization method for the specific odor type and surface.

For smoke odor, thermal fogging is often the right tool because the fog particles match the size of smoke particles and penetrate the same cavities the smoke reached. For biological odors, hydroxyl generators work well in occupied or sensitive environments because they produce no harmful residual. Ozone treatment is reserved for unoccupied spaces where a higher-concentration approach is appropriate and safe. In Albany's older housing stock, we pay particular attention to attic spaces and subfloor cavities, areas where odor-laden air stratifies and where standard surface treatments never reach.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Not every odor job calls for the same equipment, and bringing the wrong tool wastes time and money. The methods Air Care Restoration deploys in Albany include:

- **Thermal fogging**: Converts deodorizing solution into a dry fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled through wall cavities and structural voids.
- **Hydroxyl generation**: Safe for use around occupants, pets, and electronics. Useful when a property cannot be vacated.
- **Ozone treatment**: High-output ozone is effective on heavy smoke and biological odors in unoccupied structures, with proper airing-out protocols before re-entry.
- **Vapor phase deodorization**: Targets airborne odor molecules throughout a space, particularly useful in HVAC-connected environments.
- **Direct application and encapsulation**: For surfaces like concrete slabs or subfloor decking that have absorbed biological material, direct-applied enzyme treatments and sealers address what airborne methods cannot.

The method, or combination of methods, is chosen based on the odor source, the building materials involved, and whether the property can be vacated during treatment.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors working in Albany is the pier-and-beam foundation common on older properties in town. When an animal dies under the house or a slow plumbing leak creates a mold and odor problem in the subfloor, the crawl space access is often narrow and the ground is caliche hardpan, which doesn't absorb spilled biological material the way soil does in wetter climates. That means the source material and contaminated ground cover both need to be addressed, not just the structure above. Skipping the crawl space and only treating the interior floors is a reliable way to have the odor return within a month.
