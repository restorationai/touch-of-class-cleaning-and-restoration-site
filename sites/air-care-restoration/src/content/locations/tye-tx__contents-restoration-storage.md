## Why Tye Properties See Contents Damage Compound Quickly

Taylor County's climate is hard on building materials and personal property alike. The soil in this part of West Texas is highly expansive clay, which shifts with moisture cycles and puts stress on slab foundations. When a slab shifts and a pipe joint cracks, water can spread under flooring before anyone notices it at the surface. By the time a homeowner calls, upholstered furniture, area rugs, and stored documents have been sitting in elevated humidity long enough for odor and early microbial activity to take hold.

Fire and smoke losses carry their own regional wrinkle. The combination of low humidity and high heat means smoke residue dries and bonds to surfaces faster here than in more humid climates. Porous items like books, clothing, and upholstered pieces need to move off-site quickly so cleaning can begin before residue sets permanently.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Tye

Every contents job starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves the property. Each item is photographed, cataloged, and assigned a condition code so nothing is unaccounted for during transport or storage. That documentation also becomes part of your insurance claim file.

From there, the process follows the nature of the loss:

- **Pack-out and transport**: Items are wrapped and boxed using materials rated for the specific damage type. Smoke-affected contents are sealed to prevent cross-contamination during transit.
- **Cleaning and deodorization**: Depending on the item, this may involve ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for textiles and soft goods, or dry-cleaning referral for delicate fabrics. Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration) certification, so the method matches the residue type rather than a one-size approach.
- **Climate-controlled storage**: Contents are held in a controlled environment until your property is cleared for return. In Tye's summer heat, uncontrolled storage would simply continue the damage.
- **Return and placement**: Items are returned in the same organized condition they left, with the inventory reconciled on-site.

## Tye Insurance Coordination

Contents restoration is frequently a covered line item under standard homeowners policies, but the documentation has to be done correctly from the start. We photograph and inventory every item before pack-out, note pre-existing condition where relevant, and organize the claim file in a format most major carriers recognize. If your adjuster needs to inspect items before cleaning begins, we can coordinate that inspection at our storage facility rather than requiring you to manage access to a damaged property. We bill carriers directly where that arrangement is in place, which reduces the out-of-pocket coordination on your end.
