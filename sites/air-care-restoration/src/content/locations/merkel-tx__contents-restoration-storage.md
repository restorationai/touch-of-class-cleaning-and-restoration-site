## Why Merkel Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Taylor County's climate creates a specific kind of damage pattern. The IICRC standard puts that window at 24 to 48 hours, and in a Merkel summer, the heat accelerates that timeline. At the same time, the dry air can lock smoke residue into porous materials like furniture fabric, wood finishes, and clothing before the smell is even noticeable.

Many homes in and around Merkel were built in the mid-twentieth century, with construction materials that behave differently than modern composites. Solid wood furniture, older upholstery, and natural-fiber rugs absorb water and smoke differently than the engineered materials common in newer builds. That means pack-out decisions, cleaning methods, and storage conditions all have to be calibrated to what the item is actually made of, not just what category it falls into.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Merkel

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item is photographed, described, and logged so that your insurance documentation is complete and nothing gets lost in transit. Items are then sorted into three categories: salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with specialized treatment, and non-salvageable. That sorting happens on-site, not at a facility hours away, so you know what is happening to your belongings from the start.

Salvageable contents move to a climate-controlled storage facility while the structure is being dried or repaired. This matters in West Texas because items left in an uncontrolled environment during a hot Merkel summer can suffer secondary damage from heat and humidity fluctuation even after they have been cleaned. Climate-controlled storage keeps the restoration work from being undone while the rest of the job is in progress.

Cleaning methods vary by material. Ultrasonic cleaning handles hard surfaces and intricate items like electronics components, tools, and decorative objects. Ozone and hydroxyl treatments address smoke odor in soft goods. Dry cleaning and wet cleaning are selected based on fabric type and the nature of the soiling. The goal is to return items to pre-loss condition, not just to make them look acceptable.
