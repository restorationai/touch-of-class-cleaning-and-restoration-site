## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Tuscola

We start with a walkthrough of every affected structure, not just the primary residence, since a grass fire or a fire on a neighboring property can leave soot on a shop or barn that gets overlooked while everyone focuses on the house. From there the process follows IICRC-based methods: identifying whether the residue is dry smoke, wet smoke, or protein-based (common with grease or grass fires), then matching the cleaning agents and equipment to that residue type rather than using one generic approach on every surface. HVAC and duct evaluation is a standing part of the job on rural Tuscola properties, since window units and open construction let smoke particulate travel further into the living space than it would in a tightly sealed suburban home. We finish with thermal fogging or ozone treatment where odor has penetrated porous materials, followed by a walkthrough with the property owner before we close the job out.
