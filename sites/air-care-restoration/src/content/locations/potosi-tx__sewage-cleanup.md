## Why Potosi Properties Experience Sewage Backups

Much of the residential development around Potosi relies on private septic systems rather than a municipal sewer connection. That distinction matters enormously during and after heavy rainfall events. When Taylor County receives a hard, fast rain, the caliche layer beneath the topsoil limits percolation. Drain fields that function normally during dry months can become temporarily saturated, pushing effluent back through the system and into the home. Older septic tanks, particularly those installed on rural acreage before current Texas Commission on Environmental Quality standards, may lack adequate capacity or baffling, making backups more likely as systems age.

Homes on larger lots outside Abilene's city limits also tend to have longer lateral lines running from the house to the tank. Tree root intrusion along those runs is a common culprit, especially in properties with mature mesquite or cedar. A slow blockage in a lateral line often goes unnoticed until a toilet or floor drain overflows during a heavy-use period.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Potosi

Every sewage job starts with containment, not extraction. Before any equipment runs, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. Sewage is classified as Category 3 water, meaning it carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that require EPA-registered disinfectants, not standard cleaning products.

Once contained, standing waste is extracted using truck-mounted equipment capable of handling solid-laden material. Porous materials that cannot be adequately sanitized, including saturated drywall, carpet, and certain flooring substrates, are removed and bagged for proper disposal in accordance with Texas health regulations. Structural surfaces are then treated with hospital-grade antimicrobial agents, and the area is dried using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient conditions. In Potosi's dry climate, ambient humidity works in our favor during drying, but it also means odors concentrate quickly, so deodorization runs concurrently with drying rather than after.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with OSHA-trained technicians, the process follows established industry standards for Category 3 water loss from start to finish.

## Potosi Insurance Coordination for Sewage Losses

Sewage backup coverage is not automatic on most Texas homeowner policies. It typically requires a separate sewer and drain backup endorsement, and whether a loss is covered often depends on the cause: a municipal line failure versus a private septic failure can be treated very differently by adjusters. Air Care Restoration documents the loss thoroughly, including photographs, moisture readings, and a written scope, to give your carrier the information needed to process the claim accurately. ## Local Note

Properties in the Potosi area that were built on slab foundations during the 1980s and 1990s often have cast-iron drain lines running beneath the slab. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out over decades, and a partial collapse or heavy scaling inside the pipe can cause recurring backups that look like a septic issue but are actually a line failure under the foundation. If a sewage backup in a home of that era recurs without a clear septic cause, it is worth having a plumber run a camera through the under-slab lines before assuming the tank or drain field is the problem. Knowing what you are actually dealing with prevents cleanup work from being repeated.
