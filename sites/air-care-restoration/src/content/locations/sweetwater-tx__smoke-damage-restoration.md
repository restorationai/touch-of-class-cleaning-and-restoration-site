## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Sweetwater

We start by identifying what actually burned, protein-based kitchen fires, synthetic materials, or wildfire smoke, because each leaves a different residue that responds to a different cleaning agent. Dry chemical sponges lift loose soot from plaster and masonry without smearing it deeper into the surface, while wet cleaning and encapsulation handle the oily residue from synthetic materials and furniture. HVAC systems get inspected and cleaned separately, since ductwork in older Sweetwater homes often has leaky seams that pull soot-laden air back into the living space long after the fire is out. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generators handle the odor molecules that hand-cleaning can't reach, particularly in insulation and wall cavities. Every step follows the IICRC's fire and smoke restoration standard, and we document conditions with moisture and particulate readings before and after so there's a clear record for the insurance file.
