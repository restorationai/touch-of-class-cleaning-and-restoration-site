## Why Early Properties See Contents Damage Compound Quickly

Early sits in a climate zone where summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F and humidity swings sharply after thunderstorms. That combination is hard on belongings left inside a damaged structure. Smoke residue — the kind left by a kitchen fire or a fast-moving grass fire that breaches a window — bonds to porous surfaces faster in heat. Wood furniture can begin to warp within a day or two when interior temperatures spike after a roof breach or broken window. Upholstered pieces absorb odor compounds that become significantly harder to neutralize once they've had time to off-gas into the fabric.

Brown County also sees a mix of housing stock — older ranch-style homes built with solid wood cabinetry and real hardwood floors alongside newer construction with engineered materials. Each responds differently to moisture and smoke exposure. Solid wood can often be restored; certain engineered composites delaminate quickly and may not be salvageable if pack-out is delayed.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Early

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and ensures nothing is misplaced during transport.

From there, contents are moved to a controlled facility where temperature, humidity, and air quality are managed — conditions a storm-damaged Early home simply cannot provide. Cleaning methods vary by material:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, metals, ceramics, and small electronics — sound waves dislodge soot and residue without abrasion.
- **Dry-cleaning and wet-cleaning techniques** for textiles, calibrated to fiber type.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for odor neutralization in upholstered furniture and soft goods.
- **Document and photo drying** using controlled drying chambers for paper-based items.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke) and IICRC WRT (Water) certifications, the cleaning protocols follow established industry standards — not guesswork. Items are returned only when they've been cleaned, documented, and cleared.

## Early Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents claims are frequently the most contested portion of a property loss. Adjusters want itemized documentation; policyholders often don't realize that a pack-out and professional cleaning can result in a higher recovery than a straight replacement value on items that are actually restorable. The inventory Air Care Restoration produces — photographs, condition notes, cleaning records — is formatted to support the claim process directly.

Brown County homeowners dealing with wind and hail events should also be aware that some carriers treat contents damage as a separate line item from structural damage. Having a restoration contractor document contents independently, before anything is discarded, protects that portion of the claim.

One thing that comes up specifically in Early and the surrounding Brown County area: homes that have been on well water for decades sometimes have mineral deposits — iron and calcium — already present on fixtures, appliances, and even furniture hardware.
