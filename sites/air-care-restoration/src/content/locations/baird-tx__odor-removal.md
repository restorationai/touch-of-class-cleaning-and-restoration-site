## Why Baird Properties See Persistent Odor Problems

Callahan County's climate creates conditions that accelerate odor penetration. The extreme temperature swings between summer highs and cooler nights cause building materials to expand and contract repeatedly, which opens up micro-gaps in plaster walls and wood subfloors where odor compounds settle in. Many homes in and around Baird were built in the mid-20th century, when construction relied heavily on plaster-over-lath walls and uninsulated crawl spaces, materials that absorb smoke, moisture, and biological odors far more readily than modern drywall or sealed concrete.

When a kitchen fire leaves smoke residue in a home with original plaster ceilings, the soot and volatile organic compounds don't just coat the surface. They migrate into the plaster matrix itself. The same principle applies to pet urine that soaks through hardwood flooring into the subfloor below, or to mold-related musty odors that develop after a roof leak goes undetected through a Baird summer. Surface cleaning alone rarely solves these problems.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Baird

Every odor job starts with a source assessment. Deodorization equipment cannot do its job if the underlying source, charred material, saturated insulation, contaminated subfloor, is still present. Once the source is addressed, the method we use depends on what the odor compound is and what materials it has penetrated.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective for smoke odor in older Baird homes with plaster walls and wood framing. The fogging agent is heated into a fine mist that follows the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, reaching into wall cavities and ceiling voids where smoke residue has settled. **Hydroxyl deodorization** is a safer option for occupied or partially occupied spaces; hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor compounds at the molecular level without requiring the space to be vacated for extended periods. **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied structures and is highly effective for severe, embedded odors, but it requires careful application and adequate aeration time before re-occupancy, something that matters in Baird where neighbors and properties can sit close together in older residential blocks.

After treatment, we verify results rather than assume them. Odor panels and post-treatment air quality checks confirm the source has been neutralized before we close out the job.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The right tool depends on the odor type and the structure. Our technicians carry a full complement of deodorization equipment calibrated to the conditions we encounter in West Texas homes:

- **Thermal foggers** for penetrating porous materials and wall cavities after smoke events
- **Hydroxyl generators** for continuous treatment in occupied or sensitive environments
- **Ozone generators** for severe, embedded odors in vacant structures
- **Air scrubbers with HEPA and activated carbon filtration** to capture airborne particulates and VOCs during and after treatment
- **Moisture meters and thermal imaging** to confirm that odor sources tied to water intrusion are fully dried before deodorization begins

Using the wrong method in a West Texas home, for example, applying ozone in a structure that still has active moisture, can actually set odors rather than remove them. Sequence and method selection matter.
