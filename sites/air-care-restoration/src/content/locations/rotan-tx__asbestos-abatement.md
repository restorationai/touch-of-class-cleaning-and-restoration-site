## Why Rotan Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Fisher County's economy tracked closely with oil and agriculture for most of the twentieth century, and the housing and commercial construction that followed those booms reflects the materials available at the time. Asbestos was inexpensive, fire-resistant, and widely used — in the texture sprayed on ceilings, in the vinyl floor tiles laid in kitchens and hallways, in the duct insulation wrapped around HVAC systems, and in the roofing shingles on countless ranch-style homes built across the region from the 1940s through the late 1970s.

Rotan's low humidity relative to East Texas slows the natural deterioration of these materials, but it also means homeowners sometimes assume everything is fine until a remodel or a roof repair breaks the surface. Disturbing asbestos-containing materials — even something as routine as scraping a popcorn ceiling before repainting — can release fibers that are invisible to the eye and hazardous when inhaled. That's the moment an inspection matters most, before work begins rather than after.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Rotan

Every abatement project starts with a thorough inspection and, where materials are suspect, bulk sampling sent to an accredited laboratory. We do not guess at asbestos content — confirmed testing drives every decision about containment, removal method, and disposal.

Once testing identifies asbestos-containing materials, our crew establishes negative-air containment using heavy-gauge poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent fiber migration into unaffected areas of the structure. Wet methods are used throughout removal to suppress airborne fibers. All waste is double-bagged in labeled asbestos disposal bags, transported in sealed containers, and taken to a licensed disposal facility — Texas regulations govern both the handling and the final disposal site, and we follow those requirements on every job.

After removal, air clearance testing confirms fiber counts have returned to acceptable levels before containment is broken. That clearance step is not optional; it is the documented proof that the space is safe for re-occupancy or continued renovation.
