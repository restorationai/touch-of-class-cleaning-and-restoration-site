## Why Buffalo Gap Properties See Mold Issues

Taylor County's climate swings between prolonged dry stretches and sudden, intense rainfall events. When that rain arrives, the clay-dominant soil around Buffalo Gap's older foundations doesn't absorb water quickly — it sheds it toward the structure. Stone foundations common in the town's historic core are porous by nature, and mortar joints that have never been repointed allow capillary moisture to wick inward season after season. Pier-and-beam construction, still common on the older residential streets, leaves a crawl space that traps ground moisture and warm air — exactly the dark, humid environment where Cladosporium, Aspergillus, and Stachybotrys species colonize fastest.

The town's location near Buffalo Gap Lake also matters. Properties on the lower-lying ground to the west of the historic district can experience elevated ambient humidity even when there is no active leak, which keeps relative humidity inside wall assemblies above the 60% threshold where mold growth accelerates. Homeowners sometimes notice a musty smell months before visible growth appears on drywall paper or ceiling tiles.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Buffalo Gap

Every job begins with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters identify wet zones behind plaster, under flooring, and inside wall cavities before any demolition begins. That step matters especially in Buffalo Gap's older homes, where a surface reading can show dry while the underlying lath or original plank sheathing is still saturated.

Once the affected area is defined, the crew establishes negative-pressure containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Contaminated materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing where necessary — are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces are then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and the area is dried to IICRC S520 standard before any reconstruction begins. Because many Buffalo Gap homes carry lead paint on older trim and window sills, the EPA Lead-Safe certification the team holds governs how dust and debris are handled throughout.
