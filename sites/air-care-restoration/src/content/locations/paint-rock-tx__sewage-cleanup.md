## Why Paint Rock Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Paint Rock sits in a part of West Texas where the soil composition and the age of the local infrastructure work against property owners during heavy rain events. The region's expansive clay soils shift with moisture cycles — swelling after rain, contracting in drought — and that ground movement stresses buried sewer laterals and septic tank connections over time. A line that holds fine through a dry summer can crack or separate when the soil heaves after a significant rainfall, sending raw sewage back toward the lowest drain in the house.

Many homes in and around Paint Rock were built in an era when septic systems were sized for smaller households and lower water usage than is typical today. An undersized tank or a drain field that has reached the end of its service life can overflow with very little warning, particularly after a stretch of wet weather when the surrounding soil is already saturated. The result — standing sewage in a utility room, laundry area, or crawl space — is a Category 3 biohazard that requires more than a shop vac and bleach to address safely.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paint Rock

Sewage remediation is a sequenced process, and cutting steps short creates health risks that show up weeks later. Here is how the work unfolds on a typical Paint Rock job:

**Containment and hazard assessment** — The affected area is isolated immediately to prevent cross-contamination to clean zones. Technicians document the scope with photos and moisture readings before anything is moved.

**Extraction and removal** — Standing effluent is extracted with truck-mounted or portable units. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, damaged subfloor sections — that have absorbed sewage are removed and bagged for proper disposal. There is no safe way to dry and restore material that has been saturated with Category 3 water.

**Cleaning and EPA-registered disinfection** — All hard surfaces in the affected area are cleaned, then treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This step addresses bacteria, viruses, and the odor compounds that linger long after visible waste is gone.

**Drying and documentation** — Once the area is sanitized, industrial air movers and dehumidifiers bring structural materials to target moisture levels. Readings are logged daily and provided to your insurance carrier as part of the claim file.

Our team holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, so the process follows established industry protocols rather than improvised field decisions.
