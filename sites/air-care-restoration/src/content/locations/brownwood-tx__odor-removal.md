## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Brownwood

Effective odor elimination isn't a single step. The process Air Care Restoration follows is built around identifying where odors are actually living, not just where they're strongest at nose level.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization technology permanently eliminates an odor if the source material is still present. Charred wood, saturated insulation, or contaminated subfloor gets removed before any equipment is deployed.

**Thermal fogging for penetration.** A thermal fogger vaporizes a deodorizing agent into particles small enough to follow the same pathways smoke traveled, into wall cavities, behind baseboards, through subfloor gaps. This is especially useful in Brownwood's older wood-frame construction where smoke can migrate well beyond the visible burn zone.

**Ozone or hydroxyl treatment for sustained neutralization.** Depending on occupancy status and the nature of the odor, the team uses either ozone generators (in unoccupied spaces) or hydroxyl generators (which are safe to operate around people and pets) to break odor molecules at the chemical bond level rather than masking them.

**HVAC and duct deodorization.** Because ductwork acts as a distribution system for odors throughout a structure, any complete deodorization job in Brownwood includes attention to the air handling system. Skipping this step is one of the most common reasons odors return within weeks.

The IICRC FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician) certification the team holds informs every step of this sequence, ensuring the method matches the odor type and building material rather than defaulting to a one-size approach.
