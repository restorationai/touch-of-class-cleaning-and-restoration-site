## Why Robert Lee Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Coke County's climate runs hot and dry through most of the year, with intense UV exposure that bakes spray paint into exterior surfaces faster than in more humid parts of Texas. A tag left on a stucco or painted masonry wall for even a few days in summer heat can begin to bond at a molecular level with the coating beneath it, making removal more aggressive and increasing the risk of surface damage if the wrong solvent is applied. Older commercial buildings in Robert Lee often feature painted brick or limestone block exteriors, both of which are porous and absorb aerosol paint quickly. That porosity means a single pass with a pressure washer is rarely enough, and the wrong pressure setting can etch soft limestone permanently.

Residential properties in this part of West Texas also tend to sit on larger lots with outbuildings, fencing, and metal agricultural structures that can be tagged or damaged. Corrugated metal and galvanized surfaces require a different approach than masonry, and rust that forms around broken window frames or damaged metal panels can spread quickly in the region's occasional high-humidity weather windows that follow spring storm systems.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Robert Lee

The first step on any vandalism call is a surface-by-surface assessment before any product is applied. Different substrates, painted wood, bare masonry, glass, metal, and vinyl siding, each respond differently to cleaning agents, and using the wrong product can set a stain rather than remove it. Air Care Restoration's IICRC-certified team documents every affected area with photographs before work begins, which matters for insurance claims and for establishing a baseline if any surface requires a second treatment.

For graffiti removal, we match the solvent chemistry to the substrate: citrus-based degreasers for painted surfaces where the underlying coat needs to survive, stronger alkaline formulas for raw masonry, and dry-media blasting for porous stone where liquid solvents would drive pigment deeper. Broken glass cleanup follows a strict containment protocol, including fine-particle sweeping after the visible shards are removed, because glass fragments in West Texas wind conditions can migrate well beyond the immediate area. Any compromised window or door opening is boarded and weatherproofed the same day to prevent dust, insects, and heat infiltration while permanent repairs are arranged.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up in Coke County properties is the prevalence of exterior paint finishes that were applied over older oil-based coats, a common practice in West Texas construction from the 1970s through the 1990s. When graffiti removal solvents penetrate through a latex topcoat and reach that oil-based layer beneath, the interface can soften and cause peeling that looks like solvent damage but is actually a pre-existing adhesion failure waiting to happen. Identifying this before treatment, by checking paint edges at trim lines and testing a small inconspicuous area first, prevents disputes over whether the cleanup caused new damage.
