## Why Brownwood Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Brownwood's commercial corridor along Austin Avenue and the older residential blocks that fan out from downtown include a mix of mid-century brick storefronts, painted concrete block construction, and wood-frame homes built decades before modern exterior coatings existed. That building stock matters for cleanup. Spray paint on aged, unsealed brick is not the same problem as graffiti on a smooth modern stucco surface. Porous masonry absorbs pigment deeper into the substrate, which means surface scrubbing alone rarely gets the job done. Solvent-based graffiti remover applied without the right dwell time can drive the pigment further in rather than lifting it out.

Brown County's climate adds another layer. Summer surface temperatures on south- and west-facing brick walls regularly exceed air temperature by 20 to 30 degrees. By mid-morning, a tagged wall has already begun heat-curing the paint. Timing matters: the sooner cleanup begins, the shallower the penetration and the better the outcome for the underlying surface.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Brownwood

The first step on any vandalism call is a thorough site assessment before anyone picks up a brush or sprayer. We photograph and document every affected surface, note the substrate type, and identify any secondary damage, broken windows, forced entry points, or interior exposure that may not be immediately obvious. That documentation serves two purposes: it drives the right cleaning approach, and it becomes the foundation of any insurance claim.

For graffiti on masonry, we select the appropriate chemical agent for the specific paint type and substrate porosity, apply it at the correct dwell time, and use low-pressure rinsing to avoid driving residue deeper into the material. For broken glass, we remove all fragments systematically, including shards embedded in framing or weather stripping that are easy to miss and dangerous to leave. Damaged window frames, door hardware, or exterior fixtures are assessed for repair or replacement as part of the scope.

Where interior spaces have been accessed, we check for additional damage, contamination, or items that require biohazard handling. Our team holds IICRC certification and OSHA training, which means we approach every scene with documented safety protocols rather than improvised judgment calls.
