## Why Tye Properties See Mold Issues

The Taylor County climate is deceptive. Outdoor humidity readings can be low while interior spaces — especially poorly ventilated crawl spaces, attics, and utility rooms — trap condensation from HVAC systems running hard against 100°F summer temperatures. Slab-on-grade construction, which is extremely common in this part of Abilene's western corridor, eliminates the crawl space risk but creates a different one: any moisture that migrates under flooring or behind baseboards has nowhere to drain and no airflow to carry it away. Vinyl plank and laminate flooring installed over concrete slabs can trap water vapor for weeks before a surface reading even registers damp.

Tye's position just west of Abilene also means that when the region does get rain — particularly the heavy, fast-moving storms that roll through Taylor County in spring — roofs, window seals, and older HVAC penetrations can admit water quickly. A single storm event followed by days of closed-up, air-conditioned interiors is enough to start mold colonization behind drywall within 24 to 48 hours.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Tye

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a quick walk-through. Mold in West Texas homes often hides behind drywall that reads dry at the surface because the slab beneath is still off-gassing moisture upward. Once affected areas are identified, the crew establishes negative-air containment with 6-mil poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to clean areas of the structure during removal.

Contaminated materials — drywall sections, insulation, flooring substrate — are bagged and removed per IICRC S520 protocols. Structural framing and hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment (commercial dehumidifiers and air movers) runs until moisture readings return to regionally appropriate baselines, which in Taylor County's climate means accounting for both ambient outdoor humidity and the slab's residual moisture contribution. A final HEPA air scrub clears airborne particulates before containment comes down.
