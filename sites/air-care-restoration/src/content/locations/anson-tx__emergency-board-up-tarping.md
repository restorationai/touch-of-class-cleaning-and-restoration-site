## Why Anson Properties Are Vulnerable After a Loss

Jones County sits squarely in a corridor that sees hail-producing supercells, straight-line wind events, and the occasional tornado. That combination is hard on roofing materials — particularly on the older wood-framed homes that make up a significant portion of Anson's residential stock. Many of these houses were built with wood shake or early composition shingles, and when those fail under impact or wind, the decking beneath can be compromised in ways that aren't obvious from the ground. A tarp applied incorrectly — undersized, poorly anchored, or laid over debris — will lift in the next gust and leave the opening exposed again.

Fire damage is the other common driver for emergency board-up calls here. A structure fire that burns through a wall or window frame leaves the building open and unstable. In a small city like Anson, where response resources are spread across a wide geographic area, the time between a fire being knocked down and a restoration crew arriving to secure the structure matters enormously. Every hour the opening sits unprotected is an hour for smoke odor to migrate deeper into unaffected rooms, for weather to saturate insulation, and for the structure to become a safety hazard.

## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Anson

When we arrive on-site, the first step is a safety walk — no crew member enters or works adjacent to a structure until we've assessed load-bearing integrity, checked for active electrical hazards, and confirmed the roof deck can support foot traffic if tarping is required. In Anson's older housing stock, that assessment sometimes reveals concerns that aren't visible from the street: sagging ridge lines, damaged rafters, or interior walls that have shifted after a fire.

For window and door board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fastened to manufacturer-recommended specifications — not just screwed into the frame, but anchored into the surrounding wall framing where the opening allows. For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for the load and secured with weighted battens and fasteners driven into the decking, not just draped and tied. The goal is a system that holds through the next Jones County storm, not just overnight.

We document every step with photographs — the condition of the opening before we touch it, the materials used, and the finished installation. That documentation goes directly into the insurance file.
