## Why Merkel Properties Need Structural Reconstruction

Merkel's housing stock leans toward older single-family frame homes and manufactured housing on the outskirts of town, many built well before current wind-load and moisture-barrier standards. Add Taylor County's clay-heavy soil, which expands and contracts hard through West Texas wet-dry cycles, and you get foundation movement that cracks slab edges, pulls door frames out of square, and stresses plumbing joints until they fail. Severe hail and straight-line wind events common to this part of the state also tend to compromise roofing and siding in ways that aren't obvious until water gets behind the wall assembly. By the time a homeowner notices soft drywall or a sagging ceiling, the damage usually extends past cosmetic repair into the framing, insulation, and sometimes the structural sheathing, which is where a true rebuild scope starts.

## Our Reconstruction Process in Merkel

Reconstruction here starts with documenting the full extent of the loss, structural, not just surface, before any framing goes back up. Our crews remove and rebuild only what's confirmed compromised: subfloor sections softened by water intrusion, fire-damaged studs and joists, or siding and sheathing torn loose by wind. On pre-1978 homes, which are common in Merkel's older residential blocks, our EPA Lead-Safe certified team follows lead-safe work practice rules for any paint disturbance during demo. We rebuild to match existing materials and finish grade wherever possible, and where code requires an upgrade, such as updated fasteners or moisture barriers, we flag that early so it's priced into the insurance scope instead of surfacing as a surprise mid-project.
