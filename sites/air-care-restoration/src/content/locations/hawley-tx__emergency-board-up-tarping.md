## Why Hawley Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

The Abilene metro region, including Hawley and the surrounding Jones County communities, sits squarely in a corridor that sees severe thunderstorms, straight-line winds, and periodic hail events that can strip shingles and shatter windows in a single pass. Homes in this part of West Texas were often built during mid-century oil-economy booms, and many feature wood-frame construction with composition roofing that, after decades under the Texas sun, becomes brittle and especially vulnerable to impact damage. A hail strike that would dent a newer architectural shingle can punch clean through an aged three-tab roof.

Fire damage is a compounding factor here as well. The dry climate means structures ignite and spread heat quickly, and fire suppression efforts often leave window frames warped, glass blown out, and roof decking partially exposed. Without immediate board-up, wind-driven debris and overnight temperature swings can push moisture into framing that is already heat-stressed.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hawley

When a call comes in from Hawley, the first priority is a rapid site assessment. A crew member walks the perimeter to identify every breach point: broken windows, compromised entry doors, sections of missing or lifted roofing, and any structural openings left by fire department operations. Nothing gets covered without being documented first, because your insurance carrier will want a photographic record of conditions at the time of arrival.

For window and door openings, we cut plywood to fit and fasten it in a way that resists West Texas wind loads rather than just blocking the hole. Lightweight stapled plastic is not a solution in a region where gusts regularly exceed 40 mph. Roof tarping uses heavy-duty polyethylene sheeting secured with batten boards and fastened to intact decking so the tarp cannot lift and peel under sustained wind. We avoid penetrating undamaged roofing material wherever possible, which matters when a claims adjuster is measuring the original loss against any contractor-caused damage.

Every opening is logged by location and dimension. That documentation goes to you and, with your authorization, directly to your insurance adjuster.
