## Why Ballinger Properties See Distinct Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

The climate here is the first thing any cleaning crew has to account for. Runnels County averages low humidity for much of the year, which means fine construction dust becomes airborne again with the slightest air movement — long after the contractors have packed up. That same dry air causes caulk and joint compound to cure faster, but it also means residue left on surfaces bonds more stubbornly than it would in a humid environment. When seasonal storms roll through, the caliche-heavy soil outside tracks indoors on work boots and equipment tires, layering a second kind of grit on top of the construction debris.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Ballinger

Post-construction cleaning isn't a single pass with a mop. The process moves in defined phases, and skipping any of them means the next occupant is breathing in what the crew left behind.

**Rough clean** comes first — removing bulk debris, scraps of material, and construction waste before any detail work begins. In West Texas builds, this stage almost always includes clearing caliche mud and concrete splatter from entryways and garage slabs.

**Detail clean** follows: wiping every surface from ceiling to floor, cleaning inside cabinets and drawers, removing paint and sticker residue from glass, polishing fixtures, and vacuuming window tracks and door frames with HEPA-filtered equipment. HEPA filtration isn't optional on a post-construction clean — standard vacuums recirculate fine silica and drywall dust back into the air.

**Final inspection clean** happens after trades have done their punch-list walk and made last-minute repairs. Touch-ups from that final round of work leave fresh dust and debris, and the space needs a second pass before keys are handed over.

For specialty cleaning scopes — post-fire odor removal, deep cleaning after a hoarding situation, or sanitization following a tenant vacancy — the process is scoped individually based on what the space actually needs, not a one-size checklist.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up specifically on renovation projects in Ballinger's older neighborhoods: original hardwood floors that have been refinished as part of a remodel are highly sensitive to the residual sanding dust that settles between the cleaning stages. In the dry West Texas air, that fine wood dust becomes electrostatically charged and clings to baseboards, window sills, and HVAC grilles even after the floor itself looks clean. A thorough post-construction clean on a refinished hardwood job here means going back to those surfaces a second time after the floor finish has fully off-gassed — typically 24 to 48 hours after the final coat — so the cleaning crew isn't trapping solvent vapors under microfiber cloths or sealing dust into the finish.
