## Why Coleman Properties See Water Damage Issues

The clay-heavy soils common across Coleman County expand when wet and contract when dry, and that constant movement puts stress on slab foundations and the plumbing embedded in them. A pinhole slab leak can seep undetected for weeks, saturating the concrete and wicking moisture up through flooring. By the time a homeowner notices a soft spot in the vinyl or a musty smell near a wall, the subfloor or the bottom plate of an interior wall may already be compromised.

Coleman's older housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in town were built in the mid-twentieth century with materials and construction methods that respond to water very differently than newer builds. Plaster-and-lath interior walls, for example, can feel dry at the surface while holding significant moisture in the substrate — a standard pin-type moisture meter may not catch it without probing deeper. Pier-and-beam foundations, still common in the area, create a crawl space environment where standing water from a plumbing failure or a heavy rain event can linger and begin supporting mold growth within 24 to 48 hours if it isn't extracted and the space dried down properly.

Summer hailstorms and the occasional severe thunderstorm that tracks through Coleman County can compromise roofing in minutes, sending water into attic insulation and down through ceiling assemblies before a homeowner even realizes the storm has passed.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Coleman

When a call comes in, the first priority is getting eyes on the loss and stopping any active water source. Once the source is controlled, the crew performs a full moisture mapping of the affected area — walls, floors, ceilings, and any adjacent cavities — using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to find water that has migrated beyond the visible damage zone.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water and saturated material quickly, reducing the total drying load before structural drying equipment is set. Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned to create a directed drying system calibrated to the specific materials involved — concrete slab, wood subfloor, plaster, drywall, or insulation each require different airflow and humidity targets to dry safely without over-drying and causing additional structural movement.

Moisture readings are logged daily. Drying is not declared complete until affected materials reach documented dry standard, following the IICRC S500 framework. That documentation matters later when the insurance claim is reviewed.

Most standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, storm-driven water entry through a compromised roof.
