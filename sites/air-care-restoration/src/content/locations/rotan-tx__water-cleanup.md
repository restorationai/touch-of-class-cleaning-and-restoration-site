## Why Rotan Properties See Water Damage Differently

The West Texas climate creates a particular pattern for water losses in Fisher County. Extended dry spells cause clay soils to shrink and pull away from foundations; when the rains finally come — sometimes intensely — that gap allows water to migrate along the foundation perimeter and into crawl spaces or slab edges before anyone notices. Older homes in Rotan were frequently built with wood-frame construction and original cast-iron or galvanized supply lines. Those pipes corrode from the inside out, and the first sign of a failure is often a wet subfloor or a ceiling stain, not a visible drip. The delay between the leak starting and the homeowner discovering it is exactly the time mold needs to begin colonizing — researchers consistently cite 24 to 48 hours as the colonization window on wet organic materials.

Flat rooflines common in mid-century West Texas construction also concentrate runoff at the eaves rather than dispersing it, which means a compromised flashing or a clogged scupper can push water into wall cavities during a single storm event. None of this is unique to Rotan, but the combination of soil behavior, building age, and climate pattern makes water intrusion a recurring concern rather than a rare one.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Rotan

When the crew arrives, the first priority is stopping ongoing intrusion if the source is still active — shutting off the supply valve, tarping a roof breach, or sandbagging a threshold. From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration:

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has migrated beyond the visible wet area. In older Rotan homes with plaster-over-lath walls, water can travel several feet horizontally before showing on the surface.

**Extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing water and pull moisture from carpet, padding, and subfloor materials. The goal is to reduce the water load before drying equipment is placed, because drying a saturated material takes exponentially longer than drying a damp one.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Daily readings track the drying curve and confirm materials are reaching their target moisture content before equipment is removed.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, every equipment placement, and every photo is logged — both for your insurance claim and as a record that the structure was returned to a dry standard before repairs begin.
