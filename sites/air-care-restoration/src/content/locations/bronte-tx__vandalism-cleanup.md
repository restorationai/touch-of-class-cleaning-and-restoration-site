## Why Bronte Properties Are Vulnerable to Vandalism Damage

Coke County's sparse population density and limited overnight foot traffic create conditions where vandalism can go unnoticed for hours, sometimes longer. A broken window left open to a West Texas night lets in wind-driven dust and, during the region's unpredictable spring storm season, rain. What starts as a broken pane can become a water intrusion problem by morning if the weather turns. The same dry, alkaline soil that characterizes this stretch of the Concho Valley also means that spray paint and aerosol-based graffiti bond aggressively to porous masonry and concrete block, both common in Bronte's older commercial buildings. The longer a painted surface sits in the intense summer sun, the deeper the pigment cures into the substrate, making early response critical.

Bronte's building stock skews older, and many structures use painted brick or stucco exteriors that require different chemical approaches than modern composite siding. Applying the wrong solvent to aged mortar or a painted historic facade can cause more cosmetic damage than the original graffiti. Knowing the material before selecting the cleaning agent is not optional here.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Bronte

When we arrive on-site, the first step is a full damage assessment before anything is touched. We photograph and document every affected surface for insurance purposes, then categorize the damage: graffiti and surface contamination, broken glass and structural breach, interior damage from forced entry, or a combination. Each category follows a distinct workflow.

For graffiti removal, we test the substrate first. Brick, block, stucco, metal, wood, and glass each respond differently to chemical and mechanical removal methods. We use pH-appropriate solvents and, where necessary, low-pressure media blasting calibrated to avoid etching the underlying surface. On painted brick common in older Coke County commercial buildings, we often use a poultice method to draw pigment out of pores rather than drive it deeper with pressure.

Broken glass cleanup goes beyond sweeping. Shards travel further than they appear to, and in a retail or office environment, glass fragments can embed in carpet, settle into HVAC returns, or lodge in door frames. We use both visual inspection and tactile methods to clear all affected areas before boarding or glazing. If the breach left the interior exposed overnight, we also assess for secondary damage: dust infiltration, moisture, or opportunistic pest entry, all realistic concerns in this part of West Texas.
