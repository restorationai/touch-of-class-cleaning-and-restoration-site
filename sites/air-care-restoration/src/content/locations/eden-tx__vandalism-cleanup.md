## Why Eden Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Eden's building stock leans toward older commercial masonry and modest wood-frame residential construction, the kind common to small county-seat towns across West Texas. Painted brick and soft limestone facades are porous, which means spray paint does not sit on the surface the way it does on a sealed modern panel. It wicks in. If a property owner tries to pressure-wash graffiti off an unsealed limestone exterior without the right chemical pre-treatment, the pigment spreads and the substrate can spall. The dry, windy climate here also means that broken glass and debris scatter further than they would in a humid, still environment, and fine shards work into gravel and soil in ways that create ongoing foot-traffic hazards.

Concho County's low population density also means that vandalism incidents sometimes go unnoticed for a day or more, especially on agricultural outbuildings or properties on the outskirts of town. The longer spray paint cures in the West Texas sun, the harder it is to remove without abrasion, so time genuinely matters.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Eden

Every job starts with a full site assessment before anyone touches a surface. That means photographing all damage, cataloging affected materials, and identifying any safety concerns like broken glass, compromised door or window frames, or structural damage from forced entry.

Graffiti removal is matched to the substrate. Solvent-based removers work on sealed surfaces; alkaline gels are used on porous masonry to lift pigment without driving it deeper. On painted wood siding, the approach is gentler still to avoid raising grain or stripping existing paint unnecessarily. After chemical treatment, surfaces are rinsed and inspected under raking light to catch any residual ghosting before the job is closed out.

Broken glass cleanup follows a systematic grid sweep rather than a visual pass, because scattered glass in gravel or grass is almost never visible all at once. We use puncture-resistant collection methods and dispose of glass debris properly rather than bagging it in standard trash liners that can fail and create a secondary hazard for waste handlers.

If forced entry damaged a door frame, lock hardware, or window casing, we document the structural damage thoroughly for insurance purposes and can coordinate with local contractors for board-up or repair work.

## Eden Insurance Coordination

Vandalism is a covered peril under most standard commercial and homeowner property policies, but the documentation requirements matter. Carriers want itemized photo evidence, a written scope of damage, and receipts for emergency mitigation work. We build that documentation from the moment we arrive, which means your adjuster gets a clean file rather than a collection of phone photos and a verbal description.

If your policy has a deductible that makes a small graffiti claim borderline, we can walk through the scope with you before you file so you are making an informed decision rather than a reactive one.

## Local Note

One thing that catches property owners off guard in this part of Concho County: the caliche and decomposed granite that makes up most unpaved lots and driveways here holds broken glass in a way that a paved surface does not. A visual sweep looks clean, but fine shards settle between aggregate pieces and re-emerge when the surface is disturbed. We do a secondary pass with a magnetic tool for any metal hardware fragments and a fine-rake sweep of unpaved areas before signing off on a broken glass cleanup, because a missed shard in a caliche lot is a tire puncture or a foot injury waiting to happen.
