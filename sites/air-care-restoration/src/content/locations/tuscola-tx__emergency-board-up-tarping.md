West Texas wind doesn't negotiate. When a severe thunderstorm tears through Taylor County and leaves a Tuscola home with a blown-out window or a section of roof peeled back, the gap between the storm passing and the next rain cell arriving can be measured in hours — sometimes less. That window is exactly when emergency board-up and tarping matters most, and why having a crew that can reach Tuscola around the clock is the difference between a contained repair and a gutted interior.

## Why Tuscola Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After a Storm

Tuscola sits in the rolling mesquite country south of Abilene, where the landscape offers little natural windbreak. The same open terrain that makes Taylor County farmland productive also allows storm systems to build speed before they reach residential areas. Hail events here routinely punch through asphalt shingles and leave roof decking exposed; straight-line winds associated with West Texas supercells can shear off fascia boards and push in older single-pane windows without warning.

The housing stock in and around Tuscola skews toward ranch-style construction — low-pitched roofs with wide overhangs that catch wind load differently than steeper pitched roofs common in other parts of Texas. Those shallow slopes also drain slowly, meaning a compromised section of roofing can allow water to pool and penetrate before a tarp is in place. Fire damage is another common driver of board-up calls: a kitchen fire or electrical fault that vents through a soffit can leave exterior walls and window openings exposed to the elements and to unauthorized entry.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Tuscola

When the call comes in, the first step is a rapid damage assessment — identifying every breach point before any material goes up. A single broken window is obvious; wind-lifted ridge cap that has allowed moisture into the attic cavity is not. The crew walks the perimeter and the roofline before committing to a tarping plan, because a tarp anchored incorrectly on a low-pitch roof in West Texas wind can become a sail and cause secondary damage.

For window and door openings, we use structural-grade OSB cut to fit and secured with exterior-rated fasteners into the framing — not just surface-nailed to trim. This matters for insurance documentation as much as it does for security: adjusters want to see that the opening was properly protected, not just covered. Roof tarps are weighted and anchored with batten boards rather than stapled at the edges, which is the only method that holds when another storm cell rolls through Taylor County the following night.

Because Air Care Restoration is IICRC certified and OSHA trained, the crew works with an eye toward what comes next — interior drying, smoke residue cleaning, or reconstruction — so the board-up doesn't create new problems for the remediation phase.

## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in the Tuscola area: the clay-heavy soils common across Taylor County expand and shift seasonally, and older ranch homes sometimes have exterior wall framing that has racked slightly over decades of foundation movement. When a window or door opening is no longer perfectly square, a standard pre-cut board won't seat flush — and a gap at the corner is enough for wind-driven rain to enter. The crew accounts for this by scribing cuts on-site rather than cutting to nominal dimensions, a small step that makes a real difference in how well the board-up holds until permanent repairs can be made.
