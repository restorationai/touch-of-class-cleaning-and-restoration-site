## Why Eastland Properties Are Vulnerable to Water Damage

Eastland's climate runs to extremes. Summer heat bakes plumbing fittings and caulk seals until they crack, and then a fast-moving thunderstorm can drop two inches of rain in under an hour on ground too dry to absorb it quickly. That runoff finds its way into crawl spaces, garage slabs, and older masonry foundations throughout the area.

The local housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Eastland's residential properties were built in the mid-twentieth century, when builders used materials — cast-iron drain lines, galvanized supply pipes, and fiber-reinforced plaster walls — that behave very differently from modern construction when they get wet. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out over decades, and the first sign of failure is often a slow leak inside a wall that only shows up as a musty smell or a soft spot in the baseboard. Plaster walls absorb water more slowly than drywall but hold it longer, which extends drying timelines if the work isn't calibrated to the material.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Eastland

Every job starts with moisture mapping — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, floors, and ceilings to find saturation that isn't visible yet. That step matters especially in older Eastland homes where a single supply-line failure can wick moisture through multiple rooms before anyone notices.

From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and near-surface moisture from carpet, hardwood, and concrete before it migrates deeper.
- **Controlled demolition** — when drywall, baseboard, or flooring is saturated past the point of in-place drying, targeted removal creates airflow paths and prevents trapped moisture from feeding mold growth. Decisions are documented with photos for your insurance file.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the psychrometric conditions inside the structure, not a one-size setup. Daily moisture readings track progress against the IICRC S500 drying standard.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — affected cavities receive an EPA-registered antimicrobial application before any reconstruction begins.
