## Why Potosi Properties Face Particular Challenges After Vandalism

The building stock in and around Potosi leans toward ranch-style homes, metal agricultural buildings, and older masonry construction common to Taylor County's rural corridors. These materials each respond differently to vandalism damage. Spray paint soaks into uncoated concrete block and native limestone at a different rate than it does on modern painted drywall. Porous masonry can absorb aerosol pigment within minutes in the dry West Texas heat, making fast response a real factor in whether the surface can be cleaned or must be refinished entirely.

Broken glass from forced entry is another common scenario. Metal-framed windows on agricultural outbuildings and older single-pane residential windows shatter into patterns that are harder to contain than modern tempered glass, and fine shards work into gravel driveways and soil in ways that create ongoing hazards if not systematically collected.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Potosi

The process starts with a thorough site assessment before any cleaning begins. That means photographing every affected surface, cataloging the type of damage (paint, broken glass, forced-entry damage to doors or frames, debris), and identifying the substrate so the right removal method is selected. Using the wrong solvent on a painted limestone wall can drive pigment deeper rather than lift it.

Graffiti removal on masonry typically involves low-pressure chemical application followed by controlled rinsing, with care taken not to saturate wall cavities or adjacent soil. On metal surfaces common to Potosi-area outbuildings, a different approach applies: abrasive or solvent-based removal followed by a rust-inhibiting primer coat to protect exposed metal from the region's humidity swings.

Broken glass cleanup follows a systematic sweep-and-inspect protocol. We use fine-mesh collection and a final pass with specialty adhesive rollers to capture shards that standard brooms leave behind. All debris is bagged and disposed of in compliance with Texas solid waste handling requirements.

Where forced entry has compromised a door frame, window frame, or lock, we coordinate board-up and temporary securing of the structure before leaving the site, so the property is not left exposed overnight.
