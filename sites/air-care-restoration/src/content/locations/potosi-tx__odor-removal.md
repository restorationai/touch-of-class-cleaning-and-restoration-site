## Why Potosi Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same climate that makes West Texas summers punishing also accelerates odor bonding inside structures. When indoor temperatures spike, porous materials, wood framing, drywall paper, carpet backing, ceiling tiles, expand slightly and draw in odor molecules. When the air conditioning kicks on and temperatures drop, those materials contract and trap the odor deeper. This thermal cycling happens dozens of times a day during a Potosi summer.

Homes in this area also tend to rely heavily on central forced-air systems to survive the heat. That means a single odor source, whether from a kitchen fire, a deceased animal in the attic, or years of cigarette smoke, gets distributed through every room via the ductwork before anyone realizes how far it has spread. Treating the visible source without addressing the HVAC system leaves the problem half-solved.

Clay-heavy soils common across Taylor County can also affect crawl space and slab-edge conditions. When moisture intrudes through foundation gaps during a rare heavy rain event, it creates the damp, low-airflow environment that mold and musty odors need to establish themselves, even in a region most people associate with dry air.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Potosi

Effective odor elimination is not a single step. The process Air Care Restoration uses in Potosi is structured around identifying where the odor has migrated, not just where it started.

**Source removal** comes first. No deodorization chemistry works reliably if the originating material is still present. Charred debris, water-damaged insulation, or contaminated subfloor sections are removed before any treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective in West Texas homes because the fog penetrates the same pathways that heat-driven odor molecules followed. The fogging agent pairs with odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** is safe for occupied structures and works continuously over time, making it a strong choice when a Potosi homeowner cannot vacate the property during treatment. Hydroxyl generators produce the same reactive molecules the atmosphere creates naturally, breaking down odor compounds without leaving a chemical residue.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces and is especially useful in attics and enclosed areas where smoke or animal odors have concentrated. Because Potosi homes often have large attic volumes designed to buffer against roof-level heat, ozone treatment can reach areas that other methods cannot.

**HVAC and duct deodorization** closes the loop. Ductwork is treated separately to prevent re-contamination of freshly cleaned living spaces.
