## Why Ballinger Properties Are Prone to Asbestos Concerns

Runnels County's agricultural economy kept construction costs lean for decades, which meant builders relied heavily on the affordable, fire-resistant materials that dominated the market from the 1940s through the early 1980s. Asbestos was in nearly everything: the nine-inch floor tiles common in ranch-style homes, the duct insulation wrapped around older HVAC systems, the joint compound used on drywall seams, and the spray-applied acoustic texture on ceilings that homeowners now want removed before a repaint or sale.

Ballinger's climate adds another layer of risk. The region swings between dry, windy summers and occasional hard freezes in winter. That thermal cycling causes building materials to expand and contract repeatedly over decades, which can crack or crumble asbestos-containing products that were once intact and stable. Friable asbestos, the kind that can be crushed by hand pressure or that has already begun to deteriorate, releases fibers into the air far more readily than material that is still bonded. A home that tested fine twenty years ago may present a different picture today after years of West Texas weather stress.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Ballinger

Every project starts with an inspection and bulk sampling before any removal work begins. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and results drive the scope of work. Skipping this step is not an option under Texas Department of State Health Services rules, and it is not something Air Care Restoration does regardless of how straightforward a job appears.

Once the scope is confirmed, the work area is isolated with negative air pressure containment to prevent fiber migration into unaffected parts of the home. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously throughout removal. All regulated asbestos-containing material is wetted during removal to suppress fiber release, then double-bagged in labeled, sealed containers and transported to a licensed disposal facility. Final air clearance testing confirms the space is safe before containment comes down.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, which is one of the most common requests in Ballinger's older single-family homes, the process requires wetting the texture thoroughly before scraping, because dry removal sends fibers airborne immediately. The ceiling substrate is then cleaned and HEPA-vacuumed before the area is released.

## Ballinger Insurance and Regulatory Coordination

Asbestos abatement in Texas requires notification to the Texas Department of State Health Services for projects above certain thresholds, and that notification must be submitted before work begins. On the insurance side, asbestos removal coverage varies significantly by policy. Some homeowner policies cover abatement when the presence of asbestos is discovered during a covered loss, such as a roof claim or a fire. Others treat it as a maintenance exclusion.
