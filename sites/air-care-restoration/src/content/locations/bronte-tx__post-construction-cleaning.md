## Why Bronte Properties See Distinct Post-Construction Cleanup Challenges

Bronte sits in Coke County on the eastern edge of the Edwards Plateau, where the soil is a mix of clay and caliche that turns powdery in dry stretches and sticky after rain. Construction crews track both versions inside. Caliche dust is alkaline and abrasive — it settles into grout lines, scratches unprotected hardwood, and clogs HVAC filters faster than most contractors anticipate. When a renovation wraps up in late spring or early summer, the region's low humidity pulls moisture out of freshly applied caulk and joint compound quickly, which is good for cure times but means fine drywall dust stays airborne longer before it settles.

Older homes in Bronte — many built during the mid-twentieth century ranching boom — often have original hardwood floors, single-pane windows with wood frames, and interior walls with multiple layers of paint. Renovation work on these structures generates a different debris profile than new construction: lead paint chips are a real possibility in pre-1978 homes, and our team holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification to handle that material correctly. Plaster walls, where they still exist, hold dust in their texture in a way that drywall does not, requiring slower, more deliberate cleaning passes rather than a quick wipe-down.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Bronte

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through to categorize what's there: construction debris, fine particulate, chemical residues, and any materials that require special handling. From that assessment, the crew sequences the work from ceiling to floor — HEPA-filtered vacuuming of surfaces, ducts, and registers before any wet cleaning begins, so disturbed dust doesn't resettle on already-cleaned areas.

For specialty cleaning after renovation, that sequence expands. Window tracks and frames get detail-cleaned of caulk smears and paint overspray. Cabinet interiors are wiped down before hardware is installed or verified. Tile and stone surfaces are cleaned of grout haze using appropriate pH-balanced solutions — alkaline cleaners on cement grout, acidic on mineral deposits, never the reverse. Flooring receives a final clean calibrated to the material: hardwood gets a dry microfiber pass followed by a manufacturer-compatible cleaner; tile gets a scrub-and-rinse cycle. The HVAC system gets a final filter check, because caliche-laden construction air in this part of West Texas will have loaded a new filter well before the job is done.

As an IICRC Certified Firm, the team follows documented cleaning protocols rather than improvising by room — which matters when a general contractor or property owner needs written confirmation that the space was cleaned to a defined standard before occupancy.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area cleaning crews in Coke County: the water. Bronte's municipal supply draws from sources with naturally high mineral content, which is common across the Edwards Plateau. Hard water leaves visible spotting on glass and chrome fixtures almost immediately after cleaning if the rinse water isn't managed correctly. We use filtered water for final rinses on glass surfaces and chrome fixtures, and we dry immediately rather than letting rinse water air-evaporate — otherwise a freshly cleaned window can look spotted within the hour, and a client assumes the job wasn't done right. It's a small detail, but it's the kind of thing that separates a final clean that actually looks final from one that needs to be redone.
