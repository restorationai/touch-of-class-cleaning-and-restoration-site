## Why Brownwood Properties See Duct Buildup Faster

Brown County sits in a transitional zone between the Edwards Plateau and the rolling plains to the north, and the soil here is a mix of sandy loam and clay that dries to a fine powder during drought stretches. That particulate gets drawn into return-air grilles — especially on single-story ranch homes where returns are low to the floor — and coats the interior lining of flex duct and sheet metal alike. Older homes in Brownwood, many of which were built in the 1950s through 1970s when the city was growing steadily around the regional economy, often have duct systems that were sized for window units or early central air and have been retrofitted multiple times since. Those seams and transitions are prime collection points for debris, and in some cases the original insulation wrap on older flex duct has degraded enough to allow outside air infiltration, which compounds the contamination.

Beyond dust, Brownwood's position on the Central Flyway means seasonal pollen loads are significant — cedar and oak in late winter, grasses through spring and early summer. Homes that keep windows open during mild stretches before the heat sets in tend to pull more biological material into the system than homes in more urban, sealed environments.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Brownwood

The process starts with a visual inspection of the supply and return registers, the air handler, and any accessible duct runs. For most Brownwood homes, that means checking both the attic runs — where summer attic temperatures can exceed 140°F and accelerate the breakdown of duct lining — and any crawl-space or under-floor ductwork present in older construction.

Once the system is mapped, we use negative-pressure equipment to place the duct system under vacuum while mechanical agitation tools break loose debris from duct walls. The dislodged material is captured in a HEPA-filtered collection unit rather than redistributed into the living space. For systems with visible microbial growth or persistent odor, we offer an EPA-registered sanitizing treatment applied to interior duct surfaces after the mechanical cleaning is complete. Dryer vent cleaning follows the same visit when requested — a worthwhile add-on given how quickly lint accumulates in Brownwood's dry air, where static cling is a year-round factor.
