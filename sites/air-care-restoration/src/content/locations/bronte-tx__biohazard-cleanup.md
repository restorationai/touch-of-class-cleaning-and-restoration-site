Coke County's rural character shapes the types of biohazard situations that arise here. An unattended situation in a poorly ventilated storage building or a seldom-checked guest house can allow biological material to interact with porous surfaces — raw wood framing, concrete slab floors, or older linoleum — far longer than in a routinely occupied space.
