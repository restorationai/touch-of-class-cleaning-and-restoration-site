## Why Clyde Properties Hold Odors Longer Than You Might Expect

Callahan County's low humidity for much of the year is a double-edged condition. Dry air slows the natural off-gassing that sometimes helps dissipate mild odors outdoors, but indoors it allows volatile organic compounds from smoke residue, pet contamination, or sewage to concentrate in the air column rather than being absorbed into ambient moisture. Older ranch-era homes in and around Clyde often feature wood-framed walls with minimal vapor barriers, meaning smoke and cooking odors penetrate the framing itself, not just the drywall surface. Spray a deodorizer on the wall and you have masked the problem; the source is still sitting in the studs. That is why professional odor elimination here requires methods that reach beyond the surface.

Agricultural properties on Clyde's outskirts add another layer. Livestock odors, rodent activity in crawl spaces, and grain-dust accumulation in attached storage areas can all migrate into living spaces, particularly when HVAC systems pull air from unconditioned zones. ## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Clyde

Every odor job starts with a source inspection, not a spray bottle. Technicians trace the odor to its origin, whether that is charred framing from a kitchen fire, saturated subfloor from a slow leak, or HVAC ductwork carrying smoke residue from a previous owner's habits. Once the source is confirmed and any contaminated material is removed or cleaned, we select the deodorization method that fits the structure and the odor type.

**Thermal fogging** uses a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing solution heated into a fine fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, reaching inside wall cavities, under flooring, and into upholstery fibers. It is particularly effective after fire and smoke losses in Clyde's older wood-frame homes.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** generates hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules at a chemical level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. This method works well in occupied or partially occupied structures and is safe around most electronics and soft goods.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe, embedded odors. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer, and when deployed correctly in a sealed, vacated structure, it neutralizes odor compounds that surface treatments cannot reach. Proper concentration, dwell time, and post-treatment airing are critical to safe and effective results.

After treatment, we verify results with post-deodorization testing rather than relying on a technician's nose alone.
