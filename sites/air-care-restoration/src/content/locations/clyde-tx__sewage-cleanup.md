## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Clyde

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and it requires a structured response, not just a mop and bleach. Here is how the process works on a Clyde job:

**Containment and safety assessment.** Before extraction begins, technicians assess which areas are affected, establish containment to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces, and confirm that utilities are safe to work around.

**Extraction of standing waste.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing sewage from flooring, subfloor cavities, and any other affected surfaces. In slab-on-grade homes — common in this part of Texas — there is no crawl space buffer, meaning contamination can wick directly into flooring materials from below.

**Removal of unsalvageable materials.** Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 contamination — drywall, insulation, carpet, pad — are bagged, documented, and removed per EPA and OSHA guidelines. Saving saturated porous material is not a cost-saving measure; it becomes a mold and pathogen reservoir.

**Disinfection and antimicrobial treatment.** Hard surfaces, subfloor sheathing, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. The goal is not just odor control — it is verified pathogen reduction on every affected surface.

**Drying and documentation.** After sanitization, affected cavities are dried using commercial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged throughout so your insurance carrier has a clear record of the remediation scope.
