## Why Paint Rock Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Concho County's housing stock skews older, and many Paint Rock properties reflect decades of deferred maintenance or original construction that predates modern building codes. Older homes in this part of West Texas frequently feature porous concrete slab foundations, unfinished subfloor spaces, and wall cavities without vapor barriers — all of which can harbor and retain biological contamination well beyond what is visible at the surface. In the dry, high-heat climate of the Concho Valley, biological material can desiccate quickly, which may make a scene appear contained when contamination has actually wicked into porous materials beneath. That surface appearance is misleading, and it's exactly why proper ATP testing and surface sampling matter here, not just a visual inspection.

Rural properties in this region also tend to have septic systems rather than municipal sewer connections, which adds a layer of coordination when wastewater or contaminated liquids are involved. Our technicians are OSHA trained and work within EPA guidelines for handling and transporting regulated biological waste, so disposal is handled correctly regardless of the property's infrastructure.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Paint Rock

Every job begins with a careful assessment — not a rushed walkthrough. We identify all affected surfaces, including those that aren't immediately obvious, and document the scope before any physical work begins. That documentation matters for insurance purposes and for your own peace of mind.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC pathways.
- **Removal of porous materials** — in older Paint Rock homes, this may include sections of drywall, subflooring, or insulation that cannot be adequately decontaminated in place.
- **Hospital-grade disinfection** — EPA-registered disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens are applied to all affected hard surfaces.
- **Odor neutralization** — not masking. Fogging and hydroxyl treatment address odor at the molecular level, which is especially important in homes with limited ventilation common in older Concho County construction.
- **Verification** — we do not close out a job on visual inspection alone. Surface testing confirms that contamination has been reduced to safe levels before we leave.

All biological waste is packaged, labeled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility in accordance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations.
