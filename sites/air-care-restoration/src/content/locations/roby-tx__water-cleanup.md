## Why Roby Properties See Water Damage Differently Than Most Texas Towns

Fisher County's clay-heavy soil is notorious for swelling when saturated and shrinking during dry stretches, which puts constant stress on slab foundations and the plumbing embedded in them. A hairline crack in a slab that seemed harmless through a dry spring can become a slow leak that saturates the subfloor for weeks before any surface sign appears. By the time a homeowner notices a soft spot in the flooring or a spike in the water bill, moisture has often already migrated laterally under the concrete.

Roby's agricultural economy also means many properties include outbuildings, equipment sheds, or converted structures that weren't originally built with the same vapor barriers and drainage slopes as a primary residence. Water intrusion in those spaces tends to be underreported until mold or structural softening makes it impossible to ignore. The region's periodic heavy thunderstorms — capable of dropping several inches in an hour on ground that can't absorb it — create flash-flooding conditions that push water through foundation gaps, window wells, and low-lying doorways.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Roby

Effective water cleanup in this part of West Texas isn't just about running a wet-vac and setting fans. The process starts with thermal imaging and moisture mapping to find water that has already migrated beyond the visible wet area — especially important in homes with slab foundations where water travels horizontally under flooring before it ever shows on the surface.

Once the full moisture boundary is established, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted equipment capable of pulling hundreds of gallons quickly. After bulk extraction, drying is calibrated to the specific materials present: tile over a concrete slab dries differently than hardwood over a wood subfloor, and both behave differently in Roby's low-humidity summer air compared to a humid coastal climate. Drying equipment placement, airflow direction, and temperature are adjusted daily based on psychrometric readings — not guesswork — until every affected cavity reads within normal range. The work follows IICRC S500 standards throughout.

Affected materials that can't be dried in place — saturated insulation, compromised drywall, swollen cabinetry — are documented, removed, and inventoried for your insurance claim before disposal.

## Working With Your Insurance Carrier After a Roby Water Loss

Most homeowner policies in Texas cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an ice-maker line that let go. Gradual leaks and flood damage from rising surface water are typically treated differently, and knowing that distinction before you file matters. Air Care Restoration documents the loss thoroughly from the first hour: photographs, moisture readings, equipment logs, and a written scope of affected materials. That documentation goes directly to your adjuster and is formatted to support the claim rather than complicate it.

If your carrier requires a preferred vendor or has a specific documentation format, let us know at the time of the call and we'll coordinate accordingly.

## Local Note

In Fisher County, many of the older ranch-style homes in and around Roby were built on pier-and-beam foundations rather than slabs — a construction style common across this part of West Texas through the mid-twentieth century. Pier-and-beam homes have a crawl space beneath the floor, which is an advantage for plumbing access but a complication during a water loss: moisture that gets into that crawl space lingers far longer than it would on a slab, and it creates ideal conditions for mold growth on the wooden joists and subfloor decking above. When we respond to a water call in a pier-and-beam home, crawl space inspection and drying is part of the standard scope — not an add-on — because missing it means the job isn't actually done.
