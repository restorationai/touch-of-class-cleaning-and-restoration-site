## Why Clyde Properties Face Particular Vandalism Challenges

Callahan County's climate is hard on building surfaces even before vandalism enters the picture. The combination of intense UV exposure, low humidity, and occasional blowing dust means that spray paint and marker tags bond to exterior masonry, metal panels, and wood siding faster here than in more temperate parts of Texas. A tag left on a concrete block wall overnight in summer can already be UV-cured by morning, requiring more aggressive chemical treatment or mechanical removal than fresh graffiti in a cooler, shadier environment.

Many commercial properties along the Highway 283 corridor and older residential structures in Clyde were built with unpainted concrete block or rough-textured stucco, both of which are notoriously porous. Paint and permanent marker seep into those pores rather than sitting on the surface, which means a single cleanup pass rarely finishes the job. Our process accounts for that porosity from the first assessment.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Clyde

Every job starts with a walk-through to categorize the damage: graffiti type (spray paint, marker, etching), substrate material, surface condition, and any secondary damage such as broken glass, forced entry, or structural compromise. That assessment drives the method, not a one-size approach.

For graffiti on masonry or stucco, we use solvent-based removers calibrated to the substrate, followed by hot-water pressure washing where the surface can tolerate it. On wood siding or painted metal, we step down to lower-pressure methods to avoid driving contaminants deeper or stripping protective coatings. Broken glass cleanup includes full shard collection, frame inspection, and boarding or temporary glazing if a permanent replacement is not immediately available. Any biohazard component, such as human waste left during a trespass event, is handled under our standard containment and disposal protocols.

Documentation runs throughout the process. We photograph each surface before treatment, during, and after, and we compile a written damage inventory that your insurance carrier or property manager can use directly.
