## Why Merkel Properties See Water Damage Differently Than Larger Cities

Merkel sits in a stretch of West Texas where the soil shifts between clay and caliche depending on how deep you dig. That soil behavior matters for homeowners: expansive clay beneath a slab foundation absorbs water and swells, then contracts sharply during dry spells, creating micro-cracks that allow future water intrusion at the same points year after year. Many homes in the Merkel area were built in the mid-twentieth century on pier-and-beam foundations, a style that leaves a crawl space vulnerable to groundwater migration after heavy rain. Unlike poured-slab construction, pier-and-beam floors can trap moisture underneath the subfloor for days without any visible sign at the surface — by the time a homeowner notices a soft spot or musty odor, the floor joists may already be saturated.

Merkel also sits in a region that sees both flash flooding from thunderstorm runoff and occasional plumbing failures driven by temperature swings. Winter nights in Taylor County can drop below freezing, and pipes in uninsulated exterior walls or crawl spaces are vulnerable to freezing and bursting when temperatures rebound quickly the following afternoon.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Merkel

Every water cleanup job begins with a thorough moisture assessment — not just a visual walk-through. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map wet zones behind walls, beneath flooring, and inside ceiling cavities. In Merkel's older housing stock, this step is especially important because water wicks through original plaster and wood lath differently than it moves through modern drywall. Plaster walls can read dry at the surface while the lath behind them stays saturated for days.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Standing water extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors remove bulk water from floors, carpets, and crawl spaces.
2. **Controlled demolition (when needed)** — saturated drywall, insulation, or flooring that cannot be dried in place is removed to expose structural framing for drying.
3. **Drying and dehumidification** — commercial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers run continuously, monitored with daily moisture readings until materials reach target drywall moisture content per the IICRC S500 standard.
4. **Documentation and clearance** — moisture logs are recorded throughout, providing the paper trail your insurance adjuster will need to process the claim.

## Local Note

In Merkel and the surrounding Taylor County area, a significant portion of the housing stock was built before modern vapor barriers were standard practice. Crawl spaces in these homes often have bare soil as the ground cover, which allows ground moisture to evaporate upward into the floor system continuously — even without a discrete water event. After a water loss in one of these homes, drying times run longer than the industry average because the dehumidifiers are fighting both the intrusion moisture and the ambient vapor rising from below. Experienced technicians account for this by placing dehumidification equipment at the crawl space level, not just the living space above, and by extending the drying window rather than pulling equipment prematurely.
