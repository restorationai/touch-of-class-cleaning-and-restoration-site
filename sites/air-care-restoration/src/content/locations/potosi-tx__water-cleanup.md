## Why Potosi Properties Are Vulnerable to Water Intrusion

The Taylor County region around Potosi is built on expansive clay soils that swell when wet and shrink when dry. Ranch-style homes common to this part of West Texas often have long horizontal runs of supply and drain plumbing beneath the slab, and a single pinhole leak under pressure can saturate a wide area before a homeowner notices anything at the surface.

Potosi also sits in a corridor that sees significant hail and wind-driven rain during spring storm season. ## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Potosi

Every water cleanup starts with moisture mapping.
