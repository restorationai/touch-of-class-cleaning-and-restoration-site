## Why Early Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

Early's building stock is a mix of mid-century brick commercial buildings and residential construction that spans several decades, much of it using materials that absorb paint and chemical agents faster than newer surfaces. Brick, stucco, and older concrete block are common here, and all three are highly porous. Spray paint does not just sit on the surface of these materials; it wicks into the substrate within the first few hours of sun exposure. Brown County's low humidity and intense summer heat accelerate that process significantly compared to more temperate parts of Texas.

There is also a practical consideration specific to smaller cities like Early: property owners often do not discover the damage until the following morning, which means the vandalism has already been baking in the heat for hours by the time a call comes in. ## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Early

Every job starts with a full site walk to document the scope: what surfaces are affected, what materials were used (paint, marker, etching compound, impact damage), and whether any structural elements like glass, framing, or locking hardware need attention alongside the surface cleanup.

For graffiti on brick or masonry, the approach depends on how deeply the paint has penetrated. Fresh tags on sealed surfaces often respond to hot-water pressure washing combined with a chemical solvent matched to the paint type. Tags on unsealed or older brick typically require a multi-step process: chemical dwell time, agitation, rinse, and in some cases light abrasive blasting calibrated to avoid damaging the mortar joints. Rushing that sequence causes more harm than the vandalism itself.

Broken glass cleanup follows a separate protocol: visible shards are removed first, then the area is swept and vacuumed with HEPA equipment to capture fine fragments that embed in flooring, carpet, or soil. If the broken glass is part of a storefront or entry door, we board and secure the opening while permanent repairs are arranged, so the property is not left exposed.

Throughout the process, the crew photographs each stage. That documentation supports insurance claims and, where relevant, law enforcement reports.
