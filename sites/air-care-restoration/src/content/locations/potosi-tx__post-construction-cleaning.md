Post-construction dust is not ordinary house dust. ## Why Potosi Properties Face Particular Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

The West Texas climate that defines Potosi creates conditions that amplify the mess left behind after any build or renovation. Caliche-heavy soil is common across this stretch of Taylor County, and when construction traffic churns it up, the pale, alkaline dust migrates indoors through every gap in the building envelope. On windy days, which are frequent across these open plains, that dust re-enters a structure repeatedly until doors and windows are properly sealed. New construction in the area often sits exposed for weeks or months between framing and finishing, giving wind-driven soil a long window to accumulate inside wall cavities and on every horizontal surface.

The region's low annual humidity also matters. Drywall compound and grout dry faster here than in more humid climates, which is good for schedules but means fine particulate becomes airborne again with very little disturbance. A crew walking through a freshly finished space can re-suspend settled dust that a humid environment would have kept in place. That re-suspension is exactly why a systematic, sequenced cleaning approach matters more here than in many other parts of Texas.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Potosi

A proper final clean is not a single pass with a mop. The work moves from the top of each room down, and from the interior of the structure outward, so that debris dislodged from ceilings and light fixtures does not contaminate surfaces already cleaned below.

The sequence typically runs like this:

- **HVAC and duct inspection first.** Construction debris inside ductwork will redistribute throughout the building the moment the system runs. We inspect and clean supply and return registers before any surface work begins.
- **Overhead and wall surfaces.** Ceiling fans, light fixtures, window frames, and door casings collect the heaviest layer of drywall dust. These are wiped with microfiber before vacuuming floors.
- **Hard-surface floors and grout lines.** Tile grout and luxury vinyl plank seams trap fine silica that ordinary mopping smears rather than removes. We use appropriate tools and solutions matched to each flooring type.
- **Cabinet interiors, drawers, and appliance cavities.** Builders rarely clean inside upper cabinet boxes or beneath appliance kick plates. These spaces collect construction debris that homeowners discover weeks later.
- **Final inspection walk.** Every window, switch plate, outlet cover, and threshold gets a final check before we close out.

For specialty cleaning requests such as post-renovation deep cleans, pressure washing of exterior flatwork, or cleaning after tenant buildouts in commercial spaces, the scope is scoped and documented before work begins so there are no surprises on either side.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area cleaning crews in this part of Taylor County is the caliche dust behavior inside newly built homes. Because the soil is so fine and alkaline, it leaves a faint white film on dark countertops and glass that looks like hard water spotting but does not respond to the same treatments.
