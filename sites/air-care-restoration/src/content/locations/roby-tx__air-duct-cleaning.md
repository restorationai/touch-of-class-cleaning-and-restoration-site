## Why Roby Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than Most

Roby sits in a stretch of West Texas where the soil is light, the wind is persistent, and agricultural activity keeps fine particulate matter circulating in the air for much of the year. Cotton harvest season pushes lint and debris into outdoor air intakes. Dry summers mean little moisture to weigh down dust before it enters a return vent. Homes built on slab foundations — common across this part of Fisher County — often have ductwork running through unconditioned attic spaces where summer heat accelerates the degradation of duct liner insulation, releasing fibers into the airstream alongside ordinary dust.

Older homes in the area frequently have original flex duct or even older metal trunk-and-branch systems that have never been professionally cleaned. Gaps at joints and connections let unconditioned attic air — loaded with insulation particles and dust — infiltrate the supply side. What comes out of your registers is a mix of whatever the system was designed to deliver and whatever the attic has been contributing for years.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Roby

The process starts with an inspection of the full duct system — supply and return branches, the air handler cabinet, and any accessible plenums. In homes with attic-run flex duct, we check for collapsed sections and disconnected joints before cleaning begins, because pushing air through a compromised duct only redistributes debris rather than removing it.

Cleaning uses negative-air pressure: a high-powered vacuum collection unit is connected to the main trunk while rotating brush agitation works through individual branch runs. This combination dislodges compacted debris and pulls it toward the collection point rather than back into the living space. Registers and grilles are removed, cleaned separately, and reinstalled. If the system shows signs of microbial growth — common after any period of moisture intrusion or a leaking evaporator coil — we apply an EPA-registered sanitizing agent to interior duct surfaces after mechanical cleaning is complete.

Dryer vent cleaning is often added at the same visit. In Roby's dry climate, lint accumulates quickly in dryer exhaust runs, and the fire risk is real. A clogged dryer vent also forces the appliance to work harder and longer, which shows up on the electric bill.
