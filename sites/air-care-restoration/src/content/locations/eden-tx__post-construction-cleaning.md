## Why Eden Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Concho County's climate runs hot and dry through most of the year, which sounds forgiving for construction — but that aridity creates its own complications at the cleaning stage. Drywall compound and joint compound dust become almost talc-fine in low-humidity conditions, suspending in the air long after crews have packed up. It settles on surfaces that look clean at a glance but leave a hazy film under direct light. On top of that, many homes in and around Eden were built during the mid-twentieth-century ranch-house boom, with construction details — tongue-and-groove ceilings, older window framing, and original cabinetry — that require careful hand-cleaning rather than aggressive mechanical scrubbing.

New construction in the area also tends to involve significant concrete and masonry work, given the local preference for durable materials suited to the climate. Concrete splatter on tile, grout haze on flooring, and overspray on fixtures are routine finds on a post-construction walkthrough here. These aren't cosmetic afterthoughts — left untreated, hardened concrete residue can permanently etch natural stone and porcelain.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Eden

Every project starts with a structured walkthrough to document what the construction phase left behind — debris type, surface conditions, any materials that require special handling. Because Air Care Restoration holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, older Eden properties where renovation work disturbed pre-existing painted surfaces get an additional layer of attention during this assessment phase.

From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Rough clean**: removal of construction debris, drywall scraps, packaging, and bulk dust from all surfaces and floor areas
- **Detail clean**: hand-wiping of window sills, door frames, cabinetry interiors, light fixtures, and hardware; HEPA vacuuming of ductwork registers and returns
- **Surface-specific treatment**: grout haze removal, concrete splatter treatment on tile and stone, glass polishing to remove overspray and adhesive residue
- **Final inspection clean**: a second pass timed close to move-in or owner walkthrough, addressing any settling dust that accumulated between phases

The IICRC-certified team follows documented cleaning protocols throughout, which matters when a general contractor or property owner needs written confirmation that the space was cleaned to a defined standard.

## Local Note: Caliche Dust and HVAC Systems

One thing that catches out-of-area cleaning crews in Eden and the broader Concho County region: the caliche-heavy soil that characterizes this part of the Edwards Plateau becomes an extremely fine, alkaline dust when disturbed during grading and foundation work. It infiltrates HVAC systems during construction at a rate that surprises contractors used to working in clay-soil or coastal environments. A thorough post-construction clean in Eden should always include a close inspection of air handler returns and supply registers — not just a visual check, but actual removal and cleaning of register covers and a look at the condition of the filter housing. Skipping this step means the first time the HVAC runs at full capacity, that accumulated caliche dust recirculates through a freshly cleaned interior.
