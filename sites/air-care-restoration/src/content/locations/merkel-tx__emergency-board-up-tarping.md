## Why Merkel Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After a Loss

Merkel sits in a stretch of West Texas where the weather swings hard in both directions. The same dry, cracked soil that makes foundation movement a persistent issue for area homeowners also means that when rain does arrive — often in the form of severe thunderstorms with large hail — it comes fast and heavy. Roof decking that takes a hail strike is immediately at risk of water intrusion if it isn't covered quickly. Fire damage compounds this: once a roof structure is compromised by heat, even a light overnight rain can soak insulation, subfloor, and interior framing before a restoration crew can begin drying.

Merkel's housing stock includes a significant number of older wood-frame homes built during the mid-twentieth century agricultural boom in this region. Those structures often have original roof sheathing — tongue-and-groove boards rather than modern plywood — that absorbs water faster and is more prone to warping when exposed. Getting a tarp in place over that kind of decking isn't just about keeping rain out tonight; it's about preserving material that can't easily be sourced or matched during reconstruction.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Merkel

When a call comes in from a Merkel property, the first priority is a rapid exterior assessment. We identify every breach point — broken windows, compromised doors, missing or punctured roof sections — before any material goes up. Boarding a window incorrectly can trap smoke odor inside or block emergency egress points that may still be needed; the assessment prevents those mistakes.

For window and door board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fastened to the structural framing, not just the window casing. On older homes with thinner exterior walls, we adjust fastener depth and placement to avoid splitting aged wood. For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps secured with lumber battens and screws driven into sound decking — not just weighted at the edges. In West Texas wind, a tarp that isn't mechanically fastened is gone by morning.

We document every opening, every measurement, and every fastener point with photographs before and after. That documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster and becomes part of the claim file.

One thing worth knowing about Merkel's older wood-frame homes: the exterior siding on many of them is original drop siding or lap board that has been painted over multiple times across decades.
