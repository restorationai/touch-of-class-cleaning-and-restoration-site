Hawley sits in Jones County where the West Texas wind never really quits, and that wind carries fine caliche dust, cotton gin particulates during harvest season, and dry-season allergens straight into return-air grilles on homes that were often built without much thought for air sealing. Over time, those ducts accumulate a layer of grit that no filter fully stops, and the same forced-air system meant to keep your family comfortable ends up redistributing that buildup every time the thermostat kicks on. If your home has been feeling stuffier than usual or your filters are clogging faster than they used to, your ductwork is probably overdue for a professional cleaning.

## Why Hawley Homes Accumulate Duct Debris Faster Than Average

The Jones County climate runs hot and dry most of the year, which means HVAC systems in Hawley cycle hard from late spring through early fall. That extended run time pulls more air through the duct system than you'd see in a milder climate, and more airflow means more opportunity for dust, pollen, and soil particles to settle inside the ductwork.

The local soil is a reddish clay-caliche mix that dries to a fine powder in summer. When it gets tracked inside or blown through a poorly sealed crawl space or attic penetration, it finds its way into the duct system quickly. Homes in Hawley that were built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a significant portion of the housing stock in town, often have original flex duct or early sheet-metal runs that were never designed for easy cleaning and can develop small gaps at joints over decades of thermal expansion and contraction. Those gaps let attic air, which in a West Texas summer can exceed 140 degrees and carries its own load of insulation fibers and dust, bypass the filter entirely.

Dryer vents are a related concern. In older Hawley homes, dryer vent runs are sometimes longer than current best practices recommend, and the accordion-style flexible duct that was standard for years traps lint at every bend. A partially blocked dryer vent is both a fire risk and a sign that the rest of your ventilation system may need attention.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Hawley

When an Air Care Restoration crew arrives at a Hawley property, the first step is a visual inspection of every accessible register, return, and air handler cabinet. We use a camera where the duct layout allows it so you can see what we see before any equipment runs.

Cleaning is done with truck-mounted or high-powered portable negative-air equipment that puts the entire duct system under negative pressure, then dislodges debris with agitation tools working from the registers back toward the unit. Nothing gets blown loose into your living space. Once the mechanical cleaning is complete, we offer EPA-registered sanitization for the interior duct surfaces, which is especially useful in Hawley homes that have had any moisture intrusion, whether from a roof leak, a failed evaporator drain line, or condensation issues during the brief humid stretches that follow spring thunderstorms.

Dryer vent cleaning is handled as a separate line item and takes about 30 to 45 minutes on a standard residential run. We clear the vent from the interior connection point all the way to the exterior cap and confirm airflow with a simple pressure check before we leave.
