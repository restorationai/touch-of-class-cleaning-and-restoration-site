## Why Eastland Properties Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock in and around Eastland leans heavily toward mid-century construction: wood-framed walls, plaster or early drywall, pier-and-beam foundations with open crawl spaces, and attic spaces ventilated through louvered gable vents. Each of those features creates a pathway for odor molecules to migrate and settle. Smoke from a structure fire or a long-running cigarette habit doesn't stay in the room where it originated. It drafts into wall cavities through electrical penetrations, rises into attic insulation, and settles into the subflooring above a crawl space.

Eastland County's climate adds a layer of complexity. The dry heat accelerates the polymerization of smoke residue on surfaces, essentially baking odor compounds into paint, wood grain, and fabric. When cooler, more humid air returns in fall or after a rain event, those baked-in compounds off-gas again. Homeowners often notice the smell returning weeks after a basic cleaning, not because the cleaning failed, but because the source was never fully addressed.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Eastland

Effective deodorization in Eastland starts with source identification, not equipment deployment. Before any ozone treatment or thermal fogging begins, the crew walks the structure to locate the primary odor source and any secondary migration paths. In pier-and-beam homes, that often means inspecting the crawl space and the underside of the subfloor, where animal odors and mold-adjacent smells accumulate out of sight.

Once the source is located and any contaminated material is removed or treated, the process moves through layered deodorization methods calibrated to the odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** for smoke odors: a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed to penetrate the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** for occupied or semi-occupied spaces: hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor compounds without requiring the structure to be vacated for extended periods, making them practical for Eastland rental properties or homes with livestock-adjacent outbuildings nearby.
- **Ozone treatment** for severe or persistent odors: high-concentration ozone is used in unoccupied structures where other methods have not fully resolved the problem. The structure must remain unoccupied during treatment and aired out thoroughly before re-entry.

After treatment, air quality is assessed and the crew documents the process for insurance purposes if a claim is involved.
