## Why Eden Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Eden sits in the heart of the Edwards Plateau transition zone, where the soil shifts between caliche-heavy hardpan and sandy loam depending on how close you are to the Concho River drainage. That soil variability matters for septic systems: hardpan layers can cause drain fields to fail prematurely, pushing effluent back toward the home rather than dispersing it underground. Older homes in town — many built during Eden's mid-century agricultural boom — often have cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals that have corroded or collapsed over the decades. When those lines fail, raw sewage doesn't just pool in a single room; it wicks under flooring, into wall cavities, and through the subfloor before the smell is even noticeable.

The region also sees intense, short-duration thunderstorms that can overwhelm municipal lift stations and private septic systems alike. A two-inch rainfall event in an hour is enough to saturate drain fields and force sewage back through floor drains and toilets — a pattern common across this part of West Texas in late spring and early fall.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Eden

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the highest contamination level — and the process reflects that. When we arrive, the first step is containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean rooms and establishing negative air pressure where the structure allows. We then extract all standing sewage using truck-mounted and portable extraction units before any assessment of materials begins.

Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 water — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are almost always removed rather than dried in place. There is no safe way to dry contaminated porous material to a standard that protects occupants. Once the structure is cleared of saturated materials, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to all affected surfaces, including subfloor sheathing, concrete slab, and framing. Drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings across all structural layers return to pre-loss baselines. We document every reading throughout the process for your insurance file.

One thing that comes up specifically in Eden and the surrounding Concho County area: a significant number of properties run on private septic systems rather than municipal sewer, and those systems often haven't been inspected or pumped in years. When a septic overflow causes interior contamination, the source problem — a full tank, a collapsed baffle, a failed drain field — has to be addressed by a licensed septic contractor before any interior restoration holds.
