## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Buffalo Gap

Every vandalism call starts with a documented assessment before any cleaning begins. Photographs capture the full scope of damage for insurance purposes, and material type is identified before any chemical or mechanical method is applied. What removes graffiti from a painted cinder-block wall can permanently etch limestone or stain brick mortar, so surface-specific treatment is not optional, it is the baseline.

For graffiti removal, the process typically moves through these stages:

- **Surface identification and test-patch:** A small, inconspicuous area is treated first to confirm the removal agent will not cause secondary damage.
- **Chemical application and dwell time:** Graffiti removers are matched to the substrate, alkaline solutions for painted surfaces, solvent-based gels for porous stone, and gentler citrus-based products where EPA Lead-Safe protocols require a lower-impact approach on older painted surfaces.
- **Mechanical agitation and rinse:** Soft-bristle brushing or low-pressure washing removes lifted pigment without eroding mortar joints or surface texture.
- **Broken glass and debris removal:** Shattered windows and forced-entry debris are cleared, bagged, and disposed of properly. Temporary board-up or window covering is coordinated if glazing replacement is pending.
- **Final documentation:** Post-cleanup photographs close out the damage record for the insurance claim file.
