## Why Paint Rock Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Concho County's climate creates a specific set of challenges for personal property after a loss event. Summers here push temperatures well above 100°F, which accelerates mold colonization on wet contents — IICRC guidance generally puts the window for mold growth at 24 to 48 hours in warm, humid conditions, and Paint Rock's summer heat compresses that timeline further. Winter cold snaps, meanwhile, can cause pipe bursts in older ranch-style homes that were built without insulated interior plumbing runs — a common construction pattern in small Central Texas towns where hard freezes were historically infrequent enough that builders didn't always account for them.

The region's caliche-heavy soil also drains poorly in heavy rain events, meaning water that enters a structure during a storm doesn't just come through the roof — it can wick up through slab foundations and saturate flooring, baseboards, and the furniture sitting on them. Contents that look dry on the surface may already be holding moisture inside cushions, drawer cavities, or wood joints.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Paint Rock

When we arrive at a Paint Rock property, the first step is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition category before anything is moved. That documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

Items that can be restored on-site — hard-surface furniture, appliances, metal fixtures — are treated in place when conditions allow. Items that need deeper intervention are carefully packed out and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility, where they're protected from the temperature extremes that would continue damaging them if left in a compromised structure. Depending on the type of loss, restoration may involve ultrasonic cleaning for small valuables and electronics, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor, thermal fogging for deeply embedded residues, or specialized document and textile drying.

Once the structure is cleared and your home is ready, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed — not just dropped at the door.
