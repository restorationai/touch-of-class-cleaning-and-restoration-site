## Why Roby Properties See Mold Issues

Fisher County sits in a semi-arid climate that swings between prolonged dry spells and sudden, heavy rainfall events — the kind that overwhelm older gutters, foundation seals, and window caulking that may not have been updated in decades. Many homes in Roby were built during the mid-twentieth century, when vapor barriers were minimal or nonexistent and insulation choices weren't designed with moisture management in mind. That construction profile means water that enters a wall cavity or subfloor doesn't have a natural path out. It sits. Cellulose insulation, wood framing, and older drywall compounds are all organic materials that mold spores readily colonize.

Agricultural dust is another factor that doesn't come up in city-based remediation guides. The fine particulate matter common to farming communities can carry mold spores indoors through gaps in window frames and door thresholds, seeding surfaces that are only slightly damp — levels that a homeowner might not even notice until a musty odor becomes persistent.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Roby

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the visible surface where mold appears, but the materials behind it. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help identify wet zones inside walls and under flooring that look dry to the eye. Mold that's treated at the surface without addressing the moisture source will return.

Once the scope is clear, the affected area is isolated with physical containment barriers and negative air pressure to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Porous materials that cannot be adequately cleaned — certain drywall sections, insulation batts, compromised wood framing — are removed and properly disposed of. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned and treated using EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the process to capture airborne spores.

Before containment comes down, air quality is verified. The goal is returning the space to a condition that's safe to occupy and structurally dry — not just visually clean.
