## Why Hawley Properties Face Particular Contents Challenges

The housing stock in and around Hawley skews toward older ranch-style and pier-and-beam construction, many of which were built with solid wood cabinetry, upholstered furniture, and textiles that absorb contaminants deeply. When a fire or water event hits one of these homes, smoke particles and moisture do not stay on the surface. They migrate into wood grain, foam cushioning, and natural-fiber rugs in ways that require specialized cleaning methods rather than a simple wipe-down.

Jones County's climate adds another layer. Summer temperatures regularly push well past 100 degrees Fahrenheit, and relative humidity swings dramatically between storm events and dry spells. A piece of furniture that gets wet during a roof leak or burst pipe and then sits in that heat can begin showing mold colonization in as little as 24 to 48 hours. The same dry heat that follows can lock odors into fabric before they are ever properly treated. Timing matters enormously here.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Hawley

The process begins with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. This documentation matters for insurance purposes and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what condition.

From there, salvageable contents are carefully packed out and transported to a secure, climate-controlled storage facility. Items are sorted by material type because the cleaning method for a cast-iron skillet is entirely different from the approach used on a painted canvas, a leather sofa, or a child's stuffed animal. Our IICRC FSRT-certified technicians use ultrasonic cleaning, ozone treatment, thermal fogging, and dry-cleaning methods depending on what each item needs. Items that cannot be safely restored are documented for your insurance claim rather than discarded without your knowledge.

Once your home is repaired and cleared for re-occupancy, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in the rooms they came from. You do not have to manage a storage unit or coordinate a moving company.
