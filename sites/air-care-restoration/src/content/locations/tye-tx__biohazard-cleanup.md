## Why Tye Properties Present Specific Biohazard Considerations

Tye's housing stock leans toward single-family homes and manufactured residences built across several decades, many with crawl spaces or pier-and-beam foundations common throughout this stretch of West Texas. That construction style matters in biohazard situations: porous subflooring, unsealed concrete, and older HVAC ductwork can absorb or circulate contaminated particulates in ways that finished modern construction does not. The region's dry, high-desert climate — with low humidity and frequent wind — can accelerate the desiccation of biological material, which changes how it must be collected and packaged under OSHA and EPA guidelines. What looks contained on the surface may have migrated into adjacent materials, and a thorough assessment is the only way to know.

Sharps and needle cleanup is a category that comes up more often than many property owners expect, particularly in transitional rental properties and along rural roadways near the I-20 frontage. Proper sharps disposal requires puncture-resistant containment and transport by a licensed waste handler — it is not a task that should be handled with household gloves and a trash bag.

## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Tye

Every response begins with a scene assessment before any cleaning starts. The technician identifies the extent of contamination, documents affected surfaces for insurance purposes, and determines what materials can be decontaminated versus what must be removed and disposed of as regulated biological waste.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC zones.
- **Personal protective equipment and air controls** — OSHA-trained technicians work in full PPE; negative air pressure may be established depending on the scope.
- **Removal of porous materials** — carpet, padding, drywall, and subfloor sections that cannot be decontaminated are bagged, labeled, and transported off-site as regulated waste.
- **Disinfection** — hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces, with dwell times observed per manufacturer protocol.
- **Verification** — the area is inspected before containment is removed. Documentation of completed work is provided for insurance carriers and, where applicable, property management records.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with OSHA-trained technicians and EPA Lead-Safe certification, Air Care Restoration handles the regulatory side so you do not have to navigate it alone.
