## Why Paint Rock Homes Often Need More Than a Surface Remodel

Concho County's climate is not gentle to building materials. Summers push well past 100°F, and the temperature swings between seasons cause wood framing, caulk joints, and exterior trim to expand and contract in cycles that eventually open gaps. The region's caliche-heavy soil is notoriously expansive — it swells with moisture and shrinks in drought, which means slab foundations in Paint Rock can shift subtly over years, showing up as interior door frames that no longer square, cracked tile lines, or drywall seams that keep re-opening no matter how many times they are patched.

Older homes in this part of the Concho Valley were often built with materials that are no longer standard — pier-and-beam foundations, single-pane windows, galvanized supply lines, and minimal wall insulation. A kitchen remodel or bathroom update in a home like this is rarely just cosmetic; it typically uncovers plumbing that needs replacing, insulation that needs adding, or framing that needs reinforcing before new finishes go in. Having a contractor who understands that layered reality from the start saves homeowners from mid-project surprises and budget overruns.

## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Paint Rock

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough — not a quick visual scan, but a methodical assessment of the structure, utilities, and existing finishes. For post-damage rebuilds, that assessment ties directly to the mitigation documentation: moisture readings, affected material logs, and photo records that already exist from the drying phase. For planned remodels, it means measuring, probing, and asking the questions that reveal what is behind the walls before demolition begins.

From there, the process moves through permitting, material selection, demolition, rough-in work (framing, plumbing, electrical coordination), insulation and drywall, finish carpentry, tile and flooring, and final punch-out. Each phase is sequenced to minimize the time a home is in a partially finished state — a real consideration in a small community like Paint Rock, where a family may not have easy access to alternative housing during a long project.

Because Air Care Restoration is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, pre-1978 homes — and a meaningful portion of Paint Rock's housing stock falls into that category — can be handled without the project stalling at a lead-paint disclosure hurdle.

## Insurance and Post-Damage Rebuild Coordination

When a renovation follows a covered loss — a burst pipe, a fire, a hail event — the rebuild scope has to align with what the insurance carrier has approved. Air Care Restoration documents the damage thoroughly during mitigation, and that same documentation forms the foundation of the reconstruction estimate submitted to the adjuster. Working with one company across both phases reduces the back-and-forth that can stall a claim and delay the work.

For homeowners managing a claim in Concho County, it helps that the team is already familiar with the types of losses common to the area — wind-driven rain intrusion, hail damage to roofing and exterior cladding, and the occasional plumbing failure that goes unnoticed in a vacation or seasonal property until significant damage has accumulated.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors in Paint Rock and the surrounding Concho Valley: the caliche subgrade behaves unpredictably when it gets wet during a project. If demolition opens a slab or exposes a crawl space during a wet stretch, the soil underneath can shift enough to affect new tile layouts or door thresholds before the finish work is even done. Scheduling finish-floor work after the soil has had time to stabilize — and accounting for that in the project timeline rather than treating it as a delay — is the kind of local knowledge that keeps a remodel on track in this part of Texas.
