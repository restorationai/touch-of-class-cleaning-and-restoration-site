## Why Merkel Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Merkel's housing stock tells the story of a working ranching and farming community. Many homes here were built decades ago — some with original hardwood subfloors, tongue-and-groove paneling, or older concrete block construction that absorbs biological material differently than modern drywall and vinyl plank. Porous surfaces like unsealed concrete, aged wood framing, and older grout lines can harbor infectious material well below what's visible to the eye, which is why surface cleaning alone is never sufficient.

The region's dry, hot summers and periodic cold snaps also affect how biological material behaves. In high heat, decomposition accelerates and odor compounds penetrate porous building materials faster. In cooler months, the same materials can trap moisture and biological residue in ways that create secondary contamination risks if not addressed with proper ATP testing and EPA-registered disinfectants.

Rural properties around Merkel sometimes lack municipal sewer connections, relying instead on septic systems. Any cleanup involving bodily fluids or infectious material must account for proper waste disposal that does not compromise a private septic system — a detail that matters here in ways it simply doesn't in an urban setting.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Merkel

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. A trained technician walks the affected area, identifies all impacted surfaces and materials, and documents the scope before any work begins. That documentation serves two purposes: it protects you with your insurance carrier, and it ensures nothing is missed.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or HVAC pathways.
- **Removal of compromised materials** — porous materials that cannot be fully decontaminated are carefully bagged and disposed of according to Texas Department of State Health Services regulations for biohazardous waste.
- **Disinfection and treatment** — all remaining surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants. Our team operates under bloodborne pathogen protocols.
- **Verification** — ATP testing or similar surface testing confirms that treated areas meet accepted decontamination standards before the space is cleared.
- **Odor control** — where applicable, hydroxyl or thermal fogging addresses residual odor in the structure.

The goal is to return the space to a safe, livable condition — and to do it without leaving any visible sign that a crew was ever there.
