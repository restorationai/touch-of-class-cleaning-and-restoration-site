## Why Clyde Commercial Buildings See Water and Fire Damage

Much of Clyde's business stock was built with slab foundations and exposed rooftop HVAC units, both of which are common failure points. Flat or low-slope commercial roofs take a beating from West Texas hail and the wind-driven storms that move through Callahan County in spring, and a compromised roof membrane can let water track along ceiling joists for weeks before a tenant notices a stain. On the fire side, older electrical panels in buildings converted from one use to another (a former feed store now run as a retail space, for example) are a frequent ignition point, especially where wiring wasn't updated to match a new tenant's equipment load.

## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Clyde

We start with a damage assessment that separates what's structural from what's cosmetic, because a business owner needs to know what keeps them closed versus what can wait.
