## Why Baird Properties Face Particular Challenges After Vandalism

Callahan County's climate is not gentle to damaged surfaces. Summer heat regularly pushes past 100°F, and UV exposure accelerates paint oxidation, meaning spray paint and marker tags bond more deeply into porous masonry and stucco the longer they sit. Many commercial buildings along the US-283 corridor and older structures throughout Baird were built with native limestone block or painted brick, both of which are highly absorbent. Graffiti that might lift cleanly off a modern sealed surface in a week can require chemical poulticing and multiple treatment passes on weathered West Texas masonry.

Broken glass from forced entry or deliberate damage carries its own risk in this climate. Dust and windblown debris enter an unsecured opening quickly, and a property left open overnight can accumulate enough particulate contamination to complicate interior cleanup. Boarding and securing the structure is always the first physical step, not an afterthought.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Baird

Every job starts with a documented walkthrough. Crew members photograph and catalog every affected surface before touching anything, which protects your insurance claim and establishes a clear before-and-after record. From there, the process follows the damage type:

**Graffiti and surface tagging** is treated based on substrate. Painted block, brick, metal signage, and wood siding each respond differently to solvent-based removers, pressure washing, and abrasive media. We test in an inconspicuous area before committing to a method on a visible facade.

**Broken glass and structural breaches** are cleared, the opening is measured and boarded or temporarily glazed, and the surrounding area is swept for secondary glass fragments that can travel several feet from the point of impact.

**Interior damage** from forced entry, including overturned fixtures, scattered inventory, or surface defacement, is documented, cleaned, and where materials are beyond cleaning, removed and noted for the adjuster.

Because our team holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, we work to industry safety standards on every site, including proper containment when lead-based paint is disturbed on older structures. Our EPA Lead-Safe certification matters here: Baird has a stock of pre-1978 commercial and residential buildings where sanding or chemical stripping of painted surfaces triggers lead-safe work practice requirements.

## Baird Insurance and Documentation

Vandalism is a covered peril under most commercial property policies and many homeowner policies, but the claim lives or dies on documentation. Adjusters want photographs taken before cleanup begins, an itemized list of affected materials, and, where applicable, a police report number. Texas law requires reporting vandalism to law enforcement before most carriers will process the claim, so if you have not yet filed a report with the Callahan County Sheriff's Office or Baird Police Department, do that before or alongside your call to us.

We provide written scope-of-work documentation formatted for insurance submission and communicate directly with your adjuster when questions arise about what was cleaned versus what requires replacement.
