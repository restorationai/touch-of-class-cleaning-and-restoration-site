When a storm rolls through Jones County and leaves your Hawley home soaked, smoke-stained, or worse, the belongings inside often take the hardest hit. Furniture, clothing, documents, electronics, and keepsakes absorb water and odor faster than walls do, and in West Texas heat, that damage compounds quickly. Contents restoration is a specialized process, distinct from structural drying, and getting your items into a controlled environment within the first hours can be the difference between salvage and replacement.

## Why Hawley Properties See Contents Damage

Hawley sits in a part of West Texas where the weather swings hard. Hailstorms can punch through roofs and windows in a matter of minutes, letting rain drive into living spaces before a tarp can go up. In summer, temperatures regularly push past 100 degrees, which accelerates mold colonization on wet fabric and wood, sometimes within 24 to 48 hours of initial saturation. The area's older ranch-style homes, many built with wood-framed construction and minimal vapor barriers, tend to hold moisture in wall cavities and beneath flooring, meaning contents sitting on the floor can stay wet long after the visible water is gone. Dust and particulate from the region's frequent wind events also settle into upholstery and soft goods, compounding smoke and soot damage after a fire loss.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Hawley

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code so nothing is lost in transit and your insurance adjuster has a clear record. Items that can be restored are packed out to our climate-controlled facility in Abilene, where they move through a structured cleaning sequence: dry cleaning for loose soot and particulate, wet cleaning for water-soluble residues, ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and non-porous items, and ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor. Soft contents, including clothing, bedding, and drapery, go through an Esporta or equivalent wet-wash system designed for restoration rather than standard laundry. Throughout the process, your inventory stays matched to your job file so redelivery is organized, not chaotic.

Items that cannot be restored immediately, or that need to stay off-site while your home is being dried and repaired, go into secure, climate-controlled storage. That matters in West Texas: a storage unit baking at 110 degrees in July is not a safe place for wood furniture, artwork, or electronics. Our facility maintains stable temperature and humidity so items do not sustain secondary damage while your home is being brought back.

Most homeowner policies in Texas cover contents losses caused by sudden and accidental events, fire, and certain storm perils. ## Local Note

In the Hawley area, many homes have evaporative coolers rather than central refrigerated air, which is common across this part of West Texas. After a water loss, an evaporative cooler running in a wet structure adds humidity to the interior rather than removing it, which works against the drying process and can push moisture deeper into contents left in the home. If you have a swamp cooler, shut it off immediately after a water event and leave windows closed until a technician can assess the environment. It is a small step that makes a measurable difference in how quickly soft goods and furniture can be stabilized.
