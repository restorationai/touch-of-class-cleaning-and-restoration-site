## Why Paint Rock Properties See Mold Issues

The semi-arid climate around Paint Rock creates a deceptive environment. Residents sometimes assume dry West Texas air means mold isn't a serious concern — but the region's periodic flash flooding and the way older construction handles ground moisture tells a different story. Many homes in and around Paint Rock were built with pier-and-beam or shallow slab foundations that sit close to caliche-heavy soil. Caliche doesn't drain freely; after a significant rain, water pools against foundation walls and wicks upward through concrete block or older mortar joints. Combine that with a home that's been closed up through a hot summer running a window unit rather than a whole-house HVAC system, and you get pockets of elevated humidity in closets, under sinks, and behind exterior walls that never fully dry out between seasons.

Older ranch-style homes common to this part of Concho County also tend to have minimal attic ventilation by today's standards. When interior humidity climbs and a poorly sealed attic space traps heat, condensation can form on roof sheathing — a surface mold colonizes quickly and quietly.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Paint Rock

Remediation starts with containment, not cleaning. Before any mold-affected material is disturbed, the work area is isolated with negative air pressure and poly barriers so spores don't migrate to unaffected rooms. Air Care Restoration's IICRC-certified team then conducts a thorough moisture mapping of the space — using thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to locate saturation behind surfaces that look dry to the eye.

Once the moisture source is confirmed and addressed, affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, wood framing where warranted) are removed following containment protocols. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process to capture airborne spores. After physical remediation, the space is dried to target moisture content before any reconstruction begins — skipping that step is the most common reason mold returns after a remediation job.

Because Air Care Restoration is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, homes in Paint Rock built before 1978 — a meaningful portion of the local housing stock — can be handled without the additional risk of disturbing lead-based paint during material removal.

In Concho County's older ranch homes, the combination of wood-framed interior walls and metal roofing — a practical and common choice in this part of West Texas — creates a condensation pattern that surprises homeowners. By the time a ceiling stain appears, the insulation above it has often been wet long enough to support mold growth on the paper facing of the drywall — a detail that a surface inspection alone can miss.
