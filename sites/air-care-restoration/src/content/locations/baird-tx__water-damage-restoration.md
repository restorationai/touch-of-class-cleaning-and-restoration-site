## Why Baird Properties See Water Damage Issues

The soil across Callahan County is a mix of clay and caliche that behaves like a slow drain. During dry stretches it pulls away from foundations, opening gaps that channel water directly beneath slabs and pier-and-beam structures when rain finally arrives. That shrink-swell cycle is hard on older homes in particular, and a meaningful share of Baird's housing stock was built in the mid-twentieth century, when vapor barriers were minimal and subfloor materials were often solid wood or tongue-and-groove pine that swells aggressively when wet.

Water supply lines in homes of that era frequently run through exterior walls with little insulation, making them vulnerable to the hard freezes that push through West Texas every few winters. A pipe that bursts at 2 a.m. in January can saturate wall cavities, insulation, and flooring before the homeowner realizes what has happened. The damage that reads as a damp baseboard in the morning may already extend twelve inches or more into the wall system behind it.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Baird

Every job starts with moisture mapping. Before any equipment is placed, technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled, not just where it is visible. In older Baird homes with plaster-over-lath walls, water can wick laterally through the lath and read as dry at the surface while the structural members behind it remain saturated. Once the scope is mapped, the process moves through extraction, controlled demolition of non-salvageable materials, and structured drying using commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers. Documentation is generated throughout, which matters when an insurance claim is open.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Baird-area homes is the combination of a pier-and-beam foundation and a metal roof. Metal roofs shed water fast, which is an asset in a downpour, but that volume of runoff concentrates at the drip line and can overwhelm older splash blocks or deteriorated gutters.
