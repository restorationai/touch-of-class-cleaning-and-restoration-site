## Why Haskell Properties See Mold Issues

Haskell County's clay-heavy soils are slow to drain. After a significant rain event, water doesn't just run off — it pools against slab edges, seeps under pier-and-beam foundations, and wicks into wood framing that may have been in place for decades. Many homes in Haskell were built in the mid-twentieth century, when vapor barriers under slabs were either thin or absent entirely. That means ground moisture has a relatively easy path into the subfloor cavity, and once relative humidity climbs above 60 percent in an enclosed space, mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours.

The region also sees its share of roof damage from hail — a common West Texas event — that leaves attic sheathing and insulation wet for days before the leak is even discovered. Attic mold is particularly easy to overlook because it's out of sight, and by the time a homeowner smells something in the living area below, the colony is often well established.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Haskell

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We don't rely on surface readings alone — water behind plaster or inside a wall cavity can read dry at the surface while the substrate underneath stays saturated. Once we've identified the extent of contamination, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps disturbed spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal.

Affected materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing — are removed to clean, uncontaminated margins. Structural surfaces that can be saved are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Throughout the process, air samples can be collected by an independent industrial hygienist to establish a clearance baseline. We coordinate that third-party testing when it's requested or required by a carrier, and we don't close out a job until the affected area has returned to normal spore counts.

Because Air Care Restoration holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, homes built before 1978 — a common vintage in Haskell — can be handled without stopping work to bring in a separate contractor. That matters for timelines and for keeping your insurance claim from getting complicated.

## Local Note

One pattern that shows up repeatedly in Haskell's older housing stock: evaporative coolers — swamp coolers — installed on rooftops or in window openings. These units add significant moisture to interior air, which is fine when they're maintained and the home has adequate ventilation. When a cooler's water distribution pad or reservoir starts leaking, or when the unit runs into a poorly ventilated room, relative humidity inside can spike well above the threshold for mold growth within a single season. If your home uses evaporative cooling and you're seeing mold near ceiling registers or in the room directly below the rooftop unit, that appliance is the first place to investigate — not just the wall cavity where the staining is visible.
