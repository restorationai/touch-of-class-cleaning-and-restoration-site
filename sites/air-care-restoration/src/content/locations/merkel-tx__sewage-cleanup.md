## Why Merkel Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Merkel sits in a stretch of West Texas where the soil is predominantly expansive clay — it swells with the rare heavy rain and contracts sharply during the long dry spells that define the region's climate. That constant ground movement puts lateral sewer lines under stress year after year. Older homes in Merkel, many built during the mid-twentieth century when cast-iron and clay-tile pipe was standard, are especially vulnerable: those materials corrode and crack, and root intrusion from drought-stressed trees seeking moisture can collapse a line entirely.

Septic systems are common here too, and a system that hasn't been pumped on schedule can overflow into the yard or back into the structure during a wet spell. When that happens, the contamination isn't just unpleasant — Category 3 "black water" carries pathogens that make a home unsafe to occupy until proper sanitization is complete.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Merkel

Every sewage call starts the same way: contain the source, protect what hasn't been touched, and assess the full spread of contamination before a single piece of flooring comes up. In Merkel homes with slab-on-grade construction — which is common across Taylor County — sewage can wick under vinyl and tile and pool in the voids along the slab edge. Moisture readings at the surface can look acceptable while contamination persists underneath.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves through these stages:

- **Extraction and removal** — standing sewage and saturated porous materials (carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line) are removed and bagged for disposal per Texas Commission on Environmental Quality guidelines for biohazardous waste.
- **Disinfection** — EPA-registered antimicrobials are applied to all affected hard surfaces, framing, and concrete. Our technicians work in full PPE throughout.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in the slab and framing return to dry-standard levels per the IICRC S500 protocol.
- **Post-sanitization verification** — surfaces are documented before reconstruction begins, giving your adjuster a clear record of the remediated scope.
