## Our Water Cleanup Process in Ballinger

When a crew arrives at a Ballinger property, the first task is not extraction — it is mapping. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras identify where water has traveled beyond the visible wet zone. In pier-and-beam homes common to older parts of the city, that often means checking the subfloor from below before touching anything above. Water follows gravity and the path of least resistance; in a house with original hardwood over wood framing, it can pool in the crawl space while the floor above still feels solid underfoot.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction units. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern — not just aimed at wet spots. Drying targets are set to the IICRC S500 standard for the specific material class involved: concrete slab, wood framing, and gypsum board each have different acceptable moisture content thresholds before a structure is considered dry.

Progress readings are logged at every visit so there is a documented drying record — something insurance carriers and adjusters consistently require before approving final repairs.
