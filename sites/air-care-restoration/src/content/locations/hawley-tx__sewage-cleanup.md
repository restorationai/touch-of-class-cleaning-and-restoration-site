## Why Hawley Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Hawley sits in a part of West Texas where the soil composition and seasonal rainfall patterns create a particular set of challenges for residential plumbing. During the drier stretches that define most of the year, clay soils shrink and shift, placing lateral stress on older cast-iron and clay-tile sewer lines. When rains do arrive, those same soils swell and compress, pinching pipes that were already stressed. The result is a higher-than-average rate of root intrusion, joint separation, and line collapse in homes built before the widespread adoption of PVC sewer pipe.

Septic systems are common on the rural edges of Hawley, and a saturated drain field after a heavy rain event can send raw sewage back through floor drains and low-lying fixtures before a homeowner realizes what is happening. That standing water carries Category 3 contamination, meaning bacteria, viruses, and parasites that require more than a shop vacuum and a bucket to address safely.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Hawley

The first priority on any sewage call is stopping the source. If a plumber has not already been engaged, we coordinate that step before extraction begins, because cleaning contaminated water while the source is still active accomplishes nothing.

Once the source is controlled, the process moves through several distinct phases:

- **Extraction and removal**: Standing sewage is removed with truck-mounted extraction units. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, including saturated drywall, insulation, and certain flooring substrates, are documented, removed, and disposed of in compliance with Texas Commission on Environmental Quality guidelines for biohazardous waste.
- **Disinfection**: Hard surfaces, framing, and subfloor assemblies are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 water. We do not move to drying until surface contamination has been addressed.
- **Structural drying**: Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, pull residual moisture from framing and concrete. Drying is monitored with calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging, not estimated by feel.
- **Post-remediation verification**: Before the job is closed, surfaces are tested to confirm that microbial counts have returned to acceptable levels. This documentation matters for insurance claims and for any future buyer disclosure.
