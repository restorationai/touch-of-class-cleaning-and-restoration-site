## Why Albany Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Shackelford County's housing stock skews older. Many homes in and around Albany were built in the mid-20th century, with construction methods that complicate biohazard work in ways a newer build simply doesn't. Subfloor materials from that era — tongue-and-groove pine, particleboard underlayment, and older vinyl tile adhesives — are highly porous and absorb biological fluids deeply before any surface contamination is visible. That means what looks like a contained area at first inspection often requires careful moisture mapping and material testing before a remediation scope can be finalized.

The region's climate adds another layer. West Texas heat accelerates microbial activity significantly. During Albany's long, hot summers, biological material left even briefly in an enclosed space — a spare bedroom, a detached garage, a storage shed — can reach conditions that complicate safe removal far faster than in cooler climates. Early contact and prompt response matter here more than in many parts of the state.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Albany

Every job begins with a thorough assessment before any work starts. Technicians identify the full extent of contamination — including areas that aren't immediately visible — and establish a containment perimeter to protect unaffected parts of the structure. Personal protective equipment, regulated waste containers, and EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants are standard on every call.

Once containment is in place, affected porous materials are carefully removed, bagged, and transported for disposal in compliance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations governing biohazardous waste. Hard surfaces are cleaned, disinfected, and verified. Where structural materials like subfloor or drywall have absorbed contamination, those sections are removed and documented for insurance purposes before the space is treated and cleared.

Air Care Restoration holds EPA Lead-Safe certification — relevant in Albany's older homes where disturbing surfaces during remediation can uncover lead-based paint. The team follows lead-safe work practices as a matter of course, not as an add-on.
