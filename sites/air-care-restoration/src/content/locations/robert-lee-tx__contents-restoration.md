## Why Robert Lee Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Coke County's housing stock leans older, with a significant share of homes built in the mid-twentieth century using materials that react to fire and water in ways modern construction doesn't. Older wood-framed walls and original hardwood floors hold smoke odor deep in the grain. Furniture built before synthetic finishes became standard absorbs soot differently than pressed-board pieces — which actually means it can often be restored rather than replaced, if the cleaning process starts promptly.

The regional climate adds pressure to that timeline. Summer heat accelerates the bonding of smoke residue to porous surfaces, and the low humidity that defines most of the year can cause water-damaged wood and leather to crack or warp within days if items aren't stabilized in a controlled environment. Conversely, the brief periods of higher humidity that follow spring storms create conditions where mold can begin colonizing soft goods — upholstery, books, clothing, rugs — faster than many homeowners expect. Getting contents out of a compromised structure and into climate-controlled storage isn't a luxury; in this part of West Texas, it's often the difference between restoration and replacement.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Cleaning Process in Robert Lee

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Each item is photographed, described, and logged — a record that supports your insurance claim and ensures nothing gets lost in transit. From there, the process follows the damage type:

**After fire or smoke:** Dry soot is removed first using specialized sponges and HEPA-filtered equipment. Residual odor is addressed with thermal fogging or ozone treatment in our controlled storage environment, not in your home where the source contamination is still present. Upholstered furniture, draperies, and clothing go through separate cleaning streams matched to the fabric type.

**After water damage:** Wet items are triaged on-site. What can be dried in place is documented; what must leave is packed carefully to prevent cross-contamination and transported to storage where industrial drying equipment and consistent temperature control do the work that open-air drying in a West Texas summer cannot safely accomplish.

**Electronics and hard goods:** Soot and mineral deposits from water are cleaned using methods appropriate to each surface. Electronics are assessed for corrosion before any attempt at restoration — we document condition and defer to the adjuster and manufacturer guidance on what is salvageable.

Throughout the process, your inventory log is updated so you always know where each item is and what stage of cleaning or drying it has reached.

Contents claims are often the most contested part of a property loss because adjusters and policyholders can disagree on what is restorable versus what needs replacement. ## Local Note

In older Robert Lee homes with original caliche-block or adobe-influenced construction — a building style that appears in parts of Coke County reflecting regional materials available decades ago — walls hold heat and transfer it to adjacent rooms differently than wood-frame construction. After a fire, this means smoke can migrate into rooms that appear untouched, and contents in those rooms may carry odor even if they show no visible soot. During pack-out, we treat the entire structure's contents as potentially affected rather than limiting the inventory to rooms with visible damage — a practice that prevents items from being returned to a restored home only to re-introduce smoke smell months later.
