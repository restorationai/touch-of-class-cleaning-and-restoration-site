## Why San Angelo Properties See Water Cleanup Issues

The same climate that makes San Angelo's summers brutal also sets the stage for water damage in ways that aren't always obvious. The Concho Valley's clay-heavy soils expand when they finally get rain, putting lateral pressure on slab foundations and the plumbing that runs beneath them. Slab-on-grade construction is the norm across most of San Angelo's residential neighborhoods, which means a slab leak or broken supply line under the floor can saturate concrete and the flooring above it before a single drop of visible water appears at the surface.

The city's older housing stock — particularly homes built in the 1950s through 1970s — often features galvanized steel supply lines that have long since approached the end of their service life. When those pipes fail, they fail fast and fully. Meanwhile, San Angelo's position in a flash-flood-prone corridor means that even a brief, intense storm can overwhelm yard grading and push water through garage doors, window wells, and low-lying entry points before homeowners realize what's happening.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in San Angelo

When the call comes in, the first priority is stopping the source — whether that means coordinating with the homeowner to shut off the main or working around a utility that's already been contacted. From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage mitigation, adapted to what San Angelo homes actually look like.

On slab-constructed homes, moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras do the diagnostic work that eyes can't. Water migrates under tile and laminate flooring, pooling in the space between the slab and the finish material — a pocket that reads dry at the surface but stays saturated for days without intervention. Extraction equipment pulls standing water first; then industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers begin the structured drying phase. Readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented, not estimated.

In homes with older construction — particularly those with original plaster walls or wood-framed interior walls with no vapor barrier — drying timelines run longer than in newer builds. We calibrate the equipment count and placement to the actual moisture readings, not a one-size schedule.

## San Angelo Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in Texas cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe or appliance failure — but exclude gradual leaks that went unaddressed. The distinction matters because how the loss is documented from the first hour affects how the claim is evaluated. Air Care Restoration photographs affected materials, logs moisture readings, and produces a scope of work that aligns with the documentation carriers expect. We work with most major insurance carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster so you're not translating between two technical vocabularies.

If your loss involves a slab leak, be aware that Texas policies vary significantly on whether the cost of accessing the pipe (breaking the slab) is covered separately from the resulting water damage. That's a conversation worth having with your adjuster early.
