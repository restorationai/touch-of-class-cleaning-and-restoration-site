## Why Tye Properties See Vandalism Damage Differently

The built environment along the Tye stretch of I-20 is a mix of metal-panel commercial buildings, older masonry structures, and wood-framed residential properties. Each reacts to vandalism in its own way. Spray paint soaks into unpainted concrete block almost immediately, while corrugated metal siding can be etched by aerosol propellants if the coating is thin or weathered. Broken glass from a forced entry or thrown object doesn't just create a safety hazard, in Tye's frequent high-wind conditions, shards migrate across driveways and into landscaping fast, and fine glass dust can be drawn through HVAC intakes before anyone notices.

The region's intense summer heat accelerates paint cure on exterior surfaces, which means graffiti that might wipe off within a few hours in a cooler climate can bond firmly to masonry or painted metal within a single afternoon in July or August. Acting the same day is not just advisable, in West Texas summer conditions, it is often the difference between a surface clean and a full repaint.

## Our Vandalism Cleanup Process in Tye

Every job starts with a documented walkthrough. We photograph and log every affected surface before touching anything, creating a clear before-and-after record that supports insurance claims and any law enforcement documentation already in progress. That step costs nothing extra and protects the property owner.

From there, the process depends on what was hit:

- **Graffiti removal** uses chemical degreasers, pressure washing, and surface-matched solvents selected for the substrate, concrete, brick, metal, or painted wood each require a different approach to avoid secondary damage.
- **Broken glass cleanup** goes beyond sweeping. We use HEPA-filtered vacuums on interior surfaces and methodical hand-clearing on exterior areas to capture fine fragments that brooms miss.
- **Structural damage assessment** follows any forced entry. If a door frame, window casing, or lock set was compromised, we identify what needs temporary securing before permanent repair is scheduled.
- **Odor and residue treatment** addresses spray-paint fumes and any biological material left behind, using equipment and methods consistent with our IICRC fire and smoke training, which translates directly to airborne-particle and odor scenarios.

All work is documented throughout, not just at the start and finish.
