## Why Rotan Properties See Mold Issues

Fisher County's soil is predominantly clay-heavy, which means rainwater doesn't percolate quickly — it pools, it seeps under slab edges, and it finds its way into crawl spaces and utility chases. Rotan's older housing stock, much of it built in the mid-twentieth century, frequently features original pier-and-beam construction with wood subflooring that has had decades to develop small cracks and gaps. Those gaps become entry points for ground moisture every time the water table rises after a significant rainfall event.

The region's temperature swings compound the problem. Hot, dry summers push people to run air conditioning hard, which creates condensation on ductwork and inside wall cavities where insulation has settled or shifted. When that condensation meets a dust-laden surface — common in a county where windblown soil is a seasonal reality — you have the organic material mold needs to take hold. In older Rotan homes, that combination of clay soil, aging wood framing, and HVAC condensation is one of the most consistent patterns we see driving mold calls.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Rotan

Every remediation starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate hidden saturation behind drywall, under flooring, and inside wall cavities — because surface mold is almost always a symptom of a deeper moisture source that has to be addressed before any cleaning or removal begins.

Once we understand the full scope, we establish negative-air containment barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during work. This step matters especially in older Rotan homes where open floor plans or shared ductwork can carry airborne spores from one room to another quickly. Affected materials are removed according to IICRC S520 protocol and the Mold Remediation Protocol written by the independent licensed Mold Assessment Consultant assigned to the project, and structural surfaces are treated and dried until conditions meet the post-remediation verification criteria established in that protocol. One thing that catches Rotan homeowners off guard: the same dry heat that seems to fight mold in summer can actually mask an active colony.
