## Why Baird Properties See Serious Storm Damage

Callahan County sits squarely in Tornado Alley's southern reach. The flat, open terrain between Baird and the surrounding ranch land gives severe weather systems almost nothing to slow them down. Straight-line winds, large hail, and fast-moving supercells are seasonal realities, not rare events. When hail the size of golf balls drives horizontally across exposed rooflines, it doesn't just crack shingles. It forces water under flashing, splits caulk seals around vents and skylights, and compromises the envelope of the structure in ways that aren't visible until moisture has already been working behind the wall for days.

The housing stock in Baird skews older, with a meaningful portion of the residential inventory built before modern energy codes required continuous vapor barriers and sealed sheathing. That construction style is durable in many ways, but it also means storm water has more pathways into a structure and fewer barriers once it gets there. Pier-and-beam foundations, common in older Callahan County homes, create a crawl space environment that can trap moisture and accelerate secondary damage after a storm drives water under the slab perimeter.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Baird

Every storm job starts with a systematic damage assessment, not just a visual walk-around. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find water that has already migrated past the obvious breach points. A roof penetration from a downed branch or hail strike can push water two or three wall cavities away from where it entered, and surface readings alone miss that movement entirely.

Once we've mapped the damage, the work follows a clear sequence: emergency tarping and board-up to stop active intrusion, controlled demolition of compromised materials that cannot be dried in place, structural drying using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the volume and material composition of the affected space, and then reconstruction. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which matters when you're working through an insurance claim.

For tree impact damage, which is common after high-wind events in Baird, we coordinate debris removal and structural assessment together rather than treating them as separate phases. A tree on a roof is also a roof that may have shifted at the ridge or rafter connections, and that structural question has to be answered before any interior drying work begins.
