## Why Potosi Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Much of the housing stock in and around Potosi consists of single-family homes and ranch properties built across several decades, many with pier-and-beam or slab foundations, crawl spaces, and older HVAC systems that circulate air throughout the structure. In the intense West Texas heat, biological material degrades rapidly, and odors and pathogens can migrate into wall cavities, subfloor spaces, and ductwork far faster than in cooler, more temperate climates. That accelerated timeline is not a detail to overlook. Waiting even a few hours longer than necessary in summer conditions can mean the difference between a contained remediation and one that requires structural material removal.

The rural character of the area also means properties are often more spread out, with outbuildings, detached garages, or storage structures that may be part of the affected area. We assess the full footprint of the property, not just the primary living space.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Potosi

Every remediation begins with a thorough, private assessment. A crew member speaks with whoever is coordinating on-site, whether that is a family member, property manager, or legal representative, and walks the space before any work begins. We identify all affected surfaces and materials, contain the area to prevent cross-contamination, and document everything needed for insurance purposes.

Remediation follows OSHA bloodborne pathogen protocols and EPA guidelines for infectious material handling. Personal protective equipment, hospital-grade disinfectants, and ATP testing verify that surfaces are biologically clean after treatment, not just visually clean. Sharps, infectious materials, and contaminated porous materials are packaged and disposed of through licensed medical waste channels. Once the space is cleared, we treat for odor at the source rather than masking it, which matters especially in homes where HVAC systems could otherwise redistribute odor-carrying particles.
