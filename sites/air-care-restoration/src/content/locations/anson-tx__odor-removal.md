## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Anson

Professional odor elimination is not a single step, it is a sequence of methods matched to the type of odor, the materials affected, and the severity of penetration. The process typically moves through these stages:

**Source removal and cleaning:** No deodorization technology works well on top of contaminated material. Charred debris, saturated insulation, or soiled carpet must be removed before treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging:** A petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed throughout the space, following the same pathways smoke or odor traveled into materials. This is particularly effective in older Anson homes where odor has migrated into wall voids and ceiling cavities.

**Hydroxyl generation:** Hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. This method works continuously and is safe for use around most materials and finishes.

**Ozone treatment:** In unoccupied spaces, ozone treatment can address deeply embedded odors in flooring, framing, and HVAC systems. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer and requires proper protocols, the space must remain unoccupied during treatment and ventilated thoroughly afterward.

**HVAC deodorization:** In West Texas homes where central air runs nearly year-round, the duct system is often a primary odor distribution channel. Treating the source without addressing the ductwork leaves a pathway for odors to return.
