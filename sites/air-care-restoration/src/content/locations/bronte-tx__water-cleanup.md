## Why Bronte Properties See Water Damage Issues

Coke County's caliche-heavy soil absorbs moisture slowly and unpredictably. During a heavy rain event, surface water can pond against foundation perimeters before it has any chance to percolate, and that hydrostatic pressure finds the path of least resistance — a hairline crack in a slab, a gap around a pipe penetration, a low threshold at a back door. The result is water inside the structure that looks minor at the surface but has already traveled laterally under flooring.

Bronte's housing stock skews toward older construction — pier-and-beam homes and ranch-style slabs built in decades when vapor barriers were thinner and subfloor materials were solid wood rather than engineered panels. Solid wood subfloor holds moisture longer than OSB and can cup or split before surface readings show elevated moisture. That's a detail that matters when a technician is deciding whether to pull flooring or attempt in-place drying.

The region also runs on well and rural water systems in some areas, which means pressure regulators and supply lines don't always get the same maintenance cycle as municipal connections. An aging supply line that fails behind a wall can run undetected for days in a home that isn't occupied full-time — a pattern common in rural Coke County properties used seasonally.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Bronte

When a call comes in, the first priority is stopping the source — shutting the supply valve, identifying the breach, or sandbagging an entry point if the water is storm-related. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** Technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace how far water has traveled, including into wall cavities and under flooring where it isn't visible. In older Bronte homes with plaster or paneled walls, readings are taken at multiple depths because surface dryness doesn't reflect what's happening at the substrate.

**Standing water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull bulk water from hard surfaces and carpet. The faster this step happens, the narrower the drying window and the lower the likelihood of microbial growth taking hold.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned based on the moisture map — not placed generically. Drying logs are maintained throughout so there's a documented record for your insurance carrier.

**Monitoring and clearance.** Technicians return on a scheduled basis to take readings and adjust equipment placement. The job isn't closed until affected materials reach dry standard per the IICRC S500 — not when the equipment looks like it's done its job.
