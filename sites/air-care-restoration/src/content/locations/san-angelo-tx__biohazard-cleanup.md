## Why San Angelo Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Tom Green County's climate is the first factor any remediation crew has to account for. Summer temperatures in San Angelo regularly exceed 100°F, and that heat dramatically speeds the rate at which biological material degrades and odors penetrate porous surfaces — carpet padding, subflooring, drywall, and the caliche-heavy soil beneath slab foundations. Unlike humid Gulf Coast markets where moisture is the primary complication, San Angelo's dry heat causes rapid desiccation of affected materials, which can make surface areas look contained while contamination has already wicked deeper into structural layers.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in San Angelo

Every job begins with a discreet site assessment. We arrive with privacy, compassion and professional discretion.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected spaces.
- **Removal of affected materials** — biohazardous waste, including sharps, infectious material, and contaminated soft goods, is collected and packaged according to OSHA and EPA handling standards.
- **Surface treatment** — hospital-grade disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces; porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated are removed rather than treated in place.
- **Odor neutralization** — because San Angelo's heat accelerates odor penetration into structural materials, we use thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment as needed, not just surface sprays.
- **Job closeout documentation** — the space is thoroughly documented before we close out, giving you and your insurance carrier a clear record of what was done.

Our team is IICRC certified, and we handle regulated biohazardous waste disposal in compliance with Texas Department of State Health Services requirements.
