## Why Eden Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Concho County's housing stock skews older. Many of the homes and commercial properties in and around Eden were constructed during the 1950s through 1970s, a period when asbestos was routinely used in floor tiles, pipe insulation, roofing felt, duct wrap, and the textured popcorn ceilings that are still common in the region. The dry, high-desert climate here is hard on building materials: thermal cycling between cold winter nights and hot summer days causes expansion and contraction that can fracture older materials over time, releasing fibers that would otherwise stay locked in place. A ceiling that has been stable for forty years can become friable after a single hard freeze followed by a stretch of 100-degree afternoons, which is a pattern Eden sees regularly.

Agricultural and ranch properties around Eden add another layer of complexity. Older outbuildings often used corrugated asbestos-cement roofing panels, and equipment sheds from the mid-twentieth century may have pipe lagging or boiler insulation that has never been assessed. These materials are not always on a homeowner's radar until a renovation or demolition brings them into view.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Eden

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis before any abatement work begins. This step is not optional, and it is not something to shortcut: Texas Department of State Health Services rules govern asbestos abatement, and work on regulated quantities of asbestos-containing material requires proper notification and handling procedures.

Once results confirm the presence of asbestos, the work area is isolated with negative air pressure containment to prevent fiber migration into adjacent spaces. Our OSHA-trained crew removes the material using wet methods that suppress airborne fibers, double-bags all waste in labeled, sealed containers, and transports it to a licensed disposal facility. Air clearance testing after abatement confirms that fiber counts have returned to acceptable levels before containment is removed and the space is released for reconstruction. We carry EPA Lead-Safe Certification and operate under Texas Mold Remediation Contractor License MRC2262, which reflects the regulatory framework we work within on every job.

## Eden Insurance Coordination

Asbestos abatement is sometimes a covered line item under homeowner's or commercial property policies, particularly when the discovery is tied to a covered loss such as storm damage or a sudden pipe failure that exposes insulated materials. Coverage varies significantly by policy, and insurers typically want detailed documentation: laboratory reports, scope of work, photographs of affected materials before and after removal, and air clearance results. We provide that documentation as a standard part of every project, which gives your adjuster what they need to process the claim without going back and forth for additional information.
