Coleman sits in the heart of West Texas ranch country, where the same dry heat that bakes the soil in summer gives way to sudden, violent thunderstorms that can push water through a roof or wall in minutes. When a storm, fire, or burst pipe damages your home, the contents inside, furniture, clothing, documents, family heirlooms, take the hit right alongside the structure. Air Care Restoration handles contents restoration and storage for Coleman-area properties, treating your belongings with the same care you would, from the moment they leave your home to the day they come back.

## Why Coleman Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Coleman County's climate is not gentle on buildings or what's inside them. Summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F, and the region's low humidity accelerates the warping and cracking of wood furniture, photo albums, and documents left in a damaged space even for a short time. Then there's the opposite problem: the intense, fast-moving thunderstorms that roll through Coleman each spring and fall can dump inches of rain in under an hour, overwhelming older drainage systems and sending water into living areas before a homeowner can react.

Many homes in Coleman were built in the mid-twentieth century with construction methods that are durable but porous, pier-and-beam foundations, hardwood floors, plaster walls, and wood-framed cabinetry that absorbs moisture quickly and holds it. A contents crew working in this kind of home has to move fast, because smoke odor and water both migrate into older materials more deeply than they do into modern composites. Leaving a smoke-saturated upholstered chair in a warm, dry West Texas room for even a day longer than necessary allows the odor compounds to bond more tightly to the fibers.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Coleman

When we arrive at a Coleman property, the first step is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what condition.

Items that can be restored are packed and transported to our climate-controlled facility in Abilene, roughly an hour northwest of Coleman on US-84. There, each piece goes through the appropriate process for its damage type:

- **Smoke and soot:** Ultrasonic cleaning tanks remove carbon particles from hard surfaces and decorative items without abrasion. Soft goods go through ozone and hydroxyl treatment to neutralize odor at the molecular level, not just mask it.
- **Water damage:** Wet textiles are dried under controlled conditions to prevent mildew. Wood furniture is stabilized and refinished where needed. Electronics are assessed by a technician before any power is applied.
- **Document and photo recovery:** Paper items are freeze-dried or air-dried depending on the extent of saturation, then stored flat in archival conditions.

Items that cannot be restored are documented with photographs and a written explanation before any disposal decision is made, giving you and your adjuster a clear record.

## Coleman Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners policies in Texas cover contents losses caused by fire, smoke, and sudden water events. Air Care Restoration works directly with your carrier's adjuster, providing the itemized inventory, condition reports, and before-and-after photographs that adjusters need to process a contents claim efficiently. We bill carriers directly for covered work, which means you are not writing a large check and waiting for reimbursement.

If your policy includes additional living expense (ALE) coverage, the cost of temporary storage during restoration is often a covered line item. We can document storage fees in a format that most Texas carriers accept without a supplemental request.

## Local Note

Coleman's agricultural heritage means many homes hold items that urban contents crews rarely encounter: saddles, hand-tooled leather tack, cast iron cookware seasoned over decades, and antique farm implements that have been repurposed as decor. Leather is especially vulnerable after a fire or flood in West Texas conditions, because the dry ambient air pulls moisture out of already-stressed hides faster than it would in a more humid climate. When we pack leather goods from a Coleman property, we condition them before transport and store them away from any dehumidification equipment to prevent additional drying damage during the restoration period.
