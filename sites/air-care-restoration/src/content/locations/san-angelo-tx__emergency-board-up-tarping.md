## Why San Angelo Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

The climate here is not gentle. San Angelo averages more than 200 sunny days a year, but those clear skies can turn into violent thunderstorms with large hail and straight-line winds with very little warning. A roof opening left exposed even overnight can admit enough blowing dust and rain to ruin insulation, saturate ceiling joists, and soak interior walls down to the slab. In the summer, interior temperatures in an unprotected structure can spike well above outdoor air temperature within hours, accelerating mold colonization in any moisture that got in during the storm.

The city's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Older neighborhoods closer to the historic downtown core include homes built in the mid-twentieth century with wood-framed windows and original single-pane glass — openings that are not standard sizes and require custom-cut plywood rather than off-the-shelf panels. Masonry veneer construction, common throughout San Angelo, means fastening board-up panels requires different anchoring techniques than wood-frame homes; drilling into brick mortar incorrectly can cause spalling that creates a second repair problem.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in San Angelo

When you call, the first question is always the same: what is the opening, and what is the weather doing right now. That determines whether tarping or boarding takes priority. A roof breach in advance of incoming rain gets a heavy-duty polyethylene tarp secured with batten strips and screwed-down perimeter boards first — the goal is waterproofing the structure before anything else. Broken windows and door openings get measured, cut, and secured with exterior-grade plywood, fastened in a way that does not damage the surrounding frame any more than necessary.

Every opening is photographed before and after work begins. That documentation matters for your insurance claim — adjusters want to see the condition of the structure at the time of loss, not after a crew has already been on site. Our IICRC-certified team is trained in fire and smoke damage response as well, so when a board-up follows a fire, we understand what the structure has been through and handle materials accordingly, including lead-safe work practices on older homes where disturbing painted surfaces is unavoidable.

## San Angelo Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in Texas cover emergency protective measures — board-up and tarping are typically classified as "reasonable steps to prevent further damage," which is language that appears in standard HO-3 policy forms. Documenting those steps properly is what gets them reimbursed. We photograph every opening before work begins, note the materials used and the method of attachment, and provide an itemized scope of work you can hand directly to your adjuster or upload to your carrier's portal. ## Local Note

San Angelo's semi-arid climate means that after a storm opens a roof or breaks windows, the structure dries out on the surface quickly — sometimes deceptively quickly. Homeowners occasionally assume that because the interior feels dry a day or two later, no moisture damage occurred. In reality, water that entered through a roof breach can pool in wall cavities and above drop ceilings where it is not visible and where the dry West Texas air cannot reach it. A surface-dry appearance after an unprotected opening is not a reliable indicator of what is happening inside the assembly. Getting the structure sealed promptly is the first step; moisture mapping after the fact tells the rest of the story.
