West Texas storms don't announce themselves politely. Out on the Rolling Plains around Rotan, a line of thunderstorms can drop baseball-sized hail, spin up a brief tornado, and push 70-mph straight-line winds across Fisher County in under an hour — leaving roofs stripped, windows blown in, and water pouring into homes before the radar app has time to refresh. ## Why Rotan Properties See Severe Storm Damage

Fisher County sits squarely in a corridor where cold Canadian air and warm Gulf moisture collide with little topographic resistance. There are no hills to break the wind, no tree canopy dense enough to absorb hail energy, and the flat terrain means water sheets across the ground fast rather than draining gradually. Rotan's housing stock reflects decades of construction that predates modern impact-rated roofing standards — many homes carry three-tab shingles, wood fascia, and older aluminum gutters that simply weren't designed to survive a direct hail strike above 1.5 inches in diameter.

The soil profile here also matters after a storm. The clay-heavy soils common across this part of the Rolling Plains expand when saturated and drain slowly, which means water that enters a crawl space or foundation perimeter during a heavy rain event can stay there for days. That standing moisture creates the conditions for secondary damage — mold colonization can begin in as little as 24 to 48 hours in a warm, humid enclosed space — well before a homeowner realizes the problem extends beyond what's visible on the surface.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Rotan

When we arrive, the first priority is stabilization: tarping compromised roof sections, boarding broken windows, and stopping active water intrusion before it migrates further into wall cavities or subfloor assemblies. We document everything photographically as we go — not just for our records, but because thorough documentation is what makes an insurance claim defensible.

Once the structure is secured, we move into assessment. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras reveal what the eye can't — water tracking behind drywall, saturated insulation in attic bays, or wet OSB decking under intact-looking shingles. Drying equipment is deployed based on what the readings show, not on a standard formula. In a Fisher County summer, ambient heat and low humidity can actually accelerate drying, but it also means any trapped moisture becomes a faster mold risk if air circulation doesn't reach it.

Tree damage cleanup is part of almost every major storm call in this area. We coordinate debris removal alongside structural assessment so that fallen limbs aren't sitting on a compromised roof section any longer than necessary.

## Local Note

One thing that catches Rotan homeowners off guard: after a hail event, the damage to a roof can look cosmetically minor from the ground — a few dings in the gutters, some bruising on the shingles — while the actual impact points have fractured the shingle mat enough to allow water infiltration the next time it rains. In the dry stretches between West Texas storms, that hidden damage can go undetected for weeks. By the time water stains appear on a ceiling, the decking underneath may already be compromised. If your property took hail during a storm, a close-up inspection of the shingles — not just a ground-level look — is worth doing before the next rain rolls through.
