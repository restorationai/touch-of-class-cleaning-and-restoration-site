## Why Colorado City Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Mitchell County's building boom tracked closely with the broader West Texas oil economy. Homes and commercial buildings constructed between roughly 1940 and 1980 frequently incorporated asbestos-containing materials because they were cheap, fire-resistant, and widely available. That era of construction is well-represented across Colorado City's residential neighborhoods and its older downtown commercial blocks.

Common materials that may still contain asbestos in local properties include:

- **Popcorn (acoustic) ceiling texture** applied before the mid-1980s
- **9-inch and 12-inch vinyl floor tiles** and the black mastic adhesive beneath them
- **Pipe insulation and duct wrap** in older HVAC and plumbing systems
- **Roof shingles and siding panels** on homes built before modern alternatives were standard
- **Joint compound and drywall tape** from pre-1980 construction

The dry, high-wind climate of West Texas adds a layer of urgency. Asbestos fibers that become airborne during a renovation or after storm damage to an older roof can travel quickly in open, arid conditions. Disturbing suspected material without testing first is a risk that no renovation timeline justifies.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Colorado City

Every project starts with a proper asbestos inspection before any demolition or removal work begins. Samples of suspected materials are collected by trained technicians and sent to an accredited laboratory. Results drive every decision that follows.

Once a material tests positive, the abatement scope is defined clearly in writing. Our process follows Texas Department of State Health Services regulations for asbestos removal, which require licensed contractors, specific containment protocols, and proper disposal at approved facilities. As a Texas Mold Remediation Contractor (License #MRC2262) operating under the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, Air Care Restoration carries the documentation that protects you legally and practically when permits or inspections are involved.

Containment is built before any material is touched. Negative air pressure units with HEPA filtration keep fibers from migrating into living spaces. Workers in full PPE remove materials wet to suppress fiber release, double-bag waste in labeled containers, and transport it to a licensed disposal site. A final air clearance test confirms the space is safe before containment is removed.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, the process is methodical: ceiling area is sealed off, the texture is carefully wetted and scraped, and the substrate is inspected before the space is cleared. Rushing any step defeats the purpose.

## Colorado City Insurance Coordination

Asbestos abatement is sometimes a covered loss when the material has been disturbed by a covered peril, such as storm damage to a roof containing asbestos shingles or a plumbing failure that requires opening walls with asbestos-containing joint compound. Coverage depends entirely on your specific policy language, and outcomes vary.

What we can do is document the loss thoroughly: photographs, laboratory reports, scope of work, and disposal manifests. That documentation package is what adjusters need to evaluate a claim. We work with homeowners and property managers to assemble it accurately, and we communicate directly with carriers when that helps move the process forward.
