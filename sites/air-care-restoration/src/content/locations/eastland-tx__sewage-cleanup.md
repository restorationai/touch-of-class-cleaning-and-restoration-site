## Why Eastland Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Eastland's housing stock leans heavily toward mid-century and earlier construction — homes built before modern PVC plumbing became standard often have cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals that corrode, collapse, or root-intrude over decades. Once a lateral fails, raw sewage has nowhere to go but back up through the lowest fixture in the house, typically a floor drain, toilet, or utility sink in the lowest level.

The region's clay-heavy soils compound the problem. When rainfall does come — even in Eastland County's characteristically dry climate — water-saturated clay can shift and apply lateral pressure on aging sewer lines, accelerating cracks and joint separations. Septic systems in the rural stretches of the county face a different challenge: drain fields in tight clay soil drain slowly, and a system that worked fine for decades can back up into the home during a wet stretch or after years of gradual clogging.

Municipal sewer connections in older parts of town may also share combined sewer segments that can surcharge during heavy rain events, pushing wastewater back toward connected properties. If you notice gurgling drains or slow toilets across multiple fixtures at once, that's often the first sign of a mainline issue rather than a single clog.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Eastland

Sewage cleanup is not a job for a shop vac and a bottle of bleach. Category 3 contaminated water contains bacteria, viruses, and parasites that survive on surfaces long after the visible mess is gone. Our IICRC-certified team follows a structured protocol calibrated to the specific conditions in Eastland-area properties:

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. In older Eastland homes with open floor plans or pier-and-beam construction, this step matters more than it might in a newer slab-foundation build, because contaminated water can migrate under flooring into areas that look clean.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment. Saturated materials — flooring, baseboards, lower wall sections — that cannot be effectively sanitized are removed and bagged for disposal per applicable Texas Commission on Environmental Quality guidelines.

**Sanitization and antimicrobial treatment.** All affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We pay particular attention to subfloor assemblies and wall cavities in older construction, where sewage can wick upward through framing before anyone notices.

**Drying and documentation.** Commercial drying equipment brings structural materials to target moisture levels. We document moisture readings throughout the process — a record that matters when you're working with your insurance carrier.
