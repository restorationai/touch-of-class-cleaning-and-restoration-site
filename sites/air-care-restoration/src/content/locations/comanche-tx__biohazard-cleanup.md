## Why Comanche Properties Present Specific Biohazard Challenges

Comanche County sits in the Texas Rolling Plains, where summer heat regularly pushes past 100°F and humidity swings between dry spells and sudden Gulf moisture. That temperature range matters for biohazard situations: biological material degrades rapidly in heat, and porous surfaces — wood subfloors, older plaster walls, unfinished concrete in ranch-style homes — can absorb contaminants faster than you'd expect. Many homes in and around Comanche were built in the mid-20th century, when crawl spaces and pier-and-beam foundations were standard. Those foundation types allow air — and odor — to migrate underneath the structure, which means a surface cleanup is rarely the whole job.

Rural properties on the outskirts of the city add another layer: septic systems, well water, and outbuildings that aren't on municipal utilities require different containment protocols than a standard in-town residence. When infectious material or sharps are involved, protecting the surrounding soil and water supply is part of responsible remediation, not an afterthought. We arrive with privacy, compassion and professional discretion. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Assessment and containment.** The affected area is isolated before any cleaning begins. Air Care Restoration's OSHA-trained technicians identify the full scope of contamination, including areas that aren't immediately visible, such as material that has migrated beneath flooring or into wall cavities common in older Comanche homes.

**Removal and packaging.** All biohazardous material — including sharps, infectious waste, and saturated porous materials — is collected and packaged in accordance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations for biohazardous waste transport and disposal. Nothing leaves the property improperly labeled or contained.

**Cleaning and disinfection.** Hospital-grade disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces. As an IICRC Certified Firm, our protocols follow established industry standards for pathogen reduction, not a simplified checklist.

**Deodorization and clearance.** In the Texas heat, odor penetration into structural materials can be significant. We address subfloor and wall cavity odor as part of the standard scope, not as an add-on.
