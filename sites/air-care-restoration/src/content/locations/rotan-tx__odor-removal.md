## Why Rotan Properties Hold Odors Longer

Fisher County sits in a semi-arid zone where summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F and humidity swings sharply between seasons. That thermal cycling causes wood framing, plaster, and masonry to expand and contract repeatedly, opening micro-gaps that trap odor molecules. When a fire, flood, or prolonged animal intrusion deposits contaminants, those gaps act like sponges, absorbing odors that later off-gas when temperatures rise again.

Older homes in Rotan, many built during the mid-twentieth century oil and agricultural boom, frequently feature original plaster walls and pier-and-beam foundations. Both materials are far more porous than modern drywall over a concrete slab. Smoke residue and pet or livestock odors can penetrate plaster lath to a depth that standard cleaning cannot reach, and the crawl space beneath a pier-and-beam home becomes a reservoir for mold-adjacent musty smells after even a brief plumbing leak.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Rotan

Effective odor elimination is not a single step. It is a sequence, and skipping any stage means the smell returns.

**Assessment and source removal.** Every job starts with identifying what produced the odor and whether the source material is still present. Charred wood, saturated insulation, or contaminated subfloor must come out before any deodorization equipment is deployed. Treating odor over an active source is money spent twice.

**Thermal fogging.** A petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed so it penetrates the same pathways smoke or moisture used to enter the structure. This is particularly effective in Rotan's older plaster-wall homes, where odor has migrated behind finish surfaces.

**Hydroxyl generation.** Hydroxyl generators produce UV-activated radicals that break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. This method is well suited to homes with pets or sensitive materials that cannot tolerate ozone concentrations.

**Ozone treatment.** For severe smoke odor or long-term animal contamination in unoccupied spaces, high-concentration ozone is the most aggressive tool available. Ozone cannot be used while people, pets, or plants are present, and the space must be thoroughly ventilated before re-entry. Our IICRC FSRT-certified technicians follow established safety protocols on every ozone application.

**Post-treatment verification.** We do not consider a job complete because the equipment has run its cycle. We verify odor levels before leaving the property.
