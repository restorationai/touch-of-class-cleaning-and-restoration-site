That grit doesn't stay in the ducts; it recirculates every time your system kicks on, coating registers, filters, and the air your family breathes. ## Why Merkel Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Expect

Merkel sits in a stretch of West Texas where the soil is loose, the wind is persistent, and agricultural activity keeps the ambient dust load high well beyond what urban HVAC systems typically handle. Homes here run their heating systems hard from late fall through early spring, and then flip to aggressive cooling through a long, dry summer — that constant cycling pulls outdoor air through return vents repeatedly, and whatever is floating outside eventually ends up inside the ductwork.

Older ranch-style homes common to this part of Taylor County often have wider duct runs with fewer bends, which sounds like an advantage but actually allows particulate to settle and accumulate in long horizontal sections rather than being carried through to the filter. Insulated flex duct, common in additions and outbuildings, develops interior surface texture over time that grabs dust and holds it. Neither of these problems is visible from a register — they require camera inspection to diagnose properly.

Dryer vents are a separate but related concern. In single-story homes with longer vent runs to an exterior wall, lint accumulates in ways that create a genuine fire hazard. Dryer vent cleaning is often scheduled alongside duct cleaning in Merkel because the access points and equipment overlap, and because the risk in this climate — where homes run dryers year-round — is real.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Merkel

Every job starts with a camera inspection of the main trunk lines and accessible branch runs. This isn't a sales tool — it's the only honest way to know what's actually in the system before any equipment is turned on. We document what we find, including any visible mold growth, pest intrusion, or damaged insulation lining, before we quote scope.

Cleaning uses negative air pressure: a high-powered vacuum unit seals to the main trunk while agitation tools — rotary brushes and compressed air whips — break debris loose from duct walls and drive it toward the collection point rather than into the living space. Supply and return sides are addressed separately. Registers and grilles are removed, cleaned, and reinstalled. If sanitization is warranted — after confirmed mold presence or following a rodent intrusion — we apply an EPA-registered antimicrobial to interior duct surfaces after mechanical cleaning is complete.

For homes with evaporative coolers or dual systems, which appear in older Merkel properties that were retrofitted rather than built with central air, we assess each system independently. Evaporative cooler ducts carry moisture in ways that refrigerated air systems don't, and that moisture history changes what we're likely to find.
