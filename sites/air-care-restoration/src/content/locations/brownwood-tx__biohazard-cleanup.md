## Why Brownwood Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Brown County's housing stock tells a story in wood and plaster. A significant portion of Brownwood's residential properties date to the mid-twentieth century or earlier, built with construction methods that predate modern moisture barriers and subfloor membranes. When biological material contacts these older surfaces — hardwood floors laid over concrete slabs, plaster-and-lath interior walls, or porous grout in vintage tile — it penetrates quickly and does not respond to surface wiping. Pathogens and odor-causing compounds wick into substrate layers that look clean to the naked eye but remain contaminated.

Central Texas heat compounds this. Brownwood summers routinely push temperatures well above 90°F, which accelerates bacterial growth and odor development in ways that give cleanup crews very little margin. A situation that might hold for 48 hours in a cooler climate can become significantly more complex within 24 hours here. That urgency is one reason prompt, professional response matters more in this region than in many others.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Brownwood

Every job begins with a confidential assessment — no visible signage, no unnecessary personnel on-site. Our OSHA-trained technicians establish a contained work zone, identify all affected surfaces and materials, and document the scope for insurance purposes before any physical work begins.

Contaminated porous materials — flooring, drywall, insulation, upholstery — are removed, bagged, and transported in compliance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations governing biohazardous waste. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the process to capture aerosolized particles.

Once remediation is complete, the space is tested and cleared before we demobilize. In older Brownwood homes where subflooring or wall cavities may have absorbed material, we use moisture and ATP surface testing to confirm that contamination has not migrated beyond the visible affected area. The goal is a space that is genuinely clean — not just visually presentable.
