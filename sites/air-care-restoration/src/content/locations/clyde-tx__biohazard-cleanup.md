## Why Clyde Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Callahan County's housing stock leans toward older single-family homes and working ranch properties — many built with pier-and-beam foundations, hardwood subfloors, and materials that absorb biological contamination differently than modern slab construction. Porous wood, older grout lines, and unfinished crawl spaces can hold infectious material in ways that aren't visible at the surface. A cleanup that looks complete to the eye may leave contamination in the subfloor cavity or between wall cavities if the crew isn't equipped to test and treat beneath the surface.

Clyde's dry West Texas climate also plays a role. Low humidity accelerates the drying of surfaces, which can mask the presence of biological material that has wicked into porous substrates. That same dry air doesn't eliminate pathogens — it simply makes contamination harder to detect without proper testing equipment. Our technicians use ATP testing and other detection methods to confirm that a space is genuinely clean, not just visually clear.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Clyde

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. We arrive with privacy, compassion and professional discretion. From there, the process follows a structured protocol:

**Assessment and containment** — We identify the full extent of affected areas, including surfaces that may not be immediately visible, and establish containment to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the property.

**Removal and packaging** — All biohazardous materials — including sharps, needles, bodily fluids, and infectious material — are collected, packaged, and transported in compliance with Texas Department of State Health Services regulations governing medical waste disposal. Nothing is left for the property owner to handle.

**Treatment and disinfection** — Hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces. For porous materials that cannot be fully decontaminated, we remove and replace rather than treat in place.

**Verification** — Before we leave, we test to confirm the space meets safe re-occupancy standards. Documentation is provided for insurance, legal, or property management purposes.
