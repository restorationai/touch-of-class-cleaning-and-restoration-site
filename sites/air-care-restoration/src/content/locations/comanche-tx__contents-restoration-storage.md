## Why Comanche Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Comanche County's housing stock leans older. Many homes in and around the city were built in the mid-20th century, with wood-framed construction, plaster-and-lath interior walls, and original hardwood floors. Those materials absorb and release moisture slowly, which means a water loss in one room can affect furniture and personal property in adjacent rooms before anyone realizes the spread has happened. Smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire behaves similarly, in older homes with minimal vapor barriers, smoke particles travel through wall cavities and settle on contents in rooms that never saw a flame.

Comanche's position on the Lampasas Cut Plain also means the soil drains unevenly after heavy rain. Homes on low-lying lots can see groundwater intrusion or crawl-space flooding that wicks upward into stored items, seasonal clothing, and keepsakes kept in lower-level areas. That kind of slow, hidden moisture exposure is exactly what makes early pack-out and professional drying so important.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Comanche

The first step after a loss is separating what can be saved from what cannot, and doing it quickly. Our crew performs a room-by-room inventory before anything leaves the property, photographing and cataloging each item so nothing gets lost in the process. Salvageable contents are packed using appropriate materials for the item type, then transported to a climate-controlled storage facility where temperature and humidity are actively managed.

From there, the restoration work begins based on what each item needs:

- **Textile and upholstery cleaning** uses ozone treatment and thermal fogging to neutralize smoke odor embedded in fabric rather than just masking it.
- **Hard-surface cleaning** on furniture, appliances, and décor removes soot, char residue, and water staining using pH-appropriate cleaning agents calibrated to the material.
- **Document and photo drying** uses controlled drying chambers for paper goods that are wet but not destroyed.
- **Electronics assessment** is flagged for specialist evaluation before any power-on attempt, since water and smoke residue inside components can cause failure or fire risk.

Once your home is dried, repaired, and cleared, contents are returned and placed according to your original room layout documentation.
