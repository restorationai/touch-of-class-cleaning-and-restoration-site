## Restoration emergencies common in Robert Lee

Robert Lee sits in a part of West Texas where the weather does not give much warning. The region sees severe thunderstorms capable of producing large hail and high straight-line winds, both of which drive water into rooflines and shatter windows in minutes. In winter, hard freezes arrive fast — temperatures can drop below 20°F overnight — and older supply lines in uninsulated wall cavities or under pier-and-beam floors burst before a homeowner realizes the heat has gone out. The Colorado River drainage basin that runs through Coke County means that heavy rainfall events can push water toward low-lying properties faster than municipal storm infrastructure can handle it. Drought cycles followed by sudden downpours also cause the expansive clay soils common in this part of Texas to shift, opening foundation cracks that become direct pathways for water intrusion during the next rain.

## Services we provide in Robert Lee

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Robert Lee frequently starts hidden — under vinyl flooring, inside wall cavities, or beneath a slab where a supply line has been weeping for days before the surface shows any sign. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation, then dry the structure to IICRC S500 standards before any reconstruction begins. Standing water that sits more than 24 to 48 hours creates conditions for mold colonization, so speed matters from the first call.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Grass and brush fires are a real seasonal threat in Coke County, and interior kitchen or electrical fires are common in any older housing stock. Smoke residue is not just a smell — it is an acidic film that etches metal fixtures, discolors drywall, and works deeper into porous materials the longer it sits. Our fire residue and odor-control expertise is grounded in IICRC FSRT and OCT certifications. We clean and deodorize affected surfaces, assess structural integrity, and communicate technical scope information with your adjuster on the full loss.

### Mold remediation

Mold does not need a flood to take hold. A slow roof leak after a hailstorm, a condensation problem in an under-ventilated bathroom, or a failed supply line inside a wall can create enough sustained moisture for mold to establish within 48 to 72 hours. We contain affected areas, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces in accordance with the Mold Remediation Protocol written by your independent licensed Mold Assessment Consultant. Post-remediation verification — confirming the work meets the criteria established in that protocol — is completed by the independent consultant before reconstruction closes the wall back up.

### Reconstruction

Once mitigation is complete, the structure still needs to be put back together. We handle drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and finish work so you are not coordinating a separate contractor after the remediation crew leaves.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Robert Lee

Most of Robert Lee's residential housing was built between the 1940s and 1980s, which means wall assemblies are commonly wood-frame with plaster or early drywall, and foundation types split between slab-on-grade and pier-and-beam depending on the block and decade. Pier-and-beam homes are particularly vulnerable to water intrusion because the crawlspace creates a hidden environment where moisture accumulates and framing can stay wet long after the surface appears dry. Plumbing in homes of this era is often galvanized steel supply pipe, which corrodes from the inside out and can fail suddenly. Pre-1980 construction in Coke County also warrants evaluation for asbestos-containing materials in floor tile, pipe insulation, and joint compound before any demolition scope begins — work involving such materials requires a licensed abatement contractor. The clay-heavy soils underlying much of this area expand when wet and contract during drought, stressing slab edges and pier footings over time. Structural repair and significant reconstruction in Robert Lee falls under the jurisdiction of Coke County, and permits for work that affects structural elements or mechanical systems should be confirmed with the county before work begins.
