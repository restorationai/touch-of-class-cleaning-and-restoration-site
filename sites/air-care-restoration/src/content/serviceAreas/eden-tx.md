## Restoration emergencies common in Eden

Eden sits in the heart of the Texas Hill Country–Rolling Plains transition, where the weather swings hard and the ground doesn't always cooperate. Winter freeze events are the most common driver of sudden water damage: a single hard overnight freeze can split supply lines in uninsulated wall cavities, and because Eden's older homes were often built before modern insulation standards, exterior walls are especially vulnerable. Spring and early summer bring severe thunderstorms capable of driving rain through aging roof assemblies and around deteriorating window seals. When water gets into a structure here, the dry West Texas air can be deceptive — surfaces read dry to the touch while moisture lingers inside wall cavities and under flooring, giving mold a foothold before a homeowner realizes there's still a problem. Wildfire smoke is also a seasonal concern in Concho County; the same dry conditions that fuel grassland fires push fine particulate into HVAC systems and porous materials throughout a home.

## Services we provide in Eden

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Eden frequently starts at the supply lines — particularly in homes with older galvanized or copper plumbing that has thinned over decades. Slab foundations, common in mid-century construction here, can trap moisture beneath flooring with no drainage path, meaning a slow leak under a kitchen fixture can saturate a concrete slab and the subfloor above it before any visible sign appears. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, not just the visible damage, then dry to the IICRC S500 standard before any rebuild work begins.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Grassland and brush fires in the Concho County area can push smoke into homes even when the structure itself never ignites. Smoke residue from a wildfire or a kitchen fire behaves differently depending on what burned — protein smoke from cooking clings to surfaces with almost no visible trace but a persistent odor, while wood-smoke soot is more visible but penetrates porous materials quickly. Our IICRC FSRT- and OCT-trained technicians assess the residue type, clean affected surfaces, and address HVAC contamination so the smell doesn't cycle back through your system every time the heat runs.

### Mold remediation

Mold can begin colonizing wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. In Eden's climate, a slow roof leak or a sweating pipe inside a wall can create the sustained humidity a colony needs without ever producing a visible water stain. When a property owner has engaged an independent licensed Mold Assessment Consultant and received a remediation protocol, we contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces to industry standards — with the goal of meeting the post-remediation verification criteria established in the Mold Remediation Protocol. Post-remediation verification is performed by the independent Mold Assessment Consultant, not by Air Care.

### Structural drying and reconstruction

After mitigation, many Eden properties need framing repairs, drywall replacement, or flooring reinstallation. We coordinate both phases so you aren't managing two separate contractors and two separate schedules during an already stressful situation.
