## Restoration emergencies common in Potosi

Potosi sits in Taylor County on the eastern edge of the Abilene metro, and the weather here does not give property owners much grace. West Texas freeze events can drop temperatures fast enough to burst supply lines before residents realize the risk, sending water into walls, subfloors, and cabinets overnight. Summer thunderstorms roll across the Rolling Plains with enough intensity to drive wind-driven rain under rooflines and through aging window seals. The area's clay-heavy soils shift with moisture cycles, which puts lateral pressure on slab edges and can crack foundation penetrations, creating pathways for groundwater intrusion. Wildfire smoke during dry seasons can infiltrate HVAC systems and leave odor and particulate residue throughout a structure even when the fire itself never touches the property.

## Services we provide in Potosi

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Potosi frequently starts small and hides well. Clay soils that stay saturated after a storm push moisture against slabs, and supply-line failures behind walls can read dry at the surface while the framing and insulation behind them stay wet for days. Air Care Restoration uses thermal imaging and moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, not just what is visible, then extracts standing water and sets up structured drying calibrated to the conditions inside your specific building.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a fire, the visible char is only part of the problem. Smoke residue travels through HVAC ductwork and settles into porous materials throughout a home, and protein-based smoke from kitchen fires leaves a nearly invisible film that produces persistent odor. The team cleans and deodorizes affected surfaces, seals salvageable materials, and documents total losses for your insurance carrier.

### Mold remediation

In a climate that swings between dry heat and sudden humidity spikes, mold can colonize a damp wall cavity within days of a water event. Air Care Restoration contains the affected area, removes compromised materials following established remediation protocols, and treats surfaces before reconstruction begins. Property owners should not attempt to assess or disturb suspected mold growth before a professional evaluation.

### Biohazard and specialty cleanup

Unattended situations, sewage backups, and other biohazard events require personal protective equipment and proper disposal procedures that go beyond standard cleaning. Air Care Restoration handles these situations discreetly and disposes of materials in compliance with applicable regulations.

### Reconstruction

Once mitigation is complete, the same company that dried and cleaned your property can handle the rebuild, from framing and drywall to flooring and paint. Keeping one contractor through both phases simplifies the insurance claim and eliminates the coordination gap between mitigation and repair.
