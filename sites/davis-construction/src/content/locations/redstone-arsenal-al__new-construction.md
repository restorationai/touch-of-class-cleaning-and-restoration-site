## Local Note

North Alabama's clay soils don't just affect foundations during construction. They also influence grading and drainage design in ways that matter long after move-in. Lots in this region need positive drainage slopes that account for how clay behaves after heavy rain: it sheds water quickly at the surface but can hold moisture at depth for extended periods. A grading plan that looks adequate on paper can still direct water toward a foundation if it doesn't account for the specific topography of your parcel. We walk every site with drainage in mind before finalizing the grade plan, because fixing a drainage problem after landscaping is installed costs far more than designing it right the first time.
