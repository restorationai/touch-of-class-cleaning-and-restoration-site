## Equipment & Methods We Use for Post-Construction & Specialty

Drywall dust is abrasive and fine enough to scratch finished surfaces if handled with the wrong tool, so we use microfiber systems rated for post-construction debris rather than standard janitorial cloths, along with HEPA-filtered vacuums that keep dust from recirculating into freshly cleaned rooms. Adhesive and paint overspray on tile or glass gets treated with surface-appropriate solvents rather than scraping, which matters on older Athens homes where original hardwood or vintage tile can be damaged by aggressive scraping. For HVAC systems, we recommend a filter change immediately following our final clean, since construction dust tends to load the first filter fast regardless of how thorough the cleaning was.
