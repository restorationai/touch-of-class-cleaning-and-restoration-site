## Why Hazel Green's Climate and Soil Shape Every Outdoor Build

North Alabama's humidity is not a background detail when you're selecting lumber, fasteners, or post materials. Hazel Green receives warm, wet summers with soil that holds moisture well into the fall, and that persistent ground saturation accelerates rot at the base of fence posts and deck footings if the wrong materials or depths are used. The clay-heavy soils common throughout this part of Madison County also expand and contract seasonally, which can heave concrete footings that weren't poured deep enough or wide enough to resist the movement.

Pressure-treated lumber rated for ground contact is the baseline here, not an upgrade. For pergola posts and fence posts set directly in the earth, composite or aluminum alternatives are worth a serious conversation because they simply don't absorb the moisture that wood does over a decade of Alabama summers. Fasteners matter too: standard zinc-coated hardware corrodes faster in high-humidity environments, and we use hot-dipped galvanized or stainless hardware throughout.
