## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Huntsville

Every job starts with a moisture assessment before a single piece of equipment is placed. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation — including areas that look dry to the eye. In older Huntsville homes with plaster walls and original hardwood flooring, this step is especially important because water migrates differently through dense historic materials than through modern drywall and OSB.

From there, the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and monitoring:

- **Water removal and extraction** — Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from flooring, crawl spaces, and wall cavities. In homes with original hardwood floors, we adjust suction pressure to avoid surface damage while still pulling moisture from the subfloor beneath.
- **Structural drying** — Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are placed according to a drying plan specific to the room geometry and material types. We set target vapor pressure readings and return daily to verify progress.
- **Monitoring and documentation** — Every moisture reading is logged with date, time, and location. This documentation matters when you file an insurance claim — adjusters want to see a drying log, not just a final invoice.
- **Controlled demolition if needed** — When drywall or insulation cannot be dried in place, we remove only what's necessary and document the scope for your insurer before disposal.
