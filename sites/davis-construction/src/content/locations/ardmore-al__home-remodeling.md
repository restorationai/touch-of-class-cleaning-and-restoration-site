## Why Ardmore Homes Call for a Thoughtful Remodel Approach

The housing stock in this part of Limestone County leans heavily toward homes built between the 1960s and 1990s. That era of construction brought its own quirks: load-bearing walls in places that surprise you, galvanized plumbing that has quietly narrowed over decades, and insulation that was considered adequate then but falls short of current energy standards. Before a single cabinet is ordered or a floor plan is sketched, a remodel here needs to account for what is already inside the walls.

The local climate adds another layer. Ardmore gets the full force of Alabama summers, with heat and humidity that stress building envelopes and make poorly ventilated additions uncomfortable to live in. Any addition or open-concept conversion needs to be planned with HVAC capacity in mind from the start, not treated as an afterthought once framing is done.

## Remodeling Older Homes in the Ardmore Area

One pattern we see consistently in north Alabama ranch homes is that the original floor plan treated the kitchen as a separate, functional room rather than a gathering space. Opening that layout up is one of the most requested changes we handle, and in homes from this era it almost always involves a structural conversation. Removing a wall between a kitchen and living area frequently means adding a beam and adjusting point loads, work that needs to be done correctly the first time.

Bathroom remodels in homes of this vintage often reveal cast iron drain lines that are still serviceable but benefit from inspection before new tile goes down over them. Catching a corroding drain before the floor is tiled saves a homeowner from tearing out new work a few years later.

## Local Note

Homes in this part of Limestone County were often built on pier-and-beam or shallow slab foundations that respond noticeably to the region's clay soil as it expands and contracts through wet and dry seasons. That movement shows up as doors that stick in summer and gaps at baseboards in winter. When a remodel involves flooring replacement, we check for evidence of that seasonal shift before selecting materials. Rigid tile over an active slab without the right substrate preparation will crack. Knowing this going in changes the material recommendation, and it is the kind of detail that only comes from working in this specific part of Alabama.
