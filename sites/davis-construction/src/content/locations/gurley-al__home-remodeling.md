## Local Note: What Gurley's Clay Soil Means for Your Remodel

One thing that comes up repeatedly on Gurley projects is the effect of the local clay soil on interior finishes. When the ground moves seasonally, as it does here with the wet-dry cycles of a north Alabama year, the movement telegraphs into the structure above it. Tile work in bathrooms and kitchens is especially vulnerable: grout lines that look fine in spring can crack by fall if the substrate wasn't properly isolated from that movement. On Gurley remodels, we use uncoupling membranes under tile in any area where we've seen evidence of seasonal floor flex, and we discuss that recommendation with homeowners upfront rather than skipping it to hit a lower bid number.
