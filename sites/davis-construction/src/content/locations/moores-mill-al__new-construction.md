## Why Building in Moores Mill Requires Local Knowledge

Madison County's northern reaches, including the Moores Mill area, sit on a mix of clay-heavy soils and residual soils derived from weathered limestone and shale. Proper geotechnical assessment before breaking ground is not optional here; it is the difference between a slab that stays level and one that shifts.
