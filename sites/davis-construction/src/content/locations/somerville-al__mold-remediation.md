Mold in a Somerville home rarely shows up where you'd expect. Morgan County's humid summers push moisture into crawlspaces and attic framing long before a homeowner notices a musty smell in the hall closet, and by the time it's visible on drywall, it's often been colonizing the subfloor or sill plate for weeks. If you're searching for mold remediation in Somerville because of a damp smell, a recent roof leak, or a failed home inspection, the fix depends on catching it at the structural level, not just the surface.

## Why Somerville Properties See Mold Issues

Somerville sits in a stretch of north Alabama where red clay soil holds water close to the surface instead of letting it drain. That matters for the area's older farmhouses and the mix of brick-and-block ranch homes built from the 1960s through the 1980s, many of which were built with crawlspace foundations rather than slabs. Clay soil keeps crawlspaces damp for days after a heavy rain, and without a vapor barrier or proper ventilation, that moisture migrates straight into floor joists and insulation. Combine that with Alabama's long, humid growing season and you get a climate where mold doesn't need a dramatic leak to get started: a slow HVAC condensation drip or a gap in bathroom exhaust venting is often enough.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Somerville

We start with a visual and moisture-meter inspection of the affected area, including crawlspace access points, since that's where a lot of Somerville-area mold problems originate even when the symptom is a smell upstairs. Contained work areas go up with plastic sheeting and negative air pressure before anything is cut out, so spores don't spread to the rest of the house while we remove and dispose of affected drywall, insulation, or subflooring. Surfaces that can be saved are cleaned with antimicrobial treatment and dried down per industry drying standards before we close anything back up. Because so much of the area's housing stock relies on vented crawlspaces, we also check whether the crawlspace itself needs a vapor barrier or improved ventilation so the same conditions don't just regrow the problem in six months.

## Mold Repair, Rebuild, and Mold-Safe Renovation

Remediation is only half the job if drywall, baseboards, or subfloor had to come out. We handle the repair and rebuild side in-house, which matters in a market like Somerville where finding a second contractor willing to pick up mid-project drywall and trim work can add weeks to a timeline. That includes mold-safe renovation choices where it makes sense, like swapping standard drywall for moisture-resistant board in bathrooms or laundry areas, or adding a dehumidifier line to a crawlspace during the rebuild rather than waiting for a second service call. Post-remediation reconstruction is scoped and priced as part of the same project, not handed off separately, so homeowners aren't coordinating between two contractors on the same wall.

## Local note

A lot of Somerville-area homes still rely on original ductwork and crawlspace vents installed decades before today's moisture standards existed. When we're called out for a mold issue in one of these older homes, we almost always check the crawlspace vents first, since vents that were sized correctly in 1975 often can't keep up with the humidity load from a newer HVAC system or an added bathroom. Closing that gap during remediation, rather than just treating the visible mold, is usually what keeps the problem from coming back the following summer.
