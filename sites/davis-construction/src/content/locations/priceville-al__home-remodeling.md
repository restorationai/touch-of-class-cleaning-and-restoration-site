## Why Priceville Homes Present Unique Remodeling Considerations

Morgan County's growth along the U.S. 31 corridor has brought a mix of housing stock to Priceville, ranging from brick ranch homes built in the 1970s and 1980s to newer subdivisions that went up during the construction boom of the 2000s. Each era comes with its own set of remodeling challenges.

Ranch homes from the 1970s and 1980s often have original galvanized plumbing that becomes a real conversation once walls open up for a kitchen or bathroom renovation. Aluminum wiring, common in residential construction during that same period, requires careful evaluation before any electrical work is added to a remodel scope. Newer homes, meanwhile, may have engineered lumber framing or OSB sheathing that responds differently to moisture than traditional dimensional lumber, something worth factoring in when remodeling bathrooms or laundry rooms in Priceville's humid climate.

The Tennessee Valley's heat and humidity also mean that vapor management inside exterior walls is not an afterthought. A remodel that ignores how this region breathes can trap moisture in wall cavities and create problems that show up years later.
