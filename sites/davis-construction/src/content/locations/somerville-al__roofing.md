## Why Somerville Properties Face Roofing Stress

North Alabama's climate is harder on roofing materials than many homeowners realize. The region experiences genuine four-season weather: freeze-thaw cycles in January and February that work ice and water beneath shingle edges, followed by months of direct sun that accelerates asphalt oxidation. Morgan County also sits in a corridor that sees its share of severe thunderstorm activity, and the combination of high wind gusts and hail, even small-diameter hail, strips granules from shingles faster than age alone would.

Older residential construction in the Somerville area adds another layer of complexity. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s often have roof decking that was installed before modern moisture-barrier standards, and when that decking gets wet repeatedly, it develops soft spots that won't support a new shingle layer properly. A roofing contractor who doesn't probe the deck before laying new material is setting the homeowner up for a repeat job within a few years.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Somerville

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system, not just the shingles, but the decking, the fascia, the flashing around penetrations, and the ventilation profile. Inadequate attic ventilation is a chronic issue in this climate: when superheated attic air has nowhere to escape, it cooks shingles from below and dramatically shortens their service life.

Once we have a clear picture of what's there, we walk the property owner through material options suited to North Alabama conditions. Architectural shingles with a Class 4 impact rating perform significantly better against the hail events common to this region than standard three-tab products. For commercial flat or low-slope roofs, we assess drainage patterns carefully, standing water in a Morgan County summer is an invitation for membrane failure.

During installation, old material is stripped completely, the deck is inspected and any damaged sections are replaced, a proper underlayment and drip edge are installed, and new shingles or roofing membrane are applied to manufacturer specifications. We don't skip the details that determine whether a roof lasts 15 years or 30.

## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about roofing work in the Somerville area specifically: the combination of clay-heavy soils and the slope patterns on many Morgan County lots means that water sheeting off a new roof needs to be directed carefully. Downspout placement and gutter pitch that works fine on a flat suburban lot can cause erosion or foundation-adjacent pooling on the more varied terrain common here.
