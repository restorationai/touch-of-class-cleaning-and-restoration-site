## Why Arab Homes Present Specific Remodeling Considerations

Marshall County's clay soils shift with seasonal moisture, and that movement shows up inside homes in ways that matter before a remodel begins. Doors that no longer close square, hairline cracks running diagonally from window corners, and floors that feel slightly uneven underfoot are all signs that a foundation has responded to soil expansion and contraction over years. Starting a kitchen or bathroom remodel without accounting for those conditions can mean new cabinets that rack or tile that cracks within a season or two.

Arab also has a meaningful stock of homes built in the 1960s through the 1980s, a construction era that used materials and framing methods that require extra planning. Older homes in this area often have cast-iron drain lines that are worth inspecting before a bathroom renovation closes up walls, and original electrical panels that may need an upgrade before a kitchen remodel adds circuit load. Identifying those conditions early keeps a project on schedule and on budget.
