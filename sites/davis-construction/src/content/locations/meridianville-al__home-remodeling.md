## Local Note

One thing that surprises homeowners remodeling older Meridianville properties is how the clay soil beneath pier-and-beam foundations behaves differently in wet versus dry seasons. During a dry summer, piers can drop slightly as the soil contracts, and during a wet spring, they rise. If you're planning a whole-home remodel that includes new flooring throughout, timing the subfloor leveling work for a period of stable soil moisture, typically late spring after the wet season settles, produces a more consistent result than rushing it in midsummer or midwinter. It's a small scheduling detail that makes a measurable difference in how the finished floors perform over time.
