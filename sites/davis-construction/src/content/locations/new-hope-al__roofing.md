## Why New Hope Properties See Roofing Problems Earlier Than Expected

The Tennessee Valley's weather pattern is the main culprit. New Hope experiences a wide annual temperature swing, and that expansion-and-contraction cycle works on asphalt shingles the same way it works on a rubber band you stretch and release repeatedly. Add the humidity that settles into attic spaces during summer, and you create conditions where decking can begin to soften long before a shingle visibly fails at the surface.

Many homes in the New Hope area were built during the suburban growth that spread outward from Huntsville through the 1970s and 1980s. Roofing systems from that era often used lower-profile decking and ventilation designs that were adequate for the time but leave little margin against today's storm intensity. A roof that is 25 to 30 years old in this area is not aging gracefully, it is overdue for a serious inspection.

Hail events are also more common in northern Alabama than many homeowners realize. Even a moderate hail event can bruise shingles in ways that are invisible from the ground but allow moisture to wick into the mat layer, beginning a slow deterioration that shows up as interior water staining months later.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in New Hope

Every project starts with a detailed roof inspection, not a quick walk-around, but a systematic assessment of the decking, flashing, ridge, valleys, and penetrations. We document what we find with photos before any material is removed, which matters when an insurance claim is part of the picture.

Once the scope is confirmed, the old roofing system comes off in sections so the decking underneath can be evaluated board by board. Soft or delaminated decking gets replaced before anything new goes down. Skipping that step is the most common reason a new roof fails prematurely, and it is not a shortcut we take.

We install a quality underlayment appropriate for the slope and exposure, then set the shingles in a pattern that accounts for the prevailing wind direction in the valley. Ridge ventilation is sized to match the attic volume, because an under-ventilated attic in a New Hope summer can shorten a new roof's lifespan by years. Flashing around chimneys, vents, and dormers is replaced or resealed as part of the installation, not treated as optional.
