## Why Meridianville Roofs Wear Out Faster Than You'd Expect

The Tennessee Valley's humidity doesn't just make summers uncomfortable. It creates a thermal cycling problem that shortens shingle life: hot attic air trapped under a poorly ventilated deck expands and contracts with every storm front, loosening fasteners and cracking sealant strips faster than the manufacturer's rated lifespan suggests. Asphalt shingles rated for 25 or 30 years often show significant granule loss and tab curling in 15 to 18 years on homes here that were built without adequate ridge ventilation.

The clay-heavy soils common through this part of Madison County also shift seasonally, and on homes with slab-on-grade construction, that movement can telegraph up through the fascia and cause subtle racking in the roof deck that goes unnoticed until a storm exposes it. If your home was built in the 1980s or 1990s during the rapid residential expansion along the U.S. 72 corridor, there's a reasonable chance the original decking is 7/16-inch OSB that has absorbed decades of attic moisture. Replacement work here often means addressing the deck before a single new shingle goes down.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Meridianville

Every project starts with a full deck inspection, not just a shingle count.
