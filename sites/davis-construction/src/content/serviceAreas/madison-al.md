## Services we provide in Madison

### Water damage mitigation

Water damage in Madison often starts with something invisible: a slow supply-line drip under a slab, a clogged HVAC condensate drain, or storm-driven water that finds a gap in brick veneer. By the time flooring buckles or a ceiling stain appears, moisture has typically been present for days. We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map the full extent before any drying equipment goes in, then deploy commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers sized to the structure — not a one-size-fits-all setup.

### Fire and smoke restoration

After a kitchen fire or an electrical event in the attic, the visible char is only part of the problem. Smoke residue migrates through HVAC systems and settles into wall cavities, and the odor compounds bond to surfaces in ways that standard cleaning doesn't address. We use hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging alongside structural cleaning to treat the whole envelope, not just the room of origin.

### Mold remediation

Mold calls in Madison frequently trace back to HVAC condensation issues — a common problem in tightly built newer homes where vapor management wasn't detailed correctly during construction. We follow EPA guidance and industry S520 protocols, contain the affected area, and provide post-remediation clearance testing through a third-party industrial hygienist so the result is documented, not just claimed.
