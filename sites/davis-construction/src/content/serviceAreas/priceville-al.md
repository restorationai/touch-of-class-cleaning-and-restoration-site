## Restoration emergencies common in Priceville

Priceville sits in the Tennessee Valley, a region that swings between humid summers and sharp winter cold snaps, both of which stress a house in different ways. Summer thunderstorms and the occasional tropical system remnant moving up from the Gulf can dump heavy rain in a short window, and on lots with newer grading or limited storm drainage, that water finds its way toward foundations and crawlspaces fast. Winter brings its own risk: a hard freeze following a mild stretch is a classic setup for a burst supply line in an unheated crawlspace or garage. Add in the clay-heavy soils common across Morgan County, which expand and contract with moisture swings, and you get slow foundation movement that eventually shows up as cracked slabs, sticking doors, or water finding new paths into a structure it never used to reach.
