## Restoration emergencies common in Redstone Arsenal

Redstone Arsenal sits in the Tennessee River watershed in Madison County, and the same humid subtropical climate that brings heavy spring and summer thunderstorms to the rest of North Alabama drives most of the restoration calls here. Clay-heavy soils across the installation hold water close to the surface after storms, which pushes groundwater against foundations and crawlspaces faster than many homeowners expect. Aging post housing and older support buildings, some dating to the mid-20th century, carry original plumbing and roofing that wasn't built for today's storm intensity, so a failed supply line or a wind-damaged roof section can turn into a multi-room water event before anyone notices standing water under flooring.

## Services we provide in Redstone Arsenal

Water damage response on Redstone Arsenal often starts with a slow leak behind a wall or under a slab that isn't caught until flooring or baseboards show staining, especially in older quarters built before modern moisture barriers were standard. We extract standing water, pull wet insulation and drywall as needed, and dry structural materials to the IICRC S500 standard before closing a room back up.

Storm damage work follows the same seasonal pattern as the rest of Madison County: straight-line wind events and severe thunderstorms that strip shingles, crack fascia, and drive water under roof decking. We tarp and board exposed sections, then move into full roof and structural repair once the property is secure.

Reconstruction and general contracting cover what comes after the emergency work: replacing drywall, flooring, and framing that didn't survive the water or wind event, matching materials to the existing structure, and closing out the project with documentation a claims adjuster or facilities office can use.

Fire and smoke damage repair addresses both the structural loss and the lingering odor that settles into framing and insulation, with reconstruction scoped to match the surrounding building rather than leaving a visibly patched section.
