## Restoration emergencies common in New Hope

New Hope sits in Madison County's stretch of the Tennessee Valley, east of Huntsville, where humid summers and fast-moving spring and fall thunderstorm systems are routine. Wind-driven storms and the occasional tornado track that passes through north Alabama put roofs, gutters, and exterior walls at risk most years. Heavy, sustained rain events also stress aging drainage around older homes, and the region's clay-heavy soil doesn't drain quickly, so water that reaches a foundation or crawlspace tends to linger instead of running off.
