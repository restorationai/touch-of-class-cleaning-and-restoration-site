## Restoration emergencies common in Somerville

Somerville sits in the Tennessee River valley in Morgan County, and that geography shapes what goes wrong in local homes. Heavy spring storms and the occasional remnant tropical system push a lot of rain through in a short window, and properties on lower ground or near drainage ditches and creeks see water find its way into crawlspaces and basements faster than it drains back out. Humid summers slow down any structure that stays wet, which means a slow roof leak or a damp crawlspace left unaddressed for even a few weeks has real potential to turn into a mold problem. Winter brings occasional hard freezes that catch older plumbing off guard, and a burst or split pipe in an unheated crawlspace is one of the more common calls we expect to field here.
