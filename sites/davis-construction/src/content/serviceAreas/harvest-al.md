## Restoration emergencies common in Harvest

Harvest sits in the Tennessee Valley north of Huntsville, where heavy spring storm systems and summer downpours are routine. Homes here see wind-driven rain intrusion around rooflines and window flashing, and the area's clay-heavy soil sheds water slowly, which pushes it toward foundations and crawlspaces instead of draining away. A good share of the housing stock is newer construction on slab or crawlspace foundations, built during the subdivision growth that followed Redstone Arsenal and Cummings Research Park expansion, and that construction mix means water problems often show up first as trapped moisture under flooring or inside crawlspace insulation rather than obvious ceiling stains. Add in the occasional severe thunderstorm or tornado warning that this part of Madison County gets each spring, and the pattern is less about any single disaster and more about recurring water intrusion and wind damage that needs a fast, correct response before it becomes a mold or structural problem.
