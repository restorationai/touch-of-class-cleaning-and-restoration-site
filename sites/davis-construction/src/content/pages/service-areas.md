When water is coming through your ceiling or smoke has settled into every room, the last thing you want to hear is that help is hours away. Davis Construction Contractors is headquartered in Madison, AL, and keeps crews staged to reach most of the Tennessee Valley within an hour. If you're unsure whether your address falls inside our coverage, a quick call to **(256) 771-0326** will get you a straight answer in under two minutes.

## Where we work

Our team operates out of Madison and responds across the greater Huntsville metro, the communities along the Tennessee River corridor, and the surrounding North Alabama cities that share our mix of older ranch-style homes, newer subdivisions, and commercial corridors off I-565 and US-72. That means limestone-heavy soil that drains poorly after heavy rain, crawl spaces that trap humidity through long Alabama summers, and a housing stock that ranges from 1960s brick ranches to recently built neighborhoods near the Research Park. Knowing this region's quirks — the way a slow-moving storm system can flood the same low-lying streets in Madison and Huntsville back to back — shapes how we plan every dispatch.

## Response time by distance

Distance from our Madison headquarters is the honest factor that determines how fast a crew arrives:

- **Madison, Huntsville, and immediately adjacent cities** — technicians reach most addresses quickly, with active losses scheduled first.
- **Broader metro and outlying communities** (Athens, Decatur, Hartselle, Scottsboro, and similar distances) — expect **60–90 minutes** under normal conditions.
- **Edge of our coverage area** — some locations push past 90 minutes, especially during peak traffic on US-72 or when multiple jobs are running simultaneously. We'll tell you the honest ETA before you commit.

Those windows assume a direct route. A late-afternoon call during school dismissal on County Line Road or a weather event that has every restoration crew in the region moving can add time. We'll never quote you a window we can't keep.
