## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether a pipe burst at 2 a.m. or a slow leak finally gave out under your sink, standing water starts damaging structural materials within hours and creates the conditions mold needs within 24 to 48 hours; we extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Flames do obvious damage, but the acidic soot and smoke residue that settles into every surface keep corroding and discoloring long after the fire is out; we clean, deodorize, and stabilize the structure before reconstruction begins.

**Mold remediation** — North Alabama's humidity makes mold a year-round problem, especially in crawl spaces, attics, and anywhere a slow leak went unnoticed; we locate the moisture source, contain the affected area, and remove the growth safely.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Severe weather in the Tennessee Valley can strip roofing, push water through walls, and leave structural damage that isn't obvious until weeks later; we handle tarping and board-up through full rebuild so you're not managing multiple contractors.
