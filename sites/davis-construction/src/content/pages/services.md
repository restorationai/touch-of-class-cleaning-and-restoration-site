From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, Davis Construction Contractors handles the full arc of property damage — emergency mitigation, structural reconstruction, and everything in between. We work with homeowners, landlords, commercial property owners, and multi-family operators across Madison, Huntsville, and the surrounding Tennessee Valley communities. One call connects you to a crew that can stop the damage, document it for your insurer, and rebuild what was lost.

## Emergency restoration services

When something goes wrong fast, the response time matters as much as the skill. Water damage can soak into subfloor framing within hours; smoke residue bonds to surfaces and begins corroding metals within days; a biohazard scene carries health risks that compound the longer it sits. Davis Construction Contractors dispatches for **water damage restoration** — extraction, drying, and moisture mapping — as well as **fire and smoke damage restoration**, where the work goes well beyond what's visibly charred. We also respond to **storm and wind damage**, securing compromised roofs and exteriors before the next weather event makes things worse, and we handle **biohazard and trauma scene cleanup** with the discretion and proper containment those situations demand.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the bleeding; reconstruction restores the building. Once your property is dry, cleaned, and cleared, Davis Construction Contractors can take it all the way back — framing, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, painting, and finish work. Because we hold a general contractor's license in Alabama, we can pull permits, coordinate subcontractors, and manage the rebuild as a single project rather than handing you off to a stranger mid-process. That matters when your insurance adjuster needs a scope of work and a timeline: we can provide both and stay accountable to them.

## Specialty services

Some damage doesn't announce itself with standing water or a smoke alarm. **Mold inspection and remediation** addresses the colonies that grow quietly behind walls after a slow leak — common in older Madison and Huntsville homes where vapor barriers weren't part of the original build. **Odor removal** goes deeper than masking: we use hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging to neutralize smoke, pet, and sewage odors at the molecular level. When a structure is compromised and needs time before full repairs begin, **board-up and tarping services** protect the interior from weather, vandalism, and further loss.
