## What Should You Actually Look for in a Madison Remodeling Contractor?

Google ratings are a starting point, not a finish line. Here's what matters when you're comparing bids on a real project in Madison.

**Licensing and insurance.** Alabama requires a general contractor license for projects over $10,000 through the [Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors](https://www.albgc.state.al.us). Ask every contractor for their license number and verify it directly on the board's site. Licensed and insured is the floor, not a differentiator.

**Scope of self-performed work vs. subcontracting.** Some remodelers are primarily project managers who sub out every trade. Others have in-house crews for framing, finish carpentry, and tile. Neither model is wrong, but you should know which you're hiring. A company that subs everything out adds a markup layer on each trade and has less direct control over scheduling.

**Experience with Madison's specific housing stock.** Madison has two very different eras of homes: 1970s-80s brick ranch homes (common in older sections near Old Town Madison and along the County Line Road corridor) and 2000s-2020s construction in newer subdivisions like Redstone Village and Blue Springs. Older ranches often have surprises behind the walls: outdated wiring, original cast-iron drain lines, and insulation that doesn't meet current energy code. A contractor who's worked in Madison for years will price those risks in; one who hasn't may underbid and then hit you with change orders.

**HOA compliance.** Many newer Madison subdivisions have architectural review requirements. If your project changes exterior appearance (windows, siding, roofline, additions), your contractor should be familiar with the HOA approval process and ideally have handled it before in your subdivision.

**Written scope of work.** Before you sign anything, you should have a line-item scope that specifies materials by manufacturer and product line, not just "tile" or "cabinets." Vague scopes are how bids look competitive on paper and then balloon in execution.

## What Does a Home Remodel Actually Cost in Madison, AL?

Cost ranges vary significantly by project type, but here are realistic benchmarks for Madison-area projects in 2026:

- **Kitchen remodel (mid-range):** $35,000-$65,000 for a full gut with semi-custom cabinets, quartz countertops, new appliances, and tile backsplash. High-end finishes push past $80,000.
- **Bathroom remodel (primary bath):** $15,000-$30,000 for a full remodel. A simple refresh (new vanity, fixtures, tile surround) runs $6,000-$12,000.
- **Whole-home interior refresh** (paint, flooring, trim, light fixtures): $20,000-$50,000 depending on square footage and finish level.
- **Room addition:** $150-$250 per square foot for a conditioned addition with proper footings, framing, insulation, and finish work.

These ranges reflect the Madison market. Labor costs here run lower than national averages, but material costs have normalized with the rest of the country. If a bid comes in 40% below the low end of these ranges, ask detailed questions about what's included and what's not.

## What Permits Do You Need for a Home Remodel in Madison?

Most structural, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical work in Madison requires a permit through the City of Madison Building Department. This includes kitchen remodels that touch electrical or plumbing, bathroom additions, room additions, and any work that changes the home's footprint or roofline.

Cosmetic work (painting, flooring, cabinet refacing, replacing fixtures in-kind) typically does not require a permit, but the line blurs quickly once you're moving walls or adding circuits. Your contractor should pull the permits, not you. If a contractor suggests you pull the permit yourself to "save money," that's a red flag: it shifts liability to you and can complicate your homeowner's insurance.

Unpermitted work also creates problems at resale. Madison's growth has brought a lot of buyer scrutiny to disclosure requirements, and an unpermitted addition or electrical upgrade can kill a deal or require expensive remediation before closing.

## What Questions Should You Ask Before Hiring a Remodeling Company?

Before you sign a contract with any remodeler in Madison, get answers to these:

1. **Are you licensed with the Alabama Licensing Board for General Contractors?** Get the number and verify it.
2. **Do you carry general liability and workers' comp?** Ask for certificates of insurance naming you as an additional insured.
3. **Who performs the work?** In-house crews, subs, or a mix? Who manages the subs on site?
4. **Have you worked on homes similar to mine in Madison?** Ask for references from comparable projects in the area.
5. **How do you handle change orders?** Get the process in writing before work starts.
6. **What's your payment schedule?** Avoid any contractor asking for more than 10-15% upfront on a large project. Progress payments tied to milestones are standard.
7. **What's the warranty on labor and materials?** One year on labor is a common minimum; manufacturer warranties on materials vary.
