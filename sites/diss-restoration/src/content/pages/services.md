From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen fire that leaves smoke odor in every room, property damage rarely waits for a convenient moment. DISS Restoration handles the full range of residential, commercial, and multi-family losses — emergency mitigation, structural drying, rebuild, and the specialty work that falls between those categories. One company, one point of contact, from the first call through the final walk-through.

## Emergency restoration services

When water, fire, or a severe storm hits, the first hours determine how much of a structure can be saved. Standing water migrates into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies faster than it shows on the surface; smoke residue begins bonding to porous materials within minutes of a fire being extinguished; storm damage left open to the elements compounds quickly. DISS Restoration responds 24/7 to **water damage**, **fire and smoke damage**, **storm damage**, **biohazard cleanup**, and related emergency losses. Crews arrive equipped to extract, stabilize, and document — stopping the damage from spreading while the scope is still manageable.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation gets a property dry, clean, and safe. Reconstruction gets it back to livable. After a loss, the rebuild phase often involves replacing drywall and insulation, repairing or rebuilding structural framing, restoring flooring, and finishing work that matches what was there before. DISS Restoration holds the licenses and carries the crews to handle that entire scope in-house — no handoff to a separate contractor, no gap in accountability between the remediation team and the rebuild team. The same is true for renovation work that isn't tied to a loss: if a space needs updating, the infrastructure is already in place.

## Specialty services

Some jobs don't fit neatly into emergency response or full reconstruction. **Mold inspection and remediation** addresses hidden growth that often surfaces weeks after a water event — or turns up during a real estate transaction. **Odor removal** goes beyond masking: thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment reach into the same cavities where smoke and biological odors originate. **Board-up and tarping** secures a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism, preventing secondary damage before the restoration process begins. These services can stand alone or run alongside a larger mitigation and rebuild scope.

Handling mitigation and reconstruction under one roof means the team doing the drying is the same team planning the rebuild — scope gaps and finger-pointing between separate contractors don't enter the picture.
