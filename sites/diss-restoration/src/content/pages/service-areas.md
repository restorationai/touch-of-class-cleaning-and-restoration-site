DISS Restoration is based in Youngstown and answers the phone around the clock — every day, including holidays. When a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or a storm pushes water into a finished basement on a Sunday, the same dispatch process runs: you call, we assess the situation, and a crew heads your way. No answering service, no next-business-day callback.

## Where we work

Youngstown sits at the crossroads of northeast Ohio and western Pennsylvania, and that's the core of our coverage. We respond across the Mahoning Valley and into the surrounding communities on both sides of the state line — a region shaped by older housing stock, clay-heavy soils that don't drain quickly, and winters that put real stress on roofs, pipes, and foundations. If you're in that band of cities and townships stretching from the Pennsylvania border west through Trumbull and Mahoning counties, you're well within our working area.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Youngstown — places like Boardman, Austintown, Canfield, Niles, Warren, and the communities just across the line in Mercer and Lawrence counties, PA — see the fastest crew arrivals. The drive is short, and we know these roads.

Further out, we regularly serve communities across Trumbull County to the north and Columbiana County to the south and east. Response to those areas depends on crew availability and current job load, but a loss that needs immediate mitigation — standing water, active smoke damage, a sewage backup — moves to the front of the queue regardless of distance.

At the outer edge of our range, larger losses take priority. A small water intrusion sixty miles out may be better handled by a local referral we trust; a major structural fire or a Category 3 flood in the same location is a different conversation. Call us and describe what you're dealing with — we'll be straight with you about whether we're the right fit.

## Don't see your city?

The city list on this page reflects our most active service zones, not a hard boundary. If your community isn't listed, call [(724) 981-1441](tel:7249811441) and describe the loss. We dispatch outside our standard area for significant jobs, and we'd rather you reach out than assume the answer is no.
