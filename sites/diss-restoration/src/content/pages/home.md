## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in a century-old Youngstown double or a sump pump failure after a heavy Lake Erie weather system rolls through, standing water causes structural damage fast. We extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke restoration** — Smoke residue doesn't stay where the fire was. It travels through HVAC systems, settles into porous materials, and leaves an odor that won't come out with surface cleaning alone. We address the full scope of what smoke leaves behind.

**Mold remediation** — Northeast Ohio's humidity and the region's older housing stock create conditions where mold can colonize within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. We locate the source, contain the affected area, and remediate to industry standards.

**Reconstruction** — Once the damage is mitigated, getting your home or building back to its pre-loss condition takes real rebuild work. We handle that too, so you're not coordinating a separate contractor mid-claim.
