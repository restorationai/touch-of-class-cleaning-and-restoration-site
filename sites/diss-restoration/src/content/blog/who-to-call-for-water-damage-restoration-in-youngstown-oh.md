## When Is It a Restoration Company Call vs. Something Else?

Not every water problem is the same call. Here is a quick triage:

**Call DISS Restoration at (724) 981-1441 when:**
- A pipe burst, appliance failed, or a roof leak has soaked drywall, flooring, or structural materials
- Your basement flooded from a sump pump failure, drain backup, or groundwater intrusion
- A sewage line backed up into your home (this is Category 3 water, also called black water, and requires specialized handling)
- You had a fire and now have water damage from suppression efforts
- You see or smell mold after a water event

**Call a plumber first (or at the same time) when:**
- The water source is still active and you cannot shut off the supply valve yourself
- A main line, water heater, or appliance connection has failed and needs repair before drying can begin

**Call 911 or your utility company when:**
- Water is in contact with electrical panels, outlets, or live wiring
- You smell gas in the affected area
- There is structural instability (sagging ceilings, compromised floors)

**Call your insurance company after mitigation is underway:**
- Once the emergency is stabilized and documentation has started, open your claim
- DISS Restoration provides moisture readings, photo documentation, and a written scope of work that supports your claim from day one

If you are not sure which bucket your situation falls into, call (724) 981-1441 anyway. The team can help you triage over the phone.

## What Happens When You Call DISS Restoration?

When you call (724) 981-1441, you reach a live person any time of day or night. Here is what the process looks like from that first call forward.

**The first call:** You describe what happened and where. The team asks a few quick questions: Is the water source stopped? Is there a safety hazard? Roughly how much area is affected? This takes a few minutes and helps them stage the right equipment before they arrive.

**On arrival:** The crew assesses the loss using calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging to map exactly where water has traveled, including inside walls and under flooring where you cannot see it. They classify the water (clean supply line, gray water from an appliance, or black water from sewage) because the category determines the extraction and sanitization protocol.

**Extraction and drying setup:** Commercial truck-mounted or portable extractors pull standing water. Then LGR (low-grain refrigerant) dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to create a drying system. The [IICRC S500 Standard for Professional Water Damage Restoration](https://www.iicrc.org) sets the benchmarks the crew dries to, not just until things look dry.

**Monitoring:** Moisture readings are logged at each visit. Drying typically takes three to five days for a standard loss, though Category 3 water, affected building materials, and the size of the loss all affect the timeline.

**Documentation:** Every moisture reading, every photo, every equipment log is part of the file that goes to your insurance adjuster. DISS Restoration has handled this process since 2021 and understands what adjusters need to process a claim cleanly.
