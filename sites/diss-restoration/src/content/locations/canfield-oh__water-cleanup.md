## Why Canfield Properties See Water Damage Issues

Mahoning County's clay-heavy soils drain slowly. After a heavy rain, that hydrostatic pressure builds against block or poured-concrete foundations, and water intrudes through cracks, cove joints, or window wells before a sump pump can keep up. Canfield's housing stock includes a meaningful share of homes built in the mid-twentieth century, when basement waterproofing standards were far less rigorous than they are today. Those older foundations, combined with galvanized or early copper supply plumbing that has reached the end of its service life, create conditions where both storm-driven flooding and internal pipe failures are common calls.

Winter freeze-thaw cycles compound the problem. Temperatures in this part of northeastern Ohio swing repeatedly through the freezing point between November and March, stressing any pipe that runs through an uninsulated exterior wall or an unconditioned crawlspace. A pipe that survives January can split in a late-February cold snap when the metal has already been fatigued by weeks of thermal cycling.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Canfield

The first step on every job is containment and assessment. The crew identifies the water source, stops active flow if it is a plumbing failure, and uses moisture meters and thermal imaging to map how far water has traveled behind walls and under flooring before it becomes visible at the surface. Water behind plaster or older paneling can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated, so surface observation alone is not enough.

Once the scope is confirmed, truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from the structure. High-velocity air movers and industrial dehumidifiers are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for drying, which specifies airflow patterns based on the materials present and the measured moisture content. Readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented for your insurance file. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated carpet pad or compromised drywall, are removed and disposed of properly before the drying phase is complete.
