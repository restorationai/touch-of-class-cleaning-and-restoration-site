## Why Girard Properties Experience Sewer Backups

The clay and cast-iron lateral lines common beneath Girard's older neighborhoods are among the leading causes of recurring sewer backups in Trumbull County. Clay tile joints shift as soil settles, and cast-iron corrodes from the inside out — both conditions allow tree root intrusion and sediment buildup that restrict flow over time. Heavy rain events compound the problem: when the municipal combined sewer system in this part of Ohio surges, pressure can reverse flow through aging private laterals, sending raw sewage backward into the lowest point of the home.

Girard's climate adds another layer. Freeze-thaw cycles through winter and early spring repeatedly stress buried pipe joints, and the region's clay-heavy soils hold moisture against foundation walls rather than draining away from them. Homes with below-grade finished spaces — a common feature in the bungalows and two-stories built throughout Girard's residential neighborhoods — are especially exposed when a sewer lateral fails.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Girard

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification under IICRC standards — which means the response protocol is more involved than a clean-water pipe burst. The process on a Girard job typically moves through these stages:

**Containment and safety assessment.** Before extraction begins, affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Technicians assess which materials can be dried and sanitized and which have absorbed enough sewage contamination to require removal.

**Extraction and debris removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units. Porous materials — drywall, carpet, insulation, and in many Girard homes, older wood paneling — that have direct contact with raw sewage are typically removed rather than dried in place, because sanitizing agents cannot reliably penetrate saturated porous substrates.

**Antimicrobial treatment and sanitization.** After extraction and material removal, all affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. This step addresses bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens present in raw sewage. Hard surfaces, concrete block, and treated wood framing receive multiple applications with dwell times calibrated to the contamination level.

**Structural drying.** Once the space is sanitized, commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers are deployed to dry the structural assembly to pre-loss moisture levels. Moisture readings are logged throughout to document progress and confirm drying is complete before reconstruction begins.
