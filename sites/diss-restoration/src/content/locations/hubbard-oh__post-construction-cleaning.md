## Why Hubbard Properties Require More from a Post-Construction Clean

Hubbard's residential building stock trends older, and older construction introduces variables that newer builds don't. Plaster-and-lath walls — common in homes built before the mid-1960s — hold fine construction dust differently than modern drywall. Dust migrates into wall cavities, settles into original hardwood floor gaps, and coats the inside of cast-iron radiator fins in ways that a standard shop-vac pass won't resolve. If your contractor disturbed original trim or plaster during the project, that debris carries lead paint risk — something DISS Restoration is EPA Lead-Safe Certified to handle properly.

Trumbull County's climate adds another layer. Cold, damp winters mean construction projects that run into late fall or winter often leave moisture trapped under temporary coverings, behind new insulation, or in freshly poured concrete that hasn't fully cured. A thorough post-construction cleanup in Hubbard accounts for that residual moisture before flooring, cabinetry, or paint goes on top of a problem.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Hubbard

Every final clean starts with a walk-through to categorize what's present: rough debris (chunks of drywall, cut lumber, packaging), fine particulate (drywall dust, insulation fibers, sawdust), and surface residue (adhesive, caulk smear, paint overspray, grout haze). Those three categories require different tools and sequences — and skipping the sequence is how you end up smearing fine dust across a freshly painted surface.

Our process follows a top-down, room-by-room approach:

- **Rough debris removal** — bagged and hauled, not just swept to a corner
- **HEPA-filtered vacuuming** of all surfaces, including ceiling fixtures, window tracks, and HVAC returns before filters are replaced
- **Detail cleaning** of cabinetry interiors, drawer slides, and appliance cavities
- **Hard-surface cleaning** — tile grout haze removal, adhesive residue on floors, paint spots on hardware
- **Final wipe-down** of all horizontal surfaces, glass, and fixtures
- **Air quality pass** — HEPA air scrubbers run to capture airborne particulate that resettles after the physical clean

For commercial or larger residential projects in Hubbard, we can stage the clean in phases aligned with your contractor's punch-list schedule so trades aren't waiting on each other.

## Specialty Cleaning Scope: What Goes Beyond a Standard Final Clean

Some projects need more than construction debris removal. Specialty cleaning covers situations where the renovation itself created a secondary problem — smoke odor from a torch-down roofing job that drifted into the living space, biohazard concerns from a demolition that opened up old pipe insulation, or persistent chemical odors from adhesives and sealers used in a poorly ventilated space.

DISS Restoration's IICRC-certified team carries equipment for odor neutralization (thermal fogging, hydroxyl generation) and air filtration that goes well beyond what a cleaning crew with mops and buckets can address. If your renovation uncovered mold behind a wall — a common finding in Hubbard homes where older vapor barriers failed — that's a separate remediation scope, and we handle that work under our IICRC AMRT certification before the cleaning phase begins.

## Local Note

Hubbard homes built in the postwar decades often have original ductwork that hasn't been cleaned in decades. When a renovation opens up walls or ceilings near those ducts, construction debris — insulation fibers, drywall dust, and occasionally disturbed pest material — gets pulled into the system the first time the furnace kicks on. We flag this during walk-throughs and recommend a duct inspection before the HVAC is run post-construction. It's a small step that prevents a freshly cleaned home from being re-coated in fine particulate the first cold night of the season.
