## Why Boardman Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Expect

Boardman sits in Mahoning County, where the combination of humid summers and long heating seasons creates conditions that accelerate what collects inside residential ductwork. Humidity in late spring and early fall — that shoulder season when you're running neither heat nor AC consistently — lets moisture linger inside ducts long enough to give mold spores a foothold on dust and debris already coating the interior surfaces. Add in the region's history of older housing stock, much of it built in the postwar boom of the 1950s and 1960s, and you often find duct systems that were never designed for today's higher-efficiency air handlers. Those older rectangular sheet-metal runs have more seams, more potential for gaps, and more surface area where particulates settle.

Homes in the southern Boardman corridor along Route 224 frequently have original ductwork that's been patched and extended over decades as additions were built — a layout that creates dead-end runs where air stagnates and debris accumulates without ever being pushed out by normal airflow.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Boardman

The process starts with a full inspection of your supply and return system before any equipment goes in. We use a camera to check for visible mold growth, pest intrusion, or collapsed flex duct — problems that cleaning alone won't fix and that need to be addressed before the rest of the work makes sense. Once the system is mapped, we connect a high-powered negative-pressure vacuum to the main trunk line and work section by section from the furthest registers back toward the air handler, agitating debris loose with rotary brushes sized to your duct diameter.

For homes with existing moisture concerns — common in Boardman's older split-level and ranch-style builds — we offer EPA-registered sanitization applied after cleaning. This is not a substitute for addressing the moisture source, but it does reduce the microbial load on interior duct surfaces. Dryer vent cleaning is typically handled in the same visit; Boardman homes with laundry rooms on interior walls often have longer vent runs that accumulate lint faster than the code-minimum 25-foot straight-run assumption accounts for.

One thing worth knowing about Boardman's postwar ranch homes specifically: many were built with ductwork running through unconditioned crawl spaces or uninsulated attic sections.
