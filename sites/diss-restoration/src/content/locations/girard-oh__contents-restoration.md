Contents restoration and storage is rarely a simple wipe-down — smoke odor embeds itself into upholstered furniture differently in a 1940s brick bungalow than in newer construction, and water from a burst pipe in a century-old basement can wick into wood furniture and textiles before anyone realizes the floor is wet. DISS Restoration responds 24/7, documents every item before it leaves your property, and brings your belongings back cleaned, deodorized, and stored safely until your home is ready.

## Why Girard Homes See Elevated Contents Damage

Trumbull County's climate delivers a reliable cycle of freeze-thaw stress from November through March. Pipes in older Girard homes — many built before modern insulation standards — are more vulnerable to bursting inside wall cavities, and when that happens, water spreads horizontally under hardwood floors and into the lower shelves of bookcases, cabinets, and stored belongings before the leak is even visible. Summer brings its own risks: the Mahoning Valley's humidity regularly climbs into ranges where mold can begin colonizing porous materials — upholstery, cardboard boxes, paper documents — within 48 to 72 hours of a water event.

Fire damage compounds the problem. Girard's older residential neighborhoods include a significant share of homes with plaster walls and original woodwork, and those surfaces hold smoke residue and soot in ways that off-gas long after the fire is out. Furniture and textiles in the same rooms absorb those compounds deeply. Leaving affected contents in place while structural work proceeds almost always makes the odor problem worse, not better.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Girard

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory — every item photographed, catalogued by room, and assigned a condition rating before anything is moved. That documentation protects you during the insurance claim process and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

Once inventoried, items are carefully packed out and transported to a climate-controlled facility. Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and smaller collectibles, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing textiles and upholstered furniture, dry-cleaning processes for delicate fabrics, and hand-cleaning for antiques and items with sentimental or monetary value that can't tolerate aggressive methods. Electronics and documents get their own handling protocols — water-damaged paper documents, for instance, may be candidates for freeze-drying to halt further deterioration.

Storage remains climate-controlled throughout the restoration timeline. Items are returned to your Girard home on a scheduled delivery once structural work is complete and the space is safe to receive them.

## Coordinating with Your Insurance Carrier in Girard

Most homeowner policies in Ohio cover contents losses under the same claim as structural damage, but the documentation requirements can be demanding. Carriers want itemized lists with pre-loss condition assessments, and they want to see that cleaning and restoration methods were appropriate for the materials — not just a flat "pack-out and clean" line item.

DISS Restoration's IICRC-certified team produces the kind of scope documentation that adjusters expect: line-by-line inventories, before-and-after photographs, and method justifications tied to industry standards. We communicate directly with your adjuster throughout the process so you aren't playing telephone between the restoration crew and the claims office. If your carrier requires a separate contents claim supplement — which is common when structural and contents losses are large — we can support that process with the documentation already in hand.
