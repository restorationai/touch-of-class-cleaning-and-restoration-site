## Why Canfield Homes See Contents Damage More Than You'd Expect

Mahoning County's housing stock leans heavily on mid-century construction — split-levels, brick ranches, and cape cods with finished basements that were never designed with today's water loads in mind. Canfield's relatively flat terrain means drainage moves slowly after a heavy rain or snowmelt event, and water that pools against a foundation finds its way in. Once moisture reaches a finished basement, it doesn't stay on the floor — it wicks into carpet padding, climbs upholstered furniture legs, and saturates cardboard storage boxes within hours.

Fire losses carry their own contents challenge here. Older homes often have original wood-framed interiors with plaster-and-lath walls that trap smoke residue in ways modern drywall doesn't. Soot particles settle into fabric, seep into wood grain, and leave an odor that persists long after the visible damage is cleaned. Furniture and soft goods that look salvageable at first glance frequently need ultrasonic cleaning or ozone treatment to fully remove embedded smoke compounds.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Canfield

Every contents job starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your home. Each item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for your own peace of mind when items come back.

From there, the process follows the damage type:

- **Pack-out**: Items are carefully wrapped and transported to our climate-controlled facility. Fragile items, electronics, and artwork are packed separately with appropriate cushioning.
- **Cleaning and deodorization**: Depending on the material, we use dry-cleaning sponges, wet cleaning, ultrasonic baths, or hydroxyl generators. Smoke-affected textiles and upholstery go through a separate deodorization cycle before any cleaning — treating odor first prevents it from being driven deeper into fibers.
- **Drying and stabilization**: Water-damaged items that are salvageable are dried in a controlled environment, not in a garage or open warehouse where ambient humidity can re-wet them.
- **Storage**: Items remain in secure, climate-controlled storage until your home is ready to receive them. You're not pressured to take things back before the structure is dry and safe.
- **Coordinated return**: We schedule return delivery around your contractor's timeline and help you document what was restored versus what was a total loss for insurance purposes.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding FSRT (Fire and Smoke Restoration) and WRT (Water Restoration) credentials, the cleaning methods we apply are calibrated to the actual damage — not a one-size approach.

## Canfield Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover contents restoration as part of a covered loss, but the documentation burden falls on you unless someone manages it carefully. We work directly with adjusters, provide itemized inventories with photographs, and distinguish clearly between items that are restorable and items that are legitimate total losses. That distinction matters — insurers don't pay replacement cost on items that could have been cleaned, and they don't pay restoration cost on items that are genuinely beyond saving. Accurate documentation protects you either way.
