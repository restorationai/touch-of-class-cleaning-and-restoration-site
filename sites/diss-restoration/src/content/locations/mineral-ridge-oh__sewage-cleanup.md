## Why Mineral Ridge Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

The glacial till that underlies much of Trumbull County drains poorly. After heavy rain or rapid snowmelt, groundwater pressure builds against buried lateral sewer lines, and aging clay tile pipe, common in homes built through the mid-twentieth century, cracks, offsets, or allows root intrusion that eventually blocks flow entirely. When the blockage is severe enough, the path of least resistance is back through the lowest fixture in the house.

Mineral Ridge's housing stock includes a significant share of homes built between the 1940s and 1970s, many with original cast iron or clay sewer laterals that were never upgraded. These pipes are past their design life. A single severe storm event or a prolonged wet spring can be enough to tip a marginal line into full backup. Septic systems on larger residential lots face their own version of the same problem: saturated drain fields lose absorption capacity, and tank overflow becomes a real risk when the ground cannot accept effluent fast enough.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated type, and the cleanup protocol reflects that. The process is not simply pumping out water and running fans.

First, technicians contain the affected area to prevent cross-contamination from spreading to unaffected rooms. All standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction units capable of handling solid-laden waste. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, certain flooring underlayments, drywall below the flood line, insulation, are removed and bagged for disposal in compliance with Ohio EPA guidelines for Category 3 waste.

Once the space is cleared, every surface that contacted sewage is treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. This is not a single spray-and-wipe step. Technicians apply product, allow appropriate dwell time, and then verify coverage before structural drying begins. Air movers and dehumidifiers are staged to bring affected assemblies to documented dry standards, and moisture readings are logged throughout the drying period. A final sanitization pass closes out the remediation before any reconstruction work begins.

## Working with Insurance on Sewage Claims in Mineral Ridge

Sewage backup coverage is not automatic under a standard homeowner's policy. Many carriers in Ohio require a separate sewage backup endorsement, and whether that endorsement is in place determines how the claim is handled. DISS Restoration documents the loss thoroughly from the first hour on site: photographs of affected materials, moisture readings, waste boundaries, and removed materials are all captured and organized in a format carriers recognize.
