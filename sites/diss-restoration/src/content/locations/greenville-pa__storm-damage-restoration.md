## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Greenville

The first call is about stabilizing the property, not rebuilding it. That means emergency tarping or board-up on any roof or window breach to stop ongoing water intrusion, followed by extraction if rain has already entered the structure. Once the home is secure, we document everything with photos and moisture readings for the insurance claim, then move into structural drying using the IICRC S500 standard as the benchmark for how dry is actually dry, not just dry to the touch. For homes with tree impact damage, we coordinate directly with a structural engineer or roofer when load-bearing members are involved, since wind events here can crack rafters without an obvious exterior sign.
