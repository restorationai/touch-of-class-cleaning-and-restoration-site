## Why Austintown Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Austintown's housing stock tells the story. The township grew rapidly through the 1950s, 1960s, and 1970s, and a large share of the single-family homes built during that era sit on clay-tile or cast-iron lateral lines that were never designed for decades of root pressure and ground movement. When Mahoning County gets the hard freezes typical of a Lake Erie-influenced winter — followed by rapid thaw — soil shifts stress those aging joints. The result is partial collapses or offset connections that let groundwater infiltrate the line and, during heavy rain events, push sewage back through the lowest fixture in the house.

Flat lots common throughout the township also slow surface drainage, which means saturated soil stays wet longer and hydrostatic pressure against foundation walls and floor drains stays elevated well after a storm passes. Homes with older sump systems or no backwater valve are especially vulnerable during those windows.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Austintown

Raw sewage removal is a Category 3 biohazard job — the process has to be thorough, not just fast. The crew arrives with personal protective equipment, containment materials, and extraction equipment rated for solid-laden wastewater. Here is what the work actually looks like:

**Containment and source control.** Before anything else, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to clean spaces. If the backup source is still active, we coordinate with the homeowner on shutting the lateral or engaging a plumber simultaneously.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage and saturated porous materials — carpet, pad, compromised drywall, insulation — are removed and bagged for disposal per Ohio EPA solid waste guidelines. Nothing that has absorbed Category 3 water gets dried in place.

**Structural cleaning and disinfection.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. This step is not optional and not cosmetic; it addresses pathogens including bacteria and viruses present in raw sewage.

**Drying and verification.** IICRC-certified structural drying protocols guide the drying phase. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented — important for insurance claims and for confirming the space is genuinely dry before any reconstruction begins.
