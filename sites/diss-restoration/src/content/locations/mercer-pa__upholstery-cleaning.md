## Why Mercer Furniture Needs a Different Approach

Mercer sits in a humid continental climate zone: cold, damp winters and muggy summers. That swing matters for upholstery. Fabric fibers pull in ambient moisture during humid stretches and dry out during heating season, which is exactly the cycle that lets dust mites, mildew spores, and trapped odors settle deep into cushions and seams. In a lot of Mercer's older homes, furniture also sits near radiators or wood stoves, which bakes odor and oil residue into arm fabric and headrests over time. We see this most in living rooms and dens that double as the main gathering space during the colder months, when furniture gets used harder and aired out less.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fiber before we touch a cushion. Natural fibers like cotton and wool blends behave very differently under heat and moisture than synthetic weaves or leather, and using the wrong method can set a stain permanently or shrink a fabric panel. From there:

- We pretest an inconspicuous area for colorfastness and fiber reaction.
- Fabric upholstery gets a low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded soil without oversaturating the padding underneath, which is important in older Mercer homes where furniture often sits against exterior walls that run cooler and slower to dry.
- Leather upholstery is cleaned with a pH-balanced conditioner-based process, never the steam method, since heat and excess moisture can crack or dull a leather finish.
- We finish with a controlled drying pass so cushions aren't left damp long enough to develop the musty smell that shows up when moisture lingers in dense foam.

Our technicians work under IICRC-informed cleaning standards, which just means the drying targets and agitation methods aren't guesswork, they're calibrated to the material.

## Equipment & Methods We Use

Different jobs call for different tools. Hot water extraction handles most fabric sofas and sectionals well, pulling soil and moisture out together rather than pushing it deeper into the cushion core. For delicate or vintage upholstery, dry compound cleaning avoids saturating fibers that may already be brittle with age. Leather gets cleaned by hand with dedicated conditioning agents rather than any wet extraction tool. We also bring HEPA-filtered equipment for households dealing with pet dander or allergy concerns, which is a common request from Mercer clients with older furnace systems that recirculate more dust than newer forced-air setups.
