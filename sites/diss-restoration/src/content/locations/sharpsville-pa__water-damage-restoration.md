## Why Sharpsville Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Sharpsville's residential housing was built in the mid-20th century, during the steel-era boom that shaped the entire Shenango Valley. Homes from that era commonly have cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines that have been corroding for decades. When those pipes finally fail, they rarely give much warning. Basement flooding is also common here because Mercer County's clay-dominant soil holds water at the surface rather than letting it percolate away. After a heavy rain or a rapid snowmelt, that saturated ground pushes water against foundation walls and through floor drains.

The Shenango River corridor adds another layer of risk. Properties on lower ground near the river can experience water table rise during prolonged wet periods, which forces moisture up through slab floors even when no visible pipe has failed. That kind of intrusion is easy to miss until flooring starts to buckle or a musty odor settles into the lower level of the home.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sharpsville

Every job starts with moisture mapping. Before any equipment is placed, the crew uses thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly where water has traveled, including behind walls, under subfloors, and inside wall cavities. In older Sharpsville homes with plaster-and-lath construction, this step is especially important because plaster can feel dry at the surface while the wooden lath behind it stays saturated for days.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is removed with truck-mounted extraction units. Structural drying follows using industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to move air across wet materials at the rate the IICRC S500 standard requires. Drying logs are recorded daily so there is a clear record for your insurance carrier and so the crew can adjust equipment placement as readings change. Nothing is closed up until moisture readings confirm the structure has returned to normal range.
