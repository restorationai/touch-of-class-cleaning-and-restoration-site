## Why Hermitage Properties See Water Damage Issues

Mercer County's climate delivers a reliable pattern of hard freezes followed by rapid warming, and that cycle is particularly hard on older plumbing. Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s, which make up a large portion of Hermitage's residential neighborhoods, often have supply lines routed through uninsulated exterior wall cavities. When temperatures drop sharply overnight and climb again by afternoon, those pipes are under repeated stress. Crawl spaces are another common vulnerability here. The region's clay-heavy soil drains slowly, and after a heavy rain or snowmelt event, hydrostatic pressure can push groundwater through foundation walls and into crawl spaces before a homeowner notices anything on the floor above.

Basement flooding after storms is also a recurring issue across Hermitage. Sump systems that work fine through a dry stretch can be overwhelmed by a sudden thaw or a multi-day rain event, and when they fail, water spreads across concrete floors and wicks into wall framing and stored materials within hours.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Hermitage

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active source and then mapping exactly where water has traveled. In older Hermitage homes with plaster-and-lath construction, that step matters more than it might in a newer build. Plaster can read dry at the surface while the lath and the framing behind it remain saturated, which means relying on visual inspection alone leads to incomplete drying and eventual mold growth. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls and under flooring before any drying equipment is placed.

Extraction comes next, followed by the setup of commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create the airflow patterns the IICRC S500 standard calls for. Drying logs are recorded at each visit so you have documentation of daily moisture readings, which your insurance adjuster will want to see. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place are removed carefully, with attention to any lead-based paint that may be present in pre-1978 construction. Our team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification, which matters on a lot of the housing stock in this part of Mercer County.
