## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Struthers

We start by identifying the fiber or leather type, since a wool blend and a bonded leather recliner need completely different chemistry. Fabric pieces get a pre-inspection for colorfastness, then a low-moisture pretreatment to break down oils and ground-in soil before extraction. Steam cleaning follows for most synthetic and natural fabric blends, using controlled heat and suction so the padding underneath doesn't stay saturated, which matters in Struthers homes where basement-level family rooms already run humid. Leather pieces get a pH-balanced cleaner and conditioner instead of steam, since heat and excess moisture can crack or dull a leather finish over time. We finish with a grooming pass on pile fabrics and set fans where airflow is limited, which is often the case in older homes with smaller rooms and fewer windows.
