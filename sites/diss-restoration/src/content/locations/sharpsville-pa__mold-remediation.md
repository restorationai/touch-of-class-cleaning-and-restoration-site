## Why Sharpsville Properties See Mold Issues

Mercer County winters are wet and prolonged, and Sharpsville's housing inventory reflects decades of construction from the mid-twentieth century and earlier. Many homes here were built with minimal vapor barriers in the crawl space, and basement walls made of block or poured concrete absorb groundwater seasonally. When that moisture migrates inward and meets warm interior air in late winter or early spring, the relative humidity inside wall cavities climbs fast. Mold can begin colonizing a damp surface within 24 to 48 hours, and in a block-wall basement it often spreads behind paneling or under carpet before the visible signs appear.

The Shenango River corridor also means that properties close to the water table can experience slow, chronic seepage rather than a dramatic flood event.
