## Why Niles Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Needs

The Mahoning Valley's climate is the first factor. Niles and the surrounding Trumbull County area sit close enough to Lake Erie that winter storms can arrive heavy and fast, loading roofs with wet snow and ice that older structures weren't always engineered to handle. A compromised roof in January isn't a problem you can schedule for next week — exposed rafters and interior ceilings will absorb moisture within hours, and if temperatures drop overnight, that moisture freezes and expands inside wall cavities and framing.

The second factor is the housing stock itself. Much of Niles's residential fabric was built in the early-to-mid twentieth century, when the steel and manufacturing economy was booming. Those homes often have wood-framed windows set in aging masonry, slate or aged asphalt rooflines, and interior plaster walls. A broken window in a 1930s brick bungalow doesn't just let in weather — it lets in wind-driven debris that can score plaster surfaces and saturate lathe before a homeowner even realizes how much damage is accumulating.

Fire damage is another common driver. Structure fires in older Niles homes can leave wall sections and roof decking open to the elements immediately after suppression, and the combination of fire hose water and open sky is exactly the environment where mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Niles

When you call (724) 981-1441, the first thing we do is ask about the nature of the opening — fire damage, storm breach, broken glass, or structural failure — because the approach differs.
