## Why Struthers Carpets Accumulate More Than Dirt

The Mahoning Valley's climate is genuinely hard on interior textiles. Struthers averages enough freeze-thaw cycles each winter to keep entryways perpetually damp from late November through early April. That moisture — carried in on boots from driveways and sidewalks — doesn't just wet carpet fibers; it feeds the microbial activity that produces odor and, over time, degrades the backing material. The clay-loam soil common throughout this part of Trumbull and Mahoning County is particularly adhesive when wet, binding to synthetic carpet pile in a way that sandy or silty soils do not.

Older homes — and Struthers has plenty of them, with a significant portion of the housing stock dating to the postwar boom of the late 1940s through the 1960s — often have subfloor construction that holds ambient moisture longer than modern OSB or concrete slab. That trapped moisture beneath the carpet creates conditions where odors persist even after surface cleaning, which is why a process that only addresses the face fibers leaves the problem half-solved.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Struthers

Every job begins with a fiber and backing assessment. Not all carpet responds the same way to hot-water extraction, and in homes with older wool-blend or loop-pile commercial carpet — common in Struthers properties converted from rental use — the wrong temperature or dwell time can cause shrinkage or wicking. Once fiber type is confirmed, we pre-treat high-traffic lanes and stain zones with targeted chemistry matched to the soil load, then extract using truck-mounted equipment capable of reaching temperatures and suction levels that portable units cannot replicate.

For stain removal, the sequence matters: protein-based stains (pet, food) require enzyme pre-treatment before heat is applied, because heat sets protein. Tannin stains from coffee or mud respond differently. Identifying what caused a stain before treating it is the step that separates a clean carpet from a carpet with a faded ghost of the original stain. After extraction, we apply a sanitizing rinse and, where requested, a fiber protectant that extends the interval before the next cleaning is needed.

Commercial carpet cleaning in Struthers — in office suites, retail spaces, or multi-unit properties — follows the same technical logic but is scheduled to minimize disruption, with section-by-section drying that keeps portions of the space usable throughout the process.

## Local Note

Homes in Struthers built during the postwar decades frequently used a thick, dense carpet padding that was standard at the time — often a jute-fiber pad rather than the foam or rubber padding common today. Jute holds moisture aggressively and can retain odor long after the carpet face appears dry. When we encounter this combination, we flag it before cleaning begins, because the drying protocol has to account for the pad's behavior, not just the carpet above it. Homeowners are sometimes surprised that the source of a persistent musty smell is the padding, not the carpet itself — and in some cases, pad replacement is the more cost-effective long-term solution than repeated cleaning.

Truck-mounted hot-water extraction is the workhorse for most residential and commercial jobs.
