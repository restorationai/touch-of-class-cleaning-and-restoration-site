## Why Canfield Homes See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Expect

Mahoning County's climate does a specific kind of damage to HVAC systems. Winters here regularly drive indoor heating systems to run for six or seven months straight, pulling air — and whatever is in that air — through the duct network thousands of times before spring. Canfield's mix of wooded lots and open agricultural land nearby means pollen loads are high in spring and fall, and that pollen settles into return ducts along with pet dander, construction dust from any renovation work, and the fine particulate that drifts in through older window and door seals.

Homes built in the 1950s through the 1970s — a common vintage throughout this part of Mahoning County — frequently have duct systems that were never designed for modern high-MERV filtration. Owners upgrade to better filters, the static pressure climbs, and debris that would have passed through a lower-rated filter instead backs up into the duct lining. The result is a system that looks fine on the outside and runs poorly on the inside, with restricted airflow and air quality that reflects what's coating the duct walls.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Canfield

The work starts with a camera inspection of the main trunk lines and branch runs before anything is disturbed. That footage tells us whether we're dealing with standard dust accumulation, biological growth, or debris from a past renovation — each one calls for a different approach. We use negative-pressure equipment to put the duct system under vacuum while rotating brush heads and compressed-air whips dislodge material from the duct walls, so loosened debris travels toward the collection unit rather than back into your living space.

For homes with older galvanized or internally-lined flex duct, we adjust agitation pressure to avoid damaging duct seams or liner material — a step that matters more here than in newer construction where rigid metal duct is standard. After mechanical cleaning, supply and return registers are wiped down, and if biological contamination was present, an EPA-registered sanitizing agent is applied to duct interior surfaces. Dryer vent cleaning follows the same visit if requested: Canfield's longer heating seasons mean dryers run hard, and lint-restricted vents are a genuine fire risk that's easy to address while the equipment is already on-site.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

The core unit is a truck-mounted or trailer-mounted negative-pressure vacuum system with HEPA filtration — meaning the air exhausted from the collection unit is filtered before it re-enters your home. Rotary brush heads are sized to the duct diameter, and the technician switches heads between trunk lines and branch runs rather than using a single tool throughout. For supply boots and register boxes, hand tools and compressed air are used at close range. Air duct sanitization, when warranted, uses a fogging application that coats interior surfaces without leaving a residue that would restrict airflow.

One pattern that comes up in Canfield-area homes with basement furnaces: the return-air plenum — the large sheet-metal box that feeds air into the blower — is sometimes built directly against the poured-concrete or block foundation wall.
