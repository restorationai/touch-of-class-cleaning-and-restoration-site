## Why Girard Properties Face Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Girard's housing stock leans heavily toward homes built between the 1920s and 1960s — an era of balloon-frame and early platform construction, single-pane windows, and roofs that have often been layered rather than stripped over the years. When fire damages a rafter system or a storm punches through an older roof deck, the underlying structure can be softer and more irregular than what you'd find in a newer subdivision. That means tarps have to be anchored more carefully, and board-up crews need to account for window openings that aren't standard modern dimensions.

The city also sits in Trumbull County, where winters bring repeated freeze-thaw cycles that can widen a damaged opening quickly. Water that enters through an unprotected window or roof breach on a Tuesday night can be frozen into the wall cavity by Wednesday morning, accelerating structural deterioration and setting up conditions for mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of the initial intrusion.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Girard

When a call comes in, the first priority is a rapid site assessment — identifying every compromised opening, not just the obvious ones. Fire damage in particular can leave secondary venting points where heat or suppression water escaped through soffits or utility penetrations. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up, which matters later when your insurance adjuster reviews the scope.

For board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to each opening, secured with structural screws rather than staples or nails alone — a method that holds through wind events and deters opportunistic entry. For roof tarping, we run strapping across the ridge and weight or anchor the perimeter so the tarp stays put under the wind loads that come with Mahoning Valley storm fronts. We use heavy-duty polyethylene rated for extended outdoor exposure, not the lightweight sheeting that can shred in a single windstorm.

All work is documented with timestamped photos and a written scope summary formatted for insurance submission. DISS Restoration carries IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke) certification and is EPA Lead-Safe Certified — relevant on older Girard properties where disturbing window frames or exterior trim during board-up can involve lead-painted surfaces.

## Girard Insurance Coordination

Most homeowners' policies cover emergency protective measures — board-up and tarping are typically classified as mitigation, not repair, which means they fall under the same claim as the underlying loss event. We document the work in a format most major carriers accept, including itemized material and labor breakdowns. If your adjuster needs to inspect before permanent repairs begin, the board-up and tarp we install are designed to stay in place through that inspection window without compromising the evidence of the original damage.
