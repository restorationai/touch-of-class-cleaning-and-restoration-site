## Why Boardman's Building Stock Creates Specific Cleaning Challenges

Boardman sits in Mahoning County, and a significant portion of its residential housing was built between the late 1940s and early 1970s — a construction era that relied heavily on plaster-and-lath walls, older ductwork configurations, and materials that behave differently under renovation than modern assemblies do. When a contractor cuts into a plaster wall for a remodel, the resulting dust is finer and more alkaline than standard drywall dust. It settles deeper into carpet fibers, coats the inside of return-air ducts, and clings to painted trim in ways that a standard mop-and-vacuum pass won't resolve.

Boardman's climate adds another layer. Mahoning Valley winters push moisture indoors, and construction projects that run through late fall or early spring often leave buildings with elevated humidity levels — conditions where construction dust can bind to surfaces rather than staying loose and easy to remove. A final clean performed without attention to surface pH and residual moisture can leave a haze that reappears days after the crew leaves.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Boardman

Post-construction cleaning happens in phases, and skipping steps is how a "clean" building ends up with a film on the windows two weeks after move-in.

The first pass is rough-out: removing bulk debris, pulling protective coverings, clearing trade waste that subcontractors left behind. The second phase is the detail clean — wiping every surface in sequence from ceiling to floor, cleaning inside cabinets and drawers before hardware is installed, addressing window frames and tracks where compound and paint overspray collect, and flushing HVAC registers before the system runs for the first time post-renovation.

The final phase is the polish pass: streak-free glass, cleaned light fixtures, scrubbed grout lines, and a floor finish appropriate to the material — whether that's hardwood, LVP, ceramic tile, or the polished concrete that's become more common in Boardman commercial buildouts. Each surface type gets the chemistry and method it needs rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated space, construction-adjacent mold remediation, or lead-safe cleaning protocols in pre-1978 homes — the team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification and IICRC credentials in mold and fire/smoke, so the work doesn't have to be handed off to a second contractor.

## Local Note: Ductwork and the Postwar Ranch

One thing that comes up repeatedly in Boardman's older ranch-style homes during post-renovation cleaning: the original ductwork in these houses was often sized for gravity or early forced-air systems, with wide, low-profile runs and large return-air grilles positioned low on walls rather than in ceilings.
