## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fabric and fiber check, because a poly-blend sectional and a genuine leather recliner need completely different chemistry and pressure. For fabric pieces, we pre-treat stains and ground-in soil, then use low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded dirt without oversaturating the padding underneath, which matters in Ohio's humid months when slow-drying cushions can start to smell musty within a day or two. Leather gets a pH-balanced cleaner and a conditioning pass so the hide doesn't dry out and crack once winter heat kicks back on. We test an inconspicuous spot first on every piece, check colorfastness, then work section by section so nothing sits wet longer than it needs to.
