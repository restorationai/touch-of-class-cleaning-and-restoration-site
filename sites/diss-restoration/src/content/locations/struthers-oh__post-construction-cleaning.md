## Why Struthers Properties Present Distinct Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

The Mahoning Valley's climate swings — cold, damp winters followed by humid summers — mean that construction dust doesn't just settle; it absorbs moisture and bonds to surfaces if it sits too long. In older Struthers homes where original plaster walls coexist with a newer drywall addition, you can end up with two completely different surface behaviors in the same room. Plaster holds dust differently than drywall, and cleaning solutions that work on one can streak or damage the other.

Struthers also has a meaningful share of homes built before 1978, which means renovation work frequently disturbs lead-containing paint. DISS Restoration holds EPA Lead-Safe Certification, so post-renovation cleaning on those properties follows the specific protocols required to contain and remove lead dust — not just a standard wipe-down. That distinction matters for families moving back in after a kitchen gut or window replacement job.

The area's older sewer and drainage infrastructure can also complicate cleanup when contractors have been working near plumbing penetrations. Debris left in floor drains or utility chases during construction doesn't always make it obvious until water backs up weeks later.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Struthers

A post-construction clean has three distinct phases, and skipping any one of them is what leads to the callbacks contractors dread.

**Rough clean** comes first: bulk debris removal, sweeping and vacuuming construction waste, clearing window tracks and door frames of sticker residue and overspray. This phase is where HEPA-filtered vacuums matter most — standard shop vacs recirculate fine particulate rather than capturing it.

**Detail clean** addresses the surfaces that show: scrubbing grout lines, removing paint splatter from hardware and glass, cleaning inside cabinets and drawers, wiping down every electrical outlet cover and switch plate. In Struthers homes where original woodwork was preserved during a renovation, this phase requires care — aggressive chemical cleaners can strip old varnish finishes that a homeowner specifically asked the contractor to protect.

**Final inspection clean** is a walk-through pass with fresh eyes: light fixtures, window sills, baseboard tops, and HVAC vents. This is also when we verify that any protective film left by contractors on countertops or appliances has been fully removed without leaving adhesive ghost marks.

Because DISS Restoration is an IICRC Certified Firm, our crews understand moisture dynamics — if construction happened during a wet stretch and materials weren't fully dried before close-in, we flag that during the final clean rather than letting it become a mold issue six months later.
