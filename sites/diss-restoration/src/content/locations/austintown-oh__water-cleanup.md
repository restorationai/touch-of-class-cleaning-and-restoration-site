## Why Austintown Properties See Water Damage Issues

Much of Austintown's residential housing stock was built between the 1950s and 1980s, a period when builders used materials and plumbing configurations that simply age out. Galvanized steel supply lines corrode from the inside, cast-iron drain stacks develop hairline cracks, and sump pits in older crawl spaces were often undersized for the volume of groundwater the clay-rich soil pushes toward foundations during spring thaw. When those systems fail, water moves fast through subfloor assemblies and into wall cavities before the surface even looks wet. A moisture meter reading dry at the baseboard can still show saturation six inches into the wall, a pattern common in the split-level and ranch-style homes spread across this part of the township.

Austintown also sits close enough to the Mahoning River corridor that storm events can overwhelm municipal storm drains, backing water into basement floor drains. That kind of intrusion carries a different contamination profile than a clean supply-line break, and the cleanup process needs to reflect it.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Austintown

The first step on any water damage call is containment and documentation.
