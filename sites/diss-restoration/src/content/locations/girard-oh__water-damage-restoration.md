## Why Girard Properties See Water Damage Issues

Girard's housing stock leans heavily toward mid-century construction — brick ranches, Cape Cods, and two-stories built in the postwar decades when basement waterproofing standards were minimal and cast-iron drain lines were the norm. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out over decades, and by the time a section fails, it often does so catastrophically rather than with a slow drip you can catch early. Sump pump failures are another common culprit: Trumbull County's clay-heavy soil doesn't drain quickly, so a heavy spring rain or a rapid snowmelt can overwhelm a pump that's even a few years past its service life.

The Mahoning River corridor and the low-lying areas around Eagle Creek mean that some Girard addresses carry genuine flood risk during high-precipitation events. Homes closer to those drainage corridors may see water intrusion from the ground up — hydrostatic pressure pushing through block foundations — rather than from a burst pipe above grade. That distinction matters because the extraction and drying strategy is different: surface water removal is only the first step when the source is ground saturation.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Girard

Every job starts with moisture mapping. Before a single fan is placed, the crew uses thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly where water has traveled — behind baseboards, under hardwood, inside wall cavities. In Girard's older homes, that often means finding moisture in places that look dry to the eye, including plaster walls that wick water slowly and hold it long after the visible surface feels firm.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment. Structural drying then begins using industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers sized to the cubic footage of the affected space. Drying is monitored against the IICRC S500 standard — daily moisture readings, adjusted equipment placement, and a documented drying log that your insurance adjuster can use directly. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — saturated insulation, swollen OSB, compromised drywall — are removed and disposed of before reconstruction begins.

DISS Restoration holds IICRC certifications in Water Restoration, Applied Structural Drying, and Mold Remediation, which matters in a region where incomplete drying routinely leads to mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours of a loss.

## Local Note

Girard's postwar brick homes frequently have a detail that complicates water damage scoping: the original builders often poured concrete directly against the interior face of the block foundation without a drainage plane.
