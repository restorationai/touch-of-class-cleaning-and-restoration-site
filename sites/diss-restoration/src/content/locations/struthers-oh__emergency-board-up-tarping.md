## Why Struthers Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Risks

Struthers is a compact, densely built community in Mahoning County where a significant portion of the housing stock dates to the mid-20th century or earlier. When a fire damages a roof deck or a windstorm drives a tree limb through a dormer, the underlying structure is often less forgiving than modern construction — gaps in sheathing allow water to travel laterally behind walls, and older mortar joints can admit moisture quickly once the exterior envelope is breached.

The Mahoning Valley's climate adds urgency. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Struthers

When DISS Restoration arrives at a Struthers property, the first priority is a rapid exterior assessment — identifying every point of breach before any material goes up. A tarp placed over the visible hole while water is still entering through a secondary gap at a soffit or gable vent accomplishes little. The crew walks the perimeter, checks the attic access if it is safe to do so, and maps every compromised area before staging materials.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and anchored with lumber baton systems rather than relying solely on staples or adhesive — a method that holds through wind events and does not create additional puncture points in the roof deck. For fire damage board-up and broken window board-up, we cut OSB panels to fit each opening, secure them with exterior-grade fasteners, and where required, apply edge sealing to limit smoke odor migration into the interior. All work is photographed systematically before, during, and after installation to support insurance documentation.
