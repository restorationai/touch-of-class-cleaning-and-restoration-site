## Why Struthers Homes See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Expect

Much of Struthers's residential housing stock was built between the 1940s and 1970s, an era when duct systems were often oversized for the furnaces they served and insulated with materials that degrade over time. Older galvanized steel ductwork develops small seams and gaps as it expands and contracts through Ohio's wide seasonal temperature swings — gaps that pull in unconditioned air, along with whatever particulates are in the crawl space or basement. The Mahoning Valley's historically industrial air quality adds another layer: fine particulate matter settles into return-air registers and works its way deep into trunk lines over years of operation. Homes near the Mahoning River corridor, where humidity stays elevated through spring and early summer, also face a higher risk of microbial growth inside ductwork that hasn't been cleaned or sanitized recently.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Struthers

The job starts with a camera inspection of the main supply and return trunks so we can see exactly what's inside before any equipment runs. That step matters in Struthers's older homes because we occasionally find deteriorated duct liner, disconnected flex runs in attic spaces, or nesting debris from pests — conditions that change how the cleaning is approached. Once the system is mapped, we use truck-mounted negative-air equipment to place the entire duct system under negative pressure, then work branch by branch with rotary brush agitation to break loose compacted debris and drive it toward the collection point rather than into the living space. Supply registers, return grilles, and the air handler cabinet are cleaned as part of the same visit. For homes where microbial growth is visible or suspected — common after a basement flood or a long period with a humidifier running unchecked — we apply an EPA-registered sanitizing agent to interior duct surfaces. Dryer vent cleaning is available as an add-on and is worth scheduling at the same time, since lint-clogged dryer vents are a genuine fire risk that most homeowners in the area underestimate.

## Local Note

Struthers's mid-century housing stock frequently has original return-air chases built directly into wall cavities rather than using dedicated sheet-metal ductwork — a construction method that was common in the region but creates a specific challenge for duct cleaning. These panned-joist or cavity returns can't be brushed the same way a metal duct can, and they're prone to pulling in insulation fragments and drywall dust from the wall cavity itself.
