## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, cotton blends, synthetic microfiber, and leather all respond differently to heat, moisture, and cleaning agents, and treating them the same way risks shrinkage, watermarking, or cracking. Fabric upholstery gets a pre-treatment for protein or oil-based stains, followed by low-moisture or hot water extraction depending on the material, pulling soil and odor-causing residue out of the padding layer rather than just the surface fibers. Leather pieces are cleaned and conditioned separately to avoid stripping the finish coat that keeps hides from drying out and cracking, a real risk in a house that runs a wood stove all winter. Drying is accelerated with air movers so cushions and frames aren't sitting damp long enough to pick up the same mildew smell we were hired to remove in the first place.
