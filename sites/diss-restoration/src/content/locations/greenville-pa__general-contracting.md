## Why Greenville Renovations Often Turn Into Bigger Projects

Greenville's borough housing stock skews older, with a good number of homes built in the early-to-mid 1900s around the downtown core and the residential streets that fan out from it. Balloon framing, knob-and-tube remnants, plaster walls over wood lath, and cast iron or galvanized plumbing show up regularly once a wall or floor is opened. None of that is a problem on its own, but it changes how a kitchen or bathroom remodel gets priced and scheduled. Mercer County's winters also mean frozen or slow-draining supply lines are common in houses with unheated crawlspaces, and that history sometimes leaves hidden water staining or soft subfloor that only becomes visible mid-renovation.
