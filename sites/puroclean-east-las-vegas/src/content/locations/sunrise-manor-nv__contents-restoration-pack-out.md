## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Sunrise Manor

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation is what supports your insurance claim and ensures nothing is misplaced during transport.

Items that can be safely restored on-site stay on-site when possible; moving belongings unnecessarily adds handling risk. When the structure itself is too compromised — heavy smoke saturation, standing water, or active remediation underway — we pack out to a climate-controlled facility where contents are cleaned, deodorized, and stored until the home is ready to receive them.

Cleaning methods are matched to material type. Electronics go through dry cleaning and corrosion assessment. Upholstered furniture is treated with thermal fogging or ozone in a controlled environment to break down smoke molecules rather than just masking them. Hard goods — ceramics, metals, sealed plastics — are cleaned and dried individually. Textiles are laundered using restoration-grade chemistry that addresses both odor and staining without damaging fibers.

The final step is a documented return: every item is checked back against the original inventory before it re-enters the home.
