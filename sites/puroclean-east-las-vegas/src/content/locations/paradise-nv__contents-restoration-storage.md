## Why Paradise Properties See Elevated Contents Damage

The desert climate that makes Las Vegas livable most of the year creates specific conditions that accelerate damage to personal property once a loss event occurs. Low outdoor humidity sounds like an advantage, but the dry air inside Paradise homes and condos means porous materials — upholstered furniture, wood cabinetry, paper documents, electronics — desiccate and warp quickly after smoke or soot exposure. At the same time, the HVAC systems that run almost continuously to combat summer heat (routinely above 110°F) distribute smoke odor and fine particulate through every room within hours of a fire.

Water losses carry their own local wrinkle. The valley's caliche-heavy soil drains poorly, and slab-on-grade construction dominates the housing stock built between the 1960s and 1990s across Paradise Palms and similar neighborhoods. When a supply line fails under a slab or behind a wall, water has nowhere to go but across finished floors and into the base of furniture and stored items. By the time the source is found, contents on the floor are often already saturated.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Paradise

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and given a preliminary salvageability assessment — this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for your own peace of mind when you're trying to account for belongings during a stressful week.

Salvageable contents are packed out and transported to a secure, climate-controlled facility. In Paradise's summer heat, transport and storage conditions matter: electronics and wood pieces that sit in a hot truck or an unconditioned warehouse can sustain secondary damage before restoration even begins. Items go through the appropriate cleaning method for their material — ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and delicate items, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for soft goods with smoke odor, and hand-cleaning for art, documents, and heirlooms that can't tolerate immersion.

Once your property is dried, repaired, and cleared for re-occupancy, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in their original positions. The inventory created at pack-out serves as the checklist at pack-back.
