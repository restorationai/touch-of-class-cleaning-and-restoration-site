## Our Mold Remediation Process in Whitney

Mold remediation is not the same as mold removal. Scrubbing visible growth with bleach leaves behind dead spores and mycotoxins, and it does nothing about the moisture condition that fed the colony in the first place. The process that actually resolves a mold problem follows a defined sequence.

The work begins with a thorough moisture investigation — thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters across walls, ceilings, and flooring — to map where water has traveled, not just where mold is visible. In Whitney Ranch homes with stucco exteriors, water intrusion at window frames can wick laterally inside the wall for several feet before showing up as surface discoloration.

Once the affected zone is mapped, containment goes up: heavy-gauge poly barriers with negative air pressure maintained by HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps disturbed spores from migrating into unaffected rooms. Contaminated materials — drywall, insulation, baseboards — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Nevada solid waste guidelines. Remaining structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment then runs until moisture readings return to normal baselines for the region, which in Whitney's climate typically means targeting wood moisture content below 16 percent. The job isn't complete until post-remediation air sampling or a visual clearance inspection confirms the work.
