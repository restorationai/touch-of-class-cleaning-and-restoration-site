## Our Emergency Water Removal Process in Spring Valley

Emergency water removal in this area follows a specific sequence because skipping steps creates bigger problems later. The process starts with moisture mapping, using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find water that has migrated beyond the visible wet area. In slab construction, water travels laterally under flooring and can surface several feet from the original leak point. We trace it before we extract it.

Extraction comes next: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water and saturated material from carpet, pad, and hard-surface flooring. Once bulk water is removed and the space is cleaned up, readings are logged on each visit so the extent of remaining moisture is documented rather than estimated. Affected materials that cannot be salvaged in place, such as saturated drywall or insulation, are removed so the structure is ready for the drying and repair work that follows.

## Spring Valley Insurance Coordination

Most sudden water losses, a burst pipe, an appliance supply line failure, an HVAC overflow, fall under the sudden-and-accidental provisions of a standard homeowners or commercial property policy.
