## Why Henderson Homes Generate Contents Damage Calls

Henderson's housing boom over the past two decades produced large, open-concept floor plans with high ceilings, soft-close cabinetry, and premium finishes. That's good for resale value. It also means that when a water loss happens — a failed refrigerator ice-maker line, a pinhole slab leak beneath polished concrete, a burst supply line behind a bathroom wall — the affected area tends to be wide. Open floor plans allow water to travel quickly across large square footages before anyone notices.

The desert climate adds a layer of complexity that's easy to underestimate. Henderson's low ambient humidity can make a surface feel dry to the touch within hours, which sometimes leads homeowners to assume the damage is contained. In reality, contents like upholstered furniture, area rugs, and wood cabinetry can hold moisture internally long after the surface registers dry. Conversely, smoke and soot from a kitchen fire or a wildfire smoke intrusion event — which the Las Vegas Valley has experienced with increasing frequency — penetrates fabric and porous finishes rapidly in low-humidity air, bonding tightly to fibers.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Henderson

The first step on any contents call is a thorough inventory. Every item in the affected area is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before anything is moved. This documentation matters enormously for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

From there, salvageable contents are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled facility for professional cleaning and restoration. Techniques vary by material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and collectibles, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for textiles carrying smoke odor, freeze-drying for water-saturated documents and photographs. Items that cannot be safely restored are documented for your adjuster rather than discarded without record.

Once your home's structure has been dried and repaired, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed back in their original positions — a step called "pack-back" that's easy to overlook when comparing restoration providers but makes a real difference in how quickly a household returns to normal.
