## Why Paradise Properties See Contents Damage Differently

The built environment in Paradise creates specific conditions that affect how contents absorb and hold damage. Condominium towers and mid-rise apartment buildings near Harry Reid International Airport and the Las Vegas Convention Center corridor are often constructed with minimal natural ventilation — HVAC systems recirculate air constantly, which means smoke odor from a kitchen fire or mold spores from a slow leak can saturate upholstered furniture, clothing, and electronics faster than in a single-family home with operable windows.

Paradise Palms, one of the valley's most recognizable mid-century neighborhoods, has a significant stock of homes built in the 1950s and 1960s. Many of those properties still have original cabinetry, terrazzo flooring, and period furnishings that require a more deliberate restoration approach — aggressive heat or chemical cleaning methods that work fine on modern materials can permanently damage vintage finishes. Identifying what a piece is made of before choosing a cleaning protocol is not optional here.

The desert climate adds another layer. With relative humidity often dropping below 10 percent in summer, porous materials like leather, wood, and paper dry unevenly and can crack or warp before a homeowner even realizes the damage is progressing. Fast action — and controlled drying conditions in a climate-managed facility — matters more here than in more temperate cities.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Paradise

Every pack-out begins with a room-by-room inventory. Each item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before anything leaves the property. This documentation serves two purposes: it protects your belongings during transit, and it creates the paper trail your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim accurately.

Items are then sorted by restoration category. Electronics go to a certified electronics cleaning bench. Textiles — curtains, bedding, clothing — go through ozone or thermal treatment depending on the odor source. Hard goods like furniture, art, and kitchenware are cleaned using the method matched to the material, not a one-size approach. Anything that cannot be restored to pre-loss condition is documented for replacement value rather than quietly discarded.

While contents are in our care, the structure is being dried and repaired. When the property is ready, we coordinate the return and reinstallation of every catalogued item — back to the room, and as close to the original layout as the homeowner wants.
