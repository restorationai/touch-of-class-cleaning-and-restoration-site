## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Sunrise Manor

Every sewage job starts with containment — sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to the rest of the home. We photograph and document everything before touching a surface, which matters for insurance claims and for understanding the full scope of Category 3 water intrusion.

From there, the process follows a specific sequence:

- **Extraction and solid waste removal**: Standing sewage and solid matter are removed using truck-mounted extraction units. This is not optional — leaving any Category 3 water behind invalidates the sanitization step.
- **Controlled demolition**: Drywall, flooring, and insulation that have absorbed sewage cannot be dried and reused. We remove affected materials to clean structural lines, bagging and disposing of them per Clark County waste handling requirements.
- **Antimicrobial application**: We treat all exposed structural surfaces — subfloor, framing, concrete — with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. - **Drying and monitoring**: Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings return to baseline for the material type. We log readings at each visit so there's a documented drying record.
- **Post-cleanup verification**: Before reconstruction begins, we confirm that odor and moisture readings are within acceptable ranges. Sewage odor that persists after drying is a sign that contaminated material was missed — we don't close a job until that's resolved.
