## Why Boulder City Homes Create Unique Contents Challenges

Bouldier City's housing stock skews older than most of the Las Vegas Valley. The Historic District and surrounding blocks contain homes built during and shortly after Hoover Dam's construction era, many with original wood cabinetry, period furniture, and architectural details that can't be replaced at a big-box store. When water or fire damage strikes these properties, the contents aren't just household goods — they're often irreplaceable artifacts of a community with genuine historical identity.

The desert climate adds another layer of complexity. Boulder City sits at a higher elevation than the Strip and sees wider temperature swings, including occasional freezing nights in winter. Belongings that absorb moisture during a water loss — upholstered furniture, books, area rugs, wooden antiques — are then subject to rapid drying conditions outdoors that can cause warping, cracking, and secondary damage if they're not moved to a climate-controlled environment quickly. Smoke residue from a fire behaves differently in low-humidity air too: it sets faster into porous surfaces, which means the window for effective textile and upholstery cleaning is shorter here than in humid climates.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Boulder City

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, described, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation matters both for insurance claims and for ensuring nothing is misplaced during the pack-out. Items are sorted into three categories: those that can be restored, those that need professional cleaning or ozone/thermal treatment, and those that are a total loss and need to be documented for your adjuster.

Packed items are transported to a secure, climate-controlled facility where cleaning and restoration work takes place away from the active job site. Depending on the loss type, that may involve ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, dry-cleaning or ozone treatment for textiles, document drying and digitization for papers and photos, and furniture refinishing for wood pieces. Once the structure is cleared and repaired, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed according to the original room inventory.

For Del Prado and Lake Mountain Estates properties, where homes often have larger footprints and more square footage of stored contents, the inventory phase can take a full day. We don't rush it — an incomplete inventory at the start creates disputes at the end.
