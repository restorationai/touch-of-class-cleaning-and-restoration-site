## Our Basement Flood Cleanup Process in Paradise

The first step on any flooded below-grade space is a moisture mapping survey — not just mopping up what's visible. Using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters, we identify water migration behind walls, under flooring, and inside concrete block cavities before any drying equipment is placed. In Paradise's climate, surface readings can be misleading: the top inch of drywall may read near-dry while the bottom plate and framing behind it remain saturated.

Once the scope is mapped, truck-mounted extraction removes standing water, followed by placement of commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the square footage and material types in the space. Concrete slab and block wall assemblies common in Paradise's mid-century construction require longer drying cycles than wood-framed walls — drying goals are set to material-specific standards rather than a fixed number of days.

Throughout the dry-out, daily moisture readings document progress and drive decisions about when materials can be preserved versus when removal is necessary. That documentation also becomes the basis for your insurance claim file.
