## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Henderson

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. A trained technician arrives, evaluates the affected area, and determines the extent of contamination before any work begins. We identify all porous and semi-porous materials that have been compromised — flooring, drywall, insulation, cabinetry — and document everything for insurance purposes.

Contaminated materials are removed, double-bagged, and transported for proper disposal in accordance with Nevada and Clark County regulations governing biohazardous waste. We do not cut corners on disposal: sharps, needles, and infectious materials require specific handling protocols that differ from standard construction debris, and our technicians are trained to manage that chain of custody correctly.

After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants and EPA-registered antimicrobials. In Henderson's climate, we pay particular attention to HVAC intake vents and any crawl spaces or utility chases where odor-causing compounds can settle. A final ATP (adenosine triphosphate) surface test verifies that biological contamination has been reduced to safe levels before we close out the job.
