## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in North Las Vegas

Pack-out is not just moving your things — it's a documented, systematic process that protects both your belongings and your insurance claim.

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition status before anything leaves your home. This documentation matters: insurance adjusters expect it, and it protects you if a dispute arises over pre-loss value.

Items are then sorted into three categories: salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with specialized restoration (electronics, documents, artwork, textiles), and non-salvageable. Once your home is dried, repaired, and cleared, contents are returned in the same documented order they left — matched back to the inventory so nothing is misplaced.
