## Why Whitney Properties See Contents Restoration Challenges

Whitney sits in the 89122 ZIP code, where much of the housing stock was built during the suburban expansion of the 1980s and 1990s. The desert climate creates a paradox for contents recovery. Low ambient humidity means surface moisture evaporates quickly, which can make water damage look less severe than it is. Smoke from structure fires behaves differently here too: dry desert air carries fine soot particles deeper into porous materials like drapes, clothing, and upholstery before they settle.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Whitney

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything leaves your home.
