## Our Mold Remediation Process in Spring Valley

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — moisture meters, thermal imaging, and air sampling to map where contamination actually exists rather than where it's visible. That matters in Spring Valley because surface mold is often a symptom; the source is usually a slow leak behind a cabinet, a failing HVAC condensate pan, or a cracked supply line inside a wall.

Once the scope is confirmed, the affected area is sealed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered containment barriers. This prevents spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal — a step that's especially important in the open floor plans typical of 1990s-era Spring Valley tract homes, where a kitchen mold event can quickly cross into an adjacent living area without proper containment.

Physical removal follows: affected drywall, insulation, and porous materials are bagged and disposed of per Nevada solid waste guidelines. Structural surfaces are then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent and dried to industry-standard moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We don't close a job until clearance readings confirm the space is within normal parameters.
