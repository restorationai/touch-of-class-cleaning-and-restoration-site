## The Commercial Fire Restoration Process in Henderson

Commercial fire restoration in Henderson follows a structured sequence, but the specifics depend on what your building is made of, how your HVAC is configured, and what regulatory sign-offs your occupancy type requires before you can reopen.

The process typically begins with a thorough scope assessment — documenting affected areas with moisture readings, thermal imaging, and air quality sampling where smoke has migrated. For retail or food-service occupancies, this documentation also feeds directly into the Clark County Building Department permit process, which governs structural repairs and any mechanical work touched during restoration.

Content pack-out comes next for commercial spaces: equipment, inventory, and fixtures that can be salvaged are inventoried, catalogued, and moved to a controlled environment for cleaning. Structural drying and smoke remediation run in parallel — HEPA air scrubbers, hydroxyl generators, and thermal fogging address airborne particulates and embedded odor simultaneously rather than sequentially, which compresses your timeline.

Final clearance for commercial occupancies in Henderson often requires a re-inspection by the building department or fire marshal depending on the scope of structural repair. We coordinate that scheduling as part of the close-out process so you're not chasing inspectors while your space sits idle.
