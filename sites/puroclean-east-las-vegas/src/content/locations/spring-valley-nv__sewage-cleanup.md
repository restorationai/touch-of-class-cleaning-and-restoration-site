## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Spring Valley

The first thing we do on arrival is assess the contamination category and containment boundary — sewage is always treated as Category 3, meaning no porous material that absorbed it can simply be dried and left in place. Here's how the process typically unfolds:

**1. Extraction and removal.** Truck-mounted wet vacuums pull standing sewage from hard surfaces. Saturated drywall, insulation, and flooring that tested positive for contamination are removed and bagged for disposal per Clark County solid waste guidelines.

**2. Cleaning before disinfection.** EPA-registered disinfectants don't work through organic matter — surfaces must be physically cleaned first. We scrub framing, concrete slab, and any remaining fixed materials before applying disinfectant.

**3. Disinfection and deodorization.** We apply EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants to all affected surfaces, including wall cavities opened during demo. Thermal fogging addresses residual odor in the air column.

**4. Structural drying.** After sanitization, we deploy commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the square footage. Las Vegas's low ambient humidity actually accelerates drying times compared to coastal markets, but the slab-on-grade construction common in Spring Valley means we monitor moisture in the concrete itself, not just the walls.

**5. Clearance documentation.** We provide written moisture readings and a scope of work summary for your insurance adjuster and, if applicable, your HOA property manager.
