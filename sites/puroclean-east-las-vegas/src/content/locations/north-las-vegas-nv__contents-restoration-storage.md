## Our Contents Restoration Process in North Las Vegas

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, logged by category and condition, and assigned a salvageability assessment. This documentation matters for two reasons: it protects your insurance claim, and it gives you a clear record if a dispute arises later about pre-loss condition.

Salvageable contents are packed out and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility — critical in a city where ambient temperatures regularly exceed 110°F in summer. Storing damaged goods in a non-climate-controlled space, even temporarily, can cause secondary damage that voids any restoration work done before storage. Items go through the appropriate cleaning method for their material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and non-porous items, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for textiles and soft goods with embedded smoke odor, and specialized document drying for papers and photographs.

Once the structure is cleared and rebuilt, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed according to your original room layout. Nothing is returned until it passes a final inspection.
