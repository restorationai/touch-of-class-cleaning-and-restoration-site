## Our Commercial Fire Restoration Process in Sunrise Manor

Every commercial fire job starts with a systematic scope — not an estimate based on what's visible, but a full assessment of what smoke, heat, and suppression water have reached. Fire damage in a commercial building is rarely contained to the room of origin. Smoke travels through plenum spaces above drop ceilings, HVAC returns carry soot to unaffected floors, and firefighting water migrates into subfloor assemblies.

Once the scope is documented, the process moves through defined phases:

- **Stabilization and board-up**: Securing the structure against weather and unauthorized entry, critical in Sunrise Manor's high-wind corridor where afternoon gusts can push rain or debris into an open structure.
- **Soot and residue removal**: Dry-cleaning methods first on all surfaces, followed by wet cleaning where appropriate. HVAC systems are addressed as a separate scope — not an afterthought.
- **Odor neutralization**: Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation for embedded smoke odor in concrete and masonry. In larger commercial spaces, ozone treatment may be used in unoccupied phases.
- **Structural drying**: Suppression water left behind by fire departments creates secondary damage. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin meters identifies hidden saturation before reconstruction begins.
- **Documentation for reconstruction**: Every phase is photographed and logged to support the insurance claim and any Clark County permitting required for structural repairs.
