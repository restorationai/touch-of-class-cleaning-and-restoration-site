## Why Whitney Properties See Water Damage Issues

The housing stock along the East Tropicana corridor and the neighborhoods spreading south toward Boulder Highway includes a wide mix of build eras — tract homes from the 1970s and 1980s sit alongside newer construction from the 2000s boom. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Whitney

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls, under tile, and across the concrete slab — because in caliche-heavy soil, water that cannot drain down will wick laterally under a foundation and reappear in a room twenty feet from the original source.

Once the full extent is documented, standing water is removed with truck-mounted extraction units. Structural drying follows using industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to create a directed airflow across wet materials. Drying targets are set using the IICRC S500 standard as the benchmark, with daily moisture readings logged to verify progress. In Whitney's low-humidity environment, ambient conditions actually accelerate evaporation — but that same dry air can mask residual moisture trapped in stucco and the concrete block walls common in this part of the valley, so equipment stays in place until readings confirm the structure has returned to pre-loss moisture levels, not just until surfaces feel dry to the touch.

Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — saturated drywall, swollen baseboards, soaked carpet pad — are removed and documented with photographs before disposal, which your insurance adjuster will need for the claim.
