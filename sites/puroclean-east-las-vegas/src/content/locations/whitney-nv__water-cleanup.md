## Why Whitney Properties See Water Cleanup Issues

The soil profile across Whitney is dominated by caliche — a calcium carbonate hardpan that doesn't drain. When water escapes from a plumbing system or an appliance, it pools rather than percolates. Under a slab foundation, that trapped moisture has only one direction to travel: laterally, following the path of least resistance through expansion joints and into interior spaces. Homes built along Boulder Highway and deeper into Winterwood during the 1970s and 1980s often have copper supply lines that have reached or passed their service life. The Las Vegas Valley's low ambient humidity is a double-edged factor. ## Our Water Cleanup Process in Whitney

The first priority on any water cleanup call is stopping ongoing damage. That means identifying the moisture source, isolating it if possible, and beginning extraction before secondary migration makes the affected area larger. We use truck-mounted and portable extraction units to pull standing water from hard floors and saturated carpet, then deploy thermal imaging to map moisture that has traveled behind baseboards, under flooring, or into wall cavities — areas that look fine visually but read elevated on a moisture meter.

Once extraction is complete, the drying phase begins. In Whitney's low-humidity environment, refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers work efficiently, but the drying plan still has to account for the specific materials in the structure. Concrete slab floors absorb water slowly and release it slowly; drying a slab-on-grade home in Winterwood takes longer than drying a similar loss in a wood-frame structure with a crawl space. We monitor moisture readings daily and adjust equipment placement until readings return to pre-loss baselines, not just until the surface feels dry.

All affected materials are documented with photographs and moisture readings before, during, and after drying. That documentation matters for insurance claims and for confirming the structure is genuinely dry before any reconstruction begins.
