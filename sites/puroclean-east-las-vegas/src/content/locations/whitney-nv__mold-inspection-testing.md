## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Whitney

A mold inspection is not a visual walk-through with a flashlight. The process begins with a structured assessment of the property's moisture history: where water has entered, where it has been retained, and what the current moisture readings look like inside wall cavities, under flooring, and in attic spaces. Thermal imaging helps identify temperature differentials that indicate hidden moisture without requiring destructive opening of walls.

Air sampling for mold spore testing is collected both indoors and outdoors simultaneously. The outdoor sample establishes a baseline for the ambient spore population — critical in a desert environment where certain mold genera are naturally present in the soil and wind. Indoor samples are then compared against that baseline to identify elevated concentrations or species that suggest active interior growth rather than normal infiltration from outside. Samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results typically return within a few business days. The written report identifies the species detected, the spore counts by room, and the likely moisture source driving any elevated readings.
