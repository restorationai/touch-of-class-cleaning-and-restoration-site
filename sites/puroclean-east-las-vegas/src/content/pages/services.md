## Emergency restoration services

Water damage moves fast. Within 24 to 48 hours of a leak or flood, materials that look dry on the surface can already be supporting mold growth underneath. The water damage mitigation process involves extracting standing water, mapping moisture with thermal imaging and pin meters, and drying structural assemblies to documented targets — not just until things feel dry to the touch. Fire and smoke damage requires a different approach entirely: soot chemistry varies by what burned, and improper cleaning can permanently set staining into drywall, cabinetry, and soft goods. Biohazard and trauma scene cleanup follows strict handling and disposal protocols to protect occupants and workers alike. When a monsoon storm pushes water through a compromised roof or floods a ground-floor unit, storm damage response combines elements of all of the above. Each of these services has its own process, its own equipment, and its own page — use the links below to go deeper on whichever situation matches yours.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage from spreading. Reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, insulation, framing — have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins. PuroClean of East Las Vegas handles post-damage reconstruction directly, which means no handoff to a separate general contractor, no gap in accountability, and no delay waiting for a second company to schedule a walkthrough. The scope can range from patching a single section of drywall to a full room rebuild after a kitchen fire or a slab-leak repair that opened up multiple floors.

## Specialty services

Not every job fits neatly into an emergency category. Mold inspections and remediation address colonies that may have been growing quietly behind walls long before a visible stain appeared. Odor removal — whether from smoke, pets, or sewage backup — often requires hydroxyl or ozone treatment after surface cleaning, because odor molecules embed in porous materials that look clean. Board-up and tarping services protect a structure immediately after a fire, break-in, or storm event before full restoration can begin. If you're unsure whether your situation falls under one of these categories, a phone call is the fastest way to find out.
