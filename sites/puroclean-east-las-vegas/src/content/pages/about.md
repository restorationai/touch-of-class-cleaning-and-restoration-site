PuroClean of East Las Vegas is a restoration contractor serving the east side of the valley and the broader Las Vegas metro. The company was built around a straightforward premise: when water soaks through drywall at 2 a.m. or smoke settles into every surface after a kitchen fire, homeowners need someone who shows up prepared — not someone who sends a brochure. That's the work this team does.

## What we do

Most days look like this: a call comes in from a homeowner who just found standing water in a finished basement, or a property manager dealing with a sewage backup in a multi-unit building. The crew assesses the damage, documents everything for the insurance claim, sets up drying equipment or containment, and then stays with the job until the structure is dry, clean, and ready for repairs. A significant part of this work runs through insurance carriers — we handle the documentation, the moisture readings, and the billing communication so the homeowner isn't left translating between their adjuster and a contractor. The emotional weight of a loss like this is real, and the job is partly about moving fast enough that people can start to see a path forward.

## Our certifications and licensure

Restoration work sits at the intersection of construction, environmental safety, and insurance — which is why credentials in this industry carry real meaning rather than just looking good on a website. The IICRC (Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification) sets the technical standards that govern how water damage drying, mold remediation, and fire restoration are actually performed — things like acceptable moisture content before a structure can be closed up, or the air-exchange rates required during mold containment. Working to those standards protects both the property and the people living in it. For homes built before 1978, lead-containing paint is a genuine concern during any demolition or structural work; EPA Lead-Safe certification is the federal requirement that governs how that material is handled. These aren't optional credentials — they're the floor for doing the work responsibly.

## Where we work

The team covers Las Vegas and the surrounding communities across the valley — Henderson, North Las Vegas, Boulder City, Summerlin, and the unincorporated areas of Clark County. The Mojave Desert climate shapes the damage patterns here in specific ways: the soil doesn't absorb water the way it does in wetter regions, so flash floods move fast and push water into structures before most people have time to react. Older housing stock in the east valley — built during the postwar boom and again during the 1980s and 90s growth surge — often has aging plumbing and HVAC systems that fail quietly until the damage is already significant. Knowing the area means knowing what to look for.
