When water is soaking through your drywall, smoke smell is locked into every surface, or you've spotted the dark bloom of mold behind a cabinet, you need someone who can assess the damage and start work fast — not schedule you for next week. PuroClean of East Las Vegas handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, storm damage, biohazard cleanup, and reconstruction for homeowners and property managers across the Las Vegas Valley.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — A burst pipe, an overflowing appliance, or a roof leak after a monsoon storm can saturate flooring, framing, and insulation within hours. We extract standing water, dry structural materials with commercial-grade equipment, and monitor moisture levels until readings confirm the space is dry.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Even a small kitchen fire leaves behind acidic soot that continues etching surfaces long after the flames are out. We remove char, neutralize odor at the molecular level, and coordinate the full scope of repairs so you're not managing a dozen separate contractors.

**Mold remediation** — In the desert heat, a slow leak inside a wall can produce visible mold colonization in as little as 24 to 48 hours. We contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces to address the growth — not just paint over it.

**Biohazard and trauma cleanup** — Some situations require more than a mop and bleach. Our team handles sewage backups, unattended deaths, and other biohazard scenarios with proper containment, disposal, and decontamination protocols.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Restoration work sits at an uncomfortable intersection: the damage is urgent, the process is technical, and the insurance paperwork is exhausting when you're already stressed. A few things that matter when you're choosing who to call:

**Insurance billing** — We document the loss thoroughly — photographs, moisture readings, written scope — and work directly with most major insurance carriers. You shouldn't have to front the full cost of an emergency out of pocket and wait for reimbursement.

**Honest scope, no upselling** — Las Vegas has no shortage of contractors willing to tear out more than necessary to run up a claim. We assess what's actually wet, what's actually compromised, and we dry in place when drying in place is the right call.

**Full reconstruction capability** — Remediation that stops at demo leaves you with an open wall and a second contractor to find. We carry the project from initial damage assessment through finished repairs, which means one point of contact and a faster path back to normal.

**Local knowledge** — The Las Vegas Valley has its own damage patterns: monsoon season flash flooding, hard water that accelerates pipe corrosion, stucco exteriors that can hide moisture intrusion for months. That context shapes how we approach every job.
