RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning is based in Corona and dispatches around the clock — no answering service, no next-morning callback. Whether you've got a burst pipe at midnight or a furnace that quit on a cold January morning, the same team that handles routine maintenance is available to respond. Coverage spans a wide stretch of the Inland Empire and surrounding communities, so chances are good your city is already on the map.

## Where we work

Corona sits at the western edge of Riverside County, right where the 91 and 15 freeways intersect — a location that puts a large portion of Southern California's Inland Empire within practical reach. From that hub, the team works across Riverside County and into the neighboring communities of San Bernardino County to the north and east, and the foothill and valley cities to the west toward the Los Angeles County line. The region's mix of housing — tract homes from the 1970s and '80s, newer master-planned developments, and older ranch-style properties on larger lots — means the work varies considerably from one neighborhood to the next, and the crew is familiar with all of it.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Corona — Norco, Eastvale, Mira Loma, and Chino Hills — are typically the fastest to reach given proximity and freeway access. A bit farther out, the team regularly serves Riverside, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, Fontana, and Moreno Valley. Beyond that ring, coverage extends toward Temecula and Murrieta to the south, Redlands and San Bernardino to the east, and Pomona and the San Gabriel Valley cities to the west.

Traffic on the 91 and 15 corridors can be heavy during peak commute hours, so arrival order follows geography and current road conditions — cities closer to Corona generally see a crew sooner. For urgent situations, dispatchers will route accordingly and keep you updated.
