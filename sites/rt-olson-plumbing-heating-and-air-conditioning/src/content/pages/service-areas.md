RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning is based in Corona and covers a wide stretch of the Inland Empire and surrounding Southern California communities. Whether you're dealing with a burst pipe at midnight or a failing furnace on a cold January morning, the team is available around the clock — call (951) 344-5596 and a dispatcher will get you scheduled and a technician headed your way.

## Where we work

Corona sits near the western edge of Riverside County, making it a natural hub for reaching communities across the Inland Empire as well as portions of Orange County and San Bernardino County to the north. The I-15, SR-91, and I-215 corridors connect the shop to a broad band of cities — from the foothills communities east of the 15 all the way to the denser suburbs pressing up against the Orange County line. Inland valleys, hillside neighborhoods, and master-planned communities built across several decades of Riverside County growth all fall within regular service territory.

## Coverage by distance

The closest calls — cities immediately surrounding Corona — typically see the fastest arrivals. Communities like Norco, Eastvale, Mira Loma, and Jurupa Valley are a short drive under most traffic conditions. A bit farther out, Riverside, Ontario, Rancho Cucamonga, and Chino Hills are well within the regular service area, as are communities along the SR-91 toward Anaheim Hills and Yorba Linda in Orange County.

For locations toward the outer edge of coverage — think the higher-elevation communities in the San Bernardino Mountains, or cities pushing toward the Coachella Valley — travel time naturally increases, especially during peak commute hours when the 91 and 15 can back up significantly. Scheduling for those areas may shift depending on crew availability and time of day, but the team will be straightforward with you about estimated arrival windows when you call.
