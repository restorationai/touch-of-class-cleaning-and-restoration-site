RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning has been serving the Inland Empire since 2014. The company was built around a straightforward idea: homeowners dealing with a plumbing failure or a heating system that quit in the middle of a January cold snap deserve a crew that shows up prepared, not one that's still figuring out the job on the driveway. Over the past decade, that commitment has shaped everything from how calls are answered to how technicians are trained.

## What we do

On any given day, the work looks like this: a water heater that failed overnight, a slab leak that's been quietly warping hardwood floors for weeks, an air conditioner that stopped cooling right when the forecast hit triple digits. These aren't abstract service categories — they're the moments that disrupt a household and send people searching for someone they can trust at an inconvenient hour. Because emergencies don't follow business hours, RT Olson operates around the clock, every day of the year. When you call at 2 a.m., a real person answers and a technician gets dispatched — not a voicemail box and a callback promise for the morning.

For homeowners navigating an insurance claim after a covered loss, the team documents the damage, communicates directly with adjusters, and works to keep the process from becoming a second problem on top of the first.

## Our certifications and licensure

RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning holds California Contractor's License #997337. A state contractor's license isn't a formality — it means the company has met California's requirements for financial responsibility and trade competency, and that there's a formal accountability structure if something goes wrong. For homeowners, it's the difference between hiring a contractor and hiring a contractor who can be held to a legal standard.

When you're comparing bids or vetting a company after a loss, license verification takes about 30 seconds on the CSLB website. We'd encourage it.

## Where we work

The company is based in Corona, which sits at the western edge of Riverside County where the 91 and 15 freeways converge. That location puts the team within a reasonable drive of most of the Inland Empire — Riverside, Norco, Eastvale, Chino Hills, Jurupa Valley, and the communities stretching toward the San Bernardino Valley. The region's housing stock is a mix of tract homes built during the 1980s and 1990s boom years and newer construction from the 2000s forward, with older pockets in the historic cores of Riverside and Corona itself. Older galvanized supply lines, original HVAC equipment, and slab foundations over expansive clay soils are common enough that the team encounters them regularly. The Inland Empire's climate — hot, dry summers and occasional hard freezes in the foothills — puts real seasonal stress on both plumbing and HVAC systems, which is a big part of why the phone rings year-round.

## Our certifications and licensure

RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning holds California Contractors License #997337. A state license is the baseline legal requirement to pull permits and perform work on residential and commercial systems in California — it means the work is inspectable and that the company carries the required bonding and insurance.
