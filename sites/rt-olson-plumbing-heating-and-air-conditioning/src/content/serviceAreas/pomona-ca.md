### Drain cleaning and sewer service

Root intrusion is a persistent problem in Pomona's established residential blocks, where mature trees and aging clay or cast-iron sewer laterals share the same soil. Slow drains and recurring backups often point to root infiltration or pipe belly rather than a simple clog. We clear blockages and can camera-inspect the line so you know what you're actually dealing with before committing to a repair.

### Water heater repair and replacement

Pomona's hard water, typical of the Inland Empire, accelerates sediment buildup inside tank water heaters. If your unit is rumbling, producing lukewarm water, or leaking from the base, those are signs the tank is near the end. We service and replace both tank and tankless units and can advise on sizing for the household demand.

### Leak detection

Not every leak announces itself. Hidden leaks behind drywall or under slabs can run for weeks before a homeowner notices the damage. We use non-invasive detection methods to locate the source before opening walls or cutting concrete, which limits the scope of repair and the disruption to your home.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Pomona

Much of Pomona's residential housing was built between the 1940s and the 1970s, with a significant share of the single-family stock sitting on slab-on-grade foundations. Homes from that era commonly have galvanized steel supply lines that have corroded from the inside out, cast-iron drain stacks, and original copper branch lines that are now well past their design life. Homes built before the mid-1970s may also contain asbestos-containing materials in floor tiles, pipe insulation, or duct wrap, a factor that affects how mechanical and plumbing repairs are scoped. Pomona's soils include expansive clay in portions of the city, which can shift seasonally and stress slab penetrations. Permits for plumbing and mechanical work are issued through the City of Pomona's Building and Safety Division; most water heater replacements and HVAC changeouts require a permit and inspection under California's current codes.
