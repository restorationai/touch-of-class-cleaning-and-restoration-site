## Restoration emergencies common in Eastvale

Eastvale sits in the western Riverside County portion of the Inland Empire, and the conditions here create a predictable set of plumbing and HVAC problems. The Santa Ana winds that sweep through the region in fall and early winter drop indoor humidity sharply, which can cause supply lines and shutoff valves that haven't moved in years to crack when someone finally turns them.

Because most of Eastvale's residential development happened after 2000, the housing stock is dominated by large two-story production homes on slab foundations. Slab construction means no crawlspace buffer, when a supply line or drain connection fails under the concrete, the water has nowhere to go except into the slab and up through flooring. Homeowners often notice a warm spot underfoot or an unexplained spike on their Inland Empire Utilities Agency water bill before any visible damage appears.
