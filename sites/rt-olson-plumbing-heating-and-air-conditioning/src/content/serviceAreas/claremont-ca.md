## Restoration emergencies common in Claremont

Claremont sits at the foot of the San Gabriel Mountains, and that geography shapes the plumbing and HVAC problems that come up here. Summer heat in the Inland Valley routinely pushes air conditioning systems past their limits, a unit that coasted through spring can fail hard in July. The city's housing stock skews older, with a meaningful share of mid-century and early postwar construction that still carries galvanized steel supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks.
