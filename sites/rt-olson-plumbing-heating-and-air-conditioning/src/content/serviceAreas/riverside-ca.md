## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Riverside

Riverside's housing stock spans a wide range of eras. The city's older core neighborhoods contain homes built in the 1920s through 1950s, typically on raised wood-frame foundations with plaster walls and original galvanized or early copper supply lines. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, restricting flow and eventually failing at fittings. Homes of this age may also contain asbestos-containing insulation or texture materials and lead-based paint — factors that affect how interior work is scoped and handled.

Slab foundations concentrate plumbing failures at the point where supply lines pass through the concrete, and expansive clay soils common across the Inland Valley can shift slabs seasonally, stressing those penetrations.
