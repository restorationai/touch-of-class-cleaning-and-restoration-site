## Restoration emergencies common in Bloomington

Bloomington sits in the western San Bernardino Valley, where a combination of factors drives a predictable set of plumbing and HVAC emergencies. Summers here routinely push past 100°F, and that heat load punishes aging air conditioning equipment, capacitors fail, refrigerant lines develop micro-leaks, and condensate drain pans overflow into ceilings and walls when units are pushed beyond their service life. Winter nights can drop into the upper 20s, which is cold enough to freeze exposed supply lines in uninsulated garage walls or exterior hose bibs, a common call in the area's older ranch-style homes.

The region also sits on expansive clay soils that swell when wet and shrink during dry spells. That ground movement stresses slab foundations and the drain lines embedded in them, making slab leaks a recurring problem in Bloomington neighborhoods. A warm spot on a tile floor or a water bill that climbs without explanation are the two most common signals that a pressurized line beneath the slab has developed a pinhole. Left alone, that slow loss saturates the subgrade and can undermine the slab edge over time.
