### Leak detection
Slab-on-grade construction dominates Ontario, which means a pinhole in a copper supply line under the foundation produces a warm spot underfoot long before it shows up as a visible stain. Pressurized leak detection isolates the break without unnecessary concrete removal.

### HVAC repair and maintenance

### Fixture repair
