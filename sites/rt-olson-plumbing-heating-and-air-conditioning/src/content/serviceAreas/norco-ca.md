### Water heater repair and replacement
Hard water is a fact of life in the Inland Empire. The mineral content in Norco's water supply accelerates sediment buildup inside tank water heaters, which shortens their service life and drives up energy costs. If your water heater is rumbling, lukewarm, or showing rust at the connections, that sediment has likely reached a critical level.

### Leak detection
Slab leaks, pinhole leaks in copper supply lines, and irrigation-system failures are all common in Norco's housing stock. We use electronic listening equipment and thermal imaging to locate leaks without unnecessary demolition.
