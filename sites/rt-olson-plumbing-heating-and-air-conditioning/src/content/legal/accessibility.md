## Our Commitment to Accessibility

RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning is committed to making its website accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We believe that every visitor — whether you're dealing with a burst pipe at midnight or researching HVAC options for a new home — deserves a clear, frustration-free experience on our site.

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**, the internationally recognized standard for digital accessibility. These guidelines describe how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.

## What We've Done to Support Accessibility

Our site is built with screen-reader compatibility in mind. This includes the use of descriptive alternative text for images, logical heading structure, sufficient color contrast, and keyboard-navigable menus and forms. We avoid relying on color alone to convey information, and we aim to keep page layouts clear and consistent so that assistive technologies can interpret them predictably.

Contact forms and scheduling requests are designed to be operable without a mouse, and error messages are written to be descriptive rather than relying on visual cues alone.

## Known Limitations

We are a small, locally operated business, and while we make every reasonable effort to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards, some content on our site may not yet fully conform. We are actively working to identify and address gaps, and we prioritize fixes when an issue is reported by a visitor.

Third-party content — such as embedded maps or external review widgets — may fall outside our direct control, though we choose and configure these tools with accessibility in mind where possible.

## How to Report an Issue

If you encounter a barrier on our website that prevents you from accessing information or completing a task, we want to hear about it. Please contact us directly:

- **Phone:** (951) 344-5596
- **Email:** office@rtoplumbing.com
- **Mail:** 9064 Pulsar Ct. Suite J, Corona, CA 92883

When you reach out, please describe the page you were visiting, the assistive technology or browser you were using (if known), and the nature of the difficulty. We will acknowledge your message promptly and work to address the reported issue within a reasonable timeframe. If the page in question contains information you need urgently — for a service estimate, an emergency call, or scheduling — we're happy to provide that information directly over the phone or by email while we work on a fix.

## Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a one-time checkbox. As we update content, add new pages, or change site features, we review those changes against accessibility standards before they go live. We welcome feedback as an ongoing part of that process — your experience using this site is the most useful signal we have.
