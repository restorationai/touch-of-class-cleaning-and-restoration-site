## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy explains how RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning collects, uses, and protects information you share when you visit rtolsonplumbing.com or contact us directly. It applies to visitors and customers located anywhere, with specific rights noted below for California residents.

## Information We Collect

When you submit a contact form, request an estimate, or call our office, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the issue you're dealing with. We also collect basic, anonymized analytics data (pages visited, general location by region, browser type) through standard website tools. We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral ad networks, or session-recording software.

## How We Use Your Information

The information you share is used for one purpose: helping you. That means responding to your inquiry, scheduling an on-site estimate or service call, preparing documentation for your insurance claim, and following up on work in progress. We do not use your contact details for marketing campaigns or share them with third-party advertisers.

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only circumstance in which we share your details with a third party — such as your insurance carrier or a subcontractor involved in your project — is when you have authorized us to do so, either verbally or through a signed work-authorization form. If coordination with your insurer requires us to transmit documentation on your behalf, we will confirm that with you first.

## Data Retention

We retain customer records, estimates, and project documentation for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the services you requested, satisfy applicable California contractor recordkeeping requirements, and support any warranty or dispute resolution that may arise after project completion. Contact form submissions that do not result in a service relationship are not retained indefinitely.

## Your Rights as a California Resident

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information we hold about them, request deletion of that information, and opt out of any sale of their data. Because we do not sell personal data, the opt-out right has limited practical application here — but the others apply in full. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

## A Note on GDPR

Our services are limited to California. We do not actively market to or solicit customers in the European Union, and we do not anticipate that GDPR applies to our operations. If you are located in the EU and have a privacy concern, please reach out and we will address it in good faith.
