## Why Eastvale Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

Most of Eastvale's housing stock was built after 2000, which sounds like good news for plumbing, but newer construction brings its own set of drainage headaches. Tract homes built quickly during the mid-2000s boom sometimes have undersized drain lines or shallow-grade installations that trap grease, soap scum, and hair faster than older, more generously plumbed homes. Add to that the region's hard water, which is among the most mineral-laden in Southern California, and you get accelerating scale buildup inside PVC and ABS drain lines that narrows the effective opening over time.

Eastvale also sees a higher-than-average number of large, multi-bathroom homes with long horizontal drain runs to the street. The longer the run, the more opportunity for grease and debris to cool and congeal before reaching the main sewer. Kitchen sink clogs and slow shower drains are the most common calls we take in this city, and in many cases, a standard snake only clears the immediate blockage without addressing the buildup coating the pipe walls.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Eastvale

Every drain call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing: is it one fixture or multiple? Is the backup happening at the lowest point in the house? Those answers tell us whether we're dealing with a localized clog or a main line issue that affects the whole property.

For most kitchen and bathroom clogs, we begin with a motorized snake to break up the immediate obstruction. If the line has a history of recurring clogs, or if a camera inspection reveals grease coating or root intrusion, we move to hydro jetting. A hydro jet delivers a high-pressure water stream that scours pipe walls clean rather than just punching a hole through the blockage. It's the difference between clearing a drain and actually restoring its flow capacity. For properties where tree roots are the culprit, rooter service combined with a camera inspection lets us confirm the line is clear before we leave.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

Not every clog needs the same tool. Our service vehicles carry both cable machines and hydro jetting equipment so we can match the method to what the drain actually needs:

- **Cable/snake service**, effective for soft organic clogs close to the drain opening, hair accumulations in shower and tub lines, and clearing a backed-up toilet.
- **Hydro jetting**, the right choice for grease-coated kitchen lines, recurring slow drains, and any line where scale buildup has reduced diameter. The pressurized water also flushes debris all the way to the main rather than pushing it downstream where it can re-accumulate.
- **Video camera inspection**, lets us see exactly what caused the clog and confirm the line is fully clear. Particularly useful before a hydro jet run on older or thinner-walled pipe.
- **Rooter service**, for lines where tree roots have intruded through joints or cracks, mechanical cutting followed by hydro jetting removes both the roots and the debris they've collected.
