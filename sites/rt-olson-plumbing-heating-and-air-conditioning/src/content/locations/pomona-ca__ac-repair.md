## Our AC Repair Process in Pomona

Every repair call starts with a full system diagnostic, not a parts swap. A technician will check refrigerant pressure, inspect the condenser coils (outdoor units in Pomona accumulate dust and debris quickly, especially near the 10 and 60 freeway corridors where particulate levels run higher), test capacitors and contactors, and verify airflow through the supply and return registers. If the problem is a refrigerant leak, we locate the source before recharging, adding refrigerant to a leaking system is a temporary fix that costs you money twice.

For older retrofitted systems common in Pomona's established neighborhoods, we also check duct connections in the attic. ## Local Note: Attic Temperatures and Refrigerant Line Integrity

One thing that comes up repeatedly in Pomona's older retrofitted homes is refrigerant line insulation that has baked brittle over decades in superheated attics. The foam insulation around copper refrigerant lines is designed to prevent heat gain on the suction line, but once it cracks and crumbles, which happens faster in attics that routinely hit extreme temperatures, the system's efficiency drops and the compressor works under added strain. If your home was built before 1980 and the AC was added or replaced without a full attic inspection, it's worth having those lines checked when a technician is already on-site. It's a small thing to catch early and an expensive compressor failure if you don't.
