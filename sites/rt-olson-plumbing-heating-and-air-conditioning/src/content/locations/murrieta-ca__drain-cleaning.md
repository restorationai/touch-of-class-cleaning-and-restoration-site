## Why Murrieta Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

Southwest Riverside County's climate is a significant factor in how drains fail here. Murrieta sits in a warm inland valley where summer temperatures routinely push past 100°F, accelerating grease buildup inside kitchen drain lines. Cooking fats that might stay semi-liquid in a coastal kitchen congeal faster in pipes that heat up during the day and cool sharply at night. That thermal cycling also stresses pipe joints, creating small gaps where fine roots from ornamental trees and drought-tolerant landscaping, both common in Murrieta's planned communities, can work their way in over time.

The city's soil composition adds another layer of complexity. Much of Murrieta sits on expansive clay-heavy ground that shifts seasonally as it wets and dries. That movement can gradually misalign underground drain lines, creating low spots where debris collects and eventually causes recurring clogs. Homes built during the early 2000s construction boom often used ABS plastic drain lines that, while durable, can develop belly sections when the soil beneath them settles unevenly.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Murrieta

Every service call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing, which fixture is affected, how long it's been slow, whether the problem is isolated or showing up at multiple drains. That information helps determine whether the blockage is in a branch line or further down in the main sewer lateral before a single tool touches your plumbing.

For most kitchen sink clogs and shower drain backups, a professional cable auger clears the immediate obstruction. But when the same drain clogs repeatedly, or when a main line is involved, hydro jetting is the more effective solution. A hydro jet sends pressurized water through the line at high enough force to strip grease, mineral scale, and compacted debris from the interior pipe wall, not just dislodge it. The result is a line that flows like new rather than one that's merely passable. For suspected root intrusion or misaligned pipe sections, a camera inspection before and after jetting confirms the line is clear and catches any structural issues that would cause the problem to return.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

Not every drain problem calls for the same tool. The equipment used on a shower drain serving a single bathroom is different from what's needed to clear a grease-packed commercial kitchen line or a root-infiltrated sewer lateral. The approach is matched to the line size, the nature of the blockage, and what a camera inspection reveals:

- **Cable augers** for branch-line clogs in sinks, tubs, and floor drains
- **Hydro jetting** for main lines, grease-heavy kitchen drains, and lines with recurring buildup
- **Rooter service** for root intrusion in sewer laterals
- **Camera inspection** to locate the blockage precisely and confirm the line is fully clear after service

Using the right method the first time reduces the chance you're calling again for the same problem in six months.
