## Why Winchester Properties See Furnace Problems

The Inland Valley climate creates a pattern that catches homeowners off guard: months of heat followed by a short but real heating season. Because furnaces in this region sit idle for so long, components that would otherwise show gradual wear in a colder climate tend to fail abruptly when the system finally kicks on in late fall. Igniter elements crack from thermal shock after months of dormancy. Flame sensors accumulate a fine layer of oxidation that prevents them from confirming a burner is lit, triggering repeated shutoffs. Heat exchangers that were already developing stress fractures go unnoticed through the summer and then crack further under the first sustained heating load of the season.
