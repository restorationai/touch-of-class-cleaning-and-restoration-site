## Why Menifee Properties See Drain Problems

Menifee sits on expansive clay soils that shift seasonally, swelling with winter rains and contracting through the long, dry summers that define Riverside County's inland climate. That movement stresses underground drain lines over time, opening small gaps at pipe joints that tree roots exploit aggressively. Newer subdivisions built throughout the city since the mid-2000s often used PVC lateral lines that are still in good shape, but many older parcels on the city's edges carry cast iron or Orangeburg pipe that has softened and partially collapsed over decades. Either scenario can turn a slow drain into a recurring problem if the underlying cause isn't addressed, not just snaked and forgotten.

Kitchen sink clogs are especially common in Menifee's tract homes, where builder-grade drain configurations sometimes include long horizontal runs with minimal slope. Cooking grease and soap scum accumulate faster in those lines than in a well-pitched pipe, and a plunger or store-bought chemical won't reach the buildup sitting 20 or 30 feet from the sink.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Menifee

Every service call starts with a conversation about what the drain is doing, gurgling, backing up, draining slowly, or completely blocked, because the symptom tells us where to look first. For most kitchen and bathroom clogs, we begin with a professional-grade cable auger that reaches blockages standard hardware-store snakes can't touch. If the clog clears but the line shows signs of grease coating or root intrusion, we move to hydro jetting: a high-pressure water flush that scours the interior pipe wall clean rather than simply punching a hole through the obstruction.

For lines where we suspect structural issues, root damage, pipe belly, or partial collapse, we can run a camera inspection before or after jetting to confirm the pipe's condition. That step matters in Menifee because a line that looks clear after snaking may have a root mass that will reform within weeks if the pipe wall isn't fully addressed. We document what we find and walk you through the footage so you understand exactly what you're dealing with.
