## Why Anaheim Fixtures Wear Out Faster Than You'd Expect

Orange County's water supply blends imported Colorado River water with local groundwater, and the result is consistently high mineral content — hardness levels that accelerate wear on rubber washers, ceramic disc cartridges, and the brass seats inside older faucet bodies. In Anaheim's mid-century neighborhoods, where tract homes built in the 1950s and 1960s still carry original galvanized supply lines behind the walls, that mineral load compounds an already aging system. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, and the rust flakes that break free don't just discolor water — they lodge in aerators and valve seats, turning a simple drip into a fixture that can't be reseated without first flushing the line.

Hot summers push water heater output higher and thermal cycling stresses supply connections at the fixture shutoffs. ## Our Fixture Service Process in Anaheim

Every call starts with a full fixture assessment rather than a parts swap. A running toilet, for example, can fail at the fill valve, the flapper, the flush valve seat, or the float arm — and each failure has a different fix. Replacing only the flapper when the seat is pitted means the toilet runs again within weeks. The same logic applies to faucets: a dripping two-handle kitchen faucet in a home with hard water often needs the seat resurfaced or replaced, not just a new washer.

For shower valve repairs, the cartridge is pulled and inspected before ordering a replacement. Moen, Delta, Kohler, and Price Pfister each use proprietary cartridge designs, and the correct part matters — an approximate fit leads to cross-threading or incomplete shutoff. When a valve body itself is corroded or cracked, RT Olson can handle the full replacement, including the tile access if the valve is behind a finished wall.
