The Inland Valley's water supply runs hard, measurably so. ## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Wildomar

Every service call starts with a diagnosis, not an assumption. When your disposal hums but won't turn, the cause is usually a jammed flywheel, food debris, a broken bone fragment, or a utensil caught between the impeller and the grinding ring. We use the reset button and a hex wrench to free the jam manually before assuming the motor is gone. That distinction matters: a motor replacement or full unit swap costs significantly more than clearing a jam.

If the unit is leaking, we trace it to the source before recommending anything. A leak at the sink flange usually means the mounting assembly has loosened or the plumber's putty has dried out, a repair, not a replacement. A leak from the body of the unit or the dishwasher inlet port often signals internal cracking, which does warrant a new installation.

When installation is the right call, we size the motor to your household's actual usage. A single-occupancy home and a large family kitchen have different demands. We also verify the drain configuration under the sink before we mount anything, adjusting the P-trap arm or drain extension if the pitch isn't right for the new unit's discharge outlet.
