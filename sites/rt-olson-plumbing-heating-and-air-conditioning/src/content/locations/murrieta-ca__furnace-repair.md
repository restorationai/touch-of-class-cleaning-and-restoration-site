## Why Murrieta Homes See Furnace Problems When They Do

Because Murrieta's winters are relatively mild, most furnaces sit dormant from late spring through early fall. When a unit fires up for the first time in October or November, months of inactivity can reveal problems that weren't obvious in spring: cracked heat exchangers that expanded and contracted through summer heat, dirty flame sensors coated with dust from a dry Inland Southern California summer, or ignitors that have simply reached the end of their service life. Homes built during Murrieta's rapid growth period in the late 1990s and 2000s are now old enough that original HVAC equipment is approaching or past its expected lifespan, 15 to 20 years for most forced-air furnaces. That combination of age and seasonal dormancy is a reliable recipe for a no-heat call.

Local air quality also plays a role. The Santa Ana winds that push through the Temecula Valley corridor carry fine particulate matter that loads air filters faster than homeowners expect. A clogged filter starves a furnace of airflow, triggering high-limit shutoffs and leaving the blower running while the burners stay off, which is exactly what a furnace blowing cold air feels like from the register.

## Our Furnace Repair Process in Murrieta

When we arrive, the diagnostic starts before a single panel comes off. We note what the thermostat is doing, whether the blower is running, and what error codes, if any, the control board is displaying. Modern furnaces log fault sequences, and reading that history tells us whether the unit is tripping on high temperature, a pressure switch, or a failed ignition sequence.

From there, the process moves through a logical sequence:

- **Combustion and airflow check**, filter condition, return air clearances, and static pressure across the system.
- **Ignition system inspection**, hot surface ignitor resistance, flame sensor microamp reading, and gas valve operation.
- **Heat exchanger visual inspection**, cracks or corrosion here affect both efficiency and safety; we note findings and walk you through what we see.
- **Flue and venting check**, particularly relevant in Murrieta homes where attic-mounted furnaces vent through the roofline; bird nesting and debris blockage are more common than most homeowners realize.
- **Repair and test cycle**, once the repair is made, we run the unit through a full heat cycle and verify supply air temperature at the register before we leave.
