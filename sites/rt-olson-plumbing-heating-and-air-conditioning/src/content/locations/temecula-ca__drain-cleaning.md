## Why Temecula Properties See Drain Problems

Temecula sits in a basin where the soil shifts between sandy loam near the creek beds and expansive clay in the hillside developments. Clay soil moves with moisture, it swells during wet winters and contracts in the dry heat of summer. That repeated movement puts lateral drain lines under stress, causing joints to separate or sag over time. When a pipe sags, water pools in the low spot and debris accumulates instead of flushing through.

Temecula's explosive residential growth since the 1990s also means a large share of the housing stock uses ABS plastic drain lines that are now approaching or past the 30-year mark. ABS holds up well, but the fittings and glue joints can weaken, and root systems from mature landscaping, especially the olive and pepper trees common throughout the wine country neighborhoods, are opportunistic. A hairline crack in a fitting is all a root needs to get started.

Hard water is another factor. Temecula's municipal water supply carries measurable mineral content, and over years that calcium and magnesium build up on the interior walls of drain pipes, narrowing the effective diameter. A drain that once cleared a full sink in seconds may now take minutes, and that's before any grease or soap scum enters the equation.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Temecula

Every drain cleaning call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing: which fixture, how long it's been slow, whether multiple drains are affected at once. Multiple slow drains usually point to a main line issue; a single slow fixture is more often a localized clog.

For most kitchen sink and shower drain clogs, we begin with a motorized cable auger to break up the blockage and restore flow. If the clog is stubborn, or if we suspect grease buildup deeper in the line, we move to **hydro jetting**, a high-pressure water flush that scours the interior pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the obstruction. Hydro jetting is particularly effective on the long lateral runs common in Temecula's larger single-family homes, where grease and scale accumulate far from the cleanout access.

When the situation warrants, recurring clogs, suspected root intrusion, or an older home where the drain history is unknown, we can run a camera inspection through the line. Video gives a clear picture of pipe condition, joint separation, or root growth before we recommend a repair, so you're not guessing at what's happening underground.

## Local Note

Temecula's newer master-planned communities often have HOA-maintained common area sewer laterals that tie into individual home lines at the property boundary. Before we jet or auger a main line, we confirm with the homeowner where the HOA's responsibility ends and the private lateral begins, because jetting the wrong segment can void an HOA warranty or create a billing dispute.
