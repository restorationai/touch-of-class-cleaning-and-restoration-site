## Why Bloomington Properties See Air Quality Challenges

Bloomington's geography puts it downwind of freight corridors along the I-10 and I-215 interchange, and the area's mix of residential neighborhoods and light industrial parcels means outdoor particulate counts can spike quickly, especially during dry, windy stretches. Those particles don't stay outside. Every time your HVAC system cycles, it draws air through the return, and a standard one-inch filter is not built to catch the fine PM2.5 particles that irritate airways and accumulate in ductwork.

The climate compounds the problem in a second way: Bloomington's low annual humidity, particularly from late spring through early fall, allows dust and allergens to stay airborne longer than they would in a more humid climate. Conversely, during winter months when homes are sealed tight and heating systems run continuously, indoor humidity can drop to levels that dry out mucous membranes and make occupants more vulnerable to airborne pathogens. Neither extreme is comfortable, and neither is healthy over the long term.

Older housing stock in the area, single-family homes built through the 1970s and 1980s, often has ductwork that hasn't been inspected in years, with gaps, disconnected joints, or original fiberglass insulation that sheds particles into the airstream. That's the baseline we're working against before we even talk about what's living in the air.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Bloomington

Every engagement starts with an assessment rather than a product pitch. We look at your existing HVAC filtration, measure airflow at key registers, and ask about the symptoms you're noticing, whether that's visible dust, odors, or health patterns that seem tied to time spent indoors. From there, we build a solution that fits the home and the problem.

**HVAC filtration upgrades** are often the first step. Swapping a standard filter rack for a high-MERV media cabinet can capture significantly more particulate without the airflow restriction that comes with forcing a thick filter into a slot designed for a thin one. We size the upgrade to your system's blower capacity so you're not trading air quality for comfort.

**UV air purifiers installed inside the air handler** address a different category of problem, mold spores, bacteria, and viruses that pass right through even high-efficiency filters. A UV-C lamp positioned at the coil kills biological contaminants at the source before they circulate through the home. In Bloomington's warm climate, where air conditioning runs for extended periods and coil surfaces stay damp, this is a practical safeguard against coil mold that degrades both air quality and system efficiency.

**Whole-home humidifiers** are worth serious consideration for Bloomington households that run forced-air heating through the winter. A bypass or fan-powered humidifier installed on the supply plenum maintains indoor relative humidity in the range where respiratory health is best supported and where wood floors, cabinetry, and musical instruments don't suffer from the dry air.

**Indoor air quality testing** gives you a before-and-after benchmark, particulate counts, VOC levels, and humidity readings that document what the air actually contains rather than relying on symptoms alone.
