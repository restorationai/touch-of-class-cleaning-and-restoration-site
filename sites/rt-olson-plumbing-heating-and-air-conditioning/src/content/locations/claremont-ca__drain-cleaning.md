## Why Claremont Properties See Drain Problems More Than Most

Claremont's residential character is defined in part by its age. Much of the housing stock west of Indian Hill Boulevard dates to the mid-20th century, with original clay or Orangeburg sewer laterals that have been underground for sixty or seventy years. These materials are brittle, prone to root intrusion, and, in sections near the Claremont Colleges corridor, sometimes tied into shared or cross-connected lines that complicate a straightforward rooter job.

The Inland Valley's climate adds another layer. Long dry summers cause the soil to contract and shift, stressing pipe joints and creating gaps that root tips find almost immediately when the rains return. When those seasonal rains do arrive, the surge in groundwater pressure can push debris and root masses deeper into the system, turning a partial blockage into a full backup overnight. Kitchen grease buildup, already a year-round problem, accelerates during the holiday season when cooking volume spikes and FOG (fats, oils, and grease) coats the inside of drain lines that were already narrowed by mineral deposits from the region's hard water supply.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Claremont

Every drain call starts with a conversation about symptoms, where the backup is, how long it's been building, whether multiple fixtures are affected at once. Multiple slow drains or a gurgling toilet while running the washing machine usually signals a blockage in the main sewer lateral rather than an isolated fixture drain, and that changes the approach entirely.

For fixture-level clogs, a shower drain packed with hair and soap scum, a bathroom sink that drains slowly, a cable machine or hand auger clears the obstruction quickly. For main-line blockages, especially those involving root intrusion, hydro jetting is the more durable solution. A hydro jet delivers a high-pressure water stream that scours the interior pipe wall rather than simply punching a hole through the clog. The result is a line that flows at full capacity, not just well enough to pass water for a few weeks before the roots grow back.

When the cause of the blockage isn't obvious from symptoms alone, a sewer camera inspection lets us see exactly what's happening inside the pipe, root mass, offset joint, grease accumulation, or a collapsed section, before we commit to a method. That step saves time and avoids unnecessary work.
