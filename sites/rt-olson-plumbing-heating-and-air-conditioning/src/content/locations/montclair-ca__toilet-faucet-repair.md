## Why Montclair Properties See Fixture Problems

The Inland Empire's water supply is notoriously hard, Montclair is no exception. Properties built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a large share of Montclair's residential stock, often still have original brass and chrome fixtures that have been patched rather than replaced. Those stopgap repairs tend to fail at inconvenient times: a running toilet at midnight, a shower valve that won't regulate temperature, a kitchen faucet that leaks at the base every time it's used.

## Our Fixture Service Process in Montclair

Every call starts with a proper diagnosis rather than a parts swap. A running toilet can be caused by a worn flapper, a faulty fill valve, a cracked overflow tube, or a float set too high, and each fix is different. We check the actual source before quoting a repair.

For faucet work, we identify the manufacturer and valve type first. Montclair homes from the 1980s and early 1990s frequently have single-handle ball faucets or older cartridge designs that require specific replacement cartridges, not universal kits. Using the wrong cartridge creates the same drip within weeks. We carry a broad inventory of OEM-compatible cartridges, seats, and springs so the repair holds.

Shower valve repair follows the same logic. Pressure-balancing valves installed before updated code requirements may not meet current anti-scald standards. When we open a wall to service a shower valve in an older Montclair home, we note whether the existing valve is still code-appropriate and give the homeowner or property manager the information they need to make an informed decision, without upselling work that isn't necessary.

Fixture installation, whether a new toilet, a replacement faucet, or a complete shower trim kit, includes a full leak check and a flush or flow test before we leave the property.
