## Why Menifee Properties See AC Repair Issues

Menifee's climate puts residential HVAC systems through a punishing cycle. Units run nearly continuously from late May through September, accumulating more operating hours per year than systems in coastal communities ever do. That sustained demand accelerates wear on compressors, capacitors, and contactors, the components most likely to fail on the hottest day of the year, when the system has been running flat-out for weeks.

The city's rapid residential growth over the past two decades means a wide mix of equipment ages on the same street. Newer master-planned communities often have high-efficiency systems with variable-speed blowers and communicating thermostats, while homes built in the early 2000s may still be running original R-22 refrigerant equipment that's increasingly expensive to service as that refrigerant is phased out. Knowing which era of system you're dealing with changes the diagnostic approach before a technician ever opens the unit.

Dust and airborne particulates from the surrounding terrain also clog evaporator coils faster than homeowners expect. A coil that looks clean from the outside can be insulating itself with a thin layer of fine desert dust, enough to drop cooling capacity noticeably and force the compressor to work harder than it should.

## Our Air Conditioning Repair Process in Menifee

Every repair call starts with a full system diagnostic, not a guess. A technician checks refrigerant pressure, measures supply and return air temperatures, inspects electrical components for heat damage or corrosion, and evaluates airflow across the coil before recommending any part replacement. This matters in Menifee's climate because a low-refrigerant reading and a failing capacitor can produce nearly identical symptoms, warm air, short cycling, or a unit that runs constantly without cooling, and treating only one when both are present means a callback.

For refrigerant leak repair, we locate the leak before recharging the system. Topping off refrigerant without sealing the source is a temporary fix that costs you money twice. Once the leak is repaired and the system is recharged to manufacturer specification, we recheck temperatures to confirm the repair is holding before leaving the site.

Electrical component failures, capacitors, contactors, and control boards, are among the most common repairs we see during peak cooling season. These parts are stock items on our service vehicles, which reduces the time between diagnosis and a running system.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for AC Repair

Refrigerant work on systems still running R-22 requires handling a substance that is no longer manufactured domestically. We carry reclaimed R-22 for repairs on older equipment, but we'll also walk you through the cost comparison honestly: at current reclaimed-refrigerant prices, a significant R-22 leak on an aging system sometimes makes replacement the more economical path. That's a conversation we have with you before the work starts, not after.

For leak detection, we use electronic refrigerant detectors rather than relying on visual inspection alone. Leaks at brazed joints or inside the coil itself aren't visible without pressurizing the system and using a detector calibrated for the specific refrigerant type.
