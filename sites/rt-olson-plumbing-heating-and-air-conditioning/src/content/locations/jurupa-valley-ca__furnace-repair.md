## Why Jurupa Valley Homes See Furnace Problems

The housing stock across Jurupa Valley spans several decades and several different construction eras, from older ranch-style homes built when the area was still unincorporated Riverside County to newer tract developments that went up after Jurupa Valley incorporated in 2011. Older homes in this corridor frequently have original ductwork that was sized for equipment no longer in production, and when a replacement furnace is installed without a duct audit, the mismatch can cause the heat exchanger to overheat and crack, a failure that shows up as cold air blowing from vents even when the burner is firing.

The Inland Empire's dry climate also means dust and particulate accumulation inside furnace cabinets is higher than in coastal areas. Filters clog faster, secondary heat exchangers get coated with debris, and flame sensors oxidize more quickly than manufacturers' maintenance schedules assume. A furnace that ran fine last February may refuse to stay lit this December simply because the flame sensor hasn't been cleaned in two seasons.

Gas service in this part of Riverside County is delivered through SoCalGas infrastructure, and pressure fluctuations, more common during peak winter demand, can cause gas valves to behave erratically on older furnaces that were calibrated for a narrower pressure range.

## Our Furnace Repair Process in Jurupa Valley

When a technician arrives at a Jurupa Valley address, the diagnostic sequence starts at the thermostat and works inward: confirming the call for heat is reaching the control board, checking ignitor resistance, testing the flame sensor with a microamp meter, and inspecting the heat exchanger for cracks or separation. We don't skip the heat exchanger check, a cracked exchanger is a carbon monoxide risk, and in a tightly built newer home the consequences of missing it are serious.

From there, the technician checks inducer motor operation, pressure switch function, and the condition of the blower motor and capacitor. Blower capacitors are a common failure point in the Inland Empire's heat cycles, they degrade faster when the system also runs an air conditioner through long, hot summers, because the capacitor is working hard in both seasons.

If a part is needed, we carry common ignitors, flame sensors, capacitors, and gas valves on the service vehicle. Many repairs are completed on the first visit. If a part requires ordering, we'll give you a clear timeline and confirm whether the furnace can be safely operated in the interim.

## Local Note

Homes in Jurupa Valley that were built or converted during the 1980s and early 1990s sometimes have furnaces installed in attic spaces rather than garages or closets, a configuration that was common in this part of the Inland Empire when builders were maximizing square footage on smaller lots.
