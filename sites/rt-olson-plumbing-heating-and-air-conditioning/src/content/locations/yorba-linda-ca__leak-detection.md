## Why Yorba Linda Properties Experience Hidden Leaks

Yorba Linda's housing stock spans a wide range of eras — from ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and '70s when the city was still largely agricultural, to large planned communities developed through the 1990s and 2000s. Older copper supply lines from that mid-century period are now at or past their expected service life, and pinhole corrosion is a real pattern in homes of that vintage. Meanwhile, the expansive clay soils that underlie much of the city don't grip pipe bedding consistently — when soil shifts, even newer PVC or CPVC lines can develop joint separations that are impossible to see from the surface.

Irrigation systems add another layer of complexity. Yorba Linda's large lots and mature landscaping mean many properties have extensive drip and sprinkler networks, and a cracked lateral line under a lawn can lose thousands of gallons before anyone notices the grass is too green in one patch. A spike on your water bill from the Municipal Water District of Orange County is often the first real signal that something underground is wrong.

## Our Leak Detection Process in Yorba Linda

The goal of leak detection is to find the source without tearing out everything in the way. That means starting with the data — meter readings, pressure tests, and a review of which zones or fixtures are losing volume — before any equipment touches the property.

From there, the process typically moves through these steps:

- **Acoustic listening equipment** picks up the sound signature of pressurized water escaping a pipe, even through concrete slabs or packed soil. Different pipe materials and depths produce different frequencies, and experienced technicians learn to read those signals.
- **Thermal imaging** identifies temperature differentials behind walls or under flooring where moisture is migrating — useful in Yorba Linda's stucco-exterior homes where wall cavities can hold a slow leak for months before visible damage appears.
- **Tracer gas testing** is used for underground or slab leaks where acoustic methods give an approximate zone but not a precise point. A non-toxic gas mixture is introduced into the line and detected at the surface, narrowing the repair area significantly.
- **Video pipe inspection** confirms the condition of the line once a leak zone is identified, so the repair scope is clear before any excavation or access opening is made.

Because Yorba Linda has a meaningful number of HOA-governed communities, we document findings with photographs and written reports that meet the documentation requirements most associations and insurance carriers expect before authorizing repair work.
