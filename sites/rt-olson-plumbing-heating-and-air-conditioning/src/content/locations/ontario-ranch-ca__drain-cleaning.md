## Why Ontario Ranch Properties See Drain Issues Earlier Than You'd Expect

Most of Ontario Ranch's housing stock dates from the mid-2010s onward, relatively new PVC and ABS drain lines that should, in theory, be trouble-free for decades. In practice, a few local factors accelerate clogging:

**Construction debris in new lines.** During the community's rapid build-out, drywall dust, concrete splatter, and pipe-joint compound occasionally made it into drain lines before fixtures were installed. That residue hardens against pipe walls and becomes a catch point for grease and hair.

**Hard water mineral buildup.** Water delivered through this part of San Bernardino County carries high dissolved mineral content. Over time, calcium and magnesium deposits narrow the interior diameter of drain lines, particularly in shower drains and under kitchen sinks, until flow slows to a trickle.

**Clay-heavy soil movement.** The expansive soils common to the Ontario area shift seasonally with wet and dry cycles. Even relatively young underground drain lines can develop slight bellies or misalignments that trap solids and allow root intrusion from nearby landscaping.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Ontario Ranch

We don't guess at what's blocking a drain. The process starts with a camera inspection when the blockage isn't immediately obvious, a small waterproof camera travels the line and shows us exactly what we're dealing with before we touch a tool.

For grease-heavy kitchen sink clogs, we use a motorized drain snake to break the blockage and then flush the line. For stubborn buildup, mineral scale, or root intrusion, **hydro jetting** is the right call: a high-pressure water stream (up to 4,000 PSI) scours the pipe wall clean rather than just punching a hole through the clog. Hydro jetting is especially effective on Ontario Ranch's newer PVC lines, which handle the pressure well and emerge essentially like-new after a thorough flush.

Shower drain clogs, almost always a mix of hair, soap scum, and hard-water scale in this area, typically clear with a combination of cable snaking and targeted flushing. We clean up the work area before we leave.
