## Why Lake Elsinore Homes See Garbage Disposal Problems

The Inland Empire's climate swings between dry summer heat and wet winters, and Lake Elsinore sits in a valley that amplifies both. ## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Lake Elsinore

Every call starts with a diagnosis, not a parts order. A disposal that hums but won't spin is almost always a jammed impeller, often a bone fragment, a bottle cap, or hardened mineral scale, and can frequently be cleared with a hex wrench through the bottom port without replacing anything. A disposal that leaks from the top collar is usually a failed sink flange seal, a straightforward repair that does not require a new unit. A disposal that leaks from the bottom is a different story: that typically means the internal seals have failed, and replacement is the more cost-effective path.

When installation is the right call, we match the motor size to the household. A single-person condo and a busy family kitchen have genuinely different needs, and an undersized unit will jam repeatedly no matter how carefully it's used. We handle the full swap, disconnecting the drain and dishwasher inlet lines, removing the old mounting assembly, seating and torquing the new flange, and testing the drain flow before we leave. If the P-trap or drain line shows corrosion or buildup during the job, we'll flag it rather than let it become the next service call.
