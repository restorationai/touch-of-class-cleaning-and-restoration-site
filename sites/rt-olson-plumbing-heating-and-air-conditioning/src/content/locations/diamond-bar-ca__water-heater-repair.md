## Why Diamond Bar Properties See Water Heater Problems Earlier Than Expected

Homes throughout Diamond Bar tend to run water heaters hard. The combination of high water hardness and the temperature swings the Pomona Valley sees, cool, damp winters and dry summers pushing well past 100°F, puts thermal stress on tanks, anode rods, and pressure relief valves on a compressed timeline. Anode rods in this area can deplete significantly faster than the national average because harder water is more chemically aggressive toward the sacrificial magnesium or aluminum rod protecting your tank lining.

Many of Diamond Bar's residential neighborhoods were developed in the 1970s and 1980s, meaning a large share of the housing stock is now on its second or third water heater. Older installations sometimes sit in tight garage alcoves or utility closets with minimal clearance, a detail that affects both diagnosis and parts access during a repair visit. Gas shutoffs on some of these homes were installed before current code required easily accessible locations, which can add time to any service call.
