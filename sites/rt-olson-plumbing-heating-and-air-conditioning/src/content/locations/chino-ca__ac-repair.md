## Why Chino Properties See AC Repair Issues

Chino sits in a basin where hot, dry air from the east collides with the occasional marine push from the coast — a combination that puts air conditioning equipment through punishing duty cycles. ## Our AC Repair Process in Chino

A repair call starts with a full system diagnostic — not just a look at the thermostat reading. The technician checks refrigerant pressure on both the high and low sides, tests the capacitor and contactor, inspects the condenser coil for debris and damage, and measures the temperature split across the evaporator coil. That split — ideally somewhere between 16°F and 22°F — tells a lot about whether the system is moving heat effectively or struggling with a refrigerant issue.

Refrigerant leaks are one of the more common repair calls in this climate. When an AC runs as many hours per year as systems do in Chino, small leaks at Schrader valves, flare fittings, or the evaporator coil have more opportunity to develop. Finding the leak matters as much as recharging the system — adding refrigerant without locating the source is a temporary fix that will repeat itself. Technicians use electronic leak detection and UV dye to locate the source before any refrigerant is added.

For systems that need refrigerant work, RT Olson holds EPA Section 608 certification, which is required by federal law to purchase and handle refrigerants. That matters for homeowners because it means recovered refrigerant is handled properly and the repair is done to a standard that protects your equipment warranty.
