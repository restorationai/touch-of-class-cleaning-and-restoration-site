## Why Eastvale Properties See Emergency Plumbing Issues

Eastvale incorporated as a city in 2010, but most of its housing stock was built in the mid-2000s boom, master-planned communities with PEX and CPVC supply lines that are now approaching or past the 15-to-20-year mark where fittings and manifold connections begin to show wear. Those materials perform well when installed correctly, but the expansive clay soils underneath Eastvale's slab foundations create seasonal movement that can fatigue compression fittings and stress the stub-outs behind washing machine connections and under kitchen islands.

Summer is the season that catches homeowners off guard. Combine that with the region's hard water, Eastvale draws from the Chino Basin Watermaster-managed groundwater supply, which carries high mineral content, and you get accelerated scale buildup inside water heaters and at valve seats, both common triggers for emergency calls.
