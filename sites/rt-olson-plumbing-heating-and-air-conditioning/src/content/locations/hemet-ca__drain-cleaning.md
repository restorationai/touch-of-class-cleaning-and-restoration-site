## Why Hemet Properties See Drain Problems More Often Than You'd Expect

Hemet sits in the San Jacinto Valley, where water hardness levels are among the higher readings in Riverside County. That calcium and magnesium content doesn't just show up as white scale around your faucets, it builds up inside drain lines too, roughening pipe walls and giving grease, hair, and soap scum something to cling to. A drain that clears with a plunger this month may back up completely within weeks because the underlying mineral coating never went away.

Housing stock matters here as well. A significant portion of Hemet's single-family homes were built during the 1970s and 1980s, when ABS plastic drain lines were the standard. Those lines are now 40–50 years old in many properties, and older ABS can develop micro-cracks, joint separations, and root intrusion points, especially where mature trees have had decades to spread. Sewer lateral problems that look like a simple clog at the drain opening sometimes trace back to a root mass 20 feet down the line.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Hemet

Every service call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing, which fixture is slow, how long it's been building, and whether multiple drains are affected at once. A single slow shower drain usually points to a localized clog. Multiple slow drains throughout the house point toward the main line.

For most kitchen sink clogs and shower drain blockages, we begin with a professional-grade cable auger (rooter) that reaches deeper than any store-bought snake and breaks up compacted debris rather than just punching a hole through it. When the clog is more aggressive, grease buildup in a kitchen line, root intrusion in a sewer lateral, or years of scale accumulation, we move to hydro jetting. A hydro jet delivers a pressurized water stream that scours pipe walls clean rather than simply clearing a path through the obstruction. The result lasts considerably longer than a standard snake job.

For lines where root intrusion or pipe condition is in question, we can run a camera inspection before or after cleaning so you know exactly what's in the pipe and whether the line needs repair down the road.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

The right tool depends on what's in the pipe. Here's how we match method to problem:

- **Cable auger (rooter service)**: Best for hair clogs, soap blockages, and light grease in shower, tub, and bathroom sink drains. Fast and effective for localized obstructions.
- **Hydro jetting**: High-pressure water, typically 3,000–4,000 PSI, that strips grease, scale, and soft root material from pipe walls. The preferred method for kitchen lines and main sewer laterals in older Hemet homes where buildup is the real issue.
- **Video camera inspection**: A waterproof camera threaded into the line shows us root intrusion, pipe offsets, belly sections, or cracked joints that a snake won't reveal. Useful before hydro jetting an older line to confirm the pipe can handle the pressure.

Hemet's older neighborhoods often have mature landscaping, large trees planted close to sewer laterals during the 1970s buildout. If you've had the same drain clog recur two or three times in a year, there's a reasonable chance roots are involved, not just grease or hair.
