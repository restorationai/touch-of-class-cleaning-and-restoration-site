## Why Rialto Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

The Inland Empire's expansive clay soils are one of the most consistent culprits behind drain trouble in Rialto. As moisture levels fluctuate between dry summers and wet winters, the ground moves, and it takes buried drain pipes with it. Joints shift, small gaps open, and roots from nearby trees find their way in. Once roots establish inside a line, they trap grease, soap residue, and food debris until the pipe is effectively blocked.

Rialto also has a significant share of housing built during the post-war suburban boom of the 1950s through 1970s. Homes from that era frequently have original cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals that have spent decades absorbing the region's alkaline water. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out; Orangeburg, a tar-and-paper composite used as a cheap wartime substitute, deteriorates into a soft, collapsed tube over time. Either condition can cause recurring slow drains that a simple snake won't permanently fix.

Newer construction in Rialto is not immune. Tract developments built quickly during the 2000s growth surge sometimes have drain lines installed with inadequate slope, leading to standing water in the pipe and accelerated grease buildup, especially in kitchen lines.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Rialto

Every drain cleaning visit starts with a conversation about what you're seeing: which fixture is slow, whether multiple drains are affected, and how long the problem has been building. Multiple slow drains at once usually point to a blockage in the main line rather than an individual fixture branch, an important distinction that changes the approach.

For most kitchen sink clogs and shower drain backups, we begin with a professional cable auger to break through the obstruction and restore flow. If the clog is stubborn, or if grease and mineral scale have built up along the pipe walls, hydro jetting is the more effective solution. A hydro jet delivers a pressurized water stream, typically between 1,500 and 4,000 PSI depending on the pipe material and condition, that scours the interior of the line rather than simply punching a hole through the blockage.

When the cause of a recurring clog is unclear, we can run a camera inspection through the line to see exactly what's happening: root intrusion, pipe sag, corrosion, or a collapsed section. That visual confirmation means we're not guessing, and you're not paying for a repair that won't solve the actual problem.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

Cable augering handles most residential clogs efficiently, but the tool that makes the biggest difference in heavily scaled or root-infiltrated lines is the hydro jet. Unlike chemical drain cleaners, which can damage older cast-iron and Orangeburg pipe, hydro jetting uses water alone, making it safe for the pipe material while being far more thorough than mechanical snaking. For lines where we suspect structural damage, a push-camera inspection gives us footage we can review with you on-site, so the decision about next steps is based on what's actually in the pipe.
