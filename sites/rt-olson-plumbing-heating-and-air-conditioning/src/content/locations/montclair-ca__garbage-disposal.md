## Why Montclair Homes See Disposal Problems More Than You'd Expect

The Inland Empire's water supply carries a notably high mineral content, calcium and magnesium deposits that accumulate inside disposal grinding chambers over time, stiffening the impeller plate and accelerating wear on seals. Montclair's housing stock adds another layer: a significant portion of the city's residential neighborhoods were built during the 1960s and 1970s, when disposals were installed with thinner drain flanges and basic wiring that wasn't designed for today's higher-horsepower units. Upgrading to a modern 3/4 or 1 HP disposal in one of these older kitchens often means reinforcing the sink flange mount and sometimes rerouting the drain arm to meet current slope requirements, work that goes beyond a straight swap.

Montclair also sees a pattern common to the broader Pomona Valley: homes with cast-iron drain lines under the slab. When a disposal is leaking from the bottom, the leak is rarely just the disposal, it can track along the drain arm and pool inside the cabinet before anyone notices the cast-iron connection below is also corroding. Catching that early prevents a much larger repair.

## Our Repair and Installation Process in Montclair

Every disposal call starts with a hands-on diagnosis rather than an automatic upsell to replacement. A disposal that hums but won't spin usually has a jammed impeller, often cleared in minutes with the right hex wrench and a reset. A disposal that leaks from the top collar has a failed sink flange seal, which is a straightforward repair. A disposal that leaks from the bottom has a cracked internal shell and genuinely does need replacement.

When replacement is the right call, the process in a Montclair kitchen typically involves:

- Removing the old unit and inspecting the sink basket and flange for corrosion before setting the new mount
- Checking the drain arm pitch, a minimum 1/4 inch drop per foot toward the trap, and correcting it if the previous installation was flat or back-pitched
- Verifying the electrical connection meets current code; many older Montclair kitchens have a disposal wired to a switch that shares a circuit with the dishwasher, which can trip a breaker on higher-draw units
- Testing under full water flow before closing out the job

We carry a range of InSinkErator and Moen units on the truck so most installations complete in a single visit.
