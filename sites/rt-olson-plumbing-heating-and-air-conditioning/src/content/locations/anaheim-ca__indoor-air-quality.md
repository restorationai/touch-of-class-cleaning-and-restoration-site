## Why Anaheim Homes Struggle with Indoor Air Quality

Anaheim's housing stock spans several decades and construction styles, and that range matters when you're thinking about what's floating through your ductwork. Homes built in the postwar boom — many of them in the western and central parts of the city — were constructed before modern vapor barriers and tight-envelope standards existed. Attic bypasses, unsealed duct connections, and original single-pane windows all act as pathways for outdoor air to enter unconditioned. During Santa Ana wind events, that means a surge of ultra-fine dust and combustion particles from distant wildfires can bypass your HVAC system entirely and settle into living spaces.

Anaheim also sits in one of the more ozone-challenged air basins in the country. The South Coast Air Quality Management District regularly issues advisories for the region, and on high-AQI days, running your HVAC without upgraded filtration can actually pull outdoor pollutants deeper into the home. Humidity is the other variable: the region's dry season leaves indoor relative humidity well below the 40–60% range where respiratory tissue and wood furnishings are happiest, while marine layer intrusions in spring can push humidity high enough to encourage mold growth in poorly ventilated bathrooms and crawlspaces.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Anaheim

Every engagement starts with a conversation about what you're noticing — persistent odors, allergy flare-ups, dry skin and static electricity in winter, or visible dust accumulation on registers. From there, we assess your existing HVAC system: filter slot size, duct condition, system age, and whether your air handler has the static pressure headroom to support a higher-MERV media filter without starving airflow.

For homes where filtration alone isn't enough, we install whole-house air purifiers — including UV air purifiers mounted directly in the air handler — that neutralize airborne pathogens, mold spores, and volatile organic compounds before they circulate. Where dry-season air is the primary complaint, a whole-home humidifier installation tied to your existing system keeps relative humidity in a controlled range automatically, without the hassle of portable units you have to refill and clean. We also offer HVAC filtration upgrades that replace builder-grade filter slots with properly sealed media cabinets, so you're not losing conditioned air around the filter every time the system runs.

If you want data rather than guesswork, we can perform indoor air quality testing that measures particulate levels, VOCs, carbon dioxide, and humidity — giving you a baseline to measure improvement against after any equipment is installed.
