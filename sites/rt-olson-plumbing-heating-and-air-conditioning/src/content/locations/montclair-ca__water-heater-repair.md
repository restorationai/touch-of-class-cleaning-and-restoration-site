## Why Montclair Properties See Water Heater Problems

Montclair's water supply draws from a regional blend of imported and local groundwater that tends to carry elevated mineral content, the kind that leaves white crust around faucet aerators and builds up inside a water heater tank year after year. That sediment layer acts like insulation between the burner and the water, forcing the unit to run longer and hotter to hit the set temperature. You'll often hear it before you feel it: a low rumbling or popping sound from the garage or utility closet is sediment cracking as the burner fires. Left alone, that cycle shortens the life of the tank and can eventually crack the glass lining.

The Inland Empire's temperature swings also matter. Cold overnight lows in December and January stress the expansion tank and pressure relief valve in ways that mild coastal climates don't produce. A PRV that's been seating and reseating through years of temperature cycles is a common source of the slow drip you might notice around the base of the unit, and that drip is worth diagnosing before it becomes a puddle.

## Our Water Heater Repair Process in Montclair

When a technician arrives, the first step is a pressure and temperature check at the unit itself, not just a visual scan. Many water heater calls that look like a failed heating element turn out to be a tripped high-limit switch or a failing thermostat, parts that can be replaced in a single visit without pulling the tank. We carry common repair components on the truck for both gas and electric units, which matters when you need hot water restored the same day.

For gas water heaters, the more common configuration in Montclair's single-family homes, we check the thermocouple and pilot assembly before assuming the burner itself has failed. A thermocouple that's out of position or corroded will shut down the burner as a safety measure, leaving the pilot lit but the tank cold. That's a straightforward fix. If the tank has reached the point where repair no longer makes economic sense, we walk you through replacement options on the spot, including tankless units that handle the hard-water conditions here better over the long term when paired with a scale filter.
