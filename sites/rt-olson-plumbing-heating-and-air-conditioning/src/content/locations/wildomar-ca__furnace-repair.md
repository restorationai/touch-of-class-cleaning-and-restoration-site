## Our Furnace Repair Process in Wildomar

When a technician arrives at your Wildomar home, the first priority is a safety check, confirming there's no carbon monoxide risk and that the heat exchanger shows no visible cracking before anything else happens. ## Equipment and Methods for Furnace Repair

Diagnosing a furnace that won't produce heat requires more than a visual inspection. We use combustion analyzers to verify burner efficiency and carbon monoxide output, digital manometers to test static pressure across the heat exchanger, and clamp meters to verify that the blower motor is drawing correct amperage. These tools matter in Wildomar's older housing stock, where a furnace might appear to be running fine while actually operating outside safe combustion parameters.
