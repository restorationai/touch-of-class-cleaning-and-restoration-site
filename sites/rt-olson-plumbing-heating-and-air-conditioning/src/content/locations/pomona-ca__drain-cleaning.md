## Why Pomona Properties See Drain Problems

The San Gabriel Valley's water supply is notably hard, high in calcium and magnesium, and that mineral content builds up on the interior walls of drain pipes over years of use. In a home with original 1950s galvanized drains, that scale narrows the effective diameter of the pipe long before any single clog event. Add the root systems of Pomona's mature ficus, liquid amber, and jacaranda trees, and you have a recipe for recurring blockages that a plunger simply cannot fix. Roots don't just plug a line, they crack cast-iron joints and infiltrate clay sewer laterals, turning a slow drain into a full backup with very little warning.

Kitchen drains in Pomona's older housing stock tend to be the first to fail. Grease from cooking accumulates on already-narrowed pipe walls, and the relatively short runs to the city sewer main mean there's less slope to carry solids away. Shower and tub drains in homes with original tile work often have corroded strainer assemblies that catch hair and soap scum faster than modern fixtures would.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Pomona

Every drain call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing and how long it's been happening, that history matters. A drain that's been slow for weeks tells a different story than one that went from fine to fully blocked overnight. From there, the process typically moves through three stages:

**Cable and rooter service** handles most residential clogs. A sectional machine or drum auger breaks through grease caps, hair accumulations, and soft obstructions in kitchen, bathroom, and laundry lines. For most single-family homes in Pomona, this resolves the immediate backup.

**Hydro jetting** is the right tool when cable work reveals a line that's heavily scaled, grease-coated, or has recurring root intrusion. A hydro jet delivers pressurized water, typically between 1,500 and 4,000 PSI depending on pipe diameter and condition, that scours the pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the obstruction. For Pomona properties with older galvanized or clay lines, we assess pipe condition before jetting to make sure the line can handle the pressure.

**Camera inspection** is often the step that saves money in the long run. A sewer camera run through the main lateral shows whether root intrusion has cracked a joint, whether a belly has formed in the line, or whether the pipe is in good enough shape that cleaning alone will hold. We can record the footage and walk you through what we're seeing in real time.

## Local Note

Pomona has a significant number of properties built on concrete slab foundations, particularly in the tracts developed through the 1950s and 1960s. Slab homes present a specific challenge for drain cleaning: the main drain lines run beneath the concrete rather than through an accessible crawl space. If a camera inspection reveals a break or severe root intrusion in an under-slab line, the repair path is different, and more involved, than it would be in a raised-foundation home. Knowing this before you start a job shapes the equipment choices and the conversation with the homeowner.
