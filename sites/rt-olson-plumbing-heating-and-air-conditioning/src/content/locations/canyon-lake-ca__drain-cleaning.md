## Why Canyon Lake Properties See Drain Problems

The community's original sewer laterals and drain lines were sized and installed during the development boom that followed the lake's construction. Decades of use, shifting clay soils, and the root systems of the mature oaks and palms that give Canyon Lake its character all take a toll on underground pipe. Clay-heavy soil expands and contracts with seasonal moisture swings, and that movement can offset pipe joints just enough to catch grease, hair, and debris, turning a slow drain into a full blockage over a few seasons.

Hard water is another factor. The Inland Empire's water supply carries significant mineral content, and scale buildup inside drain lines narrows the effective diameter over time. A pipe that flowed freely when the home was built may now be running at a fraction of its original capacity, making it far more vulnerable to a clog from ordinary household use.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Canyon Lake

Every service call starts with a conversation about what the drain is doing, whether it's a single fixture backing up or multiple drains slowing at once, because the two point to very different problems. A single clogged kitchen sink usually means a localized grease or food-waste blockage. Slow drains throughout the house suggest the issue is deeper in the main line.

For most residential clogs, a professional cable auger, what most people call a rooter, breaks up the obstruction and restores flow quickly. When root intrusion or heavy scale is the culprit, hydro jetting is the more thorough solution. A hydro jet pushes water at high pressure through the line, cutting through roots, flushing grease, and scouring the pipe walls clean rather than just punching a hole through the blockage. For homes where the drain history is unclear, a camera inspection before jetting lets us see exactly what we're dealing with and confirm the line is clear afterward.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

The right tool depends on what's in the pipe. Cable augers handle soft blockages, hair mats, soap accumulation, food grease, efficiently and without risk to older pipe. Hydro jetting is more aggressive and more thorough, making it the preferred method when roots are involved or when a line has been slow for years rather than days. We carry both on every service vehicle so the choice is made based on the drain's condition, not on what happened to be loaded that morning.

For lines where age or condition is uncertain, a camera inspection removes the guesswork. It also protects homeowners: knowing the pipe is intact before jetting means no surprises, and the footage serves as documentation if an HOA or a home warranty company asks for proof of the work performed.
