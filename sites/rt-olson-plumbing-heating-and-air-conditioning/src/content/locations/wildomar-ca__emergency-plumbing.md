## Why Wildomar Properties See Emergency Plumbing Problems

Wildomar incorporated as a city only in 2008, but the housing stock tells a more complicated story. Older ranch-style homes along the western edge of the city were built in the 1970s and 1980s with galvanized steel supply lines that have long since reached the end of their service life. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, water pressure drops gradually, then a fitting fails suddenly, often inside a wall or under a slab. Newer tract developments built during the 2000s and early 2010s used copper and CPVC, but those materials have their own vulnerabilities in Wildomar's climate: CPVC becomes brittle after years of UV exposure in attic runs, and copper joints in uninsulated crawl spaces can crack during the sharp cold snaps the Inland Valley sees in January and February.

The region's expansive clay soils compound the problem. When Wildomar gets a heavy rain after a long dry stretch, common in a Mediterranean climate, the ground swells unevenly. That movement puts lateral stress on underground supply and drain lines, which is one reason slab leaks and main line breaks tend to cluster after the first significant storms of the season.

## Our Emergency Plumbing Process in Wildomar

Every emergency call starts the same way: a live dispatcher answers, asks the right questions to understand whether water is actively flowing, and either walks you through shutting off the main or gets a technician moving toward your address immediately.
