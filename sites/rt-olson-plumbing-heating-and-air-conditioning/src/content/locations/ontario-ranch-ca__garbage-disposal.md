## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Ontario Ranch

Every service call starts with a diagnosis, not an assumption. A humming disposal almost always means the flywheel is jammed; a technician will use the hex key reset at the base before anything else. If the unit won't reset, or if it resets and jams again within minutes, the grinding plate or impellers are typically the culprit. A leaking disposal is a different problem: leaks from the top flange usually mean a failed putty seal, while leaks from the bottom point to internal cracks that make replacement the more cost-effective path.

When installation is the right call, we match the disposal to the household's actual usage. A single-person household and a family of five with a vegetable garden have genuinely different needs. We also verify that the electrical connection under the sink is properly grounded and that the drain configuration, especially in Ontario Ranch homes with island sinks or extended drain runs, won't create a slow-drain problem after the new unit goes in.
