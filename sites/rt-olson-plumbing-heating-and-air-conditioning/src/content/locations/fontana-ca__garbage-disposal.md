## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in Fontana

Every service call starts under the sink, not at the hardware store. A disposal that hums but won't spin almost always has a jammed turntable, food debris, a piece of glass, or a utensil lodged between the impellers and the grinding ring. That's a repair, not a replacement, and it typically takes less than 30 minutes. A disposal that leaks from the bottom has a failed internal seal, which is usually a sign the unit itself is at end of life. A leak at the sink flange or the dishwasher inlet hose is a different story, those connections can be reseated or re-sealed without touching the disposal motor at all.

When replacement is the right call, the technician matches the new unit to your household's actual use pattern and your drain line configuration. Fontana kitchens with a garbage disposal plumbed into a double-bowl sink require attention to the drain trap arm arrangement, an improperly vented trap on the disposal side can cause slow draining or gurgling on the opposite bowl. The installation includes confirming the drain slope, checking the P-trap configuration, and running the unit through a full test cycle before the job is closed out.
