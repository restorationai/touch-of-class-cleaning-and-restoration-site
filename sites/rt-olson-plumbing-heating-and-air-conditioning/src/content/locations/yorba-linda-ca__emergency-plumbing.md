## Local Note

One thing worth knowing if you own a home in the hillside tracts of Yorba Linda: many of these properties were built with pressure-reducing valves (PRVs) sized for the original water pressure in the distribution system. The Yorba Linda Water District has adjusted system pressures in parts of the service area over the years, and an aging or undersized PRV can allow line pressure to creep above the range residential fixtures and supply lines are rated for.
