## Why Chino Hills Homes Face Distinct Air Quality Pressures

Chino Hills developed rapidly through the 1980s and 1990s, and the housing stock from that era typically shipped with builder-grade 1-inch fiberglass filters, equipment that captures large debris but lets fine particulate, mold spores, and VOCs pass right through. Many of those original HVAC systems are still in place, operating with filter slots that can't accommodate a thicker MERV-13 or MERV-16 cartridge without a filter rack upgrade. Compounding the issue, the region's low relative humidity through summer and fall desiccates nasal passages and airways, making residents more sensitive to airborne irritants. Then winter and early spring bring the opposite problem: marine layer moisture from the coast pushes inland, raising indoor humidity enough to encourage mold growth in attics and crawl spaces, particularly in homes with limited soffit ventilation. The swing between dry and damp conditions across a single season is one of the more underappreciated air quality drivers in this part of San Bernardino County.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Chino Hills

Every engagement starts with an assessment of what's actually in the air and how your HVAC system is handling it. We check static pressure across the air handler, evaluate your current filtration media and filter slot dimensions, and look for signs of biological growth on evaporator coils, a common finding in systems that run long cooling cycles through Chino Hills summers. From there, the path forward depends on what we find.

For homes where filtration is the primary gap, we upgrade the filter rack and install a high-efficiency media filter appropriate to your system's airflow specs. For biological contamination on coils or in ductwork, a UV air purifier installed at the air handler can neutralize mold spores and bacteria before they recirculate. Whole-house air purifiers using HEPA or activated carbon stages address VOCs and odors, relevant for Chino Hills homes near the 71 or 60 freeway corridors, where diesel particulate is a measurable contributor to indoor air loads. If humidity imbalance is the issue, a whole-home humidifier installation gives you precise control rather than relying on portable units that only treat one room at a time.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

The equipment we specify is matched to your home's square footage, duct configuration, and the specific pollutants driving your concern. UV-C germicidal lamps mounted in the air handler plenum are effective against biological contaminants and require annual lamp replacement, we walk every homeowner through that maintenance schedule before we leave. Whole-house media filtration upgrades are sized to maintain adequate airflow so your system doesn't labor against the added resistance, which matters especially in the multi-story homes common in Chino Hills where static pressure is already higher than in single-story builds. For homes where duct leakage is pulling unconditioned attic air into the living space, a real concern in 1980s and 1990s construction with flex duct runs, we can identify those breach points during the assessment and recommend sealing before any filtration upgrade, since a leaky duct system will undermine any purifier you add downstream.
