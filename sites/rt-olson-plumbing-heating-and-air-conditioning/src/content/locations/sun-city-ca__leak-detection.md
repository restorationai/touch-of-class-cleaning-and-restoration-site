## Why Sun City Properties See Hidden Leak Problems

Much of Sun City's residential stock was built during the active-adult community boom of the 1960s through the 1980s, a period when galvanized steel and early copper supply lines were standard. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out, narrowing over decades and eventually pitting through entirely. Copper from that era is generally more durable, but the solder joints and fittings used before modern standards are more vulnerable to the thermal cycling that Inland Empire summers demand. When daytime temperatures spike and cool sharply overnight, those joints flex. Over years, that movement opens micro-fractures.

The expansive clay soils common throughout this part of Riverside County compound the problem. Clay swells when wet and shrinks when dry, and in a region that swings between dry desert conditions and periodic heavy rain events, underground pipes shift with the soil. A line that ran straight when it was installed may have enough lateral stress on it today to open a joint or crack a fitting, all underground, all invisible from the surface.

## Our Leak Detection Process in Sun City

Electronic leak detection lets us locate a hidden leak without opening walls or jackhammering slabs until we know exactly where the problem is. The process starts with a pressure test on the supply system to confirm a leak exists and to isolate whether it's in the hot or cold line. From there, acoustic listening equipment amplifies the sound signature of water escaping under pressure, even through several inches of concrete. We cross-reference that with thermal imaging where the surface conditions allow it, which can reveal temperature differentials caused by water migrating through a slab or behind drywall.

In Sun City's older single-story ranch homes, where the slab is often the only thing between the living space and the ground, this non-invasive sequencing matters. A confirmed pinpoint location means a targeted repair, one core through the slab rather than a trench across the living room. We document the location with photographs before any excavation begins, which also gives you a clear record if you need to file a homeowner's insurance claim.

## Local Note: Irrigation Lines and Shared Water Meters

One thing that catches Sun City homeowners off guard: many properties in this area were built with shared or combined water meters serving both the domestic supply and the landscape irrigation system. A leak in a buried drip or spray irrigation lateral can register on your meter exactly the same way a slab leak does, elevated consumption, no visible source. Before assuming the worst, it's worth having both systems pressure-tested independently. We check both as part of a full leak investigation here, because chasing a slab repair when the actual culprit is a cracked irrigation manifold in the side yard is an expensive detour nobody needs.

If you suspect an active leak, locate your main shutoff, typically near the front of the property at the meter box, and close it if water is actively intruding into living space or if you can hear continuous flow with all fixtures off.
