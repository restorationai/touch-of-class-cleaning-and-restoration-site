San Bernardino's tap water carries some of the highest hardness levels in Southern California, a direct result of the region drawing heavily from groundwater sources that travel through limestone and mineral-rich desert geology before reaching your faucet. That hardness doesn't just leave white rings in your sinks and a filmy residue on your shower glass; it shortens the life of water heaters, clogs fixture aerators, and quietly works against every appliance connected to your plumbing. RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning installs and services water softeners, whole-house filtration systems, and reverse osmosis units throughout San Bernardino, helping homeowners and property managers finally get ahead of a problem that most of the city's water supply makes unavoidable.

## Why San Bernardino Properties See Hard Water and Filtration Issues

The Inland Empire sits in a basin where groundwater picks up calcium, magnesium, and other dissolved minerals on its way to municipal treatment and distribution. San Bernardino's water supply blends surface water from the State Water Project with local groundwater, and depending on the season and how much groundwater the city is drawing, hardness levels can climb well above 200 mg/L, roughly twice what most water softener manufacturers consider "hard." That seasonal variability matters: a system sized for average hardness may underperform during drier stretches when groundwater makes up a larger share of the blend.

Beyond hardness, older homes throughout San Bernardino, particularly the bungalows and post-war tract houses built before the city's mid-century growth boom, often have galvanized steel supply lines that have been slowly corroding for decades. Galvanized pipe releases iron and sediment into the water stream, which can discolor water, accelerate fixture wear, and foul a water softener's resin bed if a sediment pre-filter isn't installed upstream. Getting the filtration sequence right matters as much as choosing the right equipment.

## Our Water Softener and Filtration Installation Process in San Bernardino

Every installation starts with a water test, not a generic hardness strip, but a reading that captures hardness, iron content, pH, and total dissolved solids. San Bernardino's water profile varies enough across the city that we size softeners and select filter media based on what's actually coming out of your tap, not a regional average.

From there, the process typically follows this path:

- **Point-of-entry placement**: We identify the best location to treat water before it reaches your water heater and distribution lines, usually near the main shutoff, which in many San Bernardino homes is in a side-yard utility area or a garage.
- **Pre-filtration staging**: Where galvanized pipe or elevated iron is present, we install a sediment filter upstream of the softener to protect the resin bed.
- **Softener or conditioner installation**: Salt-based ion exchange systems are the most effective for the hardness levels common here. For customers who prefer a salt-free option, we install template-assisted crystallization (TAC) conditioners and explain honestly what they do and don't accomplish.
- **Reverse osmosis at the kitchen**: Whole-house softening handles scale and appliance protection; a dedicated RO system at the kitchen sink handles drinking water quality, removing nitrates, chloramines, and other dissolved compounds that softening alone doesn't address.
- **System commissioning and bypass setup**: We set regeneration cycles calibrated to your household's actual water use and install a bypass valve so service or emergencies don't leave you without water to the house.
