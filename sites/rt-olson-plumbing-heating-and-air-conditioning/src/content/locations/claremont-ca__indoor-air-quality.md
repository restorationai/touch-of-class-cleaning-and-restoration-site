That swing between parched and damp creates conditions that stress respiratory systems, feed dust mite populations, and leave HVAC filters working overtime. ## Why Claremont Homes See Elevated Air Quality Challenges

Claremont's housing stock is unusually diverse for the Inland Valley. The Claremont Colleges area includes older craftsman and Spanish-revival homes built in the mid-twentieth century, many of which have original ductwork that was never designed for today's high-MERV filtration. Forcing a modern filter into an undersized duct system restricts airflow and can actually increase particulate recirculation. Newer tract developments on the city's eastern edges tend to be tightly constructed, energy-efficient, but prone to stale air buildup when ventilation isn't actively managed.

The Santa Ana winds that sweep through the Cajon Pass each fall deposit fine particulate matter, PM2.5, throughout the region. Claremont's position near the foothills means it catches both the wind-driven dust and the wildfire smoke that funnels through the same corridors. Indoor PM2.5 levels can spike well above outdoor readings when infiltration gaps around doors and windows aren't addressed alongside filtration upgrades.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Services Process in Claremont

Every engagement starts with an assessment, not a sales pitch. We evaluate your existing HVAC system's filter slot, duct condition, and static pressure before recommending anything. That step matters here because a whole-house air purifier or HVAC filtration upgrade that's sized for a newer open-plan home can underperform or damage equipment in an older Claremont property with narrower duct runs.

From there, the path typically follows the air: filtration first, then source control, then humidity management.

- **HVAC filtration upgrades**, We match filter media to your system's actual airflow capacity, not just the slot size. High-MERV media filters trap fine particles without choking the blower motor.
- **UV air purifiers for HVAC**, Installed in the air handler, UV-C lamps neutralize mold spores and bacteria in the airstream. Particularly useful after the wet season, when duct interiors can harbor biological growth.
- **Whole-home humidifier installation**, During Santa Ana conditions, indoor relative humidity can drop below 20%. A bypass or fan-powered humidifier installed on the supply plenum keeps levels in the 35–50% range, which reduces static electricity, protects wood floors and cabinetry, and makes the air easier to breathe.
- **Indoor air quality testing**, Baseline particulate counts, VOC screening, and CO2 readings give us real numbers to work from and let you see the improvement after equipment is installed.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

For whole-house air purifiers, we install units that integrate directly with your existing air handler rather than standalone plug-in devices that only treat one room. Media air cleaners, electronic air cleaners, and combination UV-plus-media systems each have a place depending on what the assessment finds.

Whole-home humidifiers are plumbed to your water supply line and wired to the furnace control board, so they run automatically whenever the heating system operates. We size the unit to your home's square footage and construction type, a drafty 1950s ranch loses moisture differently than a 2010 stucco build with spray-foam insulation.
