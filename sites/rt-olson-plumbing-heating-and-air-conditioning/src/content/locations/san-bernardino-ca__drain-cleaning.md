## Why San Bernardino Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

The Inland Empire's water is notoriously hard, mineral-heavy water from both surface and groundwater sources moves through San Bernardino's distribution system and deposits calcium carbonate inside drain and supply lines alike. Over time, that scale acts like a narrowing sleeve inside the pipe, catching grease, soap, and hair until the passage is effectively choked. This is especially pronounced in homes built before the 1980s, where galvanized steel drains have had decades to accumulate both corrosion and mineral deposits.

Tree roots are the other persistent culprit. Mature trees planted along older residential streets send roots toward any moisture source, and a hairline crack in a clay or cast-iron sewer lateral is all the invitation they need. Once inside, roots branch and mat until the line backs up entirely. Kitchen drains tell a different story, cooking oil solidifies in the cooler sections of drain pipe, and in commercial properties along high-traffic stretches of the city, that grease accumulates quickly enough to require scheduled maintenance rather than reactive service.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in San Bernardino

Every service call starts with a conversation about what the drain is doing, how slowly it's moving, whether multiple fixtures are backing up simultaneously, and whether there's any odor. A single slow shower drain usually points to a localized clog; multiple fixtures backing up at once suggests the problem is in the main line or sewer lateral.

For most residential clogs, a professional cable auger clears the immediate blockage. For lines with heavy scale, root intrusion, or recurring buildup, hydro jetting is the more effective solution. A hydro jet pushes water at high pressure through the line, cutting through grease, scrubbing mineral scale off pipe walls, and flushing root material out of the system rather than just punching a hole through it. Where root intrusion is suspected, a camera inspection before and after the cleaning confirms the line is clear and identifies any structural damage that may need repair. We carry the equipment to handle both residential laterals and larger commercial drain lines on the same visit.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

Cable augers handle the majority of residential clogs efficiently, especially in kitchen and bathroom drains where the blockage is close to the fixture. For lines where buildup is the underlying issue rather than a single obstruction, hydro jetting delivers a more thorough clean, the high-pressure water scours the pipe wall rather than simply boring through the clog. We also carry sewer camera equipment so we can inspect the line after clearing it, which matters in older San Bernardino properties where a cleared clog can reveal a cracked lateral or a section of pipe with significant root damage that will cause problems again soon.

San Bernardino has a significant share of housing stock built in the postwar decades of the 1940s through 1960s, and many of those homes still have their original clay or cast-iron sewer laterals running from the house to the city main.
