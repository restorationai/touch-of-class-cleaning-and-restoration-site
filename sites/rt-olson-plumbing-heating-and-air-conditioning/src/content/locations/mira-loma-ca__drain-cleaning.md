## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Mira Loma

Every drain cleaning visit starts with a conversation about what you've observed, how long the slowdown has been building, whether multiple fixtures are affected, and whether you've noticed any odor or gurgling from other drains when one is running. Multiple slow drains at the same time usually point to a main line blockage rather than an isolated fixture clog, and that distinction changes the approach entirely.

For localized clogs, a kitchen sink clog removal, a shower drain clog, or a single bathroom lavatory, we use professional cable augers sized to the drain to break up and remove the obstruction without damaging the pipe wall. For main line blockages, grease-laden commercial kitchen lines, or any situation where roots have infiltrated the pipe, hydro jetting is the right tool. A hydro jet delivers a high-pressure water stream that scours the full interior circumference of the pipe, flushing debris completely out of the system rather than just punching a hole through a clog. For properties with older cast-iron lines, we calibrate the jetting pressure carefully, enough to clean thoroughly, not so aggressive that it stresses already-corroded pipe walls.

When root intrusion is suspected, a camera inspection lets us see exactly where roots have entered and how extensive the growth is, so we can give you an honest picture of whether cleaning will provide a long-term fix or whether a section of pipe needs attention.
