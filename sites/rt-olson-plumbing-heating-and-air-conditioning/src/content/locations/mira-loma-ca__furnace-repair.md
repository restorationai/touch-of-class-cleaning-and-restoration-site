## Our Furnace Repair Process in Mira Loma

Every repair call starts with a full system diagnostic, not a parts swap. A furnace blowing cold air in Mira Loma could mean a failed ignitor, a tripped high-limit switch from restricted airflow, a failed control board, or a gas valve issue, and the fix is different in each case. Guessing wastes your time and money.

The diagnostic sequence we follow:

- **Visual and combustion inspection**, check heat exchanger integrity, burner flame pattern, and flue draft before touching any components
- **Electrical measurement**, test voltage at the control board, inducer motor, and ignitor to isolate the failure point
- **Airflow assessment**, measure static pressure across the system; if duct issues are contributing to the failure, we document them so you can make an informed decision
- **Repair and verification**, after the repair, we run the furnace through a full heating cycle and recheck combustion readings before we leave
