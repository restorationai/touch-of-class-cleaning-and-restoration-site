## Our Leak Detection Process in Mira Loma

We use a layered approach because no single method finds every leak in every situation. The process typically begins with a pressure test on the supply side to confirm a leak is present and to isolate whether it's in the main line, a branch, or the irrigation system. From there we move to acoustic listening equipment, ground microphones and pipe-contact sensors that amplify the sound signature of pressurized water escaping through a crack or pinhole. On slab foundations, which are common throughout Mira Loma, we add thermal imaging to identify temperature differentials at the surface caused by water migrating under or through the concrete.

For underground service lines and irrigation laterals, we use electronic line tracing to map the pipe path before we listen, so we're not guessing where the pipe runs under years of landscaping. The goal is to hand you a precise location, marked on the surface, so any excavation or access is surgical rather than exploratory. That matters especially on properties with stamped concrete, pavers, or mature tree roots that make unnecessary digging expensive and disruptive.
