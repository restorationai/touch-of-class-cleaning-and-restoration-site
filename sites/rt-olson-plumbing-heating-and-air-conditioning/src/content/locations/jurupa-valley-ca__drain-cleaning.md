## Why Jurupa Valley Properties See Drain Problems

The soil conditions across Jurupa Valley are a significant factor that homeowners rarely consider until something backs up. Expansive clay soils contract in the dry summer heat and swell again when the rains return, that repeated movement stresses pipe joints and can cause minor offsets that catch grease, scale, and debris. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a large share of the housing stock in this area, were often plumbed with cast iron or older ABS that has had decades to accumulate buildup inside the pipe walls.

Kitchen drains in Jurupa Valley homes tend to clog faster than homeowners expect because hard water from the region leaves mineral deposits that narrow the pipe diameter over time. Combine that with cooking grease and food particles, and a drain that once drained freely can slow significantly within a few years. Shower and tub drains face a similar pattern, soap scum and hard water scale bind with hair to form blockages that a plunger simply pushes deeper rather than removes.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Jurupa Valley

Every drain call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing, which fixture is affected, whether it's a single drain or multiple, and how long the problem has been building. A single slow drain usually points to a localized clog; multiple fixtures backing up at once suggests the blockage is in the main sewer line.

For most kitchen and bathroom clogs, we begin with a professional drain snake (also called a rooter) to break up and retrieve the obstruction. When the clog is further down the line or involves root intrusion, hydro jetting is the more effective solution. Hydro jetting sends pressurized water through the pipe at high force, cutting through grease, mineral scale, and root tendrils and flushing debris downstream, leaving the pipe walls clean rather than just punched through. For main line issues, a camera inspection lets us see exactly what's inside the pipe before we decide on the right method, so we're not guessing at the fix.
