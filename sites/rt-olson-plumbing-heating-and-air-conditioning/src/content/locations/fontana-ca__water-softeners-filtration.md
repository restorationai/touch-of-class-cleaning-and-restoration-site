Fontana's tap water is some of the hardest in the Inland Empire, drawn largely from groundwater basins that carry significant mineral load by the time it reaches your fixtures. That hardness leaves white scale on showerheads, shortens the life of water heaters and appliances, and makes soap feel like it never quite rinses clean. RT Olson Plumbing, Heating and Air Conditioning installs water softeners, whole-house filtration systems, and reverse osmosis units sized for Fontana homes and the specific water chemistry coming out of your taps.

## Why Fontana's Water Demands a Treatment Solution

The San Bernardino County groundwater that supplies much of Fontana registers consistently high in calcium and magnesium, the minerals that cause hard water scaling. Depending on your neighborhood and which blended supply your street draws from, hardness levels can run well above what most water softeners are factory-set to handle. That means a unit sized for a national average will be regenerating too infrequently and leaving residual hardness behind.

Beyond hardness, Fontana sits in a basin where agricultural and industrial land use over the decades has contributed to elevated nitrate and trace contaminant concerns in some areas. Homeowners who rely on tap water for drinking and cooking often find that a whole-house carbon filter alone isn't enough, a point-of-use reverse osmosis system under the kitchen sink is the more complete answer for drinking water quality.

Fontana's dry, hot summers also accelerate scale buildup inside tankless water heaters and dishwashers. When water evaporates quickly off surfaces, it leaves mineral deposits behind faster than in cooler, more humid climates. A properly sized and programmed water softener pays for itself in reduced appliance maintenance costs over a few years.

## Our Water Treatment Process in Fontana

Every installation starts with a water test at your tap, not a generic regional estimate. We measure hardness, total dissolved solids, pH, and any specific contaminants relevant to your address before recommending equipment. That on-site reading determines the grain capacity your softener needs, the filter media appropriate for your filtration system, and whether a reverse osmosis system makes sense at the kitchen sink or at a whole-house entry point.

For softener installations, we size the resin tank to your household's daily water use and the measured hardness, then program the regeneration cycle to match, so you're not wasting salt or water on unnecessary cycles. We connect to your main supply line before the water heater so every fixture in the house benefits, and we always install a bypass valve so you can isolate the unit for service without cutting water to the whole home.

Reverse osmosis systems go in under the sink with a dedicated faucet, a storage tank, and, if you have a refrigerator with a water and ice dispenser, a line run to the fridge. We handle the full installation including the drain connection and any cabinet modifications needed to fit the tank.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

We work with salt-based ion exchange softeners for true hardness removal, salt-free conditioners for homeowners who prefer to avoid sodium in their water, and dual-media whole-house filters that address sediment, chloramines, and organic compounds in a single unit. For drinking water, our reverse osmosis systems use multi-stage membrane filtration that reduces dissolved solids, nitrates, and a broad range of contaminants to well below EPA secondary standards.

All equipment we install is rated for California's plumbing code requirements, and we pull the appropriate permit when the scope of work requires one, something not every installer bothers to do on water treatment jobs.

## Local Note

Fontana has a significant share of tract homes built during the 1980s and 1990s expansion that used copper supply lines with soldered joints. Hard water scale doesn't just coat the inside of those pipes, over years it can partially protect older solder joints from corrosion, which means that when a softener removes that scale layer, the underlying pipe condition sometimes becomes apparent for the first time. It's worth having us do a quick visual check of accessible supply lines during installation so there are no surprises after the water chemistry changes.

If you're in one of Fontana's newer master-planned communities on the east side of the city, your HOA's CC&Rs may specify where a brine discharge line can terminate or whether a softener bypass must be accessible for inspection.
