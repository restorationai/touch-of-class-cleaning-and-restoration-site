## Why Fontana Properties See Drain Cleaning Issues

San Bernardino County's water supply carries some of the highest mineral hardness readings in Southern California. Over time, calcium and magnesium deposits narrow the interior diameter of drain lines the same way arterial plaque narrows a blood vessel, slowly, invisibly, until flow drops to a trickle or stops entirely. In Fontana's older residential tracts, galvanized steel drain lines compound the problem: rust flakes off the interior walls and catches grease, hair, and food solids, building a clog that no amount of liquid drain cleaner will fully dissolve.

Fontana also sits in a basin where clay-heavy soils shift seasonally with wet winters and dry summers. That movement stresses underground drain lines, causing bellies, low spots where water pools and debris settles, and, in more severe cases, offset joints that roots from the area's mature trees find quickly. A slow-draining floor drain in a garage or a persistently sluggish bathroom line is often the first sign of a belly or root intrusion, not just a surface clog.

## Our Drain Cleaning Process in Fontana

Every service call starts with a conversation about what you're seeing and hearing, a gurgling toilet when the washing machine drains, a sulfur smell from the kitchen sink, a shower that holds two inches of water through a full rinse cycle. That symptom pattern tells us where in the drain system to look before a single tool comes off the truck.

For straightforward clogs, grease buildup in a kitchen line, hair and soap scum in a bathroom drain, a professional cable auger clears the blockage and restores flow quickly. For lines that keep coming back, or where the camera shows scale buildup along a longer run, hydro jetting is the right call. A hydro jet pushes water at high pressure through a specialized nozzle, scrubbing the pipe walls clean rather than just punching a hole through the clog. The result is a drain that flows the way it did when the house was new, not just one that's temporarily passable.

If a camera inspection reveals a belly, root intrusion, or a cracked section, we walk you through what the footage shows before recommending any repair. No surprise upsells, just a clear picture of what's actually in the pipe.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Drain Cleaning

Cable augers handle most residential clogs, kitchen sink blockages, bathroom drain backups, laundry standpipe clogs. The cable reaches deep into the line and breaks up or retrieves the obstruction without damaging the pipe.

Hydro jetting is the step up when a clog is recurring or when scale has narrowed the line significantly. The high-pressure water stream is calibrated to the pipe material, lower pressure for older, more fragile cast iron, higher for PVC, so the cleaning is aggressive on buildup without being aggressive on the pipe itself.

For any line where the cause isn't obvious from symptoms alone, we run a drain camera before and after the cleaning. The before footage confirms what we're dealing with; the after footage confirms the line is clear. You get a copy if you want one.
