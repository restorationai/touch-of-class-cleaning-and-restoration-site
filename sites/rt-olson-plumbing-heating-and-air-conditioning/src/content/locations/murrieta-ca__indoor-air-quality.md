## Why Murrieta Homes Struggle with Indoor Air Quality

Murrieta sits in the northern end of the Temecula Valley, where the terrain funnels wind from multiple directions and agricultural activity to the south and east adds biological particulates, pollen, mold spores, and fine dust, to the air mix. Homes built during the city's rapid growth periods in the 1990s and 2000s were constructed tightly for energy efficiency, which is exactly what traps those particles inside. Without mechanical ventilation or upgraded filtration, a well-sealed home in this climate becomes a collector rather than a refuge.

Common indoor air quality problems RT Olson sees in Murrieta include:

- **Dust accumulation** that returns days after cleaning, driven by low-MERV filters that let fine particles recirculate through the duct system
- **Dry indoor air** during the low-humidity months, which irritates airways and damages wood flooring and cabinetry
- **Volatile organic compounds (VOCs)** off-gassing from newer construction materials in homes that don't have enough fresh-air exchange
- **Biological growth in ductwork** following the occasional wet winter, when condensation inside poorly insulated ducts creates conditions mold spores find hospitable

## Indoor Air Quality Services We Provide in Murrieta

RT Olson approaches indoor air quality as a diagnostic process, not a product sale. The right solution depends on what's actually in your air and how your HVAC system is configured, which is why we start with an assessment before recommending equipment.

**Indoor air quality testing** identifies the specific contaminants present, particulates, VOCs, humidity levels, and biological matter, so upgrades address real problems rather than guesses.

**HVAC filtration upgrades** replace standard 1-inch filters with high-efficiency media filtration matched to your system's airflow capacity. Forcing a filter with too high a MERV rating onto an undersized blower motor is a common mistake that reduces airflow and strains equipment, we size the upgrade to your specific air handler.

**Whole house air purifiers** and **UV air purifiers for HVAC** are installed directly in the air stream, neutralizing biological contaminants that pass through even high-quality filters. UV systems are particularly effective in Murrieta's climate, where duct condensation during humid winter stretches creates recurring biological issues.

**Whole home humidifier installation** addresses the dry-air side of the equation. During Murrieta's low-humidity months, which can push indoor relative humidity well below the 30–50% range recommended for respiratory comfort, a whole-home bypass or fan-powered humidifier integrated with your HVAC system maintains consistent moisture levels without the maintenance burden of portable units.
