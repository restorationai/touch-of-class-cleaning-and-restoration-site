## Why San Bernardino Properties See More Garbage Disposal Problems

## Our Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation Process in San Bernardino

When we arrive, the first thing we do is listen. A disposal that hums but won't spin points to a jammed plate or a tripped overload. One that leaks from the bottom usually means the internal seal has failed, a repair that rarely makes financial sense on a unit more than eight years old. A disposal that simply won't turn on at all often has a tripped reset button or a failed outlet, not a dead motor.

For repairs, we carry the hex wrenches and reset tools to free jammed plates on-site, and we stock replacement seals and mounting assemblies for the most common brands, InSinkErator, Moen, and Waste King, so a leaking unit doesn't automatically mean a return trip.

For installations, we match the unit's horsepower to the household's actual use pattern and the drain line diameter we find under the sink. A single-person household with a 3-inch stub-out needs a different disposal than a large family kitchen with a dishwasher draining into the same line. We make that call based on what's actually there, not a default recommendation.
