Mold doesn't announce itself. It grows behind drywall, under subfloor, inside HVAC ducts — often for weeks before anyone notices the musty smell or the dark staining along a baseboard. If you suspect mold in your home or building, the first step isn't remediation. It's knowing exactly what you're dealing with: where it is, how far it's spread, and what species you're looking at. That's what a professional mold inspection and testing assessment delivers — a clear, documented picture so you can act on facts, not guesswork.

## How We Handle Mold Inspection

A thorough mold inspection is part detective work, part science. When a technician arrives at your Federal Way property — or anywhere in our surrounding service area — the process starts with a full visual walkthrough. We're looking for moisture intrusion points: roof penetrations, plumbing chases, crawl space vapor barriers, window seals, and anywhere condensation collects seasonally. Western Washington's wet winters and mild summers create near-perfect conditions for mold colonization, particularly in older construction with inadequate vapor barriers or in newer builds where a slow leak went undetected.

After the visual assessment, we move into air sampling and surface testing. Air samples are collected using calibrated spore trap cassettes at multiple locations — typically one outdoors as a baseline, then one or more indoors in areas of concern. Surface samples (tape lifts or swabs) are taken directly from visible growth. All samples are sent to an accredited third-party laboratory, which returns a full spore count and species identification, usually within 24–48 hours. You receive a written report that includes the lab results, our technician's findings, moisture readings from a calibrated hygrometer and thermal imaging camera, and a clear remediation recommendation if one is warranted.

The full on-site inspection typically takes one to three hours depending on the size and complexity of the property. We don't rush it — a missed moisture source is a future mold problem.

## When to Call Us

Some situations make the decision obvious. If you've had a water loss — a burst pipe, a roof leak, flooding from a storm — and the affected area wasn't dried out within 24 to 48 hours, mold spore germination is likely already underway. At that point, mold testing isn't optional; it's the only way to know whether remediation is needed and where.

Other triggers are subtler:

- A persistent musty odor in a room, basement, or crawl space that doesn't go away after cleaning
- Occupants experiencing unexplained respiratory irritation, headaches, or allergy-like symptoms that improve when they leave the building
- Visible dark or greenish staining on walls, ceilings, or grout lines — especially after a past water event
- A home sale or purchase where the inspection flagged moisture issues
- A rental property complaint from a tenant
- An HVAC system that hasn't been inspected in years and services a building with a history of leaks

One thing not to do before we arrive: don't paint over visible growth, spray it with bleach, or disturb it with a fan or vacuum. Disturbing mold colonies releases spores into the air and can spread contamination to areas that were previously clean. Leave it alone, reduce foot traffic in the affected area, and call us.

## What Sets Our Mold Inspection Team Apart

National Restoration Construction holds IICRC certification, EPA certification, and is an ANSI Certified firm — the same standards that insurance carriers and industrial hygienists look for when evaluating a remediation recommendation. Our general contractor license (#NATIORC792M6) means that if testing confirms a remediation scope, we can carry the project from assessment through reconstruction under one roof, without handing you off to a second contractor.

We use thermal imaging as a standard part of every mold assessment, not an upsell. Infrared cameras detect temperature differentials behind walls and under floors that indicate trapped moisture — often revealing hidden growth that a visual inspection alone would miss. Combined with pin and pinless moisture meters, we can map the full extent of a moisture problem before a single sample is collected.

Response time matters here too. Mold doesn't pause while you wait for a callback. We're based in Federal Way and serve King, Pierce, and Snohomish counties. Most calls receive a same-day or next-day inspection appointment.

## Insurance, Billing, and the Claims Process

Whether mold inspection and testing is covered by your homeowner's policy depends on the cause. Mold that results from a sudden, covered water loss — like a burst pipe or appliance failure — is often covered under the same claim. Mold from long-term seepage or deferred maintenance typically is not. We work directly with insurance carriers and can provide the documentation adjusters need: moisture maps, lab reports, photo logs, and a written scope of work.

If you're filing a claim, call us before you call your insurer if possible. We can help you document the loss correctly from the start, which reduces disputes and delays later in the process.

If you're paying out of pocket, we'll give you a straight answer on cost during the initial call — no obligation, no pressure.
