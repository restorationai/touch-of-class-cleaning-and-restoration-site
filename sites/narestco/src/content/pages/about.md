National Restoration Construction has been handling water damage, fire damage, mold, and full reconstruction work in the greater Seattle–Tacoma corridor since 2004. Two decades in this industry means the company has worked through every combination of soggy Pacific Northwest winter, burst pipe, kitchen fire, and slow-leak mold situation the region produces — and has built the systems to respond to them quickly, at any hour.

## What we do

On a typical day, that means a technician is loading equipment into a van before sunrise to meet a homeowner whose dishwasher supply line failed overnight, or walking a property manager through what the moisture readings in her tenant's bathroom actually mean. It means being on the phone with an insurance adjuster at 9 p.m. so a family can get a clear answer before they go to sleep. Restoration work is not a 9-to-5 job — damage doesn't schedule itself — so the company operates around the clock, 365 days a year. The calls that come in at 2 a.m. get the same response as the ones that come in at noon.

A significant portion of the work involves navigating insurance claims directly alongside homeowners. That means documenting losses in the format carriers expect, communicating with adjusters, and making sure the scope of work reflects what actually needs to happen — not just what's easiest to approve.

## Our certifications and licensure

The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — sets the technical standards the restoration industry runs on. IICRC certification means technicians are trained to those standards: how to extract and dry structural materials correctly, how to contain and remediate mold without cross-contaminating clean areas, how to document work in a way that holds up to scrutiny.

The EPA Lead-Safe Certified Firm designation matters specifically for homes built before 1978. Federal law requires contractors who disturb painted surfaces in those homes to follow lead-safe work practices — it's not optional, and not every contractor in the region holds it. National Restoration Construction does.

The company is also licensed as a General Contractor in Washington State (Certificate of Registration #NATIORC792M6, issued by the WA Department of Labor & Industries), BBB Accredited, and ANSI Certified. These aren't decorative — they represent the baseline legal and professional requirements for doing this work correctly and carrying the insurance that protects homeowners if something goes wrong on the job.

## Where we work

The primary service area runs through King and Pierce Counties — Federal Way, Auburn, Kent, Renton, Tacoma, Puyallup, Bellevue, and the surrounding communities. This stretch of the I-5 corridor sees consistent moisture challenges: wet winters, older housing stock in neighborhoods like Lakeland Hills and the Hilltop district, and the kind of crawl space and basement conditions that reward fast response. The company knows the area's building patterns well enough to move quickly without having to figure out the geography first.
