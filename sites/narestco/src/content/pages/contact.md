If water is spreading, smoke is still in the air, or you're facing something you can't wait on — call **(206) 883-0333** right now. Every hour matters, and someone answers around the clock.

## For Emergencies

Flood water, fire damage, sewage backup, biohazard — these don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. Call **(206) 883-0333** any time, day or night, and a live team member will pick up, not a voicemail box. While you wait for a technician to arrive, stay out of standing water if there's any chance of electrical contact, open windows if smoke odor is heavy, and don't run HVAC systems that could spread contaminated air. We'll walk you through anything else on the phone before we get there.

## For Estimates and Non-Urgent Inquiries

If you're not in an active emergency — you noticed a slow leak last week, you want a mold inspection scheduled, or you have questions about an insurance claim in progress — email us at **info@narestco.com** or use the contact form on this page. We respond to non-emergency messages within one business day, typically sooner. If your question involves an open insurance claim, have your claim number handy; it speeds things up considerably.

## Where We're Located

Our office and dispatch hub sits at **1530 S Dash Point Rd, Federal Way, WA 98003**, near the intersection of Federal Way and the Puget Sound shoreline communities. From here we run crews across the South King County and Pierce County corridor — including Tacoma, Auburn, Kent, Renton, Burien, Des Moines, Puyallup, and surrounding areas. If you're unsure whether you're in our service area, call or email; we'd rather tell you directly than have you guess.
