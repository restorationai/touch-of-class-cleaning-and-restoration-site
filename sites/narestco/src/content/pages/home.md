Water soaking through your ceiling at midnight. Smoke smell baked into every wall after a kitchen fire. Black mold spreading behind the drywall you just pulled back. Whatever brought you here, the damage is real and the clock is already running. National Restoration Construction has been responding to exactly these situations in Federal Way and across the South Sound since 2004 — water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, storm damage, biohazard cleanup, and full reconstruction when a room or structure needs to be rebuilt from the studs.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — A burst pipe, an overflowing appliance, or a slow roof leak can saturate subfloors and wall cavities within hours. We extract standing water, deploy industrial drying equipment, and monitor moisture levels daily until the structure reads dry — typically within three to five days depending on materials affected.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Flames destroy what they touch, but smoke and soot travel through every duct, crack, and soft surface in a home. Effective restoration means cleaning and deodorizing the entire structure, not just the charred area, and documenting every item for your insurance adjuster.

**Mold remediation** — Mold can begin colonizing a wet surface in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Our EPA-certified team contains the affected area, removes compromised materials, and treats surfaces to address the source — not just the visible growth.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Western Washington wind events and heavy rain seasons routinely push water under rooflines and through compromised siding. When damage goes beyond mitigation, our general contractor license (#NATIORC792M6 through WA State L&I) lets us handle the rebuild without handing you off to a second company.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Twenty-plus years in business means we have handled the full range of what can go wrong in a Pacific Northwest home — from the slow moisture intrusion common in older Federal Way neighborhoods to catastrophic losses that require complete interior reconstruction. We are IICRC Certified, BBB Accredited, Lead-Safe Certified, EPA Certified, and ANSI Certified. On older homes — and Federal Way has a significant stock of housing built before 1980 — lead-safe and EPA protocols are not optional. We follow them by default.
