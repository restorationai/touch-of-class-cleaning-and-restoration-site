Water damage doesn't wait for business hours, and neither does the anxiety that comes with it. The guides here are written to help you make fast, informed decisions — whether that's shutting off the right valve, documenting damage before the adjuster arrives, or understanding why your contractor is recommending a specific drying method. You'll also find walkthroughs of the insurance claim process, because the paperwork side of a loss is often more confusing than the damage itself.

A lot of what we cover is specific to the Pacific Northwest. Western Washington's wet winters, the freeze-thaw cycles that crack foundations in the South Sound, the older housing stock in neighborhoods like Lakeland and Steel Lake that still carries original plumbing — these details matter when you're trying to figure out whether a damp crawl space is seasonal condensation or the start of a mold problem. We publish this content because useful information helps people protect their homes, full stop. If you end up calling us, great. If you read something here and handle it yourself, or ask better questions of whoever you do hire, that's a good outcome too.
