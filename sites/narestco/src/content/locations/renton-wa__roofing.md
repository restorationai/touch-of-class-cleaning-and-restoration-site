## Why Renton Roofs Wear Out Faster Than You'd Expect

The Pacific Northwest's reputation for rain understates what actually happens at the roof level. Renton averages well over 150 days of measurable precipitation per year, and the Cedar River valley creates localized moisture patterns that push humidity into attic spaces even on dry days. That persistent dampness is what accelerates shingle granule loss, softens decking, and gives moss a foothold within a few years of installation if the roof pitch and material choice are not matched to the exposure.

Homes in the Renton Highlands, many built during the postwar suburban expansion of the 1950s and 1960s, frequently have original or once-replaced three-tab shingles that are now past their rated lifespan. Older craftsman-era construction on Renton Hill often has lower-slope roof sections where standing water is a chronic problem, and original wood decking beneath later shingle layers can hide rot that only becomes visible during a full tear-off. ## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Renton

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system, not just the shingles. We check decking condition, flashing at chimneys and dormers, ridge and soffit ventilation, and the condition of any existing underlayment. In Renton's climate, proper ventilation is not optional: an under-ventilated attic traps warm moist air that condenses on the underside of the decking, causing rot from the inside out regardless of how new the shingles are.

Once the scope is confirmed, we pull the required permits through the City of Renton's permit office. Renton requires a roofing permit for full replacements, and inspections are scheduled through the city's building division. We manage that process so homeowners are not navigating permit applications on their own. Tear-off and decking repair happen before any new material goes down, and we do not skip the ice-and-water shield at eaves and valleys, which is required by Washington's residential building code and is especially important given Renton's rainfall intensity.

Material selection is calibrated to the specific property. Steeper-pitched roofs on Renton Hill craftsman homes typically perform well with architectural shingles rated for high-wind and algae resistance. Low-slope sections on older homes may be better served by modified bitumen or TPO systems rather than shingles. We walk through the options with each property owner before anything is ordered.
