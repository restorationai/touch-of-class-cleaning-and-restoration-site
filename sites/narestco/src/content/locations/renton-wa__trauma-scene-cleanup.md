## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Renton

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. We use unmarked vehicles when requested, and we coordinate directly with the property owner, next of kin, or property manager — whoever is the appropriate point of contact — before entering the space. No assumptions, no unnecessary personnel.

From there, the process follows IICRC and ANSI standards for biological decontamination:

- **Containment and assessment** — We establish a defined work perimeter to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or units.
- **Removal of affected materials** — Only what testing and visual assessment confirms must go. We document everything for insurance purposes.
- **Treatment and disinfection** — Hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants, applied in the correct dwell times, not rushed.
- **Air quality verification** — Particularly relevant in older Renton homes where HVAC systems can distribute particulates through the structure.
- **Clearance documentation** — A written record of what was done, what was removed, and the post-remediation condition of the space.

All waste is transported and disposed of in compliance with Washington State Department of Ecology requirements for biohazardous materials.
