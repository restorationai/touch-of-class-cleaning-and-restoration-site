## Why Gig Harbor Homes Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock along the harbor rim and up through the Finholm District includes a significant number of pre-1960 structures built with old-growth fir framing, board-and-batten sheathing, and minimal vapor barriers. These materials are extraordinarily absorbent. Smoke particles, pet dander proteins, and microbial off-gassing from mold don't just coat surfaces — they penetrate into the wood grain itself. In a newer home with OSB sheathing and vinyl flooring, a single round of thermal fogging might be sufficient. In a 1940s fishing-era cottage near Skansie Brothers Park, the same treatment may need to be followed by hydroxyl deodorization running continuously for 48 to 72 hours to reach odor molecules embedded several layers deep.

The vented crawl space design common to steep shoreline lots in the 98335 ZIP code creates a secondary odor pathway that many contractors miss entirely. Ground moisture migrates upward, carrying fungal spores and the musty smell of decomposing organic material into living spaces through floor register gaps and unsealed penetrations. If the crawl space source isn't addressed alongside the visible interior, deodorization of the main living area will fail within weeks.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Gig Harbor

Every job starts with a source assessment, not a spray bottle. A technician walks the structure — attic, crawl space, wall cavities where accessible — to identify every origin point before any treatment begins. Skipping this step is the reason most DIY and low-cost deodorization attempts fail.

Once the source is confirmed and any underlying water or mold damage is stabilized, we select the appropriate treatment method or combination of methods:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog that penetrates porous surfaces and neutralizes odor molecules at the molecular level; particularly effective after smoke and fire events
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone generation in unoccupied spaces; breaks down organic odor compounds but requires strict re-occupancy protocols
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — UV-generated hydroxyl radicals that work continuously in occupied or semi-occupied spaces; slower than ozone but safer for homes with pets or sensitive materials
- **Encapsulant sealing** — applied to framing or subfloor surfaces where odor-saturated wood cannot be replaced; locks residual compounds below the surface before new finishes are installed

For smoke odor removal specifically, we also clean HVAC ductwork and replace filters, because recirculated air will recontaminate a freshly treated space if the distribution system is ignored.

## Local Note: Crawl Space Ventilation and Odor Recurrence

One pattern we see repeatedly in Gig Harbor that surprises homeowners: a musty or mildew odor returns within a few months of treatment, even after a thorough job. In most cases, the culprit is the vented crawl space design itself. The original building code rationale for venting crawl spaces — allowing moisture to escape — works poorly in a maritime climate where ambient outdoor humidity is high year-round. Venting a crawl space in Gig Harbor can actually introduce more moisture than it removes during the wet season. We often recommend converting vented crawl spaces to sealed, conditioned crawl spaces as a permanent odor-prevention measure, and we can coordinate that work alongside deodorization rather than treating it as a separate future project.
