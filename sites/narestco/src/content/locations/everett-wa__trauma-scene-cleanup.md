## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Everett

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. We use unmarked vehicles when requested, and we coordinate entry with whoever is managing the property — whether that's a family member, a property manager, or a legal representative. We do not begin work until we understand who has authority over the space.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment and assessment** — We identify the affected surfaces and materials, document conditions for insurance purposes, and establish a contained work zone to prevent cross-contamination.
- **Removal of affected materials** — Porous materials that cannot be fully decontaminated are removed and disposed of in accordance with Washington State Department of Ecology guidelines for biohazardous waste.
- **Cleaning and disinfection** — We apply hospital-grade disinfectants to all affected hard surfaces, using EPA-registered products appropriate for the specific biological agents present.
- **Deodorization and air treatment** — HEPA filtration and hydroxyl generator technology address airborne particulates and odor. In Everett's frequently damp conditions, we run dehumidification concurrently to prevent secondary contamination.
- **Final clearance documentation** — We provide written documentation of completed work, which your insurance carrier and any future occupants may require.
