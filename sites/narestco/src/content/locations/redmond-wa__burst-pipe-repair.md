## Why Redmond Properties See Burst Pipe Emergencies

Redmond sits in a part of the Pacific Northwest where winters are mild by national standards but still cold enough to cause real problems. A hard freeze — the kind that arrives in January after a week of temperatures hovering just above freezing — can catch supply lines in uninsulated garage walls, crawl spaces, or exterior-facing cabinets completely off guard. The housing stock in neighborhoods like Education Hill, Grass Lawn, and Overlake includes a significant number of homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, many with original copper or galvanized steel supply lines that have been quietly corroding for decades. Add in the region's high water pressure from municipal service and the pressure fluctuations that follow a sudden cold snap, and you have conditions where a water line break can happen with almost no warning.

Newer construction isn't immune either. PEX tubing — common in Redmond homes built after 2000 — handles freeze-thaw cycles better than copper, but fittings at manifolds and shutoff valves remain vulnerable, and a single failed crimp ring can release hundreds of gallons before anyone notices.

## Insurance Claims for Burst Pipe Damage

Sudden and accidental pipe bursts are covered under most standard homeowners' policies in Washington. What trips people up is documentation — adjusters need moisture readings, photo evidence of affected materials, and a written scope of work before they approve payment. We produce that documentation as part of our standard process, not as an add-on.

We work directly with all major carriers and can communicate with your adjuster on your behalf. A few things remain in your hands: filing the initial claim, paying your deductible, and decisions about policy-level coverage questions. But the technical paperwork — moisture logs, equipment records, line-item estimates in Xactimate format — we handle that.

One honest note: if a pipe has been leaking slowly for weeks or months, insurers may classify the damage as a maintenance issue rather than a sudden loss. If you're unsure how your situation will be categorized, call us first. We can help you understand what the documentation will show before you file.
