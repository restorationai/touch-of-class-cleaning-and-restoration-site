## What Trauma Scene Cleanup Involves in Port Orchard

Trauma scene cleanup is a specialized branch of biohazard remediation governed by OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards and Washington State Department of Labor & Industries regulations. It covers situations that go well beyond what a standard cleaning service is equipped or legally permitted to handle — including unattended deaths, accidents, and other events that leave behind biological material requiring proper containment, removal, and decontamination.

Our technicians arrive in unmarked vehicles. Work is conducted with full personal protective equipment and follows strict chain-of-custody protocols for waste disposal through licensed medical waste carriers. Every surface, porous material, and HVAC pathway that may have been affected is assessed before any work begins. The goal is complete decontamination verified by testing — not a surface wipe-down.
