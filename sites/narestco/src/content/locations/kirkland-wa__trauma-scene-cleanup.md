## Why Kirkland's Housing Stock Shapes Trauma Cleanup Work

Kirkland is not a uniform city. The older waterfront homes in Houghton and along Juanita Bay were built in an era when subfloor construction, wall cavities, and HVAC layouts looked very different from what you find in a 2018 Finn Hill townhome. Porous materials — original hardwood subfloors, older plaster, uncoated concrete in utility spaces — absorb biological contaminants more aggressively than modern surfaces and require additional treatment cycles to reach the decontamination standards set by OSHA and Washington State Department of Labor & Industries.

The Pacific Northwest climate compounds this. Kirkland's wet winters and moderate humidity mean that even after a scene is visually clean, residual moisture in porous substrates can accelerate microbial growth if remediation stops short of full decontamination. Our IICRC-certified technicians test affected surfaces rather than assume they're clear, which matters especially in older homes where moisture already has established pathways through the structure.

## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Kirkland

Every job begins with a confidential assessment — no marked trucks, no unnecessary personnel on-site. A project lead walks the affected area, identifies all impacted surfaces and materials, and outlines a written scope before any work begins. That scope goes to you (and, if applicable, your insurance carrier) before we touch anything.

Decontamination follows a strict sequence: containment of the affected zone, removal of non-salvageable materials in sealed, compliant waste containers, treatment of all porous and semi-porous surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants, and a final ATP or surface-swab verification test. Odor control — using hydroxyl generators or thermal fogging depending on the space — is addressed as a separate, dedicated phase, not an afterthought. Structural repairs, if needed, are handled under our General Contractor license (#NATIORC792M6), so you're not coordinating a second contractor while you're already managing a difficult situation.
