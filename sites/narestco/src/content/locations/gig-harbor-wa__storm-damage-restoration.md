## Why Gig Harbor Properties See Disproportionate Storm Damage

Gig Harbor's maritime position on the Kitsap Peninsula means it catches weather systems that have already picked up moisture crossing Puget Sound. That persistent marine humidity — the kind that keeps the Gig Harbor waterfront looking perpetually damp even on clear days — is already working against your home before a named storm arrives. When wind gusts spike, the combination of salt air and mechanical force is particularly hard on wood-framed structures.

The older cottages and fishing-era houses clustered in Harborview and Millville are especially vulnerable. Many were built on vented crawl spaces that made sense for the era but create a direct pathway for storm-driven ground moisture and wind-pushed rain to pool beneath the floor system. Once water sits in a crawl space after a storm, fungal colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours in Gig Harbor's cool, humid baseline conditions — faster than most homeowners expect. Steep shoreline lots compound the problem: drainage that looks fine on a dry day overwhelms during a sustained blow, and water finds the foundation before it finds the street.

View homes along the harbor rim face a different but related issue. Exposed ridge positions that make for spectacular scenery also mean sustained wind exposure, and roofing materials — especially on homes built before modern wind-resistance standards — can lose shingles or flashing in gusts that wouldn't trouble a sheltered inland property.
