## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Bremerton

Every inspection starts with a visual assessment calibrated to what we actually find in Bremerton homes: crawl space access panels, basement rim joists, bathroom exhaust routes that terminate in attic cavities rather than outside, and any plumbing that runs through an unconditioned space. We use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to map wet zones that aren't yet visible to the eye — critical in plaster-walled homes where moisture migrates slowly and hides behind surfaces that look and feel dry.

Air sampling follows. We collect spore trap samples from each area of concern plus a control sample from outside the building, then send everything to an accredited third-party laboratory. Results typically return within 24–48 hours and include a species breakdown and spore count comparison against outdoor baseline levels. If surface sampling is warranted — on visible growth or staining — we collect tape-lift or bulk samples at the same time. You receive a written report that documents findings, lab results, and recommended next steps, formatted to satisfy insurance adjusters and property managers alike.
