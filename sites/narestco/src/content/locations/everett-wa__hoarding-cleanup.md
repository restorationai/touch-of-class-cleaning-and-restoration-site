## Why Everett Homes Can Be Especially Challenging for Hoarding Remediation

Everett's housing stock spans nearly a century of construction, and that range creates real variation in what a cleanup team encounters. Older homes in the Riverside and Lowell neighborhoods — many built before 1960 — often have unfinished basements, crawl spaces with minimal vapor barriers, and original hardwood or fir subfloors that absorb moisture and odor far more readily than modern materials. When accumulated belongings have been in place for years, those surfaces can hold biological material, mold spore colonies, and pest evidence that aren't visible until the space is cleared.

Snohomish County's wet winters compound this. Everett averages around 38 inches of rain annually, and low-lying areas near the Snohomish River see periodic flooding that can push groundwater into crawl spaces — sometimes unnoticed beneath stacked belongings. By the time a cleanup is initiated, a secondary moisture or mold issue may already be present. Our IICRC-certified technicians assess for both during the initial walkthrough, so nothing gets missed before remediation begins.

## Our Hoarding Cleanup Process in Everett

Every project starts with a confidential, no-pressure site assessment. We document conditions for insurance and legal purposes if needed, then work with the client — or their designated family member or attorney — to establish a sorting protocol before anything leaves the property. Nothing is discarded without authorization.

From there, the process moves in deliberate phases:

- **Sorting and removal** — items are categorized for salvage, donation, or disposal according to the client's direction. We work at a pace that respects the emotional weight of the process.
- **Structural assessment** — subfloors, walls, and crawl spaces are inspected for moisture intrusion, pest activity, or compromised materials that need remediation beyond standard cleaning.
- **Deep cleaning and sanitization** — surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. As a Lead-Safe Certified Firm, we follow strict containment protocols in pre-1978 homes, which make up a significant portion of the older Everett neighborhoods we serve.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation may be used depending on the severity and the home's ventilation characteristics.
- **Final documentation** — photos and a written summary are provided, which can support insurance claims, probate proceedings, or property sales.

## Local Note

One pattern we've seen repeatedly in Bayside and the older blocks near Forest Park: homes with original cedar siding and minimal attic insulation tend to trap humidity in ways that accelerate secondary mold growth once a hoarding situation is cleared. The clearing itself changes the airflow inside the home — suddenly, surfaces that were insulated by stacked belongings are exposed to ambient moisture. We account for this by running dehumidification during and after cleanup, not just after, which shortens the overall remediation window and reduces the chance of a mold issue emerging weeks later.
