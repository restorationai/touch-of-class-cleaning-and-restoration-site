## Our Biohazard and Trauma Cleanup Process in Port Orchard

Every job begins with a confidential assessment — no marked vehicles, no unnecessary personnel on-site. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — Affected areas are isolated using negative air pressure and physical barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or HVAC systems.
- **Removal and packaging** — All biohazardous materials are collected, packaged, and labeled in compliance with Washington State Department of Ecology regulations and transported by licensed medical waste carriers.
- **Surface decontamination** — We apply hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected hard and porous surfaces. - **Clearance documentation** — We provide written documentation of completed remediation, which insurers and property managers routinely require before a space can be reoccupied.
