## Why Renton Properties Are Prone to Persistent Odors

Renton sits at the south end of Lake Washington, and the climate shows it. From October through April, the region averages over 30 inches of rain, and older homes in neighborhoods like Highlands, Kennydale, and the Renton Hill area — many built in the 1950s through 1970s — often have crawl spaces and basements that collect moisture year after year. That moisture creates the conditions mold and mildew need to colonize within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion event, and the musty, earthy odor that follows can migrate through subfloor systems into living spaces well before visible growth appears.

Smoke odor is the other major driver. Renton's mix of older wood-frame single-family homes and multi-unit apartment buildings near downtown and the Highlands means that even a contained kitchen fire can push smoke residue — soot particles and volatile organic compounds — deep into insulation, HVAC ductwork, and porous surfaces like drywall and carpet backing. That residue doesn't just smell; it continues off-gassing for months if it isn't treated at the source.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process

Professional odor elimination isn't a single step. It's a sequence, and skipping any part of it is why DIY attempts and general cleaning services rarely hold.

**Assessment first.** A technician walks the property with calibrated moisture meters and air quality tools to map where the odor is concentrated and what's causing it. Treating a smoke odor the same way you'd treat a sewage odor produces poor results — the source dictates the method.

**Source removal.** Deodorization can't overpower a contaminated material that's still in the building. Charred wood, saturated insulation, or mold-colonized drywall has to come out before treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging.** For smoke odor removal, thermal fogging disperses a deodorizing solvent as a fine fog that penetrates the same surfaces smoke particles reached — inside wall cavities, behind baseboards, into upholstery fibers. The fog bonds with odor-causing compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.

**Hydroxyl deodorization and ozone treatment.** Hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules continuously and are safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces. Ozone treatment is reserved for vacated areas — ozone at effective concentrations is a powerful oxidizer that eliminates odors embedded in porous materials, but the space must be cleared of people, pets, and plants during treatment and properly ventilated afterward. We determine which technology fits your situation based on the source, the severity, and the occupancy status of the property.

**Air scrubbing and clearance.** HEPA air scrubbers run throughout the process to capture particulates. Before we leave, we verify that odor levels have returned to acceptable thresholds — not just that the space smells better to us in the moment.
