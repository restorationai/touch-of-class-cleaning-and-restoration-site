If your roof is showing lifted tabs, granule loss in the gutters, or soft spots near the ridge, those are not cosmetic problems, they are the early stages of a failure that the next stretch of Pacific Northwest rain will make significantly worse. National Restoration Construction handles roofing installation and replacement for Fife properties, working from our Federal Way base with a crew that understands what this specific stretch of Pierce County demands from a roof.

## Why Fife Properties Experience Accelerated Roof Wear

The geography here works against roofing materials in ways that are easy to underestimate. Fife's position in the Puyallup River valley means fog and moisture linger at ground level and collect on roof surfaces even when it is not actively raining. That persistent dampness feeds algae and moss growth, which lifts shingle edges and breaks down the asphalt binder underneath. Homes in the city's older residential pockets, many built during the post-war industrial expansion tied to the port and rail yards, often still have original roof decking that absorbs moisture once the shingles above it degrade. By the time a leak appears at the ceiling, the decking may already be soft in multiple locations.

Commercial and light-industrial properties along the I-5 corridor face a different set of stressors: low-slope or flat membrane roofs that pool water if drains are even partially clogged, and foot traffic from HVAC maintenance that punctures membranes over time. Both residential and commercial owners in Fife tend to discover roofing problems later than they should, because the damage often starts at the deck or underlayment level where it is not visible from the ground.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Fife

Every project starts with a full deck inspection before any material is ordered. In Fife's climate, skipping that step means installing new shingles over compromised plywood or OSB that will fail within a few seasons regardless of shingle quality. We remove existing roofing down to the deck, probe for soft spots, and replace deteriorated sections before the underlayment goes down.

Underlayment selection matters more here than in drier climates. We use synthetic underlayment rated for high-moisture exposure rather than the felt paper that was standard on most older Fife homes. Ice-and-water shield is installed at eaves and valleys, which are the two locations where standing water from Puget Sound storms most reliably finds a path through. Ridge ventilation is calibrated to the attic volume of the specific structure, because inadequate ventilation in a damp climate traps condensation against the underside of the deck and shortens roof life from the inside out.

For material selection, architectural asphalt shingles with a Class 4 impact rating perform well across Pierce County's storm season and carry insurance discount eligibility with many carriers. We also install metal roofing, which handles moss resistance and longevity better than asphalt in this climate, and TPO or modified bitumen for flat and low-slope commercial applications.

On older Fife homes where lead paint may be present on fascia or trim disturbed during a tear-off, Lead-Safe protocols are followed without exception.
