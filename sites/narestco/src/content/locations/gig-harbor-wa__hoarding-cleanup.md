## Why Gig Harbor Homes Present Unique Hoarding Remediation Challenges

The Kitsap Peninsula's salt-air climate is relentless. Relative humidity in Gig Harbor routinely sits above 70% through the fall and winter months, and homes that have been closed up for extended periods — common in hoarding situations — hold that moisture in. In the older Harborview and Millville neighborhoods, vented crawl spaces are the norm rather than the exception. Ground moisture migrates upward through subfloor assemblies, and when airflow is restricted by floor-level accumulation, fungal growth can establish itself in as little as 48 to 72 hours after a minor moisture event. By the time a hoarding cleanup is initiated, secondary mold or odor conditions are frequently present and need to be assessed alongside the primary work.

Steep shoreline lots — particularly those draining toward foundations near the harbor rim — compound the issue. Water that pools against a foundation during a wet season can saturate crawl spaces undetected for months. Our IICRC-certified technicians assess moisture levels in the subfloor and wall cavities before any sorting or removal begins, so remediation addresses the full scope of what's present, not just what's visible.

## Our Hoarding Cleanup Process in Gig Harbor

Every engagement starts with a private, no-pressure walkthrough — no judgment, no unnecessary personnel on site. We work with the property owner, family members, or a designated representative to understand what matters, what can be released, and what the property's end goal is.

From there, the process moves in defined stages:

- **Assessment and moisture mapping** — We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to identify any water intrusion or fungal growth before removal begins. In Gig Harbor's older housing stock, this step frequently reveals crawl-space or subfloor conditions that need to be addressed concurrently.
- **Categorized sorting and removal** — Items are sorted into categories: retain, donate, recycle, and dispose. Nothing is discarded without authorization. We use sealed, odor-controlled containers for materials that require careful handling.
- **Gross filth and biohazard remediation** — Where applicable, affected surfaces are treated by EPA-certified technicians following OSHA protocols. We hold a Lead-Safe Certified Firm designation, which matters in pre-1978 homes — common in the Millville and Finholm District areas.
- **Structural cleaning and deodorization** — Walls, floors, and HVAC surfaces are cleaned and treated. Persistent odors from long-term accumulation require commercial-grade deodorization equipment, not consumer products.
- **Final documentation** — We provide written records of work completed, which insurers and property managers often require to close a claim or prepare a home for sale or transfer.
