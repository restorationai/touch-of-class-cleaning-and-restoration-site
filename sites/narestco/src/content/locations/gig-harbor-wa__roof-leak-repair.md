## Why Gig Harbor Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Roof Leak Damage

Gig Harbor's maritime climate is the defining factor. Annual rainfall averages around 50 inches, but it's the relentless low-grade drizzle — not dramatic downpours — that does the slow, invisible work. Roofing materials stay damp for weeks at a stretch, accelerating the breakdown of asphalt shingles, wood shake, and the butyl-tape seals around skylights and chimneys.

The older fishing-era cottages and bungalows in the Harborview and Millville neighborhoods compound the problem. Many of these homes were built in the mid-20th century on vented crawl spaces, with roof assemblies that weren't designed for continuous moisture exposure. Attic ventilation is often undersized or partially blocked by added insulation — a combination that traps humid air against the sheathing and accelerates fungal colonization within 24 to 48 hours of a leak event. Salt air carried up from the harbor rim adds a corrosive element that shortens the lifespan of metal flashings faster than you'd see in an inland community.

Steep shoreline lots throughout the 98335 ZIP code also mean roof pitches are frequently severe, drainage runs hard toward foundations, and any water that enters the building envelope has momentum behind it.

## Our Roof Leak Cleanup and Repair Process in Gig Harbor

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across the attic deck, insulation batt, ceiling cavity, and wall framing below the leak path. We don't guess at the water's travel route; we trace it.

Once the scope is clear, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Emergency tarping and source control** — if the roof is still open to weather, we secure it before interior work begins. Gig Harbor's unpredictable shoulder-season storms mean a temporary roof cover isn't optional.
2. **Saturated material removal** — wet insulation, damaged sheathing, and compromised drywall are removed and documented for your insurance adjuster. We photograph and itemize everything.
3. **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run on a monitored drying schedule, typically 3–5 days depending on attic depth and ambient humidity. We pull daily moisture readings and adjust equipment placement.
4. **Roof repair and interior rebuild** — as an IICRC-certified firm and licensed Washington State general contractor (#NATIORC792M6), we handle both the restoration side and the finished repair, including shingle replacement, flashing, and ceiling or drywall work — no handoff to a second contractor.

## Gig Harbor Insurance Coordination

Roof leak claims in Gig Harbor frequently involve a coverage question that catches homeowners off guard: insurers distinguish between sudden-and-accidental water intrusion (typically covered) and gradual deterioration (typically excluded). The slow-drip nature of maritime climate damage means adjusters sometimes push toward the gradual-damage exclusion. We document the loss with the specificity — moisture readings, thermal images, timeline of material saturation — that gives your adjuster a clear picture of when the acute event occurred versus pre-existing wear. We work directly with all major carriers and can coordinate a joint scope meeting with your adjuster on-site.

One thing consistent across Gig Harbor jobs, particularly in the older Harborview cottages near Skansie Brothers Park: attic sheathing in these homes is often old-growth fir or hemlock, which holds moisture differently than modern OSB.
