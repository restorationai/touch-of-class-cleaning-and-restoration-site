Gig Harbor is a close-knit maritime community, and when something traumatic happens inside a home — whether on the quiet residential streets of Artondale or in one of the older fishing-era cottages tucked along the Millville waterfront — the last thing a family should have to manage is the cleanup. National Restoration Construction responds to trauma scene situations with the discretion, technical training, and proper certifications this work demands. ## Why Gig Harbor's Housing Stock Shapes Trauma Cleanup Work

Gig Harbor's older homes — particularly the pre-1960 cottages and bungalows in Harborview and Millville that date back to the peninsula's commercial fishing era — present specific conditions that affect how decontamination work is planned and executed. Many of these structures were built with vented crawl spaces and tongue-and-groove subfloor assemblies that have absorbed decades of marine moisture from the salt-air climate off the harbor. Biological materials can migrate into porous subfloor materials more readily than in newer construction with sealed concrete slabs, which means assessment has to go deeper than the visible surface.

The persistent marine moisture common to the Kitsap Peninsula also means that any porous material left in place after an incident is at elevated risk of secondary microbial growth — a concern our IICRC-certified technicians account for during the initial scope assessment. Lead-Safe Certified Firm protocols are applied on pre-1978 structures, which covers a significant portion of the housing stock in the older neighborhoods closest to the harbor rim.

## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Gig Harbor

Every trauma cleanup engagement begins with a private, unmarked arrival. We coordinate directly with the property owner, family representative, or legal authority who has authorized access — no unnecessary personnel, no visible branding on the vehicle if requested. The process follows ANSI and OSHA standards for bloodborne pathogen remediation and biohazard decontamination.

After the affected area is secured, technicians conduct a thorough assessment of all surfaces — flooring, walls, subfloor, HVAC intake proximity — before any removal begins. Affected materials are bagged, containerized, and transported in compliance with Washington State Department of Ecology requirements for regulated biological waste. Once physical removal is complete, the area is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the specific pathogen risk. A final clearance check confirms the space is safe for re-occupancy before the crew leaves the site.

Documentation is provided throughout — both for the property owner's records and for submission to a homeowner's insurance carrier, which is how most trauma cleanup work in Gig Harbor is ultimately billed.

## Local Note

One thing our crews have learned working in Gig Harbor's older shoreline neighborhoods: the vented crawl spaces under many Harborview and Millville homes create a draft pathway that can carry odor — and in some cases, biological contamination — horizontally beneath the floor deck. In newer construction this pathway is typically sealed. In these older homes, a proper trauma cleanup scope must include crawl-space inspection and, where necessary, treatment of the underfloor assembly. Skipping that step and treating only the visible floor surface above is one of the most common reasons a property owner calls a second company after the first one has already left.
