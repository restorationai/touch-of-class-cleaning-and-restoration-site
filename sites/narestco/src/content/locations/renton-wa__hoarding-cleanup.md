## Why Renton Properties Present Unique Hoarding Cleanup Challenges

Renton's housing stock spans a wide range of eras and construction types. The craftsman-era homes on Renton Hill and in the Kennydale corridor were built with smaller floor plans, narrower doorways, and basement crawlspaces that were never designed for the kind of remediation work that a long-term hoarding situation demands. Accumulated materials in these layouts compress into tight quarters, and the basement moisture common to homes near the Cedar River watershed means that organic materials stored at grade level — cardboard, paper, fabric — can develop secondary biological concerns over time.

Beyond the structural quirks, Renton sits in a climate zone where the wet season runs roughly October through April. Homes in the 98055 and 98056 ZIP codes that have experienced deferred maintenance alongside hoarding conditions often show compounding issues: softened subfloors, compromised vapor barriers, and in some cases pest activity that must be documented and addressed before cleanup can proceed. Our teams assess all of these factors during the initial walkthrough so that nothing is missed and no scope is understated.

## Our Hoarding Cleanup Process in Renton

Every project begins with a private, no-pressure assessment. We come to the property — not a call center — and we listen before we plan. From there, the process moves in deliberate phases:

**Phase 1 — Sorting and categorization.** Items are separated with the property owner or designated family member present when possible. Nothing of potential value is discarded without consent. We document the process photographically for insurance and legal purposes if needed.

**Phase 2 — Removal and hauling.** We coordinate licensed waste hauling and, where applicable, donation drop-offs. For Renton addresses near The Landing or along the South Lake Washington shoreline, we confirm disposal routing in advance to avoid conflicts with commercial traffic patterns in that corridor.

**Phase 3 — Structural assessment and remediation.** Once the space is cleared, our Lead-Safe Certified technicians evaluate surfaces, subfloors, and HVAC components. In older Renton Hill craftsman homes, we pay particular attention to original hardwood and fir subfloor condition, which can be salvageable with proper treatment even after years of stress.

**Phase 4 — Cleaning, sanitizing, and deodorizing.** We use EPA-registered disinfectants and commercial-grade HEPA filtration throughout. The goal is a space that is safe, odor-neutral, and ready for whatever comes next — whether that's a family moving back in, a property sale, or a rental turnover.
