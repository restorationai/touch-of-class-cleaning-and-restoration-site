## What Crime Scene Cleanup Actually Involves in Federal Way

Forensic cleanup is regulated work, not a job for a general cleaning service. Biohazardous materials require containment, transport, and disposal under Washington State Department of Ecology guidelines and OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen standards. Our technicians are IICRC certified and operate as an EPA Certified firm, which matters when a property contains materials that require documented chain-of-custody disposal.

## Our Cleanup Process

Every job begins with a walk-through assessment before any work starts. We document conditions, identify affected materials, and establish a containment perimeter. Nothing is disturbed until the scope is clear.

From there, the process follows a consistent sequence:

- **Containment and PPE setup** — negative air pressure where needed, full personal protective equipment for all technicians
- **Removal of affected materials** — porous surfaces, soft goods, and structural components that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard
- **Surface decontamination** — EPA-registered disinfectants applied to all affected hard surfaces, with dwell times verified
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment depending on the space and HVAC configuration
- **Clearance verification** — ATP testing or independent third-party verification when requested by the property owner or insurer
- **Documentation package** — a written record of all materials removed and disposal manifests, which insurers and property managers routinely require

We work quietly and without marked vehicles when requested. Discretion is not an add-on — it is standard.
