## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Renton

Every job starts with a room-by-room assessment before a single cleaning product is opened. Smoke behaves differently depending on what burned — a kitchen grease fire leaves a wet, sticky residue that smears if you wipe it wrong; a structural fire involving synthetic materials leaves a dry, powdery soot that goes airborne the moment you disturb it. Misidentifying the residue type is one of the most common reasons DIY cleanup makes things worse.

Once we've mapped the damage, the process typically moves through these phases:

- **Containment and air scrubbing.** HEPA air scrubbers run continuously to capture airborne particulates and pull the odor compounds out of the breathing space before technicians begin surface work.
- **Dry and wet cleaning.** Dry chemical sponges lift loose soot from ceilings and walls without smearing. Wet cleaning agents — matched to the residue type — follow on hard surfaces, cabinetry, and structural materials.
- **HVAC and duct inspection.** Smoke travels wherever air moves. We inspect and, when necessary, clean ductwork so your system doesn't re-contaminate cleaned rooms the first time it cycles on.
- **Odor neutralization.** Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation break down odor molecules at the molecular level rather than masking them. This is the step that determines whether your home smells like itself again or like a campfire for the next two years.
- **Structural repair and reconstruction.** For surfaces too deeply saturated to clean — charred framing, smoke-stained drywall, damaged flooring — our general contractor license (WA L&I #NATIORC792M6) means we can take the job through full rebuild without handing you off to a second company.
