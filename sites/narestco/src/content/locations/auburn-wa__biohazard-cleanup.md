## What Our Biohazard and Trauma Cleanup Response Looks Like in Auburn

When our crew arrives, the first priority is containment — isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to the rest of the property. We work in full personal protective equipment and follow OSHA bloodborne pathogen protocols throughout every phase of the job.

From there, the process moves through removal of biologically contaminated materials, treatment of affected surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants, and air quality control using HEPA filtration equipment. Porous materials — flooring, drywall, subfloor — that cannot be fully decontaminated are removed and disposed of in accordance with Washington State Department of Ecology standards for biohazardous waste. We don't cut corners on disposal because improper handling creates liability for the property owner.

The final step is clearance verification. Before we leave, we confirm that the space meets safe re-occupancy standards. You'll receive documentation of the work completed, which matters both for your own records and for any insurance claim.
