## Our Mold Remediation Process in Bremerton

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging, pin-type and non-invasive moisture meters, and a visual assessment of likely intrusion points. We identify the water source first, because remediation without fixing the source is a temporary fix at best.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment:** Negative air pressure chambers using 6-mil poly sheeting isolate the affected area. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously to capture airborne spores before they migrate.
- **Controlled demolition:** Mold-compromised drywall, insulation, and in some cases structural wood is removed to clean material. In Bremerton's older homes, this often means working around knob-and-tube era wiring runs — we document and flag anything that warrants an electrician before we proceed.
- **Antimicrobial treatment:** EPA-registered antimicrobial agents are applied to all affected surfaces, including framing cavities and subfloor sheathing.
- **Drying and verification:** Commercial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings return to regional baseline levels. We don't close a job until clearance readings confirm the environment is dry.
- **Reconstruction:** As a licensed general contractor (WA L&I #NATIORC792M6), we handle the rebuild — drywall, insulation, vapor barriers — so you're not coordinating a second contractor after remediation.

## Local Note: What Plaster Walls Mean for Mold Jobs in Bremerton

In Bremerton's pre-1950 housing stock — especially the bungalows and craftsman-style homes in Manette and West Bremerton — interior walls are often original plaster over wood lath rather than drywall. Plaster is denser and absorbs moisture more slowly than modern gypsum board, which sounds like an advantage, but it also releases that moisture more slowly during drying. On jobs where mold has colonized the wall cavity behind plaster, drying cycles routinely run 30 to 50 percent longer than they would in a comparable newer home.
