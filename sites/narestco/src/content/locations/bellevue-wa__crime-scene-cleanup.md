## Our Process for Bellevue Properties

Every job begins with a documented assessment before any remediation work starts. We photograph and log the affected zones, identify all porous and semi-porous materials that may require removal, and establish a containment perimeter. This documentation protects you, your insurer, and — in HOA-governed properties — your property management company.

Remediation proceeds in stages: removal of non-salvageable materials, treatment of affected structural surfaces with hospital-grade disinfectants, odor neutralization using hydroxyl or thermal fogging technology depending on the space, and a final verification step using ATP testing or similar surface-contact methods to confirm pathogen levels are within safe parameters. All biohazardous waste is transported in sealed, labeled containers to a licensed medical waste disposal facility — we do not use standard waste haulers.

We work quietly and without marked vehicles when requested. Discretion is a standard part of how we operate, not an upgrade.
