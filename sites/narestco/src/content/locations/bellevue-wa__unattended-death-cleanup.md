## What Bellevue's Building Stock Means for This Type of Cleanup

Bellevue's residential landscape is unusually varied for a city its size. Newer townhome and condo developments in areas like Crossroads and Factoria are built with engineered flooring systems, spray-foam insulation, and vapor barriers that, while energy-efficient, trap odors and biological material in ways that older stick-frame construction does not. Odor compounds can migrate into HVAC ductwork, sub-floor cavities, and shared wall assemblies within 24 to 48 hours in a sealed modern unit.

Older ranch-style homes in Lake Hills and Newport Shores present a different challenge: original hardwood subfloors and older insulation batts absorb biological fluids more deeply, requiring more aggressive extraction and longer treatment cycles. Neither scenario is harder than the other — they're just different, and the remediation plan has to reflect the actual structure, not a generic checklist.

- **Post-remediation verification** — air and surface sampling, coordinated with an independent industrial hygienist when required, confirms the space meets clearance standards before we close out the job.
