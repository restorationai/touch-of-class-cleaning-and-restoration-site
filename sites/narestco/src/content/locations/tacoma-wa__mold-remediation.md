## Why Tacoma Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Mold

Tacoma sits in one of the wettest corners of the Pacific Northwest. Annual rainfall averages around 38 inches, but it's the persistent gray drizzle — months of humidity that never fully dries out — that creates the conditions mold thrives in. Crawl spaces under older Craftsman bungalows in neighborhoods like Proctor or Hilltop often lack adequate vapor barriers, and the original wood framing in homes built before 1970 absorbs moisture readily. Newer construction in areas like South End or Fircrest isn't immune either: tightly sealed building envelopes trap interior humidity, and a single slow plumbing leak behind a cabinet can go unnoticed long enough for a serious colony to take hold.

Common triggers we see in Tacoma properties include failed sump pumps during heavy rain events, condensation buildup in poorly ventilated attics, and water intrusion through aging concrete block foundations. The problem usually isn't dramatic — it's quiet, hidden, and already spreading by the time you smell it.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Tacoma

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace contamination beyond what's visible to the eye — because mold inside a wall cavity doesn't announce itself. Once we understand the full scope, we establish negative-air containment using heavy-gauge poly barriers and industrial air scrubbers equipped with HEPA filtration. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected areas of your home while work is underway.

Affected materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sections — are removed and bagged following IICRC S520 protocol. Structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, and dried to target moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We don't consider the job done until post-remediation air quality testing confirms the space is clear. Because we hold a General Contractor Certificate of Registration through WA State Department of Labor & Industries (#NATIORC792M6), we can handle the rebuild — framing, drywall, insulation, finish work — without handing you off to a separate contractor.

## Navigating Insurance for Mold Damage in Tacoma

Whether mold is covered depends on what caused the moisture. Sudden, accidental water damage — a burst pipe, an appliance failure — typically triggers coverage under a standard homeowner's policy. Gradual leaks or long-term neglect usually don't. We've worked with most major carriers active in Washington State, and we can document the damage thoroughly: written scope of work, moisture readings, photographs, and itemized estimates formatted the way adjusters expect to receive them.

We don't bill on your behalf or make coverage promises — that's between you and your insurer — but we make sure the paperwork we hand you is complete and defensible. If your claim is denied or disputed, having a detailed remediation report from an IICRC-certified firm matters.

## What to Do Before We Arrive

If you can identify the moisture source, stop it — shut off the water supply, move belongings away from the affected area, and increase ventilation if the outdoor air is dry. Don't attempt to scrub or bleach visible mold growth; surface cleaning without containment disperses spores and can make the problem harder to remediate. Leave the affected area as undisturbed as possible and let us assess it properly.
