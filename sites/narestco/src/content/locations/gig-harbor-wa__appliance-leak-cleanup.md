## Why Gig Harbor Homes Are Especially Vulnerable to Appliance Leaks

Gig Harbor's housing stock tells two different stories. Along the harbor rim and Harborview Drive, you'll find older cottages and fishing-era houses — many built in the 1940s through 1960s — sitting on vented crawl spaces that were designed for a different era of construction. When a washing machine supply line fails or a refrigerator ice maker line drips unnoticed for weeks, water doesn't just pool on the kitchen floor. It finds the path of least resistance: seams in vinyl flooring, gaps around cabinet toe-kicks, and ultimately the subfloor above that crawl space. Ground moisture already accumulates in those crawl spaces during Gig Harbor's wet season; add appliance water on top and you've created near-ideal conditions for mold colonization — which IICRC standards peg at 24–48 hours under the right temperature and humidity.

Newer construction in neighborhoods like Artondale and Crescent Valley sits on slab foundations, which changes the loss profile. A dishwasher leak on a slab home can wick laterally through concrete and saturate baseboards and lower drywall cavities before the homeowner notices any standing water on the surface. Neither scenario is simple, and both require moisture mapping with calibrated equipment — not just a visual inspection and a few fans.

## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Gig Harbor

When we arrive — typically within 90 minutes of your call for addresses in the 98335 and 98332 ZIP codes — the first step is containment and documentation, not demolition. We use thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to map exactly how far water has traveled, which matters both for drying scope and for your insurance claim.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Source confirmation** — We verify the appliance is isolated (shutoff valve closed, supply line disconnected) before any drying equipment goes in.
- **Standing water extraction** — Truck-mounted or portable extraction units pull bulk water from hard floors, carpet, and subfloor cavities.
- **Structural drying** — Commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run in a calculated configuration based on the room's cubic footage and measured humidity levels, not a generic "put fans everywhere" approach.
- **Crawl space assessment** — For Harborview and Millville homes with vented crawl spaces, we inspect below the subfloor for moisture intrusion and fungal growth. If we find it, we document it and discuss remediation options before proceeding.
- **Daily monitoring** — We return each day to log moisture readings until structural materials reach target dryness per IICRC S500 standards.
- **Reconstruction coordination** — As a licensed general contractor (WA L&I #NATIORC792M6), we can handle cabinet reinstallation, flooring replacement, and drywall repair in-house rather than leaving you to coordinate a separate contractor.

## Local Note: Crawl Space Moisture and the Timing Problem

Here's something we've learned working in the older neighborhoods around the Gig Harbor waterfront: the vented crawl spaces under many of these homes already carry elevated ambient humidity through most of the fall and winter. When we run structural drying equipment after an appliance leak, we have to account for that baseline — otherwise the dehumidifiers are fighting both the appliance water and the ground moisture simultaneously, and drying timelines stretch well beyond the standard 3–5 days. We typically install a temporary crawl space vapor barrier or seal existing vents during the drying phase to isolate the loss area from ambient ground moisture. It adds a half-day of labor upfront but cuts total drying time meaningfully and reduces the risk of secondary mold growth. It's the kind of adjustment that only makes sense once you've run enough jobs in maritime crawl space territory.
