## Why Bellevue Homes and Properties Need a General Contractor Who Understands the Region

Bellevue's housing stock is a study in contrasts: mid-century ramblers in Factoria and Eastgate that haven't seen a structural update since the 1970s sit a few miles from newer construction in Bel-Red and Wilburton. Both present real renovation challenges. Older homes often carry surprises behind the drywall — outdated wiring, galvanized pipes that have been corroding quietly for decades, or building materials that require Lead-Safe certified handling during demolition. Newer builds aren't immune either; rapid construction timelines sometimes leave flashing and waterproofing details that fail under the sustained rain the Puget Sound region delivers every fall and winter.

Bellevue averages around 50 inches of rain per year, and that moisture finds its way into crawl spaces, around window frames, and through roof penetrations in ways that accelerate the need for renovation work — especially in split-level homes where water migrates downhill into finished lower levels. A remodel that looks cosmetic on the surface often reveals moisture damage once walls come open. Having a general contractor who's also IICRC Certified means we recognize those conditions and address them as part of the project, not as a change-order surprise three weeks in.

## What Our Renovation and General Contracting Process Looks Like in Bellevue

Every project starts with a detailed walkthrough — not a sales pitch, but a room-by-room assessment that documents existing conditions, identifies any hidden damage, and produces a written scope of work before a single tool comes off the truck. For post-damage rebuilds, we coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster and work from the claim documentation to make sure the rebuild scope aligns with what's been approved.

From there, the process moves in a logical sequence:

- **Demolition and hazardous material abatement** — We're a Lead-Safe Certified Firm and EPA Certified, which matters in Bellevue homes built before 1978. Proper containment and disposal aren't optional; they're built into our standard process.
- **Structural and mechanical rough-in** — Framing corrections, subfloor replacement, plumbing and electrical rough-in all happen before any finish work begins. We coordinate licensed subcontractors and manage the permit process with the City of Bellevue's Development Services department.
- **Insulation and weatherproofing** — Given the climate, this step gets real attention. Vapor barriers, proper attic ventilation, and window flashing are the details that determine whether a renovation holds up for 20 years or starts failing in five.
- **Finish work and final inspection** — Tile, cabinetry, fixtures, paint, trim. We don't hand off finish work to whoever's available; the same crew that managed your project manages the close-out.

Kitchen remodels typically run four to eight weeks depending on scope. Bathroom remodel timelines are shorter — often two to four weeks for a full gut-and-replace. Full post-damage rebuilds vary with the extent of structural involvement, but we provide milestone schedules upfront so you're never guessing where the project stands.

## Navigating Insurance Claims for Post-Damage Rebuilds

If your renovation need came out of a covered loss — water damage, fire, a tree through the roof — the insurance claim process adds a layer of complexity that most homeowners aren't prepared for. We've worked with virtually every major carrier operating in the greater Seattle area and understand how to document scope, submit supplements for work that wasn't captured in the initial estimate, and keep the project moving while the claim is still being adjusted.

What you handle: filing the initial claim, paying your deductible, and communicating your preferences on finishes and layout. What we handle: the adjuster communication, the line-item documentation, the permit coordination, and the construction itself. We're licensed through Washington State Department of Labor & Industries (General Contractor Certificate of Registration #NATIORC792M6) and carry the insurance required to work on claims-based projects.
