## Why Gig Harbor's Housing Stock and Climate Shape Biohazard Response

The older fishing-era and mid-century homes concentrated in the Millville and Harborview areas present a specific challenge for biohazard remediation that newer construction does not. Many of these properties were built with vented crawl spaces and subfloor assemblies that have absorbed decades of marine moisture from the harbor air. When a biohazard event occurs — particularly one involving fluids that reach flooring or subfloor materials — that pre-existing moisture saturation means contaminants can migrate deeper and faster than they would in a dry-climate home. Porous hardwood, aged OSB, and older concrete foundations in this part of 98335 and 98332 do not behave the same way as modern materials, and remediation scopes that ignore that reality tend to leave problems behind.

Gig Harbor's salt-air maritime climate also affects how long biological material remains active on surfaces. Higher ambient humidity slows the natural desiccation process, which means response time matters more here than in drier inland markets. We factor that into how we prioritize dispatch.
