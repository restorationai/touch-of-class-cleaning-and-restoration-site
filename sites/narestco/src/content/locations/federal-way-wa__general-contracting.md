## What a Renovation or Remodel Project Actually Looks Like Here

Our general contracting work starts with a thorough walkthrough — not a quick eyeball estimate, but a documented assessment that identifies what's cosmetic and what's structural. ## Kitchen and Bathroom Remodels: Where Most Federal Way Projects Start

The two rooms that drive the most home remodeling calls in this area are kitchens and bathrooms — and for good reason. A kitchen remodel in a Federal Way home from the '80s or '90s often means dealing with outdated ventilation, undersized electrical circuits, and cabinetry that's been absorbing moisture from a Pacific Northwest climate for decades. A bathroom remodel frequently uncovers tile backer that was never waterproofed correctly, leading to framing damage behind the walls that only shows up once demo begins.

We don't treat those discoveries as surprises to charge extra for — we build contingency into the scope conversation upfront, so you have a realistic picture before work starts. Finished tile, fixtures, cabinetry, countertops, and trim are all handled in-house or through vetted subcontractors we've worked with for years. The goal is a finished room, not a handoff to three different vendors.

## Working With Your Insurance Carrier

For projects that stem from a covered loss — water damage, fire, storm — the rebuild scope is typically written by an adjuster using estimating software like Xactimate. Our project managers read those scopes line by line, identify line items that are missing or undervalued, and communicate directly with your carrier to get the scope corrected before work begins. You shouldn't have to be the go-between on technical construction questions.
