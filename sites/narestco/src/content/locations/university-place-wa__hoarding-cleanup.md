## Why University Place Homes Present Unique Hoarding Cleanup Challenges

University Place's housing stock is dominated by single-story ramblers and split-levels built between roughly 1958 and 1978 — homes with original cast-iron drain lines, galvanized supply plumbing, and crawl spaces that were never designed with modern vapor barriers in mind. When a hoarding situation has developed over many years inside one of these homes, secondary damage is almost always present beneath the surface. Slow appliance leaks, condensation in under-ventilated crawl spaces, and compromised plumbing that went unnoticed under accumulated belongings frequently produce moisture damage, mold colonization, and deteriorated subfloor material that only becomes visible once the cleanup begins.

The split-level layout common in the 98466 ZIP code creates an additional logistical layer: narrow interior stairwells, low-clearance garages, and grade-level lower floors that limit how equipment and materials can be moved in and out. Our crews account for this during the initial walkthrough, not after the job has started.
