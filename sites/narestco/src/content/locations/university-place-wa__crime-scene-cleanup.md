## What Crime Scene Cleanup Involves in University Place Homes

Crime scene cleanup — sometimes called forensic cleanup or biohazard remediation — is the professional removal, disinfection, and restoration of a space after a violent crime, unattended death, or suicide. It is not work for a general cleaning service. Biohazardous material must be collected, packaged, and transported under Washington State Department of Ecology and OSHA standards, and the space must be tested and documented before it can be safely reoccupied.

University Place's housing stock adds a layer of complexity that matters here. The 1960s and '70s ramblers and split-levels that dominate neighborhoods like Beckonridge and Narrows View were built with open subflooring systems, original hardwood over plywood decking, and in many cases minimal vapor barriers in the crawl space. Biohazardous fluids that reach flooring seams or penetrate into a crawl space don't stop at the surface — they wick into structural wood and insulation in ways that require careful moisture mapping and, in some cases, controlled demolition of affected materials. Our IICRC-certified technicians are trained to assess depth of penetration, not just visible surface contamination.

## Our Cleanup Process in University Place

Every job follows a documented protocol, but the sequence is adapted to what we find on-site.

**Initial assessment and containment.** When we arrive, we establish a contained work zone using negative air pressure and physical barriers. This protects adjacent rooms and prevents cross-contamination — important in the open floor plans common in University Place's mid-century ramblers.

**Biohazard removal and surface treatment.** All affected materials — porous surfaces, soft goods, and structural elements that cannot be adequately decontaminated — are removed, bagged in compliance with WAC 173-303 hazardous waste regulations, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. Hard surfaces are treated with hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants.

**Odor neutralization.** Odor-causing compounds penetrate wall cavities and HVAC systems faster than most people expect. We use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to address what you can't see, not just what you can.

**Documentation and clearance.** We provide written documentation of the scope of work, materials removed, and methods used — the paperwork your insurance adjuster and, if applicable, a future property transaction will require.
