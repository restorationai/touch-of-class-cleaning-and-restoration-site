## Why Kent Homes Can Be Especially Challenging to Remediate

Housing stock across East Hill and West Hill ranges from 1960s split-levels to newer construction, and the differences matter during cleanup. Older homes in the 98042 and 98031 ZIP codes frequently have original subfloor materials — particleboard or tongue-and-groove planking — that absorb moisture and organic matter far more readily than modern OSB. Once materials have been stacked against walls or floors for an extended period, that absorption accelerates. By the time a cleanup crew arrives, what looks like a structural surface issue may require partial subfloor replacement rather than cleaning alone.

Kent's climate adds another layer. The region averages roughly 38 inches of rain annually, and homes with compromised ventilation — a common finding in heavily cluttered properties — tend to trap humidity year-round. Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event, and in a home where airflow has been restricted for years, it often already has. Our IICRC-certified technicians assess for hidden moisture and biological growth before any removal begins, so the remediation plan reflects what's actually present, not just what's visible.
