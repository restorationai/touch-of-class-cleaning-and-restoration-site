## Why Redmond Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Mold

Redmond sits in one of the wettest corridors in the greater Seattle metro. Annual rainfall averages hover around 50 inches, and the months between October and April rarely give building materials a chance to fully dry out. That persistent dampness matters because mold doesn't need a flood — it needs humidity and an organic surface. Crawl spaces under older ramblers in the Overlake and Education Hill neighborhoods, the vaulted ceilings of newer Sammamish Plateau construction, the finished basements that back up to hillsides near Bear Creek — all of these are spots where moisture quietly accumulates and mold quietly follows.

Redmond's housing stock also includes a significant number of homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, when vapor barriers and ventilation standards were less rigorous than they are today. Those homes can harbor mold behind original drywall, in attic sheathing, and under vinyl flooring for years before anyone notices. If your home falls into that era, a slow roof leak or a dishwasher supply line drip can seed a problem that spreads well beyond the original wet spot.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Redmond

When a technician arrives at your Redmond property, the first priority is containment — stopping the spread before any physical removal begins. That means sealing affected areas with polyethylene sheeting and establishing negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers, so disturbed spores can't migrate to clean rooms while work is underway.

From there, the process follows IICRC S520 standards for mold remediation:

- **Moisture mapping** — We use thermal imaging cameras and pin-type moisture meters to trace contamination beyond what's visible. Mold cleanup that misses the moisture source fails.
- **Controlled demolition** — Porous materials that can't be cleaned (drywall, insulation, certain subfloor sections) are bagged and removed. We're a licensed general contractor (WA L&I #NATIORC792M6), so structural repairs happen under the same roof as remediation — no hand-off to a second company.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — Framing, concrete, and other salvageable surfaces are treated and dried to documented moisture content levels.
- **Clearance testing** — We recommend a third-party post-remediation verification test before reconstruction begins. We can coordinate that referral.
- **Rebuild** — Drywall, insulation, flooring, and finishes are restored to pre-loss condition.

## Navigating Insurance for Mold Damage in Redmond

Whether your mold claim gets covered depends heavily on the cause. Most homeowner policies cover mold that resulted directly from a sudden, covered peril — a burst pipe, a storm-driven roof leak, an appliance failure. Mold traced to long-term neglect or gradual seepage is frequently denied.

We work directly with all major carriers and can document the loss in the format adjusters require: moisture readings, photo logs, scope of work, and itemized estimates. We'll communicate with your adjuster throughout the process. What we can't do is guarantee a coverage outcome — that decision belongs to your insurer. What we can do is make sure the documentation gives your claim the strongest possible foundation.
