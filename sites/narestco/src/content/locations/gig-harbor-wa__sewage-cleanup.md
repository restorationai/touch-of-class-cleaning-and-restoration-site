## Why Gig Harbor Properties See Sewage Backup More Often Than You'd Expect

Gig Harbor's geography works against its plumbing. Steep shoreline lots along the harbor rim drain toward foundations rather than away from them, and during the region's persistent wet seasons, saturated soil increases hydrostatic pressure on aging lateral sewer lines. Many homes in Artondale and Crescent Valley were developed in eras when sewer laterals were installed with clay or Orangeburg pipe — materials that collapse, root-infiltrate, and backflow under pressure. Combine that with the salt-air maritime climate that accelerates corrosion on metal fittings, and you have conditions that push sewage cleanup calls well above inland averages.

Septic systems add another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Gig Harbor properties in the 98332 and 98329 ZIP codes rely on private septic rather than municipal sewer connections. When a septic tank overflows or a drain field fails — often after a heavy rain event saturates the absorption field — the contamination can spread across a yard, into a crawl space, or even surface near a neighboring property line. That's not just a plumbing problem; it's a Category 3 biohazard event that requires licensed remediation, not a shop vac and bleach.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Gig Harbor

Every sewage job starts with containment, not extraction. Before any water moves, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination into clean zones — especially important in the open crawl-space layouts common to older Gig Harbor homes, where air circulation can carry aerosolized pathogens into living spaces above.

Once contained, we remove all standing sewage and Category 3 water using truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials — insulation batts, damaged subfloor sections, saturated drywall — are removed and bagged for disposal rather than dried in place; no amount of drying makes contaminated porous material safe to leave behind. After structural drying with commercial-grade desiccants and air movers, all affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. As an IICRC Certified firm and EPA Certified contractor, our sanitization protocols follow S500 standards, which means documentation at every stage — something your insurance adjuster will ask for.

For homes with crawl-space involvement, we inspect and replace vapor barriers, treat floor joists with antimicrobial sealant, and verify moisture readings before closing out the job. We're a licensed General Contractor (WA L&I #NATIORC792M6), so structural repairs — subfloor replacement, framing, drywall — happen under the same contract rather than requiring a second crew.
