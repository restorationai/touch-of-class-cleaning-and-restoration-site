## Why Bremerton's Older Housing Stock Complicates Unattended Death Cleanup

Bremerton's residential neighborhoods were built largely to house shipyard workers during and after World War II. Homes in the 98312 ZIP code — covering much of West Bremerton and Kitsap Lake — and across East Bremerton frequently feature original hardwood subfloors laid over unconditioned crawl spaces with minimal vapor barriers. In a situation involving an unattended death, biological fluids can wick through flooring layers and reach the crawl space within a relatively short timeframe, particularly during the wet months between October and April when ambient moisture levels are already elevated.

Plaster-and-lath wall construction, common in pre-1950 homes throughout Charleston, absorbs and retains odor compounds differently than modern drywall. Remediation in these structures requires slower, more deliberate decontamination protocols — including targeted removal of affected substrate — rather than the surface-level treatment that might suffice in a newer build. Our IICRC-certified technicians account for this during the initial assessment, which is why that first walk-through is never rushed.

## Our Cleanup Process, Calibrated for Bremerton Conditions

Every unattended death cleanup begins with a confidential assessment. We arrive in unmarked vehicles and coordinate directly with whoever has legal authority over the property — a family member, estate attorney, landlord, or property manager. In Bremerton, where Navy families frequently rent homes and landlords may be managing properties remotely, we're accustomed to handling that coordination by phone or email when an in-person contact isn't available.

The remediation itself follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or the HVAC system.
- **Removal of affected materials** — porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to OSHA and EPA standards are carefully removed and disposed of through licensed biohazard waste channels.
- **Surface decontamination** — EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces, including subfloor decking and any exposed framing.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are used to address odor compounds that have migrated into wall cavities, crawl spaces, and HVAC pathways.
- **Clearance verification** — we don't consider the job complete until the space meets documented decontamination standards.

All work is performed under our Washington State General Contractor Certificate of Registration (#NATIORC792M6) and in compliance with WAC regulations governing biohazard remediation.
