## Why Crime Scene Cleanup in Gig Harbor Requires Specialized Attention

Gig Harbor's housing stock tells a story in wood and crawl space. Many of the older cottages in Millville and the Finholm District were built during the fishing and boat-building era — structures with vented crawl spaces, tongue-and-groove subfloors, and original hardwood or fir planking that absorbs biological material differently than modern engineered flooring. Porous subfloor assemblies can wick contaminants downward before they are visible on the surface, which means a standard surface wipe-down is never sufficient. Thorough forensic cleanup here requires moisture readings, subfloor inspection, and in some cases removal of material below the finished floor plane.

The maritime climate compounds this. Salt-air humidity keeps interior relative humidity elevated year-round, slowing the drying window and accelerating the conditions under which pathogens remain viable on porous surfaces. Remediation protocols that work in a dry-climate home need to be extended in Gig Harbor — longer dwell times for EPA-registered disinfectants, additional air scrubbing passes, and careful attention to wall cavities where moisture has already found a foothold.

## Our Crime Scene Cleanup Process in Gig Harbor

Every job begins with a confidential assessment — no neighbors notified, no unmarked vans that broadcast what's happening inside. Our IICRC-certified technicians wear full PPE and follow OSHA Bloodborne Pathogen standards throughout. The process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Containment and assessment.** We identify all affected surfaces, including secondary contact areas that are easy to overlook. In older Gig Harbor homes, this often means checking wall base trim, subfloor seams, and any crawl-space access points near the affected room.

**Removal of biohazardous material.** All waste is packaged and transported in compliance with Washington State Department of Ecology regulations for biohazardous waste — nothing is left curbside or in standard household waste streams.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** We apply EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants at concentrations and dwell times calibrated for the surface type. Gig Harbor's ambient humidity means we extend dwell times and run HEPA-filtered air scrubbers longer than in lower-humidity environments.

**Clearance verification.** Before we leave, we document the remediated areas with post-cleanup readings and photographs for your records and your insurance carrier.
