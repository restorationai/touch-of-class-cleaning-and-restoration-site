## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Auburn

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. A lead technician arrives in an unmarked vehicle, evaluates the affected area, and walks the property contact through what the process will involve — without unnecessary detail and without an audience. From there, the work follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment and personal protective setup** — the affected space is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC pathways.
- **Removal of biologically affected materials** — all waste is handled and transported under Washington State Department of Ecology and OSHA guidelines, packaged for proper disposal at licensed facilities.
- **Surface decontamination** — EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces; porous materials that cannot be fully decontaminated are documented and removed.

- **Post-cleanup verification** — before we close out, affected surfaces are tested to confirm decontamination meets OSHA and ANSI standards.
