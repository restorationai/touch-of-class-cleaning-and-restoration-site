## Why Kent Properties Present Unique Considerations for Trauma Cleanup

Kent's housing stock is more varied than many neighboring cities. In the Kent Valley's commercial and light-industrial corridors, the challenge shifts: warehouse concrete, floor drains, and shared ventilation systems require a different containment approach entirely.

Kent's climate adds another layer.
