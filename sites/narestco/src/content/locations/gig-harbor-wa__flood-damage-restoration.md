## Why Gig Harbor Properties See Flood Damage Differently

The housing stock along the harbor rim and up into neighborhoods like Harborview and Millville tells the story clearly. Many of these cottages and fishing-era houses were built on vented crawl spaces — a sensible design for the era, but one that leaves a direct pathway for ground moisture and flood water to migrate upward into subfloor assemblies. After a heavy rain event, water doesn't just pool visibly; it wicks into rim joists, saturates fiberglass batt insulation that then holds moisture like a sponge, and creates the warm, dark, humid conditions that allow fungal growth to establish within 48 to 72 hours.

Steep shoreline lots compound the problem. Properties along the harbor rim drain toward their own foundations rather than away from them, so even a moderate rain event can push water against crawl-space vents or through foundation cracks that would be inconsequential on a flat lot. The salt-air climate also accelerates corrosion on vapor barriers, sump pump components, and any metal flashing that's supposed to redirect water — meaning the first defense is often already compromised before a flood event begins.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Gig Harbor

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is stopping active intrusion and documenting the full moisture boundary — not just what's visibly wet. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map water migration behind walls and beneath flooring before a single piece of drywall comes down. In Gig Harbor's older crawl-space homes, that initial assessment almost always extends below the subfloor, because water that entered at grade level has usually traveled further than it appears.

Extraction comes next: truck-mounted and portable units remove standing water from living areas, crawl spaces, and any below-grade storage. We then deploy commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, calibrated to the actual square footage and the ambient humidity level — which in a maritime climate like this runs significantly higher than the national average the equipment manufacturers use for their baseline settings. Drying logs are recorded daily so you and your insurance adjuster have a documented drying curve, not just a verbal assurance.

Structural materials that can't be dried in place — waterlogged insulation, swollen hardwood subfloor panels, compromised vapor barriers — are removed, documented, and disposed of properly. As an IICRC Certified firm and Lead-Safe Certified Firm licensed through WA State Labor & Industries (#NATIORC792M6), we follow EPA protocols on any pre-1978 materials, which applies to a meaningful share of Gig Harbor's older housing stock.

## Local Note: Crawl-Space Moisture in Harborview and Millville

One pattern we see repeatedly in the older cottages near Skansie Brothers Park and along the lower Millville streets: homeowners address the visible flood damage inside the living space — pull the wet carpet, dry the hardwood — but don't realize the crawl space beneath them is still holding standing water or saturated soil. Because these crawl spaces are vented rather than encapsulated, that residual moisture continues to drive humidity upward into the structure for weeks after the interior looks dry. We always scope the crawl space with a camera and take moisture readings at the mudsill before we close out any job in these neighborhoods. Skipping that step is the single most common reason a Gig Harbor homeowner calls a second contractor six months later with a mold problem.
