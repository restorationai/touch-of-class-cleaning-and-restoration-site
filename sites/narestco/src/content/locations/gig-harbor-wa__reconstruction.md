## Why Gig Harbor Properties Face Distinct Reconstruction Challenges

Gig Harbor sits on the Kitsap Peninsula side of the Tacoma Narrows, and the geography shapes how buildings age and fail. Steep shoreline lots funnel stormwater directly toward foundations, and the fishing-era cottages throughout Millville and the older blocks near the Finholm District were built on vented crawl spaces designed for a drier era. Those crawl spaces trap ground moisture year-round in a maritime climate that rarely dries out completely — even in July. When a pipe bursts or a roof fails, water doesn't just soak the visible surface; it migrates into subfloor assemblies that may already be hosting fungal growth from seasons of elevated humidity.

Fire losses add another layer of complexity. Salt air accelerates oxidation of metal connectors and fasteners, so structural members that look intact after a fire may have compromised hardware that only a hands-on inspection reveals. Reconstruction here means accounting for what the climate has already done to a building, not just what the loss event did.

## Our Reconstruction Process, Calibrated to Gig Harbor Conditions

Every project starts with a documented scope assessment — photographs, moisture readings, and a structural evaluation — before a single board is pulled. For properties in the 98335 ZIP code and surrounding shoreline areas, that assessment specifically flags crawl-space conditions, existing rot at sill plates, and the state of metal hardware throughout the framing. We don't assume the pre-loss condition was sound.

From there, the rebuild sequence follows a logical order that protects the work already done:

- **Structural framing and sheathing** — replacing compromised members with properly treated lumber rated for high-humidity exposure where applicable
- **Exterior envelope** — roofing, siding, and flashing detailed to shed the persistent marine moisture that characterizes this climate
- **Mechanical rough-ins** — coordinating with licensed subs for electrical, plumbing, and HVAC before walls close
- **Insulation and vapor management** — specifying products suited to Gig Harbor's mixed-marine climate zone rather than defaulting to inland-market standards
- **Interior finishes** — drywall, trim, flooring, and paint matched to the existing structure or upgraded per owner preference

## Local Note: What Gig Harbor's Crawl Spaces Tell Us Before We Start

Anyone who has worked extensively in the older neighborhoods between the Finholm District and the harbor rim knows that the crawl space is the first place to look and the last place to underestimate. Homes built in the 1940s through 1960s in this area often have original cedar sill plates sitting directly on concrete foundations with minimal barrier between soil and wood. After a water loss — even a contained one on the first floor — moisture migrates down and lingers in that crawl environment for weeks. We've found active fungal growth on subfloor joists in homes where the visible damage upstairs looked minor. Building that inspection into every Gig Harbor reconstruction scope isn't extra caution; it's standard practice here.
