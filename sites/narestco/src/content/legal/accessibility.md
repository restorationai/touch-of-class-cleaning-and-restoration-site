## Our Commitment to Accessibility

National Restoration Construction LLC is committed to making narestco.com usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** — the current benchmark recognized by accessibility practitioners and referenced in federal and state digital-access guidance.

This statement applies to all pages on narestco.com.

## What We've Done to Support Accessible Use

Our site is built with semantic HTML so that headings, landmarks, and interactive elements are correctly announced by screen readers. Images that carry meaning include descriptive alternative text. Color contrast ratios on body text and interactive controls meet or exceed the 4.5:1 minimum required at AA. Forms — including our contact and estimate-request forms — have visible labels associated with each input field, and error messages are written in plain language so they're useful whether you're reading them visually or hearing them read aloud.

Links are written to make sense out of context, so a screen reader user navigating by link list can understand where each one leads without reading the surrounding paragraph. We avoid using color alone to convey information.

## Known Limitations

We're a small, locally owned contractor — not a large web team — and we acknowledge that some content may fall short of full WCAG 2.1 AA conformance. Older PDFs (such as archived permit documents or insurance worksheets) may not be fully tagged for assistive technology. We are working to remediate these as we update our document library.

If you encounter a barrier that prevents you from getting information or requesting service, please contact us directly and a team member will provide the content in an accessible format or assist you by phone.

## How to Report an Issue

If a page, form, or document on narestco.com is difficult or impossible to use with your assistive technology, we want to know. You can reach us through any of the following:

- **Email:** info@narestco.com
- **Phone:** (206) 883-0333
- **Mail:** National Restoration Construction LLC, 1530 S Dash Point Rd, Federal Way, WA 98003

Please describe the page or feature you were trying to use and the assistive technology or browser you were using. We will acknowledge your message promptly and aim to address reported barriers within **10 business days** — or, where a fix requires development work, provide a direct alternative in the interim so you're not left waiting.

## Third-Party Content

Some content on our site is provided by third-party services (for example, embedded maps or review widgets). We do not control the accessibility of those third-party interfaces, but we will flag issues to those providers on your behalf when reported to us.

## Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a one-time checkbox. We review our site when we publish new content or make design changes, and we take reported issues seriously. If you have suggestions beyond a specific bug — ways we could make the site more usable for you — we welcome that feedback at the contact information above.
