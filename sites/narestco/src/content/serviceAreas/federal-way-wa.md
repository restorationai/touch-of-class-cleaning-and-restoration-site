## Restoration services we provide in Federal Way

### Water damage restoration

A burst pipe behind a wall, a failed water heater in the garage, a backed-up sewer line in the crawl space — water damage moves fast. Within the first hour, porous materials like drywall and hardwood flooring begin absorbing moisture. Within 24–48 hours, conditions are right for mold to take hold. Our technicians arrive with truck-mounted extraction equipment, industrial air movers, and desiccant dehumidifiers to pull moisture out of the structure before secondary damage sets in. Every job is documented with moisture readings so you have a clear record for your insurance claim.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Fire damage is visible. Smoke damage is everywhere else. Soot particles and odor compounds penetrate HVAC ductwork, insulation, and wall cavities long after the flames are out. We perform controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, apply HEPA-filtered air scrubbing, and use thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment for persistent odor — not just surface deodorizers that mask the smell for a few weeks.

### Mold remediation

Federal Way's wet winters and older housing stock create conditions where mold can colonize behind vinyl wallpaper, inside crawl spaces, and above drop ceilings without any visible sign until the smell gives it away. Our IICRC-certified remediation process contains the affected area, removes contaminated materials, and addresses the moisture source — because remediation without fixing the underlying leak is just a temporary fix.

### Biohazard and trauma cleanup

Unattended deaths, sewage backups, and hoarding situations require more than standard cleaning equipment. This work is governed by OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards and Washington State Department of Ecology guidelines. Our technicians are trained and equipped to handle these situations discreetly, and we work directly with families and property managers to reduce the burden on everyone involved.

### Reconstruction and rebuild

Most restoration companies hand you off to a general contractor once the mitigation is done. We hold a Washington State contractor's license and handle the rebuild ourselves — framing, drywall, flooring, painting — so you're not coordinating two separate companies and two separate timelines.
