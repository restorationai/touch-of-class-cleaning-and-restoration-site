## Restoration services we provide in Tacoma

### Water damage restoration

Tacoma's wet winters and aging housing stock — a lot of it Craftsman bungalows and mid-century construction with original plumbing — mean burst pipes, failed supply lines, and roof-driven leaks are a year-round reality. We extract standing water, dry structural assemblies with industrial desiccant and air-mover systems, and document every moisture reading for your insurance claim. Acting within the first 24–48 hours matters: that's the window before wet framing and insulation become a mold problem.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Fire damage is rarely just what burned. Smoke residue travels through HVAC ducts, settles into porous surfaces, and leaves an acidic film that keeps corroding metal and fabric long after the flames are out. We handle debris removal, structural drying, odor neutralization with thermal fogging and hydroxyl generators, and full reconstruction — so you're working with one contractor from the emergency call through the finished repair.

### Mold remediation

Tacoma's marine climate keeps indoor humidity elevated for most of the year. A slow roof leak or a poorly ventilated crawl space can support visible mold colonization within 24–72 hours of moisture intrusion. Our IICRC-certified technicians contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, and treat surfaces to manufacturer and EPA guidelines. We also coordinate post-remediation testing so you have documentation before reconstruction begins.

### Biohazard and trauma cleanup

Sewage backups, unattended deaths, and hoarding situations require licensed handling of potentially infectious material — this is not a job for a general cleaning crew. Our technicians follow OSHA bloodborne-pathogen protocols, use appropriate PPE, and dispose of waste through licensed carriers. We work discreetly and efficiently, and we can coordinate directly with property managers or estate representatives when needed.

### Reconstruction

Most restoration companies stop at mitigation and hand you off to a separate contractor for the rebuild. We carry a Washington State general contractor's license and handle structural repairs, drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and finish work in-house. One point of contact from day one through move-back-in.

## Why Tacoma property owners face restoration emergencies

Tacoma averages around 38 inches of rain annually, most of it arriving as persistent drizzle from October through April rather than dramatic storms. That steady moisture infiltrates older rooflines, works through cracked foundation walls in the city's hillside neighborhoods, and keeps crawl spaces damp for months at a time. The city's housing stock skews older — large portions of neighborhoods like North End, Proctor, and Stadium District are pre-1960 construction, which often means galvanized supply lines near the end of their service life and minimal vapor barriers under the house. Add the occasional atmospheric river event that overwhelms storm drains, and Tacoma properties see a consistent mix of slow-leak water damage, mold, and weather-driven flooding every year.
