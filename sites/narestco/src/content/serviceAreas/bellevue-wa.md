## Restoration services we provide in Bellevue

### Water damage restoration
Bellevue's wet winters push a lot of water into places it doesn't belong — through aging crawl space vapor barriers, around improperly flashed windows, and up through slab foundations during heavy rain events. When water gets in, we extract it, document moisture readings with thermal imaging, and deploy industrial air movers and dehumidifiers to dry structural materials before secondary damage (and mold) can take hold. We handle everything from a single flooded bathroom to a multi-story commercial building.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration
Smoke residue doesn't stay in the room where the fire started. Soot travels through ductwork, settles into insulation, and bonds to painted surfaces within hours. We contain the affected areas, remove charred materials, and use hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging to neutralize odor at the molecular level — not just mask it. Structural repairs and repainting are handled in-house, so you don't coordinate multiple contractors.

### Mold remediation
The Pacific Northwest's humidity creates ideal conditions for mold growth, and Bellevue's older Craftsman neighborhoods and mid-century split-levels often have crawl spaces or attics that haven't been properly ventilated for decades. If you're seeing discoloration on drywall or smelling something musty after a leak, mold can colonize new surfaces in as little as 24–48 hours. Our EPA-compliant remediation process isolates the affected area, removes contaminated material, and addresses the moisture source so it doesn't return.

### Biohazard and trauma cleanup
Some situations require more than a mop and a strong stomach. Sewage backups, unattended deaths, and hoarding cleanouts involve pathogens that standard cleaning doesn't eliminate. Our OSHA-trained technicians follow strict containment and disposal protocols, and we work discreetly — arriving in unmarked vehicles when requested.

### Reconstruction and build-back
Restoration isn't finished when the drying equipment comes out. We're licensed general contractors in Washington State, which means we can rebuild what was damaged — drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and full room reconstruction — under one contract. That matters when you're trying to get your home or business back to normal as fast as possible.

## Why Bellevue property owners face restoration emergencies

Bellevue sits between Lake Washington and the Cascade foothills, and the city averages around 38 inches of rain per year — most of it falling between October and April in sustained, soaking events rather than brief downpours. That sustained saturation is hard on roofing, siding, and foundation drainage systems. Many of Bellevue's established neighborhoods — Factoria, Somerset, Crossroads — have housing stock from the 1960s through 1980s where original plumbing, roof underlayment, and crawl space encapsulation are well past their service life. On the commercial side, Bellevue's dense downtown core and Eastgate office parks mean that a single plumbing failure can affect multiple tenants and trigger complex insurance claims. We've worked in all of these contexts.
