**Standing water in your home doesn't wait for business hours, and neither should your response.** A burst supply line, an overflowing washing machine, or a few inches of floodwater from a storm drain backup can turn into a much bigger problem within 24 to 48 hours as moisture wicks into drywall, baseboards, and subflooring. We answer 24/7, and the sooner water is extracted, the less material you lose.

## What Emergency Water Cleanup actually involves

Emergency water cleanup is the first phase of recovery after any water intrusion, whether it's a clean supply line break, an appliance failure, or floodwater from heavy rain. It covers locating the water, pulling it out of the structure, and getting the drying process started before mold colonization or further structural damage sets in. This is different from mold remediation or reconstruction: it's the rapid, time-sensitive stage that happens in the first hours and days, using truck-mounted or portable extraction units, air movers, and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage. For Houston homes, this often means dealing with slab foundations where water travels under cabinetry and through wall cavities in ways that aren't visible at the surface, or pier-and-beam homes in older neighborhoods where water pools under the structure and needs to be addressed from below as well as above.

## Our process

1. **Water source identification and shutoff.** Before anything else, we confirm the source is stopped and classify the water by category (clean, gray, or contaminated) since that determines PPE, disposal, and drying protocols.
2. **Extraction.** Standing water is pulled using extraction equipment matched to the depth and volume, carpet and pad are addressed on-site, and water trapped in hard flooring or under cabinetry is identified with moisture meters.
3. **Moisture mapping.** We document moisture levels in affected materials (drywall, subfloor, framing) with meters and, where needed, thermal imaging, to set a drying baseline the rest of the job is measured against.
4. **Structural drying setup.** Air movers and dehumidifiers are placed and calibrated based on the room's size, materials, and moisture readings, following IICRC S500 drying principles rather than a one-size-fits-all equipment count.
5. **Daily monitoring and adjustment.** Drying equipment placement and settings are checked and adjusted over the following days until moisture readings in the structure return to a normal, dry standard, not just until the floor feels dry to the touch.

## What separates a good water cleanup response from a bad one

The most common mistake in water cleanup is declaring a space "dry" based on surface appearance instead of actual moisture readings. Carpet can feel dry within hours while the pad and subfloor underneath stay saturated for days, and drywall can look fine while the back side stays wet against the stud. A rushed job pulls out the dehumidifiers too early, and the homeowner is dealing with a mold problem three weeks later.

Adjusters and experienced technicians both look for the same things: documented moisture readings at the start and throughout the job, photos of the affected areas before demolition of any materials, and a drying log that shows progress toward dry standard rather than a flat "we dried it" statement. Category 3 (contaminated) water also requires different handling entirely, including removal of porous materials that can't be sanitized, which is a decision that needs to be made correctly the first time, not revisited after materials are already drying in place.

## Seasonal & regional considerations

Houston's humidity makes drying timelines longer than in drier climates, since ambient moisture in the air slows how quickly materials give up water even with equipment running. Heavy spring and hurricane-season rain events also bring repeated flooding risk to low-lying neighborhoods and areas near bayous, and slab foundations common across the region can trap water under flooring long after a storm has passed. Homes with older cast iron or galvanized plumbing, common in many inner-loop neighborhoods, are also more prone to pipe failures that trigger a water cleanup call with little warning.
