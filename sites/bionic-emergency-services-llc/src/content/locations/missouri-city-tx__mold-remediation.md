## Our Mold Remediation Process in Missouri City

We start by identifying and documenting the moisture source, because remediation that doesn't address the water behind it just invites regrowth. Containment goes up next, typically negative-air chambers with physical barriers sealing the work area from the rest of the home, which matters in Missouri City's slab-on-grade houses where HVAC returns can otherwise pull spores through the whole duct system. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet padding, get removed and bagged for disposal rather than just surface-treated. Non-porous and semi-porous materials are cleaned with HEPA vacuuming and antimicrobial application, and air scrubbers run throughout to manage airborne spore counts. Once visible mold and the moisture source are addressed, we bring in moisture meters to confirm the surrounding framing and subfloor have returned to normal readings before closing out containment, drawing on the same structural drying principles we apply on water loss jobs.

## Missouri City Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner policies treat mold as a secondary damage item tied to a covered water loss, like a burst supply line or roof leak, rather than a standalone peril, so documentation of the original water event matters as much as the mold itself.
