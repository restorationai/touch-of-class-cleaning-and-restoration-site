Most Katy homes are built on slab foundations with HVAC systems that cycle hard against humid, 90-plus-degree summers. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Katy

We start with a moisture inspection, not a guess. Before any demolition begins, we use moisture meters and, where needed, thermal imaging to map how far water traveled behind walls, under flooring, or into insulation. That matters in Katy because slab construction hides a lot: a leak under a kitchen can travel several feet before it surfaces as a visible stain.

Once we understand the extent, we set up physical containment with negative air pressure to keep spores from spreading into HVAC systems or adjoining rooms, a standard approach drawn from the IICRC S520 mold remediation guidelines. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subflooring, are removed and bagged for disposal rather than treated in place, since saturated porous materials rarely come back to a safe condition. Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial solutions, and the space is dried using the same structural drying principles our team applies on water damage calls, since mold remediation only holds if the underlying moisture source is actually gone. Our crew's water restoration and structural drying training means we're not just removing mold, we're addressing why it grew there in the first place.
