## Our Mold Remediation Process in Spring

We start with a visual inspection and moisture mapping to find where the water intrusion originated, because treating the mold without fixing the source just buys you a repeat visit in a few months. Once we've identified the affected materials, we set up physical containment with negative air pressure so spores don't travel into unaffected rooms during removal. Porous materials that have absorbed moisture, like drywall and insulation, typically get removed rather than treated, while structural wood and framing are cleaned and treated following the industry's S520 remediation guidelines. We finish with HEPA air scrubbing and a final moisture check before closing out containment, and for most residential jobs we coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster on photo documentation of the affected materials.
