## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Missouri City

The first call after a storm is usually about keeping more water out, not drying what's already wet. We tarp open roof sections and board broken windows or doors first, because a hole left overnight during an active weather pattern can double the damage by morning. Once the property is secured, we extract standing water, pull wet insulation and drywall that won't dry in place, and set drying equipment calibrated to IICRC structural drying standards, adjusting for Missouri City's humidity, which stays high even after the rain clears and slows evaporation more than a drier climate would. Where trees have come down on a structure, we coordinate removal before we assess hidden roof or decking damage underneath, since limbs often shield the full extent of the breach until they're off the roof.
