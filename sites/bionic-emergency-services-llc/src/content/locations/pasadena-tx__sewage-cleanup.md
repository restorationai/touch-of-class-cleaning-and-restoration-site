## Why Pasadena Properties See Sewage Backups

Pasadena sits low and flat along the Houston Ship Channel, and that geography shapes how sewage problems start here. Heavy Gulf downpours can surcharge the city's sewer mains faster than they drain, and when a main backs up, the water has nowhere to go but back up through the lowest fixtures in a house, often a floor drain or a downstairs toilet. Homes in Downtown Pasadena and along the Spencer Highway corridor tend to be older, with cast iron or clay sewer laterals running under the slab. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Pasadena

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water, meaning every porous material it touches, carpet pad, baseboard, drywall below the flood line, generally has to come out rather than be dried in place. We start by containing the affected area to stop cross-contamination into adjoining rooms, then extract standing water and remove contaminated materials. After that, affected surfaces get cleaned and treated with hospital-grade antimicrobials, and the space is dried and monitored following the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. Our technicians hold IICRC WRT and ASD training, which matters most on a sewage job: knowing how deep moisture has traveled into a slab-on-grade assembly common in older Pasadena construction changes how long drying actually takes.
