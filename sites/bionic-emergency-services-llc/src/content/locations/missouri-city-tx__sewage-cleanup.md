## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Missouri City

We start by identifying the contamination category under IICRC S500 and S540 guidelines, since raw sewage is Category 3 water and every porous material it touches gets treated as a loss, not a dry-and-reuse situation. That means carpet pad, baseboards, and often lower drywall come out rather than get dried in place. We extract standing wastewater, remove and bag contaminated materials for disposal, and apply EPA-registered antimicrobials to all affected structural surfaces before setting containment and negative air where needed. Once the structure is cleaned and sanitized, we run air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the humidity load Fort Bend's climate adds to the equation, since muggy outdoor air slows drying more here than it would in a drier region. We verify the sewer line itself isn't still the problem before we close out, because sanitizing a bathroom twice in one month because the root intrusion was never addressed helps no one.
