## Why Jersey Village Properties See Water Damage Issues

Jersey Village sits in northwest Harris County near the Addicks Reservoir watershed, an area that has absorbed some of the region's heaviest rain events over the past decade. The flat terrain and heavy clay soil common across this part of Houston drain slowly, so yard flooding and sheet flow toward foundations are common after a sustained storm. Add in a housing stock that's heavy on homes built in the 1960s through 1980s, many with original cast iron or galvanized plumbing, and you get a city where slab leaks, water heater failures, and roof penetrations during storms account for a steady share of our calls. Humidity in the Gulf Coast climate also means any standing water left untreated for more than a day or two starts feeding mold growth behind baseboards and inside cabinet toe-kicks.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Jersey Village

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mounted or portable pumps depending on how the home is accessed, then move to moisture mapping. On slab homes, which make up a large share of Jersey Village's housing stock, we pay close attention to moisture wicking up through the slab edge and into drywall, since that pattern reads differently on a meter than a surface spill. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard: we set air movers and dehumidifiers based on room volume and material type, then track moisture content daily until framing, subfloor, and drywall return to a dry standard before any rebuild starts. Older homes with original plaster-skim drywall sometimes hold moisture longer than homes with newer board, so we adjust drying timelines rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.
