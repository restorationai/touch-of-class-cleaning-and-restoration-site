## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Pearland

Extraction comes first, using truck-mount or portable pumps depending on how much water is standing and how accessible the affected rooms are. From there we moisture-map the structure, because in slab homes that's the only reliable way to tell if water has migrated under flooring that looks dry on top. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidification sized to the room's humidity load, not a flat equipment count. Houston-area humidity means we often run drying equipment longer than a drier climate would require, and we check moisture content daily rather than assuming a fixed timeline, especially in homes with engineered hardwood or MDF cabinetry, both of which are common in Pearland's newer builds and both of which swell quickly if drying stalls.
