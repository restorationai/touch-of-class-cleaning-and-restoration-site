## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Missouri City

We start with a walk-through to separate what's structurally sound from what isn't, board up or tarp any compromised openings, and then move into soot and residue removal using HEPA vacuums and chemical sponges matched to the surface, drywall, brick veneer, cabinetry, whatever the home has. Any water from suppression efforts gets extracted and the remaining structure is dried and documented following IICRC structural drying standards, because skipping that step is exactly how a fire claim turns into a mold claim. Odor control, typically thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment, comes after the structure reads dry, not before, since treating odor in a wet assembly just masks it temporarily.
