Proximity to the ExxonMobil Baytown Refinery corridor also means some residents deal with industrial-adjacent incidents, grease fires, and electrical fires tied to older HVAC systems straining against Houston-area summers. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Baytown

We start with board-up and tarping to secure the structure, especially important given how quickly afternoon humidity and surprise rain can turn a fire-damaged roof or window opening into a secondary water problem. From there, our technicians remove charred debris, assess structural members for integrity, and begin soot and residue removal using HEPA vacuums and dry-chemical sponges on porous surfaces before wet cleaning. Odor control is its own phase, not an afterthought, because Gulf Coast humidity lets smoke odor re-activate on hot afternoons if it isn't fully neutralized at the source. Our IICRC-trained technicians document every step for your insurance file, from pre-loss condition photos to moisture readings, since fire jobs here frequently involve both soot and water damage from suppression efforts.
