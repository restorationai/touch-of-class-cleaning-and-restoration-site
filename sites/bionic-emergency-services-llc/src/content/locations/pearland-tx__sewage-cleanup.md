## Why Pearland Properties See Sewage Backups

Much of Pearland sits on expansive clay soil that swells and contracts with Gulf Coast humidity swings, and that movement shifts sewer laterals over time, especially in the older sections near Old Townsite and Southdown where cast iron and clay pipe are still common. Newer subdivisions on the west side, built out fast over the last two decades, lean on PVC lines that handle soil movement better but are more vulnerable to root intrusion and grease buildup from heavy kitchen use. Add in the city's low gradient and the proximity of Clear Creek, and heavy rain events can push municipal sewer capacity to its limit, backing flow up into the lowest fixtures in a house before it ever reaches the street.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Pearland

We start by identifying the source, whether it's a sewer line backup, a septic overflow for properties still on septic near the county line, or a municipal surcharge event during heavy rain. Standing sewage and saturated materials get removed first, porous materials like carpet pad, baseboards, and often drywall below the flood line don't get cleaned, they get discarded, because sewage contamination isn't something you can fully sanitize out of soft or porous surfaces. Hard surfaces and structural framing are cleaned with EPA-registered disinfectants and dried per IICRC S500 guidelines, and we run air scrubbers through the sanitization phase to manage odor and airborne contaminants before anyone moves back in.

## Local note

Homes built along the Clear Creek floodplain edge, particularly on the east side of Pearland, sometimes have sewer cleanouts set lower than the surrounding grade. During a heavy rain event, that low cleanout can become the easiest path for wastewater to surface in a yard or garage before it ever backs up into the house itself, so we always check the exterior cleanout first on a sewage call in those areas, it often tells us more about the source than the fixture that's actually overflowing indoors.
