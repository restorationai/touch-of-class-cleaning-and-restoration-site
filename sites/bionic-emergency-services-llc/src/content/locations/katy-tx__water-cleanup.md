## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Katy

The first call is about stopping the source and assessing what's actually wet, not just what's visible. We pull moisture readings from walls, subfloor, and baseboards because water tracks sideways under laminate and tile far faster than it shows on the surface. In homes near Seven Meadows and Firethorne, where slab-on-grade construction is standard, we pay close attention to what's happening beneath engineered wood flooring, since trapped moisture there can sit unnoticed for days while the surface looks dry. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water with truck-mount or portable units depending on access, followed by structural drying set up and monitored against the IICRC S500 standard until materials read dry, not just dry to the touch. We document every step with photos and moisture logs, which matters when a claim needs to show the timeline from first call to final dry reading.
