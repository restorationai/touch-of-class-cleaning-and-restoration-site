## Why Pearland Properties See Mold Issues

Pearland's combination of high humidity, heavy clay soil, and rapid new construction creates a few recurring patterns. Older homes in Old Townsite and Southdown, some dating back decades, often have original plumbing and window flashing that wasn't built for today's rainfall totals. Newer subdivisions on the west side, closer to the SH 288 corridor, trade age for speed: slab foundations poured fast on expansive clay can shift slightly as the soil swells and contracts with the seasons, opening hairline gaps around pipe penetrations and tub surrounds. Add in central AC systems that run nearly year-round to fight Gulf Coast humidity, and condensation around poorly insulated ductwork becomes its own quiet mold source, especially in attics and closets that share a wall with an exterior-facing HVAC run.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Pearland

We start with a visual inspection and moisture mapping to find where water is actually getting in, not just where the mold is visible. Containment comes next: we isolate the affected area with physical barriers and negative air pressure so spores don't travel into unaffected rooms while work is underway, which matters in open-concept Pearland floor plans where a kitchen, living room, and hallway often share the same air. Affected porous materials like drywall, insulation, and carpet padding are removed and bagged for disposal. Hard surfaces are treated and HEPA-vacuumed, and the space is dried per the IICRC S500 drying standard before we close anything back up. We don't paint over a mold problem, we remove the material it's growing on.
