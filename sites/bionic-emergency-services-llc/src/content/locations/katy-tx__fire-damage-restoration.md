## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Katy

We start with board-up and roof tarping if the structure is exposed, then move into soot and residue assessment room by room. Synthetic stucco (EIFS) finishes common on Cinco Ranch and Firethorne exteriors hold smoke odor differently than traditional brick veneer, so we pay close attention to attic insulation and exterior wall cavities where odor tends to linger after visible soot is gone. Water introduced during firefighting gets extracted and the structure is dried using IICRC-based structural drying protocols, since leftover moisture behind drywall or under engineered flooring becomes a secondary mold problem within a few days in this climate. Once the structure is dry and stable, we clean or replace HVAC ductwork, since smoke particles recirculate through the system long after the fire is out, and address odor at the source rather than masking it.
