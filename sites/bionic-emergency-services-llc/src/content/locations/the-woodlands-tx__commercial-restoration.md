## Our Commercial Restoration Process in The Woodlands

Commercial jobs start differently than residential ones. Before extraction equipment comes off the truck, we're usually on the phone with a property manager or facilities contact confirming which tenants are affected, where the electrical panel is, and whether the space needs to stay partially open during mitigation. In a multi-tenant building near College Park or Indian Springs, that might mean running containment barriers and negative air through a single unit while the business next door keeps its lights on and customers walking in. Drying is monitored with moisture meters against the IICRC S500 standard rather than guesswork, and we don't call a space dry until the readings in the wall cavities and subfloor match the readings at the surface.
