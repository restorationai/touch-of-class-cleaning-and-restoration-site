## Why Jersey Village Properties See Water Damage

Jersey Village sits inside the White Oak Bayou watershed, one of the drainage systems that carries a large share of northwest Harris County's stormwater toward downtown Houston. That means heavy rain events don't just cause localized puddling, they can back up street drainage and push water toward low-lying slabs faster than homeowners expect. Many homes in this area were built decades ago on concrete slab foundations with plumbing runs embedded in or under the slab, which makes slab leaks and supply line failures a recurring source of indoor flooding separate from storm events entirely. Add in Houston's humidity, and a wet subfloor or soaked baseboard that isn't dried quickly becomes a mold problem within 24 to 48 hours, not days.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process

We start by identifying the water source, whether it's a failed supply line, a slab leak, a roof intrusion, or storm runoff that found its way in through a door threshold or garage slab. Standing water gets extracted first with truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment, then we move to assessing what's underneath: carpet pad, subfloor, drywall, and baseboard all hold moisture differently. Moisture readings are taken at the start and logged throughout the job so drying is measured against the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessed at. Structural drying equipment, air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the affected square footage, stays in place until readings confirm the materials are back to a dry standard, not just dry to the touch.

## Insurance and Documentation

Most water damage claims in this area come down to proving when the loss happened and what caused it, which matters for distinguishing a sudden pipe failure from gradual seepage that some policies exclude. We photograph and document moisture levels, affected materials, and the suspected source before any demolition begins, and we provide that documentation in a format adjusters can use directly. If a property is part of an HOA-managed community, we coordinate scheduling around any posted work-hour restrictions or dumpster placement rules so the cleanup doesn't create a second problem on top of the first.
