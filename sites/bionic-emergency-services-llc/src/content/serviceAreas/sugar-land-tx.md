## Restoration emergencies common in Sugar Land

Sugar Land sits along the Brazos River, and that proximity shapes a lot of the water damage calls we see here. Heavy Gulf Coast rain events can push the river toward flood stage and back up drainage in low-lying areas near New Territory and parts of Riverstone, while the rest of the year brings the humidity and heat that let mold colonize a wet wall cavity within days, not weeks. Add in a housing stock where many homes are now thirty-plus years old, and slow leaks behind tile, under slab plumbing, or inside AC closets often go unnoticed until flooring or drywall is already compromised. Summer storms and the occasional hurricane remnant also bring straight-line wind and hail, which is why board-up and roof tarping calls spike alongside water intrusion claims during storm season.
