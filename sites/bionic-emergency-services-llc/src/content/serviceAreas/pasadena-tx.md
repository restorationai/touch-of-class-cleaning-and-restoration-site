## Restoration emergencies common in Pasadena

Pasadena sits low in the Houston Ship Channel's drainage basin, and that geography shapes what goes wrong in a home or business here. Tropical storms and slow-moving summer downpours can dump several inches of rain in an hour, and streets around the Spencer Highway corridor and Red Bluff have flooded in past storms before ditches and bayous can carry the water off. Slab foundations common in older Pasadena neighborhoods don't tolerate that kind of saturation well; water finds its way under flooring and into wall cavities faster than most homeowners expect. Add Gulf Coast humidity that keeps materials damp for days after a storm passes, and conditions for mold growth start within 24 to 48 hours of any unaddressed moisture. Industrial activity near the Ship Channel also means commercial and residential biohazard calls happen here that wouldn't come up in a purely residential suburb.
