## Our Commitment

BIONIC Emergency Services LLC wants every visitor, including people who use assistive technology, to be able to find information about our restoration services and reach us quickly during a stressful situation. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA across this website, and we treat accessibility as an ongoing part of how we build and update pages rather than a one-time fix.

## What We've Built Toward That Goal

Our site is structured so that headings, links, and form fields are labeled in a way that screen readers can interpret correctly. We rely on sufficient color contrast, resizable text that doesn't break layout, and keyboard-navigable menus and forms so visitors who cannot use a mouse can still request a call or submit a contact form. Images that convey meaning include descriptive alt text. We test pages periodically as content changes, since new pages or updated service information can introduce issues even when the underlying template is sound.

## Known Limitations

No website is perfectly accessible at all times, especially one that gets updated with new location and service pages. Third-party embeds, such as mapping tools or review widgets, are sometimes outside our direct control and may not fully meet the same standard. When we identify a gap, whether through our own review or a visitor report, we prioritize fixing it based on how much it affects someone's ability to get information or reach us.

## If You Run Into a Problem

If you use assistive technology and hit a barrier anywhere on this site, including trouble reading a page, filling out the contact form, or navigating to our service or location pages, please let us know. Tell us the page you were on, what device or software you were using (screen reader, browser, mobile vs. desktop), and what happened. That detail helps us reproduce and fix the issue faster than a general complaint.

You can reach us by phone at (713) 338-2424 or by email at shane@bionic24365.com. If a phone call isn't practical for your situation, email is the more reliable way to document the specific problem and get a written reply.

## Response Timeline

We review accessibility reports as they come in and aim to acknowledge them within a reasonable window, generally a few business days. Straightforward fixes, like a missing alt tag or a labeling error on a form, are typically addressed quickly. Larger structural issues may take longer to resolve properly, but we will follow up to let you know we received your report and what we're doing about it.

## Alternative Access

If a specific page or form is not accessible to you right now, we're glad to take your information over the phone and handle a scheduling request or inquiry directly, so an accessibility gap on the site doesn't stand between you and getting help with a water, fire, storm, or biohazard loss.
