This page collects the practical guides we write for Houston homeowners and property managers dealing with water intrusion, storm damage, fire aftermath, and the insurance paperwork that comes with all three. Expect posts on what to do in the first hour after a pipe bursts, how mold behaves in our humidity once drywall stays wet, what adjusters actually look for during a claim walkthrough, and how Gulf Coast storm season affects roofs, foundations, and drainage across the area.

Some posts walk through a specific process, like how a drying plan is set up and monitored, or what documentation an insurance carrier needs to move a claim forward. Others are seasonal: what to check before a tropical system tracks toward Houston, or why older neighborhoods with slab foundations see different water problems than newer construction on the city's outer edges. The goal is the same either way: give you a clear, honest picture of what you're dealing with and what reasonable next steps look like.

None of this is meant to replace a professional assessment, and some of it may be more detail than you need in the middle of an active emergency. But if you're trying to understand a situation before you make a call, or you just want to know what to expect once help arrives, that's exactly what this section is for.
