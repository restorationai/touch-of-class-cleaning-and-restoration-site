Water and fire damage don't wait for convenient timing, and neither do we. BIONIC Emergency Services LLC answers calls 24/7 and dispatches crews based on where the damage is and how urgent the loss is. Whether you're in a high-rise downtown or a ranch house on the outskirts of the metro, the same team and the same process apply: we assess, we document for your insurance carrier, and we get to work.

## Where we work

BIONIC is based in Houston and responds throughout the greater Houston metro, including the surrounding communities across Harris County and into the neighboring counties that make up the region. Houston's sprawl means "local" covers a lot of ground, from inner-loop neighborhoods with older plumbing and foundation quirks to newer subdivisions further out that deal with their own drainage and clay-soil movement. We work across that whole footprint, not just the zip codes closest to our base.

## Coverage by distance

Crews are typically dispatched to the closest available team first, so jobs inside the Loop and in the inner suburbs tend to see us soonest simply because of proximity and traffic patterns on corridors like I-10, 610, and Beltway 8. Calls further out in the metro are still a priority, we just factor in drive time honestly rather than promise a clock we can't keep during rush hour or after a region-wide storm event. For cities at the edge of our normal coverage, we'll tell you plainly where you fall in the queue when you call, instead of guessing.

Gulf Coast weather adds its own wrinkle: heavy rain events and tropical systems can generate calls across the whole metro at once, which is part of why we triage by severity as well as location. Active water intrusion and fire-damaged structures move to the front of the line regardless of which suburb they're in.

## Don't see your city?

If your city isn't listed on this site, that doesn't automatically mean we can't help. Give us a call at (713) 338-2424 and describe the loss. For larger losses, we'll often extend outside our usual radius, and for smaller jobs further out we can at least point you toward whether we're a fit or whether another provider will get to you faster.
