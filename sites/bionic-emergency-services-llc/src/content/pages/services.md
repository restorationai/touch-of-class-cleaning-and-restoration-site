BIONIC Emergency Services LLC handles the full arc of property damage recovery in the Houston area: the urgent mitigation work that stops a loss from spreading, and the reconstruction that brings a home or business back to pre-loss condition. We work with homeowners, property managers, and commercial building owners, and we handle both sides of a claim so you're not coordinating between separate mitigation and rebuild companies during an already stressful situation.

## Emergency restoration services

Water intrusion, whether from a burst pipe, a failed appliance supply line, or a roof leak during a Gulf Coast storm, needs extraction and structural drying fast, before moisture works into subfloor and framing. Fire and smoke damage bring their own complications: soot residue etches into surfaces within hours, and odor can embed in drywall and fabric long after the flames are out. Storm damage across the Houston area often means wind-driven rain finding its way past compromised roofing or siding, and we respond to stabilize the structure before secondary damage sets in. Biohazard situations, including sewage backups and trauma-related cleanup, require careful containment and disposal under health and safety protocols. We answer 24/7 and bring IICRC-trained technicians to assess the loss and begin mitigation.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once a structure is dry, demolished materials, or stabilized after storm or fire damage, it needs to be rebuilt. Our reconstruction work covers drywall, flooring, painting, and framing repairs tied directly to a covered loss, along with general contracting for the renovation work that follows, cabinetry, trim, fixtures, and finishes. Because the same company handles mitigation and rebuild, there's no gap where a job sits waiting for a second contractor to pick up documentation or scope.

## Specialty services

Not every call starts with standing water or visible flames. Mold inspection addresses the conditions that show up after a slow leak or chronic humidity, the kind of growth that can establish on wet drywall or framing within a day or two of unresolved moisture. Odor removal tackles smoke, mildew, or biohazard odor that lingers after the visible damage is gone. Emergency board-up and tarping secures a property against weather and intrusion when a window, door, or roof section has failed, buying time until permanent repairs can begin.

## One call, start to finish

A property loss rarely stays in one lane. Water damage leads to drywall demolition, which leads to flooring and paint. Fire damage means soot cleanup today and framing repair next month. Keeping mitigation and reconstruction under one roof means one point of contact, one set of documentation for your insurance carrier, and no handoff between companies while your property sits half-finished.
