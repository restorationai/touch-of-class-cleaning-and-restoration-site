## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Connell

We start with a walkthrough to separate what's structurally sound from what isn't, since smoke and heat can compromise framing and drywall in ways that aren't obvious at first glance. Soot is removed from hard surfaces, HVAC systems are inspected because ductwork spreads smoke particles through the whole house even from a small contained fire, and porous materials like insulation or carpet padding that absorbed smoke are typically removed rather than treated. Our fire and smoke work follows IICRC FSRT guidelines, which means the deodorizing step isn't just masking the smell with an air freshener. Depending on how deep the smoke traveled into wall cavities, we may use thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment to break down odor at the molecular level instead of covering it up.
