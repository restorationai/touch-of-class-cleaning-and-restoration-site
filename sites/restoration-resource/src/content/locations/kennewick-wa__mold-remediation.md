Kennewick sits in the rain shadow of the Cascades, which means most homeowners here think of mold as someone else's problem, something that happens in Seattle basements, not in the high desert. But mold remediation calls in Kennewick follow a different pattern: evaporative coolers, pressurized irrigation, and slab foundations create pockets of moisture that a dry climate hides well until the drywall starts to soften or the smell shows up in a hallway closet.

## Why Kennewick Properties See Mold Issues

The Columbia Basin's low humidity is real, but it creates its own blind spots. Many Kennewick homes still rely on evaporative (swamp) coolers for summer cooling, and those units add moisture directly into the duct system and attic space, which is the opposite of what a standard AC unit does. When that moist air meets a cooler surface, condensation collects in places nobody checks until a musty smell shows up months later. Add in neighborhood irrigation systems and sprinkler heads that sit close to foundations, and you get a second pathway: water wicking up through slab-on-grade construction that's common in newer Kennewick subdivisions. Combine that with a plumbing leak or a roof that wasn't flashed quite right, and mold can establish itself in wall cavities well before a visible stain appears.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Kennewick

We start with moisture mapping, not demolition. A thermal camera and a moisture meter tell us where water is actually traveling inside a wall or under flooring, which matters in Kennewick because slab foundations don't behave like crawlspace or basement construction when it comes to how moisture spreads. Once we've confirmed the extent of the colonization, we set up physical containment with negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into unaffected rooms during removal. Affected materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subflooring, are removed and bagged for disposal rather than treated in place, because surface cleaning doesn't address what's growing inside porous material. HEPA air scrubbers run throughout the job, and our crews work under IICRC AMRT mold remediation protocols, which govern containment, PPE, and clearance standards regardless of the climate the job happens in. The last step is addressing the moisture source itself, whether that's a duct insulation gap from a swamp cooler or a sprinkler head that needs to be redirected, because remediation without a fix just buys a few dry months before the same spot comes back.

Mold claims are handled differently than water or fire claims by most carriers, often with sub-limits or exclusions tied to how long moisture was present before it was addressed. ## Local note

One thing that trips up a lot of Kennewick homeowners: caliche and compacted sandy soils in parts of the Tri-Cities don't drain the way heavier clay soils do elsewhere in the state, so a drainage problem that would show up as standing water in a wetter climate instead shows up as slow, chronic dampness along a foundation line. By the time it's visible as a stain inside, the mold has usually had weeks, not days, to establish itself behind the wall.
