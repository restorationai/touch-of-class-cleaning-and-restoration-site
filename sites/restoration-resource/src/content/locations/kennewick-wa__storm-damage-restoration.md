## Why Kennewick Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Kennewick sits in the Columbia Basin's rain shadow, which means most of the year is dry, but when a storm system does roll through, it often arrives with gusting wind rather than steady rain. That combination catches roofs off guard. Shingles and flashing that have baked under summer sun for years become brittle, and a single wind event can lift a section clean off a roof deck. Add the mature trees found in many of Kennewick's older residential blocks and you get a predictable pattern: broken limbs through roofing, siding, and fence lines after any significant wind advisory for Benton County. Basalt-heavy soil in this part of the basin also drains slowly once saturated, so wind-driven rain that gets past a compromised roof or siding seam tends to sit in wall cavities and attic insulation longer than it would in sandier soil, giving mold a head start if drying is delayed.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Kennewick

We start with a roof and exterior assessment to find where wind and water actually entered, not just where the damage is visible from the ground.
