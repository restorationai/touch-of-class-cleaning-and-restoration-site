## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Finley

The first call is about stopping the source and getting water off the floor and out of the materials that absorb it fastest. On a rural Finley property that often means checking whether the loss is coming from inside plumbing or from an outside source like a saturated yard or overflow near an irrigation ditch, since the mitigation plan differs depending on whether the water is clean supply line water or carries contamination from ground or septic sources. Once the source is controlled, we extract standing water, pull back flooring where needed, and set containment before running drying equipment. Crawlspace losses get particular attention here: we check vapor barriers, insulation batts, and floor joists directly rather than assuming a dry-looking subfloor means the structure underneath is dry too.
