## Why Mesa Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Mesa sits in the Columbia Basin, where long, dry summers and low humidity push grass and field fires toward homes, barns, and equipment sheds every season. Many properties here run on propane for heat and cooking rather than natural gas lines, and a propane appliance fire behaves differently than a typical kitchen fire: it tends to burn hotter and spread faster into attached garages or mudrooms before anyone smells smoke. Older farmhouses and manufactured homes common to the area also have different wall cavities and insulation types than newer stick-built construction, which changes how deep soot and heat penetrate before a crew ever arrives.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Mesa

We start with a damage assessment that separates structural loss from smoke and soot contamination, because those two problems are cleaned differently and billed differently to most insurance carriers. On rural Mesa properties, that assessment usually extends past the house itself: detached garages, shops, and outbuildings often sit close enough to the structure to pick up smoke residue even when the fire never reached them directly.
