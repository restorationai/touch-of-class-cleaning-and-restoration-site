## Why West Pasco Properties See Water Damage Issues

The region's low humidity keeps a lot of minor leaks invisible longer than they would be in a wetter climate. Water evaporates off exposed surfaces quickly here, so a slow pipe leak under a kitchen cabinet or behind a washing machine can read as "dry" on the surface while subfloor and framing stay saturated underneath. Add in winter freeze-thaw cycles that stress pipe joints and irrigation backflow devices, plus newer-construction plumbing runs that sometimes get installed under tight deadlines during a development push, and West Pasco sees its share of pinhole leaks, supply line failures, and water heater ruptures that go undetected until the damage has already spread.
