## What we cover

Restoration Resource handles property damage from the first phone call through the final repair, for homeowners, landlords, and commercial property managers across the Pasco area. That means emergency mitigation when water, fire, or storm damage hits, and the reconstruction work that follows once the structure is dry, clean, or rebuilt. One crew, one point of contact, start to finish.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage restoration covers burst pipes, failed appliances, and supply line leaks, with extraction and structural drying to stop secondary damage before it spreads into framing and subfloor. Fire damage and smoke damage services address soot residue, charring, and the acrid smell that settles into drywall and fabric long after the flames are out. Storm damage response covers wind, hail, and water intrusion from roof and window failures common to the region's seasonal weather. Biohazard cleanup handles sewage backups, trauma scenes, and other contamination that requires controlled disposal and disinfection, not just cleanup. Each of these is a call-now situation, and we answer 24/7.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Once the affected area is stabilized, our reconstruction team rebuilds what the loss took out: drywall, flooring, cabinetry, trim, and framing repairs sized to match the original structure. We also take on general contracting work tied to a restoration project, so a kitchen damaged by a water leak or a bedroom affected by smoke doesn't sit half-finished waiting on a separate contractor. The same team that documented the damage during mitigation carries that documentation into the rebuild, which keeps the insurance file consistent from claim to closeout.

## Specialty services

Not every call is a flood or a fire. Mold inspection and remediation address growth that shows up after a slow leak or a humid crawlspace, handled by technicians trained to IICRC mold remediation standards. Odor removal tackles smoke, pet, and biological odors that linger after the visible damage is gone. Emergency board-up and tarping secures a property against weather and intrusion after a break-in, storm, or fire, buying time before permanent repairs begin.
