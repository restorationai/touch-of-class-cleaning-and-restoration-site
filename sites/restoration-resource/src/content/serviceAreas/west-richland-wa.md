## Restoration emergencies common in West Richland

West Richland sits in the high desert of the mid-Columbia Basin, and that climate drives most of the calls we get here. Winters bring hard freezes that split supply lines in crawlspaces and unheated garages, and the thaw that follows often reveals the break only after water has already soaked subfloor and insulation. Summers run hot and dry with wildfire smoke drifting in from regional fires, which settles into HVAC systems and soft materials even in homes that never saw flame. Irrigation leaks, sprinkler line breaks, and slow roof leaks from wind-driven dust and debris are also common given the area's newer residential construction and heavy reliance on irrigation for landscaping on otherwise arid lots.
