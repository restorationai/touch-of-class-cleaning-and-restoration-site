## Information We Collect

When you reach out through our contact form, request an estimate, or call our office, we collect the information you choose to share: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or service you need. If you upload photos of the loss, we retain those as part of your file. Our website also uses basic analytics tools that record general visit data, such as which pages were viewed and how visitors arrived at the site. This analytics data is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule on-site estimates, and prepare documentation for insurance claims when you are working with a carrier. If you ask us to coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster, we use your contact and property details to communicate with that carrier on your behalf. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing campaigns or automated decision-making.

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent visitor information to third parties. We share details with an insurance carrier, adjuster, or property manager only when you have authorized that coordination as part of your claim or project. We may also share limited information with service providers who support our business operations, such as scheduling or billing software, under the condition that they handle it securely and do not use it for their own purposes.

## Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and project-related records for as long as reasonably necessary to support your service history, respond to warranty or insurance questions, and meet standard recordkeeping practices. Analytics data is generally retained in aggregate form and is not tied to an individual visitor after a limited period.

## Your Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to request what personal information we hold about you and to ask that it be deleted, subject to our legitimate recordkeeping needs. Restoration Resource operates within the United States and does not knowingly collect information from visitors in the European Union, so the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) does not generally apply to our services; however, we are happy to address privacy questions from any visitor in good faith. To make a request regarding your information, contact us using the details below.

## Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar tools to understand general traffic patterns and improve site performance. You can adjust your browser settings to limit or block cookies, though some site features may not function as intended if you do.
