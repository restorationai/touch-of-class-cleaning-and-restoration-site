## Restoration emergencies common in Hurlburt Field

Hurlburt Field sits on a low, sandy strip of the Florida panhandle between Choctawhatchee Bay and the Gulf, which means tropical systems and their storm surge are the dominant threat rather than an occasional nuisance. Slab-on-grade homes near the water table also see moisture wick up through concrete after heavy rain events, showing up as soft baseboards or a musty smell long after the storm has passed.
