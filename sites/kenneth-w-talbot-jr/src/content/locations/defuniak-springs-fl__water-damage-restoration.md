## Why DeFuniak Springs Properties See Water Damage Issues

Walton County's climate is the first factor worth understanding. The Panhandle averages a long, humid summer with frequent pop-up storms, and DeFuniak Springs, sitting inland from the Gulf, can receive intense rainfall that overwhelms gutters, window seals, and older drainage systems in ways coastal properties are better engineered to handle. Humidity rarely drops low enough between storms for wet building materials to dry on their own, which means moisture that enters a wall cavity or subfloor in July can still be feeding a mold colony by September.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. DeFuniak Springs has a meaningful share of older homes, some dating to the early twentieth century, built with wood-framed walls, tongue-and-groove subfloors, and plaster finishes that behave very differently from modern drywall when they get wet. Plaster can read nearly dry at the surface on a moisture meter while the wood lath behind it stays saturated. Older cast-iron or galvanized supply lines are also more prone to pinhole leaks that go unnoticed until a cabinet base or a section of flooring has been wet for months.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in DeFuniak Springs

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment, not just the visible wet area, but the adjacent walls, the subfloor, and any crawl space below. We use thermal imaging alongside pin and pinless moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation before any equipment is placed, because equipment placed in the wrong location dries the room, not the structure.

Once the scope is mapped, water extraction comes first. Standing water is removed with truck-mount and portable extractors, then we transition to structural drying: commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to drive moisture out of cavities and into the air stream where the dehumidifiers can capture it. Drying logs are recorded daily so there's a documented record of moisture readings over time, something insurance adjusters expect and that protects you if a secondary claim arises later.

Materials that cannot be dried in place, saturated insulation, wicking drywall, compromised subfloor sections, are removed and documented before disposal. We don't dry over materials that will harbor mold; that only delays a larger problem.

Most standard homeowners' policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, a storm-driven roof leak, but the documentation requirements matter. ## Local Note

DeFuniak Springs has a notable inventory of older craftsman and Victorian-era homes near the historic downtown core, and those structures often have balloon-frame construction, a framing method where wall cavities run continuously from the foundation sill to the roof line without the fire stops and horizontal blocking that modern platform framing uses. When water enters a balloon-frame wall, it can travel vertically the full height of the structure before anyone sees a stain. If your home was built before roughly 1940 and you've had any kind of roof or plumbing leak, it's worth having the full wall cavity checked, not just the area nearest the visible damage.
