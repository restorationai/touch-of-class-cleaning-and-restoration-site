## Why Eglin AFB Properties Face Distinct Fire Damage Challenges

Eglin AFB encompasses a vast footprint of housing, support facilities, and administrative buildings across the western Panhandle. Military family housing on the installation tends to follow construction cycles tied to federal appropriations, meaning some units were built in earlier decades with materials and framing methods that respond to fire and smoke differently than newer construction. Older wood-framed structures absorb smoke odor into the framing itself, not just the drywall, and surface cleaning alone will not eliminate it. Synthetic insulation and vapor barriers common in mid-century military housing also off-gas when heated, leaving a chemical residue that standard cleaning misses.

The regional climate adds another layer. Northwest Florida's high humidity means that even after flames are extinguished, moisture in the air bonds with soot particles on walls and ceilings, creating a greasy film that becomes harder to remove the longer it sits. Saltwater-laden air from the Gulf intensifies corrosion on metal fixtures, appliances, and HVAC components that were exposed to heat and smoke.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Eglin AFB

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence that protects a property from compounding damage follows a consistent logic. When we arrive, the first priority is a thorough assessment of structural integrity and the full extent of smoke migration, including inside ductwork, attic spaces, and wall cavities where smoke travels along pressure differentials.

From there, the process moves through these stages:

- **Board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry
- **Debris removal** of charred materials, with documentation for insurance purposes
- **Dry ice blasting or HEPA-filtered sanding** on smoke-stained framing before any new materials go in
- **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation** to neutralize odor molecules embedded in porous surfaces
- **HVAC decontamination**, because smoke infiltrates duct systems and will redistribute odor throughout the home if the system isn't cleaned before it runs again
- **Structural drying** to address any water intrusion from firefighting efforts
- **Reconstruction coordination** to return the space to pre-loss condition

Our OSHA trained team documents every phase, which supports a cleaner insurance claim and gives the property owner a clear record of what was done.
