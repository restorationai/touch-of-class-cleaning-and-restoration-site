## Our Mold Remediation Process in Harold

Every job starts with a thorough inspection rather than an assumption. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help locate saturation behind surfaces that look dry to the eye, because mold growing inside a wall cavity reads completely different from surface growth on a bathroom tile. Once the affected area is mapped, the crew establishes physical containment using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal.

Affected porous materials, typically drywall, insulation, and in some cases subflooring, are removed and bagged for disposal according to Florida Department of Environmental Protection guidelines. Structural framing and other salvageable surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Industrial-grade HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process to capture airborne spores. The OSHA-trained team at Veterans Remediation & Restoration follows established industry protocols at every stage, and the work area is not cleared until post-remediation verification confirms the spore count has returned to an acceptable baseline.
