## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Santa Rosa Beach

When a call comes in, the first priority is stopping the source, a shut-off at the meter, a clamped supply line, or coordination with a plumber if the break is inside a wall. From there, the process follows a structured sequence calibrated to coastal conditions:

**Moisture mapping** comes before any equipment placement. In a high-humidity environment like Santa Rosa Beach, ambient air moisture can fool a single-point reading. The crew uses thermal imaging alongside pin and pinless moisture meters to trace how far water has migrated, under tile, into wall framing, beneath engineered flooring.

**Water extraction** removes standing water from hard surfaces and pulls trapped moisture from carpet padding and subfloor assemblies. In slab-on-grade homes common to this area, extraction from the slab-to-subfloor interface is a step that gets skipped by inexperienced crews, and the resulting hidden moisture is exactly what drives mold growth in the weeks after a loss.

**Structural drying** uses commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers positioned to create directed airflow through wall cavities and floor assemblies. Drying targets are set to the IICRC S500 standard for the material class affected. In Santa Rosa Beach's climate, ambient humidity often works against the drying process, so equipment capacity matters more here than in drier inland markets.

**Daily monitoring** tracks the drying curve. Readings are logged and documented, something insurance carriers and property managers expect before authorizing repairs.
