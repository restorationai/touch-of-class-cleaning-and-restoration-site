## Why Westville Properties See Water Damage Issues

The Panhandle's climate is the dominant factor. Westville and the surrounding Holmes County area receive significant rainfall concentrated in the summer months, and the region's high humidity means building materials that get wet stay wet longer than they would in a drier climate. Homes built on pier-and-beam or block foundations, which are common in older parts of this area, can trap moisture beneath the structure where it is invisible until flooring begins to buckle or a musty odor signals a problem that has been developing for weeks.

Older construction in this part of Florida often features wood-framed walls with minimal vapor barriers, meaning water that enters through a roof penetration, a failed window seal, or a slow plumbing leak wicks deeply into wall cavities. Surface readings from a moisture meter can look acceptable while the framing behind the drywall remains saturated. That gap between what looks dry and what is dry is where long-term structural damage and mold colonization begin.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Westville

Every job starts with a full moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated meters, not a visual walk-through. Standing water is extracted first using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment. Once the bulk water is removed, the focus shifts to structural drying: industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are placed in a calculated pattern based on the affected square footage and the material types involved. Concrete, wood subfloor, and drywall each release moisture at different rates, and the drying plan accounts for that.

Drying logs are documented daily. Moisture readings are recorded at each monitoring point so there is a clear record of progress, which matters both for the homeowner's peace of mind and for the insurance claim file. No materials are released or closed in until readings confirm they have reached acceptable drywall and wood moisture content levels. In Westville's humidity, dehumidifier capacity is sized up compared to what would be adequate in a lower-humidity region, because the ambient air is already carrying significant moisture and a undersized unit will run continuously without achieving the target conditions.
