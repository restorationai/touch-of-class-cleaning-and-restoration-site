## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Laurel Hill

We start by identifying the water category, clean supply line, gray water, or contaminated, because that determines how we handle contents, flooring, and drywall from that point forward. Extraction comes first: pumps and truck-mount vacuums pull standing water off floors and out of crawl spaces before it has a chance to wick further into framing and insulation. From there we run moisture meters across walls, subfloor, and joists, drying materials to the benchmarks set out in the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing based on how a surface looks or feels. In a crawl space home, that often means placing air movers and dehumidifiers under the house, not just in the living space, because that's usually where the water actually pooled.
