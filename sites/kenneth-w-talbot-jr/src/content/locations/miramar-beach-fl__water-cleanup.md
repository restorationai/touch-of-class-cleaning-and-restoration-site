## Our Water Cleanup Process in Miramar Beach

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing intrusion, whether that means isolating a supply line, sandbagging a threshold, or coordinating with a plumber on an emergency shutoff. From there, the work follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping**, Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters document the full spread of water, including behind tile, under laminate, and inside wall cavities. In Miramar Beach's condo and townhome stock, water frequently travels along shared party walls before it becomes visible.

**Standing water removal**, Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet systems. In slab-on-grade construction, this step also targets the gap between flooring and concrete where water pools invisibly.

**Structural drying**, Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Given the ambient humidity here, dehumidifier capacity is typically sized up relative to what the same square footage would require in a drier climate.

**Monitoring and documentation**, Daily readings track drying progress and create the paper trail that insurance adjusters and property managers need to close out a claim.
