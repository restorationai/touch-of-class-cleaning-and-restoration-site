## Why Westville Properties Face Particular Fire Damage Challenges

Holmes County's housing stock skews older. Many homes in and around Westville were built in the mid-twentieth century using balloon-frame or platform-frame construction with pine board sheathing, materials that ignite readily and allow fire to travel vertically through wall cavities with very little resistance. That same construction style means smoke and soot can migrate into attic spaces and subfloor areas that look completely untouched from the main living areas.

The Panhandle's climate adds another layer of complexity. Summer humidity in this part of Florida routinely climbs above 80 percent, which means fire-damaged materials that absorbed water from suppression efforts are sitting in conditions that accelerate secondary damage. Wet insulation, charred wood, and saturated drywall in a humid environment can begin supporting mold growth within 24 to 48 hours of the fire being extinguished. Addressing both the fire damage and the moisture left behind by suppression water is not optional; it is part of the same job.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Westville

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a logic that protects the structure and the people inside it.

**Stabilization first.** Before any cleaning or reconstruction begins, the structure needs to be secured. That means boarding windows and doors, tarping any roof penetrations, and assessing load-bearing elements that may have been compromised by heat or suppression water.

**Damage documentation.** We photograph and document every affected area systematically. This record matters for your insurance claim and for making sure nothing is missed during the cleanup phase.

**Soot and smoke removal.** Dry soot from fast-burning fires behaves differently than the wet, oily residue left by smoldering synthetic materials. We identify the type of residue present and use the appropriate cleaning method, because using the wrong technique can permanently set staining into porous surfaces like brick, concrete block, or older plaster.

**Odor elimination.** Smoke odor is not a surface problem. Particles penetrate drywall, subfloor, cabinetry, and HVAC ductwork. We treat the source rather than masking it, which may include thermal fogging, hydroxyl treatment, or duct cleaning depending on what the structure requires.

**Reconstruction.** Once the structure is clean and dry, we rebuild to pre-loss condition, coordinating with your insurance adjuster throughout.
