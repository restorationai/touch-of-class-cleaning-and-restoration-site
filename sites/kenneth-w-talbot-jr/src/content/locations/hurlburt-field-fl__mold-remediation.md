## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hurlburt Field

Every remediation starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Surface readings alone can be misleading in this climate because concrete block construction, common throughout the area, can read dry at the face while retaining significant moisture behind the block or in the cavity fill. We trace the moisture source before any removal begins, because cutting out mold without correcting the water intrusion path simply delays the next outbreak.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative air pressure containment to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the structure. Affected porous materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of per EPA guidelines. Hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the project. Our team is OSHA trained, and we follow industry-standard protocols for personal protective equipment and containment throughout every phase.

Drying equipment, including commercial dehumidifiers calibrated for high-humidity coastal environments, remains on-site until moisture readings confirm the structure is within acceptable parameters. In this part of Florida, ambient outdoor humidity means standard drying timelines often run longer than they would in a drier climate. We account for that in our project planning rather than pulling equipment prematurely.
