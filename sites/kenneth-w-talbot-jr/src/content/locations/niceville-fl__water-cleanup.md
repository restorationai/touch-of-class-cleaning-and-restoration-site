## Our Water Cleanup Process in Niceville

The first step on any water loss is stopping the spread. Our crew identifies the moisture boundary using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not just the wet spot you can see, but the full perimeter of saturation behind drywall, under tile, and beneath vinyl plank. In slab-on-grade construction, water under a floating floor can extend ten or fifteen feet from the visible damage without any surface indication.

Once the scope is mapped, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable units, then place commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in a calculated pattern designed for the room geometry and material types. In Niceville's climate, ambient outdoor humidity regularly runs above 70 percent, which means open-window drying is counterproductive, the equipment has to work against the outdoor air, not with it. We monitor moisture readings at every visit and adjust equipment placement until structural materials reach target dryness levels before any rebuild begins.

## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Niceville's bayou-adjacent neighborhoods is how quickly elevated outdoor humidity can stall the drying process if a crew relies on ventilation alone. During summer months, the dew point here regularly sits above 70°F, meaning the air outside is already nearly saturated.
