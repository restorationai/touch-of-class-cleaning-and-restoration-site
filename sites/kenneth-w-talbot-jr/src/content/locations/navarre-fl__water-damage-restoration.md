## Why Navarre Properties Face Persistent Water Damage Risk

Santa Rosa County's coastal position puts Navarre in the direct path of Gulf storm systems, but the risk does not begin and end with named storms. The high humidity that blankets this stretch of the Panhandle from spring through fall means that any moisture intrusion, whether from a roof leak, a failed supply line, or an HVAC condensate overflow, lingers. Relative humidity indoors can stay elevated long after the visible water is gone, and that residual moisture is exactly what feeds mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a water event.

The housing stock along the Sound adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in the Navarre Beach and waterfront communities were built on elevated piers or slab-on-grade foundations designed to manage flood loads, but those same designs create crawl spaces and under-floor cavities where standing water collects and evaporates slowly. Vinyl plank and tile floors common in coastal construction can trap moisture beneath them, reading dry at the surface while the subfloor stays saturated for days.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Navarre

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not a visual walkthrough. Water follows the path of least resistance through wall cavities, under baseboards, and into structural framing, so the visible wet area is rarely the full picture.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves through these stages:

- **Water extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extraction units remove standing water from flooring, carpet, and low-lying areas before drying equipment is placed.
- **Structural drying**: Industrial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Drying targets follow IICRC S500 standards.
- **Monitoring**: We return on a set schedule to read moisture levels and adjust equipment placement. Drying is documented daily so you and your adjuster have a clear record.
- **Controlled demolition if needed**: When water has reached wall cavities or subfloor assemblies, targeted removal of affected material is sometimes the only way to dry the structure completely. We keep cuts as small as the moisture data supports.
