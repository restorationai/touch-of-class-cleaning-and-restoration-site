## Why Ponce de Leon Properties See Mold Issues

The combination of climate and construction style here is a persistent driver of mold growth. Older wood-framed homes in this part of Holmes County were often built with minimal vapor barriers beneath the floor system, and crawlspaces that lack adequate cross-ventilation become humid chambers where mold colonizes floor joists, subfloor sheathing, and insulation batts long before a homeowner notices any odor or discoloration above. Air conditioning systems that run heavily through the long Florida summer can also create condensation problems inside ductwork and at supply registers, seeding mold growth in areas that look perfectly clean from the outside. A musty smell in a back bedroom or bathroom is often the first sign, but by that point the colony is usually well established behind the drywall or beneath the flooring.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Ponce de Leon

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection, not just a visual scan. Moisture meters and thermal imaging help locate saturation behind surfaces that read dry to the touch, which matters especially in homes with older plaster or thick wood paneling common in rural Holmes County construction. Once the affected area is mapped, the crew establishes containment using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal. Contaminated materials, whether drywall, insulation, or wood framing, are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines before the structural surfaces are treated and dried down to acceptable moisture levels. ## Local Note

One thing that comes up repeatedly in Holmes County crawlspace work: older homes in this part of the Panhandle were sometimes insulated with fiberglass batts stapled directly to the underside of the floor joists with no vapor barrier between the insulation and the ground. When that insulation gets wet, it holds moisture against the wood for weeks and is nearly invisible as a problem from inside the house. If your home was built before the 1980s and you have a crawlspace, that insulation is worth inspecting after any plumbing leak or period of heavy rain, even if the floors above feel and look fine.
