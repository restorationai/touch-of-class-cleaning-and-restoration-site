## Why Crestview Properties See Water Damage More Than You Might Expect

Okaloosa County's climate is not forgiving to building materials. Crestview averages more than 60 inches of rainfall per year, well above the national average, and the area's sandy loam soils drain quickly in some spots but can become saturated and slow-draining in low-lying sections of town. Homes built on slabs, which are common throughout the Crestview area, can still suffer water intrusion when grade slopes toward the foundation or when HVAC condensate lines back up inside a wall cavity.

Older housing stock in Crestview, particularly homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, often used galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out. A pinhole leak behind drywall can run undetected for weeks, feeding mold growth in a wall cavity long before a homeowner notices a soft spot or a musty smell. Polybutylene pipe, installed in many homes through the early 1990s, is another common failure point in this region. When those lines let go, water spreads fast across concrete slab and into adjacent rooms.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Crestview

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing water entry if the source is still active, shutting a supply valve, diverting a drain, or tarping a roof breach. From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Assessment and moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find water that has migrated behind walls, under flooring, or into ceiling cavities. Water visible on the surface is rarely the full picture.

**Standing water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from floors quickly. On slab-on-grade homes common in Crestview, water under luxury vinyl plank or ceramic tile requires targeted extraction at seams and transitions before the subfloor bond is compromised.

**Structural drying.** High-capacity dehumidifiers and directional air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed randomly. Drying logs are recorded daily so the process is documentable for your insurance adjuster.

**Content handling.** Saturated furniture, cabinetry, and personal items are assessed on-site. Items that can be dried in place are treated; items that cannot are inventoried before removal.
