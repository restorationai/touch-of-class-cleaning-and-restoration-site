## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Laurel Hill

The process starts with a thorough moisture assessment. Before any equipment is placed, the crew maps the actual extent of saturation using moisture meters and thermal imaging. Water visible on the surface is rarely the full picture: in wood-frame construction, water wicks along wall studs and floor joists well beyond the point of entry.

Once the scope is clear, water extraction begins. Standing water is removed first with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, followed by targeted extraction from carpet and padding where applicable. Structural drying comes next: industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned to create a controlled drying environment calibrated to the actual square footage and material types in the affected area. Drying is monitored on a documented schedule, with moisture readings logged at each visit until materials reach acceptable levels. Nothing is closed up until the numbers confirm it.
