## Why Navarre Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Navarre sits on a barrier peninsula between the Gulf of Mexico and Santa Rosa Sound, which means most of the housing stock is slab-on-grade with limited crawlspace to dry out once water gets under flooring. Many homes along and south of Highway 98 were built to withstand wind-borne debris under Florida's building code, but that same code doesn't stop water intrusion around roof penetrations, garage doors, or lanai screens once a named storm sits offshore for a day or two. Sandy soil drains fast on the surface but pushes groundwater up around foundations during storm surge events, so basements are rare but slab perimeters and garage floors take on water anyway. Add in salt-laden air that accelerates corrosion on exposed fasteners and HVAC components, and storm damage repair in Navarre tends to involve more than just drying carpet.
