## Why Hurlburt Field Properties Face Distinct Storm Damage Challenges

The Panhandle's storm season runs longer than most of Florida, with named systems capable of making landfall anywhere from June through November and severe squall lines arriving well outside that window. Properties near Hurlburt Field sit close to Choctawhatchee Bay and the Santa Rosa Sound, which means storm surge is a real threat even from storms that make landfall farther east or west. Wind-driven rain finds its way into soffit vents, attic spaces, and around window frames that may have been sealed years ago and have since dried out and cracked in the Panhandle heat.

Base-adjacent neighborhoods built during earlier construction eras often feature wood-frame construction with older roofing systems, and the combination of high humidity and salt air accelerates the deterioration of roofing materials between storms. When a fast-moving squall tears away shingles or drops a pine tree across a roofline, water entry can begin within minutes and reach wall cavities and subfloor assemblies before the rain even stops.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Hurlburt Field

Every storm job starts with a thorough exterior and interior assessment. We document wind damage, impact damage, and water intrusion points before any material is moved or covered, because that documentation matters when you file with your carrier. Tarping and board-up happen immediately after assessment to stop the weather from continuing to enter the structure.

Once the envelope is secured, water extraction and structural drying begin. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras let us find water that has migrated behind walls or under flooring without tearing into surfaces unnecessarily. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run on a schedule calibrated to the ambient humidity, which in the Panhandle is rarely forgiving. Wet insulation, saturated drywall, and damaged subfloor sections are removed when moisture readings confirm they cannot be dried in place. Throughout the process, our OSHA-trained crew follows industry drying standards so the structure is genuinely dry, not just surface-dry.
