Veterans Remediation & Restoration is based in Freeport and serves homeowners and property managers across the Florida Panhandle and surrounding Gulf Coast communities. Our team is available around the clock, when a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or a storm pushes water through your roof on a Sunday, you can reach a live person and get a crew moving. Coverage decisions are made by dispatch based on your location and the nature of the loss, not by a rigid boundary on a map.

## Where we work

Our home base sits in Freeport, in Walton County, which puts us within practical driving distance of a wide band of the Panhandle, from the bay communities along U.S. 98 to the inland neighborhoods north toward the Alabama state line. The Gulf Coast geography here means our crews are familiar with the challenges that come with it: high humidity that accelerates mold colonization after a water loss, sandy soils that drain quickly in some areas but leave crawl spaces vulnerable in others, and the older wood-frame construction common in many established neighborhoods that holds moisture differently than newer builds. That regional knowledge shapes how we assess and dry a structure, not just how far we drive.

## Coverage by distance

The cities and communities closest to Freeport, including Niceville, Fort Walton Beach, Destin, DeFuniak Springs, and Panama City, are our most frequently served areas, and dispatch can typically have a crew on the road quickly after your call. Communities farther along the Panhandle, such as Pensacola to the west or Panama City Beach and beyond to the east, fall within our broader service footprint. Arrival times at the outer edges of coverage will naturally be longer than for nearby locations, and for large commercial losses or catastrophic storm events, we coordinate logistics accordingly. We do not quote specific arrival windows in minutes for distant locations, what we can tell you is that dispatch prioritizes active water intrusion and fire losses above all else, regardless of distance.

## Don't see your city?

The service-area list on this page covers our most common dispatch zones, but it is not a hard cutoff. For significant losses, major flooding, fire damage, large commercial properties, we routinely travel outside our listed coverage. Call us at (337) 344-1248 and describe your situation. If we can get to you and do the job right, we will.
