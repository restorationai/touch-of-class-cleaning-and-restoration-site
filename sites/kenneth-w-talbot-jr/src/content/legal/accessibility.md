## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Veterans Remediation & Restoration is committed to ensuring that everyone who visits this website, regardless of ability or assistive technology, can access the information they need. Whether you are navigating with a screen reader, using keyboard-only controls, or relying on browser zoom or high-contrast settings, we want the experience to work for you.

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. These guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), describe how to make web content more accessible to people with a range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

## What We Have Done to Support Accessibility

This site is built with screen-reader compatibility in mind. That means meaningful alternative text on images, logical heading structure, descriptive link labels, and form fields paired with visible labels so assistive technologies can interpret them correctly. Color contrast ratios are chosen to remain legible for visitors with low vision or color-vision differences, and interactive elements are reachable by keyboard without requiring a mouse.

We recognize that accessibility is not a one-time checkbox, it requires ongoing attention as the site is updated. We review new content and design changes with these standards in mind.

## Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content may not yet meet every WCAG 2.1 AA criterion. Third-party tools or embedded content, such as mapping widgets or scheduling integrations, may fall outside our direct control. We are actively working to identify and address gaps as they are discovered.

## How to Report an Issue

If you encounter a barrier on this site, a page that does not work with your screen reader, a form you cannot complete, an image missing a description, or anything else that makes it harder to get the information you need, please let us know. We take these reports seriously and will work to address confirmed issues within a reasonable timeframe.

When you contact us, it helps to describe the specific page or element where you experienced the difficulty and the assistive technology or browser you were using. That detail lets us reproduce and resolve the problem more quickly.

## Alternative Access to Information

If a portion of this site is not accessible to you and you need information about our services, estimates, or scheduling, please call or email us directly. We are glad to provide information in an alternative format or walk you through anything on the site by phone.
