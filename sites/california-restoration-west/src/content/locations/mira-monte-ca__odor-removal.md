## Why Mira Monte Properties Hold Odors Longer

The Ojai Valley's geography plays a direct role in how odors behave inside local homes. Temperature inversions are common here: cooler air settles into the valley floor and canyon pockets while warmer air sits above, creating a lid that traps pollutants and smoke both outdoors and inside structures with passive ventilation. Homes built into the hillsides often rely on operable windows and natural airflow rather than forced-air systems, which means there is no mechanical path to flush contaminated air out quickly.

Mira Monte's housing stock skews older, with many properties constructed in the 1950s through 1970s using wood-paneled interiors, textured ceilings, and plaster walls. These porous surfaces absorb smoke and biological odors far more aggressively than modern drywall, and they release those compounds slowly over weeks. A smoke odor that seems manageable on day one can intensify as temperatures rise and the materials off-gas. Crawlspace construction is also common here, and a moisture or animal intrusion odor in that cavity can migrate into living spaces through gaps in subfloor framing before a homeowner realizes where it is coming from.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Mira Monte

Effective odor elimination is not a single step. The process begins with identifying the odor source and the surfaces it has penetrated. In Mira Monte homes with wood paneling or plaster, that means probing beyond the surface layer before any treatment begins.

Depending on what the assessment finds, the crew deploys one or more of the following methods:

- **Thermal fogging** pushes a deodorizing agent in a fine, heated mist that penetrates the same porous surfaces smoke originally reached, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing compounds in the air and on surfaces. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators can run safely in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner cannot vacate the property entirely.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces and is particularly effective for heavy smoke odor in enclosed rooms. Ozone concentration and dwell time are calibrated to room volume and surface porosity.
- **Direct surface treatment** addresses materials like upholstery, carpet padding, and wood trim that have absorbed odor at depth and will continue to off-gas if left untreated.

The IICRC-certified team documents conditions before and after treatment, which supports insurance claims and gives the homeowner a clear record of what was done.
