## Our Mold Remediation Process in Carpinteria

Every project starts with a thorough assessment using moisture meters, thermal imaging, and air sampling to map where active growth is and where conditions are simply favorable for it. Once the scope is defined, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms. Final clearance air sampling confirms that spore counts inside the contained area have returned to levels consistent with or below outdoor baseline readings before containment is removed.
