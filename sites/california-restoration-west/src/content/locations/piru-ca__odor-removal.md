## Why Piru Properties Hold Odors Longer

The climate in this part of Ventura County works against you when odors take hold. Summer temperatures in the canyon regularly push past 100°F, and that heat accelerates the bonding of smoke particles and microbial byproducts to porous surfaces. Older homes in the Piru area, many built during the mid-20th century agricultural boom when the region's citrus and walnut orchards were at their peak, frequently feature original wood-framed walls with minimal vapor barriers. Those walls absorb odor-causing compounds the way a sponge absorbs water: quickly on the surface, but deeply enough that surface-level cleaning barely touches the problem.

The canyon's low humidity swings are also a factor. Dry conditions cause porous materials like plaster, unfinished wood, and older grout to contract slightly, pulling absorbed odors inward rather than releasing them. When humidity rises again, those same materials off-gas, and the smell returns. A single round of spray deodorizer will not interrupt that cycle.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Piru

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying the source material, not just masking what's airborne. The process typically moves through three stages.

**Source removal and surface prep.** Charred material, saturated insulation, or contaminated contents have to come out before any deodorization technology can work. Leaving odor-saturated material in place and running equipment around it is a common shortcut that guarantees the smell returns.

**Primary treatment matched to the odor type.** Smoke odors from structure fires or wildfire intrusion respond well to thermal fogging, which produces a dry fog that penetrates the same micro-pores the smoke originally entered. Biological odors from mold, sewage backup, or decomposition are better addressed with hydroxyl generators, which produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. Ozone treatment is reserved for unoccupied spaces where deep penetration is needed and re-occupancy can be delayed, since ozone concentrations effective against odors are not safe for people, pets, or certain plants.

**Post-treatment verification.** Air quality readings and a physical walkthrough confirm the odor is gone at the source, not just diluted. Documentation from this step is useful if an insurance claim is open.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The right tool depends on the odor class and the building's construction. For Piru's older wood-frame homes, thermal fogging is particularly effective because the fog particles are small enough to follow the same pathways smoke traveled through wall cavities and attic framing. Hydroxyl generators run continuously over 24 to 72 hours and are well-suited to spaces where pets or sensitive plants cannot be relocated. Ozone generators deliver the deepest penetration but require strict protocols around re-entry timing. Our IICRC-certified team selects and documents the method based on the specific loss, not a one-size approach.
