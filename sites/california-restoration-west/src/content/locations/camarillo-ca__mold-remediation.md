## Why Camarillo Properties See Mold Issues

Camarillo sits in the Oxnard Plain, where coastal fog and onshore flow keep relative humidity elevated even when temperatures are mild. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s — a significant portion of Camarillo's residential stock — were constructed with paper-faced drywall and minimal vapor barriers, materials that absorb and hold moisture far more readily than modern building assemblies. Stucco exteriors common throughout the area can develop hairline cracks that channel irrigation water or rain directly into wall cavities, where it sits unseen.

Slab-on-grade foundations, prevalent across many Camarillo neighborhoods, eliminate the under-floor air circulation that a raised foundation provides. When a slab plumbing leak goes undetected — even a slow weep at a fitting — moisture wicks into the surrounding concrete and adjacent framing. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure, which means a slow leak discovered during a weekend is already a remediation job, not just a plumbing repair.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Camarillo

Every job begins with a thorough moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a visual scan. We identify the moisture source first, because remediating mold without eliminating the water intrusion point means the colony returns. Once the source is confirmed, we establish containment using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal.

Affected materials — drywall, insulation, framing where warranted — are removed and bagged in accordance with EPA guidelines. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the project. Structural surfaces that remain are treated and dried to IICRC S520 standard before any reconstruction begins. We document moisture readings at every phase, which matters both for your peace of mind and for insurance claim substantiation.

Because Camarillo's ambient humidity can slow drying times compared to drier inland areas, we size our drying equipment to the actual vapor pressure conditions on-site rather than applying a one-size calculation. A room that would dry in two days in the Inland Empire may need an extra day here — and we account for that in the drying plan rather than closing out prematurely.

Camarillo's older stucco homes — particularly those built before the mid-1980s — frequently have original single-pane aluminum windows that condensate heavily during the marine layer season.
