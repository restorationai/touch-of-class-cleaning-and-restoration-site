## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Castaic

Encapsulation in Castaic is not a one-size approach. The process begins with a thorough inspection of the crawl space using moisture meters and thermal imaging to map where ground moisture is entering and where wood members are already elevated in moisture content. Any standing water or active mold growth is addressed before encapsulation begins, because sealing a wet crawl space traps the problem rather than solving it.

Once the space is dry and clean, the crew installs a heavy-duty reinforced polyethylene liner across the entire floor and up the foundation walls, sealing all seams with moisture-resistant tape and securing the liner to the walls above the frost line. Vents are evaluated and, where appropriate, sealed to convert the crawl space to a conditioned or semi-conditioned environment, which is increasingly the recommended approach in climates like Castaic's where outdoor humidity fluctuates dramatically between seasons. A drainage mat or sump system is added when the inspection reveals that groundwater intrusion is a recurring risk rather than an isolated event.
