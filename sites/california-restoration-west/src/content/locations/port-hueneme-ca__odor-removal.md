## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Port Hueneme

Effective deodorization is a sequence, not a single treatment. The process starts with source removal: any charred material, saturated insulation, or contaminated contents that are physically holding the odor compound are documented and removed before any chemical or mechanical treatment begins. Spraying a deodorizer over a smoke-damaged wall that still has soot embedded in the paint accomplishes nothing lasting.

Once the source is addressed, the method is matched to the odor type and the structure:

- **Thermal fogging** vaporizes a deodorizing solvent that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules bonded to framing, insulation, and ductwork. It is particularly effective in Port Hueneme homes where open-rafter attic spaces allowed smoke to spread widely.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor compounds at a molecular level. Because hydroxyl generators do not require occupants or pets to vacate, they are often the right choice for occupied rental units near the harbor district.
- **Ozone treatment** delivers a high concentration of O3 that oxidizes odor compounds on contact. Ozone requires the space to be unoccupied during treatment and a thorough airing-out period afterward, which we schedule carefully given Port Hueneme's marine layer patterns that can slow off-gassing on overcast mornings.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs continuously throughout the project to capture particulates that carry odor, protecting both the structure and the crew.

After treatment, we verify results with calibrated equipment rather than relying on a technician's nose, which desensitizes quickly in a treated space.
