## Why Oxnard Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same marine layer that keeps Oxnard cooler than the inland valleys creates a humidity baseline that most deodorization equipment is designed around, but rarely optimized for. Relative humidity along the coast routinely sits higher than in Simi Valley or the Santa Clarita corridor, and porous materials like drywall, carpet padding, and wood framing absorb ambient moisture continuously. When a fire, flood, or sewage event introduces a secondary odor source, those already-saturated materials act like a sponge, trapping odor molecules well below the surface layer.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Oxnard

Effective odor elimination is a sequence, not a single treatment. Spraying a deodorizing agent over an untreated source is the fastest way to produce a complaint callback two weeks later. The process starts with identifying every source, which in coastal properties often means checking wall cavities, subfloor voids, and HVAC ductwork before any equipment is deployed.

Once source materials are addressed, the deodorization phase uses the method matched to the odor type and the structure:

- **Hydroxyl generation** works well in occupied or partially occupied spaces because it is safe around people, pets, and electronics. Hydroxyl units run continuously and are effective against biological odors including mold and sewage.
- **Thermal fogging** pushes a deodorizing fog through the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, neutralizing residue inside wall cavities and ceiling spaces that surface cleaning cannot reach.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied structures with severe smoke or organic odors. High-concentration ozone requires full evacuation and proper re-entry protocols, and the timeline is coordinated around the property's occupancy status.

All drying and deodorization work is performed to IICRC standards by an IICRC Certified Firm with OSHA-trained technicians on every job.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

The equipment selection for an Oxnard property accounts for the ambient humidity. Running an ozone generator in a space with 70% relative humidity produces a fraction of the odor-neutralizing effect compared to the same unit in a controlled environment. Before ozone or hydroxyl treatment begins, dehumidification brings the space to a workable humidity range, which also accelerates the deodorizing chemistry.

For smoke odor removal after a kitchen or structure fire, thermal fogging is often the most effective single-method treatment because the fog particles are sized to match smoke particles, allowing them to reach the same surfaces the smoke contaminated. Hydroxyl generators are then run as a follow-up to address residual odors in soft goods and furnishings that remain in the space.

Post-treatment air quality verification confirms that odor compounds have been reduced to acceptable levels before the job is closed out.
