## Why Lake Sherwood Properties Hold Odors Longer

The hillside terrain around Lake Sherwood creates natural air pockets that limit cross-ventilation inside homes. When smoke or biological odors enter a structure, they have fewer pathways to dissipate naturally. Custom-built estates in this area frequently feature high ceilings with exposed beam work, stone fireplaces, and generous use of natural wood throughout. These materials are highly porous and absorb odor-causing compounds readily. Aged wood beams in particular can hold smoke volatiles long after surfaces appear clean, releasing them again when indoor temperatures rise. Homes with canyon-facing exposures also tend to pull outside air through gaps in rooflines and attic vents during wind events, drawing combustion particles into spaces that are difficult to access and treat without specialized equipment.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Lake Sherwood

Effective odor elimination starts with locating every affected surface, not just the obvious ones. Our IICRC-certified team begins with a structured assessment: mapping odor intensity room by room, identifying hidden reservoirs like wall cavities, HVAC ductwork, and crawl spaces, and documenting findings before any treatment begins.

From there, we select the method matched to the source and the structure:

- **Thermal fogging** converts a deodorizing solvent into a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, reaching inside wall cavities and behind cabinetry where surface sprays cannot.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules in the air and on surfaces. Because hydroxyl generators do not require the space to be vacated, they work well in occupied homes or during phased treatments.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces and is particularly effective on heavy smoke odor embedded in structural materials. Ozone concentration and exposure time are calibrated to the volume of the space.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs continuously throughout treatment to capture particulate matter that becomes airborne during the process.

After treatment, we retest odor levels before releasing the space, so the result is confirmed rather than assumed.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Odor Removal

Smoke odor from wildfire events carries a different chemical fingerprint than cigarette smoke or cooking odors. Wildfire smoke contains polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons and fine particulate matter that bond tightly to natural wood and stone, both of which are common in Lake Sherwood's architectural style. Standard masking agents do not address these compounds. Hydroxyl and ozone treatments work because they oxidize the odor molecules themselves rather than covering them. Thermal fogging is particularly useful when smoke has penetrated wall assemblies, since the fog behaves like a gas and follows the same pressure pathways the original smoke used. We carry all three technologies on our service vehicles so the right tool is available at the first visit rather than a follow-up.
