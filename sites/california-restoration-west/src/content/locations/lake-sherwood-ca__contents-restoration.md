## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Lake Sherwood

The process begins with a detailed inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything leaves your property. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear chain of custody from the moment we begin the pack-out.

From there, contents move to a climate-controlled storage facility where they are protected from additional humidity or temperature swings while your home is being repaired. Cleaning methods are matched to each material: ultrasonic tanks for hard goods and non-porous items, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor in soft goods, and hand-cleaning with appropriate solvents for artwork, antiques, and electronics. Items that cannot be restored are documented thoroughly so your adjuster has the evidence needed to process replacement claims.

Once your home passes the structural and environmental clearances required after a major loss, contents are returned, placed, and unpacked. Nothing is rushed back into a space that is still drying or still carrying elevated particulate levels.
