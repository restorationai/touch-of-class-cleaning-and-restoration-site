Simi Valley's warm, dry summers and periodic Santa Ana wind events create a deceptive environment for odors: low humidity keeps surfaces feeling dry while smoke particles, pet dander, and mold volatiles settle deep into porous materials and linger long after the visible source is gone. ## Why Simi Valley Properties Hold Odors Longer Than You Might Expect

Simi Valley's housing stock spans several decades and construction styles, from the ranch-style homes built during the city's rapid growth in the 1960s and 1970s to newer planned communities closer to the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library corridor. Older homes in this area frequently feature wood-paneled interiors, acoustic ceiling tiles, and original ductwork, all of which absorb odor molecules at a higher rate than modern materials. Once smoke, pet urine, or sewage odors penetrate these porous surfaces, they off-gas slowly over weeks and months.

The valley's geography compounds the problem. Simi Valley sits in a basin largely enclosed by the Santa Susana Mountains to the north and the Simi Hills to the south. During temperature inversions, which are common in inland Southern California, air stagnates rather than dispersing. That means smoke from a kitchen fire, a garage fire, or a nearby wildfire does not simply drift away. It settles, and it works its way into every gap in the building envelope.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Simi Valley

Effective odor removal follows the contamination, not just the smell. After an initial walkthrough to identify source materials and affected zones, the process typically moves through several stages calibrated to what the property has actually absorbed.

**Source removal** comes first. No deodorization method works reliably if charred materials, saturated insulation, or contaminated flooring remain in place. Once the source is addressed, the choice of treatment method depends on the type of odor and the materials involved.

**Thermal fogging** uses a heated solvent-based deodorizer converted into a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled, bonding with odor molecules in wall cavities, subfloor spaces, and ductwork. It is particularly effective after fire and smoke losses in Simi Valley's older ranch homes, where smoke can travel through open attic spaces above wood-frame ceilings.

**Hydroxyl generation** produces hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level without requiring occupants or pets to vacate for extended periods. This is often the preferred method when a home is partially occupied or when sensitive materials are present.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe odor contamination. Ozone is a powerful oxidizer, and it requires careful application and a proper airing-out period before re-occupancy. Our IICRC-certified team determines the appropriate concentration and dwell time based on the size of the space and the severity of the loss.

After treatment, post-deodorization verification confirms that odor levels have returned to an acceptable baseline before the job is closed out.
