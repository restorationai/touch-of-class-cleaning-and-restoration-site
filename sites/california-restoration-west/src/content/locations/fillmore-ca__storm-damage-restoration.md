California Restoration West responds to storm damage calls in Fillmore around the clock, bringing IICRC-certified crews and industrial drying equipment to a community where the landscape itself amplifies what the weather delivers.

## Why Fillmore Properties See Storm Damage Differently Than Coastal Cities

Fillmore's position roughly 35 miles inland from the Ventura coast means it misses the marine layer that moderates temperatures on the shoreline — but it catches the channeled energy of storms that accelerate through mountain passes. During Santa Ana wind events, gusts that seem manageable in Ventura can arrive in Fillmore with enough force to strip roofing, drop mature citrus and oak trees onto structures, and drive rain horizontally under eaves and through window seals that were never designed for that angle of entry.

The valley floor's clay-heavy soils, common throughout this part of Ventura County, absorb moisture slowly and drain even more slowly. After a heavy storm, water that has nowhere to go pools against foundation walls, finds its way through crawlspace vents, and wicks into wood framing before a homeowner realizes the exterior has been breached. Homes built in Fillmore's older residential core — many of them constructed in the mid-20th century with wood-framed walls and original stucco exteriors — are particularly vulnerable to this kind of slow infiltration because the stucco can mask moisture intrusion for days.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Fillmore

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence that protects a Fillmore home follows a consistent logic: stop additional intrusion, document everything, dry what can be dried, and replace what cannot.

**Emergency stabilization** comes first. If a tree has breached a roof or a window has failed, we tarp and board before any drying equipment goes in — moisture control is pointless if the sky still has access to the interior.

**Moisture mapping** follows immediately. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water movement through walls, subfloors, and ceiling assemblies. In Fillmore's older stucco homes, water often migrates laterally through the wall cavity well beyond the visible damage point, so we map a wider perimeter than the obvious wet zone.

**Structural drying** uses commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers positioned to create directed airflow through affected cavities. Our IICRC ASD-certified technicians set drying goals based on the IICRC S500 standard and monitor readings daily until materials reach acceptable equilibrium moisture content.

**Documentation for insurance** runs parallel to every step. We photograph affected materials, log moisture readings with timestamps, and produce a scope of damage that gives your adjuster the data they need to process the claim.
