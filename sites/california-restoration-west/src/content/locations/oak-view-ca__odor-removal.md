## Why Oak View Properties Hold Odors Longer

The Ojai Valley's geography creates a natural inversion layer during certain seasons, trapping particulates close to the ground and giving smoke and combustion byproducts more time to penetrate surfaces than they would in a coastal or open-valley setting. Many homes in the Oak View area were built in the mid-twentieth century with tongue-and-groove wood paneling, plaster walls, and original crawl spaces, all of which are highly porous and absorb odor molecules at a rate that painted drywall simply does not. Insulation from that era was often fiberglass batts installed without a vapor barrier, meaning smoke and pet odors can migrate into wall cavities and re-off-gas for weeks after surface cleaning.

The region's periodic wildfire smoke events compound the issue. Even homes that never experienced direct fire damage can absorb fine particulate matter through HVAC systems, attic vents, and gaps around older single-pane windows. That particulate bonds with interior surfaces and requires more than ventilation or masking sprays to neutralize.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Oak View

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying the compound, not just the symptom. Smoke odor from a wood-burning source requires a different approach than protein smoke from a kitchen fire, and both differ from microbial off-gassing caused by mold or sewage. Our IICRC-certified technicians assess the affected materials and select from a tiered set of methods.

**Thermal fogging** uses a solvent-based or water-based deodorizer converted to a fine fog that penetrates the same porous pathways smoke traveled. It is particularly effective on the wood framing and paneling common in Oak View's older housing stock.

**Hydroxyl generation** produces hydroxyl radicals that break down odor molecules at the molecular level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods, making it practical for occupied rentals or phased re-entry after a wildfire evacuation.

**Ozone treatment** delivers a high concentration of oxidizing gas that neutralizes embedded odors in unoccupied spaces. Because ozone requires the structure to remain unoccupied during treatment and for a clearance period afterward, we coordinate timing carefully with property owners and any tenants.

After treatment, we verify results with post-deodorization testing rather than relying on a technician's nose alone.
