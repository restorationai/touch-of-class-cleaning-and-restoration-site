## Why Carpinteria Properties Hold Odors Longer

The town sits in a narrow coastal plain bracketed by the Santa Ynez Mountains to the north and the Pacific to the south. That geography funnels cool, humid air through the area most mornings and evenings, keeping indoor relative humidity elevated even when there has been no water event. Elevated humidity does two things that matter for odor: it keeps odor molecules suspended in air longer, and it allows porous materials like wood framing, plaster, and insulation to absorb and re-release odor compounds over time.

Carpinteria also has a meaningful stock of older residential buildings, many of them built before modern vapor barriers and tight building envelopes were standard practice. Older plaster walls, original hardwood subfloors, and crawl spaces with limited airflow are common in neighborhoods close to the historic downtown corridor. These materials are far more absorbent than modern drywall or engineered flooring, which means smoke, pet, or biohazard odors penetrate deeper and require more than surface-level treatment to fully neutralize.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Carpinteria

Effective deodorization is not a single step. It is a sequence calibrated to the odor source, the affected materials, and the environmental conditions at the property.

The process begins with source identification. An odor that smells like smoke on the surface may have penetrated HVAC ductwork, wall cavities, or attic insulation. Treating only what is visible leaves the underlying source intact, and the odor returns within days.

Once the source is confirmed, the appropriate treatment method is selected:

- **Thermal fogging** uses a heated solvent that converts deodorizing agents into a fine fog, allowing them to penetrate the same porous surfaces that absorbed the original odor. It is particularly effective after fire and smoke events.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing molecules at the chemical level. Hydroxyls are safe for occupied spaces and do not require evacuation, which matters for occupied rentals and commercial properties.
- **Ozone treatment** is highly effective for severe odors but requires the space to be unoccupied during treatment and properly ventilated afterward. Given Carpinteria's predictable morning marine layer, we time ozone treatments to take advantage of afternoon ventilation windows when outdoor air is drier and circulation is better.

After treatment, affected surfaces are wiped or sealed as needed, and air quality is assessed before the space is returned to use.
