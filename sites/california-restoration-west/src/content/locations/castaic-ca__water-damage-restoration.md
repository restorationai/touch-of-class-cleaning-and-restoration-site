## Why Castaic Properties See Water Damage Issues

The Santa Clara River corridor and the terrain surrounding Castaic Lake create drainage patterns that affect properties throughout the area. During heavy rain, hillside runoff can overwhelm residential drainage systems quickly, especially on lots graded toward the home rather than away from it. Many homes in the Castaic area were built during the rapid suburban expansion of the 1980s and 1990s, when tract construction moved fast and vapor barriers, waterproofing membranes, and crawl space drainage were not always installed to the standards used today.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Castaic

When a crew arrives, the first step is moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to find where water has traveled, not just where it is visible. In slab-on-grade homes common to this part of Los Angeles County, that often means checking under flooring at the perimeter and probing wall cavities where water migrates by capillary action.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to create a controlled drying environment. Drying targets are set per the IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 where microbial risk is present. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying record is available for your insurance adjuster.
