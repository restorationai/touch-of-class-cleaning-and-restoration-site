## Why Camarillo Properties See Persistent Odor Problems

The same coastal airflow that makes Camarillo pleasant to live in also keeps relative humidity elevated through much of the year, especially in the morning hours. Odor molecules bind to moisture, and in a home that hasn't fully dried after a water loss or that sits near agricultural land, those molecules find plenty of surfaces to cling to. Camarillo's housing stock spans several decades, and older homes with original drywall, plaster, or hardwood subfloors absorb odors more deeply than newer construction. A smoke event in a home with original wood framing and plaster walls isn't just a surface problem. The smoke particulates penetrate into the material itself, and no amount of ventilation alone will clear it.

Agricultural activity in the surrounding areas also means that certain odors, including fertilizer, composting, and livestock, can infiltrate homes through HVAC systems and crawl spaces. When those smells combine with an interior source like a mold colony or a sewage leak, the result is layered and stubborn.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Camarillo

Effective deodorization isn't a single step. The process begins with source identification, because treating the symptom without removing the cause only delays the problem. Once the source is addressed, the method is chosen based on the material affected and the severity of penetration.

For smoke odor, including wildfire smoke that has worked its way into a home's envelope, thermal fogging is often the most effective approach. The fogging agent penetrates the same pathways the smoke used, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them. For spaces where occupants or pets need to return quickly, hydroxyl deodorization is a non-toxic alternative that works through UV-generated hydroxyl radicals and does not require the space to be vacated for extended periods.

Ozone treatment is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe contamination. Ozone is highly effective but requires careful application and complete ventilation before re-entry. Our IICRC-certified team selects the right method for each situation rather than defaulting to one approach for every job.

After treatment, we verify results with odor testing rather than relying on a technician's nose alone. In a coastal climate like Camarillo's, where humidity can reactivate odor compounds, confirming that the source has been neutralized, not just suppressed, matters.
