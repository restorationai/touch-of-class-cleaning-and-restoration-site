## Why Somis Properties See Distinctive Storm Damage

The land around Somis is predominantly agricultural and semi-rural, which shapes how storm damage plays out differently here than in a dense suburban grid. Many properties carry older wood-frame structures, detached outbuildings, and long private driveways lined by mature trees, all of which become liabilities in a strong Santa Ana wind event or a sustained Pacific storm. Clay-loam soils common to the Ventura County interior drain slowly, so even a moderate rain event can leave standing water against foundation stems for hours. That prolonged saturation wicks into wood framing, crawl spaces, and any unprotected slab penetrations before the storm has even passed.

Tree damage is a particular concern here. The large eucalyptus, pepper trees, and oak specimens that shade many Somis parcels are beautiful in dry weather and genuinely dangerous in a wind-driven rain event: saturated soil reduces root anchoring, and a tree that has stood for decades can fail without much warning. Roof punctures, crushed fencing, and compromised exterior walls from fallen limbs are among the most common calls we receive after a significant storm moves through the county.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Somis

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence that protects your property is consistent. The first priority is emergency stabilization: boarding or tarping breached rooflines, securing broken windows, and stopping active water intrusion before it migrates further into wall cavities or subfloor assemblies. We document everything photographically from the moment we arrive, which matters when an insurance claim is filed.

Once the structure is stabilized, we assess moisture penetration using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water that enters through a roof breach or a flooded crawl space doesn't stay where it lands; it travels along framing members and insulation batts, sometimes appearing in rooms well away from the original entry point. Our IICRC-certified team uses structural drying protocols aligned with the S500 and S520 standards, deploying commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers sized to the actual affected square footage rather than a generic estimate. Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours on wet organic material, so the drying timeline is not flexible.

After drying is confirmed with post-drying moisture readings, we coordinate any needed reconstruction: framing repairs, drywall replacement, insulation, and exterior work. We keep a documented scope of work throughout so your adjuster has a clear picture of what was damaged and what was done.

## Somis Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental storm damage, including wind, falling trees, and rain intrusion caused by a storm breach. What they typically do not cover is pre-existing deterioration that a storm made worse, which is why thorough documentation from the first hour on-site matters.
