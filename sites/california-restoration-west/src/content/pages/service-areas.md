California Restoration West operates around the clock from its Ventura base, ready to dispatch crews the moment a call comes in — whether that's a burst pipe at 2 a.m. or smoke damage discovered on a Sunday afternoon. Coverage spans a wide stretch of Southern California's coast and inland valleys, and because the team runs 24/7, scheduling is never limited to business hours.

## Where we work

Ventura sits near the center of our service territory, which makes it a practical hub for reaching communities along the Pacific Coast Highway corridor, through the Conejo and Simi valleys, and into the Santa Barbara and Los Angeles County edges. The region's mix of older craftsman bungalows, mid-century ranch homes, and newer hillside construction means our crews encounter everything from galvanized plumbing failures in homes built before copper became standard to storm-drainage issues common in areas where the hillsides drain fast and hard after a dry season breaks.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Ventura — Oxnard, Port Hueneme, Camarillo, Thousand Oaks, and Moorpark — typically see the fastest crew arrivals because dispatch routes are straightforward and traffic on the 101 and 118 corridors is manageable outside peak hours. Santa Barbara and Carpinteria to the north are well within regular rotation, as are communities along the 23 and 118 toward Simi Valley and the western San Fernando Valley.

Farther reaches of Ventura County, including Fillmore, Santa Paula, and Ojai, are part of regular coverage, though canyon roads and seasonal conditions on Highway 33 can affect arrival order during and after major storms — exactly when calls tend to spike. The eastern edge of our territory approaches the Los Angeles County line, where we coordinate dispatch timing around freeway conditions on the 405 and 101.

## Don't see your city?

The city list linked on this page covers our most active service zones, but it isn't a hard boundary. For significant losses — a large commercial water event, a fire that affects multiple units, or a mold situation that has spread through a building — we regularly dispatch beyond the listed area. Call (805) 290-7292 and describe the situation; if we can get a crew to you, we will.
