When water backs up through a slab, smoke works its way into drywall, or mold starts spreading behind a bathroom wall, you need someone who can respond fast and actually knows what they're doing. California Restoration West handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, and mold remediation for homeowners and property managers throughout Ventura County — around the clock, every day of the year.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe, an appliance failure, or water intrusion after a storm, standing water causes structural damage within hours and creates ideal conditions for mold growth within days. We extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue doesn't stay where the fire was. Soot particles migrate through HVAC systems and settle into porous materials throughout a home. We assess the full extent of the damage — not just the charred surfaces — and work through odor neutralization and structural cleaning.

**Mold remediation** — Ventura's coastal air keeps humidity elevated year-round, and older construction throughout the area can harbor moisture in wall cavities and crawlspaces long after a visible leak is fixed. We locate the source, contain the affected area, and remediate to industry standards.

**Structural drying** — Water behind plaster or stucco can read dry at the surface while the framing and lath stay saturated. Using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, we verify drying progress at depth — not just at the wall face.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Restoration work is only as good as the training and equipment behind it. California Restoration West holds IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration (WRT), Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT), and Applied Structural Drying (ASD), and operates as an IICRC Certified Firm. Our team is also OSHA trained — relevant when a loss involves hazardous materials or compromised structural conditions.

We're available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, because water damage and fire losses don't wait for business hours. Calling sooner rather than later limits how far damage spreads and typically reduces the overall scope of work.

On the insurance side, we document losses thoroughly — photographs, moisture readings, written scope — and work directly with most major carriers. That documentation matters when an adjuster is reviewing your claim, and it means you're not left assembling paperwork on your own during an already stressful situation.
