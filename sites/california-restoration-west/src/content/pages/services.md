From a burst pipe discovered at midnight to smoke-blackened walls after a kitchen fire, property damage rarely waits for a convenient moment. California Restoration West handles the full range of restoration work — mitigation, cleanup, and rebuild — for homeowners, commercial property owners, landlords, and property managers across Ventura and the surrounding communities. One call starts the process; one company sees it through.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still setting, or a biohazard situation needs immediate containment, the clock matters. Water damage response stops active intrusion, extracts standing water, and begins structural drying before secondary damage — swollen framing, delaminated subfloor, mold colonization — has a chance to take hold. Fire and smoke damage work addresses not just charred material but the soot and acidic residue that continue etching surfaces long after the flames are out. Storm damage services cover wind-driven rain intrusion, roof breaches, and debris impact on both residential and commercial structures. Biohazard cleanup is handled by OSHA-trained technicians following regulated disposal protocols, returning the space to a safe, habitable condition. All emergency services are available around the clock.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction restores the property. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins — replacing drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing, and any structural components that couldn't be saved. Because California Restoration West handles both sides of the process, there's no handoff gap between the remediation crew and a separate general contractor. Scope is documented during mitigation, so reconstruction estimates reflect what was actually removed, not a separate site visit weeks later. The same applies to larger renovation projects tied to a loss, where code-required upgrades or layout changes can be incorporated into the rebuild.

## Specialty services

Some situations call for targeted work outside the emergency response track. Mold inspection and remediation — performed by IICRC AMRT-certified technicians — identifies hidden growth, establishes containment, and removes affected material without cross-contaminating clean areas. Odor removal goes beyond masking: thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatments reach into wall cavities and HVAC pathways where surface cleaning can't. Board-up and tarping services secure a structure immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism damage, preventing weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while the restoration plan is finalized.
