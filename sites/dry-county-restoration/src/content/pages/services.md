When water is soaking through your drywall at 2 a.m., or smoke residue is settling into every surface after a kitchen fire, the last thing you need is to coordinate four different contractors. Dry County Restoration handles the full arc of property damage — from the first hour of emergency response through the final coat of paint — for homeowners, commercial property owners, landlords, and property managers across Corona and the surrounding Inland Empire communities.

## Emergency restoration services

Some losses can't wait for a callback. Water intrusion spreads fast — within 24 to 48 hours, saturated framing and drywall become hospitable to mold. Fire and smoke leave behind acidic soot that etches surfaces the longer it sits. That's why the emergency side of the operation runs around the clock. **Water damage restoration** addresses extraction, structural drying, and moisture mapping before secondary damage takes hold. **Fire and smoke damage restoration** combines soot removal, odor neutralization, and content pack-out so the structure can be properly assessed. **Biohazard cleanup** — sewage backups, trauma scenes, and similar situations — is handled by OSHA-trained technicians using containment protocols that protect the rest of the property. **Storm damage restoration** covers wind-driven rain intrusion, roof breaches, and the flooding that follows Southern California's increasingly intense weather events. Board-up and tarping are available as immediate stabilization when the structure is exposed.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and cleared, the rebuild phase begins: framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Handling both phases under one contractor means the crew that documented what came out knows exactly what needs to go back in — no gaps in scope, no finger-pointing between a remediation company and a separate GC. This matters especially for insurance claims, where a continuous scope of work is easier to document and supplement.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an emergency, but some problems quietly worsen when ignored. **Mold inspection and remediation** — performed by IICRC AMRT-certified technicians — addresses visible growth and the hidden colonization that can develop behind walls after a slow leak. **Odor removal** handles smoke, pet, and biological odors that survive surface cleaning, using equipment like hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging rather than masking agents. **Board-up and emergency securing** stabilizes a property after break-ins, vandalism, or storm damage until permanent repairs can begin.
