Dry County Restoration is based in Corona and covers a wide stretch of the Inland Empire, the eastern edges of Los Angeles County, and portions of San Diego County. Because water doesn't wait for business hours, dispatch is available around the clock — call (951) 667-9910 any time and a real person will route a crew toward you. The service-area pages linked below break down exactly what we handle in each city.

## Where we work

Corona sits at the junction of Riverside and Orange counties, which puts the team within practical reach of a broad arc of Southern California communities. To the west, coverage extends through the Chino Hills corridor and into the eastern San Gabriel Valley. To the north, crews travel up through the Pomona Valley and into the foothills communities along the base of the San Gabriel Mountains. South and east, the territory stretches through Temecula wine country and into the higher-elevation communities of the San Jacinto Mountains. The region's mix of housing — 1960s tract homes with original copper plumbing, newer master-planned communities built on expansive clay soils, and hillside properties with complex drainage — means the team works across a wide range of loss types and building conditions on any given week.

## Coverage by distance

Cities immediately surrounding Corona — Norco, Eastvale, Mira Loma, Chino, Ontario, and Riverside — are the closest dispatches and typically see crews arrive first. The next ring out includes Rancho Cucamonga, Fontana, Moreno Valley, Perris, and Lake Elsinore. Communities farther out, such as Temecula, Murrieta, Hemet, and the foothill cities of Claremont and Upland, are well within the service area but travel time extends with distance — and on the I-15 or SR-91 during peak commute hours, that distance matters. For losses at the outer edge of coverage, the team coordinates arrival windows honestly rather than promising a clock time that traffic can undercut.

## Don't see your city?

The linked pages don't cover every ZIP code we'll travel to. For significant losses — a burst pipe that has spread across multiple floors, a fire with structural involvement, a large commercial property — it's worth a call regardless of where you are. Reach out at (951) 667-9910 and describe the situation; if the job warrants the drive, the answer is usually yes.
