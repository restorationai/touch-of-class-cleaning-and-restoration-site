## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Dry County Restoration is committed to making our website usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We believe that access to information about emergency restoration services should not depend on a visitor's physical or cognitive abilities.

This site is designed and maintained with the goal of conforming to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** — the widely recognized benchmark for accessible web content.

## What We've Done to Support Access

Our site is built to work with common assistive technologies. That means meaningful alt text on images, sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds, logical heading structure so screen readers can navigate by section, and form fields that are properly labeled so they can be completed without a mouse. Navigation is designed to be operable by keyboard alone, and we avoid content that flashes or auto-plays in ways known to cause problems for users with vestibular or photosensitive conditions.

We also aim to keep page language clear and direct — which matters for screen readers that interpret language settings, and for visitors using cognitive aids or translation tools.

## Known Limitations

We are a small restoration contractor, not a dedicated web development firm. While we work to maintain accessibility across the site, some third-party content — such as embedded maps or insurance-carrier portals we link to — may not be fully within our control. If you encounter a barrier on any page we own and operate, we want to know about it.
