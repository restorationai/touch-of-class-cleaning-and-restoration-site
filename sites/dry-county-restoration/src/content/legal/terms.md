## Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Dry County Restoration collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website. It applies to visitors located in California and elsewhere in the United States.

### What We Collect

When you fill out a contact or estimate request form, we collect the information you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage. We also collect standard anonymous analytics data (pages visited, browser type, general location) through website analytics tools. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

### How We Use It

Information you submit is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site assessment, and — if you authorize us to work with your insurance carrier — to document and coordinate your claim. We do not use your contact information for marketing campaigns or share it with third parties for any purpose unrelated to your restoration project.

### How We Share It

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only circumstance in which we share your information with outside parties is when you have explicitly authorized us to communicate with your insurance carrier or a third-party administrator as part of the claims process.

### Retention

Contact and project records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the service relationship and meet any applicable legal or regulatory obligations under California law. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us directly.

### Your Rights (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives you the right to know what personal information we hold about you, request its deletion, and opt out of any sale of that information — though we do not sell personal data. To exercise any of these rights, reach out to us at the contact information below.

### Privacy Questions

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at stephen@drycountyrestoration.com or by phone at (951) 667-9910.

## Terms of Service

These terms describe the general conditions under which Dry County Restoration provides services. The actual service relationship is governed by the signed work-authorization form you receive before any work begins — that document controls scope, pricing, and authorization, not this page.

### Service Area

Dry County Restoration operates within the state of California. Services outside our current service area may not be available. Contact us to confirm coverage for your specific location before scheduling.

### Estimates

Any estimate provided — whether by phone, email, or during an initial site visit — is an assessment based on visible conditions at the time. It is not a binding contract. Work does not begin and pricing is not finalized until both parties have signed a written work-authorization agreement.

### Payment Terms

Accepted payment methods and due dates are specified in your signed work-authorization form. Where Dry County Restoration is coordinating directly with your insurance carrier, payment terms reflect the agreed billing arrangement outlined in that authorization. Any amounts not covered by insurance remain the property owner's responsibility as described in the signed agreement.

### Insurance Coordination

We are experienced in working alongside insurance carriers and can document losses, photograph affected materials, and communicate with adjusters on your behalf — but only with your written authorization. We do not guarantee specific claim outcomes, as coverage determinations are made solely by your carrier.

### Scope of Work

Work performed will match the scope described in the signed work-authorization form. If conditions discovered during the project differ materially from what was visible at the time of the estimate, we will notify you before proceeding with any work outside the original scope.

### Liability

Dry County Restoration operates as a licensed contractor under California state regulations. Our liability is limited to the services described in the signed work-authorization agreement. We are not responsible for pre-existing conditions, hidden defects, or damage caused by circumstances outside the scope of the authorized work.

### Disputes

Any dispute arising from services provided will first be addressed through direct communication between the parties. If a resolution cannot be reached, disputes are subject to the laws of the State of California and the jurisdiction of Riverside County courts.
