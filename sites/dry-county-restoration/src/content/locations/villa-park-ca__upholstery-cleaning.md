## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Villa Park

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, cotton blends, microfiber, wool, and genuine or bonded leather all respond differently to moisture and heat. Fabric pieces get a pre-inspection for colorfastness, followed by dry soil removal, a pH-balanced pre-treatment, and hot water extraction or low-moisture steam depending on the fabric's tolerance. Leather pieces are cleaned with a dedicated leather cleaner and conditioner rather than the same solution used on fabric, since the wrong product can strip natural oils and accelerate the cracking already common in Villa Park's dry climate. We finish with controlled-airflow drying so cushions and frames don't sit damp long enough to invite odor or mildew, particularly important given how quickly indoor humidity can swing in this part of the county.
