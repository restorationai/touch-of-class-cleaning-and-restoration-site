## Why Chino Properties See Water Damage Differently

Chino's residential build-out accelerated through the 1980s and 1990s, which means a large share of the city's single-family homes are now old enough for original polybutylene or early CPVC supply lines to be showing their age. Those materials are prone to slow pinhole leaks that go unnoticed inside walls or under slab until a floor buckles or a cabinet base turns soft. The city also sits on the Chino groundwater basin, and in wetter winters the water table in lower-lying sections of town can rise enough to push moisture through slab cracks — a problem that looks like a plumbing leak on the surface but has a completely different cause and fix.

Chino's HOA-governed communities — and there are many, particularly in the newer planned developments along the southern and eastern edges of the city — add another layer. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Chino

The first thing our crew does on arrival is locate and confirm the water source is controlled — shut-off confirmed, or the utility valve at the meter closed if needed. From there, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters establish exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and under flooring. In Chino's stucco-exterior homes, water that enters through a roof or window can track down the interior face of an exterior wall for several feet before showing on the surface.

**Water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing water and pull residual moisture from carpet, pad, and subfloor. On slab foundations — the standard in most of Chino's housing stock — we pay particular attention to the seam where flooring meets the slab, where water pools and lingers.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Drying is documented daily against the IICRC S500 standard so there's a clear record for your insurance carrier.

**Clearance readings.** We don't call a job complete until every affected material reads at or below the baseline moisture levels for its material class. That documentation matters if a carrier or HOA asks for proof of completion.
