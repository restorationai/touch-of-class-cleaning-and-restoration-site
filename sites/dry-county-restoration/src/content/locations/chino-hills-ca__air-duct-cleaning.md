## Why Chino Hills HVAC Ducts Accumulate Debris Faster Than You'd Expect

The geography here works against clean ductwork in a few specific ways. The Chino Hills terrain funnels wind through residential corridors, carrying fine clay-rich dust from the surrounding hills and agricultural land to the east. That particulate is fine enough to pass through standard filters and coat duct walls, damper blades, and coil surfaces. Homes built during the 1990s and early 2000s — when much of the residential development in this area expanded rapidly — often used flex duct systems that develop small sags and kinks over time, creating low spots where debris settles and moisture can pool if the system ever runs in high-humidity conditions. Add in the region's periodic wildfire smoke events, which push fine ash particles deep into return air pathways, and the case for periodic professional cleaning becomes concrete rather than precautionary.

Dryer vents are a related concern that often gets overlooked. The combination of long duct runs common in two-story homes and the dry, lint-heavy environment means dryer vents in Chino Hills homes can reach dangerous restriction levels faster than homeowners expect.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Chino Hills

The process starts with a visual inspection of the supply and return registers, the air handler cabinet, and any accessible duct sections — looking specifically for visible debris accumulation, evidence of moisture intrusion, and the condition of flex duct connections. Before any cleaning begins, the crew establishes negative pressure inside the duct system using a truck-mounted or portable HEPA-filtered vacuum unit, so that dislodged debris moves toward the collection point rather than into the living space.

Agitation tools — rotary brushes and compressed-air whips — work through each branch run to break up compacted dust and debris from the duct walls. The main trunk lines are addressed last, after branch runs are cleared. If sanitization is warranted — particularly after a rodent intrusion, a mold-related event, or a wildfire smoke episode — an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent is applied by fogging or coating after the mechanical cleaning is complete. The system is then inspected again before registers are reinstalled and the air handler is returned to service.

For dryer vent cleaning, the technician measures airflow before and after to confirm the restriction has been cleared — a step that matters for both safety and appliance efficiency.

## Equipment and Methods We Use

The truck-mounted vacuum system creates the negative pressure that makes mechanical agitation effective — without it, brushing duct walls simply redistributes debris rather than removing it.
