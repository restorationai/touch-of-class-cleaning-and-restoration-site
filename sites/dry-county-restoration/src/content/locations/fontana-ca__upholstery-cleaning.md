## Why Fontana Furniture Wears Differently

Fontana sits in a part of San Bernardino County that runs hot and dry for most of the year, with Santa Ana wind events pushing fine dust and ash into homes even when windows stay shut. That dust settles into upholstery fibers and works its way past the surface, which is why a couch that looks lightly dirty on top often tests much dirtier once we pull fibers apart during inspection. The city's role as a logistics and distribution hub also means many households have at least one family member commuting past warehouses and rail yards daily, tracking in fine particulate on clothing that ends up ground into sofa arms and cushions. Add in hard water common across the region, and light-colored fabric can develop faint mineral rings after DIY spot-cleaning attempts that never fully dried.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Fontana

We start with a dry-soil extraction pass, pulling out the embedded dust and grit before any moisture touches the fabric, since wetting a heavily soiled cushion first just turns dust into mud. From there we test for colorfastness and fiber type, because a poly-blend sectional and a natural-fiber accent chair need different agitation and different dwell times for cleaning solution. Most fabric pieces get hot water extraction, which lifts embedded soil without over-wetting the padding underneath, a real concern in a climate where humidity swings can leave a cushion feeling dry on top while the foam core stays damp for days. Leather pieces get a separate cleaning and conditioning step, since Fontana's dry air pulls moisture out of hide faster than more humid climates and untreated leather here cracks noticeably sooner.

## Equipment & Methods We Use

For fabric upholstery we rely on hot water (steam) extraction as the default method because it reaches into seams and tufting where dust and skin oils build up, and we run low-moisture cleaning on pieces that need a faster dry time or can't tolerate heavy water exposure, like antique or delicate weave fabrics. Leather gets pH-balanced cleaners followed by a conditioning treatment, never generic all-purpose spray, since the wrong product strips the finish and accelerates the exact cracking that Fontana's climate already encourages. We adjust dwell time and extraction passes based on how soiled a piece is rather than running a single fixed cycle on every job, which matters more here than in cooler, damper cities where dirt sits closer to the surface.
