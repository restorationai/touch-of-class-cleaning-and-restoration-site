## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Villa Park

We start by finding the actual water path, not just the visible damage, using moisture meters and thermal imaging to track how far water has moved under flooring, through baseboards, or inside slab penetrations. On properties with detached garages, barns, or guest units, which are common in Villa Park, we check those structures separately since they're often on their own plumbing branch and can hold hidden moisture even when the main house tests dry. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water from carpet, pad, and any low points before setting air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the room's size and the material affected. Drying is monitored daily and documented against the IICRC S500 standard, which gives both the homeowner and the insurance adjuster a clear record of when materials actually reach dry standard rather than just looking dry on the surface.
