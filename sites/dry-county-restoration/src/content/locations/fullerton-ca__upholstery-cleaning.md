## Why Fullerton Furniture Wears Differently

Orange County's groundwater runs hard, and that mineral content shows up as dull, stiff-feeling fabric and faint white residue on cushions cleaned with tap water and an over-the-counter spray. Add the Santa Ana wind pattern that blows dust and fine particulate through open windows and screen doors for weeks at a stretch each fall, and upholstery in this part of the county collects more embedded grit than the fabric on a similar piece in a marine-layer coastal town. A lot of the housing stock here also dates to the postwar suburban boom that shaped much of inland Orange County, meaning many living rooms still hold furniture passed down or purchased decades ago, natural fibers, older foam, and fabric blends that don't tolerate aggressive chemical cleaners well.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Fullerton

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, cotton, linen, synthetic blend, or leather, since each responds differently to heat, moisture, and pressure. Fabric pieces get a pre-treatment for the visible stains and odor sources, then hot water extraction that lifts embedded soil without oversaturating the padding underneath, which matters in our dry climate where cushions can otherwise trap moisture and develop a musty smell before they fully dry. Leather gets a separate, gentler process: a pH-balanced cleaner, careful conditioning afterward, and no steam, since heat and moisture break down leather's finish over time. We finish every job with a grooming pass to restore the nap and set the fabric texture evenly.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

We use low-moisture hot water extraction as our default method for fabric upholstery, which pulls soil and water out in the same pass so cushions dry faster in Fullerton's low-humidity air, typically within a few hours rather than overnight. For delicate or vintage fabrics common in older Fullerton homes, we run a dry-solvent or encapsulation method instead, since some natural fiber blends shrink or water-spot under standard extraction. Leather pieces get manual cleaning and conditioning tools rather than any wet extraction equipment, since leather and water don't mix well regardless of the method.
