## Why Norco Homes See Accelerated Duct Buildup

Norco's zoning allows horses, chickens, goats, and other livestock on residential lots — which is rare for a city of its size anywhere in California. That agricultural character means fine organic dust (hay fiber, dried manure particulate, arena footing material) is airborne at ground level far more often than in a typical suburb. Return-air grilles set low on walls or near floor level — common in the ranch-style homes that dominate Norco's housing stock — pull that dust directly into the duct system before the filter has a chance to catch it.

The climate compounds the problem. Norco sits in the western Inland Empire where summer temperatures routinely exceed 100°F. Systems run long cycles, move high volumes of air, and generate enough static pressure to dislodge debris that has been loosely adhering to duct walls for years. When monsoon moisture pushes in from the southeast in late summer, humidity spikes can create conditions inside poorly sealed ducts where dust and organic material begin to hold together — a precursor to the kind of buildup that eventually affects airflow and indoor air quality.

Older ranch homes — many built in the 1960s and 1970s when Norco was being subdivided from agricultural land — frequently have duct runs that were sized for the original single-speed equipment of that era. Those larger, lower-velocity ducts are especially prone to sediment settling at horizontal runs and at elbow transitions.

## Our Duct Cleaning Process in Norco

Every job starts with a camera inspection of the main trunk lines and accessible branch runs. This tells us what we're dealing with before any equipment goes in — whether it's compacted debris at a low-velocity horizontal run, microbial growth near a poorly sealed plenum, or simply years of fine dust layered on duct walls.

We use negative-pressure extraction: a high-powered vacuum unit is connected at the air handler, and the entire duct system is placed under negative pressure before agitation begins. Rotary brush tools and compressed-air whips work from each register back toward the collection point, so loosened material travels toward the vacuum rather than into the living space. Registers and grilles are removed, cleaned separately, and reinstalled.

For systems where inspection reveals microbial growth or persistent odor — something that can happen when monsoon-season humidity has been drawn into an older, less-airtight duct system — we apply an EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to interior duct surfaces after cleaning. We also clean dryer vents as a standalone service or alongside duct cleaning; in Norco's larger homes, dryer vent runs are often longer than standard, and lint accumulation in those extended runs is a genuine fire risk.
