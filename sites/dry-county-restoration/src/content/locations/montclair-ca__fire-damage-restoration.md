## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Montclair

We start with a damage assessment that separates heat damage, smoke penetration, and water used in suppression, because in older Montclair homes these three problems behave differently in plaster versus drywall. Soot removal comes next, using dry sponging and HEPA vacuuming on porous plaster before we bring in wet-cleaning agents, since plaster that's scrubbed too early can smear residue deeper into the wall rather than lifting it out. Odor control follows, typically thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment, calibrated longer for homes with original wood trim and cabinetry, which hold smoke odor more stubbornly than the engineered wood common in newer construction. Structural drying and rebuild close out the job, and our team carries IICRC training in water and structural drying so the moisture side of a fire loss, hose water, suppression runoff, doesn't turn into a secondary mold problem while the smoke damage is being addressed.

## Local note

A number of Montclair's older single-story homes still have swamp coolers or retrofit window units rather than central HVAC, and those systems pull outside air directly into the living space.
