## Why Ontario Furniture Gets Dirty Faster

Ontario sits in a part of the Inland Empire where Santa Ana wind events push fine dust and pollen indoors for weeks at a time, and the region's logistics and warehouse economy adds its own layer of airborne particulate that settles into fabric weave even in homes with central air. Add in hard water common across much of the Inland Empire, which leaves mineral residue on any upholstery near a window AC unit or humidifier, and you get fabric that looks dingy well before it's actually worn out. Pet ownership rates in the area's single-family tract homes also mean dander and oils accumulate in cushions long before an odor becomes obvious to the people living with it every day.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Ontario

We start with a fabric or leather identification step, because a hot, arid climate like Ontario's treats materials differently than a coastal city would. Fabric pieces get a dry soil pre-vacuum to pull out the dust load before any moisture touches the fibers, since wetting embedded grit first just grinds it deeper. From there we use hot water extraction calibrated to the fabric's moisture tolerance, followed by a controlled drying pass, important in a region where indoor humidity swings hard between air-conditioned summer interiors and drier winter air. Leather pieces get a pH-balanced cleaning and conditioning treatment rather than steam, since leather that's already been drying out under a sunny window doesn't respond well to added heat.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

For fabric sofas, sectionals, and dining chairs we run truck-mounted or portable hot water extraction depending on access, paired with low-moisture encapsulation on pieces that can't tolerate a long dry time. Leather gets cleaned with dedicated leather-safe solutions and conditioned afterward to replace the oils extraction and everyday sun exposure strip away. We test colorfastness on an inconspicuous area first, which matters more than people think on older upholstery that's spent years in a west-facing Ontario living room.
