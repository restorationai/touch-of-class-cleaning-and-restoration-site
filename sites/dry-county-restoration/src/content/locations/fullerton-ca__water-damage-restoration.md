## Why Fullerton Properties Are Prone to Water Damage

Fullerton's residential neighborhoods were built in waves — many homes from the 1950s through the 1970s feature galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out, often showing no exterior warning before they fail. Slab-on-grade foundations, common throughout the city, hide plumbing runs that are difficult to access and even harder to dry once saturated. The expansive clay soils across much of northern Orange County shift with seasonal moisture changes, stressing sewer laterals and underground supply lines over time. When those lines crack, water doesn't just pool — it wicks upward through the slab and into flooring assemblies before a homeowner notices anything is wrong. Older homes in Fullerton's established neighborhoods also tend to have original cast-iron drain lines that corrode and back up, sending sewage-contaminated water across finished floors.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Fullerton

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — we use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to locate water that has migrated beyond the visible wet area. In Fullerton's older homes, water behind plaster or beneath hardwood can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated. Once we understand the full scope, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then establish a drying system calibrated to the specific materials and square footage involved.

Structural drying in Fullerton follows the IICRC S500 standard, which means we set drying goals based on the materials present — not a fixed number of days. We place commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in configurations that create directed airflow through wall cavities and under flooring. We return daily to take psychrometric readings and adjust equipment until every monitored assembly reaches its drying goal. Documentation from each visit goes into a moisture log that your insurance adjuster can review directly.

## Fullerton Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, a washing machine supply line failure, an ice maker connection that lets go. What they typically exclude is long-term seepage or deferred maintenance, which is why our documentation process matters from the first hour on-site. We photograph affected materials before any removal, record moisture readings across the loss area, and produce a scope of work formatted to align with Xactimate line items that most major carriers use for claims review. We bill carriers directly where assignment of benefits is in place, and we communicate with adjusters throughout the process so you are not left translating between your contractor and your insurance company.
