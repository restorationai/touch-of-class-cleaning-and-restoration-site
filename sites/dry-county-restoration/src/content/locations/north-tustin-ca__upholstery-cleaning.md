## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, cotton blends, synthetic microfiber, top-grain or bonded leather, since each reacts differently to moisture and cleaning agents. Fabric pieces get a pre-inspection for colorfastness, a low-moisture pretreatment to loosen embedded dust and oils, then hot water extraction with upholstery-specific tools that control how much moisture reaches the cushion fill. Leather pieces are cleaned with pH-balanced solutions and finished with a conditioner suited to the hide type, which matters more in North Tustin's drier air than it would in a coastal city where leather stays naturally supple longer. Drying times run faster here too, thanks to the lower ambient humidity, but we still use air movers on heavier fill cushions to prevent moisture from sitting against the frame.
