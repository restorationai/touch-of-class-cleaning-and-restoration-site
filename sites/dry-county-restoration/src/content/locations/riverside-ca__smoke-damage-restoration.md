## Why Riverside Properties See Smoke Damage Issues

Riverside sits in the Inland Empire, tucked against the Box Springs and Jurupa hills, which puts the city squarely in the path of wildfire smoke carried down from mountain and foothill fires during late summer and fall. Santa Ana wind events push that smoke and fine ash into neighborhoods miles from any flame, and it doesn't just coat exterior surfaces. Older homes built in the 1950s through 1970s, common across much of Riverside, often still run original or lightly updated ductwork that pulls smoke particulate straight into the return air system and recirculates it through every room. Add in the valley's heat, which keeps windows and attic vents open for ventilation much of the year, and you get smoke infiltration patterns that are more aggressive and more widespread than what we see in tighter, more insulated coastal construction.

## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Riverside

We start with a walkthrough to identify what kind of smoke we're dealing with, protein smoke from a stovetop fire behaves very differently than the dry, fine particulate from wildfire ash, and that determines our cleaning agents and sequence. Hard surfaces get wiped down with specialized sponges and solvents matched to the residue type. Soft goods, upholstery, drapery, carpet, get HEPA vacuumed and treated or removed depending on saturation. Because heat accelerates the way smoke odor bonds to porous materials, we move faster on drywall, insulation, and wood framing in Riverside than we would in a milder climate, since a delay of even a day or two can mean the difference between surface cleaning and having to seal or replace material. HVAC duct cleaning is almost never optional here. If the system ran during or after the event, we treat the ducts as a contamination source, not just a symptom.
