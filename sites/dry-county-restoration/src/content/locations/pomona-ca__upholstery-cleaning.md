Pomona's inland valley climate means dust, pollen, and Santa Ana wind debris settle into fabric fibers faster than they do closer to the coast, and upholstery in Pomona homes often shows dulling and odor buildup well before the cushions look visibly dirty. Whether it's a leather sectional collecting sun-baked oils near a west-facing window or a fabric sofa that's absorbed a season of dry heat and pet dander, getting the right cleaning method matched to the material matters more here than people expect.

## Why Pomona Furniture Wears Differently

Pomona sits far enough inland that summer heat and low humidity pull moisture out of leather and natural fibers, which makes upholstery more prone to cracking, fading, and stiffening if it isn't conditioned correctly after cleaning. At the same time, the fine dust that blows through the valley during Santa Ana wind events works its way deep into upholstery weave, well past what a vacuum can reach. Add in the area's harder water, which can leave mineral residue behind if the wrong extraction method is used, and it becomes clear why a generic cleaning approach often falls short on local furniture. Homes built from the 1950s through the 1970s, a common era across Pomona's residential stock, frequently still have original built-in banquettes or window seat upholstery that needs gentler handling than modern factory fabric.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fiber or leather type, since fabric, aniline leather, and pigmented leather all respond differently to moisture and cleaning agents. For fabric sofas and sectionals, we typically use low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded dust and oils without oversaturating the cushion fill, which matters in Pomona's dry climate because over-wetted foam can take longer to fully release moisture even with fans running. Leather pieces get a pH-balanced cleaning solution followed by a conditioning treatment to replace the oils that inland heat strips out over time. As an IICRC Certified Firm, our technicians follow manufacturer-recommended methods for each material rather than treating every couch the same way, and we test an inconspicuous area first on any fabric we haven't worked with before.
