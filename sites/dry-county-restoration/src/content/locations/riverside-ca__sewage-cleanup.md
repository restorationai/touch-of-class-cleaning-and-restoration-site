## Why Riverside Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Riverside's housing stock spans a wide range of eras, from Craftsman bungalows built in the early 1900s to post-war tract homes and newer HOA developments near the city's eastern edges. The older neighborhoods closer to downtown were largely built when clay tile was the standard for sewer laterals. Clay pipe joints shift over time as Riverside's expansive adobe soils swell with winter rain and contract through the long, dry summers — a cycle that cracks joints, allows root intrusion, and narrows the effective diameter of the line. When grease buildup or a heavy rain event adds pressure, those compromised laterals back up into the lowest fixtures in the home.

The city's connections to the regional sewer system also mean that a capacity surge during a major storm can push wastewater backward through private laterals before the city main even shows a blockage. Homeowners sometimes see the backup and assume the problem is isolated to their plumbing — but the source may be further down the line, which affects both the remediation scope and the insurance documentation required.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Riverside

Arrival starts with containment, not extraction. Before any equipment runs, the affected area is isolated with poly sheeting and negative air pressure to prevent aerosolized contaminants from spreading through HVAC systems or into adjacent rooms. That step matters in Riverside's drier climate, where low indoor humidity can actually accelerate the airborne dispersal of dried sewage particulate.

Once containment is established, standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units, and affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — are assessed for removal. Category 3 contamination standards (per the IICRC S500) require that porous materials in direct contact with raw sewage be removed rather than dried in place. We document every material decision with photographs and moisture readings, which becomes the basis for your insurance claim.

After demo, all affected structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials. We then run structural drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — calibrated to Riverside's ambient conditions. Drying logs are recorded daily and provided to your adjuster or contractor at closeout.
