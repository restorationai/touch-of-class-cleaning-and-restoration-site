## Why Chino Hills Upholstery Wears Differently

Much of Chino Hills was built from the late 1980s through the early 2000s, and that generation of homes leans heavily on open floor plans, vaulted ceilings, and oversized windows facing the hills. That's great for natural light, but it also means fabric and leather furniture sit in direct sun for long stretches of the day. UV exposure dries out leather fibers and fades fabric dye unevenly, usually on the side of a sectional closest to the window. Combine that with the area's low humidity and frequent wind events, and dust settles into cushions faster than in coastal or shaded neighborhoods. Households with equestrian hobbies or large dogs add another layer: dander, dirt, and hay dust get worked into fibers in a way that a regular vacuum pass doesn't reach.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fabric and fiber test to confirm whether a piece can safely take hot water extraction or needs a drier, low-moisture method. Natural fiber blends and older leather pieces common in Chino Hills' custom and semi-custom homes often call for a gentler approach than mass-market furniture. After pre-treating soiled areas and any pet-related odor sources, we extract with hot water and a controlled amount of moisture, since over-wetting is the biggest risk with upholstery, especially foam-filled cushions that can take days to dry fully if soaked. Leather pieces get cleaned and conditioned separately to replace oils lost to sun exposure and dry air. Most jobs finish with fans placed to speed drying so furniture is usable again the same day rather than left damp overnight.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

We run truck-mounted and portable hot water extraction systems depending on the piece and the access at the property, since some of Chino Hills' hillside lots have longer driveways or multi-level layouts that make a portable unit more practical for a single sofa or loveseat. For leather, we use pH-balanced cleaners and conditioners rather than generic household products, which tend to strip finish and accelerate cracking, especially on pieces that already see a lot of sun. Fabric protection can be applied after cleaning on request, which helps in households where pet hair, dirt from trail rides, or everyday wear are recurring issues rather than one-time spills.
