Water damage in Pomona rarely shows up as a dramatic flood. More often it starts as a slow slab leak under a 1940s bungalow or a washing machine line that finally gives out in a converted garage, and by the time carpet feels spongy or a baseboard bows, water has already worked its way into subfloor and framing. Dry County Restoration handles water removal, extraction, and structural drying for Pomona homes and businesses, and we answer 24/7 because standing water doesn't wait for business hours.

## Why Pomona Homes Face Water Damage Risks

Pomona sits in the eastern San Gabriel Valley, where clay-heavy soil expands and contracts with the seasons more than homeowners expect. That soil movement stresses slab foundations and underground supply lines, and it's a major reason we see repeat slab leaks in neighborhoods built out during the city's mid-century growth years. Add in a housing stock that ranges from 1920s and 1930s bungalows to 1950s and 60s tract homes, many still running original cast iron drain lines or galvanized steel supply pipe, and you get a city where plumbing failure is often the real root cause behind a water loss, not just winter storm runoff. When Los Angeles County does get hit with an atmospheric river system, Pomona's older storm drain infrastructure in some areas struggles to keep pace, and water can back up against foundations faster than it drains away.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process for Pomona Properties

The first visit is about mapping the loss, not just mopping it up. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to trace water behind baseboards, under tile, and into wall cavities before we commit to a drying plan, because a wet-looking spot on drywall can hide a much larger saturated area in the framing behind it. In older Pomona homes with plaster-and-lath walls, extraction happens fast, but the plaster itself releases trapped moisture slowly, so we run drying equipment longer and monitor daily with meters rather than guessing at a fixed timeline. Newer construction with drywall and engineered flooring dries faster but is less forgiving of shortcuts, so we set up air movers and dehumidifiers according to IICRC S500 drying standards and adjust placement as rooms read dry at different rates. As an IICRC-certified firm with WRT and ASD credentials on staff, we document moisture readings at each visit so the drying log holds up if your insurance carrier requests it.

## Insurance and Permit Considerations in Los Angeles County

Most water losses we see in Pomona fall under a homeowner's policy's sudden-and-accidental water damage coverage, but coverage details shift depending on whether the source was a supply line failure, a roof leak, or groundwater intrusion, so we photograph and document the source before any demolition starts. If a loss involves a condo or an HOA-governed property, which is increasingly common in Pomona's newer developments, we coordinate directly with property management on shared-wall access and any required notice periods before equipment goes in. For losses that require tear-out of drywall, flooring, or cabinetry beyond cosmetic repair, permitting runs through the City of Pomona's building department, and we flag that early so reconstruction doesn't stall waiting on paperwork.

## Local note

A lot of Pomona's housing stock predates copper repiping, which means when we find one slab leak under an older home, we check the rest of the supply line run before closing out the job, because galvanized and cast iron pipe of that era tends to fail in clusters rather than as a single isolated event. Catching a second weak spot during the same visit saves a homeowner a repeat call a few months later.
