## Why Norco Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

Norco sits in a basin where the Santa Ana River corridor and its surrounding clay-heavy soils create drainage challenges that compound during wet winters. Clay soil doesn't absorb water quickly, so when rain saturates the ground, hydrostatic pressure builds against older sewer laterals — many of which were installed decades ago and have never been lined or replaced. That pressure can force raw sewage backward through floor drains, toilets, and utility sink connections before a homeowner notices any warning sign.

The city's famously horse-friendly zoning also means a significant share of properties rely on private septic systems rather than municipal sewer connections. Septic tanks that haven't been pumped on schedule — or that are undersized for a household that's grown — can overflow into yards, crawl spaces, and outbuildings with little warning. Either scenario, municipal backup or septic overflow, leaves behind pathogens, hydrogen sulfide gas, and organic solids that require professional extraction and sanitization, not a mop and a bottle of bleach.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Norco

When we arrive, the first priority is containment. Raw sewage carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that spread on foot traffic, HVAC airflow, and even pet movement through the home. We establish containment barriers before any extraction begins.

From there, the process moves through several defined stages:

- **Extraction and solid removal** — standing sewage and solid waste are removed using truck-mounted extraction units and hand tools rated for Category 3 material.
- **Structural assessment** — porous materials like drywall, insulation, carpet, and subfloor that have direct sewage contact are almost always non-salvageable and must be removed. We document everything photographically for your insurance file.
- **EPA-registered disinfection** — affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade antimicrobial agents. On Norco properties with concrete slab foundations, we pay particular attention to cracks and control joints where contaminated water can wick laterally.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — after sanitization, structural drying begins per the IICRC S500 standard. In Norco's dry inland climate, ambient conditions can help — but concrete and tile assemblies retain moisture longer than they appear to on the surface, so we monitor with moisture meters, not assumptions.
- **Post-remediation verification** — before we close out, affected areas are re-tested to confirm contamination levels are within safe limits.
