## Why Fullerton's Building Stock Shapes Every Renovation

Fullerton's age diversity is one of the things that makes it genuinely interesting to work in — and one of the things that can surprise a homeowner who assumes a remodel is straightforward. Homes built before the 1950s frequently have original knob-and-tube wiring, cast-iron drain lines, and plaster-over-lath wall systems. A kitchen remodel in a pre-war bungalow isn't just a cabinet swap; it often surfaces electrical panels that need upgrading, subfloor rot that wasn't visible until the old tile came up, or galvanized supply lines that are decades past their useful life.

Southern California's climate adds another layer. The region's dry summers and periodic heavy rain events — especially during El Niño years — put pressure on older flat rooflines and aging stucco exteriors. Moisture that gets behind stucco in a Fullerton home can sit undetected for months, quietly compromising framing before anyone notices a soft spot in the wall. Renovation work here often means uncovering deferred maintenance that the previous owner didn't know existed.
