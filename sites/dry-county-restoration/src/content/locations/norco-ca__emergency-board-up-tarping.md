## Why Norco Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Norco sits in the Inland Empire's wind corridor, where Santa Ana conditions can push gusts well past 50 mph through the Cajon and San Gorgonio passes before funneling south through Riverside County. That wind doesn't just spread fire — it tears at roofing material, lifts ridge caps, and drives rain horizontally into any opening left unprotected after a structural event. A tarp that would hold fine in a calmer climate can fail here if it isn't weighted, strapped, and anchored with the right ballast and tie-down spacing.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Much of Norco developed in the 1960s through the 1980s as an equestrian community, and many of those original homes feature wood-shake roofs, older single-pane windows, and barn or workshop structures on the same parcel as the main house. Wood shake is particularly vulnerable after fire or impact damage — the substrate beneath can be compromised even when the surface looks intact — and boarding or tarping those structures requires assessing the underlying decking before any crew member walks the roof.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Norco

When you call (951) 667-9910, the first question is what type of opening needs to be secured: windows, doors, a garage bay, or a roof section. That determines what we load before we leave Corona.

**Roof tarping** starts with a visual assessment of the decking and rafters from below before anyone climbs. We use reinforced polyethylene tarps — minimum 6-mil, typically heavier for larger spans — secured with 2×4 batten boards screwed into solid decking, not just the tarp grommets. On older homes with skip sheathing under wood shake, we locate solid nailing points first. The tarp is lapped over the ridge when possible so wind-driven rain can't work underneath.

**Board-up** for windows and doors uses 5/8" OSB cut to fit the rough opening, secured with structural screws into the framing — not just the trim. For sliding glass doors or large picture windows common in Norco ranch-style homes, we may use multiple panels with a center mullion to keep deflection within safe limits. Every opening gets photographed before and after installation for your insurance file.

For fire-damaged properties, we coordinate with the fire department's incident commander or the property owner to confirm the structure has been cleared for entry before crews go inside.
