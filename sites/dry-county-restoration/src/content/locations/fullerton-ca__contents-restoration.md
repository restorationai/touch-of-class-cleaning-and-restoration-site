A slow roof leak during a wet winter can saturate the insulation above a bedroom closet for weeks before anyone notices, leaving smoke-stained artwork, water-warped furniture, and mildew-laced upholstery that look like a total loss but often aren't. ## Why Fullerton Properties See Contents Damage the Way They Do

Fullerton's housing stock is unusually layered. The city has neighborhoods of craftsman bungalows and Spanish Colonial Revival homes built in the 1920s through 1940s sitting alongside mid-century ranch houses and newer infill construction. Older homes in the areas around Commonwealth Avenue and the historic downtown core frequently have original hardwood floors, built-in cabinetry, and plaster walls — materials that absorb moisture and smoke odor in ways that modern drywall simply doesn't. When a kitchen fire or a burst supply line hits one of these homes, the contents inside — upholstered furniture, rugs, books, clothing, electronics, family heirlooms — are exposed to conditions that require more than a wipe-down.

Fullerton's position in the Santa Ana winds corridor adds another layer. Wind-driven wildfires to the north and east regularly push ash and fine particulate into homes through gaps around windows and attic vents, depositing a thin, oily soot film on surfaces that can penetrate fabric and corrode metal if left untreated for more than a few days.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, logged by category, and assigned a preliminary salvageability rating. That documentation matters enormously for insurance claims and for your own peace of mind — you know exactly what left your home and what its condition was when it did.

From there, salvageable items are carefully packed and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility. Belongings restoration isn't one process — it's several running in parallel:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, collectibles, and non-porous items where immersion-level agitation removes soot and residue without abrasion
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods and furniture with embedded smoke odor
- **Dry cleaning and wet cleaning** for textiles, based on fiber content and soil type
- **Freeze-drying referral** for documents and photographs that have been wet — a service we coordinate with certified document recovery specialists

Items are returned only when the structure is ready and dry, so nothing goes back into a space that could re-damage it.

## Fullerton Insurance Coordination

Contents pack-out and belongings restoration are typically covered under the personal property portion of a standard homeowner's policy, but the documentation requirements vary by carrier. We generate a line-item inventory with pre-restoration condition photos that adjusters can use directly, which tends to reduce back-and-forth on disputed items. If your carrier requires an independent contents appraisal before approving restoration costs, we can pause specific items and hold them in storage while that review happens — nothing gets cleaned or altered before authorization on items flagged for appraisal.
