## Why Jurupa Valley Properties See Storm Damage Issues

Jurupa Valley sits along the Santa Ana River corridor in a part of Riverside County that swings between long dry stretches and sudden, intense storm events. A lot of the housing stock here is a mix: older ranch-style homes on larger agricultural or equestrian-zoned parcels, newer tract developments, and everything in between. The older properties often have outbuildings, barns, and detached garages that were built for a drier climate and aren't always sealed or flashed to handle wind-driven rain. When a storm rolls through with strong gusts, those structures take damage first, roof fascia pulls loose, fencing goes down, and water finds its way under doors that have shifted slightly over decades of settling. Flat or gently sloped lots in low-lying sections of the city can also pool water faster than it drains, especially where soil has been compacted by equestrian or agricultural use.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Jurupa Valley
