## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Montclair

We start with moisture mapping, not guesswork. Pinless and pin-type meters tell us how far water has traveled into drywall, baseboards, and subfloor, which matters in a slab home where the leak source and the visible damage are often ten feet apart. Extraction comes next, pulling standing water and saturated carpet pad before it's airborne as vapor. From there we set up structural drying per the IICRC S500 standard, using air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the room and the material, not a flat equipment count. Homes with original lath and plaster in the older pockets of Montclair dry slower than drywall because the material holds moisture at a different rate, so we adjust drying time and check readings daily instead of assuming a standard three-day dry-out will hit every wall. Once materials test dry, we document it before any rebuild starts.
