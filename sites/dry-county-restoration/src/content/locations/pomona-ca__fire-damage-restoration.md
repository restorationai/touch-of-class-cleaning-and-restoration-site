## Why Pomona Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Pomona sits in a part of Los Angeles County where Santa Ana wind events push fire risk up every fall, and even a contained kitchen or electrical fire can spread smoke faster through a home when those winds are blowing. A lot of Pomona's residential stock dates back to the 1920s through the 1950s, with plaster walls, older electrical panels, and original wood framing that soot and heat penetrate differently than modern drywall and engineered lumber. Older wiring in these homes is also a recurring fire cause we see across the region, which means an electrical fire often means both fire damage and a code-driven panel upgrade before reconstruction can start. Add in the valley's dry heat for much of the year, and smoke odor tends to bake into porous materials, plaster, and old insulation rather than lifting off the way it might in a damper climate.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Pomona

We start with a damage assessment that separates what's salvageable from what isn't, because older plaster and lath holds onto smoke differently than drywall and often needs more aggressive sealing rather than straight replacement. Board-up and tarping come first if the structure is exposed, followed by water extraction if fire suppression left standing water, which is common with older single-pane windows and roof penetrations. From there we run HEPA air scrubbers and thermal fogging to address soot particulate and odor before any rebuild work begins, and we document every step with photos for the insurance claim. Reconstruction, when needed, follows current code, which in a lot of Pomona's older neighborhoods means updating electrical and sometimes plumbing connections that wouldn't have been touched otherwise.
