## Why Northborough Homes See Upholstery Wear

Northborough sits in Worcester County, where winters mean months of salt and sand treatment on the roads, and that grit rides in on shoes and paws long before anyone notices it in the carpet, let alone the sofa arm where a dog rests its head. Many of the town's colonial and farmhouse-style homes still use wood-burning fireplaces or stoves through the cold months, and fine soot particles settle onto nearby upholstery even when nothing ever looks visibly dirty. Add in New England's humid summers, when finished basements and family rooms trap moisture, and fabric furniture can start to hold a musty edge that air fresheners only mask. None of this requires a disaster to cause damage, it's simply the accumulated effect of the climate and the way these homes are used season to season.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, since a poly-blend sectional, a wool-blend wingback, and a top-grain leather recliner all call for different chemistry and pressure. Fabric pieces typically get a pre-treatment for oils and ground-in soil, followed by hot water extraction that lifts residue without oversaturating the cushion fill, a real concern in humid months when drying time already runs longer. Leather gets cleaned with a pH-balanced solution and then conditioned so the hide doesn't dry out or crack, which matters in a region where indoor heat runs dry for half the year. We also test colorfastness on an inconspicuous spot before any full cleaning, since older or antique pieces common in longtime Northborough homes don't always respond the way modern fabric does.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

We use truck-mounted hot water extraction for most fabric upholstery, which pulls more moisture out during the cleaning than portable units can manage, shortening dry time in a climate where summer humidity already slows things down. Upholstery-specific attachments keep pressure low enough for delicate weaves while still reaching into seams and tufting where soil and pet dander collect. For leather, we skip water extraction entirely in favor of specialized cleaners and conditioners designed to restore suppleness without stripping the finish.
