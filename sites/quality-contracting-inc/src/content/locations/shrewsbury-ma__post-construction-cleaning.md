## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Shrewsbury

Every post-construction clean follows a sequenced approach, because cleaning out of order means re-doing work. ## Local Note: Dust Migration in Shrewsbury's Older Cape-Style Homes

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Shrewsbury's Edgemere neighborhood and similar pockets of postwar capes: the original forced hot air systems in these homes — many still on their first or second furnace — have ductwork that was never designed to be airtight. During a renovation, fine construction dust gets drawn into the return air path and redistributes through the entire house, including rooms sealed off from the work zone. A thorough post-construction clean in these homes includes inspecting and wiping down supply and return registers, and sometimes recommending a duct cleaning before the HVAC system runs again.
