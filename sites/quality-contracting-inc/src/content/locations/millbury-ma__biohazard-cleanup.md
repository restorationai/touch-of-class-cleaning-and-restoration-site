## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Millbury

Every job begins with a walk-through assessment — no assumptions, no shortcuts. Technicians identify the full extent of the affected area, including any secondary surfaces that may have been contacted. In older Millbury homes, that often means checking beneath original flooring and behind plaster walls, not just the visible surfaces.

Containment is established before any work begins. This protects unaffected areas of the home and limits the movement of potentially infectious material. All biological waste is collected, packaged, and transported in accordance with Massachusetts Department of Public Health regulations and EPA guidelines for infectious waste — the same standards that govern medical and clinical settings.

Decontamination follows, using hospital-grade disinfectants appropriate to the specific pathogens of concern. Sharps and needle cleanup follows strict OSHA protocols for puncture-resistant containment. Once the area is cleared, we conduct verification testing to confirm the space meets established safety thresholds before we close out the job. Documentation is provided for insurance purposes and, when relevant, for property managers or landlords.
