## Why Leicester Properties See More Storm Damage Than Nearby Towns

Elevation is only part of the story. The mill-era housing stock in Cherry Valley and Rochdale — many homes dating to the late 1800s and early 1900s — was built with construction methods that predate modern wind-load standards. Older balloon-frame walls, original single-pane windows, and aging asphalt shingles that have already survived decades of New England winters are far more vulnerable to a 60-mph gust than a newer build. When a large oak or maple lets go and lands on one of these homes, the structural damage tends to run deeper than it would on a contemporary ranch.

Properties ringing Lake Sargent and Stiles Reservoir face an additional layer of complexity. Many are on private wells and septic systems, which means a storm that compromises the ground around the foundation — through soil erosion, flooding, or a fallen tree disturbing the yard — can affect more than just the structure. Saturated soil near a septic field or a cracked well casing is a secondary problem that shows up days after the initial cleanup, and it's one we watch for on every lakeside job.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Leicester

The first priority after any storm is stopping the damage from spreading. That means emergency tarping and board-up to close the breach — whether it's a roof section lifted by wind, a wall opened by a falling limb, or windows blown in along the Route 9 corridor. Once the structure is stabilized, we conduct a systematic damage assessment that distinguishes cosmetic loss from structural compromise and documents everything in a format your insurance adjuster can work with directly.

From there, the scope drives the sequence. Tree and debris removal clears the way for structural repairs. Water that entered through the breach gets extracted and the affected materials — insulation, sheathing, interior finishes — are evaluated for salvage versus replacement. Drying equipment goes in immediately on any wet material, because in Leicester's cooler temperatures mold can begin colonizing damp wood within 48 to 72 hours, especially in the less-ventilated spaces common in older homes. Structural repairs follow: framing, roofing, siding, windows. We coordinate with local inspectors and pull the required permits so the work is code-compliant and documented for resale or insurance purposes.

## Leicester Insurance Coordination

Storm damage claims in Massachusetts are generally covered under the dwelling portion of a standard homeowners policy, but the documentation requirements matter. Adjusters want to see the cause of loss clearly established — wind versus pre-existing wear, for example — and they want a detailed scope before authorizing repairs. We photograph and document the damage thoroughly before any debris is moved, produce a line-item estimate in a format most major carriers recognize, and can communicate directly with your adjuster to answer technical questions about scope and method. For Leicester homeowners with older homes, it's worth noting that some carriers will flag deferred maintenance on roofs or siding as a partial exclusion; we'll flag anything we see that could complicate your claim so you're not surprised later.
