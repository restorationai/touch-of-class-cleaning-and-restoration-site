Massachusetts also enforces some of the stricter asbestos notification and disposal regulations in New England. ## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Grafton

Every project starts with a visual inspection and, where needed, bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples go to an accredited laboratory; we don't guess. If asbestos is confirmed, we develop a project-specific abatement plan that accounts for the building's age, the type of material involved, and the scope of the planned renovation or repair.

Containment comes next. We seal off the work area with poly sheeting and negative air pressure units so disturbed fibers can't migrate into living spaces or HVAC systems. Wet methods are used throughout removal to suppress airborne fiber counts. All removed material is double-bagged in labeled, leak-tight containers and transported by a licensed waste hauler — not left in a dumpster on the curb.

After removal, the area is HEPA-vacuumed and wiped down. Air clearance testing confirms fiber counts are within acceptable limits before containment is removed and the space is returned to use. That final clearance step matters — it's the documentation your contractor, your insurer, and a future buyer's inspector will want to see.
