## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process

The work starts with securing the affected area and doing a full assessment before anything is touched, since bodily fluids and other infectious material can travel further into subflooring, grout lines, and wall cavities than they appear to on the surface. Our technicians work in appropriate PPE and remove porous materials that cannot be safely disinfected, such as carpet, padding, and in some cases drywall or subfloor, while non-porous surfaces are cleaned and disinfected using EPA-registered products applied per the IICRC S540 standard for biohazard remediation. Waste is bagged, labeled, and transported for disposal according to state and federal regulations for biomedical waste, not left for a homeowner to deal with through a regular trash pickup. Once cleaning is complete, we do a final walkthrough so the property owner knows exactly what was done and what, if anything, still needs repair or replacement before the space is usable again.
