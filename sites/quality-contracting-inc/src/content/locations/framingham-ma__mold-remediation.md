Middlesex County sits in a climate band where freeze-thaw cycling is hard on foundations. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Framingham

Effective mold remediation is a contained, sequenced process — not a spray-and-wipe job. The work begins with a thorough inspection to identify moisture sources and map the extent of affected materials. Visible mold is only part of the picture; moisture readings taken at the wall cavity, subfloor, and framing tell the fuller story.

Once the scope is clear, containment goes up — plastic sheeting and negative air pressure isolate the work area so spores aren't redistributed through the rest of the home during removal. Affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing) are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned and treated. HEPA air scrubbers run throughout the process and during a final clearance period. The moisture source that allowed mold to establish — whether a slow foundation seep, a failed bathroom exhaust, or a plumbing leak — is addressed before any reconstruction begins. Skipping that step means the mold returns.
