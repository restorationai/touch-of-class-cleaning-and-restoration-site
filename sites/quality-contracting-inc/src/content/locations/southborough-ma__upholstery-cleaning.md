## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, because a microfiber sectional, a cotton-blend armchair, and an aniline leather sofa each need a different approach. Water-safe fabrics get a pre-treatment to break down oils and protein-based soiling like pet accidents, followed by low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded dirt without oversaturating the cushion foam underneath, which matters in humid months when foam that stays wet too long can start to smell musty within days. Leather gets cleaned with a pH-balanced solution and conditioned afterward so it doesn't dry out further, which is especially relevant for pieces positioned near fireplaces or radiators common in older Southborough homes. We finish with a grooming pass to reset the fabric nap so cushions and arms look even, not flattened from the cleaning process.
