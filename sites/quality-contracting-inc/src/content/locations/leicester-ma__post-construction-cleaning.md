## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Leicester

Every job starts with a walk-through to assess what trades have been through the space and in what order. Electricians, plumbers, tile setters, painters, and finish carpenters each leave a different residue profile. We work top-to-bottom and rough-to-fine across three passes.

The first pass handles construction debris removal — off-cuts, packaging, fasteners, and anything the crew left behind. The second pass is a detail clean: HEPA-filtered vacuuming of every surface including ceiling fixtures, window tracks, cabinet interiors, and HVAC vents, followed by damp-wiping with appropriate solutions matched to the surface (nothing alkaline on natural stone, nothing solvent-based near fresh paint). The third pass is the final inspection clean — glass polishing, grout haze removal from tile, and a floor finish that's calibrated to the flooring type. On older Leicester properties with original hardwood, we use pH-neutral cleaners and avoid steam, which can raise grain or push moisture into subfloor gaps.

Specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor cleaning, biohazard-adjacent construction debris, or deep cleaning of a space that sat unoccupied through a Leicester winter — get scoped separately based on what we find in the walk-through.
