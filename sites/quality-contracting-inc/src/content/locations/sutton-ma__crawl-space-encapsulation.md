## Our Encapsulation Process, Calibrated to Sutton Conditions

Encapsulation in a Sutton crawl space isn't a one-size job. The first step is an honest assessment: we measure moisture levels in the soil and wood framing, look for evidence of prior water intrusion, check whether any existing vents are contributing to condensation cycles, and identify whether the sump system (if there is one) is properly sized and draining to daylight rather than back toward the foundation.

From there, the core work involves sealing the crawl space from the ground up. A heavy-duty polyethylene vapor barrier — typically 20-mil reinforced liner — is laid across the entire soil floor and lapped up the foundation walls, sealed at seams and at every penetration (pipes, posts, access hatches). Vents are closed and sealed. Insulation is moved from the floor joists to the foundation walls, which is the correct placement in a conditioned or semi-conditioned crawl space. If humidity control is needed year-round, a crawl-space-rated dehumidifier with a drain line is installed so it never requires manual emptying.

On homes with active water intrusion — common on the uphill side of properties near Route 146 where runoff concentrates — we coordinate interior drainage channel installation before the liner goes down, so water has a managed path to the sump rather than pooling under the membrane.

## Local Note: What the Ledge Tells Us Before We Start

One thing that shapes crawl space work specifically in Sutton is how often we encounter foundations poured directly against granite ledge. When a contractor blasted or hand-chipped a crawl space out of ledge decades ago, the resulting floor is uneven, sometimes sloped, and occasionally has natural fissures that channel water in from the hillside. Standard vapor barrier installation assumes a relatively flat soil floor — on ledge, we use additional mastic sealant and mechanical fasteners at the base of the wall to prevent the liner from separating where the ledge face is irregular. Homeowners in South Sutton and West Sutton especially tend to have this condition, and it's worth asking any contractor you speak with whether they've worked on ledge-floor crawl spaces before, because the prep work is meaningfully different.
