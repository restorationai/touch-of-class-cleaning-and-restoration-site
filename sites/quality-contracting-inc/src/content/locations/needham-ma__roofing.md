## Why Needham Roofs Wear Out the Way They Do

Needham's climate does not give roofs an easy life. Temperatures routinely swing from the single digits in January to the upper 90s in July, and that thermal cycling stresses shingles, ridge caps, and the underlayment beneath them year after year. Ice dams are a particular concern on homes with attic insulation that predates modern energy codes: heat escaping through the roof deck melts snow at the ridge, that meltwater runs down and refreezes at the cold eaves, and the resulting ice dam forces water back under the shingles.

The town's mature tree canopy is one of its defining features, but it also means roofs collect significant debris. Moss and lichen establish quickly in shaded sections, and the organic mat they form holds moisture against the shingles long after a rain event ends. Left alone, that moisture accelerates shingle deterioration and can eventually compromise the decking underneath. A full replacement is often the more economical path once decking damage is involved, because patching around soft or delaminated plywood rarely holds through another New England winter.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Needham

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system, not just the shingles. The crew checks decking condition, evaluates the attic ventilation setup, and examines all penetrations, valleys, and step flashings where water most commonly finds a path inside. That inspection drives the scope of work before any materials are ordered.

During tear-off, damaged decking sections are replaced rather than covered over. New underlayment goes down across the full deck, followed by ice-and-water shield at the eaves and in valleys, which is especially important given how reliably Needham winters produce ice dam conditions. Shingles are installed to manufacturer specifications for fastener pattern and exposure, and all flashings around chimneys, skylights, and dormers are replaced rather than reused. Ridge ventilation is assessed and upgraded if the existing system is undersized for the attic volume.

Cleanup is part of the job. Magnetic rollers cover the yard and driveway for nails, and all debris is hauled away the same day.
