## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Shrewsbury

The first step on any job is a detailed pack-out inventory. Every item leaving the property is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code before it's wrapped and loaded. This documentation matters enormously for insurance claims — adjusters want to see pre-cleaning condition, not just a final bill.

From the property, contents move to a controlled facility where temperature and humidity are maintained. Smoke-damaged textiles go through ozone treatment or thermal fogging depending on the odor profile. Hard surfaces — wood furniture, cabinetry components, collectibles — are cleaned using ultrasonic equipment or hand methods matched to the material. Electronics are assessed by a certified electronics technician before any attempt at restoration. Items that cannot be restored are documented in the same system so your adjuster has a clear total-loss record.

Once the structure is dried, repaired, and cleared, belongings are returned in the same organized sequence they were packed — rooms reassembled, not just boxes dropped at the door.

## Shrewsbury Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in the 01545 ZIP code area cover contents losses under the personal property portion of the policy, but the documentation requirements vary by carrier. Some adjusters want a room-by-room inventory with replacement value estimates; others want restoration cost estimates first.
