## Why Bellingham Homes Need a Different Renovation Approach

Bellingham sits in the Blackstone Valley, a part of Massachusetts that industrialized early and kept building steadily through the postwar decades. That means a renovation crew here regularly runs into a mix of eras under one roof: original plaster-and-lath sections next to additions from the 1970s or 1990s, knob-and-tube remnants behind newer wiring, and plumbing that's been patched more than once. A kitchen remodel that looks straightforward on paper can turn up a wall that isn't load-bearing on the plans but is in practice, or a subfloor that needs leveling before new cabinets go in. New England's freeze-thaw cycle also puts real stress on foundations and exterior work, so timing for additions, siding, or anything touching the building envelope has to respect the frost line and the town's inspection schedule.
