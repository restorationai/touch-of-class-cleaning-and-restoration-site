## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Sutton

The work moves in a deliberate sequence, because cleaning out of order means re-cleaning. We start at the top of each space — ceiling fans, light fixtures, exposed beams, window sills — and work downward so that anything dislodged lands on surfaces not yet cleaned. HEPA vacuuming comes before any wet wiping, and wet wiping comes before floor finishing.

For specialty cleaning scopes — post-fire odor removal from a kitchen renovation that scorched cabinetry, deep cleaning after a bathroom gut-and-replace, or removing adhesive residue and grout haze from new tile work — the process gets tailored to the material. Grout haze on ceramic or porcelain requires a pH-specific cleaner applied at the right dwell time; rushing it etches the tile surface. Paint overspray on hardwood needs a different approach than overspray on vinyl. We assess the surfaces before we open a single product.

When HVAC systems have been running during construction — which is common in occupied partial-renovation projects — we recommend a duct inspection and filter replacement as part of the final clean scope. Construction dust that's been pulled into ductwork will redistribute the moment the system runs at full capacity after the crew leaves.
