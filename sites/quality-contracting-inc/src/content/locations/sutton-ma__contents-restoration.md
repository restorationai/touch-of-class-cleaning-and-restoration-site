## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Sutton

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing gets lost or misattributed during the restoration cycle.

From there, contents are carefully packed out and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Items are sorted by damage type: smoke-affected textiles go to ultrasonic or immersion cleaning; hard surfaces get HEPA-vacuumed and wiped with appropriate solvents; electronics are assessed for corrosion and function before any cleaning begins. Documents and photographs require a separate freeze-drying or air-drying protocol if they've been exposed to water.

Storage continues in a monitored, climate-controlled environment until your home in the 01590 ZIP code area is ready to receive contents back. Final delivery is coordinated with your general contractor or project manager so items return only after flooring, painting, and any remaining construction dust are resolved — not before.

## Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims in Sutton

Contents losses are frequently the most contested part of a homeowner's insurance claim. Adjusters often apply depreciation schedules to furniture, clothing, and electronics that don't reflect actual replacement cost, and without a professional inventory, items get missed entirely. The pack-out documentation we create — photographs, condition ratings, item-by-item lists — gives your adjuster a complete picture and gives you a record to reference if any line items are disputed.

Sutton homeowners on private well and septic systems should also be aware that sewage backup events (a failed septic system overflowing into a basement, for example) are often treated differently under standard homeowners policies than clean-water losses.
