## Why Southbridge Town Furniture Wears Differently

Southbridge grew up as a mill town, and a lot of its housing stock, including converted triple-deckers and smaller single-family homes near the old factory districts, was built before central air conditioning was standard. That means radiator heat in winter and open windows in summer, both of which affect upholstery. Dry radiator heat pulls oils out of leather and can make fabric fibers brittle over a season, while humid summer air near the Quinebaug River valley gives odor-causing bacteria more to work with in fabric that's already holding onto pet dander, cooking residue, or just years of daily use. We see it most in living rooms that sit close to exterior walls, where temperature and humidity swings are the sharpest.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start with a fabric identification step before any cleaning solution touches the piece, because a cotton blend, a synthetic microfiber, and a leather sofa all need different treatment. For fabric upholstery, that usually means a low-moisture pretest on an inconspicuous area, followed by hot water extraction or a dry compound method depending on how the fabric reacts. Leather upholstery gets a different track entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner, manual conditioning, and no steam, since heat and moisture can crack or discolor older leather. Furniture steam cleaning works well for heavier fabric pieces that have absorbed years of household odor, but we control the moisture carefully so cushions don't stay damp longer than they should.

## Fabric and Leather Considerations for Older Homes

A lot of the couches and chairs we clean in Southbridge Town have been in the same room for a decade or more, sometimes longer than the current owner has lived there. That means we're often dealing with fading from sun exposure through older single-pane windows, or fabric that's absorbed years of wood stove smoke in homes that still use supplemental heat. Sofa cleaning and couch cleaning on pieces like this takes a slower, more deliberate approach than a routine maintenance clean, since aggressive extraction can pull dye or finish out of fabric that's already stressed by age and light exposure.

## Local note

Southbridge Town's position along the Quinebaug River valley means humidity lingers a little longer after a cleaning than it would in a drier inland town, so we build extra air movement into the drying step on fabric upholstery, especially in basement family rooms or first-floor living rooms with limited cross-ventilation. Skipping that step is how a freshly cleaned sofa ends up smelling musty again within a week.
