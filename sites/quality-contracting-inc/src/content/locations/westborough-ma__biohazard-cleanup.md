## Why Westborough Properties Present Specific Challenges

Westborough's housing stock spans a wide range of construction eras. Porous building materials — plaster, older hardwood subfloors, unfinished concrete in basements — absorb biological material differently than modern drywall or sealed concrete, and that affects both the scope of cleaning and the disposal requirements.

The town's climate adds another layer.
