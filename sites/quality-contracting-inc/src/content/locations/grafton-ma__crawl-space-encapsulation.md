## Why Grafton Properties See Crawl Space Moisture Problems

After a wet spring or a heavy autumn rain, groundwater doesn't just run off — it wicks upward through gravel and clay subsoil and condenses on the underside of floor joists and subfloor sheathing.

Homes in the 01519 ZIP code, especially those predating 1950, frequently have vented crawl spaces that were standard practice at the time. ## Our Crawl Space Encapsulation Process in Grafton

Encapsulation is not simply rolling out a sheet of plastic. The process starts with a thorough inspection of the crawl space — foundation walls, rim joists, existing insulation, any evidence of standing water or prior flooding, and the condition of structural wood members. If there is active moisture intrusion, that source has to be addressed before any barrier goes down.

Once the space is prepared, a heavy-duty reinforced vapor barrier — typically 12- to 20-mil polyethylene — is sealed to the foundation walls and mechanically fastened, not just draped. Seams are overlapped and taped so the system functions as a continuous envelope rather than a patchwork. Rim joists are insulated and air-sealed, which matters especially in Grafton's older homes where gaps between the foundation and the sill plate are common.

Depending on the crawl space's ventilation configuration and the home's HVAC setup, a dehumidifier or a controlled mechanical ventilation strategy may be integrated into the finished system. The goal is a crawl space that stays below 60 percent relative humidity year-round, protecting floor framing, insulation, and indoor air quality above.
