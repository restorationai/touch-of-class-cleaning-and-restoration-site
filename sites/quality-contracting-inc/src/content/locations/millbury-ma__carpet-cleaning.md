## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Millbury

Every job starts with a fiber and stain assessment before any solution touches the carpet. Wool and wool-blend rugs — more common in the older homes along the river — require a lower-pH rinse than the nylon and polyester found in most post-2000 construction. Getting that wrong causes dye bleeding or fiber distortion that no amount of re-cleaning fixes.

From there, the process runs in four stages:

1. **Dry soil removal** — commercial-grade HEPA vacuuming pulls out the abrasive particulate that cuts carpet fibers when left in place.
2. **Pre-treatment** — targeted spotting agents are applied to stains (pet urine, grease, tannin-based stains from river-adjacent mud) and allowed to dwell before agitation.
3. **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted equipment delivers heated water at controlled pressure and immediately recovers it, pulling suspended soil and cleaning solution out of the pile. Truck-mount systems generate consistent heat regardless of outdoor temperature, which matters during a Massachusetts February job.
4. **pH-balanced rinse and speed-dry** — a final rinse neutralizes any residual alkalinity, and high-velocity air movers reduce dry time so the carpet is walkable within a few hours rather than overnight.
