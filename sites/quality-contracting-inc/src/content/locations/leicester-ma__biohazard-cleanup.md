## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Leicester

Every job begins with a thorough assessment before any material is disturbed. We identify the full scope of contamination — including areas that aren't immediately visible — and establish containment barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the structure. Personal protective equipment and regulated waste protocols are in place from the moment our team enters the space.

For blood cleanup, bodily fluid cleanup, and infectious material cleanup, we use EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants appropriate to the pathogen risk. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated — sections of flooring, drywall, insulation — are removed, packaged as regulated biohazardous waste, and transported to a licensed disposal facility. We do not leave that step to the homeowner.

Once remediation is complete, the affected area is tested and cleared before we close out the job. Documentation of the process is provided, which matters both for your own records and for any insurance claim you may be filing.
