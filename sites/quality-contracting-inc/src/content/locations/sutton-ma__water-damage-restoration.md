## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sutton

Every job begins with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. In older Sutton homes, we check not just the obvious wet surfaces but the subfloor cavity, the rim joist, and any plaster walls adjacent to the loss area — because water travels farther in these structures than it does in modern construction.

Once we understand the full extent of the damage, we move to water extraction. High-capacity truck-mounted extractors pull standing water quickly, and portable units reach corners and finished spaces that hoses can't. After extraction, we set a calculated array of commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and axial air movers sized to the actual cubic footage and material types in your home — not a one-size formula.

We monitor drying progress with daily moisture readings and adjust equipment placement until materials reach their target dryness values. Every reading is logged and time-stamped, which matters when your insurance adjuster asks for documentation. We also coordinate directly with most major carriers, handling the photo documentation and scope writing so you're not navigating that process alone while your home is still drying out.
