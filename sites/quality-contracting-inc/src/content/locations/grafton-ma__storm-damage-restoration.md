## Why Grafton Properties See Distinct Storm Damage Patterns

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Grafton

The first priority after any storm event is stopping ongoing damage. That means emergency tarping or board-up before any interior assessment begins — a step that matters especially for the older homes near Grafton Common where a compromised roof can soak plaster walls and original hardwood floors within hours.

Once the structure is stabilized, the process moves to a systematic damage assessment: roof, exterior envelope, attic, and then interior moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Structural repairs, window replacement, and interior drying happen in sequence, not simultaneously, so that each phase is inspected and recorded before the next begins.
