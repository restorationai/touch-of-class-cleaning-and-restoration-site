## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Millbury

A final clean isn't a single pass with a mop. The sequence matters, and we work through a defined progression so nothing gets re-contaminated after it's been cleaned.

We start with rough-out debris removal — bagging and hauling construction waste that trade contractors left behind, clearing window tracks, and vacuuming framing cavities before drywall or trim goes back on (or, on a completed project, before finish cleaning begins). From there, we move to detail dusting: every surface from ceiling to baseboard, in order, using HEPA-filtered vacuums and microfiber that captures rather than redistributes. Window interiors, light fixtures, cabinet interiors, and HVAC registers all get addressed before floors are touched.

Floor care is calibrated to the material. New hardwood gets a dry buff and tack-cloth pass before any wet cleaning. Tile gets grout haze removed with the appropriate pH-balanced solution — not a general-purpose cleaner that can etch the surface. Concrete gets a damp mop followed by a dry pass to pull up the fine dust that wet mopping alone leaves behind.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated space, deep cleaning after a hoarding situation before a remodel, or sanitizing a property before new tenants move in — we scope those individually and confirm materials and methods before starting.
