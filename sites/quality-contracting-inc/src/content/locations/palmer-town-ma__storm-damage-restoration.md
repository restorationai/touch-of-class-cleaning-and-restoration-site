## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Palmer Town

The first visit is about stopping further loss, not full repair. That means emergency tarping over compromised roof sections, board-up on broken windows or doors, and extraction of any standing water before it sits long enough to wick into subfloor or framing. From there we document everything with photos and moisture readings, because that record matters as much for your insurance claim as it does for our drying plan. Structural drying follows standard industry drying principles, with air movers and dehumidification sized to the actual square footage and material types affected rather than a one-size-fits-all setup. Once materials read dry, we scope the rebuild, whether that's a patched roof section, replaced drywall, or a full room reconstruction after a tree limb comes through a ceiling.
