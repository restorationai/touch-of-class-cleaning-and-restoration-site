## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Shrewsbury

The first priority on any sewage call is stopping ongoing exposure. That means identifying whether the source is a municipal lateral backup, a failed septic component, or a broken interior line — because the remediation path differs for each. Once the source is controlled, standing water and solid waste are removed using truck-mounted extraction equipment rated for category 3 (black water) contamination.

After bulk removal, every affected surface — concrete slab, subfloor sheathing, framing, drywall — is assessed for absorption. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage-contaminated water typically cannot be dried in place; they are removed and disposed of following Massachusetts DEP guidelines for contaminated building materials. What remains is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants applied by spray and fogging to reach seams, floor joist bays, and wall cavities that extraction equipment cannot access. Drying equipment — desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — runs until moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines, which we document with thermal imaging and pin-type meters before closing out the job.
