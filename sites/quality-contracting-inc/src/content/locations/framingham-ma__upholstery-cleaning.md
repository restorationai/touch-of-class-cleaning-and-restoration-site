## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, because a microfiber sectional, a cotton-blend loveseat, and a leather recliner all call for different chemistry and pressure. For most fabric pieces we use hot water extraction, sometimes called furniture steam cleaning, which forces heated water and a cleaning solution deep into the fibers and then pulls it back out along with dirt, oils, and allergens. Leather upholstery gets a different approach entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner formulated to lift grime without stripping the finish, followed by a conditioner that helps prevent the cracking that dry winter heat tends to cause in this region. Before we treat anything, we test a hidden spot for colorfastness, especially on older or vintage pieces where dye stability can be unpredictable. Drying time depends on fabric density and how much humidity is in the room that day, which in Framingham's climate can vary noticeably between a July afternoon and a January one.
