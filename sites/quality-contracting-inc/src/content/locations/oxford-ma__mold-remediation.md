## Our Mold Remediation Process in Oxford

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the visible surface where mold is growing, but the moisture source driving it. Without addressing the source, remediation is temporary. We use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map wet zones inside walls and under subfloors before any demolition begins.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal — a step that matters especially in the older two-family homes around Oxford Center, where HVAC systems often share air between units. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing with active growth — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Structural surfaces are then treated and dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

We don't consider a job finished until clearance readings confirm the affected area is back within normal parameters. That documentation also matters if you're filing an insurance claim or planning to sell the property.
