## Why Marlborough Homes Face Accelerated Roofing Wear

Marlborough's climate sits in a band that gets genuine four-season stress: freeze-thaw cycles that can run thirty or more times in a single winter, humid summers that accelerate granule loss on asphalt shingles, and fall leaf accumulation that holds moisture against flashings and valleys. The city's older housing stock compounds the problem. Many homes built before 1970 were constructed with board sheathing rather than plywood or OSB, and that original decking can hide significant rot beneath a surface that still looks intact from the ground. A proper replacement here means pulling back layers to inspect what is underneath, not just nailing new shingles over old problems.

The local soil profile also matters more than homeowners expect. Marlborough's terrain includes areas with clay-heavy subsoil that drains slowly after heavy rain. When gutters fail or downspouts discharge too close to the foundation, that standing water works its way upward, and a compromised roof edge becomes the second point of entry. Replacing the roof without addressing the drainage relationship is a short-term fix.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Marlborough

Every project begins with a full deck inspection, not just a shingle count. The crew removes existing material down to the sheathing, documents any soft spots or delaminated sections, and replaces compromised decking before a single underlayment strip goes down. In Marlborough's climate, ice-and-water shield is installed at eaves and valleys as standard practice, not an upgrade, because the freeze-thaw cycle at this latitude creates ice dam conditions that standard felt cannot handle.

Flashing details around chimneys, skylights, and dormers receive particular attention on older Marlborough homes, where original step flashing is often corroded or was never properly integrated with the roofing system to begin with. New flashing is set in roofing cement and mechanically fastened before the field shingles are laid. Ridge ventilation is evaluated on every job: inadequate attic airflow is one of the fastest ways to void a shingle warranty and one of the most common oversights on older homes in this region.

Once installation is complete, the site is walked with a magnetic roller to collect fasteners from the driveway and landscaping, and the work area is left clean before the crew departs.
