## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Millbury

Every project starts with a thorough asbestos inspection and bulk sampling before any abatement work begins. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and no removal work starts until results confirm what materials are present and at what concentration. This sequence matters — it's required under Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection (MassDEP) regulations, and skipping it exposes homeowners to significant legal and financial risk.

Once testing confirms asbestos-containing materials, the abatement crew establishes a regulated work area with negative air pressure containment, HEPA filtration, and full personal protective equipment. All removed material is wetted to suppress fiber release, double-bagged in labeled disposal bags, and transported to a licensed disposal facility — a step that's non-negotiable under both MassDEP and EPA guidelines. Air clearance testing after abatement confirms the space is safe before containment comes down.

For homeowners in the 01527 ZIP code dealing with a renovation project, this process typically adds one to three days to a project schedule depending on the scope of materials involved. Planning for that window upfront prevents costly delays when a contractor is ready to swing a hammer.
