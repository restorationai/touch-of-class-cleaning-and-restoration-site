If you've noticed stale air or uneven heating this past winter, the ducts are a reasonable first place to look.

## Why Newton Properties See Duct Issues

The climate here does most of the damage quietly. Newton sits in a zone where summer humidity regularly pushes above 60 percent, and that moisture finds its way into duct interiors — especially in homes where the system runs through unconditioned attic or basement spaces. Older homes with plaster walls and minimal vapor barriers are particularly susceptible: condensation forms on the inside of supply runs during shoulder-season temperature swings, and that damp surface becomes a surface where dust and biological growth take hold.

Newton's tree canopy is dense and beautiful, but it contributes a steady load of fine particulate — pollen, leaf debris, mold spores from decomposing organic matter — that gets drawn into return-air grilles, especially in homes with older, loosely fitted registers. Homes built before the 1960s often have larger-gauge return grilles without modern filtration collars, so the duct interior sees more of what's in the ambient air than a newer build would.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Newton

The work starts with a visual inspection of every accessible register, the air handler, and the main trunk lines. In Newton's older colonials and Victorians, that sometimes means tracing duct runs through finished basements or crawl spaces that weren't designed for easy access — we map the system before we commit to a scope.

Cleaning uses truck-mounted negative-air equipment that puts the entire duct system under continuous vacuum while rotating brush heads and compressed-air whips dislodge debris from the duct walls. The debris travels to the collection unit rather than recirculating into the living space. For systems where biological growth is visible or suspected — common in homes where a humidifier has been running for years without maintenance — we apply an EPA-registered sanitizing agent to interior duct surfaces after the mechanical cleaning is complete.

Dryer vent cleaning is part of the same visit if you want it. Newton's taller, multi-story homes often have long dryer vent runs — sometimes exceeding 20 feet with multiple elbows — that accumulate lint faster than the appliance manufacturer's maintenance schedule assumes. A partially blocked dryer vent is a fire risk, and it's one of the more straightforward things to address while the crew is already on-site.
