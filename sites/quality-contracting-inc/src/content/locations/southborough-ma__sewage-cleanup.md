## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Southborough

The first priority on any sewage job is containment. Before any material is removed, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination from foot traffic or HVAC circulation. All standing waste water is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction units capable of handling solids, and affected porous materials — drywall, carpet, insulation, wood framing at or below the flood line — are removed and bagged for disposal in accordance with Massachusetts Department of Environmental Protection guidelines for Category 3 waste.

Once the structure is cleared of contaminated material, every surface that contacted sewage is treated with an EPA-registered disinfectant. This is not a single-pass wipe-down. Hard surfaces are cleaned, disinfected, and inspected under controlled lighting before the space is considered sanitized. Moisture readings are taken throughout, because sewage backups frequently drive water into wall cavities and under slab edges that look dry at the surface. Drying equipment — air movers and dehumidifiers — runs until readings confirm the structure has returned to normal moisture levels for the season. In Southborough's damp spring and fall months, that drying phase often requires more time than the same job would in a drier climate.
