## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Sutton

Every job starts with a fiber and backing assessment — not every carpet in a 1920s Wilkinsonville triple-decker should be cleaned the same way as a modern synthetic in a Sutton Center colonial. We identify the fiber type, check pile density, and look for any pre-existing damage before a drop of cleaning solution touches the surface.

From there, the process moves through:

- **Dry soil extraction** — commercial-grade vacuuming to pull out the grit layer before it turns to mud during wet cleaning
- **Pre-treatment** — targeted spotting agents applied to stains, pet odor zones, and high-traffic lanes; dwell time is adjusted based on fiber type and stain age
- **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted equipment delivers heated solution at controlled pressure, then recovers it along with suspended soil; this is the method most carpet manufacturers recommend for deep cleaning and warranty compliance
- **Pile grooming and speed-dry** — a grooming rake resets the fiber direction and accelerates dry time; in Sutton's humid summer months, we run additional air movement to compensate for the ambient moisture that slows evaporation near the ponds

For commercial properties — office suites along Route 146, the occasional retail space, or rental units being turned over — we also offer low-moisture encapsulation cleaning when a faster dry time is a priority.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Carpet

Truck-mounted hot-water extraction remains the industry benchmark for deep carpet cleaning because the machine's heating and vacuum power far exceed what portable units can deliver. The heat helps break the bond between soils and fiber; the high-recovery vacuum pulls moisture back out quickly, reducing dry time and the risk of secondary mildew — a real concern in Sutton homes where basement humidity can migrate upward through older flooring systems.

For carpet stain removal, we carry a tiered set of spotting agents: oxidizing treatments for organic stains (coffee, wine, pet urine), reducing agents for certain dyes, and enzyme-based formulas for biological material. Matching the chemistry to the stain type — rather than applying a single all-purpose product — is what separates a clean that lasts from one that reappears after the carpet dries.

Carpet sanitization treatments are available on request, particularly useful for households with allergy sufferers or for rental properties between tenants.
