## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Shrewsbury

Every job starts with a systematic exterior and interior assessment before any debris is moved. We photograph and document all damage — roof decking, siding, soffits, windows, and any water intrusion points — in a format that aligns with what insurance adjusters expect to see. That documentation step matters more than most homeowners realize; incomplete photo evidence is one of the most common reasons storm claims are underpaid.

From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Emergency tarping and board-up** to stop active weather intrusion at the roof or wall breach
- **Debris removal and hazard clearing**, including limb and tree sections that may be bearing load on the structure
- **Moisture mapping** of the interior using thermal imaging and pin-type meters to locate water that has migrated behind walls or under subfloor
- **Structural drying** with commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers, monitored daily until readings reach target levels
- **Repair and reconstruction**, from shingle replacement and fascia work to interior drywall, insulation, and finish carpentry

We handle the full scope in-house rather than handing off reconstruction to a separate contractor, which keeps the timeline tighter and the communication cleaner.

Most storm damage claims in Shrewsbury run through standard homeowners' policies, but the details matter.
