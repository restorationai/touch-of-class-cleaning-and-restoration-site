## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Grafton

Every job begins with a careful, private assessment. A technician walks the affected area, identifies all surfaces and materials that may have been exposed, and documents the scope before any work starts. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for confirming that nothing is missed.

Contaminated porous materials — flooring, subfloor, drywall, insulation — are removed, double-bagged in certified biohazard containers, and transported to a licensed medical waste disposal facility. Non-porous surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens, applied at the correct concentration and contact time. We do not cut corners on dwell time; the chemistry only works when it's given the time the label requires.

Sharps and needle cleanup follows a separate protocol: puncture-resistant containers, specific handling procedures, and chain-of-custody documentation for disposal.
