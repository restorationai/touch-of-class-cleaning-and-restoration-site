## Local Note

Homes in Bramanville and along the older mill-village streets near the Blackstone River frequently have plaster-and-lath interior walls rather than drywall. When a renovation involves opening those walls — for a bathroom addition, new electrical runs, or structural work — the demo process is slower and dustier than a standard drywall tear-out, and the debris is heavier. More importantly, plaster applied before 1978 can contain lead paint, which triggers Massachusetts Lead Law requirements for renovation work. That means specific containment, certified practices, and proper disposal — not something to skip over in the interest of moving faster. Any homeowner in the older parts of Millbury should ask their contractor directly how they handle lead paint compliance before the first wall gets opened.
