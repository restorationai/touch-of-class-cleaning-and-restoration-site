## Carpet Stain Removal: What Actually Works

Not every stain responds to the same treatment, and using the wrong chemistry can set a stain permanently. Pet urine, for example, requires an enzyme-based treatment that breaks down uric acid crystals — not just a deodorizer that masks the smell. Red-dye stains from beverages need a reducing agent, not an oxidizer. We identify the stain type before applying anything, which is the step most DIY attempts skip.

For Shrewsbury homeowners dealing with recurring dark traffic lanes that return within weeks of cleaning, the culprit is usually residue from over-the-counter carpet shampoos. Those products leave a sticky film that attracts new soil faster than untreated carpet would. A thorough extraction cleaning followed by a low-residue rinse pass breaks that cycle.
