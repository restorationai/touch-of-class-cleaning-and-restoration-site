## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Sutton

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated category — and the process reflects that. When we arrive, the first priority is containment: stopping the source if it hasn't already been addressed, and establishing a perimeter so contaminated material doesn't migrate into clean areas of the home. In older Sutton homes, that means checking whether sewage has tracked into adjoining rooms through gaps in original flooring or along the base of fieldstone walls.

Extraction comes next. We remove standing sewage and saturated material using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment. Porous materials — carpet, insulation, older wood subfloor — that have been in contact with raw sewage are typically removed rather than dried in place; no drying protocol recovers Category 3 contamination from an absorbent surface. Once bulk material is out, we apply EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces, following contact-time requirements that actually neutralize pathogens rather than just masking odor. Dehumidification and air movement run through the drying phase, and we verify moisture readings before closing out.
