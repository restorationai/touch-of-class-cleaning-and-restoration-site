## Our Mold Remediation Process in Sutton

Effective mold remediation isn't a single step — it's a sequenced process designed to stop active growth, remove contaminated materials safely, and verify the space is dry and clear before work is closed out.

The process typically begins with a visual inspection and moisture mapping. Using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, we identify not just where mold is visible but where elevated moisture readings suggest hidden growth behind walls or beneath flooring. In older Manchaug and Wilkinsonville homes with plaster walls and wood lath, this step is especially important because plaster holds and releases moisture slowly, meaning surface readings can understate the actual saturation depth.

Once the scope is established, we set up physical containment — poly sheeting, negative air pressure, and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers — to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected areas of the home during removal. Contaminated drywall, insulation, and framing are removed and bagged in accordance with industry protocols. After removal, affected surfaces are treated and dried to target moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We document every phase with photographs and moisture readings, which matters when you're filing an insurance claim.
