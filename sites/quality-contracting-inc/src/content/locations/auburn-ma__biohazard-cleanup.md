## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Auburn

Every biohazard situation is different, but the process follows a disciplined sequence designed to protect both the occupants and the property.

The first step is a careful assessment of the affected area — identifying the extent of contamination, what materials are involved, and what structural elements may need to be removed rather than cleaned. From there, the team works in full personal protective equipment, using EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants and HEPA-filtered equipment to clean, disinfect, and deodorize the space. All biohazardous materials — including sharps, contaminated soft goods, and porous building materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated — are packaged and transported by a licensed medical waste carrier in compliance with Massachusetts Department of Public Health requirements.

The final step is a post-remediation verification, confirming that the space is safe for re-occupancy.
