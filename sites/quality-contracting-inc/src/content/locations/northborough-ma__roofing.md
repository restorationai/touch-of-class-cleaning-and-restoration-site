If your roof is showing curled shingles, granule loss in the gutters, or daylight around flashing at a chimney or dormer, those are not cosmetic problems, they are the beginning of a moisture path into the structure beneath. ## Why Northborough Properties See Roofing Problems

Northborough's housing stock spans a wide range of construction eras, from mid-century colonials and split-levels to newer subdivisions built in the 1990s and 2000s. Older homes frequently have original roof decks with board sheathing rather than plywood panels, and those boards can cup, crack, or develop gaps over decades. When shingles age on top of cupped boards, the fasteners lose purchase and the roof surface becomes uneven, which accelerates granule shedding and shortens the remaining life of the shingles significantly.

The town's elevation and tree canopy also matter. Northborough has substantial wooded areas, and overhanging branches deposit organic debris that holds moisture against shingles and valleys. That persistent dampness promotes moss and algae growth, which lifts shingle edges and creates entry points for ice and water. In neighborhoods where mature oaks and maples shade north-facing slopes, algae streaking is common enough that it should be part of any pre-replacement inspection conversation.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Northborough

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system, not just the visible surface. We check decking condition, assess the state of underlayment, and look at all penetrations, plumbing vents, skylights, chimneys, and HVAC curbs, because those are the points where failures concentrate. If the decking has soft spots or rot from a slow leak, we replace those sections before laying new material. Installing new shingles over compromised decking is a shortcut that creates another failure within a few years.

For most Northborough residential roofing projects, we remove the existing shingles down to the deck rather than layering over them. Massachusetts building code generally limits the number of shingle layers on a roof, and a full tear-off gives us a clean surface to work from and lets us install a continuous ice and water shield along the eaves and in valleys, a non-negotiable step given the ice dam exposure this region sees. We then install synthetic underlayment across the field, set new drip edge, and apply the shingle system in a pattern that accounts for the roof's pitch and exposure.

We walk the completed installation before we leave and document the work with photos, which is useful if you need to provide evidence to a homeowner's insurance carrier or if the roof becomes part of a future property transaction.

## Local Note

Northborough has a meaningful number of homes built in the 1970s and early 1980s with low-slope sections over additions or attached garages. Those low-slope areas, often three-in-twelve pitch or less, require a different approach than the main steep-slope roof. Standard three-tab or architectural shingles are not rated for low-slope application; those sections need a modified bitumen membrane or a similar low-slope system to perform correctly. It is a detail that gets missed when a contractor prices only the main roof and treats the addition as an afterthought. We scope each section of the roof separately and specify the right material for the right pitch.
