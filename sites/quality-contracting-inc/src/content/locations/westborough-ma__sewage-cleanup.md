## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Westborough

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated category in the industry — which means the response is not simply a matter of pumping out standing water. Every sewage cleanup in Westborough follows a structured sequence:

**Containment and safety assessment.** Before any extraction begins, the affected area is assessed for structural integrity and electrical hazards. Sewage-saturated subfloors can delaminate; basement slab cracks can allow contamination to migrate laterally.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, compromised insulation — are removed and bagged for proper disposal. These materials cannot be dried and reused after Category 3 exposure.

**Disinfection and sanitization.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. In finished basements, this step often requires multiple applications because sewage wicks into block foundation walls and concrete that appear dry at the surface.

**Structural drying.** Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in framing and subfloor assemblies return to baseline. In Westborough's humid summers, ambient moisture slows this phase; equipment is sized to account for outdoor conditions, not just the affected room.

**Documentation.** Moisture readings, photos, and material removal logs are compiled throughout — both for your records and for insurance claim support.
