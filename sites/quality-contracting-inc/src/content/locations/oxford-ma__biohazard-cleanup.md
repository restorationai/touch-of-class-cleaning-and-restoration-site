## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Oxford

Every job begins with a careful assessment of the affected area before any materials are moved or disturbed. This step matters especially in older Oxford homes, where contamination may have traveled into adjacent rooms, wall cavities, or below-grade spaces that aren't immediately visible.

Once the scope is established, the work proceeds in controlled stages: containment of the affected zone, removal of contaminated materials following state and federal protocols, application of EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the specific biological hazard, and verification that surfaces meet safety standards before the space is released.
