## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Auburn

A post-construction clean has a defined sequence, and skipping steps is how dust ends up back on surfaces two days after a client moves in. The process starts with a rough clean: removing bulk debris, pulling protective film off floors and fixtures, and clearing construction materials the trades left behind. HVAC registers are masked or pulled so that the air system doesn't redistribute fine particulate the moment it kicks on — a step that matters especially in Auburn homes where ductwork often runs through unconditioned attic space and can harbor construction dust for months if not addressed at closeout.

The second phase is the detail clean: wiping every horizontal surface from ceiling to floor in sequence, cleaning window tracks and sills where silica and drywall mud accumulate, removing paint overspray from glass, and cleaning grout lines before sealer is applied. The final phase is a polish pass — the stage that makes the space ready for photography, punch-list walkthroughs, or immediate occupancy. For specialty situations such as post-fire board-up cleanouts, post-flood reconstruction finishes, or commercial spaces with epoxy floor coatings, the methods and products shift accordingly.

## Equipment and Methods for Construction Debris Cleaning

Standard vacuums recirculate fine particles; HEPA-filtered equipment captures them. For post-construction work in Auburn, that distinction matters on jobs involving cut drywall, fiber cement siding trim, or any work done in homes with original plaster walls. Microfiber flat-mop systems are used on finished hardwood and LVP rather than wet string mops that can raise grain or push water under seams. For grout haze and tile film — common after bathroom and kitchen renovations — the cleaning agents are pH-matched to the tile and grout type rather than applied generically. Window cleaning after construction uses a two-stage process: a razor scraper pass for paint and caulk spots, followed by a streak-free detail wipe.

For commercial projects along the Auburn Mall area or the industrial and flex spaces off Pakachoag Street, large-format floor scrubbing equipment handles concrete and VCT efficiently without the streaking that results from mopping construction-soiled floors.
