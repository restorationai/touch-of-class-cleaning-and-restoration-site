## Why Leicester Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable After Structural Damage

The housing stock around Rochdale and Cherry Valley grew up alongside 19th-century mill operations, and many of those homes were built with balloon-frame construction — a style where wall cavities run continuously from foundation to roof without fire stops. When a window breaks or a section of roof is compromised, wind and water travel vertically through those open cavities with almost no resistance. A single unprotected opening during a New England storm can push moisture into attic framing, insulation, and plaster ceilings within a single weather event.

Properties ringing Stiles Reservoir and Lake Sargent add another layer of complexity. Many are on private wells and septic systems, which means there's no municipal water shutoff to call — and when a pipe bursts behind a wall that's now exposed to the elements, the damage compounds quickly. The seasonal nature of some of those lakeside homes also means a broken window or lifted roof section can go unnoticed for days if the owner isn't on-site.

Leicester's elevation also accelerates ice dam formation in winter. A roof tarp installed incorrectly — too loose, wrong overlap, inadequate ballasting — will fail under the freeze-thaw cycling that hits this hilltop town harder than surrounding communities. That's a detail that matters when choosing who does the tarping.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Leicester

When a call comes in from a Leicester address, the first step is a rapid damage assessment on arrival — identifying every compromised opening, not just the obvious ones. Fire events in particular create pressure differentials that can push out window glazing in rooms adjacent to the origin room; those secondary openings are easy to miss and expensive to overlook.

For board-up work, we use minimum ¾-inch plywood secured with structural screws into framing members, not just into window trim. On the older homes common to Cherry Valley and Leicester Center, window and door framing dimensions often don't match modern standard sizes, so material is cut and fitted on-site rather than pulled pre-cut from a truck. That takes more time but produces a board-up that actually holds through a storm rather than racking loose at the first gust.

Roof tarping follows IICRC and industry-standard protocols for overlap and ballasting. On steep-pitched roofs — common on the late-19th-century vernacular architecture throughout Leicester — tarps are anchored with weighted boards rather than just stapled, and ridge lines are sealed separately to prevent wind from getting underneath and lifting the entire assembly. We document every opening covered with photographs before and after, which matters when the insurance adjuster arrives.

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors working in Leicester's older village neighborhoods: the window openings in many pre-1940 homes along Cherry Valley Road and the streets surrounding Leicester Town Common are not standard modern dimensions, and some have true-divided-light sash with exterior wood stops that are fragile and historically significant.
