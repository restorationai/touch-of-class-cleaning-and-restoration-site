## Why Framingham Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Framingham's housing stock spans more than a century of construction. The city's older residential neighborhoods include a significant number of pre-1960 multi-family homes where building systems — plumbing chases, shared ductwork, and original hardwood subfloors — create pathways that can complicate containment. Porous materials like horsehair plaster, original wood framing, and older grout lines can hold biological material below the visible surface long after the surface itself appears clean.

The city's climate adds another layer. Massachusetts winters drive residents indoors for months at a time, and when a biohazard situation goes unaddressed in a sealed, poorly ventilated space, conditions deteriorate faster than they would in a milder climate. Framingham's humidity patterns in spring and summer also accelerate microbial activity in affected materials. Prompt, thorough remediation — not surface cleaning — is the only reliable path to a safe, habitable space.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Framingham

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. Vehicles are unmarked, and the crew works without drawing unnecessary attention to the property — a detail that matters in denser residential areas where neighbors are close. Before any physical work begins, the affected area is assessed and contained to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or units.

Remediation follows a structured sequence: removal of affected porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, treatment of hard surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological hazard, HEPA air filtration to capture airborne particulates, and a final verification step before containment is lifted. All collected materials are transported and disposed of in compliance with Massachusetts Department of Public Health regulations governing infectious waste — documentation is provided to the property owner upon completion.

For multi-family properties, we coordinate directly with landlords or property managers to minimize disruption to uninvolved units while ensuring the affected space is fully cleared before re-occupancy.
