## Why Hopkinton Homes See Duct Buildup Faster Than You'd Think

Hopkinton's housing stock is a mix of older colonial and cape-style homes built in the mid-twentieth century and newer construction that expanded rapidly as the town grew through the 1990s and 2000s. The older homes often have original duct layouts with narrow trunk lines and tight bends — configurations that trap debris and are harder to clean with low-powered equipment. Newer subdivisions, by contrast, frequently used flexible ductwork during construction, and flex duct's corrugated interior surface collects particulates more aggressively than rigid sheet metal.

The town's location also matters. Hopkinton borders several conservation areas and wetlands, and homes near those lower-lying zones tend to pull more moisture-laden air through their intake systems during shoulder seasons. That moisture creates conditions where dust and biological matter can adhere to duct walls rather than simply passing through. Homeowners who have had any water intrusion — a basement seep, a slow roof leak — often find the duct system has been quietly circulating the byproducts of that moisture for months before anyone notices.

## Our Duct Cleaning Process, Calibrated to What We Find Here

Every job starts with a visual inspection of the supply and return registers, the air handler, and any accessible duct runs. For older Hopkinton homes with original metal ductwork, we use a high-powered truck-mounted vacuum combined with rotary brush agitation to break loose compacted debris without damaging joints or insulation wrap. For homes with flexible duct — common in the subdivisions built during Hopkinton's growth period — we adjust brush pressure and use compressed air whips that clean the corrugated surface without tearing the liner.

If we find biological growth, heavy debris, or evidence of pest intrusion in the duct system, we'll walk you through what we're seeing before any additional work begins. Sanitization is available when conditions warrant it, applied as a fine mist after the mechanical cleaning is complete and the system has been vacuumed clear. Dryer vent cleaning is a separate but related service we handle on the same visit when requested — a clogged dryer vent is a fire risk that's easy to overlook until lint buildup becomes significant.

## Local Note

Hopkinton's older neighborhoods — particularly the areas closer to the town center along Main Street and the surrounding streets developed in the mid-1900s — frequently have duct systems that were retrofitted into homes originally built without central air. Those retrofit installations sometimes run ducts through uninsulated crawl spaces or along exterior walls, exposing the ductwork to significant temperature differentials. That thermal cycling causes duct joints to work loose over time, and loose joints mean conditioned air is escaping into wall cavities while unconditioned air is being drawn back in. It's worth asking a technician to check joint integrity while the system is open — it's a quick visual check that can explain both energy loss and air quality issues that cleaning alone won't fully solve.
