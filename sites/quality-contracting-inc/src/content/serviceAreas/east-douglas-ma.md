## Restoration emergencies common in East Douglas

East Douglas sits in a part of Worcester County where older housing stock, wooded lots, and New England winters combine in predictable ways. Heavy snowmelt and spring rain push groundwater against older basement walls, and homes on wooded or sloped parcels often see roof and gutter failures during ice storms that back up meltwater under shingles. Many houses in this part of the county still rely on private wells and septic systems, so a failed sump pump or a backed-up line does not just mean standing water, it can mean a sewage cleanup on top of the water damage. Summer humidity is another factor: once water sits in a basement or crawlspace for more than a day or two, mold can begin colonizing framing and subfloor long before it is visible at the surface.
