Add in New England's ice and wind storms, which regularly take down tree limbs and power lines, and you get a steady mix of water intrusion, frozen-pipe bursts, and storm-damaged roofs that we see again and again in this part of Worcester County.

Much of Southbridge's housing dates to the late 19th and early 20th century, built around the town's mill and manufacturing history, with wood-frame construction, plaster walls, and full basements rather than slabs.
