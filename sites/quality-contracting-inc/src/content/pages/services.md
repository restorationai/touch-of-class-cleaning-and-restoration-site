From a burst pipe flooding a finished basement to smoke damage working its way into every corner of a commercial kitchen, property damage rarely fits a neat category — and the response shouldn't either. Quality Contracting, Inc. serves homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial clients across Auburn and the surrounding Central Massachusetts region, handling everything from the first emergency call through the final coat of paint. One company, one point of contact, start to finish.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, the clock matters. Standing water can begin warping hardwood within hours; soot residue bonds to surfaces the longer it sits; storm debris left in an open structure invites secondary moisture damage before the adjuster even arrives. Quality Contracting, Inc. handles water damage extraction and structural drying, fire and smoke damage cleanup, biohazard and trauma scene remediation, and storm damage response. Each of these services involves its own equipment, its own safety protocols, and its own documentation requirements — and each has a dedicated page walking through exactly what the process looks like on your property.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the building back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry, stable, and cleared for rebuild, Quality Contracting, Inc. moves directly into the repair phase — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because the same crew that documented the damage is managing the rebuild, nothing gets lost in translation between a remediation subcontractor and a separate general contractor. This integrated approach also matters for insurance: scopes of work, material specifications, and photo documentation carry through from the loss event to the completed repair without gaps. Beyond insurance-driven work, the team also takes on renovation projects where the goal is improvement rather than restoration.

## Specialty services

Some property problems don't fit cleanly into "emergency" or "rebuild." Mold inspections and remediation address hidden growth that often surfaces weeks after a water event — or quietly behind walls that were never properly dried after a slow leak. Odor removal goes beyond masking; persistent smoke, mildew, or biohazard odors require treating the source materials and, in some cases, running hydroxyl or ozone equipment through the affected space. Board-up and tarping services secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism, preventing weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while the larger scope of work is being assessed. These services are available individually or as part of a broader project.
