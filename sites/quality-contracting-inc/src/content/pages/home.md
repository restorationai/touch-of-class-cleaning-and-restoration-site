## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in January or a slow leak that finally gave out, standing water causes structural damage and creates the conditions for mold growth within 24 to 48 hours. Fast extraction and proper drying are what stop a bad situation from becoming a much worse one.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — The fire may be out, but smoke residue keeps working. It penetrates drywall, insulation, and HVAC systems, and the acidic soot begins etching surfaces within hours. Restoration means cleaning and deodorizing the structure itself, not just the visible char.

**Mold remediation** — Central Massachusetts homes — especially older construction common in Auburn, Worcester, and Shrewsbury — trap moisture in ways that invite mold behind walls and under flooring. Remediation means containing the affected area, removing compromised materials, and addressing the moisture source so the problem doesn't return.

**Storm damage repair** — A nor'easter or a summer microburst can leave a roof open to the sky overnight. Rapid board-up and tarping prevent secondary water intrusion while a full repair plan is put in place.
