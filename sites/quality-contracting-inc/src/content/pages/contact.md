## For urgent situations

Call **(508) 756-8800** and speak directly with someone who can assess your situation and get a crew scheduled. Our business hours cover the times most emergencies are reported and acted on — call during those hours and we'll move quickly to get you on the calendar. If you reach voicemail outside of business hours, leave a detailed message and we'll return your call at the start of the next business day.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If you're planning ahead — requesting a damage assessment, asking about a reconstruction project, or sorting out an insurance question before work begins — email us at **info@qualitycontracting.us** or use the contact form on this page. We typically respond to email and form submissions within one business day. The more detail you include (type of damage, approximate affected area, whether an insurance claim is already open), the faster we can give you a useful answer.
