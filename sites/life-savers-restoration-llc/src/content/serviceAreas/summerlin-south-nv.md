## Restoration emergencies common in Summerlin South

Summerlin South sits in a desert valley where the restoration risks look different from wetter climates, but they're no less serious. Monsoon storms in late summer can dump an inch of rain in under an hour, overwhelming roof drains and street gutters built for a mostly dry year, which pushes water into garages, patios, and ground-floor rooms faster than homeowners expect. Outside of storm season, the bigger threat is usually mechanical: slab leaks from aging supply lines, water heater failures, and washing machine supply hose ruptures that go undetected for hours in homes with few ground-floor drains. Low ambient humidity actually works against homeowners here too, because it masks moisture problems at the surface while drywall, insulation, and subflooring stay wet underneath, setting up conditions for mold colonization within just a day or two of an unaddressed leak.
