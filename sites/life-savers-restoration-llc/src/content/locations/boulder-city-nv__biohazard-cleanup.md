## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Boulder City

Every situation is different, and the process is calibrated to what's actually present — not a one-size checklist. In general terms, the work moves through these stages:

**Assessment and containment.** Technicians identify the full scope of affected materials before any cleaning begins. Containment barriers are established to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home or building.

**Removal of contaminated materials.** Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated — certain flooring, insulation, drywall — are carefully removed and packaged as regulated biohazardous waste. In Boulder City's older homes, this step sometimes reveals secondary issues such as deteriorated subfloor material or legacy building products that require additional handling protocols.

**Cleaning and disinfection.** Hospital-grade disinfectants and EPA-registered antimicrobials are applied to all affected surfaces. The goal is pathogen reduction to a safe, habitable standard — not simply the elimination of visible staining.

**Verification.** Before the crew leaves, the area is re-assessed. Documentation is prepared for insurance and, where applicable, for property records.
