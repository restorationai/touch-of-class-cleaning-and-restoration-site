## Why Sunrise Manor Properties See Water Damage Differently Than the Rest of the Valley

The housing stock across Sunrise Manor spans several distinct eras, and the construction era matters enormously when water gets in. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s — a large share of the neighborhood — were framed with materials and techniques that predate modern moisture-resistant building codes. Older galvanized and copper supply lines in these properties have had decades to develop pinhole leaks or corrode at fittings. When a fitting finally lets go inside a wall cavity, the water often travels horizontally along the top plate before it ever shows up as a stain on the ceiling below.

The valley's caliche soil layer, which sits close to the surface throughout much of Clark County, resists drainage. During the monsoon season — typically July through September — short, intense storms can dump an inch of rain in under an hour. That water has nowhere to go quickly. It pools against foundations, finds gaps in weatherstripping, and enters through garage door thresholds and sliding door tracks. Slab-on-grade construction, which dominates in Sunrise Manor, means there is no crawl space buffer: water that gets under the slab or along its perimeter affects flooring and framing directly.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Sunrise Manor

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment — not a visual scan, but readings taken with calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging where indicated. In Sunrise Manor's older ranch-style homes, water behind plaster or original drywall can read dry at the surface while the underlying framing stays saturated. We map the full moisture boundary before any equipment is placed, because drying to an arbitrary perimeter leaves wet material behind.

From there, the process moves through extraction, evaporative drying, and dehumidification in sequence:

- **Water removal and extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and water trapped in carpet padding. In slab construction, we pay particular attention to the seam between flooring and the slab edge, where water migrates under baseboards.
- **Structural drying**: Desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers are sized to the affected cubic footage, not placed by guess. Air movers are positioned to create directed airflow across wet surfaces, not just circulate room air. Drying is monitored daily against the IICRC S500 standard.
- **Documentation**: Moisture readings are logged at each visit. That log becomes part of your insurance claim file and documents that materials reached dry standard before any reconstruction begins.
