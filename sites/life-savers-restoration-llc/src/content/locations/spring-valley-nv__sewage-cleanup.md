## Why Spring Valley Properties See Sewage Backup Problems

The Las Vegas Valley's rapid growth over the past three decades means Spring Valley has a wide mix of housing stock — from older ranch-style homes built in the 1970s and 1980s with original cast-iron or clay sewer laterals, to tightly packed HOA subdivisions constructed in the 2000s where shared infrastructure carries heavy load. Clay and cast-iron laterals age, shift with the region's expansive desert soils, and crack under root intrusion from the ornamental trees common in Spring Valley landscaping. When those laterals fail, the backup doesn't announce itself gently — it surfaces through floor drains, toilets, and shower pans, often all at once.

The valley's caliche soil layer — a hardened calcium carbonate crust found throughout the Clark County area — can impede proper drainage around foundations. When heavy monsoon rain hits (and the Las Vegas Valley does get intense summer storms), the ground sheds water quickly rather than absorbing it, and that surge can overwhelm municipal sewer capacity and push sewage backward through residential lines. Spring Valley homeowners near lower-lying streets are particularly vulnerable during these events.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Spring Valley

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification in the IICRC S500 standard — which means standard water extraction protocols aren't enough. Every sewage cleanup we perform follows a containment-first approach: affected areas are isolated before any material is disturbed to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

The process moves in a defined sequence:

1. **Extraction and removal** — Standing sewage and saturated materials are removed using truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials like carpet, pad, and compromised drywall that cannot be decontaminated are bagged and disposed of in accordance with Clark County waste handling requirements.
2. **Structural drying** — Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on moisture mapping with calibrated meters. In Spring Valley's climate, ambient humidity is typically low, which can accelerate surface drying — but subfloor assemblies and wall cavities can stay saturated longer than readings suggest, so we monitor daily rather than assume.
3. **Antimicrobial treatment** — All affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. This step addresses bacteria, viruses, and the conditions that accelerate mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a sewage event.
4. **Verification and clearance** — We document moisture readings throughout the drying cycle and provide a final report suitable for your insurance claim or HOA compliance records.
