## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Indian Springs

Every job starts with a scene assessment to identify affected materials and establish a safe containment zone. Our technicians wear OSHA-trained PPE protocols and use EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for bloodborne pathogens and infectious material, following OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards and IICRC guidance throughout. Porous materials that cannot be fully decontaminated, such as carpet padding or certain subfloor sections, are removed and bagged for proper transport and disposal rather than treated on-site. Sharps and needles are handled with puncture-resistant containers built for regulated medical waste. Once materials are removed, we clean and disinfect all affected surfaces, then verify the space before closing out the job, documenting each step for insurance and, where applicable, law enforcement records.
