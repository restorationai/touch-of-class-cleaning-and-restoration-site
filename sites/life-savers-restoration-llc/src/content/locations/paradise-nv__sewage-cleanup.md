## Why Paradise Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Paradise is not an incorporated city — it's an unincorporated community within Clark County, which means building stock, infrastructure age, and utility maintenance responsibilities vary more than they would in a municipality with a single code-enforcement department. Many residential and commercial properties along the corridor between the Strip and the older residential neighborhoods to the east were developed in the 1960s and 1970s, when cast-iron and Orangeburg sewer laterals were standard. Those materials corrode, collapse, and root-intrude. When a lateral fails, sewage doesn't just back up into a floor drain — it can migrate under a slab, saturating the substrate and wicking into interior walls before the odor even registers.

The desert climate adds a layer most homeowners don't anticipate. When raw sewage sits on a surface at 105°F, bacteria and pathogen load multiplies rapidly. What would be a Category 3 water loss in a cooler climate is still a Category 3 here — but the window for safe remediation without structural replacement is shorter. Ventilation during cleanup also requires more care: opening windows to exhaust odor and humidity is standard practice, but in peak summer it can drive interior temperatures high enough to stress drying equipment.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paradise

Every sewage job starts with containment, not extraction. Before any water is moved, the crew establishes a contamination boundary, suits up in appropriate PPE, and documents the affected area with photos and moisture readings — the same documentation your insurance adjuster will need. That step protects both the unaffected areas of your property and the integrity of your claim.

Once containment is set, standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted units capable of handling solid waste. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that have contacted Category 3 water are removed rather than dried in place. There is no safe way to dry and retain sewage-saturated porous material; the pathogens do not leave with the moisture. After demo, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants, and structural drying equipment is deployed to bring framing, subfloor, and remaining assemblies to pre-loss moisture levels. The IICRC AMRT credential on the team means mold risk during the drying phase is actively managed, not just monitored.

## Paradise Insurance and HOA Coordination

Clark County's unincorporated status means HOA rules in Paradise communities can vary significantly from one development to the next.
