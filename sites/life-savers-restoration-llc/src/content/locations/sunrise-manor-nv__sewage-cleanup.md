## Why Sunrise Manor Properties Are Vulnerable to Sewage Backups

Much of Sunrise Manor developed rapidly through the 1950s, 1960s, and into the 1970s, and the lateral sewer lines serving those neighborhoods are now decades past their designed service life. Clay pipe joints shift and crack as the caliche-heavy soil underneath expands and contracts through the desert's temperature swings — triple-digit summers followed by cold winter nights put stress on underground infrastructure that homeowners rarely think about until sewage is already on the floor.

The valley's periodic monsoon season adds another layer of risk. A short, heavy rain event can overwhelm municipal sewer capacity, creating pressure surges that push effluent back through floor drains, toilets, and laundry connections in lower-lying homes. Slab-on-grade construction — the dominant foundation type throughout the area — means there is no crawl space to buffer the spread. Contaminated water moves laterally under flooring and into wall bases before it becomes visible.

Landscaping choices also matter here. Homeowners who planted trees and shrubs to combat the heat often unknowingly directed root systems toward the moisture in sewer laterals. Root intrusion is one of the leading causes of repeated backups in residential neighborhoods across the Clark County unincorporated areas that make up Sunrise Manor.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Sunrise Manor

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most hazardous contamination level under the IICRC S500 standard — which means the process is more involved than a standard water loss and cannot be shortcut.

When the crew arrives, the first priority is containment: sealing off affected rooms to prevent cross-contamination to living areas, and ensuring proper PPE protocols are in place throughout the work. Standing effluent and solid waste are extracted and disposed of in compliance with Clark County health and waste disposal requirements.

Once the visible contamination is removed, affected materials are assessed. In Sunrise Manor's slab-built homes, this typically means careful inspection of baseboards, lower drywall sections, and any flooring that sits directly on the slab — tile grout lines and the adhesive beneath vinyl plank flooring are common pathways for sewage moisture to wick upward. Materials that cannot be adequately sanitized are removed and documented for your insurance claim.

Sanitization follows extraction. Hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces, including subfloor concrete. Drying equipment — air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated for the low ambient humidity that Las Vegas's climate provides — then brings structural moisture readings down to pre-loss levels. Because the desert air is already dry, structural drying in Sunrise Manor can move faster than in more humid climates, but that speed advantage only applies after thorough sanitization is complete. Cutting the process short to save time creates conditions for microbial growth in the materials that remain.
