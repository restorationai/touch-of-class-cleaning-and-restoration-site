## Why Sunrise Manor Properties See Mold Problems

It seems counterintuitive, but desert construction practices can actually accelerate mold risk indoors. Many homes and apartment complexes in the Sunrise Manor area were built during Nevada's rapid growth periods of the 1970s through the 1990s, when evaporative (swamp) coolers were the dominant cooling technology. Those systems introduce outside air and moisture directly into the living space — and when a cooler's water distribution pad, reservoir, or ductwork develops a slow drip, that moisture has nowhere to escape in a tightly built interior. Stucco exteriors common throughout the area look solid but can trap water behind the finish coat when window flashing or roof-to-wall transitions fail, feeding mold colonies inside wall cavities long before a stain appears on the surface.

Summer monsoon season adds another variable. Brief, intense rain events can overwhelm flat or low-slope roofs common on mid-century ranch-style homes, driving water into attic spaces that rarely see inspection. The combination of heat-driven thermal cycling and intermittent moisture creates conditions where mold can establish a foothold in insulation and roof sheathing without triggering an obvious water intrusion event.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Sunrise Manor

Every project begins with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — not just a visual scan. Technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate elevated readings inside walls, under flooring, and above ceilings before any containment goes up. In Sunrise Manor's stucco-clad homes, this step is especially important because surface readings can look normal while moisture is trapped in the substrate behind the finish.

Once the affected zone is identified, we establish negative-air containment using heavy-gauge poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps mold spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the removal process. Contaminated materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing where necessary — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Nevada solid waste guidelines. Remaining structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to manufacturer-specified moisture content before any rebuild begins. We document every step with photographs and moisture readings, which becomes the paper trail your insurance adjuster needs.
