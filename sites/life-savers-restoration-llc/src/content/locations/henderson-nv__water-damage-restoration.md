## Why Henderson Properties See Water Damage Differently

Henderson's growth happened in distinct waves, and the era a home was built shapes how water behaves inside it. Homes constructed during the rapid expansion of the 1990s and early 2000s — common across the city's master-planned communities — often feature post-tension slab foundations. When a supply line fails beneath that slab, water travels along the tension cables and can surface several rooms away from the actual break point. That distance between source and symptom is one of the first things a trained technician accounts for during initial moisture mapping.

The region's low annual rainfall also means that plumbing systems, water heaters, and HVAC condensate lines carry a disproportionate share of the water damage load compared to storm-driven flooding seen in wetter climates. Appliance failures, slow slab leaks, and failed supply hoses behind refrigerators and washing machines are the dominant culprits here. Add Henderson's hard water — calcium and mineral deposits accelerate corrosion inside supply lines and water heater tanks — and the conditions for sudden, unexpected releases are built into the infrastructure of many homes.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Henderson

When a call comes in, the first priority is stopping water from traveling further. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full extent of saturation — not just the visible wet area. In Henderson's stucco-exterior homes, water that enters through a roof penetration or window seal can travel down the interior face of an exterior wall for several feet before it registers as a stain or odor.

Once the scope is mapped, water removal begins using truck-mounted extraction equipment capable of pulling standing water from flooring, subfloor assemblies, and wall cavities. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned based on psychrometric calculations rather than guesswork. Daily moisture readings document the drying curve so that equipment is removed only when materials reach acceptable equilibrium — not on a fixed calendar schedule. The team holds IICRC certifications in Water Restoration (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD), which means the drying plan is built on training specific to how water moves through building assemblies.

Most standard homeowners' policies in Nevada cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, a failed appliance supply line, an overflowing fixture.
