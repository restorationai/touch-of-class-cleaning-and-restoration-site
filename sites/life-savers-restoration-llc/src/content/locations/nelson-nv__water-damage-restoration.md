## Why Nelson Properties See Water Damage Issues

The desert climate around Nelson is deceptive. Most of the year there's little moisture to worry about, but the summer monsoon season brings short, intense storms that can dump a significant amount of rain in under an hour. Eldorado Canyon's dry washes and gravel roads aren't built to absorb that volume, so runoff finds the path of least resistance, and that's sometimes straight into a crawlspace or under a slab. Combine that with older plumbing in some of the area's original structures and well systems that fail without warning, and you get a pattern of water loss that's less about burst city pipes and more about flash intrusion and slow, hidden leaks in outbuildings that don't get checked daily.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Nelson

The extraction and drying process itself follows the same IICRC S500 structural drying standard we use anywhere, but the sequence gets adjusted for Nelson's building stock. We start with moisture mapping to find out whether water traveled under a pier-and-beam foundation or pooled against a slab, since those two conditions dry very differently. Standing water comes out with truck-mount or portable extraction units, then we set air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated for a dry desert climate, where ambient humidity is usually low but trapped moisture under flooring or inside wall cavities can still read wet for days if it's not actively pulled out. For well-fed homes, we also check whether the water source itself needs to be isolated before drying can even start, since a slow leak from a pressure tank will keep feeding the problem otherwise.
