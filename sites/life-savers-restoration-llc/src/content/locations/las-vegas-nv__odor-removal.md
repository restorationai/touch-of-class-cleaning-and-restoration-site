Las Vegas's desert climate does strange things to odor. Heat above 105 degrees pushes volatile compounds out of carpet pad, drywall, and cabinetry faster than in milder climates, which means a smoke smell or pet odor that seemed manageable in spring can turn overpowering by July. Life Savers Restoration LLC treats the source of the odor, not just the symptom, using equipment and methods matched to how Las Vegas homes are built and how this climate behaves.

## Why Las Vegas Properties See Odor Problems

Most Las Vegas housing stock is stucco-over-frame construction built from the 1990s forward, with tight building envelopes designed to keep refrigerated air in and desert heat out. That same tight envelope traps odor. Cigarette smoke, cooking grease, and pet accidents don't vent the way they might in an older, leakier home; they settle into HVAC ductwork, carpet pad, and the gypsum board itself. Many properties also run evaporative cooling or a mix of swamp cooler and refrigerated air, and the added humidity from a swamp cooler can reactivate dormant odors in carpet and baseboards during the hottest months. On top of that, Clark County's dust and the occasional wildfire smoke drifting in from California during fire season settle into HVAC filters and soft furnishings, adding a smoky, dusty undertone that homeowners sometimes mistake for a plumbing or mold issue.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Las Vegas

We start by identifying the odor source rather than masking it; this usually means pulling baseboards, checking under carpet pad, and inspecting HVAC returns, since Las Vegas's recirculated air systems move odor through a house quickly. Once we know what we're dealing with, we match the method to the material. Hydroxyl generators handle occupied spaces where people or pets are present, since hydroxyl treatment doesn't require evacuation the way ozone does. Ozone treatment is reserved for vacant properties or sealed rooms, typically after a fire or a heavy smoker's residence changes hands, because ozone is effective at breaking down smoke molecules but isn't safe to run around people, plants, or pets. For porous surfaces and tight corners, thermal fogging distributes a deodorizing agent as a fine, heated mist that mimics how smoke originally penetrated the material, reaching into cracks that a surface cleaning would miss. We finish with HEPA air scrubbing to clear residual particulate, which matters in a city where return air ducts are already working overtime against outdoor dust.
