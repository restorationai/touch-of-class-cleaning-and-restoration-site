## Why Henderson Properties Experience Sewage Backups

The Las Vegas Valley's rapid build-out means Henderson has housing from multiple eras sitting side by side — newer master-planned communities with modern PVC plumbing and older mid-century neighborhoods where cast-iron drain lines have been underground for decades. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out, and the mineral-heavy water common throughout Clark County accelerates scale buildup that narrows pipe diameter over time. When grease, wipes, or root intrusion meet an already-narrowed line, a backup is often the first visible sign of a problem that has been developing for years.

Henderson's caliche soil layer — a calcium carbonate hardpan common across the Mojave — creates drainage conditions that differ sharply from cities built on loam or clay. During the monsoon season (roughly July through September), sudden heavy rainfall can overwhelm municipal storm and sewer infrastructure simultaneously. When storm inflow infiltrates the sanitary sewer system faster than the system can move it, sewage surcharges back through the lowest fixture in a home, typically a basement floor drain or ground-floor toilet. Properties in lower-elevation pockets of the valley are especially vulnerable during those late-summer storm events.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification in the IICRC S500 standard — because it carries bacteria, viruses, and parasites that survive on porous surfaces long after visible moisture is gone. The process reflects that risk at every step.

**Containment first.** Technicians establish a contamination perimeter, seal HVAC returns to prevent pathogen spread through ductwork, and set up negative air pressure where needed. Personal protective equipment is non-negotiable before any extraction begins.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted or portable units. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and pad — that have contacted Category 3 water are removed rather than dried in place. There is no safe way to dry contaminated porous material to an acceptable standard; removal is the only compliant path.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage-contact pathogens. The sulfur and ammonia compounds in raw sewage bind to surfaces and require enzymatic treatment, not just surface spray, to eliminate odor at the source.

**Structural drying.** Once contaminated materials are out and surfaces are disinfected, drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — runs until moisture readings in framing and subfloor return to regional equilibrium moisture content, which in Henderson's dry climate is typically lower than in coastal markets. Psychrometric data is logged throughout.

**Clearance documentation.** Before reconstruction begins, moisture mapping confirms the structure is dry. This documentation matters for insurance carriers and, in HOA communities, for the association's property manager who may require proof of remediation before permitting repairs to shared walls or flooring.

## Henderson Insurance and HOA Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is often a rider on a standard homeowner's policy rather than a base-coverage item — many Henderson homeowners discover this only after a loss. Life Savers Restoration documents the damage thoroughly from the moment technicians arrive: photographs, moisture readings, material inventories, and scope notes formatted for adjuster review. Direct billing to most major carriers is available, which keeps the homeowner from fronting remediation costs out of pocket while a claim is processed.

Henderson properties built in the 1990s and early 2000s — a large portion of the city's single-family inventory — commonly used ABS plastic drain lines that can become brittle with age in the desert heat, particularly in unconditioned spaces like garage utility chases and exterior wall cavities.
