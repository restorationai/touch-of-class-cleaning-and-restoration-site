From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely waits for a convenient moment. Life Savers Restoration LLC handles the full arc of recovery — emergency mitigation, structural drying, mold remediation, and complete reconstruction — for homeowners, landlords, commercial tenants, and property managers across Henderson and the greater Las Vegas Valley.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, the clock matters. Standing water migrates into wall cavities and under flooring within hours; smoke residue begins bonding to surfaces almost immediately after a fire is extinguished; a sewage backup introduces pathogens that make a space unsafe until properly contained. The emergency services here address exactly those situations: **water damage restoration**, **fire and smoke damage restoration**, **biohazard and trauma cleanup**, and **storm damage response**. Each service is available around the clock, and the IICRC-certified crew that responds is trained specifically in that loss type — not a generalist team pulling double duty.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction brings the property back. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because Life Savers Restoration LLC holds both restoration and general contracting capabilities, the transition from tear-out to finished room doesn't require handing off to a separate contractor. That matters for timeline, for cost tracking, and for keeping a single point of accountability on the job. The same applies to commercial spaces where downtime is a direct business cost.

## Specialty services

Some situations don't fit neatly into emergency or rebuild categories. **Mold inspection and remediation** — performed by an IICRC AMRT-certified technician — addresses fungal growth whether it's discovered during a water loss or found independently during a home sale inspection. **Odor removal** handles persistent smoke, pet, or chemical smells that linger after the visible damage is gone, using hydroxyl or thermal fogging equipment rather than masking agents. **Board-up and tarping** secures a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism damage, preventing secondary losses before full restoration begins. **Lead-safe work practices**, required in older construction when disturbing painted surfaces, are covered under EPA Lead-Safe certification.

When a property loss happens, the last thing anyone needs is to manage three separate vendors — a mitigation company, a mold tester, and a general contractor — each with their own schedule and scope disputes. One call to Life Savers Restoration LLC starts a documented, coordinated process from the first moisture reading through the final coat of paint. If you're not sure which service applies to your situation, describe what you're seeing and the team will point you in the right direction.
