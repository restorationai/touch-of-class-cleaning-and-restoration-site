Life Savers Restoration LLC operates around the clock out of Henderson, NV, covering the greater Las Vegas Valley and surrounding communities. When you call, a dispatcher is available any hour of the day or night to confirm coverage, gather details about your loss, and get a crew moving toward your property as quickly as road conditions allow.

## Where we work

Henderson sits at the southeastern edge of the Las Vegas Valley, which puts the team within practical reach of a wide arc of communities — from the urban core of Las Vegas and North Las Vegas to the master-planned neighborhoods of Summerlin and the fast-growing cities along the US-95 and I-15 corridors. The valley's compact geography means most of the metro area is reachable without the mountain passes or rural highway stretches that slow response in other parts of Nevada. Boulder City, Laughlin, and communities along Lake Mead's shoreline fall within the broader service footprint as well.

## Coverage by distance

The closest calls — Henderson neighborhoods, Green Valley, Whitney Ranch, and adjacent unincorporated Clark County — get the fastest crew arrival because dispatch time and drive time are both short. Properties in central Las Vegas, Summerlin, and North Las Vegas are the next tier, reachable via the freeway grid that connects most of the valley. 

For locations at the outer edges — Pahrump to the west, Mesquite to the northeast, or communities across the Nevada-Arizona state line near Laughlin — travel time increases meaningfully, particularly during peak traffic windows on US-95 and I-15. Callers in those areas should expect a longer window between first contact and crew arrival, and the dispatcher will give an honest estimate when you call. Desert heat also affects how quickly certain losses — especially water intrusion — escalate, so calling immediately rather than waiting for morning makes a real difference regardless of where you are.
