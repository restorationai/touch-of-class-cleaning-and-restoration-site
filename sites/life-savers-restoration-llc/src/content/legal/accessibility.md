## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Life Savers Restoration LLC is committed to making its website usable by as many people as possible, including those who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** — the widely recognized benchmark for accessible web content.

If you are visiting our site to report damage, request an estimate, or reach our team during a stressful situation, the last thing you should encounter is a barrier. That goal shapes how we approach the design and content of every page.

## What We've Done to Support Accessibility

Our site is built with screen-reader compatibility in mind. This includes meaningful alternative text for images, logical heading structure, sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds, and form fields that are properly labeled so assistive software can identify them. Navigation is operable by keyboard alone, and we avoid content that flashes or autoplays in ways that could cause difficulty for visitors with vestibular or photosensitive conditions.

We also write our content in plain language — not because it's a technical requirement, but because homeowners dealing with water damage or fire loss shouldn't have to parse dense jargon to understand what we do or how to reach us.

## Known Limitations

We are a small, locally operated restoration company, not a large web development team. While we make every reasonable effort to maintain accessibility, there may be areas of the site that fall short of our goals — particularly in third-party tools or embedded content we do not fully control. We treat any reported issue as a priority and work to address it within a reasonable timeframe.

## How to Report an Issue

If you encounter a barrier on our website — a page that isn't readable with your screen reader, a form you cannot complete, or any other accessibility concern — please let us know. We take these reports seriously and will follow up promptly.

- **Email:** info@lifesaversrestoration.com
- **Mail:** Life Savers Restoration LLC, 7685 Commercial Way Ste G, Henderson, NV 89011

When you contact us, please describe the page or feature you were trying to use and the nature of the difficulty. That detail helps us identify and fix the problem faster.

## Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is not a one-time checkbox. As we update content, add new pages, or change site features, we review those changes against accessibility standards before publishing. We welcome feedback from visitors at any time — it's one of the most direct ways we learn where improvements are needed.
