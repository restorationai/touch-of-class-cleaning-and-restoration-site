## Restoration emergencies common in Shaler Township

Shaler Township sits in the hill-and-valley terrain north of Pittsburgh where Allegheny County's weather patterns concentrate moisture in ways flat suburbs do not feel as sharply. The township's housing stock leans heavily on the post-WWII era through the 1970s, meaning many homes carry older cast-iron drain lines, galvanized supply pipes, and plaster-and-lath wall assemblies that hold moisture long after the surface feels dry.
