## Restoration emergencies common in Hempfield Township

Hempfield Township sits in Westmoreland County where the Allegheny Plateau gives way to rolling terrain drained by Sewickley Creek and its tributaries. That geography shapes the losses here. Spring snowmelt and heavy summer convective storms push water into basements faster than sump pumps can keep up, particularly on lots where the grade has settled toward the foundation over decades. Winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that crack supply lines inside exterior walls and split cast-iron drain stacks in unheated crawlspaces. The township's housing stock spans post-war Cape Cods and ranches from the 1950s through 1970s, tract colonials from the 1980s and 1990s, and newer construction along the Route 30 corridor, meaning restoration crews encounter everything from knob-and-tube-era wiring in older additions to polybutylene supply lines in mid-90s builds. Fire losses in the area frequently involve attached garages and finished basements, where smoke and soot travel through HVAC returns before the occupant realizes the extent of the spread.

## Services we provide in Hempfield Township

### Water damage restoration

Water losses in Hempfield Township commonly start in the basement or crawlspace, but the real damage accumulates inside wall cavities and beneath hardwood or laminate flooring where moisture meters read normal at the surface while the substructure stays saturated. Katofsky Construction LLC uses thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, then extracts standing water, establishes a drying system calibrated to the structure, and documents conditions throughout for your insurance carrier.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke from a contained kitchen or garage fire travels through duct systems and settles into porous materials across the entire structure. In Hempfield Township homes with finished lower levels, that migration path is especially wide. The restoration scope covers odor neutralization, soot removal from framing and mechanicals, and coordination with your adjuster on what is cleanable versus what requires replacement.

### Mold remediation

The combination of humid Westmoreland County summers and the older basement construction common in the township creates conditions where mold can colonize within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Hempfield Township

Most of Hempfield Township's residential stock was built between the 1950s and the 1990s, with full basements being the dominant foundation type across that era.
