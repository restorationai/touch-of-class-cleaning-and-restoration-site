## Restoration emergencies common in Murrysville

## Services we provide in Murrysville

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Murrysville frequently starts below grade. Finished basements with carpet over concrete slab, or wood-framed lower levels built into a hillside, trap moisture in ways that a surface reading will miss. Thermal imaging and moisture meters placed at the wall cavity level tell a more complete story. Katofsky Construction LLC extracts standing water, sets drying equipment calibrated to the actual moisture load, and monitors daily until structural readings confirm the assembly is dry.

### Mold remediation

The combination of Murrysville's humid summers and older construction that predates modern vapor barriers creates conditions where mold can establish itself inside wall cavities without any visible surface sign. Remediation here means containment, physical removal of colonized material, and treatment of the underlying surface, followed by clearance testing to confirm the work is complete. We do not simply encapsulate and move on.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue from a house fire does not stay in the room where the fire started. It follows air pathways through ductwork, into closets, and behind wall plates. In the split-level and multi-story homes common throughout Murrysville, that migration can spread damage across several floors before the fire is even out. Restoration scope here includes odor neutralization, soot cleaning on structural surfaces, and content evaluation, not just the visible char.
