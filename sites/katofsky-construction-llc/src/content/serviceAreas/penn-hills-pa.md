## Restoration emergencies common in Penn Hills

Penn Hills sits in the rolling terrain east of Pittsburgh, where Allegheny County's freeze-thaw cycles and heavy spring rainfall create predictable stress on older homes. The borough's housing stock is largely mid-century construction, built during the postwar suburban expansion of the 1950s and 1960s, and those homes carry plumbing, roofing, and drainage systems that are now well past their design life. Basement flooding is a recurring problem here: the hillside topography channels surface water toward foundations, and clay-heavy soils common throughout this part of Allegheny County shed water rather than absorbing it, pushing hydrostatic pressure against block and poured-concrete walls. Ice damming along rooflines during January and February thaws sends meltwater under shingles and into attic assemblies. Chimney fires and kitchen fires in homes with original mid-century wiring are another pattern worth naming. Mold follows any of these events quickly in Penn Hills's humid continental climate, where interior moisture stays elevated through much of the spring and fall.

## Services we provide in Penn Hills

### Water damage restoration

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke from a kitchen fire or an electrical event travels through wall cavities and HVAC ducts, depositing oily residue on surfaces far from the origin point. In Penn Hills homes with original ductwork and plaster ceilings, that migration can be extensive. The restoration scope covers odor neutralization, surface cleaning, and content pack-out when needed.

### Mold remediation

Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. Penn Hills's older housing stock, much of it with limited vapor control in crawlspaces and basements, creates conditions where mold establishes quickly after a flood or a slow plumbing leak. Remediation work follows established containment and removal protocols to address the affected materials and the moisture source driving growth.

Katofsky Construction LLC handles biohazard situations, including sewage backups, with the discretion and proper personal protective equipment the work requires. Sewage intrusion in Penn Hills is not uncommon when aging municipal lateral lines back up during heavy rain events.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Penn Hills

The majority of Penn Hills's residential housing was constructed between roughly 1945 and 1975, meaning the dominant assemblies are brick veneer or aluminum-sided wood frames over poured-concrete or concrete-block basements.
