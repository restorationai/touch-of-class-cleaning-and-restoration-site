## Restoration emergencies common in Allison Park

Allison Park sits in a hilly stretch of Allegheny County where many homes rely on basements cut into clay-heavy soil, and that combination drives most of the calls we get. Spring thaw and heavy rain push groundwater against foundation walls faster than older drain tile can move it, and basements that stayed dry for years can take on water during a single hard storm. Winter brings its own version of the same problem: freeze-thaw cycles crack foundation walls and split supply lines in unheated crawlspaces, and a pipe that bursts overnight can soak framing and insulation before anyone notices. Remnants of tropical systems moving up from the Gulf occasionally stall over the region and drop several inches of rain in a short window, which is when sump pumps get overwhelmed and sewer backups become a real risk in older neighborhoods with combined or aging lines.
