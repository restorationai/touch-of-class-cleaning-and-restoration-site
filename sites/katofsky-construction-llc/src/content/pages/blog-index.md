Pittsburgh homes carry decades of history in their bones, and that history comes with its own set of vulnerabilities: clay-heavy soil that holds water after a heavy rain, aging cast-iron drains in Squirrel Hill bungalows, stone foundations in Mt. Washington rowhouses that were never designed to handle a modern sump failure. When something goes wrong, the first hour matters more than most homeowners realize, and the decisions made in that hour shape everything that follows, including what insurance will cover.

The posts here are written to help you navigate those moments clearly. You will find step-by-step guides for the first 24 hours after a water loss or fire, plain-language walkthroughs of how insurance claims actually work, seasonal primers on the freeze-thaw cycles and storm patterns that hit the Pittsburgh region hardest, and honest explainers on what restoration work involves so you know what to expect before anyone sets foot in your home. Some of it is practical enough to use on its own. That is intentional. If an article helps you protect your property or ask the right questions, it has done its job regardless of whether you ever pick up the phone.
