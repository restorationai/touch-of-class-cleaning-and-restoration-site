## Where we work

The team is based in Pittsburgh and works across Allegheny County and into the neighboring counties that make up the broader Pittsburgh region. That includes communities along the three rivers corridor, the South Hills, the North Hills, the Mon Valley, and the communities east toward the Laurel Highlands foothills. Western Pennsylvania's mix of older housing stock, steep terrain, and clay-heavy soils creates specific conditions that affect how water moves through a structure and how quickly mold can take hold after a loss, so familiarity with the region matters.

## Coverage by distance

Cities and boroughs closest to Pittsburgh receive the fastest order-of-arrival. As distance increases, travel through the region's river valleys and hilly terrain adds time, particularly during peak commute hours on corridors like Route 30, I-376, or the Parkway North. Communities at the outer edge of the coverage area are still served, but scheduling may look different from a same-day call closer to the city. The individual service-area pages note any location-specific considerations worth knowing before you call.
