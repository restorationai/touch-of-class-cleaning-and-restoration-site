When water backs up through a drain, mold spreads behind drywall, or a fire leaves smoke embedded in every surface, Katofsky Construction LLC is the call Pittsburgh homeowners and property managers make. We handle water damage restoration, mold remediation, and fire and smoke damage restoration, and we are available around the clock because damage does not wait for business hours.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, Whether it is a burst pipe in a finished basement, a slow roof leak that has soaked the insulation, or a sewage backup that has reached the subfloor, standing water starts destroying structural material within hours. We extract, dry, and document the loss so nothing is left saturated behind walls or under flooring.

**Mold remediation**, Pittsburgh's humidity and older housing stock create ideal conditions for mold to take hold after any moisture event. As an IICRC-certified firm with technicians holding the Applied Microbial Remediation Technician credential, we contain, remove, and treat affected areas following established remediation protocols rather than surface-level cleaning.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces long after the flames are out. The smell that seems to fade often means particles have settled into porous materials: drywall, wood framing, HVAC ductwork. We address both the visible char and the invisible residue that causes long-term odor and corrosion.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

A certification on a wall matters most when a technician is standing in your basement at 2 a.m. making decisions.
