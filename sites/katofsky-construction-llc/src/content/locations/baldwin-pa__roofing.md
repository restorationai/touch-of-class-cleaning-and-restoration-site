Baldwin's hillside terrain and the region's punishing freeze-thaw cycles put residential roofs through a stress test that flat-country homes rarely face. When it's time to replace or install a new roof in Baldwin, the work has to account for what's underneath, not just what goes on top.

## Why Baldwin Properties See Roofing Problems Early

Allegheny County's climate sits in a zone where temperatures swing hard enough through winter and spring to cycle through freeze and thaw dozens of times in a single season. That repeated expansion and contraction works at every nail, seam, and flashing joint. Baldwin's elevated position south of Pittsburgh means it catches the full force of lake-effect moisture moving down from the northwest, and the borough's characteristic slope means water doesn't drain the same way it would on a flat suburban lot. Ice dams form along lower eaves when attic heat escapes unevenly, and once a dam holds standing water against shingles for more than a day or two, the underlayment below starts to lose its integrity. Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s, which make up a substantial share of Baldwin's housing stock, often have original roof sheathing that is narrower and more prone to cupping than modern OSB panels, making a full tear-off and deck inspection a necessary part of any honest replacement job.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Baldwin

Every project starts with a full deck inspection before a single bundle of shingles is staged. On Baldwin's steeper-pitched roofs, that means walking the entire surface, probing for soft spots, and checking the condition of the ridge board and rafter tails where rot tends to hide. If the decking needs partial or full replacement, that work happens before any new underlayment goes down.

Underlayment selection matters more on hillside lots than on flat terrain. A synthetic underlayment rated for high-wind exposure provides a secondary water barrier that standard felt cannot match, and it stays workable in cold temperatures when crews are pushing to beat a weather window. Ice-and-water shield is run well past the eave line and into every valley, not just the minimum required by code, because Baldwin's roof pitches and exposure make those zones the first to fail.

Flashings around chimneys, dormers, and pipe boots are replaced as a standard part of any full roof replacement, not left in place to save cost. Reusing old step flashing under new shingles is one of the most common reasons a roof leaks within two or three years of installation.

## Baldwin Insurance Coordination

Hail and wind events that push through the Mon Valley and up into Allegheny County's southern boroughs regularly generate insurance claims, and Baldwin is no exception. The documentation process for a roofing claim requires more than a few photos: adjusters want to see the pattern of impact damage across the field of the roof, close-up evidence of granule loss and bruising on individual shingles, and confirmation that the damage is storm-related rather than wear-related. Katofsky Construction LLC documents losses thoroughly before any material is removed, which gives the homeowner a defensible record if the initial estimate is disputed.
