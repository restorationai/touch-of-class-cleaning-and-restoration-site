A large share of Shaler Township's residential stock was built between the 1940s and the 1970s, an era when construction methods and materials differed significantly from today's code requirements. None of these are dealbreakers, but they do change the scope and sequencing of a project in ways that a contractor unfamiliar with the area might not anticipate at the estimate stage.
