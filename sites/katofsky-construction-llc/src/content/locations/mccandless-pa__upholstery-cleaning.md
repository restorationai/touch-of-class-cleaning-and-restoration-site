## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in McCandless

Every piece of furniture is different, and the cleaning method has to match the fabric. Before any moisture or cleaning agent touches a surface, the crew inspects the manufacturer's care tag and does a fiber identification check. Delicate fabrics like silk blends or loosely woven natural fibers need low-moisture methods; dense synthetic weaves and most polyester blends handle hot-water extraction well.

For fabric upholstery, the typical sequence runs like this: dry pre-treatment to break up surface soils, followed by a pH-balanced pre-spray calibrated to the fiber type, then hot-water extraction with a portable unit brought directly into your home. The extraction step pulls the cleaning solution and suspended soils back out of the fabric rather than leaving them to wick back to the surface as the piece dries. Drying time in a McCandless home during heating season is usually faster than in summer because the forced-air heat keeps indoor humidity low, which helps fibers release moisture quickly.

Leather upholstery gets a separate protocol. Leather is cleaned with a pH-neutral solution applied by hand, conditioned after cleaning to prevent the drying and cracking that Pittsburgh's low winter humidity accelerates, and buffed to restore the surface. We do not use steam directly on leather.
