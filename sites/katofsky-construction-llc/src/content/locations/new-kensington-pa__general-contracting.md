## Why New Kensington Homes Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The residential neighborhoods climbing the hillsides above the Allegheny in New Kensington were built largely between the 1910s and the 1950s, during the city's steel and aluminum manufacturing peak. That era of construction means several things for any remodeling project today.

Plaster-and-lath walls are common, and they behave very differently from modern drywall when you open them up. Electrical systems in these homes frequently predate modern panel standards, meaning a kitchen remodel that looks straightforward on paper can surface knob-and-tube wiring the moment a wall comes down. Cast-iron drain stacks, galvanized supply lines, and undersized vent stacks are standard discoveries in bathrooms that have not been touched since the Eisenhower administration.

The valley geography also matters. Basements in hillside homes here are prone to hydrostatic pressure from the surrounding grade, which affects foundation work and any below-grade renovation. Before a single tile goes down in a basement finishing project, it is worth understanding where water has historically moved through that space.

Westmoreland County winters add another layer. Freeze-thaw cycles stress older masonry, and work that involves exterior openings needs to be sequenced carefully to avoid exposing the interior during the wrong stretch of February.

## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing in New Kensington: many of the two-story frame homes built on the steeper lots above the river were constructed with balloon framing rather than the platform framing that became standard after World War II. In balloon-framed walls, the stud cavities run continuously from the foundation sill to the roof line with no fire blocking at the floor level. That has implications for insulation work, for any fire damage that travels inside a wall, and for how a renovation crew needs to approach opening and closing those wall cavities. It is not a problem, but it is something a contractor who has not worked in this housing stock can miss until they are already mid-demo.
