## Why Latrobe Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Latrobe's residential neighborhoods carry a lot of housing stock from the mid-20th century and earlier, and those homes were built when combined sewer systems were the norm. When heavy rain saturates the ground, municipal sewer lines can surcharge, forcing raw sewage back up through floor drains and toilets. The rolling terrain around the Loyalhanna Creek corridor adds to the problem: water tables rise quickly after significant rainfall events, and homes with older cast-iron or clay-tile lateral lines are especially vulnerable to root intrusion and joint separation.

Septic systems are common on the outskirts of the Latrobe area as well. A failing drain field or a tank that has not been pumped on schedule can produce an overflow that is every bit as hazardous as a municipal sewer backup, and the cleanup process is equally intensive.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Latrobe

Category 3 water, the classification that covers raw sewage, cannot be treated like a standard water loss. Every porous material it contacts, including drywall, insulation, carpet, and subflooring, is a contamination risk and typically requires removal rather than drying in place.

Here is how the process unfolds on a Latrobe job:

1. **Containment and PPE staging**: The affected area is sealed off to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.
2. **Solid waste extraction**: Visible sewage and solids are removed and disposed of according to Pennsylvania DEP guidelines for biohazardous waste.
3. **Structural material removal**: Saturated drywall, insulation, and flooring are demoed to the extent contamination has traveled, which in older Latrobe homes with plaster walls can mean careful assessment of how far moisture has wicked into the lath.
4. **EPA-registered disinfection**: All hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants and allowed proper dwell time.
5. **Drying and dehumidification**: Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings confirm the structure is dry.
6. **Post-treatment documentation**: Moisture logs and photo documentation are compiled for your insurance carrier.
