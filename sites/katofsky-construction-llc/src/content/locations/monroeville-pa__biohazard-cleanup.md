## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Monroeville

Every situation is different, and the process is calibrated to what the property actually needs rather than a fixed script. When the crew arrives, the first priority is a careful assessment of the affected area to determine the full scope before any materials are disturbed. Personal protective equipment, containment, and proper air handling are established before remediation begins.

Biological material, sharps, and infectious waste are collected, containerized, and disposed of in accordance with Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection guidelines for regulated medical and biohazard waste. Nothing is placed in standard refuse. Affected porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated are removed and documented. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the specific category of contamination. A final verification step confirms the area meets established safety standards before containment is removed.

Throughout the process, the crew works with discretion. Unmarked vehicles are available on request, and the team communicates directly with whoever the property owner designates, keeping the situation as private as the circumstances allow.
