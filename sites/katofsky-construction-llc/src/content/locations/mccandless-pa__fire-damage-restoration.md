## Why McCandless Homes Are Particularly Vulnerable After a Fire

The housing stock throughout McCandless spans several construction eras, and each era comes with its own fire-damage profile. Homes built in the 1950s through the 1970s frequently used plaster-and-lath wall systems and older balloon-frame construction. In a balloon-frame house, wall cavities run continuously from the foundation to the roof line with no fire-blocking between floors, which means smoke and heat can travel vertically through the structure far faster than in platform-framed homes built after the 1980s. That vertical spread leaves soot deposits in unexpected places, including inside wall cavities on floors that appeared unaffected.

Allegheny County's cold winters also complicate post-fire conditions. After suppression crews saturate a structure, temperatures in the teens or twenties can freeze standing water inside walls before drying equipment can reach it, causing secondary damage that compounds the original loss. Coordinating the drying phase quickly, before a cold snap sets in, is a real operational concern in the North Hills from November through March.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in McCandless

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence of work follows a disciplined path. The first step is a thorough structural and safety assessment before any cleaning or pack-out begins. Smoke residue is categorized by type, because wet smoke from slow-burning fires behaves differently than dry smoke from fast, high-temperature fires, and the wrong cleaning agent can permanently set a stain into porous surfaces like brick, wood trim, and older plaster.

Once the structure is confirmed safe, the team moves through board-up and tarping if the roof or windows were compromised, then begins controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment address odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Content cleaning and pack-out, where personal belongings are inventoried, transported, and cleaned off-site, runs parallel to the structural work so the home can be cleared for the next phase without delay.
