## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process for Allison Park Homes

We start by identifying the fiber and construction, since a cotton-blend sectional, a leather recliner, and a microfiber loveseat each need a different approach and the wrong one can set a stain permanently or damage the finish. Fabric pieces typically get a pre-treatment for protein stains (pet accidents, food, body oils) followed by hot-water extraction that lifts soil and residue without oversaturating the cushion fill, which matters in Allison Park's humid months when over-wetting slows drying and invites odor. Leather upholstery is cleaned with pH-balanced solutions and conditioned afterward so the material doesn't dry out or crack, particularly important on pieces that sit near radiators or wood-stove heat through a Hampton Township winter. We also address the wood or metal frame and any decorative trim so the finished piece looks complete, not just spot-cleaned.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

Our crews use low-moisture steam extraction as the default for most fabric upholstery, since it cleans deeply without leaving cushions waterlogged for days, and we adjust extraction time and drying passes based on how humid the room already is. For delicate or antique upholstery, a category common in the area's older homes, we test colorfastness on an inconspicuous spot before applying any solution. Leather gets cleaned with dedicated leather-safe products rather than general upholstery shampoo, and we carry separate tools for fabric and leather work so there's no cross-contamination between jobs.
