## Why Latrobe's Housing Stock Complicates Fire Damage

Westmoreland County's coal and steel heritage left Latrobe with a dense inventory of pre-1960 homes, many of them built with balloon-frame construction. In a balloon-frame house, wall cavities run uninterrupted from the basement sill plate to the roof rafters. During a fire, that open channel acts like a flue, pulling smoke and heat vertically through the entire wall system before the fire itself reaches those spaces. After the fire is extinguished, those same cavities hold smoke residue and odor compounds in places that are invisible from the surface and unreachable with standard cleaning equipment.

Plaster walls compound the problem. Plaster is denser than drywall and draws soot particles into its porous surface under the pressure differential that forms during active burning. A wall that looks gray or tan after a kitchen fire may have soot bonded into the plaster substrate, not just sitting on top of it. Surface wiping alone will not remove it, and painting over contaminated plaster traps odor that re-emerges when temperatures rise in summer.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Latrobe

Every fire loss starts with a structured assessment before any cleaning begins. The crew documents the extent of char, smoke migration, and soot deposit patterns throughout the structure, including attic spaces, crawlspaces, and HVAC ductwork, which carry smoke residue to rooms that never saw direct flame exposure.

From there, the process moves through several distinct phases:

- **Stabilization**: Board-up and tarping where the fire compromised the building envelope, preventing weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while restoration is underway.
- **Debris removal**: Charred materials, compromised insulation, and unsalvageable contents are removed and documented for insurance purposes before any cleaning begins.
- **Soot and smoke cleaning**: Dry chemical sponges, wet cleaning agents, and thermal fogging are matched to the surface type. Plaster, wood trim, masonry, and HVAC components each require a different approach.
- **Odor neutralization**: Smoke odor is not cosmetic. Hydroxyl generators and ozone treatment reach into porous materials where surface cleaning cannot, breaking down the organic compounds responsible for persistent smoke smell.
- **Structural drying**: Water from suppression efforts is addressed using drying equipment and moisture mapping to prevent mold from taking hold in the weeks following the fire.

All drying work follows IICRC standards, and Katofsky Construction LLC is an IICRC Certified Firm.

## Local Note

Latrobe's older homes frequently have coal-converted heating systems, where original coal boilers were retrofitted for oil or gas over the decades. Those conversions often left original flue liners and ash chutes in place inside the masonry. When fire reaches a chimney chase in one of these homes, soot and creosote from decades of prior use can re-mobilize and travel through the ash system into basement utility areas, creating contamination in spaces that appear completely unaffected by the fire upstairs. A thorough fire assessment in Latrobe always includes the basement and any legacy flue or ash-handling infrastructure, not just the rooms where burning occurred.
