## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Murrysville

When a call comes in, the first step is a rapid site assessment to identify every compromised opening: broken windows, kicked-in doors, fire-vented walls, and any roof penetrations. We document each opening with photographs before work begins, which matters for your adjuster's review later.

For window and door board-ups, we use structural-grade plywood cut and fastened to protect the framing, not just cover the hole. On fire-damaged walls, we assess whether the framing can accept fasteners or whether an alternative anchoring method is needed to avoid worsening the structural condition.

Roof tarping in Murrysville requires attention to pitch. Many homes in this part of Westmoreland County carry steeper roof pitches than you find closer to the city, which affects how a tarp is anchored and weighted. A tarp that lifts in a 40 mph gust causes more damage than no tarp at all. We secure tarps with weighted lumber and anchor lines rated for the roof pitch and anticipated wind load, not with sandbags dropped at the ridge and called done.
