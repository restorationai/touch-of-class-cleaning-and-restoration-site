## Why Latrobe Properties See Duct Issues

Latrobe sits in a valley where humidity climbs in summer and cold air settles in winter, a pattern that stresses duct systems in ways that flat-terrain climates don't. The region's housing stock leans heavily on construction from the mid-20th century, when many homes were built with metal trunk-and-branch duct systems that were never designed for the high-efficiency air handlers now installed in them. Gaps at joints that were acceptable with older equipment become significant leakage points when a modern blower pushes more static pressure through the same passages.

Homes built in Latrobe during the postwar boom often have duct runs that pass through unconditioned crawl spaces or unfinished basements. Those sections collect moisture seasonally, which creates conditions where dust accumulates faster and, over time, can encourage microbial growth inside the duct lining. Older flex duct, where it was retrofitted into these systems, is especially prone to interior surface degradation that traps particulates.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Latrobe

Before any equipment goes into the system, the process starts with an inspection. A camera or inspection mirror goes into the main trunk and several branch runs to document what is actually present, not just what is assumed. That step matters in older Latrobe homes because duct configuration varies widely: some systems have dampers that were welded shut decades ago, and some branch runs dead-end into walls where a prior renovation sealed off a room.

Cleaning uses a negative-pressure vacuum collection system connected to the main trunk while rotary brush agitation works through each supply and return branch. The vacuum keeps dislodged material from recirculating into the living space. After the mechanical cleaning, all accessible registers and grilles are removed, wiped down, and reinstalled. If sanitization is requested, an EPA-registered antimicrobial is applied to interior duct surfaces, which is particularly relevant in systems that show visible biological growth or have been closed up through a humid Westmoreland County summer.

Dryer vent cleaning is handled as a separate service and is worth scheduling at the same visit. Latrobe homes with long or multi-elbow dryer vent runs, common in older two-story construction where the laundry room sits far from an exterior wall, accumulate lint faster than the manufacturer's annual cleaning recommendation accounts for.

## Local Note

Something worth knowing if your home was built before 1970: Latrobe and the surrounding Westmoreland County communities have a significant number of homes that were originally heated with gravity furnaces before being converted to forced-air systems. Those conversions often repurposed the original large-diameter gravity ducts as return-air plenums, which means the return side of the system can be dramatically oversized and lined with decades of accumulated debris that a standard cleaning scope may not fully address. When we encounter this configuration, we extend the cleaning time and adjust the vacuum connection point to make sure the full return path is covered, not just the visible register openings.

If your home has an unusually large floor register in a central hallway, that is often a sign of this conversion history and worth mentioning when you call.
