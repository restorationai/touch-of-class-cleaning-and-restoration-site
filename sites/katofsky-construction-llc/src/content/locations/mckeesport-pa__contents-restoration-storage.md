## Why McKeesport Properties See Contents Loss Differently

McKeesport sits at the confluence of the Youghiogheny and Monongahela rivers, and that geography shapes how water behaves here. The Mon Valley's humidity stays elevated through much of the year, and the city's terrain channels runoff in ways that catch homeowners off guard during heavy rain events. Basements in the brick rowhouses and Craftsman-era homes that define much of the city's residential fabric tend to take on water through mortar joints and floor drains rather than dramatic pipe failures. That kind of slow intrusion is deceptive: by the time standing water is visible, upholstered furniture, area rugs, and cardboard boxes of stored items have already been absorbing moisture for hours. Smoke and soot from structure fires behave differently in homes with plaster walls and older wood framing too. Residue travels farther through gaps in the building envelope and settles into fabrics and finishes in rooms that never saw a flame.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in McKeesport

The first step is documentation, not packing. Every item in the affected area is photographed and inventoried before anything moves. That record protects you with your insurance carrier and gives us a baseline for tracking every piece through cleaning and return. Items are then sorted by material type and damage category: what can be cleaned on-site, what needs to go to a climate-controlled facility, and what is beyond restoration. That honest triage matters because treating a saturated particle-board bookcase the same as a solid-oak dresser wastes time and money.

Textiles, documents, photographs, and electronics each require different handling. Soft goods go through thermal or ozone treatment depending on the contamination type. Hard surfaces are cleaned with agents matched to the finish. Electronics are assessed before any attempt at restoration because energizing a water-damaged device without proper drying can cause secondary damage. Throughout the process, items in storage are held in a climate-controlled environment, not a warehouse that swings between cold and humid with the seasons.

Katofsky Construction is an IICRC Certified Firm, and our technicians hold IICRC credentials in water damage restoration and applied microbial remediation. That training shapes how we handle items that have been exposed to mold-affected materials, which is a real consideration in McKeesport's older homes where a slow basement leak can go unnoticed long enough for mold to colonize stored belongings.

## Insurance Coordination for McKeesport Claims

Contents claims are often the most disputed part of a homeowner's insurance settlement because adjusters and policyholders don't always agree on what is restorable versus what needs to be replaced. Our itemized documentation, including photographs, condition notes, and cleaning records, gives your adjuster the evidence needed to process the claim accurately.
