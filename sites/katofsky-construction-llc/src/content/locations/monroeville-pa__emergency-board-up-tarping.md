## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Monroeville

When a call comes in from a Monroeville address, the first step is a rapid site assessment to identify every breach: broken windows, kicked-in doors, roof sections missing shingles or decking, and any wall openings left by fire department ventilation cuts. Each opening is measured and documented with photographs, which becomes part of the loss file for insurance purposes.

For window and door board-up, panels are cut to fit the rough opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails, and sealed at the edges to limit wind infiltration. For roof tarping, crews anchor tarps using a cap-and-batten method, running weighted lumber along the tarp edges and securing it to the roof deck rather than relying on adhesive or simple tie-downs that wind can defeat overnight. On steeper-pitched roofs common to older Monroeville neighborhoods, fall-protection equipment is part of every job.

All work is documented before and after, with material specifications noted, so the insurance adjuster receives a clear picture of what was installed and why.
