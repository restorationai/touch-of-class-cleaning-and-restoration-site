## Why McCandless Properties Present Specific Biohazard Considerations

The housing stock across McCandless spans several decades of construction. Homes built in the 1950s through 1970s often feature porous concrete block foundations, older subflooring materials, and HVAC configurations that can complicate thorough decontamination. Porous surfaces absorb biological material differently than modern finishes, and standard surface cleaning is not sufficient when contamination has reached the substrate. Proper remediation in these homes requires material testing, controlled removal, and verification rather than a surface-level pass.

Allegheny County's climate also plays a role. The region's humid summers and variable shoulder seasons mean that any biological material left unaddressed even briefly can interact with ambient moisture in ways that accelerate secondary concerns. Prompt, thorough response is not just about the immediate situation; it protects the long-term integrity of the structure.
