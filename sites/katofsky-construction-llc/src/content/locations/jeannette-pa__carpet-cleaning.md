## Why Jeannette Homes See Stubborn Carpet Wear

Much of Jeannette's residential stock was built between the 1920s and the 1960s, when the glass industry was the engine of the local economy. Those homes tend to have original hardwood underneath the carpet, which is worth protecting, but the subfloors can be uneven and the carpet padding installed over decades of renovation layers. Older padding holds moisture longer than modern foam, which means spills that seem minor at the surface can stay damp at the base of the pile for days. In a climate that averages significant humidity through late spring and summer, that retained moisture creates conditions where odors develop and, over time, biological growth can begin.

The soil around Jeannette also plays a role. The region's clay-dominant ground does not drain quickly, so wet weather drives more foot traffic through muddy or wet yards before people step inside. That clay particulate is fine enough to work deep into carpet fibers and abrade them from the base up, dulling color and shortening the life of the carpet even when the surface looks relatively clean.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Jeannette

The process starts with a dry inspection, reading the pile direction, fiber type, and any existing staining patterns before a drop of water touches the carpet. Pre-treatment is applied to high-traffic lanes and stained areas, allowing dwell time to break down the bond between the soiling and the fiber. Hot-water extraction then pulls the loosened material out rather than pushing it further in, using truck-mounted equipment that maintains consistent temperature and suction throughout the job.

For older homes with wood subfloors, moisture control during extraction is deliberate. The goal is to leave the carpet with enough residual moisture to finish drying within a few hours, not enough to saturate the pad and reach the wood below. Airflow equipment is positioned to accelerate drying, particularly in rooms that do not get strong cross-ventilation, which is common in the tighter floor plans of Jeannette's mid-century bungalows and row-style homes.

Carpet sanitization is available as an add-on for households with pets, allergy concerns, or any recent water intrusion. The IICRC-certified team applies an EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment after extraction, which addresses odor-causing residue at the fiber level rather than masking it.
