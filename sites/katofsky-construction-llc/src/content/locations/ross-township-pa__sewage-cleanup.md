## Why Ross Township Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Much of Ross Township's residential housing was built between the 1940s and 1970s, a period when clay tile and cast iron were the standard materials for drain lines. Decades of root intrusion, ground shifting, and corrosion leave those laterals prone to partial collapses and blockages that back wastewater into the lowest point of a home, usually a basement floor drain or a first-floor toilet. The township's hilly terrain also means that during heavy rain events, storm inflow can overwhelm combined sewer segments and push raw sewage upstream into residential connections.

Homes along the lower elevations near McKnight Road and the creek drainages that feed into Girty's Run are particularly vulnerable to this kind of sewer surcharge. When the ground is saturated after a prolonged wet period, even a modest blockage downstream can send Category 3 water, which is what the industry calls raw sewage, backing up through floor drains before a homeowner realizes anything is wrong.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Ross Township

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 contaminated water, meaning it carries bacteria, viruses, and pathogens that require full personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants, not just a shop vac and bleach. The process follows a specific sequence:

**Containment and assessment.** The affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. Moisture meters and thermal imaging identify where sewage has wicked into walls, subfloor material, or insulation.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction equipment. Porous materials, including saturated drywall, carpet, and insulation, that have had direct contact with Category 3 water are removed and bagged for disposal per Allegheny County waste handling requirements.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Fogging equipment reaches into cavities that manual wiping cannot. The goal is documented pathogen reduction, not just surface cleaning.

**Drying and verification.** Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings return to baseline for the material type. Final readings are documented before equipment is removed.

Our team holds IICRC certification in Water Damage Restoration (WRT) and Applied Microbial Remediation (AMRT), which covers the mold risk that follows any sewage event where drying is delayed.

## Local Note

Ross Township's postwar housing stock frequently used a combination of concrete block foundation walls and poured concrete floors with minimal vapor barriers beneath the slab. When sewage backs up through a floor drain in these basements, the concrete itself can absorb contaminated water several inches deep. Surface cleaning alone is not sufficient. Proper remediation requires treating the concrete with penetrating disinfectants and confirming that moisture levels in the slab return to acceptable levels before any restoration work begins. Skipping this step is one of the more common reasons a sewage cleanup job develops a persistent odor problem weeks later.
