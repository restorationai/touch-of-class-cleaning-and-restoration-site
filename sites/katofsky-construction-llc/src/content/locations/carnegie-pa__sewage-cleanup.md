## Why Carnegie Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The Chartiers Creek corridor creates a natural low point that the borough's storm and sanitary systems have to work against during heavy rain events. When the combined load on aging municipal lines exceeds capacity, the pressure reverses and finds the path of least resistance, which is often a basement floor drain in a 1940s or 1950s ranch or split-level. Many of these homes were built before modern backflow prevention was standard, and the clay tile laterals connecting them to the street main have had decades to crack, root-intrude, or shift with the soil. Septic systems on the borough's outskirts face their own stress when ground saturation reduces the drain field's ability to absorb, pushing effluent back toward the structure. These are not freak events in Carnegie; they follow a predictable seasonal pattern tied to spring snowmelt and summer thunderstorm clusters that move through the Allegheny County foothills.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Carnegie

The first priority on any sewage call is containment. Standing sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most hazardous designation in the IICRC S500 framework, and it requires full personal protective equipment and isolation of the affected zone before any extraction begins. The process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Extraction and removal.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull liquid waste and saturated debris. Porous materials that have contacted raw sewage, including drywall, insulation, and carpet, are removed and bagged for disposal according to Allegheny County waste handling requirements. There is no drying a contaminated material back to safe condition.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Once surfaces are cleared, EPA-registered disinfectants are applied at appropriate dwell times to concrete, framing, and any hard surfaces that can be salvaged. This is not a single-pass wipe-down. Sewage odor compounds penetrate into block and mortar, and eliminating them requires multiple treatment rounds and, in some cases, thermal fogging.

**Drying and verification.** After sanitization, the space is dried with industrial air movers and dehumidifiers. Moisture readings are logged at multiple points until the structure reaches acceptable levels. As an IICRC Certified Firm, the documentation produced during this phase is the same format insurers and adjusters expect to see.
