## Why North Huntingdon Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Much of North Huntingdon's residential development happened in the 1950s through the 1970s, when clay-tile and Orangeburg sewer laterals were standard. Those materials crack, root-intrude, and collapse over decades, and the township's rolling terrain means that low-lying lots collect groundwater pressure against those aging pipes every time the region gets a heavy rain event. When the main line surcharges during a storm, the path of least resistance is often straight back through a floor drain or toilet into a finished lower level.

Septic systems are still common on the township's outer parcels, and a saturated drainfield after a wet spring can push partially treated effluent back toward the structure. That type of overflow carries a different contamination load than a municipal sewer backup, and the sanitization protocol has to account for it. Either way, the material left behind is classified as Category 3 water under the IICRC S500 standard, meaning every porous surface it touched is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in North Huntingdon

The first step on any sewage call is containment. Before extraction equipment moves, the crew identifies the source, isolates the affected zone, and establishes a decontamination barrier so contaminated material does not travel to clean areas of the home. In North Huntingdon's split-level and bi-level floor plans, which are common throughout the township, that boundary often runs at the stair landing between the garage-level utility area and the main living floor.

Extraction follows: truck-mounted or portable units pull standing sewage from hard surfaces, and affected carpet, pad, and saturated drywall are removed and bagged for disposal. The IICRC-certified technicians then apply an EPA-registered disinfectant to all contacted surfaces, including subfloor sheathing, concrete, and any framing that tested positive for moisture. Structural drying runs until readings confirm that the framing and substrate have returned to normal moisture content for the region's ambient conditions, not just until the surface feels dry to the touch.

Documentation runs parallel to every step: moisture readings, photographs, and a written scope are prepared to support your insurance claim from day one.
