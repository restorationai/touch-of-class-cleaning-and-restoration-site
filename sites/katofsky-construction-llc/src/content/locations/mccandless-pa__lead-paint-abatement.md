## Why McCandless Properties See Lead Paint Concerns

Allegheny County's North Hills developed in waves. McCandless saw significant residential construction from the 1940s through the 1970s, which means a large portion of its single-family homes fall squarely within the era when lead-based paint was standard. Homes built before 1960 are particularly likely to have multiple layers of lead paint on original woodwork, plaster walls, and exterior siding.

The region's climate adds a mechanical dimension to the problem. Western Pennsylvania winters are hard on painted surfaces. Freeze-thaw cycles cause wood to expand and contract, which accelerates paint chalking and peeling, especially on exterior trim and window frames. Peeling or deteriorating lead paint is the primary pathway for lead dust exposure, particularly for children under six who spend time on floors and near windowsills. What looks like ordinary weathering on a McCandless home built in 1955 may actually be a lead hazard that requires professional assessment before any renovation work begins.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in McCandless

The process begins with testing, not assumptions. Lead paint testing identifies which surfaces contain lead and at what concentration, which determines the appropriate response: full abatement, encapsulation, or lead-safe work practices during renovation.

For abatement projects, the work sequence follows EPA RRP guidelines and Pennsylvania Department of Labor and Industry requirements. Containment is established before any disturbing of painted surfaces. Plastic sheeting seals the work area, HEPA-filtered negative air machines prevent cross-contamination, and workers use personal protective equipment throughout. Painted components are carefully removed or treated using wet methods that suppress dust generation.

After abatement, a thorough HEPA vacuum and wet-wipe cleaning removes residual dust from all surfaces, including floors, windowsills, and horizontal ledges where lead dust settles. Clearance testing by a certified inspector confirms that dust wipe samples fall below EPA clearance levels before containment is removed and the space is returned to normal use. Documentation is provided for your records, which matters if you are selling the property or working through a lender that requires lead clearance.
