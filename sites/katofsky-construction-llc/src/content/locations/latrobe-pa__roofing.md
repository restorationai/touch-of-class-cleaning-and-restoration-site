## Why Latrobe Properties See Roofing Problems

Latrobe sits in a pocket of southwestern Pennsylvania where elevation and geography conspire against roofing materials. The Chestnut Ridge to the west funnels cold air and moisture directly into the Loyalhanna Creek valley, and properties on the hillier grades north and south of town face wind-driven rain that probes every seam. Many homes in the area were built between the 1920s and the 1960s, which means original roof decks are often board sheathing rather than plywood or OSB. Board sheathing can cup and gap over decades, creating an uneven nailing surface that shortens the life of any new shingle layer installed over it without proper prep work.

The area also sees significant ice dam formation in winter. When attic heat escapes through an under-insulated roof deck, snow melts at the ridge and refreezes at the cold eaves. That ice backs water under shingles and into the home. A replacement project that does not address attic ventilation and insulation at the same time is solving half the problem.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Latrobe

Every project starts with a full deck inspection, not just a shingle count. On older Latrobe homes, that means checking individual boards for rot, cupping, and nail pull-through before a single new shingle goes down. Soft or deteriorated decking is replaced in kind so the new roof has a solid substrate.

From there the process follows a clear sequence: ice-and-water shield along all eaves and valleys, synthetic underlayment across the field, new drip edge installed to current code, and then the chosen shingle or roofing material laid from the bottom up. Flashings at chimneys, dormers, and sidewalls are replaced rather than reused, because old step flashing bent around a new shingle profile rarely seals correctly. Ridge ventilation is assessed and upgraded if the existing profile is undersized for the attic volume.

For properties where the existing roof layer count is at or near the local maximum allowed before a full tear-off is required, we pull permits through Westmoreland County and schedule inspections so the finished job is documented and the homeowner has a clean record for insurance and future sale.
