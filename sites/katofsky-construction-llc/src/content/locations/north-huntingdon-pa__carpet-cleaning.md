## Why North Huntingdon Carpets Take a Harder Hit

Westmoreland County's clay-dominant soil is dense and sticky. When it dries on carpet fibers, it bonds at a microscopic level that surface cleaning cannot address. During the colder months, residents track in road salt and de-icing compounds that are corrosive to carpet backing if left to accumulate. The area also sees significant humidity swings between summer and winter, which can cause carpet fibers to expand and contract, loosening the grip that protects pile density over time.

Older homes throughout North Huntingdon, particularly those built in the 1960s and 1970s, often have wall-to-wall carpet installed over concrete slab or older subfloor materials. When moisture gets beneath that carpet, whether from a plumbing issue or a humid basement, it can linger far longer than in newer construction with better vapor barriers. That combination of age, subfloor type, and regional climate means professional cleaning here is not just about appearance; it is about protecting the investment beneath your feet.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in North Huntingdon

Homes in North Huntingdon that were built with finished basements in the 1970s and 1980s frequently have carpet glued directly to concrete rather than stretched over pad.
