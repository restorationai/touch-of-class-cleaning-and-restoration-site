## Why Latrobe Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

Latrobe's housing stock leans heavily on construction from the early to mid-twentieth century. Brick rowhouses, wood-frame craftsman bungalows, and older two-story colonials dominate many of the city's streets, and those structures come with their own vulnerabilities. Older window frames are often set in deteriorating wood surrounds, meaning a single broken pane can compromise a much larger section of the wall assembly. Roof decking on homes built before the 1960s is frequently skip-sheathed, the kind of board-on-board construction that gives a tarp fewer solid anchor points than modern plywood decking does.

Westmoreland County also sits in a climate band that delivers significant freeze-thaw cycling through late fall and early spring. A fire that vents through a roof in October leaves the interior exposed to temperatures that can drop below freezing within days. Without a properly secured tarp, insulation gets saturated, pipes freeze, and what was a fire loss becomes a concurrent water and freeze loss. Insurers track those secondary claims, and they are almost always avoidable.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Latrobe

When a call comes in, the first step is a quick phone assessment to understand the type of opening, the approximate size, and whether the structure is stable enough for a crew to work safely. That information shapes what materials come on the truck.

For window and door board-ups, the crew uses exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to the rough opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails, which pull out under wind load. Door openings get framed so the panel can be removed and reinstalled cleanly when the contractor or adjuster needs access.

Roof tarping on Latrobe's older homes requires extra attention to the anchor strategy. Crews use weighted lumber battens along the tarp edges rather than relying on staples or adhesive alone, because skip-sheathed decks do not hold fasteners the same way modern OSB does. The tarp is lapped over the ridge when the damage runs close to it, preventing wind from catching an edge and peeling the cover back during overnight gusts off the Chestnut Ridge.

## Insurance Coordination for Latrobe Board-Up Claims

Board-up and tarping are almost universally covered under the emergency services provision of a standard homeowner's policy, but the documentation has to be done correctly for the carrier to accept it. The crew photographs every opening before any material goes up, documents the cause of loss as observed, and provides a written scope of the work performed. ## Local Note

Older homes in Latrobe with slate roofs present a specific challenge that comes up more often here than in newer suburban markets. Slate is heavy and brittle, and fire or impact damage that cracks a section can leave loose slates balanced against each other above an open hole. A tarp crew that does not account for that instability risks dislodging material onto the workers below. The approach on slate roofs is to clear a safe perimeter, assess the loose field from a ladder before stepping onto the surface, and anchor the tarp from the edges rather than walking the damaged zone. It takes a few extra minutes, but it is the only safe way to work on a compromised slate field.
