## Why New Kensington Homes Create Specific Contents Challenges

The older construction common throughout western Westmoreland County and the Alle-Kiski Valley tends to concentrate belongings in tighter spaces than newer builds. Finished basements, enclosed porches, and attic storage rooms are standard in the brick and frame homes that line New Kensington's residential streets. When water intrudes, it reaches stored items quickly, and the same dense layout that makes these homes charming makes ventilation and drying slower.

Smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire also behaves differently in plaster-walled rooms. Plaster is porous and holds odor compounds deeper than drywall does. Furniture, textiles, and documents sitting in those rooms absorb smoke at a corresponding depth, which means surface cleaning alone rarely works. Effective contents restoration here requires ozone treatment, ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, and controlled drying chambers for textiles rather than a simple wipe-and-return.

Flood events tied to the Allegheny River corridor add another layer. When river levels rise, basements in lower-lying sections of New Kensington can take on water that carries silt and biological material, contaminating stored items beyond simple water damage. Sorting what is restorable from what is not, and documenting everything for insurance, is a critical first step before any cleaning begins.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in New Kensington

The process starts on-site with a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, categorized, and assigned a condition code before anything leaves the property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was taken and in what condition.

Items that can be restored are packed and transported to a climate-controlled facility. Hard goods go through ultrasonic cleaning, which uses high-frequency sound waves in a cleaning solution to pull contaminants out of crevices that hand-cleaning misses. Electronics are assessed for corrosion and function. Soft goods, including upholstered furniture, rugs, and clothing, go through thermal or ozone treatment to address odor at the fiber level rather than masking it.

Items that need extended storage while your home is being repaired stay in a secure, humidity-controlled environment. You receive an itemized list so you know exactly what is in storage. When the structure is ready, contents are returned, placed, and checked against the original inventory.

Katofsky Construction is an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding Water Restoration Technician and Applied Microbial Remediation Technician credentials, which means the same team assessing your structure can evaluate whether contents have been affected by mold growth after prolonged moisture exposure.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier

Contents losses are often the most contested part of a property damage claim. Carriers want documentation showing pre-loss condition, the nature of the damage, and the cost to restore versus replace each item. The inventory and photo record created at the start of every job is built with that process in mind. Katofsky Construction works directly with adjusters, provides line-item documentation, and can separate restorable items from total losses in a format carriers recognize.
