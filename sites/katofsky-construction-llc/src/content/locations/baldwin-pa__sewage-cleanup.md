## Why Baldwin Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Baldwin Borough was built out largely in the mid-20th century, and much of its residential housing stock sits on lots served by original clay-tile or cast-iron sewer laterals that have spent decades shifting with the South Hills terrain. Clay tile joints separate over time, allowing root intrusion and sediment buildup that narrows the pipe and sets up the conditions for a backup during any heavy rainfall event. The Allegheny County combined sewer system, which serves portions of the South Hills corridor, can also surcharge during significant storms, pushing water back up through the lowest fixtures in a home before a homeowner realizes what is happening.

Basements in this part of Allegheny County are common, and a finished basement is often the first casualty. A floor drain that has been quiet for years can become the entry point for several inches of raw sewage in under an hour when a lateral fails or the municipal line surcharges. Older homes with floor drains that lack functioning check valves are especially vulnerable.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Baldwin

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage cleanup, and the process reflects that difference at every step.

**Containment first.** Before extraction begins, affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the home. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the job to capture airborne particulates.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units capable of handling solids. Saturated materials, including drywall, insulation, carpet, and pad, that have contacted Category 3 water are removed. There is no safe way to dry and retain porous materials that have absorbed raw sewage.

**Disinfection.** Hard surfaces, concrete floors, block walls, and structural framing are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for Category 3 contamination. Multiple applications are standard, not optional.

**Drying and documentation.** Once contaminated materials are out and surfaces are disinfected, the drying phase begins using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged throughout so there is a clear record for your insurance carrier.

## Baldwin Insurance and Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is not automatic on a standard homeowner's policy. Many Allegheny County homeowners carry it as a rider, but the language varies by carrier, and some policies exclude damage caused by municipal sewer surcharge versus a private lateral failure. That distinction matters when a claim is filed.
