If you're dealing with failing shingles, sagging decking, or a roof that's simply reached the end of its service life, Katofsky Construction LLC provides roofing installation and replacement work calibrated to the specific demands of this community.

## Why West Mifflin Properties See Roofing Problems

The Pittsburgh metro's climate is genuinely hard on roofing materials. West Mifflin experiences the same freeze-thaw cycling that affects the broader Allegheny County area, but the borough's hillside topography creates additional stress. Water that pools or runs unevenly across a roof deck during a thaw can refreeze at eave lines overnight, forcing ice under shingle edges and lifting the sealant strips that hold them down. On steeper pitches common to the hillside streets throughout the borough, wind-driven rain also finds its way under improperly seated flashing around chimneys and dormers.

The housing stock here skews older. Many homes were built during the mid-20th century boom tied to the steel industry, which means original rooflines may still have board sheathing rather than plywood or OSB decking. When that sheathing has absorbed moisture over multiple seasons, a surface shingle replacement alone won't solve the problem. A thorough inspection of the decking condition before any new material goes on is essential.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in West Mifflin

Every project starts with a full roof assessment, not just a visual scan from the ground. We inspect the decking, evaluate existing flashing at all penetrations, check soffit and ridge ventilation, and document any areas of rot, delamination, or structural deflection before a single shingle comes off.

Once the scope is clear, tear-off and disposal are handled in a single mobilization when possible. Decking repairs or full sheathing replacement happen before any new underlayment is installed. We use synthetic underlayment rather than traditional felt on most West Mifflin installations because it holds up better during the multi-day install windows that Allegheny County weather sometimes forces.

Flashing is replaced, not reused. Reusing old step flashing or chimney flashing around the brick chimneys common to mid-century construction in this area is one of the leading causes of callbacks on roofing work. New flashing, properly integrated with the underlayment and shingle courses, is the only approach that holds over time.

Final inspections include checking ridge cap alignment, verifying all penetrations are sealed, and confirming gutters and drip edge are properly integrated so water sheds cleanly off the new surface.
