## Why Hempfield Township's Housing Stock Makes Lead Paint a Real Concern

Westmoreland County developed steadily through the postwar decades, and Hempfield Township followed that pattern. Much of the township's residential construction happened between the 1940s and the mid-1970s, which means a significant portion of single-family homes, duplexes, and rental properties were built during the era when lead-based paint was standard. The paint is not always visible as a hazard. It may sit under multiple coats of latex, appear intact on a door frame, or hide inside a window channel where friction grinding occurs every time the window opens. That friction is one of the most common sources of lead dust in older homes, and lead dust does not announce itself.

The township's mix of older ranch homes, cape cods, and two-story colonials also means varied construction details. Plaster-and-lath walls, original wood windows, and painted radiators are common in homes built before 1960. Each of those surfaces can harbor lead paint in concentrations that require professional abatement rather than a coat of encapsulant and a handshake.

## Our Lead Paint Abatement Process in Hempfield Township

Every project begins with a thorough assessment. Before any abatement work starts, affected surfaces are identified and documented. If the property has not been tested, we can coordinate lead paint testing so the scope of work is based on actual conditions rather than assumptions.

Once the scope is confirmed, the work area is isolated using poly sheeting and negative air pressure where required. Residents and occupants are kept clear of the containment zone throughout the process. Our crew removes or treats lead-containing materials using methods appropriate to the surface type: full removal, enclosure, or encapsulation depending on what the surface and the project require. Debris is packaged and disposed of according to EPA and Pennsylvania DEP requirements for lead-containing waste.

After abatement, the area is cleaned using HEPA vacuuming and wet-wiping protocols before clearance testing is conducted. Clearance is not a formality. It is the verification step that confirms the space is safe to reoccupy, and it is documented in writing.

Katofsky Construction LLC holds EPA RRP (Renovation, Repair, and Painting) certification, which is required under federal law for work that disturbs lead paint in pre-1978 housing. That certification matters when you are dealing with a rental property, a home sale, or a renovation that involves a contractor pulling permits.
