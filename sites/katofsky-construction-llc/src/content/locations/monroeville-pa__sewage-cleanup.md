## Why Monroeville Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Much of Monroeville's residential development happened between the late 1940s and early 1970s, which means a large share of the borough's sewer laterals are now 50 to 75 years old. Clay tile pipe, the standard of that era, is brittle, prone to joint separation, and highly susceptible to root infiltration from the mature trees that shade so many of the area's streets. When a lateral fails, the backup does not always announce itself with a slow drain first. It can arrive suddenly during a storm, when the municipal system is overwhelmed and pressure reverses through the lowest fixture in the house, typically a basement floor drain, a laundry tub, or a toilet on the lower level.

Allegheny County's combined sewer infrastructure in older corridors compounds the problem. During significant rainfall, stormwater and sanitary sewage share the same pipe, and surges push raw sewage backward into homes before the system can catch up. Monroeville homeowners with finished basements, home offices, or in-law suites below grade are particularly exposed to this kind of loss.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Monroeville

The first priority on arrival is containment. Sewage-affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the home, and the crew establishes negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers before any material is disturbed. Solid waste and contaminated water are extracted using truck-mounted and portable units rated for Category 3 water, the classification for water that contains sewage and presents a direct health risk.

Once extraction is complete, all porous materials that absorbed sewage, including drywall, insulation, carpet, and padding, are removed and bagged for disposal per Allegheny County health guidelines. Hard surfaces, framing, concrete, and any salvageable structure are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution and allowed to dwell before being rinsed and dried. Drying is monitored with calibrated moisture meters and thermal imaging to confirm that no residual moisture remains behind walls or under flooring before the space is closed back up. The IICRC S500 standard guides every step of the water and contamination removal process.

## Monroeville Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is frequently a separate rider on a standard homeowners policy, and whether that rider applies depends on the cause of the loss. A sewer line backup originating from the municipal system is documented differently than a failure of a private lateral or a malfunctioning sewage ejector pump. The crew photographs the source, the affected area, and the materials removed before anything is discarded, giving the adjuster a complete picture of the loss. Katofsky Construction works directly with most major carriers and can communicate scope and documentation in the format adjusters expect, which reduces back-and-forth and keeps the claim moving.

## Local Note

Homes in Monroeville built on the hillside lots east of the commercial corridor along William Penn Highway often have walk-out basements with the floor drain sitting at or near the elevation of the street-level sewer main. That geometry means even a modest pressure surge in the line can push sewage into the basement before a backflow preventer has time to seat, if one is installed at all. Older homes in this area frequently lack backflow prevention entirely. When the crew arrives at one of these properties, the assessment includes checking whether the floor drain has any protection, because that information matters both for the immediate cleanup and for the conversation with the homeowner about preventing the next event.
