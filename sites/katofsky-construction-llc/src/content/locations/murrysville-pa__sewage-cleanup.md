## Why Murrysville Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Murrysville sits in a part of Westmoreland County where residential development has layered decades of construction on top of one another. Older sections of town built in the mid-20th century often have clay tile sewer laterals that have had decades to crack, shift, or collect root intrusion from the mature trees common in established neighborhoods. Newer subdivisions that pushed outward from Route 22 and Route 286 frequently rely on private septic systems, and those systems can be stressed by the heavy clay soils found across much of this region. Clay drains slowly, which means a saturated yard after a sustained rain event can push back against a septic drain field and force effluent toward the house rather than away from it.

The topography here matters too. Murrysville's rolling terrain means some homes sit at low points relative to the street, making them more susceptible to sewer surcharges during heavy storms when the municipal system is overwhelmed. A backup that seems minor at first can involve significant volumes of Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification, by the time a homeowner realizes the floor drain in the basement is no longer draining.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Murrysville

Raw sewage removal is not a job for a shop vac and a mop. The IICRC S520 and related standards classify sewage-affected materials differently than clean water losses, and the remediation process reflects that. Here is what the work looks like on a Murrysville property:

**Containment first.** Before extraction begins, affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the home. Basement areas, which are the most common point of entry in this region, are assessed for spread to adjoining utility rooms or finished spaces.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage and contaminated water are extracted using truck-mounted or portable units capable of handling solids. Saturated porous materials, including carpet, padding, drywall, and insulation that have contacted Category 3 water, are removed. These materials cannot be dried and reused safely.

**Cleaning and disinfection.** Hard surfaces are cleaned, then treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for sewage contamination. This step is not optional or cosmetic. Pathogens including E. coli and other bacteria require targeted treatment, not just drying.

**Drying and documentation.** Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are deployed to bring structural moisture levels back to pre-loss baselines. Moisture readings are logged throughout the process to support insurance documentation.

## Murrysville Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is a common point of confusion for homeowners. Standard homeowner policies frequently exclude sewer backup unless a specific rider was added at purchase. When coverage does apply, the documentation requirements are detailed: photographs of affected materials before removal, moisture logs, and itemized scope of work. ## Local Note

Homes in the older residential sections of Murrysville built before the widespread adoption of PVC plumbing often have cast iron drain lines inside the house connecting to clay tile laterals outside. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out over decades, and the interior surface roughens in a way that catches solids and accelerates blockages. When a sewage backup occurs in one of these homes, the interior drain line condition is worth noting to your plumber before the lateral is assumed to be the sole problem. The cleanup scope and the plumbing repair scope are separate, but understanding the full picture early prevents a second backup from following the first.
