## Why Green Tree Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The Borough of Green Tree was largely built out mid-century, and much of that housing stock sits on slab or shallow basement foundations that put plumbing fixtures close to the main sewer lateral. When the lateral backs up, the lowest drain in the house is the first to overflow, which is almost always a basement floor drain or a laundry tub. In homes with original cast-iron drain lines, root intrusion and joint separation are common contributors, especially along streets where large street trees have had decades to work into the pipe.

Allegheny County's combined sewer system, which carries both stormwater and sanitary waste in a single pipe, is a known factor in communities like Green Tree. A significant rain event can spike flows well beyond what the system was designed to handle, and backflow preventers, where they exist, are only as reliable as their last inspection. Homes without a functioning backflow preventer are particularly vulnerable during the kind of multi-inch rainfall events that move through the Pittsburgh corridor in spring and late summer.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Green Tree

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated category recognized by the IICRC S500 standard. That classification drives every decision on a job site, from the personal protective equipment the crew wears to the disposal pathway for saturated materials.

The process starts with containment. Affected areas are isolated so that cross-contamination doesn't spread to unaffected parts of the home. Solid waste and standing sewage are extracted and disposed of through licensed waste haulers. Porous materials that have absorbed contaminated water, including drywall, insulation, and carpet, are removed rather than dried in place, because drying does not eliminate the pathogens that sewage introduces.

Once the space is cleared down to hard surfaces, the sanitization phase begins. Hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected concrete, framing, and subfloor surfaces. Dwell times matter here: the disinfectant has to remain wet on the surface long enough to be effective, and that step is not rushed. After sanitization, the space is dried with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, and moisture readings are documented before the job is closed out.

## Green Tree Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup coverage is not automatically included in a standard homeowner's policy. Many carriers offer it as a rider, and whether it applies depends on the cause of the backup, whether it originated from the municipal main or from a failure inside the property. Katofsky Construction documents the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials, moisture readings, and a written scope of work that gives an adjuster a clear picture of what was found and what was done. That documentation package is what carriers need to process a claim, and having it organized from the start avoids the back-and-forth that delays settlements.
