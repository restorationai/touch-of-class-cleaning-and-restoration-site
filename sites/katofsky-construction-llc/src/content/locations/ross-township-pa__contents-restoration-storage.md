When that happens, the contents inside the home often matter as much as the structure itself. ## Why Ross Township Homes Create Specific Contents Challenges

Much of Ross Township's residential development took place in the mid-twentieth century, and that era of construction carries predictable patterns. Homes built between the 1940s and 1970s frequently have plaster-and-lath interior walls, cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines, and basements with limited drainage. When a supply line fails or a sump pump loses power during a heavy rain, water moves quickly through those basements and into finished living spaces where furniture, stored boxes, seasonal items, and personal keepsakes absorb moisture fast.

Allegheny County's climate adds pressure to that scenario. Humid summers mean that wet contents left in a closed room can begin developing mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of the initial loss. That window is short, and it is why contents need to be assessed, inventoried, and moved to a controlled environment quickly rather than left in place while structural drying runs its course.

Smoke and soot from kitchen fires or chimney malfunctions create a separate problem in these homes. Older plaster walls and wood trim absorb odor compounds deeply, and contents stored in adjacent rooms pick up that residue even when they were not directly in the fire's path.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Ross Township

The work starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves the property. Every item is photographed, cataloged by room and condition, and assigned a tracking number. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives homeowners a clear record of what was taken and what condition it was in at departure.

From there, contents move to a climate-controlled storage facility where drying, cleaning, and deodorization can happen without competing with the humidity or temperature swings of a partially damaged home. The IICRC-certified team uses ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor, and thermal drying for soft goods and documents where recovery is possible. Electronics and sensitive items are evaluated separately, with realistic assessments of what can and cannot be restored.

Once structural work on the property is complete, contents are returned room by room using the original inventory as a guide. Nothing is returned to a space that has not been cleared for occupancy.

## Insurance Coordination for Ross Township Contents Claims

Contents losses in Ross Township are typically covered under the personal property portion of a homeowner's policy, but the documentation requirements vary by carrier. Katofsky Construction works directly with adjusters, providing the item-level inventory, condition photographs, and restoration cost estimates that carriers need to process a contents claim.
