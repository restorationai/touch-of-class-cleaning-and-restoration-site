## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in West Mifflin

Every call begins with a brief intake to understand the nature of the situation so the crew arrives prepared rather than assessing on the fly. From there, the process follows a consistent sequence:

**Containment first.** Affected areas are isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where the space allows, limiting cross-contamination to unaffected rooms or units.

**Personal protective equipment and regulated waste handling.** Biological material is treated as regulated medical waste from the moment the crew enters. All materials removed from the property are packaged, labeled, and transported by a licensed disposal partner.

**Surface treatment and verification.** After visible material is removed, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. The goal is not just visual cleanliness but a measurable reduction in pathogen load on all contact surfaces.

**Documentation for insurance.** Photographs, material logs, and a written scope of work are prepared for submission to the property owner's carrier. Most homeowner and renter policies include some coverage for biohazard remediation, and having organized documentation from the start makes the claims process significantly smoother.
