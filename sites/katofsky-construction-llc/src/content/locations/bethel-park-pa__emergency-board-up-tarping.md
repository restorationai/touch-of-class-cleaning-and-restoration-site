## Why Bethel Park Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Emergencies

Bethel Park's housing stock skews heavily toward mid-century construction, with a large share of homes built between the 1950s and 1970s. Those older structures often feature original wood-frame windows, single-pane glass, and aging roof decking that can fail faster under storm stress than newer materials. When a tree limb punches through a roof or a fire vents a window, the underlying structure is frequently less forgiving than it looks from the street.

The region's weather adds pressure. Southwestern Pennsylvania sees ice storms in late winter that can crack or collapse sections of older roof framing, and the freeze-thaw cycle through February and March is particularly hard on flashing and ridge caps. A tarp installed improperly, or not at all, lets melt water work down into attic insulation and ceiling joists within hours. In summer, severe thunderstorm lines roll in fast off the Ohio River valley, and the time between a storm warning and actual wind damage is often shorter than a contractor's drive time, which is why having a 24/7 contact already saved in your phone matters.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Bethel Park

When you call, the first thing we establish is the nature of the opening: fire damage board-up, broken window board-up, and roof tarping each require a different approach, and the scope determines what materials and crew we dispatch.

For broken window board-up, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to the window frame, secured in a way that does not further damage the surrounding trim or siding. On older Bethel Park homes with original wood casings, that distinction matters because a poorly fitted board can split aged wood and create a secondary repair.

For roof tarping, we anchor heavy-duty polyethylene tarps using cap nails and wood battens along the ridge and eaves rather than relying on weight alone. A tarp that lifts in a 30-mph gust provides no protection and can cause additional damage when it snaps back. We size the tarp to overlap the ridge by several feet on both sides so water cannot track underneath.

For fire damage board-up, we work around any active investigation or utility lockout, coordinating with the property owner and, when necessary, the municipality before accessing the structure.

## Bethel Park Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover emergency board-up and tarping as a mitigation expense, meaning the cost is typically separate from your main claim deductible. We document every opening with photographs before and after securing it, note dimensions and materials used, and provide a written scope that your adjuster can review directly. That documentation record is especially useful when a loss involves both structural damage and interior water intrusion, since adjusters often want to see that protective measures were taken promptly to limit further damage.

## Local Note

Bethel Park's hillside lots, common throughout the borough's older residential sections, sometimes create access challenges that flat-lot work does not. Roof sections on the downhill side of a split-level or bi-level home can sit significantly higher above grade than the street-facing elevation suggests.
