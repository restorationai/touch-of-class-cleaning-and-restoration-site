## Why Murrysville Carpets Face a Tougher Season Than Most

The Allegheny foothills climate that defines this part of Westmoreland County delivers real extremes: freeze-thaw cycles through January and February, then a humid stretch from June through August that keeps indoor relative humidity elevated. Carpets absorb that ambient moisture, which softens the fibers and makes them a better trap for airborne particles and allergens. Homes built on the hillsides common throughout Murrysville often have basements or crawl spaces that contribute to that humidity load, and wall-to-wall carpet on slab-on-grade or over unconditioned crawl space is especially vulnerable to odor buildup over time.

The region's red clay soil compounds the problem. Clay particles are fine enough to pass through door mats and settle into carpet backing, where standard vacuuming cannot reach them. Over months, that grit acts like sandpaper on fiber roots, dulling the pile and shortening the carpet's useful life. A proper deep clean lifts that clay suspension rather than simply redistributing it.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process, Calibrated to Local Conditions

Because Murrysville homes deal with clay soil and seasonal humidity, the process here requires more than a hot-water extraction pass. The sequence matters:

**Pre-inspection and fiber identification.** Older homes in this part of Westmoreland County sometimes have original wool or wool-blend carpet in formal rooms, or lower-grade olefin in finished basements. Each fiber type responds differently to pH levels and heat, so identifying the material before applying any chemistry prevents damage.

**Dry soil removal.** A commercial-grade counter-rotating brush machine loosens dry clay and grit before any moisture is introduced. Skipping this step turns dry clay into a muddy slurry that is harder to extract.

**pH-balanced pre-treatment and dwell time.** Traffic lanes and stain zones receive targeted pre-treatment matched to the soil type. Clay-based staining responds to a slightly alkaline solution; tannin stains from coffee or tea need an acidic counterpart. Dwell time is not rushed.

**Truck-mounted hot-water extraction.** High-temperature extraction at proper pressure flushes the loosened soil and chemistry out of the pile. The truck-mount unit stays outside, so fumes and noise stay out of the living space.

**Post-treatment rinse and pH correction.** A final rinse pass removes residual cleaning agents. Leaving detergent in the fiber is one of the most common reasons carpets re-soil quickly after cleaning.

**Accelerated drying.** Given Murrysville's tendency toward elevated indoor humidity, air movers are positioned to reduce dry time and discourage any microbial activity under the padding.

Homes in the hillside sections of Murrysville that were built in the 1970s and 1980s frequently have carpet installed over concrete subfloor in finished lower levels, sometimes without a proper vapor barrier beneath the padding.
