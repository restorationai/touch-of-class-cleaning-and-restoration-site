## Why Mount Lebanon's Housing Stock Shapes Fire Damage Recovery

A fire in a 1940s or 1950s brick home in Mount Lebanon behaves differently than one in a newer frame house. Older plaster walls act like a sponge for smoke particulates and firefighting water simultaneously. The lath cavities behind those walls can hold moisture for weeks after suppression, which means a crew that clears visible char and calls it done is leaving hidden damage behind. Knob-and-tube wiring, still present in some of the borough's older properties, is frequently a contributing factor to electrical fires and must be flagged for the licensed electrician before any restoration work touches those walls. The combination of dense, mature construction and older mechanical systems means fire damage here rarely stays confined to one room.

Mount Lebanon's tree canopy, one of the densest in Allegheny County, also plays a role. Mature oaks and maples close to rooflines can carry embers during a structure fire, and the borough's tight lot spacing means adjacent properties sometimes sustain smoke and heat exposure even when the fire itself was contained to a single address.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Mount Lebanon

The first step on any fire-damaged property is a thorough scope of loss, not just the rooms with visible char but every space smoke could have reached through HVAC returns, wall penetrations, and attic bypasses. In Mount Lebanon's older colonials and split-levels, that means pulling registers, inspecting crawl spaces, and probing wall cavities with moisture meters before any demolition begins.

From there, the process moves through controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, structural drying where firefighting water has saturated framing or subfloors, and systematic smoke and soot removal using HEPA filtration and appropriate chemical agents matched to the surface type. Porous masonry, original hardwood, and plaster each respond to different cleaning chemistry. Odor neutralization follows, using thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation depending on the scope and occupancy status of the property. Documentation runs parallel to every phase, with photographs and written records structured to support your insurance claim from the first day on site.
