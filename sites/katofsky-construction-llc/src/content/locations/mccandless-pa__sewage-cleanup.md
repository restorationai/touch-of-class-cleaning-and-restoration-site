## Why McCandless Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Allegheny County's hillside terrain shapes how stormwater and groundwater move through McCandless neighborhoods. During heavy rain events, the regional combined sewer system can become overwhelmed, and that pressure has nowhere to go but back through the lowest fixtures in a home. Homes built in the 1950s through 1970s, which make up a substantial portion of McCandless's residential stock, frequently have clay tile lateral lines that have cracked, shifted, or been infiltrated by tree roots over the decades. Cast-iron interior stacks corrode from the inside out, narrowing the effective diameter of the pipe long before any visible sign appears.

Septic systems are also present in pockets of the township where public sewer access was extended later. An aging septic tank or a saturated drain field after a wet spring can produce an overflow that is every bit as hazardous as a municipal sewer backup. Either way, the material on the floor is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification in the IICRC S500 framework, and it requires a response that goes well beyond mopping and drying.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in McCandless

The first priority on arrival is containment. Sewage-affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination into unaffected rooms, and any HVAC returns in the zone are sealed so that airborne pathogens are not circulated through the home. Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 water, including drywall, carpet, and insulation, are removed and bagged for proper disposal. These materials cannot be dried and reused; the contamination is absorbed into the substrate, not just on the surface.

Once the affected area is cleared, surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants at dwell times appropriate for the pathogen load involved. Structural cavities that may have received splash or seepage are addressed before any drying equipment is placed. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers then run until moisture readings in the subfloor and wall framing return to acceptable baselines. Katofsky Construction LLC holds IICRC certification as a firm, with technicians credentialed in Water Restoration (WRT), so the drying protocol follows the same standard regardless of how severe the initial backup was.

Homeowner's insurance coverage for sewage backup is not automatic. ## Local Note

In McCandless, homes built on sloped lots, which is common throughout the township's hillier sections, sometimes have basement floor drains that sit below the municipal sewer main elevation. These properties are particularly vulnerable to backflow during heavy rain because gravity works against them. Some have backwater valves installed; many do not. When a sewage backup occurs in one of these below-grade spaces, the volume of material that enters before the flow stops can be significantly larger than in a flat-lot home. It is worth asking a licensed plumber, after remediation is complete, whether a backwater valve installation makes sense for the property.
