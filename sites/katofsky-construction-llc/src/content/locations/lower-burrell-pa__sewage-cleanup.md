## Why Lower Burrell Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Lower Burrell's housing stock spans a wide range of eras, and a significant portion of the city's residential neighborhoods includes homes built in the mid-twentieth century when combined sewer systems were standard. Those systems were designed to carry both sanitary waste and stormwater in a single pipe. During heavy rain events, which are common throughout Westmoreland County's spring and fall seasons, the combined volume can exceed pipe capacity and push raw sewage backward through floor drains, toilets, and basement utility sinks.

Older clay tile lateral lines that connect homes to the municipal main are especially vulnerable. Tree roots find the joints in clay pipe and infiltrate over decades, narrowing the passage until even normal household use causes a blockage. Homes on sloped lots, common throughout this part of the Allegheny Valley, can also experience pressure-driven backups when the grade works against drainage during a saturated soil event. If your home was built before the 1970s and has not had a camera inspection of the lateral, the root intrusion question is worth asking before the next heavy rain answers it for you.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lower Burrell

The first step on any sewage call is containment. Standing sewage spreads pathogens through direct contact and through the aerosols that form when water is disturbed, so the affected area is isolated before any material is moved. Personal protective equipment is on before entry.

Once the space is secured, we extract standing water and remove saturated materials that cannot be effectively sanitized, including porous flooring, drywall below the flood line, and insulation that has absorbed sewage. What remains gets treated with EPA-registered disinfectants applied at concentrations and dwell times calibrated for Category 3 contamination, not the lighter-duty products used for clean water losses.

Drying follows sanitization. Sewage events in basement spaces, which are the most common presentation in Lower Burrell's older homes, often involve concrete block foundation walls that wick moisture laterally. We use penetrating moisture meters and thermal imaging to confirm that wall assemblies and subfloor systems are genuinely dry, not just dry at the surface. Documentation of moisture readings at each stage is part of every job file and is formatted to support insurance claims.

As an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding Water Damage Restoration and Applied Microbial Remediation credentials, the work follows established industry standards for contaminated water losses.
