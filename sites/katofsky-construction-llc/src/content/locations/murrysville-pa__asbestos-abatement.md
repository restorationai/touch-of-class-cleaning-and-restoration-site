## Why Murrysville Properties Face Elevated Asbestos Risk

The housing stock across Murrysville skews older than many Pittsburgh suburbs. Homes built between the late 1940s and early 1980s commonly incorporated asbestos in a range of materials: spray-applied popcorn ceilings, vinyl floor tiles and their adhesive backing, pipe insulation around basement mechanicals, roofing felt, and joint compound used throughout drywall finishing. Because Murrysville developed steadily through that entire window, a large share of the community's single-family homes fall squarely in the risk era.

Westmoreland County's climate adds a secondary concern. Freeze-thaw cycles through the winter months stress building envelopes, and repeated moisture intrusion can degrade the binders holding asbestos fibers in place. Pipe insulation that has been wet and dried repeatedly, or ceiling tiles that absorbed attic condensation over decades, may be in a more friable condition than materials in drier climates. Friable material releases fibers far more readily when disturbed, which is why a visual inspection alone is not sufficient before any demolition or remodeling work.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Murrysville

Every project begins with sampling. Suspect materials are collected following established protocols and submitted to an accredited laboratory for analysis. Results typically return within a few business days, and we walk you through exactly what was found and where before any abatement work is scheduled.

Once confirmed, abatement follows a structured containment approach. The work area is isolated with heavy-gauge polyethylene sheeting and placed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents fiber migration into adjacent living spaces. Workers wear full personal protective equipment, and all removed material is wetted during removal to suppress fiber release, then sealed in labeled, leak-proof containers for transport to a licensed disposal facility.

In Pennsylvania, asbestos abatement is regulated through the Department of Environmental Protection. Notifications and proper disposal documentation are required for projects that meet certain thresholds, and Katofsky Construction handles that regulatory paperwork as part of the scope. You will not be left to navigate DEP filing requirements on your own.

After removal, air clearance testing confirms fiber levels have returned to acceptable limits before containment is dismantled and the space is released for renovation work.
