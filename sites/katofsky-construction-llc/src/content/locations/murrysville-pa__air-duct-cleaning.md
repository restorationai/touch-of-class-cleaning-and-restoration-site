## Why Murrysville Properties See Duct Buildup Faster Than You Might Expect

The Allegheny Plateau climate that defines this stretch of western Pennsylvania means high humidity in spring and fall, followed by months of dry forced-air heat in winter. That cycle pulls airborne particles into return vents, deposits them on interior duct surfaces, and then bakes them in place when the heat runs. Homes built in the 1970s and 1980s, which make up a large share of Murrysville's residential stock, commonly used fiberglass duct liner on the interior of sheet-metal trunks. That liner traps particulates more aggressively than bare metal, and once it gets damp from a brief condensation event, it holds odors and provides a surface where biological growth can take hold.

Homes that added central air conditioning after original construction are especially worth watching. Retrofit ductwork often has more joints, more flex connections, and tighter bends than a purpose-designed system, and each of those transitions is a place where dust accumulates and where small gaps can draw in unconditioned air from attic or crawl space.

## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Murrysville

The process starts with a visual inspection of every accessible register, return grille, and main trunk before any equipment goes in. That walk-through matters because Murrysville homes vary considerably in layout, from split-levels with ductwork running through interior partition walls to ranch-style homes where the entire distribution system sits in an unfinished basement. Knowing the layout before the work starts prevents surprises and lets the crew protect finished surfaces in living areas.

Once the system is mapped, high-powered negative-pressure equipment is connected at the main trunk. Every supply and return branch is agitated with flexible brush tools and compressed air whips, driving loosened material toward the collection point rather than back into the living space. After the main cleaning, all registers and grilles are cleaned separately before reinstallation. If air duct sanitization is warranted, an EPA-registered antimicrobial is applied to interior duct surfaces as a final step.

Dryer vent cleaning follows a separate process. The exhaust line from the dryer to the exterior termination cap is cleared with a rotary brush system, and the termination cap is inspected for blockage or damage. In Murrysville homes where the dryer is on an upper floor or in a finished laundry room, the vent run is often longer and has more elbows than a basement installation, which means lint accumulates faster and the cleaning takes more care.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in Murrysville homes from the late 1970s and early 1980s is ductwork that passes through uninsulated garage ceilings on its way to second-floor bedrooms. In winter, that section of duct gets cold enough to cause brief condensation when the furnace first cycles on, and over years that moisture introduces a musty quality to the air in upstairs rooms that homeowners often mistake for a mold problem in the walls. Cleaning and inspecting that specific duct segment, and confirming whether insulation wrap is missing, often resolves the odor complaint without any additional remediation work.
