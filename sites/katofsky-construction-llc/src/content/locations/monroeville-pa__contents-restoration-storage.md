When a burst pipe, basement flood, or kitchen fire leaves your furniture, clothing, documents, and keepsakes soaked or coated in soot, the contents often take the most personal toll. Katofsky Construction LLC responds around the clock to protect, pack out, and restore those items so that the things you cannot replace have the best possible chance of coming back to you.

## Why Monroeville Properties See Contents Damage

The Pittsburgh region's freeze-thaw cycle is relentless from November through March, and Monroeville is no exception. Homes built in the postwar boom years, many of them raised ranches and split-levels with partially finished lower levels, carry plumbing that runs through uninsulated interior wall cavities and crawl spaces. When a pipe lets go in that configuration, water does not stay in one room. It wicks into carpet padding, travels along subfloor seams, and reaches stored boxes, furniture legs, and fabric items before a homeowner even realizes there is a problem.

Fire losses carry their own contents challenge. Smoke from a kitchen fire moves through HVAC ductwork and deposits a fine oily residue on surfaces in rooms that never saw a flame. Upholstered furniture, drapes, and clothing in bedrooms down the hall can absorb odor compounds that will not respond to ordinary cleaning. The faster those items are removed from the affected environment, the better the restoration outcome.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Monroeville

The work begins with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, logged by category, and assessed for restorability before anything is packed. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what condition.

Items that can be restored are transported to a climate-controlled facility for treatment. Depending on the loss type, that can mean ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-saturated soft goods, freeze-drying for wet documents and photographs, or specialized textile cleaning for rugs and upholstered pieces. Our IICRC-certified team applies the appropriate method to each item category rather than running everything through the same process.

Storage continues in the controlled environment until your home is ready to receive contents back. Re-delivery is coordinated with your reconstruction timeline so that restored items are not sitting in a damaged space and accumulating secondary exposure.

Most homeowner policies in Pennsylvania cover contents losses tied to a sudden and accidental event, and the inventory we create at pack-out is structured to support that claim. ## Local Note

Monroeville's postwar housing stock includes a significant number of homes with original hardwood floors and solid-wood furniture, both of which respond differently to smoke and water than the composite and laminate materials common in newer construction. Solid wood swells, warps, and checks when it stays wet, but it also responds well to controlled drying and refinishing if the process starts quickly. Smoke residue on original hardwood can be cleaned without stripping the finish if the oily layer has not had time to polymerize. The window for saving that kind of material is measured in days, not weeks, which is why early pack-out matters as much as the cleaning methods applied later.
