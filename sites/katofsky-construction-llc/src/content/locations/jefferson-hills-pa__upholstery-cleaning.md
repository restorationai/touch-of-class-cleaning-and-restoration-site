## Why Jefferson Hills Furniture Needs More Than Surface Cleaning

Jefferson Hills sits in Allegheny County's South Hills region, an area built out largely during the 1960s-1980s suburban boom, with a mix of split-level and ranch-style homes on sloped, wooded lots. Central air in these houses tends to run hard through Pennsylvania's humid summer stretch, and that humidity settles into upholstery fibers along with dust, pet dander, and cooking odors that get trapped in a home closed up for air conditioning. In winter, the opposite problem shows up: dry forced-air heat pulls moisture out of fabric and leather, making both more prone to cracking or matting if they're not cleaned and conditioned on a regular schedule. Hillside lots also mean steeper driveways and walkways, which brings more outdoor grit into living rooms than you'd see on flatter ground.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start every job with a fiber and dye test on a hidden section of the piece, whether it's a cotton-blend sectional or a leather recliner, because not every fabric responds the same way to moisture or cleaning agents. From there we vacuum with an upholstery attachment to lift dry soil before it turns to mud under water, apply a pretreatment matched to the fiber type, and use low-moisture hot water extraction for fabric pieces so the frame and padding aren't left damp for days. Leather gets a different approach entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner followed by a conditioner that restores flexibility without leaving a greasy film. We finish by grooming the fabric nap and setting fans or air movers when a piece needs a faster dry time, which matters more in Jefferson Hills homes during the humid summer months when ambient moisture in the air slows natural drying.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Upholstery

Steam and hot water extraction remain the standard for most fabric upholstery because they lift embedded soil and allergens that a dry-cleaning powder just pushes around. For delicate or vintage fabrics, we scale back to a low-moisture method that avoids over-wetting the cushion cores, since a soaked cushion can take two to three days to fully dry in a closed-up house and risks mildew odor if it doesn't. Leather is treated as its own category entirely, cleaned and conditioned rather than steamed, because heat and excess water can dry out and crack natural hides over time. We bring the same IICRC-informed cleaning standards we use on water and mold jobs to upholstery work, which means moisture is measured, not guessed at, before we call a piece finished.

## Local note

Many Jefferson Hills properties sit on hillside lots with steep driveways, which means residents track more de-icing salt, sand, and gravel into upholstered furniture during Pennsylvania's snow season than homes in flatter parts of the county. Left in fabric fibers, salt residue draws moisture out of the air and re-soils a cushion within weeks of cleaning if it isn't fully broken down first. We adjust our pretreatment specifically for that salt buildup before extraction rather than treating every Jefferson Hills job the same way we would a summer soil job.
