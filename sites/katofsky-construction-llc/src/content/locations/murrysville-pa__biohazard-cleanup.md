## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Murrysville

Every situation is different, and the process is calibrated to what the property actually needs, not a fixed checklist. The general sequence follows established protocols for infectious material cleanup:

**Assessment and containment** come first. Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination with the rest of the structure. In older Murrysville homes where HVAC ductwork may run through finished spaces adjacent to the affected area, containment planning accounts for air movement.

**Removal of affected materials** is handled by technicians using appropriate personal protective equipment. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are bagged, labeled, and transported for disposal in compliance with Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection guidelines for regulated medical and biological waste.

**Cleaning and treatment** of remaining surfaces uses EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological materials involved. Hard, non-porous surfaces respond well to this step; porous substrates like concrete or wood often require removal rather than treatment alone.

**Verification** closes the process. The goal is a space that is safe for re-occupancy, with documentation that can be provided to property owners, insurers, or estate representatives as needed.
