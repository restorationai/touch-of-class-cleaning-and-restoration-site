North Huntingdon sits in the Westmoreland County hills where wet winters, clay-heavy soil, and a housing stock that ranges from mid-century ranch homes to newer subdivisions create conditions where water, fire, and mold losses can hit belongings hard and fast. When a burst pipe soaks a finished basement or smoke from a kitchen fire works its way into upholstered furniture and clothing, the contents of your home often need as much attention as the structure itself. Katofsky Construction LLC handles that side of the loss: the furniture, the keepsakes, the electronics, and everything in between.

## Why North Huntingdon Homes See Significant Contents Damage

Westmoreland County's freeze-thaw cycle is hard on plumbing. Temperatures routinely drop below freezing through January and February, and older supply lines in crawl spaces or exterior walls can fail without warning. When that happens in a home with a finished lower level, water migrates quickly into carpet padding, under laminate flooring, and into the base of wood furniture before the homeowner even realizes there is a problem.

Smoke damage follows a different pattern. North Huntingdon's mix of ranch-style homes and split-levels from the 1960s and 1970s often have open floor plans that allow smoke and soot to travel across large areas in minutes. Synthetic materials in modern furnishings produce oily, acidic soot that bonds to fabric, wood grain, and electronics surfaces. The longer that residue sits, the deeper it penetrates and the harder it is to reverse.

Mold is the third driver. Clay soils in this part of Westmoreland County drain slowly, and basements in homes built before modern waterproofing standards can see recurring moisture intrusion. Soft goods stored in those spaces, including seasonal clothing, holiday decorations, and upholstered pieces, are often the first things colonized.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in North Huntingdon

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was taken and what its pre-loss condition was.

Items that can be safely cleaned on-site are handled there. Furniture, hard goods, and non-porous surfaces are cleaned using methods matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for electronics and small valuables, dry-sponge and chemical sponge techniques for soot-affected surfaces, and controlled drying for water-saturated wood pieces.

Contents that need deeper treatment are packed out to a climate-controlled storage facility. Pack-out is especially useful in North Huntingdon when a loss affects multiple rooms and the structural drying process requires the space to be cleared. Items are stored in a secure, temperature-regulated environment and returned once the home is ready.

Ozone and hydroxyl treatment addresses odor in soft goods and upholstery when smoke or mold has embedded itself in fibers. These are not masking agents; they break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level. The IICRC-certified team applies the appropriate method based on material type and the nature of the loss.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier in North Huntingdon

Contents losses are covered under most standard homeowners policies, but the documentation requirements can be detailed. Carriers typically want itemized inventories with pre-loss values, photographs of damage, and a clear record of what was restored versus what was deemed a total loss.

Katofsky Construction works directly with adjusters and provides the documentation package they need. For North Huntingdon homeowners, that often means coordinating with carriers who handle a high volume of Westmoreland County claims and have specific expectations around line-item contents valuation. Having a structured inventory from the start shortens the back-and-forth and keeps the claim moving.
