## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Green Tree

Every situation is different, but the process follows a consistent, methodical sequence designed to protect both the occupants and the crew.

First, the affected area is assessed and contained. Personal protective equipment, negative air pressure where appropriate, and physical barriers prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the home or building. Then biological material is carefully removed following EPA guidelines for infectious waste handling. All material is packaged and transported for proper disposal through licensed channels; nothing is left in a residential trash stream.

After removal, surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants appropriate to the specific pathogens of concern. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated are removed and disposed of properly. A final verification step confirms that affected surfaces meet accepted remediation standards before containment is lifted. Our team holds IICRC certification, which means the drying and structural assessment protocols we apply are grounded in the same standards used across professional restoration work.
