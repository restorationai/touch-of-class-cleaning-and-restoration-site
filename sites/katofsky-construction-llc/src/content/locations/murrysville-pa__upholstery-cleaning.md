## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Murrysville

Every job starts with a fabric identification step, because the cleaning method that works on a synthetic microfiber sectional will damage a vintage wool armchair. We check manufacturer codes, test a small hidden area for colorfastness, and adjust pH levels in our cleaning solution accordingly before touching the main surface.

For fabric upholstery, we use hot-water extraction calibrated to the material weight and pile depth. Heavier fabrics common in traditional furniture get slower, lower-pressure passes to avoid over-wetting, which matters in Murrysville's humid conditions because over-saturated cushions dry slowly and can develop mildew odor before the interior foam fully releases moisture. We follow extraction with a grooming pass to reset the nap and a drying step using directed airflow.

For leather upholstery, the process shifts entirely. We use pH-balanced leather cleaners applied by hand, followed by a conditioner appropriate to the leather type, whether full-grain, top-grain, or bonded. No steam, no high-pressure spray. The goal is to clean without stripping the protective finish that keeps the leather flexible through Murrysville's seasonal temperature swings.

The IICRC-certified team handles both fabric and leather in a single visit when a home has mixed furniture types, so you are not scheduling two separate appointments.
