## Why Mount Lebanon Homes See Upholstery Soil Build-Up

The Allegheny County climate is hard on fabric. Humid summers cause fibers to swell and trap particulates deeper into the weave; cold, dry winters mean static cling pulls pet hair and dust into cushion fabric and keeps it there. Many Mount Lebanon homes were built between the 1920s and 1960s, and they tend to have less aggressive HVAC filtration than newer construction. That means airborne particles, cooking oils, and fine particulates cycle through living spaces and settle into upholstery at a higher rate than you might expect.

Homes with original hardwood floors and plaster walls also generate a fine plaster dust that is nearly invisible but accumulates in fabric over time, dulling colors and contributing to that flat, tired look upholstered pieces develop. Pets compound the problem quickly, and the combination of animal dander, tracked-in soil from the area's clay-heavy ground, and seasonal pollen creates a layered soil profile that requires more than a steam pass to fully extract.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process in Mount Lebanon

Every piece starts with a fiber and construction assessment before any moisture or cleaning agent touches the fabric. Upholstery cleaning is not one-size-fits-all: a tightly woven wool blend from a mid-century parlor chair requires a different approach than a modern microfiber sectional or a full-grain leather sofa. The wrong pH, temperature, or agitation method can shrink, bleed, or distort fabric permanently.

Once the fabric type is confirmed, we pre-treat soiled areas with agents matched to the fiber chemistry, then use low-moisture hot-water extraction calibrated to the material. For delicate or antique pieces common in older Mount Lebanon homes, we use dry-compound or encapsulation methods that clean without saturating the fill or backing. Leather upholstery gets a separate protocol: pH-balanced cleaning followed by a conditioner application that restores suppleness without leaving a greasy film. Throughout the process, airflow is managed to bring pieces to a safe dry state before we leave.

## Equipment and Methods for Fabric and Leather Upholstery

The distinction between fabric and leather upholstery cleaning matters more than most homeowners realize. Fabric pieces, particularly those with down or foam fill, need controlled moisture levels to prevent mold colonization inside the cushion, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in humid conditions. Our truck-mounted extraction equipment generates consistent heat and suction, but for upholstery we dial extraction pressure down significantly from what we would use on carpet, protecting seams and backing material.

For leather, moisture is the enemy in a different way: too much water lifts the finish and can cause cracking as the piece dries. We use purpose-formulated leather cleaners and work in sections, keeping the surface uniformly conditioned. Aniline and semi-aniline leathers, which absorb dye and finish differently than protected leather, get a gentler pass and a longer conditioning phase.
