## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy explains how Katofsky Construction LLC collects, uses, and protects information submitted through katofskyconstruction.com. It applies to visitors who contact us through the site, request an estimate, or otherwise share personal information with us. If you have questions at any point, reach us directly at michael@katofskyconstruction.com or (412) 304-9284.

## Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact or estimate-request form, we receive the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or project. We also receive basic, anonymized analytics data through standard website tools, such as which pages were visited and what device or browser was used. This analytics data does not identify you personally.

We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral ad networks, or third-party data brokers.

## How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used for one purpose: to help you. That means responding to your inquiry, scheduling an on-site estimate, documenting loss details for insurance coordination, and communicating about the scope and progress of your project. We do not use your contact information for marketing campaigns, and we do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

## Sharing With Third Parties

We share your information only when you authorize it or when it is necessary to complete the work you hired us for. The most common example is coordinating with your insurance carrier: if you ask us to communicate with your adjuster or submit documentation on your behalf, we will do so using only the information relevant to your claim. We do not share your data with any other outside party without your explicit consent.

## Data Retention

Project records, including contact details and job documentation, are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill our legal and contractual obligations under Pennsylvania law. If you would like your information removed from our records and there is no active legal or insurance matter requiring its retention, contact us and we will honor that request promptly.

## Your Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we hold about you, request its deletion, and opt out of any sale of that information. We do not sell personal information, so the opt-out right is not applicable here, but all other CCPA rights apply. Residents of other states with similar consumer privacy laws may contact us with the same requests and we will respond in good faith.

Because Katofsky Construction LLC operates exclusively in the Pittsburgh, PA area and does not target residents of the European Union, GDPR is unlikely to apply. If you believe it does apply to your situation, contact us and we will work with you accordingly.
