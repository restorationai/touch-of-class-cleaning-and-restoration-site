## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Katofsky Construction LLC is committed to making katofskyconstruction.com usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification. We believe access to information about restoration and construction services should not depend on a visitor's physical or cognitive abilities.

This site is built with the goal of conforming to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, the current recognized standard for web accessibility. Those guidelines address how content is perceived, operated, navigated, and understood across a wide range of devices and assistive tools.

## What We Have Done to Support Accessible Use

The site is structured with semantic HTML so that screen readers can interpret headings, links, and form fields in a logical order. Images include descriptive alternative text. Color contrast ratios are chosen to remain readable for visitors with low vision or color-vision differences. Interactive elements, including contact forms and navigation menus, are designed to be reachable and operable by keyboard alone, without requiring a mouse.

We review the site periodically and address accessibility issues as they are identified. Because web accessibility is an ongoing practice rather than a one-time checklist, we expect to continue improving over time.

## Known Limitations

We are a small contracting business, not a dedicated web development team. Some third-party content or embedded tools integrated into this site may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA on their own. Where we identify those gaps, we work to provide accessible alternatives or workarounds. If you encounter a barrier that prevents you from completing a task on this site, please reach out directly and we will assist you through another channel.

## How to Report an Accessibility Issue

If you find any part of this site difficult to use, we want to know. Please contact us with a description of the page or feature you were trying to access and what happened. We will review the issue and work toward a resolution within a reasonable timeframe, typically within five to ten business days for straightforward problems.

## Alternative Ways to Reach Us

If a specific page or form is not working for you, calling or emailing directly is always an option. We are happy to answer questions about our services, schedule an estimate, or walk through any information on the site by phone. No one should have to navigate a technical barrier to get help after a loss or damage event.
