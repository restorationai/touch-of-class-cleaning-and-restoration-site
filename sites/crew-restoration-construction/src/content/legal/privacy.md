## What This Policy Covers

This privacy policy explains how Crew Restoration & Construction collects, uses, and protects information you share when you visit this website or contact us about a restoration or construction project. We've written it in plain language because you shouldn't need a law degree to understand what happens with your information.

This policy applies to residents of South Dakota and any other state whose residents may visit this site, including California residents covered under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). While GDPR applies primarily to residents of the European Union — an audience we don't actively serve — we've adopted its core principles of transparency and data minimization as good practice regardless.

## Information We Collect

When you fill out a contact or estimate request form on this site, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or project. We may also collect basic, anonymized analytics data — page views, general geographic region, device type, and referral source — through standard web analytics tools. This analytics data is not linked to any individual and helps us understand how visitors find and use the site.

We do not use tracking pixels, behavioral advertising networks, or third-party data brokers. We do not collect payment card information through this website.

## How We Use Your Information

Information you submit is used for one purpose: to help you. That means responding to your inquiry, scheduling an on-site estimate, and — if you authorize us to work with your insurance carrier — coordinating documentation, scope-of-loss reports, and billing on your behalf. We will not contact your insurer without your explicit direction.

We may use your email or phone number to follow up on an open estimate or to send a single scheduling confirmation. We do not send marketing emails or add you to any mailing list without a separate, clear opt-in.

## How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only parties who may receive your information are your insurance carrier or adjuster — and only when you've asked us to coordinate with them — and any subcontractors directly involved in completing your approved scope of work, who are bound by confidentiality expectations consistent with this policy.

## Data Retention

Project records, including contact information and documentation submitted for insurance purposes, are retained for a minimum of five years to satisfy standard contractor recordkeeping requirements under South Dakota regulations. Analytics data is aggregated and not tied to individuals.

## Your Rights

You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion of your data where no legal retention obligation applies. California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what categories of data we collect and to opt out of any sale of personal information — though we do not sell data. To exercise any of these rights, reach out directly.
