## Privacy Policy

**Effective date: June 2025**

When you fill out a contact form, request an estimate, or call Crew Restoration & Construction, you share information with us — your name, phone number, email address, property address, and a description of the damage or project. We also collect basic, anonymized analytics data (pages visited, general geographic region, browser type) through standard website tools. We do not use tracking pixels, sell your data, or share it with advertisers.

### How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site estimate, document your loss for insurance purposes, and coordinate the scope of work. If your project involves an insurance claim, we will share relevant documentation — photographs, moisture readings, scope reports — with your carrier, but only with your explicit authorization. We do not contact your insurer on your behalf without your knowledge.

### Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. The only third parties who may receive your data are your insurance company (with your authorization) and any software tools we use to manage scheduling or project documentation, which are bound by their own data-protection agreements.

### Retention

We retain contact and project records for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the services you requested and to comply with applicable South Dakota contractor recordkeeping requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us at the address below, subject to any legal retention obligations.

### Your Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we hold, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of any sale of your data (we do not sell data). Residents of other states with similar privacy statutes may have comparable rights. To exercise any of these rights, reach out to us directly.

### Privacy Questions

Email **estimates@crewthatcares.com** or call **(605) 965-2727**. Our mailing address is 3503 South Norton Avenue, Sioux Falls, SD 57110.
