## Restoration emergencies common in Lennox

Lennox sits in the heart of Lincoln County, where the same flat, fertile ground that makes the surrounding farmland productive also creates drainage challenges for residential properties. When snowmelt or a heavy late-spring rain saturates the soil, water has nowhere to go quickly — and it finds its way into crawl spaces, basement slab edges, and window wells. The housing stock here reflects the town's steady residential growth: a mix of older homes near Downtown Lennox with aging plumbing and original sump systems, and newer subdivisions where grading sometimes hasn't fully settled. Both have their vulnerabilities. Older homes can carry galvanized supply lines that corrode from the inside out; newer builds sometimes have vapor barriers or drainage tile that wasn't installed to handle a 100-year rain event. South Dakota's freeze-thaw cycle compounds the problem — a pipe that survives January can split in the first hard freeze of November when it's least expected. Smoke and fire calls follow a similar seasonal pattern, with heating equipment fires peaking in late fall when furnaces and wood stoves are fired up for the first time after months of sitting idle.
