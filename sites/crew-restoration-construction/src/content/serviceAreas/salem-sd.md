## Services we provide in Salem

### Water damage mitigation and drying

Water damage in Salem often starts small — a slow supply line leak behind a kitchen cabinet, a failed sump pump during a spring storm — and grows into a structural problem once moisture reaches subfloor OSB or wall cavity insulation. ## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Salem

Salem's housing runs older than the regional average — turn-of-the-century and pre-war homes near the courthouse square, then a mid-century layer, with farm properties on the edges.
