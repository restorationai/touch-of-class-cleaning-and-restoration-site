## What to Look for When Choosing a Water Damage Company in Sioux Falls

The right restoration company for a Sioux Falls water loss has three things in place before they arrive: certification, scope, and insurance coordination capability.

**IICRC certification** is the baseline. The [IICRC S500](https://www.iicrc.org) sets the drying standard: specific moisture content targets for different materials, equipment placement protocols, and daily monitoring logs. Without it, you have no guarantee the structure is actually dry before walls close, which is how hidden mold problems start. See [How Quickly Does Mold Grow After Water Damage?](/blog/mold-after-water-damage/) for why this matters.

**Full-scope capability** means the company can handle extraction, drying, demolition of unsalvageable materials, mold remediation if needed, and reconstruction. If your restoration company hands you off to a separate contractor for repairs, you are managing two schedules, two scopes of work, and two sets of communication. That adds weeks to a loss that should take days to stabilize.

**Insurance coordination** is often the part homeowners underestimate. A good restoration company documents the loss in a format adjusters accept: moisture readings with timestamps, equipment logs, photo documentation, and a written scope of work. Without that paper trail, claim disputes become common. The [EPA's guidance on mold and moisture](https://www.epa.gov/mold) also reinforces why proper documentation of the drying process protects you long-term.
