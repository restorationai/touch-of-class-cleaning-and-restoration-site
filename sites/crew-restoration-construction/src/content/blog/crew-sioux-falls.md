Crew Restoration & Construction handles both the mitigation side (stopping the damage and drying things out) and the rebuild side (putting the structure back together). That full-service scope matters because most homeowners deal with a restoration company AND a general contractor after a major loss. Having one team that covers both reduces the gap where things fall through.

## What Happens When You Call?

When you call (605) 965-2727, expect an initial triage conversation. Have the following ready:

1. **Your address and zip code** so they can confirm you are in the service area and route the right crew.
2. **A brief description of the loss** - water, fire, storm, mold, or something else. Category matters: a clean water pipe burst (Category 1) is handled differently than a sewage backup (Category 3).
3. **Whether the source is controlled** - if a pipe is still flowing or a roof is still open to the weather, that affects what crew and equipment come first.
4. **Your insurance carrier and claim number** if you have already filed. If you have not filed yet, that is fine - the documentation process starts on arrival regardless.

For insurance claims, the crew documents moisture readings, affected materials, and the scope of work in a format that aligns with what adjusters need.
