When something goes wrong with a property — a burst pipe at 2 a.m., a kitchen fire, a basement full of standing water after a South Dakota storm — the last thing you want is to manage three different contractors. Crew Restoration & Construction handles the full arc of damage recovery: emergency mitigation, structural drying, demolition of unsalvageable material, and the rebuild that follows. We work with homeowners, commercial property managers, landlords, and multi-family operators across the Sioux Falls area.

## Emergency restoration services

Some damage doesn't wait for business hours, and the first 24–48 hours after a loss are often the ones that determine how much of a structure can be saved. **Water damage restoration** stops active intrusion, extracts standing water, and begins the drying process before secondary damage — warped subfloors, saturated wall cavities, the early stages of mold colonization — can take hold. **Fire and smoke damage restoration** addresses more than char: smoke residue penetrates drywall, ductwork, and soft goods, leaving an odor and a chemical film that standard cleaning won't touch. **Storm damage restoration** is particularly relevant across the Great Plains, where hail, wind, and rapid freeze-thaw cycles can compromise roofing, siding, and building envelopes in a single event. **Biohazard cleanup** covers situations — sewage backups, trauma scenes, hazardous spills — where standard cleaning crews aren't equipped to work safely. Each of these services has its own dedicated page with process details, timelines, and what to expect on-site.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation gets the property stable; reconstruction gets it livable again. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure has been cleared, the rebuild phase begins — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because Crew Restoration & Construction handles both sides of the process, there's no handoff gap where a new contractor has to re-assess what the mitigation crew already documented. We also take on renovation and general contracting projects that aren't damage-related, so if a loss reveals underlying issues worth addressing — outdated electrical, aging plumbing, a layout that never worked — those can be folded into the same scope of work.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an emergency, and not every problem fits a standard category. **Mold inspection and remediation** is often needed after a slow leak goes undetected — the kind that shows up as a musty smell in a finished basement or discoloration behind drywall. **Odor removal** uses targeted treatment rather than masking agents, addressing the source rather than covering it. **Board-up and tarping services** secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or vandalism damage, preventing weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while the full restoration plan is developed.

The advantage of working with a single company across mitigation and reconstruction is a cleaner paper trail, consistent documentation for your insurance claim, and one point of contact from the first call to the final walkthrough.
