When water is soaking through your drywall, smoke smell has settled into every room, or you've spotted black growth behind a wall, the last thing you need is a contractor who can't pick up the phone. Crew Restoration & Construction handles water damage, fire and smoke damage, mold remediation, storm damage, biohazard cleanup, and full reconstruction — all under one roof, right here in Sioux Falls.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether a pipe burst at 2 a.m. or a slow leak finally gave out under your kitchen sink, standing water can reach the subfloor and wall cavities faster than most people expect; we extract, dry, and document everything.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces long after the flames are out, so cleanup and deodorization need to start quickly — we assess structural damage, remove charred material, and treat affected areas so the odor doesn't come back.

**Mold remediation** — In South Dakota's humid summers, mold can begin colonizing a wet surface within 24 to 48 hours; we locate the moisture source, contain the affected area, and remove mold-damaged material safely.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Sioux Falls sees its share of hail, high winds, and heavy snow loads; when a storm tears through a roof or pushes water into a basement, we handle both the immediate mitigation and the rebuild so you're not coordinating two separate contractors.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Restoration work lives or dies on documentation. Insurance adjusters need moisture readings, photo logs, and scope-of-loss reports — not a contractor's word. Every job we run is documented from the first hour: moisture mapping, equipment placement records, and daily drying logs that give your insurance carrier what it needs to process the claim. We bill most major carriers directly, which means you're not writing a large check and waiting for reimbursement.

Property managers dealing with multi-unit buildings know how quickly one flooded unit can affect the units below it. We're experienced working in occupied buildings and coordinating access with tenants, not just single-family homes.

The work is done by our own crews — not day-labor subcontractors pulled in when volume spikes. That matters when someone needs to answer a question about what happened behind your wall three weeks after the job closed.
