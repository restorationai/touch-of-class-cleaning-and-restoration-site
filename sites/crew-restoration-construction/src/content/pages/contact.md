## For urgent situations

Water doesn't wait, and neither should you. The faster extraction and drying begins, the less structural damage and secondary mold growth you'll face — mold can begin colonizing wet materials in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Call **(605) 965-2727** during business hours and we'll get you scheduled as quickly as possible, prioritizing active losses ahead of routine work.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If you're planning ahead — a post-storm inspection, a reconstruction bid, or questions about coverage before you file a claim — email us at **estimates@crewthatcares.com**. Include your address, a brief description of the damage, and any photos you have. We typically respond to estimate requests within one business day. For faster back-and-forth, a phone call during business hours is still the most direct route.
