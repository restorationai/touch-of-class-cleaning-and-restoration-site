Crew Restoration & Construction serves homeowners and property managers across a wide stretch of southeastern South Dakota and into the surrounding region. When you call, our team assesses your location and schedules the right crew for the job — call us at (605) 965-2727 during business hours to confirm availability and get on the schedule fast. The layout below links to every individual service-area page, so you can jump straight to your city.

## Where we work

Based in Sioux Falls, our crews work outward across the communities that make up the greater Sioux Falls metro and the rural counties surrounding it. That means the established neighborhoods of central Sioux Falls, the fast-growing suburbs to the north and west, and the smaller towns that dot the I-29 and I-90 corridors. South Dakota's climate — hard freezes, spring snowmelt, and the occasional severe thunderstorm — means water intrusion, ice damming, and storm damage are year-round realities for properties throughout the region, and we've worked in most of them.

## Coverage by distance

The communities closest to Sioux Falls — places like Brandon, Harrisburg, Tea, and Renner — are our most frequent service calls. Crews can reach these areas quickly, which matters when a burst pipe is still running or smoke odor is setting into drywall.

Further out, we regularly work in cities like Brookings to the north, Yankton to the south, and Mitchell to the west. Travel time to these areas is longer, and we'll be straightforward with you about when a crew can realistically arrive. For jobs in these communities, we prioritize getting the right equipment on-site over rushing a partial response.

At the edge of our coverage — think the Minnesota border communities or the more remote stretches of the James River Valley — dispatch depends on the scope of the loss and crew availability. Large commercial losses or major structural damage often justify the drive; smaller single-room jobs may be better handled by a local contractor closer to you.

Traffic on I-229 during peak hours and winter road conditions on rural county routes can affect arrival windows. We'll give you an honest estimate when you call, not a number we can't back up.

## Don't see your city?

The service-area pages on this site aren't an exhaustive list of everywhere we'll go — they're a starting point. If your town isn't listed, call (605) 965-2727 anyway. For significant water, fire, or storm losses, we regularly dispatch to communities outside our standard coverage map. A two-minute phone call is the fastest way to find out.
