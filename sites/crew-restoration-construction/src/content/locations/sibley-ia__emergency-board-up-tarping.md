## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Sibley

When a call comes in from a Sibley address, the first thing we assess is the type and scale of the breach. Fire damage board-up, broken window board-up, and roof tarping each follow a different sequence, and the local building stock shapes every decision.

For **fire damage board-up**, we start with a perimeter walk to identify structural compromises before anyone enters. In older Downtown Sibley commercial buildings, party walls and shared facades are common — we document adjacent exposures before installing plywood to avoid inadvertently trapping heat or moisture between structures.

For **roof tarping**, we use 6-mil or heavier reinforced poly tarps secured with batten boards and mechanical fasteners rather than sandbags alone — sandbags shift in the persistent northwest Iowa wind. The tarp is sized to extend at least three feet past the damaged area on all sides and is lapped over the ridge when the breach is on a slope, directing water away from the opening rather than pooling at it.

For **broken window board-up**, we cut OSB to fit the rough opening, use construction adhesive at the perimeter, and fasten with corrosion-resistant screws into the surrounding framing — not just the window casing, which on pre-1960 homes is often dried out and won't hold a fastener under wind load.

Every job includes a written damage inventory with timestamped photographs, formatted to support an insurance claim from the first page.
