## Why Adrian Properties See Siding and Gutter Issues

Adrian sits at roughly 1,600 feet of elevation on open prairie — there's very little tree cover or topography to break the wind coming across the Coteau des Prairies. That persistent northwest wind drives rain and snow horizontally into west- and north-facing siding in a way that coastal or wooded markets simply don't experience. Vinyl and steel siding panels that were installed without adequate back-flashing or that have lost their original caulk seal become entry points for wind-driven moisture.

Gutters face a related problem. The same open exposure that accelerates siding wear also fills gutters with wind-deposited debris — grit, crop dust, and the occasional shingle granule — faster than you'd expect for a town this size. When gutters clog and overflow during a heavy spring melt, the water doesn't have far to travel before it's pooling against a foundation or wicking behind the bottom course of siding. ## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Adrian

Every project starts with a full exterior walk — not a quick glance from the driveway.
