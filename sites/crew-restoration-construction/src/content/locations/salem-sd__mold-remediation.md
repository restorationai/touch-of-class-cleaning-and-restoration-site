## Why Salem Properties See Mold Issues

The I-90 corridor west of Sioux Falls gets significant snowmelt pressure every spring. Salem's older housing stock — many homes near the McCook County Courthouse date to the early and mid-20th century — was built with minimal vapor barriers and uninsulated rim joists. That combination means moisture migrates inward rather than draining away. Basement walls made of poured concrete or stone rubble absorb ground saturation and wick it into framing lumber, paper-faced insulation, and drywall. By the time a homeowner notices a musty odor in a first-floor closet, the mold colony behind the wall may already be several weeks old.

Agricultural dust is another local factor that doesn't get enough attention. Grain and soil particulates common around the McCook County Fairgrounds area settle into HVAC systems and provide organic material that feeds mold growth when humidity spikes. If your air handler hasn't been inspected after a wet season, it can distribute spores throughout the house even after surface mold has been cleaned.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Salem

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. We identify the moisture source first — because mold removed without fixing the underlying water intrusion will return within weeks. Once the source is addressed or documented for repair, we establish negative-air containment barriers using 6-mil poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing — are removed and bagged following established remediation protocols. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Structural cavities are dried to target moisture content before any reconstruction begins. We don't close up walls until moisture readings confirm the assembly is within safe parameters, because sealing damp framing is one of the most common reasons mold problems recur.

For homes with finished basements or crawl spaces — common in Salem's older neighborhoods — we pay particular attention to subfloor assemblies and band joists, which are frequently the first places mold establishes a foothold after a leak.

## Salem Insurance Coordination

Mold claims in South Dakota can be complicated by policy language that distinguishes between "sudden and accidental" water damage and long-term seepage. If your mold problem traces back to a burst pipe or appliance failure, coverage is more likely than if it developed from years of slow infiltration. We document the loss thoroughly — moisture readings, photographs of affected assemblies, written scope of work — and provide the paperwork your adjuster needs to process the claim. We bill carriers directly when the policy allows it, reducing the out-of-pocket burden during what is already a stressful situation.
