## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Crooks

When we arrive on site, the first step is a rapid exterior walk to catalog every opening — not just the obvious ones. Hail damage in particular tends to create small punctures in valleys and around chimney flashings that are easy to miss in low light. We photograph each opening before work begins, which creates the documentation your insurance adjuster will need.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure and secure them with weighted lumber battens rather than staples alone. On a steep-pitch roof common in older West Crooks homes, that means running battens down both sides of the tarp and anchoring them to the fascia rather than relying on the shingles themselves, which are often fragile after a hail event.

For window and door board-up, we cut plywood to fit flush against the exterior frame and fasten it with structural screws rather than nails, which back out in freeze-thaw cycling. Where an opening is large enough to allow wind load, we add interior bracing to keep the panel from flexing inward. Every board-up installation gets a perimeter foam-backer seal to block drafts and moisture infiltration at the edges.

Before we leave, we walk through the interior with you to confirm that no secondary openings were missed and that the structure is stable enough to leave unattended.
