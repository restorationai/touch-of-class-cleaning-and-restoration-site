## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Flandreau

Every job begins with a thorough assessment before any materials are disturbed. From there, the work follows a structured sequence:

- **Removal of contaminated materials** — porous items that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are bagged, sealed, and transported for proper regulated disposal in accordance with South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources requirements.

- **Post-remediation verification** — before the containment comes down, affected areas are inspected to confirm the space meets safe re-occupancy standards.

All waste transport and disposal follows applicable state and federal protocols for infectious material. Documentation is provided for your records and for any insurance claim.
