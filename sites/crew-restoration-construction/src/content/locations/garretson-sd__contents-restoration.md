## Why Garretson Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Garretson sits in a climate band that swings hard between humid summers and brutally dry winters. ## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, tagged, and logged before it's moved — that documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what condition. Fragile or high-value pieces get individual wrapping; larger furniture is blanket-wrapped and loaded with care.

At our facility, items go through category-specific cleaning. Textiles are treated with ultrasonic or immersion cleaning depending on the soil load. Hard surfaces — wood furniture, ceramics, metals — are cleaned and deodorized using methods matched to the material and the type of damage (smoke residue behaves differently than flood sediment, and both require different chemistry). Electronics and documents get routed to specialized vendors when needed.

Once items are cleaned and dried, they move into climate-controlled storage until your Garretson home is repaired and ready. You're not paying to store damaged goods in your garage or a rented unit across town — everything stays in one place, tracked, and protected.
