## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Canton

Every job starts with a quick exterior walk to assess the full scope of the breach — roof, windows, doors, and any compromised soffits or fascia. We document everything with photographs before we touch anything, which matters later when the insurance adjuster needs to understand the pre-mitigation condition.

For window and door board-ups, we use ¾-inch plywood cut to fit and fastened with structural screws rather than nails, which hold through wind loading and are easier to remove cleanly when the permanent repair begins. On fire-damaged structures where framing may be weakened, we assess load-bearing capacity before staging ladders or walking the roof line.

Roof tarping on Canton's older homes requires extra attention to the ridge and eave details. We anchor tarps over the ridge and weight or screw the edges down into sound decking — never into damaged or delaminated sheathing that could pull free in wind. On steep-pitch roofs common to the Victorian and Craftsman-era homes near Downtown Canton, we use non-penetrating ballast systems where the existing surface is too fragile for fasteners.
