## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Le Mars

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed and logged, which matters both for your own records and for insurance documentation. Items are then sorted into three categories: items that can be cleaned on-site, items that need pack-out to our facility for deeper treatment, and items that are beyond restoration.

Pack-out is often the right call for Le Mars homes where the structure itself needs significant drying or reconstruction. Once the structure is ready and you've confirmed the timeline, contents are returned, inventoried again on delivery, and placed back in position.
