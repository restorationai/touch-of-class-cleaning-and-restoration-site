## Why Canton Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The housing stock in the 57013 ZIP code skews older, with many homes built before modern PVC sewer lines became standard. Cast iron and clay-tile lateral lines — common in homes near the Lincoln County Courthouse corridor and throughout Downtown Canton — corrode, root-infiltrate, and collapse in ways that PVC simply doesn't. When those laterals fail, the sewage has nowhere to go but back through floor drains, toilets, and sink traps.

Seasonal patterns make things worse. Spring snowmelt along the Big Sioux River area raises the local water table, and saturated soil puts hydrostatic pressure on aging sewer laterals and septic drain fields alike. A line that holds all winter can fail in March or April when the ground is still partially frozen and meltwater has nowhere to go. Homes near Newton Hills State Park that rely on private septic systems face a related risk: drain fields don't percolate effectively when the soil is frost-locked, and tanks can back up into the home before a homeowner realizes the system is overwhelmed.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Canton

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 — the most contaminated water type — and the cleanup protocol reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first step is containment: sealing off affected areas to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage — drywall, carpet, subflooring, insulation — are removed and bagged for proper disposal, because no amount of surface cleaning makes those materials safe to leave in place.

Extraction of standing sewage comes next, using truck-mounted or portable extraction units depending on site access. After extraction, all affected hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 losses. This isn't a single-pass wipe-down — it's a multi-step process that includes application dwell time, mechanical agitation on porous concrete, and a second disinfection pass before any drying equipment is placed.

Drying follows sanitization, not the other way around. Introducing airflow before surfaces are disinfected spreads pathogens. Once the environment is safe, commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers are positioned to dry structural cavities. In Canton's older homes, where wall cavities are often insulated with loose-fill or have no insulation at all, moisture readings are taken at multiple depths to confirm drying is complete before reconstruction begins.
