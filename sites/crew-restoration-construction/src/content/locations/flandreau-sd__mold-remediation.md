## Why Flandreau Properties See Mold Problems

The Big Sioux River area is the most obvious culprit — properties close to the river corridor experience higher ambient humidity and periodic flood intrusion that keeps subfloor assemblies and rim joists damp long after the water recedes visibly. But the problem isn't limited to riverfront addresses. Flandreau's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built before 1970, many of them with stone or poured-concrete foundations that wick groundwater rather than block it. Older vapor barriers — or no vapor barrier at all — let soil moisture migrate steadily into crawl spaces through every freeze-thaw cycle South Dakota delivers.

Downtown Flandreau sees a different pattern: commercial buildings and older mixed-use structures with flat or low-slope roofs that pool water after heavy rain. A slow roof leak in that kind of assembly can saturate insulation and wood decking for months before anyone notices discoloration on a ceiling tile. By the time the stain appears, mold colonies are typically well established behind the finish surface.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Flandreau

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the room where mold is visible, but adjacent cavities, HVAC returns, and any area that shares a moisture pathway with the affected space. Mold follows water, and water rarely stays where it first appears.

Once the scope is mapped, the crew establishes physical containment using polyethylene sheeting and negative air pressure. This keeps spores from migrating to clean areas of the home while work is underway — a step that matters especially in older Flandreau homes where open floor plans and unsealed duct chases can distribute airborne particles quickly.

Contaminated materials that cannot be cleaned — compromised drywall, saturated insulation, deteriorated wood sheathing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Salvageable structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and dried to manufacturer-specified moisture content before any reconstruction begins. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the process. After remediation, air quality testing by an independent party confirms the space is clear before we close out the job.
