## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Adrian

Every situation is different, and the process is always explained to the property owner or point of contact before work begins — no surprises, no unnecessary detail shared beyond what's needed. Here's how a typical remediation unfolds:

**Assessment and containment** come first. The affected area is evaluated, access is controlled, and the rest of the property is protected from cross-contamination. In smaller homes common to East Adrian's residential blocks, this often means working carefully in tight interior spaces where containment barriers need to be thoughtfully placed.

**Removal of affected materials** follows. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated — flooring, subfloor sections, drywall — are bagged, labeled, and transported in compliance with Minnesota Department of Health and EPA guidelines for infectious waste. This is not standard trash removal; it requires proper manifesting and disposal through licensed channels.

**Cleaning and disinfection** use EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants applied at the correct dwell times. The goal is verified decontamination, not just visual cleanliness.

**Odor neutralization** is the final step before clearance. Enzymatic treatments and where appropriate, air scrubbing with HEPA filtration, address residual odors that linger in porous building materials — a particular concern in older Adrian homes where subfloor and wall cavities have had decades to accumulate organic material.
