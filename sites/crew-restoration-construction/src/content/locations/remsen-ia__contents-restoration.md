## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Remsen

The first step on any contents job is a structured pack-out — a room-by-room inventory of every affected item, photographed and catalogued before anything leaves the property. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and for making sure nothing gets lost in transit.

From there, items are sorted into three categories: restorable with on-site cleaning, restorable with off-site treatment, and non-restorable (documented for replacement claims). Furniture restoration after fire or smoke damage typically involves ozone or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize odor molecules embedded in upholstery and wood grain — not just surface cleaning. Water-damaged textiles go through controlled drying before any cleaning begins, because agitating wet fabric accelerates fiber breakdown.

Contents that need extended treatment or that can't safely return to a home still under reconstruction are moved to a climate-controlled storage facility. Items are tracked individually so you know exactly where each piece is at every stage. When your home is ready, we coordinate return delivery and placement.

## Remsen Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents claims are often the most contested part of a homeowner's insurance settlement — adjusters want itemized documentation, and policyholders rarely have it ready. The pack-out inventory we create from the start of every job is designed to serve as that documentation. We photograph items before, during, and after treatment, note pre-loss condition based on visible evidence, and record cleaning or restoration methods used on each piece.

Most standard homeowner policies in Iowa cover contents restoration as part of a covered peril loss. If your policy includes ALE (additional living expense) coverage, stored contents during reconstruction may also be covered under that provision. We can provide the line-item documentation your adjuster needs — but we recommend you contact your carrier early and ask specifically about contents pack-out and off-site storage coverage before work begins.
