Black mold — technically *Stachybotrys chartarum* — thrives in exactly these slow, chronic moisture conditions. But it's far from the only species that causes problems. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Fulda

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping and visual inspection. Before any material is removed, the affected area is isolated with negative-air containment — plastic sheeting, sealed doorways, and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running continuously. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms while work is underway.

Contaminated porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet padding — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions and dried to IICRC S520 standard moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. Drying equipment (desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers) stays on-site until moisture readings confirm the structure is within safe parameters — not just until the surface looks dry.

For homes near the lake shoreline where elevated ambient humidity is a factor, drying timelines can run longer than inland properties. We account for that in the project scope rather than pulling equipment prematurely and leaving residual moisture behind.
