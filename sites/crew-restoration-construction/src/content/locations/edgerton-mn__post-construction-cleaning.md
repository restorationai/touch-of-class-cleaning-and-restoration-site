## Why Edgerton Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Edgerton's housing stock skews older, and older construction means more surface complexity. Original plaster ceilings, wood-framed windows with painted sashes, and tongue-and-groove subfloors all react differently to construction dust and adhesives than modern materials do. Fine silica dust from concrete cutting or drywall sanding migrates further in older homes because gaps in original framing and flooring allow it to travel — you'll find it in rooms two walls away from where the actual work happened.

The region's climate adds another layer. Southwest Minnesota winters are dry and cold, which causes construction dust to carry static charge and cling stubbornly to surfaces. Spring and early summer bring humidity swings that can cause freshly installed wood flooring or millwork to shift slightly, trapping debris along seams. A final clean done without accounting for these seasonal conditions often leaves behind residue that only becomes visible once the humidity normalizes and surfaces settle.

For commercial or public-adjacent projects near landmarks like the Edgerton Public Library, there's also the matter of community visibility — a building that reopens with hazy windows or dusty sills reflects on the project as a whole, not just the cleaning crew.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Edgerton

Post-construction cleanup isn't a single pass — it's a sequenced process, and the sequence matters.

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work begins, we remove bulk debris: scrap lumber, packaging, drywall cutoffs, and anything the trades left behind. This phase protects finished surfaces from being scratched by material dragged across them during cleanup.

**Dust containment and surface cleaning.** We use HEPA-filtered vacuums on all surfaces — walls, ceilings, window frames, cabinetry interiors — before any wet cleaning begins. Wiping a dusty surface without vacuuming first just smears fine particulate into the finish. In Edgerton's older homes, this step is especially important on original trim and plaster, which can absorb moisture from wet wiping if dust isn't removed first.

**Detail and specialty cleaning.** This is where the work gets specific: removing paint overspray from hardware, cleaning grout haze off new tile, polishing windows inside and out, and wiping down every cabinet box and drawer interior. For renovation projects that involved any specialty materials — stained concrete, exposed brick, custom millwork — we adjust our cleaning agents to match the surface chemistry.

**Final walkthrough.** We inspect under natural light and with task lighting, because construction dust shows differently depending on the angle. Anything flagged gets addressed before we close out.
