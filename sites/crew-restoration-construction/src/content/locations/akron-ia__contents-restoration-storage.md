## Our Contents Restoration Process in Akron

The first step is a detailed inventory. Every item in the affected area is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. This documentation matters for insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was recovered and what wasn't.

From there, salvageable contents are packed out and transported to a climate-controlled facility. Textiles go through specialized laundering and deodorization. Hard surfaces are cleaned using methods matched to the material, whether that's ultrasonic cleaning for delicate items, dry-cleaning sponges for electronics, or ozone and hydroxyl treatment for persistent smoke odor. Documents and photographs are stabilized and, where possible, restored.

Storage during your home's repair phase is included in the process. Your belongings stay in a secure, monitored environment until the structure is ready, so you're not scrambling to find a rental unit or stacking boxes in a relative's garage.

Once repairs are complete, contents are returned and placed back in the home, room by room, based on your inventory record.

## Akron Insurance Coordination

Contents losses are often the most contested part of a homeowner's claim. Carriers want itemized documentation, replacement cost valuations, and evidence that restoration was attempted before items were written off. The inventory and photo record we build at the start of the job is structured to support that process. We work directly with adjusters and can provide the documentation they need in the format they expect, which reduces back-and-forth and keeps your claim moving.

If your policy includes additional living expense coverage, the pack-out and storage portion of contents restoration typically qualifies. Your adjuster can confirm the specifics of your policy, but having professional documentation from the start puts you in a stronger position.
