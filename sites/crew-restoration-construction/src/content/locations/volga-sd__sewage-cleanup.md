## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Volga

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the work sequence reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first priority is safety assessment: identifying whether the source is still active, whether structural materials are compromised, and whether the space is safe to enter without respiratory protection. Once the area is secured, extraction begins using truck-mounted and portable units capable of pulling sewage-laden water from carpet, subfloor cavities, and crawl spaces.

After bulk extraction, affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet padding — are removed and bagged for proper disposal. What remains gets treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 contamination. This isn't a surface wipe-down; application is thorough and dwell times are observed so the chemistry can neutralize pathogens rather than just mask odor. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, with moisture readings logged at each visit until materials reach target levels. A final sanitization pass confirms the space is clean before any reconstruction begins.
