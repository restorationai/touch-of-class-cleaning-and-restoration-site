## Why Dakota Dunes Properties See Elevated Contents Damage

Dakota Dunes sits at the confluence of the Missouri and Big Sioux Rivers, which gives the community its scenic appeal and its flood exposure. Even in years without major river events, the water table in low-lying sections of the development stays high enough that a slow appliance leak or a failed sump pump can wick moisture into flooring and cabinetry faster than it would in an upland neighborhood. The riverside setting also means humidity levels fluctuate sharply between seasons — dry winters followed by humid, warm springs accelerate mold colonization on porous materials like upholstered furniture and wood cabinetry. Mold can begin colonizing damp organic material in as little as 24 to 48 hours, so the window for saving contents without deep cleaning is short.

The housing stock in Dakota Dunes skews toward larger, custom-built homes — many in The Dunes golf community carry high-end finishes, custom cabinetry, and furniture that would be expensive or impossible to replace at current prices. That raises the stakes on getting the cleaning and restoration process right the first time.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The first step is a room-by-room inventory before anything leaves your home. Every item is photographed, logged by condition, and assigned a category — salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with restoration work, or unsalvageable. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear picture of what to expect back.

From there, contents are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled facility. Cleaning methods vary by material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics components, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor penetration on soft goods, hand-cleaning for artwork and fragile items. Furniture with fire or smoke residue often needs multiple cleaning passes — smoke particles are acidic and continue breaking down finishes and fabrics if residue isn't fully removed.

Once your home has been dried, repaired, and cleared for re-occupancy, we coordinate return delivery and place items back in their original rooms using the inventory photos as a guide.

## Coordinating with Insurance for Dakota Dunes Claims

Contents restoration is a covered line item under most standard homeowners policies, but the documentation requirements matter. Carriers want itemized inventories, pre-cleaning condition photos, and cleaning cost breakdowns — not a single lump-sum invoice. Crew Restoration & Construction structures its pack-out documentation to match what adjusters need, which reduces back-and-forth and speeds up claim settlement.
