## Our Mold Remediation Process in Worthing

Every project starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping using thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters. We identify the moisture source first — because remediating mold without correcting the underlying water intrusion is temporary work at best. Once the source is documented, the affected area is isolated with negative air pressure containment using 6-mil poly sheeting and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Physical removal follows containment: compromised drywall, insulation, and porous materials are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines. Structural framing and concrete surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. HEPA vacuuming clears residual spore load from surfaces and HVAC registers. We then run commercial-grade dehumidification and air movement equipment until moisture readings confirm the space has reached acceptable levels. A final visual inspection and, when the scope warrants it, independent air quality sampling closes out the job — giving you documentation for your records and your insurer.
