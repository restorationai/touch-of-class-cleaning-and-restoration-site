## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Beresford

Every job starts with a full moisture assessment using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We map wet zones across floors, walls, and subfloor assemblies before a single piece of equipment is placed — because moving air through a space before you understand where the water actually is just spreads the problem.

Once the scope is clear, standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access. Structural drying follows: industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the room's cubic footage and the materials involved, and air movers are set to create a drying vortex across wet surfaces. We log temperature, relative humidity, and material moisture readings daily — that documentation matters for your insurance adjuster and gives you a clear record of progress.

If flooring, drywall, or insulation has absorbed enough water that it can't be dried in place within a reasonable window, we remove only what needs to go. Selective demolition keeps reconstruction costs down and preserves original materials where they can be saved.
