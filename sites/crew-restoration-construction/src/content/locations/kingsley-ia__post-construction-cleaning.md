## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Kingsley

Every post-construction clean starts with a walkthrough before a single cloth is touched. We identify the trade sequence — what was done last, where dust migration paths ran, which surfaces have adhesive or caulk overspray — and build the cleaning order around that. Working top-to-bottom and room-by-room, we HEPA-vacuum all flat surfaces, light fixtures, ductwork registers, and window tracks before any wet cleaning begins. That sequence matters: mopping before vacuuming just turns dust into a paste that's harder to remove from grout lines and plank flooring.

For specialty cleaning phases — post-fire odor treatment on a renovated kitchen, deep-clean of a commercial space after contractor work, or final clean on a rental property being turned over after renovation — we adjust chemistry and equipment to the surface. Concrete floors get a different approach than refinished hardwood. Fresh paint needs a gentler touch than tile. We document conditions before and after with photos, which is useful if a general contractor or property owner needs sign-off on the cleaning scope.
