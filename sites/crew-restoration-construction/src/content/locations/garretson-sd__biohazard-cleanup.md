Garretson sits in a part of Minnehaha County where the housing stock skews older, particularly in and around Downtown Garretson. The regional climate adds another layer of complexity. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Garretson

Every situation is different, and we approach each one without assumptions. When we arrive, the first step is a thorough assessment to identify all affected surfaces — visible and non-visible. Biohazard contamination involving blood, bodily fluids, sharps, or infectious material does not stay where it appears to be; it migrates. We use ATP testing and other diagnostic tools to map the true extent before any removal begins.

Once the scope is established, our team removes and properly packages all contaminated materials following state and federal infectious waste disposal regulations. Surfaces are then treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific pathogen risk present. After disinfection, we conduct clearance testing to confirm the space is safe before we close out the job. Throughout the process, we work quietly and efficiently — minimizing foot traffic, using covered containers, and keeping the situation as private as possible for anyone on or near the property.
