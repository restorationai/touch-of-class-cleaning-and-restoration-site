## Why Dakota Dunes Properties Demand More from Final Cleans

Dakota Dunes (ZIP 57049) sits at the confluence of the Missouri and Big Sioux rivers, and that geography shapes more than the scenery. The sandy, silty soil that makes the area so distinctive also means construction sites track an unusually fine particulate through finished spaces — dust that settles into HVAC returns, behind cabinet faces, and along the bottom edges of baseboards long after crews have packed up. In the upscale developments around the Dakota Dunes Country Club, builders are working with high-specification materials: engineered hardwood, natural stone countertops, custom millwork, and large-format porcelain tile. Each of those surfaces has a specific cleaning protocol. Grout haze left on stone can etch permanently. Protective film left too long on windows bonds in heat. These aren't problems you solve with a mop and a bottle of all-purpose cleaner.

The 2011 Missouri River flood reshaped how many properties in this community were rebuilt and reinforced, and a number of homes in the Two Rivers area were substantially reconstructed in the years that followed. Post-flood rebuilds often layered new finishes over remediated substrates, meaning a thorough final clean sometimes uncovers residual issues — efflorescence on concrete, mineral staining near slab edges — that need to be addressed before a homeowner moves back in.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Dakota Dunes

Every project starts with a walk-through to document the scope: what trades were on-site, what materials were installed, and what the general contractor's punch-list looks like. That context determines the sequence and the chemistry.

**Rough clean first.** Debris, drywall mud chunks, tape scraps, and bulk dust come out before any detail work begins. HVAC vents and returns are masked or vacuumed to prevent recirculating fine particulate through the system during cleaning.

**Surface-specific detail clean.** Stone and tile receive appropriate pH-balanced cleaners to lift grout haze without etching. Glass is polished free of paint overspray and sticker adhesive. Cabinetry interiors are wiped down — sawdust in drawer boxes is a common oversight on rushed final cleans. Light fixtures, hardware, and appliances get individual attention.

**Final inspection pass.** We walk the space with the same critical eye a buyer or property manager would use — checking window tracks, threshold transitions, and the tops of door casings where dust collects and often gets missed.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans, pressure washing of driveways and hardscaping, or interior air scrubbing after heavy sanding — we scope those needs separately and bring the right equipment for each.

Homes in the Dunes golf community are frequently built with large south- and west-facing window arrays to capture views of the course and the river corridor.
