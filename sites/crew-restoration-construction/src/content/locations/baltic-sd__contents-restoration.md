## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Baltic

Every pack-out starts with a detailed inventory. Each item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before it leaves your property. This documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of exactly what was taken and in what condition.

From there, items are transported to a climate-controlled facility where the actual restoration work happens:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** removes soot, smoke residue, and mold from hard surfaces, metals, ceramics, and some electronics without abrasive contact.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** neutralizes smoke and mold odors embedded in upholstery, clothing, and soft goods.
- **Document and photo drying** uses controlled-environment drying to stabilize paper items before any cleaning or dehumidification is attempted.
- **Furniture assessment** determines whether pieces can be fully restored or need specialist refinishing — we document the condition either way for your adjuster.

Items stay in secure, climate-controlled storage until your home in Baltic is dried, repaired, and ready. We coordinate the return delivery and help place items back in the correct rooms.
