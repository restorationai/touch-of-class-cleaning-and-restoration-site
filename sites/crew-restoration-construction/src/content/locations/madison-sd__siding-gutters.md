## Why Madison Properties See Siding and Gutter Issues

The housing stock in and around Downtown Madison includes a meaningful number of homes built in the 1950s through the 1970s — an era when T1-11 plywood siding and aluminum gutters were standard. Those materials have a finite lifespan, and by now many of them are well past it. Plywood siding that has been painted over repeatedly traps moisture behind the paint film; when temperatures drop below freezing, that trapped moisture expands and the substrate delaminates from the inside out. Homeowners often notice bubbling or soft spots long before they see visible rot, and by the time they do, the sheathing underneath has usually been compromised as well.

Properties closer to the Lake Herman area face an additional factor: the prevailing southwest winds drive rain and sleet at a low angle against north- and west-facing walls. Gutters on those elevations fill with ice dams faster than gutters on protected sides of the same structure, and the weight of that ice can pull hangers away from the fascia. Once a gutter section separates even slightly, water routes behind the fascia board rather than away from the foundation — which is exactly the kind of slow, hidden damage that turns a straightforward gutter replacement into a fascia and soffit repair job.

## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Madison

Every project starts with a full exterior inspection rather than a material-specific estimate. That means we look at the fascia, soffit, and sheathing condition before quoting siding, and we assess the slope, downspout placement, and splash block positioning before quoting gutters. In Madison's climate, those components are interdependent — replacing siding over wet sheathing, or hanging new gutters on a rotted fascia, produces a callback within two seasons.

For siding replacement, we typically remove all existing material back to the sheathing, inspect for moisture intrusion, and install a housewrap barrier before new panels go up. Fiber cement siding performs well in South Dakota's temperature range and holds paint longer than vinyl in high-UV summers. For homes near Prairie Village or the older blocks of Downtown Madison where architectural character matters, we can match lap profiles and corner trim details to what was original to the structure.

Gutter installation uses seamless aluminum formed on-site, which eliminates the joints that are most likely to separate under ice load. Downspout extensions are sized and positioned to move water at least four feet from the foundation — a detail that matters more in Madison's clay-heavy soils, which drain slowly and can direct water back toward a basement wall if extensions are too short.
