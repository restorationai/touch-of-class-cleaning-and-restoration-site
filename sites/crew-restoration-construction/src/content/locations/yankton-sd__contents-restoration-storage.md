## Our Contents Restoration Process in Yankton

The process begins with a detailed inventory at your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before it moves. Items that can be restored on-site stay on-site when the structure allows; everything else is packed out to a climate-controlled storage facility where it remains secure and accessible throughout the repair timeline.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Ultrasonic cleaning works well for hard goods, tools, and non-porous collectibles. Documents and photographs get priority triage. Wet paper begins to deteriorate within hours, so freeze-drying is often the right intervention for items that cannot be air-dried quickly.
