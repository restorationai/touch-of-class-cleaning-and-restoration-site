## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Worthing

Every contents pack-out starts with a room-by-room inventory. We photograph and catalog each item before it leaves your home, so there's a clear chain of custody from your living room to our storage facility. That documentation matters when you're working through an insurance claim.

Once items are at our facility, cleaning methods are matched to the material. Electronics and hard surfaces get wiped down with appropriate solutions. Upholstered furniture and textiles go through ultrasonic cleaning or dry-cleaning processes depending on fiber type. Smoke-damaged items — common after kitchen fires in the tightly built homes near East Worthing — are treated with thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation to break down odor molecules rather than just masking them.

Items that pass inspection go into climate-controlled storage. Temperature and humidity are managed so that a leather couch or a wood dining set doesn't develop secondary damage while your home is being repaired. When reconstruction wraps up, we coordinate the return and place items back in the rooms where they belong.
