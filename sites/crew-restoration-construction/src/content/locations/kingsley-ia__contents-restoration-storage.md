When a water loss saturates that environment, upholstered pieces and wood furnishings wick moisture deeply before the surface even feels wet.

Summer storm seasons in western Iowa also bring rapid humidity swings. Contents that sit in that environment for even 24 to 48 hours before being moved to a controlled setting can cross from restorable to unsalvageable. ## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Kingsley

The process begins with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating. This documentation protects you during the insurance claim and ensures nothing is misplaced during transport.
