## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Hinton

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional damage. That means emergency tarping of breached roofs, boarding windows where glass is broken or frames are racked, and removing hanging tree limbs or debris that could fall on occupied spaces. Tree damage cleanup around structures requires careful sequencing — a limb that has punched through a roof still carries load, and cutting it without understanding how it's bearing can cause further collapse.

Once the structure is stabilized, the assessment phase documents every affected surface: roof decking, wall cavities, ceilings, insulation, and any mechanical systems exposed to water intrusion. Structural drying follows the same evaporation-and-dehumidification principles used in water damage work — because a storm that opens your roof is, functionally, also a water damage event.
