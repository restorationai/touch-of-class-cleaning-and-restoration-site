## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Beresford

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory. Every item that can be safely moved is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before it leaves your property. This documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing gets lost or confused during the restoration phase.

Pack-out is done with moving-grade materials — not contractor bags. Fragile items, electronics, and artwork are wrapped and crated separately. Once contents reach our facility, the cleaning method is matched to the item: ultrasonic cleaning for hard surfaces and small valuables, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke-saturated fabrics and upholstery, and dry-cleaning coordination for garments that need professional textile care. Items that pass cleaning inspection go into climate-controlled storage until your home is ready. Nothing is returned until the structure is dry, repaired, and safe.
