## Why Adrian Properties See Roofing Problems Early

The climate story here is straightforward but worth understanding before you invest in a new roof. Adrian sits in a region that averages more than 40 inches of annual snowfall, and the real damage comes not from the snow itself but from the melt-refreeze cycle at the eave line. Ice dams form when heat escapes through the attic, melts the lower snowpack, and then refreezes at the cold overhang — backing water under shingles and into the decking. Many homes in and around Downtown Adrian were built in eras when attic insulation standards were minimal, which makes ice dam damage a recurring problem rather than a one-off event.

Beyond ice, the hail corridor that runs through southwest Minnesota is well-documented by insurance adjusters who work this part of the state regularly. A single severe storm can strip the protective granule layer from an entire roof slope, leaving asphalt exposed to UV degradation. What looks like surface cosmetic damage in June becomes a leak by the following February.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Adrian

Every project starts with a thorough inspection of the existing roof system — not just the shingles, but the decking, flashing, ridge ventilation, and soffit condition. In older homes common to East Adrian, we frequently find original board sheathing beneath layers of roofing that have been added over decades. Where that decking is soft, cupped, or delaminated, we replace it before laying new underlayment, because a new roof installed over compromised decking will fail prematurely regardless of shingle quality.

Once the deck is confirmed sound, we install a self-adhering ice-and-water shield along the eaves and in all valleys — this is non-negotiable in a climate like Adrian's, not an upsell. Synthetic felt underlayment goes over the field, followed by the shingle system of your choice. We walk you through material options — architectural shingles rated for high-wind zones, impact-resistant Class 4 shingles that can reduce insurance premiums, metal roofing for longevity — with honest guidance on what makes sense for your home's age, pitch, and budget.

Flashing details at chimneys, dormers, and pipe boots get particular attention. These are the points where most leaks originate, and cutting corners on step flashing or counter-flashing is the most common mistake made by crews rushing through a job.
