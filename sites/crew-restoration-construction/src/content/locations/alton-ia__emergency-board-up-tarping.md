## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Alton

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is a rapid exterior assessment: we identify every breach — broken glass, fire-damaged siding, missing shingles, collapsed soffit — before we touch anything. That full-perimeter walk matters because a single missed opening can undermine every other protective measure.

For window and door openings, we cut OSB panels to fit the rough opening, secure them with structural screws rather than staples, and seal the perimeter with weatherproof tape to block wind infiltration. On roof losses, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind exposure, weighted and fastened with lumber battens screwed into sound decking — not just draped and weighted with sandbags, which can shift in the kind of gusts northwest Iowa sees regularly. For fire-damaged structures, we assess load-bearing integrity before sending anyone to an upper story or roof, and we document every opening with photographs for the insurance file.

If interior contents are exposed, we can deploy temporary interior barriers to limit smoke odor migration and protect salvageable belongings while the structure is being secured from outside.
