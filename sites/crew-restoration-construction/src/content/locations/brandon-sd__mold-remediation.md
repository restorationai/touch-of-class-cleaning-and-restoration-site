## Why Brandon Properties See Mold Issues

Brandon's building boom means a large share of the housing stock was framed within the last 15 to 20 years. Modern engineered lumber and OSB sheathing are efficient and cost-effective, but they are also more moisture-sensitive than the old-growth dimensional lumber used in older Sioux Falls neighborhoods. When a dishwasher supply line weeps behind a cabinet for weeks, or a bathroom exhaust fan vents into an attic instead of outside — a common framing-stage shortcut — OSB can begin to degrade and support mold growth before the homeowner notices any discoloration.

South Dakota's climate adds another layer. Brandon winters are cold and dry, which slows surface evaporation indoors and can trap condensation inside wall cavities near exterior sheathing. Then spring arrives fast: snowmelt saturates the clay-heavy soils common in the Split Rock area, and sump pumps in newer subdivisions work overtime. If a pump fails or a drain tile backs up, a finished basement can accumulate several inches of standing water in hours — and the clock on mold colonization starts immediately.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Brandon

Every job begins with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water intrusion back to its source, because treating visible mold without eliminating the moisture pathway guarantees a return visit. Once the scope is clear, we establish negative-air containment barriers — plastic sheeting, zipper doors, and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running continuously — to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms.

Affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet padding) are removed, bagged, and disposed of per EPA guidelines. Hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, then treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. We don't consider the job done until post-remediation moisture readings confirm the structure is dry and the affected area is ready for reconstruction. If your lender or insurance adjuster requires a third-party clearance test, we can coordinate timing with an independent industrial hygienist before we close out the project.
