## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Akron

Post-construction cleanup is not the same as a deep house clean, and the difference shows in the tools and the order of operations. The process starts with a rough-out phase: removing all visible debris, packaging waste, and leftover materials before any surface cleaning begins. Attempting to mop or wipe while construction debris is still present just redistributes the problem.

From there, the sequence moves top to bottom and dry to wet. Ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the tops of door frames are detail-vacuumed with HEPA-filtered equipment before walls and trim are wiped. Window interiors — frames, sills, and glass — get a dedicated pass because construction adhesive and paint overspray concentrate there. Floors are the final phase: hard surfaces are scrubbed with appropriate pH-neutral cleaners that won't lift new grout or damage fresh finish, and any carpet areas receive a thorough extraction to pull out embedded drywall particulate that vacuuming alone won't reach.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation odor neutralization, pressure washing of exterior flatwork, or cleaning of newly installed cabinetry before move-in — those are scoped and sequenced after the primary construction debris cleaning is complete.
