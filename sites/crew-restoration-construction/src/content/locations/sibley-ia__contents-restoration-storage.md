## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Sibley

The process starts at your property. Every item is inventoried and photographed before anything moves, creating a documented record that your insurance adjuster can review line by line. Fragile items, electronics, documents, and artwork are packed separately from soft goods and furniture. Anything that can be cleaned and restored is tagged for treatment; items that cannot be restored are documented for your claim rather than quietly discarded.

From Sibley, contents travel to a climate-controlled storage facility where temperature and humidity are maintained at levels that prevent secondary mold growth during the restoration window. Soft contents, including clothing, bedding, and upholstered furniture, go through ozone treatment or thermal fogging to address smoke odor at the fiber level, not just at the surface. Hard surfaces are cleaned using methods matched to the material, whether that is ultrasonic cleaning for small valuables and collectibles or hand-wiping for finished wood furniture. Once the structure is cleared and ready, contents are returned, inventoried again on delivery, and placed according to your direction.
