## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Brandon

The first priority after a storm is stopping ongoing water intrusion. Before any restoration work begins, the team assesses structural stability, then deploys emergency tarps or plywood sheeting over breached roof sections and boards compromised windows. This step matters most in Brandon's climate because a secondary storm system can follow within 24–48 hours of the first — leaving a damaged roof unprotected overnight is a risk that turns a manageable claim into a catastrophic one.

Once the structure is secured, the scope of interior damage is documented room by room. Moisture meters and thermal imaging identify water that has migrated into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies — damage that isn't visible but will produce mold growth within 48–72 hours if not addressed. Structural drying equipment is placed based on the specific materials involved: newer Brandon homes with spray-foam insulation in the attic require different drying strategies than homes with blown-in fiberglass, because foam-sealed assemblies trap moisture and need targeted dehumidification rather than open-air drying.

Debris removal, structural repairs, and finish reconstruction follow once materials are confirmed dry. Crew Restoration & Construction handles both the mitigation and the rebuild under one contract, which simplifies the insurance process and eliminates the gap that sometimes occurs when a separate general contractor takes over mid-claim.
