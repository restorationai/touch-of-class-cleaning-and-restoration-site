## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Elk Point

Every project begins with a thorough inspection and sample collection before any physical work starts. Suspected materials — popcorn ceiling texture, vinyl floor tile, pipe wrap, joint compound, roofing shingles — are sampled and sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis. We do not skip this step and assume; confirmed identification protects you, your family, and our crew.

Once testing confirms asbestos-containing material, we establish regulated work areas using negative air pressure containment. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously during removal to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. All removed material is wetted to suppress fiber release, sealed in labeled disposal bags, and transported to a licensed disposal facility — South Dakota requires asbestos waste to go to an approved landfill with proper manifesting, and we handle that paperwork on your behalf.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, we wet the surface before any scraping begins, work in contained sections, and perform a visual clearance check before demobilizing containment. If your project involves a renovation permit in Union County, we can coordinate documentation with the county building department to keep your project on schedule.

## Local Note

One thing that comes up repeatedly on older Elk Point properties: asbestos-containing duct insulation on gravity furnace systems. Many homes built before 1960 in this part of Union County were originally heated with large floor-register gravity furnaces, and the ductwork wrapping on those systems was almost universally insulated with asbestos paper or corrugated asbestos board. When homeowners upgrade to modern forced-air systems, contractors sometimes disturb this old duct insulation without realizing what it is. If your home still has a gravity furnace or you're mid-renovation and discovered old duct wrap, stop work and call for testing before proceeding — disturbed asbestos duct insulation spreads fibers throughout the entire duct system and into living areas quickly.
