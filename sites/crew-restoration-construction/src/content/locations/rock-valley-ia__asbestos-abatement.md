## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Rock Valley

Every project begins with a thorough asbestos inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and we walk you through the written report before any removal plan is finalized. That transparency matters — you should understand exactly what was found, where it is, and why the recommended scope of work is what it is.

Once a removal scope is confirmed, the work area is sealed with poly sheeting and placed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered equipment. Workers wear full respirator protection and disposable coveralls. Asbestos-containing materials are wetted during removal to suppress fiber release, then double-bagged in labeled, puncture-resistant containers for transport to a licensed disposal facility. Iowa requires that asbestos waste be disposed of at an approved landfill — we handle all manifesting and chain-of-custody documentation so your project stays compliant with Iowa DNR notification requirements.

After removal, air clearance testing confirms fiber counts are below acceptable thresholds before containment is broken down. You receive a final clearance report — the document your contractor, insurer, or future buyer may ask to see.
