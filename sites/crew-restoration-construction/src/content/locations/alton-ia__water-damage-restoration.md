## Why Alton Properties See Water Damage Issues

Alton sits in a low-lying stretch of northwest Iowa where clay-heavy soils don't drain quickly after heavy rain. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Alton

When we arrive at a property, the first step is moisture mapping — using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly how far water has traveled beyond the visible wet zone. Water follows the path of least resistance through subfloor seams, along joists, and behind baseboards, so the wet area you can see is rarely the full extent of the problem.

Once we know the scope, we move to water extraction using truck-mounted and portable extraction units that pull standing water and saturated material moisture out of carpet, padding, and subfloor. After extraction, we set a calculated drying system: commercial-grade dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, and desiccant units when ambient humidity is high — which in Iowa's summer months, it frequently is. We monitor moisture readings at every visit and adjust equipment placement until structural materials reach target drying goals. Affected drywall, insulation, or flooring that cannot be dried in place is removed and documented for your insurance claim.
