## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Rock Rapids

The process starts before the crew leaves the truck. Dispatch collects basic structure information — story count, roof pitch, approximate opening dimensions — so the team arrives with the right lumber dimensions, fastener types, and tarp weight already staged. On arrival, the priority is a quick safety walk: live electrical hazards near a fire-damaged opening, unstable roof sections, or compromised floor joists near a flood entry point all change the sequencing of the work.

For roof tarping, the crew anchors tarps using wood battens screwed through the tarp into solid decking, not just weighted edges that the next gust will shift. Ridge-to-eave runs are preferred over flat draping because they shed water rather than pool it. For window and door board-ups, half-inch or three-quarter-inch OSB is cut to overlap the frame by at least two inches on each side, then secured with structural screws rather than nails — a detail that matters when Iowa wind gusts return before permanent repairs are complete. Every opening gets photographed before and after installation, which feeds directly into the insurance documentation package.
