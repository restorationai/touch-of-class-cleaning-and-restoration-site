## Why Biohazard Situations in Tea Require Specialized Response

Biohazard cleanup isn't a job for a general cleaning crew or a property manager with a mop. Blood-borne pathogens, infectious fluids, and improperly discarded sharps carry real health risks that persist long after the visible mess is gone. Porous materials — carpet padding, subfloor OSB, drywall — can harbor contamination that isn't visible to the eye. Proper remediation means removing and disposing of affected materials according to South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources guidelines for regulated medical waste, not just wiping surfaces down.

Tea's rapid residential buildout means a significant portion of the housing stock is less than ten years old. That's actually a complicating factor in some biohazard situations: newer construction often uses thicker carpet padding and engineered wood subfloor products that absorb fluids more readily than older hardwood or concrete slab. Penetration can be deeper than it looks, and testing — not assumption — determines what stays and what goes.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Tea

Every job starts with a thorough assessment before any cleaning begins. We identify the full scope of affected materials, establish a containment perimeter, and put on appropriate personal protective equipment. Nothing moves until the boundaries of contamination are understood.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence:

- **Removal of contaminated materials** — soft goods, flooring layers, and any structural components that have absorbed biological material beyond safe limits
- **Surface disinfection** — hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants applied to all affected hard surfaces, with appropriate dwell times
- **Sharps and needle cleanup** — if present, handled with puncture-resistant containers and disposed of through licensed medical waste channels
- **Odor treatment** — hydroxyl or thermal fogging where residual odor remains after material removal
- **Post-remediation verification** — we don't call a job done until the space tests clean

We document everything photographically throughout the process, which matters both for your peace of mind and for insurance purposes.
