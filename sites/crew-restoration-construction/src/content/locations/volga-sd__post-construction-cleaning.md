## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Volga

A post-construction clean is not a deep house clean with extra vacuuming. The sequence matters. We start with rough debris removal — pulling out cut lumber ends, bagged drywall scraps, and packaging material before any surface work begins. From there, the process moves through four distinct phases.

**Phase 1 — Mechanical debris removal.** HEPA-filtered commercial vacuums pull drywall dust from ductwork registers, windowsills, and cabinet interiors. Blowing dust around with a broom at this stage embeds it further into surfaces.

**Phase 2 — Surface cleaning.** Walls are wiped for compound smears and pencil marks. Windows get scraped for paint and sticker adhesive, then cleaned with streak-free solution. Tile and grout receive grout haze removal using appropriate pH-balanced cleaners — a step that's often skipped but makes the difference between tile that looks cloudy and tile that looks installed correctly.

**Phase 3 — Specialty surfaces.** Hardwood floors get adhesive residue removed before any finishing coat. Stainless appliances are wiped in the direction of the grain to avoid micro-scratching. Lighting fixtures are cleared of dust before bulbs are seated.

**Phase 4 — Final walkthrough and touch cleaning.** We walk the space with the contractor or owner against a punch-list, addressing anything flagged before sign-off.
