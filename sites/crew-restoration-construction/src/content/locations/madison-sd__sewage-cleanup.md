## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Madison

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage cleanup, and the process reflects that. When we arrive on-site, the first priority is containment — stopping the source if it hasn't been stopped already, then establishing a work perimeter to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. We use negative air pressure and physical barriers when the affected area is adjacent to living spaces.

Extraction comes next. Standing sewage is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access. In Madison's older homes, crawl spaces are often low-clearance and require portable equipment and specialized wands to reach pooled water under the structure. After bulk extraction, all affected hard surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination.

Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are almost always non-salvageable after raw sewage contact. We document everything removed for your insurance claim before disposal. Structural drying follows with commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers, and we monitor moisture readings daily until the structure reaches dry standard. Final sanitization and an odor-neutralizing treatment close out the job before any reconstruction begins.
