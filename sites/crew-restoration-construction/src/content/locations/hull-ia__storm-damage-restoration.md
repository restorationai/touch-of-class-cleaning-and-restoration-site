## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Hull

Every job starts with a thorough damage assessment before a single piece of material is removed. ## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about older homes near Downtown Hull: many were built with full-dimension lumber framing and plank sheathing rather than the engineered wood panels common in post-1980 construction. That's actually a structural advantage in wind events — the older framing tends to be stiffer — but it complicates drying after water intrusion. Plank sheathing holds moisture longer than OSB and can harbor mold growth in wall cavities before surface readings show anything alarming. When we're working a water-intrusion case in an older Hull home, we extend our moisture monitoring period and use cavity drying equipment rather than relying solely on surface dehumidification.
