## Our Mold Remediation Process in Parker

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to locate wet building materials that aren't visible to the naked eye — because treating surface mold without addressing the moisture source guarantees a return visit. Once we've identified the full scope, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. That containment keeps spores from migrating into unaffected rooms while work is underway.

Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed and bagged for proper disposal. Structural surfaces that can be saved are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial solutions and physically abraded where needed. We don't rely on encapsulants as a shortcut; if a material can't be cleaned to bare substrate, it comes out. After remediation, we dry the cavity to documented moisture readings before any reconstruction begins. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for any future buyer's inspection.
