## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly on older homes in South Kingsley and the blocks surrounding Downtown Kingsley: the original roof decking is often 1×6 skip sheathing rather than plywood or OSB. This was standard construction for the era, and it's structurally sound, but it changes the installation approach. Ice-and-water shield doesn't adhere the same way to skip sheathing, and some roofing crews will try to install over it without addressing the gaps. The correct approach — and the one that actually holds up — is to overlay the skip sheathing with a layer of OSB before laying new underlayment. It adds material cost, but it's the difference between a roof that lasts 25 years and one that develops problems in 8.
