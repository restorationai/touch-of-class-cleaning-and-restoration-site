Kingsley sits in the rolling terrain of the Loess Hills, a landscape that funnels and amplifies wind in ways that flat-ground communities don't experience the same way. ## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process, Calibrated to Kingsley Conditions

Every job starts with a rapid visual assessment of the full structure, not just the obvious breach. On older Kingsley homes, that means checking for secondary damage — a roof impact that displaced flashing, a window frame that's racked out of square, a soffit section that's hanging loose. We document everything photographically before touching anything, which matters when you're filing a claim.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind conditions, secured with lumber battens and screws driven into sound decking — not just weighted down at the edges. In an area where a second storm can arrive within 24 to 48 hours of the first, a tarp that's simply draped and weighted is a liability. For window and door openings, we cut and fasten plywood panels to fit the rough opening, using exterior-grade screws into the framing rather than staples, which pull out under lateral wind load. Every panel is labeled with the opening dimensions so your contractor can order replacement windows without a second site visit.
