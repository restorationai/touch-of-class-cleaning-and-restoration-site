## Why Lennox Homes Often Need More Than a Simple Refresh

Lennox sits in Lincoln County's path of steady residential growth, which means the community is a mix of decades-old farmhouse-style homes and newer construction pushing outward from the 57039 ZIP code's core. The older homes — many built before modern vapor barriers and insulation standards — carry their own set of renovation challenges. Crawl spaces that were never properly sealed let ground moisture migrate into subfloor assemblies.
