## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Harrisburg

Every job starts with a walkthrough to establish the true boundaries of contamination — not just the visible area. From there, the affected space is isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where necessary to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms. Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are removed, bagged, and transported to a licensed biohazardous waste facility in compliance with South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources requirements.

Surface decontamination follows using hospital-grade disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens. Sharps and needle cleanup, when present, is handled with puncture-resistant containers and proper chain-of-custody documentation. The final step is a post-remediation verification — not a visual check, but an ATP surface test that confirms biological material has been reduced to safe levels before we remove our containment. The home should be ready for normal occupancy when we leave, not just look like it is.
