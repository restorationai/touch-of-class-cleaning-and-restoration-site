## Why Tea Properties See Water Damage Differently

Tea has grown faster than almost any small city in South Dakota over the past decade. That rapid expansion means the housing stock is overwhelmingly new construction, with slab-on-grade foundations dominating the newer subdivisions near Tea Area High School and Legacy Park. Slab foundations eliminate the crawl space moisture problems common in older Sioux Falls neighborhoods, but they introduce a different vulnerability: when water intrudes, it travels laterally under luxury vinyl plank or tile with no air gap to slow it down. By the time a wet spot appears at a seam or a baseboard, the moisture front may have already spread several feet in every direction.

South Dakota's freeze-thaw cycle compounds the risk. Pipes in exterior walls and garage-adjacent spaces are exposed to dramatic temperature swings between November and March. A single hard overnight freeze after an unseasonably warm week is often what triggers a burst pipe in homes built quickly during Tea's boom years, when insulation details around plumbing chases sometimes received less attention than they deserved.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Tea

When you call (605) 965-2727, the first priority is stopping ongoing water entry if it hasn't been addressed yet. From there, the process follows a clear sequence calibrated to Tea's typical construction:

**Moisture mapping before anything is moved.** Our IICRC-certified technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full extent of saturation. In slab-on-grade homes this step is especially important because water migrates invisibly beneath hard flooring.

**Standing water removal.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from affected rooms. On new construction with engineered wood subfloor systems, speed here is critical: those materials begin to swell and delaminate faster than traditional plywood.

**Structural drying.** High-capacity desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, combined with directional air movers, are positioned based on the moisture map rather than placed at random. Drying logs are kept throughout so documentation is available for your insurance adjuster.

**Clearance readings.** We don't call a job complete based on a visual check. Moisture readings at the affected materials must return to normal range for the region before equipment is pulled.
