## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Yankton

Every job starts with a walk-through to document what trades were on-site, what materials were used, and what the general contractor or homeowner considers the punch-list priority. That conversation shapes the sequence of the clean.

**Rough clean first.** Larger debris, scrap material, and packaging come out before any detail work begins. Attempting fine cleaning over construction rubble wastes time and damages equipment.

**Top-down detail clean.** Ceiling fans, light fixtures, and the tops of cabinets collect the heaviest dust accumulation. We work downward — walls, countertops, appliances, and finally floors — so that dislodged particles don't re-contaminate surfaces we've already cleaned.

**Surface-specific treatments.** Hardwood floors get a dry microfiber pass followed by an appropriate wood-safe cleaner. Tile receives grout haze remover where needed, then neutralization and a final rinse. Windows get a two-stage clean: first removing construction adhesive or paint overspray with a razor scraper, then a streak-free finish wash.

**Final inspection and touch.** We walk the space with the client before signing off. If a contractor returns for a punch-list item after our clean, we can schedule a return touch to address whatever new mess that work creates.
