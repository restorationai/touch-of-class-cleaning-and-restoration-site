## Why Salem Properties See Roofing Problems Earlier Than Expected

The South Dakota plains are hard on roofing materials in ways that coastal or heavily wooded markets simply aren't. Salem and the surrounding McCook County area experience sharp freeze-thaw cycling every spring — overnight lows still dipping below freezing while afternoon sun heats dark shingles to 150°F or more. That thermal shock accelerates shingle brittleness and loosens the sealant strips that hold tabs down during wind events. Add hail that averages golf-ball size in bad years and sustained southwest winds that push water under lifted edges, and a roof that looks fine from the street can be failing at the decking level.

Older homes in the Downtown Salem area — many built in the mid-20th century — often have original or once-replaced roofs with a single layer of 3-tab shingles over skip sheathing rather than solid decking. When those roofs are replaced, the decking situation has to be assessed and often upgraded before new materials go down. Skipping that step is one of the most common reasons a new roof underperforms in this climate.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Salem

Every project starts with a documented inspection — photos of the field of the roof, close-ups of flashing at chimneys and pipe boots, and a look at the decking condition from inside the attic if access allows. We note hail strikes, granule accumulation in gutters, and any soft spots that signal decking rot. That documentation matters whether you're filing an insurance claim or simply making a capital improvement decision.

Once scope is agreed on, material selection is next. For Salem's wind exposure, we default to architectural shingles with a Class 4 impact rating and a minimum 130-mph wind warranty — a meaningful upgrade from standard 3-tab that holds up better through the storm seasons that track along the I-90 corridor. Underlayment, ice-and-water shield at eaves and valleys, and proper attic ventilation are all part of the installation, not optional add-ons. Ventilation is especially important here: inadequate soffit-to-ridge airflow in a cold South Dakota winter creates ice dams and moisture buildup that shorten roof life regardless of shingle quality.

Tear-off, decking repair, and installation are typically completed in one to two days for a standard residential footprint. We haul debris the same day and do a magnet sweep of the yard before we leave.
