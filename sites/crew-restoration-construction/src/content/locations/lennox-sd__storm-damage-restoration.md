## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Lennox

The first priority after any storm event is stopping new damage from compounding the old. That means emergency tarping of breached rooflines, boarding windows where glass has failed, and a thorough moisture check of any interior space that was exposed — because water that enters through a compromised roof on a Tuesday can begin supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours if it isn't extracted and dried.

From there, the process moves through a documented scope of damage, which matters both for accurate repair estimates and for insurance claims. We photograph and catalog affected materials before anything is removed — structural sheathing, insulation, interior finishes — so there's a clear record of pre-repair conditions. Structural repairs follow: roof decking replacement, rafter or truss repair where impact has caused deflection, siding replacement, and window or door reframing when frames have racked. We handle reconstruction through completion, so you're not coordinating between a mitigation company and a separate contractor to get your home back to livable condition.
