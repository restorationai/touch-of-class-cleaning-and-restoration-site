## Why Le Mars Properties See Water Damage Issues

The housing stock in Le Mars spans more than a century. Homes near the Plymouth County Courthouse and throughout the older residential blocks downtown were frequently built with full or partial basements, stone or clay-tile foundations, and cast-iron or galvanized plumbing that has long since exceeded its service life. ## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Le Mars

The first thing a technician does on arrival is locate the water source and stop it if it is still active. From there, the work follows a defined sequence rather than a guess.

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters establish exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and under flooring. Water behind plaster can read dry at the surface while the lath stays saturated several inches in.

**Extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and surface saturation from carpet, hardwood, concrete, and subfloor. The faster extraction happens, the shorter the drying cycle and the lower the risk of secondary damage.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers are placed according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. Readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented, not estimated.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** Once materials reach target moisture levels, affected areas are treated to inhibit microbial growth before any reconstruction work begins.

Documentation runs through the entire process, which matters significantly when an insurance claim is involved.
