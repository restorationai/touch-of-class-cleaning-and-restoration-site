## Why Luverne Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Luverne sits at the edge of the Coteau des Prairies, and the region's heavy clay soils don't absorb moisture the way sandier ground does. After a significant prairie storm — the kind that rolls in fast off the open land west of Blue Mounds State Park — municipal storm and sanitary sewer systems can become overwhelmed. When inflow and infiltration push the system past capacity, the path of least resistance is back through floor drains, basement toilets, and utility sinks in low-lying homes.

Older housing stock compounds the problem. Many residences in and around Downtown Luverne were built in the mid-20th century with clay tile sewer laterals. Those laterals crack, shift, and allow tree roots to colonize the line over decades. A slow-developing blockage can go unnoticed until a heavy rain event tips the system over the edge and raw sewage appears in a finished basement. Septic systems on the rural edges of Rock County face their own pressures: saturated soil after a wet spring reduces the drain field's ability to accept effluent, and a failing system can back up into the home within hours of the ground becoming waterlogged.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Luverne

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification — which means the cleanup protocol is more involved than a standard water loss. The process begins with containing the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, then removing standing sewage and all porous materials that absorbed it. Drywall, carpet, and insulation that have contacted raw sewage cannot be dried in place; they need to come out.

Once the space is cleared, every hard surface is treated with an EPA-registered disinfectant rated for Category 3 contamination. We apply it systematically — subfloor, framing, concrete, HVAC registers if the system was running during the event — and allow appropriate dwell time before wiping and inspecting. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers sized to the space. In Luverne's humid summer months, ambient moisture in the air slows drying curves, so we monitor moisture readings daily and adjust equipment until framing and subfloor readings return to pre-loss baselines. Documentation of every reading is kept for your insurance file.

## Luverne Insurance Coordination for Sewage Claims

Sewage backup coverage is not automatically included in a standard homeowner's policy — it typically requires a separate sewer backup endorsement. That distinction matters when you're filing a claim, because insurers will ask whether the source was a municipal line failure, a private lateral, or a septic system, and each may be handled differently. We document the loss thoroughly: photographs of affected materials before removal, moisture readings, a written scope of work, and itemized line items that match the format adjusters expect. If your carrier needs to send their own inspector, we can coordinate access and answer technical questions about the remediation scope.
