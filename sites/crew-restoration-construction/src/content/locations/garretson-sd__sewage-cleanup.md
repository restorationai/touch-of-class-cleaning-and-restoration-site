## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Garretson

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 — "black water" — under the IICRC S500 standard, meaning it carries pathogens that require full containment and hospital-grade sanitization, not just extraction. Here is how we work through a job in Garretson:

**Containment and safety setup.** We isolate the affected area immediately to prevent cross-contamination to living spaces. Technicians use PPE rated for biohazard exposure before any material is touched.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that have contacted Category 3 water are bagged and disposed of per South Dakota solid waste guidelines. There is no "dry it and save it" option with raw sewage.

**Surface cleaning and antimicrobial treatment.** Every hard surface in the affected zone is scrubbed, then treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. We pay particular attention to subfloor seams and floor joist cavities, where sewage wicks upward and hides.

**Drying and dehumidification.** After sanitization, commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. In Garretson's colder months, we account for the fact that unheated basements and crawl spaces dry more slowly — equipment run times are extended accordingly.

**Post-cleanup documentation.** We photograph every stage and provide a written scope of work, which your insurance adjuster will need.
