## Our Contents Restoration Process in Harrisburg

The first step is a careful, itemized pack-out. Every piece of furniture, every box, every textile gets photographed and catalogued before it leaves your property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of condition at the time of loss.

From there, contents go to a controlled environment where each category gets the appropriate treatment. Hard surfaces are cleaned with methods matched to the type of contamination, smoke residue, sewage-affected water, and clean-water flooding each require a different approach. Soft goods like upholstered furniture and area rugs go through professional cleaning and, where possible, deodorization. Electronics and sensitive documents are assessed separately, because water and heat damage those items differently than they affect wood or fabric.

Storage is climate-controlled and secured. Your belongings don't sit in an open warehouse while your home is being dried and rebuilt. When the structure is ready, we coordinate a scheduled pack-back so items return to the right rooms in the right order, reducing the chaos of move-in day after a stressful loss.

Crew Restoration & Construction is IICRC certified, which means the cleaning and drying standards applied to your contents follow the same industry framework used for the structure itself.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier in Harrisburg

Contents claims are often the most disputed part of a property loss. Carriers want documentation, and the more detailed your pack-out inventory, the smoother that process tends to go. We photograph items, note pre-loss condition, and provide the kind of organized records that support a clean claim submission.

If your policy includes contents coverage, restoration is almost always preferable to replacement from the carrier's perspective. A professionally cleaned and restored piece of furniture costs less than a replacement line item, and it preserves something that may have personal value beyond its dollar amount. We communicate directly with adjusters when needed so you're not translating between a technical restoration process and an insurance form.
