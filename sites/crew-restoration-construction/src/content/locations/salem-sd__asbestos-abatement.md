## Why Salem Properties See Asbestos Concerns

McCook County's housing stock tells the story plainly. Many of the older homes clustered near Downtown Salem and around the McCook County Courthouse were built between the 1940s and the early 1980s — precisely the window when asbestos appeared in everything from joint compound to vinyl floor backing to attic insulation batts. Popcorn ceilings applied before 1978 are a particular concern; the spray-applied texture was frequently mixed with chrysotile asbestos to improve fire resistance and adhesion, and sanding or scraping even a small section without testing first can release fibers throughout a room.

South Dakota's climate adds a layer of urgency. The freeze-thaw cycles that hit the region every winter cause building materials to expand and contract repeatedly. Over decades, that mechanical stress cracks older floor tiles, loosens pipe wrap, and causes ceiling texture to flake — all of which can disturb asbestos-containing materials that were otherwise stable. A cracked 9×9 floor tile in a 1960s basement is not just a cosmetic problem; it is a potential exposure point that needs professional assessment before anyone starts demo work.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Salem

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and bulk sampling. We collect material samples from suspect areas — ceiling texture, floor tiles, pipe insulation, drywall compound, roofing materials — and send them to an accredited laboratory for polarized light microscopy analysis. You receive written results before any abatement work begins, which also gives you documentation your insurance carrier or a future buyer can review.

Once a positive identification is confirmed, the work area is isolated with poly sheeting and negative air pressure units equipped with HEPA filtration. This containment prevents cross-contamination to adjacent rooms, which matters especially in occupied homes where family members may be present in other parts of the house during the project. Abatement technicians work in full PPE — respirators, disposable coveralls, gloves — and all removed material is wetted, double-bagged in labeled 6-mil poly, and transported to a licensed disposal facility that accepts regulated asbestos waste. South Dakota's disposal requirements apply to every load we haul, and we maintain the chain-of-custody paperwork throughout.

Final air clearance sampling is conducted after cleanup and before containment is removed. That clearance report is part of the project file we hand you at closeout.

## Salem Insurance & Documentation

Asbestos abatement is not always a covered line item under a standard homeowner's policy, but it frequently becomes relevant when a covered loss — a burst pipe, a hail-damaged roof, a fire — requires demolition of materials that turn out to contain asbestos. In those situations, the abatement must be completed before reconstruction can begin, and insurers need a documented chain of evidence: lab reports, containment logs, disposal manifests, and air clearance results. We produce all of that paperwork in a format that adjusters recognize, which reduces back-and-forth and keeps your claim moving.
