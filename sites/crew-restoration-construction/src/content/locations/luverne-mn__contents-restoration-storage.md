## Why Luverne Properties See Contents Losses

Rock County's prairie climate is the defining factor. Severe thunderstorms in late spring and early summer can produce hail large enough to breach rooflines, and the same storm systems that make Blue Mounds State Park dramatic also push wind-driven rain into attic spaces and around window frames. Once water gets inside, it moves fast, and in older homes throughout the 56156 ZIP code, it has more places to hide.

Luverne's housing stock includes a meaningful number of homes built before 1960, particularly in the blocks surrounding Downtown Luverne and near the Rock County Courthouse. Many of these homes have plaster-and-lath walls, original hardwood floors, and older cabinetry, all materials that hold moisture longer than modern construction and that require more careful handling during contents pack-out. A couch or bookcase left in a room with wet plaster walls will continue absorbing humidity even after visible water is gone. Removing contents early breaks that cycle.

Fire losses are the other common driver. Heating equipment works hard through southwestern Minnesota winters, and chimney or furnace-related fires leave soot that travels farther than most homeowners expect. Smoke residue can coat contents two or three rooms away from the fire's origin, and that residue becomes harder to remove the longer it sits.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Luverne

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition rating. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was taken and where it went.

From there, contents move to a controlled storage facility where temperature and humidity are managed, not a warehouse where conditions vary by season. Items that can be restored go through cleaning appropriate to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for soft contents with smoke odor, and specialized document drying for papers and photographs. IICRC-certified technicians guide the process, applying industry standards to decisions about what can be saved and what cannot.

Once the structure at your Luverne property is dry, repaired, and cleared, contents are returned, placed, and unpacked. The goal is a documented chain of custody from the moment we arrive to the moment your belongings are back in place.

## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about older homes in the Downtown Luverne area: many were built with full basements that have minimal window egress and limited mechanical ventilation. When a basement floods or a main-floor fire sends smoke downward, basement contents, stored furniture, seasonal items, archived documents, absorb odor and moisture at a higher rate than the same items would on an upper floor. We factor that in during pack-out, prioritizing basement contents for faster processing and more aggressive odor treatment, because letting them sit even a day longer than necessary usually means a harder restoration job.
