## Why Le Mars Properties See Accelerated Roofing Wear

Plymouth County sits squarely in a corridor where summer hail tracks are common and winter wind chill accelerates the brittleness of aging asphalt shingles. Le Mars's mix of historic homes — many built before 1960, particularly in and around Downtown Le Mars — and mid-century ranch-style construction means roofing contractors encounter a wide range of decking conditions: original skip-sheathing under old three-tab shingles, layers of re-roofing that have added weight over the decades, and ridge lines that have shifted slightly as foundations settled in the region's clay-heavy soil.

That clay soil matters more than most homeowners realize. Seasonal ground movement puts stress on exterior walls and, by extension, on the roof-to-wall connections. ## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Le Mars

Every project starts with a full deck inspection — not just a visual pass from the ladder, but a hands-on check for soft spots, delaminated OSB, and any areas where ice dams have driven moisture under the existing underlayment. In Le Mars, ice damming along north-facing eaves is a recurring issue, so we pay particular attention to eave protection: a self-adhering ice-and-water barrier extended well up the slope, not just the code-minimum 24 inches.

Once the deck is confirmed sound (or repaired), we install synthetic underlayment rated for high-wind exposure — a practical choice given that Plymouth County sees gusts that can exceed 60 mph during spring storm systems. Shingle selection is matched to the home's profile: architectural shingles with a Class 4 impact rating are a common recommendation for Le Mars properties given the hail exposure, though we walk through the options with each homeowner so the final choice fits both the budget and the insurance picture.

Flashing at chimneys, skylights, and wall intersections is replaced as a standard part of any full roof replacement — not left in place unless it genuinely passes inspection. Step flashing on dormers and additions, common on the older homes near Downtown Le Mars, gets particular attention because those are the spots where slow leaks hide for years before showing up on a ceiling.

## Insurance and Documentation for Le Mars Roofing Claims

Hail and wind damage claims are among the most common homeowner insurance events in northwest Iowa, and the documentation process matters as much as the repair itself. We photograph the damage systematically — granule loss patterns, impact marks on soft metals like gutters and vents, and any lifted or cracked shingles — in a format that aligns with what adjusters are looking for.
