## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Hinton

The first step on any water loss is a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water behind plaster or beneath vinyl flooring can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated, so we map the full extent of the loss before extraction begins.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Standing water extraction** using truck-mounted and portable units capable of removing hundreds of gallons from flooded basement or main-floor spaces.
- **Structural drying** with commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, air movers positioned to maximize airflow through wall cavities and floor assemblies.
- **Daily moisture monitoring** to track drying progress against the IICRC S500 standard, adjusting equipment placement as readings change.
- **Controlled demolition** of materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated insulation, compromised drywall, or subfloor sections that have begun to swell or delaminate.
- **Documentation** throughout, including photos, moisture logs, and scope notes formatted for insurance carrier review.

The goal is to reach documented dry standard before any reconstruction begins, not to rush to the finish line and leave hidden moisture behind.
