## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Brandon

Every contents job starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your home. Each item is photographed, catalogued by room, and assigned a condition rating. This documentation protects you during the insurance claim process and gives us a clear return checklist when your home is ready.

From there, the pack-out team moves your belongings to a climate-controlled storage facility where they stay protected during the structural repair phase — whether that takes a week or several months. Items that need cleaning go through the appropriate process for their material type: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and collectibles, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for fabrics and upholstered pieces carrying smoke or musty odors, and dry-cleaning or wet-cleaning for textiles depending on fiber content and dye stability. Electronics are assessed separately, since smoke residue and corrosion from water contact require a different evaluation than physical cleaning alone.

Once your home passes the structural clearance, we schedule a coordinated return and place every item back in its original room based on the inventory photos. Nothing gets dropped at the door and left for you to sort.
