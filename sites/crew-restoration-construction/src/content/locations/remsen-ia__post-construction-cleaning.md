Remsen's housing stock compounds the challenge. ## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Remsen

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through before a single piece of equipment comes off the truck. We document what trades were on site, what materials were installed, and where the highest concentrations of debris are — because the cleaning sequence matters. Rough cleaning before fine cleaning, top-to-bottom always, and HVAC last so that disturbed particulate doesn't re-coat freshly cleaned surfaces.

**Rough clean:** Bulk debris removal — scrap lumber, packaging, wire off-cuts, leftover fasteners. We sweep and HEPA-vacuum all horizontal surfaces, including the tops of door frames and window casings that a standard cleaning crew misses.

**Detail clean:** This is where the specialty work happens. Drywall compound haze on windows gets removed with appropriate glass-safe solutions. Grout lines are cleaned before sealer is applied. Paint overspray on hardware is addressed with solvent-matched to the finish type. Adhesive residue on flooring is lifted without pulling up the finish coat.

**Final clean:** White-glove inspection pass — every light fixture, every cabinet interior, every baseboard return. We use microfiber at this stage, not cotton rags, because microfiber traps particulate rather than redistributing it.

**HVAC and duct registers:** We pull registers, vacuum the duct collar, and wipe down the register face before reinstalling. For larger renovations, we can coordinate with a duct cleaning specialist if the scope warrants it.
