## Why Merrill Properties See Mold Issues

Merrill sits in a low-lying corridor where the Floyd River's flood plain edges close to residential streets. After a heavy spring thaw or a summer storm that overwhelms drainage, water doesn't always stay outside. Older homes in East Merrill — many built in the mid-twentieth century with minimal vapor barriers in their crawl spaces — are especially vulnerable. Concrete block foundations common in that era are porous by nature; they wick groundwater slowly and steadily, keeping the relative humidity in a basement or crawl space elevated long after the visible water is gone.

The region's climate adds another layer of pressure. Northwest Iowa winters are cold and dry, but the transition into spring brings rapid temperature swings that cause condensation to form on cold surfaces inside walls and attic spaces. That condensation, combined with any organic material — wood framing, paper-faced insulation, older cellulose ceiling tile — gives mold exactly what it needs to colonize. By the time a homeowner in Downtown Merrill notices discoloration on a ceiling or a persistent earthy odor, the colony is often already well-established behind the visible surface.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Merrill

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — moisture readings across walls, floors, and ceilings using thermal imaging and pin-type meters to locate the full extent of saturation, not just the visible damage. Mold follows moisture, so mapping where the water traveled tells us where the mold is hiding.

Once the scope is defined, we establish containment using poly sheeting and negative air pressure to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the removal process. This step matters especially in occupied homes where family members may be present during the day. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, subflooring — are removed and bagged for disposal per EPA guidelines. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. After physical removal, HEPA air scrubbers run continuously to filter airborne spores from the contained work area.

Before we close walls or restore finishes, we take post-remediation moisture readings to confirm the structure has reached acceptable drying targets. If a third-party air quality test is requested — something some insurers or buyers' inspectors require — we coordinate that with an independent party so the clearance result is unbiased.
