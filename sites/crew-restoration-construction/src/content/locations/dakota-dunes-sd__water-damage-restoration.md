## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Dakota Dunes

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls and under flooring before a single dehumidifier is placed. In riverside communities like Two Rivers, water often travels farther than it appears — a leak at a second-floor bathroom can wick down interior wall cavities and pool inside a first-floor soffit without showing visible staining for days.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Water extraction** — truck-mounted extractors pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and subfloor assemblies. For slab areas, we use specialty drying mats that draw moisture directly through concrete.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the psychrometric readings taken during inspection, not by guesswork.
- **Daily monitoring** — moisture readings are logged each day and compared against drying targets. We adjust equipment placement when readings plateau rather than waiting for the scheduled end date.
- **Controlled demolition if needed** — when wet insulation or saturated drywall cannot dry in place within acceptable timelines, we remove the minimum necessary material and document everything for your insurance claim.

The goal is to reach dry standard before secondary damage — mold colonization typically begins within 48 to 72 hours of a moisture event — has a chance to take hold.
