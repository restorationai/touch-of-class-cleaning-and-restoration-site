## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Rock Rapids

The first step on any water damage call is stopping the source if it has not already been stopped, then assessing the full scope of saturation using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. Water visible on the surface is rarely the whole story: it migrates under hardwood, behind baseboard trim, and into wall cavities where it cannot be seen without equipment.

Once the scope is mapped, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted or portable extraction units rated for high-volume removal. After bulk extraction, the drying phase begins. IICRC-certified drying protocols guide the placement of industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers to pull moisture out of structural materials systematically rather than just moving air around. Readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented, which matters for both structural integrity and insurance claims.
