## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Hinton

Every job begins with a contained assessment. Before any materials are moved or surfaces treated, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC zones. This is especially relevant in Hinton's older ranch-style and two-story homes where return-air vents are often centrally located and can distribute particles through the structure if work is done carelessly.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Removal of affected porous materials** — flooring, drywall, insulation, or soft goods that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are bagged, sealed, and transported to a licensed disposal facility in accordance with Iowa Department of Natural Resources regulations.
- **Disinfection of hard surfaces** — EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants are applied at dwell times sufficient to neutralize bloodborne pathogens and other infectious agents per OSHA Bloodborne Pathogens Standard (29 CFR 1910.1030) requirements.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment addresses residual odor at the molecular level rather than masking it.
- **Post-remediation verification** — surfaces are tested or visually cleared before the area is released for reconstruction or reoccupancy.

For sharps and needle cleanup, each item is individually containerized using puncture-resistant sharps containers before any other work begins — protecting our crew and preventing secondary injury.
