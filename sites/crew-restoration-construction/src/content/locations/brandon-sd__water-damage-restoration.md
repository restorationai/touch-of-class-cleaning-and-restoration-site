## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Brandon

The first step on every job is moisture mapping — not a visual scan, but thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, floors, and ceiling assemblies. Water travels along framing cavities and vapor barriers in ways that aren't visible, and skipping this step leads to hidden pockets of moisture that grow mold within three to five days.

Once the scope is mapped, water extraction begins using truck-mounted or portable extraction units sized to the volume of standing water. In finished basements — common in Brandon's newer homes — we remove baseboards and drill weep holes at the base of drywall to allow trapped moisture to escape before drying equipment is placed. Structural drying then runs with commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers positioned according to a drying plan, not just set in the middle of the room. Daily moisture readings track progress and determine when materials have reached acceptable dryness levels before any reconstruction begins.

Most standard homeowners' policies in South Dakota cover sudden and accidental water damage — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an ice dam backup — but not gradual seepage or maintenance-related deterioration.
