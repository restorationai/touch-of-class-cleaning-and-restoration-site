## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Garretson

Every board-up and tarping job starts with a walk of the exterior to map every penetration — broken glass, kicked-in doors, roof punctures, soffit damage — before a single sheet of plywood goes up. That documentation matters for your insurance adjuster as much as it matters for the repair crew that follows.

For window and door openings, we cut plywood to fit flush against the frame, fasten it with structural screws rather than nails, and seal the perimeter to limit air and moisture infiltration. On roof work, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for the wind loads common to this part of South Dakota, secured with battens and weighted edges rather than just stapled at the ridge. A tarp that peels back in a 50-mph gust has done nothing — proper anchoring is the whole job.

When interior access is safe, we also document visible water intrusion lines, photograph affected materials, and note anything that will affect the drying or reconstruction scope. That information goes directly to the adjuster so the claim file reflects the full scope of loss from day one.
