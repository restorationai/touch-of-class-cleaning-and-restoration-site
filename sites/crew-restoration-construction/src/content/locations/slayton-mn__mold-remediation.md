## Why Slayton Properties See Elevated Mold Risk

Slayton sits at the heart of Murray County's lake district, and that geography shapes the moisture conditions inside local buildings in specific ways. Spring thaw in this part of Minnesota arrives fast and heavy — frozen ground can't absorb meltwater quickly enough, and that water finds its way through foundation cracks, window wells, and aging sump systems. Homes in the Lake Sarah area are especially exposed: lots that slope toward the shoreline often see water intrusion in basements and crawl spaces every April and May.

The housing stock compounds the problem. Many Slayton homes were built in the mid-20th century, when vapor barriers were minimal or nonexistent and basement walls were poured without modern waterproofing membranes. Older balloon-frame construction — common in the blocks surrounding Downtown Slayton — allows moisture to travel vertically inside wall cavities, seeding mold in places that aren't visible until drywall is removed. By the time a musty smell reaches the living space, growth may already be established behind the finish surfaces.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Slayton

Every project begins with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration paths through walls, subfloors, and ceiling assemblies — because in Slayton's older homes, the visible staining is rarely the full extent of the problem.

Once we understand the scope, we establish containment barriers using 6-mil poly sheeting and negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps disturbed spores from spreading to unaffected rooms while remediation work is underway. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to Minnesota Pollution Control Agency guidelines.

After physical removal, we treat structural surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents and allow adequate dry-out time before any reconstruction begins. We don't close up walls until moisture readings confirm the assembly has returned to normal levels for this climate. Rushing that step is how mold comes back.

## Working with Insurance on Mold Claims in Slayton

Mold coverage varies significantly depending on whether the growth is tied to a sudden covered event — a burst pipe, a failed water heater — or to long-term moisture accumulation.
