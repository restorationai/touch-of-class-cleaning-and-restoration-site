## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Brookings

Every post-construction clean follows a sequenced approach, because order matters. Cleaning a window before the overhead debris is cleared just means cleaning it twice.

**Rough clean first.** Crews remove bulk debris — scrap lumber, packaging, broken tile, leftover fasteners — and bag or haul it off the property. This stage also includes sweeping and vacuuming framed cavities before drywall or trim is installed, if the schedule allows for phased cleaning.

**Detail clean second.** This is where the precision work happens: scraping paint overspray from glass, wiping down every cabinet interior, cleaning light fixtures without scratching new finishes, removing sticker residue from appliances, and vacuuming HVAC registers before the system runs for the first time. Drywall dust is particularly invasive — it finds its way into every horizontal surface and behind outlet covers. We treat it accordingly.

**Final clean third.** The final pass brings the space to occupancy-ready condition. Floors are cleaned and inspected under raking light to catch streaks or haze. Bathrooms are sanitized. Windows get a streak-free finish inside and out. The goal is that a property manager, buyer, or building inspector can walk through without finding a single item on a punch list that falls on the cleaning crew.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans on occupied homes, construction dust removal from HVAC systems, or cleaning after partial remodels where furniture and belongings are still present — the process is adapted to protect what's staying while thoroughly addressing what the construction work left behind.
