## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Canton

The first priority on any water loss is stopping what's still moving. A technician identifies the source, whether that's a failed appliance line, a burst supply pipe, or groundwater intrusion, and confirms it's controlled before extraction begins. Standing water removal comes next, using truck-mounted and portable extractors calibrated for the volume and the surface type. Hardwood floors and plaster walls common in older Canton homes require lower-pressure extraction passes to avoid further mechanical damage.

Once bulk water is out, the team sets moisture readings across every affected material: subfloor, wall cavities, ceiling assemblies, and any adjacent rooms that share framing. Drying equipment, industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers depending on ambient conditions, is placed according to a drying plan, not just scattered around the room. Readings are logged daily so the drying curve is documented for your insurance carrier and so the crew knows when materials have genuinely reached safe moisture content, not just surface-dry.
