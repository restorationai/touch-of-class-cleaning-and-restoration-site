## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Orange City

Every post-construction clean follows a top-down, inside-out sequence so debris doesn't migrate to already-cleaned surfaces. The process typically moves through four stages:

**Rough clean** — Removing bulk debris, scrap material, and packaging. We coordinate with the general contractor on dumpster access and staging, which matters more than it sounds on tighter Downtown Orange City lots where alley access is limited.

**Detail clean** — HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all surfaces including inside cabinets, above ceiling fan blades, inside window frames, and along baseboard edges. Drywall dust is fine enough to become airborne again with foot traffic, so this stage uses commercial HEPA equipment rather than shop vacs that recirculate particulate.

**Surface treatment** — Cleaning and polishing hard surfaces, removing construction adhesive and silicone residue from glass, scrubbing grout haze from tile, and treating any alkaline joint-compound residue on hardwood or plaster surfaces with appropriate pH-balanced cleaners.

**Final walk and specialty items** — Window cleaning inside and out, light fixture cleaning, appliance wipe-down, and any specialty items called out in the scope: pressure washing exterior concrete, cleaning HVAC registers, or deodorizing spaces where new materials off-gassed during a long construction period.

For commercial projects — retail spaces, office buildouts, or facilities connected to Northwestern College — we can also coordinate post-clean air quality verification if the tenant or property manager requires documentation before occupancy.
