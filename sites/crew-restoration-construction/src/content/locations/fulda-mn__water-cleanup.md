## Our Water Cleanup Process in Fulda

The first step on any water damage call is assessment, not assumption. Moisture meters and thermal imaging reveal what the eye misses, including water that has wicked up behind drywall or settled beneath vinyl plank flooring. Once the scope is mapped, water extraction begins using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment calibrated for the volume present. Drying is documented throughout, which matters for insurance purposes and for your own peace of mind.
