## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lennox

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage cleanup. Category 3 water — the classification that covers raw sewage and septic overflow — requires a containment and removal protocol that goes well beyond extraction and drying.

The process starts with assessing how far contamination has traveled. Sewage wicks into porous materials fast: concrete block, OSB subfloor, drywall below the flood line, and insulation batts all hold pathogens even after visible water is gone. We remove standing sewage using truck-mounted extraction, then identify and remove contaminated porous materials that cannot be safely dried and disinfected in place. Hard surfaces — concrete floors, tile, PVC pipe exteriors — are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination.

Once the space is cleared and treated, we run commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers to bring structural moisture levels down to pre-loss readings. A final sanitization pass addresses residual odor compounds and any surface biofilm that formed during the event. We document conditions at each stage with photographs and moisture readings, which matters when you're filing a claim with your homeowner's insurer.
