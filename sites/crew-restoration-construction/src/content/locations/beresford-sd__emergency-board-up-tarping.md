## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Beresford

Every job starts with a rapid exterior assessment — we walk the full perimeter before touching anything, photographing every breach point for your insurance documentation. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind conditions, secured with lumber battens screwed into sound decking rather than nailed, which matters on older Beresford roofs where decking may already be brittle. Tarps are lapped and sealed at ridges and valleys to prevent wind lift during the next weather event.

For window and door openings — common after fire damage or a break-in — we cut plywood panels to fit flush with the exterior frame, fasten them with structural screws into the framing members (not just the casing), and apply weatherstrip foam at the perimeter to block wind infiltration. On brick-faced homes, we use masonry anchors rather than lag screws to avoid cracking the mortar. Every panel is labeled on the interior face so your contractor knows exactly what's behind it when permanent repairs begin.
