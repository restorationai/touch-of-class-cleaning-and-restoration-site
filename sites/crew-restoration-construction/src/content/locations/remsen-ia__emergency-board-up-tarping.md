## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Remsen

Every job starts with a fast visual survey of the full structure — not just the obvious breach. Fire damage, for instance, can compromise roof decking several feet beyond the visible char line, and boarding only the broken windows while leaving a weakened soffit gap defeats the purpose. We photograph and document every opening before we cover it, which also gives your insurance adjuster a clear before-and-after record.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for the wind exposure typical of open Iowa terrain, anchored with 2×4 cap boards screwed through the tarp into the roof deck rather than relying on bungee straps or rope. On skip-sheathed roofs common in older Remsen homes, we add plywood bridging panels beneath the tarp to create a solid nailing surface. Window and door board-ups use exterior-grade OSB cut and fitted on-site, secured with structural screws rather than nails so the boards can be removed cleanly when permanent repairs begin.

We also check that every utility entry point — gas meter, electrical service entrance, HVAC penetrations — is accounted for in the scope. A boarded-up home with an unsecured dryer vent is still an open invitation for moisture and pests.
