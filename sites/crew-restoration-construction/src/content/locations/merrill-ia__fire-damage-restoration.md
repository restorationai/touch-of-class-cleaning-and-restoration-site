## Why Merrill Properties Face Distinctive Fire Damage Challenges

Merrill sits in Plymouth County along the Floyd River, and its housing stock reflects that history — a mix of early-twentieth-century farmhouse-style homes and modest postwar construction, many with balloon-frame walls that act like chimneys during a fire. Balloon framing has no fire blocking between floors, so flames and smoke migrate vertically inside wall cavities with almost no resistance. By the time a fire is extinguished, smoke residue can be present two or three floors away from the ignition point, in spaces that look completely untouched.

The region's cold winters add another layer of complexity. Firefighting water freezes quickly on exterior surfaces in January and February, and the moisture driven into wall assemblies during suppression doesn't evaporate on its own when temperatures drop below freezing. Structural fire damage in Merrill often means dealing with both smoke contamination and trapped moisture simultaneously — two problems that require different equipment and sequencing to address correctly.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Merrill

The first hour on-site is assessment, not demolition. Before any material is removed, the crew documents the full scope — photographing affected rooms, probing wall cavities for smoke migration, and moisture-mapping areas where suppression water penetrated. That documentation matters for insurance claims and for building an accurate scope of work.

Once the assessment is complete, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Structural stabilization** — boarding windows, tarping the roof if the fire breached the envelope, and confirming the building is safe to work in.
- **Debris removal and controlled demolition** — pulling only what cannot be restored. Salvageable framing, flooring, and trim is cleaned rather than replaced when the damage allows.
- **Smoke and soot cleaning** — dry-chemical sponges for loose soot, followed by alkaline or acidic cleaners matched to the surface type. Protein residue from kitchen fires requires different chemistry than the dry soot from a structural fire.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to reach the odor molecules embedded in porous materials that surface cleaning cannot access.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines, not just until the space feels dry.
- **Reconstruction** — framing, insulation, drywall, and finish work handled in-house so the project doesn't stall waiting for a separate contractor.

## Local Note

Homes within a few blocks of Merrill City Park tend to be among the older properties in town, and several have the original plaster-on-lath wall systems rather than drywall.
