## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Fulda

The first step when we arrive at a Fulda property is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed for damage type — water, smoke, soot, or mold. This documentation matters both for the homeowner's records and for the insurance claim file.

Salvageable items are carefully packed out and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility, where they are kept secure while structural drying and repairs proceed on the property. Contents cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for small hard goods and electronics, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke-affected fabrics and upholstery, and controlled drying chambers for water-damaged books, documents, and artwork. Furniture restoration — refinishing, re-gluing, reupholstering — is handled as a separate phase once items are fully stabilized.

Once the home is ready, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in their original rooms. We provide a final inventory reconciliation so homeowners know exactly what was restored, what was replaced, and what was deemed a total loss.
