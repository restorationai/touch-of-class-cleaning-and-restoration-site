## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Elk Point

Sewage cleanup isn't a single task — it's a sequence, and skipping steps creates problems that show up weeks later as odor, mold, or structural damage.

We start with containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into living spaces. Then we extract all standing waste using truck-mounted or portable extraction units suited to the space — whether that's a finished basement, an unfinished crawl space, or a utility room. Solid waste and contaminated porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor sections) are bagged and disposed of according to South Dakota Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources guidelines for Class III biohazardous waste.

After extraction and demo, every affected surface is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants formulated for Category 3 water — the classification that covers raw sewage. We apply antimicrobial agents to framing and concrete to address bacteria and prevent secondary mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in a warm, humid space. Structural drying with commercial dehumidifiers and air movers follows, with moisture readings tracked until materials return to acceptable levels. We document every step with photos and moisture logs — useful for both your peace of mind and your insurance adjuster.
