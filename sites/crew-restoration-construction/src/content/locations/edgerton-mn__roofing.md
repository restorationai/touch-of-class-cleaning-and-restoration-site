## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Edgerton

Every project starts with a full deck inspection before a single bundle of shingles is ordered. We photograph existing conditions, probe for soft decking, and check the fascia and soffit for rot that would undermine a new drip edge installation. In Edgerton's climate, proper ice-and-water shield placement isn't optional — we run it a minimum of 24 inches inside the warm wall line, which in a cold Minnesota winter is the difference between a roof that performs and one that leaks by February.

Once the deck is confirmed sound (or repaired to be sound), we install synthetic underlayment rated for high-wind zones, then work from eave to ridge with shingles rated for the wind speeds this part of Pipestone County regularly sees. Ridge ventilation is sized to match the intake at the soffits — a step many contractors skip, but one that directly affects how long your new roof lasts by preventing the heat and moisture buildup that degrades shingles from the inside out. Cleanup is same-day; we run a magnet sweep for fasteners before we leave the property.
