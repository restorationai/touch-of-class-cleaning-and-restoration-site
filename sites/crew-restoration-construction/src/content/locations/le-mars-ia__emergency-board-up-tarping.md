## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Le Mars

When we get a call from a 51031 address, the first thing we do is gather enough information to load the right materials before leaving — plywood thickness, approximate opening count, roof pitch if tarping is involved. On-site, the sequence is straightforward but deliberate:

1. **Hazard assessment first.** Fire-damaged structures, in particular, can have compromised floor joists or unstable masonry. We walk the perimeter before anyone goes inside.
2. **Roof tarping.** We use reinforced poly tarps rated for sustained wind, secured with furring strips screwed into sound decking — not just weighted down at the edges. A tarp that lifts in the next storm is worse than no tarp at all.
3. **Window and door board-up.** Exterior-grade plywood, cut to fit the rough opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails so panels can be removed cleanly when the permanent repair crew arrives.
4. **Documentation.** Every opening is photographed before and after boarding. That photo set goes directly into the insurance claim file.
