## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

Every contents job starts with a room-by-room inventory before a single box is moved. We photograph and log each item — serial numbers on electronics, descriptions of textiles, condition notes on furniture — so there's a complete record that serves both your memory and your insurance claim. That documentation matters especially in Harrisburg, where homeowners are often navigating a claim for the first time and need clear evidence of what was affected.

From there, affected contents are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Smoke-damaged items go through ultrasonic cleaning or ozone treatment depending on the material; water-damaged soft goods are assessed for salvageability and dried under controlled conditions. Electronics are evaluated by technicians before any power is applied. Furniture restoration after fire or water damage often involves refinishing, re-upholstering, or odor neutralization at the surface level — not just a wipe-down.

Once your home is structurally restored and cleared for re-occupancy, we coordinate the return and room placement of your belongings. Nothing comes back until the environment is ready to receive it.
