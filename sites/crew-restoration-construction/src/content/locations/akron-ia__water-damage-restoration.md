## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Akron

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment — not just the visible wet area, but the surrounding materials. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map exactly how far water has migrated into walls, flooring, and structural cavities. In older Akron homes, water often travels farther than it appears because original wood framing and plaster absorb and wick moisture in ways that modern drywall doesn't.

Once the scope is mapped, water removal comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from floors, carpeting, and subfloor assemblies. After extraction, we set drying equipment — commercial-grade dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — positioned specifically to the room geometry and material types present. Drying timelines are monitored with daily moisture readings; we don't pull equipment until the materials reach target levels, not just until things feel dry to the touch.

If structural materials — subfloor sheathing, wall framing, insulation — have absorbed enough moisture to be compromised, we handle controlled demolition and reconstruction as part of the same project. You won't be coordinating a separate contractor to finish what the water started.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage — a burst pipe, an appliance failure, a sump pump overflow — though coverage for gradual seepage or flood events varies by policy.
