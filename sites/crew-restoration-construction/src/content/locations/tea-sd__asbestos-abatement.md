## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Tea

Every abatement project starts with a thorough asbestos inspection — sampling suspect materials and sending them to an accredited laboratory for analysis. We do not guess, and we do not skip this step even when a material "looks like" a known product. Once results confirm the presence of asbestos, we build a site-specific abatement plan that accounts for the scope of the affected area, the condition of the materials, and the occupancy status of the building.

During abatement, the work area is isolated with negative air pressure containment using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. Technicians wear full PPE, and all removed materials are wetted to suppress fiber release before being sealed in labeled, puncture-resistant disposal bags. South Dakota follows EPA NESHAP regulations for asbestos disposal, which means waste must be transported to an approved landfill — we handle the manifest documentation so you are not navigating that paperwork alone. After removal, air clearance sampling confirms fiber levels have returned to acceptable thresholds before containment is dismantled and the space is released for reoccupancy or continued renovation.
