## Our Water Cleanup Process in Garretson

When Crew Restoration & Construction arrives at a Garretson property, the first step is moisture mapping. Using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters, the crew identifies where water has traveled beyond the visible wet zone. Water behind plaster or inside wall cavities can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated, so the mapping phase drives every decision that follows.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. After bulk water removal, the crew sets industrial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, positioned to create a drying chamber around affected materials. Drying logs are recorded daily so the process stays on track and insurance documentation stays complete.

Materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated carpet padding or swollen subfloor panels, are removed and disposed of before drying equipment is set. Leaving wet materials in place extends drying time and raises the risk of secondary mold growth. The IICRC-certified crew follows the S500 standard for water damage restoration throughout the process.
