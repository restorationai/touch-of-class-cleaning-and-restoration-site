## Why Kingsley's Housing Stock Shapes Every Renovation Decision

Plymouth County farm towns like Kingsley grew fast in the early twentieth century and then largely stopped. That means a significant share of homes in the 51028 ZIP code were built under construction standards that predate modern energy codes, modern load calculations, and modern moisture management. Original balloon-frame construction — common in homes built before 1950 — carries wall cavities that run uninterrupted from foundation to roofline, which creates real challenges when you're opening walls for a kitchen remodel or running new electrical for a bathroom addition. Insulation is often absent or compressed to near-zero R-value. Plaster-and-lath walls, still common in homes near the Kingsley-Pierson School corridor, behave differently from modern drywall: they're harder to cut cleanly, they hide surprises behind them, and they require a different finishing sequence to match original texture.

Soil movement is another factor that doesn't show up on a renovation estimate until you start pulling flooring.
