## Why Hull Properties See Mold Issues

Hull sits in a region where the soil holds moisture long after a rain event, and the area's agricultural surroundings mean humidity levels inside older structures can spike quickly when field irrigation is running and windows are left open. Many of the homes in and around Downtown Hull were built in an era when vapor barriers were minimal or absent entirely — a common pattern across small Dutch-American communities in northwest Iowa where homes were built to last structurally but not necessarily with modern moisture management in mind. That combination of high ambient humidity, older construction, and limited sub-slab ventilation creates ideal conditions for mold growth inside wall cavities, beneath flooring, and in attic spaces where condensation collects through winter.

Basement moisture is the most frequent trigger. Sioux County's clay-heavy soils drain slowly, so water from a heavy rain or a failed sump pump doesn't just evaporate — it wicks through foundation walls over days and weeks, keeping relative humidity above 60% in spaces that rarely get direct sunlight or airflow.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hull

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping.
