## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Slayton

Every contents job starts with a room-by-room inventory. Each item is photographed, tagged, and logged before it leaves the property — a step that matters enormously for insurance documentation and for your own peace of mind. Nothing gets lumped into a box without a record.

From there, items are transported to a controlled facility where the cleaning method is matched to the material:

- **Ultrasonic cleaning** for hard goods, collectibles, and non-porous items — sound waves dislodge soot and residue without abrasive contact.
- **Ozone and hydroxyl treatment** for soft goods, upholstered furniture, and textiles carrying smoke or mold odor.
- **Hand cleaning and dry-cleaning coordination** for delicate fabrics, artwork, and heirlooms that can't tolerate automated processes.
- **Climate-controlled storage** for items that can't return to the property while structural drying or reconstruction is underway.

Once the home is cleared for re-occupancy, items are returned in the same documented order they left — matched back to the inventory log so nothing is misplaced.
