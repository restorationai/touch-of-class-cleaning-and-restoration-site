## Why Kingsley Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Many of the homes in and around Downtown Kingsley were built in the mid-20th century, when construction materials and ventilation standards looked very different from today. Older wood-framed structures with original subfloor planking, unfinished basement walls, and minimal vapor barriers can allow biological contaminants to migrate into porous surfaces far more quickly than a newer build would. What appears contained to one room may have already reached wall cavities or crawl spaces beneath the main floor.

The Loess Hills climate compounds this. Western Iowa summers bring sustained humidity that accelerates biological breakdown and, with it, the spread of pathogens into surrounding materials. Acting promptly matters — not just for safety, but to limit how far remediation ultimately needs to reach.
