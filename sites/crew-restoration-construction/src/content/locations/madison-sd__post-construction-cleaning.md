## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Madison

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through to map the debris load and flag sensitive surfaces — original woodwork, specialty tile, fresh paint — before any equipment touches the space. From there, the process moves in a deliberate top-down sequence:

**Rough clean:** Bulk debris removal, disposal of scrap materials, and initial HEPA vacuuming of walls, ceilings, and exposed framing cavities. This phase catches the heavy particulate before it gets redistributed.

**Detail clean:** Window frames, sills, and tracks cleaned individually. Cabinet interiors wiped with appropriate solvents for adhesive and caulk overspray. Light fixtures disassembled where needed. Vent covers removed and registers vacuumed to the duct collar.

**Final clean:** Hard-surface floors cleaned with pH-appropriate solutions matched to the material — different chemistry for sealed concrete, LVP, tile grout, and hardwood. A final HEPA pass on all horizontal surfaces. Spot-check of outlets, switch plates, and door hardware for compound residue.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans after smoke or odor events, construction sites with biohazard concerns, or spaces requiring allergen reduction before occupancy — the scope is scoped separately during the walk-through.
