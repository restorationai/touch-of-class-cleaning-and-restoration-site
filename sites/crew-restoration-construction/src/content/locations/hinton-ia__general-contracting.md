- **Finish work and punch list** — Cabinetry, tile, trim, fixtures, and paint. We walk the completed space with you before we consider the job closed.

## Local Note

One thing that catches out-of-area contractors in Hinton: the soil in the West Fork Little Sioux River floodplain has a higher clay content than you'd expect this far from the river's main channel. Clay soil expands when it's wet and contracts when it dries, and that movement telegraphs into concrete slabs and foundation walls over time. Before any addition or basement finishing project, it's worth having the foundation assessed — not because every home has a problem, but because clay-driven movement that's been happening for 30 years can show up as a crack pattern that looks cosmetic until you open the wall and see it isn't. We flag this early so it doesn't become a mid-project surprise.
