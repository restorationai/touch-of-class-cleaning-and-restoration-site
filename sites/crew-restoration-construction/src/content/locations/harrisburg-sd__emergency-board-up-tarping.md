## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Harrisburg

When we arrive at a Harrisburg property, the first step is a rapid walk-around to identify every breach point — not just the obvious ones. Fire damage, for example, often weakens roof decking several feet beyond the visible char line, and a tarp anchored only over the burn area can still allow water intrusion through adjacent compromised sheathing.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps weighted and fastened with wood battens and screws driven into solid decking — not just weighted at the edges. On steeper-pitched roofs common in newer Harrisburg builds, we use ridge anchoring to prevent the tarp from riding up in high wind. For board-up work on broken windows and doors, we cut plywood to fit each opening precisely, fasten it into the structural framing (not just the trim), and apply edge sealing where weather exposure is a concern. Every opening gets documented with photos before and after closure, which matters when you're filing a claim.
