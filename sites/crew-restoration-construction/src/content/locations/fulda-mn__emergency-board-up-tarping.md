## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process, Calibrated for Fulda Conditions

When we receive a call from a 56131 address, the first step is a rapid phone assessment: what type of opening, how large, what's the current and forecast weather, and is the structure safe to access. That information shapes what materials and crew size we dispatch.

For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for high-wind conditions — not the lightweight blue tarps available at hardware stores. Tarps are secured with batten boards screwed into sound decking, not simply weighted at the edges, which means they hold through sustained wind rather than peeling back in the next gust. For openings in walls or broken windows, we cut and install OSB panels sized to the rough opening, sealed at the perimeter with foam backer and weatherproof tape to block wind-driven rain.

Fire-damaged structures require additional steps. After a fire, structural members may be weakened in ways that aren't immediately visible, so our crew assesses load-bearing walls and roof framing before staging on or near the structure.
