## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Crooks

The first step on any fire loss is stabilization — securing the structure, boarding openings, and documenting the full extent of damage before anything is disturbed. That documentation matters both for structural planning and for insurance purposes, so it's done methodically with photographs and written scope notes.

From there, the process moves through debris removal, dry-ice or soda blasting of char on structural members where salvage is possible, and HEPA vacuuming of all surfaces before any wet cleaning begins. Smoke residue has different chemistry depending on what burned — synthetic materials like carpet and furniture produce a wet, oily soot that smears if wiped dry, while wood and paper fires leave a dry, powdery residue that responds differently to cleaning agents. The approach is calibrated to what actually burned in the structure, not a one-size protocol.

Odor neutralization follows cleaning. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are both used depending on the materials present and the degree of penetration. Ductwork is cleaned and treated separately. Reconstruction — drywall, insulation, trim, paint, flooring — is handled in-house, which means one point of contact from the first call through the final walkthrough.
