## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Kingsley

The first priority after any storm event is stopping additional water from entering the structure. Our crews begin with emergency tarping and board-up — physical protection that buys time before the full scope of damage can be assessed. We document everything photographically before touching a damaged material, which matters enormously when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

Once the structure is secured, we move into water extraction and structural drying. Storm intrusion water is categorized differently than a clean supply-line leak — it has traveled across roofing, through insulation, and sometimes through debris, which affects how we handle affected materials. Wet insulation in an attic or wall cavity loses its R-value and becomes a mold substrate within 24 to 48 hours; we remove and bag it rather than attempt to dry it in place.

After drying is confirmed with moisture readings, we move into reconstruction: roof decking, sheathing, drywall, insulation, siding, and interior finishes. Handling both the mitigation and the rebuild under one contractor means the handoff between phases doesn't fall through the cracks — a common frustration when homeowners manage two separate companies.
