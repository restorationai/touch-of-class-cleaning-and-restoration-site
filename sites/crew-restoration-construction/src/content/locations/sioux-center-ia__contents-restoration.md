## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Sioux Center

When we arrive at a Sioux Center property, the first priority is triage: identifying which items can be restored and which are beyond recovery. We photograph and inventory every piece before anything moves — that documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home.

Items that can be saved are carefully packed and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility. Textiles go through ultrasonic or ozone treatment to remove smoke and microbial odors. Hard-surface items — wood furniture, cabinetry components, decorative pieces — are cleaned using methods matched to the material, whether that's dry-ice blasting for charred wood or specialized solvents for smoke-stained finishes. Electronics and documents receive separate handling, since heat and moisture damage them differently than they damage upholstery.

Throughout the process, your inventory stays accessible. You can request specific items if you need them while your home is being repaired. Once the structure is ready, we coordinate return delivery and help you place items back in the appropriate rooms.
