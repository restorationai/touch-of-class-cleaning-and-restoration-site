## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Rock Rapids

The process starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating. That documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing gets lost or confused during the restoration and storage phase.

Pack-out is handled with care — fragile items wrapped individually, electronics bagged and labeled, clothing sorted by type. From Rock Rapids, contents are transported to our secure climate-controlled storage facility where they're protected from additional humidity fluctuation during the weeks your home is being dried and repaired.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Hard surfaces — wood furniture, lamps, décor — are cleaned with appropriate agents and inspected for smoke or soot penetration. Once your home is ready, we coordinate delivery and placement — returning items to the rooms where they belong rather than stacking boxes at the door.
