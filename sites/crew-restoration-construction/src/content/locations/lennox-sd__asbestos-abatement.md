## Why Lennox Properties Carry a Higher Asbestos Risk

The residential growth that shaped neighborhoods around Downtown Lennox and the Lennox City Park area happened largely between the 1940s and the early 1980s — precisely the window when asbestos was woven into floor tiles, pipe insulation, roofing felt, joint compound, and textured ceiling coatings. Builders in smaller Lincoln County towns often used the same regional suppliers as larger Sioux Falls contractors, which means the same product lines showed up here. Homes that haven't been gut-renovated since original construction are likely to contain at least one asbestos-containing material, sometimes several in the same room.

The local climate adds pressure. South Dakota's freeze-thaw cycles cause foundation movement and pipe stress every winter. When pipe insulation cracks or drywall joint compound crumbles from seasonal shifting, fibers that were once stable become friable — meaning they can release into the air. A home that tested clean on a visual inspection five years ago may have changed status after a hard winter.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Lennox

Every project starts with a formal inspection and bulk sampling — we collect material samples from suspected areas and send them to an accredited laboratory before any removal work begins. You get written results, not a verbal estimate. That documentation matters for insurance claims, real estate transactions, and your own records.

Once testing confirms the presence of asbestos-containing material, the work area is isolated with negative-air pressure containment using HEPA-filtered equipment. This keeps fibers out of adjacent living spaces during removal. Workers wear full respiratory protection and disposable coveralls; all removed material is wetted, double-bagged, and transported to a licensed disposal facility following South Dakota Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources (DANR) requirements. After abatement, air clearance sampling confirms fiber counts are below action levels before containment is removed.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal — one of the most common requests we see in older Lennox homes — the process involves careful wet scraping to suppress dust, not dry sanding. The difference matters enormously for fiber release.
