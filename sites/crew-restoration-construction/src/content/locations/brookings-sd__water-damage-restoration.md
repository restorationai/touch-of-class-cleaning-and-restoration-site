## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Brookings

Every job starts with a moisture assessment using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Water that looks contained to one room rarely is — it travels along joists, wicks into wall cavities, and pools in low spots under flooring. We map the full moisture boundary before any equipment is placed.

Extraction comes next. For standing water, truck-mounted extraction units pull water faster and more completely than portable units, which matters when you're dealing with a finished basement in the Sexauer Park area that has carpet, padding, and a concrete slab underneath. Once bulk water is removed, we transition to structural drying: commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, air movers positioned to create directed airflow across wet surfaces, and daily moisture readings to track drying progress against the IICRC S500 standard.

For Brookings rental properties, we document every step with photographs and moisture logs — the kind of documentation a property manager needs to report the loss to their carrier and demonstrate to a tenant that the unit was properly remediated.
