## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Adrian

The first step on any water damage call is a moisture assessment, not just a visual one. Water behind plaster or under original hardwood floors in a mid-century Adrian home can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters give a full picture before any drying equipment is placed.

Once the scope is mapped, standing water is extracted with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access. In tighter Downtown Adrian basements with low clearance or narrow stairwells, portable units reach areas that larger equipment cannot. After bulk extraction, the focus shifts to structural drying: industrial air movers are positioned to create directed airflow across wet materials, and commercial dehumidifiers pull moisture from the air as it evaporates from walls, subfloor, and framing. Drying logs are kept throughout the process, which satisfies IICRC S500 documentation standards and gives your insurance adjuster the data they need to process the claim.

If the water source was a sewage backup or contaminated field drainage, affected porous materials are removed rather than dried in place, and antimicrobial treatment is applied to structural surfaces before any rebuild begins.
