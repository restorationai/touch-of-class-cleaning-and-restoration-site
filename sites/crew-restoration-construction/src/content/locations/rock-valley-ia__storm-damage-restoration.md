## Why Rock Valley Properties See Severe Storm Damage

The same geography that makes Sioux County productive farmland makes Rock Valley vulnerable to the full menu of Great Plains storm hazards: straight-line winds that routinely exceed 70 mph during summer derechos, hail that strips shingles and dents aluminum fascia, and rapid-onset flooding when the Rock River rises after a heavy rain event. Historically, low-lying properties near the Rock River have taken on water before county road crews can even assess culvert capacity — basements flood, crawl spaces saturate, and framing lumber begins absorbing moisture within the first few hours.

Older homes near Downtown Rock Valley — many built in the mid-20th century with wood-framed construction and original windows — are especially exposed. Single-pane glass fails under hail impact that modern double-pane units would survive, and older roof decks with thinner sheathing can delaminate after a single hard storm. That combination of aging materials and river-adjacent topography means storm losses here tend to involve multiple damage categories at once: structural, water intrusion, and debris — all needing attention in the right sequence.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Rock Valley

The first step on any storm job is a systematic exterior and interior assessment before any materials are moved or discarded. Tarping and board-up happens immediately to stop rain from entering through a compromised roof or broken window — every hour of additional water intrusion widens the scope of the job. We document everything photographically as we go, which matters for your insurance adjuster later.

Once the structure is stabilized, the work shifts to water extraction and drying if the storm brought interior moisture with it. Industrial air movers and desiccant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in walls, subfloor, and framing return to acceptable levels — skipping this step is how mold problems develop within the first week after a storm. Debris removal, structural repairs, and reconstruction follow in a sequence designed to avoid rework: you do not want new drywall going up over framing that hasn't fully dried.

For tree damage — a common outcome when storms push through the mature tree canopy around Rock Valley City Park and residential streets nearby — we coordinate safe removal of lodged limbs and assess the structural integrity of any roof or wall section the tree contacted before reconstruction begins.
