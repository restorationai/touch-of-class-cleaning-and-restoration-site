## Why Worthington Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

The housing stock in and around Downtown Worthington includes a significant number of homes built between the 1920s and the 1960s. That era of construction used materials and methods that hold up differently than modern builds — knob-and-tube wiring tucked inside plaster walls, balloon-frame construction that channels air (and moisture) vertically through the entire structure, and basement foundations that were poured before waterproofing membranes were standard practice. A kitchen remodel that looks straightforward on paper can open up into an electrical upgrade, a subfloor replacement, and a conversation about insulation the moment the first cabinet comes off the wall.

Homes in the Lake Okabena area carry an additional layer of complexity. One thing that catches out-of-area contractors off guard in Worthington is the combination of expansive clay soils and dramatic freeze-thaw cycles.
