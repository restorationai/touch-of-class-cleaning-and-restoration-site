## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Edgerton

Every project starts with a full exterior inspection before a single panel comes off. We check the condition of the housewrap or felt paper beneath existing siding, probe fascia and soffit for moisture damage, and assess whether the existing gutter pitch is recoverable or needs a full reset. In Edgerton's climate, skipping that diagnostic step means installing new material over a wet or rotted substrate — which fails prematurely regardless of product quality.

For siding replacement, we work with fiber cement and engineered wood products that hold paint and resist freeze-thaw cycling better than standard vinyl in extreme cold. Where vinyl is the right choice for budget or aesthetic reasons, we specify panels rated for the upper Midwest temperature range and install with appropriate expansion gaps — a detail that gets cut on rushed jobs and causes buckling by the second winter.

Gutter work here almost always involves upsizing. We typically recommend 5-inch or 6-inch seamless aluminum gutters fabricated on-site, with hanger spacing tightened to 18 inches rather than the standard 24 — because prairie wind torque on a long gutter run will pull standard-spaced hangers out of fascia within a few freeze-thaw cycles. Downspout extensions are sized and angled to move water well clear of foundations.
