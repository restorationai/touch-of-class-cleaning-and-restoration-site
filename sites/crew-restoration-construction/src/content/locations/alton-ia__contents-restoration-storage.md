## Why Alton Properties See Contents Damage

Alton sits in Sioux County on relatively flat terrain where the Floyd River drainage can slow runoff during heavy rain events or rapid snowmelt. Homes in and around Downtown Alton were built across several decades, and many of the older structures use plaster-and-lath interior walls rather than modern drywall. When water intrudes, plaster absorbs moisture slowly and releases it even more slowly, which means furniture, textiles, and stored items nearby stay in a humid environment far longer than homeowners expect. That extended exposure is where secondary damage, warping, odor, and surface contamination, takes hold on contents even after the visible water is gone.

Soot and smoke from heating equipment or a kitchen fire compounds the problem differently. Older wood-framed homes near St. Mary's Catholic Church and throughout the surrounding residential blocks often have less airtight construction, which lets smoke particles migrate into closets, upholstered furniture, and stored clothing more readily than in tightly built modern homes.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Alton

The process begins with a detailed inventory. Every item in the affected area is photographed, categorized, and logged before anything moves. That documentation matters for insurance purposes and gives families a clear record of what left the home and in what condition.

From there, contents fall into one of three paths:

- **Clean and return on-site**: Items that can be dried, deodorized, and cleaned without leaving the property stay put, reducing the disruption to daily life.
- **Pack-out and off-site restoration**: Furniture, electronics, artwork, clothing, and keepsakes that need more intensive treatment are carefully packed and transported to a controlled facility where technicians can work on them without the constraints of an active job site.
- **Secure storage**: Restored items are held in a climate-controlled environment until the structural work is complete and the home is ready to receive them.

Odor treatment for smoke or sewage-affected items uses hydroxyl or ozone technology depending on the material. Upholstered pieces get ultrasonic or foam cleaning. Hard surfaces are wiped, treated, and inspected before storage. Nothing goes back into the home until it passes a final check.
