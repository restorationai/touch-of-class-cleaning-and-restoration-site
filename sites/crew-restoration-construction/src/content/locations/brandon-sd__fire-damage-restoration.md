## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Brandon

After a fire, the sequence of work matters as much as the work itself. The process Crew Restoration & Construction follows in Brandon:

**Stabilization first.** Board-up, roof tarping, and temporary structural shoring happen before any cleaning begins. Brandon's wind exposure on the open prairie means an unsecured structure can sustain additional weather damage within hours.

**Scope documentation.** Every affected surface — walls, ceilings, HVAC ducts, insulation, cabinetry — is documented with photos and moisture readings before anything is removed. This documentation is the foundation of your insurance claim.

**Soot and smoke removal.** Different fire types leave different residue. Kitchen fires produce wet, greasy soot. Structural fires burning synthetic materials leave dry, powdery residue that smears if cleaned incorrectly. The cleaning method is matched to the residue type, not applied generically.

**Odor neutralization.** Smoke odor embedded in drywall, insulation, and wood framing requires more than surface treatment. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach into porous materials that wiping alone cannot address.

**Reconstruction.** Crew Restoration & Construction handles both the mitigation and the rebuild, which means one point of contact from demo through finished drywall, paint, and flooring — no handoff gap between the remediation crew and the contractor.
