## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Madison

The process starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled behind walls, under flooring, and into ceiling cavities before a single panel is removed. In Madison's older plaster-and-lath construction, this step matters more than it does in a modern drywall home because moisture migrates differently through dense plaster and can read dry at the surface while the lath behind it stays saturated.

Once the scope is confirmed, standing water is extracted with truck-mounted and portable units depending on access. Structural drying follows using industrial air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated to the space's square footage and ambient conditions. Drying logs are maintained throughout, which matters when an insurance adjuster asks for documentation. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place are removed, and the structure is treated before any reconstruction begins.
