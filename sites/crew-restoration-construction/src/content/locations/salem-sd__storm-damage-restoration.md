## Why Salem Properties See Serious Storm Damage

Salem sits in the heart of South Dakota's severe weather corridor. Spring and early summer bring supercell thunderstorms capable of producing large hail, damaging winds, and the occasional tornado — the same systems that spin off the Black Hills to the west and accelerate across the flat terrain before reaching McCook County. That geography matters for homeowners: there is no natural windbreak between the open fields and your roof.

The housing stock compounds the risk. Many homes near Downtown Salem and along the streets radiating out from the McCook County Courthouse were built in the mid-20th century, when roof decking was often 1×6 skip sheathing rather than continuous plywood. That style of construction holds up well to normal weather but can fail quickly when hail punches through aged asphalt shingles — and once the decking is wet, the damage clock starts. Interior framing, insulation, and ceiling materials can absorb significant moisture within 24 to 48 hours if the breach is not covered promptly.

Large trees near the McCook County Fairgrounds area and throughout older residential blocks add another layer of risk. Mature cottonwoods and elms are beautiful, but a storm with 70-mph gusts can drop a limb — or an entire trunk — onto a roof, a fence, or a parked vehicle with little warning.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Salem

The first step after any storm event is a thorough exterior and interior assessment. We photograph and document every point of damage before a single piece of debris is moved — that documentation is what your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim accurately. From there, the work breaks into two parallel tracks: emergency stabilization and systematic restoration.

Emergency stabilization means stopping the weather from doing more damage while permanent repairs are planned. That typically involves tarping compromised roof sections, boarding broken windows, and removing any structural debris that poses an immediate hazard. In Salem, where the next storm system can arrive within days, getting a quality tarp installed correctly — sealed at the edges, weighted against wind — is not optional.

Once the structure is protected, we assess interior moisture, remove water-damaged materials that cannot be dried in place, and begin the drying process using commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers. Structural repairs — framing, sheathing, roofing, siding, and interior finishes — follow once the building is confirmed dry. We handle both the mitigation and the reconstruction, so you are not coordinating between two separate contractors.
