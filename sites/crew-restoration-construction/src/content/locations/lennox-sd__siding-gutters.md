## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Lennox

Every project starts with a close-up inspection of the existing siding and gutter system — not just the obvious damage, but the flashing, the drip edge, the fascia condition, and the downspout discharge points. In Lennox, we pay particular attention to the north and west elevations of a home, which take the hardest wind and weather exposure. If we find soft spots in the sheathing or fascia, we document them before any new material goes on; covering deteriorated substrate is a mistake that costs far more to fix later.

For siding replacement, we remove the old material completely rather than layering over it, which lets us inspect and dry the wall assembly and add a housewrap moisture barrier where one is missing or damaged. Material selection matters here — fiber cement holds up well against South Dakota's UV intensity and temperature swings, while insulated vinyl offers a practical upgrade in energy performance for homes that lose heat through thin exterior walls. We match profiles and trim details to what the home already has wherever possible, which is especially important in the older blocks near Downtown Lennox where consistent streetscape appearance matters to neighbors.

Gutter work in Lennox almost always means sizing up. Seamless gutters eliminate the mid-run joints that collect debris and eventually leak; they are cut on-site to the exact run length, which means a cleaner fit on homes with non-standard rooflines.
