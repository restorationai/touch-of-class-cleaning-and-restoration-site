## Why Hull Properties See Distinctive Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Hull sits in the heart of Sioux County, where the surrounding farmland means construction sites are constantly exchanging fine soil particles with the outdoors — even when windows are sealed. During the spring thaw and fall harvest seasons, that grit intensifies. Combine it with the region's cold winters, which push contractors to keep buildings sealed tight (trapping airborne particulates indoors for weeks), and you end up with a post-build environment where dust has migrated into HVAC returns, settled behind cabinet faces, and embedded itself in grout lines long before the final walk-through.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Hull has a meaningful share of older homes — some dating back to the mid-20th century — that are being renovated rather than torn down. In those properties, renovation debris can include legacy materials that require careful handling, and original plaster or lathe walls absorb fine particulates differently than modern drywall, making surface cleaning insufficient on its own.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Hull

Every post-construction clean starts with a structured walk-through before a single mop touches the floor. We document what trades were on-site, what materials were used, and what surfaces are newly finished versus original — because the cleaning method for freshly stained hardwood is not the same as for a decades-old subfloor that's been exposed during a gut renovation.

From there, the process moves top to bottom:

- **Air scrubbing first.** HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run throughout the space to pull airborne particulates before they resettle on cleaned surfaces.
- **Dry debris removal.** Construction dust, drywall crumble, and insulation fragments are vacuumed with HEPA equipment — not swept, which redistributes fine particles.
- **Detail cleaning of fixtures and millwork.** Window tracks, door hardware, light fixtures, and cabinetry interiors are wiped individually. Paint overspray and caulk smears are removed without damaging new finishes.
- **Hard surface and floor care.** Adhesive residue, grout haze, and compound splatter are treated with appropriate solvents matched to the floor type — tile, LVP, hardwood, or polished concrete each require a different product and technique.
- **Final HEPA vacuum and wipe-down.** Every horizontal surface gets a second pass after the deep clean settles.
