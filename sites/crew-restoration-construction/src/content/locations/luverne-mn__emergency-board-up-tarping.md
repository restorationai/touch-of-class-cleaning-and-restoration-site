## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Luverne

The first step on any call is a perimeter walk to identify every breach — broken windows, door frames kicked out during firefighting, roof sections lifted by wind, and any structural openings that aren't immediately visible from the street. For window and door openings, we use minimum ½-inch OSB secured with structural screws into the existing framing — not staples, not tape, not cardboard.
