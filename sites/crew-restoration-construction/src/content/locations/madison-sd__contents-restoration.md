## Why Madison Properties See Contents Damage

Madison sits in a part of South Dakota where the seasons swing hard. Winters bring freeze-thaw cycles that stress older plumbing, and spring snowmelt can push water into basements and crawl spaces faster than sump pumps can keep up. The housing stock around Dakota State University includes a mix of mid-century rentals and owner-occupied homes that were built before modern moisture barriers were standard — meaning when water gets in, it travels farther and soaks into more porous materials before anyone notices. Cabins and seasonal properties along the Lake Herman shoreline face a different pattern: they may sit unoccupied for weeks, giving water damage or mold a head start on furniture, bedding, and stored items before the owner even knows there's a problem.

Fire damage in the 57042 ZIP code follows a similar pattern. Older wood-frame homes hold smoke odor in wall cavities and subflooring, and that odor transfers to soft contents — upholstered furniture, curtains, clothing — even in rooms that weren't directly involved in the fire. Contents that look visually intact can still carry enough soot and odor to be unusable without professional cleaning.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything leaves your property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was removed and in what condition.

From there, items are packed and transported to a controlled facility where cleaning methods are matched to the material. Ultrasonic cleaning works well for hard goods — ceramics, metal fixtures, small appliances — because it uses high-frequency sound waves in a cleaning solution to lift soot and residue from irregular surfaces without abrasion. Soft contents like upholstery, area rugs, and clothing go through separate processes depending on fiber type and the nature of the soiling. Documents and photographs get specialized drying and stabilization treatment.

Storage is climate-controlled throughout. In a region where summer humidity can spike and winter temperatures drop well below zero, temperature-stable storage isn't optional — it's what keeps wood furniture from warping and electronics from developing condensation damage while your home is being restored.

Once your structure is cleared and dried, items are returned in the same documented order they left, placed back in the rooms where they belong.

Contents losses are frequently the most disputed part of a property insurance claim. Our inventory and photo documentation is built to support your claim from the start — line-item records that match the format insurers expect, with condition notes that justify cleaning versus replacement decisions.
