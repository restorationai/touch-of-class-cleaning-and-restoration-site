## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Hinton

The first priority on any water loss is stopping the source and understanding the category of water involved — clean supply-line water behaves differently than a backed-up drain or a sump overflow that may carry contaminants from the yard. Once the source is controlled, the extraction phase begins. Industrial truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and subfloor assemblies far faster than wet-vacs or fans alone.

After extraction, the real work is structural drying. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras map where water has migrated — inside wall cavities, under tile, into floor joists — so drying equipment is placed where it actually matters rather than just in the middle of the room. High-capacity dehumidifiers and directional air movers run continuously, typically for three to five days depending on material depth and ambient humidity. Readings are logged daily so the drying curve can be tracked and documented, which matters both for confirming the structure is dry and for supporting an insurance claim.

If building materials — drywall, insulation, damaged subfloor sections — cannot be dried in place, they are removed and the cavities are dried before reconstruction begins.
