## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hawarden

The first priority on any board-up call is a rapid structural read of the damage. Our crew walks the perimeter and identifies every breach — broken windows, compromised entry doors, sections of missing or lifted roofing — before a single sheet of plywood goes up. That assessment shapes the material plan: lighter poly sheeting works for small roof punctures in calm conditions, but a heavy-gauge reinforced tarp anchored with lumber battens is the right call when another storm cell is forecast to move through the Big Sioux corridor overnight.

For roof tarping, we work from the ridge down, overlapping sections so water sheds away from the opening rather than pooling at the edges. Battens are screwed — not stapled — into sound decking on either side of the damaged area to hold tension without creating new penetration points. For broken window board-up and fire damage board-up, we cut plywood to fit within the frame opening and secure it with structural screws rather than nails, which pull out under wind load. Every board-up installation is photographed before and after for your insurance documentation.
