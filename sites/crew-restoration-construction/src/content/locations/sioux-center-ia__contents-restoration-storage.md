## Our Contents Restoration Process in Sioux Center

When we arrive at a Sioux Center property, the first step is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, cataloged, and assessed: salvageable, restorable with treatment, or beyond reasonable restoration. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for keeping your family informed about what is being moved and where.

Items that can be restored leave the property in sealed, labeled containers and go into climate-controlled storage while structural drying or repairs happen in the home. Textiles, clothing, and soft goods go through specialized cleaning that addresses both the visible soiling and the odor compounds embedded in the fibers. Hard surfaces, furniture, and electronics each follow a different protocol. Sensitive documents and photographs receive careful freeze-drying or air-drying treatment depending on the extent of saturation. Nothing is discarded without your approval.

Once the structure is cleared and repairs are complete, we coordinate a scheduled return of contents, placing items back in the rooms where they belong rather than stacking boxes at the door.
