## Why Hull Properties Are Prone to Sewage Backup

Hull's 51239 ZIP code sits in the heart of northwest Iowa's agricultural belt, and that geography shapes how drainage systems behave. Spring thaw in Sioux County can saturate the ground quickly, raising the water table and putting lateral sewer lines under pressure they weren't designed to handle. Many homes in and around Downtown Hull were constructed before modern PVC plumbing became standard, meaning clay tile or cast-iron drain lines are still common. Those older materials crack, root-intrude, and corrode — and when they fail, they fail fast.

Septic systems serving properties on the edges of town face a different challenge: compacted agricultural soil limits absorption field performance, especially after a wet winter. When a drain field is overwhelmed, sewage can surface in a yard or reverse into the home through the lowest floor drain. It's a pattern we've seen repeatedly in Sioux County communities, and Hull is no exception.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Hull

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 — the most contaminated type of water loss — because it contains bacteria, viruses, and parasites. The cleanup process reflects that. Here's how we approach a sewage backup in a Hull home:

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean living spaces. If the event happened in a basement, we establish a barrier before any material is moved.

**Extraction and solid waste removal.** Standing sewage water is extracted using truck-mounted units. Solid waste and saturated porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subflooring — are bagged and removed according to Iowa solid waste handling guidelines.

**Disinfection and sanitization.** Every surface that contacted sewage is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We don't skip the structural framing, floor joists, or wall cavities — those are the places contamination hides and where odor persists if left untreated.

**Drying and monitoring.** After sanitization, we deploy desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the space. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches acceptable levels, protecting against secondary mold growth.

**Documentation.** We photograph every stage of the loss — essential for insurance claims and for your own records.
