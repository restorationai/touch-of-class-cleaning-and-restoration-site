## Our Contents Restoration Process in Remsen

The process begins with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your home. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating so that nothing is lost in the shuffle and your insurance documentation is complete from day one. Items that can be restored on-site are treated there; items that need deeper cleaning, deodorization, or structural drying are packed out carefully and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility.

At the facility, contents go through a triage process: electronics are assessed for corrosion or heat damage, soft goods like clothing and bedding are cleaned using ultrasonic or ozone treatment depending on the type of contamination, and hard surfaces are cleaned with methods matched to the material. Wood furniture that has swollen from moisture exposure is dried at controlled rates to prevent cracking. Documents and photographs that have been wet are stabilized using freeze-drying techniques when the damage warrants it. The IICRC-certified team applies the same drying and decontamination standards to your belongings that they apply to the structure itself.

Once your home is restored and cleared, contents are returned, placed, and inventoried again to confirm everything came back in the condition documented at pack-out.
