## Why Merrill Properties Face Accelerated Roof Wear

Northwest Iowa sits in a climate band that delivers more freeze-thaw cycles per winter than most of the Midwest — temperatures that drop below zero overnight and climb above freezing by afternoon. That daily expansion and contraction works at every nail hole, every flashing joint, and every seam in the underlayment. Shingles rated for 30 years in a milder climate often show granule loss and cracking closer to 18–20 years in this part of Plymouth County.

The Floyd River's proximity also means humidity levels stay elevated longer into the fall, slowing the drying of any moisture that gets under a damaged shingle. On older homes — many of which were built before 1960 and still have board sheathing rather than plywood or OSB — that trapped moisture can rot the deck before the damage is visible from the street. A full replacement that includes deck inspection and spot repair is almost always the responsible scope of work on homes in the 51038 ZIP code.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Merrill

Every project starts with a physical inspection of the deck, not just the surface layer. We pull back damaged sections to check for soft spots, rot, and any previous repairs that were made over compromised wood. If the deck is sound, we install a synthetic underlayment rated for Iowa's wind and moisture conditions before any shingles go down — felt paper is simply not adequate for this climate.

We remove old shingles in full rather than layering over them. A second layer adds weight, traps heat, and hides deck problems. On homes near Merrill City Park and in the older blocks of Downtown Merrill, we've found that double-layer roofs were installed as recently as the 1990s, meaning some properties are now carrying three generations of shingles. Stripping to bare deck is the only way to know what you're working with.

Flashing at chimneys, skylights, and wall intersections is replaced — not reused — on every full replacement. This is the most common point of failure on re-roofs where the original contractor cut corners. Ridge ventilation is sized to the attic square footage so the finished system breathes correctly through Iowa's temperature extremes.

## Local Note: What Older Merrill Homes Reveal Under the Shingles

One pattern we've seen repeatedly on homes in the older residential blocks near Downtown Merrill: original 1×6 skip sheathing — the spaced board decking used under wood shakes before plywood became standard — was left in place when asphalt shingles were installed in the 1970s and 1980s. That skip sheathing isn't designed to support the load of modern architectural shingles, and the gaps between boards create soft spots that installers without local experience can miss.
