## Why Canton Properties See Contents Damage That's Hard to Reverse

Cantons's position along the Big Sioux River corridor puts residential neighborhoods in the path of seasonal humidity swings and periodic flooding that accelerate damage to soft goods, wood furniture, and paper documents. The homes clustered around Downtown Canton and along older residential streets — many of them built in the early to mid-twentieth century — were constructed with dense old-growth wood framing, horsehair plaster, and hardwood floors that hold moisture differently than modern OSB and drywall. That's not a flaw; it's a characteristic. But it does mean that a slow roof leak or an appliance failure left unaddressed for even 48 to 72 hours can push mold colonization into upholstered furniture and wooden cabinetry before the smell is obvious.

Smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire compounds the problem. Older plaster walls are porous, and soot particles settle into fabric fibers and wood grain quickly. Without prompt pack-out and professional cleaning, that acrid odor becomes permanent in curtains, mattresses, and wood pieces that could otherwise be saved.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Restoration Process in Canton

Every job begins with a detailed inventory. Before a single box leaves your home, our crew photographs and documents each item — furniture, clothing, electronics, artwork, kitchenware — so you have a complete record for your insurance claim and so nothing is misplaced during storage or transport.

From there, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

- **Pack-out**: Items are carefully wrapped and loaded using materials appropriate to the piece — padded blankets for furniture, archival boxes for documents and photographs, sealed containers for soft goods.
- **Cleaning and deodorization**: Depending on the type of damage, we use ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-saturated textiles, and dry-cleaning methods for delicate fabrics. Smoke-damaged wood furniture often requires hand-cleaning with specialized solvents before any refinishing is considered.
- **Climate-controlled storage**: Your belongings are held in a secure, climate-controlled facility while structural work is completed at your property. Given Lincoln County's temperature swings — from humid summers to hard-freeze winters — climate control isn't optional for wood furniture, electronics, or anything with adhesive components.
- **Return and placement**: When your home is ready, we return and place items according to your original floor plan documentation.

## Canton Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents losses are frequently underestimated in insurance claims because adjusters work from replacement-cost schedules, not from what the item actually means to you or what professional restoration could recover. Our documentation process — itemized inventory, condition photographs, and cleaning or restoration estimates — gives your adjuster a complete picture and reduces the likelihood of a lowball settlement on personal property.

Most homeowner policies in South Dakota include personal property coverage that applies to contents damaged by fire, water, or smoke. We work directly with carriers to submit documentation and, where possible, bill them directly so you are not fronting the full cost of pack-out and storage while the claim is processed.
