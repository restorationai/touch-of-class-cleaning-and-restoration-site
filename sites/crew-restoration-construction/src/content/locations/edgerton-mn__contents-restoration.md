## Why Edgerton Homes Face Distinct Contents Damage Risks

Edgerton sits in a part of southwestern Minnesota where winters are long and temperature swings between seasons are dramatic. That freeze-thaw cycle is hard on older plumbing — and many homes in and around Downtown Edgerton were built in the mid-twentieth century, when supply lines ran closer to exterior walls than modern building codes require. When those pipes let go in January or February, the water doesn't just soak the floor; it wicks into wood furniture legs, saturates area rugs, and climbs upholstered cushions before the homeowner even realizes what's happening.

Fire risk follows a similar pattern. Older homes in this area often have original wood-framed construction with less compartmentalization, which means smoke travels farther and faster through living spaces. A stove fire that's contained to the kitchen can leave a film of oily smoke residue on furniture, books, and clothing two rooms away. That residue sets permanently if it isn't treated within the first 24 to 48 hours — which is exactly why prompt pack-out matters.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Edgerton

The first step is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and assessed before anything leaves the property. This documentation serves two purposes: it gives your insurance adjuster an accurate picture of the loss, and it gives you a clear record of exactly what we've taken and where it's going.

Items that can be restored on-site — hard furniture, non-porous surfaces, metal fixtures — are cleaned and treated at the property when conditions allow. Everything else is carefully packed and transported to our climate-controlled storage facility, where contents are held securely while your home undergoes structural drying, smoke remediation, or reconstruction. Textiles go through ultrasonic or ozone-based cleaning depending on the type of soiling. Electronics are assessed by a qualified technician. Documents and photographs get specialized freeze-drying or air-drying treatment if water damage is involved.

Once your home is ready, we coordinate a scheduled return and help you place items back in the rooms where they belong. Nothing is dropped and forgotten.
