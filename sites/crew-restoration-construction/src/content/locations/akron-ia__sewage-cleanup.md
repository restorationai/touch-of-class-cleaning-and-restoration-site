## Why Akron Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Akron sits at the confluence of two drainage patterns — the Big Sioux River floodplain to the west and the gently rolling farmland that funnels runoff toward town from the east. That geography matters for sewer systems. Much of the residential stock near Downtown Akron was built in the mid-twentieth century, when clay-tile sewer laterals were standard. Those pipes are now 60 to 80 years old, and they crack, root-intrude, and offset at the joints over time. When spring snowmelt or a heavy summer storm saturates the ground, hydrostatic pressure pushes water into those cracks — and whatever is already in the line gets pushed backward into the lowest point of the building.

Septic systems on the rural edges of town face a different version of the same problem. Plymouth County's heavy clay soils drain slowly, and a saturated drain field during a wet season can force a septic overflow back toward the home. Either scenario — municipal sewer backup or septic overflow — qualifies as a Category 3 biohazard event, meaning the water contains pathogens that require more than a shop vac and bleach to address safely.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Akron

The first priority on any raw sewage job is containment — stopping the spread of contaminated material before it wicks into subfloor sheathing, wall cavities, or HVAC ductwork. Our crew establishes a containment perimeter, then removes standing water using truck-mounted extraction units powerful enough to pull sewage from below floor-level drains.

Once the bulk liquid is out, the real work begins. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and in older Akron homes, wood-fiber ceiling tile — that have direct sewage contact are removed and bagged for disposal according to Iowa Department of Natural Resources guidelines. Hard surfaces are cleaned in multiple passes: a detergent wash, an EPA-registered disinfectant application with appropriate dwell time, and a final antimicrobial treatment to inhibit secondary mold growth, which can colonize damp framing within 24 to 48 hours in Iowa's humid summers.

After sanitization, we run commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the specific cubic footage of the affected space. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so you have a documented drying record — useful if your insurance carrier requests proof of proper remediation.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not a job where consumer-grade equipment produces professional results. We use truck-mounted wet-extraction units with significantly higher lift capacity than portable machines, which matters when pulling contaminated water from a basement that sits several feet below grade — common in the older homes near Akron-Westfield School and the surrounding residential blocks. Fogging equipment delivers antimicrobial agents into wall cavities and joist bays that manual wiping cannot reach. Thermal imaging cameras help us locate moisture that has migrated behind finished surfaces without requiring us to open every wall.

For septic overflow situations, we coordinate with local pumping contractors if the tank itself needs service before interior cleanup can begin — a step that is easy to overlook but essential to prevent an immediate re-backup.
