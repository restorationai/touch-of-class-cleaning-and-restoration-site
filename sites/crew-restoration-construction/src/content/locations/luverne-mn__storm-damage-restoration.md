## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Luverne

The first priority after any severe weather event is stopping additional loss. That means getting eyes on the structure quickly, identifying breaches in the envelope — roof, windows, siding, foundation — and securing them before the next rain event. We deploy temporary tarps, board up compromised windows and doors, and document every point of entry with photos and written notes that are formatted to support an insurance claim from the start.

Once the structure is stabilized, we move into assessment and scope. For storm jobs in Luverne, that typically includes:

- **Roof inspection** for hail impact, wind uplift, and flashing failure
- **Attic evaluation** for water intrusion and insulation saturation
- **Interior moisture mapping** using thermal imaging and pin meters to find water that has migrated behind walls or under flooring
- **Structural check** on any areas where tree limbs or debris made contact

Drying equipment — commercial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers — goes in immediately where water has entered. We track moisture readings daily until materials reach documented dry standards, because cutting that process short is the single most common reason storm-damaged homes end up with mold problems.

Reconstruction follows once the structure is dry and the insurance scope is agreed upon. We handle roofing, siding, windows, drywall, and finish work in-house, which means one point of contact from the emergency call through the final walkthrough.
