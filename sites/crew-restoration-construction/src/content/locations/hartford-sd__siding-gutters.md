## Why Hartford Properties See Siding and Gutter Problems

The housing stock in the 57033 ZIP code is a mix of newer construction near the expanding residential edges of town and older ranch-style and two-story homes closer to Downtown Hartford. Newer vinyl siding installed during the building boom of the early 2000s is now hitting the age range where UV degradation, impact damage from hail, and failed J-channel seams become common. Older homes, especially those with original wood lap siding, are contending with decades of moisture cycling — wood that swells in humid summers and contracts in January can split at the nail lines and create channels for water infiltration that aren't obvious until you see it from a ladder.

Gutters compound the problem. Hartford's mature street trees drop heavy leaf loads in fall, and standard 5-inch K-style gutters clog fast. When they overflow, water pools against the foundation rather than running away from it — a particular concern on lots with flatter grades. Downspout extensions that terminate too close to the house are one of the most common issues we find on initial inspections here.

## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Hartford

Every project starts with a walk-around inspection — we're looking at the full exterior, not just the section you called about. Siding failure and gutter failure are usually connected, and fixing one without addressing the other means you'll be back on the phone in two years.

For siding replacement, we remove the damaged panels or full runs, inspect the housewrap or felt paper underneath, and replace any deteriorated sheathing before new material goes on. We work with fiber cement, engineered wood, and vinyl depending on your budget and the home's existing profile. Fiber cement holds up well in South Dakota's climate extremes and doesn't expand and contract the way vinyl does in a -20°F January, which matters when you're talking about a house that may face northwest winds with nothing between it and the Dakotas.

For gutters, we size the system to the roof's drainage load — many Hartford homes that were built with 5-inch gutters would perform better with 6-inch seamless aluminum, especially on longer roof runs. We fabricate seamless gutters on-site, which eliminates the seam joints that are the most common leak points in sectional systems. Downspout placement and termination distance from the foundation are mapped before installation, not after.
