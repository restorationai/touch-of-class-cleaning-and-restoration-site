## Our Contents Restoration Process in Tea

The first step is a thorough inventory. Every item in the affected area is documented, photographed, described, and logged, before anything moves. That record matters for your insurance claim and for returning items to the right place when the work is done.

Items that can be restored on-site are treated there when conditions allow. Furniture, hard goods, and electronics that need deeper cleaning or controlled drying are packed out carefully and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Pack-out is not a shortcut; it protects belongings from secondary damage while structural drying and repairs happen in the home, and it gives technicians the space and equipment to work on contents properly.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard surfaces and metals, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor in soft goods, dry cleaning referrals for garments that need specialist care, and document freeze-drying for papers and photographs when water exposure has been significant. IICRC-certified technicians guide each decision, because the wrong method on the wrong material causes more damage than the original loss.

Once the home is ready, contents are returned, re-inventoried, and placed back according to the original documentation.
