## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Brandon

Every post-construction clean follows a top-down, inside-out sequence. Rough clean comes first — removing bulk debris, pulling protective film off appliances and fixtures, clearing out cardboard and packaging. From there, the detail phase begins: vacuuming window tracks and door frames with HEPA-filtered equipment before any wet wiping, so airborne dust is captured rather than redistributed. Cabinet interiors get wiped, light fixtures are cleaned of construction adhesive, and outlet covers are removed to clear drywall compound from the boxes.

The final phase — what most clients think of as the "move-in ready" clean — covers glass polishing, grout haze removal from tile surfaces, floor buffing, and a full inspection pass under raking light to catch any smears or streaks the detail crew may have missed. For specialty cleaning requests (post-renovation deep cleans in occupied homes, commercial tenant build-outs, or cleaning after insurance repair work), the process is scoped individually based on what trades were involved and what surfaces need protection.
