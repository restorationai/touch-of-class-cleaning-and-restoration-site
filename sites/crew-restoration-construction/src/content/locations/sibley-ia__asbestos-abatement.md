Osceola County's role as a rural county seat meant that Sibley's housing stock grew steadily through the mid-twentieth century — courthouses, schools, storefronts, and single-family homes all went up during the decades when asbestos was considered a marvel of fireproofing and insulation. Northwest Iowa's climate adds another layer of risk. ## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Sibley

Every project starts with a thorough asbestos inspection and bulk sampling of suspect materials. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and we review results with you before any abatement work begins. Once ACMs are confirmed, the process follows a defined sequence:

**Containment setup.** We seal the work area with polyethylene sheeting and establish negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This prevents fiber migration into adjacent rooms — critical in Sibley's older homes where ductwork and open floor plans can carry particles quickly.

**Wet removal.** ACMs are wetted before removal to suppress fiber release. Materials are carefully detached — whether that's scraping a popcorn ceiling, lifting floor tiles, or removing pipe insulation — and immediately bagged in labeled, sealed containers.

**Disposal.** Iowa requires asbestos waste to be transported and disposed of at a licensed facility. We handle all manifesting and transport documentation so you have a complete paper trail if a future buyer or insurer asks for records.

**Air clearance.** After abatement, air samples confirm fiber levels are below regulatory thresholds before containment is removed and the space is returned to you.
