## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Flandreau

The first step is a detailed pack-out inventory. Every item removed from your home is photographed, catalogued by room, and assigned a condition rating before it leaves the property. Once your home is cleared for reoccupancy, every item is returned to the room it came from, using the original inventory as a map. Nothing gets shuffled or misplaced in the process.
