## Why Orange City's Housing Stock Shapes Biohazard Remediation

Many of Orange City's residential properties — particularly those near Downtown Orange City and along the older blocks surrounding Windmill Park — were built in the mid-twentieth century or earlier. That era of construction typically means narrower crawl spaces, original hardwood subfloors, and plaster-over-lath wall assemblies rather than modern drywall. These materials are more porous and more difficult to decontaminate than their contemporary equivalents. Infectious material that reaches a plaster wall or an unsealed wood subfloor can penetrate deeply, which means surface-level cleaning is never sufficient. Proper remediation in these homes requires controlled demolition of affected materials, HEPA-filtered air scrubbing, and EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants applied at the correct dwell time — not a general wipe-down.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Orange City

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. We arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested, and we do not discuss the nature of the work with neighbors or bystanders. After the initial walkthrough, we establish a containment perimeter using physical barriers and negative air pressure where the layout allows, preventing cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home or building.

All biohazardous materials — including sharps, needles, blood-saturated porous materials, and any items classified as infectious waste — are collected, packaged, and transported in compliance with Iowa Department of Natural Resources regulations for medical and biohazardous waste disposal. Nothing is bagged and left at the curb. Disposal is handled through licensed channels, and we document the chain of custody throughout.

After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens. We follow that with ATP testing or surface sampling where warranted to verify that decontamination is complete before we clear the space. The final walkthrough is done with the property owner or their designated representative — never announced as complete until you are satisfied.
