## Why Baltic's Housing Stock and Setting Shape Renovation Decisions

The homes closest to the Big Sioux River area tend to be older, many built in an era when vapor barriers were minimal or nonexistent and basement walls were poured without modern drainage provisions. When those homeowners decide to finish a lower level or update a main-floor bathroom, the renovation has to account for what's already there — not just what the design calls for. That might mean adding a sump system before laying new flooring, or sistering floor joists that absorbed years of seasonal humidity before the finish work can begin.
