## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Worthington

A post-construction clean isn't a single pass — it's a sequenced process, and the sequence matters. We start from the top of each space and work down, removing bulk debris and packaging materials before any surface cleaning begins. HEPA-filtered vacuums capture fine particulate from walls, window frames, and rough-in cavities before it gets disturbed and redistributed. Ductwork and return-air grilles get vacuumed and wiped so the first time the HVAC system runs, it isn't circulating construction dust through the finished space.

After the initial debris removal, we move into detail cleaning: grout haze removal from tile surfaces, adhesive residue from windows and hardware, paint overspray from trim and fixtures, and sticker residue from appliances. Final surfaces — countertops, cabinetry, flooring, glass — are cleaned to a move-in standard, not a contractor standard. We photograph the space before and after, which is useful documentation for general contractors closing out a project and for property owners handing a space to tenants or buyers.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated structure, deep sanitization before a medical or food-service occupancy, or trauma-scene decontamination in a property undergoing renovation — the process is scoped separately and uses appropriate EPA-registered products and containment protocols for the situation.
