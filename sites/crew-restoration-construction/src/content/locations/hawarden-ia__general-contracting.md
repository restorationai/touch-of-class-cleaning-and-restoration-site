## Why Hawarden's Housing Stock Shapes Every Renovation

A lot of the residential properties in and around Downtown Hawarden were built before 1960, which means the moment you open a wall, you're likely to find knob-and-tube wiring, plaster-and-lath construction, or cast-iron drain lines that haven't been touched in decades. None of that stops a remodel — but it changes the plan. Budgets that look straightforward on paper can shift when a bathroom tile project reveals a subfloor that's been slowly rotting from a slow supply line leak, or when a kitchen expansion uncovers a load-bearing wall that wasn't on any drawing.

The Big Sioux River corridor also brings real humidity swings through the seasons. Summer moisture pushes into crawl spaces and basement walls; winter cold snaps drive pipe failures that can soak framing before a homeowner even notices. ## Our General Contracting Process in Hawarden
