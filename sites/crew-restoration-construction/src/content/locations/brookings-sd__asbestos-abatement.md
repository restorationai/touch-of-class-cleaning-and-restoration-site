## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Brookings

Every project starts with asbestos testing — bulk sampling of suspect materials collected by a qualified inspector and sent to an accredited laboratory. We don't guess, and we don't skip this step even when a material "looks like" asbestos. Once results confirm the presence of asbestos-containing material, we develop a written abatement scope that identifies whether encapsulation or full removal is the right approach for your specific situation.

For removal projects, the work area is sealed with heavy-gauge polyethylene sheeting and placed under negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. Workers wear full protective equipment and respirators rated for asbestos fiber exposure. Removed materials are wetted to suppress fiber release, double-bagged in labeled asbestos waste bags, and transported to a licensed disposal facility — South Dakota has specific requirements for asbestos waste manifesting and disposal that govern how this material leaves your property. After abatement is complete, air clearance testing confirms fiber levels have returned to background before containment is removed and the space is released for reoccupancy or renovation.
