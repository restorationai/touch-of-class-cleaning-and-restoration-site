## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Volga

Every job starts with a rapid site assessment — identifying every breach point before we touch a single sheet of plywood. That means walking the roofline, checking the attic space for hidden openings, and documenting window and door frames that have shifted or cracked under structural stress. We photograph everything, which matters for your insurance claim.

For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps — minimum 6-mil, typically thicker for larger spans — secured with wood battens screwed into the decking rather than relying on edge stakes alone. On skip-sheathed roofs common in older Volga homes, we bridge the gaps with additional backer boards before laying the tarp so the surface is supported and the material doesn't sag and pool water. Ridge caps get special attention; an unsecured ridge is where most tarps fail first in high wind.

Board-up work on broken windows and compromised doors uses exterior-grade plywood cut to fit and fastened with screws rather than nails, so removal during reconstruction is clean and doesn't further damage the frame. For fire-damaged openings where the framing itself is weakened, we add perimeter blocking before mounting the panel.
