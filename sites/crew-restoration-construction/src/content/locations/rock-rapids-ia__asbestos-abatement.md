## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Rock Rapids

No two abatement jobs are identical, but the sequence of steps is consistent and non-negotiable.

**Inspection and bulk sampling** come first. A technician collects samples from suspect materials — ceiling texture, floor tile and mastic, pipe insulation, drywall joint compound — and sends them to an accredited laboratory. Results typically return within 24–72 hours. We do not skip this step based on visual assessment alone; many asbestos-containing materials are indistinguishable from non-asbestos versions by appearance.

**Regulated work area setup** follows a confirmed positive. The affected space is isolated with poly sheeting and negative-air pressure units equipped with HEPA filtration. Workers wear full-face respirators and disposable protective suits. Foot traffic through the containment zone is controlled.

**Wet removal** is the standard method for most friable materials. Wetting the material before disturbing it suppresses fiber release. Removed material is double-bagged in labeled, sealed poly bags and transported to an approved disposal facility — Iowa Department of Natural Resources regulations govern both the handling and the disposal destination.

**Air clearance sampling** after removal confirms fiber levels have returned to acceptable thresholds before containment is broken and the space is released for reconstruction.
