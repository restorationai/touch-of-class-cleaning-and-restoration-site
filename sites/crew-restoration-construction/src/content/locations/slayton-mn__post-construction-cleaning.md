## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Slayton

Post-construction cleaning isn't a single pass — it's a sequenced process that has to happen in the right order or you're just redistributing debris.

**Rough clean first.** We remove bulk debris, pull protective coverings, and clear construction materials before any fine cleaning begins. Skipping this step and going straight to detail work means dust gets knocked back into the air.

**HEPA-filtered vacuuming throughout.** Every surface — walls, window sills, light fixtures, cabinet interiors, HVAC vents — gets vacuumed with HEPA-rated equipment before any wet cleaning begins. This is especially important in older Slayton homes where ductwork may not have been sealed during construction, meaning fine dust has migrated well beyond the work zone.

**Detail and final clean.** Glass polishing, grout haze removal, fixture cleaning, floor finishing — this phase is where the space transitions from a job site to a livable or leasable property. For specialty cleaning requests (post-fire odor, post-flood remediation cleaning, or deep sanitization after a renovation in an occupied building), we adjust chemistry and methods to the specific surface and contaminant.
