## Our Water Cleanup Process in Sibley

The first priority on any water damage call is stopping the source, then understanding how far the water has traveled. Standing water is not always visible. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras reveal saturation behind plaster walls, under vinyl flooring, and inside wall cavities that look dry at the surface. In Sibley's older housing stock, plaster-over-lath construction is common, and that assembly holds moisture differently than modern drywall. The plaster face may read dry while the lath and framing behind it remain saturated for days.

Once the scope is mapped, truck-mounted extraction equipment pulls standing water from carpets, subfloors, and hard surfaces. Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are then positioned to drive evaporation systematically, following drying targets calibrated to the IICRC S500 standard. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated insulation or buckled flooring, are removed to allow airflow to reach the structure behind them. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented for your insurance file.
