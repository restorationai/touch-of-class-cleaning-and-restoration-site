## Why Adrian Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Adrian sits in a part of southwest Minnesota where the soil is predominantly heavy clay — the same dense, moisture-retaining soil that makes this corner of Nobles County excellent farmland but hard on underground infrastructure. Clay soil doesn't drain lateral pressure well. During spring thaw or after a heavy rain, saturated ground shifts and squeezes aging sewer lines, causing cracks, root intrusion, and full blockages. Homes built in the mid-20th century — a common vintage in and around Downtown Adrian — often still have original clay tile sewer laterals that were never replaced. Those pipes are brittle, prone to joint separation, and a prime target for tree root infiltration.

Septic system overflows are another pattern we see in this area. Properties on the rural edges of Adrian that rely on private septic rather than municipal sewer can experience drain field saturation after a wet spring, pushing raw effluent back toward the home or surfacing in the yard. Either scenario — municipal backup or septic overflow — introduces Category 3 "black water" contamination that requires professional extraction, disposal, and sanitization. This is not a shop-vac-and-bleach situation.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Adrian

Every sewage job starts with containment. Before we extract anything, we isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination from spreading through the rest of the structure — especially important in older homes where HVAC returns may be located in or near the basement. Once contained, we remove standing sewage using truck-mounted extraction equipment capable of handling the volume and solids load that raw sewage carries.

After extraction, affected materials are assessed. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, wood subfloor — that have been saturated with Category 3 water are almost always removed rather than dried in place. Trying to dry contaminated porous materials creates conditions where pathogens remain embedded even after surface drying. We document everything we remove with photographs and itemized notes, which matters when you're filing an insurance claim.

Once the structure is cleared down to cleanable surfaces, we apply EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This isn't a single-pass spray — it's a methodical, dwell-time-based process applied to floors, walls, framing, and any mechanical components that were exposed. We follow up with dehumidification and air movement to bring structural moisture levels back to normal before any reconstruction begins.
