## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Worthing

The first priority on any water loss is stopping the source and assessing how far moisture has traveled. Water moves laterally under flooring and vertically through wall assemblies faster than most people expect, so we use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full affected area before any equipment is placed.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete, including water that has pooled in low spots under slab-on-grade sections common in newer Worthing construction. Once bulk water is removed, we set industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers in a configuration sized to the actual cubic footage of the space, not a one-size number. Readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented for your insurance carrier and the job is not closed until materials reach target moisture content.

If structural materials are too saturated to dry in place, controlled demolition of drywall or flooring may be necessary. We explain the scope before any material is removed and photograph everything for the claim file.
