## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Worthington

When a crew arrives at a Worthington property, the first step is always assessment, not assumptions. Technicians map the affected area using moisture meters and thermal cameras to find water that has traveled behind walls or under flooring, places that look dry but aren't. Standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units before any drying equipment is placed.

Once extraction is complete, the drying phase begins. Industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the room layout and the materials involved. Concrete basement floors, older plaster walls, and wood subfloors all release moisture at different rates, and the equipment placement accounts for that. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying progress is documented, useful for your own records and for any insurance claim.

If materials are saturated beyond recovery, the crew identifies them clearly and explains what needs to come out before mold has a chance to colonize. In warm months, mold can begin developing on wet organic material within 24 to 48 hours, which is why extraction and airflow matter from the first hour.
