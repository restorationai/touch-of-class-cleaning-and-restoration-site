## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Hull

Every project begins with a thorough asbestos inspection and bulk sampling. Suspect materials are collected following EPA sampling protocols and sent to an accredited laboratory. Once a scope is confirmed, the work area is isolated using negative air pressure containment with HEPA filtration — a critical step in an occupied home or a building adjacent to neighbors. A final visual inspection and air clearance sampling confirm the space is safe before containment barriers come down.
