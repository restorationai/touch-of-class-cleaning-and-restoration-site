## Why Hartford Properties See Water Damage Differently

The soil profile across this stretch of Minnehaha County holds moisture tenaciously. After a heavy spring thaw or a sustained rain event, the ground around foundations becomes saturated before surface runoff has a chance to move. Homes in the Buffalo Ridge area, many of them built during Hartford's rapid growth period over the past two decades, were constructed on slabs or shallow crawlspaces that sit close to that saturated zone. That design works fine in normal conditions, but when a water heater fails or a supply line lets go, the concrete slab acts as a reservoir rather than a barrier, water spreads laterally under LVP flooring and into adjacent rooms faster than it would in a basement-heavy housing stock.

Hartford is also a growing community, which means a mix of newer construction and some older homes that predate modern vapor barriers and house wrap. ## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Hartford

The first thing a Crew technician does on arrival is map the moisture, not just the visible water. Using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, we trace where water has traveled behind walls and under subfloors before any extraction begins. In Hartford's newer slab-on-grade homes, that mapping step often reveals that water has migrated further than the visible wet zone by several feet.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and surface moisture from carpet, pad, and hard flooring.
- **Controlled demolition** (when necessary): Saturated drywall, baseboards, or flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed to expose the structure and allow airflow.
- **Drying system placement**: Industrial air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just the wet spots you can see. Drying targets follow the IICRC S500 standard.
- **Daily monitoring**: A technician returns each day to read moisture levels and adjust equipment placement until structural materials reach documented dry standards.
- **Documentation**: Every reading, every photo, and every equipment log is recorded for your insurance claim.
