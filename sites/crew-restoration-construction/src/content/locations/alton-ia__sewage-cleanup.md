## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Alton

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 — the most contaminated water classification in the restoration industry — and the response has to match that risk level. When we arrive at an Alton property, the first priority is containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC pathways. From there, the process moves through extraction of standing sewage, removal of unsalvageable porous materials (flooring, drywall, insulation) that cannot be adequately disinfected, and thorough cleaning of all hard surfaces with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents.

Drying comes next. Because many older Alton homes have subfloor assemblies with minimal airflow — crawl spaces with limited clearance are common in this part of Sioux County — we deploy desiccant dehumidifiers and directed airflow systems calibrated to the actual moisture readings we take, not a generic drying schedule.
