## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Brookings

Every project starts with a walkthrough — not a quick glance, but a structured assessment that documents existing conditions, identifies anything that will affect scope (asbestos-containing materials in older homes, moisture behind walls, load-bearing elements that aren't obvious from the floor plan), and produces a written estimate with line-item detail. From there, we handle permitting through the City of Brookings building department.
