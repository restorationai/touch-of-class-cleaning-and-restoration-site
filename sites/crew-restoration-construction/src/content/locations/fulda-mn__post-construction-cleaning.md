## Why Fulda Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Fulda sits in a geography that works against a quick sweep-and-done cleanup. The proximity to two lakes means humidity levels inside newly framed or remodeled structures can stay elevated well into the fall, allowing fine construction dust — silica particles from cut concrete block, joint compound powder, insulation fibers — to settle into porous surfaces rather than simply sitting on top of them. Pull up a window sill in a Lakeside Fulda home after a summer renovation and you'll often find a gray film worked into the wood grain that a standard mop won't touch.

Murray County's older housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Many homes in and around Fulda were built during the mid-twentieth-century agricultural boom, which means original woodwork, plaster ceilings, and older HVAC returns that collect fine particulate differently than modern construction. Cleaning those surfaces requires slower, more deliberate methods — HEPA-filtered vacuuming before any wet wiping, not after — to avoid grinding debris into finishes that can't be easily replaced.

## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Fulda

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through to categorize what's present: rough debris (lumber scraps, packaging, hardware remnants), surface dust, adhesive and caulk residue, and window or fixture film left by protective tape. We don't combine those phases — each requires different tools and a different sequence.

**Phase 1 — Rough debris removal.** All loose material is bagged and staged for disposal. We coordinate with local haulers familiar with Murray County disposal requirements so nothing sits on a driveway longer than necessary.

**Phase 2 — HEPA vacuuming.** Every horizontal surface, return vent, window track, and cabinet interior is vacuumed with commercial HEPA equipment before any liquid cleaner is introduced. Skipping this step simply redistributes fine dust into the air and onto wet surfaces.

**Phase 3 — Detail cleaning.** Baseboards, window frames, light fixtures, and hardware get hand-wiped with appropriate cleaners matched to the surface — no single product on every substrate. Tile grout haze from new bathroom work requires a different chemistry than adhesive residue on hardwood.

**Phase 4 — Final inspection pass.** We walk the space in natural light and with a work light held at a low angle — the same way a critical client will look at it — catching any remaining smears, dust shadows, or missed corners before we close out.
