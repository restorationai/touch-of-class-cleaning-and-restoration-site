## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Worthington

Every project starts with a full exterior inspection — not just the obvious damaged panel or the one leaking corner. We check the condition of the housewrap or felt paper underneath, inspect the flashing at windows and doors, and probe the fascia and soffit for soft spots before we pull a single board. Skipping that step is how contractors end up installing new siding over rotted sheathing.

For siding replacement, we remove the existing material, address any sheathing damage, install a continuous weather-resistant barrier, and then hang new panels with manufacturer-specified fastener patterns and clearances. For gutter work, we measure the actual roof drainage area to size the gutter correctly — a 5-inch K-style gutter that works fine on a small ranch is undersized for a two-story with a steep pitch and a large roof plane. We cut and form seamless gutters on-site, set the slope to drain fully to the downspout, and position downspout extensions to discharge well away from the foundation.
