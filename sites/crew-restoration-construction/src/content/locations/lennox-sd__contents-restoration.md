## Why Lennox Homes Face Distinct Contents Risks

Lennox sits in a part of Lincoln County where the seasons swing hard. South Dakota winters push indoor heating systems to their limits, and the freeze-thaw cycles that roll through the 57039 ZIP code between November and March are a reliable driver of burst supply lines and failed water heaters. When a pipe lets go overnight, standing water reaches upholstered furniture, area rugs, and wood cabinetry within minutes. By morning, soft goods have already begun wicking moisture into their cores.

Summer brings the opposite pressure. High humidity off the surrounding farmland — combined with older homes near Downtown Lennox that may lack modern vapor barriers — creates conditions where mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. Contents that look surface-dry can still be holding enough moisture internally to sustain microbial growth through a storage period if they are not properly dried and treated before being boxed.

Fire risk is real here too. Residential heating equipment, agricultural outbuildings close to property lines, and older electrical panels in pre-1980 housing stock all contribute to fire and smoke claims in the area. Smoke residue is not just a surface problem — it penetrates fabric fibers, wood grain, and even the pages of books, and it continues off-gassing long after the visible soot is wiped away.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Lennox

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition code before anything is moved. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state.

Pack-out is done with padded moving blankets, sealed cartons, and labeled manifests — not garbage bags and guesswork. Fragile items, electronics, and artwork are wrapped and crated separately. Once contents reach our facility, the cleaning phase is matched to the damage type: ultrasonic tanks for hard goods and small valuables, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for smoke-saturated soft goods, and industrial drying chambers for water-affected items that need controlled dehumidification before cleaning can begin.

Storage is climate-controlled throughout. Items do not sit in an unheated warehouse through a South Dakota winter. When your home is structurally ready, we coordinate the return delivery and place items back in their original rooms using the inventory photos as a guide.
