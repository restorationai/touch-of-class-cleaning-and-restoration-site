## Why Volga Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Volga's housing stock skews older, with a number of homes in and around Downtown Volga built before modern subfloor and wall-cavity standards were common. That matters in biohazard situations because porous materials — original hardwood planking, older OSB subflooring, unsealed concrete in crawl spaces — absorb biological material more readily than newer construction. Contamination that looks surface-level often isn't. A thorough assessment has to account for what's underneath, not just what's visible.

South Dakota's climate adds another layer. The freeze-thaw cycle that defines Kingsbury County winters can cause micro-cracking in concrete slabs and foundation walls, creating pathways for contaminants to migrate into areas that wouldn't be affected in a warmer-climate home. During warmer months, the humidity that rolls in off Oakwood Lakes and the surrounding wetlands accelerates the biological activity that makes prompt remediation so important — the window for containing a situation before it compounds is shorter here than in drier climates.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Volga

Every situation is different, and the process is shaped by what we find during the initial walk-through — not a checklist applied the same way every time. In general terms, the work moves through four stages.

First, we assess the full scope of affected materials, including areas that may not be immediately obvious. Second, we contain the work area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected parts of the structure — this is especially important in the smaller floor plans common to older Volga homes, where a hallway or shared wall can sit just feet from an affected room. Third, we remove and properly dispose of all contaminated materials in accordance with South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources guidelines for regulated biological waste. Fourth, we treat and verify the affected surfaces using EPA-registered disinfectants and document the results.

Throughout the process, a single point of contact on our crew handles communication with the property owner or family member, so no one has to repeat difficult details to multiple people.
