## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Rock Valley

The process starts before a single mop hits the floor. We walk the site with the general contractor or property owner to identify sensitive surfaces, flag any areas where subcontractors may have left adhesive overspray or caulk smears, and confirm which specialty areas — HVAC returns, cabinetry interiors, light fixture housings — need detail attention.

From there, the sequence matters:

1. **Rough clean** — bulk debris removal, construction waste bagging, broom-down of all horizontal surfaces from ceiling to floor.
2. **Detail clean** — window tracks, door hardware, outlet covers, cabinet interiors, and any built-in millwork. This is where most crews cut corners; we don't.
3. **Final clean** — HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all surfaces, microfiber wipe-down of counters, fixtures, and glass, floor cleaning calibrated to the specific material (sealed concrete, hardwood, LVP, or tile), and a final air quality pass to clear suspended particulate.
4. **Specialty services** — pressure washing of exterior flatwork, construction adhesive removal, post-renovation deep cleaning of HVAC grilles, and odor neutralization if paint or sealant off-gassing is present.

We document the completed condition with photos before we leave, which matters if a landlord, buyer, or inspector has questions later.
