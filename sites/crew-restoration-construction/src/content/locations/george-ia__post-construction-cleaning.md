## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in George

Every post-construction clean we do in the 51237 area begins with a detailed walk-through to document what trades left behind: paint overspray, caulk smears, grout haze, sticker adhesive on new windows, and the fine silica dust that settles from cutting cement board or tile.
