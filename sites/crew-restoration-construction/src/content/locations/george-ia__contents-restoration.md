## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in George

The process starts with a thorough inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed for restorability — this documentation also serves as the foundation for your insurance claim. Items that can be safely transported are carefully packed using appropriate materials: padded wrapping for furniture, sealed containers for smaller items, climate-controlled handling for anything sensitive.

From George, contents travel to our secure storage and processing facility where cleaning begins. Depending on the type of damage, that may involve ultrasonic cleaning for hard surfaces and small objects, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor penetration in soft goods, or dry-cleaning methods for delicate fabrics. Electronics go through a separate evaluation process. Nothing is returned to your home until both the item and the structure have been cleared — returning clean contents to a home that still has elevated moisture or residual smoke is a common mistake that causes secondary damage.

Storage is climate-controlled and inventoried, so you can request a status update on specific items at any point during the restoration process.
