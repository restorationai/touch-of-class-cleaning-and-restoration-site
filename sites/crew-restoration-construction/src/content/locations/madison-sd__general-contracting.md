Lake County's freeze-thaw cycles are punishing. Many homes in the 57042 ZIP code were built in eras when vapor barriers were optional and insulation was an afterthought, so a kitchen remodel that looks cosmetic on the surface often reveals outdated wiring, under-insulated exterior walls, or subfloor damage once the first layer of flooring comes up.

One pattern that shows up repeatedly in Madison renovation work: homes built in the 1940s and 1950s — a significant portion of the residential stock in and around Downtown Madison — often used true-dimension lumber, meaning a "2x4" is actually a full two inches by four inches rather than the 1.5" × 3.5" dimensional lumber used today.
