## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Edgerton

Every abatement project starts with sampling and testing — not assumptions. Before any material is touched, we collect bulk samples from suspect areas and send them to an accredited laboratory. Results typically return within a few business days, and we walk you through what was found in plain language before any work begins.

Once asbestos-containing materials are confirmed, the work area is isolated using polyethylene sheeting and negative air pressure machines equipped with HEPA filtration. This containment prevents fibers from migrating into adjacent rooms — important in smaller Edgerton homes where living spaces sit close together. Workers wear full PPE including respirators rated for asbestos work, and all removed materials are wetted to suppress fiber release, then sealed in labeled, leak-tight bags for disposal at a licensed facility.

After removal, air clearance testing confirms fiber counts are below regulatory thresholds before containment is torn down. You receive documentation of the entire process — useful if you're selling the property, pulling a building permit through Pipestone County, or filing with an insurance carrier.
