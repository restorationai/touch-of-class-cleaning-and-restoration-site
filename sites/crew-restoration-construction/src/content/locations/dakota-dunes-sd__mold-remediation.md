## Our Mold Remediation Process in Dakota Dunes

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the visible surface but the wall cavities, subfloor, and HVAC pathways where mold travels. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the full extent of contamination before any demolition begins, because cutting into a wall without knowing what's behind it spreads spores rather than containing them.

Containment comes next. Negative air pressure chambers with HEPA-filtered exhaust units isolate the work area so spores don't migrate into clean living spaces or the home's duct system. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Remaining structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. We then run commercial-grade dehumidification and air scrubbing until moisture readings return to normal baselines for the region's climate. The job isn't finished until the air is clean, not just the surfaces.
