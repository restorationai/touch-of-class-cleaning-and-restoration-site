## Our Mold Remediation Process in Brookings

Every remediation job starts with a thorough inspection — not just of the visible staining, but of adjacent cavities, subfloor materials, and HVAC pathways that can carry spores into unaffected rooms. Moisture readings guide the scope; we don't pull materials that don't need to come out, and we don't leave materials that do.

Once the scope is established, containment goes up. Negative air pressure barriers isolate the work area from the rest of the structure, and air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the project. This matters especially in occupied rentals near campus, where a tenant may still be living in unaffected rooms during the work.

Affected drywall, insulation, and framing are removed and bagged for disposal. Remaining structural surfaces are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Drying equipment — dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the square footage and material type — runs until moisture readings return to normal range. We document conditions with photos and moisture logs throughout, which becomes important if an insurance claim is involved.
