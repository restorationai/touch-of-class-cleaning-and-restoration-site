Elk Point is the Union County seat and one of South Dakota's oldest incorporated towns, which means a meaningful share of its housing stock predates modern construction standards. ## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Elk Point

Every post-construction clean starts with a walkthrough, not a mop bucket. Before any crew member touches a surface, we assess what trades were on-site, what materials were used, and what the substrate underneath actually is. Plaster versus drywall, original fir floors versus luxury vinyl plank — the cleaning method changes depending on what's there.

From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Rough clean**: Removal of all construction debris, packaging, tape, and bulk material. Anything the trades left behind that isn't part of the finished structure comes out first.
- **Detail clean**: Cleaning of all surfaces top to bottom — light fixtures, window sills, cabinet interiors, door hardware, baseboards, and floor transitions. We pay particular attention to HVAC registers and returns, which act as collection points for fine dust during active construction.
- **Final clean**: A white-glove pass that covers glass, mirrors, appliance surfaces, and any surface that will be visible at move-in. This is the stage where specialty cleaning comes in — grout haze removal, adhesive residue treatment, paint overspray correction, and stainless steel polishing where applicable.
- **Inspection walkthrough**: We walk the space with the property owner or general contractor before we close out. If something doesn't meet the standard, we address it before we leave.

For commercial properties or larger residential projects in the 57025 ZIP code, we can also coordinate with the general contractor on phased cleaning — rough clean after framing and drywall, final clean after paint and trim — so the space is ready for inspections and occupancy without a last-minute scramble.
