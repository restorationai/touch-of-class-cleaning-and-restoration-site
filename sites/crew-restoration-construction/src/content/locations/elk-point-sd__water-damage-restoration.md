## Why Elk Point Properties Are Vulnerable to Water Damage

Union County's position at the confluence of the Missouri and Big Sioux rivers means the water table in and around Elk Point can rise quickly after heavy rain or rapid snowmelt — conditions the region sees every spring. Homes built before the 1960s, which make up a significant share of the housing stock near the Union County Courthouse and along the older residential blocks of West Elk Point, were often constructed with full basements and minimal waterproofing by today's standards. Sump pump failures during a spring storm are among the most common calls we receive from this area.

Beyond flooding, aging supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks in older homes are prone to slow leaks that go undetected for weeks. By the time a homeowner notices a soft spot in the floor or a musty smell in a closet, moisture has already migrated into wall cavities and floor joists. Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure — which is why the speed of extraction and drying matters as much as the equipment itself.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Elk Point

Every job starts with a thorough moisture assessment using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. We map the full extent of saturation — including areas that look dry but aren't — before any equipment is placed. This step is especially important in older Elk Point homes where plaster walls and tongue-and-groove subfloors hold water differently than modern drywall and OSB: the surface may feel dry while the structural layer beneath is still well above acceptable moisture content.

Once we understand the scope, we begin water extraction using truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of pulling standing water and deep-seated moisture from carpet, hardwood, and subfloor assemblies. We then set a calculated drying system — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers — positioned to create the airflow patterns the affected materials actually need, not just the ones that are easiest to set up. Drying logs are recorded daily so you and your insurance adjuster have documentation of progress at every stage.

If building materials are saturated beyond recovery — saturated drywall, swollen cabinetry, compromised insulation — we handle the controlled demolition and debris removal as part of the mitigation scope, then coordinate reconstruction so you're working with one point of contact from damage through finished repair.

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in Elk Point's older neighborhoods: the original hardwood floors common in pre-1950 construction here are often face-nailed tongue-and-groove strips over a diagonal subfloor — a two-layer assembly that traps water between the boards and the subfloor sheathing.
