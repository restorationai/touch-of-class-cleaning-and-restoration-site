## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Salem

The goal on every job is to stop the exposure before the next weather event compounds the loss. When we arrive on-site, the first step is a rapid visual assessment — identifying every breach point, from a fire-damaged roof deck to a broken window from a vehicle impact. For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for wind uplift, secured with lumber battens screwed into the decking rather than just weighted at the edges. That matters in Salem, where sustained winds can run 30–40 mph even on a calm-looking afternoon. Battened tarps stay put; weighted tarps blow off.

For fire damage board-up, we cut plywood panels to fit each opening precisely, fasten them with structural screws into the surrounding framing, and apply security-grade hardware where the structure will be unoccupied. Broken window board-up follows the same principle — a panel that fits flush, not one that rattles in the wind and invites opportunistic entry. We photograph every opening before and after, which matters when your insurance adjuster is reviewing the claim.
