## Our Water Cleanup Process in Brandon

The first priority on any water cleanup call is stopping ongoing intrusion, then establishing exactly how far moisture has traveled. Water follows framing cavities, subfloor seams, and HVAC chases in ways that look invisible from the surface. Our IICRC-certified technicians use thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, not just the visible wet zone. That distinction matters in Brandon's newer construction because engineered subfloor panels and composite cabinetry absorb water quickly but show surface readings that can understate the saturation depth.

Once the scope is mapped, we set up commercial-grade extraction equipment to pull standing water and begin structural drying with calibrated desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers. Drying targets follow the IICRC S500 standard, and we document daily moisture readings so your insurance adjuster has a clear record of progress. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place, such as saturated insulation or swollen cabinetry, are removed and documented before disposal.
