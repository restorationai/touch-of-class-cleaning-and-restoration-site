## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Lennox

The process begins with a thorough inventory. Every item that can be safely moved is catalogued, photographed, and assessed before anything leaves your property. This documentation serves two purposes: it protects your belongings during transit and storage, and it gives your insurance adjuster a clear record of what was affected and what condition it was in.

From there, contents go to a controlled facility where they are dried, cleaned, deodorized, or treated depending on the type of damage. Textiles go through thermal fogging or ozone treatment to address smoke odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Hard surfaces are cleaned with appropriate agents based on the material. Electronics and documents are assessed for salvageability before any restoration is attempted.

Once your home or the affected space is cleared and restored, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed. The goal is to hand you back your belongings in the condition they were in before the loss, or as close to it as the damage allows.

## Lennox Insurance Coordination

Contents restoration is a covered loss under most standard homeowners policies when the cause of damage is a covered peril, such as a burst pipe, fire, or storm. The documentation Crew Restoration & Construction produces during pack-out, including itemized inventories and photographs, is formatted to support your claim and reduce back-and-forth with your adjuster. If your carrier requires a scope of loss or a contents list before approving storage costs, that paperwork is part of the process, not an add-on.

If you are unsure what your policy covers, reviewing your declarations page before calling is useful. Most policies distinguish between actual cash value and replacement cost value for contents, and that distinction affects what you receive, not what we restore.
