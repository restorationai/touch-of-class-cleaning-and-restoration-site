## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Harrisburg

The first thing we do on arrival is assess the water category and the extent of saturation, because those two factors determine every decision that follows. Standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable units capable of pulling water from carpet, pad, and subfloor assemblies. Once bulk water is out, we use thermal imaging and moisture meters to map saturation behind walls and under flooring, because water that reads dry at the surface can still be soaking the framing beneath.

Drying equipment, including industrial air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers, is positioned based on that moisture map, not placed generically around the room. We monitor readings daily and adjust the drying system as conditions change. Documentation is collected throughout: moisture readings, photos, equipment placement, and daily logs. That documentation matters when your insurance adjuster reviews the claim.

All drying is performed to the IICRC S500 standard, which establishes the drying goals and protocols the industry and most carriers recognize.
