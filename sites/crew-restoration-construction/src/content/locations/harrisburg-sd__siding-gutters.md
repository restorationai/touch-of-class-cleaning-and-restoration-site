## Why Harrisburg Properties See Siding and Gutter Problems

Lincoln County averages around 25 inches of precipitation per year, and a meaningful share of that falls as ice or heavy wet snow between November and March. When gutters are undersized — a common spec on production homes built quickly during Harrisburg's growth surge — they overflow during spring melt events and push water behind the fascia board. From there it wicks into the soffit, then the wall cavity. Homeowners often don't notice until they see paint bubbling or feel a soft spot in the sheathing.

Siding takes its own beating. The open prairie exposure west of Harrisburg High School means wind gusts regularly hit 50–60 mph during spring storms. Fiber cement and vinyl panels that weren't face-nailed at the correct interval can rack or pull away from the wall, leaving a channel for driven rain. Homes built in the last decade often used housewrap that was never taped at seams — fine when the siding is tight, a problem the moment a panel lifts.

## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Harrisburg

Every job starts with a walk-around assessment — not a sales pitch, an actual inspection. We check flashing at every penetration point (windows, doors, hose bibs), probe the fascia and soffit for soft spots, and document gutter slope with a level before we ever talk materials. That baseline matters because replacing siding over wet or compromised sheathing just traps moisture and repeats the cycle.

For siding replacement, we strip back to the sheathing, inspect and replace any damaged OSB or plank, install a continuous weather-resistant barrier with taped seams, and then hang new panels to manufacturer spec. For gutters, we size the system to the actual roof square footage and pitch — not whatever the builder spec'd — and position downspouts to discharge away from foundation grade. On homes near Willow Creek where lot drainage can be sluggish, we pay particular attention to downspout extensions and splash block placement.

We coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster when storm damage is involved, providing photo documentation and a written scope that matches the carrier's preferred format. That keeps the claim moving and reduces the back-and-forth that delays your project start.
