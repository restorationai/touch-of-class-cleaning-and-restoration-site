## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Akron

Every project starts with a full deck inspection — not just a visual scan from the driveway. We walk the roof, probe soft spots, and check flashing at every penetration point: chimneys, vents, dormers, and valleys. On homes with existing wood shake or three-tab asphalt, we assess whether a tear-off is necessary or whether the deck can support a direct overlay. Given the age of many Akron homes, tear-off is more common than not; installing over a compromised deck just transfers the problem forward.

Once the deck is clean and any rotted sheathing is replaced, we install a synthetic underlayment rated for high-wind zones before the field shingles go down. Ridge ventilation is calibrated to the attic's square footage — an often-skipped step that directly affects how long the new roof lasts in temperature extremes. Flashing is hand-formed and sealed at every transition rather than relying on factory-bent stock pieces that rarely conform precisely to older roof geometry.

Material selection matters here. Architectural shingles with a Class 4 impact rating perform significantly better against the hail events that track through northwest Iowa each spring. We walk through product options with each homeowner, including wind-resistance ratings and manufacturer warranty terms, so the choice fits both the budget and the actual exposure the roof will face.
