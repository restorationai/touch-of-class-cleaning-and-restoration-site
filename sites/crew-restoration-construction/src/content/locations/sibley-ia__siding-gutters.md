## Why Sibley Properties See Siding and Gutter Problems

Sibley sits at the edge of the Coteau des Prairies, a high plateau that funnels wind with very little natural windbreak. Gutters take a parallel beating. Ice dams are a real concern on the older homes clustered near Downtown Sibley and around the Osceola County Courthouse, where rooflines were designed decades before modern ventilation standards.
