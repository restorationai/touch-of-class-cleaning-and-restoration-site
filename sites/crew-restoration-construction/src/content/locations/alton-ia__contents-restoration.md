## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Alton

The process starts on-site with a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and sorted into categories: salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with restoration, or total loss. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear picture of what you're getting back.

Salvageable items are carefully packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility. Cleaning methods are matched to the material — ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for textiles and upholstered furniture, dry-cleaning methods for delicate fabrics, and specialized document drying for paper records and photographs. Smoke odor is treated at the molecular level, not masked with deodorizer spray. Items remain in secure, climate-controlled storage until your home in the 51003 ZIP code is ready to receive them, at which point we coordinate re-delivery and help you place items back in the appropriate rooms.
