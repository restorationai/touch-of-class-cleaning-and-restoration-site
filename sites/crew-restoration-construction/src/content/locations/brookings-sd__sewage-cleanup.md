## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Brookings

Every sewage job starts with containment. Before any extraction begins, we establish a contamination perimeter and put negative air pressure in place where the layout allows, preventing aerosolized pathogens from migrating into unaffected living areas.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted wet-vac systems pull standing sewage from hard floors and low-lying areas. Saturated materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — are assessed individually. In Brookings homes with finished basements (a common feature given the cold winters), we often encounter multiple material layers: carpet over pad over OSB over concrete. Each layer holds contamination differently and requires a separate decision about removal versus salvage.

After extraction, affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for Category 3 water events. We apply antimicrobial agents to framing, concrete, and any salvageable structural materials, then document the application for your insurance file. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines. We don't close a job until the numbers confirm the structure is dry, not just dry to the touch.
