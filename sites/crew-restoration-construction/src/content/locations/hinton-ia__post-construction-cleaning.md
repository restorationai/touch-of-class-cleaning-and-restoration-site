## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Hinton

Every job starts with a walk-through to document the scope — noting which surfaces are finished versus unfinished, whether the HVAC system has been run during construction (a common source of duct contamination), and whether any specialty materials like quartz countertops or engineered hardwood need product-specific care. From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Rough debris removal** comes first: broken tile, scrap drywall, packaging material, and adhesive residue are cleared before any fine cleaning begins. Bringing fine-cleaning tools into a space still holding bulk debris wastes time and risks cross-contaminating cleaned surfaces.

**HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all surfaces** — ceilings, walls, window sills, cabinet interiors, and light fixtures — captures the airborne-particle layer before wet cleaning. Skipping this step and going straight to wet methods turns dust into a paste that smears into grout lines and wood grain.

**Detail cleaning** follows: grout haze removal from tile, paint overspray from hardware and glass, adhesive residue from flooring, and sticker removal from windows and appliances. This is the phase most general cleaning crews underestimate; it often takes longer than the rough phase on a well-finished Hinton home.

**Final inspection and touch-up** closes the job. We walk the space with the property owner or general contractor, identify anything that needs a second pass, and confirm the space meets the standard expected for occupancy or handoff.
