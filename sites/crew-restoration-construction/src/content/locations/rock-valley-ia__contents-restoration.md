## Why Rock Valley Properties See Contents Damage Differently

Rock Valley sits in the northwest corner of Iowa where the Rock River has a long history of seasonal flooding. Properties closest to the river — particularly those in lower-elevation sections near Rock Valley City Park — can see water intrusion that moves fast and spreads far. What makes contents damage in this part of Iowa distinct is the combination of flood exposure and the region's wide temperature swings. Winters here are genuinely cold, and homes that have been shut down after a loss can drop below freezing quickly. That matters for contents: wood furniture that absorbed floodwater and then freezes can crack along grain lines in ways that are difficult to reverse. Textiles left damp in a cold, unheated space grow mold within 24 to 48 hours.

Many homes in Rock Valley were built in the mid-twentieth century, which means you'll find solid hardwood furniture, wool area rugs, and older upholstered pieces — items worth restoring rather than replacing if they're handled correctly and quickly.

## Our Contents Pack-Out and Storage Process in Rock Valley

The first step is a detailed inventory. Every item that leaves your property is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before it's packed. This documentation matters both for your peace of mind and for the insurance claim.

From there, contents move to a climate-controlled storage facility where they're assessed for the appropriate cleaning method. Hard surfaces — cabinetry, lamps, decorative items — are cleaned using ultrasonic equipment or hand-wiped with appropriate solutions depending on the material and the type of residue. Soft goods like clothing, bedding, and drapery go through ozone treatment or thermal fogging to address smoke and odor, then laundered or dry-cleaned as the fabric requires. Electronics are evaluated separately; corrosion from flood exposure isn't always visible, and a professional assessment prevents a dangerous situation when power is restored.

Items are stored securely until your home is ready to receive them. We coordinate the return delivery and placement so you're not managing a moving process on top of everything else.

## Rock Valley Insurance Coordination for Contents Claims

Contents claims are often the most contested part of a property loss. Insurers want itemized documentation, replacement cost valuations, and clear evidence that restoration — rather than replacement — was the appropriate call for each item. The inventory process Crew Restoration & Construction uses is built to satisfy that standard. Photographs, condition notes, and cleaning records are compiled into a format that adjusters recognize, which reduces the back-and-forth that slows settlements.

If your policy includes actual cash value rather than replacement cost coverage for personal property, that affects what you'll recover — worth a conversation with your adjuster before the pack-out begins so there are no surprises.
