## Why Elk Point Properties Face Distinct Contents Challenges

Elk Point sits in Union County near the Missouri River, and the combination of river-valley humidity, wide seasonal temperature swings, and a housing stock that includes some of South Dakota's oldest residential structures creates conditions that accelerate damage to personal property. Homes built before 1950 — and there are many in and around the 57025 ZIP code — often have plaster walls, original hardwood floors, and wood-framed furniture with hide-glue joinery. When water or smoke reaches these interiors, the absorption patterns differ significantly from modern construction. Fabrics and upholstery in older homes tend to trap odor molecules deeper because ventilation was designed for different airflow standards. That means standard surface cleaning is rarely enough; contents from these properties typically require ultrasonic cleaning, ozone treatment, or thermal fogging to fully neutralize odor.

Seasonal freeze-thaw cycles also matter here. A pipe that fails in January doesn't just wet the floor — it can push water under baseboards and into furniture legs before anyone notices, especially in homes with crawl spaces or uninsulated utility rooms. By the time a homeowner calls, secondary damage to wood furniture and stored items may already be underway.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Elk Point

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory before a single item leaves your property. Each piece is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating — this documentation protects you during the insurance claim process and ensures nothing gets lost in transit. From there, the process moves through four stages:

**Pack-out and transport.** Belongings are wrapped, boxed, and loaded using materials appropriate for fragile or antique items. We treat a hand-stitched quilt from a West Elk Point farmstead differently than a mass-produced sofa — because they are different.

**Cleaning and restoration.** Depending on the type of damage, we use ultrasonic cleaning tanks for hard goods and non-porous items, dry-cleaning methods for delicate textiles, and controlled ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke-affected fabrics. Furniture with structural damage — swollen drawers, delaminating veneer, warped frames — is assessed for repair versus replacement and documented either way.

**Secure climate-controlled storage.** While your home is being dried, rebuilt, or remediated, your contents stay in a secure, climate-controlled facility. You receive an itemized list and can request access to specific items if needed.

**Return and placement.** When your home is ready, we return everything to the rooms where it belongs, cross-referencing the original inventory so nothing ends up in the wrong place.
