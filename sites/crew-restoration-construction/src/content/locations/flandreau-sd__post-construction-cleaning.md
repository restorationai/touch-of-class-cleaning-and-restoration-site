## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Flandreau

Every post-construction clean we do in the 57028 ZIP code follows a sequenced approach rather than a room-by-room sweep. We start at the ceiling and work down — literally. Overhead surfaces, light fixtures, and HVAC registers get cleaned before floors are touched, because disturbing upper surfaces after floors are done just redeposits debris. That sequence matters more in Flandreau's climate than in milder regions, because static electricity from low-humidity winter air causes fine construction dust to cling to vertical surfaces like walls and window frames rather than settling to the floor.

After the rough clean removes bulk debris, we move into the detail phase: window tracks, door hardware, cabinet interiors, grout lines, and any specialty surfaces the contractor flagged. Final clean is the phase most clients care about most — it's what a property inspector or a new tenant sees. We use HEPA-filtered vacuums throughout to avoid recirculating fine particulate, and we verify HVAC filters are replaced or cleaned before we leave, since construction dust that gets pulled through a system on day one of occupancy undoes a significant portion of the work.
