## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Tea

Every project starts with an in-person walkthrough, not a phone estimate. We want to see the actual framing, the existing mechanical runs, and the condition of materials behind walls before we commit to a scope or a price. For kitchen and bathroom remodels, that means checking supply line age, verifying that existing electrical circuits meet current load requirements, and confirming that subfloor deflection is within tolerance for the tile or hardwood being specified.

Once scope is agreed, we handle permitting through Lincoln County and coordinate all required inspections — rough-in, framing, final — so the homeowner doesn't have to manage that process. For post-damage rebuilds, we also interface directly with insurance adjusters, providing line-item documentation that matches the carrier's estimating format and reducing back-and-forth delays. Trades are sequenced so the project moves in a logical order: demo, structural, rough mechanical, insulation, drywall, finish work. We don't hand off coordination to the homeowner mid-project.
