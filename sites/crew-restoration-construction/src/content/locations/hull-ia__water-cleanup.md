## Why Hull Properties Face Distinct Water Damage Risks

Sioux County's agricultural drainage system moves field water efficiently, but the same flat topography that makes the land productive can work against residential properties during heavy rain events. Hull's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built in the mid-twentieth century, when basement waterproofing standards were minimal and footer drains were sometimes absent entirely. Many of these homes have poured concrete or block foundations that develop hairline cracks over decades of freeze-thaw cycling, cracks that stay invisible until a wet spring pushes groundwater through them.

The region's winters add another layer of risk. Pipes routed through exterior walls or unheated crawl spaces are common in homes built before modern energy codes, and a sustained cold snap can freeze and rupture supply lines with little warning. When that happens inside a finished basement, the water has nowhere to go but into flooring assemblies and wall cavities.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Hull

When a crew arrives at a Hull property, the first priority is stopping active water intrusion if the source is still running, shutting off the main, capping a broken line, or clearing a blocked drain. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water movement behind walls and under flooring. Water that looks contained at the surface is often traveling further than it appears, especially in homes with plank subfloors or original hardwood over a concrete slab.

**Extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water and saturated material from carpet, pad, and porous flooring before drying equipment is placed. Skipping this step and going straight to air movers extends drying time significantly.

**Structural drying.** Industrial dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just placed in the middle of the room. Readings are logged on a drying schedule and checked at each visit to confirm the structure is progressing toward dry standard per the IICRC S500.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, every photo, and every equipment placement is recorded. That documentation matters when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim.
