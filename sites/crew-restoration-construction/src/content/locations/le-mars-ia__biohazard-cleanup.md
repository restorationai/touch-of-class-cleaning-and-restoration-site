## Why Le Mars Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Porous materials like unfinished wood framing and older concrete basement floors can absorb biological contamination in ways that standard surface cleaning does not address. Iowa's climate adds another layer. In warmer months, high humidity accelerates the breakdown of biological material and increases the risk of secondary microbial growth if cleanup is delayed. Timing matters in ways that are specific to this region.

One thing that comes up specifically in Le Mars's older residential areas is the presence of original tongue-and-groove hardwood subfloors beneath finished flooring. On jobs in these homes, we probe the subfloor assembly and inspect the joist bay directly rather than assuming contamination stayed where it started.
