## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Dakota Dunes

Every job begins with a contained assessment — we arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested, and our team does not discuss the nature of the work with neighbors or bystanders. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Containment and PPE staging.** Affected areas are isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where the space allows. This prevents cross-contamination to adjacent rooms.

**Material removal and packaging.** All biohazardous materials — including sharps, bodily fluids, blood, and infectious debris — are collected, containerized, and transported in compliance with South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources requirements for regulated medical waste. Disposal is handled through licensed channels, not standard waste streams.

**Surface treatment.** Hospital-grade disinfectants are applied to all affected surfaces, including hidden areas behind baseboards and under flooring when penetration is indicated. We do not skip the subfloor check in a community where luxury finishes often sit over finished concrete or multi-layer underlayment.

**Clearance documentation.** Before we close out the job, we provide written documentation of the work completed — useful for insurance claims and, in some cases, required by HOA management.
