## Why Remsen Properties See More Sewage Backup Issues

Plymouth County's clay-heavy soil is a significant contributor to sewer line stress. Clay expands when it absorbs moisture and contracts during dry spells, and that constant ground movement puts lateral sewer lines — the pipes running from your home to the municipal main — under chronic pressure. Root intrusion from the mature trees lining many of Remsen's older residential streets compounds the problem. Add Iowa's freeze-thaw cycles, where the ground can shift several inches between November and March, and you have conditions that accelerate pipe joint failure and offset connections.

Homes built before 1960, which make up a large portion of the residential blocks surrounding St. Mary's Catholic Church and Remsen City Park, often still have original clay tile or cast-iron drain lines. These materials are long past their design life. A single heavy rain event — the kind that overwhelms the municipal storm-sewer capacity — can push sewage backward through floor drains and toilets before a homeowner has any warning.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Remsen

The first priority when our crew arrives is containment. Raw sewage spreads contamination through foot traffic and airborne droplets, so we establish a controlled work zone before extraction begins. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Extraction and removal** — Standing sewage and saturated materials are removed using truck-mounted extraction equipment. Flooring, drywall, and insulation that have absorbed Category 3 water cannot be dried in place and must be removed to prevent ongoing contamination.

**Disinfection and antimicrobial treatment** — Every affected surface is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This is not a general-purpose cleaning step; the chemistry and dwell time matter, and we follow protocols calibrated to the specific pathogens associated with raw sewage.

**Structural drying** — Once contaminated materials are out and surfaces are treated, industrial air movers and dehumidifiers bring the structural moisture content down to safe levels. In Remsen's humid summer months, ambient humidity slows drying, so we monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement accordingly.

**Final verification** — Before we close out a job, we document moisture readings and surface conditions so you have a clear record — useful both for your own peace of mind and for any insurance claim.
