## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Garretson

Proper asbestos abatement is a regulated process, not a weekend demo project. Before any material is removed, we conduct a thorough asbestos inspection and collect samples for laboratory analysis. If testing confirms the presence of asbestos-containing materials, we establish a contained work zone — sealing off the affected area with negative air pressure and heavy-duty poly sheeting so fibers cannot migrate to other parts of the home or property.

Removal follows strict protocols: workers in full protective equipment, wet methods to suppress fiber release, and double-bagged waste that is labeled and transported to a licensed disposal facility. Once abatement is complete, air clearance testing confirms the space is safe before containment is removed and the area is cleared for reconstruction. Every step is documented, which matters when you are selling a property or filing an insurance claim.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically — one of the most common requests we receive from Garretson homeowners updating older interiors — the process requires careful wet application to the textured surface before scraping, preventing the dry material from releasing fibers into the living space.
