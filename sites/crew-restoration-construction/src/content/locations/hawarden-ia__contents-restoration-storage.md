## Why Hawarden Properties See Contents Damage

Hawarden sits in the Big Sioux River floodplain, and that geography shapes how water damage unfolds here. Spring snowmelt and heavy summer rain can push the river toward low-lying properties faster than a sump pump can respond. But flooding isn't the only threat. The housing stock in and around the Downtown Hawarden corridor includes homes built in the early and mid-twentieth century, many with plaster walls, original hardwood floors, and wood-framed furniture that reacts to moisture differently than modern materials. Smoke and soot from a fire in a tight, older floor plan can penetrate upholstered pieces and fabric window treatments within hours. Even a slow pipe leak behind a kitchen wall can create enough ambient humidity to warp wood furniture and cause musty odor to settle into stored clothing and bedding.

The Central Avenue district, with its mix of older residential and light commercial buildings, sees contents losses that span both categories: a business owner's equipment alongside a family's personal property, sometimes in the same structure. That combination requires careful inventory and separation from the start.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything moves. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating. This documentation matters for two reasons: it protects your insurance claim, and it ensures nothing is misplaced during the restoration window.

From there, contents are sorted into three tracks. Items that can be cleaned on-site are treated immediately using dry-cleaning sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and appropriate wet-cleaning methods depending on the material. Items that need more intensive work, including ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing soft goods, and freeze-drying for water-damaged documents and photos, are transported to our facility. Items that are unsalvageable are documented for your adjuster before disposal so you have a complete record.

Storage is climate-controlled and secured. Your belongings stay inventoried and accessible throughout the restoration, and delivery back to your home is coordinated once the structure is cleared.

## Working with Your Insurance Carrier

Contents losses in Hawarden are typically covered under the personal property portion of a standard homeowner's policy, and flood-specific losses may fall under a separate NFIP or private flood policy depending on your coverage. The distinction matters because documentation requirements differ between the two. We photograph and itemize to the standard that both policy types expect, which reduces the back-and-forth with adjusters and helps move your claim forward.

We communicate directly with your carrier throughout the process. If a supplement is needed because additional items are identified during cleaning, we handle that documentation. You are not left to negotiate line items while also managing a displaced household.
