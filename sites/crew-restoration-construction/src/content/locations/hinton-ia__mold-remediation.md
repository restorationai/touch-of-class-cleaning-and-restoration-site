## Our Mold Remediation Process in Hinton

Every job starts with a visual inspection and moisture mapping — not just of the visible mold, but of the surrounding materials. Mold you can see is rarely the full extent of the problem. We use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to trace where water traveled before it evaporated, because active spore growth often follows that hidden path.

Once the scope is established, we set up physical containment using 6-mil poly sheeting and negative air pressure with HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps disturbed spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during removal — a step that matters especially in older homes where HVAC ductwork may run through the same wall cavities being remediated. Affected porous materials (insulation, drywall, wood trim) are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Structural framing that can be dried and treated in place is cleaned with an EPA-registered antimicrobial and dried to verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins.

We don't close a job until clearance readings confirm the remediated area is back within normal parameters.
