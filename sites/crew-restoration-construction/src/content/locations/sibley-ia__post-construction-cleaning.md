## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Sibley

A post-construction clean isn't a single pass with a mop. The sequence matters, and skipping steps creates rework.

**Rough clean first.** Before any detail work, large debris — cut lumber, packaging, excess caulk beads, drywall scraps — is removed from the site. This protects finished surfaces from being scratched during the cleaning phase.

**Top-down particulate removal.** Starting at ceiling height and working down, every surface is HEPA-vacuumed before any wet cleaning begins. On Sibley jobs involving older plaster ceilings, this step takes longer because plaster texture traps dust in ways that smooth drywall doesn't.

**Hard-surface detail cleaning.** Windows, hardware, cabinetry, tile grout, and fixtures are cleaned with appropriate solutions — no one product works on construction adhesive residue, paint overspray, and grout haze simultaneously. Each requires a targeted approach.

**HVAC and register flush.** Construction dust inside ductwork will redistribute the moment the system runs. Registers are pulled, vacuumed, and wiped; the system is run briefly to push any remaining loose debris to filters, which are then replaced.

**Final walk-through inspection.** A second set of eyes goes through every room with a raking light to catch streaks on glass, dust on top of door frames, and any missed adhesive residue before the client or general contractor does their punch-list walk.
