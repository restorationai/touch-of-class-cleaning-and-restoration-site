## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Edgerton

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory. Every item is photographed, logged, and assessed before anything leaves the property. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for your own peace of mind.

Items that can be restored are packed and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility where they're cleaned using methods matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and delicate objects, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor, specialized laundering for textiles and soft furnishings. Wood pieces that have absorbed moisture are dried under controlled conditions rather than rushed, because forcing wood to dry too quickly causes warping and cracking that makes the damage permanent.

Items that are genuinely beyond restoration are documented thoroughly before any disposal decision is made, so your adjuster has a clear record. Nothing is thrown away without your knowledge.

Once the structural work on your home is complete, contents are returned, placed, and accounted for against the original inventory. The goal is a closed loop: every item that left your home is either back in it or represented in a documented loss.
