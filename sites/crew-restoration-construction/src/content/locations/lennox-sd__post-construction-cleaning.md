## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Lennox

Every post-construction clean starts with a walkthrough to assess what trades have been through the space and what they left behind. Then comes the detail phase: HEPA vacuuming of all surfaces including walls and ceilings, cleaning of light fixtures and vents, scrubbing of window tracks and frames, and removal of paint overspray, adhesive residue, and grout haze from hard floors.

For specialty cleaning requests — whether that's post-renovation deep cleaning of a kitchen that had cabinets replaced, or a final clean on a commercial build-out in the 57039 ZIP code — we adjust the scope to match the project. We match the method to the material, not the other way around.

The final phase is a detail inspection pass: wiping down every switch plate, cleaning inside every cabinet, polishing fixtures, and removing any protective film left on appliances or windows by the installing contractor.
