## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Harrisburg

The first priority on any water loss is stopping the spread. When we arrive, we identify the moisture boundary using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a visual guess — because water in a newer Harrisburg home travels fast through open-web floor trusses and blown-in insulation before it becomes visible on a ceiling below.

Once the boundary is mapped, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then establish a drying system calibrated to the actual material readings. Engineered hardwood and LVP flooring common in Journey subdivision homes respond differently to drying pressure than solid hardwood or tile; we adjust airflow and dehumidification accordingly rather than running a one-size drying profile. We document moisture readings daily and provide a written drying log — something insurers and contractors both need if reconstruction follows.

When structural materials like OSB sheathing or drywall have absorbed beyond restorability thresholds, we handle selective demolition and can coordinate reconstruction through the same project, so you're not managing two separate contractors.
