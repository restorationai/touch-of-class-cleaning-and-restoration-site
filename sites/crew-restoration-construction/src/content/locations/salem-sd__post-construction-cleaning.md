## Why Salem Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Salem sits in a high-plains climate where temperature swings between seasons are dramatic and construction seasons are compressed. ## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Salem

Every post-construction clean follows a top-down, inside-out sequence. We start at ceiling level — light fixtures, fans, exposed beam ledges, and the tops of cabinets — and work downward so dislodged debris doesn't re-contaminate surfaces we've already cleaned. HVAC registers and returns are vacuumed and wiped before the system is run, preventing dust from recirculating through the finished space.

Windows get a dedicated pass: first a dry wipe to lift construction film, then a streak-free solution treatment. Grout lines in tile work are detail-cleaned with narrow brushes before sealer is applied. Hard floors are dust-mopped, then damp-mopped with pH-neutral cleaners appropriate to the finish. Carpet protection film — if left by the flooring crew — is removed carefully to avoid adhesive transfer.

For specialty cleaning requests, such as post-renovation deep cleaning of commercial kitchens, medical offices, or rental units in the 57058 ZIP code being turned over after a major update, we adjust the scope accordingly: sanitizing food-contact surfaces, cleaning inside cabinetry and drawers, and deodorizing spaces where construction odors have lingered.

One pattern we've noticed on Salem jobs that doesn't show up the same way in larger metro areas: when construction wraps in late fall and the home is sealed up tight for winter, fine drywall dust and insulation fibers have nowhere to go.
