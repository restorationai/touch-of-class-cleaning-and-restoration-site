## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Canton

Every job begins with a protected assessment — our crew arrives in appropriate personal protective equipment, evaluates the full scope of affected materials, and establishes a containment boundary before any removal begins. Structural materials that cannot be safely decontaminated are properly disposed of and documented. The goal when we leave is a space that is genuinely safe, not just visually clean.

For older Canton homes with plaster walls or original hardwood floors, we adjust our approach: plaster releases absorbed contamination more slowly than modern materials, so dwell times for disinfectants are extended and post-treatment verification is more rigorous. We document every step, which matters both for your peace of mind and for any insurance claim that follows.
