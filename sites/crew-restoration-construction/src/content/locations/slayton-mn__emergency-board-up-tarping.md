## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Slayton

When we arrive at a Slayton property, the first priority is a fast visual assessment of every compromised opening — not just the obvious ones. Smoke pressure during a fire can crack window glazing in rooms that weren't directly involved, and those hairline breaks become entry points for weather and pests. We photograph each opening before we touch anything, creating a documented record that supports your insurance claim.

For broken window board-up, we cut plywood to fit the rough opening, fasten it with structural screws rather than nails, and seal the perimeter with foam backer to block wind infiltration. For roof tarping, we use reinforced poly tarps rated for high-wind exposure — not the lightweight blue tarps sold at hardware stores. The tarp is secured with batten boards screwed into the decking on either side of the damaged area, which distributes load and prevents the tarp from peeling back in the next wind event. On older tongue-and-groove sheathing, we take care with fastener placement to avoid splitting already-stressed boards.

If fire damage is involved, we coordinate board-up with any active scene requirements from the Slayton Fire Department before entering, and we document the condition of the structure for the adjuster.
