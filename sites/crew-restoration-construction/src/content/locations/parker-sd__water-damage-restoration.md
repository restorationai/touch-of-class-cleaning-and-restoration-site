## Why Parker Properties See Water Damage Issues

Turner County's climate delivers hard winters followed by rapid spring thaws, and that freeze-thaw cycle is one of the most consistent sources of water damage in Parker homes. Frost line penetration can stress older supply lines, and when temperatures swing sharply in late February or March, pipe failures tend to cluster. The clay-dominant soils common across this part of South Dakota don't absorb surface water efficiently — during heavy spring rains, that water pools against foundation walls and finds any crack or gap in the masonry.

Parker's housing stock skews older, with a significant share of homes built before the 1970s. These structures often have original cast-iron or galvanized steel supply lines that have reached or exceeded their service life, and they may lack modern vapor barriers in crawl spaces. When a leak develops in one of these homes, moisture wicks into original wood framing and subfloor boards more aggressively than it would in newer engineered lumber — and it holds there longer.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Parker
