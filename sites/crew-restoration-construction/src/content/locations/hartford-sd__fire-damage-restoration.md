## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Hartford

The work begins with a room-by-room scope — not just the rooms with visible char, but every space smoke could have reached. That means testing ductwork, pulling outlet covers to check wall cavities, and probing subfloor assemblies where suppression water may have pooled unseen.

From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Board-up and stabilization** — securing openings against weather and unauthorized entry, critical in a community where properties can sit exposed between the fire event and insurance adjuster visits.
- **Structural assessment** — identifying load-bearing members compromised by heat before any debris removal begins.
- **Controlled demolition** — removing only what cannot be restored, documented photographically for insurance purposes.
- **Soot and smoke residue cleaning** — dry chemical sponges for loose soot, alkaline cleaners for protein-based residues (common after kitchen fires), and HEPA vacuuming throughout.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment to address smoke compounds embedded in framing, insulation, and HVAC systems.
- **Reconstruction** — rebuilding to current South Dakota code, which may require updates to electrical, insulation, or egress that weren't present in the original structure.
