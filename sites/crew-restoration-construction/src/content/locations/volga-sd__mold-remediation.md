## Why Volga Properties See Mold Issues

South Dakota's eastern prairie climate delivers dramatic temperature swings between seasons, and Volga sits squarely in that pattern. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Volga

Every job starts with a thorough moisture investigation, not just a visual scan. We use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to map where water has traveled behind walls and under flooring — because mold growing in a wall cavity won't be visible from the surface. That mapping shapes the containment plan.

Containment comes before any physical removal. We seal affected areas with poly sheeting and establish negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers, which prevents spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the remediation work. In Volga's older homes, where HVAC ductwork often runs through unconditioned spaces, we also isolate and cover supply and return registers to keep the system from redistributing spores.

Once containment is established, affected materials are removed following established industry protocols — wet insulation, compromised drywall, and visibly colonized wood framing are bagged and disposed of properly. Structural wood that tests within salvageable moisture content is treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution and allowed to dry to target levels before any reconstruction begins. We don't close walls until moisture readings confirm the substrate is ready.
