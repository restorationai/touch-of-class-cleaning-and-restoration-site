## Our Contents Restoration Process in Brookings

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed and catalogued, which protects you during the insurance claim and gives us a baseline for tracking what goes where. Salvageable contents are packed out carefully and transported to a climate-controlled storage facility while the structure is dried, cleaned, or repaired. That separation matters: contents left on-site during drying or mold remediation absorb additional moisture and odor, which turns a restorable item into a write-off.

In the facility, different categories of items go through different processes. Upholstered furniture and textiles are treated with thermal or ozone-based deodorization to break down smoke and mildew compounds at the molecular level, not just mask them. Hard surfaces, cabinetry, and electronics are cleaned with appropriate solvents and evaluated for function. Documents and photographs, which are often the hardest losses to accept, can sometimes be stabilized and dried if they reach us before mold colonizes the paper, typically within 24 to 48 hours of water exposure. Once the structure is cleared, contents are returned, inventoried again, and placed.
