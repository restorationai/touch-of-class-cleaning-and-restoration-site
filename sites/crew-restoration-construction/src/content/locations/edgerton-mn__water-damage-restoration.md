## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Edgerton

When we arrive at a property, the first priority is stopping the source and measuring the spread. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map exactly how far water has migrated into walls, subfloors, and ceiling cavities — because in a 1950s or 1960s home, water travels further than it looks on the surface.

Once the scope is documented, we move into extraction. Industrial truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from flooring and cavities before it can wick further into structural materials. After extraction, we set a drying system calibrated to the specific material types and ambient conditions: commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern across wet surfaces, and daily moisture readings to track progress against drying targets. Nothing gets signed off as dry until the readings match the dry standard for that material — not a visual inspection, not a gut call.

If flooring, drywall, or insulation has absorbed beyond the threshold for in-place drying, we remove and bag the material, document it with photographs and measurements for your insurance file, and prepare the structure for reconstruction once drying is confirmed complete.
