## Why Crooks Properties See Mold Issues

The Sioux Falls metro fringe has a specific moisture problem that purely urban neighborhoods don't face as acutely: agricultural drainage. Fields surrounding Crooks are tile-drained, and during heavy rain events that tile system can back-pressure into low-lying yards and foundations. Homes near West Crooks with older poured-concrete or block foundations are especially vulnerable — hairline cracks that look cosmetic become wicking points for soil moisture over a decade or two.

The region also swings between hard freezes and rapid thaws. A mid-February warm spell followed by a refreeze can crack supply lines inside exterior walls — a slow drip that goes unnoticed until the drywall paper starts to bubble. By that point, Cladosporium or Aspergillus colonies are often already visible. Black mold (Stachybotrys chartarum) typically follows chronic, sustained moisture rather than a single event, but it does appear in Crooks homes where a roof leak or plumbing failure went unaddressed through a full winter.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Crooks

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to find wet material that isn't visible to the eye. We don't guess at the boundary of the affected area; we measure it.

Once we know the scope, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms while we work. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, subflooring — are removed to clean margins, bagged, and disposed of properly. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution.

Drying is not optional after mold abatement. If the moisture source that fed the colony isn't corrected and the structure isn't brought to normal moisture content, mold returns. We use commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and axial air movers sized to the space — not the residential units you can rent at a hardware store. Final moisture readings are documented before any reconstruction begins.
