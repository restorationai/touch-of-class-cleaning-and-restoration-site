## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Dakota Dunes

The first step after a fire is a thorough damage assessment — not just of what burned, but of where smoke traveled. In a two-story home near the Dunes golf community, that often means tracing odor pathways through shared ductwork to bedrooms and bonus rooms far from the fire's origin. We document everything photographically before touching a surface, which matters both for your insurance claim and for our own restoration plan.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves through several concrete phases:

- **Board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry
- **Soot and smoke residue removal** using dry chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and wet cleaning agents matched to the surface type — stone, wood, painted drywall, and fabric each require a different chemistry
- **Odor neutralization** through thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation, not masking sprays
- **Structural drying** of any water-saturated materials from suppression efforts, monitored with moisture meters until readings return to baseline
- **Reconstruction** of damaged framing, drywall, flooring, and finishes, coordinated with subcontractors who work in the local market

We handle the full scope under one contract, which simplifies insurance billing and avoids the coordination gaps that happen when a cleanup crew hands off to a separate rebuild contractor.
