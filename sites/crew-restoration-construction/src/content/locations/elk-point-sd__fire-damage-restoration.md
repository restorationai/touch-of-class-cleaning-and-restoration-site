## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Elk Point

Every post-fire restoration job starts with a thorough scope — not a quick visual walk-through, but a systematic assessment of smoke migration, structural integrity, and material salvageability. For Elk Point properties, that often means probing plaster walls and original hardwood subfloors that may look intact but have absorbed odor compounds at depth.

From there, the process moves through board-up and weatherproofing (critical in South Dakota's shoulder seasons), debris removal, HEPA-filtered air scrubbing, and chemical sponge dry-cleaning of soot-coated surfaces before any wet cleaning begins. Applying water to dry soot smears it and sets it permanently — a mistake that costs more to correct than it saves in time. Odor neutralization follows structural cleaning, using thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation depending on the materials involved. Reconstruction is coordinated as a single project rather than handed off to a separate contractor, keeping the timeline tighter and the communication cleaner.
