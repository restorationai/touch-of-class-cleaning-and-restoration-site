## Why Fulda Properties Are Vulnerable to Severe Fire Damage

Fulda's housing stock skews older. Many homes in and around Downtown Fulda were built in the mid-20th century, when balloon-frame construction was standard. In balloon framing, wall cavities run continuously from the basement sill plate to the roof rafters — an open channel that lets fire and smoke travel vertically through the entire structure in minutes. What looks like a contained kitchen fire can mean smoke contamination in every room above it by the time the engine company arrives.

Murray County winters also affect how fire damage behaves after the fact. When firefighting water hits a structure in sub-zero temperatures, it freezes inside wall cavities, under flooring, and in insulation batt — creating secondary water and ice damage that compounds the fire loss. Crews working a winter fire in Fulda have to account for both the burn damage and the freeze cycle that follows the suppression effort.

The proximity to Seven Mile Lake and Fulda Lakes also means elevated ambient humidity during warmer months. That moisture accelerates the rate at which soot and smoke residue bond to porous surfaces like drywall, wood trim, and fabric — the longer those surfaces sit untreated, the harder the cleaning process becomes.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Fulda

The first step on any fire loss is a thorough scope — not just the rooms with visible char, but every space smoke could have reached. We use thermal imaging and air sampling to trace smoke migration through wall cavities and ductwork before any demolition begins. That scope drives the estimate and the insurance documentation.

Once the scope is set, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Emergency board-up and tarping** to secure the structure against weather and unauthorized entry
- **Selective demolition** of unsalvageable materials — charred framing, melted insulation, smoke-saturated drywall
- **HEPA vacuuming and dry-chemical sponge cleaning** of smoke residue on salvageable surfaces
- **Hydroxyl or ozone treatment** for odor neutralization in enclosed spaces
- **Structural drying** if firefighting water is present, using commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers
- **Reconstruction** — framing, drywall, insulation, finish work — handled by the same crew, so there is no handoff gap between mitigation and rebuild

Keeping mitigation and reconstruction under one contract matters in a small market like Fulda, where scheduling a separate general contractor can add weeks to a family's displacement.

## Local Note

Older homes in the blocks surrounding Downtown Fulda frequently have original plaster-and-lath interior walls rather than modern drywall. Plaster is denser and more alkaline than gypsum board, which changes how smoke residue adheres to it — and how cleaning chemistry needs to be adjusted. Aggressive alkaline cleaners that work well on drywall can etch or discolor plaster finish coats. Our crews identify plaster surfaces during the initial scope and switch to pH-neutral cleaning agents and dry-sponge methods on those walls, which preserves the original finish and avoids a costly replaster on rooms that didn't need replacement.
