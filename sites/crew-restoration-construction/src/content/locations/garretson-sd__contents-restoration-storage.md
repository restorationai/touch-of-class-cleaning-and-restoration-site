## Why Garretson Homes Face Particular Contents Risks

The housing stock in and around Downtown Garretson leans older, with a significant share of homes built in the mid-twentieth century or earlier. Those homes often have original wood floors, plaster walls, and older cabinetry that hold moisture and odor differently than modern construction. When a pipe bursts or a sump fails, water wicks into wood furniture and upholstered pieces quickly because the surrounding structure is already slower to dry. Smoke from a kitchen or chimney fire in an older home tends to penetrate deeper into porous surfaces, including the contents stored in adjacent rooms.

The proximity to Palisades State Park also means many Garretson properties sit near low-lying creek drainage. Split Rock Creek flooding can push water into basements and lower living spaces, and the contents stored there, seasonal gear, family archives, appliances, are often the first casualties. High humidity following a flood event accelerates mold colonization on fabric and paper; under the right conditions, that process begins within 24 to 48 hours of initial exposure.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Garretson

Contents restoration starts with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued by room, and assessed for the type of damage present: water saturation, smoke or soot coating, odor penetration, or structural compromise. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was recovered and what was not.

Items that can be restored are packed out to a climate-controlled facility where the actual cleaning work happens. Depending on the material, that may involve ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, dry cleaning or laundering for textiles, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor, or specialized drying chambers for documents and electronics. The IICRC-certified technicians on the Crew team follow structured protocols for each material category rather than applying a one-size approach.

While restoration work is underway, your belongings remain in secure, climate-controlled storage. That matters especially during the Garretson construction season, when structural repairs to your home can take weeks and you need a reliable place for your contents in the meantime. Once the structure is ready and the contents have been cleaned and cleared, everything is returned, room by room, to its original placement.

## Working with Insurance on Contents Claims in Garretson

Contents losses are often the most contested part of a property insurance claim because adjusters and policyholders sometimes disagree on what is restorable versus what needs replacement. The detailed inventory and condition documentation Crew produces at the start of the job gives your adjuster a clear, itemized picture of the loss. We work alongside your carrier throughout the process and can provide the supporting documentation needed for contents line items on your claim. If you have questions about what your policy covers for personal property, your agent or adjuster is the right resource, but we can help you understand what the restoration process looks like so you can have an informed conversation with them.
