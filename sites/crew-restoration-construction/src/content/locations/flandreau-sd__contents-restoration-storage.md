## Why Flandreau Properties Face Distinct Contents Challenges

Flandreau's position along the Big Sioux River corridor makes it one of the more flood-aware communities in eastern South Dakota. Homes near the river bottom can experience water intrusion that moves fast and carries silt, which settles into upholstery fibers and cabinet interiors in ways that ordinary cleaning cannot reach. Beyond flooding, the region's wide temperature swings, from bitter January cold to humid July heat, accelerate the kind of secondary damage that makes contents restoration time-sensitive: mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours on porous materials left damp, and smoke odor from a fire bonds more stubbornly to fabrics in high-humidity conditions.

Downtown Flandreau's older commercial and residential buildings, many constructed in the mid-20th century, often feature original hardwood furniture, plaster walls, and older cabinetry that require gentler handling than modern particleboard pieces. These materials are worth saving, but they respond differently to moisture and cleaning agents. Knowing that distinction before a technician reaches your door is the difference between restoration and replacement.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Flandreau

Every contents job begins with a detailed inventory. Before a single item leaves your property, our team photographs and logs it, so you and your insurance adjuster have a clear record of what was removed, its condition at the time of pack-out, and where it is stored. That documentation matters especially when claims involve older or handcrafted items, which are common in homes on and around the Flandreau Santee Sioux Reservation and in the established neighborhoods near Royal River Casino.

Once items are transported to our climate-controlled storage facility, the restoration process is matched to the type of damage:

- **Smoke and soot:** Dry-cleaning sponges and HEPA vacuuming remove loose particulate first. Ultrasonic cleaning equipment reaches into crevices on hard goods that hand-wiping cannot. Ozone and hydroxyl treatments address embedded odor in textiles.
- **Water and flood damage:** Contents are assessed for salvageability, dried using controlled airflow, and treated for microbial risk before being cleaned and deodorized.
- **Document and photo recovery:** Wet paper items are freeze-dried or air-dried under controlled conditions to prevent further deterioration.

Our IICRC-certified technicians guide each step, and items are not returned until they meet the same cleaning standards we apply to the structure itself.

## Insurance Coordination for Flandreau Contents Claims

Contents losses are often the most disputed portion of a property insurance claim because adjusters and policyholders sometimes disagree on replacement value versus restoration cost. Our detailed pack-out inventory, combined with before-and-after documentation for each item, gives your adjuster the evidence needed to process the claim accurately. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster throughout the process, reducing the back-and-forth that extends timelines.

If your policy includes Additional Living Expenses coverage, contents storage fees are frequently reimbursable while your home is being repaired. Ask your adjuster specifically about that line item when you file.
