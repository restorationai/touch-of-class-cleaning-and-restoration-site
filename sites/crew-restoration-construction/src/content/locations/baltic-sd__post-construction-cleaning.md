## Why Baltic Properties See Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Baltic's housing stock skews toward established single-family homes built when construction standards were different, alongside newer infill builds and additions that mix old and new materials in the same structure. That combination creates cleaning complications that a generic janitorial crew isn't equipped to handle. Older plaster and lathe walls common in homes near Downtown Baltic absorb construction dust differently than modern drywall — particulates settle deeper into porous surfaces and require HEPA-filtered equipment rather than a standard shop vac.

The region's climate adds another layer. South Dakota's temperature swings between winter and early spring mean that construction projects often wrap up while humidity is still fluctuating. Concrete and grout that cured during cold snaps can leave a heavier efflorescence bloom on surfaces, and adhesive residues from flooring installs behave differently when they've been exposed to repeated freeze-thaw cycles. Getting those surfaces truly clean — not just wiped — requires the right chemistry and the patience to do a second pass.

Homes in the Big Sioux River area also face a seasonal reality: spring runoff can push moisture into basements and crawl spaces right around the time a renovation is finishing up. If a project involved any below-grade work, a proper final clean has to account for residual moisture that a crew may have tracked in, not just the debris they left on the surface.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Baltic

Every post-construction clean starts with a walk-through to document what trades were on-site and what materials were used. That matters because the cleaning sequence changes depending on whether the project involved tile work, spray foam insulation, fresh paint, or all three. We work top-to-bottom and inside-out: ceiling fixtures and vents before floors, interior surfaces before exterior thresholds.

The rough clean removes bulk debris — drywall scraps, packaging, tape, and drop cloth residue. The detail clean follows with HEPA vacuuming of every horizontal surface, window track scrubbing, cabinet interior wipe-downs, and grout haze removal from tile. The final clean is the pass that makes the space look occupied rather than just finished: glass polished streak-free, hardware wiped of fingerprints and adhesive, and floors inspected under raking light to catch anything missed.

For specialty cleaning needs — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated kitchen, deep-cleaning a rental unit after a long tenancy, or sanitizing a space following a biohazard event — the process is scoped individually. There's no one-size template because the contaminants and surfaces are never identical.
