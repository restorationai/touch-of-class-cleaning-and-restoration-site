## Why Merrill Properties See Siding and Gutter Deterioration Faster Than You'd Expect

The Floyd River valley creates a microclimate that amplifies freeze-thaw cycling. Moisture from the river basin rises overnight, soaks into any gap in a siding lap or a caulked joint, and then freezes when temperatures drop below 32°F — which in northwest Iowa can happen on the same calendar day as a 55-degree afternoon in March or November. That repeated expansion cracks paint films, lifts vinyl panels off their locking channels, and works water behind fiber cement boards before a homeowner notices anything from the street.

Gutters compound the problem. Cottonwood and elm seeds common along the Floyd River corridor pack into 5-inch K-style gutters in late spring and again in fall. When downspouts clog, water backs up under the first course of shingles and seeps behind the top row of siding — the exact entry point that leads to rotted OSB sheathing or, in older homes, rotted board-and-batten. Homes built before 1970, which make up a significant share of Merrill's residential stock, were often sided with wood products that have long since lost their factory finish and are now absorbing rather than shedding water.

## Our Siding and Gutter Process in Merrill

Every project starts with a physical inspection of the full exterior, not just the section that caught your eye. We probe the sheathing layer at corners, around window and door penetrations, and at the roofline where gutter overflow most commonly saturates the wall assembly. That inspection shapes the scope: sometimes a partial siding replacement and a gutter cleaning with new downspout extensions solves the problem; sometimes the sheathing needs to come out before new siding goes on.

For siding installation, we work with vinyl, fiber cement (James Hardie and comparable products), and engineered wood depending on the project's exposure and budget. In Merrill's wind corridor, we pay particular attention to nailing patterns and panel overlap — under-nailed vinyl in a sustained 40-mph northwest wind will rack and gap within a season. For gutters, we install seamless aluminum runs fabricated on-site, sized to the actual roof pitch and square footage rather than defaulting to a standard width. Downspout placement is coordinated with the grade around the foundation so water exits well away from the structure.

Throughout the job, we document existing conditions with photos before demolition and again at each stage — useful if an insurance claim is involved, and useful for your own records regardless.

## Local Note: What Older Merrill Homes Reveal Once Siding Comes Off

Contractors who haven't worked in small Plymouth County communities are sometimes surprised by what's behind the siding on homes built in the 1940s and 1950s near the original Downtown Merrill blocks. Many of those houses were sided over original clapboard without a housewrap or felt layer — just new vinyl or aluminum laid directly over the old wood. When that outer layer comes off, the clapboard underneath is often checked and gray, and the sheathing boards beneath it are skip-nailed with large gaps. That gap-sheathing pattern means you can't rely on the existing substrate for a clean nailing base; furring or a full sheathing replacement is frequently the right call before new siding goes on. We flag this possibility upfront so the project scope doesn't expand unexpectedly mid-job.
