## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Volga

Every project starts with a full roof inspection — not a visual scan from the driveway, but a hands-on assessment of decking condition, flashing integrity, ventilation, and existing underlayment. In Volga's climate, ventilation is not optional; inadequate attic airflow is a primary driver of ice dam formation and premature shingle failure, so we evaluate ridge and soffit ventilation as part of every scope.

Once the scope is confirmed, we strip existing materials down to the deck, inspect and replace any soft or compromised sheathing, and install a synthetic underlayment rated for the temperature ranges South Dakota produces. For most residential replacements in Volga, we specify impact-resistant Class 4 shingles — a designation that can also qualify homeowners for insurance premium reductions, which we document for your carrier.

The job closes with a full debris removal pass and a final inspection walkthrough with the homeowner before we consider the project complete.
