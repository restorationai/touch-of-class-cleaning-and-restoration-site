## Why Hawarden Properties See Asbestos Concerns

Sioux County's cold, dry winters create a specific maintenance pattern: older homes in Hawarden get updated in waves, often when a furnace fails or a bathroom is gutted. ## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Hawarden

Every project starts with a formal inspection and bulk sampling — no assumptions based on visual appearance alone. Samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and results typically return within a few business days. If asbestos-containing materials are confirmed, we establish a contained work zone using negative air pressure and HEPA filtration before any removal begins. Workers wear full personal protective equipment throughout, and all removed material is wetted, double-bagged, and transported to an approved disposal facility in compliance with Iowa DNR and EPA regulations.

For popcorn ceiling asbestos removal specifically, we wet the texture thoroughly before scraping to suppress fiber release — a step that's easy to skip but critical for keeping air counts below action levels. After removal, the area undergoes air clearance testing before containment is broken. You receive documentation of the clearance results, which your contractor, lender, or insurance carrier may require before work continues.
