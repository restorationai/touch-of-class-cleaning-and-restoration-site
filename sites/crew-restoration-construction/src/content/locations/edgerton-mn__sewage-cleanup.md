## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Edgerton

The first thing a crew does on arrival is assess the contamination boundary — how far the sewage has traveled across flooring, how high it has wicked up drywall or paneling, and whether the HVAC system has pulled airborne particulates into ductwork. That boundary determines the scope of removal.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted or portable wet-vac units pull standing waste from hard surfaces and subfloor cavities. Any porous material — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that has direct contact with Category 3 water is bagged and removed. There is no salvaging those materials safely.

Once the space is clear, EPA-registered antimicrobial agents go on every affected surface: concrete, framing lumber, joists, and any remaining wall material. The chemistry targets the bacteria, viruses, and fungi that raw sewage introduces. After application and dwell time, industrial air movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in the structural materials return to pre-loss baselines. A final post-sanitization inspection confirms the space is safe before reconstruction begins.
