## Why Fulda Properties See Siding and Gutter Deterioration Faster Than You'd Expect

Murray County sits in a climate band that delivers genuine extremes — subzero wind chills in January and humid, storm-heavy summers that push heavy rain through in short windows. That combination is hard on exterior materials in a specific way: siding expands and contracts with temperature swings, and the joints that seal panels against wind-driven rain open up over time. Once water finds a path behind the siding, it has nowhere to go except into the sheathing and framing.

Gutters face the same seasonal stress. Freeze-thaw cycles through late winter and early spring cause standing water in low-pitched gutter runs to ice, expand, and pull fasteners loose from the fascia. By the time the ice clears, the gutter may be sagging or separated at the seam — which means the first real rain of April is running directly down the foundation wall instead of away from it. Homes near Lakeside Fulda and along the recreational corridors around the lakes tend to see this pattern earlier because wind off the water accelerates both drying cycles in summer and freeze events in winter.
