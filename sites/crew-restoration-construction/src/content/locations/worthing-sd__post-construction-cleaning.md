## Our Post-Construction Cleaning Process in Worthing

A final clean is not a single pass. The work moves in deliberate phases so nothing gets re-contaminated after it's been cleaned.

**Rough clean** comes first — bulk debris removal, scraping adhesive labels off windows, pulling protective film off appliances and countertops, and collecting all loose material before any wet cleaning begins.

**Detail clean** follows. This is where the real time goes: wiping down every cabinet box interior and exterior, cleaning inside every drawer, scrubbing grout haze off tile, polishing fixtures, and cleaning inside window tracks. HVAC registers are pulled and vacuumed before air is circulated through the system — skipping this step means blowing construction dust back onto every surface you just cleaned.

**Final inspection clean** addresses anything that was touched or scuffed during the builder's punch-list walk. Paint touch-ups, final hardware installation, and contractor foot traffic all leave new marks. This pass ensures the space is genuinely move-in ready, not just visually clean at a distance.

For specialty cleaning requests — post-renovation deep cleans in occupied homes, pressure washing of driveways and flatwork, or cleaning after a partial remodel where the rest of the house stayed lived-in — the scope is scoped individually based on what trades were on site and how long the project ran.
