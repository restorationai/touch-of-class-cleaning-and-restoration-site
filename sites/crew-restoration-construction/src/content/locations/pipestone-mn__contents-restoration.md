## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Pipestone

The process begins with a detailed inventory at your property — every item is photographed, catalogued by category, and assigned a condition rating before anything is moved. ## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about Pipestone specifically: the pink Sioux quartzite that defines the town's historic buildings also shows up as decorative stone, fireplace surrounds, and accent features inside older homes. Quartzite is porous enough to absorb smoke residue and soot, and it reacts poorly to acidic cleaners — a common ingredient in off-the-shelf soot removers. If your home has quartzite features or decorative stonework, flag that during the initial call. Cleaning those surfaces requires a pH-neutral approach and hand application rather than spray-and-wipe, and it's the kind of detail that gets missed when a crew is treating every surface the same way.
