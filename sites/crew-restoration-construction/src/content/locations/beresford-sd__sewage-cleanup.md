## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Beresford

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage mitigation. The contamination profile requires a stricter protocol from the first minute on-site.

**Containment and safety setup** comes first. Technicians establish a work perimeter, put on appropriate PPE, and assess whether HVAC systems need to be isolated to prevent aerosolized pathogens from spreading to unaffected areas of the home.

**Extraction and removal** follows. Standing sewage is removed with truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — that have absorbed Category 3 water are documented, photographed, and removed. There is no drying protocol that renders saturated porous material safe after sewage contact; removal is the only defensible standard.

**Structural cleaning and disinfection** covers every hard surface the sewage touched. We use EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination, applied in the correct dwell time, not wiped on and immediately off. Subfloor, concrete, and framing are treated and allowed to reach verified moisture content before any reconstruction begins.

**Drying and clearance** uses calibrated desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers positioned per drying science principles. Moisture readings are logged daily until the structure reaches baseline — typically measured against unaffected areas of the same home.
