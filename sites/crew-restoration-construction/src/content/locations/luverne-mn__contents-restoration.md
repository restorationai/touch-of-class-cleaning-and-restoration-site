## Why Luverne Homes See More Contents Damage Than You'd Expect

Luverne sits at the edge of the Coteau des Prairies, where spring and summer storm cells build quickly and hit hard. Wind-driven rain finds its way through roof decking, around window frames, and into attic spaces with very little warning. The housing stock around Downtown Luverne skews older — many homes were built between the 1920s and 1960s — and those structures tend to have plaster-and-lath interior walls, original hardwood floors, and built-in cabinetry that absorbs moisture differently than modern construction. Once water gets into a room with plaster walls, it doesn't stay on the surface; it migrates laterally behind baseboards and under flooring, and the belongings sitting on or near those surfaces start wicking moisture within the first few hours.

Fire damage adds another layer of complexity. Wood-burning fireplaces and older forced-air furnaces are common in Rock County homes, and a chimney fire or furnace puff-back coats every soft surface in the house with oily soot that bonds quickly to fabric fibers and porous materials. The longer that residue sits, the harder it is to reverse — which is why getting contents out of the affected space and into a controlled cleaning environment matters as much as putting out the fire.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Luverne

The first step on any job is a detailed inventory. Every item we remove from your home — furniture, clothing, electronics, artwork, documents, kitchen goods — is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating before it leaves the property. That documentation becomes part of your insurance claim file, which simplifies the adjuster review process considerably.

From there, contents move to our secure storage facility where temperature and humidity are controlled. Wet or smoke-affected items go through the appropriate cleaning process: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-saturated soft goods, and specialized textile cleaning for upholstered furniture and area rugs. Items that are restorable get cleaned, dried, and held until your home is ready. Items that are beyond recovery are documented for your claim rather than discarded without your knowledge.

Once structural repairs are complete, we coordinate the pack-back — returning your belongings to the rooms where they belong, in the condition we promised.

## Local Note

One thing that catches homeowners off guard in the Luverne area: the combination of low humidity in winter and high humidity in summer creates a wide seasonal swing in how porous materials behave. Antique wooden furniture that came through a summer flood may look stable once it dries, but if it dried too quickly in a heated space during a cold-weather event, the wood fibers contract unevenly and joints fail months later. We account for this by slowing the drying process on high-value wood pieces and monitoring moisture content with pin meters rather than relying on visual inspection alone. It's a small step that prevents a lot of expensive surprises down the road.
