## Why Worthing Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Because Worthing (ZIP 57077) has grown rapidly as a Sioux Falls satellite community, its housing stock skews newer — many homes built in the 2000s and 2010s with open floor plans, engineered hardwood, and LVP flooring. South Dakota's climate adds another layer of complexity. Worthing sits in a region where summer humidity can spike and winters push indoor heating systems hard. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Worthing

Every biohazard situation is different, but the framework is consistent: contain, remove, disinfect, verify, and restore. When our crew arrives at a Worthing property, the first priority is assessing the full extent of contamination — not just what is visible. That means checking adjacent surfaces, HVAC returns if the system was running, and any porous materials in the affected area.

All biohazardous materials are collected and transported in accordance with South Dakota Department of Environment and Natural Resources regulations and applicable federal OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards. Nothing is bagged and left curbside. Disposal follows a documented chain of custody, which matters both for your peace of mind and for any insurance or legal documentation that may follow.

After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific pathogen risk. We then conduct post-remediation verification — swab testing or ATP testing depending on the situation — before any restoration work begins. You receive written documentation of the process, which is increasingly required by South Dakota insurers handling these claims.
