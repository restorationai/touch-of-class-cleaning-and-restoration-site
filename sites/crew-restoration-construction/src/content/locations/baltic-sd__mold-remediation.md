South Dakota's temperature swings also matter. Homes that are tightly sealed against winter cold can trap interior humidity, and when warm air hits a cold basement wall, condensation forms. Over a season or two, that's enough to feed a mold problem even without a flood event.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Baltic

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the visible surface, but moisture readings behind walls, under flooring, and inside HVAC cavities. Mold that's visible on drywall is almost always a sign of a larger colony hidden in the wall assembly behind it.

Once the scope is mapped, the affected area is isolated with physical containment barriers and negative air pressure so that spores disturbed during removal don't migrate to unaffected rooms. This step matters especially in smaller Baltic homes where living spaces are close together and an HVAC system can distribute spores quickly if containment is skipped.

Contaminated materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing that has exceeded surface mold thresholds — are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Remaining structural surfaces are treated and dried to documented moisture levels. We don't consider remediation complete until moisture readings confirm the environment won't support regrowth. Final clearance testing, when requested or required by an insurer, is coordinated with an independent party so the result carries credibility with all stakeholders.
