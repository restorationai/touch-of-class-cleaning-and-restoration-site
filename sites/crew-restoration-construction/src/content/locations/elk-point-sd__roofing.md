## Why Elk Point Properties See Accelerated Roofing Wear

The Union County area experiences a climate that sits at an uncomfortable crossroads: enough summer heat to bake asphalt shingles, enough winter cold to make them brittle, and spring storm corridors that push hail and high-wind events through the Missouri River valley with regularity. Homes near Downtown Elk Point — many of them built in the early to mid-twentieth century — were often roofed with materials and installation practices that predate modern wind-resistance ratings. A 3-tab shingle nailed with four fasteners instead of six might have survived a decade, but it's increasingly vulnerable as storm intensity trends upward.

Beyond weather, the age of the housing stock matters. Older homes in this part of South Dakota frequently have roof decking made from 1×6 skip sheathing rather than plywood or OSB. When a full replacement is on the table, that decking often needs evaluation — and sometimes replacement — before new shingles go down. Skipping that step leads to soft spots, fastener pull-through, and a shortened lifespan for an otherwise good roof.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Elk Point

Every project starts with a physical inspection — not a drive-by estimate. We get on the roof, check flashing around chimneys and pipe boots, probe the decking for soft areas, and document what we find with photos. That documentation matters if you're filing an insurance claim after hail or wind damage, and it gives you an honest baseline regardless of how you're paying.

Once the scope is clear, we pull any required permits through Union County. Elk Point is the county seat, and the permitting process here is straightforward, but skipping it creates problems at resale and can void manufacturer warranties on architectural shingles. We handle that paperwork as part of the job.

During installation, old shingles and underlayment come off completely — we don't layer over existing material when a full replacement is warranted. Decking is inspected board by board. New synthetic underlayment goes down before any shingles, ice-and-water shield is run along eaves and valleys where freeze-thaw cycles create the most risk, and ridge ventilation is verified or added to prevent heat and moisture buildup in the attic. Cleanup and haul-off are included; we're not leaving a dumpster in your driveway longer than necessary.
