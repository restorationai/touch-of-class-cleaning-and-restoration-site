## Why Garretson Properties Present Unique Post-Construction Cleaning Challenges

Garretson sits in a part of South Dakota where the soil is a mix of clay-heavy glacial till and the distinctive red quartzite dust that weathers off the Sioux quartzite formations visible at Palisades State Park. That quartzite dust is not ordinary construction grit. It is fine, abrasive, and electrically static — it clings to painted surfaces, embeds in grout lines, and coats HVAC filters faster than standard drywall compound dust does. Contractors working near the Palisades area often track it indoors on boot treads, and once it dries on finished flooring, it can scratch hardwood if swept dry rather than HEPA-vacuumed first.

Garretson's housing stock also skews older in its core neighborhoods. Many homes in and around Downtown Garretson were built in the mid-twentieth century with original hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath wall sections, and older window casings that collect debris in ways that newer construction does not. A renovation on one of these homes requires a cleaning crew that understands how to treat original materials — not just blast everything with a pressure washer and call it done.

South Dakota's climate adds another layer. The region swings between humid summers and brutally dry winters. Construction projects that wrap up in late fall often leave interiors with elevated dust loads right as heating systems kick on for the first time — circulating fine particulates through every room. A thorough post-construction clean before that first heat cycle protects both the occupants and the HVAC equipment.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Garretson

Every post-construction clean Crew Restoration & Construction performs follows a structured sequence, because order matters. Cleaning ceilings after you've cleaned floors is how you do the job twice.

**Rough clean first.** We remove bulk debris — lumber scraps, packaging, adhesive backing, and loose material — before any fine cleaning begins. On older Downtown Garretson properties, this phase sometimes uncovers surprises: original flooring under temporary protection paper, or plaster debris that settled behind baseboard trim during demo.

**HEPA vacuuming before any wet work.** Every horizontal surface, window track, cabinet interior, and duct register gets HEPA-vacuumed before we introduce any moisture. This is especially important given the quartzite-influenced dust profile in this area — wet-mopping abrasive particulates into grout or hardwood grain causes damage that is difficult to reverse.

**Detail cleaning of fixtures, hardware, and glass.** Construction adhesive, paint overspray, caulk smears, and sticker residue on windows and fixtures require solvent-appropriate spot treatment. We match the solvent to the surface — what removes silicone caulk from glass is not what you use on powder-coated hardware.

**Final inspection walk-through.** Before we close out, we walk the space with the property owner or general contractor using raking light — a handheld light held at a low angle to the surface — to catch dust, streaks, and missed spots that overhead lighting hides. This is standard practice on our final cleans regardless of property size.
