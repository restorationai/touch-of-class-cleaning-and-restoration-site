## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Worthing

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the cleanup process reflects that. When we arrive at a Worthing property, the first step is containment: sealing off the affected area from the rest of the home to prevent cross-contamination on HVAC systems and foot traffic. Porous materials that have absorbed raw sewage — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed and bagged for disposal per South Dakota Department of Agriculture and Natural Resources guidelines.

Once the space is cleared, we apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to all affected hard surfaces, subfloor, and framing. This isn't a single spray-and-wipe step; we use a dwell-time protocol that gives the disinfectant adequate contact time to address pathogens. Structural drying follows, using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers staged to pull moisture out of the framing before mold has a chance to colonize — a window that is typically 24 to 48 hours after initial exposure. We document conditions at every phase with moisture readings and photographs, which also supports your insurance claim.
