## Why Remsen Properties See Water Damage Issues

Plymouth County's freeze-thaw cycle is one of the most reliable sources of water damage in northwest Iowa. Temperatures regularly swing below zero in January and February, then climb above freezing within days — a pattern that stresses supply lines running through uninsulated exterior walls, a construction detail common in homes built before modern energy codes. Sump pump failures during spring thaw are the other frequent culprit. When the ground is saturated and the pump motor gives out at 2 a.m., a finished basement can take on several inches of water before anyone notices.

The St. Mary's district, with its concentration of older single-family homes close to the church and school, tends to have original clay tile drain systems that crack under root pressure and soil movement. Those cracks allow groundwater infiltration that looks, at first, like a simple wet floor — but the moisture source is continuous, not a one-time event, and the drying approach has to account for that.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Remsen

The first thing we do on any water damage job is find the source and stop it. That sounds obvious, but in Remsen's older homes it sometimes means tracing moisture back through a finished wall before any extraction equipment comes off the truck. Once the source is controlled, we use truck-mounted extractors to pull standing water from flooring, then deploy industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the ambient humidity typical of northwest Iowa summers.

We monitor moisture readings in subfloor assemblies, wall cavities, and framing throughout the drying period — not just at the surface. Plaster walls, which are still common in homes near Downtown Remsen, hold moisture differently than modern drywall. The dense base coat releases water slowly, so drying timelines run longer than they would in a newer build. We document every reading and share the data with your insurance adjuster so the claim reflects actual conditions, not a generic estimate.

If flooring, drywall, or cabinetry is saturated beyond recovery, we handle the controlled demolition and reconstruction as part of the same project. You don't need a separate contractor for the rebuild.

## Local Note: What Remsen's Soil Does to Basements

The glacial till soils common in Plymouth County hold water longer than sandy or loam soils further south.
