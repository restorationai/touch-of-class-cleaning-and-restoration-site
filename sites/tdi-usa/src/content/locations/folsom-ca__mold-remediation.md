## Why Folsom Properties See Mold Issues

Folsom's climate is drier than the Bay Area or coastal Northern California, but that dryness is deceptive. The city averages enough winter rainfall to saturate the clay-heavy soils common throughout the foothills, and that moisture has nowhere fast to go. Homes built on slab foundations — which make up a significant portion of Folsom's housing stock, particularly in subdivisions developed through the 1990s and 2000s — can experience moisture wicking upward through concrete when exterior drainage is inadequate. Add a plumbing leak or a failed irrigation line near the foundation, and you have sustained dampness in exactly the spaces where mold colonizes fastest: under flooring, behind baseboards, and inside wall assemblies.

Folsom also has a large inventory of homes built during the rapid growth era of the late 1980s through the mid-2000s. Many of those homes used paper-faced drywall throughout, including in bathrooms and laundry rooms where humidity is chronically elevated. Paper facing is a preferred food source for mold, and once the paper is colonized, the gypsum core degrades quickly. Homes in that construction era often also have HVAC systems that share return-air pathways across multiple rooms — which means airborne spores from one affected area can redistribute throughout the house if the system runs while mold is active.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Folsom

Every mold job begins with a thorough assessment — not a visual scan, but moisture readings taken with calibrated meters at walls, floors, and ceilings in and around the affected area. Mold that reads dry at the surface can be hiding behind plaster or inside insulated wall cavities where moisture has been trapped for weeks. Once we understand the full extent of the moisture intrusion, we establish containment using negative air pressure and poly barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.

Remediation follows the IICRC AMRT standard: affected porous materials are removed and bagged for disposal, surfaces are treated and HEPA-vacuumed, and the structural cavity is dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. We use air scrubbers with HEPA filtration throughout the process to capture airborne spores. Final clearance — whether a visual inspection or a third-party air quality test — is coordinated based on the scope of the job and any requirements from your insurance carrier or HOA.
