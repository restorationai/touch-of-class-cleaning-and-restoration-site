## Our Water Cleanup Process in Citrus Heights

When a crew arrives at a Citrus Heights property, the first step is containment and safety — shutting off the water source if it's still active, then assessing electrical panels and GFCI zones near the affected area before any equipment goes in. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters identify where water has traveled beyond the visible wet zone. In slab-on-grade homes common throughout Citrus Heights, water under a concrete floor reads differently than water in a crawl space — the tools and the interpretation both matter.

**Standing water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units remove bulk water from flooring, carpet, and pad. Saturated carpet and pad in a Citrus Heights home during a July heat wave can begin off-gassing and supporting microbial growth quickly, so extraction speed is not just about structural protection — it's about air quality.

**Structural drying.** High-capacity desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with directed air movers, are positioned based on the moisture map — not placed generically around the room. Drying logs are maintained throughout, and readings are taken at each visit to confirm the structure is trending toward dry, not stalling.

**Documentation.** Every affected material, moisture reading, and equipment placement is photographed and logged. That documentation goes directly to your insurance carrier.
