## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Roseville

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment — not all carpet responds the same way to the same chemistry, and the alkaline dust common in this part of Placer County calls for a different pre-treatment than the organic staining you'd see in a wetter climate. From there, the process moves through:

- **Pre-treatment and dwell time** — a pH-appropriate solution is applied to break down bonded soil and stain compounds before any agitation begins.
- **Hot-water extraction** — truck-mounted equipment delivers high-temperature water under pressure and recovers it immediately, pulling suspended debris and residue out of the pile rather than redistributing it.
- **Targeted stain treatment** — pet enzyme treatments, tannin removers, and oxidizing agents are selected by stain type, not applied generically.
- **Grooming and directional drying** — pile is reset and airflow is directed to reduce dry time, which matters in Roseville's low-humidity summer air but requires more attention in the cooler, damper months of December through February.

For commercial carpet cleaning — offices, retail spaces, medical suites — we schedule around your operating hours and use low-moisture methods where faster dry times are a business requirement.
