## Why Davis Properties Present Unique Renovation Challenges

Davis has a distinct housing profile. A significant portion of the city's residential stock was built during the post-war enrollment booms at UC Davis, meaning homes constructed in the 1950s through the 1970s are common throughout the city. These properties often carry original galvanized plumbing, aluminum wiring in older panel configurations, and single-pane windows that no longer meet California's Title 24 energy standards — all of which surface during renovation planning and require coordination with licensed subcontractors.

The local soil is another factor contractors can't ignore. Yolo County's expansive clay soils shift with seasonal moisture cycles, and that movement shows up as cracked foundations, sticking doors, and tile floors that pop loose over time. Any renovation touching flooring, framing, or exterior walls needs to account for that ongoing movement rather than simply patching cosmetic symptoms.

Davis also falls under the City of Davis Building and Safety Division's permit jurisdiction, and the city has historically maintained active code enforcement around energy efficiency, accessibility, and historic character in certain older neighborhoods. Pulling permits correctly and scheduling inspections in sequence is part of the job — not an afterthought.
