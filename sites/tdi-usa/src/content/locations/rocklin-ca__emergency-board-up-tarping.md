## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Rocklin

When a call comes in, the crew mobilizes with pre-cut plywood stock, commercial-grade poly tarps, and the fastening systems needed to secure openings without causing additional damage to the structure — an important consideration when a Placer County adjuster will be documenting every material condition.

The sequence on-site follows a consistent pattern:

1. **Hazard assessment first.** Before any material goes up, the crew walks the perimeter to identify structural instability, active utility risks, and access constraints. Properties near Rocklin's busier arterials sometimes require traffic coordination for ladder and staging placement.
2. **Opening inventory.** Every breached window, door frame, soffit penetration, and roof opening is documented with photos and measurements — the same documentation package that goes to your insurance carrier.
3. **Board-up.** Openings are covered with plywood cut to fit, secured with structural screws rather than nails where the frame condition allows, and sealed at the edges to limit smoke and weather infiltration.
4. **Roof tarping.** Tarps are weighted and anchored with a batten-and-screw system rather than simple sandbags, which shift in the afternoon winds that funnel through the foothill terrain. Ridge lines and valleys get extra attention because that's where water concentrates during the region's winter rain events.
5. **Final documentation.** The crew photographs the completed work for the claims file before leaving the site.
