## Our Water Cleanup Process in Roseville

Water cleanup is not a single step — it's a sequence, and the sequence matters because skipping or rushing any stage leaves moisture that feeds mold within 24 to 72 hours of initial saturation.

**Inspection and moisture mapping** comes first. Our IICRC-certified technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the obvious wet zone. On slab foundations common throughout Roseville, water moves laterally under flooring before it wicks up walls, so the wet boundary is almost always larger than it looks.

**Standing water extraction** follows immediately. Truck-mounted extractors pull free water from carpet, hardwood, and tile faster than portable units, which matters on larger floor plans typical of Roseville's newer subdivisions.

**Structural drying** is where the work is. Our team sets industrial-grade air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the square footage and material types in the affected area. Drying targets follow the IICRC S500 standard. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so you — and your insurance adjuster — can see documented progress rather than a technician's verbal assurance.

**Content and material assessment** runs parallel. Flooring, cabinetry, and insulation are evaluated for restorability versus replacement, and that documentation feeds directly into your insurance claim.
