## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Folsom

The process starts with a detailed inventory at the loss site before anything is moved. Contents restoration isn't a single method — it's a matched set of techniques. Ultrasonic cleaning tanks handle hard goods and non-porous items. Once cleaned and documented, items are stored in a secure, climate-controlled environment until your home is ready to receive them. That timeline matters in Folsom's climate — storing restored contents in an uncontrolled space during a 105°F July week can undo cleaning work and reintroduce odor.
