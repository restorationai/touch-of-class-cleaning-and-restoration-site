## Why Rancho Cordova Properties See Distinctive Post-Construction Cleanup Challenges

Rancho Cordova sits in the Sacramento Valley, where summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F and the air is dry enough that fine construction particulate stays airborne far longer than it would in a coastal market. Drywall compound dust, silica from cut concrete backer board, and sawdust from trim work can remain suspended for days in a sealed interior, then resettle the moment an HVAC system cycles on. That cycle — disturb, resettle, disturb again — means a single-pass wipe-down misses the second and third wave of contamination.

The building stock adds another layer. Rancho Cordova includes a wide range of construction eras: post-war tract homes near the older neighborhoods along Coloma Road sit alongside commercial tilt-ups and newer mixed-use infill. Older residential interiors often have original hardwood or terrazzo that requires different cleaning chemistry than the luxury vinyl plank going into contemporary remodels. Getting the floor type right before applying any cleaning agent is not optional — the wrong product on unsealed terrazzo or pre-finished hardwood causes damage that costs more to fix than the original cleanup.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Rancho Cordova

Every post-construction clean starts with a scope walk, not a mop. Before any crew member touches a surface, a project lead identifies material types, flags sensitive finishes, checks whether mechanical systems are operational, and notes whether any trades are still completing punch-list items in adjacent areas. Cleaning into an active construction zone creates rework — for the contractor and for us.

From there, the sequence runs top to bottom and inside to outside:

- **Rough clean**: removal of construction debris, tape, sticker residue, overspray, and bulk dust from all horizontal and vertical surfaces
- **Detail clean**: window frames, sills, and glass; cabinet interiors and hardware; light fixtures and switch plates; HVAC registers and return grilles
- **Final clean**: floor-type-specific treatment (stone, hardwood, tile, LVP, or carpet), followed by a final pass on all glass and reflective surfaces
- **Specialty services** as scoped: pressure washing of exterior flatwork, concrete sealer removal, construction adhesive removal, or post-renovation deep cleaning of kitchens and bathrooms

For commercial projects in Rancho Cordova's industrial and flex-space corridors, we also coordinate with the general contractor on debris haul-out timing so cleanup doesn't stall certificate-of-occupancy inspections.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Post-Construction Cleaning

Construction dust in the fine-particulate range — the kind generated by angle grinders, drywall sanders, and concrete saws — requires HEPA-filtered vacuuming before any wet cleaning begins. Wiping a surface before vacuuming it simply spreads the contamination. Our crews use commercial-grade HEPA vacuums on all surfaces prior to any liquid application, which is the step most general-purpose cleaning companies skip because the equipment cost is significant.

For specialty cleaning scopes — post-fire odor neutralization in a renovated kitchen, biohazard-adjacent cleanup in a medical office buildout, or deep cleaning after a hoarding situation prior to renovation — our IICRC-certified team applies the appropriate protocol for the contamination type rather than defaulting to a standard janitorial approach.
