That combination — fine particulate soil, seasonal pollen, and high tenant turnover — creates carpet conditions that surface cleaning simply can't address. TDI® USA's professional carpet cleaning service is built for exactly this kind of accumulated, embedded grime.

## Why Davis Properties See Persistent Carpet Problems

The UC Davis campus anchors this city's identity, and the housing stock that surrounds it reflects decades of student and faculty occupancy. Many homes and apartments in the neighborhoods closest to campus were built in the 1960s and 1970s, when carpet padding was thicker and less moisture-resistant than modern materials. That older padding acts like a sponge — it holds pet dander, spilled liquids, and fine clay-based soil tracked in from Davis's extensive bike paths and agricultural outskirts long after the surface fibers look clean.

Davis's climate adds another layer. The valley's dry summers mean dust and allergen particles become airborne easily and settle deep into carpet pile. Then the wet season arrives — typically November through March — and foot traffic brings in a different kind of soil: the dark, clay-heavy mud common to Yolo County's flat terrain. Clay particles are particularly difficult to extract because they bind to carpet fibers at a microscopic level and don't release with low-moisture or bonnet-style cleaning methods.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Davis

Every job starts with a fiber and soil assessment. Not all carpets respond the same way to hot water extraction, and older synthetic blends common in 1970s-era Davis rentals can shrink or distort if water temperature and dwell time aren't calibrated correctly. We identify the fiber type, check for previous cleaning residue (a common problem in high-turnover rental units where DIY machines leave soap behind), and pre-treat stains based on their chemistry — protein-based stains from food and pet accidents respond to enzyme treatments, while tannin stains from coffee or wine need a different approach entirely.

For most residential and commercial carpets in Davis, we use truck-mounted hot water extraction — the method the IICRC recognizes as the most effective for deep soil removal. The machine stays outside while the wand and hose work through the interior, which matters in smaller Davis apartments and faculty co-ops where interior space is tight. After extraction, we apply a rinse pass to remove cleaning agent residue, then use high-velocity air movers to accelerate drying. In Davis's dry summer air, carpets typically dry faster than in coastal climates; during the wet season, we adjust airflow accordingly.

For commercial properties — office suites near the downtown core, retail spaces, or university-adjacent businesses — we schedule around operating hours and use low-moisture encapsulation methods when a faster return-to-use time is the priority.
