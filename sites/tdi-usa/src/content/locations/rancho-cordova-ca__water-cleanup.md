## Why Rancho Cordova Properties See Water Damage Differently

The city's residential core was largely built during the postwar boom that followed Mather Air Force Base's expansion. Many of those homes — ranch-style, single-story, slab-on-grade — have galvanized or early copper supply lines that are now well past their design life. When those pipes fail, water doesn't travel down to a basement; it spreads laterally under vinyl flooring, behind drywall, and into wall cavities before anyone notices a wet spot on the surface.

Rancho Cordova's clay soils compound the problem outdoors. During the wet season, the ground saturates quickly and lateral water movement toward foundations increases. Window wells, garage slabs, and low-lying patio areas are common entry points. In the drier months, soil shrinkage can open small gaps around foundation penetrations — gaps that become water pathways the next time a washing machine hose lets go or a dishwasher supply line cracks.

The American River Parkway corridor to the north also means that properties near the river can see elevated groundwater during high-flow years, pushing moisture up through slab cracks even when no fixture has failed.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Rancho Cordova

Every water cleanup job starts with a thermal imaging and moisture meter scan of the affected area — not just the visible wet zone. In Rancho Cordova's slab-foundation homes, water migrates under flooring in patterns that look dry at the surface but register clearly on a moisture map. That scan determines where extraction equipment goes and which materials need to come out versus which can be dried in place.

Extraction comes first: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water and saturated material moisture before drying can begin. Then we set a calculated drying system — industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers positioned to create directed airflow through wall cavities and under flooring. Drying is monitored against the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged until affected materials reach documented dry standard. Nothing is closed up until the numbers confirm it.

If flooring or drywall has to come out, we handle controlled demolition and debris removal as part of the same visit — not a separate appointment.
