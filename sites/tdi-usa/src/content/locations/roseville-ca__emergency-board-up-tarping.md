## Why Roseville Properties Face Distinct Board-Up and Tarping Challenges

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Roseville

When a call comes in, the first priority is a rapid site assessment — identifying every breach point, not just the obvious one. Fire events in particular tend to produce multiple openings: the room of origin may have broken windows from heat, the attic may have a ventilation gap from suppression activity, and roof decking can be weakened even where it looks intact. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up, which matters for insurance purposes and ensures nothing is missed.

Board-up panels are cut to fit each opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails where the substrate allows, and sealed at the edges to limit air and moisture infiltration. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for UV exposure — not lightweight blue poly — secured with weighted battens and cap sheets where the pitch and condition of the roof allow safe anchor points. On properties where the roof structure is compromised enough to make walking it unsafe, we work from ladders and use extension poles to position and secure material without adding load to weakened decking.

Documentation runs throughout the process: photos of each opening before and after, a written scope of what was installed and where, and dimensions that your insurance adjuster can use to estimate the cost of permanent repairs.
