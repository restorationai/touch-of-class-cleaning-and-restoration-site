## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in West Sacramento

Every situation is different, and the process is shaped by what the property needs — not a checklist applied the same way everywhere. In general terms, the work moves through these stages:

**Assessment and containment.** Before any cleaning begins, the affected area is assessed and isolated. This protects unaffected parts of the property and prevents cross-contamination during work.

**Removal of affected materials.** Porous materials — flooring, subflooring, drywall, insulation — that cannot be safely decontaminated are removed and disposed of in accordance with California Department of Public Health regulations for biohazardous waste. This is not standard construction debris; it requires licensed transport and disposal.

**Cleaning and disinfection.** Surfaces are cleaned using EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological hazard present. Technicians work in appropriate personal protective equipment throughout.

**Verification.** Before the space is returned to occupants or property management, the area is verified to confirm that no biological material or hazardous residue remains. Documentation is provided, which matters for insurance claims and for property records.
