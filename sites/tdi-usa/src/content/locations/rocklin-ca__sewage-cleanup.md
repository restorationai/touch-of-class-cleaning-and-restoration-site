## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Rocklin

Sewage cleanup is not a wet-vac-and-bleach job. Category 3 contamination — raw sewage — carries pathogens that survive on porous surfaces long after visible moisture is gone. The process TDI® USA follows in Rocklin moves through defined stages:

**Containment and assessment.** Technicians establish a containment barrier to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Moisture meters and thermal imaging map how far sewage-laden water has migrated — often farther than the visible stain line suggests, particularly in homes with engineered wood subfloors or floating laminate that channels water laterally.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted or portable units rated for contaminated water. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and saturated subfloor sections — are removed and bagged for disposal per Placer County waste handling guidelines. Non-salvageable material is documented for your insurance claim.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for Category 3 losses. This step addresses both pathogens and the persistent odor compounds that remain after water is gone. Hydroxyl or ozone generation may be used in enclosed spaces where odor has penetrated wall cavities.

**Structural drying.** After sanitization, drying equipment — air movers, desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers — runs until moisture readings return to regional equilibrium levels. In Rocklin's dry-summer climate, ambient conditions can assist drying, but enclosed subfloor cavities still require directed airflow to prevent secondary mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event.
