## Why Davis Properties See Distinctive Fire Damage Patterns

The Central Valley's low humidity during summer and fall means combustion byproducts dry and bond to surfaces quickly. Smoke residue that might stay tacky and easier to lift in a coastal climate can become baked-on within hours in Davis's heat. That matters because the treatment window for porous materials — wood framing, drywall, insulation, and the plaster found in many older homes near the UC Davis campus core — narrows faster here than it would in San Francisco or Sacramento's river-adjacent neighborhoods.

Davis also has a significant stock of homes built in the 1950s through 1970s, when open-plan attic spaces and whole-house ventilation were common design choices. In a fire event, that attic access becomes a highway for smoke and soot. Odor molecules travel into insulation batts, roof sheathing, and even the framing itself, which is why surface cleaning alone rarely resolves the smell. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment are often necessary steps, not optional add-ons.

The city's tree canopy — one of the densest of any California city its size — also means fire debris, ash, and water runoff from suppression hoses can complicate exterior cleanup and drainage. Crews working in Davis need to account for root systems and landscaping when staging equipment and managing runoff.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Davis

When we arrive, the first priority is a structured assessment: documenting every affected room with moisture readings, thermal imaging, and air quality sampling before a single item is moved. Insurance carriers require this documentation, and it protects you if a supplemental claim becomes necessary later.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Board-up and stabilization** — securing windows, doors, and any compromised roof sections to prevent weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while work is ongoing.
- **Contents inventory and pack-out** — salvageable belongings are catalogued, packed, and transported to a climate-controlled facility for cleaning and deodorization off-site.
- **Structural cleaning** — soot and smoke residue are removed from framing, subfloor, and wall cavities using dry-chemical sponges, HEPA vacuuming, and wet-wash methods calibrated to the surface type.
- **Deodorization** — thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation reaches the same porous pathways smoke used to penetrate the structure.
- **Controlled demolition and rebuild coordination** — when materials are beyond cleaning, we document, remove, and coordinate reconstruction so you have one point of contact through the full project.
