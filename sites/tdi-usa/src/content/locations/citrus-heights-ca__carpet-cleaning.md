## Why Citrus Heights Carpets Take a Harder Hit

The Sacramento Valley's dry summers create a specific soiling pattern that shows up clearly in Citrus Heights homes. Fine particulate dust — the kind that settles during weeks without rain — works its way deep into carpet fiber before anyone notices it on the surface. By the time a carpet looks dirty, the grit has already begun abrading fiber at the base. Then the rainy season arrives, and that same dust turns to tracked-in mud. Homes with dogs, kids, or high foot traffic through a garage or back patio accumulate a compressed layer of soil at the base of the pile that vacuuming alone cannot reach.

The area's older tract housing also tends to have carpet in bedrooms and hallways that hasn't been replaced since the original installation — sometimes 20 or more years of embedded allergens, pet dander, and dust mite debris. Deep carpet cleaning in these homes isn't cosmetic; it's a meaningful step toward improving indoor air quality, especially for households with allergy sensitivities during the Valley's high pollen season.

## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Citrus Heights

Every job starts with a dry inspection — fiber type, backing condition, any existing stains or pet odor zones that need pre-treatment before hot water extraction begins. We use truck-mounted extraction equipment, which delivers consistent water temperature and suction pressure regardless of how far the hose runs from the driveway to a back bedroom. That matters in the larger floor plans common to Citrus Heights subdivisions, where a master suite can be 60 or more feet from the curb.

Pre-conditioner is applied and allowed to dwell, loosening the bond between soil and fiber. Hot water extraction then flushes the pile from the top down, pulling emulsified soil and cleaning solution out together. For pet stain removal, we treat the subfloor area as well as the carpet face — odor-causing bacteria live in the backing and pad, not just the visible surface. After extraction, high-velocity air movers accelerate drying so carpets are typically ready for foot traffic within a few hours rather than overnight.

For commercial carpet cleaning — office suites, retail spaces, medical waiting rooms — we schedule around business hours and use low-moisture methods where drying time is a constraint.
