## Why Roseville Properties See Contents Restoration Challenges

Roseville's growth over the past two decades has produced a wide mix of housing stock — from the newer stucco-and-tile construction in communities like Fiddyment Farm and Westpark to older ranch-style homes in the central city built in the 1960s and 1970s. That variety matters for contents work. Newer homes tend to have open floor plans where smoke and soot travel farther before settling, coating furniture and stored items across a wider footprint. Older homes often have more porous interior finishes — wood paneling, original hardwood floors, plaster ceilings — that off-gas absorbed odors back onto contents even after the structure is cleaned. Either way, the belongings need to leave the building.

The Sacramento Valley's summer heat compounds the problem. When a loss happens between June and September, smoke residue bakes into upholstery faster, and moisture from a water loss evaporates unevenly — drying the surface of a mattress or sofa while trapping humidity inside the fill material. Contents that stay in a hot, poorly ventilated room deteriorate faster than the same items would in a cooler climate.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in Roseville

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed — salvageable, restorable, or non-salvageable — and that documentation goes directly into your insurance claim file. From there, a pack-out crew carefully wraps and boxes items using moving-grade materials and transports them to a secure, climate-controlled storage facility.

At the facility, each category of item goes through the appropriate cleaning method. Textiles — curtains, clothing, upholstered cushion covers — go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize smoke odor at the molecular level, then ultrasonic or hand cleaning depending on the fabric. Hard goods like furniture frames, cabinetry hardware, and decorative items are cleaned with pH-appropriate solutions matched to the finish. Electronics are assessed by a qualified technician before any attempt is made to power them on. Nothing is returned to the home until the structure has been cleared and the restored items have passed a final inspection.

Storage is available for as long as the reconstruction phase requires — whether that is two weeks or several months — with documented chain of custody throughout.
