## Why Rancho Cordova Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The Sacramento Valley's wet-dry climate cycle is hard on underground plumbing. Then the first heavy winter rains — the kind that funnel through the American River watershed — saturate that same soil quickly, and municipal mains can surcharge, pushing sewage back up through the lowest fixture in a home: usually a floor drain, toilet, or washing machine standpipe in a garage or basement.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Rancho Cordova

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated category recognized by the IICRC S500 standard — and the cleanup protocol reflects that. The process starts with containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, then extracting standing sewage with truck-mounted or portable extraction units capable of handling solid-laden water.

Once bulk material is removed, affected porous materials — drywall, baseboards, subflooring, insulation — are assessed and, where contaminated, removed and bagged for disposal per Sacramento County waste handling guidelines. What remains gets treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants applied at dwell times sufficient to address bacterial and viral pathogens. Structural drying follows using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, with moisture readings logged daily until affected assemblies reach documented dry standard. The team holds IICRC AMRT and OSHA training, which matters specifically on sewage losses where airborne pathogen risk during demolition is real.

Documentation — photographs, moisture logs, scope of work — is compiled throughout, both for your insurance carrier and for any Sacramento County permit requirements that apply when structural materials are removed.
