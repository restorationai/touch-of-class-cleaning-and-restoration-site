## Why Citrus Heights Properties See Water Damage Issues

The housing stock across much of Citrus Heights reflects a postwar suburban build-out: slab-on-grade foundations, galvanized or early copper plumbing, and stucco exteriors that can look intact while concealing moisture intrusion behind the paper backing. Galvanized supply lines from that era are prone to pinhole corrosion that starts as a slow weep and becomes a flood without warning. Slab foundations complicate water removal because there is no crawl space to ventilate — moisture that migrates under a slab can raise humidity across an entire floor plan before a single surface reading looks alarming.

Citrus Heights also sits in a region where Sacramento Municipal Utility District (SMUD) power interruptions during heavy storms can knock out sump pumps at exactly the wrong moment. A garage or low-lying utility room can take on several inches of water in the time it takes to find a flashlight. That's not a hypothetical risk — it's a pattern that repeats every wet season across the area's flatter residential streets.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Citrus Heights

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Calibrated meters and thermal imaging cameras identify where water has traveled — behind baseboards, beneath flooring, inside wall assemblies — before a single piece of equipment is placed. Once the scope is confirmed, extraction comes first: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and subfloor materials.
