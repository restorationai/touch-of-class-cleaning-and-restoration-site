## Why Roseville Properties See Water Damage Issues

The Placer County foothills climate delivers most of its annual rainfall between November and March, often in concentrated storms that overwhelm gutters, window wells, and crawl spaces within hours. Roseville's older west-side neighborhoods — built when drainage infrastructure was sized for a much smaller city — can see surface water pooling against foundations before a storm even peaks. On the newer east side, large-format tile and engineered hardwood floors common in tract construction from the 2000s and 2010s can trap water underneath for days without showing visible damage at the surface; by the time a homeowner notices a soft spot or a musty smell, the subfloor may already be compromised.

Roseville also sits in a region where municipal water pressure tends to run on the higher end, which accelerates wear on washing machine hoses, refrigerator ice-maker lines, and older polybutylene or galvanized supply lines still present in homes built before the mid-1990s. A pinhole leak behind a cabinet can saturate wall cavities and insulation for weeks before it announces itself.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Roseville

Every job starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters — not a visual walk-through. Water behind plaster or stucco can read dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated, and in Roseville's stucco-clad homes that pattern is common. Once we know where the moisture is, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then place industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers in a configuration calculated to the specific square footage, material types, and ambient conditions of your property.

Drying is documented daily with moisture readings logged against the IICRC S500 standard, so there is a written record showing when structural materials reach acceptable drywall and framing moisture levels. That documentation matters when your insurance adjuster reviews the claim. As an IICRC Certified Firm with Water Damage Restoration Technician (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD) certifications, TDI® USA follows the same protocols whether the loss is a single bathroom or a multi-room flood.
