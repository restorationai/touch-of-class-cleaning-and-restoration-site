Roseville also sits in a climate zone where interior humidity swings sharply between the wet season (November through March) and the dry summer months. A thorough post-construction clean accounts for both.

## Our Post-Construction and Specialty Cleaning Process in Roseville

Every project starts with a walkthrough to scope the work before a single microfiber cloth comes out of the van. We identify construction debris categories — drywall dust, adhesive overspray, paint splatter, caulk smears, window film residue — and sequence the clean from the top of the structure down, from rough to detail, so we're not recontaminating surfaces we've already finished.

The process typically runs in three passes:

**Rough clean** — removal of bulk debris, scrap material, and packaging. Broom and vacuum work on floors, window frames, and rough-framed areas still holding sawdust.

**Detail clean** — HEPA-filtered vacuuming of all surfaces including light fixtures, cabinet interiors, window tracks, and HVAC registers. Drywall dust is electrostatically charged and clings; standard vacuums recirculate it. Our equipment captures particles down to 0.3 microns so what we pull off the surface stays out of the air.

**Final polish** — glass cleaning, fixture wipe-down, floor finishing appropriate to the surface type (hardwood, LVP, tile, or polished concrete), and a final walkthrough against the punch-list.

For specialty cleaning scopes — post-fire odor neutralization in a remodeled kitchen, biohazard-adjacent construction in a medical or commercial tenant improvement, or deep cleaning after a water-damaged renovation — the process is calibrated to that specific scope. Our team holds IICRC certifications in fire and smoke restoration and mold remediation, which matters when a construction project has had a secondary loss event mid-build.
