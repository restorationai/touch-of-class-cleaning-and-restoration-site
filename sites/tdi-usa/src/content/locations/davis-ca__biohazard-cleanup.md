## Why Davis Properties Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Davis's housing stock spans several distinct eras. The older bungalows and craftsman-style homes near the downtown core often have original hardwood subfloors and plaster-over-lath wall assemblies — materials that are porous in ways that modern drywall simply is not. When biological contamination reaches those surfaces, it can wick into the substrate well beyond what is visible at the surface. A room that looks contained may not be. That reality shapes how our OSHA-trained technicians assess a scene before any work begins: containment first, then methodical surface-by-surface evaluation.

The city also has a large population of rental properties serving UC Davis students and staff. Landlords and property managers frequently encounter situations — hoarding conditions, unreported leaks combined with biological waste, or sharps and needle cleanup in common areas — that require both regulatory compliance and careful coordination with tenants or next-of-kin. California's landlord-tenant laws add a layer of documentation responsibility that we account for in every project file.

Davis's warm, dry summers and mild but damp winters also matter. Biological material left in an enclosed space during a Central Valley summer can deteriorate and off-gas rapidly. Prompt response is not just a comfort — it is a practical necessity for limiting the scope of remediation.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Davis

Every job begins with a confidential assessment. We arrive in unmarked vehicles when requested, and our crew does not discuss the nature of the work with anyone outside the authorized contact. That level of discretion is standard, not an upgrade.

From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment and PPE establishment** — the affected area is isolated using negative air pressure and physical barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms.
- **Removal of biohazardous materials** — all contaminated items, including porous materials that cannot be decontaminated, are packaged and disposed of in compliance with California Department of Public Health regulations for regulated medical waste.
- **Surface treatment and disinfection** — hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces, with dwell times calibrated to the specific pathogen risk profile of the situation.
- **ATP and visual clearance verification** — we do not declare a space clean based on appearance alone. Adenosine triphosphate (ATP) testing gives an objective measure of biological residue before we close out.
- **Documentation for insurance and legal purposes** — photo documentation, a written scope of work, and a certificate of completion are provided as standard deliverables.

Our team holds IICRC certification and is OSHA trained, which matters specifically in California where regulatory scrutiny of biohazard disposal is among the strictest in the country.

## Local Note

Davis's older rental housing — particularly the blocks of duplexes and apartment conversions built in the 1960s and 1970s to accommodate UC Davis's rapid enrollment growth — frequently has concrete slab foundations with minimal subfloor air gap. When biological contamination reaches a slab, there is no crawl space to ventilate and no subfloor cavity to dry; the remediation boundary is the slab surface itself, which simplifies containment but requires more aggressive surface treatment than a wood-framed floor assembly would. Crews who are not familiar with this construction pattern sometimes underestimate the lateral spread of contamination along the slab. Our technicians account for this during the initial assessment.
