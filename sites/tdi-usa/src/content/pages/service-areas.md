TDI® USA operates around the clock — if water is spreading across your floor at 2 a.m. or smoke is still settling at midnight, the line is staffed and dispatch is moving. Our Sacramento headquarters anchors a service footprint that extends across the greater Sacramento Valley and into the surrounding region. When you call, a real person confirms your address, assesses the urgency, and routes the nearest available crew — no voicemail triage, no next-business-day callbacks.

## Where we work

Our team is based in Sacramento and responds across the greater Sacramento metro, the surrounding foothill communities to the east, the Delta corridor to the west, and the communities running north and south along the Central Valley floor. Sacramento sits at the convergence of the American and Sacramento rivers, which means the region sees a mix of seasonal flooding, aging levee-adjacent neighborhoods, and a wide range of housing stock — from mid-century tract homes in the suburbs to older craftsman and Victorian-era structures closer to the urban core. Each of those building types carries its own quirks when water, fire, or mold enters the picture, and our crews work across all of them.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to our Sacramento base see the fastest crew arrivals under normal conditions. From there, coverage extends outward to communities throughout Sacramento County, into portions of Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, and San Joaquin counties, and along the Highway 50 and I-80 corridors where traffic and mountain grades can affect travel times. The farther a property sits from our dispatch point — particularly once routes climb into the Sierra Nevada foothills — the more travel time factors into scheduling. For properties at the outer edge of our footprint, we are transparent about estimated arrival windows before you commit, so there are no surprises.

## Don't see your city?

The linked service-area pages cover the locations we respond to most regularly, but that list is not a hard boundary. For significant losses — major flooding, large-scale fire damage, commercial properties — we routinely dispatch beyond our standard coverage zone. If your city isn't listed, call us at +1 (916) 966-6000 and describe the situation. We'll tell you honestly whether we can get there and how quickly.
