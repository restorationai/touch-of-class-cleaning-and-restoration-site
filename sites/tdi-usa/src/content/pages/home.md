When water soaks through your subfloor, smoke odor settles into your drywall, or mold starts spreading behind a wall you can't see, you need a crew that knows exactly what they're looking at — and what to do next. ## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether a pipe burst at 2 a.m. or a slow leak has been wicking into your subfloor for weeks, standing water and hidden moisture need to be extracted and dried before secondary damage sets in. The window between a wet structure and active mold growth is shorter than most people expect.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue doesn't stay where the fire was. Soot particles travel through HVAC systems, settle into soft surfaces, and leave an odor that ordinary cleaning can't reach. Proper restoration means addressing the chemistry of what burned, not just what's visibly charred.

**Mold remediation** — Sacramento's warm, dry summers can mask moisture problems that fester inside walls, under flooring, and in crawl spaces — especially in older homes with plaster construction or original cast-iron plumbing. Remediation done right removes the source, not just the surface growth.

**Reconstruction** — Once the damage is mitigated and the structure is stable, getting your home back to livable condition requires the same attention to detail as the emergency work that came before it. TDI® USA handles the rebuild so you're not coordinating a separate contractor.
