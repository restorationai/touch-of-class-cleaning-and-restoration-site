## Restoration emergencies common in Folsom

Folsom sits at the edge of the Sierra Nevada foothills, and that geography shapes the losses that happen here. The city's Mediterranean climate delivers dry, hot summers and concentrated winter rainfall — the kind of rain that arrives fast and overwhelms drainage systems that sat idle for months. Foothill soils in this part of Sacramento County include decomposed granite and clay-heavy pockets that drain unevenly; when a storm saturates the ground, water finds the path of least resistance into crawlspaces, garages, and slab perimeters.

Summer brings its own risk. Wildfire smoke from the Sierra and the Eldorado National Forest to the east regularly blankets Folsom, leaving fine particulate and odor compounds on HVAC filters, ductwork, and soft furnishings — damage that looks invisible until an air-quality test or a persistent smell makes it undeniable. The same dry heat that drives fire risk also stresses plumbing: thermal cycling causes fittings to work loose over years, and a pinhole leak behind drywall can go unnoticed until mold colonizes the wall cavity.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration
