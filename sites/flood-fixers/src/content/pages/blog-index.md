San Diego's mild reputation hides some real property risks — El Niño winters that overwhelm aging stucco homes, marine-layer moisture that quietly feeds mold inside wall cavities, and the occasional Santa Ana wind that turns a small fire into a big insurance claim. The posts here cover what actually happens when those risks land on your doorstep: how fast water damage spreads, what a public adjuster does versus what your carrier's adjuster does, which mold species show up most often in coastal zip codes, and what a restoration crew is actually doing on day one versus day five.

You'll also find step-by-step guides for the moments right after a loss — what to document before touching anything, which calls to make in which order, and how to read a scope of work so you're not signing something you don't understand. Some posts are specific to San Diego (the difference between Category 1 and Category 3 water intrusion in a slab-foundation home matters differently here than in Phoenix). Others are universal enough to apply anywhere. Either way, the goal is to give you information that's genuinely useful whether you're dealing with an emergency right now, preparing for one, or just trying to understand a claim that's already in progress — no phone call required.
