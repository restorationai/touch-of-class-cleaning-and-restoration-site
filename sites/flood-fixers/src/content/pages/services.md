From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to fire-blackened walls that need to come down and be rebuilt from the studs, Flood Fixers handles the full arc of property damage — emergency response, structural drying, hazardous material removal, and complete reconstruction. We work with homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial tenants across San Diego County, and we bill insurance directly so you're not chasing reimbursements on your own.

## Emergency restoration services

When damage is active, the clock matters. Water left standing for 24–48 hours is long enough for mold to begin colonizing porous materials; smoke residue starts bonding to surfaces within hours of a fire. Flood Fixers dispatches for **water damage** (flooding, burst pipes, appliance failures, sewer backups), **fire and smoke damage** (including the acrid, oily residue that travels far beyond the burn zone), **biohazard and trauma cleanup** (handled by trained technicians following OSHA protocols), and **storm damage** (wind-driven rain intrusion, roof breaches, post-storm flooding). Each of these services has its own dedicated page with process details, equipment specs, and what to expect on the first visit.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts your property back together. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is verified dry and safe, our licensed general contracting team takes over — framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because the same company that documented the damage is also doing the rebuild, there's no gap between the mitigation report and the scope of work. Insurers get a consistent file; you get a single point of contact instead of coordinating between a restorer and a separate contractor.

## Specialty services

Some problems don't announce themselves with standing water or visible char. **Mold inspection and remediation** addresses hidden growth behind walls, under subfloors, and inside HVAC cavities — common in San Diego's coastal neighborhoods where marine layer humidity keeps building envelopes damp longer than inland areas. **Odor removal** uses hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging to neutralize smoke, pet, and sewage odors that surface cleaning alone won't touch. **Board-up and tarping** secures a property immediately after a fire, break-in, or storm event to prevent secondary damage and unauthorized entry while the insurance process moves forward.

Every job Flood Fixers takes on moves through a single, documented workflow: emergency response → damage assessment → mitigation → clearance testing → reconstruction.
