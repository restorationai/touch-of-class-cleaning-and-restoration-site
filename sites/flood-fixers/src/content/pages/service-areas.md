Flood Fixers runs 24/7 emergency dispatch out of San Diego and reaches most of the greater San Diego metro — coastal communities, inland valleys, and the South Bay — within an hour of your call. Whether you're dealing with a burst pipe at midnight or smoke damage discovered on a Sunday morning, a certified technician can be at your door fast, with the drying equipment already loaded.

## Where we work

Our team is based in San Diego and responds across the coastal strip from La Jolla south to Chula Vista, east through the inland valleys of El Cajon, Santee, and Lakeside, and north along the I-15 and I-5 corridors through Escondido, Vista, and Oceanside. We also cover the South Bay cities — National City, Bonita, and Imperial Beach — where older housing stock and marine-layer humidity create their own set of moisture challenges. If your property sits within San Diego County, you're almost certainly within our service footprint.

## Response time by distance

Distance from our San Diego headquarters is the clearest predictor of arrival time, though I-5, I-8, and I-15 traffic can shift those numbers — especially during the morning and evening commute windows.

- **Central San Diego and nearby cities** (Mission Valley, North Park, Chula Vista, La Mesa): typically **under 45 minutes**.
- **Mid-range cities** (Escondido, El Cajon, Santee, Oceanside, National City): **45–75 minutes** under normal conditions.
- **Outer coverage** (Fallbrook, Ramona, Alpine, Campo, Tecate corridor): **75–120 minutes** — mountain grades and two-lane roads add time, not just miles.

For active water losses, every minute the water sits is another few square feet of subfloor or drywall absorbing moisture. If you're at the edge of our range, call us anyway — we'll give you an honest ETA and walk you through immediate steps to limit damage while we're in transit.

## Don't see your city?

Our listed service areas cover the bulk of where we dispatch daily, but they're not a hard boundary. For larger losses — significant flooding, fire damage affecting multiple rooms, or commercial properties — we routinely travel beyond the standard footprint. Call **(855) 204-1124** and describe the situation. If we can get there responsibly and serve you well, we will.
