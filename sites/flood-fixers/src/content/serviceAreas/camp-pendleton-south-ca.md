## Restoration emergencies common in Camp Pendleton South

Camp Pendleton South sits on the San Diego County coast, which means the housing stock deals with salt air, onshore humidity, and the kind of wind-driven rain that finds every gap in a roofline or window flashing. A lot of the area is family housing built in waves to serve the base, often with shared wall assemblies and slab foundations, so a leak in one unit can travel further than a homeowner expects before it shows up as a stain or a soft spot in flooring. Coastal moisture also means mold has a head start here: once humidity gets into wall cavities or under cabinetry, it does not need much time to start colonizing framing or drywall paper. Add in the occasional Santa Ana wind event that can drive wildfire smoke into the area, and the mix of emergencies we see locally tends to split between water intrusion, humidity-driven mold, and smoke odor remediation.
