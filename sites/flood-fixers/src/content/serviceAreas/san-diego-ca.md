## Restoration emergencies common in San Diego

San Diego's reputation for perfect weather masks a surprisingly active loss environment. The region swings between years of drought and sudden atmospheric river events — the kind that dropped several inches of rain in 24 hours across coastal neighborhoods in early 2024, overwhelming gutters and flat roofs that hadn't seen real rain in months. That dry-then-drenched cycle is hard on roofing membranes, stucco cladding, and the slab foundations common in mid-century ranch homes throughout neighborhoods like Clairemont and Kensington.

Indoors, the culprits are often quieter. San Diego's older housing stock — craftsman bungalows in North Park, 1950s duplexes in City Heights, condo towers near the Gaslamp Quarter — carries aging galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks that corrode from the inside out. A slow leak behind a vanity can go undetected for months in a dry climate where residents aren't conditioned to watch for moisture. By the time it's visible, mold has often been colonizing the wall cavity for three to six weeks. The city's mild temperatures (rarely below 55°F even in winter) mean mold finds San Diego interiors hospitable year-round.

Wildfire smoke is a seasonal reality as well. When fires push east-to-west through the inland valleys, fine particulate and odor compounds settle into HVAC systems, soft furnishings, and porous drywall — even in coastal ZIP codes like 92103 and 92116 that are miles from the fire perimeter.

## Services we provide in San Diego

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in San Diego frequently starts small and spreads laterally through stucco-frame construction faster than homeowners expect. We use thermal imaging and moisture mapping to trace water migration beyond the visible wet zone, then set commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant drying systems calibrated to San Diego's coastal humidity levels. Most residential drying projects reach target moisture content within three to five days.

### Mold remediation

San Diego's combination of older building envelopes and marine-layer humidity creates persistent mold risk in bathrooms, crawl spaces, and any wall cavity that has seen undisclosed water intrusion. Our remediation process follows EPA guidelines and California Department of Public Health protocols. We contain the affected area, remove compromised materials, treat structural surfaces, and provide clearance documentation — which many San Diego HOAs and property management companies require before reconstruction can begin.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a kitchen fire or a wildfire-smoke event, the visible char is rarely the biggest problem. Smoke residue penetrates HVAC ductwork, cabinet interiors, and wall finishes throughout a home. We perform full content inventories, apply hydroxyl and thermal fogging treatments, and restore or replace structural elements to current California building code — important in a city where permit inspectors are active and unpermitted repairs can complicate future sales.

### Reconstruction

Flood Fixers holds a California contractor's license, so we can carry a job from emergency mitigation through finished reconstruction without handing the project to a third party. For San Diego property owners, that continuity matters — it keeps the insurance scope of work consistent and eliminates the gap where projects stall between mitigation and rebuild.
