## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Spring Valley

Every project starts with a scope-of-work walkthrough that treats the home as a system, not a collection of rooms. For post-damage rebuilds — the work that follows water extraction, fire cleanup, or mold remediation — that means reviewing the remediation documentation before framing begins, so new materials go into a verified dry, clean environment.

For elective remodels, the process is similar in discipline if not in urgency:

- **Pre-construction assessment** — structural review, utility mapping, permit research through San Diego County's online portal
- **Design and material selection** — we work with what the homeowner wants and what the structure will support, flagging conflicts early
- **Permitted demolition and rough-in** — framing, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC rough-in inspected before walls close
- **Finish work** — tile, cabinetry, countertops, flooring, paint, and fixture installation coordinated to minimize trade overlap
