## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in San Marcos

The first thing a Flood Fixers technician does on arrival is not pull out equipment — it's trace the water. Using thermal imaging cameras and non-invasive moisture meters, we map exactly where water has traveled before a single panel is removed. In San Marcos homes with stucco exteriors, water from a roof or window leak often tracks down the sheathing behind the stucco skin and pools at the base plate, completely invisible from inside. Structural drying follows immediately — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed at random. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until every affected material hits its target moisture content. ## San Marcos Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, a failed appliance supply line, an overflowing fixture.
