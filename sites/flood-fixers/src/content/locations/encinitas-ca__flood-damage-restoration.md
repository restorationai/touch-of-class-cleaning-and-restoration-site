## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Encinitas

When a technician arrives, the first priority is stopping active water intrusion if it hasn't stopped on its own, then a full moisture mapping of the structure using thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters. This tells us exactly where water has traveled — not just where it's visible.

Extraction comes next. Standing water pulls out fast with truck-mounted extraction units. The slower, harder work is the moisture trapped inside wall cavities, under tile, and beneath engineered hardwood floors — materials common in the mid-2000s construction that expanded through the Cardiff-by-the-Sea and Encinitas Ranch areas. We use desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers staged strategically through the structure, combined with high-velocity air movers positioned to create a drying vortex rather than just blowing air around.

Drying typically runs three to five days for standard residential flood damage, with daily moisture readings to confirm progress. We document every reading — this matters when you're filing a homeowner's insurance claim and the adjuster wants to see a drying log rather than just a final invoice.
