## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Carlsbad

The first thing a technician does on arrival is identify the water category — clean supply line water, gray water from an appliance or drain backup, or black water from a sewage event — because that determines every downstream decision about protective equipment, disposal, and salvageability of materials. In Carlsbad homes with finished basements, that assessment includes checking whether luxury vinyl plank or engineered hardwood flooring has been installed over slab, since both trap water underneath and accelerate subfloor damage in ways carpet doesn't.

Once the source is confirmed stopped, truck-mounted extraction units pull standing water from the slab and from any carpet or pad. Structural drying follows: industrial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers placed strategically based on the room's cubic footage, paired with high-velocity air movers directed at wall cavities and floor assemblies. Moisture readings are logged at every session — typically every 24 hours — until affected materials reach target moisture content per IICRC S500 drying standards. If drywall or framing shows readings above threshold after 72 hours, controlled demolition opens the cavity to allow direct airflow rather than letting the structure dry from the outside in, which almost never works on its own.
