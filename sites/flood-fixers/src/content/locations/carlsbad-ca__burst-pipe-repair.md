## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Carlsbad

The first priority when we arrive is stopping the water source. If the break is on a supply line, we isolate the affected zone or shut the main at the meter. Carlsbad Municipal Water District meters are typically located at the curb strip, and our technicians carry the T-bar tools to shut them without waiting for a utility crew.

From there, the process moves in this sequence:

1. **Moisture mapping** — We use thermal cameras and calibrated pin-and-pinless meters to trace exactly where water traveled. In Carlsbad's stucco-exterior homes, water often wicks laterally inside wall assemblies further than the visible damage suggests.
2. **Extraction** — Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing water from hard floors and carpet. For slab-on-grade homes with tile over concrete, we use specialty drying mats to draw moisture up through the slab rather than letting it migrate.
3. **Controlled demolition** — We remove only what can't be dried in place. Baseboards, drywall sections, and flooring are documented with photos before removal for your insurance adjuster.
4. **Drying and monitoring** — Industrial dehumidifiers and air movers run on a schedule we adjust daily based on psychrometric readings. Most Carlsbad jobs reach drying goals in three to five days given the region's low ambient humidity — faster than comparable jobs in more humid coastal markets.
5. **Pipe repair and reconstruction** — Once the structure is dry and documented, we repair or repipe the failed section and restore finishes to pre-loss condition.
