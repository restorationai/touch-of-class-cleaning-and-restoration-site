## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Carlsbad

Every job starts with thermal imaging and moisture mapping before a single piece of equipment is placed. In Carlsbad's slab-on-grade homes, this step is non-negotiable — a moisture meter reading at the surface tells you almost nothing about what's happening two inches into the concrete or underneath engineered hardwood flooring.

Once the scope is confirmed, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted units capable of pulling several hundred gallons per hour. For finished spaces in communities like Bressi Ranch where HOA rules govern noise and parking, we coordinate equipment staging in advance to avoid violations and keep neighbors informed.

After extraction, the structural drying phase begins. Carlsbad's coastal humidity — typically 70–80% relative humidity on marine-layer mornings — means standard drying calculations used in drier climates will underestimate the time needed. We set refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to actual psychrometric conditions on the day of the job, not to a generic chart. Moisture readings are logged at every visit so you have a documented drying record, which your insurance adjuster will want.

If building materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor sheathing — have absorbed enough water that drying in place isn't viable, selective demolition removes only what's necessary. Nothing gets torn out without a moisture reading to justify it.
