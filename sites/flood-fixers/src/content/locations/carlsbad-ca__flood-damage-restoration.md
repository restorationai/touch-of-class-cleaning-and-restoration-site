## Why Carlsbad Properties Are Vulnerable to Flood Damage

The geography here works against you during heavy rain. ## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Carlsbad

Every job starts with a moisture mapping walk-through before any equipment is placed. Using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters, the crew identifies where water has traveled — not just where it's visible. In slab-on-grade homes, that often means water has tracked under baseboards and into wall cavities 10 to 15 feet from the original entry point.

Once the scope is confirmed, extraction comes first. Truck-mounted extractors pull standing water and saturated carpet padding; for hardwood or tile over concrete, desiccant dehumidifiers and directional air movers are positioned to draw moisture up through the floor assembly rather than just treating the surface. Structural drying typically runs 3–5 days in Carlsbad's moderate coastal climate, though homes with poor attic ventilation or enclosed crawl spaces can run longer. Daily moisture readings are logged and shared with the homeowner and, when applicable, the insurance adjuster.

Demolition is kept to the minimum necessary. If drywall reads above 16% moisture content after 48 hours of active drying, it comes out — partial cuts rather than full-sheet removal wherever the framing allows. That approach keeps reconstruction costs lower and gets you back to normal faster.
