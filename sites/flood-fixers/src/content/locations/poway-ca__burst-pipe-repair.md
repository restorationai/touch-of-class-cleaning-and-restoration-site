## Why Poway Homes Are Vulnerable to Burst Pipes

Poway's housing stock spans several distinct eras. Neighborhoods developed in the 1970s and early 1980s — particularly around the Old Poway Park corridor and the established tracts near Poway Road — often still have original copper or galvanized steel supply lines. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out; you won't see the problem until a section fails under pressure. Meanwhile, homes built during the 1990s and 2000s boom years frequently used polybutylene or early CPVC fittings that age poorly under the region's hard water conditions. San Diego County water is notoriously mineral-heavy, and scale buildup accelerates joint failure on both older metal and early plastic plumbing.

The other factor is thermal cycling. Poway doesn't get prolonged freezes, but it gets repeated overnight dips followed by warm afternoons — that expansion-and-contraction cycle stresses fittings at bends and tee connections more than a single sustained cold snap would. Pipes in unconditioned garage spaces, exterior walls facing north, and crawl spaces beneath homes in the 92064 ZIP code are the most common failure points we see.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Poway

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the water source — either at the fixture shutoff or the main, depending on where the break is. From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

**1. Damage mapping.** We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to trace exactly where water traveled. In Poway's slab-on-grade homes, water often migrates 15–20 feet from the break point along the path of least resistance before surfacing, so visual inspection alone misses the full extent.

**2. Extraction.** Truck-mounted extraction pulls standing water from flooring and subfloor assemblies. In homes with tile over concrete — common throughout Poway's ranch-style layouts — we check for water trapped beneath the tile using non-invasive sensors before deciding whether the floor covering needs to come up.

**3. Structural drying.** Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are staged based on the moisture map. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until the structure reaches target dryness levels, typically within 3–5 days depending on the volume of water and materials affected.
