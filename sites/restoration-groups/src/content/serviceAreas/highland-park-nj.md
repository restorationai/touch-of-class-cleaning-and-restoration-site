## Restoration emergencies common in Highland Park

Highland Park's geography and housing stock create a specific pattern of losses that shows up in our dispatch logs year after year. The Raritan River traces the entire southern border of the borough, and the low-lying stretches along River Road and through Donaldson Park proved how dangerous that proximity is during Ida in 2021, when those areas flooded catastrophically. The same topography that makes the Triangle and Meadow neighborhoods feel tucked-in and quiet also means groundwater has nowhere to drain quickly during heavy rain events — a pattern that has grown more frequent as nor'easters and remnant tropical systems push more moisture into central New Jersey.

The housing stock compounds the risk. The borough's 1910s through 1940s colonials, bungalows, and two-families — dense along the streets that fan out from the Raritan Avenue business district — were built with original galvanized supply lines and cast-iron drains that are now well past their design life. When those pipes fail, they fail inside finished basements, behind plaster walls, or under tile floors that have been renovated multiple times. Detecting the moisture boundary requires thermal imaging and calibrated meters, not a visual pass. The student-rental and multi-unit stock near Rutgers University across the river adds another layer: deferred maintenance, high occupancy, and property managers who may not learn about a slow leak until a ceiling has already softened.

## Services we provide in Highland Park

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Highland Park most often involves one of three patterns: storm-driven river flooding along the southern edge of the borough, supply-line failures in pre-war plumbing systems, and sewer backups triggered when the municipal system surpasses capacity during heavy rain. ### Mold remediation

Mold can begin colonizing wet materials within 24 to 48 hours. In Highland Park's older housing stock, that window matters because moisture often hides inside wall cavities and under original hardwood floors before anyone notices a smell. Our remediation process includes containment, HEPA filtration, and removal of affected materials, followed by clearance testing. We do not provide medical advice about exposure, but we do leave the affected space dry and within industry-standard spore counts.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

The Raritan Avenue corridor's restaurants and mixed-use buildings see commercial fire and suppression-system losses, and residential cooking fires are common in the borough's dense attached housing. Smoke residue penetrates HVAC systems, insulation, and wood framing long after flames are out. We handle soot removal, odor neutralization, and content pack-out, then rebuild to match original finishes.

Most of the borough's homes sit on full basements with footings set below the New Jersey frost line, which is why a supply failure two floors up tends to end in a finished lower level.
