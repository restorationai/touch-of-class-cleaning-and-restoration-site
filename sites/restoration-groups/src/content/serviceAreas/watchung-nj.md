## Restoration emergencies common in Watchung

Watchung sits in the hilly, wooded terrain shaped by the Watchung Mountains, and that topography drives a lot of what we see here. Homes built into sloped lots often have basements or walkout lower levels that collect runoff during heavy rain, especially where grading has settled over the decades. Winter brings its own version of the same problem: freeze-thaw cycles crack aging supply lines and push ice into gutters and roof valleys, and a sudden thaw can send more water into a foundation than the drainage system was ever built to handle. Summer storms rolling through Union County bring wind and lightning that knock down tree limbs onto roofs, and the dense tree cover that makes Watchung attractive also means more debris and more roof punctures after a bad storm.

Storm damage work here usually means tarping a roof after a tree limb comes down or drying out a home after wind-driven rain gets past flashing. We board up openings, tarp roofs, and begin extraction the same visit when conditions allow.
