## Restoration emergencies common in Bloomfield

Bloomfield's housing stock is the first thing any experienced restoration tech notices on arrival. Bloomfield's older homes trap moisture in ways newer construction doesn't: plaster over wood lath, unvented crawl spaces, and original single-pane windows that sweat all winter.
