The city's residential neighborhoods — Elmora, Midtown, Peterstown, North Elizabeth — are dense with early-1900s two- and three-family frame homes. ## Services we provide in Elizabeth

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Elizabeth frequently involves more than one affected unit, and the source is often hidden — a slow riser leak behind tile, a failed washing machine supply line on an upper floor, or storm surge that wicked into subfloor framing. We locate moisture with thermal imaging and moisture meters before we touch a wall, so we're not tearing out dry material or missing wet cavities that will grow mold within 24 to 48 hours.

### Sewage and drain backup cleanup

Combined-sewer backups are routine in Elizabethport and across the lower-lying sections of the city. Sewage-contaminated water (Category 3) requires containment, proper PPE, EPA-compliant disinfection, and careful disposal — not a shop vac and a fan. We handle extraction, antimicrobial treatment, and controlled demolition of contaminated materials, and we document every step for your insurance carrier.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

In tightly packed frame construction, smoke travels fast — through wall gaps, HVAC chases, and shared attic spaces. A contained kitchen fire in one unit often leaves smoke odor and soot residue in adjacent units by morning. We scope the full affected area, not just the room of origin, and use hydroxyl or thermal fogging where appropriate to address odor at the molecular level rather than masking it.

### Mold remediation

Elizabeth's humidity, frequent flooding history, and older building envelopes create favorable conditions for mold colonization. After any water intrusion, mold can establish in as little as 24 to 48 hours on organic materials like wood framing and drywall. We follow established remediation protocols, contain the work area, and provide clearance documentation — which insurers and prospective buyers increasingly require.
