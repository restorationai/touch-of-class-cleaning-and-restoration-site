### Mold remediation

Mold colonizes wet drywall and wood framing within 24 to 48 hours of a water event — a timeline that matters in Clifton's older housing stock, where wall cavities often trap moisture long after surfaces feel dry. We contain affected areas, remove compromised materials, treat structural surfaces, and clear the space through post-remediation verification testing. ### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue from a kitchen fire in a Richfield split-level or an electrical fire in a Botany Village multi-family behaves differently depending on what burned and how long the structure was exposed. Protein smoke from cooking fires leaves an almost invisible film that carries a persistent odor; synthetic materials produce a thick, oily soot that embeds in porous surfaces. We assess the burn pattern, remove unsalvageable materials, clean and deodorize salvageable surfaces, and coordinate board-up and structural repairs.
