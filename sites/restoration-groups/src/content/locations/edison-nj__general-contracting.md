## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Edison

Every project starts with a scope walkthrough — not a sales visit. We document existing conditions, note any post-damage material that needs removal before finish work begins, and flag permit requirements with the Edison Township Division of Building and Housing before a contract is signed. Edison requires permits for structural changes, electrical panel upgrades, plumbing reroutes, and most basement finishing work; pulling those permits correctly from the start protects your certificate of occupancy and your homeowner's insurance coverage.

From there, the sequence is straightforward but disciplined:

1. **Demolition and hazardous material abatement** — older Edison homes (ZIP codes 08817 and 08820 are dense with pre-1978 construction) frequently contain lead paint and asbestos-containing floor tile. We assess and handle abatement before any finish trades enter.
2. **Structural and rough-in trades** — framing, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC rough-in are coordinated and inspected before walls close.
3. **Insulation and air sealing** — Edison's humid summers and cold winters make this step critical for energy performance and moisture control, especially in basement remodels.
4. **Finish work** — drywall, tile, cabinetry, flooring, and trim installed to spec, with a final walkthrough tied to the township's certificate of occupancy inspection.

When a renovation follows water damage, fire, or a mold remediation, we coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster to reconcile the rebuild scope against the claim estimate — reducing the back-and-forth that delays project start.
