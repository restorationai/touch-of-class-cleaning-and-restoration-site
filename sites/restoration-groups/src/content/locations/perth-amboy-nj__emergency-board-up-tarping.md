## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Perth Amboy

When you call (855) 650-7422, the first question is always structural: is the building safe to approach, and has the fire department or utility company cleared the scene? Once we have access, the process moves in a deliberate sequence.

**Assessment and documentation** come first. We photograph every opening, measure it, and note the substrate — whether we're anchoring into a brick rowhouse facade, a wood-frame soffit, or a commercial storefront's aluminum framing. Documentation at this stage is what your insurance adjuster will rely on, so we treat it as part of the job, not an afterthought.

**Board-up** uses 3/4-inch OSB cut to fit and secured with structural screws into solid framing members, not just into trim or siding. On older Perth Amboy homes where exterior trim may be deteriorated, we locate framing with a stud finder before drilling to avoid creating new damage. Window openings get a plywood panel that overlaps the rough opening by at least four inches on each side.

**Roof tarping** on a steep-pitched Victorian or Craftsman roof requires ballasted attachment — we use weighted lumber battens secured over the tarp rather than relying on adhesive or staples, which fail under wind load. Tarps are lapped over the ridge when possible so water sheds away from the breach rather than pooling at it.

Finally, we leave a written scope summary on-site and email it to you and your insurance carrier the same day.
