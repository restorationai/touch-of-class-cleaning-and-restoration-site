## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Morristown

Every flood job starts with a moisture survey, not a guess. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map exactly how far water has migrated — behind baseboards, under hardwood, into wall cavities — before a single piece of material is removed. In Morristown's older housing stock, that step matters more than usual: plaster walls common in pre-1940 homes absorb water at a different rate than modern drywall and can hold moisture deep in the substrate even when the surface feels dry to the touch.

Once the scope is mapped, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Water extraction** using truck-mounted and portable units to pull standing water and saturated material
- **Controlled demolition** of non-salvageable materials — wet insulation, wicking drywall, swollen subfloor sections — documented with photos for your insurance adjuster
- **Drying and dehumidification** using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, with daily moisture readings logged until the structure reaches dry standard per the IICRC S500
- **Antimicrobial treatment** of affected cavities before any rebuild begins
- **Reconstruction coordination** so you have one point of contact from extraction through finished repairs

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our drying protocols are documented and defensible — something insurance carriers and Morristown's building department both appreciate when permits are pulled for structural repairs.
