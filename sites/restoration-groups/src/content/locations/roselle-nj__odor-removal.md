## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Roselle

Effective odor elimination isn't a single treatment — it's a sequence calibrated to what caused the odor and what materials absorbed it. The process typically moves through four stages:

**Source removal and cleaning.** No deodorization technology works if the source material is still present. Contaminated insulation, saturated drywall or plaster, and affected contents are removed or thoroughly cleaned before any chemical or equipment treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging.** A petroleum- or water-based deodorant is heated into a fine fog that penetrates the same porous pathways smoke or sewage odors traveled — into wall cavities, subfloor gaps, and HVAC ductwork. This is particularly effective in Roselle's older homes where odor has migrated deep into plaster.

**Hydroxyl or ozone generation.** Hydroxyl generators run safely in occupied or partially occupied spaces and break down odor-causing molecules at the molecular level over a sustained treatment period. Ozone treatment is faster but requires the space to be vacated; it's often the right choice for vacant properties or commercial spaces on the Chestnut Street corridor where downtime is a cost.

**Encapsulation and air scrubbing.** HEPA air scrubbers run continuously during and after treatment to capture particulates, and affected surfaces may receive an encapsulant coating as a final barrier — especially on concrete block basement walls where odor compounds can leach back out over time.
