## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Water Damage Differently

The housing stock here is old in ways that change how water behaves inside a building. Late-1800s and early-1900s frame construction — common throughout the neighborhoods north and south of Smith Street — typically means plaster-and-lath walls, cast-iron drain stacks, and galvanized supply lines that have been patching and corroding for a century. When a galvanized line finally lets go, the water does not stay in the wall cavity the way it might in a newer home with moisture-resistant drywall. It wicks into the plaster, travels down the lath, and pools in subfloor assemblies before anyone notices a stain on the ceiling below.

The multifamily nature of so much Perth Amboy housing adds another layer. A supply line failure in a third-floor unit in a converted three-family becomes a tenant-to-tenant claim across two or three floors, each with its own finish materials, its own occupant, and its own insurance picture. Waterfront properties in ZIP code 08861 carry the added exposure of coastal storm surge — Sandy in 2012 demonstrated what a significant tidal event can do to the low blocks near Raritan Bay Waterfront and the ferry slip area, and that coastal-storm risk has not gone away.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Perth Amboy

Every job starts with moisture mapping before a single piece of equipment is placed. In Perth Amboy's older building stock, surface readings are routinely misleading — plaster can feel dry to the touch while the lath behind it holds enough moisture to feed mold growth within days. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace the actual migration path of the water, not just the visible wet area.

From there the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Standing water removal** using truck-mounted and portable extractors, including submersible pumps for deeper basement flooding common in the Dunham Heights and Budapest areas
- **Structural drying** with industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space
- **Moisture monitoring** logged daily so drying progress is documented — critical for insurance claims and for knowing when it is genuinely safe to close walls
- **Antimicrobial treatment** of affected surfaces where contamination risk warrants it, particularly in basement losses where groundwater or sewage backup may be involved

In multifamily buildings, we coordinate access across units and communicate with property owners and tenants separately so the process does not stall waiting on a door to be unlocked.
