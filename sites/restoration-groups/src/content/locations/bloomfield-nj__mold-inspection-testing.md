## Why Bloomfield Properties Are Prone to Hidden Mold

Bloomfield's housing stock is one of the older and denser in Essex County. The colonials, two-families, and Tudor-style homes built between roughly 1910 and 1940 — common throughout North Center and Brookdale — were constructed before vapor barriers were standard practice. Finished basements in these homes often sit directly against fieldstone or poured-concrete foundations that wick ground moisture year-round. Add to that the original galvanized supply lines and cast-iron waste stacks that are now well past their service life, and slow leaks behind walls become a routine discovery during inspections.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Bloomfield

A mold assessment is not a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. The inspection begins with a detailed moisture survey using calibrated thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to map elevated readings behind walls, under flooring, and inside ceiling cavities — without opening walls unnecessarily. In Bloomfield's older homes, where plaster-over-lath construction is still common, moisture can travel laterally through wall cavities in ways that drywall construction does not, so the survey area typically extends beyond the obvious damage zone.

Air sampling is collected using calibrated spore-trap cassettes at multiple locations — typically one outdoor baseline sample plus two to three interior samples depending on the home's layout and the number of suspect zones. Swab or tape-lift surface samples are added when visible growth is present or when the moisture map points to a specific cavity. All samples are submitted to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results are typically returned within two to three business days. The written report identifies genus-level mold types, spore concentrations relative to outdoor baseline, and a prioritized scope of findings — the same documentation your insurance adjuster or a prospective buyer's attorney will ask for.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), the inspection protocol follows industry standards for mold assessment, which matters when you're coordinating with a carrier or a real estate transaction.
