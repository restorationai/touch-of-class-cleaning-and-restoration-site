## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Montclair

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source. If the homeowner hasn't already shut the main, we locate the shutoff — in older Montclair homes that's sometimes a gate valve in a basement utility room that hasn't been turned in thirty years and may need persuasion. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings before anything is moved, which matters when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim later.

Extraction comes next. Standing water in a finished basement gets pulled with truck-mounted extraction equipment. Then we map the moisture migration — in plaster-wall homes, water wicks laterally through wall cavities much farther than the visible damage suggests, so we use thermal imaging and pin-type meters to trace the actual wet boundary before setting drying equipment. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard: air movers and dehumidifiers positioned to create a drying system, not just pointed at the wet spot. We monitor daily until materials reach target moisture content.

Once the structure is dry, we coordinate or perform the pipe repair itself — replacing the failed section with appropriate materials and pressure-testing before closing any walls. We handle the full sequence so you're not managing a separate plumber, a separate water mitigation company, and a separate contractor for the repairs.
