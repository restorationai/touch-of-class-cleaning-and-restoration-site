## Our Water Cleanup Process in Somerville

Every job begins with a full moisture assessment — not just the visible wet area, but the surrounding structure. In Somerville's older homes, water behind plaster can read dry at the surface while the underlying lath and framing remain saturated. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the actual wet zone before any equipment is placed, because drying to the wrong target leaves hidden moisture that feeds mold within 24 to 72 hours.

Once the scope is confirmed, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then position desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers in a pattern calculated for the room geometry and material types. In finished basements — common throughout the West End and along Mercer Street — that often means pulling baseboard trim and drilling small relief holes in drywall to allow airflow behind the wall cavity, rather than drying the surface and leaving wet framing inside.

We document moisture readings daily, adjusting equipment placement as materials dry, and we don't call a job complete until readings return to regional equilibrium — typically in the range accepted by the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying.
