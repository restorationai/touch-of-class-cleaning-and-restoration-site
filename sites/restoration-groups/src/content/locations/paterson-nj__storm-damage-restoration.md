## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Paterson

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence we follow is consistent and designed for the realities of Paterson's building stock.

**Emergency stabilization first.** When a tree has punched through a roof or a wall section is exposed to the sky, the priority is stopping additional water intrusion. We tarp, board, and brace before anything else — because in Paterson's dense rowhouse blocks, an unsecured opening doesn't just threaten one unit, it can compromise the shared wall of the property next door.

**Damage documentation.** We photograph and catalog every affected surface, structural member, and system before moving or removing anything. This documentation is the foundation of your insurance claim, and we build it to the carrier's standards from the start.

**Water extraction and drying.** Standing water comes out with truck-mounted extractors. Structural drying follows using industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, calibrated to the material — plaster walls common in pre-war Paterson homes release moisture more slowly than modern drywall and require extended drying cycles and more frequent moisture readings to confirm they've reached acceptable levels.

**Debris removal and structural assessment.** Tree limbs, damaged roofing materials, and compromised framing come out safely. We assess whether structural repairs require a licensed contractor coordination or can be handled within our scope as an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor.

**Reconstruction.** From re-roofing to interior finish work, we carry the job through to a livable, code-compliant result — so you're not managing a handoff between a mitigation company and a separate rebuild crew.
