## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Hackensack

Every job starts with an odor source assessment. We locate the origin — charred framing, saturated insulation, a contaminated drain line — before selecting a treatment method. Masking an odor without addressing its source is a short-term fix that fails within weeks.

Depending on what we find, we deploy one or more of the following methods:

- **Thermal fogging** — a heated solvent-based fog that penetrates porous surfaces and bonds with odor-causing molecules at the molecular level. Particularly effective in smoke-damaged rooms where soot has embedded in plaster or wood trim.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — UV-generated hydroxyl radicals that break down odor compounds in the air and on surfaces. Safe to run in occupied or partially occupied buildings, which matters in multi-unit properties near the Bergen County Courthouse district where tenants can't always vacate.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone that oxidizes odor molecules in sealed, unoccupied spaces. We use this in vacant units or after-hours in commercial spaces along the Main Street business district where foot traffic is cleared.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and negative air pressure** — removes particulates and prevents cross-contamination to unaffected areas during the process.

After treatment, we conduct clearance testing to confirm odor levels have returned to baseline. We document every step for insurance carriers and property managers.
