## Why Elizabeth's Building Stock Complicates Fire Damage

Most of the residential housing in ZIP codes 07201 and 07202 was built before modern fire codes required compartmentalization between units. Balloon-frame construction — common in the two- and three-family homes that line the streets of Peterstown and Midtown — uses continuous wall studs that run from the foundation to the roof, creating unobstructed vertical channels. When a fire burns in one unit, hot gases, smoke, and soot ride those channels upward and laterally before anyone realizes the adjacent unit is affected.

Plaster-and-lath walls compound the problem. Plaster is dense and slow to release absorbed odors, which means smoke smell that seems minor on day one becomes overwhelming by day three as the wall mass off-gasses. Standard odor neutralization timelines calibrated for modern drywall construction often fall short in these older buildings — a reality that shapes how we scope and price every Elizabeth job.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Elizabeth

The first priority after the fire department releases a property is stabilization — boarding windows and doors, tarping any roof damage, and shutting off utilities if that hasn't already happened. In Elizabeth's tightly packed neighborhoods, an unsecured structure is a liability for the whole block, so we move on that immediately.

Next comes a systematic damage assessment. We use thermal imaging cameras to map heat patterns inside wall cavities and ceilings, which is especially useful in older balloon-frame buildings where fire or smoke may have traveled far from the visible burn zone. Every affected surface is documented with photographs and moisture readings before any material is removed — that documentation record is what your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim accurately.

Content pack-out follows for salvageable belongings. Smoke-damaged furniture, clothing, and personal items are inventoried, packed, and transported to our facility for ozone treatment and cleaning — or, when items are beyond recovery, documented for the insurance loss inventory. Structural cleaning uses HEPA-filtered negative air machines to prevent soot from migrating to unaffected areas while crews clean framing, subfloor, and mechanical systems. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation address odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Once the structure passes clearance, our reconstruction team handles drywall, flooring, cabinetry, and finish work so you're dealing with one contractor through the entire process.
