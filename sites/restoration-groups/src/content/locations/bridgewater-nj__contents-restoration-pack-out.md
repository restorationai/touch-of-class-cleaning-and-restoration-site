## Why Bridgewater Properties Face Elevated Contents Risk

Bridgewater's housing stock tells two different stories. The older tracts in Finderne and the Bound Brook-adjacent flats sit in low-lying terrain that has flooded catastrophically more than once — most recently during Tropical Storm Ida in 2021, when the Middle Brook and Raritan River overwhelmed storm infrastructure across the township's southern sections. Homes built in the 1950s and 1960s in those areas frequently have finished basements with aging cast-iron drains and galvanized supply lines that are past their service life. When those pipes fail — or when floodwater enters — the basement contents (home offices, storage, entertainment rooms) absorb damage quickly.

The Route 22 corridor and the office parks clustered near the Route 22/287 interchange add a commercial dimension. Sprinkler-discharge events, roof leaks over tenant spaces, and overnight HVAC condensation failures can soak inventory, equipment, and records in ways that require immediate pack-out before any structural drying can begin. Leaving contents in place during drying often means secondary smoke-odor or mold contamination — which is why pack-out is not optional on most significant losses.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Bridgewater

Every contents job starts with a room-by-room inventory before a single item is moved. Each piece is photographed, tagged, and logged against a digital manifest — a record that matters both for your insurance claim and for the return process weeks later.

From there, the process branches based on what the loss involved:

- **Water losses**: Saturated upholstered furniture, area rugs, and soft goods are separated from salvageable hard goods. Items that can be restored go to a climate-controlled facility for controlled drying, deodorization, and cleaning. Items that cannot — particleboard furniture that has swollen beyond recovery, for instance — are documented and set aside for the adjuster's review rather than quietly discarded.
- **Fire and smoke losses**: Smoke residue penetrates fabric, wood grain, and HVAC-circulated soft goods faster than most homeowners expect. Thermal fogging, ozone treatment, and ultrasonic cleaning are used selectively based on material type. Bridgewater's older split-levels with open stairwells allow smoke to travel vertically throughout the home, meaning contents on upper floors often need treatment even when the fire origin was in the basement or garage.
- **Mold events**: Contents near the loss area are evaluated for surface mold versus deep colonization. Items that can be wiped, HEPA-vacuumed, and treated are restored; items that cannot be cleaned to a safe standard are documented for the claim.

When the structure is ready, contents are returned in the same room-by-room sequence documented at pack-out — not unloaded from a truck and sorted on-site.
