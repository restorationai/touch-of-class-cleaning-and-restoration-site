## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in West Orange

Every job starts with a documented scope — not a verbal estimate. When we arrive, we photograph and moisture-map the full affected area before a single piece of material is moved. For residential losses, that means checking wall cavities, subfloor assemblies, and insulation bays that may have absorbed water invisibly. In older homes with plaster walls, we use thermal imaging alongside pin and pinless moisture meters, because plaster holds water differently than drywall and can read dry on the surface while staying saturated an inch in.
