## Why Paramus Properties Are Prone to Sewage Backups

Paramus was built out fast in the postwar decades, and most of the housing stock in ZIP code 07652 reflects that era: ranch homes, colonials, and split-levels with original cast-iron or Orangeburg drain lines that are now 60-plus years old. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out; Orangeburg — a tar-and-paper composite used through the mid-1970s — absorbs moisture and eventually collapses. Neither material was designed to last this long, and both fail with little warning.

The town's geography compounds the risk. Low-lying streets near Van Saun County Park and the Saddle River floodplain sit at elevations where municipal sewer mains are under hydrostatic pressure during heavy rain events. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paramus

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the process for handling it is more involved than a standard water loss. Here's how a typical job unfolds:

**Containment and safety assessment.** Before any extraction begins, the affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination. In a finished Paramus basement, that often means sealing HVAC vents and doorways before touching anything.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted or portable units. Porous materials — drywall, carpet, insulation, wood subfloor — that have direct sewage contact are removed. There is no drying a contaminated porous material back to safe; it comes out.

**Antimicrobial treatment.** Once the space is cleared, all affected hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. This step addresses bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens present in raw sewage. We document the products used and their dwell times — this matters for insurance documentation and for your own peace of mind.

**Structural drying.** After sanitization, drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers, air movers — is placed to bring the structural materials that remain down to acceptable moisture content, verified with moisture meters and thermal imaging. The IICRC S500 standard governs our drying protocols as an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213).

**Clearance documentation.** Before equipment comes out, we confirm readings are within normal range. You get a written moisture log — useful for insurance claims and for any future buyer's disclosure.
