## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Clark

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the water. If the homeowner hasn't already located the shutoff, we find it — in Clark's older homes that often means tracing lines in a finished utility room or crawl space rather than a clean mechanical room. Once flow is stopped, we use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to map exactly how far water has traveled through walls, subfloor, and framing.
