## Why Maplewood Homes Are Particularly Vulnerable to Sewage Backups

The housing stock in the 07040 ZIP code is part of what makes Maplewood so architecturally distinctive — and part of what makes sewage events so damaging when they happen. Many homes in the Hilton and Jefferson neighborhoods were built before modern PVC plumbing existed. Their original cast-iron drain lines are now 80 to 100 years old. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out; root intrusion from the mature oaks and maples that line these streets accelerates the process. When a main sewer line partially collapses or a root mass blocks flow, the path of least resistance is backward — into the basement.

Stone foundations common in this housing stock are porous by design. They were built to "breathe," not to hold back a hydrostatic surge from a saturated yard after a nor'easter. When groundwater and sewer pressure combine, finished basement spaces — the home office, the playroom, the laundry area — take the hit. Original plaster walls and wood subfloors absorb contaminated water quickly, and the contamination spreads further than it looks on the surface.

Septic systems are less common in Maplewood's denser residential blocks, but homes on larger lots toward the South Mountain Reservation boundary occasionally run private systems. A septic overflow behaves differently from a municipal sewer backup — the tank contents are more concentrated, and the affected area often extends into the yard — but the indoor cleanup protocol is equally rigorous.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Maplewood

Every sewage call starts with a containment assessment before anyone moves material around. Tracking Category 3 water through a house — from the basement up the stairs into the kitchen — is a common mistake that turns a contained loss into a whole-house remediation. We establish a contamination boundary first.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Extraction** — Standing sewage and contaminated water is removed using truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment. We don't leave it to gravity.
- **Removal of unsalvageable materials** — Saturated drywall, insulation, and flooring that has absorbed Category 3 water cannot be dried in place. It comes out. In Maplewood's older homes, this often means original hardwood subfloor sections and plaster-over-lath walls that have wicked contamination up into the framing cavity.
- **Disinfection** — All structural surfaces in the affected area are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This is not a general-purpose cleaner — it targets the bacteria, viruses, and pathogens present in raw sewage.
- **Drying and dehumidification** — Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. In Maplewood's plaster-walled basements, this takes longer than it would in a newer home with fiberglass-batted drywall construction.
- **Post-remediation verification** — We document moisture readings and surface conditions before closing out the job.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process follows the S500 standard for water damage and the guidelines for sewage contamination — not a shortcut version of it.

## Local Note: What Maplewood's Plaster Walls Mean for Sewage Cleanup Timelines

In most newer construction, a sewage-affected basement wall is drywall over fiberglass batt — you cut it out, bag it, and the framing behind it dries relatively quickly. In the Tudors and colonials throughout Maplewood Village and the Hilton neighborhood, you're often dealing with plaster-over-wood-lath construction. Plaster is dense and holds moisture longer than drywall, but the real issue is the lath behind it: thin wood strips that absorb contaminated water and are difficult to dry in place once saturated. In a Category 3 sewage event, saturated lath almost always has to come out. Homeowners are sometimes surprised by the extent of demolition — it looks more aggressive than they expected — but leaving contaminated organic material inside a wall cavity creates a mold problem within days.
