## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Linden

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item is moved. Each piece is photographed, condition-noted, and logged — a record that matters both for insurance documentation and for returning everything to the right room when the home is ready. Items are then sorted: what can be restored, what needs specialist attention (electronics, documents, artwork), and what the carrier will need to see before a decision is made.

Packed contents move to a climate-controlled facility where technicians work through each category methodically. Soft contents — clothing, bedding, upholstered items — go through an ozone or hydroxyl treatment cycle to neutralize smoke and microbial odors rather than masking them. Hard goods are cleaned surface by surface. Documents and photos that have gotten wet are stabilized quickly, because the window for preventing permanent ink migration or paper degradation is narrow. Throughout, the inventory log stays live so homeowners can check the status of specific items without having to call.

Once the structure in Linden passes clearance, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed according to the original room documentation.
