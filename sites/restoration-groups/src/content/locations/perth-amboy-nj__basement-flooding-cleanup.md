## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Perth Amboy

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing water entry if the source is still active — shutting a supply valve, sandbagging a window well, or clearing a floor drain that's backing up. From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

**Water extraction** comes first. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from the floor, and we use weighted extraction tools on any carpet or padding. In Perth Amboy's older basements, that often means working around low ceiling clearances, coal-chute blockouts, and mechanical equipment that can't be moved.

**Moisture mapping** follows immediately. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters tell us how far water has wicked into concrete block walls, wood sill plates, and any framing. In a century-old building, water travels further and faster through porous masonry than most homeowners expect.

**Drying and dehumidification** runs continuously — typically 3 to 5 days for a Category 1 loss, longer if sewage or tidal water is involved. We place industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in calculated positions, then take daily moisture readings to confirm the structure is actually drying, not just surface-dry.

**Documentation** runs the entire time: photos, moisture logs, and equipment placement records that your insurance adjuster will need.

## Insurance Coordination for Perth Amboy Flood Losses

Most standard homeowner and landlord policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe or an appliance failure — but exclude gradual seepage and rising floodwater unless the property carries separate NFIP (National Flood Insurance Program) flood coverage. Perth Amboy's coastal position means a meaningful share of properties here do carry NFIP policies, and flood claims follow a different documentation and adjustment process than standard HO claims.

We work with both. Our moisture logs, photo documentation, and scope of work reports are formatted to meet adjuster requirements whether the carrier is a private insurer or FEMA's flood program. For multifamily owners dealing with tenant-to-tenant claims — common in the city's dense rowhouse blocks — we can provide separate scopes for each affected unit to simplify the claim process.
