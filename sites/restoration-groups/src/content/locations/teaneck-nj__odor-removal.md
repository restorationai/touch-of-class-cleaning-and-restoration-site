## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Teaneck

Effective deodorization isn't a single step — it's a sequence calibrated to the odor source, the affected materials, and the construction of the building.

The process begins with a thorough source assessment. Odor molecules don't stay where the damage happened; they migrate into HVAC systems, settle into soft goods, and absorb into plaster and wood substrates. In Teaneck's older homes, that often means tracing smoke odor from a first-floor kitchen fire all the way into second-floor bedroom closets through gaps in original balloon-frame construction.

Once the source and migration path are mapped, we select the appropriate treatment method:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing agent as a fine dry fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled — effective for post-fire work in homes with original plaster walls and irregular cavity spaces.

- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe, deeply embedded odors — particularly useful in vacant units or commercial spaces along the Cedar Lane district after a significant fire or long-term moisture event.
- **Direct application and encapsulation** addresses surfaces that have absorbed odor compounds — subfloor sheathing, wall cavities, HVAC duct interiors — using EPA-registered antimicrobial sealants where appropriate.

Older construction creates specific challenges that off-the-shelf deodorization equipment isn't designed for.
