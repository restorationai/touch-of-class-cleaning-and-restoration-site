## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Perth Amboy

Effective deodorization starts with source identification, not equipment deployment. A technician walks the property to locate the origin — whether that's charred structural framing, a sewage-saturated subfloor, or a pet-contaminated crawl space — before selecting a method. The wrong tool applied to the wrong source wastes time and leaves odor behind.

For smoke odor removal in fire-damaged properties, **thermal fogging** is often the right first step. The fogging agent is heated into a dry vapor that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules embedded in wall cavities, ceiling assemblies, and HVAC ductwork. In occupied or partially occupied multifamily buildings — common in Perth Amboy — we coordinate fogging windows carefully so adjacent tenants are not displaced unnecessarily.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** is the preferred method when occupants or pets need to return to the space quickly. Hydroxyl generators produce reactive molecules that break down odor compounds without requiring the space to be vacated for extended periods, making them practical for the city's dense residential buildings where relocating a household is a real hardship.

**Ozone treatment** delivers the deepest penetration for severe cases — heavy smoke saturation, long-term pet odors, or post-flood microbial odors in unoccupied structures. Because ozone at treatment concentrations requires the space to remain unoccupied, we use it selectively and schedule it to minimize disruption.

All work is documented with pre- and post-treatment readings so there is a clear record for insurance purposes.
