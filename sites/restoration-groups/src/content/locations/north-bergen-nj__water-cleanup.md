## Our Water Cleanup Process in North Bergen

The first thing we do on arrival is locate the water source and stop the loss if it hasn't been stopped already. From there, the process follows a defined sequence calibrated to what we actually find in North Bergen's older housing stock.

**Inspection and moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind plaster walls and under hardwood subfloors. In pre-war construction, water can travel several feet from the visible wet area before the surface reads damp.

**Extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from finished basements, crawl spaces, and ground-floor units. In multi-family buildings, we coordinate access floor by floor to confirm water hasn't migrated to adjacent units.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers run continuously until moisture readings return to acceptable baselines. Drying logs are maintained daily — these records matter when your adjuster reviews the claim.

**Content handling.** Furniture, personal property, and tenant belongings are documented, moved to a dry area, or flagged for pack-out depending on the scope of saturation.
