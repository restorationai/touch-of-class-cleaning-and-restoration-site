## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Parsippany

The first step on any flooded basement call is a moisture mapping walk-through before a single pump is placed. Water in a finished basement doesn't stay where you see it — it wicks into wall cavities behind paneling, saturates carpet padding and subfloor, and travels along concrete seams to areas twenty feet from the source. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full extent before extraction begins.

Once the scope is mapped, truck-mounted extractors pull standing water while portable units address low-clearance areas common in the older ranch-style homes in ZIP code 07054. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to create directed airflow through wall cavities — not just across the surface of materials. Drying logs are recorded daily and shared with you and your insurance adjuster in real time.

Demolition of unsalvageable materials — wet drywall, saturated insulation, swollen cabinetry — is performed to the flood cut line only, preserving as much of the original structure as the drying data supports. Every cut and every moisture reading is photographed for the insurance file.
