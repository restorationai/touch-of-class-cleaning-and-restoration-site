## Our Water Cleanup Process in Scotch Plains

Water cleanup in a mid-century split-level is not the same job as water cleanup in a newer construction home, and we calibrate accordingly. ## Local Note: What Split-Level Framing Does to Drying Timelines

Here's something that surprises homeowners in Willow Grove and Ashbrook the first time they go through a water loss: the staggered-floor framing in a classic split-level creates enclosed joist bays that trap humid air. Standard air-mover placement that works fine in a ranch or a colonial doesn't move air through those cavities the way it needs to. We use directed drying — positioning equipment to create deliberate airflow paths through the framing — and in some cases drill small vent holes in baseboards to allow air circulation inside the wall cavity. Those holes get patched before we leave. The point is that drying a split-level to the same standard as a simpler floor plan takes a different setup, and skipping that step is how you get mold growth behind finished walls six weeks after a cleanup that looked complete on the surface.
