## Why Englewood Properties Hold Odors Longer

Older construction absorbs odor molecules differently than modern materials. Pre-1950 plaster walls common on the East Hill are porous in ways that standard drywall is not — smoke particles and microbial VOCs penetrate the substrate rather than sitting on the surface. A single deodorizing spray or an HVAC filter swap won't reach them. The same applies to the original hardwood subfloors found throughout Highwood and the older apartment buildings near Downtown: once urine, smoke, or floodwater saturates the wood grain, surface treatment alone leaves the odor source intact.

Deodorization in those situations isn't cosmetic; it's the final phase of a remediation sequence.

## Our Deodorization Process, Calibrated for Englewood Conditions

Every job starts with an odor source assessment — not just a walkthrough, but a systematic identification of where the contamination actually lives. In Englewood's older multifamily buildings, that often means checking wall cavities, crawlspaces, and HVAC ductwork before any treatment begins. Masking an odor without finding its source is a short-term fix that fails.

Depending on what we find, we deploy one or more of the following methods:

- **Thermal fogging** — a heated solvent-based fog that penetrates porous surfaces including plaster, grout, and wood framing, bonding with odor molecules at the molecular level. Particularly effective after smoke events in older East Hill homes where the structure itself has absorbed the contamination.
- **Hydroxyl generation** — UV-light technology that produces hydroxyl radicals to neutralize odor compounds in the air and on surfaces. Safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, which matters in multi-unit buildings where full vacancy isn't always possible.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration oxidation that eliminates persistent biological odors (mold, pet, decomposition) in unoccupied spaces. We follow strict protocols for re-entry timing and ventilation, especially in tighter floor plans common to the Fourth Ward's apartment stock.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct cleaning** — for smoke and mold odors that have cycled through a forced-air system, surface treatment alone won't hold. We address the ductwork directly.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our technicians follow structured drying and deodorization protocols — not a general-purpose cleaning approach.
