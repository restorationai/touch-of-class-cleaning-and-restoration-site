## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Bayonne

Every smoke damage job starts with a systematic inspection rather than a visual scan. Soot comes in multiple forms — dry and powdery from fast-burning fires, wet and oily from smoldering synthetic materials, and protein-based (nearly invisible but intensely odorous) from kitchen fires. Identifying the soot type determines which cleaning chemistry and technique will actually work, and getting it wrong means the smell returns within weeks.

Once we've mapped the affected zones, the process moves through these stages:

- **Containment and ventilation** — negative air pressure prevents cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, critical in Bayonne's multi-unit buildings where a loss on one floor can affect tenants above and below.
- **Dry soot removal** — HEPA vacuuming of surfaces, ductwork registers, and structural cavities before any wet cleaning begins.
- **Surface cleaning** — alkaline or solvent-based cleaners matched to the soot type, applied to walls, ceilings, woodwork, and contents.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation to reach residue inside wall cavities and HVAC systems that surface cleaning can't access.
- **Structural drying** — smoke damage often follows water damage from suppression hoses; we monitor moisture levels throughout until readings return to pre-loss baselines.
- **Reconstruction coordination** — when plaster, drywall, or cabinetry must be replaced, we document scope and coordinate with our rebuild division so you're working with one point of contact.
