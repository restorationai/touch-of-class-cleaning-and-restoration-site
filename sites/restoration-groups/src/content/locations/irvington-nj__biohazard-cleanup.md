## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Irvington

Every job begins with a thorough assessment of what materials have been affected and to what depth. In Irvington's older housing stock, that often means checking beneath surface flooring, inside wall cavities, and along the base of basement walls before any remediation begins.

Containment is established before any work starts, limiting cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home. Technicians work in appropriate personal protective equipment throughout. Affected porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are removed and disposed of in accordance with New Jersey regulations governing infectious and biohazardous waste — this is not standard trash removal, and the disposal chain is documented. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate to the specific biological hazard present. The space is then cleared for re-occupancy only after verification that contamination has been addressed.

Because the work involves regulated waste streams and specific disposal requirements, it matters that the team performing it is an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) operating under proper licensure — not a general cleaning service.
