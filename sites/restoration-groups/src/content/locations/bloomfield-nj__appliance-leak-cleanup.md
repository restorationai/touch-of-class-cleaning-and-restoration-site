## Why Bloomfield Homes Are Especially Vulnerable to Appliance Leaks

Bloomfield's housing stock tells the story. The Tudors and colonials concentrated around Brookdale and the streets feeding Watsessing Park were built between roughly 1910 and 1940, and most still run original galvanized supply lines to appliances. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out — it doesn't fail dramatically, it seeps. A slow drip behind a dishwasher or under a refrigerator can saturate particleboard cabinet bases and the subfloor beneath before a homeowner notices the puddle. By the time there's visible water, the structural materials underneath have often been wet for days.

Postwar cape cods and ranches in North Center and Brookside tend to have sump-pit setups in finished basements, which adds a second failure point: when a washing machine drain line overwhelms the utility sink or the sump pump can't keep up during a summer downpour, the entire finished basement becomes the collection basin. ## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Bloomfield

Every appliance leak job starts with source confirmation. We identify whether the water is clean supply-line water, gray water from a dishwasher or washing machine drain, or potentially contaminated if there's any sewer backup component. That classification determines the PPE, the extraction method, and how aggressively affected materials need to be removed rather than dried in place.

Once the source is controlled, we use truck-mounted and portable extraction units to pull standing water from hard and soft surfaces, then deploy commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the square footage and material density of the affected space. In Bloomfield's older homes, plaster walls and original hardwood floors behave differently than modern drywall and engineered flooring — plaster holds moisture deeper and releases it more slowly, which means drying timelines run longer and moisture readings need to be tracked over multiple days rather than assumed complete after 48 hours. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation, including inside wall cavities and beneath flooring, before we ever set equipment placement.

All drying is performed to the IICRC S500 standard, and documentation — photos, moisture logs, equipment placement records — is generated throughout the job for insurance purposes.

## Bloomfield Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies in New Jersey cover sudden and accidental appliance discharge — a washing machine hose that ruptures qualifies; a slow drip that went unaddressed for months typically does not. We document the loss thoroughly from the moment we arrive: photos of the appliance, the failure point, the affected materials, and the moisture readings at each measurement location.
