## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in West Orange

The first thing we do on arrival is assess the contamination category and contain the affected zone. Sewage is always treated as Category 3 — the highest-risk classification — which means full PPE, containment barriers, and no shortcuts on material removal. Porous materials that have absorbed sewage (carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation) are removed and bagged for disposal. There is no drying a sewage-soaked carpet back to a safe condition; it comes out.

Once the bulk material is removed, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then apply an EPA-registered disinfectant to all affected hard surfaces — concrete floors, block walls, framing lumber. We follow the IICRC S500 and S520 standards for water and sewage loss, which govern dwell times, dilution ratios, and documentation requirements. Structural drying begins after sanitization is confirmed, using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the square footage and material types in the affected space.

Final clearance involves surface testing before any reconstruction begins. We document the process with photographs and moisture readings at every stage — that documentation matters when you're filing a claim with your homeowner's insurer.
