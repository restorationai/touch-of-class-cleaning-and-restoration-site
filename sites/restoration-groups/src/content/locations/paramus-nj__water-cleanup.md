## Why Paramus Properties See Water Damage So Often

Paramus was built out largely in the 1950s and 1960s, and that era left a specific fingerprint on the housing stock. Ranch homes, colonials, and split-levels across West Paramus and East Paramus were plumbed with galvanized steel supply lines that have long since passed their service life. When galvanized corrodes from the inside out, it doesn't fail gradually — it pinhole-leaks behind walls for weeks before a joint finally gives way and releases full pressure into a finished basement or a kitchen cabinet bay.

Cast-iron drain stacks from the same era crack under root intrusion and freeze-thaw stress, sending gray water into crawl spaces and utility areas. And Paramus sits in a low-lying section of Bergen County where the Saddle River drainage basin means that heavy rain events — Ida in 2021 being the most recent and severe — push groundwater through foundation walls and floor drains faster than sump pumps can keep up. Homes near Van Saun County Park and along the low corridors feeding into it are particularly exposed to that kind of lateral water intrusion.

The commercial side of Paramus tells a different story. The Route 17 retail corridor — anchored by Westfield Garden State Plaza and Paramus Park Mall — generates its own category of water loss: overnight sprinkler-head failures, roof membrane breaches that pool above drop ceilings, and HVAC condensate overflows that can saturate thousands of square feet of commercial flooring before a morning crew arrives. Retail water losses move on a different clock than residential ones, and the documentation requirements for commercial carriers reflect that.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Paramus

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Paramus's postwar construction uses plaster over lath in older sections and paper-faced drywall in additions and renovations — those materials behave differently under a moisture meter and respond to drying at different rates. We use thermal imaging alongside pin and pinless meters to locate saturation that doesn't show at the surface, because water behind plaster can read nearly dry at the face while the lath and framing behind it stay fully saturated.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is extracted with truck-mounted or portable units depending on access. In finished basements — common throughout the 07652 ZIP code — that means moving contents, pulling saturated carpet and pad, and assessing whether the subfloor and bottom plates of partition walls have absorbed enough moisture to require controlled demolition. We follow IICRC S500 drying standards throughout, placing refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers in configurations calculated for the room volume and materials present, then monitoring daily until all readings reach target.

## Paramus Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners' policies in New Jersey cover sudden and accidental water discharge — a burst pipe, a failed washing machine supply line, an appliance leak — but exclude gradual leaks and most groundwater intrusion. The distinction matters because it affects what your adjuster will approve and how the scope of work gets written. We document losses with photographs, moisture logs, and equipment placement records formatted to meet carrier requirements, and we work directly with adjusters so the paperwork doesn't fall on you during an already stressful situation.
