## Our Basement Flood Cleanup Process in Livingston

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Standing water is extracted first using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, but the real work is what comes after — identifying where water has migrated behind finished walls, under LVP flooring, and into the framing cavities that are common in Livingston's split-level construction.

In homes with older cast-iron drain systems, we check for sewage contamination before treating the loss as a clean-water event. A backed-up floor drain changes the category of the loss and the personal protective protocols the crew uses. Affected materials are documented with photos and moisture readings logged to IICRC S500 drying standards — the same documentation your insurance adjuster will request.

Drying equipment — desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — is placed based on a psychrometric calculation for the space, not a one-size-fits-all grid.
