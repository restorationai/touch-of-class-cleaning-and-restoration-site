## Why Linden Properties Hold Odors Longer

The same building characteristics that give Linden's neighborhoods their character work against you after a fire or flood. Plaster walls common in homes near the Wheatsheaf and Bower sections absorb moisture and combustion byproducts more deeply than modern drywall. When smoke or floodwater penetrates plaster, the odor compounds don't sit on the surface — they migrate into the substrate and re-volatilize slowly for weeks, sometimes months. Finished basements, nearly universal in Linden's split-level stock, compound the problem: a single sewage backup or Morses Creek overflow event can saturate carpet, subfloor, and framing in a space with limited air circulation, creating conditions where microbial odors intensify rather than dissipate on their own.

## Our Deodorization Process in Linden

Effective odor elimination isn't a single step — it's a sequence calibrated to what caused the odor and what materials absorbed it.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization technology works reliably if saturated materials are still present. We remove charred debris, water-damaged porous materials, and contaminated contents before any treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective in Linden's older homes because the fogging agent — a deodorizing solvent heated to a fine dry vapor — penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled: wall cavities, subfloor gaps, plaster voids. It neutralizes odor molecules at the molecular level rather than masking them.

**Hydroxyl generation** runs continuously during and after other work. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators are safe to operate in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner can't vacate for days. Hydroxyls break down volatile organic compounds in the air and on surfaces without leaving a chemical residue.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for vacant, sealed spaces — an empty basement after a sewage loss, or a closed commercial unit on the Wood Avenue corridor after a kitchen fire. Ozone is one of the most powerful oxidizing agents available for odor elimination, but the space must be cleared of people, pets, and plants for the duration.

**HVAC and duct deodorization** closes the loop. In Linden's split-levels and raised ranches, forced-air systems pull air from finished basements and distribute it through the whole house. Treating the living space without addressing the ductwork leaves a re-contamination pathway open.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process follows the S520 and applied structural drying standards — documentation that matters when an insurance adjuster is reviewing the scope.
