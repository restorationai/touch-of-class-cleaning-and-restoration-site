## Why Linden Properties See Burst Pipe Problems

Linden's housing stock tells the story. The majority of single-family homes in the 07036 ZIP code were built between 1945 and 1965 — an era when galvanized steel supply lines and cast-iron drain stacks were standard. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out over decades, narrowing to a fraction of its original diameter before it finally splits. Cast-iron drains crack under the freeze-thaw stress that New Jersey winters deliver reliably every year. In finished basements — and nearly every Linden cape and colonial has one — that failure means water is already soaking into framing, insulation, and flooring long before it's visible.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Linden

The first priority on any pipe-break call is stopping the water. If the homeowner hasn't already shut the main, our crew locates and closes it on arrival — in Linden's older homes, the shutoff is often in the basement utility area or, in some Wheatsheaf-area properties, near the meter pit at the curb. From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

1. **Damage mapping** — moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras trace how far water has migrated through walls, subfloor, and ceiling assemblies. In finished basements, water routinely travels 15–20 feet from the break point before it's detected.
2. **Standing water extraction** — truck-mounted extraction removes bulk water from flooring and carpet. In slab-on-grade sections common in some Linden split-levels, we use specialty floor drying mats to pull moisture from concrete.
3. **Structural drying** — industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed by guesswork. Drying is documented daily against IICRC S500 standard targets so your insurance adjuster has a clear record.
4. **Pipe repair** — licensed plumbing repair is coordinated as part of the same job, not handed off to a separate contractor with a separate schedule. We hold an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor credential through the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs, which covers the repair scope.
5. **Reconstruction** — drywall, insulation, flooring, and trim are restored to pre-loss condition once structural drying goals are confirmed.
