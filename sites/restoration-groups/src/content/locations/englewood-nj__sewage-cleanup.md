Englewood's housing stock is one of the widest-ranging in Bergen County. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Englewood

The first priority on arrival is containment. Raw sewage spreads pathogens on foot traffic, HVAC airflow, and capillary wicking through porous materials, so the affected area is isolated before any extraction begins. We photograph and document everything for your insurance file before moving a single item.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable wet-vac units pull standing sewage from hard floors and carpet. Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet padding, wood subfloor — that have direct contact with Category 3 water are removed rather than dried in place; no drying protocol makes contaminated drywall safe to leave behind. In Englewood's older multifamily buildings, this often means coordinating with landlords and adjacent tenants, since a backed-up basement unit can wick moisture into shared framing.

After extraction and demolition of unsalvageable material, we apply EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces, including concrete slab, framing, and HVAC components if sewage aerosols reached ductwork. Drying equipment — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers — runs until moisture readings in walls and subfloor reach target levels verified with thermal imaging and pin meters. We provide written moisture logs so your adjuster and contractor have documentation for reconstruction.

## Local Note

Englewood's East Hill estates frequently have finished basements with original 1920s–1940s concrete block foundations rather than poured concrete.
