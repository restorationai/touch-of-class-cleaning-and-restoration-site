## Our Deodorization Process in Hillside

Effective odor removal is a sequence, not a single treatment. We begin with a structured inspection — probing subfloor cavities, testing air quality, and identifying every contaminated material before equipment is staged. From there the process is calibrated to what the structure actually needs:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates the same microscopic pores that absorbed smoke or sewer gases, neutralizing odor molecules rather than masking them. It is particularly effective in Hillside's older homes where smoke from a kitchen or fireplace event has migrated into original plaster walls and exposed ceiling joists.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing compounds in occupied or partially occupied spaces — useful in multi-unit two-families in the Saybrook and Winans areas where a tenant may need to remain in an adjacent unit.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for vacant, sealed spaces where concentration can be controlled. We do not use ozone in occupied structures or around pets and plants.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and negative air pressure** capture particulates and prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms during the treatment window.

After treatment, we verify results with post-clearance odor testing rather than relying on subjective assessment.
