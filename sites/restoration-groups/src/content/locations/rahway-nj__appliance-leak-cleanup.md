## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Rahway

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the water source if it hasn't been shut off — then a full moisture mapping of the affected area using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. Appliance leaks are deceptive: a dishwasher leak under a kitchen island can wick laterally six feet in either direction through the subfloor before it becomes visible. We map the actual wet boundary, not just the visible stain.

Extraction comes next. Standing water is removed with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, followed by targeted drying with commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers placed to dry cavities, not just surfaces. In Rahway's older homes with original hardwood floors over plank subfloor, we use floor mat drying systems when the wood can be saved — a slower process, but one that avoids unnecessary tear-out and the cost of hardwood replacement.

All moisture readings are logged throughout the drying period, typically three to five days, and we don't close out a job until structural materials test dry to IICRC S500 standard. If affected materials — drywall, insulation, cabinetry — can't be dried in place, we document and remove them, photograph everything for your insurance file, and coordinate reconstruction through our licensed team.
