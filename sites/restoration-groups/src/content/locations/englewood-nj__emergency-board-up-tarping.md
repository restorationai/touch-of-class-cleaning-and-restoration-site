## Local Note: Slate Roofs and Tarp Anchoring on East Hill

One thing that catches out-of-area crews on East Hill jobs is the slate. Driving screws or nails through slate to anchor a tarp destroys tiles that cost $15 to $40 each to replace — and matching 100-year-old Vermont slate is not a same-week project. The correct approach is to run tarp anchor lines over the ridge and down the opposite slope, secured to the fascia or to purpose-built ridge hooks, keeping all penetrations off the slate surface entirely. It takes an extra 20 minutes but saves the homeowner from a roofing argument on top of an already stressful claim.
