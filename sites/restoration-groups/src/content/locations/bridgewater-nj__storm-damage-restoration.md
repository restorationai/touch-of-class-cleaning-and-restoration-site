## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Bridgewater

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive, the first priority is making the structure safe and stopping active water intrusion — that means emergency tarping of damaged rooflines, boarding compromised windows or doors, and extracting any standing water before it migrates into wall cavities or subfloor assemblies. In Bridgewater's older split-levels, that subfloor is often a finished lower level sitting on a slab, which means water trapped under vinyl plank or carpet padding can begin promoting mold colonization in as little as 24 to 48 hours.
