## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Piscataway

Every job begins with a full structural assessment before any cleaning starts. We map smoke migration using thermal imaging and air sampling, tracing where particles have traveled beyond the obvious burn area — often into HVAC returns, attic spaces, and finished basement ceilings. This matters especially in the older tracts near the Stelton commercial corridor, where ductwork may run through uninsulated crawl spaces that concentrate residue.

From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

- **Content removal and pack-out** — salvageable belongings are inventoried, packed, and transported for off-site cleaning so they don't continue absorbing odor while structural work proceeds.
- **Dry and wet smoke residue removal** — dry-smoke deposits from fast-burning wood fires are vacuumed with HEPA equipment before any wet cleaning; wet-smoke residue from slow, smoldering fires requires chemical sponges and alkaline cleaners matched to the surface type.
- **Odor neutralization** — hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging reach into cavities and porous materials that surface wiping can't address. We do not mask odors with fragrance; we break down the odor compounds.
- **Structural drying and encapsulation** — where smoke-saturated materials must remain (structural framing, for example), we apply sealant-grade encapsulants rated for smoke residue before any rebuild begins.
- **Post-cleaning air quality verification** — clearance readings confirm residue levels are within acceptable thresholds before we close out the job.

All work is performed by our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians and documented to the photo and moisture-log standard that NJ homeowner's carriers require for claim processing.
