## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Somerville

Every commercial loss is different, but the sequence that protects a Somerville business follows a consistent logic: stop the source, document the damage, extract standing water, dry the structure, and restore to pre-loss condition — all while keeping the business operational wherever possible.

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through walls, under flooring systems, and into structural cavities — critical in older Main Street buildings where water can travel surprising distances along original wood framing before surfacing.
