## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Fair Lawn

Commercial losses are not residential losses at scale — they involve active businesses, tenant relationships, liability documentation, and often multiple affected units or floors. The process reflects that.

First contact triggers dispatch from our Fair Lawn-area crews, available around the clock. On arrival, the priority is stopping ongoing damage: locating the source, isolating affected systems, and establishing a containment perimeter that keeps unaffected areas of the building operational wherever possible. Keeping part of your business running during remediation is not always possible, but it is always the goal.

Moisture mapping comes next. Commercial spaces — especially older Fair Lawn retail and office buildings with concrete slab floors, plaster walls, or suspended ceilings — hide water in ways that a surface reading misses entirely. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full migration path before any drying equipment is placed. Drying plans are documented to IICRC S500 standards, which matters when your insurance adjuster is reviewing the claim.

For fire and smoke losses, the process shifts to structural assessment, soot mapping, and odor neutralization. Smoke residue in commercial HVAC systems is a particular concern — a single fire event in one unit can push odor and fine particulates through ductwork into adjacent tenant spaces, creating secondary claims.
