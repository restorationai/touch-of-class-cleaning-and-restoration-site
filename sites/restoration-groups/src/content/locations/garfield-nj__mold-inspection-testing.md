## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Garfield

A mold inspection is not the same as mold remediation. The inspection phase is diagnostic: our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians come in to find the source of moisture, map the extent of any visible growth, and collect samples that go to an accredited third-party laboratory — not an in-house kit.

In Garfield's older homes, that process typically involves:

- **Moisture mapping** with calibrated meters along basement walls, floor joists, and interior wall cavities — areas where cast-iron drain connections and aging supply lines are most likely to have been seeping slowly.
- **Air sampling** in occupied rooms and in unconditioned spaces like crawl spaces and unfinished basement sections, which establishes a spore count baseline and identifies species present.
- **Surface sampling** (tape lifts or swabs) when visible growth is found on wood framing, drywall paper, or HVAC duct insulation.
- **Thermal imaging** to locate cold, damp wall cavities that don't show moisture on the surface — particularly useful in the plaster-over-wood-lath assemblies common in pre-war construction.

Lab results typically return within 24 to 48 hours. The written report identifies species, concentration levels, and affected areas, and gives you a documented baseline whether you're coordinating with a remediation contractor, an insurance adjuster, or a landlord.
