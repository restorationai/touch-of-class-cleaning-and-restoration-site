## Maplewood Insurance Coordination

Sudden and accidental appliance leaks — a burst ice maker line, a failed dishwasher door seal, a ruptured washing machine hose — are typically covered under standard homeowners policies as sudden water damage. Slow leaks that developed over time are a different story, and adjusters will look for evidence of long-term seepage. We photograph and document the loss thoroughly at the start of every job: the source, the migration path, the affected materials, and the moisture readings. That documentation gives your adjuster a clear picture of what happened and when, which supports your claim and reduces back-and-forth.
