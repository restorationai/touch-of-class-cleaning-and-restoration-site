## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Paterson

Every job starts with a rapid perimeter assessment. We photograph and document every compromised opening before touching anything — this record matters when your insurance adjuster arrives. From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Structural triage.** We identify which openings are actively admitting weather or pose a security risk and prioritize those first. On Paterson's attached rowhomes, we also check adjacent units — fire and wind damage in a shared-wall structure rarely stops at the property line.

**Roof tarping.** Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are secured with batten boards screwed into sound decking, not simply draped and weighted. On pitched roofs common to the frame two- and three-families in the Hillcrest area, we ridge-cap the tarp to prevent wind lift and channel runoff away from the fascia.

**Window and door boarding.** Openings are measured individually and cut from 3/4-inch CDX plywood. Panels are secured with structural screws into the framing — not just the trim — so they hold through wind gusts and resist forced entry. On brick facades, we use masonry anchors where frame attachment isn't possible.

**Documentation handoff.** Before we leave the site, you receive a written scope with photos, dimensions of every boarded opening, and the tarp coverage area. That package is formatted to support a standard insurance claim submission.
