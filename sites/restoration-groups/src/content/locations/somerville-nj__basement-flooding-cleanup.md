## Why Somerville Basements Flood More Than You'd Expect

Somerset County's compact borough seat sits at the confluence of two waterways — the Raritan River to the south and Peters Brook threading through lower-lying sections of town. That geography means the water table in parts of Somerville runs close to grade, and saturated soil after prolonged rain pushes hydrostatic pressure against foundation walls long before surface flooding is visible. Homes along Gaston Avenue and the East Main corridor, many built before World War II, were constructed without modern waterproofing membranes. ## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Somerville

Every job starts with a moisture mapping walk-through. Before a pump touches the floor, a technician reads the foundation walls, subfloor, and any framing with calibrated moisture meters and a thermal camera — because in Somerville's older finished basements, water migrates behind paneling and under tongue-and-groove flooring in ways that aren't visible to the eye. That initial map drives every decision that follows.

**Water removal.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from the slab. In basements with floor drains backed up from a surcharge event — common when Peters Brook overtops and reverses municipal laterals — we clear the drain path before extraction begins.

**Structural drying.** Commercial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned based on the moisture map rather than placed at random. Drying logs are recorded daily and provided to your insurer as part of the IICRC S500-compliant documentation package.

**Affected material assessment.** Saturated drywall, insulation, and carpet padding in a finished basement are typically removed within the first 24 hours to prevent secondary mold colonization — mold can begin establishing colonies on wet organic material in as little as 48 to 72 hours under warm, humid conditions. We photograph and itemize every removed material for the claim.

**Clearance verification.** The job isn't closed until moisture readings return to baseline for the surrounding building materials. In Somerville's older homes, that baseline is documented, not assumed.
