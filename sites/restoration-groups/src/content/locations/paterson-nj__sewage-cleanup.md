## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Paterson

Sewage water is classified as Category 3 — the highest contamination level — which means extraction alone is not enough. Every job follows a documented protocol built on IICRC S500 and S520 standards.

**Step 1 — Containment and PPE:** Technicians establish containment barriers before touching standing water. In Paterson's multi-family buildings, this step matters more than in single-family homes: an unsealed stairwell or shared hallway can carry aerosolized pathogens to unaffected units.

**Step 2 — Extraction:** Truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing sewage from floors, crawlspaces, and any below-grade areas. In basement apartments common to the 07503 and 07501 ZIP codes, access is often through a single narrow stairwell, so we carry compact extraction units rated for tight residential spaces.

**Step 3 — Removal of contaminated materials:** Porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, wood subfloor — that have direct sewage contact are removed and bagged for disposal. In Paterson's older plaster-and-lath construction, we assess each wall cavity individually; plaster absorbs and holds contamination differently than modern drywall and often requires more aggressive material removal.

**Step 4 — Hospital-grade disinfection:** Hard surfaces, concrete, and structural framing are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 losses. We apply, dwell, and re-apply — a single wipe-down is not a sanitization protocol.

**Step 5 — Drying and documentation:** Commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in walls and subfloors reach target levels. All readings are logged and photographed for insurance documentation.
