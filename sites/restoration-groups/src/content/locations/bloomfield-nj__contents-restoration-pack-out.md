## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition category — this documentation supports your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what left your home and in what state. Items that can be restored are packed in labeled, sealed containers and transported to a climate-controlled facility for cleaning and deodorization. Soft contents go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment to address smoke or microbial odors; hard surfaces are cleaned with appropriate agents matched to the material.

While your contents are off-site, structural work — drying, mold remediation, reconstruction — can proceed without the complications of working around furniture or risking secondary damage to belongings. Once the structure passes clearance, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed according to your direction. The inventory log closes out the process and feeds directly into insurance documentation.

For Bloomfield's older plaster-and-lath construction, this sequencing matters more than it does in newer builds. Plaster releases absorbed moisture slowly and requires extended drying cycles; having contents out of the space means drying equipment can be positioned optimally and monitored without interruption.
