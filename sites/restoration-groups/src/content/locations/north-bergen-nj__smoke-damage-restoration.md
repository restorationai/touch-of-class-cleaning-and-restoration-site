## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in North Bergen

Smoke damage restoration is not a single task — it is a sequence of decisions made in the right order. When the team arrives at a North Bergen property, the first step is a thorough scope assessment: mapping where smoke traveled, testing surfaces for soot type (dry, wet, or protein-based, each requiring a different cleaning chemistry), and identifying what structural materials are salvageable versus what needs to be removed.

For the older plaster walls common throughout North Bergen's pre-war housing stock, we use dry chemical sponges and low-alkaline detergents calibrated to the surface rather than aggressive scrubbing that can fracture aged plaster. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously during the process to capture airborne particulate. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl generators are deployed for deep odor neutralization in spaces where smoke has penetrated porous materials — wood trim, subfloors, and the fabric of upholstered contents.

Every affected surface is documented with photographs and moisture readings before, during, and after treatment. That documentation matters when you're filing a homeowner's or landlord's policy claim with a carrier that wants proof of scope and methodology.
