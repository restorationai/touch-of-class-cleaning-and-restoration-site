## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Garfield

Every storm job starts with a damage assessment that separates what's structurally urgent from what can be sequenced. In Garfield's older frame homes, that means checking roof decking and rafter condition before anything else — a compromised structure changes every decision downstream. We document everything photographically from the first hour, which matters when you're filing a claim with your carrier.

Once the structure is stabilized — emergency tarping for roof breaches, board-up for broken windows or compromised siding — we shift to moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled inside wall cavities, under flooring, and into subfloor assemblies. In a finished basement in the Dahnert's Lake area, that can mean water has wicked into framing behind drywall without a visible stain anywhere.

Extraction and drying follow the IICRC S500 standard for water damage and the S520 for any mold risk.
