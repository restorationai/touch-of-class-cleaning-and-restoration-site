## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Millburn

Every water loss is different, but the sequence of decisions matters. Water extraction comes next. Structural drying follows IICRC S500 standards: we calculate the drying zone, place refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers based on the moisture load, and position air movers to create the circulation pattern the materials need.
