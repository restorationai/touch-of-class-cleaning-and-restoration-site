## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Springfield

The first step on any Springfield job is a room-by-room inventory before anything moves. Every item is photographed, logged by category and condition, and tagged — a process that matters both for insurance documentation and for returning belongings to the right room when restoration is complete. Nothing leaves the property without a record.

From there, the decision tree branches based on what each item actually needs:

- **Soft contents** (upholstered furniture, clothing, bedding, rugs) go to our climate-controlled facility for ozone treatment, thermal fogging, or laundering depending on the contaminant involved.
- **Hard contents** (furniture, cabinetry components, decorative items) are evaluated for on-site cleaning versus pack-out. Items that can tolerate the drying environment may stay; items that would warp, delaminate, or off-gas in a high-temperature drying setup are moved.
- **Documents and media** (paper records, photos, electronics) are triaged immediately — freeze-drying is the standard intervention for waterlogged documents, and the window for that option closes within the first day or two.
- **Electronics** are never powered on after a water or smoke event until they have been assessed; corrosion inside a circuit board is invisible until the device fails.

Once the space is cleared, structural drying can proceed without equipment working around furniture. That matters in Springfield's older colonials, where plaster walls and original hardwood floors require longer drying cycles and unobstructed airflow to reach equilibrium.
