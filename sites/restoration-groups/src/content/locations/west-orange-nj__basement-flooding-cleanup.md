## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in West Orange

Every flooded basement job starts with a moisture mapping inspection before a single piece of equipment is placed. We use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to locate water that has migrated behind finished walls or beneath subfloor assemblies — the kind of hidden saturation that causes mold to colonize within 24 to 48 hours if left undiscovered.

Once the scope is established, the process moves in sequence:

- **Water extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from concrete floors, carpet, and subfloor cavities. In homes with older stone foundations, we pay particular attention to the perimeter wall base where water tends to pool.
- **Material assessment** — saturated drywall, insulation, and carpet padding are typically non-restorable and need to come out before drying begins. We document all removed material with photos for your insurance file.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage drying. Readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented, not assumed.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — applied to concrete, framing, and any remaining porous surfaces to inhibit microbial growth during the drying period.
- **Clearance verification** — we don't pull equipment until moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines, confirmed with the same meters used at intake.

The Restoration Group is an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) and a licensed New Jersey Home Improvement Contractor, so the documentation we produce meets the standard carriers and adjusters expect.
