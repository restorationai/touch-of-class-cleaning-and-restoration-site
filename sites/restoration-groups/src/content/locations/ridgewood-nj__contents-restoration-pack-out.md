## Our Pack-Out and Restoration Process

Contents restoration starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code — critical documentation for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing is lost in transit. Items are then carefully packed using materials matched to the object: padded wrapping for artwork and mirrors, sealed containers for documents and electronics, climate-appropriate boxing for textiles.

Once at our secure facility, contents go through the appropriate restoration track. Textiles and soft goods are processed through HEPA-filtered cleaning systems. Hard surfaces — furniture, cabinetry, decorative objects — are cleaned, deodorized, and dried under controlled conditions. Electronics are assessed by qualified technicians before any power-on attempt. Documents and photographs, which are especially vulnerable in a flood scenario, can be freeze-dried or air-dried depending on saturation level and material type.

Throughout the process, your inventory log is updated in real time. When your home is dried, repaired, and cleared for reoccupancy, we schedule a coordinated return and placement — every item goes back to the room it came from.
