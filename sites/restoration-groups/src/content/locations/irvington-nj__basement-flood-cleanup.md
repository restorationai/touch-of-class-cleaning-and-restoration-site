## Our Basement Flood Cleanup Process in Irvington

Every job starts with containment and assessment, not assumptions. Standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of pulling water from carpet, subfloor, and floor drains. Moisture mapping follows immediately — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has wicked into walls, under flooring, and behind built-ins before it shows any visible sign.

Because so many Irvington homes have plaster-over-lath construction rather than modern drywall, the drying phase requires careful management. Plaster is dense and releases absorbed moisture slowly; surface readings can look acceptable while the lath behind the wall remains saturated. Drying equipment — industrial dehumidifiers and axial air movers — is positioned to address wall cavities, not just open floor space, and readings are logged daily until the structure reaches target moisture levels consistent with the IICRC S500 standard.

If the flood source involved a drain backup or sewage component, affected materials are handled as a Category 3 loss: porous materials that cannot be fully sanitized are removed rather than dried in place. That's a frequent consideration in Irvington's older plumbing systems, where a single storm can overwhelm a cast-iron lateral and push sewage into the basement.

## Working with Insurance on Irvington Basement Flood Claims

Most sudden-discharge losses — a burst pipe, a failed water heater, an overflowing fixture — are covered under standard homeowners' policies. Flood damage from surface water intrusion is a separate category and typically requires a separate flood policy. That distinction matters in Irvington, where some losses involve both a plumbing failure and storm-driven water entering through window wells at the same time.

The Restoration Group documents losses thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected materials, scope of work, and equipment placement logs. That documentation supports the adjuster's review and helps avoid disputes over what was wet and what was dry at the time of service. We bill carriers directly and work with policyholders to navigate the claim process, including supplement requests when hidden damage is uncovered during drying.
