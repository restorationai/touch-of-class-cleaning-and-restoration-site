Irvington's residential fabric is dominated by early-1900s construction — prewar frame houses, attached rowhouses, and multifamily buildings that were never designed with today's understanding of moisture management. ## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Irvington

A mold inspection is not a visual scan and a handshake. The process starts with a structured walkthrough of the property — attic, basement, crawl spaces, bathrooms, and any area with a documented or suspected moisture history. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate elevated readings inside wall cavities and under flooring without destructive opening.

When sampling is warranted, we collect air samples from affected rooms and from an unaffected control area, typically outdoors or in an unrelated part of the building. Surface swab or tape-lift samples are taken from visible growth to identify genus and concentration. All samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory; results typically return within 24 to 72 hours and include a full spore count and species breakdown.

In Irvington's older multifamily stock, we pay particular attention to shared wall assemblies between units. Mold in one unit's bathroom wall can colonize the adjacent unit's framing without producing visible signs on either side — a detail that matters enormously when a landlord or property manager needs to understand the full scope before remediation begins.
