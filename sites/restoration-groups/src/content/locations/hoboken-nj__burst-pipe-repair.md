## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Hoboken

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is stopping active discharge. If the building's main shutoff is inaccessible — common in older Hoboken rowhouses where shutoffs are buried in finished basement ceilings — we coordinate with the property owner or building super to locate secondary isolation valves before extraction begins.

Once flow is controlled, we use thermal imaging cameras to trace where water has traveled inside walls, under flooring, and into adjacent units. In Hoboken's brick and plaster construction, water wicks laterally through mortar joints in ways that standard moisture meters miss — thermal imaging catches it. We then deploy truck-mounted extractors and desiccant dehumidifiers calibrated to the higher ambient humidity common in below-grade and waterfront units. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged until affected assemblies reach target values. As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our documentation is formatted to satisfy both insurance adjusters and NJ Division of Consumer Affairs requirements.
