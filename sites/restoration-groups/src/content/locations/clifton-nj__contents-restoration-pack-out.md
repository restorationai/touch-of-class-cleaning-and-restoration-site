## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Clifton

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves the property. Every piece is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating — a step that matters enormously when an insurance adjuster is reviewing the claim. Items are then packed using materials appropriate to their fragility and packed into labeled, sealed containers for transport.

At our climate-controlled facility, contents are assessed for the type of damage present. Smoke and soot residue from a kitchen fire behaves differently than flood sediment from a Passaic River overflow event — the cleaning chemistry, dwell times, and drying protocols differ accordingly. Soft goods such as upholstered furniture and area rugs go through a separate textile cleaning line. Electronics are evaluated by a qualified technician before any restoration attempt. Hard goods — wood furniture, cabinetry, decorative items — are cleaned, dried, and treated for any secondary mold risk that can develop when items have been wet for more than 24 to 48 hours.

Once your home has passed structural drying and any necessary remediation, contents are returned in the same documented order they left. Nothing is returned to a space that isn't ready to receive it.
