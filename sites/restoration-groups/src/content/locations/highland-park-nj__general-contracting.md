## Local Note: What Ida Left Behind in Finished Basements

One pattern we've seen repeatedly in Highland Park homes near the Raritan River corridor: homeowners who had finished basements inundated during the 2021 flooding had the visible damage remediated, but the original cast-iron cleanout and floor drain assemblies were never inspected after the event.
