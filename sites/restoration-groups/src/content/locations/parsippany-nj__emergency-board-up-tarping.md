## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Parsippany

Every job starts with a rapid damage assessment before a single sheet of plywood goes up. We photograph and document the full scope of the breach — not just the obvious opening, but adjacent soffit damage, compromised flashing, and any structural shifts that could affect how materials are anchored. This documentation matters for your insurance adjuster and for the integrity of the repair itself.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced poly tarps rated for sustained wind loads, secured with batten boards and screws driven into sound decking — not just weighted at the edges. On the older ranch and cape homes in the 07054 ZIP code, we often find that decking boards have dried and separated over decades, so we probe for solid anchor points rather than assuming the first board we hit will hold.

Board-up on windows and doors uses 5/8-inch exterior-grade plywood, cut to fit and secured with case-hardened screws rather than nails, which back out under repeated wind pressure. For historic properties in Mount Tabor, we take care to avoid fastening into original trim profiles that would complicate future restoration work.
