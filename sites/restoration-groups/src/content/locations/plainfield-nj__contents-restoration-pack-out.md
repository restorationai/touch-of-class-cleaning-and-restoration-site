## Why Plainfield's Housing Stock Complicates Contents Losses

The Van Wyck Brooks Historic District and the surrounding blocks of Sleepy Hollow and Netherwood contain some of the finest late-19th-century residential architecture in central New Jersey — and some of the most challenging conditions for contents recovery. Original plaster walls and horsehair-insulated cavities hold moisture far longer than modern drywall, which means belongings stored against those walls can absorb secondary moisture even after a loss appears contained. Cast-iron drain stacks common in pre-1940 homes are prone to slow seeps and sudden failures, and finished basements — a fixture in Plainfield's deeper-lot properties — concentrate furniture, electronics, and stored family items directly in the flood zone.

Fire losses in older homes add a different layer. Knob-and-tube-era wiring, balloon-frame construction, and original wood lathe allow smoke and soot to migrate through wall cavities and settle on contents in rooms that never saw flames. A kitchen fire in a Queen Anne on the West End can leave a fine protein residue on upholstered furniture two floors away.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Plainfield

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assessed — salvageable, restorable, or total loss — so your insurance documentation is complete from the start. Items that can be restored are carefully packed, labeled, and transported to our climate-controlled facility for cleaning, deodorization, and drying under controlled conditions away from the active loss site.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and non-porous items, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing soft contents, and dry-cleaning referrals for textiles that require it. Electronics are assessed separately — corrosion from floodwater is not always visible on the surface, and a device that powers on at the scene may fail within days. We document the condition of every item before and after treatment so the record is clear for your adjuster.

Once your home is dry and restored, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed — a process we coordinate around your schedule and the realities of getting a moving truck to Plainfield addresses, some of which sit on narrow streets in the historic districts.
