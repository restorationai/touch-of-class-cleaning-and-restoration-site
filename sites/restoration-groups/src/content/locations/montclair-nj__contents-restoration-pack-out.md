## Why Montclair Homes Generate Complex Contents Losses

Montclair's housing stock is one of the oldest and most architecturally varied in Essex County. The Estate Section's grand center-hall colonials and Victorians often contain original built-ins, plaster medallions, and period furnishings that simply cannot be treated the same way as flat-pack furniture from a newer suburb. Plaster walls — common throughout Upper Montclair and the Estate Section — absorb and hold moisture differently than modern drywall, which means contents stored against those walls can sustain hidden secondary damage long after the obvious wet zone is addressed.

The Yantacaw Brook and Third River tributaries that drain the Watchung ridge have a documented history of overwhelming storm sewers during heavy rain events. The flooding that struck Frog Hollow and the South End during Ida in 2021 showed how quickly Category 3 water — raw sewage mixed with stormwater — can migrate from a basement floor drain into adjacent rooms, contaminating soft goods, upholstered furniture, and paper records in a matter of minutes. That level of contamination changes the entire contents protocol: items that can be decontaminated must be separated immediately from items that cannot, and that triage has to happen on-site before anything is packed.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Montclair

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before a single box is packed. Every item is photographed, described, and assigned a condition code. This documentation serves two purposes: it gives you a complete record for your insurance claim, and it tells our technicians exactly what cleaning method each item requires before it leaves the property.

Pack-out follows a deliberate sequence. Fragile or high-value items — the kind of antique mirrors and oil paintings you find in Estate Section homes — are wrapped and crated separately from textiles and everyday goods. Contaminated items are sealed and transported in dedicated vehicles to prevent cross-contamination. Items that are salvageable go to our climate-controlled facility for ultrasonic cleaning, ozone treatment, thermal fogging, or dry-cleaning, depending on the material and the type of loss. Items that cannot be restored are documented for your adjuster before disposal.

Once the structure is dried and cleared, contents are returned in the same inventory order, with a delivery manifest so you can confirm every item accounted for.
