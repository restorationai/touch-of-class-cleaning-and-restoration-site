## Our Water Cleanup Process in Edison

Water damage restoration is not simply extraction and a few fans. The IICRC S500 standard — the framework our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) work follows — requires drying to be verified with moisture readings, not estimated by feel or appearance.

Here is what the process looks like on a typical Edison residential loss:

1. **Assessment and containment** — Technicians map the affected area with thermal imaging and moisture meters. In a finished basement, water behind drywall often reads dry at the surface while the framing and insulation behind it are saturated.
2. **Standing water removal** — Truck-mounted or portable extractors pull bulk water from carpet, pad, and hard flooring. The faster this step happens, the lower the likelihood of secondary mold colonization, which can begin within 24 to 48 hours in humid conditions.
3. **Controlled demolition if needed** — Wet drywall, saturated insulation, and swollen flooring that cannot be dried in place are removed. We document every material before and after for your insurance claim.
4. **Drying and dehumidification** — Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run in a calculated configuration based on the room's cubic footage and the materials present. Readings are logged daily.
5. **Final clearance** — Moisture levels in structural materials are confirmed at or below baseline before equipment comes out. We do not close a job because the air feels dry.
