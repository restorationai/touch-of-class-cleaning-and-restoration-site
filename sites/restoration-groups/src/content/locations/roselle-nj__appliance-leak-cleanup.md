## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Roselle

Every job starts with the same question: how far did the water actually travel? Appliance leaks are deceptive because the source is point-specific but the migration is not. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map the full wet footprint before removing a single baseboard. In Roselle's older homes, that often means tracing moisture through original tongue-and-groove subfloor, into wall cavities insulated with horsehair or early fiberglass batts, and down into basement ceiling assemblies.

Once we know the scope, extraction comes first — standing water, saturated carpet, and wet pad pulled and bagged. We then set a calculated drying system: low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers, axial air movers positioned to create directed airflow through cavities, and where needed, injectidry panels to dry wall cavities without full demolition. Readings are logged daily against the IICRC S500 standard so the drying record is defensible for your insurance claim. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — particleboard cabinet bases, swollen laminate flooring, wet insulation — are documented with photographs before removal.
