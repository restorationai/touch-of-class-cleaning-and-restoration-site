## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Hillside

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence is consistent: stabilize, document, clean, restore.

**Emergency board-up and stabilization** comes first. Broken windows, compromised roof sections, and open doorways need to be secured before any cleaning begins — both to protect the structure from weather and to preserve the scene for your insurance adjuster.

**Soot and smoke mapping** follows. Not all soot behaves the same way. Dry soot from fast-burning paper or wood brushes off surfaces differently than wet, oily soot from burning synthetic materials — the kind more common in kitchen fires involving modern appliances and cabinetry. We identify the soot type in each affected area before selecting cleaning chemistry, because using the wrong method can permanently set staining into plaster or masonry.

**Structural cleaning and odor elimination** address the surfaces you can see and the cavities you can't. In Hillside's older homes, that often means cleaning inside wall cavities and attic spaces, not just wiping down visible surfaces. Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation are used for odor control in spaces where direct surface cleaning isn't possible.

**Controlled demolition and reconstruction** close out the job when fire or water from suppression efforts has compromised structural materials. As an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we carry the work from demo through finished reconstruction under a single scope — no hand-off to a separate GC.
