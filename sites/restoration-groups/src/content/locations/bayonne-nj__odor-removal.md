## Our Deodorization Process in Bayonne

Effective odor removal starts with identifying the source, not masking the symptom. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate hidden wet material in walls and subfloors — because in Bayonne's older homes, the odor you smell in the living room often originates in a wall cavity two rooms away.

Once the source is confirmed and any contaminated material is removed or dried, we select the deodorization method based on what we're dealing with:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing solvent as a dry fog that penetrates the same porous surfaces smoke traveled through — effective for post-fire odor in plaster walls and wood framing.
- **Hydroxyl generation** uses UV-light-driven oxidation to neutralize odor molecules in occupied or semi-occupied spaces. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators are safe to run with occupants present, which matters in a multi-unit building where you can't vacate every tenant.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe odor loading — a sealed basement after a sewage backup, for example — where high-concentration oxidation is the fastest path to neutralization.
- **HEPA air scrubbing** runs continuously during and after treatment to capture particulates that carry odor compounds.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process follows the S520 and S500 standards for source removal before deodorization — industry protocol that prevents odor rebound weeks after treatment.
