## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Scotch Plains

When a call comes in from Scotch Plains, we move on it around the clock. The first priority on arrival is a room-by-room scope: documenting char depth, mapping smoke migration with a borescope where wall cavities are suspect, and identifying materials that can be cleaned versus those that need controlled demolition. Smoke odor is the piece homeowners underestimate most. Protein smoke from a kitchen fire and the heavy carbon soot from a structural fire require completely different chemistry. We use hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging calibrated to the specific smoke type, not a one-size approach. Porous materials — insulation, drywall, carpet padding — that have absorbed smoke beyond a cleanable threshold get removed and replaced rather than masked. For Scotch Plains homes with original plaster walls, we assess each surface individually; plaster can sometimes be cleaned and sealed where drywall would need full replacement, but it requires the right sequence of chemical sponging before any wet cleaning begins.
