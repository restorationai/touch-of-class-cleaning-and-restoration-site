## Why Montclair Properties See Flood Damage Differently

Montclair's housing stock is part of what makes flood losses here more complicated than a typical suburban water claim. The grand Tudors and center-hall colonials in the Estate Section, the Victorians near Watchung Plaza, and the older row-style homes in the South End were built in an era before modern waterproofing membranes and sump redundancy. Many have plaster-and-lath walls that absorb water slowly but release it even more slowly — a wall that reads dry on a surface pin meter can still be holding moisture deep in the substrate weeks after the event.

The topography compounds the problem. Montclair sits on the eastern slope of the Watchung ridge, and storm runoff from higher elevations funnels downhill fast. When a heavy system stalls over the area, that hillside grading overwhelms residential sump pumps in neighborhoods like Upper Montclair before homeowners realize anything is wrong. Cast-iron drain lines common in pre-1950 homes corrode and crack, and sewer backups during high-flow events push contaminated water into finished spaces — a Category 3 loss that requires a different remediation protocol than a clean-water pipe burst.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Montclair

Every flood job starts with a moisture mapping survey before a single piece of equipment is placed. In Montclair's older homes, that means probing plaster walls, checking beneath original hardwood floors, and inspecting the rim-joist cavities where water migrates invisibly after a basement flood. We photograph and document the full scope before extraction begins — partly because it's the right way to work, and partly because thorough documentation is what separates a smooth insurance claim from a disputed one.

Extraction comes next: truck-mounted units pull standing water from basements and ground-floor rooms, followed by targeted drying with commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers calibrated to the actual cubic footage of the space. Drying timelines in Montclair's plaster-wall homes routinely run longer than the industry average for drywall construction — plaster releases absorbed moisture gradually, and pulling equipment too early is one of the most common reasons a flood job ends in a mold callback weeks later. We monitor moisture readings daily and don't close out a drying phase until the numbers hit the IICRC S500 standard for the material type.

For sewer-backup losses — which were widespread after Ida and remain common during heavy rain events along the Yantacaw corridor — affected materials are treated as contaminated. That means antimicrobial treatment, controlled demolition of saturated porous materials, and air scrubbing before any reconstruction begins.

## Montclair Insurance Coordination

Flood and water damage claims in Montclair often involve two separate policies: standard homeowners coverage for sudden and accidental discharge (a burst pipe, a failed appliance) and a separate flood insurance policy — either through the NFIP or a private carrier — for ground-surface flooding events.
