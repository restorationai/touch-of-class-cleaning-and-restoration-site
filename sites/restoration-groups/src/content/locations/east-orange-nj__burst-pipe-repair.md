## Why East Orange Properties See Burst Pipe Damage So Often

East Orange's housing stock is one of the oldest in Essex County. The three-story walk-ups and two-family Victorians throughout ZIP codes 07017 and 07018 were built when galvanized iron was standard. That material has a functional lifespan of roughly 40–70 years. A lot of it is well past that now, and the corrosion happens from the inside out — a pipe can look intact on the outside right up until the moment it fails.

Freezing plays a role too. East Orange winters regularly push pipes in uninsulated exterior walls and unheated attic chases past their limit. A hard cold snap — the kind that follows a January thaw — creates pressure differentials that split already-weakened galvanized sections overnight. By morning, water has been running for six or more hours.

Multi-unit buildings compound every loss. A single burst riser in a six-unit building on a block near Doddtown or Presidential Estates can affect three or four apartments simultaneously, turning what would be a contained repair into a multi-unit displacement event. Landlords, tenants, and property managers all have different stakes, and the documentation requirements multiply accordingly.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in East Orange

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping active water flow — that means locating the shutoff, whether it's in the basement mechanical room or a street-level curb stop, and confirming the source before any drying equipment goes in. In older East Orange buildings, shutoffs are sometimes unmarked or shared across units, so we do a quick building survey before assuming control is established.

Once water is stopped, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then deploy commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers calibrated to the room volume. Plaster walls — common in pre-war East Orange buildings — hold moisture longer than modern drywall and require extended drying cycles monitored by daily moisture readings. We don't pull equipment early because a number looks close; we pull it when the readings confirm the structure is dry.

On the repair side, our NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor status means we can handle the pipe repair itself — not just the water damage cleanup — so you're not coordinating two separate contractors.
