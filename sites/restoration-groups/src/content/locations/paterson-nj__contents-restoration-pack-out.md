## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Paterson

The first thing we do on-site is slow the clock. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water event, so the priority is separating salvageable items from unsalvageable ones and getting the salvageable items out of the affected environment.

Every item is photographed and inventoried before it moves — this documentation matters for insurance claims and for returning belongings to the right rooms when restoration is complete. Items are packed using industry-standard materials and transported to a climate-controlled facility where the actual restoration work happens: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone and hydroxyl treatment for odor, specialized drying chambers for documents and soft goods, and hand-cleaning for artwork and fragile items.

For Paterson's older multi-family buildings, we coordinate access carefully. Narrow stairwells in brick walkups require smaller equipment and more hands. We communicate with building owners and, where relevant, other tenants whose units may be affected by staging in common areas.

Paterson's converted mill buildings present a specific challenge that doesn't come up in newer construction: sprinkler discharges in these spaces often involve water that has been sitting in aging iron pipes for extended periods, which means it arrives at the contents already discolored and potentially contaminated.
