## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Piscataway

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source if it hasn't been shut off already — we carry valve keys and know how to isolate supply lines quickly without waiting for a plumber. From there, the process follows a defined sequence calibrated to what we actually find on-site:

**Moisture mapping first.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has traveled behind baseboards, under flooring, and into wall cavities. In Piscataway's older homes, water from a refrigerator leak on a main floor can wick down through subfloor gaps into a finished basement ceiling within hours.

**Targeted extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard floors and carpet. For appliance leaks under cabinetry — common with dishwashers and refrigerators — we use specialty tools designed to reach confined spaces without full cabinet removal when possible.

**Structural drying.** Refrigerant-based dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed randomly. In finished basements common to this area, we often need to inject air into wall cavities to dry framing that can't be reached from the surface.

**Daily monitoring.** We return each day to log moisture readings and adjust equipment placement. Drying timelines for appliance leaks in finished spaces typically run three to five days, though older plaster-and-lath walls — still present in some Piscataway homes — release moisture more slowly and can extend that window.

**Documentation for insurance.** Every moisture reading, photo, and equipment placement log is compiled into a structured claim file. We work directly with most major carriers and can bill them directly in most cases.
