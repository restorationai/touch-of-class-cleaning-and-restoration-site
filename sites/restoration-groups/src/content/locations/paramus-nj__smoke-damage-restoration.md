## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Paramus

When we arrive at a Paramus property, the first step is containment and assessment — not cleaning. We use thermal imaging and air quality monitoring to map where smoke has traveled, including inside wall cavities and below subfloors. That data drives the scope of work, so we're not guessing at what needs to come out.

From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

- **Structural surface cleaning**: HEPA-vacuuming and dry-chemical sponging of ceilings, walls, and exposed framing before any wet cleaning begins. Wet cleaning smoke-saturated plaster before dry-removing loose soot drives particles deeper into the substrate.
- **HVAC and duct decontamination**: Full duct cleaning and fogging, critical in homes with original forced-air systems.
- **Odor neutralization**: Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to reach the same porous surfaces smoke penetrated — inside wall cavities, under flooring, inside cabinetry.
- **Content pack-out and cleaning**: Furniture, clothing, and personal items are inventoried, transported, and cleaned off-site when in-place cleaning isn't sufficient.
- **Final air quality verification**: Clearance testing before the job is closed.

All drying and decontamination work is performed to IICRC standards by our certified team (IICRC Certified Firm #210213).
