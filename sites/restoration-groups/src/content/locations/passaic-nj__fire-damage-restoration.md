## Why Passaic Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Fire Spread

Passaic is one of New Jersey's most densely built small cities, and that density shapes how fire damage behaves here. The majority of the housing stock — particularly in the Third Ward and Fourth Ward — dates to the 1910s through 1940s. Those buildings were constructed with balloon framing, a technique where wall cavities run uninterrupted from the basement sill plate to the roof rafters. In a modern platform-framed house, fire tends to stay contained to one floor. In a balloon-framed walkup off Main Avenue or a rowhouse near Third Ward Park, fire and superheated smoke can travel vertically through the wall cavity and reach the attic before the first engine company arrives.

Knob-and-tube wiring remnants — still present in some of these older structures — are a known ignition risk, particularly when they've been buried under added insulation over the decades. Overcrowded rental units with overtaxed electrical panels compound the problem. And because so many Passaic properties are multi-family, a single fire event can displace multiple households and trigger simultaneous insurance claims across different carriers.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Passaic

We arrive with a full assessment before any work begins. That means moisture mapping alongside the fire inspection — because Passaic firefighting operations use significant water volume, and water intrusion in a structure that's already compromised by heat is a fast path to secondary mold damage within 48 to 72 hours.

From there, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Board-up and structural stabilization** — securing windows, doors, and any compromised load-bearing elements before the site is left unattended.
- **Debris removal and category sorting** — separating salvageable contents from unsalvageable materials, with documentation for your insurance adjuster.
- **Smoke and soot removal** — thermal fogging, dry-ice blasting on brick and masonry surfaces, and HEPA vacuuming of ductwork and wall cavities where smoke has migrated.
- **Deodorization** — hydroxyl generation for occupied adjacent units in multi-family buildings, where ozone treatment isn't safe to use.
- **Structural drying** — addressing firefighting water before reconstruction begins.
- **Reconstruction** — framing, drywall, insulation, and finish work completed under our NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor license.
