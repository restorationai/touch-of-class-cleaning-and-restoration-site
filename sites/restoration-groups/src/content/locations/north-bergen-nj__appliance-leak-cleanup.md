## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in North Bergen

The first thing we do on-site is identify the source and confirm it's isolated — a shut-off valve that's corroded open is common in older North Bergen plumbing, and we carry portable shutoff tools for exactly that scenario. Once the water stops moving, we map the affected area with a thermal imaging camera and calibrated moisture meters, tracing the migration path through subfloor, walls, and ceiling assemblies.

Extraction comes next. In the multi-family homes typical of this area, that often means working in tight stairwells and low-clearance basement spaces with truck-mount or portable extraction units. We set drying equipment — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — positioned to create a drying chamber around the wet materials rather than just blowing air at the surface.

We monitor moisture readings daily and adjust equipment placement until structural materials reach their target dry standard per the IICRC S500. In older plaster-and-lath walls, which still appear in many Woodcliff-area homes, that process takes longer than it would in modern drywall construction — we build that timeline into our scope so nothing gets closed up prematurely.
