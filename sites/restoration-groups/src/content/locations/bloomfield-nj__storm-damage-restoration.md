## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Bloomfield

Every storm job starts with a documented scope — not a verbal estimate. When we arrive, we photograph and measure all affected areas before touching anything, because insurance adjusters for Essex County losses want a clear pre-mitigation record. From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

1. **Emergency board-up and tarping** — roof penetrations, broken windows, and compromised doors are secured first to stop active water intrusion.
2. **Structural water extraction** — standing water in basements and crawl spaces is removed with truck-mounted and portable extraction units. In Bloomfield's older finished basements, that often means pulling saturated carpet and pad before the subfloor can be assessed.
3. **Moisture mapping** — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters identify water that has wicked into plaster walls, subfloors, and wall cavities. In pre-1950 construction, water travels farther laterally than it does in modern drywall assemblies.
4. **Drying and dehumidification** — industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run in a closed system until readings return to baseline. We document daily readings for the insurance file.
5. **Debris removal and reconstruction scope** — fallen tree limbs, damaged roofing materials, and compromised structural members are catalogued for the rebuild estimate.
