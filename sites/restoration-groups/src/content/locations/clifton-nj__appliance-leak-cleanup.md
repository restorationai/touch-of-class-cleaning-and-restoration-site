## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Clifton

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active flow — confirming the supply valve is fully closed and that water hasn't migrated to an adjacent unit in a two-family, which is a common scenario in the older rental stock near Delawanna and along the Route 3 corridor. From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Moisture mapping** using thermal imaging and pin-type meters to trace exactly how far water traveled under flooring, inside wall cavities, and into subfloor assemblies — not just where it's visibly wet.
- **Extraction** of standing water and saturated materials using truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access.
- **Controlled demolition** of saturated drywall, toe-kick panels, or flooring sections when readings indicate material cannot be dried in place — we document every cut for your insurance claim.
- **Structural drying** with calibrated air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers, positioned to create the airflow patterns that dried concrete and masonry foundations actually need, not just the open floor space.
- **Daily monitoring** with moisture logs until all readings return to the regional equilibrium moisture content for northern New Jersey.

For water heater leak cleanup specifically, we also assess the mechanical room for secondary damage to adjacent framing and insulation, which in Clifton's basement-heavy homes often means a finished rec room wall is on the other side.
