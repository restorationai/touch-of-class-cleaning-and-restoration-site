## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Rahway

Every burst pipe job starts with the same two priorities: stop the water and document the damage before anything dries or gets moved. Our technicians locate the source, confirm the supply is isolated, and photograph the affected area in detail — this documentation is what your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim accurately.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from flooring, carpet, and wall cavities. In Rahway's older homes, that often means working around original hardwood floors and plaster walls, both of which hold moisture differently than modern materials.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to dry the structure from the inside out. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented, not estimated.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — any materials that were wet for more than 24–48 hours get treated to inhibit secondary microbial growth. Rahway's humid summers make this step non-negotiable.
- **Repair coordination** — once the structure is dry and cleared, we coordinate or perform the physical pipe repair and any necessary drywall, flooring, or trim work, so you're dealing with one contractor rather than three.
