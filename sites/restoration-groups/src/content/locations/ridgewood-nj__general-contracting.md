## Why Ridgewood's Older Homes Require a Different Renovation Approach

A large share of the homes in 07450 were constructed between 1900 and 1950, and that era of construction introduces complications that newer-build markets simply don't face. Plaster walls, while durable, require careful removal sequencing — disturb them incorrectly and you're pulling down horsehair substrate that can harbor decades of settled dust and, in pre-1978 homes, lead-based paint. Load-bearing walls in these Tudors and colonials rarely follow the patterns a modern framer would expect; structural surprises are common once ceilings open up.

The mature tree canopy that makes neighborhoods like the Old Country Club and Willard so appealing also means root intrusion into older clay sewer laterals is a recurring discovery mid-renovation.
