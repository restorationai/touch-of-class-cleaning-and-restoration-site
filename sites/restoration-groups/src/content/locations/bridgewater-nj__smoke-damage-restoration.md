## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Bridgewater

When we arrive at a Bridgewater property, the first thing we do is map the smoke migration path — not just the room where the fire occurred. We use thermal imaging to locate soot deposits inside wall cavities and above drop ceilings, and we test HVAC ductwork for particulate contamination before we ever turn the system back on. Dry soot from fast-flaming fires (common in kitchen grease fires and electrical fires) is vacuumed with HEPA equipment before any wet cleaning begins — wiping dry soot spreads it. Wet soot from slow, smoldering fires — the kind that often start in upholstered furniture or overloaded electrical panels in older homes — requires alkaline cleaning agents to neutralize the acidic residue.
