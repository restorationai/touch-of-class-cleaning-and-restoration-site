## Why East Orange Basements Flood — and Why It's Complicated Here

East Orange sits in a low-lying section of Essex County where the Second River and nearby tributaries historically drain slowly after heavy storms. Tropical Storm Ida in 2021 made that visible: low ground throughout the city backed up fast, and basement mechanical rooms that had never flooded before took on water. That's not a one-time anomaly — it reflects the underlying drainage reality for properties in ZIP codes 07017 and 07018, where combined sewer systems can surcharge during intense rainfall and send water back up through floor drains before a homeowner has any warning.

The housing stock compounds the risk. Galvanized steel water supply pipes common in pre-1940 buildings corrode from the inside out, narrowing over decades until a joint finally fails. Cast-iron drain stacks crack under the weight of a century of use. Flat roofs on brick three-families — common in the Elmwood and Doddtown sections — pond water and push it down through parapet walls into the top floor and eventually the basement sump pit. Each of these failure modes produces a different contamination profile, and the cleanup approach has to match.

## Our Basement Flood Cleanup Process in East Orange

The first step on any basement call is identifying the water source and classifying it — clean supply-line water, gray water from a washing machine or drain backup, or Category 3 sewage. That classification drives every decision that follows: what PPE the crew wears, which materials can be dried in place versus removed, and what disinfection protocol applies.

Once the source is controlled, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then use thermal imaging and penetrating moisture meters to map exactly where water has traveled inside block foundation walls, concrete floors, and wood framing. In East Orange's older buildings, water frequently wicks up through the mortar joints of brick foundation walls and into the sill plate before it shows on the surface — missing that hidden moisture is how mold colonies get a foothold within 48 to 72 hours.

Structural drying uses commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create a controlled drying chamber. Readings are logged daily against the IICRC S500 standard until affected assemblies reach documented dry goal. Nothing is closed in until the numbers confirm it.

## Insurance Coordination for East Orange Flood Claims

Most basement flood losses in East Orange involve at least one coverage question: Is this a sewer backup claim, a sudden and accidental discharge, or a flood exclusion situation? The answer matters because sewer backup coverage is a separate rider on most homeowner policies, and many older multi-family buildings in East Orange carry commercial landlord policies with different deductible structures.
