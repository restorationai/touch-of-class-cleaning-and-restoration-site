## Our Water Cleanup Process in Clifton

Every water cleanup starts with finding all the water — not just the visible puddle. In Clifton's older homes, that means using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to check inside plaster walls, under hardwood subfloors, and behind tile backer in bathrooms. Water that reads dry at the surface can leave lath and framing saturated for weeks if drying equipment is placed based on what's visible rather than what the instruments show.

Once the full moisture map is established, the process moves through these stages:

- **Extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete before evaporation equipment is deployed.
- **Controlled drying**: Industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture readings, not a standard room count. Clifton's humid continental climate means ambient humidity during spring and fall storms can work against the drying process if equipment placement isn't calibrated to outdoor conditions.
- **Daily monitoring**: Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented — data that matters when an insurance adjuster reviews the claim file.
- **Antimicrobial treatment**: Applied to affected structural cavities where Category 2 or Category 3 water is involved, which is common after any event connected to backed-up drains or floodwater from the Passaic watershed.
