## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process

A thorough mold assessment isn't a visual walk-through with a flashlight. The process starts with a detailed moisture survey using calibrated thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to map wet building materials that aren't yet showing visible growth. In Scotch Plains' older homes, we pay particular attention to finished basement walls, rim-joist cavities, and the areas around aging supply-line penetrations — these are the locations where slow leaks hide longest.

Once the moisture map is complete, we collect air samples from affected rooms and an outdoor control sample, then send everything to an accredited third-party laboratory. The lab report identifies mold species, spore concentrations, and how indoor counts compare to outdoor baseline levels — the data your doctor, your insurance adjuster, or a potential home buyer actually needs. If surface sampling is warranted (for instance, to confirm whether a stain on a basement wall is mold or efflorescence), we collect tape-lift or swab samples from specific locations and include those in the lab submission. You receive a written report with findings, photographs, and a clear explanation of what the numbers mean.

The Restoration Group is an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), and all inspections are conducted in accordance with current IICRC S520 guidelines for mold assessment.
