## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Highland Park

When we arrive at a flooded basement in the 08904 ZIP code, the first task is source control — stopping active water intrusion before extraction begins. That means shutting supply lines, confirming the sump pit is clear, and assessing whether any sewage backflow is present, which changes both the extraction equipment and the disposal protocol.

Once the source is controlled, truck-mounted extractors pull standing water from the floor. In Highland Park's older finished basements, that often means pulling saturated carpet and pad immediately — they hold water like a sponge and prevent the concrete slab beneath from drying. Drywall that has wicked water above the flood line gets flood-cut, typically 12 inches above the visible stain, to expose the wall cavity and allow air movement around the framing.

Structured drying follows: industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers, positioned to create a drying vortex through the space.
