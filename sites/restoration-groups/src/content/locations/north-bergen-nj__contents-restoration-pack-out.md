## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in North Bergen

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything moves. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition code — this documentation is the foundation of any insurance claim and protects you if there's a dispute about what was present before the loss.

From there, salvageable contents are carefully packed, labeled, and transported to a climate-controlled facility where they undergo cleaning and restoration appropriate to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and electronics, dry cleaning and ozone treatment for textiles and soft contents, and specialized document drying for paper records. Items that cannot be restored are documented for replacement value rather than quietly discarded.

Once the structure itself has been dried and cleared — typically after moisture readings confirm the building envelope is within acceptable limits per the IICRC S500 standard — contents are returned and placed according to the original inventory. In North Bergen's multi-family buildings, we coordinate delivery timing carefully to avoid conflicts with other tenants and narrow stair access.
