## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Roselle

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing intrusion — tarping damaged roof sections, boarding compromised openings, and isolating any active water source. On properties near Warinanco Park where basement flooding is the primary loss, we deploy truck-mounted extraction equipment to pull standing water before it migrates into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies.

Once the structure is stabilized, we conduct a full moisture mapping survey using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. In Roselle's older homes, this step is especially important: plaster-and-lath walls common in pre-1950 construction hold moisture differently than modern drywall — they don't wick water as aggressively, but they release it slowly and unevenly, which means drying timelines run longer and require more precisely placed drying equipment. We document every affected material, photograph the damage for your insurance carrier, and build a scope of work before any demolition begins. For roof and exterior damage — fallen trees, displaced flashing, damaged siding — we coordinate temporary weatherproofing immediately and schedule permanent repairs as a second phase once the interior is stabilized and the insurance adjuster has completed their inspection.
