## Why East Orange's Building Stock Complicates Fire Damage

Most of East Orange's residential fabric was built between 1905 and 1945, and that matters the moment a fire investigator clears a scene. Balloon-frame construction — common in the Victorians and two-families scattered through Elmwood and Ampere — allows superheated gases and smoke to travel vertically inside wall cavities without any fire blocking to slow them down. What looks like a contained kitchen fire on the second floor can deposit soot protein residue inside third-floor wall voids and attic spaces that were never touched by flames.

Older galvanized plumbing and cast-iron stacks in these buildings also mean that when firefighters apply water suppression, you're not just dealing with fire damage — you're dealing with secondary water intrusion through original plaster ceilings and subfloors that have absorbed moisture for decades. That combination of smoke migration and water saturation inside century-old assemblies requires a restoration approach calibrated to the building, not a generic checklist.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in East Orange

After the fire department releases the property, the clock starts on smoke odor and soot damage. Protein-based smoke from kitchen fires and acrid chemical smoke from synthetic materials behave differently, and both require targeted treatment before general cleaning begins.

Our process on East Orange properties typically follows this sequence:

1. **Structural assessment and safety walk** — We identify compromised load-bearing elements, document the loss with photographs and moisture readings, and flag any areas requiring emergency board-up or tarping before work begins.
2. **Content pack-out and inventory** — Salvageable contents are catalogued, packed, and transported to our facility for cleaning and deodorization. This protects belongings from secondary soot damage during structural work.
3. **Soot and smoke residue removal** — Dry sponge cleaning, HEPA vacuuming, and chemical sponging are matched to surface type. Original plaster walls common in 07017 and 07018 ZIP code properties require gentler abrasion than modern drywall to avoid surface damage.
4. **Deodorization** — Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach the same cavities smoke traveled through. In balloon-frame structures, we may open wall sections to treat interior cavities directly.
5. **Structural drying and reconstruction prep** — Firefighting water is extracted, affected assemblies are dried to IICRC S500 standard, and the scope of structural repairs is documented for your insurance carrier.
