## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Englewood

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source — shutting the supply valve, pulling the appliance away from the wall, and doing a quick visual inspection to understand how far water has traveled. In East Hill estates with original hardwood and plaster, that travel path is often longer than it looks because plaster absorbs moisture slowly and releases it even more slowly. We use thermal imaging cameras and non-invasive moisture meters to map the actual wet zone, not just the visible stain.

Extraction comes next. For washing machine floods and water heater failures, we deploy truck-mounted or portable extraction units to pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and subfloor assemblies. In finished basements — common throughout Englewood's single-family stock — we assess whether flooring needs to come up to dry the subfloor beneath, or whether directed drying with desiccant dehumidifiers and axial air movers can achieve the same result without demolition.

Drying typically runs three to five days, monitored with daily moisture readings logged against the IICRC S500 standard. We don't call a job dry because it looks dry — we call it dry when the numbers confirm it. Documentation is captured throughout: photos, moisture logs, and a written scope, all formatted for insurance submission.
