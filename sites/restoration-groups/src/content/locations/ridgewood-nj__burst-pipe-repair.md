## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Ridgewood

The first priority on every pipe-burst call is stopping the water. Our technicians locate and isolate the affected line — working with your home's main shutoff or, when necessary, coordinating with the property's utility service — before any cleanup begins. Once the source is controlled, we conduct a full moisture survey using thermal imaging and non-invasive moisture meters to map exactly how far water has traveled inside walls, under flooring, and into subfloor assemblies.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from finished basement floors, carpeting, and hardwood. In Ridgewood's older homes, we pay particular attention to plaster-and-lath wall assemblies, which absorb and hold moisture differently than modern drywall — water migrates laterally through plaster much more readily, meaning the wet zone is often wider than it appears on the surface.

Structural drying follows IICRC S500 standards: commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, and daily moisture monitoring until all readings return to baseline. We document every reading with time-stamped data logs, which form the evidentiary record your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim. Pipe repair or coordination with a licensed plumber is handled concurrently so the structure is dry and the line is restored before we demobilize.
