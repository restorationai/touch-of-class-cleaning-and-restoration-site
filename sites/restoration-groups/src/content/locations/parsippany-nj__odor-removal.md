## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Parsippany

Effective odor removal isn't a single step. After an initial walkthrough to identify odor sources and affected materials, the process typically moves through several phases:

**Source removal first.** No deodorization technology works well if the source material is still present. Charred debris, saturated drywall, or contaminated insulation gets removed before treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging** uses a petroleum- or water-based deodorizing solution heated into a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke or odor-causing gases traveled — into wall voids, under flooring, into HVAC ductwork. It's particularly effective in Parsippany's older homes where smoke has followed air channels through balloon-frame or platform-frame construction into attic spaces.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** generates hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing volatile organic compounds (VOCs) at the molecular level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators can run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters for commercial clients in the Waterview corridor who need to maintain partial operations.

**Ozone treatment**, when appropriate, is used in unoccupied spaces for heavy smoke or biological odors. The space must be fully vacated during treatment and properly ventilated before re-occupancy.

Final verification includes air quality checks and a walkthrough with the property owner before the job is closed out.
