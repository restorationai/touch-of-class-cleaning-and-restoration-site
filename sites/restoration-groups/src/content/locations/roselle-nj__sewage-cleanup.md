## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Roselle

Every sewage loss is a Category 3 (black water) event by IICRC classification, which means the work follows a defined sequence — not a judgment call. Here is how the process runs on a typical Roselle residential job:

**1. Containment and safety assessment.** We identify the source, confirm the line is cleared or capped, and establish containment barriers so contaminated air and particulates don't migrate into living areas above the basement.

**2. Extraction and solid waste removal.** Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing water. Solid waste is bagged and disposed of in accordance with New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection guidelines for biohazardous material.

**3. Controlled demolition.** In Roselle's older homes, finished basements often have paneled walls over masonry block and drop ceilings over original framing. We remove only what is contaminated — but we do not leave porous material that tested positive for sewage contact in place. Drywall, insulation, and carpet that absorbed Category 3 water are bagged and removed.

**4. Antimicrobial treatment.** Structural surfaces — concrete slab, block walls, framing — are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. We do not skip this step on older masonry, which is porous enough to harbor bacteria even after it looks dry.

**5. Drying and documentation.** Commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings return to baseline. Every step is photographed and documented for your insurance carrier.
