## Why Avenel Properties See Water Damage Issues

Avenel sits within Woodbridge Township on low, flat ground, and the clay-heavy soil common to this part of Middlesex County doesn't drain quickly after a hard rain. That means storm water can sit against foundation walls for hours instead of soaking away, pushing moisture through hairline cracks into finished basements. Add in decades-old cast iron and clay sewer laterals under many of the township's older blocks, and you get two common failure points: hydrostatic pressure seeping through block walls, and slow-building backups from roots or sediment in aging lateral lines. Both show up the same way to a homeowner, a damp smell and a soft spot in the carpet, but they call for different mitigation approaches.
