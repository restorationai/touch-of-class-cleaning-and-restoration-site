## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Piscataway

Effective deodorization is not a single step — it's a sequence matched to the odor source, the affected materials, and the building's ventilation characteristics.

**Source removal first.** No deodorization method works if the contaminated material is still present. We remove charred debris, saturated insulation, or mold-colonized drywall before deploying any odor-control equipment. Masking an active source with ozone or hydroxyl just delays the problem.

**Thermal fogging for smoke and fire losses.** A fogging agent is heated into a dry vapor that penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled — wall cavities, subfloor gaps, HVAC plenums. This is particularly effective in Piscataway's older plaster-wall homes, where smoke has had time to migrate into structural voids before the fire was suppressed.

**Hydroxyl generation for occupied or sensitive environments.** Hydroxyl generators produce UV-catalyzed radicals that oxidize odor molecules without requiring occupants or pets to vacate. We use hydroxyl treatment in Society Hill townhome and condo units where HOA rules or shared-wall construction make ozone impractical, and in Rutgers-area student housing where rapid reoccupancy is a priority.

**Ozone shock treatment for severe, unoccupied spaces.** High-concentration ozone is the most aggressive option for catastrophic smoke damage or long-term mold odor in vacant properties. The space must remain unoccupied during treatment and ventilated thoroughly afterward — we manage that protocol and confirm safe re-entry levels before handing the space back.

**HVAC deodorization.** Duct systems redistribute odors throughout a structure every time the system cycles. We treat supply and return plenums and, where ductwork has absorbed contamination, coordinate duct cleaning before sealing the job.
