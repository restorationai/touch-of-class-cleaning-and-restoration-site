## Why Montclair Properties Are Especially Prone to Persistent Odors

Montclair's housing stock is one of the oldest and most architecturally varied in Essex County. The Estate Section's grand colonials and the Victorian doubles near Watchung Plaza were built when building materials were dense and porous — plaster over wood lath, solid-wood millwork, and brick chimneys that draw smoke deep into wall cavities. Those same qualities that make these homes beautiful make them odor sponges.

The Third River (Yantacaw) and its tributaries run through the lower elevations of town, and the flooding that followed Tropical Storm Ida in 2021 left a lasting lesson: sewer backups in Frog Hollow and the South End pushed raw sewage into finished basements, and the resulting odor — a combination of hydrogen sulfide, mercaptans, and biological off-gassing — penetrated subfloor assemblies that had been in place for 80 or more years. In older homes with minimal vapor barriers, that odor migrates upward into living spaces quickly.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Montclair

Every job begins with a source assessment. Odor treatment applied before the source is fully identified or removed is temporary at best. Our IICRC Certified Firm technicians (Firm #210213) trace odor back to its origin — whether that is charred framing behind a plaster wall in Upper Montclair, a failed cast-iron drain beneath a South End basement slab, or smoke residue drawn into an HVAC system serving a large Estate Section home.

Once the source is addressed, we select the appropriate deodorization method based on the structure and the odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** — a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing solvent is heated into a fine fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke or sewer gas traveled, pairing with odor molecules on contact. Particularly effective in the tight wall cavities of pre-war construction.
- **Hydroxyl generation** — UV-light-driven hydroxyl radicals break down odor-causing compounds without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. Useful in occupied commercial spaces along the Bloomfield Avenue corridor and in multi-family buildings where tenant displacement is a concern.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is introduced into a sealed, unoccupied space to oxidize odor compounds. Highly effective for smoke odor in larger Victorian-era rooms with high ceilings and complex trim profiles, but requires full evacuation during treatment.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and activated carbon filtration** — used in tandem with the above methods to capture particulate odor carriers and VOCs during and after treatment.

After treatment, we conduct a clearance check using calibrated air sampling before closing out the job.

## Local Note: Plaster Walls and the Odor Problem Nobody Warns You About

Montclair's pre-1950 homes — and there are thousands of them, from the Victorian doubles near Watchung Plaza to the center-hall colonials in the Estate Section — have plaster walls that behave very differently from modern drywall when exposed to smoke or sewer gas. Plaster is alkaline and highly porous, and it does not just hold odor on its surface; it absorbs odor-laden moisture into its full depth. Painting over smoke-affected plaster without proper chemical neutralization is a common mistake: the odor bleeds through the new paint within weeks. We treat plaster surfaces with an encapsulant-grade odor counteractant before any repainting is done, and we communicate that step clearly to homeowners and their contractors so the sequence is not skipped.
