## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Maplewood

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive at a property in the 07040 ZIP code, the first priority is a rapid structural and safety assessment — checking for compromised load-bearing elements from fallen trees, exposed electrical, and gas line integrity before anyone moves equipment inside. From there, the process follows a documented path:

**Emergency stabilization** — boarding windows, tarping roof penetrations, and deploying temporary barriers to stop active water intrusion while permanent repairs are scoped.

**Water extraction and moisture mapping** — standing water is extracted with truck-mounted units, then thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters map the full extent of saturation.
