## Our Mold Remediation Process in Paterson

Every job starts with a thorough assessment — not a visual scan, but moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin meters to locate hidden saturation behind plaster, inside stud bays, and under subfloors. In Paterson's older buildings, that step is non-negotiable: plaster walls absorb and hold water differently than modern drywall, and the moisture readings that would signal "dry" in a newer home often understate actual saturation in a 100-year-old brick structure.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers to prevent spores from migrating to clean areas of the building. This matters especially in multi-family walkups where a mold loss in one unit can affect adjacent units through shared wall cavities and pipe chases.

Remediation follows EPA and IICRC guidelines: controlled removal of contaminated materials, HEPA vacuuming of structural surfaces, application of EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, and documentation at every stage. As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process is calibrated to the S520 standard for mold remediation — not a general cleaning protocol. After remediation, we recommend independent post-clearance air testing before any reconstruction begins, which is increasingly required by Passaic County landlord-tenant proceedings when a tenant has filed a complaint.
