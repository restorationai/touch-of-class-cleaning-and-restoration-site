## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Highland Park

The first step on-site is a room-by-room contents inventory before anything moves. Every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a restoration or disposal recommendation. This documentation matters enormously for insurance claims and gives homeowners a clear record of what existed before the loss.

Items that can be restored leave the property in sealed, labeled boxes and crates — a process called pack-out — and go to a climate-controlled facility for cleaning, drying, and deodorization. Upholstered furniture gets ultrasonic or ozone treatment depending on the contamination type. Documents and photographs go through freeze-drying or air-drying protocols depending on how wet they are and how long they've been wet. Electronics are evaluated by a technician before any power is applied.

Items that stay on-site are moved to unaffected rooms or covered and protected while structural drying proceeds. Once the structure is cleared, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed according to the homeowner's direction. Nothing gets lost in a staging area.
