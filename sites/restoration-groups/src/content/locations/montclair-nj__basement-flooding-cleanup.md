## Why Montclair Properties Flood More Than Neighboring Towns

Montclair sits on the eastern slope of the Watchung ridge, and that hillside grade is the root cause of most basement flooding calls in town. When a significant storm rolls through — the kind Ida delivered in September 2021 — stormwater accelerates downhill and overwhelms sump systems in minutes, particularly in lower-lying pockets like Frog Hollow and the South End where the Yantacaw Creek tributaries run close to the surface. Cast iron corrodes from the inside out; a line can look intact from the exterior while flaking away internally, until one cold snap or heavy use causes a catastrophic failure. Finished basements — home theaters, in-law suites, home offices — mean that when water enters, it's not just a concrete floor at stake. It's framing, drywall, carpet, and personal property.

## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Montclair

The first priority on any flooded basement call is source control. If the water is still entering — whether from a failed sump, a cracked foundation wall, or a backed-up floor drain — we identify and isolate the source before extraction begins. Pumping a basement that's still taking on water is a losing effort.

Once inflow is stopped, truck-mounted extractors pull standing water fast. For finished spaces, that means careful attention to carpet seams, wall base, and any built-in cabinetry where water wicks upward invisibly. We use thermal imaging cameras to map moisture migration behind plaster and drywall — critical in Montclair's older homes, where plaster walls can absorb and hold water for days without showing surface staining.

Drying follows a structured protocol aligned with IICRC S500 standards. Refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers are placed based on the cubic footage of the affected space, and air movers are positioned to create a drying vortex rather than simply blowing air around. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so you have a documented drying record — something most insurance carriers require before they'll approve a rebuild estimate.

If the flooding involved sewage backup — common in the South End and Frog Hollow during surcharge events — the cleanup protocol shifts to Category 3 water remediation. That means full PPE, antimicrobial treatment of all affected surfaces, and disposal of porous materials that cannot be safely restored.

## Local Note: Plaster Walls and Hidden Moisture in Montclair's Older Homes

Here's something that surprises homeowners in Montclair's Estate Section and older Upper Montclair neighborhoods: plaster walls dry significantly more slowly than modern drywall, and they do it quietly. A plaster wall can read damp on a moisture meter for five to seven days after the visible water is gone, because the lime-and-sand matrix releases moisture gradually rather than all at once. We account for this by extending the drying phase and taking daily readings rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.
