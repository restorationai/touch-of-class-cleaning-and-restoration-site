## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Ridgewood

Every fire loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive at a Ridgewood property, the first priority is a structural safety assessment — confirming the building is safe to enter and identifying any compromised load-bearing elements, especially in older homes where fire may have weakened original timber joists or ridge beams. We document everything photographically before touching a surface, which matters both for your insurance claim and for any permit filings required by Bergen County.

From there, the work moves through board-up and emergency tarping to secure the structure, followed by controlled debris removal and soot mapping. Smoke residue behaves differently depending on what burned: protein smoke from a kitchen fire leaves a nearly invisible but intensely odorous film on every surface, while synthetic materials produce a thick, oily soot that bonds aggressively to porous materials like plaster and brick. We identify the smoke type before selecting cleaning agents and techniques — dry chemical sponging for dry soot, alkaline cleaners for protein residues — rather than applying a single method to every surface. Odor neutralization using thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation follows structural cleaning, targeting the deep-set smoke that standard surface wiping never reaches. Reconstruction is coordinated in-house, so there's no gap between remediation and rebuild.
