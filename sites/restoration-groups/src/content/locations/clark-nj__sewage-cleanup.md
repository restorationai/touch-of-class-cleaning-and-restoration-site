## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Clark

Sewage is classified as Category 3 — "black water" — under the IICRC S500 standard, meaning no affected material is treated as salvageable without documented sanitization. The process follows that framework from the first minute on-site.

The crew arrives with personal protective equipment, establishes a containment perimeter, and stops the source if it hasn't already been addressed. Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted units capable of handling solids; this is not a shop-vac job. Once the bulk liquid is removed, affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, wood subfloor — are assessed. In Clark's finished basements, that often means removing the lower 12 to 24 inches of drywall to expose the framing and confirm contamination hasn't wicked above the waterline.

After demo, every hard surface is treated with an EPA-registered disinfectant rated for Category 3 contamination. Dwell time is documented. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously to capture aerosolized particulates. Moisture readings are logged at each stage so the drying record is defensible to your insurance adjuster. Nothing is closed up until the readings confirm the structure is dry and the sanitization protocol is complete.
