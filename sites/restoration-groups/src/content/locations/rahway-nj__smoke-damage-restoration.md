## Why Rahway's Older Housing Stock Complicates Smoke Damage

Rahway's residential character is shaped by its age. The blocks radiating out from the Union County Performing Arts Center and the Main Street business district are dense with pre-1940 construction — Queen Anne foursquares, craftsman bungalows, and narrow colonials that were built before modern vapor barriers, sealed wall cavities, or synthetic insulation existed. Those original plaster walls and wood-lath interiors are highly porous. Smoke doesn't just coat the surface; it migrates into the substrate. In a newer home, a technician might clean and seal drywall in a single pass. In a Rahway Victorian, the same room can require multiple rounds of HEPA vacuuming, dry-chemical sponging, and hydroxyl or thermal fogging before odor readings drop to an acceptable level.

The transit-oriented development around Rahway Train Station adds a different dimension: multi-unit buildings and mixed-use commercial spaces where a single unit fire triggers sprinkler activation across multiple floors, leaving neighbors with both smoke infiltration and water intrusion. Our crews are equipped to triage both loss types simultaneously, which matters when a building manager is fielding calls from a dozen tenants at once.

## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Rahway

Every job begins with a structured assessment — not a visual walkthrough, but a systematic room-by-room inspection that uses air sampling and surface testing to map where smoke has traveled beyond the fire compartment. In Rahway's older homes, that often means checking the attic space above finished third floors, the basement ceiling joists, and the interior of forced-air duct systems that run through uninsulated chases.

From there, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

1. **Content protection and pack-out** — salvageable furniture, textiles, and personal items are inventoried and moved to a clean staging area or our facility before any aggressive cleaning begins.
2. **Dry residue removal** — HEPA-filtered vacuums and dry-chemical sponges lift loose soot from ceilings, walls, and structural surfaces without smearing it deeper into the material.
3. **Wet cleaning and degreasing** — alkaline cleaners neutralize the acidic soot compounds that corrode metal fixtures and discolor paint. Protein-based smoke from kitchen fires requires a different chemistry than the carbonaceous residue from a structural fire, and our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians adjust accordingly.
4. **Deodorization** — hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging reach the same hidden cavities the smoke reached, breaking down odor molecules rather than masking them. In homes with original plaster, this stage runs longer than industry averages.
5. **Final clearance and documentation** — air quality readings and surface swabs are logged and provided to the homeowner and their insurer.

## Local Note: Plaster Walls and Smoke Odor Persistence

One pattern we see repeatedly in Rahway's pre-war housing is smoke odor that seems to disappear after initial cleaning, then returns weeks later — especially in humid summer months when the Rahway River valley holds moisture in the air. What's happening is that the plaster substrate, which absorbed smoke compounds during the fire, slowly off-gasses as humidity causes the wall to expand and contract. Standard drywall jobs don't behave this way. For these homes, we apply an encapsulating primer-sealer to affected wall surfaces after cleaning, which locks residual compounds in place and prevents the seasonal rebound. It adds a step, but it's the difference between a job that holds and one that generates a callback in August.
