## Our Water Cleanup Process in Bridgewater

Every job starts with a moisture survey, not an estimate. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map where water has actually traveled — not just where it's visible. In Bridgewater's older finished basements, water behind drywall often reads dry at the painted surface while the framing and insulation behind it remain saturated. Finding that hidden moisture early is the difference between a clean dry-out and a mold problem six weeks later.

Once the scope is mapped, standing water is extracted with truck-mounted or portable extraction units, followed by targeted placement of commercial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers. Drying logs are recorded daily and compared against psychrometric targets so the structure reaches documented dry standard — not just "feels dry." Affected materials that cannot be dried in place (saturated insulation, wicking baseboards, compromised subfloor sections) are removed and documented for the insurance claim before the space is closed up.
