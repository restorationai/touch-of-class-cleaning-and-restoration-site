## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hackensack

Every job starts with a documented site assessment — photographing all structural openings, measuring dimensions, and noting any secondary hazards like downed utilities or unstable framing. That documentation goes directly into your insurance file, which matters when adjusters are reviewing scope.

For window and door openings, we cut and install 3/4-inch OSB panels secured with structural screws into framing members, not just the surrounding trim. On older Hackensack homes where window framing has shifted over decades, we shim and seal edges with foam backer rod to eliminate gaps that would let wind-driven rain past the panel face.

Roof tarping uses reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and anchored with lumber battens screwed into sound decking — never just bungee-corded to gutters. On steeper-pitch roofs common in the Heights, we use fall-protection equipment and extend tarp coverage at least four feet past every damaged section to account for wind lift at the ridge. When decking is too deteriorated to hold fasteners reliably, we carry plywood overlay material to create a solid anchor base before the tarp goes down.
