## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Hackensack

The first thing we do on arrival is assess the water source and category. Groundwater intrusion, a failed sump pump, a burst galvanized supply line, and a sewage backup each carry different contamination levels and require different protective protocols. We don't skip this step — misclassifying the water type affects both the safety of the work and the accuracy of your insurance documentation.

Once the source is controlled or confirmed stopped, we deploy truck-mounted and portable extraction equipment to pull standing water from the floor. In older Hackensack homes with concrete block foundations and unfinished floors, water migrates into the block cavities and under any slab sections — we probe for those pockets rather than assume surface extraction is complete.

Drying follows immediately. We place commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, positioning them to create a directed airflow pattern across walls, floor assemblies, and any stored contents still in the space. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented — that data matters when your adjuster reviews the claim. Affected framing, insulation, and drywall that cannot be dried in place are removed and properly disposed of before mold has a chance to colonize, which in a humid New Jersey summer can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a loss.
