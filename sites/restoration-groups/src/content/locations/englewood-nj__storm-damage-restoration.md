## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Englewood

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence that limits further damage is consistent. When we arrive on-site, the first priority is a rapid exterior and interior assessment — identifying active water intrusion points, structural compromise from fallen trees or limbs, and any immediate safety concerns like downed power lines or compromised load-bearing elements. We coordinate with JCP&L (Jersey Central Power & Light) if utility shutoffs are needed before work can begin safely.

Once the site is safe, we move to emergency board-up and tarping to stop the weather from doing more damage while the restoration plan is built. Wet materials are documented, photographed, and moisture-mapped using thermal imaging and calibrated meters — the same documentation your insurance adjuster will need. Structural drying follows IICRC S500 and S520 standards, using commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers sized to the actual cubic footage of the affected space, not a generic estimate.

Tree and debris removal is coordinated as part of the same job rather than handed off to a separate crew, which matters when a fallen oak is still pinning a section of roof open to the sky.
