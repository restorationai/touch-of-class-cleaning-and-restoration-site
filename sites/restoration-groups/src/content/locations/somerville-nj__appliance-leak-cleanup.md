## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Somerville

Every appliance leak call starts the same way: we identify the source and confirm it's isolated before any drying equipment goes in. A refrigerator ice maker line that's still weeping behind the unit will defeat every dehumidifier on the truck. Once the source is controlled, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

**Extraction first.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard floors, carpet, and the gaps between subfloor layers. In older Somerville homes with original hardwood over board subfloor, we use weighted extraction tools to draw water from between the boards before it causes cupping.

**Moisture mapping.** Thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters let us trace exactly how far water traveled — including into wall cavities, under baseboards, and into adjacent rooms. In a balloon-framed Victorian, water in a second-floor kitchen wall can appear in the first-floor ceiling within hours.

**Structural drying.** High-capacity LGR dehumidifiers and air movers run in a calculated configuration based on the affected square footage and material types. We set drying goals per the S500 standard and monitor daily until materials reach acceptable moisture levels.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, photo, and drying log gets compiled into a report formatted for insurance submission. We work directly with most major carriers and can bill them directly so you're not fronting the full cost out of pocket.
