## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Hackensack

Every smoke job starts with a structured assessment before any cleaning begins. That means mapping the fire origin, tracing the smoke migration path through the structure, and testing surface pH — alkaline smoke from synthetic materials behaves differently than the acidic residue from wood fires, and using the wrong cleaning chemistry on the wrong surface etches finishes or sets stains permanently.

Once the scope is established, the process moves in a defined sequence:

- **Ventilation and air scrubbing** — HEPA-filtered negative-air machines establish directional airflow to pull contaminated air out rather than recirculate it through the structure.
- **Dry soot removal** — loose particulates are vacuumed with HEPA equipment before any wet cleaning touches the surface; wiping soot before vacuuming drives it deeper into porous materials.
- **Surface cleaning** — walls, ceilings, cabinets, and structural members are cleaned with chemistry matched to the residue type. In Hackensack's older plaster interiors, this often requires multiple passes.
- **Deodorization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation run simultaneously to reach residue inside wall cavities, ductwork, and subfloor spaces that surface cleaning can't access.
- **Content pack-out and cleaning** — furniture, clothing, and personal items are inventoried, transported to a controlled facility, and cleaned separately when on-site conditions don't allow safe restoration.

The team holds IICRC Certified Firm status (#210213), and all work is documented with photos, moisture and air-quality readings, and written scope — the same documentation your insurance adjuster will need to process the claim.
