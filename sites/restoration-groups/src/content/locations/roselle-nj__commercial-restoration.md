## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Roselle

Every commercial loss is different, but the sequence that protects your business — and your insurance claim — follows a consistent logic.

**Rapid assessment and containment.** When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing damage: shutting the water source, boarding a compromised roof section, or isolating a fire-affected zone so the rest of the building stays operational if possible. For retail and office spaces, keeping unaffected square footage open is often the difference between a temporary disruption and a full closure.

**Moisture mapping before any demolition.** We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly where water has traveled — through concrete block, behind tile, under commercial-grade flooring — before a single piece of material is removed. **Structured drying and documentation.** Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed by habit. Readings are logged daily.
