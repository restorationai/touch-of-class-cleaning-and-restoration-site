Livingston's housing stock tells the story. ## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Livingston

A mold inspection isn't a visual sweep — it's a structured investigation that starts before we open a single wall. When we arrive at a Livingston property, we begin with a moisture mapping survey using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to identify elevated readings in walls, ceilings, and subfloor assemblies. Moisture data guides where we collect air samples and surface swab samples, so testing is targeted rather than random.

Air samples are drawn with calibrated pumps at a measured volume and sent to an accredited third-party laboratory for spore analysis. We collect both indoor samples and an outdoor control sample — that comparison is what separates a meaningful result from a number without context. Surface samples from suspect areas (tape lifts or swabs) identify the genus and species present, which matters when a remediation contractor needs to scope the work correctly. We document everything with photographs, moisture readings, and a written report that is formatted to support an insurance claim or a real estate disclosure, depending on why you called.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our inspection protocols follow the same standards that govern remediation work, which means the assessment we deliver is one a licensed remediator can act on immediately.
