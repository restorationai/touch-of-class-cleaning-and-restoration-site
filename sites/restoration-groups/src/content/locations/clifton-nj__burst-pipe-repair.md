## Local Note: What Clifton's Plaster Walls Mean for Drying Time

Many of the capes and colonials in Richfield and Montclair Heights still have original plaster-on-lath wall construction rather than modern drywall. Plaster is denser and absorbs water more slowly than gypsum board, which sounds like an advantage — but it also releases that moisture more slowly during the drying phase. In practice, plaster walls in a water-damaged Clifton home can extend the structural drying timeline by a third or more compared to a newer home with standard drywall. We account for this during equipment setup, running dehumidifiers longer and positioning air movers to draw moisture through the plaster face rather than relying on evaporation alone. Rushing a plaster dry-out to meet an arbitrary schedule is how secondary mold problems start.
