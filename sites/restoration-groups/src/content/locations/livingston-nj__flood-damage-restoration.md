## Why Livingston Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Flood Damage

Livingston's western edge runs along the Passaic River, and the Canoe Brook threads through the interior of town before draining into that system. During heavy rain events, low-lying streets near the West Essex Trail and neighborhoods like Northland and Collins can see surface water accumulate quickly — faster than municipal storm drains can handle. The problem compounds underground: many of the 1950s–70s split-levels and ranches that make up a large portion of Livingston's housing stock still carry original cast-iron drain lines. Those pipes corrode from the inside out, and a moderate storm surge can push sewage-laced water back through floor drains before a homeowner realizes the drain is no longer a one-way valve.

On the newer end of the spectrum, the wave of McMansion rebuilds on large finished basements — home theaters, gyms, wine cellars — means the dollar exposure per inch of flood water is dramatically higher. Flooring, framing, insulation, and mechanicals all compete for drying capacity at the same time.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Livingston

When you call (855) 650-7422, the process starts before a crew arrives.
