## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Englewood

The process begins with a room-by-room contents inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, described, and assigned a condition category — undamaged, restorable, or total loss — so the documentation is in place before the pack-out begins. That inventory becomes the backbone of the insurance claim and the roadmap for what goes to our climate-controlled facility for cleaning and restoration versus what stays on-site.

Pack-out itself is methodical. Fragile items — the kind common in older Englewood homes, from antique furniture to framed artwork — are wrapped and crated individually. Electronics are bagged and documented before transport. Soft contents like clothing, bedding, and upholstered furniture go through ozone treatment, ultrasonic cleaning, or thermal fogging depending on the contaminant involved. Hard surfaces are cleaned at the item level, not batch-processed.

Once the structure is dried and repaired, contents are returned in the same documented sequence they left — a process called pack-back — so the homeowner or tenant isn't left sorting through boxes to figure out what went where.

## Local Note

Older East Hill homes in Englewood frequently have horsehair plaster walls and ceilings rather than modern drywall. Plaster is denser and absorbs odor compounds — smoke, sewage, mildew — more deeply than drywall does. That matters for contents stored against those walls: upholstered furniture and soft goods can pick up embedded odors from the structure itself, not just from the loss event. Recognizing that dynamic early changes the cleaning sequence. Items that appear undamaged may still need odor treatment before pack-back, and skipping that step means the smell returns once the home is closed up again.
