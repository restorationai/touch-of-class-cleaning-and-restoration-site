## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in East Brunswick

The first thing we do on arrival is locate and isolate the source. Once the source is confirmed and the water is shut off, we extract standing water with truck-mounted extraction units, then begin targeted demolition of only the materials that cannot be dried in place. One pattern we see repeatedly in East Brunswick's older finished basements: the original plumber ran galvanized supply lines through the rim joist area — the band of framing that sits on top of the foundation wall — and those sections were never insulated when the basement was finished decades later.
