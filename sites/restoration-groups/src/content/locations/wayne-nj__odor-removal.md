## Why Wayne Properties See Persistent Odor Problems

The lake communities — Packanack Lake, Pines Lake, and Lionshead Lake — are lined with midcentury ranches and split-levels, most with finished basements that sit at or near the water table. When a sump pump fails or a slow pipe leak goes unnoticed for a week, the odor that develops isn't just at the surface. It migrates into fiberglass batt insulation, into the wood framing behind paneling, and into carpet padding that acts like a sponge. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 48 to 72 hours of a moisture event, and by the time a homeowner notices the smell, the source is often hidden inside a wall cavity.

Smoke odor presents its own challenge in Wayne's older housing stock. The 1960s colonials and capes common throughout the 07470 ZIP code were built with plaster walls and real wood millwork — materials that absorb smoke particles deeply. A kitchen fire or a chimney backdraft doesn't just coat surfaces; it penetrates the substrate. Wiping down counters and repainting over the soot smell is a temporary fix that usually fails within weeks as temperatures rise and the residue off-gasses again.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Wayne

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying the source, not masking it. The team inspects the affected area, documents moisture readings and contamination zones, and then selects the treatment method matched to the odor type and the building materials involved.

For smoke and fire residue — common after kitchen fires and chimney events in Wayne's older homes — **thermal fogging** delivers a deodorizing agent in a dry fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, neutralizing odor molecules inside wall cavities and behind cabinetry rather than just on open surfaces.

For biological odors from flood water, sewage backup, or long-term pet contamination, **hydroxyl deodorization** is often the right tool. Hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing compounds at a molecular level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied spaces and don't require a lengthy airing-out period after treatment — a practical advantage when a homeowner needs to return to the property quickly.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for situations where the space can be vacated and sealed — typically severe smoke damage or post-flood biological odor in a vacant home. Ozone is highly effective at penetrating porous materials, but it requires careful post-treatment ventilation before re-occupancy.

All work is performed to IICRC standards by an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), and the process is documented with before-and-after readings to support insurance claims.
