## Why Woodbridge Properties See More Burst Pipe Events

Woodbridge Township's housing stock tells the story. The bulk of residential construction here happened between 1945 and 1975 — capes and colonials in Colonia and Fords, split-levels in Iselin and Avenel, ranches in Woodbridge Proper — and those homes carry plumbing that is now 50 to 70 years old. Galvanized steel supply lines corrode from the inside out; the pipe looks intact on the outside right up until it doesn't. Early copper runs are vulnerable at the solder joints, especially where pipes pass through uninsulated exterior walls or unheated crawl spaces that drop below freezing during a hard January cold snap.

The climate compounds the risk. Central New Jersey sits in a freeze-thaw band where overnight temperatures can plunge into the teens after a mild week, catching homeowners off guard. Pipes in garage walls, under kitchen cabinets on exterior walls, and in the rim-joist cavities of older basements are the most common failure points we see across the 07095 and 07064 ZIP codes. Add in the fact that many Woodbridge homes have finished basements — often converted to living space without permits that might have required insulation upgrades — and a burst pipe can mean saturated carpet, swollen framing, and drywall that begins supporting mold growth within 48 to 72 hours of the initial break.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Woodbridge

The first call triggers dispatch.
