## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in West Orange

Every fire loss starts with a structured assessment before any cleaning or demolition begins. The team documents char depth, smoke penetration, and water intrusion with moisture meters and thermal imaging — critical in older West Orange homes where wall cavities can hide both smoldering material and saturated insulation behind intact-looking surfaces.

Once the scope is clear, the process moves through four phases:

**1. Emergency stabilization** — board-up, roof tarping if needed, and industrial air scrubbers running HEPA filtration to begin capturing airborne particulates. In multi-story Victorians, this step also includes sealing HVAC returns so the system doesn't redistribute smoke throughout unaffected rooms.

**2. Structural drying** — firefighting water is extracted and affected materials are dried per the IICRC S500 standard before any rebuild begins. Skipping this step in a home with a finished basement is a fast path to mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours.

**3. Smoke and soot removal** — dry chemical sponges, alkaline cleaners, and thermal fogging are matched to surface type. Exposed old-growth framing gets different treatment than painted drywall or finished cabinetry.

**4. Odor neutralization and reconstruction** — hydroxyl generators run continuously during rebuild phases.
