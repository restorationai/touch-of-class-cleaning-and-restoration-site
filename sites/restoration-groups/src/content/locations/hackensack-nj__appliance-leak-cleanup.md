## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Hackensack

The first step on any appliance leak call is source control — confirming the supply valve is fully closed and that the leak has actually stopped. In older Hackensack homes with original shutoff valves that haven't been turned in decades, those valves sometimes don't seat properly. We carry replacement angle stops and can isolate the line so extraction can begin without water continuing to migrate.

From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

1. **Moisture mapping** — thermal camera and calibrated moisture meters establish the full wet boundary, including inside wall cavities and under flooring where water travels along joists.
2. **Extraction** — truck-mounted or portable extraction removes standing water and pulls moisture from carpet padding, hardwood, and subfloor material before it reaches equilibrium with the surrounding structure.
3. **Controlled demolition (if needed)** — saturated base cabinets, swollen laminate flooring, and wet insulation behind walls are removed to expose materials that can't be dried in place. We document everything photographically before any material is removed.
4. **Drying system placement** — LGR dehumidifiers and axial air movers are positioned according to the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying. In multi-unit buildings, we coordinate placement to avoid blocking egress corridors.
5. **Daily monitoring** — moisture readings are logged each day until the structure reaches dry standard, typically three to five days depending on material depth and ambient conditions.
