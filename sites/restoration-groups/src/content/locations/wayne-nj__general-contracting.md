## Local Note: What Repeat Flooding Does to a Finished Basement Renovation

Here's something that matters specifically in the Hoffman Grove and Mountain View sections of Wayne: homes that flooded during Ida in 2021 and were rebuilt with standard fiberglass batt insulation in basement stud cavities are statistically likely to face mold colonization within two to three years if the underlying water intrusion path wasn't corrected. When we scope a basement renovation in these neighborhoods, we treat any prior flood history as a trigger for moisture mapping before framing begins — not because it's required, but because rebuilding over a wet foundation assembly is one of the more predictable ways a renovation becomes a remediation project eighteen months later. Closed-cell spray foam or rigid foam board against the foundation wall, combined with a properly sized and redundant sump system, changes the long-term outcome significantly.
