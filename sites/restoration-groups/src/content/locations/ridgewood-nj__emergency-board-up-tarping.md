## Local Note: Slate Roofs and Non-Standard Openings

One thing crews learn quickly working in Ridgewood's older neighborhoods: slate roofs cannot be walked the same way asphalt shingles can, and the substrate beneath a failed slate section is often original skip sheathing — widely spaced boards with gaps — rather than solid decking. That means a tarp anchor point that would hold fine on a modern roof can pull through on a 1920s slate roof if it's placed without accounting for where the sheathing runs. The Restoration Group's crews carry roof jacks and work from the eave line when conditions require it, rather than walking the field of the roof, to avoid turning a tarping call into a structural repair. It's a small adjustment that matters on the historic homes throughout the Heights and Old Country Club.
