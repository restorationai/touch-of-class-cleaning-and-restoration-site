## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Morristown

Every job starts with a moisture assessment, not a sales pitch. When we arrive, we use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation — including areas that look dry to the eye. In Morristown's older homes, that step matters more than most: plaster walls hold water differently than modern drywall. The surface can feel dry while the lath and framing behind it remain saturated, which means a crew that skips the mapping phase will leave wet material behind.

Once we understand the scope, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Water extraction** using truck-mounted and portable extraction units to pull standing water from flooring, subfloor, and carpeting
- **Structural drying** with industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, calibrated to the actual square footage and material types on-site
- **Air movement** using high-velocity axial fans positioned to create the airflow patterns that accelerate evaporation without pushing moisture into unaffected cavities
- **Daily monitoring** with psychrometric readings logged at each visit so we can document drying progress for your insurance carrier
- **Controlled demolition** only when materials cannot be dried in place — we remove the minimum necessary and document everything photographically before disposal

For multi-unit losses in Morristown's newer buildings, we coordinate directly with property managers and building engineers to isolate affected units, protect common areas, and sequence the work to minimize tenant disruption.
