## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Hillside

A mold inspection is not a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. Our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians follow a structured assessment protocol that accounts for the specific construction patterns common in Hillside.

The inspection begins with a moisture mapping phase. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to read behind drywall, under flooring, and along foundation walls — the areas where Hillside's older homes hide chronic dampness. We check the sump pit, the perimeter drain tile condition, and the condition of any visible supply and drain lines.

Air sampling comes next. We collect spore trap samples from the areas of concern and from a control location outside the structure. Those samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory for analysis. The lab report identifies the genera and relative concentrations of mold spores present indoors compared to the outdoor baseline — giving you a defensible, documented picture of indoor air quality rather than a guess.

For properties where surface mold is visible or suspected inside a wall cavity, we also collect tape-lift or swab samples to confirm species identification. This matters when the remediation scope needs to be defined precisely, or when a buyer, seller, or insurer requires documentation.

We deliver a written assessment report that includes moisture readings, sampling locations, lab results, and a plain-language explanation of what the findings mean for your property.
