## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Fort Lee

The goal of a pack-out is to move salvageable contents out of the loss environment before ongoing moisture, smoke odor, or soot causes irreversible damage — and to do it in a way that holds up to insurance documentation. The process begins with a room-by-room inventory: every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a disposition (restore, clean, or total loss). Nothing leaves the property without a signed chain-of-custody record.

From there, contents are transported to a climate-controlled facility where the actual restoration work happens away from the active loss site. Textiles go through ozone or thermal treatment to neutralize smoke odor. Electronics are assessed and dried using low-humidity chambers before any power-on testing. Furniture is cleaned, dried to measured moisture content, and stored until the structural repairs at the Fort Lee property are complete. When the unit is ready, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed — a full-circle process that most homeowners don't realize is possible until they see it.

For high-rise losses in the 07024 ZIP code, logistics require an extra layer of coordination: building management notification, elevator reservation, and in some towers, a certificate of insurance on file before any equipment or crew enters. We handle that paperwork before the first truck arrives.
