## Why Highland Park Properties Hold Odors Longer

Pre-war and early-postwar construction — the dominant housing type throughout Livingston Manor and the Triangle neighborhood — was built with materials that absorb and retain odor-causing compounds far more stubbornly than modern construction. Plaster walls, original pine subfloors, and cellulose-based insulation act like sponges. When Ida's floodwaters inundated low-lying sections near Donaldson Park and River Road in 2021, many homeowners dried out the visible water only to find a persistent musty odor weeks later — because the smell had migrated into wall cavities and floor systems that surface cleaning never reached.

The borough's aging cast-iron drain stacks also contribute. Partial blockages and root intrusion create sewer-gas odor events that travel through floor drains into finished basements, a pattern common in the denser blocks north of Raritan Avenue. Identifying whether an odor is structural, biological, or mechanical is the first step — and it changes the entire treatment approach.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Highland Park

Every job starts with a source investigation, not a spray bottle. Our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians trace odors to their origin — whether that's char residue in wall framing after a kitchen fire, microbial off-gassing in a damp crawlspace, or pet urine that has wicked into a subfloor. Once the source is confirmed and any contaminated material is removed or treated, we select the deodorization method matched to the space and odor type:

- **Thermal fogging** disperses a deodorizing solvent as a fine mist that penetrates the same porous surfaces smoke traveled through — effective for fire and smoke odors in older plaster-and-lathe construction common throughout Highland Park's 08904 ZIP code.
- **Hydroxyl generation** uses UV-light-driven oxidation to break down odor molecules in occupied or sensitive spaces — a good fit for multi-unit rentals near Rutgers where tenants may still be present in adjacent units.
- **Ozone treatment** delivers high-concentration oxidation for severe cases in unoccupied structures, such as a vacant two-family that sat with water damage for an extended period.
- **Encapsulant sealers** lock in residual odor compounds in concrete block foundations or subfloor sheathing when full replacement isn't warranted.

Most residential jobs in Highland Park require a combination of two methods — one to address surface and airborne compounds, one to reach what's inside the structure.
