## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Hillside

Every job starts with a rapid site assessment — identifying every breach point before a single panel goes up. On Hillside's older two-families, that means checking shared-wall penetrations, checking whether a fire has compromised the party wall between units, and confirming the roof deck is structurally sound enough to anchor tarp straps safely.

For window and door openings, we cut and install 3/4-inch CDX plywood secured with structural screws — not staples, not adhesive — into the surrounding framing. On masonry facades common in the Winans and Hillside Center sections, we use masonry anchors rated for the substrate. For roof tarping, we use 6-mil reinforced poly tarps weighted and strapped at the ridge and eaves, with lumber battens screwed through the tarp into the decking to prevent wind uplift. On a steep-pitch colonial roof, that anchoring detail is not optional — a loose tarp on a 9/12 pitch becomes a sail in a nor'easter.

We photograph every opening before and after installation, document panel dimensions and anchor points, and provide a written scope summary the same day — formatted to support your insurance claim from the first hour.
