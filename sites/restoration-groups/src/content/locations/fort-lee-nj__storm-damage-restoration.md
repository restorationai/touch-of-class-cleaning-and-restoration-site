## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Fort Lee

Every job begins with a thorough damage assessment before any material is moved or removed. One thing that catches out-of-area crews working in Fort Lee's older Coytesville and Palisade-area homes: the original plaster-on-wood-lath wall construction common in pre-1950 buildings holds moisture in a fundamentally different way than modern drywall.
