## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Fair Lawn

Effective odor elimination isn't a single product — it's a sequenced protocol matched to the source and the substrate. When a crew arrives at a Fair Lawn address, the first step is source identification: locating the origin (flood intrusion, fire residue, sewage backup, pet contamination, or biological matter) before any treatment begins. Masking an odor without removing its source is the most common reason deodorization fails.

Once the source is confirmed and any contaminated material is removed or dried to IICRC S500/S520 standards, the team selects the appropriate deodorization method:

- **Thermal fogging** — a heated solvent-based fog that penetrates the same pathways smoke or sewer gas traveled, neutralizing odor molecules on contact. Particularly effective in the plaster-walled Radburn homes where odors migrate through wall cavities.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — UV-generated hydroxyl radicals that break down odor compounds at the molecular level. Safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, making it well-suited for commercial properties on the Broadway corridor where full closure isn't practical.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone temporarily converts odor molecules into odorless compounds. Requires the space to be vacated during treatment and properly ventilated afterward; used selectively on severe smoke or flood odors in unoccupied residential properties.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct deodorization** — for odors that have traveled into forced-air systems, which is common in Fair Lawn's postwar colonials with original ductwork.

The method — or combination of methods — is chosen based on the odor type, the building materials present, and whether the space needs to remain accessible during treatment.
