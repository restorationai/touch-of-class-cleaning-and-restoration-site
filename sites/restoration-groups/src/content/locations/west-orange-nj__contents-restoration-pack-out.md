## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in West Orange

The goal of a pack-out is to move salvageable belongings out of a damaged environment before secondary damage — mold colonization, smoke odor setting into fabric, rust forming on metal — makes restoration impossible. The process starts with a room-by-room inventory: every item photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition code before anything is boxed. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for returning the right items to the right rooms when reconstruction is complete.

From there, contents move to a climate-controlled facility where each category of item gets the appropriate treatment. Textiles go through ozone or hydroxyl deodorization. Hard goods — ceramics, metals, plastics — are cleaned with methods matched to the material and the type of contamination. Documents and photographs can often be freeze-dried or air-dried when treated quickly. Electronics are assessed by a qualified technician before any cleaning attempt, because the wrong method destroys a circuit board faster than the water did.

Pack-out is performed by an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), and every step of the chain-of-custody is documented so your adjuster has a clear record of what was removed, its condition, and what treatment it received.
