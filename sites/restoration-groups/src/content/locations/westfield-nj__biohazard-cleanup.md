## Why Westfield Homes Present Unique Biohazard Challenges

Westfield's housing stock is part of what makes the town distinctive — and part of what makes biohazard remediation here more complex than in newer construction. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Westfield

Every biohazard call begins with a discreet arrival — unmarked vehicles are available on request — and a thorough assessment before any work begins. The scope of contamination is mapped using ATP testing and visual inspection, not assumption. From there, the process follows a structured sequence:

**Containment and PPE establishment** — The affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to adjoining rooms. In Westfield's older open-floor-plan Colonials and Tudors, this step requires careful attention to HVAC returns and shared air pathways.

**Removal of porous materials** — Contaminated carpet, padding, drywall, or subfloor sections are bagged, labeled, and transported as regulated biohazardous waste in compliance with New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection disposal requirements.

**Hospital-grade disinfection** — EPA-registered disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens are applied to all affected hard surfaces, with dwell times observed per manufacturer protocol.

**Clearance verification** — We do not close out a job on visual inspection alone. ATP surface testing confirms that treated areas meet accepted decontamination benchmarks before we leave the property.
