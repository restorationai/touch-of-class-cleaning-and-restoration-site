## Our Renovation and Remodeling Process in West Orange

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough — not a sales visit. For post-damage rebuilds — the work that follows a water loss, fire, or storm event — we coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster, photograph affected materials before removal, and scope the rebuild to match the pre-loss condition while bringing the assembly up to current code where required.
