## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Hillside

The process begins with a room-by-room contents inventory before a single item moves. Every piece is photographed, logged by condition, and assigned a salvageability assessment — a step that matters enormously when an adjuster is reviewing the claim weeks later.

Items that can be restored are packed in protective materials and transported to a climate-controlled facility where technicians use ultrasonic cleaning, ozone treatment, and thermal drying chambers depending on the material type. Upholstered furniture, hardwood pieces, textiles, electronics, and documents each follow a different protocol. Items that are a total loss are documented with equal care, because that documentation is what supports your insurance claim.

Once your home or commercial space is dried and cleared, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed — not just stacked in a corner. For Hillside's older two-family homes, where the affected unit may be a rental, we coordinate access and scheduling with both the property owner and the occupant so the return goes smoothly.
