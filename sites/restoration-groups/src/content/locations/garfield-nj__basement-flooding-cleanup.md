## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Garfield

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection before any equipment is staged. In Garfield's older homes, water rarely stays where it entered — it wicks up wood framing, travels under vinyl flooring, and collects inside hollow-core doors. Technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace the full extent of saturation before extraction begins.

Standing water is removed with truck-mounted and portable extraction units. For basements with finished walls, drying requires more than fans pointed at the floor: structural cavity drying systems direct airflow behind wall panels and into joist bays, where moisture hides longest. Desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers are sized to the actual cubic footage of the space, not a one-size estimate. Readings are logged daily against the IICRC S500 drying standard so the job closes only when materials have returned to acceptable moisture levels — not on a fixed calendar schedule.

If sewage or river water is involved — common in Garfield basement events tied to drain backup or Passaic overflow — affected materials are handled as contaminated waste. Porous materials that cannot be dried and sanitized to standard are removed and documented for your insurance claim.
