## Why Bayonne Properties See Elevated Mold Risk

The geography does a lot of the work here. Low-lying blocks near the Newark Bay waterfront and around Constable Hook took on significant storm surge during Sandy in 2012, and water that saturates a brick rowhouse foundation or a finished basement doesn't always dry completely — especially when the original cast-iron floor drains are partially blocked or the sump pit is undersized. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials in as little as 24 to 48 hours after a moisture event, and in a home where the basement ceiling is finished with drywall over original framing, that growth can be invisible for months.

Beyond storm exposure, the housing stock itself is a factor. Most of Bayonne's residential blocks in the 07002 ZIP code were built before 1950, with original supply plumbing that's now failing on schedule — pinhole leaks inside plaster walls, slow drips under cast-iron radiators, and condensation on uninsulated cold-water lines in unconditioned basements. Tight rental turnover compounds the problem: a slow leak in a tenant unit may go unreported for weeks before a landlord is notified, by which point mold colonies are well established.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Bayonne

A mold inspection is not a visual sweep with a flashlight. The process starts with a structured walkthrough of the property — attic to basement — looking for moisture intrusion points, visible growth, staining, and building envelope failures. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to identify wet material behind finished surfaces without opening walls unnecessarily.

When sampling is warranted, we collect air samples using calibrated spore-trap cassettes at multiple locations inside the property and a control sample outside, following IICRC assessment protocols. Surface swabs or tape-lift samples are added when visible growth needs to be identified by species. All samples go to an accredited third-party laboratory, and results typically return within 24 to 48 hours. The written report we provide documents findings, moisture readings, sample locations, lab results, and a recommended scope — which is what you'll need if you're coordinating with an insurance adjuster, a landlord, or a remediation contractor.

For commercial properties along the Route 440 corridor or at Bayonne Crossing, we also offer indoor air quality testing protocols calibrated to occupied retail and office environments, where HVAC cross-contamination and fresh-air intake placement are common contributing factors.

## Local Note: What Bayonne's Plaster Walls Mean for Sampling

In Bayonne's pre-war two- and three-family homes, the interior walls are frequently original three-coat plaster over wood lath rather than modern drywall. Plaster is less porous than drywall and doesn't wick moisture the same way, but it also masks moisture readings on standard pin-type meters — the wet zone is often in the lath and framing behind the plaster face, not in the plaster itself. When we inspect these properties, we supplement pin-meter readings with non-invasive radio-frequency moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate hidden wet framing before deciding whether a wall opening is necessary. Getting this right at the inspection stage prevents both under-scoping (missing active mold) and over-scoping (unnecessary demolition of plaster that's structurally sound).
