## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in East Orange

A thorough mold inspection in East Orange is not a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. ## Local Note: What East Orange's Plaster Walls Mean for Mold Testing

In the older Elmwood and Ampere sections of East Orange, many buildings still have original lime plaster over wood lath — a wall assembly that behaves very differently from modern drywall when it gets wet. Plaster is more alkaline, which can slow visible mold growth on the surface, but the wood lath behind it is highly susceptible once moisture penetrates. This means a wall can look clean while harboring active mold colonies in the cavity. We account for this during inspections by using longer-probe moisture meters and by collecting air samples inside wall cavities when readings suggest hidden moisture — a step that's easy to skip but critical in this housing stock.
