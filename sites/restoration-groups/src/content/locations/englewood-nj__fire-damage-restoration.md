## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Englewood

Every job begins with a structured assessment before a single item is moved. We document the full scope with photos and moisture readings, identify structural concerns the fire marshal may have flagged, and establish a containment perimeter to prevent cross-contamination of unaffected areas.

From there, the process follows a deliberate sequence:

- **Board-up and tarping** — open windows, roof breaches, and compromised doors are secured immediately to prevent weather intrusion and unauthorized entry.
- **Debris removal and content sorting** — salvageable contents are catalogued and moved to a clean staging area; non-salvageable materials are documented for your insurance claim before disposal.
- **Smoke and soot cleaning** — we use dry chemical sponges on porous surfaces, alkaline cleaners on protein-based smoke (common in kitchen fires), and thermal fogging or hydroxyl generators to neutralize odor molecules embedded in structural materials.
- **Structural drying** — fires are extinguished with water, and that water often saturates subfloors, wall cavities, and insulation. We deploy desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the material types present, which in older Englewood homes often means extended dry times for plaster assemblies.
- **Reconstruction coordination** — as an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we can carry the project through repairs and rebuild, eliminating the gap between mitigation and reconstruction that leaves homeowners managing two separate contractors.
