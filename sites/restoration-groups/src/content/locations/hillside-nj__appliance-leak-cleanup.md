## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Hillside

Every call starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. When we arrive, we use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has actually traveled — not just where it's visible. In Hillside's older construction, water routinely migrates further than it appears: under vinyl tile, behind plaster-over-block walls, and into the cavities of built-in cabinetry.

Once we understand the full extent of saturation, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then set a calculated drying system — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to dry the structure from the inside out. For appliance leak jobs that involve finished basement spaces — common throughout Hillside — we assess whether materials can be dried in place or need controlled demolition (removing baseboards, drilling weep holes, or cutting drywall above the flood line) to allow airflow to framing. ## Working with Your Insurance Carrier

Sudden and accidental appliance leaks — a burst ice maker line, a failed washing machine hose, a ruptured water heater — are typically covered under standard homeowners policies as sudden water damage. Slow leaks that went unaddressed are a different story, and carriers distinguish between the two during the claim review.

We document the loss thoroughly from the moment we arrive: photos of the source appliance, moisture readings at every affected material, and a written scope of work. That documentation is what keeps a claim from being disputed. We work directly with most major carriers and can communicate with your adjuster on your behalf so the process doesn't stall while your home is mid-dry.
