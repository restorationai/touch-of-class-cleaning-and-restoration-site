## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Union

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, described, and logged — a record that serves both the restoration technicians and your insurance adjuster. Items are then sorted by material type and exposure level: electronics and documents get priority handling, upholstered furniture and textiles go through a separate assessment, and hard goods are evaluated for cleaning versus replacement.

Packed items are transported to a climate-controlled facility where drying, ozone treatment, ultrasonic cleaning, or thermal fogging can be applied depending on what each item needs. Ozone and thermal fogging are particularly relevant for Union homes that have experienced smoke or soot from a fire — smoke residue embeds into porous materials at the molecular level and doesn't respond to surface wiping alone. Once items are restored and documented, they're stored securely until your home is ready to receive them back.

For homes in the 07083 ZIP code where finished basement living spaces are common, pack-out often includes media rooms, home offices, and gym equipment — categories that require specialized handling and that carry significant replacement value on an insurance claim.
