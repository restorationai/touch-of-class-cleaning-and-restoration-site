## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Irvington

The first step on any contents loss is a room-by-room inventory before anything moves. Every item is photographed, logged, and tagged — this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for returning items to the right room after restoration is complete.

From there, affected contents are carefully packed out to a controlled facility environment where temperature, humidity, and air quality can be managed consistently — conditions that simply cannot be replicated inside a wet Irvington rowhouse in the middle of remediation. At the facility, items go through the appropriate restoration track: ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods and non-porous surfaces, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke and odor-affected textiles and soft goods, document drying and digitization where applicable, and electronics assessment.

Items that cannot be restored are documented with the same rigor as salvageable ones — your adjuster needs that record. Once the structure is dry and cleared, contents are returned, unwrapped, and placed back per the original inventory. Nothing gets lost in the shuffle.
