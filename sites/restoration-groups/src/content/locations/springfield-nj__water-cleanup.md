## Our Water Cleanup Process in Springfield

When we arrive, the first task is understanding where the water actually is — not just where it's visible. In Springfield's older homes, that means thermal imaging and moisture meters applied to the base of block foundation walls, the bottom plates of framed interior walls, and the subfloor above the basement slab. Water that reads dry at the surface of a finished drywall panel can leave the wood framing behind it saturated for days.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete. Once the bulk water is out, we map the moisture throughout the affected space and establish a drying system — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with high-velocity air movers positioned to create the airflow patterns the IICRC S500 standard calls for. We monitor and log readings daily until structural materials reach their dry standard, then document everything for your insurance carrier.

For finished basements with framed walls, we make targeted cuts at the base of drywall panels to allow air circulation behind the surface rather than tearing out entire walls unnecessarily. In block-foundation homes, we address the wall cavity separately from the framing, because the two dry at different rates.
