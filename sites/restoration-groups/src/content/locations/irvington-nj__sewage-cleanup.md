## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Irvington

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the response protocol reflects that. When we arrive, the first priority is stopping any active flow and assessing whether the lateral line or the main stack is the source. In Irvington's older multifamily homes, a backup on the first floor often means the second-floor unit's drain is also compromised, so we inspect the full stack before assuming the scope is limited.

Once containment is established, we extract standing sewage using truck-mounted units and portable extractors capable of handling solids. Affected porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor — are removed to the flood cut line and bagged for disposal per NJ DEP guidelines. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage pathogens, applied in multiple passes. We use ATP meters to verify surface cleanliness before any drying equipment is placed, because odor masking is not sanitization. Structural drying follows, with readings logged daily until materials reach target moisture content.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) and NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we document every phase with photos and moisture logs — the same documentation your insurance adjuster will need to close the claim.
