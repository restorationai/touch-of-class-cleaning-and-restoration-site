## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in North Bergen

Effective deodorization in North Bergen's older housing requires more than spraying a neutralizer and opening windows. The process starts with a source identification walk-through — locating the origin of the odor, whether that's char residue behind plaster lathe after a smoke event, mold colonies in a basement slab cavity after a flood, or a decomposition situation in a rental unit. Once the source is documented and removed or treated, the deodorization method is selected based on the space and the odor type.

For smoke odor in enclosed residential spaces, **thermal fogging** delivers a deodorizing agent in a heated vapor that penetrates the same porous surfaces the smoke originally reached — behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, and into subfloor assemblies. For ongoing biological odors in occupied or semi-occupied buildings, **hydroxyl generators** are the preferred tool: they produce hydroxyl radicals that oxidize odor molecules without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods, which matters in a densely populated town where displacing tenants even briefly has real consequences. In severe cases — heavy smoke damage, long-term animal or hoarding situations — **ozone treatment** is deployed in unoccupied spaces, with re-entry protocols strictly observed. Every job closes with air quality verification to confirm the odor compounds have been reduced to acceptable levels, not just masked.
