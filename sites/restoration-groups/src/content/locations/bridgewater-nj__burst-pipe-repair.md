## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Bridgewater

When you call, a crew is dispatched immediately — 24 hours a day. The first priority on arrival is source control: locating the break, shutting the supply, and assessing how far water has traveled. In Bridgewater's older homes, that assessment often means pulling back carpet in finished basements and probing wall cavities with non-invasive moisture meters before making any decisions about demolition. We use thermal imaging cameras to map hidden moisture migration behind drywall and under flooring without unnecessary tear-out.
