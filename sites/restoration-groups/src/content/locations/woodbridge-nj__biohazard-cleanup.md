## Why Woodbridge Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Much of Woodbridge's housing stock dates from the 1940s through the 1970s — an era of plaster-and-lath walls, cast-iron drain stacks, and finished basements that sit close to the water table. That construction profile matters during biohazard remediation. Surface cleaning alone is not sufficient; affected materials often require controlled removal and disposal under New Jersey Department of Environmental Protection guidelines for regulated medical and biological waste.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Woodbridge

Every call begins with a brief intake to understand the scope and the property type. From there, the process follows a consistent clinical sequence:

- **Containment** — affected areas are isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where the space allows, preventing cross-contamination to adjacent rooms or HVAC systems.
- **Personal Protective Equipment and regulated handling** — all technicians work in full PPE. Biological materials, sharps, and infectious waste are collected and packaged according to state transport and disposal requirements.
- **Surface treatment and verification** — affected surfaces are treated with hospital-grade disinfectants. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated are removed and disposed of through licensed channels.
- **Documentation** — we provide written documentation of the scope of work, materials removed, and disposal method, which is often required by insurance carriers and, in some cases, by NJ property transfer disclosures.
