## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Linden

Every project starts with a detailed scope walkthrough — not a ballpark estimate over the phone. 1. **Permitting** — We pull all required permits through Linden's Construction Office. Skipping this step on a finished-basement remodel or a load-bearing wall removal creates title problems at resale and can void homeowner's insurance coverage on the affected space.

3. **Material procurement** — Supply-chain delays are real. We source materials early and stage them to avoid holding up framing or finish work.
4. **Final walkthrough and punch list** — Before a project closes out, every item on the original scope is verified complete and the space is left clean.

## Local Note: What Linden Basements Tell You Before Demo Starts

Finished basements in Linden's split-levels and colonials were often completed by the original owners in the 1960s and 1970s without permits, which means the framing, electrical, and plumbing inside those walls were never inspected. When Ida's floodwaters pushed up through Morses Creek and the Rahway River into low-lying areas near Tremley Point in 2021, many of those finished basements flooded and had to be gutted — which finally gave homeowners and contractors a clear look at what was behind the drywall.
