## Why Millburn's Housing Stock Makes Renovations More Complex

A significant portion of homes in the 07078 ZIP code — covering Short Hills and the surrounding estate neighborhoods — were built between 1910 and 1950. That era of construction means a few things that affect every renovation scope:

- **Knob-and-tube or early Romex wiring** is still present in some attics and walls. Any wall-opening triggers an electrician coordination requirement before framing can close.
- **Plaster over wood lath** is the standard wall system, not drywall. Matching plaster profiles on a partial demo costs more time and material than a straight drywall replacement, and it matters in rooms where the original detail is part of the home's value.
- **Cast-iron drain stacks and copper supply lines** age at different rates. A kitchen or bathroom remodel that opens the walls often reveals deteriorated sections that need to be addressed in the same scope — or the client faces a callback in two years.
- **Finished basements and wine cellars** in Short Hills estate homes carry high-value contents and custom millwork. Scoping a basement renovation — or a post-flood rebuild — requires careful documentation before demolition begins.

Millburn's proximity to the West Branch of the Rahway River also shapes what "renovation" means here.
