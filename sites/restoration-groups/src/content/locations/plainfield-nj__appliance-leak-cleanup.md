## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Plainfield

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through wall cavities and under flooring — critical in older Plainfield homes where water travels farther than expected through plaster, horsehair insulation, and tongue-and-groove subfloor boards.

Once we've mapped the loss, extraction comes first. Standing water under appliances and inside cabinet toe-kicks is removed with truck-mounted and portable extractors before any drying equipment is placed. Appliance connections — supply lines, drain hoses, water heater relief valves — are documented photographically for your insurance claim before anything is moved or disconnected.

Drying is calibrated to the structure, not a generic formula. Plaster walls common in Plainfield's pre-1950 homes release moisture more slowly than modern drywall; drying timelines are set accordingly, and moisture readings are logged daily.
