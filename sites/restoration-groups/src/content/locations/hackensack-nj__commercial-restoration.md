## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Hackensack

Every commercial job starts with a documented scope — not a verbal estimate. When we arrive, we photograph affected materials, map moisture readings across the loss area, and identify secondary damage that isn't visible yet (wet insulation inside wall cavities, for example, or subfloor saturation under tile). That documentation serves two purposes: it drives the drying plan, and it gives your insurance adjuster a clear picture of the loss from hour one.

For water damage, drying is performed to the IICRC S500 standard using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, high-velocity air movers, and thermal imaging to confirm cavities are dry before we close walls. For fire and smoke damage, we sequence odor control and soot removal carefully — surface cleaning before air scrubbing, not the other way around — because disturbing soot without containment spreads it into HVAC systems and clean areas. Structural repairs and reconstruction are handled in-house, which eliminates the coordination gap between mitigation and rebuild that often adds weeks to a commercial timeline.

We carry IICRC Certified Firm status (#210213) and hold an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor license through the NJ Division of Consumer Affairs, which matters when Bergen County building officials require documentation before a certificate of occupancy is reissued on a commercial space.
