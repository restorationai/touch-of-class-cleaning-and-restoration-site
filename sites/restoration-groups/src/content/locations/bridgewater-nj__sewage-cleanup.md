## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bridgewater

Sewage cleanup is not the same job as a clean-water flood. Every technician we dispatch to a Bridgewater address arrives in full PPE — gloves, Tyvek suits, respirators — before anything is touched. The process follows the IICRC S500 and S520 standards for contaminated water losses.

First, we stop the source. If the backup is still active, we coordinate with the homeowner and, when necessary, Somerset County utilities to confirm the lateral is cleared before we begin extraction. Then we remove all standing water and solid waste using truck-mounted extraction equipment. Porous materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed and bagged for disposal per New Jersey solid waste guidelines. We do not attempt to dry contaminated porous materials in place.

Once the structure is cleared, we apply EPA-registered disinfectants to all affected surfaces, including concrete block walls and floor joists that absorbed splatter. Antimicrobial treatment follows. We then set drying equipment — industrial dehumidifiers and air movers — and monitor moisture readings daily until the structure reaches dry standard. Documentation is thorough throughout: photos, moisture logs, and a written scope that your insurance adjuster can work from directly.
