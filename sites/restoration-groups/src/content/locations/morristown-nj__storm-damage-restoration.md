## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Morristown

Every storm job starts with a systematic damage assessment before any material is moved or removed. For Morristown's older homes, that means probing beneath original hardwood floors and behind plaster walls — not just reading surface moisture with a pin meter. Plaster holds water differently than drywall; it can read dry at the surface while the wood lath behind it stays saturated for days.

Once the scope is documented with photos and moisture mapping, we move through a structured sequence:

- **Emergency stabilization** — tarping breached roofs, boarding compromised windows and doors, and stopping active water intrusion before secondary damage spreads.
- **Water extraction and structural drying** — commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the specific material assemblies in the affected space. Drying protocols follow IICRC S500 standards.
- **Debris and tree damage cleanup** — coordinating with licensed tree services when large limbs or full trees have impacted the structure, then assessing whether the impact zone has compromised load-bearing elements.
- **Structural and finish repairs** — framing, sheathing, roofing, siding, and interior finishes restored to pre-loss condition. As an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we carry the work through to completion rather than handing off to a separate rebuild crew.
