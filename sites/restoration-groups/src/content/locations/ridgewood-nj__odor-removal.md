## Our Deodorization Process in Ridgewood

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying every source — not just the obvious one. Our IICRC Certified Firm technicians (Firm #210213) conduct a structured inspection before any equipment is placed, tracing odor pathways through the structure. In Ridgewood's older homes, that often means checking inside plaster wall cavities, crawl spaces under rear additions, and finished basement ceiling assemblies where water has tracked unseen.

Once sources are mapped, we select the right treatment method for the material and the contamination type:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates the same pathways smoke traveled during a fire, neutralizing odor molecules bonded to wood, plaster, and fabric rather than simply masking them.
- **Hydroxyl generation** is safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces and is well-suited to the large, open floor plans common in Ridgewood's colonial-era homes.
- **Ozone treatment** is deployed in unoccupied structures for severe smoke or biological odors — particularly effective in sealed basement spaces with limited airflow.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct deodorization** address airborne particulates and HVAC systems that have been circulating contaminated air.
