## Our Water Cleanup Process in Englewood

Water cleanup in an older Englewood home requires more than a shop vac and a few fans. The process starts with moisture mapping — using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to find water that has moved behind walls, under flooring, or into structural cavities before it becomes a mold problem. Plaster walls common in East Hill properties can read dry at the surface while the lath behind them stays saturated; equipment placement and drying targets have to account for that.

From there, the process moves through standing water extraction, structural drying with commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, and air movement calibrated to the specific materials in the space. We document moisture readings throughout — both because the IICRC S500 standard requires a drying log and because your insurance carrier will ask for one. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place are carefully removed, and we work with your adjuster to document scope before demolition begins so there are no disputes about what was wet and what was not.
