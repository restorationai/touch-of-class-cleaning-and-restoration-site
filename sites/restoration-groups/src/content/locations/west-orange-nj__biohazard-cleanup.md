That variety matters in biohazard remediation. Finished basements are especially common across West Orange's residential neighborhoods, and they introduce an additional layer of complexity. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in West Orange

Every job begins with a private, unmarked arrival. There are no branded vehicles announcing the situation to neighbors. Once on-site, our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians assess the full scope — identifying all affected surfaces, materials, and air pathways — before any work begins.
