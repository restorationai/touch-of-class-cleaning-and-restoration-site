## Our Reconstruction Process in West Orange

Every reconstruction engagement starts with a thorough scope-of-loss assessment — not a quick walk-through, but a methodical documentation of affected structural systems, mechanical rough-ins, and finish materials. We photograph, measure, and log everything before a single piece of damaged material is removed, because that documentation is what drives an accurate insurance estimate and protects the property owner if the claim is disputed.

Once scope is agreed upon, demolition of unsalvageable material is completed and the structure is dried and stabilized before any rebuild begins. This sequencing matters: starting framing over residual moisture is one of the most common causes of mold callbacks six months after a reconstruction job closes. We follow IICRC S500 and S520 drying standards before transitioning to rebuild, and our status as an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) means that documentation is available to your adjuster.

The rebuild phase moves through rough framing, mechanical rough-ins (plumbing, HVAC, electrical), insulation, drywall, and finish work in a sequenced schedule designed to minimize re-inspection delays with the West Orange Building Department. We pull all required permits and coordinate inspections so the property owner isn't managing that process on their own during an already stressful period. Final walkthrough includes punch-list review against the original scope so nothing is left open.
