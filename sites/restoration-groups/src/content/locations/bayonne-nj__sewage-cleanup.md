## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bayonne

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 contaminated water under the IICRC S500 standard, which means standard wet-vac extraction is only the beginning. Here is how a typical job unfolds:

**1. Containment and assessment.** We isolate the affected area with poly barriers to prevent cross-contamination into living spaces. In Bayonne's attached rowhouses and multi-family buildings, that containment step is critical — shared wall cavities and common basement areas can carry contamination horizontally into adjacent units if the work zone isn't sealed.

**2. Solid waste removal and bulk extraction.** All standing sewage, saturated contents, and non-salvageable porous materials (drywall, insulation, carpet) are removed and disposed of per New Jersey solid waste regulations.

**3. Antimicrobial treatment.** Every affected surface — concrete, subfloor, framing, block walls — is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 losses. We don't skip the block walls; in Bayonne's older homes, those walls wick moisture and harbor pathogens long after the visible water is gone.

**4. Structural drying.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run until moisture readings return to pre-loss baselines. Cast-iron pipes and dense masonry foundations hold ambient humidity longer than modern construction, so we monitor daily rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.

**5. Post-remediation verification.** Before we close out, we document surface and air readings and photograph all treated areas — documentation your insurance adjuster will need.
