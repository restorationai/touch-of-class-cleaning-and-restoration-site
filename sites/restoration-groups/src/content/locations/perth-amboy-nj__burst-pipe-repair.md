## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Burst Pipe Issues

The housing stock in Perth Amboy is some of the oldest and densest in Middlesex County. Walk through the Budapest neighborhood or along the streets radiating off Downtown's Smith Street corridor and you'll find late-1800s and early-1900s frame two- and three-families that were built before modern copper or PEX supply lines existed. Many of those buildings still carry original galvanized steel pipes — material that corrodes from the inside out over decades, narrowing the bore and developing pinhole failures that can suddenly rupture under winter pressure spikes.

Cold snaps hit Perth Amboy hard because the city's dense urban blocks mean a lot of pipes run through uninsulated exterior walls and unheated crawlspaces. When temperatures drop into the low teens — not unusual in January and February — the water inside those galvanized lines can freeze and expand with enough force to split a fitting or crack a section of pipe outright. Multifamily buildings compound the problem: a burst on the third floor of a converted rowhouse can travel down through two occupied units before anyone realizes where the water is coming from.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Perth Amboy

The first priority on any burst pipe call is stopping the water. Our crew locates the main shutoff — which in Perth Amboy's older multifamily buildings is often buried behind a finished utility room or tucked into a shared basement — and isolates the affected zone. If the break is on a supply line, we coordinate with the building owner on notifying tenants and, when necessary, contacting the Perth Amboy Water Department to shut the curb stop.

Once the source is controlled, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then begin moisture mapping with thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. In plaster-wall construction common to Perth Amboy's pre-war homes, we pay close attention to the cavity between the plaster and the exterior sheathing — water migrates laterally inside those walls in ways that drywall construction doesn't, and a reading that looks contained at the surface can mask saturation a foot in either direction.

Drying equipment — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers — is placed according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration. We monitor daily, adjusting equipment until materials reach documented drying goals. Pipe repair or replacement is coordinated with a licensed plumber; we handle the water damage remediation side and can connect you with vetted trade partners for the plumbing work itself.
