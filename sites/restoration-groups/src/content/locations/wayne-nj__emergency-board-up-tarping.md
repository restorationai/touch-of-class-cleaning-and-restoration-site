## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Wayne

When a call comes in from Wayne, the first question is what type of opening needs to be secured and what's driving the urgency. A fire-damaged home with missing windows and a compromised roof deck needs a different approach than a commercial unit with a single broken storefront panel.

For residential board-up, the crew arrives with pre-cut OSB panels in common window and door sizes, structural screws rated for exterior use, and framing lumber to bridge larger openings. Panels are installed to cover the full frame — not just the glass opening — so wind-driven rain can't track behind the board. For roofs, heavy-duty polyethylene tarps (minimum 6-mil, typically 10-mil for storm damage) are secured with wood battens screwed into the decking, not just weighted down at the edges. A tarp that lifts in a 40 mph gust does nothing.
