## Why Somerville's Housing Stock Shapes Every Renovation

Most of the residential fabric in ZIP code 08876 predates the postwar building boom. That means knob-and-tube wiring tucked inside balloon-framed walls, plaster-and-lath ceilings that behave nothing like modern drywall, and original cast-iron radiator systems that complicate kitchen and bathroom layouts. On the commercial side, Main Street storefronts were built to the lot line with shared party walls, which means a kitchen remodel or interior reconfiguration almost always triggers a review under the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code — not just a simple permit pull.

The Raritan River and Peters Brook have left their mark on the borough's lower elevations. Homes that took on water during Tropical Storm Ida in 2021 — and some that flooded during Floyd and Irene before that — often have subfloor assemblies, wall cavities, and basement framing that were patched rather than properly rebuilt. When we open those walls for a renovation, we frequently find moisture damage that was never fully addressed. Catching it during a planned remodel is far less costly than discovering it during a future sale inspection.
