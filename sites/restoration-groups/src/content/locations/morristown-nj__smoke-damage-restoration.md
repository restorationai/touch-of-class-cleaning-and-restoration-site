## Why Morristown Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Smoke Damage

The density of pre-1920 construction in the 07960 ZIP code creates conditions that amplify smoke damage in ways that newer builds don't. Older homes rely on balloon-frame construction — vertical stud cavities that run uninterrupted from basement to attic — which acts like a chimney flue during a fire, pulling smoke into wall cavities far from the origin room. ## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Morristown

When we arrive at a Morristown property, the first priority is a room-by-room assessment to map smoke migration — not just the fire room, but every space connected by ductwork, stairwells, or open framing. We use thermal imaging to locate residue in wall cavities and check for secondary damage like heat-warped pipes in homes with aging galvanized plumbing, which is common in the older stock near the Green and Franklin Corner.

From there, the process moves through four concrete phases:

1. **Containment and ventilation** — negative air pressure units isolate affected zones and begin exhausting smoke-laden air before any surface work starts.
2. **Dry residue removal** — HEPA-vacuuming and dry-chemical sponging of ceilings, walls, and contents before any wet cleaning, which prevents smearing oily soot into porous surfaces.
3. **Chemical neutralization** — pH-appropriate cleaners matched to the surface: alkaline solutions for protein smoke from kitchen fires, acidic formulas for synthetic materials common in newer mid-rise finishes.
4. **Thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment** — deodorization that penetrates the same pathways smoke used to travel, including inside wall cavities and ductwork, rather than masking odor at the surface.

Final air quality verification confirms residue levels are within acceptable ranges before we close out the job.
