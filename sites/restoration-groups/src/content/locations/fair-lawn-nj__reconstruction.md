## Our Reconstruction Process in Fair Lawn

Every reconstruction project starts with a scope-of-loss assessment — not a sales walk, but a documented inventory of what structural and finish materials are compromised and what can be retained. In Radburn-area homes, that often means evaluating original hardwood flooring, plaster walls, and period millwork that owners want to preserve where possible. We photograph and document everything before demolition begins, which matters both for insurance purposes and for matching materials on older homes where standard-grade lumber or drywall profiles don't replicate the original.

From there, the sequence runs: structural drying confirmation (we won't frame over wet materials), permit pull with the Fair Lawn Building Department, framing and sheathing, mechanical rough-ins coordinated with licensed subcontractors, insulation, drywall or plaster repair, and finish work.
