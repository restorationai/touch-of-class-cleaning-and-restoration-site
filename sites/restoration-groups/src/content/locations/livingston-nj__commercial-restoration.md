## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Livingston

Every commercial loss starts with a documented scope — not an estimate scribbled on a clipboard, but a written moisture map, photographic inventory of affected materials, and a drying log that satisfies both your insurance adjuster and any code-compliance review.

**Step 1 — Containment and safety.** We isolate the affected area to keep the rest of your operation running wherever possible. For retail or medical tenants, minimizing the footprint of disruption is as important as the remediation itself.

**Step 2 — Extraction and structural drying.** Truck-mounted extractors remove standing water fast. We then place commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the actual square footage and material types — concrete slab dries differently than a suspended ceiling cavity.

**Step 3 — Material assessment.** We distinguish between what can be dried in place and what must be removed. In older Livingston commercial buildings with plaster or original tile, this assessment matters: aggressive demolition can trigger asbestos or lead protocols that add time and cost if not identified upfront.

**Step 4 — Documentation and closeout.** We provide the drying logs, moisture readings, and photo documentation your carrier needs to process the claim, and we coordinate directly with adjusters to keep the process moving.
