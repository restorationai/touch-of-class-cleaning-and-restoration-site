## Our Commercial Restoration Process in West Orange

Every commercial job begins with a documented scope — not an estimate, a scope. That distinction matters for insurance carriers and for your tenants or employees who need a realistic return-to-occupancy timeline.

**Step 1 — Emergency stabilization.** On water losses, we deploy truck-mounted extraction immediately. On fire losses, we board and tarp the structure within the first response to prevent secondary weather intrusion — critical given West Orange's exposure to northeast storm tracks.

**Step 2 — Moisture mapping and air quality baseline.** We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through wall cavities and under flooring. On any loss involving sewer backup or suspected mold, we establish an air quality baseline before demolition begins.

**Step 3 — Controlled demolition and drying.** Affected materials are removed to clean, dry substrate. Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run on a monitored drying schedule — we don't pull equipment because a surface feels dry; we pull it when psychrometric readings confirm the structural materials have reached equilibrium moisture content per the IICRC S500 standard.

**Step 4 — Reconstruction and documentation.** Structural repairs, flooring, ceiling grid, and finishes are restored to pre-loss condition. Every phase is photographed and logged for the insurance file.
