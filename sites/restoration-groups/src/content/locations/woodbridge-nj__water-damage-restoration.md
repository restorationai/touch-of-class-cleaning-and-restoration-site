## Local Note: What Plaster Walls Mean for Drying Timelines

Many homes in Woodbridge Proper and Colonia still have original plaster-and-lath wall construction rather than modern drywall. This matters significantly during structural drying. Plaster is denser and more hygroscopic than gypsum board — it absorbs water more slowly during a flood event, but it also releases that moisture far more gradually during drying. A wall cavity that would reach acceptable moisture content in three days with standard drywall can take five to seven days with plaster, and pulling equipment too early risks trapping residual moisture that feeds mold growth behind the surface. We account for this in every drying plan on older Woodbridge properties, extending equipment run times and adjusting air mover placement rather than defaulting to a standard three-day schedule.
