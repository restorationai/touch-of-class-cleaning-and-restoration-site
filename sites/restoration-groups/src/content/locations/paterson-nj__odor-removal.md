## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Paterson

Effective deodorization isn't a single product — it's a sequence of methods matched to the odor source, the material it's in, and the building layout. After an initial assessment, we identify whether the source is combustion residue, microbial growth, sewage, or a combination, then select accordingly.

**Thermal fogging** is particularly effective in Paterson's older plaster-wall homes. The fog penetrates the same micro-pores that absorbed smoke or fire residue, neutralizing odor molecules rather than masking them. For spaces where occupants or pets need to return quickly, **hydroxyl deodorization** runs continuously without requiring evacuation — hydroxyl generators produce the same reactive molecules the sun produces outdoors, breaking down odor compounds at the molecular level.

**Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe odor loads — heavily smoke-damaged rooms, long-vacant units with entrenched mold odor, or vehicles. Ozone is highly effective but requires the space to be sealed and vacated during treatment and aired out thoroughly before re-entry. We follow IICRC S520 and S500 guidelines on dwell times and post-treatment ventilation.

For sewage-related odors — common in Paterson's older cast-iron drain systems, especially after street-level flooding — we combine enzymatic treatment of affected surfaces with HEPA air scrubbing to capture airborne particulate before applying deodorization agents.
