The housing stock tells most of the story. ## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Scotch Plains

Sewage cleanup is not the same as water damage cleanup — the contamination level demands a stricter protocol from the first minute on-site.

**Containment first.** Before any extraction begins, we isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean living spaces. In Scotch Plains split-levels, where the family room, a bedroom, or a home office often sits directly adjacent to the utility area, containment barriers matter more than in a simple unfinished basement.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted units. Saturated materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed and bagged on-site. We do not attempt to dry contaminated porous materials in place; they come out.

**Sanitization and disinfection.** After the structure is cleared of contaminated material, all affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. Concrete floors, framing lumber, and subfloor sheathing are treated in multiple passes. This step is documented with photos for your insurance file.

**Drying and clearance.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers bring structural moisture down to pre-loss levels, verified with moisture meters. We do not close out a job until readings confirm the structure is dry.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process follows the S500 and S520 standards for water and sewage damage — the same framework your insurance adjuster will reference when reviewing the claim.
