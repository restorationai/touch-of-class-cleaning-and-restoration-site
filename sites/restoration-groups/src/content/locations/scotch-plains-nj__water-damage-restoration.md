## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Scotch Plains

Every job starts with moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the visible wet zone — because in a finished basement, water that enters near the utility room rarely stays there. It travels under flooring, behind baseboards, and into wall cavities before it becomes visible.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Extraction first.** Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water and saturated material before any drying equipment goes in. In a typical Scotch Plains split-level with a finished lower level, this often means pulling carpet and pad and evaluating the subfloor before setting drying equipment.
- **Structural drying.** High-capacity desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers combined with directed airflow units dry the structural assembly — not just the surface. We set daily moisture targets and log readings so you have documentation for your insurance claim.
- **Antimicrobial treatment.** Mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours in a wet basement. We apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to affected surfaces as a preventive measure, not an afterthought.
- **Reconstruction coordination.** Once materials are dry and documented, we coordinate finish work — drywall, flooring, trim — so you're dealing with one company, not three.
