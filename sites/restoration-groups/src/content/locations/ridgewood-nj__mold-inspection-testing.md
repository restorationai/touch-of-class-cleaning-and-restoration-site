## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process

A mold assessment isn't a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. Our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) inspectors follow a structured protocol that starts with a full moisture mapping of the affected areas using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters. In Ridgewood's older homes, this step is especially important because moisture often hides behind original horsehair plaster or inside the rubble-stone foundation walls that are common in the Heights and Somerville sections of the village.

From there, we collect air samples and, where indicated, surface swab or tape-lift samples from suspect materials. Samples are submitted to an accredited third-party laboratory — results typically return within 24 to 48 hours. The lab report identifies the genus and concentration of mold spores present, which determines whether conditions fall within normal outdoor baseline ranges or indicate an active indoor amplification problem.

We document everything with photographs and a written report that details moisture readings, sample locations, lab findings, and recommended next steps. That report is formatted to be useful to your insurance adjuster, a remediation contractor, or a real estate attorney — whoever needs it.
