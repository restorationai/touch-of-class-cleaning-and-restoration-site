## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in North Bergen

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the water. If the building's main shutoff is seized or inaccessible — a common situation in older North Bergen multi-families — we carry the tools to isolate the affected line without waiting on a plumber to arrive. From there, the process follows a documented sequence calibrated to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

We extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then use thermal imaging cameras to map moisture that has migrated behind plaster walls, under tile, and into subfloor assemblies. In multi-family buildings, this matters more than in a single-family home: water traveling between units creates liability questions and requires documentation that each affected space has been properly dried — not just the unit where the pipe broke.

Drying equipment — industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers — is placed based on the moisture map, not by guesswork. We monitor readings daily and adjust until structural materials return to acceptable moisture content. If damaged materials need to come out (saturated drywall, buckled hardwood, compromised insulation), we handle controlled demolition and coordinate with licensed plumbers and general contractors for the repair and rebuild phases.
