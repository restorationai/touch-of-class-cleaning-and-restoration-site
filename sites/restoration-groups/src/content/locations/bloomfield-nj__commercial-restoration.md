## Our Commercial Restoration Process in Bloomfield

Every commercial job starts with a documented scope — not an estimate scrawled on a notepad. When we arrive on-site, we use thermal imaging and moisture mapping to establish exactly where water or smoke damage has migrated, including into wall cavities, under tile, and inside HVAC plenums. That documentation matters for two reasons: it drives the drying and remediation plan, and it becomes the evidence package your insurance adjuster needs to approve the claim without a fight.

For water losses, we extract standing water with truck-mounted extraction units, then deploy commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the square footage and material types involved. Concrete block walls common in Bloomfield's older commercial basements dry on a different curve than steel-stud drywall — we set equipment and monitor daily moisture readings until materials reach target levels, not until the crew decides it "looks dry."

For fire and smoke losses, the process moves in phases: emergency board-up and tarping if the structure is compromised, then dry-ice or thermal fogging for smoke odor, followed by surface cleaning of ceilings, walls, and HVAC components before any reconstruction begins.
