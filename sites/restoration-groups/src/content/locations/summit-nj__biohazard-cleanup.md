## Why Summit's Housing Stock Shapes Biohazard Remediation

The same architectural character that makes Summit's homes exceptional — plaster walls over wood lath, original hardwood floors, deep finished basements, ornate millwork — creates real complexity during remediation. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Summit

Every job begins with a contained assessment.
