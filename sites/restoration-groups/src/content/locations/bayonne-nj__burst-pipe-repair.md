## Why Bayonne Properties See Burst Pipe Losses More Than Most

Bayonne's housing stock tells the story. The dense blocks of Bergen Point and the rowhouses running through Downtown Bayonne were built between roughly 1900 and 1940, and a large share of them still carry original galvanized steel or early copper supply lines. Galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside out — the interior bore narrows with rust over decades, pressure builds unevenly, and a hard freeze or even a sudden pressure spike from the city main can split a weakened section without warning.

## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process in Bayonne

The first priority when we arrive is stopping the source. If the homeowner or building super hasn't already shut the main, we locate the shutoff — in older Bayonne homes that can mean tracing a line through a finished basement to a meter pit near the curb — and confirm flow has stopped before any equipment goes down.

From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

1. **Moisture mapping** — Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters trace exactly where water has traveled, including inside wall cavities and under flooring. In plaster-wall construction common to Bergen Point and Constable Hook, water migrates laterally along wood lath before it shows on the surface, so we map the full affected zone rather than just the visible damage.
2. **Extraction** — Truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard floors, carpet, and subfloor assemblies. Cast-iron floor drains in older Bayonne basements are often partially blocked with sediment; we work around them rather than relying on gravity drainage alone.
3. **Structural drying** — LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to dry the structure to IICRC S500 standard moisture targets. Drying logs are recorded daily and shared with your insurance adjuster.
4. **Pipe repair coordination** — We work alongside a licensed plumber to restore the broken line while drying is underway, so the two scopes don't create scheduling delays for the homeowner.
5. **Controlled demolition if needed** — Saturated drywall or plaster that won't dry in place is removed cleanly, documented with photos, and disposed of properly so mold doesn't colonize behind a closed wall cavity.

## Local Note: What Older Bayonne Construction Does to Drying Timelines

In the plaster-and-lath walls found throughout much of Downtown Bayonne and the older blocks near Stephen R. Gregg Park, water absorption behaves differently than in modern drywall construction. Plaster is dense and slow to release moisture — a wall that reads wet on a meter on day one can still be releasing vapor on day four or five, well after the surface feels dry to the touch. We account for this by extending monitoring intervals and adjusting dehumidifier placement rather than calling a job complete based on surface readings alone.
