## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Bridgewater

When we arrive, the first step is stopping the source — shutting the supply valve to the appliance if it hasn't been done already. From there, we use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to map exactly how far the water has traveled. In Bridgewater's split-levels, water from a first-floor dishwasher leak routinely tracks down into the lower level through the toe-kick cavity and along the subfloor, so we never assume the wet zone ends at the visible stain.

Extraction comes next: truck-mounted and portable wet-vac units pull standing water from hard surfaces and carpet, followed by the placement of desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the square footage of the affected space. We dry to the IICRC S500 standard, taking daily moisture readings and adjusting equipment placement until structural materials reach documented dry standard. Every reading is logged — not for our benefit, but because your insurance carrier will ask for it.

If flooring or drywall has absorbed enough moisture that it cannot be dried in place, we perform targeted demolition: removing only what needs to come out, not entire rooms. We photograph and document all removed materials before disposal.
