## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Bloomfield

Every job starts with a moisture survey before any equipment is placed. In Bloomfield's older homes, that means probing plaster walls, original hardwood floors, and the rubble-stone or poured-concrete foundation walls that absorb and hold water differently than modern construction. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation — not just the visible wet area.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

1. **Standing water extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull bulk water from floors and cavities.
2. **Contamination classification** — sewer-backup events (Category 3) require antimicrobial treatment and controlled demolition of porous materials before drying begins. This step is non-negotiable in post-flood basement jobs.
3. **Structural drying** — high-capacity desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers paired with directional air movers run until moisture readings return to regional baseline. In a finished Bloomfield basement, that typically takes three to five days depending on the extent of saturation and ambient humidity.
4. **Documentation** — we photograph affected materials, log daily moisture readings, and produce a scope of loss report formatted for insurance carriers.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) and NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor, we can carry the project from emergency extraction through reconstruction under one contract if needed.
