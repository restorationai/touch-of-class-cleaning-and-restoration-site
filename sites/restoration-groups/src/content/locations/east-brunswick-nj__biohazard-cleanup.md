## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in East Brunswick

Every engagement begins with a private, respectful assessment. A certified technician evaluates the affected area, identifies all impacted materials, and establishes a containment perimeter before any removal begins. We follow protocols aligned with OSHA bloodborne pathogen standards and applicable New Jersey Department of Health guidelines for the handling and disposal of infectious and biohazardous waste.

Containment is established first — negative air pressure where appropriate, physical barriers to protect unaffected areas of the home. Affected porous materials are removed, bagged, and transported for disposal through licensed channels. All surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. The space is then cleared and, where applicable, tested before containment is lifted. Throughout, our crew works in marked vehicles that don't announce the nature of the work — discretion matters, and we treat it that way.

Sharps and needle cleanup follows a separate handling protocol: puncture-resistant containers, chain-of-custody documentation, and disposal through a licensed medical waste transporter operating in Middlesex County. This is not a step that can be improvised.
