## Why Linden Homes See Appliance Leaks More Often

Linden's housing age is the single biggest factor. A washing machine supply hose installed in 1978 and never swapped out is statistically overdue. Cast-iron drain lines common in 07036 homes from that era corrode from the inside out, and a dishwasher draining into a partially blocked cast-iron branch line can back up and overflow the base cabinet before the cycle finishes. Refrigerators with ice maker lines — especially units pushed tight against walls in galley kitchens — develop slow pinhole leaks at the saddle-valve fitting that go undetected for months, quietly saturating subfloor sheathing below vinyl or tile.

The climate adds pressure. ## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Linden

When a call comes in from a Linden address, the first question is always source control: is the appliance isolated, or is water still flowing? If the homeowner hasn't been able to shut the supply valve — common with older gate valves that haven't moved in years — a technician can walk them through it over the phone or arrive and handle it on-site.

Once the source is stopped, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Moisture mapping** — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters locate water that has migrated beyond the visible wet area, including into wall cavities and under flooring.
- **Extraction** — truck-mounted or portable extraction removes standing water and surface saturation from carpet, pad, and hard flooring.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to dry the structure, not just the surface. In Linden's older homes with plaster-and-lath walls, drying timelines run longer than they would in newer drywall construction because plaster releases absorbed moisture slowly and unevenly.
- **Monitoring** — daily moisture readings track drying progress and determine when materials have reached acceptable levels before any reconstruction begins.

For water heater leaks in mechanical rooms adjacent to finished space, we also assess whether insulation in the surrounding wall cavities has been compromised — fiberglass batt holds water and won't dry in place.
