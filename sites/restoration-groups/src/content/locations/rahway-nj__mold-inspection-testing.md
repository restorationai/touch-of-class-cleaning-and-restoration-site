## Why Rahway Homes Are Particularly Vulnerable to Hidden Mold

Rahway's housing stock is one of the more varied in Union County. The Victorian-era and 1920s-built homes near Downtown Rahway and the Main Street business district were constructed before modern vapor barriers existed. Original plaster walls, balloon-frame cavities, and fieldstone or brick foundations all absorb and hold moisture differently than contemporary construction — and they rarely dry out completely on their own. Postwar capes and colonials in neighborhoods like Hazelwood and Milton Lake typically have finished basements built over aging cast-iron drain lines. When those drains back up slowly — a slow seep rather than a dramatic flood — moisture accumulates in wall cavities and under carpet for months before anyone notices.

The transit-oriented development around the Rahway Train Station and the mixed-use buildings along Main Street add another layer of complexity. Multi-unit residential and commercial buildings with shared HVAC systems can circulate mold spores between units, meaning a single affected space can affect indoor air quality throughout an entire floor. A mold assessment in these buildings requires sampling from multiple zones, not just the visible problem area.

## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Rahway

Every inspection begins with a visual survey and moisture mapping. Using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters, we trace moisture intrusion patterns through walls, ceilings, and subfloor assemblies — the same materials that absorbed floodwater in Ida-era losses and that continue to hold residual dampness in older Rahway homes. We document conditions with photographs and written notes before any samples are collected.

Air sampling captures what you can't see. We collect spore trap air samples from affected areas and from an outdoor control location, then send them to an accredited third-party laboratory. The lab identifies species and quantifies spore counts, which tells us whether indoor levels are elevated relative to outdoor baseline — a critical distinction when a homeowner wants to know if a musty smell is cosmetic or a genuine indoor air quality concern. Surface sampling (tape lifts or swabs) is added when visible growth needs to be speciated or when a remediation contractor needs documentation for their scope of work.

Results typically return from the lab within 24 to 48 hours. We walk you through the written report in plain language, explain what the species findings mean for your household, and — if remediation is warranted — provide a clear summary that any licensed contractor can use to build a remediation plan. We do not perform remediation on the same job, which keeps our inspection findings objective.
