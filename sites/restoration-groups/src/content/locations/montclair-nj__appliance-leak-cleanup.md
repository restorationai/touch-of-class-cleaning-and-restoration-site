## Why Montclair Properties See More Appliance Leak Damage

Montclair's housing stock is part of the problem. ## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Montclair

The first thing we do on-site is locate all the water — not just what's visible. In Montclair's plaster-wall homes, moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras are essential because plaster holds water differently than drywall: it absorbs slowly and releases slowly, and a wall that feels dry to the touch can be holding significant moisture two inches in. We map the full moisture boundary before we move any equipment.

From there, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage remediation:

- **Extract standing water** using truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access (many Montclair basements have low clearance or narrow exterior stairwells).
- **Remove saturated materials** — baseboards, flooring, cabinetry — that cannot be dried in place without trapping moisture behind them.
- **Deploy drying equipment**: commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to dry cavities, not just surfaces.
- **Monitor daily** with moisture readings logged at each visit so drying progress is documented — critical for insurance claims.
- **Verify dry standard** before any reconstruction begins.

For appliance-specific leaks, we also help identify the source so your plumber or appliance technician can make the repair before we close the job.
