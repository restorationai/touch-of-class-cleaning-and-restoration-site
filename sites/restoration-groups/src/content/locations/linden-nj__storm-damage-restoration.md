## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Linden

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence we follow in Linden is calibrated to what we actually find here.

**Immediate stabilization.** We tarp compromised rooflines, board breached windows, and extract standing water before secondary damage can set in. In postwar split-levels, that means checking the sub-slab area and the mechanical room — water migrates fast in those layouts.

**Moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through wall assemblies. In Linden's older homes, water that enters at the roofline can travel inside a wall cavity to the basement level before showing any visible sign. We document every reading for your insurance claim.

**Structural drying.** Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Drying protocols follow the IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 for any mold-affected material we encounter.

**Debris removal and repair coordination.** Tree limbs, damaged roofing material, and compromised siding are cleared and catalogued. We coordinate with licensed subcontractors for structural repairs and can manage the rebuild phase so you're not juggling multiple contractors.
