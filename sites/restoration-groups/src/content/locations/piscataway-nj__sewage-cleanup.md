## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Piscataway

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated classification under IICRC standards — and every step of the remediation reflects that. When the crew arrives, the first priority is containment: stopping the source if it's still active, then establishing physical barriers to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected areas of the home.

Extraction comes next. Standing sewage is removed using truck-mounted or portable extraction units, depending on access. In finished Piscataway basements — which often have low ceilings, narrow egress windows, and furniture staged close to the walls — portable units are frequently the right tool. Once bulk waste is removed, affected materials are assessed. Porous materials that have absorbed Category 3 contamination — drywall, carpet, padding, insulation — are typically removed rather than dried in place. There is no reliable way to sanitize saturated porous material after raw sewage contact.

After demo, all hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants, applied in two passes with dwell time between applications. The space is then dried using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, and clearance readings are taken before reconstruction begins. The IICRC Certified Firm standard (#210213) that governs this work requires documentation at each phase — that documentation also becomes part of the insurance claim file.
