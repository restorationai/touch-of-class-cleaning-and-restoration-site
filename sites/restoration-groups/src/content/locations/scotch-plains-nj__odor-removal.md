## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Scotch Plains

Every job starts with a source assessment — not a walk-through with a clipboard, but a systematic identification of where odor-causing compounds are actually concentrated. Smoke residue in a Scotch Plains colonial may be heaviest in the attic because heat carries particles upward through open stairwells before the fire is suppressed. Sewage odor in a Southside ranch may trace back to a cracked cast-iron cleanout under the slab, not the visible floor drain. Getting the source wrong means treating the symptom.

Once the source is confirmed, we select from a tiered set of methods:

- **Thermal fogging** — a solvent-based fog that follows the same pathways smoke traveled, bonding with odor molecules in wall cavities, attic spaces, and subfloor voids. Effective for post-fire scenarios where smoke penetrated porous materials.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** — hydroxyl generators produce oxidizing radicals that break down odor compounds at the molecular level without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. Useful in occupied or partially occupied spaces, including commercial properties on Route 22 that cannot close for a full day.
- **Ozone treatment** — high-concentration ozone is one of the most effective tools for severe smoke or biological odor in unoccupied spaces. We seal the structure, run the treatment cycle, then ventilate fully before re-entry. Not appropriate for occupied spaces or areas with sensitive electronics.
- **HEPA air scrubbing and duct cleaning** — forced-air systems in mid-century homes are often the last reservoir of odor. We address ductwork as part of the scope, not as an afterthought.

All drying and deodorization work follows IICRC standards — The Restoration Group holds IICRC Certified Firm status (#210213) — and we document conditions before, during, and after treatment for insurance purposes.
