## Why Staten Island Properties See More Burst Pipe Losses

The borough's housing stock tells the story. Blocks of frame homes built between the 1920s and 1950s — concentrated in Port Richmond and St. George — still carry original galvanized or cast-iron supply lines that have spent a century contracting and expanding with the seasons. ## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process

The first priority on arrival is water isolation. Our technicians locate the main shutoff — or coordinate with building management if it's a shared system — and stop the flow before any extraction begins. From there the process follows a documented sequence:

**Extraction and moisture mapping.** Truck-mounted extractors pull standing water from finished and unfinished spaces. Thermal imaging cameras identify water that has migrated behind drywall or under hardwood flooring, which is common in the older balloon-frame construction found throughout Port Richmond and South Beach. Every affected area is logged with moisture readings before drying equipment is placed.

**Structural drying.** Industrial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run continuously. Drying timelines are monitored against IICRC S500 standards — typically three to five days for Category 1 losses in conditioned space, longer if plaster walls or dense subfloor assemblies are involved.

**Pipe repair coordination.** We work alongside licensed plumbers to complete the break repair and pressure-test the line before any wall or ceiling is closed. Restoration closes out only after the repaired system holds pressure and moisture readings return to baseline.

**Documentation for insurance.** Every step — moisture logs, thermal images, equipment placement records, and scope of work — is packaged for your carrier. We bill most major insurers directly.

## Local Note: Plaster Walls and Drying Time in Older Staten Island Homes

In the pre-1950 frame homes concentrated around St. George and Port Richmond, interior walls are often original lime plaster over wood lath rather than modern drywall.
