## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Livingston

Every basement flooding job starts with a moisture assessment before a single piece of equipment is placed. We use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to map how far water has migrated into concrete block, framing, and subfloor — because water visible on the floor is rarely the full picture.

From there, the process follows a clear sequence:

- **Standing water extraction** — truck-mounted or portable submersible pumps remove bulk water first. In older Livingston homes with low ceiling heights or tight mechanical rooms, portable units often work better than dragging hoses from a truck.
- **Structural drying** — commercial-grade LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned to dry the slab, framing, and any remaining wall assemblies according to the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.
- **Material evaluation** — drywall, insulation, and flooring are assessed individually. Saturated fiberglass batt insulation in a 1960s block wall cavity holds moisture long after the surface feels dry; we document what needs to come out and what can be saved.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — applied to concrete and framing surfaces where warranted, to address the conditions that allow mold to colonize within 24–48 hours of a water intrusion.
- **Monitoring and sign-off** — we return to check moisture readings until the structure meets dry standard, then provide a drying log for your insurance file.
