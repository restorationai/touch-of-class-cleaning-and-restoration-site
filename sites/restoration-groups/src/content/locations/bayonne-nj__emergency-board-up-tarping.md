The process moves in a deliberate sequence:

1. **Site safety assessment** — we confirm structural stability before any crew member enters or mounts a ladder. Fire-damaged framing and flood-weakened decking require a visual check before work begins.
2. **Opening measurement and material cut** — boards are cut on-site to fit actual rough openings, not nominal dimensions. On older Bergen Point and Midtown homes, that difference matters.
3. **Secure fastening** — panels are screwed, not stapled, into structural framing members. On brick rowhomes, we use masonry anchors where stud access is limited.
4. **Roof tarping** — tarps are overlapped, weighted at edges, and secured with batten strips rather than just stapled at the perimeter. On a peninsula exposed to Kill Van Kull wind, edge fastening alone fails.
5. **Photo documentation** — every opening, every panel, every tarp anchor point is photographed before we leave. That documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster.
