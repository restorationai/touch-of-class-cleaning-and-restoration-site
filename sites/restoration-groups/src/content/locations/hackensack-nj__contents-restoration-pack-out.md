## Why Hackensack Properties See Contents Losses Differently

Hackensack's building stock creates conditions that complicate contents recovery in ways that don't show up in suburban ranch-house markets. Plaster, unlike modern drywall, holds moisture deep in its substrate while the surface reads dry, which means soft goods sitting against those walls stay damp long after the obvious water is gone.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Hackensack

The process begins with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition code — this documentation is the foundation of any insurance claim and protects you if a dispute arises over what was salvageable. Items are then carefully packed, labeled, and transported to a climate-controlled facility where cleaning, drying, deodorization, and where appropriate, ozone or hydroxyl treatment can happen away from the active job site.

Off-site processing matters in Hackensack's denser housing stock. In a three-family home in the Heights or a mid-century cape near Johnson Park, there simply isn't enough clear floor space to clean and dry contents in place while structural drying equipment is running. Moving contents out protects them from secondary damage — cross-contamination from demolition dust, re-wetting from dehumidifier condensate, or physical damage from foot traffic.

Once the structure passes drying validation and any necessary repairs are complete, contents are returned in the same organized sequence they left, with a final inventory check against the original log.
