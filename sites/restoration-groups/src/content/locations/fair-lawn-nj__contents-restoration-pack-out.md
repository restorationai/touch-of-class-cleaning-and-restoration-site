## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Fair Lawn

The first step on any pack-out is a room-by-room contents inventory — every item photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a restoration or disposal recommendation before anything leaves the property. That documentation matters for insurance purposes and it matters for you: you know exactly what went where and why.

Salvageable items are packed, labeled, and transported to our climate-controlled processing facility. There, depending on what the contents have been exposed to, we deploy ultrasonic cleaning tanks for hard goods and non-porous items, ozone and hydroxyl chambers for smoke and odor mitigation, freeze-drying equipment for water-damaged documents and photographs, and dry-cleaning or wet-cleaning for textiles and soft goods. Electronics are evaluated by a certified electronics technician before any cleaning attempt — forcing a power cycle on a water-exposed device causes more damage than the original event.

Once the structure is dried, repaired, and cleared, contents are returned, unpacked, and placed back in their original positions per the photographic inventory. Nothing is left for you to sort through.
