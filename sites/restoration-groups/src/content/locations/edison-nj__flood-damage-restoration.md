## Why Edison Properties Are Especially Vulnerable to Flood Damage

Edison is a sprawling township, and its flood risk isn't uniform. The southern sections closest to the Raritan River corridor sit at lower elevation, and the Mill Brook drainage network that threads through residential areas like Stelton and Clara Barton can back up quickly during heavy rain events. During Ida, some of these neighborhoods saw standing water that persisted for days — long enough for mold to begin colonizing wall cavities and subfloor assemblies.

Beyond storm surge, the township's housing stock creates its own vulnerabilities. A large share of Edison's homes were built between the late 1940s and the 1970s. Slab-on-grade construction in some of the township's multifamily complexes adds another wrinkle: water migrates laterally under the slab before it ever surfaces, meaning the visible damage is often a fraction of what's actually wet.

## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Edison

The first thing we do on arrival is establish the true scope of the loss — not just what's visibly wet. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to map saturation through drywall, insulation, and subfloor assemblies. In Edison's older split-levels, this step routinely reveals water that has tracked down interior wall cavities to the lowest level of the home.

Once we've mapped the moisture, we extract standing water with truck-mounted extraction units, then deploy commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers. Drying is calibrated to the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged and documented — that documentation matters for insurance claims and for confirming the structure is genuinely dry before any rebuild begins.

For contents, we triage on-site: salvageable items are moved to a dry area or packed out for off-site cleaning, while materials that can't be dried to standard (saturated particleboard cabinetry, compromised insulation, swollen laminate flooring) are removed and disposed of properly. We handle the full sequence — mitigation through structural drying — so you're not coordinating between multiple contractors during an already stressful situation.
