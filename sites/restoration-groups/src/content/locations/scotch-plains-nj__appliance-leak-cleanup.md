## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Scotch Plains

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the water source if it hasn't been shut off already — appliance supply valves are often corroded and stiff in older Scotch Plains homes, and we carry the tools to close them without damaging aging fittings. From there, the process follows a documented sequence:

- **Moisture mapping** with calibrated meters and thermal imaging to trace exactly how far water has migrated — under cabinets, into adjacent rooms, and down through floor assemblies.
- **Extraction** of standing water using truck-mount and portable units, including crevice tools for the gaps under appliances and cabinet bases.
- **Controlled demolition** where necessary — saturated particleboard cabinet bases, wet drywall, and compromised flooring sections are removed to expose the structure for drying rather than trapping moisture inside.
- **Drying system placement** — commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, positioned based on the moisture map, not a generic formula.
- **Daily monitoring** with written moisture logs until readings confirm the structure meets IICRC S500 drying standards.
