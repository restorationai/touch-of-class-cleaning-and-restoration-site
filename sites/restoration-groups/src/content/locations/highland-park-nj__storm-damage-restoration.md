## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Highland Park

Every storm job starts with a damage assessment that separates what's cosmetic from what's structurally urgent. In Highland Park's older homes, that distinction matters more than it does in newer construction — a soft spot in a plaster ceiling may indicate that water has been wicking through original horse-hair lath for hours, while the exterior still looks intact.

Our process moves through four stages:

1. **Emergency stabilization** — tarping compromised roof sections, boarding broken windows, and extracting any standing water from basements or crawlspaces before secondary damage compounds the loss.
2. **Moisture mapping** — thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration through walls, subfloors, and framing. In pre-war homes, water travels farther and faster through plaster than through modern drywall, so we extend the mapping perimeter beyond the obvious wet zone.
3. **Structural drying** — commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to dry cavities, not just surfaces. Drying timelines are validated against the IICRC S500 standard and logged daily.
4. **Repair and rebuild** — from replacing storm-damaged roofing and siding to restoring finished interiors. We hold an NJ Licensed Home Improvement Contractor credential, which means we can carry the job from emergency response through final reconstruction without handing it off.
