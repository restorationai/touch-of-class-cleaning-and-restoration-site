## Our Mold Inspection and Testing Process in Morristown

A mold assessment is not a visual walkthrough with a flashlight. The process begins with a detailed moisture mapping of the structure — using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to identify wet or elevated-moisture areas behind walls, under flooring, and above ceilings that show no visible staining. In Morristown's older homes, that step often reveals surprises: plaster walls absorb and retain moisture differently than modern drywall, so elevated readings can persist in areas that look and feel dry to the touch.

Once moisture mapping is complete, air samples and surface samples are collected following IICRC protocols. Air samples establish a spore count and species profile for the indoor environment and compare it against an outdoor baseline taken the same day — that comparison is what distinguishes a normal background level from an actionable indoor problem. Surface samples from suspect areas confirm the genus and concentration of any colonies present. All samples are analyzed by an accredited third-party laboratory, and results are typically available within 24 to 48 hours. You receive a written report with findings, laboratory data, and a clear explanation of what the numbers mean for your property and your family.
