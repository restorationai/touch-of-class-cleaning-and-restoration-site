## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Clifton

Sewage is classified as Category 3 contaminated water — the most hazardous category under the IICRC S500 standard — and every step of the remediation process reflects that. When we arrive, the first priority is containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms, particularly important in Clifton's open-plan split-levels where a basement staircase opens directly into a main living area.

Extraction comes next. We remove standing sewage and saturated materials — flooring, drywall, insulation, and any porous contents that cannot be safely restored. In the postwar homes common throughout Clifton, this often means removing original tongue-and-groove subfloor or concrete board that has absorbed effluent below the visible surface. We don't stop at what's visible; we probe and moisture-map the full affected area before any material decisions are made.

After physical removal, the space is treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants applied to all hard surfaces, framing, and concrete.
