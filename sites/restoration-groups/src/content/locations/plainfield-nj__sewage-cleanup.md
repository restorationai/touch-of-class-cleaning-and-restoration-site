## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Plainfield

Raw sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the most contaminated type — and the cleanup process reflects that. When we arrive, the first priority is containment: stopping the source if it hasn't been stopped already, then establishing work zones to prevent cross-contamination from the affected area into clean portions of the home.

Extraction comes next. Standing sewage is removed using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, followed by careful removal of porous materials — drywall, insulation, carpet, and subfloor sections — that cannot be adequately sanitized once saturated with Category 3 water. In Plainfield's older homes, this step requires attention to lead paint and asbestos-containing materials that may be present in pre-1978 construction; we follow NJ Division of Consumer Affairs guidelines and flag those conditions before demolition begins.

After structural drying using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers, every affected surface is treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. We document moisture readings, affected material quantities, and sanitization steps throughout — documentation that matters both for your health and for your insurance claim. As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), our process follows the S500 and S520 standards for contaminated water losses.
