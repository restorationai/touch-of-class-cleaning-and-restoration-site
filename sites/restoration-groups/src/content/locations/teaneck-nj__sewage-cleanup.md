## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Teaneck

Sewage cleanup is not a wet-vac job. Raw sewage contains bacteria, viruses, and parasites that survive on porous surfaces long after the visible water is gone. Our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians follow a structured protocol calibrated to the conditions inside Teaneck's older homes.

**Containment and extraction first.** We establish containment to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected living areas — especially important in multi-story homes where HVAC returns can spread airborne pathogens. Truck-mounted extraction equipment removes standing sewage, and portable units reach tight utility rooms and crawl spaces common in pre-war construction.

**Controlled demolition where necessary.** Drywall, carpet, and insulation that have absorbed Category 3 water cannot be dried in place — they must come out. We document every removed material with photos and measurements for your insurance claim before anything is discarded.

**EPA-registered disinfection.** After extraction and demo, we apply hospital-grade, EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to all affected concrete, framing, subfloor, and remaining wall cavities. In Teaneck's older homes, we pay particular attention to the mortar joints in block foundation walls, which wick moisture and hold contamination longer than poured concrete.

**Drying and clearance.** Industrial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. We document final readings before equipment is removed.
