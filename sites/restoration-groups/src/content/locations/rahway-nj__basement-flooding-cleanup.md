## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Rahway

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection before a single pump runs. We use thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters to trace exactly where water has migrated — under slab edges, behind framing, into subfloor assemblies — because water in a Rahway basement rarely stays where it's visible. Once the scope is documented and photographed for your insurer, extraction begins with truck-mounted or portable submersible units sized to the volume.

After bulk water is removed, the drying phase is calibrated to the specific materials present. Plaster walls common in older Rahway homes release moisture more slowly than drywall and often read as "dry" on surface meters while staying wet at the substrate. We set desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and axial air movers in a calculated pattern, then return daily to log psychrometric readings — temperature, relative humidity, and specific humidity — until the structure reaches its pre-loss moisture baseline. Work is performed to IICRC S500 standard by our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians.

If sewage or river backflow is involved — common in the lower-elevation streets near Rahway River Park — the cleanup protocol shifts to a Category 3 water loss. That means PPE, EPA-registered disinfectants applied to all affected surfaces, and controlled demolition of porous materials that cannot be adequately sanitized.
