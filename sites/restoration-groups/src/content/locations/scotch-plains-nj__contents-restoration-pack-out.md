## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process

The first step on any pack-out job is a thorough room-by-room inventory before a single item moves. Every piece is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition code — that documentation becomes the backbone of your insurance claim and ensures nothing is misplaced during transport. Items are then sorted: what can be restored on-site, what needs to travel to our climate-controlled facility for deeper treatment, and what must be documented as a total loss for the carrier.

At the facility, textiles go through specialized laundering and deodorization. Hard goods — furniture, cabinetry, decorative items — are cleaned with methods matched to the material and the type of loss (water, smoke, or soot each leave different residues). Electronics are assessed by technicians before any power is applied. Documents and photographs with water damage go into controlled drying immediately, because the window to prevent permanent staining and mold colonization on paper is narrow — typically 24 to 48 hours from initial wetting. Once the structure in Scotch Plains is cleared, belongings are returned, unwrapped, and placed back in the rooms where they belong.

## Local Note

Split-levels throughout Willow Grove and the Ashbrook area often have a half-level below grade that functions as a utility and storage room — the kind of space that accumulates decades of belongings in cardboard boxes directly on a concrete slab. When water enters that space, the boxes wick moisture upward and the contents inside can be saturated even if the water line only reached a few inches. On pack-out jobs in these homes, we treat everything stored at floor level as potentially compromised until it is individually inspected, rather than assuming items above the visible waterline are dry.
