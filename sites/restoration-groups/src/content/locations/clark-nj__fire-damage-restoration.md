The housing stock that defines Clark — 1950s and 1960s ranches, capes, and split-levels — shares a few traits that matter when fire damage restoration begins. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Clark

Every job starts with a structured assessment, not a visual walkthrough. **Step 1 — Stabilize and Secure.** Board-up and roof tarping happen first if the structure is open to weather. Clark's Union County location means cold-weather jobs can see overnight temperatures drop fast, and an unsecured structure loses heat through fire-damaged walls quickly, complicating drying.

**Step 2 — Soot and Smoke Removal.** Dry soot is vacuumed with HEPA equipment before any wet cleaning begins. Wet-cleaning dry soot smears it deeper into porous surfaces.
