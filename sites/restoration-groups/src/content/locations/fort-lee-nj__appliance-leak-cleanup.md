## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Fort Lee

Every appliance leak response starts with source confirmation — not just mopping up what's visible. A technician identifies whether the failure point is a supply line, drain hose, door seal, or internal component, and whether the water has already traveled beyond the room of origin. Once the scope is mapped, we extract standing water using truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then place calibrated drying equipment — low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers and axial air movers — positioned to dry the specific material assembly involved. We document every reading with photos and moisture logs, which matters when an insurance adjuster or HOA property manager needs a paper trail.
