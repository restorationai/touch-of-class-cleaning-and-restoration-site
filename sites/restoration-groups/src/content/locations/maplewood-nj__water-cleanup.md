## Why Maplewood Properties See Water Damage Differently

Maplewood's housing stock is its charm and its vulnerability. The craftsman bungalows in the Hilton neighborhood and the stately colonials near Tuscan were built in an era of stone rubble foundations, original plaster walls, and cast-iron drain stacks. None of those materials behave like modern construction when water enters.

Plaster absorbs moisture deeply and releases it slowly — a wall that reads dry at the surface with a pin-type meter can still be holding significant moisture in the lath and framing behind it. Cast-iron drains corrode from the inside over decades; a slow seep at a joint can saturate a floor system for weeks before it becomes visible. And finished basements — extremely common in the 07040 ZIP code — trap water under laminate or carpet where it begins supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of the initial event.

The sump systems in many of these homes were retrofitted rather than designed in. During a hard rain event, a single sump pump failure can introduce several inches of standing water into a finished space faster than a shop vac can keep up.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in Maplewood

Every water cleanup starts with a thorough moisture assessment — not a visual inspection, but thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters that map the actual wet boundary behind walls and under flooring. In Maplewood's older homes, that boundary is almost always larger than it looks.

From there, the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and monitoring:

- **Standing water removal**: truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from basements and crawl spaces, including from below carpet pad and subfloor where gravity alone won't drain it.
- **Structural drying**: commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers, paired with high-velocity air movers, are positioned to dry the structure — not just the air. Drying targets follow the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.
- **Daily monitoring**: moisture readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is documented. This matters both for your peace of mind and for the insurance claim record.
- **Material decisions**: original plaster in good condition is often worth saving with controlled drying. When plaster or lath is beyond recovery, selective demolition removes only what's necessary — not entire walls.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), the crew applies drying science rather than guesswork to every job.

## Local Note: What Maplewood's Plaster Walls Mean for Drying Timelines

Homeowners used to modern drywall sometimes expect a water cleanup to wrap up in three or four days. In Maplewood's pre-war housing stock, that timeline is often optimistic. Original plaster — particularly three-coat lime plaster over wood lath, which is common in the Tuscan and Jefferson neighborhoods — can hold moisture for seven to ten days even under active drying conditions. The lath itself wicks water laterally, spreading the wet zone along the wall cavity well past the point of entry. Rushing the drying phase to hit an arbitrary deadline risks trapping moisture that feeds mold behind a freshly painted surface. Monitoring readings, not a calendar, should determine when drying is complete.
