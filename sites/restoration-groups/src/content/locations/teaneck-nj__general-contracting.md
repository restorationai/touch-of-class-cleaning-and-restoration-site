## Our Renovation and General Contracting Process in Teaneck

Every project starts with a thorough walkthrough, not a quick estimate. - **Finish work and punch-out**: Drywall, tile, cabinetry, fixtures, paint, and trim — coordinated so trades don't stack on top of each other.
