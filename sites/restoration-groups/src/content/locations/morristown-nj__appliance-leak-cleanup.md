## Our Appliance Leak Cleanup Process in Morristown

When we arrive at a Morristown property, the first step is always moisture mapping — not just the visible wet area, but the full migration path. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water traveled behind baseboards, under cabinets, and into adjacent rooms. In older homes near the Speedwell neighborhood, that often means checking the finished basement ceiling directly below the kitchen or laundry room, because water follows gravity through gaps in aging subfloor assemblies.

Once we understand the scope, we extract standing water and begin controlled drying using commercial-grade air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the space. Drying timelines are validated against the IICRC S500 standard — we don't pull equipment based on a calendar date; we pull it when moisture readings confirm the structure has returned to normal levels. Affected materials that cannot be dried in place — saturated insulation, swollen hardwood, wet drywall below the flood cut line — are removed and documented for your insurance claim.

For appliance leaks specifically, we also identify the source before we leave. If the ice maker line fitting is still compromised or the dishwasher door gasket is the culprit, we note it in our report so your plumber or appliance tech can address it before reinstallation.
