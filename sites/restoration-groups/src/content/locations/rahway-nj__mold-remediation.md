## Why Rahway Properties See Mold Issues

Rahway's housing stock is a significant factor. The Victorian-era and 1920s homes concentrated near Downtown Rahway and the blocks surrounding the Union County Performing Arts Center were built with balloon-frame construction and plaster walls — cavity systems that channel water vertically and hold moisture in ways modern stick-frame homes don't. Postwar capes and colonials throughout Hazelwood and Leesville typically have finished basements sitting over aging cast-iron drain lines and original galvanized supply pipes. When those pipes weep, sweat, or fail slowly, the moisture migrates into subfloor assemblies and wall cavities before anyone notices.

The transit-oriented development around Rahway Train Station adds a different profile: multi-unit buildings and mixed-use commercial spaces where a single sprinkler activation or a slow roof drain can affect multiple tenants and shared wall assemblies. Mold in those settings spreads faster because HVAC systems are often shared or closely interconnected.

New Jersey's humid summers accelerate the problem. When outdoor humidity climbs above 60% — a routine condition from June through September — basements and crawl spaces that lack proper vapor barriers become incubators. Homes in the 07065 ZIP code that flooded during Ida and were dried with consumer-grade fans rather than professional desiccant dehumidifiers are especially vulnerable to recurring growth.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Rahway

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection — thermal imaging and pin-type meters to locate active wet zones behind walls, under flooring, and inside ceiling assemblies before any demolition begins. In Rahway's older homes, that step matters more than it does in newer construction because water travels farther through balloon-frame cavities before it pools somewhere visible.

Once the scope is confirmed, we establish negative-pressure containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This keeps spores from migrating to unaffected rooms while remediation is underway — critical in occupied homes and multi-unit buildings near the Main Street business district where adjacent units share air pathways. Affected materials — drywall, insulation, wood framing with active growth — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per EPA guidelines. Structural surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial, and dried to confirmed moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

As an IICRC Certified Firm (#210213), we follow the S520 Standard for Professional Mold Remediation throughout. That protocol matters when your insurance adjuster or a future home buyer's inspector reviews the remediation documentation.

## Rahway Insurance Coordination

Mold claims tied to a sudden and accidental water loss — a burst pipe, an appliance failure — are generally coverable under standard homeowner's policies. Mold that developed gradually from a slow leak or deferred maintenance is more likely to be disputed. We document the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected assemblies, and a written scope of work that gives your adjuster the information needed to process the claim. We work directly with most major carriers and can communicate with your adjuster on your behalf to reduce the back-and-forth during an already stressful situation.
