## Why Elizabeth Properties Make Contents Losses More Complicated

Elizabeth's housing stock is overwhelmingly early-1900s multifamily — two- and three-family frame homes built close together, with shared walls, stacked units, and plumbing chases that run through all three floors. A single pipe failure or a stovetop fire doesn't stay contained. Smoke migrates through gaps in original plaster, water follows the path of least resistance through old floor joists, and by the time one household calls for help, a neighboring unit's contents are already affected.

## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Elizabeth

The process starts before anything leaves the building. Every item in the affected area is photographed, inventoried, and assigned a condition rating. This documentation matters for insurance purposes and for tracking what goes where — especially in multi-unit buildings where one landlord's property and a tenant's personal belongings may be intermingled in the same space.

Once the inventory is complete, contents are carefully packed and transported to our climate-controlled facility for cleaning, deodorization, and drying. Depending on the loss type, that may mean ultrasonic cleaning for hard goods, ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor-bearing soft goods, or document drying and digitization for papers and photographs. Items that can safely stay on-site are treated in place to avoid unnecessary handling.

When the structure is cleared and repaired, contents are returned in the same organized condition they left — matched to the inventory, placed back in the rooms they came from.

## Insurance Documentation for Elizabeth Losses

Contents claims are where insurance disputes most often arise. Carriers want itemized inventories with pre-loss value estimates, photos of damage, and a clear record of what was restored versus what was deemed a total loss. We produce that documentation as a standard part of the pack-out process — not as an add-on. For landlord policies covering multi-unit buildings in the 07201 and 07206 ZIP codes, where a single loss event may generate claims from both the property owner and individual tenants, having a single organized inventory from one company simplifies the adjustment process considerably.
