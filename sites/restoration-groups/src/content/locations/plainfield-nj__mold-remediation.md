## Why Plainfield Properties See More Mold Problems

The city's celebrated Victorian and Queen Anne homes — concentrated in areas like the Van Wyck Brooks Historic District and Netherwood — were built with materials and construction methods that respond to moisture very differently than modern builds. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Plainfield

Every job starts with a thorough inspection — not just the obvious wet area, but the adjacent wall cavities, subfloor, and HVAC returns. In Plainfield's older homes, mold rarely stays where the leak was. It follows the framing.

Once we've mapped the affected zone, we establish negative-air containment using 6-mil poly barriers and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers. This matters especially in homes where original plaster and lathe construction means disturbing one wall can send spores through gaps that simply don't exist in modern drywall builds. Contaminated materials — drywall sections, insulation, affected framing — are bagged and removed per EPA guidelines before any surface treatment begins.

Drying and treatment follow IICRC S520 standard protocols. We use commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and antimicrobial applications on affected structural surfaces, then re-test air quality before containment comes down.
