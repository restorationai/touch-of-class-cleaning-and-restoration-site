## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Paramus

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before a single item leaves the property. Every piece is photographed, logged by condition, and assigned a barcode so its location in our climate-controlled storage facility is traceable throughout the job. That documentation matters for your insurance claim — a detailed contents inventory reduces disputes with adjusters and gives you a clear record of what was present before the loss.

After pack-out, items are assessed individually. Upholstered furniture from a flooded basement gets a different treatment than smoke-affected cabinetry from a kitchen fire. Textiles go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment where appropriate; hard surfaces are cleaned with professional-grade solutions calibrated to the material. Electronics are evaluated by a qualified technician rather than assumed to be a total loss — many components survive water events if they are dried and inspected promptly. Once the structure has been fully dried and cleared, contents are returned, placed, and reassembled.
