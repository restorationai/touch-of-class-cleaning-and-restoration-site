## Why Wayne Properties See Sewage Backup So Often

Wayne's plumbing vulnerabilities are baked into its geography and housing stock. The Hoffman Grove and Mountain View sections sit in the Passaic River floodplain — during Tropical Storm Irene in 2011 and again during Ida in 2021, river surcharging overwhelmed municipal sewer infrastructure and forced raw sewage up through floor drains in hundreds of homes. That pattern repeats on a smaller scale every time the Passaic crests after a nor'easter.

Away from the river, the lake communities tell a different story. Homes around Packanack Lake and Pines Lake are largely midcentury ranches and split-levels with finished lower levels that sit close to the water table. Their sewer laterals — many original to the 1950s and 60s — are cast iron or Orangeburg pipe that has been corroding or collapsing for decades. Root intrusion from mature oaks and maples accelerates the problem. A lateral that holds fine in dry weather can fail completely when saturated soils shift after a wet spring.

Commercial properties along the Route 23 and Route 46 corridors face a separate set of risks: grease trap failures, high-volume drain systems, and sprinkler-line interactions that can introduce sewage-contaminated water into finished retail and restaurant spaces.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Wayne

Every sewage job starts with containment, not extraction — because tracking contaminated water through an unaffected part of the house turns a localized loss into a whole-floor problem. Our IICRC Certified Firm (#210213) technicians establish a work perimeter, suit up in appropriate PPE, and assess the source before touching a pump.

From there, the process follows a sequence calibrated to what we actually find in Wayne homes:

**1. Extraction and bulk removal.** Truck-mounted extraction equipment pulls standing sewage from the floor. Saturated materials — carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation — are removed and bagged for disposal. In finished basements common to the Packanack Lake and Pines Lake neighborhoods, that often means demo to the stud line on lower wall sections.

**2. Surface cleaning and disinfection.** Concrete slabs, block walls, and framing are cleaned with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 losses. We don't skip the block wall cavities — sewage wicks laterally through CMU mortar joints and can harbor bacteria well above the visible waterline.

**3. Drying and dehumidification.** Desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers run until moisture readings in structural materials reach dry standard per the IICRC S500. In Wayne's humid summers, that can take longer than homeowners expect — we monitor daily and document readings for your insurance file.

**4. Post-cleanup verification.** Before we close out, surfaces are tested and a final inspection confirms no residual contamination. Documentation goes to you and, if applicable, directly to your carrier.
