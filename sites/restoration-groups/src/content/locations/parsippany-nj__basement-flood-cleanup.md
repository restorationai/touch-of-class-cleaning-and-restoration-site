## Our Basement Flood Cleanup Process in Parsippany

The first step on any flooded basement is containment — stopping the water source if it's still active, then removing standing water with truck-mounted extraction equipment before it migrates further into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies. In Parsippany's split-levels and raised ranches, water often travels horizontally under the slab before it becomes visible, which means moisture mapping with thermal imaging and calibrated meters is essential before any drying equipment is placed.

Once extraction is complete, we establish a drying chamber using commercial-grade LGR dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers sized to the actual cubic footage of the space — not a one-size estimate. Readings are logged daily against the IICRC S500 standard so there's a documented drying record your adjuster can review. Affected drywall, insulation, and flooring are removed to the extent necessary to reach the wet substrate; we photograph and document everything before any material comes out.

For basements with finished ceilings below first-floor bathrooms or laundry — a common configuration in the Troy Hills and Rainbow Lakes neighborhoods — we use cavity drying equipment to dry wall assemblies in place when the structure allows, which reduces tear-out and rebuild cost.
