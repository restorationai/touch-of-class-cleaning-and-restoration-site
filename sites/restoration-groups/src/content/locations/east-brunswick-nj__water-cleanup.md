## Our Water Cleanup Process in East Brunswick

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters tell us what the surface cannot — whether water has tracked behind a finished basement wall in Tanglewood or wicked up into the framing of a Farrington-area colonial's first floor. We document everything before extraction begins, which creates the paper trail your insurance carrier will need.

Extraction comes next. Truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete. Once the bulk water is out, we set drying systems — industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers — positioned based on the actual moisture readings, not a generic room count. We monitor and log drying progress on a defined schedule, adjusting equipment placement as materials dry at different rates. Drying is complete when readings return to established baselines for the material type, not when the floor feels dry underfoot.

If affected materials — drywall, insulation, subfloor — cannot be dried in place within acceptable timeframes, we discuss controlled demolition with you before touching anything. In finished basements, that conversation often involves understanding what was original construction versus a later renovation, since material layers affect both drying time and what's salvageable.
