## Why Morristown's Housing Stock Complicates Fire Damage Recovery

Morristown's appeal is also its restoration challenge. ## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Morristown

Every fire loss starts with a thorough assessment before any cleaning begins. Soot chemistry varies by what burned — synthetic materials produce a dry, powdery residue; proteins from kitchen fires leave a thin, nearly invisible film that smells intensely and bonds to surfaces; wood and structural materials produce wet, oily soot that stains porous surfaces quickly. Identifying the soot type determines which cleaning agents and methods go to work first.

From there, the process moves through these stages:

- **Board-up and structural stabilization** — securing open windows, doors, and roof penetrations to prevent weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while work proceeds
- **Content pack-out** — furniture, textiles, and personal items are inventoried, photographed, and moved to a controlled environment for cleaning or proper disposal
- **Structural soot and smoke removal** — walls, ceilings, framing, and HVAC components cleaned using HEPA vacuuming, dry chemical sponges, and wet cleaning methods matched to the surface
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to break down smoke odor compounds embedded in porous materials, not just mask them
- **Reconstruction coordination** — once the structure is clean and dry, we scope and manage the rebuild, from drywall and trim to full kitchen or room restoration

For homes in the Historic District or near the Ford Mansion corridor, we pay particular attention to salvaging original millwork, built-ins, and plaster details wherever structurally sound — replacement in kind for historic materials is both more expensive and harder to source, so preservation is always the first option.
