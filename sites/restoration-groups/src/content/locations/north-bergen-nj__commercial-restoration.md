North Bergen's commercial stock is older and denser than most Hudson County neighbors realize. Tonnelle Avenue's big-box and light-industrial properties carry high-volume HVAC and sprinkler systems that, when they fail, can release thousands of gallons before an alarm triggers a shutdown.

## Our Commercial Restoration Process in North Bergen

Every commercial job starts with a scope-of-loss assessment that accounts for occupancy — a retail space on Bergenline that needs to reopen by Monday morning is a different project than a vacant warehouse off Tonnelle that can be dried over five days. We document the loss with moisture mapping, thermal imaging, and photo documentation formatted for insurance carriers before a single piece of equipment is placed.

For water losses, we extract standing water, establish drying zones, and deploy commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the actual cubic footage — not residential units dragged into a commercial space. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with daily moisture readings logged so the adjuster has a defensible drying record. For fire and smoke losses, we assess char depth, soot type (wet vs. dry), and HVAC contamination before beginning any cleaning — smoke that's been pulled through a commercial air handler can re-deposit on surfaces in rooms that never saw flames.

We coordinate directly with your insurance carrier and, where applicable, your property management company or commercial landlord, so you are not acting as the relay between three parties while also trying to run a business.
