## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in Morristown

The first priority on any flooded basement call is stopping active water intrusion before extraction begins. If the source is a failed sump pump or a backed-up floor drain during a storm event, we assess whether the water table has equalized before we pump — pulling water too fast from a hydrostatic situation can crack older foundation walls, which is a real concern in the rubble-stone basements common near the Morristown Green.

Once the source is controlled, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration:

- **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from carpet, concrete, and subfloor assemblies. In finished basements with engineered hardwood or laminate, we probe moisture content before deciding whether flooring can be dried in place or must be removed.
- **Structural drying** — industrial desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers combined with high-velocity air movers create a controlled drying environment. Older homes with plaster-on-lath walls (common in the Historic District) require extended drying schedules — plaster holds moisture longer than drywall and releases it unevenly, so we take daily moisture readings rather than running on a fixed-day clock.
- **Antimicrobial treatment** — standing water in a basement, especially after a sewer backup or river flooding event, introduces bacteria and begins mold colonization within 24–48 hours. We apply EPA-registered antimicrobial agents to concrete, framing, and any porous materials that were in contact with Category 2 or Category 3 water.
- **Documentation** — moisture maps, photos, and drying logs are compiled throughout the job to support your insurance claim.

Most homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water losses — a burst pipe, a washing machine supply line failure, or a sump pump overflow rider if you purchased that endorsement.
