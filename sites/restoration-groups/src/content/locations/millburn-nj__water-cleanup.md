## Our Water Cleanup Process in Millburn

Water cleanup in an older Millburn Colonial or Tudor isn't the same as drying out a 1990s builder-grade ranch. The process has to account for what's behind the walls.

In homes with plaster-over-lath construction — common throughout Short Hills and Wyoming — water wicks laterally along the lath and reads dry at the surface while the substrate stays saturated. ## Local Note

In the plaster-wall homes that dominate Short Hills and Old Short Hills, one thing catches homeowners off guard: the drying timeline is longer than what they've read online. Modern drywall releases moisture relatively quickly under proper airflow. Plaster-over-lath holds water in the lath matrix and in the brown coat beneath the finish surface, releasing it slowly even under aggressive drying conditions. A room that looks and feels dry to the touch may still be holding elevated moisture at the lath level.
