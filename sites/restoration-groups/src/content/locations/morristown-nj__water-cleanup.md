## Our Water Cleanup Process in Morristown

Every water loss is different, but the sequence that protects a Morristown property follows a consistent logic: stop the source, extract the water, document the damage, dry the structure, and verify the result with calibrated readings before packing out.

When we arrive, we use thermal imaging cameras alongside penetrating and non-penetrating moisture meters to map the full extent of saturation — including water that has wicked up behind plaster or traveled under original hardwood subfloors. In older Morristown homes, that mapping step is especially important: water behind plaster can read dry at the surface while the lath beneath stays saturated for days, setting up conditions for mold colonization if drying is cut short.

Extraction comes next, using truck-mounted and portable units calibrated to the space. Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not guesswork. Drying targets follow the IICRC S500 standard, and we document daily moisture readings so your insurance adjuster has a clear, timestamped record of the drying progression.
