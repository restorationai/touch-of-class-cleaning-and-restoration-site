## Why Perth Amboy Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The geography here matters. Perth Amboy sits at the confluence of the Raritan River and Raritan Bay, and the low-lying waterfront blocks — the same ones that took tidal surge during Sandy in 2012 — remain vulnerable to storm-driven sewer surcharging. During a hard nor'easter or a fast-moving summer thunderstorm, the municipal combined sewer system can reach capacity quickly, and that pressure has nowhere to go except back through the lowest fixture in the building: usually a basement floor drain, a laundry tub, or a ground-floor toilet.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Perth Amboy

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water — the highest contamination level — and the cleanup protocol reflects that. We don't treat it like a burst supply line.

**Containment and safety setup.** Before extraction begins, we establish a contamination perimeter, suit up in full PPE, and cut power to any submerged outlets. In older Perth Amboy homes, electrical panels are often located in the same basement that flooded, so this step is non-negotiable.

**Extraction and removal.** Truck-mounted extraction pulls standing sewage from the space. Porous materials — drywall below the flood line, saturated insulation, compromised flooring — are removed and bagged for disposal per NJ Department of Environmental Protection guidelines. We photograph everything before it leaves the site.

**EPA-registered disinfection.** All affected surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents rated for Category 3 contamination. In Perth Amboy's older masonry basements, we pay particular attention to mortar joints and exposed brick, which are porous and hold pathogens longer than poured concrete.

**Drying and clearance.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. Our process follows IICRC S500 drying standards. We document readings at every check so your adjuster has a complete record.
