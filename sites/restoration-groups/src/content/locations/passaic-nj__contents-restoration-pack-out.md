## Our Contents Restoration & Pack-Out Process in Passaic

The process begins with a detailed inventory at the loss site. Every item is photographed, logged, and assigned a condition rating before anything is moved. In Passaic's narrow walkups — buildings where stairwells are tight and hallways are shared — this step also involves coordinating with landlords or property managers to protect neighboring tenants' belongings and common areas during pack-out.

Salvageable contents are carefully packed, loaded, and transported to a climate-controlled facility where restoration specialists assess each item individually. Textiles go through thermal or ozone deodorization. Hard goods and furniture are cleaned using methods matched to the material — ultrasonic tanks for non-porous items, hand-cleaning for antiques and heirlooms. Documents and photographs, which are common losses in households that have been in the same Passaic building for generations, can often be freeze-dried and digitized if treated quickly.

Throughout the process, the inventory log serves as the backbone of your insurance claim. Our team documents replacement value versus restoration cost for each item, which is the format most carriers require for contents claims under a standard HO-3 or renter's policy.

Once the structure is cleared and dried, contents are returned, placed, and reconciled against the original inventory. Nothing is marked closed until the homeowner or tenant has signed off on the return.

## Local Note

In Passaic's older brick multi-families, especially those built before World War II, wood-framed interior walls are often covered with multiple layers of wallpaper rather than modern drywall. When smoke or water penetrates these walls, the paper layers trap odor and moisture in ways that aren't visible from the surface. Contents near these walls — particularly upholstered pieces pushed against them — absorb secondary contamination even after the primary source is addressed. Experienced pack-out crews flag these items for deeper deodorization treatment rather than standard cleaning, which prevents odor from returning weeks after a homeowner moves back in.
