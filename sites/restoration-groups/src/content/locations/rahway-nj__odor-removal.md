## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Rahway

Effective deodorization isn't a single treatment — it's a sequence of methods matched to the odor source, the affected materials, and the building's construction. The process typically follows this order:

**Source removal first.** No deodorization technology eliminates an odor that's still being generated. Charred wood, contaminated insulation, and saturated drywall are removed before any treatment begins.

**Thermal fogging** penetrates the same pathways smoke or odor-laden air traveled through — wall voids, subfloor cavities, HVAC ductwork. The fog particles are sized to reach surfaces that sprays and wipes cannot. This is particularly effective in Rahway's older homes, where smoke from a kitchen fire can migrate through gaps in original plaster lath and settle in adjacent rooms.

**Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules at a chemical level. Unlike ozone treatment, hydroxyl generators are safe to run in occupied or semi-occupied spaces and do not require building evacuation — an important consideration for multi-unit properties near the Merck & Co. campus corridor or in the Hazelwood neighborhood where owner-occupied duplexes are common.

**Ozone treatment**, when conditions permit full evacuation, delivers high-concentration oxidation for severe smoke or sewage odors embedded in structural materials. Re-entry protocols are followed strictly after treatment.

**HEPA air scrubbing** runs continuously throughout the process to capture particulates released during source removal and treatment.

All drying and deodorization work is performed to IICRC standards — the same protocols insurers and adjusters reference when evaluating claims.
