## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Bridgewater

Every call starts with a rapid site assessment. When the crew arrives, the first priority is identifying all breach points — broken windows, compromised doors, sections of missing roof deck — before any material goes up. In Bridgewater's older ranch and split-level stock, that often means checking crawlspace vents and garage side doors that aren't obvious from the street but are just as exposed.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for extended outdoor exposure, secured with furring strips screwed into the decking — not just weighted at the edges. On steeper-pitch roofs common in the newer colonial developments near Chimney Rock Park, we rig anchor points that hold through sustained wind rather than relying on gravity alone. Tarps are sized to overlap the ridge and extend well past the damaged area so wind-driven rain can't find an edge.

Board-up on windows and doors uses 5/8-inch plywood cut to fit the opening, secured with structural screws into the framing — not stapled to the siding. For fire damage board-up where the frame itself is compromised, we sister in temporary framing before the panel goes up. Every opening gets photographed before and after for your insurance file.
