## Why Ridgewood's Older Housing Stock Matters for Biohazard Remediation

Ridgewood's architectural character — grand Victorians, Tudors, and colonials built across several decades of the early twentieth century — creates conditions that affect how biohazard remediation is performed. Porous plaster walls, original hardwood subfloors, and unfinished stone or brick foundation areas in deep finished basements can absorb biological material in ways that modern construction does not. Surface cleaning alone is insufficient when contamination has reached a substrate that wicks and holds moisture. Proper remediation in these homes requires material assessment, controlled removal where necessary, and treatment of underlying surfaces — not just what's visible.

The compact layout of many Ridgewood properties also means that affected areas are often adjacent to living spaces, shared HVAC returns, or finished rooms that need to be protected during the remediation process. Containment barriers and negative air pressure are standard parts of how we work, regardless of the size of the affected area.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Ridgewood

Every biohazard call begins with a confidential assessment. A certified technician evaluates the scope of contamination, identifies affected materials and surfaces, and outlines a remediation plan before any work begins. From there, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Containment**: The affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination with unaffected parts of the property.
- **Personal protective equipment and regulated waste handling**: All materials that cannot be decontaminated are collected, packaged, and disposed of through licensed medical waste channels — not standard trash removal.
- **Surface treatment**: EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces. Porous materials that cannot be adequately decontaminated are removed and documented.
- **Clearance verification**: We confirm that treated areas meet remediation standards before containment is removed and the space is returned to the family or property manager.
