## Our Burst Pipe Cleanup and Repair Process

The first thing we do on arrival is stop the loss — confirm the water is off, identify whether a pressure-reducing valve or secondary shutoff is involved, and assess whether the break is on the supply side or a drain line. Supply-line breaks are under pressure and dump far more water in a short window; drain failures tend to be slower but often go undetected longer.

From there, the process moves through four stages:

1. **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors pull standing water from hard floors, carpet, and subfloor assemblies. In split-levels and raised ranches common to the 08854 ZIP code, we trace water migration through floor cavities with thermal imaging before assuming the visible wet area is the full extent.
2. **Pipe repair or isolation** — our licensed crew repairs the failed section or installs an isolation fitting to restore water service to the rest of the structure while drying is underway. We do not subcontract this step.
3. **Structural drying** — commercial-grade LGR dehumidifiers and axial air movers are positioned to dry wall cavities, subfloors, and framing to IICRC S500 standard moisture targets. Drying typically runs three to five days depending on material depth and ambient conditions.
4. **Documentation** — moisture readings, equipment logs, and photo documentation are compiled throughout for your insurance carrier.
