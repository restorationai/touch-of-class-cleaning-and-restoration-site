## Our Basement Flooding Cleanup Process in North Bergen

Every job starts with a moisture survey before a single pump runs. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map where water has traveled beyond the visible puddle — under flooring, inside block-wall cavities, and behind the framing of finished basement walls. In North Bergen's older multi-family stock, water routinely migrates between units through shared masonry, so we check adjacent spaces as a matter of course.

Once we understand the full extent, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then transition to structural drying. Drying in North Bergen's dense housing requires careful equipment placement: ceiling heights in pre-war basement units are often under seven feet, and stairwells are narrow. We use low-profile desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside axial air movers positioned to create a directed airflow pattern rather than just blowing humid air around the space. Materials that cannot be dried in place (saturated carpet, compromised drywall, swollen wood subfloor) are removed and disposed of in accordance with NJ solid waste guidelines. We photograph everything before and after removal for the insurance file.
