## Who should I call first for water damage in Kenilworth, NJ?

Call a water damage restoration company first, not your insurance company and not a general contractor. A certified restoration crew stops the damage from spreading, extracts the water, and begins drying your structure within hours. That documented response is also what protects your insurance claim later.

The one exception: if the water source is still flowing, call a licensed plumber before the restoration crew, or at the same time. A burst galvanized supply line or a failed water heater in one of Kenilworth's 1920s-1950s capes needs to be shut off at the source before extraction begins. If you can reach the main shutoff yourself, do it now.

## Who do you call for different types of water emergencies?

Not every water emergency gets the same call. Here is the triage breakdown:

**Active water source (pipe still running, appliance still leaking)**
Shut off the water at the nearest valve or the main. Then call a plumber if the shutoff did not stop the flow. Then call a restoration company. Both can run in parallel once the water is off.

**Electrical hazard (water near a panel, outlets, or appliances)**
Do not enter the area. Call your utility company (PSE&G for most of Kenilworth) or 911 first. Once the power is confirmed off, call restoration.

**Gas smell alongside the water**
Leave the building immediately. Call 911 and your gas utility from outside. Do not re-enter until cleared. Restoration comes after.

**Sump pump failure or basement flooding from Black Brook backup**
This is the most common Kenilworth call, especially after a hard summer storm. No safety hazard beyond wet floors. Call a restoration company directly. Extraction, drying, and a moisture assessment are the first steps. If you want to see what a full basement flood response looks like, the [Burst Pipe Emergency Checklist](/blog/burst-pipe-emergency-checklist/) covers the same sequence step by step.

**Sewage backup**
Category 3 water, also called black water, carries pathogens and requires full PPE and sanitization, not just extraction. Call a restoration company that handles sewage cleanup specifically. Do not attempt to clean this yourself.

**Storm damage with a breached roof or broken windows**
Call a restoration company that offers emergency board-up and tarping. Stopping additional water intrusion is the first job before any interior drying begins.

## What happens when you call for water damage restoration in Kenilworth?

A restoration company answers your call, asks a few triage questions (what is the source, how long has the water been present, is the area safe to enter), and dispatches a crew. Here is what to have ready:

- Your address and the best way to reach you
- Where the water is and how deep (ankle, knee, or just surface wet)
- Whether the source is stopped
- Your insurance carrier and policy number if you have it handy (not required to start)

When the crew arrives, they will do a moisture assessment using a calibrated moisture meter and thermal imaging to map the full extent of the damage, including areas that look dry but are not. They will extract standing water, set air movers and LGR dehumidifiers, and establish drying targets based on the [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) for water damage restoration.

For a typical Kenilworth cape or colonial with a flooded basement, the drying phase runs three to five days. A technician checks moisture readings daily and adjusts equipment placement until the structure reaches its dry standard. All readings are logged for your insurance carrier.

## When should I call my insurance company?

Call your insurer after mitigation has started, not before. Your first call should always be to stop the damage. Insurers generally do not penalize you for starting mitigation immediately, and most policies require you to take reasonable steps to prevent further damage. Waiting for an adjuster before extracting water can void that protection.

Once extraction is underway, call your carrier and report the loss. Give them the restoration company's contact information. A good restoration crew will communicate directly with the adjuster, provide moisture logs and scope documentation, and handle the back-and-forth so you do not have to.

Homeowners policies in New Jersey typically cover sudden, accidental water damage: a burst pipe, a failed water heater, an appliance line that let go overnight. They typically do not cover gradual leaks, flooding from outside the home (that requires a separate flood policy through the [NFIP](https://www.fema.gov/flood-insurance)), or sewer backup unless you have a specific endorsement. For a full breakdown, see [Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Water Damage?](/blog/does-homeowners-insurance-cover-water-damage/)

## What to do right now if you have water damage in Kenilworth

Here is the short version:

1. Stop the source if you can reach the shutoff safely.
2. If there is an electrical or gas hazard, get out and call 911 or your utility first.
3. Call a restoration company. For Kenilworth, that number is **(855) 650-7422**.
4. Move valuables and electronics off wet floors if it is safe to do so.
5. Do not run fans or your HVAC to "dry things out" before a professional assesses the damage. Fans can spread contaminated air and push moisture into walls.
6. Document everything with photos before any cleanup begins.
7. Call your insurance carrier once mitigation is underway.
