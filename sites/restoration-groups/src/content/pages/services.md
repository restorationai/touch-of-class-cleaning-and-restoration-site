From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely arrives on schedule. The Restoration Group handles the full arc of recovery — emergency mitigation, structural drying, contents cleaning, and complete reconstruction — for homeowners, commercial property owners, and multi-family building managers across northern and central New Jersey. One company, one point of contact, from the moment damage happens through the day you hand back the keys.

## Emergency restoration services

When the situation is urgent, speed and process both matter. Water damage response starts with extracting standing water and deploying industrial drying equipment before hidden moisture can work into wall cavities, subfloor, or framing — mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. Fire and smoke damage requires a different discipline: smoke residue is acidic and continues etching surfaces long after the flames are out, so stabilization and cleaning begin as soon as the structure is cleared. Biohazard cleanup — sewage backups, trauma scenes, infectious-material incidents — follows strict containment and disposal protocols to protect everyone on site. When severe weather tears off roofing or breaks windows, emergency board-up and storm damage services secure the structure against further loss until permanent repairs begin. All emergency response is available around the clock.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction brings the property back. After drying, cleaning, and demolition of unsalvageable materials, the rebuild phase covers framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work — everything needed to return a space to pre-loss condition or better. Because the same team that documented the damage oversees the rebuild, there are no handoff gaps, no conflicting scopes of work, and no finger-pointing between a mitigation contractor and a separate GC. For commercial clients, that continuity often means a meaningfully shorter timeline before the space is operational again.

## Specialty services

Some situations don't fit neatly into emergency or rebuild categories. Mold inspection and remediation addresses visible growth and hidden colonies identified through moisture mapping and air sampling. Odor removal — from smoke, pets, sewage, or long-term neglect — uses thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment rather than masking agents. Contents pack-out and cleaning moves salvageable belongings off-site for professional restoration while structural work proceeds. Board-up and tarping services protect vacant or storm-damaged properties between the loss event and the start of formal repairs.
