From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen fire that's left soot on every surface, property damage rarely arrives on a convenient schedule. Go Agree Restoration of NC serves homeowners, landlords, property managers, and commercial clients across the Middlesex area and the surrounding communities of central North Carolina — handling everything from the first emergency response through the final coat of paint. One company, one point of contact, start to finish.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke is still settling, or a storm has left your roof exposed, the clock matters. Water damage response focuses on stopping the source, extracting standing water, and drying structural materials before secondary damage — warped subfloors, saturated wall cavities, the early stages of mold colonization — has a chance to take hold. Fire and smoke damage requires a different kind of urgency: smoke residue is acidic and continues etching surfaces long after the flames are out, so content cleaning and structural deodorization need to start quickly. Storm damage services cover wind-driven water intrusion, fallen-debris impact, and the board-up and tarping that protects a compromised structure while permanent repairs are planned. Biohazard cleanup — including trauma scenes, sewage backups, and hazardous material exposure — is handled with the containment protocols and disposal procedures the work demands.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction restores the property. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, the rebuild phase begins: framing, drywall, insulation, flooring, cabinetry, painting, and finish work. Because Go Agree Restoration of NC handles both sides of the process, there's no handoff gap — no waiting for a separate general contractor to get up to speed on what was removed and why. The scope of reconstruction work ranges from a single water-damaged bathroom to full room rebuilds following significant fire or storm loss.

## Specialty services

Not every call is a declared emergency. Mold inspection and remediation addresses growth that's been discovered during a renovation, flagged by a home inspector, or noticed as a persistent musty odor in a basement or crawl space — common in older North Carolina housing stock where humidity and inadequate vapor barriers create the right conditions. Odor removal services go beyond masking: thermal fogging and hydroxyl treatment neutralize smoke, pet, and sewage odors at the molecular level. Board-up and emergency tarping provide immediate structural protection after break-ins, vandalism, or storm impact when permanent repairs can't begin right away.
