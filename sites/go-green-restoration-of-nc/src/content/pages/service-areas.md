Go Agree Restoration of NC serves homeowners and property managers across central North Carolina from its base in Middlesex. Scheduling is handled during business hours — call **(919) 906-5473** to confirm availability and get a crew dispatched to your address. Whether you're dealing with standing water in a crawl space, smoke odor baked into drywall, or a mold colony growing behind a bathroom wall, the first step is always the same: a quick call to find out when we can be on-site.

## Where we work

Middlesex sits in Nash County, roughly midway between Raleigh and Rocky Mount along the US-264 corridor. That central position puts a wide band of the Triangle, the Coastal Plain, and the upper Piedmont within practical driving range. The team regularly works across Wake, Nash, Johnston, Wilson, Edgecombe, and Franklin counties — a stretch of North Carolina where older ranch-style homes on crawl space foundations and newer suburban construction sit side by side, each presenting its own moisture and structural challenges.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to Middlesex — including Rocky Mount, Wilson, Zebulon, Wendell, and Knightdale — are typically the first stops on any dispatch run. From there, coverage extends into the broader Raleigh–Durham metro, as well as communities like Smithfield, Selma, and Tarboro that sit along the region's main highway corridors.

At the outer edge of the coverage area, travel time is longer and scheduling may require more lead time, particularly during busy storm seasons when multiple losses are active simultaneously. I-87, US-64, and US-264 are the main arteries the crew travels; significant congestion around the Raleigh beltline or post-storm road closures can affect order of arrival. Honest scheduling means giving you a realistic window rather than a promise that traffic can break.

Call **(919) 906-5473** during business hours and describe the situation. If the job is outside normal range, you'll get a straight answer about whether it makes sense, not a runaround.
