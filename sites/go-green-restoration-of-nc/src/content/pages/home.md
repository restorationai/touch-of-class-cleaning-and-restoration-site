When water backs up through a crawl space, smoke soaks into drywall, or mold starts spreading behind a bathroom wall, the damage doesn't wait — and neither should the call for help. Go Agree Restoration of NC serves homeowners and property managers in Middlesex and the surrounding area with professional damage restoration work: getting properties dried out, cleaned up, and back to livable condition as quickly as the job can be done right.

## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe in January or a slow leak that finally showed itself as a stained ceiling, standing water and saturated materials need to be extracted and dried before secondary damage (warped floors, structural rot, mold colonization) sets in. The clock starts the moment moisture enters a wall cavity.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Visible char is only part of the problem. Smoke residue penetrates porous surfaces — drywall, insulation, wood framing, soft goods — and the acidic soot keeps corroding metal and discoloring surfaces long after the flames are out. Proper restoration means cleaning and treating what's salvageable, not just painting over it.

**Mold remediation** — North Carolina's humid summers give mold a head start, and it can begin colonizing damp materials within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event. Safe remediation means containing the affected area, removing compromised materials, and addressing the moisture source so the problem doesn't return.

**Storm and structural damage** — Severe weather across Johnston and Nash counties can leave roofs breached, windows blown in, and interiors exposed to rain for hours before the storm passes. Prompt board-up and tarping limits how far the damage spreads while full repairs are scheduled.

## Why homeowners and property managers call us

Restoration work is not a commodity — the difference between a job done correctly and one done quickly-but-wrong often shows up months later as a mold problem, a failed floor, or an insurance dispute. A few things that matter when you're choosing who to call:

**Documentation for your insurance claim.** We photograph affected areas, log moisture readings, and produce the written scope that carriers need to process a claim. Most major insurance companies are billed directly, so you're not writing a large check out of pocket and waiting for reimbursement.

**Honest scoping.** Before any work begins, you get a clear explanation of what's damaged, what the remediation process looks like, and what the realistic timeline is. No vague estimates, no surprise line items.

**Local knowledge.** Middlesex sits in a part of NC where older housing stock — crawl space foundations, pier-and-beam construction, homes with minimal vapor barriers — responds to moisture events differently than a newer slab-on-grade build. That context shapes how we approach drying and remediation work here.
