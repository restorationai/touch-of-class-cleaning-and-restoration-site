## What Should You Actually Look For When Hiring a Mold Company in Raleigh?

The most important factor is whether the company fixes the moisture source, not just the visible mold. Remediating spotting on a ceiling without repairing the roof leak or plumbing issue behind it means the same call in six months.

Ask any company you're considering three questions: Do they run a post-remediation clearance test before calling the job done, following the [IICRC](https://www.iicrc.org) S520 mold remediation standard? Do they document moisture readings with a written scope before starting demolition or containment? And can they handle the underlying repair, whether that's a crawl space vapor barrier, a bathroom exhaust fan, or a roof flashing fix, or will you need to hire a second contractor?

The [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/mold) notes that mold remediation should always start with fixing the water problem; removing mold without addressing moisture is a temporary fix at best. That's the standard worth holding any Raleigh company to, including the ones listed above.

## What Does the Mold Remediation Process Actually Look Like?

A proper mold job follows a sequence: inspection and testing, containment, removal, drying, and clearance verification. Skipping any of these steps is the most common shortcut that leads to a second infestation.

1. **Inspection and testing.** A technician identifies the extent of visible growth and checks for hidden moisture behind walls, under flooring, or in the crawl space using a moisture meter, sometimes paired with air or surface sampling.
2. **Containment.** Poly sheeting isolates the work area and a negative-air HEPA scrubber keeps spores from spreading to the rest of the home during removal.
3. **Removal.** Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subflooring, are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces get HEPA vacuumed and treated.
4. **Drying.** Air movers and dehumidifiers bring the space back to a normal moisture reading, typically under 15% on wood framing.
5. **Clearance.** A final visual check, and often a post-remediation air or surface test, confirms the space is back within normal fungal ecology before rebuild work starts.

Homes in older Raleigh neighborhoods near Duke Park-style construction or crawl-space-foundation builds around the outer zip codes tend to add a step: crawl space encapsulation to prevent the moisture from returning through the foundation vents.

## What Should You Do Next?

Start with an inspection, not a remediation quote sight unseen. A legitimate mold company should be willing to test and document before recommending a scope of work, and should explain the moisture source in plain language before any demolition begins.

If you're seeing spotting, smelling a musty odor, or dealing with a known water event in the last few weeks, moving quickly matters. Mold can begin colonizing within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture, a window covered in more detail in our post on [how fast mold grows after water damage](/blog/how-fast-does-mold-grow-after-water-damage/). Schedule a mold inspection while the problem is still contained to one room rather than waiting for it to spread through the HVAC system.

Whichever Raleigh company you choose, get the scope in writing, ask what standard they remediate to, and confirm they'll address the moisture source, not just the mold you can see.
