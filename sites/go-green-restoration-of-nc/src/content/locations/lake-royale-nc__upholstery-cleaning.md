## Why Lake Royale Furniture Needs Different Care

Lake Royale is a lake community, and homes built around the water tend to run more humid than homes a few miles inland, especially in units that sit closed up during the week and only get used on weekends. That combination of intermittent use and higher ambient moisture is exactly what upholstery fibers don't like. Fabric absorbs the moisture in the air, dust settles into it undisturbed, and by the time a family arrives for a weekend at the lake, cushions can smell musty even though nothing actually spilled or flooded. Boat gear, sunscreen, wet swimsuits on the couch after a day on the water, and sandy feet also add oils and grit that regular vacuuming doesn't pull out. We see this pattern most in homes that are used seasonally rather than year-round, where the furniture sits unattended between visits.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type and testing an inconspicuous spot before anything else touches the piece, since lake houses often mix synthetic blends, natural fibers, and leather recliners under one roof. For fabric sofas and sectionals we use low-moisture hot water extraction, which lifts embedded soil and odor-causing residue without oversaturating the padding underneath, a real concern in furniture that's already been sitting in a humid room. Leather pieces get a separate conditioning step so the material doesn't dry out or crack after cleaning. Drying is assisted with air movers to bring moisture content down quickly, which matters more here than in a climate-controlled inland home because ambient humidity around the lake slows natural air-drying. We finish with a protectant application on request, which helps fabric resist the next round of sunscreen, sweat, or spilled drinks.
