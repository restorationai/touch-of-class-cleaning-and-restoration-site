## Why Wendell Properties See Water Damage Issues

Eastern Wake County sits on heavy Piedmont red clay, which drains slowly and holds water against foundations after the region's frequent summer thunderstorms. Homes built before municipal water expanded into outlying Wendell often still rely on well and septic systems, and a failing pump or a saturated drain field can push water back into a crawlspace for days before a homeowner catches it. Newer construction near town has PVC supply lines and slab foundations, which handle storm runoff differently but are vulnerable to slow leaks at tub surrounds and ice maker lines that go undetected until flooring starts to cup or baseboards swell.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Wendell

We start by extracting standing water with truck-mount or portable extraction units, then move to moisture mapping. In crawlspace homes, that means checking joists and subfloor insulation, which can hold water long after the surface reads dry. In slab homes, we're watching for moisture wicking up through baseboards and into the first few inches of drywall. Air movers and dehumidifiers are set and monitored daily, with drying logged against the IICRC S500 standard until materials return to a normal moisture range. Only then do we move into reconstruction, whether that's replacing subfloor in an older farmhouse or swapping out baseboard and drywall in a newer build off NC-97.
