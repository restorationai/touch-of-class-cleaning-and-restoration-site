## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Knightdale

We start every call the same way: stop the source, assess the extent, and get extraction equipment running before more material absorbs water it can't easily release. In slab homes, that often means pulling baseboards and drilling small weep holes in drywall to let trapped water out of the wall cavity rather than waiting for it to wick upward. In crawl space homes, we're checking vapor barriers, insulation, and joist moisture content directly, since a crawl space can read dry at the vent opening while the framing twelve feet in is still saturated. Structural drying equipment gets placed based on moisture mapping, not guesswork, and we track readings daily until materials return to a normal moisture range, documented per the IICRC S500 water damage standard.
