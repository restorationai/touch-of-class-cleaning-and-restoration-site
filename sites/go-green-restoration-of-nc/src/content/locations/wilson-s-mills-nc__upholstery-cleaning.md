## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type and testing for colorfastness before any solution touches the piece, because a cleaning method that's safe on a poly-blend sectional can strip the finish off aniline leather. For fabric upholstery, we use low-moisture steam extraction that lifts embedded soil and allergens without oversaturating the cushion fill, which is the part of the couch that actually holds humidity the longest in this climate. Leather gets a different treatment entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner followed by a conditioner that restores flexibility instead of leaving a film. Drying time depends on fill density and ambient humidity, so on higher-humidity days we bring in air movers to push drying along rather than let the piece sit damp overnight.
