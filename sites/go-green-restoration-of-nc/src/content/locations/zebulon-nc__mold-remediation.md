## Why Zebulon Properties See Mold Issues

Zebulon's climate is the primary driver. Johnston County averages over 46 inches of rain annually, and summer relative humidity regularly sits above 70% — conditions that allow mold to begin colonizing a wet surface in as little as 24 to 48 hours. That window matters enormously after a slow leak behind a vanity, a condensation problem on HVAC ductwork, or a crawl space that floods during a heavy rain event.

The town's housing stock adds another layer of complexity. Zebulon has a mix of older ranch-style homes built in the 1960s through 1980s and newer subdivisions that expanded rapidly during the growth boom of the 2000s and 2010s. The older homes often have pier-and-beam or block foundations with vented crawl spaces that were standard practice at the time but are now understood to pull humid outside air directly under the living area. That warm, moist air meets cooler subfloor surfaces and condenses — feeding mold colonies on floor joists, insulation batts, and rim boards that homeowners may not discover for years. Newer construction isn't immune either; tighter building envelopes can trap interior moisture if ventilation systems aren't balanced correctly.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Zebulon

Every job starts with a thorough visual inspection and moisture mapping. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find wet material that isn't visible to the naked eye — because treating only what you can see leaves the source intact. Once we understand the full scope, we establish containment using negative air pressure and 6-mil poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating into unaffected rooms during the removal process.

Contaminated porous materials — drywall, insulation, compromised wood framing — are carefully removed, bagged, and disposed of per North Carolina solid waste guidelines. Hard surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed, treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution, and dried to measured moisture content targets rather than a fixed number of days. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the project to capture airborne spores. Before we close out any job, we verify that moisture readings have returned to normal baselines, because remediation without moisture correction is remediation that will need to be repeated.

## Local Note

One pattern we see repeatedly in Zebulon's older neighborhoods is crawl space insulation that was installed with the vapor barrier facing the wrong direction — kraft-faced batts stapled to floor joists with the paper facing down toward the ground instead of up toward the conditioned space. Over time, that installation traps ground moisture against the wood rather than blocking it, and the result is widespread joist mold that looks far worse than the small wet spot that started it. If your home was built before 1990 and you haven't had a crawl space inspection recently, it's worth having someone look before a minor issue becomes a structural conversation.
