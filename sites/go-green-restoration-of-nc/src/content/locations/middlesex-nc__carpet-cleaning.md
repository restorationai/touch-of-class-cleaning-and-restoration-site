## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Middlesex

We start every job with a dry pre-inspection and fiber identification. Not all carpet responds the same way to heat or chemical agitation — older loop-pile and Berber styles common in mid-century Middlesex homes can fuzz or distort if treated the same way as modern cut-pile synthetics. Once we know what we're working with, we apply a pH-balanced pre-treatment to break down the clay-based soiling and any organic staining before hot-water extraction begins.

Hot-water extraction — sometimes called steam cleaning, though the water never actually becomes steam — forces heated water into the pile under pressure and pulls it back out along with dissolved soils, allergens, and residue. We calibrate dwell time and water temperature to the specific fiber and soil load rather than running every room on the same setting. After extraction, we apply a fiber rinse to neutralize any remaining cleaning agents, which is especially important in homes where children or pets spend time on the floor. Depending on airflow and humidity on the day of service, carpets are typically dry and walkable within two to four hours.

## Stain Removal and Carpet Sanitization

Red clay stains are among the most stubborn we encounter in this area, and the instinct to scrub them while wet is exactly the wrong move — it drives the clay deeper into the fiber structure. We treat set clay stains with a targeted enzyme solution that breaks the mineral bond before any agitation. Pet urine and odor removal follows a different protocol: we use UV light to map the full extent of contamination (which is almost always larger than the visible stain), apply a bio-enzymatic treatment to the fiber and backing, and extract thoroughly. Surface deodorizers alone don't eliminate the source — they mask it temporarily, and the smell returns with the next humid stretch of weather.

Carpet sanitization is available as an add-on for households with allergy sufferers or immunocompromised family members. We apply an EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment post-extraction that reduces bacteria, dust mite allergens, and mold spores in the carpet pile.
