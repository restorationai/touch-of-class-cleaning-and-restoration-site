## Why Raleigh Upholstery Holds On to Moisture and Odor

Raleigh's summer humidity regularly sits in a range that keeps textiles from fully releasing moisture on their own, which matters more for upholstery than almost any other surface in the house. Fabric sofas in older Historic Oakwood bungalows, where central air is often a retrofit rather than original equipment, tend to hold onto damp-weather odors longer than newer builds near Brier Creek. Leather pieces, common in the larger North Hills homes, react differently: heat and humidity dry out the natural oils in the hide, which is why leather that looks fine in October can start cracking by the following summer if it's never conditioned. Add in heavy pollen weeks each spring and pet traffic in family rooms, and most Raleigh upholstery needs a deeper clean than a rental carpet-cleaner attachment can manage.

## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start with a fabric and fiber test on a hidden section, because a fabric-safe method for a poly-blend sectional near NC State University student housing is not the same method we'd use on a silk-blend armchair in an older Five Points home. From there:

- **Dry soil removal** with upholstery-rated vacuum tools to pull out pollen, dander, and grit before any moisture is introduced
- **Pre-treatment** matched to the stain type, whether that's a wine spill or general body-oil buildup along armrests and headrests
- **Low-moisture steam extraction** for fabric pieces, calibrated lighter in humid months so the cushion core doesn't stay damp long enough to develop odor
- **Conditioning treatment** for leather furniture to replace oils lost to heat and sun exposure, especially on pieces that sit near south-facing windows
- **Controlled drying**, sometimes with a small air mover, so the piece is usable again the same day rather than staying damp overnight

This calibration matters more in Raleigh than in drier climates. Over-wetting a sofa here, even slightly, gives ambient humidity enough of a head start to cause a sour smell within 24 to 48 hours.
