## Why Zebulon Properties See Insulation Problems

Johnston and Wake County's climate is the underlying driver. Zebulon sits in a humid subtropical zone where summer humidity regularly pushes above 80%, and that moisture finds its way into crawl spaces and attics alike. Homes in the 27597 ZIP code — especially those built before 1990 on pier-and-beam foundations — were often insulated with unfaced fiberglass batts stapled between floor joists. That material has a short useful life when it's exposed to ground moisture vapor from an unsealed dirt crawl space. Over time, the batts sag, fall, or become saturated, and at that point they're holding moisture against your subfloor rather than keeping heat out.

Attic insulation in Zebulon faces a different but related problem. The region's temperature swings — hot, wet summers followed by cold snaps that can drop into the teens — cycle insulation through expansion and compression repeatedly. Blown-in fiberglass or cellulose that was installed at the correct depth years ago may have settled to half its original R-value. An attic that should be performing at R-38 might be delivering R-20 or less, and you'll feel it in your HVAC runtime before you ever see it.
