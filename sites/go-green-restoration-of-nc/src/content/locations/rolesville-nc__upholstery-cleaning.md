## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process for Rolesville Fabrics and Leather

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, because a synthetic blend sectional and a genuine leather recliner need completely different treatment. For fabric upholstery, we pre-vacuum to lift dry soil, apply a fabric-safe pre-conditioner to break down oils and protein stains (common with pet owners and households with young kids), then hot water extract using low-moisture steam cleaning equipment calibrated to avoid oversaturating cushions and frames. Leather upholstery gets a different approach entirely: a pH-balanced cleaner, gentle agitation, and a conditioning step to keep the hide from drying out or cracking, which matters more here than in drier regions because North Carolina's humidity swings can make leather brittle if it's not conditioned regularly. Drying times in this climate run longer than in arid states, so we use airflow equipment to speed evaporation and prevent the musty smell that lingers in fabric left damp too long in humid air.
