## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Zebulon

When we arrive at a Zebulon property, the first task is stopping any ongoing source — shutting off the water main, identifying the failed component, or confirming the storm event has passed. From there, the work follows a structured sequence:

**Moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly how far water has traveled. In Zebulon's older ranch homes, water that enters a hallway bathroom often wicks along the subfloor toward the kitchen before it becomes visible — mapping it accurately prevents incomplete drying.

**Water extraction.** Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from hard surfaces, carpet, and pad. Crawl spaces under Zebulon homes frequently hold several inches of water after a plumbing failure or heavy rain event, and we extract those separately before addressing the living areas above.

**Structural drying.** Commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers are positioned based on the moisture map, not placed randomly. We set drying targets and monitor readings daily, adjusting equipment placement as materials dry unevenly.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, equipment placement, and daily log entry is recorded and formatted for insurance submission. We work directly with adjusters to provide the documentation carriers need to process claims without unnecessary back-and-forth.
