## Why Youngsville Properties See Water Damage Issues

Franklin County's clay-heavy soil doesn't drain the way sandy or loamy soil does. During heavy rain it holds water close to the surface, which pushes groundwater against foundations and crawlspace vents that weren't built to handle sustained saturation. A lot of Youngsville's older housing stock, especially homes built before the area's recent growth, sits on raised crawlspace foundations rather than slabs. That's a mixed blessing: crawlspaces keep living space off the ground, but they also give water a hidden place to pool under subfloor insulation for weeks before anyone notices the smell.
