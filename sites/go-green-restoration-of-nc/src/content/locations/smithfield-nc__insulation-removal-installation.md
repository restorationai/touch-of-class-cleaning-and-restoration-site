## Our Insulation Removal & Installation Process in Smithfield

Every project starts with a physical inspection — not a phone estimate. We walk the crawl space and attic to document existing insulation depth and condition, check for moisture intrusion or mold colonization, and assess vapor barrier integrity before any material is pulled.

For crawl space insulation replacement, we remove all compromised batts, bag and dispose of them properly, and address any standing moisture issues before new material goes in. For attic insulation removal, we use commercial-grade vacuum equipment to extract settled or contaminated blown-in material without spreading particulates through living spaces.
