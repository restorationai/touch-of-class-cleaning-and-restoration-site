## Our Carpet Cleaning Process in Wilson

A thorough cleaning on a Wilson property starts before any water touches the carpet. Hot water extraction (commonly called steam cleaning) is the core of the process. Our truck-mounted units heat water to temperatures that kill bacteria and dust mites without saturating the backing — a critical distinction in Wilson's climate, where over-wet carpet in summer humidity can take 24 hours or longer to dry and risks mildew forming in the pad before the surface feels dry to the touch. For stain removal, we identify the stain type before choosing a treatment. Tannin stains from sweet tea or wine respond to a different chemistry than protein-based stains from pet accidents or food. Treating them the same way is the most common reason DIY attempts set a stain permanently.
