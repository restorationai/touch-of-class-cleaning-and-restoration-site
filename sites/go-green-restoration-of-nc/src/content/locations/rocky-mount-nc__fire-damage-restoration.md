## Why Rocky Mount Properties See Fire Damage Differently

The housing stock across Rocky Mount spans everything from mid-century brick ranches to newer construction near NC Wesleyan University, and each era presents its own complications after a fire. Older homes — particularly those built before the 1980s — often used knob-and-tube or aluminum branch wiring that may have contributed to the fire and will need evaluation before any restoration work can proceed. Plaster walls and original hardwood subfloors absorb smoke odor more deeply than modern materials, which means surface cleaning alone won't eliminate the smell. The region's humidity compounds this: soot that might dry and flake off in an arid climate stays tacky and penetrates deeper into porous surfaces here, making chemical sponging and thermal fogging more critical steps than they would be further west.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Rocky Mount

After a fire, the first priority is stabilization — boarding openings, tarping the roof if the structure is compromised, and preventing rain or humidity from turning a fire loss into a combined fire-and-water claim. Once the site is secured, the work moves through a structured sequence:

- **Damage assessment and documentation** — every affected surface is photographed and catalogued for the insurance claim before anything is moved or removed.
- **Debris removal and dry demolition** — charred materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of properly. Structural members are evaluated for integrity before crews work beneath them.
- **Soot and smoke residue cleaning** — dry chemical sponges handle loose soot; HEPA-filtered vacuums capture fine particulate. Wet cleaning follows on surfaces that can tolerate it.
- **Odor neutralization** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach into wall cavities and ductwork where surface cleaning cannot. In Rocky Mount's climate, skipping this step means odor returns with the next humid stretch.
- **Structural drying and moisture control** — even a fire loss involves water from suppression. Drying equipment runs until moisture readings confirm the structure is within acceptable range before any rebuild begins.
- **Reconstruction** — framing, drywall, painting, flooring, and finish work are handled in-house so the job doesn't stall waiting on a separate contractor.
