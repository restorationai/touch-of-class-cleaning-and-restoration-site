## Fabric, Leather, and Steam: Matching Method to Material

Leather upholstery is common on sectionals and recliners throughout Archer Lodge, and it needs conditioning after cleaning, not just extraction, or the material dries out and cracks along the seams. We clean leather with pH-balanced products and follow with a conditioner suited to the finish, whether that's aniline, semi-aniline, or a protected top coat. Fabric upholstery, including microfiber and natural fiber blends, generally responds well to steam cleaning, but wool and silk blends require a gentler, low-moisture process to avoid shrinkage or watermarking. We flag delicate or antique pieces before we start so there's no surprise about which method we're using and why.
