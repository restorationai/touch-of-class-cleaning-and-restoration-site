## Our Upholstery Cleaning Process

We start by identifying the fabric or leather type, since a cotton-blend sofa and an aniline leather chair need completely different chemistry. For fabric pieces, we pre-treat soiled and high-contact areas (armrests, cushion seams), then hot-water extract using low-moisture methods that pull dirt and allergens out without oversaturating the padding underneath. That low-moisture step matters more in Durham than in drier regions, because a cushion that stays damp for even a day in a humid house is an invitation for mildew to take hold before the fabric fully dries. Leather upholstery gets cleaned and conditioned separately, with attention to cracking and sun-fade patterns common on pieces positioned near south-facing windows. Drying is accelerated with airflow equipment, and we check moisture levels before considering a job complete rather than guessing based on how the fabric feels on top.
