## Why Durham Properties See Elevated Fire Damage Complexity

Durham's housing stock tells the story of several different eras of construction, and each era brings its own fire damage profile. The craftsman bungalows clustered around Duke Park and Trinity Park were built with old-growth lumber — dense, resinous wood that chars slowly but holds smoke compounds tenaciously. Plaster-and-lath walls, common in homes built before the 1950s, don't just absorb soot on the surface; smoke particulates migrate into the wall cavity and settle on the lath strips behind the plaster face. You can repaint the plaster and still smell the fire six months later.

Durham's climate adds another layer. The region's humid summers mean that after a fire is extinguished, residual moisture from suppression water combines with heat-damaged materials to create ideal conditions for secondary mold growth — sometimes within 48 to 72 hours. Fire damage here rarely means fire damage alone; it almost always means coordinating smoke remediation, structural drying, and mold prevention in the same scope of work.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Durham

The first step on any fire loss is a thorough assessment — not just of what burned, but of what the smoke reached. Smoke travels through HVAC systems, settles in closets on the opposite end of the house, and coats insulation inside wall cavities. We use thermal imaging and air sampling to map the full extent of contamination before a single item is moved.

From there, the process moves through controlled demolition of unsalvageable materials, dry and wet soot removal using HEPA-filtered equipment, and odor neutralization with hydroxyl generators or thermal fogging depending on the material types involved. Structural members that are charred but still load-bearing are evaluated for retention — in older Durham homes, replacing original old-growth framing is often more disruptive than treating and encapsulating it. Every decision is documented with photographs and written scope notes, which feeds directly into the insurance claim file.

Content pack-out — removing salvageable belongings for off-site cleaning — is coordinated early so that personal items aren't sitting in a smoke-saturated environment while structural work proceeds.
