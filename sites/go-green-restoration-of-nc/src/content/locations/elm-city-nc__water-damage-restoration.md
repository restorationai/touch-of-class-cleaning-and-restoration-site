## Our Water Damage Restoration Process

We start with extraction, pulling standing water with truck-mount and portable units depending on how much access the property allows, since many Elm City driveways and rural lot layouts don't accommodate a full-size truck close to the door. From there we check moisture levels in subflooring, crawlspace framing, and wall cavities with meters rather than guesswork, because a floor that reads dry on the surface can still be holding water in the joists underneath. Structural drying follows, using air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space and run on a schedule we track daily, adjusting as the materials give up moisture. For crawlspace-heavy homes, we pay particular attention to vapor barriers and vented foundation walls, since trapped moisture under the house is often the real source of a musty smell showing up in the living space weeks after the original leak.
