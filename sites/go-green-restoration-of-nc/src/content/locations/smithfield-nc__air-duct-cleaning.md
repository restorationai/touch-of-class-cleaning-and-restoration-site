## Our Air Duct Cleaning Process in Smithfield

The process starts with a camera inspection of the main trunk lines and branch runs before any equipment is connected. This tells us whether we're dealing with standard dust accumulation, biological growth, or construction debris from a recent renovation — each requires a different approach.

For most residential systems, we use negative-pressure extraction: a high-powered vacuum unit creates suction at the main return while technicians work through each supply register with rotary brush agitation. This pushes debris toward the collection point rather than redistributing it into the living space. For systems showing signs of microbial growth — common in Smithfield homes that have had any history of crawl-space moisture intrusion — we follow extraction with an EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment applied to interior duct surfaces.

The full process on a standard single-family home typically runs three to five hours. We seal registers as we go, so the rest of the system stays under negative pressure throughout.
