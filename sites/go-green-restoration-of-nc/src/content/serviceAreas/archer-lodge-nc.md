## Restoration emergencies common in Archer Lodge

Archer Lodge sits in Johnston County, where heavy summer thunderstorms and the remnants of Atlantic hurricanes can drop several inches of rain in a short window. Many homes here sit on crawlspaces rather than full basements, and in a county with a mix of clay and sandy loam soils, saturated ground can push water laterally into a crawlspace long after the rain stops. That combination, slow-draining soil plus crawlspace construction, is why we see water intrusion and the musty crawlspace smell that follows it more than almost any other call type in this area. Winter brings occasional hard freezes that catch older copper and galvanized supply lines off guard, and summer humidity means a water event that sits even a day or two can start supporting mold growth on drywall and framing.
