From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely arrives on a convenient schedule, and the decisions you make in the first hours shape how well the building recovers. Arch Environmental Group LLC handles the full arc of that recovery: emergency mitigation, environmental testing and abatement, and structural reconstruction. We serve homeowners, commercial property owners, landlords, and property managers across Kingsburg and the surrounding San Joaquin Valley.

## Emergency restoration services

When water, fire, or an unexpected hazard forces you out of your normal routine, the priority is stopping the damage from spreading. Standing water under flooring can begin warping subfloor panels within hours; smoke residue from even a contained kitchen fire bonds to wall cavities and HVAC surfaces faster than it looks. Our emergency services cover water damage extraction and structural drying, fire and smoke damage stabilization, biohazard and trauma scene cleanup, and storm or wind damage response. Each service starts with a thorough assessment so the scope of work is documented before a single piece of equipment rolls in, a step that matters both for your peace of mind and for any insurance claim that follows.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the bleeding; reconstruction restores the building. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry, stable, and clear of contaminants, our team moves into the rebuild phase, replacing drywall, flooring, cabinetry, insulation, and structural framing as needed. Because we handle both phases under one roof, there is no handoff gap where a second contractor has to re-document what the first one found. That continuity keeps timelines tighter and gives insurers a cleaner paper trail from initial loss to final inspection.

## Specialty services

Some of the most consequential work on a restoration project happens before demolition begins. Arch Environmental Group LLC holds certifications as a Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector/Assessor, and our NIOSH 582 microscopist credential covers fiber identification for asbestos-containing materials. In the San Joaquin Valley's older housing stock, much of it built before federal asbestos and lead-paint regulations tightened, those credentials are not a formality; they determine whether a renovation can legally proceed. We also provide mold inspection and assessment, indoor air quality sampling, odor source identification, and emergency board-up and tarping to secure a property after sudden damage.

## One call from loss to livable

The restoration industry has a habit of splitting mitigation and reconstruction into separate contracts, which leaves property owners coordinating between two companies while the clock runs on temporary housing or lost business revenue. Our integrated approach means the same team that assesses the damage, removes the hazardous materials, and dries the structure is also the team that rebuilds it.
