Whether you've found a suspect material before a renovation, are worried about your indoor air, or have a mold concern after a leak, the fastest path to answers is a phone call. Reach Arch Environmental Group LLC directly at **(559) 296-2088**.

## For time-sensitive testing

Some testing can't wait. A post-flood mold assessment matters most while the 24-to-48-hour colonization window is still open, and a pre-renovation asbestos or lead survey has to land before your contractor's start date. If your situation is time-sensitive, call **(559) 296-2088** during business hours and we'll get you scheduled as quickly as availability allows. The sooner the testing happens, the sooner you have documented answers to act on.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If you're planning ahead, requesting a mold inspection, asking for a testing estimate, or following up on a previous report, email us at **info@archenviroservice.com** or use the contact form on this page. We aim to respond to all non-urgent inquiries within one business day. Having a few details ready when you reach out (the concern you're testing for, the approximate size of the area, and whether an insurance claim is open) helps us give you a more useful first response.
