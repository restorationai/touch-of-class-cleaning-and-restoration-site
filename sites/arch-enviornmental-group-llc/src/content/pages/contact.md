Whether you're dealing with standing water, smoke damage, or a mold concern, the fastest path to answers is a phone call. Reach Arch Environmental Group LLC directly at **(559) 296-2088**.

## For urgent situations

If you have an active loss, a burst pipe, sewage backup, fire damage, or anything that's getting worse by the hour, call **(559) 296-2088** during business hours and we'll get you scheduled as quickly as possible. The sooner the call comes in, the sooner we can assess the damage and stop it from spreading to unaffected materials.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If you're planning ahead, requesting a mold inspection, getting a remediation estimate, or asking about a previous claim, email us at **info@archenviroservice.com** or use the contact form on this page. We aim to respond to all non-urgent inquiries within one business day. Having a few details ready when you reach out (the type of loss, approximate square footage affected, and whether an insurance claim is open) helps us give you a more useful first response.
