## Services we handle

**Asbestos Testing & Consulting**
Older homes and commercial buildings throughout the Central Valley were routinely built with asbestos-containing materials, floor tiles, pipe insulation, roofing felt, textured ceilings. Before a renovation or demolition project begins, a certified asbestos consultant collects samples and submits them for laboratory analysis, giving you documentation that protects workers, occupants, and your project timeline.

**Lead Inspection & Risk Assessment**
Paint applied before 1978 may contain lead, and disturbing it, even during a minor repair, can create a hazard for children and adults alike. A formal lead inspection identifies the presence and condition of lead-based paint; a risk assessment goes further, evaluating exposure pathways and recommending next steps.

**Air Quality & Bulk Sampling**
When something in the air is causing headaches, irritation, or unexplained odors, environmental sampling can identify the source. Using NIOSH 582 microscopy methods, we analyze fiber and particulate samples with laboratory-grade precision, the kind of documentation insurers, contractors, and health departments recognize.
