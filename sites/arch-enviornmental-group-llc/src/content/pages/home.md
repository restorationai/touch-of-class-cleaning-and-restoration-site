## Services we handle

**Asbestos consulting and inspection**, Older California homes and commercial buildings frequently contain asbestos in floor tile, pipe insulation, roofing materials, and textured ceilings. A certified asbestos consultant can identify suspect materials, collect samples, and produce the written assessment your contractor or abatement crew needs before any demolition or renovation begins.

**Lead inspection and risk assessment**, Lead-based paint is common in homes built before 1978, and disturbance during remodeling can create a hazard for anyone on the property. A licensed lead inspector and assessor evaluates painted surfaces, identifies deteriorated areas, and provides the risk assessment report required by lenders, buyers, or local code for renovation work.

**Environmental consulting for contractors and property owners**, Renovation projects, property sales, and insurance claims all generate paperwork that requires a qualified environmental professional to sign off.
