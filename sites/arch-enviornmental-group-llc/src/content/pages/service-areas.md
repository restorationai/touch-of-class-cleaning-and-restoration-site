## Where we work

Kingsburg sits near the geographic center of the San Joaquin Valley, which puts the team within practical reach of a wide corridor running from Fresno south through Tulare and Kings counties. The Valley's flat terrain and interconnected highway grid, Highway 99 being the main spine, means crews can move efficiently between communities without the mountain passes or coastal congestion that slow response in other parts of California. Agricultural and residential properties alike are common calls throughout this region, and the team is familiar with the construction styles and infrastructure typical of both older Central Valley towns and newer suburban developments.

## Coverage by distance

The closest service area surrounds Kingsburg itself, Selma, Fowler, Reedley, and Dinuba are all nearby. Moving outward, the team regularly works in Fresno, Visalia, Hanford, and Clovis. Communities in Tulare County such as Tulare, Porterville, and Exeter fall within the broader coverage footprint as well. At the edges of the service area, response depends on job scope and current crew availability, a large commercial loss or a complex remediation project may justify dispatch to locations that wouldn't make sense for a smaller call. Traffic on Highway 99 through Fresno can affect arrival order during peak hours, so callers from the northern or southern ends of the Valley should factor that in when planning.
