## What we do

Day to day, this work means showing up to properties where something has gone wrong or where a renovation is about to start and someone needs to know what's in the walls before the demo crew arrives. ## Our certifications and licensure

The credentials we hold are specific to environmental consulting, and each one requires demonstrated competency, not just a fee.

**Certified Asbestos Consultant (CAC)** is a California-specific designation that authorizes the holder to conduct asbestos surveys, oversee abatement projects, and certify clearance after work is complete. It's the credential regulators and contractors look for before any demolition or renovation on a building that may contain asbestos-containing materials.

**Lead Inspector/Assessor** certification covers the evaluation of lead-based paint hazards, required under federal and state rules before renovation work on pre-1978 housing, and essential for any property transaction or rental compliance situation where lead is a concern.

**NIOSH 582 Microscopist** is a laboratory-level credential in phase contrast microscopy, the technique used to analyze air samples for asbestos fibers. Having a certified microscopist involved in the analytical process means the fiber counts behind your clearance report are performed to a recognized standard, not estimated.

Together, these certifications cover the full cycle: identify the hazard, document it, oversee its removal, and confirm it's gone.
