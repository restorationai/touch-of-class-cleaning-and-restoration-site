Arch Environmental Group LLC opened in Kingsburg, California in 2026 with a specific focus: the kind of environmental hazard work that most general contractors aren't equipped to handle, asbestos, lead, and airborne particulates that require certified assessment before any demolition or remediation can safely begin. The work is technical, the stakes for occupants are real, and getting it wrong creates liability that follows a property for years.

## What we do

On any given day, that means walking a pre-1978 house in the San Joaquin Valley before a kitchen gut, collecting bulk samples from suspect floor tile or pipe insulation, and writing a report a contractor or property owner can actually hand to a building department. It means sitting across from a homeowner who just found out their renovation has to stop until the popcorn ceiling is cleared, and explaining what the testing process looks like and how long it takes, without padding the timeline or the invoice. Environmental consulting isn't emergency response in the way water damage is, but the calls still come at stressful moments: a real estate transaction about to close, a school district facing an abatement deadline, a landlord who just got a notice from a tenant.

## Our certifications and what they mean

Arch Environmental Group holds credentials that are specific to this work and not interchangeable with general contractor licensing.

**Certified Asbestos Consultant** is a California-specific designation issued through the Division of Occupational Safety and Health (Cal/OSHA). It authorizes the holder to conduct asbestos surveys, collect samples, interpret results, and write management or abatement plans, work that is legally restricted to credentialed consultants in this state.

**Lead Inspector/Assessor** certification covers the federal and state requirements for evaluating lead-based paint hazards. In California, any work that disturbs painted surfaces in pre-1978 residential or child-occupied facilities requires a qualified assessment. This credential means we can identify the hazard, document it properly, and give contractors and owners a clear picture of what the work scope actually requires.

**NIOSH 582 Microscopist** is a proficiency certification for phase contrast microscopy, the method used to analyze air samples for asbestos fibers. Having a certified microscopist in-house means sample analysis follows a recognized quality standard, not just a lab's internal protocol.

These aren't marketing designations. They are the credentials California and federal regulations require before this work can legally be performed or signed off on.
