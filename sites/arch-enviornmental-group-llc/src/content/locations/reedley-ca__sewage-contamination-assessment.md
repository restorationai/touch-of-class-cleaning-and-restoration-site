## Why Reedley Properties Are Vulnerable to Sewage Contamination Events

Reedley's residential areas include a significant share of homes built in the 1950s through 1970s, when cast-iron and clay-tile sewer laterals were standard. Those materials have a finite service life, and decades of the Valley's expansive clay soils shifting through wet winters and dry summers can stress joints and cause root intrusion or partial collapse. A slow-developing lateral failure can go unnoticed until a backup pushes Category 3 water, the industry classification for sewage-contaminated water, into a crawl space, bathroom, or utility room.

The surrounding agricultural economy also means that municipal sewer systems in Fresno County communities like Reedley handle fluctuating loads tied to seasonal packing and processing activity. Pressure surges and system stress during peak harvest periods are a real, if underappreciated, factor in residential backup events.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Reedley

Assessment is not cleanup, it is the documented investigation that tells every party involved what they are actually dealing with. Our process begins with a structured site evaluation: we identify the source of the event, map the affected zones, and determine what building materials and contents were reached by contaminated water.

From there, we collect samples, surface swabs, bulk material samples, and air samples where indicated, and submit them to an accredited laboratory. Results identify the specific bacterial and pathogenic load present, which matters both for remediation scoping and for satisfying insurance carrier requirements. We produce a written assessment report that documents conditions at the time of inspection, sample chain-of-custody records, and lab findings. That report is what your adjuster, your remediation contractor, and, if needed, your attorney will rely on.

In Reedley's older housing stock, we pay particular attention to subfloor assemblies. Tongue-and-groove wood subfloors common in mid-century construction wick contaminated water laterally well beyond the visible wet area. Thermal imaging and moisture mapping help us define the true extent of impact before any remediation scope is written.

## Documentation, Insurance, and What to Expect

Sewage contamination claims are among the more documentation-intensive residential insurance claims. Carriers want evidence of the source, the extent of contamination, and the pre-remediation conditions, not just a contractor's invoice. An independent third-party assessment from a certified consultant carries more weight with adjusters than a remediation contractor's own scope, because there is no financial interest in the findings.

As a Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector/Assessor, Arch Environmental Group also flags a concern that comes up more often than homeowners expect in Reedley's older homes: sewage events that require opening walls or disturbing flooring in pre-1980 construction can expose asbestos-containing materials or lead paint.
