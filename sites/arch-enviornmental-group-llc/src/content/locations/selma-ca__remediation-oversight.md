## Why Selma Properties Face Distinct Remediation Challenges

The San Joaquin Valley's extreme heat does something counterintuitive: it drives moisture into wall cavities and subfloor assemblies during cooler nights, then seals it in once daytime temperatures spike. Older homes in Selma, many built during the postwar agricultural boom when the city was expanding rapidly, often used construction materials and techniques that did not account for this daily moisture cycling. Plaster walls, wood-frame floors over unvented crawlspaces, and flat or low-slope rooflines are common in the older residential neighborhoods throughout the city. These assemblies can harbor mold, asbestos-containing materials, and lead-based paint simultaneously, which means a remediation project that looks straightforward on the surface may require layered scopes of work and careful sequencing to address all hazards without cross-contaminating clean areas.

Agricultural activity in the surrounding Fresno County region also contributes to indoor air quality concerns that are less common in urban markets. Pesticide drift, dust intrusion, and fungal spores associated with field crops can complicate air sampling interpretation during and after remediation, a factor that matters when you are trying to establish a defensible clearance baseline.

## Our Remediation Oversight and Project Management Process in Selma

Third-party remediation oversight means the consultant writing the protocol and monitoring the work is not the same entity doing the physical removal, that separation is the foundation of an unbiased result. The process begins with a site assessment that documents existing conditions: material sampling where asbestos or lead is suspected, moisture mapping, and a written scope of work that specifies containment requirements, personal protective equipment levels, disposal procedures, and clearance criteria.

During active remediation, on-site monitoring confirms that the contractor is following the approved scope. This includes verifying negative air pressure in containment zones, checking that waste is properly bagged and labeled for transport to a licensed disposal facility, and collecting air samples at intervals specified by the project protocol. When work is complete, final clearance sampling, conducted independently from the remediation contractor, provides the documentation that the space is safe to reoccupy and that the project is defensibly closed out.

For commercial properties and landlords managing multiple units in Selma, project management services extend to contractor bid review, schedule coordination, and communication with property owners, tenants, and insurance adjusters throughout the project lifecycle.
