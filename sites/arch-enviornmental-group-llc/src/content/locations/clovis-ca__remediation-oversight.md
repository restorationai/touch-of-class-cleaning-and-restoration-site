## Why Clovis Properties Face Distinct Remediation Challenges

The San Joaquin Valley's dry heat creates a deceptive environment. Surface readings can look clean while contamination persists in wall cavities, beneath flooring, or in attic insulation that was never disturbed during a renovation. Clovis has a substantial inventory of residential and commercial buildings constructed during the postwar growth decades, structures where asbestos-containing floor tile, pipe insulation, and textured ceiling coatings were standard materials. Those same buildings often received cosmetic updates in the 1980s and 1990s that layered new finishes over original materials without proper abatement.

The Valley's agricultural dust load also matters. Elevated ambient particulate means air sampling during and after remediation requires careful baseline calibration, a clearance sample taken without accounting for background conditions can produce a misleading pass. Independent oversight catches that problem before a clearance report is signed.

## What Remediation Oversight and Project Management Looks Like in Practice

Arch Environmental Group operates as the owner's representative on remediation projects, not as the contractor doing the physical work. That separation is the point. Oversight begins before a shovel or scraper touches anything:

**Scope review.** We evaluate the abatement contractor's proposed scope against the hazmat survey findings. If the protocol calls for removing only friable asbestos while leaving non-friable material in place, we assess whether that decision is defensible given the disturbance activity planned.

**Pre-abatement setup verification.** Containment barriers, negative air pressure, HEPA filtration unit placement, and decontamination unit configuration are confirmed before work begins. A containment failure discovered mid-project costs far more than the time spent verifying setup.

**In-progress monitoring.** We conduct periodic site visits during active abatement to verify that work practices match the approved protocol and that workers are following personal protective equipment requirements.

**Clearance and documentation.** Post-abatement visual inspection and air or wipe sampling, collected and analyzed independently, confirm that the space is ready for re-occupancy or reconstruction. Final project documentation is organized for the property owner, their insurance carrier, and any regulatory file.
