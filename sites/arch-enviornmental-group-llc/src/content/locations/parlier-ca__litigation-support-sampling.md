## Why Parlier Properties Generate Environmental Disputes

Fresno County's agricultural corridor, including Parlier and the surrounding communities along Manning Avenue and Highway 99, has a housing stock that reflects decades of rapid, lower-cost construction built to house a growing farmworker population. Many of these residences use slab-on-grade foundations with minimal vapor barriers, making them particularly susceptible to moisture wicking from the ground during irrigation season and winter rains. Older stucco exteriors common throughout the city can develop hairline cracks that admit water without producing an obvious interior stain for months. By the time a tenant notices a musty odor or visible growth, the colonization behind the wall assembly may be well established. These are exactly the conditions that produce habitability disputes, and those disputes require sampling data that can withstand cross-examination.

## Our Forensic Sampling Process in Parlier

Forensic sampling for litigation is not the same as a routine inspection. Every decision, from where samples are collected to how the chain of custody is documented, is made with the assumption that opposing counsel will scrutinize the methodology. Our process begins with a pre-sampling protocol review: we identify the specific allegations in the dispute, map the sampling locations to those allegations, and photograph conditions before anything is disturbed. Air samples, surface swabs, and bulk material samples are collected following AIHA and ASTM-referenced methods, sealed, and submitted to an accredited third-party laboratory under documented chain of custody. As a certified asbestos consultant and lead inspector assessor with NIOSH 582 microscopist credentials, we can also perform fiber identification in-house where microscopy is relevant to the claim, which is particularly useful when older building materials in Parlier properties are involved. A written report is prepared to meet the evidentiary standards attorneys need for deposition, mediation, or trial.
