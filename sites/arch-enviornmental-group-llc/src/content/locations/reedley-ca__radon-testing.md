## Why Reedley Properties Warrant a Closer Look at Radon

The San Joaquin Valley's geology is shaped by alluvial deposits carried down from the Sierra Nevada over millennia. That sediment, a mix of decomposed granite, clay, and silt, can contain naturally occurring uranium and radium at concentrations that vary block by block. In Reedley, older homes built before the 1970s typically have pier-and-beam or partial crawl space foundations, which are more permeable to soil gas than modern monolithic slabs. Crawl spaces that stay damp through Reedley's mild, foggy winters and dry, baking summers create a pressure differential that draws soil gases, including radon, upward into the living space. Homes with original mortar joints, unsealed utility penetrations, or aging vapor barriers are particularly susceptible. Even newer construction on the valley floor is not automatically exempt; slab cracks from soil settlement and unfinished sumps can serve as entry points.

## Our Radon Testing Process in Reedley

Arch Environmental Group LLC conducts radon testing using continuous electronic radon monitors, devices that log hourly radon concentrations over a 48-hour minimum test period. This is meaningfully more informative than a passive charcoal canister, which gives you a single averaged number with no sense of how radon levels fluctuate across the day or in response to weather changes. In Reedley, where overnight temperature inversions during the valley's tule fog season can suppress outdoor air exchange and push indoor radon concentrations higher, that hourly data matters.

The process begins with a closed-house protocol: windows and exterior doors remain closed for at least 12 hours before the monitor is placed and throughout the test period. The monitor is positioned in the lowest livable area of the home, a finished basement if present, or the ground floor, away from drafts, exterior walls, and high-humidity zones like laundry rooms. At the end of the test period, the data is downloaded, reviewed against EPA action-level guidance (4 pCi/L), and delivered in a written report. If levels indicate a concern, the report includes context for next steps, though Arch Environmental Group does not perform radon mitigation, so you receive an objective finding with no financial interest in the outcome.
