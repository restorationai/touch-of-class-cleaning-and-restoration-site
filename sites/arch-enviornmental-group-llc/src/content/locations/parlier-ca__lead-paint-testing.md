## Our Lead Paint Testing and Inspection Process in Parlier

The inspection begins with a room-by-room assessment of painted surfaces, identifying components by type, substrate, and condition. From there, XRF (X-ray fluorescence) analysis is used to measure lead concentration in paint layers without destructive sampling. XRF testing is non-invasive, which matters for homeowners who want answers without putting holes in their walls, and it produces results on-site rather than waiting days for a laboratory.

When XRF readings fall into an inconclusive range or when a surface type requires confirmation, paint chip samples are collected and submitted to an accredited laboratory. The final report documents every tested component, the measured lead levels, the condition of each surface, and a clear summary of whether any component meets the federal definition of a lead-based paint hazard. That report is the document your contractor, your lender, or your county health contact will ask for.

Arch Environmental Group LLC holds certification as a Lead Inspector Assessor, which means the inspection is performed by a credentialed professional, not a general contractor running a field kit. That distinction matters when the report needs to satisfy a lender, a property sale disclosure requirement, or a pre-renovation notification under EPA's Renovation, Repair, and Painting rule.

## Lead Testing Before Renovation: What Parlier Contractors and Homeowners Need to Know

Fresno County follows California's Title 17 regulations alongside federal EPA RRP requirements, and those rules apply to any renovation that disturbs painted surfaces in pre-1978 housing. If you are planning a kitchen remodel, window replacement, or any work that will sand, scrape, or cut into older painted surfaces, a lead inspection before the work begins protects everyone involved: the occupants, the workers, and the contractor's license.

For property owners preparing to sell, a lead inspection report can also simplify disclosure. California's transfer disclosure requirements include lead paint, and having a professional inspection on file gives buyers factual documentation rather than leaving them to assume the worst about an older home.

## Local Note

One pattern that shows up consistently in older Parlier homes is the combination of original wood window frames and subsequent aluminum storm window additions. The original wood frames were almost always painted with lead-based paint, and the addition of the outer storm layer traps moisture between the two frames, accelerating paint breakdown on the interior wood surface.
