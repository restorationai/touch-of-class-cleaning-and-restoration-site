## Why Lemoore Properties Are Worth Testing

The Central Valley's dry summers and temperature swings between seasons do real work on older paint. Heat causes painted surfaces to expand; cool nights contract them. Over years, that cycling produces fine cracks and chalking on exterior wood, the exact conditions where lead-containing paint begins to shed dust. Homes built before 1978 are the primary concern, and Lemoore has a meaningful share of them. Residential construction in Kings County accelerated in the 1950s and 1960s to support the growth tied to Naval Air Station Lemoore, which means a substantial portion of the city's single-family housing stock falls squarely in the pre-1978 window. Older rental properties and duplexes near the historic downtown core are especially common candidates for testing.

Lead hazards are not always visible. Paint that looks intact on the surface can still contain lead at levels that matter during sanding, scraping, or demo. A lab-confirmed result tells you what you are actually dealing with before any contractor starts cutting into walls.

## Our Lead Paint Testing and Inspection Process in Lemoore

Arch Environmental Group LLC holds the combined credential of Lead Inspector Assessor, which means the same professional who tests your property can also interpret the results in regulatory context, not just hand you a lab report.

The process starts with a visual survey of all painted surfaces, documented room by room. From there, we use XRF (X-ray fluorescence) analysis, a non-destructive method that reads lead concentration through the paint layers without gouging or chipping the surface. XRF is fast, accurate, and produces results on-site rather than requiring a multi-day lab turnaround for every reading. Where XRF is inconclusive on a specific substrate, paint chip samples are collected and sent to an accredited laboratory for confirmation.

A full written report follows, identifying the location, condition, and lead concentration of every tested component. That report is formatted to satisfy California Department of Public Health requirements and is the document your contractor, your insurance carrier, or a prospective buyer's lender will ask to see.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Lead Testing

XRF technology is the industry benchmark for lead paint inspection, and it is what we bring to Lemoore properties. The instrument measures lead in milligrams per square centimeter through multiple paint layers simultaneously, which matters on older Central Valley homes where you may be looking at five or six layers of paint applied over decades. Results are logged digitally, tied to the specific building component and location, and compiled into the inspection report.

For surfaces where XRF produces an inconclusive reading, such as certain thin metal substrates or highly curved surfaces, paint chip sampling provides the backup confirmation. Every sample goes to a California-accredited lab.
