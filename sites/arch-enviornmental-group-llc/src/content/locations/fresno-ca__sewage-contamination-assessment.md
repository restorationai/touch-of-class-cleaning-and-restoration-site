## Why Fresno Properties Face Elevated Sewage Contamination Risk

Fresno sits in the San Joaquin Valley on clay-heavy soils that shift seasonally as they absorb and release moisture. That movement puts lateral sewer lines, particularly in neighborhoods with older housing stock, under chronic stress. Homes built during Fresno's mid-century growth periods often have original cast-iron or Orangeburg sewer laterals that have degraded over decades. Root intrusion from the large shade trees planted along older residential streets compounds the problem, narrowing lines until a grease buildup or heavy rain event triggers a backup.

The city's combined infrastructure age means sewage events are not rare. When they happen, Category 3 water, the classification for sewage and grossly contaminated water, does not stay where it lands. It wicks into subfloor assemblies, travels along wall cavities, and in slab-on-grade construction (common throughout Fresno's postwar residential tracts) it has nowhere to drain, pooling beneath flooring materials where it is invisible to the eye but detectable through proper testing.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Fresno

A thorough assessment starts before any remediation begins. Arch Environmental Group's certified consultants collect surface samples, air samples where aerosolization is a concern, and bulk material samples from affected assemblies. Laboratory analysis identifies the specific bacterial and pathogenic load present, not just whether contamination exists, but its extent and concentration.

In Fresno's climate, timing matters acutely. Sampling is conducted with documentation protocols that satisfy both California Department of Public Health guidance and the evidentiary standards most property insurance carriers require when reviewing Category 3 loss claims. We photograph affected areas, map contamination boundaries, and produce a written assessment report that travels with the file through remediation and clearance.

Clearance testing, conducted after remediation is complete, is a separate, independent step that confirms the affected area meets established safety benchmarks before occupants return or reconstruction begins. That separation between assessment and clearance is not a formality; it is the scientific check that protects everyone involved.

California homeowners' policies vary significantly in how they treat sewer backup losses. Some require a separate endorsement; others cover Category 3 events under the base policy with conditions. What most carriers share is a requirement for third-party documentation: an independent assessment report that establishes the scope of contamination before remediation costs are approved.

## Local Note

Fresno's slab-on-grade construction, the dominant foundation type across the city's postwar residential expansion, creates a specific challenge during sewage assessments that is easy to underestimate. Unlike homes with crawl spaces, there is no accessible void to inspect beneath the floor. Contamination that has migrated under a concrete slab can only be detected by lifting flooring materials and sampling the substrate directly, or through targeted moisture and ATP surface testing at the slab perimeter. Assessors who skip this step in Fresno slab homes routinely miss the contamination that matters most.
