## Why Centerville Properties Generate Environmental Disputes

The Central Valley's climate is not gentle on building materials. Summers in the Centerville area regularly push past 100 degrees Fahrenheit, and the seasonal shift to wet winters can drive moisture into structures that spent months baking and contracting. Older wood-frame homes in this part of Fresno County often have minimal vapor barriers, and crawl spaces that drain poorly after heavy rain. That combination creates conditions where mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a moisture event, yet remain hidden behind wall cavities or under flooring for months before a tenant or buyer notices.

Properties built before 1980 throughout this region also carry a meaningful probability of containing asbestos in floor tile, pipe insulation, roofing materials, or textured ceiling coatings. When a landlord undertakes repairs without proper assessment, or when a contractor disturbs suspect materials during a remodel, the resulting exposure dispute often requires forensic-grade sampling to establish what was present, when it was disturbed, and what concentrations a building occupant may have encountered.

## Our Forensic Sampling Process, Calibrated for Central Valley Litigation

Litigation sampling is not the same as a routine pre-renovation survey. Every sample we collect is treated as potential evidence: chain-of-custody documentation begins at the point of collection, sample containers are labeled with location coordinates and collection conditions, and laboratory analysis is performed by accredited third-party labs that can provide testimony-ready reports.

For mold disputes, we combine bulk sampling, surface swab or tape-lift samples, and air spore-trap cassettes to build a spatial picture of contamination. This matters in cases where one party claims mold was confined to a single room and the other disputes the scope. For asbestos-related claims, our Certified Asbestos Consultant credential supports the collection and interpretation of bulk material samples, and our NIOSH 582 Microscopist certification means fiber identification is performed to the analytical standard courts expect.

When habitability is the central question, we document conditions photographically, record temperature and relative humidity at multiple points, and collect samples in a sequence that preserves the as-found condition of the space before any remediation alters the evidence.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up in Fresno County habitability cases involves properties with evaporative coolers rather than central air conditioning. In the Centerville area, swamp coolers are common in older rental stock, and they introduce outdoor air, including outdoor spores and particulates, directly into living spaces. During high-pollen or high-dust seasons, this can elevate indoor air readings in ways that complicate mold causation arguments. When we conduct forensic air sampling in properties with evaporative cooling systems, we document the system type, its operational status at the time of sampling, and outdoor conditions simultaneously, so that the data can be interpreted accurately rather than misread as evidence of active mold growth when the source is actually the cooling system itself.
