## Why Orange Cove Properties Are Vulnerable to Sewage Contamination

Much of Orange Cove's residential housing stock was built during mid-century agricultural expansion, when cast iron and clay tile sewer laterals were standard. Those materials have a finite service life, and decades of soil movement driven by the valley's expansive clay soils can crack or offset them without any visible warning above ground. When a lateral fails, sewage does not always back up dramatically through a floor drain. It can seep into a crawl space, saturate subfloor framing, or wick into wall cavities at grade level before anyone notices an odor.

The region's irrigation season also affects groundwater pressure around shallow foundations. As surrounding fields are flood-irrigated, soil saturation can push existing sewer line weaknesses to the point of failure. Properties on the western edge of the city, closer to agricultural parcels, tend to see this pattern more than those on higher ground. Assessment work here has to account for contamination pathways that are not always obvious from a surface inspection alone.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Orange Cove

A sewage contamination assessment is a structured investigation, not a visual walkthrough. Surface and air sampling follows. Bacterial contamination testing uses swab and bulk samples sent to an accredited laboratory.
