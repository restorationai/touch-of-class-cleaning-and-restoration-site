## Why Reedley Properties Face Distinct Compliance Pressures

Reedley's commercial building stock skews older. California's asbestos regulations, enforced by Cal/OSHA and CARB's Airborne Toxic Control Measure, require a certified asbestos consultant to survey any structure before demolition or renovation that disturbs regulated building materials. ## Local Note

One thing that catches Reedley facility managers off guard: California's CARB Airborne Toxic Control Measure for asbestos requires notification to the local air district, the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District, before any demolition or renovation that may disturb asbestos-containing material, regardless of the quantity involved. This is a separate requirement from Cal/OSHA notification and has its own lead-time rules. Missing it is one of the more common compliance gaps we see in facilities that have otherwise done their homework, and it can delay a project start even when the asbestos abatement contractor is already scheduled.

If your project timeline is tight, getting the survey, the air district notification, and the abatement contractor coordinated in the right sequence is something worth discussing before you break ground, not after.
