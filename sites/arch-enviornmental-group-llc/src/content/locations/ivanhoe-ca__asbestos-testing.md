## Why Ivanhoe Properties Carry a Higher Asbestos Risk

Tulare County's rural communities, including Ivanhoe, developed rapidly in the post-World War II decades when asbestos was still widely used in residential and agricultural construction. Single-family homes built before 1980 frequently contain asbestos in spray-applied ceiling texture (the kind commonly called popcorn ceilings), vinyl floor tiles, duct insulation, roofing felt, and joint compound. Farm outbuildings and packing shed structures from the same era often used corrugated asbestos cement panels for roofing and siding, which remain stable when intact but become a serious concern the moment cutting, drilling, or demolition begins.

The dry, hot Central Valley climate is relevant here. Decades of heat cycling cause building materials to expand and contract repeatedly, which can cause bonded or friable asbestos-containing materials to crack and separate over time. What looked stable in a 1970s inspection may not be stable today.

## Our Asbestos Testing and Inspection Process in Ivanhoe

A proper asbestos survey is not a visual guess. Our certified asbestos consultant follows a systematic process calibrated to the type of property and the scope of planned work.

**Bulk sampling:** We collect physical samples from suspect materials, including ceiling texture, floor tiles, drywall compound, insulation, and roofing materials. Sampling is done in a controlled manner to minimize fiber release.

**Laboratory analysis:** Samples are submitted to an accredited laboratory for polarized light microscopy (PLM) analysis, the standard method for identifying asbestos fiber type and concentration in bulk materials.

**Written report:** You receive a clear, written inspection report that identifies which materials contain asbestos, their condition, and their location in the structure. This report is what contractors, escrow officers, and county building departments need before permitted renovation work can proceed.

For Ivanhoe properties where multiple suspect materials are present across a large footprint, such as a farmhouse with attached outbuildings, we scope the survey to cover all areas that could be disturbed during the planned work.
