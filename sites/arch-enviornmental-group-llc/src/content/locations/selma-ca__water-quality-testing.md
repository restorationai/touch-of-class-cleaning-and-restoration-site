## Why Selma Properties Face Distinct Water Quality Pressures

The San Joaquin Valley has one of the most agriculturally intensive groundwater basins in the country, and Selma's position within Fresno County places it squarely in a region where nitrate loading from fertilizer applications has been documented in shallow aquifers for generations. Private wells drilled to shallower depths are particularly susceptible to seasonal infiltration, especially after winter rains push surface runoff downward through sandy loam soils common in this part of the valley. That same soil profile drains quickly, which sounds like a benefit until you realize it also gives contaminants a faster path to groundwater rather than filtering them out.

Municipal water in Selma is treated and monitored under California's Safe Drinking Water Act, but the distribution infrastructure in older sections of the city may include service lines and interior plumbing that predate modern material standards. Homes built before the late 1980s can have lead solder at copper joint connections, a source of lead exposure that treatment at the plant does nothing to address, because the leaching happens inside the home, not before the water enters it.

## Our Water Quality Testing Process in Selma

Testing begins with a site assessment to identify which sources need sampling, municipal tap, well head, point-of-use fixtures, or all three. For homes on private wells, we collect samples at the wellhead and at interior taps to distinguish between contamination entering the well and contamination introduced by household plumbing. Samples are handled under chain-of-custody protocols and submitted to a certified laboratory for analysis.

Depending on the scope of testing you need, a standard panel for a Selma property typically covers:

- **Coliform and E. coli bacteria**, the baseline for any well or tap water safety check
- **Nitrates and nitrites**, especially relevant given the valley's agricultural history
- **Lead and copper**, critical for homes with older plumbing or service connections
- **pH, hardness, and total dissolved solids**, indicators of water chemistry that affect both health and plumbing longevity
- **Pesticide and herbicide screening**, available as an add-on panel for properties near active or former agricultural operations

Results come back from the lab with a plain-language summary so you understand exactly what was found, what the regulatory thresholds are, and what your options are if any parameter comes back elevated.

## Local Note

One thing worth knowing if you own a home in Selma that was built in the 1960s or 1970s: the galvanized steel supply lines common in that construction era don't just corrode on the outside. As galvanized pipe ages, zinc and iron scale build up on the interior walls, and that buildup can harbor bacteria and elevate metal readings at the tap even when the water entering the home from the street tests clean. If your home is in that age range and you've noticed discolored water or a metallic taste, a point-of-use sample from the kitchen tap, not just the hose bib, is the right place to start.
