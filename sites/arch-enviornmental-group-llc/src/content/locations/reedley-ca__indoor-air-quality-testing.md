## Why Reedley Properties Face Distinct Air Quality Pressures

The San Joaquin Valley already carries some of the highest ambient particulate matter levels in the United States, driven by agricultural operations, vehicle traffic on Highway 99, and geography that traps pollutants against the Sierra Nevada foothills. Reedley's position on the valley floor means outdoor air quality advisories are common, and those particles, fine dust, pesticide drift, pollen from stone-fruit orchards, find their way indoors through gaps in older window seals, attic bypasses, and HVAC systems that weren't designed with valley air in mind.

Reedley's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built in the mid-twentieth century, when construction standards paid little attention to vapor barriers or airtight building envelopes. Crawl spaces beneath older ranch-style homes can accumulate moisture from irrigation-saturated soil, creating conditions where mold spores and elevated humidity migrate upward into living areas. Combine that with the region's periodic flooding risk along the Kings River corridor and you have a setting where indoor air quality problems tend to compound quietly over time rather than announcing themselves with obvious water damage.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Testing Process in Reedley

Air quality testing isn't a single sample and a printout. A meaningful assessment starts with a visual walkthrough, looking at HVAC filter condition, attic access points, crawl space moisture indicators, and any visible staining that might suggest past or ongoing moisture intrusion. In Reedley's climate, we pay particular attention to evaporative cooler systems, which are common here and, when not properly maintained, can introduce biological contaminants directly into the airstream.

From there, air samples are collected using calibrated pumps and cassettes appropriate to what we're testing for, mold spores, asbestos fibers, or general particulate and VOC levels depending on your concern. Samples go to an accredited laboratory, and results are interpreted in the context of what we observed on-site. As a certified asbestos consultant, lead inspector/assessor, and NIOSH 582 microscopist, Arch Environmental Group LLC can analyze fiber samples in-house at the microscopy level, which reduces turnaround time on asbestos-related questions compared to labs that outsource that analysis.

A written report follows, with findings explained in plain language and, where a problem is identified, a clear description of what remediation would look like, not a vague recommendation to "consult a contractor."
