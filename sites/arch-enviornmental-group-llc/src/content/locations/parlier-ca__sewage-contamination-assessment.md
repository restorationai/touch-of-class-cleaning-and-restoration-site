## Why Parlier Properties Face Elevated Sewage Contamination Risk

Fresno County's agricultural corridor, including Parlier, has a significant share of housing built during mid-century growth periods, when cast-iron and clay-tile sewer laterals were standard. Those materials have a finite service life. Clay tile in particular is prone to root intrusion from the mature trees common along residential streets, and a single root mass can cause a full blockage that sends sewage back through the lowest fixture in a home. The problem is compounded in summer: high soil temperatures accelerate the biological activity in any standing contaminated water, meaning the window between a backup event and measurable bacterial spread into subfloor materials is shorter here than in cooler coastal climates.

Parlier's relatively flat topography also means gravity drainage has limited slope to work with. When the municipal main is under stress during heavy winter storm events, or when a lateral is partially obstructed, the path of least resistance is often back into the structure rather than forward to the collection system. Assessment work here requires sampling strategies that account for both the visible affected zone and the areas where contaminated water may have wicked laterally under flooring before the event was discovered.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Parlier

A sewage contamination assessment is not a visual inspection. It is a structured sampling and documentation protocol designed to answer a specific question: which materials and surfaces carry measurable biological contamination, and at what levels?

For properties in Parlier, the process typically includes:

- **Initial site evaluation** to identify the source, the flow path, and the materials affected, including subfloor assemblies common in older single-family construction.
- **Surface and bulk sampling** of flooring, wall cavities, and any porous materials within the affected zone, collected using chain-of-custody protocols for laboratory analysis.
- **Air sampling** where the event has been contained for more than a short period, since off-gassing from Category 3 water can affect indoor air quality beyond the immediate contact zone.
- **Laboratory analysis** through accredited third-party labs, with results that specify organism types and concentrations rather than a simple pass/fail.
- **Written assessment report** documenting findings, affected material boundaries, and recommended remediation scope, formatted to support insurance claims and contractor work plans.

## Local Note: Subfloor Construction in Parlier's Residential Areas

A detail that matters specifically in Parlier and the surrounding San Joaquin communities: a substantial portion of the single-family housing stock uses concrete slab-on-grade construction rather than raised wood-frame foundations. After a sewage backup, slab floors present a different assessment challenge than wood subfloors. Contaminated water does not wick through concrete the way it does through plywood, but it does migrate along the slab surface and into the gap between the slab and any vinyl or tile flooring above it. That interface is frequently overlooked in a visual-only inspection. Sampling that gap, rather than just the surface of the flooring, is what produces an accurate contamination boundary and prevents a situation where flooring is replaced but the slab edge remains a contamination source.
