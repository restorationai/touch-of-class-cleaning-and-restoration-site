## Why Selma Properties Generate Environmental Disputes

Fresno County's agricultural corridor, including Selma and the surrounding unincorporated parcels, has a significant inventory of housing built before the 1980s, an era when asbestos-containing materials were standard in floor tile, pipe insulation, and roofing, and when lead-based paint was the norm rather than the exception. Rental housing in this corridor turns over frequently, and conditions that go unreported during one tenancy can become the center of a habitability dispute during the next. The valley's temperature swings, triple-digit summers followed by damp, foggy winters, drive condensation cycles inside wall cavities that accelerate mold colonization. A small roof leak or a slow drain-line failure in a crawl space can seed a colony within days under those conditions, and by the time a complaint is filed, the visible evidence may have been painted over, dried out, or disturbed. Forensic sampling captures what the eye no longer sees.

## Our Forensic Sampling Process for Legal Matters

Litigation sampling is not the same as a standard inspection. Every decision, where to sample, what method to use, how to package and transport the sample, is made with the understanding that the results may be presented in a deposition, submitted as an exhibit, or reviewed by an opposing expert. The process begins with a documented site assessment: photographs, moisture readings, and written observations that establish conditions at the time of inspection. Bulk samples, swab samples, air cassettes, and tape-lift samples are collected using NIOSH- and EPA-accepted protocols, labeled with unique identifiers, and shipped to an accredited third-party laboratory under documented chain of custody. The resulting report is written to be read by a judge or jury as readily as by an industrial hygienist, findings stated plainly, methodology cited, and conclusions bounded by what the data actually supports. As a Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector/Assessor with NIOSH 582 microscopist credentials, the principal analyst can also serve as a qualified expert witness when the matter proceeds to hearing or trial.

## Local Note

One pattern that comes up repeatedly in San Joaquin Valley habitability cases involves adobe-style and stucco construction common in Selma and the broader Fresno County area. Stucco exteriors can mask water intrusion at window frames and parapet walls for extended periods, the surface looks intact while moisture migrates inward along the lath and into the wall assembly. By the time interior mold is visible or a tenant reports musty odors, the affected area behind the stucco may be substantially larger than the interior evidence suggests. Forensic sampling in these cases often requires invasive access, small exploratory openings, to document the true extent of colonization, and that scope needs to be agreed upon and documented before the inspection begins, particularly when the property is still occupied or when the opposing party has a right to be present.
