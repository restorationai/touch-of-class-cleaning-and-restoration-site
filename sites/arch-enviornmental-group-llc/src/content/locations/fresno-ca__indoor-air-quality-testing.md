## Why Fresno Properties See Elevated Indoor Air Quality Concerns

The Central Valley's geography traps pollution in ways coastal cities don't experience. Fresno sits in a natural bowl bordered by the Sierra Nevada to the east and the Coast Ranges to the west, which limits air circulation and allows particulates to accumulate, a pattern that's well-documented by the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District. During harvest season, field burning and tractor dust add agricultural particulates to an already burdened airshed. Those fine particles don't just stay outside; they infiltrate through gaps around windows, attic vents, and HVAC return air intakes.

Indoors, the problem compounds. Fresno's housing stock includes a significant number of homes built in the postwar boom of the 1950s and 1960s, when asbestos-containing materials were standard in floor tiles, pipe insulation, and ceiling texture. Homes from that era, common in established central Fresno neighborhoods, may have undisturbed asbestos that poses no immediate risk, but renovation or water damage can change that calculus quickly. Lead-based paint is similarly common in pre-1978 construction. A thorough indoor air quality inspection accounts for both legacy materials and current airborne contaminants.

## Our Indoor Air Quality Testing Process in Fresno

Every engagement starts with a walkthrough assessment, not a checklist, but a systematic look at how the building breathes. We note HVAC configuration, insulation type, visible moisture staining, and any recent renovation activity that could have disturbed older materials. From there, we collect air samples calibrated to the specific concern: bulk and air sampling for asbestos fibers, swab or air-o-cell sampling for mold spores, and wipe sampling for lead dust where applicable.

Samples are analyzed by accredited laboratories, and results are returned in a written report that identifies what was found, at what concentrations, and what those levels mean relative to established health guidelines. The report is written to be usable, by you, by a remediation contractor, or by an insurance adjuster, not just filed away. Our team holds certifications as a Certified Asbestos Consultant, Lead Inspector/Assessor, and NIOSH 582 Microscopist, which means the sampling and analysis meet the evidentiary standards required for regulatory compliance and insurance documentation.

## Local Note: Valley Fever and Indoor Air in Fresno

One factor that sets Fresno-area indoor air quality work apart from most of California is Coccidioides, the soil fungus responsible for Valley Fever. The spores live in the dry, alkaline soils of the San Joaquin Valley and become airborne when soil is disturbed by construction, grading, or even strong wind events. While Valley Fever testing falls under medical diagnosis rather than environmental sampling, property owners undertaking significant excavation or renovation near undisturbed soil should be aware that construction dust in this region carries a risk profile that doesn't exist in coastal markets. It's a reason to take indoor particulate control seriously during any Fresno renovation project, and it's context we bring to every consultation.
