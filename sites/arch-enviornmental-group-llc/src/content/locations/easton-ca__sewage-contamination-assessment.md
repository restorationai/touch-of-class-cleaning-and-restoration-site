## Why Easton Properties Face Distinct Sewage Contamination Risks

The San Joaquin Valley's expansive clay soils shift seasonally with wet and dry cycles, and that movement puts lateral sewer lines under chronic stress. Older homes in Fresno County's unincorporated communities, including areas around Easton, were often built on slab foundations with cast-iron or orangeburg sewer laterals that have long exceeded their service life. When those lines fail, sewage doesn't always follow a predictable path: it can migrate beneath slab edges, wick into subfloor assemblies, or surface through floor drains and low-point fixtures before a homeowner notices anything more than an odor.

Category 3 water, the classification that covers sewage and grossly contaminated water, carries bacterial and viral loads that surface cleaning cannot address. A visual inspection alone cannot confirm whether a space is safe. Sampling for coliform bacteria, enterococcus, and other indicators is the only way to establish a defensible baseline, and in Fresno County that documentation is often required before a contractor can sign off on clearance.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Easton

Every assessment begins with a structured site walkthrough to map the contamination boundary, where sewage contact is confirmed, where it's suspected, and where it definitively stopped. That boundary determination drives the sampling plan.

Surface and air samples are collected from affected and adjacent areas using chain-of-custody protocols accepted by accredited laboratories. We document construction materials, approximate age, and condition, because a 1960s-era home with original hardwood subfloor and plaster walls holds contamination differently than a 1990s tract home with OSB sheathing and vinyl plank. The report we deliver identifies contaminated zones, the sample results, and recommended remediation scope, in a format structured for your insurance adjuster and, if required, for county health review.

Because Arch Environmental Group LLC holds certifications as a Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector/Assessor, we can also flag whether disturbing contaminated materials during remediation is likely to disturb lead paint or asbestos-containing materials, a real consideration in Valley homes built before 1980. That integrated assessment prevents a remediation contractor from encountering a regulatory stop-work situation mid-project.

Sewage backup claims are among the more scrutinized categories in residential property insurance.
