## Why Bakersfield Properties Generate Environmental Disputes

Kern County's climate is one of the more challenging in California for indoor environmental quality. Summer temperatures regularly push past 100°F, and many older properties rely on evaporative coolers rather than refrigerated air. Swamp coolers introduce substantial moisture into interior air, and when duct seals age or pans crack, that moisture can saturate wall cavities without triggering a visible leak. The result is mold colonization that develops slowly, often going unnoticed until a tenant complains or a property sale triggers an inspection.

Bakersfield's expansive clay soils compound the problem. Seasonal shrink-and-swell cycles crack slab foundations and stem walls, allowing soil gases and moisture to migrate into crawl spaces and lower floors. In older neighborhoods, plumbing runs through slab, making even minor pipe failures difficult to detect and easy for a landlord or tenant to dispute. These conditions feed a steady volume of habitability claims, insurance disputes, and personal injury cases where objective forensic data is the deciding factor.

## Our Forensic Sampling Process for Litigation

Forensic sampling for litigation is different from a standard inspection. Every step is documented with the evidentiary chain of custody that attorneys and expert witnesses depend on. The process typically moves through these stages:

**Site assessment and scope definition.** Before any samples are collected, we document the physical conditions, photograph affected areas, and establish a sampling plan tied to the specific allegations in the dispute. Scope is set in consultation with the retaining attorney or party.

**Calibrated sampling.** Depending on the claim, sampling may include bulk material collection, air cassette sampling, surface swab or tape-lift samples, or soil and water specimens. As a NIOSH 582 Microscopist and Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector/Assessor, Arch Environmental Group applies the analytical methods appropriate to the contaminant in question, whether that is mold, asbestos, lead, or another hazardous material.

**Accredited laboratory analysis.** All samples are submitted to accredited third-party laboratories with documented chain-of-custody forms. Results are not interpreted informally; they are presented in written reports structured for legal use.

**Expert reporting and testimony.** The final deliverable is a written forensic report that an attorney can use in mediation, arbitration, or trial. When required, Arch Environmental Group provides expert witness testimony explaining sampling methodology, findings, and their significance.

## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing in Bakersfield: many single-family rentals built during the 1980s and 1990s used paper-faced drywall in bathrooms and laundry areas rather than moisture-resistant board. When evaporative cooler systems run heavily through summer and then sit idle through dry winters, that drywall can cycle through wet and dry phases repeatedly over years. By the time a habitability dispute surfaces, the mold growth is often inside the wall cavity rather than on the surface, which means surface swabs alone will not capture the full picture. Forensic bulk sampling and cavity investigation are frequently necessary to document the actual extent of contamination for a Kern County case.
