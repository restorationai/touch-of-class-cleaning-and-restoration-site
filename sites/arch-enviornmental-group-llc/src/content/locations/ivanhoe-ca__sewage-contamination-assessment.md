## Why Ivanhoe Properties Face Elevated Sewage Contamination Risk

The soils across Tulare County's valley floor shift seasonally. During wet winters and early-spring irrigation cycles, high moisture content in clay-dominant soils can exert lateral pressure on older sewer laterals, causing joint separations that allow Category 3 water, the technical classification for sewage-contaminated water, to migrate into crawl spaces and subfloor cavities before any visible sign appears inside the home.

Many residential properties in and around Ivanhoe were built in construction eras when cast-iron or Orangeburg pipe was standard. Both materials degrade over decades, and Orangeburg in particular becomes soft and deformable under sustained soil pressure. A line that appears functional can fail partially, allowing slow seepage that colonizes subfloor insulation and wood framing with bacteria long before a homeowner notices an odor. Assessment, not just cleanup, is what establishes whether contamination has traveled beyond the obvious impact zone.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Ivanhoe

A sewage contamination assessment is a structured, documentation-first process. It is not a cleanup service, and it is not a visual inspection alone. The steps below reflect how Arch Environmental Group approaches these engagements in Tulare County properties:

**Scope and safety review.** Before any sampling begins, the affected area is evaluated for structural safety and active hazards. Access points to crawl spaces, wall cavities, and mechanical chases are identified.

**Surface and air sampling.** Swab samples are collected from affected hard surfaces, subfloor materials, and any porous materials within the contamination radius. Where airborne particulate is a concern, air samples are collected and submitted to an accredited laboratory.

**Laboratory analysis.** Samples are analyzed for bacterial indicators consistent with sewage contamination, including coliform organisms. Results are returned with chain-of-custody documentation.

**Written assessment report.** The deliverable is a formal written report that identifies contaminated zones, documents sampling locations with photographs, and provides findings suitable for submission to an insurance carrier or a remediation contractor.

This process is calibrated to the construction realities common in Tulare County: pier-and-beam foundations with limited crawl-space clearance, older subfloor assemblies, and in some cases, homes on private septic systems where the contamination source itself requires characterization.
