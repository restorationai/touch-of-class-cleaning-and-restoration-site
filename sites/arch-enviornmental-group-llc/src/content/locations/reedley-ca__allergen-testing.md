## Why Reedley Homes Are Especially Prone to Indoor Allergen Buildup

The San Joaquin Valley's agricultural landscape creates an allergen environment unlike most of California. Reedley is surrounded by stone fruit orchards, grape vineyards, and row crops, all of which release organic particulates, mold spores from irrigation, and pollen that infiltrate homes through window gaps, door seals, and return-air vents. Valley homes also tend to run central air conditioning for six or more months a year, which means allergens get recirculated continuously through ductwork rather than flushed out by natural ventilation.

Older housing stock in Reedley, including bungalows and ranch-style homes built in the mid-twentieth century, often features original duct systems with accumulated debris, wall-to-wall carpet over concrete slabs, and minimal vapor barriers. These conditions create ideal reservoirs for dust mites, which thrive in humidity levels that spike during the valley's brief but intense winter fog season. Even homes that feel clean to the eye can harbor significant allergen loads in materials that don't show visible contamination.

## Our Allergen Testing Process in Reedley

Allergen testing is not a single swab and a result, it's a methodical sampling protocol calibrated to the specific materials and conditions in your home. The process typically begins with a walkthrough to identify high-probability accumulation zones: bedroom carpeting, upholstered furniture, HVAC filter housings, and any areas with visible dust buildup or pet activity.

Dust samples are collected using standardized collection methods, often a measured vacuum technique that pulls material from a defined surface area of carpet or settled dust from hard surfaces. These samples are then sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis. Depending on what you're testing for, the panel can screen for dust mite allergen (Der p 1 and Der f 1), cat and dog dander proteins, cockroach allergen, and rodent urinary proteins, each of which requires a different analytical method.

In Reedley's climate, we pay particular attention to HVAC return registers and filter housings, where valley dust concentrates. A home that tests low in the living room may still show elevated allergen loads at the air handler, a detail that matters when you're trying to understand why symptoms persist even after cleaning.
