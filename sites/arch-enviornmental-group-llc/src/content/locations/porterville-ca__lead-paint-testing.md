## Why Porterville Properties See Elevated Lead Paint Risk

Tulare County's agricultural economy shaped Porterville's growth in waves, and much of the city's older housing stock reflects construction norms from the 1940s through the early 1970s. Homes built during that era commonly received multiple coats of oil-based paint on window trim, porch railings, exterior siding, and interior woodwork, all of which could contain lead at concentrations well above current safety thresholds.

Porterville's climate adds another layer of concern. The Central Valley's intense summer heat and dry winters cause wood to expand and contract significantly across the year. That movement accelerates paint deterioration, and as old paint chalks, cracks, and flakes, lead-containing dust settles on windowsills, floors, and soil near foundations. Homes with original single-pane windows are especially prone to friction surfaces where lead dust is generated every time the sash slides. For families with young children, that dust is the primary exposure pathway, not the paint film itself.

## Our Lead Paint Testing and Inspection Process in Porterville

Arch Environmental Group LLC holds a Lead Inspector Assessor certification, which means the inspection goes beyond a simple swab kit. The process begins with a visual survey of all painted surfaces, noting condition, substrate type, and any signs of deterioration. From there, XRF (X-ray fluorescence) testing is performed directly on surfaces without disturbing the paint. XRF delivers an immediate, non-destructive reading of lead concentration in milligrams per square centimeter, and it can distinguish between multiple paint layers without sampling.

When surface geometry or substrate type makes XRF readings less reliable, paint chip samples are collected and submitted to an accredited laboratory for confirmation. The result is a room-by-room report that identifies which surfaces contain lead, at what concentration, and in what condition. That report is the document your contractor, your escrow officer, or your property manager needs before any renovation work begins.

For rental properties, the inspection also supports compliance with California's landlord disclosure requirements under Health and Safety Code Section 17920.10, which applies to pre-1978 residential units.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Lead Testing

XRF analyzers are the industry standard for lead paint inspections because they are fast, non-destructive, and defensible in regulatory and legal contexts. The instrument is held against a painted surface and produces a result in seconds, allowing an inspector to test dozens of components in a single visit. Unlike consumer test kits, which produce a color-change reaction that can be ambiguous or miss low-level lead, XRF data is quantitative and traceable.

For properties with textured coatings, stucco, or surfaces where XRF geometry corrections are needed, paint chip sampling provides a backup method. Samples are handled under chain-of-custody protocols and analyzed by a certified laboratory, with results typically returned within a few business days.
