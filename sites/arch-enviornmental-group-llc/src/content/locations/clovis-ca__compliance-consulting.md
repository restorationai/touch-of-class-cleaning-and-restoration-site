## Why Clovis Properties Face Distinct Compliance Challenges

The San Joaquin Valley's air quality designation shapes nearly every aspect of environmental compliance work here. Fresno County sits in a nonattainment zone for both ozone and particulate matter, which means any project that disturbs suspect materials, asbestos-containing flooring, lead-based paint, or silica-bearing soils, carries heightened scrutiny from Cal/OSHA and the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District. Contractors who operate under the assumption that Central Valley rules mirror coastal California standards frequently find themselves out of compliance before the first tool hits the wall.

Clovis's older commercial corridors along Clovis Avenue and Shaw Avenue include buildings constructed before the 1980s when asbestos was still common in floor tile, pipe insulation, roofing felts, and textured ceiling coatings. Newer industrial and light-manufacturing facilities north of Herndon Avenue face a different set of obligations, OSHA air monitoring during renovation, workplace exposure assessments for dust and chemical vapors, and AHERA compliance inspections if any portion of the structure was ever used for K-12 education. Both ends of the building-age spectrum require documentation, and that documentation has to survive a regulatory audit.

## Our Environmental Compliance Consulting Process in Clovis

Every engagement starts with a pre-project records review. Before anyone sets foot on the property, we examine available building permits, prior inspection reports, and any existing O&M (operations and maintenance) plans for regulated materials. That desk review shapes the scope of the physical inspection and prevents redundant sampling costs.

On-site, our certified asbestos consultant conducts a thorough AHERA-protocol inspection or a Cal/OSHA pre-renovation survey, depending on the project type. Bulk samples are collected using chain-of-custody procedures and submitted to an accredited laboratory. Parallel to material sampling, workplace exposure assessments and OSHA air monitoring can be conducted during active work phases, personal breathing-zone sampling, area monitoring, and real-time direct-read instrumentation where the situation calls for it.

Once lab results are in, we produce a written compliance report that meets the format requirements of the applicable regulatory body, whether that is Cal/OSHA, the EPA, or the local air district. The report includes findings, a material inventory, and a clear set of recommended next steps so your contractor, property manager, or legal team has an actionable document rather than a stack of raw data.

## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing if you own or manage commercial property in Clovis: the San Joaquin Valley's extreme heat cycles cause roofing materials to age faster than manufacturer service-life estimates assume. Built-up roofing systems and older asphalt-felt underlayments on flat commercial roofs in this climate can become friable well before the 20- or 30-year mark on paper. If your building has a flat or low-slope roof installed before the mid-1990s and you are planning any rooftop HVAC work or re-roofing, an asbestos survey of the roofing assembly before work begins is not just good practice, it is required under Cal/OSHA's asbestos regulations for construction. Skipping that step has cost Clovis-area property owners significant abatement and penalty expenses that a pre-project survey would have avoided entirely.
