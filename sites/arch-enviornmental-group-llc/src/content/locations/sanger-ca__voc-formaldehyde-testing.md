## Why Sanger Properties See Elevated VOC and Formaldehyde Concerns

The Central Valley's climate is a significant factor in how volatile organic compounds behave inside a home. VOCs off-gas faster and at higher concentrations when temperatures rise, meaning a newly installed laminate floor, fresh cabinetry, or spray foam insulation that might be borderline acceptable in a cooler coastal climate can push well above safe thresholds during a Sanger summer. Homes that are tightly sealed for energy efficiency compound the problem: without adequate ventilation, chemical concentrations accumulate rather than dissipate.

Formaldehyde is a particular concern in newer construction and remodeled spaces. It's present in composite wood products, particleboard, MDF, plywood, used in cabinets, flooring, and subfloors. It's also found in certain adhesives, paints, and insulation materials. In Sanger's agricultural region, some older homes also contain materials that predate modern emissions standards, which can create a different but equally real exposure profile.
