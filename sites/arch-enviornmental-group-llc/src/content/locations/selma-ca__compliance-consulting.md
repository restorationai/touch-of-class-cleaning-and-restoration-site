## Why Selma Properties Face Distinct Compliance Pressures

The San Joaquin Valley's agricultural economy shapes Selma's built environment in ways that aren't obvious until you're standing inside a structure. Many of the commercial and light-industrial buildings along the Highway 99 corridor and the older downtown blocks were constructed during the 1950s through 1980s, precisely the window when asbestos-containing materials were standard in roofing, floor tile, pipe insulation, and ceiling texture. Renovation or demolition work on these structures triggers AHERA and Cal/OSHA requirements that can halt a project if pre-work surveys and air monitoring aren't in place.

Beyond building materials, Selma's proximity to active agricultural operations means that ambient dust and pesticide residue can complicate baseline air quality readings during workplace exposure assessments. Establishing a clean pre-work air sample in this environment requires someone who understands the regional context, not just the laboratory protocol.

## Our Environmental Compliance Consulting Process in Selma

Every engagement starts with a site-specific scoping conversation. We want to understand what's being planned, a tenant improvement, a full demolition, a change in occupancy, before we prescribe the compliance pathway. From there, the typical workflow for a Selma commercial or industrial property moves through these stages:

**Pre-renovation or pre-demolition survey**: A certified asbestos consultant walks the affected areas, collects bulk samples from suspect materials, and documents findings in a format that satisfies Cal/OSHA and local air district requirements.

**AHERA compliance inspection** (for school and public-building clients): We conduct the formal inspection, prepare the required management plan documentation, and identify any friable or damaged materials that require immediate attention under federal AHERA standards.

**OSHA air monitoring**: During active abatement or renovation work, we deploy calibrated personal air sampling equipment to verify that worker exposures stay within permissible limits. Samples are analyzed by our NIOSH 582-certified microscopist, which means the fiber counts come from someone with the specific training the method requires, not a general-purpose lab tech.

**Compliance audit and gap analysis**: For businesses that have inherited properties or operational histories without clean documentation, we conduct a structured audit to identify where the regulatory record has gaps and what corrective steps close them.

## Local Note

One thing that catches property owners off guard in Selma's older commercial zones is the prevalence of built-up roofing systems that contain both asbestos felts and coal tar pitch, a combination that requires specific sampling and disposal handling beyond a standard asbestos survey. If your building has a flat or low-slope roof installed before 1985 and you're planning any rooftop HVAC work or re-roofing, flag that detail before the survey so the scope covers both regulated materials. It's a common oversight in this part of the Valley, and catching it early prevents stop-work situations mid-project.

The San Joaquin Valley Unified Air Pollution Control District also has notification requirements for certain demolition and renovation projects that run parallel to, but are separate from, Cal/OSHA obligations. Making sure both notification pathways are satisfied before work begins is part of what a thorough compliance consultation covers.
