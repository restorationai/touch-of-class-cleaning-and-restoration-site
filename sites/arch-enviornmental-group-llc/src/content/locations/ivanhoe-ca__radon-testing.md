## Why Ivanhoe Properties See Radon Concerns

Radon forms from the natural decay of uranium in soil and rock. In the San Joaquin Valley, the alluvial soils and decomposed granite that underlie communities like Ivanhoe can contain elevated uranium concentrations, making radon migration into homes a genuine concern rather than a remote possibility. Homes with crawl spaces, which are common in this part of Tulare County, offer radon a direct path indoors. Concrete slab foundations with unsealed penetrations around plumbing are another common entry point.

The Central Valley's climate adds a layer of complexity. Hot, dry summers cause homeowners to seal windows tightly and run air conditioning continuously, which reduces natural ventilation and allows radon to accumulate indoors. In winter, the same pattern repeats with heating. Year-round, Ivanhoe homes can be tighter than their construction era would suggest, simply because of how residents manage the heat.

## Our Radon Testing Process in Ivanhoe

A radon inspection begins with a site assessment to identify the foundation type, any visible cracks or gaps at the slab or crawl space perimeter, and the location of the lowest livable area. That lowest level is where testing equipment is placed, because radon concentrations are typically highest closest to the soil.

Arch Environmental Group uses continuous radon monitors rather than passive charcoal canisters when conditions allow. Continuous monitoring records radon levels at regular intervals over the test period, producing a time-resolved dataset rather than a single averaged number. This matters in homes where occupancy patterns, HVAC cycling, or window use might otherwise skew a short-term passive result. The EPA recommends closed-house conditions for at least 12 hours before and during a short-term test, and we walk homeowners through exactly what that means before we place equipment.

Results are reviewed against the EPA action level of 4 pCi/L. If levels come back at or above that threshold, we explain what mitigation options typically look like and what to expect from the process, without pressure and without upselling services you may not need.

## Equipment and Methods We Use for Radon

Continuous radon monitors are calibrated instruments that log hourly or sub-hourly readings throughout the test period. Unlike a passive canister that gets mailed to a lab after the fact, a continuous monitor lets us review the data curve immediately after retrieval. Sudden spikes can indicate a weather event, a door left open, or an HVAC system operating in a way that affected the test, and we can account for those variables in our interpretation.

For homes with crawl spaces, which are particularly common in rural Tulare County, we also assess whether the space itself warrants a separate measurement. A dirt-floor crawl space with no vapor barrier is essentially an open conduit between the soil and the living area above it.
