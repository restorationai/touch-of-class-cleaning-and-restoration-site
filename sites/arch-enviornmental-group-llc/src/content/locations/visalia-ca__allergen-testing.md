## Our Allergen Testing Process in Visalia

Allergen testing is a sampling discipline, not a walk-through. The process starts with a structured assessment of the home, identifying the rooms and surfaces most likely to harbor concentrated allergen loads, including sleeping areas, pet zones, and return-air vents. Dust samples are collected using validated wipe or vacuum methods depending on the surface type, then sent to an accredited laboratory for quantitative analysis.

The lab panel we use screens for the most clinically relevant indoor allergens: dust mite allergen (Der p 1 and Der f 1), cat dander (Fel d 1), dog dander (Can f 1), and cockroach allergen (Bla g 2), among others. Results come back as measured concentrations, not a simple positive/negative, so you can see whether levels fall below, within, or above thresholds associated with sensitization risk. That specificity matters when you're deciding whether to replace carpet, upgrade filtration, or address a pet-dander problem in a particular room rather than the whole house.
