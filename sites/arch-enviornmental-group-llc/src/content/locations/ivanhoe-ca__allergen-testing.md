## Why Ivanhoe Homes Accumulate Allergens Differently

The San Joaquin Valley's air quality is among the most studied in California, and Tulare County properties like those in Ivanhoe sit in a basin that traps particulates during temperature inversions. That geography matters indoors. Fine agricultural dust, including pollen from surrounding orchards and vineyard operations, finds its way into crawl spaces, attic insulation, and duct systems. Older wood-frame homes common to the area often lack the tight building envelopes of newer construction, so allergen infiltration is ongoing rather than event-driven.

Dust mite populations thrive when indoor humidity climbs, and even in a dry climate, evaporative coolers, which are widely used in this part of the Valley, introduce enough moisture to create hospitable conditions in mattresses, upholstered furniture, and carpet padding. Pet dander compounds the picture quickly in homes with dogs or cats, settling into the same soft surfaces and becoming airborne again with foot traffic or HVAC cycling.

## What Allergen Test Results Tell You

A laboratory report showing elevated dust mite allergen in a bedroom does not automatically mean remediation. Results are most useful when read alongside the physical conditions we document on-site. High Der p 1 levels in a room with wall-to-wall carpet and an evaporative cooler vent directly overhead points toward a specific intervention: reducing humidity at the source, replacing or encasing bedding, and improving filtration. Pet dander readings help families with allergic members make informed decisions about room access and cleaning frequency. The goal is a clear action list, not a general recommendation to clean more often.
