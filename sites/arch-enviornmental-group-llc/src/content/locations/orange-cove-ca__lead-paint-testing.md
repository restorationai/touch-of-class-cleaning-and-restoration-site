## Why Orange Cove's Housing Stock Warrants a Closer Look

Fresno County's agricultural towns developed quickly and affordably, which meant builders used what was available and cost-effective. Lead-based paint was the standard primer and finish coat for decades. In Orange Cove, single-family homes on the older residential streets near the downtown core frequently retain original painted woodwork, plaster walls, and steel-framed windows, all surfaces where lead paint was routinely applied. Deteriorating paint on sun-baked exterior surfaces is a particular concern here: the San Joaquin Valley's intense summer heat and low humidity cause paint to chalk, crack, and peel faster than in coastal climates, which accelerates the release of lead dust onto soil and interior surfaces. That dust is the primary exposure pathway for children and pregnant women.

Renovation activity is the other trigger. California's RRP (Renovation, Repair, and Painting) rule requires contractors working in pre-1978 housing to follow lead-safe work practices, and many lenders and buyers now request a formal lead inspection or risk assessment before closing on older properties in Fresno County.

## Our Lead Paint Testing and Inspection Process in Orange Cove

Arch Environmental Group uses XRF (X-ray fluorescence) technology as the primary field tool for lead paint testing. XRF testing is non-destructive, reads lead concentration in milligrams per square centimeter in seconds, and produces a component-by-component record of every tested surface. That report is specific enough to tell a contractor exactly which door frames or window sills need containment before work begins, rather than treating an entire room as a hazard.

For properties where a full lead risk assessment is needed, the process goes further: dust wipe samples are collected from floors and windowsills, soil samples may be taken from areas where exterior paint has historically shed, and the findings are compiled into a risk assessment report that meets California Department of Public Health standards. As a certified Lead Inspector/Assessor, Arch Environmental Group produces documentation that satisfies lender requirements, county health referrals, and contractor pre-renovation compliance needs.

The inspection scope is always matched to the purpose. A buyer who needs a disclosure-level answer before closing needs something different from a landlord responding to a county health department notice, and the report format reflects that.
