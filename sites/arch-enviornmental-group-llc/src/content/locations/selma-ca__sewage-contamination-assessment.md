## Why Selma Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Sewage Contamination Events

The San Joaquin Valley's clay-heavy soils shift seasonally, contracting in the dry summer months and swelling again when winter rains arrive. That ground movement puts steady lateral stress on older clay-tile and cast-iron sewer laterals common in Selma's established residential areas, accelerating cracking and root intrusion over time. A slow leak from a compromised lateral can saturate subfloor cavities and wall voids for weeks before a visible backup occurs, meaning the contamination footprint is often larger than it first appears.

Selma's agricultural economy also means that many properties, particularly on the city's eastern and southern edges, connect to infrastructure that was sized and installed decades ago, when the surrounding land use looked very different. Capacity stress during heavy rain events can push Category 3 water (sewage-contaminated water) back through floor drains, laundry connections, and low-lying fixtures. Because the San Joaquin Valley's rainy season is compressed into a short winter window, these surges tend to be sudden rather than gradual.

## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Selma

A sewage contamination assessment is a structured investigation, not a visual inspection. The process begins with a site walkthrough to identify all affected zones, including areas that may not show obvious surface contamination but are downslope or hydraulically connected to the backup point. In Selma homes with slab-on-grade construction (common in tracts built from the 1960s through the 1980s), this step requires particular attention to expansion joints and plumbing penetrations where Category 3 water can wick laterally under the slab.

From there, we collect environmental samples, surface swabs, air samples where indicated, and bulk material samples from porous substrates like drywall, insulation, and subflooring, following established industrial hygiene protocols. Our team holds credentials including Certified Asbestos Consultant and Lead Inspector Assessor designations, which matters in Selma's older housing stock where sewage events in pre-1978 construction can disturb lead-based paint or asbestos-containing floor tile, adding a regulatory layer to what might otherwise be a straightforward cleanup.

Laboratory results are compiled into a written assessment report that documents contamination boundaries, identifies materials requiring remediation, and provides clearance criteria that a remediation contractor must meet before the property is considered safe for reoccupancy. That report is the foundation for insurance claims, contractor scopes of work, and any regulatory notifications required by Fresno County Environmental Health.

## Selma Insurance Coordination

Sewage backup claims are a distinct coverage category under most homeowner policies, separate from standard water damage, and carriers frequently require third-party documentation before approving remediation scopes. An independent assessment report from a certified environmental consultant carries weight with adjusters precisely because it comes from outside the remediation contractor's chain of interest. We provide the documentation package your carrier needs: sample chain-of-custody records, laboratory analytical reports, site photographs, and a written narrative of contamination boundaries and recommended remediation scope.
