## Our Sewage Contamination Assessment Process in Porterville

A thorough assessment starts before any sampling begins. The first step is a visual and moisture survey of all potentially affected surfaces, including subfloor assemblies, wall cavities adjacent to the source, and any HVAC returns that could have drawn contaminated air through the structure. In Porterville's older bungalow-style homes, where floor joists often sit close to grade on pier-and-beam foundations, that survey frequently extends into the crawl space.

Sampling follows a structured protocol calibrated to the loss type. Surface swab samples are collected from materials that appear dry but tested positive for elevated moisture, because contamination can persist after visible water has evaporated. Air samples may be collected where there is reason to believe aerosolization occurred during the backup event. All samples are sent to an accredited laboratory, and the resulting report documents contamination levels, affected materials, and a remediation scope that your contractor and insurance adjuster can act on directly.
