## Restoration emergencies common in Lemoore

Lemoore sits in the southern San Joaquin Valley, where the climate creates a specific and recurring set of property risks. Summers are long and intensely dry, with temperatures that routinely push above 100°F, while winters bring dense tule fog and periodic atmospheric river events that can drop several inches of rain in a short window. That combination matters because the valley floor has limited natural drainage. When heavy rain arrives after months of drought, hardpan soils shed water quickly rather than absorbing it, and low-lying properties can see sheet flooding across yards, through garage slabs, and into crawlspaces before the storm is even over.

The presence of Naval Air Station Lemoore also shapes the local housing market. The city has seen multiple waves of residential construction tied to base expansion, meaning the housing stock spans several decades and several generations of building materials and methods. Older sections of town carry the hazard profile of mid-century construction; newer subdivisions on the city's edges were built to more recent California standards but still sit on the same challenging valley soils.

## Services we provide in Lemoore

### Mold testing and assessment

Mold growth in the San Joaquin Valley is often tied to slow leaks rather than dramatic flooding. A dripping supply line inside a wall cavity, a failed shower pan, or condensation behind poorly ventilated stucco can feed a colony for months before the smell becomes noticeable. Arch Environmental Group performs air sampling, surface sampling, and moisture mapping to establish whether mold is present, what species are involved, and where the moisture source is. Testing is kept separate from remediation so your results are objective.

### Water damage testing and documentation

Water damage in Lemoore frequently involves slab-on-grade construction, where moisture migrates under flooring and reads dry at the surface long after the source is fixed. Thermal imaging and moisture meter readings taken at multiple depths give an accurate picture of what is actually wet versus what looks dry. That documentation matters when you're filing an insurance claim or negotiating a scope with a contractor.

### Indoor air quality testing

With NAS Lemoore nearby and the valley's agricultural activity, indoor air quality questions come up for both residential and commercial properties. Arch Environmental Group tests for particulates, VOCs, and biological contaminants, providing a written report that property owners can use with their HVAC contractors, physicians, or insurance carriers.

### Asbestos and lead testing

Properties built before the mid-1980s in Lemoore may contain asbestos in floor tiles, duct insulation, popcorn ceilings, or roofing felt. Lead-based paint is common in homes built before 1978. Before any renovation or remediation scope disturbs these materials, bulk sampling and lab analysis confirm what's present so the work can be planned safely and in compliance with California regulations.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Lemoore

Lemoore's residential core includes a significant share of homes built between the 1950s and 1980s, most of them slab-on-grade with stucco exteriors and either flat or low-slope roofs. That era of construction commonly used galvanized steel supply lines, which corrode from the inside out, and cast-iron drain lines that can crack as soils shift. Pre-1980 homes should be assumed to contain asbestos-containing materials in flooring, texture coats, and mechanical insulation until testing says otherwise. Lead paint is a parallel concern in the same vintage.

The valley floor soils in Kings County include expansive clays that swell when wet and shrink during dry summers, creating foundation movement that can open cracks in slabs and stucco.
