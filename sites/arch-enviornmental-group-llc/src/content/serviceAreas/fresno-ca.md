## Restoration emergencies common in Fresno

Fresno sits in the southern San Joaquin Valley, where the climate runs to extremes that most coastal Californians don't experience. Summers push well past 100°F for weeks at a time, and that heat drives condensation problems when air conditioning systems are overtaxed or poorly maintained, moisture accumulates inside wall cavities and attic spaces before anyone notices. Winters bring dense tule fog and periodic heavy rain events that overwhelm flat or minimally graded lots, sending water under slab foundations or into crawlspaces in older neighborhoods. The Valley's air quality is among the most challenged in the country, with wildfire smoke from the surrounding Sierra Nevada foothills compounding already elevated particulate levels during late summer and fall. For property owners, that combination, heat-driven moisture, seasonal flooding risk, and persistent air quality pressure, means environmental concerns here are rarely just one thing at a time.

## Services we provide in Fresno

### Mold testing and indoor air quality

Mold in Fresno tends to follow the same pattern: a slow leak behind a wall, a window unit that drains improperly, or a bathroom exhaust fan venting into an attic rather than outside. Because the surface can look and feel dry while the cavity stays damp, visual inspection alone misses a lot. We collect air and surface samples, send them to an accredited laboratory, and return a written report that tells you what species are present, at what concentrations, and whether levels indoors are elevated relative to the outdoor baseline.

### Asbestos and lead testing

A significant portion of Fresno's housing was built before 1980, and much of the central city's residential stock dates to the postwar boom of the late 1940s through the 1960s. Homes of that era commonly contain asbestos in floor tile, duct insulation, roofing felt, and textured ceiling coatings, as well as lead-based paint on interior and exterior surfaces. Before any renovation or demolition work, a materials survey is the step that keeps a remodel from becoming a hazardous-waste incident. We collect bulk samples and provide laboratory-confirmed results that contractors and permitting offices can rely on.

### Indoor air quality and industrial hygiene assessments

Commercial property managers dealing with tenant complaints about odors, headaches, or respiratory symptoms often need documentation before they can act. We conduct systematic air quality assessments, testing for VOCs, particulates, carbon dioxide, and biological contaminants, and produce reports written in plain language that building owners, HR departments, and insurers can all read without a technical background.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Fresno

Fresno's housing stock spans roughly a century of construction, with the largest concentrations built between the late 1940s and the 1980s. Postwar homes in the central city are predominantly slab-on-grade with stucco exteriors and wood-frame walls, a combination that conceals moisture intrusion effectively until secondary damage appears. Plumbing in homes built through the 1970s often includes galvanized steel supply lines that corrode from the inside out, and cast-iron drain lines that crack as the expansive clay soils beneath them shift seasonally. That clay soil is a consistent factor across much of the Fresno metro: it swells when wet and contracts sharply in the dry season, stressing slabs, foundation perimeters, and underground utilities alike. Homes built before 1980 should be presumed to contain asbestos-containing materials until tested. Structural repair and renovation work in Fresno is permitted through the City of Fresno Development and Resource Management Department, and work that disturbs suspect materials typically requires documentation of the material's status before a permit is issued.
