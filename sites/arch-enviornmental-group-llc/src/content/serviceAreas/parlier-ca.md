## Restoration emergencies common in Parlier

Parlier sits in the southern San Joaquin Valley, where the climate creates a predictable set of property risks. Summers are long and dry, with heat that pushes aging supply lines and water heaters past their limits. When the rainy season arrives between November and March, the clay-heavy valley soils shed water rather than absorbing it, and low-lying parcels can accumulate standing water faster than drainage infrastructure handles it. Older homes with minimal crawlspace ventilation trap that seasonal moisture, and mold colonization can begin within 24 to 48 hours of saturation. The region also sits in a high seismic zone, meaning foundation movement is not unusual over the life of a structure, and cracks that open in stucco or concrete block walls become pathways for water and pests. Wildfire smoke from the Sierra foothills and coastal ranges periodically blankets the valley, leaving soot and volatile residue on HVAC filters, attic insulation, and interior surfaces.
