## Our Commitment to Accessibility

Arch Environmental Group LLC is committed to ensuring that archenviroservice.com is accessible to everyone, including people who use assistive technologies such as screen readers, keyboard navigation, or display magnification tools. We believe every visitor, whether you're a homeowner dealing with a water loss, a property manager coordinating remediation, or an insurance adjuster reviewing documentation, deserves a clear, usable experience on our site.

## What Standard We Aim to Meet

We work toward conformance with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. These guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), define how to make web content more accessible to people with visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive disabilities. Specifically, we aim to maintain:

- Sufficient color contrast between text and background elements
- Descriptive alt text on meaningful images
- Logical heading structure so screen readers can navigate the page efficiently
- Keyboard-accessible navigation throughout the site
- Form fields and interactive elements that are properly labeled

We recognize that accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time checkbox. As we update content and site features, we review them against these standards.

## Known Limitations

Despite our efforts, some content on the site may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA. Older documents (such as PDF forms) or third-party embedded content may have limitations outside our direct control. We are actively working to identify and address these gaps.

## How to Report an Issue

If you encounter a barrier on our site, a page that doesn't work with your screen reader, a form you can't complete, an image without a description, or anything else that makes the site harder to use, please let us know. We take these reports seriously and will work to address confirmed issues within a reasonable timeframe.

You can reach us through any of the following:

- **Email:** info@archenviroservice.com

When you contact us, please describe the page you were visiting, the assistive technology or browser you were using, and the specific problem you encountered. That detail helps us reproduce and resolve the issue faster.

## Alternative Access to Information

If a portion of the site is inaccessible and you need information promptly, such as service details, an estimate request, or emergency contact information, please call us directly at **(559) 296-2088**. We are glad to provide information by phone or to send materials in an alternative format upon request.

## Ongoing Improvement

Accessibility improvements are part of how we maintain this site over time. We review new content before it goes live and periodically audit existing pages. Feedback from visitors with disabilities is one of the most direct ways we learn where the experience falls short, and we welcome it.
