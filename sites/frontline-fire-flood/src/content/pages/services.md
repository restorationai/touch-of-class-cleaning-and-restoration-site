From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to smoke damage that lingers weeks after the flames are out, Frontline Fire & Flood handles the full range of property damage restoration across Lakewood and the surrounding South Sound region. We work with homeowners, commercial property managers, landlords, and multi-family operators, anyone who needs a single, accountable crew from the moment damage happens through the day the property is back to normal.

## Emergency restoration services

When water is still moving, smoke residue is still bonding to surfaces, or a storm has left a building exposed, every hour matters. Water damage restoration addresses everything from appliance failures and sewer backups to roof leaks and flooding, including moisture hidden behind drywall or under subfloor where surface readings look fine but the structure stays wet. Fire and smoke restoration goes beyond charred material: smoke travels through HVAC systems and settles into porous surfaces far from the burn point, and that residue becomes harder to neutralize the longer it sits. Biohazard cleanup, sewage intrusion, trauma scenes, infectious material, requires containment and disposal protocols that protect occupants and workers alike. Storm damage response covers wind-driven rain, fallen trees, and the secondary water intrusion that follows when a roof or wall is breached. All of these services are available around the clock, because damage doesn't wait for business hours.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the property back. Once affected materials have been removed and the structure is dry and stable, Frontline Fire & Flood's reconstruction team takes over, reframing walls, replacing drywall, restoring flooring, and finishing surfaces to match what was there before. Because mitigation and rebuild are handled by the same company, there's no handoff gap where a property sits open waiting for a second contractor to schedule. We're also licensed for general contracting work, so pre-existing conditions uncovered during a loss, deteriorated sheathing, outdated plumbing connections, aging electrical, can be addressed in the same project scope rather than deferred.

## Specialty services

Some damage isn't dramatic but is no less serious. Mold inspection and remediation, performed by an IICRC AMRT-certified team, identifies colonization that often develops within days of an unaddressed moisture event and removes it using containment and air-filtration methods that protect the rest of the structure. Odor removal treats the source rather than masking it, using equipment matched to the specific compound causing the smell. Board-up and tarping services secure a property immediately after fire, storm, or break-in damage, limiting weather exposure and liability while the full scope of work is being planned.

The advantage of working with one company across all of these services is continuity. The crew that documents your loss, communicates with your insurance adjuster, and dries your structure is the same organization overseeing the rebuild, no version gaps, no finger-pointing between contractors, no starting over with someone who wasn't there.
