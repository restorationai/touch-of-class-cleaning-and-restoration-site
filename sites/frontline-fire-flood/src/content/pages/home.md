## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration**, Whether a pipe burst at 2 a.m. or a slow leak has been hiding behind drywall for weeks, standing water and trapped moisture cause structural damage fast. We extract, dry, and document everything to IICRC S500 standards so nothing is left wet inside a wall cavity.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration**, After a fire is out, the real damage often keeps spreading. Smoke residue is acidic and corrosive; soot embeds into porous materials within hours. Our IICRC FSRT-certified process addresses odor at the source rather than masking it.

**Mold remediation**, Mold can begin colonizing a damp surface in as little as 24 to 48 hours. Our IICRC AMRT-certified team contains the affected area, removes compromised materials safely, and addresses the moisture source so the problem doesn't return.

**Structural drying**, Not every loss looks dramatic. Sometimes the visible surface reads dry while the framing, subfloor, or plaster lath behind it stays saturated. Our IICRC ASD-certified structural drying protocols use calibrated equipment and moisture mapping to confirm the building is genuinely dry before we close anything up.
