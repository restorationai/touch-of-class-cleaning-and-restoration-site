## Why Parkland Properties See Mold Issues

Parkland sits in the western shadow of the Cascades, which means it collects the same heavy marine moisture as the rest of Pierce County, but its housing stock amplifies the risk. A significant portion of Parkland's residential neighborhoods were built between the late 1950s and early 1980s, an era when vapor barriers in crawl spaces were inconsistent or absent entirely, and when wall cavities were insulated with materials that absorb and hold moisture rather than drain it. Homes with original single-pane windows and older forced-air systems frequently develop condensation on interior surfaces through the long rainy season, feeding mold growth in corners, closets, and behind exterior-facing walls long before visible staining appears.

Crawl space mold is particularly common in Parkland. The region's clay-heavy soils drain slowly, and ground moisture wicks upward into unencapsulated crawl spaces year-round. By the time a homeowner notices a musty smell in the living space above, the floor joists and subfloor sheathing below may already show significant colonization.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Parkland

Effective mold remediation is not surface cleaning, it is a structured containment, removal, and verification process. When our IICRC AMRT-certified technicians arrive at a Parkland property, the first step is a thorough assessment: identifying moisture sources with thermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters, mapping the extent of affected materials, and determining whether spore counts in adjacent spaces have already elevated.

Containment comes next. We isolate the work area with negative air pressure and poly barriers so that disturbed spores cannot migrate to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, damaged wood sheathing, are removed and bagged per EPA guidelines. Non-porous surfaces are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Throughout the process, commercial-grade air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously inside the containment zone.

Because many Parkland homes have older construction, our technicians are EPA Lead-Safe Certified, which matters when remediation involves disturbing painted surfaces in pre-1978 homes. We handle the required lead-safe work practices on-site so the project does not stall waiting for a separate contractor.

Final clearance is confirmed through post-remediation verification, visual inspection and, where warranted, air sampling, before containment comes down.

## Parkland Insurance Coordination

Mold remediation coverage depends heavily on the documented cause of loss. When mold results from a covered peril, a sudden pipe burst, storm-driven water intrusion, or appliance failure, most homeowner policies will cover remediation costs, provided the claim is filed promptly and the damage is well-documented. Delayed reporting or a loss that appears to have developed slowly over months can complicate coverage.

Our team photographs affected materials systematically, documents moisture readings, and prepares scope-of-work documentation in the format most carriers expect. We work directly with adjusters and can bill most major insurance companies, reducing the out-of-pocket burden during an already stressful situation.
