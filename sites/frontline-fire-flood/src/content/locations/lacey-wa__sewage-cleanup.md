## Why Lacey Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Thurston County's soil profile, a mix of glacial outwash sands and clay-heavy pockets, drains unevenly. During prolonged wet seasons, saturated ground raises hydrostatic pressure against older clay or Orangeburg sewer laterals, which were commonly installed in Lacey neighborhoods developed through the 1970s and 1980s. Those pipes crack, offset, or collapse under the load, sending sewage backward into floor drains, toilets, and washing machine standpipes before a homeowner realizes anything is wrong.

Lacey also has a significant share of properties on private septic systems rather than the city sewer main, particularly in areas that were rural until relatively recently. Septic tank overflows and drain field failures produce the same Category 3 contamination as a municipal sewer backup and require the same level of personal protective equipment, containment, and antimicrobial treatment. Neither situation is a DIY cleanup.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lacey

When a crew arrives at your Lacey property, the first priority is containment, preventing sewage-contaminated water from wicking further into unaffected rooms or the HVAC system. We establish a work perimeter, suit up in full PPE, and begin extraction with truck-mounted and portable units capable of pulling standing effluent from hard floors, carpet, and subfloor cavities.

Once visible waste is removed, the process shifts to controlled demolition of non-salvageable materials. Saturated drywall, insulation, and carpet padding that have contacted raw sewage are bagged and disposed of per Washington State Department of Ecology guidelines for biohazardous waste, they cannot simply go to the curb. Structural framing and concrete slab surfaces are then treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents, applied in multiple passes. Moisture readings guide the placement of commercial air movers and dehumidifiers, and we monitor readings across multiple days to confirm the structure is drying to IICRC S500 standard before any rebuild begins.
