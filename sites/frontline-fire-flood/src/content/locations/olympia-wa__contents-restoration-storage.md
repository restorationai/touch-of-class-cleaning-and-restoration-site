## Our Contents Restoration Process in Olympia

The process begins with a detailed inventory before anything leaves your property. Every item is photographed, catalogued, and assigned a condition rating, this documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for ensuring nothing is misplaced during the pack-out. Fragile or high-value items are wrapped and boxed separately from general household goods.

From there, contents move to a controlled environment where technicians can work without the time pressure of an active loss site. Ultrasonic cleaning handles hard surfaces, ceramics, metals, glass, and electronics casings, by using high-frequency sound waves in a cleaning solution to remove soot and residue from crevices that a cloth can't reach. Soft contents go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment for odor neutralization depending on the material's sensitivity, followed by dry cleaning or wet cleaning based on fabric type.

Storage is climate-controlled and secured, which matters in Olympia's wet season when even a warehouse without active humidity management can push relative humidity high enough to cause secondary damage to items that were clean when they arrived. When your home is ready, contents are returned room by room against the original inventory.
