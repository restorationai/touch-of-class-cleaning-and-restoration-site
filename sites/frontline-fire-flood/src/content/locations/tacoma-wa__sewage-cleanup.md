## Why Tacoma Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Tacoma sits on a mix of glacial till and clay-heavy soils that drain poorly during the wet season, which runs roughly October through April. When heavy rain saturates the ground faster than the municipal system can handle it, combined sewer lines, common in older parts of the city, can surcharge and push effluent back through the lowest fixture in a building. Properties in historic neighborhoods like the North End and Hilltop, where housing stock frequently dates to the early 1900s, often have original clay tile lateral lines that have cracked, root-intruded, or settled over the decades. A compromised lateral doesn't just back up once, it backs up repeatedly until the line is repaired or replaced.

Septic systems in Tacoma's more suburban and transitional zones face a different pressure: saturated drain fields during wet months lose their ability to percolate, and effluent can surface in yards or migrate toward foundation walls. Either scenario, municipal sewer backup or septic overflow, qualifies as a biohazardous loss and requires professional extraction and sanitization, not a mop and a bottle of bleach.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Tacoma

The first priority on any sewage call is stopping the source and establishing containment. If the backup is still active, we coordinate with the property owner to confirm the line is cleared or the system is pumped before extraction begins, putting equipment into an active flow wastes time and spreads contamination further.

Once the source is controlled, the process moves through these stages:

- **Extraction and removal**, truck-mounted extraction units pull standing sewage and contaminated water from floors, sumps, and crawl spaces. Porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, subflooring, that have absorbed Category 3 water are removed rather than dried in place. Restoration industry standards do not allow drying contaminated porous materials; they have to go.
- **Structural cleaning and disinfection**, hard surfaces are cleaned with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. This includes floor joists, concrete slabs, wall framing, and any mechanical equipment in the affected zone.
- **Drying and monitoring**, after sanitization, the structural assembly is dried using commercial desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers. In Tacoma's climate, ambient humidity during the rainy season can work against drying if equipment is undersized; we monitor grain depression daily and adjust capacity as needed.
- **Clearance documentation**, before reconstruction begins, we document that affected surfaces meet post-remediation standards, which your insurance adjuster and any future buyer's inspector will want to see.

Our team holds IICRC Water Restoration Technician (WRT) and Applied Microbial Remediation Technician (AMRT) certifications, and the firm is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, relevant in Tacoma's older housing stock where disturbing building materials during demo can expose lead-based paint.

In Tacoma's older craftsman and Victorian-era homes, particularly those in the North End and Stadium District, cast iron drain stacks are common.
