## Why Olympia Properties Face Elevated Risk After Structural Damage

Thurston County averages well over 50 inches of rainfall annually, and Olympia sits at the wet end of that range. A roof breach that might stay dry for a few days in eastern Washington will be actively leaking here within the first rain event, often the same night as the loss. That urgency is compounded by the age of much of Olympia's housing stock. Neighborhoods closer to the downtown core and the waterfront include a significant number of homes built before 1960, many with original wood-framed windows, older roofing substrates, and lap siding that wicks moisture aggressively once the exterior envelope is broken. A temporary tarp or plywood panel isn't just a cosmetic placeholder on these structures, it's actively preventing the kind of moisture intrusion that turns a manageable repair into a full gut-and-rebuild.

Wind events also matter here. Storms tracking through the South Sound corridor regularly produce gusts that can lift improperly weighted tarps or shift panels that aren't anchored with the right fastener spacing. The board-up and tarping work we do is built to hold through those conditions, not just look secured at the moment we leave.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Olympia

When we arrive on-site, the first step is a perimeter walk to identify every opening, not just the obvious ones. Fire damage in particular can warp door frames and crack window glazing in areas that aren't immediately visible from the street. We photograph and document each opening before any material goes up, which matters for your insurance claim.

For window and door openings, we use exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to the rough opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails so the panel can be removed cleanly when permanent repairs begin. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and anchored at the ridge and eave lines, with additional ballast or mechanical fastening depending on the pitch and the forecast. On steeper roofs common in older Olympia construction, we adjust anchor points to account for runoff channeling so water doesn't pool under the tarp edge and find its way into the attic cavity.

We also coordinate directly with your insurance adjuster. Our documentation, photos, measurements, material specs, is formatted to support your claim from the start rather than leaving you to reconstruct the sequence of events later.

## Local Note: Older Siding and the Board-Up Fastener Question

Olympia has a meaningful number of homes clad in original cedar lap siding or early-era T1-11 panels that are already weathered and sometimes partially delaminated. On these surfaces, driving standard deck screws to anchor board-up panels can split the siding substrate and create new water entry points around the fastener holes. We carry a range of fastener types and backing plates specifically to address this, including techniques that anchor to the framing behind the siding rather than through it, so the board-up itself doesn't cause the kind of damage it's meant to prevent. It's a small detail that makes a real difference when the adjuster walks the property later.
