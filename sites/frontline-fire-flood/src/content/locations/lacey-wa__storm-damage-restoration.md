## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Lacey

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence of decisions matters. When we arrive, the first priority is stabilizing the structure, tarping breached rooflines, boarding compromised windows, and stopping active water intrusion before any drying equipment goes in. Putting dehumidifiers in a space that's still taking on water is a waste of time and your money.

Once the envelope is secured, we conduct moisture mapping using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to find water that has already migrated into wall cavities, subfloor assemblies, and insulation bays. In Lacey's crawl-space-heavy housing stock, that step is critical, water that enters through a foundation vent or a cracked stem wall can wick into floor joists and go undetected until rot or mold is already established. We document everything photographically for your insurance carrier, then develop a drying plan calibrated to the actual materials affected, not a generic timeline.
