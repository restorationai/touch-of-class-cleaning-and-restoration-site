## Why Tacoma Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After a Loss

A significant share of Tacoma's residential neighborhoods, from the Victorian-era homes near the Stadium District to the bungalows spread across Hilltop, were built before modern sheathing and vapor barriers were standard. That construction style means exterior walls have fewer redundant layers between the interior and the elements. When a window breaks or a fire burns through a roof section, rain doesn't just wet the surface; it wicks into old-growth fir framing and plaster lath that can hold moisture for weeks.

Tacoma also sits in a climate band where the rainy season effectively runs from October through April, and atmospheric river events can drop several inches of rain in 24 hours. A property left open overnight during one of those events isn't just damp, it's a mold-colonization timeline that starts within 24 to 48 hours. Board-up and tarping aren't cosmetic steps; in this climate, they're the difference between a contained loss and a full structural remediation.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Tacoma

When you call, a crew is dispatched immediately, day or night. The first task on arrival is a rapid exterior assessment: which openings are actively letting in weather or allowing access, and what's the structural condition of the area around each breach. On older Tacoma homes, we pay close attention to the condition of the fascia and decking before anchoring a tarp, because rotted wood won't hold fasteners under load and a tarp that pulls free in a windstorm creates a second loss event.

For window and door openings, we use OSB panels cut and fitted to the frame, secured in a way that meets the property's immediate security needs without causing additional damage to the surrounding trim. Roof tarps are weighted and anchored using a ridge-cap method rather than nailing directly into the roofing material where possible, which matters when the same roof will need to be assessed by an insurance adjuster. Every opening is photographed before and after boarding, and that documentation goes directly into the claim file.

We hold IICRC certifications in fire and smoke restoration and structural drying, and our team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, relevant on Tacoma properties built before 1978, where disturbing painted surfaces during board-up work requires specific handling protocols.

## Local Note

Tacoma's older neighborhoods contain a meaningful number of homes with balloon-frame construction, a framing style common before the 1940s where wall cavities run continuously from the foundation to the roof without the fire blocking found in platform-frame buildings. After a fire in a balloon-frame home, heat and smoke can travel vertically through wall cavities in ways that aren't obvious from the exterior. When we board up a fire-damaged balloon-frame structure, we note this for the restoration team and the adjuster, because the visible damage footprint often understates what's happening inside the walls. It's the kind of detail that changes the scope of a claim.
