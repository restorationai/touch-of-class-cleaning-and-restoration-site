## Why Bellevue Properties Face Acute Exposure After a Loss

Bellevue sits in a climate band where October through April brings sustained moisture, not dramatic single-storm events, but persistent low-pressure systems that keep relative humidity elevated and push water into any opening a fire or impact has created. A roof breach that might stay dry for 48 hours in a drier climate can absorb meaningful water infiltration overnight here.

The city's building stock adds another layer of complexity. Bellevue's residential neighborhoods include a significant share of homes built during the post-war suburban expansion of the 1950s and 1960s alongside newer construction that accelerated through the tech-driven growth of the 1990s and 2000s. Older homes often have skip-sheathing under composition roofing, a board-and-gap pattern that gives tarps less continuous surface to seal against than modern OSB decking. Getting a tarp to lie flat and anchor properly on that substrate takes more fastener points and a heavier-gauge poly than a standard residential kit.

On the commercial side, Bellevue's density of mid-rise mixed-use and office construction means broken storefront glazing after a vehicle impact or vandalism is a recurring scenario. Boarding a large commercial opening requires different panel sizing and framing than a residential window, and in Bellevue's downtown core, the work often happens alongside active pedestrian traffic.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Bellevue

When you call, the first questions are about the type of opening, the roof pitch if tarping is needed, and whether the structure is safe to enter. That assessment shapes what the crew loads before leaving.

**Structural openings and windows:** Panels are cut from exterior-grade plywood and secured with structural screws into framing members, not just into siding or trim. For fire-damaged frames where the surrounding wood is compromised, we sister in temporary blocking so the panel has something solid to bite into.

**Roof tarping:** We use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for the weight of Pacific Northwest rainfall and anchor them with cap nails and baton strips across the ridge and down the field. On skip-sheathed roofs common in Bellevue's older neighborhoods, we add additional batten rows to bridge the gaps in the decking and prevent the tarp from sagging into cavities where pooled water can work through.

**Documentation:** Every opening is photographed before and after boarding. Measurements, materials used, and fastener locations are logged for the insurance claim file. If your adjuster needs a scope of the temporary protection work, it's already prepared.

All work is performed by an IICRC-certified firm with FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration Technician) credentialing on staff, which matters when board-up is the first step in a fire damage claim sequence.
