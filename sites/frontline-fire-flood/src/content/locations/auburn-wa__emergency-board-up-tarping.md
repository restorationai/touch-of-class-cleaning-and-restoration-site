When fire scorches a wall, a storm peels back roofing, or a vehicle punches through a garage door, the structure is open to whatever the weather decides to do next, and in Auburn, that weather rarely waits. Frontline Fire & Flood provides 24/7 emergency board-up and tarping so that a bad night doesn't become a gutted building.

## Why Auburn Properties Face Elevated Exposure After Structural Damage

The Green River Valley's geography channels moisture in ways that matter for open structures. Low-lying areas near the valley floor can see ground fog and sustained drizzle that keeps relative humidity elevated for days, conditions that accelerate mold colonization in exposed wall cavities and insulation. Auburn's housing stock spans several eras: mid-century ranch homes with original wood siding, 1970s and 1980s tract construction with composition sheathing, and newer mixed-use and multifamily development closer to Auburn Way. Older wood-framed homes absorb wind-driven rain quickly once a window or roof section is compromised, and that saturation can reach wall framing and subfloor before a homeowner has even filed a claim.

Auburn also sits along active freight and commuter rail corridors, and the city's industrial zones generate occasional structure fires that leave commercial buildings open to the elements. Whether the damage is residential or commercial, the exposure window, the time between the loss event and a secured, weather-tight structure, is what determines how much secondary damage follows.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Auburn

When you call, the first question is what you're dealing with: fire damage, storm damage, a vehicle impact, vandalism, or something else. That determines what materials and crew size we dispatch. For a broken window or door, we cut and install OSB panels secured with structural screws rather than staples, which hold through sustained wind and deter unauthorized entry. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps weighted and fastened at the decking level, not just draped over the peak, so they stay in place through the kind of multi-day rain events Auburn sees in fall and winter.

On fire-damaged structures, board-up serves a second purpose beyond weather protection: it secures the site against entry while the insurance adjuster and fire investigator complete their work. We document the condition of every opening before we cover it, photographs, measurements, and notes on pre-existing conditions, because that documentation supports your claim and protects you if questions arise later. Our team holds IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke Restoration Technician) certification, so the crew reading the structure understands fire behavior and can flag secondary hazards like compromised load-bearing elements before anyone steps into an unsafe area.

## Auburn Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner and commercial property policies cover emergency protective measures as part of the loss, but the documentation has to be there to support the line item. We provide a written scope of work, before-and-after photographs, and a material and labor summary formatted for adjuster review. If your carrier wants a direct conversation about the protective measures taken, we're available for that. We bill major carriers directly when the policy allows it, which means you're not fronting the full cost out of pocket while the claim processes.
