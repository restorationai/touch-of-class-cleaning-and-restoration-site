## Why Auburn Properties See Contents Damage Differently

The Green River Valley's geography matters here. Auburn's lower-lying areas have historically dealt with flooding, and while the Howard Hanson Dam upstream provides meaningful flood control, heavy rain seasons still push groundwater up through slab foundations and into crawl spaces, particularly in older ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and 1970s that are common throughout the city. When water intrudes from below, it doesn't just damage flooring; it wicks into furniture legs, cardboard storage boxes, fabric upholstery, and anything else sitting at or near floor level.

Fire losses in Auburn carry their own wrinkle. The valley's temperature inversions, cold air settling low while warmer air sits above, can trap smoke odor in a home longer than you'd expect after a fire is extinguished. Smoke particles settle into soft goods, electronics, documents, and clothing at a molecular level, and without proper HEPA vacuuming, ultrasonic cleaning, and ozone or hydroxyl treatment, that odor migrates to every room. An IICRC FSRT-certified approach to contents means treating the items themselves, not just airing out the structure.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Auburn

The process starts with a room-by-room contents inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, categorized by material type, and assessed for restorability versus replacement, documentation your insurance adjuster needs and that protects you from disputes later. Items that can be restored are packed out in labeled, sealed containers and transported to a climate-controlled facility for cleaning.

Cleaning methods are matched to the material. Electronics go through dry cleaning and inspection before any power-on test. Textiles, curtains, upholstery, clothing, are processed through HEPA vacuuming and then either laundered or dry-cleaned depending on fabric. Hard goods like furniture, kitchenware, and decorative items are cleaned with appropriate solutions and, where smoke odor is present, cycled through deodorization treatment. Documents and photographs get priority handling; some can be air-dried and stabilized, while severely saturated paper may require freeze-drying referral.

Secure, climate-controlled storage holds everything until your home passes the structural drying and repair milestones. At pack-back, items are returned to the rooms and positions noted in the original inventory, not just stacked in a garage for you to sort out.
