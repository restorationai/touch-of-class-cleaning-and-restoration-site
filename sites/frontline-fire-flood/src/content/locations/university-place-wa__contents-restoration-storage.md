## Our Contents Restoration Process in University Place

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory, every item photographed, categorized, and logged before anything moves. That documentation matters for insurance purposes and for your own peace of mind. From there, contents fall into one of three tracks:

**Pack-out and off-site restoration** is the right call when the structure itself needs drying, demolition, or remediation work that would re-expose cleaned items to dust, odor, or humidity. We transport belongings in sealed, climate-controlled conditions to our Lakewood facility, where they're cleaned, deodorized, and stored until your home is ready.

**On-site cleaning and drying** works when damage is contained to a single room and the rest of the home is unaffected. Ultrasonic cleaning equipment handles hard goods, ceramics, metals, some electronics components, while textile cleaning addresses upholstered furniture, rugs, and drapes.

**Specialty handling** covers documents, photographs, artwork, and electronics. Wet documents can sometimes be freeze-dried to halt further deterioration; smoke-damaged photographs respond to specific dry-cleaning methods that standard laundering would destroy. We assess each category separately rather than applying a single process across the board.

All restored items are tracked through the same inventory system used at intake, so nothing is misplaced between pack-out and return delivery.

## Working with Insurance on Contents Claims in University Place

Contents losses are frequently underestimated in the initial claim because adjusters focus on structural line items first. A detailed, itemized inventory, completed before anything is discarded or moved without documentation, is the single most important thing a homeowner can have when negotiating a contents claim. We provide that inventory as part of our standard process, formatted in a way that most major carriers accept without requiring reformatting.

If your policy includes ALE (additional living expense) coverage, off-site storage of your contents during repairs may be a covered line item. We can communicate directly with your adjuster about storage duration and conditions so that reimbursement is as straightforward as possible.
