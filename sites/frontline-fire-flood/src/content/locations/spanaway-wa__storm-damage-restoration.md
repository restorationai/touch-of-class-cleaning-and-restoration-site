When a Douglas fir drops across your roof at 2 a.m. or a wind-driven rain finds the gap behind your fascia board, the damage compounds fast, and Frontline Fire & Flood responds 24/7 from our Lakewood base to stop it.

## Why Spanaway Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Storm Damage

The geography here works against homeowners in a few specific ways. Spanaway sits on glacially deposited soils that drain unevenly, some parcels shed water quickly while neighboring lots pool it for days after a heavy storm. That standing water doesn't just threaten landscaping; it wicks under slab foundations and into crawl spaces, where it accelerates wood rot and mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of the initial event.

The housing stock adds another layer of complexity. A significant portion of Spanaway's residential neighborhoods were built during the post-war boom of the 1950s through 1970s, when roofing materials, sheathing, and window flashing were not engineered for the kind of atmospheric river events that now roll through Pierce County several times each winter. Older asphalt shingles lose their granule coating over time, and once a storm strips a section, the underlayment beneath can saturate in a single rain event. Wind damage that looks cosmetic from the street, a few missing shingles, a lifted ridge cap, often means water has already reached the decking.

Tall conifers throughout the area are a year-round risk. Root systems in the clay-heavy soils here can become unstable after prolonged wet seasons, and a wind event that wouldn't topple a tree in drier conditions can bring one down across a roof or fence line.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Spanaway

When we arrive on-site, the first priority is stopping active water intrusion. That means emergency tarping of roof breaches, boarding compromised windows or doors, and identifying any interior pathways where rainwater is still migrating. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to map the full extent of saturation, because water travels, and the wet area visible to the eye is rarely the full picture.

Once the structure is stabilized, we document everything: photographs, moisture readings, and a written scope of damage that your insurance adjuster can work from directly. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers calibrated to the affected materials. Crawl spaces and wall cavities get particular attention here, because Spanaway's damp climate means any residual moisture left behind becomes a mold problem within days.

Debris removal, fallen limbs, damaged roofing materials, compromised insulation, is handled as part of the same project rather than handed off to a separate contractor, which keeps the timeline tighter and the chain of documentation clean for your claim.

Frontline Fire & Flood holds IICRC certifications in Water Damage Restoration, Structural Drying, and Applied Microbial Remediation, and we are EPA Lead-Safe Certified, relevant for the older homes in the area where lead-based paint may be present in materials disturbed during storm repairs.

## Local Note

Crawl-space flooding is one of the most underreported storm consequences in Spanaway's older ranch-style and split-level homes. Because crawl spaces aren't living areas, homeowners often don't discover saturation until they smell the mildew weeks later, by which point the floor joists and subfloor sheathing may already show early fungal growth. After any significant storm event, it's worth pulling the crawl access hatch and looking for standing water or condensation on the vapor barrier before assuming the structure came through clean. If you see either, call before you start drying it yourself, improper airflow in a confined crawl space can spread spores rather than eliminate them.
