## Our Water Cleanup Process in University Place

Every job starts with moisture mapping, not assumptions. Using thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters, the crew identifies where water has traveled, not just where it's visible. Water behind plaster or under hardwood can read dry at the surface while the subfloor below stays saturated. That hidden moisture is where mold starts.

Once the extent of the loss is documented, standing water is extracted with truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Structural drying follows using commercial-grade desiccant and refrigerant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers positioned to create directed airflow through wall cavities and under flooring. Drying is monitored daily against psychrometric targets until affected materials reach pre-loss moisture levels, the process follows the IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

Because many University Place homes have finished crawl spaces or partial basements, the crew pays close attention to vapor transmission from below. A room can appear dry while the crawl space directly underneath is still holding moisture that will wick upward for weeks.
