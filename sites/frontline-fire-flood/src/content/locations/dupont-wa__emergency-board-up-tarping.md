## Why DuPont Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Risks

DuPont's development history is unusual for Pierce County. Much of the city's residential stock was built in concentrated waves, first as company housing tied to the DuPont explosives plant, then in rapid suburban expansion as the old industrial land was redeveloped into planned communities in the 1990s and 2000s. That newer construction era means many homes here use engineered lumber framing, composite sheathing, and OSB roof decking. Those materials perform well under normal conditions, but they absorb moisture faster than older-growth dimensional lumber when exposed to Puget Sound rainfall through a compromised roof or broken window. A tarp delay of even 12 to 24 hours can mean the difference between a contained repair and saturated sheathing that requires full panel replacement.

The area also sits close to Joint Base Lewis-McChord, which generates steady traffic on Center Drive and Ponder Drive, the main arteries through DuPont's residential sections. Emergency access coordination matters here, and our crews account for gate and traffic patterns when routing to a loss site.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in DuPont

When you call, the first question we ask is what's open to the weather and what caused it, that shapes what we load on the truck. A fire-damaged structure needs different staging than a storm-breached roof or a smashed sliding door.

For window and door board-up, we cut plywood to fit the rough opening, fasten it with structural screws rather than nails (which back out under wind load), and seal the perimeter against driven rain. For roof tarping, we use reinforced poly tarps weighted and anchored with 2x4 batten boards screwed through the tarp into the decking, not just draped over the ridge and held down with sandbags, which fail in the kind of gusts DuPont sees off the Sound. We photograph every step for your insurance file.

If the structure has fire or smoke damage, our IICRC FSRT-certified technicians assess whether the framing is stable before crew members go onto the roof, a step that matters on the engineered-truss construction common in DuPont's planned communities, where fire can compromise a truss faster than it would traditional rafter framing.

## DuPont Insurance Coordination

Most homeowner policies cover emergency protective measures, board-up and tarping are typically classified as mitigation, not repair, which means they're billable under the same claim as the underlying damage. We document the loss condition before we touch anything: photos of the breach, the cause where visible, and the extent of immediate weather exposure. That documentation package is formatted to support your adjuster's review and is provided at no extra charge. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your adjuster to keep the process moving.

If your DuPont home is part of a homeowners association, check your CC&Rs, some planned communities here have aesthetic standards that apply even to temporary protective measures. We've encountered this in similar master-planned developments and can use materials and colors that satisfy most HOA requirements while still providing a weathertight seal.
