## Why Yelm Properties Face Distinctive Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Yelm's growth over the past two decades has layered newer construction on top of an older rural base. The area around Yelm's downtown core still has homes built in the 1970s and 1980s with wood-framed single-pane windows and shake or composition roofs that have seen decades of Puget Sound-adjacent weather cycles, freeze-thaw in winter, dry heat in summer, and sustained rain in the shoulder seasons. Older shake roofs in particular are brittle at the edges; when a branch or windborne debris strikes, the damage often radiates further than it looks from the ground, leaving underlayment exposed across a wider area than a single missing shingle would suggest.

The surrounding Nisqually River basin also means that storm events here can arrive with more moisture than the inland location implies. When a roof breach or broken window goes unprotected overnight, attic insulation and wall cavities can absorb enough water in a single rain event to create conditions favorable for mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. Tarping that is improperly weighted or anchored, a common failure with DIY attempts, can actually funnel water into the breach rather than away from it.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Yelm

When a call comes in from the Yelm area, the first question is structural: what is exposed, and what is the weather window? Our IICRC-certified team arrives with pre-cut plywood panels, heavy-gauge polyethylene tarps, and the fastening hardware to secure both to a range of roof and wall assemblies, from standard OSB sheathing to the older skip-sheathing found under many Yelm-area shake roofs.

For roof tarping, we run the tarp past the ridge line and weight or nail the leading edge so wind cannot catch it from below. On older homes where the fascia or rake boards are soft with age, we reinforce the anchor points rather than relying on deteriorated wood to hold tension. For window and door board-up after fire or impact damage, we use structural plywood cut to the rough opening and secured with exterior screws, not staples, so the panel holds against attempted entry and weather pressure equally.

If fire damage is involved, we coordinate with the fire marshal's release before beginning work, since Thurston County fire investigators sometimes need to document the scene before the structure is altered. We do not begin board-up on a fire-loss property until we have confirmed clearance.
