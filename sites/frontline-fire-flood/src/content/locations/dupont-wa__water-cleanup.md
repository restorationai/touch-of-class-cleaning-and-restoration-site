## Why DuPont Properties See Water Damage Differently

DuPont's housing stock tells two distinct stories. The historic district near the old company town, some of those structures date to the early twentieth century, was built with materials and construction methods that behave very differently under moisture than modern framing. Older plank subfloors and dimensional lumber joists absorb and hold water longer than engineered wood products, and they're more prone to cupping and rot if drying isn't handled correctly. On the newer side, the master-planned neighborhoods that grew up through the 1990s and 2000s used tighter building envelopes that can actually trap moisture inside wall cavities once water finds a way in.

The regional climate adds pressure. Western Washington's extended wet season, rain that arrives in October and doesn't fully let go until late spring, means structures here cycle through wet and dry repeatedly. Crawl spaces are especially vulnerable: the combination of ground moisture wicking upward and inadequate vapor barriers creates conditions where a single plumbing failure can find an already-damp substrate.

## Our Water Cleanup Process in DuPont

When we arrive, the first priority is stopping the source if it's still active, then assessing the true extent of the moisture, not just what's visible. Water travels. It follows framing cavities, migrates under flooring, and pools in the lowest accessible space. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map where the water actually went, not just where it pooled on the surface.

Extraction comes next: truck-mounted and portable units pull standing water and saturated material out of carpet, pad, and subfloor. Then we set a drying system, commercial-grade air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers positioned based on the room geometry and material types present. Every affected structure gets daily moisture readings logged against the IICRC S500 standard so drying progress is documented, not estimated. For homes with crawl spaces, we address that zone separately; a dried living space above a wet crawl will re-absorb moisture within days if the crawl isn't treated.

Frontline Fire & Flood is an IICRC Certified Firm with technicians holding Water Restoration Technician (WRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD) credentials, and the team is EPA Lead-Safe Certified, relevant in DuPont's older housing stock where disturbing building materials requires that precaution.

Most standard homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an overflow, though gradual leak damage is frequently excluded.
