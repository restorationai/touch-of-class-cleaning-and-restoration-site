## Why Seattle Properties Create Unique Challenges for Contents

Seattle's housing stock spans more than a century of construction, and older homes, particularly those built before World War II in neighborhoods like Capitol Hill and Queen Anne, tend to have plaster walls, single-pane windows, and less vapor control than modern builds. When water or fire damage occurs, contents absorb odors and moisture faster because interior humidity climbs quickly and stays elevated. Smoke from a kitchen or electrical fire in a tightly built older home doesn't just coat surfaces, it penetrates upholstered furniture, paper, and fabric in ways that require professional ozone treatment or thermal fogging rather than simple surface cleaning.

Beyond the housing stock, Seattle's proximity to Puget Sound means ambient relative humidity rarely drops low enough for passive air-drying to protect salvageable items. Contents left in place during structural drying are often re-exposed to elevated moisture levels even after extraction equipment is running. Removing items to a controlled storage environment isn't a luxury here, it's frequently the difference between a piece of furniture being restored and it being written off entirely.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Seattle

Every item that leaves your property is inventoried before it moves. We photograph and log contents room by room, assign condition ratings, and provide you with a complete chain-of-custody record, something insurance adjusters working Seattle claims consistently request during the documentation phase.

From there, the process depends on what each item needs:

- **Pack-out and transport**: Items are wrapped, boxed, and transported to our climate-controlled facility. Fragile or high-value pieces are handled with padded materials and documented individually.
- **Cleaning and deodorization**: Smoke-affected textiles go through thermal fogging or ozone chambers. Hard surfaces are cleaned using methods matched to the material, ultrasonic cleaning for small electronics and jewelry, hand-cleaning for antiques and artwork.
- **Controlled drying**: Items with moisture exposure are dried in a low-humidity environment with monitored temperature and airflow, separate from the ambient conditions at your property.
- **Secure storage**: Contents remain in our facility under climate control until your home is ready to receive them. You are not paying for a commercial storage unit you have to manage, we coordinate return delivery directly with your restoration timeline.
