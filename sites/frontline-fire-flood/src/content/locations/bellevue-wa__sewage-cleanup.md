## Why Bellevue Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Bellevue sits on a mix of glacial till and clay-heavy soils that drain poorly during sustained rainfall, the kind of slow, soaking rain that defines western Washington from October through April. When ground saturation rises, hydrostatic pressure pushes against older lateral sewer lines, and roots from the area's mature tree canopy exploit any crack or joint in clay or cast-iron pipe. The result is a partial or complete blockage that sends sewage back the way it came: into your home.

The city's housing stock tells two different stories. Neighborhoods developed in the 1960s and 1970s, many of them now sitting under high-value renovations, still carry original vitrified clay laterals that were never designed for today's household water loads. Meanwhile, the dense mixed-use corridors that have grown up around the Bel-Red area include newer buildings with more complex shared sewer tie-ins, where a blockage in one unit can affect multiple floors or adjacent tenants. Both scenarios require professional extraction and sanitization, not just clearing the clog.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Bellevue

Category 3 water, the classification for raw sewage, is treated as a biohazard from the moment we arrive. The process follows IICRC S500 protocols and moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Containment first.** We isolate the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean spaces. In finished basements common to Bellevue's hillside homes, this means sealing HVAC returns and doorways before any material is disturbed.

**Extraction and removal.** Standing sewage is extracted with truck-mounted units. Porous materials, carpet, pad, drywall, insulation, that absorbed Category 3 water are removed and bagged for disposal. There is no drying and saving contaminated porous material; that's not a cost-cutting option, it's a health code requirement.

**Disinfection and deodorization.** Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobials. We pay particular attention to subfloor cavities, which trap moisture and odor long after the visible mess is gone.

**Structural drying.** Once the space is sanitized, commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers bring the structural moisture content down to pre-loss levels. Our IICRC ASD-certified technicians monitor readings daily until the structure passes.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, photo, and material removal log goes into a file formatted for insurance review.
