## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lacey

When you call, the first question we ask is what type of event you have, fire, storm, structural failure, or break-in, because the approach differs. A fire-damaged opening may have weakened framing that cannot support a standard plywood panel without additional blocking. A roof breach from a fallen tree requires assessing the load still on the structure before anyone climbs.

Our general sequence:

1. **Site assessment**, We walk the perimeter and identify every breach: broken windows, compromised doors, open roof sections, and any structural concerns that affect safe access.
2. **Roof tarping**, Heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are secured with batten boards screwed into the roof deck, not just weighted down. This keeps the tarp in place through sustained wind rather than peeling back in the next gust.
3. **Window and door board-up**, We cut OSB panels to fit each opening, secure them with structural screws, and where framing is intact, add perimeter blocking so the panel cannot be pushed inward.
4. **Documentation**, Every opening is photographed before and after. That photo set goes to your insurance carrier as part of the emergency mitigation record.
5. **Handoff**, We leave a written scope of what was secured, what materials were used, and what the next recommended steps are, whether that is a full roof replacement, fire restoration, or structural assessment.

All work is performed by an IICRC-certified team operating under license #CCFRONTFF761CB.
