## Why DuPont Properties See Asbestos Concerns

DuPont's residential development didn't follow a single wave, the city grew in distinct phases tied to the military presence at Joint Base Lewis-McChord and later to the master-planned communities built in the 1990s and 2000s. The older housing stock, particularly homes from the 1940s through the early 1980s, is where asbestos risk concentrates. Textured ceilings, vinyl floor tiles, joint compound, and HVAC insulation installed during that era routinely contained asbestos fibers. Pierce County's wet winters accelerate the deterioration of older building materials, and when insulation or tile adhesive starts to crack and crumble, a condition called friable asbestos, fibers can become airborne. A damp crawl space or a roof that's been leaking seasonally for years can push materials that were once stable into a friable state faster than most homeowners realize.

## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in DuPont

Every abatement project starts with a proper inspection and, where warranted, bulk sampling sent to an accredited laboratory, because visual identification alone cannot confirm asbestos content. Once results come back, the scope of work is defined: encapsulation for stable, non-friable materials in low-disturbance areas, or full removal for anything that's damaged, friable, or in the path of planned renovation.

During removal, the work area is isolated with negative-air containment and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running continuously. Workers wear full PPE and follow Washington State Department of Labor & Industries notification requirements, which apply to most renovation and demolition projects involving regulated amounts of asbestos-containing material. Waste is double-bagged, labeled, and transported to a licensed disposal facility, not a standard landfill. After removal, air clearance testing confirms fiber levels are below action thresholds before containment comes down. The team holds EPA Lead-Safe certification and is OSHA trained, which matters when abatement overlaps with lead paint, common in the same pre-1980 homes where asbestos shows up.

## DuPont Insurance & Permit Coordination

Asbestos abatement in Washington requires advance notification to the Puget Sound Clean Air Agency for projects that meet or exceed regulated thresholds, a step that catches some homeowners off guard when they're mid-renovation.
