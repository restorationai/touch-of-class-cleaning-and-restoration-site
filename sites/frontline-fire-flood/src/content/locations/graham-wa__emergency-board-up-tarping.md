## Why Graham Properties See Elevated Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Graham's semi-rural character means a lot of the housing stock sits on larger lots with mature Douglas fir and big-leaf maple trees overhead, beautiful in the summer, a liability in a windstorm. Fire damage creates a different but equally urgent exposure. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Graham

When a call comes in, the first step is a fast size-up by phone: what type of event, how many openings, is the structure still occupied, and are utilities live. That information shapes what materials go on the truck before we leave Lakewood.

On arrival, the crew walks the exterior before touching anything, identifying every breach, assessing roof load and structural stability, and flagging any hazards like downed lines or compromised decking. Board-up work uses exterior-grade plywood cut and fitted to each opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails where the framing condition allows. For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps anchored with 2x4 cap boards screwed into the decking on either side of the damaged area, not just draped and weighted. A tarp that lifts in a 40 mph wind gust does nothing.

If the loss involves fire or smoke, we document every opening photographically before and after boarding, both for the insurance adjuster and to establish a clear scope for the reconstruction phase that follows.
