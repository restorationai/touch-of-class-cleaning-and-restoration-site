## Why South Hill Properties See Elevated Contents Risk

The clay-dominant soil across much of unincorporated Pierce County drains poorly, which means water from a single heavy storm or a slow plumbing failure doesn't just stay in the crawlspace, it migrates. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, which make up a large share of South Hill's residential neighborhoods, were frequently constructed with finished lower levels and carpeted family rooms that sit close to grade. When water intrudes at that level, it contacts furniture, electronics, stored clothing, and documents before a homeowner often realizes what's happening.

Fire losses carry their own contents challenge here. South Hill's residential density means homes share fencelines and sometimes attic space in townhome configurations. Smoke from a contained kitchen fire can travel through HVAC returns and deposit soot on belongings two or three rooms away from the origin point, items that look untouched but carry odor and acidic residue that continues breaking down fabric and finish if left untreated.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in South Hill

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything leaves your home. Every item is photographed, catalogued by condition, and assigned a priority, salvageable with cleaning, salvageable with specialized treatment, or non-salvageable, so your insurance adjuster and you have a clear, documented record from day one.

Salvageable contents are packed and transported to our climate-controlled facility. Soft goods, upholstered furniture, clothing, bedding, curtains, go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize smoke odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Hard surfaces are cleaned using methods matched to the material: ultrasonic cleaning for small items and collectibles, hand-wiping with appropriate solvents for electronics and art, and dry-ice blasting for items with heavy soot. Documents and photographs receive freeze-drying if water damage is involved.

Items remain in secure, temperature- and humidity-controlled storage until your home is structurally ready to receive them. We coordinate the return delivery with your contractor's schedule so contents aren't sitting in a partially dried or freshly painted space.
