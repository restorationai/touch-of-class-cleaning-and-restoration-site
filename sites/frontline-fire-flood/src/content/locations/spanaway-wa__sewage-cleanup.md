## Why Spanaway Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

The area around Spanaway has a layered drainage challenge that many residents don't think about until something goes wrong. A significant share of properties, particularly those on larger lots south of Spanaway Lake and in older residential pockets closer to the Graham border, are served by private septic systems rather than Pierce County sewer mains. When those systems reach capacity during the region's wet season, or when drain fields become saturated after extended rainfall, sewage can reverse course into the lowest fixtures in the house: floor drains, basement toilets, laundry tubs.

Properties that *are* connected to the municipal system face a different but equally real risk. Older lateral lines, tree-root intrusion, and grease accumulation in aging cast-iron pipe can cause sewer line backups that push effluent back through tub drains and toilets with little warning. Pierce County's clay-heavy soils in parts of Spanaway also limit how quickly ground saturation dissipates after heavy rain, which can overwhelm even well-maintained systems during back-to-back storm events.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Spanaway

Every sewage call follows a structured sequence, because cutting corners on Category 3 contamination creates health risks that linger long after the visible mess is gone.

**Containment and safety assessment** come first. The affected area is isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Personal protective equipment is mandatory for every crew member on-site.

**Extraction and removal** follow immediately. Standing sewage is extracted using truck-mounted equipment. Porous materials, drywall, insulation, carpet, subfloor, that absorbed contaminated water are removed rather than dried in place. This is not optional: the IICRC S500 and S520 standards are explicit that Category 3 materials cannot be restored through drying alone.

**Antimicrobial treatment and sanitization** address what extraction leaves behind. Hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for sewage contamination. We document every step with photographs for your insurance file.

**Structural drying** of salvageable materials follows, using calibrated desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and air movers. Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is verifiable, not estimated.

**Clearance verification** closes the job. We don't call a space clean because it looks clean, final moisture readings and visual inspection confirm the environment is ready for reconstruction.

Homeowners insurance coverage for sewage backup varies significantly by policy.
