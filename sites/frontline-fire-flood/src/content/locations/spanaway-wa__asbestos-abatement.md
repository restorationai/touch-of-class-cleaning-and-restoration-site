## Our Asbestos Abatement Process in Spanaway

Every project starts with a thorough inspection and, where suspect materials are present, sample collection sent to an accredited laboratory for analysis. We don't guess at what's asbestos-containing, lab confirmation drives the scope of work.

Once the scope is confirmed, the process follows a defined sequence:

- **Containment**: Poly sheeting and negative air pressure isolate the work area from the rest of the structure. HVAC systems are sealed to prevent cross-contamination.
- **Wet removal**: Asbestos-containing materials are kept wet during removal to suppress fiber release. Popcorn ceiling texture, floor tile, and mastic are removed by hand, no grinding, no dry scraping.
- **Bagging and labeling**: All waste is double-bagged in labeled, puncture-resistant containers per Washington State Department of Ecology requirements.
- **Air clearance**: Clearance air sampling is conducted after cleanup and before containment is removed. We coordinate with an independent industrial hygienist when project specifications or permit conditions require third-party verification.
- **Proper disposal**: Asbestos waste is transported to a permitted disposal facility, not a standard construction debris site.

Washington State requires notification to the Department of Ecology for regulated asbestos projects above threshold quantities. We handle that notification as part of the project, so the permit and regulatory paperwork doesn't fall on the property owner.
