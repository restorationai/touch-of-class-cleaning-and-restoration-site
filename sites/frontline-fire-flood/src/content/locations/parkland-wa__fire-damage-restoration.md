## Our Fire and Smoke Restoration Process in Parkland

Every fire loss is assessed before any cleaning begins. The first step is a room-by-room inspection that documents structural damage, smoke migration paths, and any materials that require containment or testing before they're disturbed. Smoke doesn't respect walls, it follows HVAC ducts, travels through electrical chases, and settles in closets two rooms away from the point of origin.

From there, the process moves through:

- **Structural stabilization**, boarding windows, tarping roof sections, and securing the building envelope against Parkland's rain before secondary water damage compounds the loss.
- **Debris removal and content inventory**, separating salvageable contents from unsalvageable ones, with documentation that supports your insurance claim.
- **Smoke and soot cleaning**, dry chemical sponges for loose soot, thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation for odor penetration in structural cavities, and HEPA-filtered air scrubbers running continuously during the process.
- **Structural drying**, fire suppression water introduces its own damage; drying is conducted per the IICRC S500 standard alongside smoke remediation, not after it.
- **Reconstruction coordination**, when framing, drywall, or subfloor needs replacement, the scope is documented and handed off cleanly for rebuild.
