## Why Spanaway Properties See Mold Issues

Pierce County's west side receives consistent rainfall through the fall and winter months, and Spanaway's relatively flat terrain means water doesn't always move away from structures quickly. Crawl spaces are a particular concern here. Many of the single-story homes built in Spanaway during the 1970s and 1980s were constructed over vented crawl spaces with minimal ground-cover vapor barriers, a design that made sense in drier climates but invites ground moisture to migrate upward into floor joists and subfloor sheathing in the Pacific Northwest. When that moisture stays elevated for more than 24 to 48 hours, mold colonization can begin on wood framing even if the living space above feels dry.

Newer subdivisions on the eastern edges of the city have their own vulnerabilities. Tighter building envelopes and reduced natural ventilation can trap humidity generated by cooking, bathing, and even breathing, especially during the months when windows stay closed. A slow plumbing leak behind drywall in one of these homes can go undetected for weeks, giving mold a substantial head start before the odor becomes noticeable.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Spanaway

Remediation is not the same as cleaning. Wiping visible mold from a surface without addressing the moisture source and the affected substrate beneath it typically results in regrowth within weeks. The process we follow is structured around the IICRC S520 standard for mold remediation and calibrated to what we actually find in Spanaway homes.

The first step is a thorough moisture investigation, thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to map where water has traveled, not just where mold is visible. Containment comes next: negative air pressure barriers isolate the work area so that disturbed mold spores don't migrate to unaffected rooms during removal. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, compromised wood, are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously throughout the process.

Before containment comes down, air quality is verified. We work with independent industrial hygienists when post-remediation clearance testing is required, ensuring the result comes from a third party with no stake in the outcome.

## Spanaway Insurance Coordination

Whether mold is covered under a homeowner's policy depends heavily on the cause. Mold that resulted from a sudden, accidental water loss, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, is often covered. Mold that developed slowly from a long-term leak or deferred maintenance typically is not. We document the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected materials, and a written scope of work that gives your adjuster what they need to make a coverage determination. We bill carriers directly when coverage applies so you're not managing that process while also dealing with the disruption of a mold loss.

## Local Note

Crawl spaces in Spanaway-area homes built before the mid-1990s often have inadequate cross-ventilation, fewer vents than current code recommends, and in some cases vents that have been blocked by landscaping or insulation added during energy upgrades. When we inspect these spaces, we frequently find that the mold problem visible near a floor register or along a baseboard is actually sourced from below, not from a plumbing issue inside the living space. Addressing the crawl space, improving ventilation, installing or replacing the ground cover vapor barrier, is often as important as the remediation work itself. Skipping that step is one of the most common reasons mold returns after a remediation.
