## Why Gig Harbor Properties Are Especially Vulnerable After Structural Damage

The housing stock here spans a wide range, waterfront craftsman bungalows built in the mid-20th century, hillside timber-frame homes on the bluffs above the harbor, and newer construction in the developments spreading toward the Highway 16 corridor. Older wood-frame homes common in the established neighborhoods closer to downtown Gig Harbor often have cedar shake or composition roofs that have been patched over multiple decades. When a branch comes through or a fire damages a roof section, the underlying sheathing may already be weathered, meaning a tarp needs to be anchored more carefully to avoid pulling fasteners through soft wood.

The region's rainfall, consistent from October through April, means that even a six-hour window of exposure can allow enough water intrusion to saturate insulation, soak ceiling joists, and begin the conditions that lead to mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours. Board-up and tarping isn't just cosmetic protection; in Gig Harbor's climate, it's the line between a contained loss and a cascading one.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Gig Harbor

When you call, a crew mobilizes immediately, day, night, or weekend. The first step on arrival is a rapid structural assessment: we identify every breach point, not just the obvious ones. Fire damage, for example, often compromises soffits and fascia boards that aren't visible from the ground but allow wind-driven rain to enter the attic cavity.

For window and door board-up, we use plywood cut and fitted to the opening, secured with structural screws rather than nails, so the panel holds under wind load without damaging the surrounding frame further. On roof damage, heavy-duty polyethylene tarps rated for the Pacific Northwest's sustained rain are secured with batten boards and ridge anchoring, not just weighted at the edges, so they stay in place through multi-day storm systems rather than lifting at the first gust off the Sound.

We document every step with photographs before, during, and after installation. That documentation goes directly into the claim file, which matters when you're working with your insurance adjuster on scope and coverage.
