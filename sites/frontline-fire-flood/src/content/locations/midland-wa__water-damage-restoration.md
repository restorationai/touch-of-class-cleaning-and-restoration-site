## Why Midland Properties See Water Damage Issues

Midland's unincorporated stretch of Pierce County was built out in waves, from mid-century ramblers to more recent subdivisions, and that mix shows up in how water moves through a house. Crawlspace foundations are common here, and when groundwater rises during the wet season, a crawlspace that was never fully sealed can wick moisture into subfloor and insulation long before anyone notices a damp spot on the carpet above. Add in Western Washington's long stretch of fall and winter rain, and you get a pattern we see constantly: slow, hidden saturation rather than a dramatic flood. By the time a homeowner smells something musty or notices a floor that feels soft underfoot, the water has usually been working on the structure for days.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Midland

We start every call with moisture mapping, using meters to find out how far water has traveled past the visible wet area, because in a crawlspace home that's often the real story.
