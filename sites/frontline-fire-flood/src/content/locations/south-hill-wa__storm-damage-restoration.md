## Why South Hill Properties See Significant Storm Damage

The housing stock across South Hill spans several decades of construction, and that mix matters when a storm hits. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s, common throughout the area's rapid suburban growth period, often used OSB sheathing and felt underlayment that performs adequately under normal rainfall but can delaminate or wick moisture aggressively when a windstorm lifts flashing or cracks a ridge cap. Older homes with original cedar shake roofing are especially vulnerable: shakes that look intact from the street may be brittle and split beneath the surface, allowing water to travel laterally into the attic cavity before it ever shows as a ceiling stain.

Pierce County's clay-heavy soils compound the problem at grade level. After a sustained storm, that soil reaches saturation quickly and sheds water toward foundations rather than absorbing it. Crawl spaces in South Hill homes frequently collect standing water after heavy events, and that water, hidden beneath the subfloor, can go undetected for days while it wicks into floor joists and rim boards.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in South Hill

Every job starts with a full exterior and interior assessment before any restoration work begins. On the exterior, the crew documents wind-lifted roofing, displaced flashing, damaged siding, and any tree impact points. Temporary tarping and board-up happen the same visit when the structure is open to weather, there is no benefit to waiting on that step.

Inside, moisture mapping with thermal imaging and calibrated meters identifies where water has traveled beyond the obvious wet spots. Water that enters through a compromised roof line or a broken window often tracks down wall cavities and pools at the base plate, a location that reads dry at the surface but stays saturated behind the drywall. Structural drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, using industrial air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers sized to the affected area. Readings are logged daily so drying progress is documented for your insurance file.

Debris removal, broken branches, displaced roofing material, damaged siding, is handled as part of the restoration scope, not as a separate add-on that delays the structural work.

## South Hill Insurance Coordination

Storm damage claims in Pierce County typically involve wind and hail coverage under standard homeowners policies, but the documentation requirements matter. Carriers want photo evidence of the storm event's point of entry, moisture readings at the time of discovery, and a clear scope of affected materials before authorizing work. ## Local Note

South Hill's newer subdivision developments, particularly those built on former agricultural land toward the county's eastern edges, often have shallow storm drainage infrastructure that backs up during sustained rainfall events. When that happens, water doesn't just pool in yards, it can reverse-flow through area drains connected to garage slabs or crawl space vents positioned low on the foundation wall. Homeowners in these areas sometimes assume the water entry is a plumbing failure when it's actually a drainage backflow event, which is covered differently under most policies. Identifying the source correctly at the outset protects the claim.
