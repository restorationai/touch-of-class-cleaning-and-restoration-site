## Restoration emergencies common in Fircrest

Fircrest sits in the wet, mild climate of South Puget Sound, which means the restoration calls we see here follow a predictable seasonal rhythm. Fall and winter bring sustained Pacific storm systems that push moisture into older roof assemblies and around window flashing that was never built for today's rain volumes. Many homes in the area were built before modern vapor barrier and drainage practices were standard, so a slow roof or foundation leak can go unnoticed for weeks before it surfaces as a stain, a musty smell, or soft flooring. Add a aging water heater or an old galvanized supply line failing under pressure, and the result is often a mix of water damage and the early stages of mold growth by the time anyone calls for help.
