## Restoration emergencies common in Wollochet

Wollochet sits on the water, and that shapes almost every loss we see out here. Homes close to the bay deal with wind-driven rain off Puget Sound, salt-laden air that accelerates corrosion on exterior fixtures and HVAC components, and heavy winter storm systems that push moisture sideways into siding and window flashing that older homes were never detailed to handle. Properties set back from the water on wooded lots carry a different risk: mature fir and cedar stands that drop limbs and whole trees during the Pacific storm season, opening roofs to sudden water intrusion. Combine that with the region's long, wet winters and you get a steady pattern of slow leaks that go unnoticed in crawlspaces until a musty smell or a soft floor gives it away, by which point mold has usually had the two to three days it needs to establish itself in wet framing.

## Services we provide in Wollochet

Water damage restoration is our most frequent call here, often tied to roof intrusion after a windstorm, a failed supply line under a kitchen or bath, or groundwater finding its way into a crawlspace built close to grade near the bay. We extract standing water, pull moisture readings from subfloor and framing, and set equipment to dry the structure to the IICRC S500 standard rather than guessing at a timeline.

Fire and smoke damage restoration covers both structure fires and the lingering effects of wildfire smoke that can settle into a home's HVAC system and soft furnishings during dry summer stretches. We clean and deodorize affected materials and coordinate with your carrier on what can be restored versus what needs replacement.

Mold remediation is closely tied to our water work in Wollochet, since crawlspace and attic moisture here rarely announces itself until colonization is already underway. We contain the affected area, remove compromised material, and verify conditions before closing the job out.

Biohazard and trauma cleanup, along with general storm and wind damage response, round out what we handle for homeowners and property managers in the area, including emergency board-up when a storm or tree limb has opened up a roof or window.

## Building stock, site conditions, and permits in Wollochet

Much of the housing around Wollochet ranges from older waterfront cabins and mid-century homes to newer construction built as the peninsula filled in, which means we see a real mix of galvanized and copper supply lines alongside more recent PEX systems, and crawlspace foundations are far more common here than basements given the terrain and proximity to the bay. Older homes in the area can carry asbestos-containing materials in flooring, siding, or pipe insulation, which has to be accounted for before demolition starts on any water or fire job.

Soil near the water tends to hold moisture and drain slowly, which keeps crawlspaces damp longer after a storm than homes further from the bay.
