## Restoration emergencies common in Weston

Weston sits in western Broward County on land that was engineered out of the Everglades, which means the water table is never far below grade. During South Florida's June-through-November rainy season, a single afternoon storm can drop three inches of rain in under an hour. That kind of rainfall overwhelms gutters, infiltrates through sliding-glass-door thresholds, and backs up into garages and finished basements in communities like Savanna and The Ridges. The area's housing stock — largely built between the late 1980s and early 2000s — means a significant share of homes have original cast-iron drain lines that are now corroding, polybutylene supply lines that were never replaced, and flat or low-slope tile roofs where cracked underlayment hides until water is already inside the attic. Humidity stays high enough year-round that a wet wall cavity can begin supporting mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of a leak — a window that leaves very little time between discovery and remediation.

Fire calls in Weston tend to involve kitchen fires and electrical events in those same 25-to-35-year-old homes, where smoke and soot travel quickly through open-plan layouts and settle into HVAC ductwork. Because Weston's neighborhoods are densely landscaped and closely built, a single-family fire can also raise air-quality concerns for adjacent properties.

## Services we provide in Weston

### Water damage restoration

### Mold remediation

Broward County's climate makes mold a near-certainty after any water intrusion that goes unaddressed for more than a day or two. Smoke residue from a kitchen fire in a Weston home can penetrate drywall, cabinetry, and ductwork within hours. We assess the full path of smoke migration, clean or remove affected materials, and deodorize using hydroxyl or thermal fogging — not just surface sprays that mask odor temporarily.
