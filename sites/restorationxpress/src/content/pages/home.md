## Services we handle

**Water damage restoration** — Whether it's a burst pipe, appliance overflow, or roof leak after a summer storm, standing water needs to be extracted and structural materials dried completely before mold gets a foothold — typically within 24 to 48 hours of exposure.

**Fire and smoke damage restoration** — Smoke residue penetrates porous materials fast and the odor doesn't go away on its own; the restoration process involves controlled demolition of charred material, deep cleaning of salvageable surfaces, and odor neutralization — not just a coat of paint.

**Mold remediation** — South Florida's humidity makes mold a year-round threat, not a seasonal one. Remediation means containing the affected area, removing colonized material safely, and treating the source moisture so the problem doesn't return behind the next wall.

**Storm damage and reconstruction** — Hurricane season in Broward County can leave roofs compromised, windows breached, and interiors soaked within hours. From emergency tarping and board-up through full structural rebuilds, the work gets managed as one continuous project rather than handed off between contractors.
