RestorationXpress grew out of a straightforward observation: when a pipe bursts at 2 a.m. or a kitchen fire leaves a family displaced, the contractor who shows up matters as much as the equipment they bring. Based in Davie, FL, the company was built around the idea that restoration work is as much about clear communication and honest timelines as it is about drying equipment and air scrubbers. We're not going to invent a founding myth — what we can tell you is that this company has been doing this work in South Florida long enough to know what the humidity does to a water-damaged wall cavity, and why that changes everything about how you dry a structure.

## What we do

On any given day, the work looks like this: a homeowner calls because they noticed a soft spot in the floor near the washing machine and aren't sure if it's serious. A property manager emails photos of ceiling staining after a roof leak during a tropical storm. A family is waiting to hear whether their home is safe to re-enter after a fire. We walk through the damage, explain what we're seeing in plain language, and put together a scope of work that reflects what actually needs to happen — not a padded estimate.

A significant part of the job is working alongside insurance carriers. We document losses thoroughly — moisture readings, photographs, written scope — so the claim process moves as smoothly as possible and homeowners aren't left guessing what their policy covers. That documentation work isn't glamorous, but it's often what determines whether a family gets a fair settlement.

## Our certifications and licensure

Certifications in this industry aren't just wall decorations. The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — sets the technical standards that define how water damage is actually categorized, how drying is validated, and what "complete" looks like before a job is closed. Working to those standards protects homeowners from shortcuts that look fine on the surface and cause problems six months later.

For older homes — anything built before 1978 — lead-containing paint is a real concern any time walls or trim are disturbed during demolition or reconstruction. Federal law requires contractors to follow specific containment and disposal procedures in those situations. We follow the applicable regulatory requirements for work in pre-1978 structures.

We carry the insurance coverage appropriate for this type of work, which matters when a contractor is inside your home with heavy equipment and open walls.

## Where we work

Davie is our home base, and most of our work is concentrated in Broward County — Fort Lauderdale, Hollywood, Pembroke Pines, Miramar, Cooper City, Weston, and the surrounding communities. We also serve parts of Miami-Dade and Palm Beach counties. South Florida's climate — the combination of heat, humidity, and a hurricane season that runs half the year — creates specific restoration challenges you don't see in drier parts of the country. Mold can begin colonizing wet building materials within 24 to 48 hours in these conditions, which is why response time and proper drying validation matter more here than almost anywhere else.
