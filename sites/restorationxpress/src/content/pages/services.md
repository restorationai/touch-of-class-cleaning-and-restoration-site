From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen gutted by fire, property damage rarely follows a schedule or a tidy scope of work. RestorationXpress handles the full range of damage restoration and rebuild services for homeowners, landlords, commercial property owners, and multi-family communities across Davie and the broader South Florida area — so you're not coordinating three different contractors when you're already dealing with a loss.

## Emergency restoration services

When water, fire, or an unexpected event hits, the first hours matter most. Water damage left standing for 24–48 hours creates conditions where mold can begin to colonize; smoke residue bonds more deeply into porous surfaces the longer it sits; storm debris left exposed turns a manageable repair into a structural problem. RestorationXpress responds to water damage emergencies — burst pipes, appliance failures, sewage backups, and roof-leak intrusion — extracting standing water and drying structural materials before secondary damage sets in. Fire and smoke damage services cover soot removal, odor neutralization, and content cleaning after a blaze, because the acrid smell of smoke residue can linger in drywall and HVAC systems long after the flames are out. Biohazard cleanup addresses situations that require safe, regulated handling of hazardous materials — sewage contamination, trauma scenes, and similar events. Storm damage response covers wind-driven rain intrusion, impact damage, and flooding common to South Florida's hurricane season.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage; reconstruction puts the property back together. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and stable, RestorationXpress transitions directly into the rebuild phase — replacing drywall, flooring, cabinetry, roofing components, and structural framing as the scope requires. Because the same team that documented the damage is managing the rebuild, there's no gap where details fall through the cracks between a remediation contractor and a separate GC. Work is scoped to match pre-loss conditions, and upgrades can be incorporated when owners want to improve on what was there before.

## Specialty services

Not every call is an active emergency. Mold inspection and remediation services address visible growth and hidden moisture problems — particularly relevant in South Florida's humidity, where a slow roof leak or a poorly sealed bathroom can produce significant mold colonization inside wall cavities. Odor removal treatments go beyond masking, using thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation to neutralize odor molecules embedded in building materials. Board-up and tarping services secure a property immediately after a fire, storm impact, or break-in, protecting it from weather and unauthorized entry while the restoration plan comes together.
