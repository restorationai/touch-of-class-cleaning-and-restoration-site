RestorationXpress is based in Davie and serves homeowners and property managers throughout South Florida's most densely populated corridor. When you call **(954) 932-5420**, you'll speak with someone who can confirm coverage for your address and get you scheduled promptly. Because our hours vary, the fastest way to lock in a visit is a direct call during business hours — we'll match your situation to the next available crew.

## Where we work

Our team operates out of Davie, which sits near the geographic center of Broward County — a location that gives us practical access to communities stretching from the Atlantic coastline west toward the Everglades edge, and north-to-south from the Broward-Palm Beach county line down into Miami-Dade. South Florida's flat terrain and dense highway grid means a crew leaving Davie can reach most Broward addresses without the kind of geographic bottlenecks you'd face in hillier or more rural states. That said, the same I-95 and I-595 corridors that connect the region can slow travel during peak commute windows, which is worth keeping in mind when you call to schedule.

## Coverage by distance

The cities closest to our Davie headquarters — including Weston, Pembroke Pines, Hollywood, Fort Lauderdale, and Plantation — represent our most accessible service zone. Crews dispatched to these areas spend the least time in transit and can often fit same-day scheduling when the calendar allows.

Moving outward, we regularly work in communities across central and northern Broward County, as well as in southern Palm Beach County and the northern reaches of Miami-Dade. Properties in these areas are well within our normal working range, though scheduling may shift by a day depending on current job load and traffic conditions along US-1, the Turnpike, or I-95.

At the outer edge of our coverage — think Boca Raton to the north or Homestead to the south — we take jobs selectively, typically for larger losses where the scope of work justifies the drive. If you're near the boundary, call us anyway; we'd rather have the conversation than have you assume we can't help.

## Don't see your city?

Our listed service areas cover the most common requests, but they aren't a hard boundary. Significant water losses, fire damage, or mold jobs in neighboring communities are worth a quick call to (954) 932-5420. If the job is outside our range, we'll tell you honestly — and we can often point you toward a qualified referral rather than leaving you to search from scratch.
