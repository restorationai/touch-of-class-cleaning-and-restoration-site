## Why Hollywood Properties See More Water Damage Than You'd Expect

Broward County's water table sits unusually high — in many Hollywood ZIP codes, including 33019 along the barrier island, the saturated soil layer is only a few feet below grade. That means a slab leak or a backed-up floor drain doesn't just wet the surface; hydrostatic pressure can push moisture upward through concrete for hours after the visible source is stopped. Older homes near Hollywood Beach were built before modern vapor barriers were standard, so crawl spaces and slab edges that look dry can be wicking moisture into wall cavities continuously during the rainy season.

Coastal humidity compounds the problem. The salt-laden air that drifts in off the Broadwalk corridor accelerates corrosion on supply lines and HVAC drain pans, two of the most common sources of slow water intrusion in beachside properties. Homes in Emerald Hills, farther inland, deal with a different pattern: the neighborhood's mature tree canopy means roof debris accumulates faster, leading to clogged gutters and fascia rot that channels stormwater into attic spaces during summer squalls.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Hollywood

Every job starts with moisture mapping — not a visual inspection, but thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters across walls, ceilings, and floors. In Hollywood's older housing stock, this step is non-negotiable. A reading that looks acceptable at the surface can mask a wet wall cavity that will grow mold within 48 to 72 hours if left unaddressed.

Once the scope is documented, extraction comes first. For standing water, we deploy truck-mounted and portable extraction units capable of pulling hundreds of gallons from flooring assemblies before structural drying equipment is positioned. Drying in Hollywood's climate requires dehumidifiers rated for high ambient humidity — standard residential units are undersized for the moisture loads common in coastal Broward properties. We use refrigerant and desiccant dehumidifiers alongside high-velocity air movers, adjusting the drying configuration based on daily psychrometric readings until materials reach target moisture content.

Documentation runs parallel to every phase: photos, moisture logs, and equipment placement records. That file matters when it's time to work through your insurance claim.

## Local Note: What Hollywood's Older Homes Do to Drying Timelines

Homes built in Hollywood Lakes and along the streets nearest Anne Kolb Nature Center in the 1950s and 1960s frequently have original plaster walls over metal lath rather than modern drywall. Plaster absorbs water more slowly than drywall but releases it more slowly too — a plaster wall that reads wet on a moisture meter on day one may still be releasing measurable vapor on day five or six. We account for this when setting drying timelines and don't pull equipment based on a calendar estimate. Pulling dehumidifiers too early in a plaster-wall home is one of the most common reasons secondary mold growth appears weeks after a job is "finished" by another crew.
