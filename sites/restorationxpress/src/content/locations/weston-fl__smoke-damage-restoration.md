## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Weston

When we arrive at a property in ZIP codes 33326 or 33327, the first step is a structured scope walk — not a sales pitch. We use thermal imaging to map where heat traveled and air sampling to identify the concentration and type of smoke residue (wet smoke from slow-burning materials behaves very differently from dry smoke produced by fast, high-temperature fires). That distinction drives every decision that follows.

Once the scope is documented, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

- **Containment and HVAC isolation** — smoke particles recirculate through ductwork long after a fire is out; we seal returns and supplies before any cleaning begins.
- **Dry soot removal** — chemical sponges and HEPA-filtered vacuuming lift loose carbon before any wet product is applied, preventing the smearing that turns cleanable residue into a permanent stain.
- **Surface neutralization** — alkaline cleaning agents counteract the acids in smoke residue on walls, ceilings, and hard surfaces.
- **Odor elimination** — thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach into wall cavities, subflooring, and furniture upholstery that surface cleaning cannot access.
- **Structural and content assessment** — we document what can be restored versus what needs replacement, with photo evidence formatted for insurance carriers.
