## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Cooper City

Every job starts with a moisture mapping inspection. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration behind walls, under tile, and inside ceiling assemblies — not just the visible wet area. In the slab-on-grade construction typical throughout Cooper City, water wicks laterally through concrete and can surface several feet from the original intrusion point.

Once the affected zone is mapped, water removal begins with truck-mounted extraction units capable of pulling hundreds of gallons from carpet, pad, and subfloor quickly. For hardwood or engineered flooring — common in updated Embassy Lakes homes — technicians deploy drying mats that draw moisture upward through the surface rather than forcing demolition of intact flooring.

After extraction, industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned according to a drying plan calibrated to the room's cubic footage and current ambient humidity. Readings are logged daily. The goal is to reach IICRC S500 drying standards before any repairs begin, which protects both the structure and the insurance claim.
