## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Cooper City

The first step on every job is a systematic inspection of the HVAC system and ductwork — not just the visible surfaces. We use thermal imaging to locate residue that has migrated behind walls and above drop ceilings, which is especially relevant in the split-level and vaulted-ceiling floor plans common in Rock Creek. From there, the process moves through these stages:

**Containment and air scrubbing.** HEPA-filtered negative air machines are staged to prevent cross-contamination while cleaning is underway. In occupied homes, this step protects unaffected rooms.

**Dry and wet chemical sponge cleaning.** Soot type determines the method. Protein-based smoke from kitchen fires requires enzymatic treatments; wood and synthetic smoke from structural fires calls for dry chemical sponges followed by alkaline cleaning agents to neutralize acidity.

**Odor neutralization.** Thermal fogging and hydroxyl generation reach the same microscopic spaces smoke did — inside upholstery fibers, inside wall cavities, inside the HVAC plenum. Masking sprays are not used; they fail within weeks in Florida's heat.

**Surface restoration and repainting.** Stucco exteriors and textured interior walls common in Cooper City's housing stock require shellac-based stain-blocking primer before any finish coat, or smoke odor will bleed through latex paint within months.
