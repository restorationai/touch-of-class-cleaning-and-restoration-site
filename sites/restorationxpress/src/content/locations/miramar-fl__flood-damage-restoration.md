## Our Flood Damage Restoration Process in Miramar

Every flood cleanup job begins with a thorough moisture mapping of the affected structure. Technicians use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond what is visible — under baseboards, inside wall cavities, beneath subfloor materials. This step matters especially in Miramar's older eastern stock, where water can travel several rooms from the original breach point before it becomes visible.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. Affected materials — saturated drywall, soaked insulation, buckled flooring — are removed to the drying line and bagged for disposal. Industrial-grade air movers and desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are then positioned based on the room geometry and material types present. Drying typically takes three to five days, with daily moisture readings confirming progress. Antimicrobial treatment is applied to structural surfaces before any reconstruction begins, which is particularly important given Miramar's year-round humidity.
