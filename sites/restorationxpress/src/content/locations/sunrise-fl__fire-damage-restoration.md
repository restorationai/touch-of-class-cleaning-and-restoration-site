## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Sunrise

Every job starts with a room-by-room structural and smoke assessment before a single surface is touched. We document the extent of char, soot migration, and water intrusion — photographs and written notes that become part of the insurance claim file. From there, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

**Stabilization and board-up.** Compromised windows, doors, and roof sections are secured to prevent weather intrusion and unauthorized entry. Sunrise's afternoon thunderstorm season means an open roof can introduce a second water loss within hours.

**Water extraction and drying.** Firefighting water is removed using truck-mounted extractors, followed by commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to Broward County's ambient humidity levels. We do not skip this phase even when the fire appears to be the primary concern — wet structural members behind smoke-damaged walls are a hidden liability.

**Soot and smoke removal.** Dry chemical sponges remove loose soot before any wet cleaning begins. HEPA vacuuming clears ductwork and cavities. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl generation neutralizes embedded smoke odor in wall cavities and soft materials — a step that matters especially in older homes where smoke has penetrated behind original wood paneling.

**Structural repair and reconstruction.** Charred framing, drywall, and cabinetry are removed and rebuilt to current Florida Building Code standards, with all required Broward County permits pulled before work begins.
