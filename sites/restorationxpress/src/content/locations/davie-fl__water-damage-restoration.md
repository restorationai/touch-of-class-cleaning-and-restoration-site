## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Davie

The first step on any job is a moisture mapping survey. We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace water migration beyond the obvious wet zone — because in a slab-on-grade home, the source of a wet living room floor is often a bathroom or laundry room on the other side of a wall. Once we know the full extent of saturation, we deploy truck-mounted extraction equipment to pull standing water from carpet, tile grout, and subfloor assemblies before it can migrate further.

Structural drying follows a specific protocol: industrial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned to create a drying chamber around the affected area, and high-velocity air movers accelerate evaporation from porous materials. Drying logs are recorded daily — moisture readings, temperature, relative humidity — so there is a documented record of the drying progress. That documentation matters when you file with your insurance carrier, and it matters if a mold question comes up six months later.

For homes with affected drywall or insulation, we perform controlled demolition only where necessary — removing wet material to the dry line rather than tearing out entire rooms. In Davie's older construction, that often means opening wall cavities in sections to verify that framing has reached acceptable moisture levels before any rebuild begins.
