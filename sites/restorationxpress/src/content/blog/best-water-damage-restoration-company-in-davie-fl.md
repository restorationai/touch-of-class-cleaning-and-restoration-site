## What Should You Look for in a Water Damage Restoration Company?

The right water damage restoration company does three things well: responds fast, documents thoroughly, and dries to a verifiable standard. South Florida's climate makes all three non-negotiable.

**Response speed** matters because the [IICRC S500 standard](https://www.iicrc.org) defines water damage categories and classes that change as water sits. Category 1 water (clean, from a supply line) can become Category 2 or 3 as it contacts building materials and organic matter. In Davie's August heat and humidity, that escalation happens faster than in drier climates. A company that can reach your home in Forest Ridge or Shenandoah quickly limits how far the loss spreads.

**Documentation** protects your insurance claim. The scope of work, moisture readings, equipment logs, and photo documentation that a professional restoration company produces are what your adjuster needs to approve the claim. Verbal estimates and handshake agreements leave homeowners exposed. Ask any company you call whether they provide a written scope before work begins.

**Drying to standard** means the crew uses calibrated equipment (LGR dehumidifiers, air movers, moisture meters) and pulls readings until the affected materials reach the IICRC S500's drying goals, not until the crew decides it looks dry. Incomplete drying is the most common cause of mold growth after a water loss.
