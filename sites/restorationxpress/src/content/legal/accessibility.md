## Our Commitment to Accessibility

RestorationXpress is committed to ensuring that everyone who visits this website can access the information they need — regardless of the device they use, the assistive technology they rely on, or the accessibility needs they bring. When a pipe bursts or smoke damage spreads through a home, the last thing anyone should face is a website that gets in the way.

We aim to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA**. These guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), define a broad standard for making web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, voice control software, or other assistive tools.

## What We've Done to Support Accessible Use

This site has been built with accessibility in mind from the ground up. That includes:

- **Screen reader compatibility** — page structure uses semantic HTML so that headings, landmarks, and content regions are announced correctly by assistive technology.
- **Keyboard navigation** — all interactive elements, including navigation menus, contact forms, and call-to-action buttons, are reachable and operable without a mouse.
- **Color contrast** — text and background combinations are chosen to meet or exceed WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios, making content readable for users with low vision or color vision differences.
- **Descriptive link text and image alt attributes** — links are labeled to describe their destination, and images that carry meaning include alternative text descriptions.
- **Responsive layout** — the site adapts to a range of screen sizes and zoom levels without loss of content or functionality.

We recognize that accessibility is not a one-time checklist. As we update content and add new features, we continue to evaluate our pages against WCAG 2.1 AA criteria.

## Known Limitations

We make every effort to identify and resolve accessibility barriers, but some third-party content or embedded tools may not fully meet our standards. If you encounter a page, form, or document that presents a barrier, we want to hear about it.

## How to Report an Accessibility Issue

If any part of this website is difficult to use or inaccessible to you, please contact us directly. We take these reports seriously and will work to address confirmed issues within a reasonable timeframe — typically within **5 business days** for acknowledgment and as quickly as practical for resolution.

If you need information from this site in an alternative format — or if you'd prefer to handle scheduling, estimates, or documentation entirely by phone — our team is available during business hours to assist you directly. No one should have to navigate a website barrier to get help after a loss.
