## Restoration emergencies common in Lehi

Lehi sits at the transition between Utah Lake's valley floor and the Traverse Mountains, and that geography shapes the kinds of losses property owners deal with. Winters bring hard freezes that can split supply lines in uninsulated garage walls or crawlspace rim joists, the temperature swings between a cold snap and an afternoon thaw are fast enough that pipes fail before anyone notices. Spring snowmelt and occasional heavy rain push water against foundations, especially on lots where the grade has settled toward the house over time. Summer brings intense but short thunderstorms that can overwhelm gutters and window wells in an afternoon. Lehi's rapid growth also means a wide range of housing ages on the same street: a 1990s tract home with original polybutylene supply lines may sit next to a 2018 build with PEX, and each fails differently.

## Services we provide in Lehi

### Water damage restoration

Water damage in Lehi frequently starts behind walls or under slabs, a slow supply-line weep that reads dry at the surface while the framing stays saturated. Thermal imaging and moisture mapping locate the actual wet boundary before any drying equipment goes in. Extraction, structural drying, and documentation for insurance follow the IICRC S500 standard, which most carriers require for a clean claim.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue is acidic and keeps damaging surfaces, metal fixtures, painted drywall, HVAC ductwork, long after the flames are out. Restoration scope includes odor neutralization, content pack-out when needed, and coordinating with your adjuster on what is cleanable versus what requires replacement.

### Mold remediation

Utah's dry climate does not make mold impossible, it just means mold needs a moisture source to get started, and Lehi's basement-heavy housing stock gives it plenty of opportunity after a slow leak or an inadequate crawlspace vapor barrier. Containment, HEPA air filtration, and removal of affected materials follow established remediation protocols. Post-clearance testing is available through a third-party industrial hygienist.

### Reconstruction

When structural materials have to come out, subfloor, framing, drywall, cabinetry, reconstruction is handled in-house rather than handed off to a separate contractor. That matters for timeline and for keeping a single point of contact with your insurance carrier through the full scope.
