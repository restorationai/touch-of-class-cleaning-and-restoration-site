## Restoration emergencies common in Highland

Highland sits at the foot of the Wasatch Range in northern Utah County, and that geography shapes the losses that happen here. Spring snowmelt off the mountains arrives fast, and when it coincides with saturated soil, water finds its way through foundation walls and window wells before homeowners realize there is a problem. Winters bring hard freezes, pipes in exterior walls, garage conversions, and uninsulated crawlspaces are especially vulnerable to bursting when overnight temperatures drop sharply and then rebound. Summer afternoons can bring short, intense thunderstorms that overwhelm gutters and downspouts on the steeply pitched roofs common in the area. Fire damage is a secondary concern: the wildland-urban interface along the foothills means smoke and ember exposure is a real seasonal risk, and interior smoke residue from a nearby brush fire can saturate soft goods and HVAC systems even when the structure itself is untouched.
