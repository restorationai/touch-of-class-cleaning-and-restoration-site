## Services we provide in Orem

### Water damage restoration

Water losses in Orem frequently start small, a supply line under a bathroom vanity, a water heater in a utility closet, and grow behind finished walls before anyone notices. The work involves more than extraction. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and calibrated meters locates saturation that looks dry at the surface, and drying equipment is staged to meet the IICRC S500 standard rather than pulled when the floor feels dry underfoot.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

After a fire, smoke residue settles into HVAC ductwork, attic insulation, and wall cavities well beyond the burn area. Orem homes with forced-air systems can distribute odor and fine particulate through the entire structure within hours. Restoration here means addressing the full distribution path, not just the room where the fire started.

### Mold remediation

Mold can begin colonizing damp building materials within 24 to 48 hours of a water event. In Orem's older housing stock, especially homes with crawlspaces or unfinished basements, elevated humidity and limited airflow create conditions where mold establishes quietly. Remediation work follows established containment and removal protocols; the goal is to correct the moisture source and remove affected material, not mask it.

### Biohazard and specialty cleanup

Unattended deaths, sewage backups, and hoarding situations require a different protocol than standard restoration, proper PPE, regulated waste disposal, and documentation. This work is handled discreetly and in compliance with applicable safety standards.

### Reconstruction

When structural materials have to come out, subfloor, framing, drywall, cabinetry, reconstruction brings the property back to a livable, code-compliant condition. FIX Restoration carries this work through from demolition to finish so property owners are not left coordinating a separate general contractor.
