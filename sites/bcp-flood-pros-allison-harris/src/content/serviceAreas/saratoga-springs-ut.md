## Restoration emergencies common in Saratoga Springs

Saratoga Springs sits at the edge of Utah Lake on the western bench of the Wasatch Front, and that geography shapes the losses that show up here. Mold follows moisture quickly in any of these scenarios, colonization in wall cavities can begin within 24 to 48 hours of saturation.

## Services we provide in Saratoga Springs
