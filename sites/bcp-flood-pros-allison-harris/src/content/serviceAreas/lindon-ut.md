## Restoration emergencies common in Lindon

Lindon sits at the base of the Wasatch Front, and that geography drives most of what goes wrong with local buildings. Spring snowmelt off the mountains pushes groundwater levels higher than homeowners expect, and that pressure finds its way into basement walls and window wells before it ever appears on a weather radar. Winter freeze-thaw cycles crack supply lines in uninsulated garage walls and exterior hose bibs, often releasing water into wall cavities that stay hidden until mold is already colonizing the framing. Summer monsoon moisture, while brief, arrives faster than drainage systems in many neighborhoods can handle, sending water through foundation cracks and under slabs. Wildfire smoke from the Wasatch and Uinta ranges periodically settles into the valley, leaving fine particulate and odor inside homes through HVAC systems that were running when the smoke arrived.

### Fire and smoke damage restoration

Smoke residue behaves differently depending on what burned and how hot the fire ran. FIX Restoration identifies the smoke type before selecting cleaning chemistry, which matters for protecting cabinetry, HVAC components, and finished surfaces common in Lindon's newer construction.
