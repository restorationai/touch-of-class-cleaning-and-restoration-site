FIX Restoration is based in American Fork and serves communities throughout Utah County and the greater Wasatch Front. When you call, our team works to schedule your assessment or emergency visit as quickly as possible during business hours. Whether you're dealing with water damage, mold, fire, or something else entirely, the first step is a phone call, we'll tell you right away whether your address falls within our coverage and when we can be there.

## Where we work

Our home base sits in American Fork, near the center of Utah County, which puts us within practical reach of a wide band of communities along the Wasatch Front corridor. From the foothills communities east of I-15 to the valley cities stretching north toward Salt Lake County and south toward Juab County, most of the region is familiar territory. The Wasatch Range to the east and Utah Lake to the west define the geography here, it's a relatively compact, north-south valley, which means driving distances between cities are shorter than they might look on a map.

## Coverage by distance

Cities closest to American Fork, Lehi, Pleasant Grove, Lindon, Orem, and Provo, are our most routine service stops. A bit farther out, we regularly work in Spanish Fork, Springville, Mapleton, and Saratoga Springs, as well as communities to the north like Vineyard and parts of south Salt Lake County. The edge of our standard coverage area runs into the more rural stretches of Utah County and the canyon communities along the Alpine Loop and Provo Canyon corridors, where road conditions and season can affect scheduling. For locations at the outer range, we'll give you an honest estimate of when a crew can arrive rather than a number we can't stand behind.

## Don't see your city?

The linked service-area pages below cover our most common destinations, but the list isn't a hard boundary. For a significant loss, major flooding, a structure fire, extensive mold, it's always worth a call to (801) 930-9750. We'll let you know whether we can help and, if we can't, we won't leave you without a direction to turn.
