If you're dealing with active water damage, fire damage, or a biohazard situation, don't fill out a form, call **(801) 930-9750** right now and talk to someone who can help.

## For urgent situations

Water moves fast. A pipe burst that soaks drywall for a few extra hours isn't just a bigger cleanup, it's the difference between drying in place and tearing out walls. When something is actively spreading, call **(801) 930-9750** as soon as you can during business hours and we'll get a crew scheduled as quickly as possible. The sooner we can assess the loss, the more we can limit it.

## For estimates and non-urgent inquiries

If the situation is stable, a slow leak you caught early, a mold concern you want evaluated, a question about a past claim, email is a good starting point. Reach Scott directly at **scott.noland@gofloodpros.com** with a brief description of the damage and any photos you have. That context helps us come prepared and give you a more accurate estimate on the first visit. You can expect a reply within one business day.

## Where we're located

FIX Restoration is based in American Fork at **250 W Main St, American Fork, UT 84003**, near the center of Utah County. From here we serve communities throughout the Wasatch Front and surrounding areas, including Lehi, Pleasant Grove, Orem, Provo, Lindon, Cedar Hills, Highland, and Eagle Mountain. Utah County's mix of older ranch-style homes with crawl spaces and newer construction on expansive clay soils means water and moisture problems show up in ways that vary a lot by neighborhood and decade of build, if you're not sure whether what you're seeing is urgent, call and describe it. We'd rather answer a question than have you wait on something that's getting worse.
