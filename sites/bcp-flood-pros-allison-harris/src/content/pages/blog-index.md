Water damage, fire cleanup, mold, and the insurance process that follows, these aren't topics most people research until something goes wrong. The posts here are written for that moment: when you're standing in a wet basement at 11 p.m. when you've just noticed a dark stain spreading across a ceiling, or when an adjuster has used a term you've never heard and you need to understand it before you sign anything.

You'll find emergency response guides that walk through what to do in the first hour after a loss, plain-language breakdowns of how insurance claims work from first notice through final payment, and what-to-expect explainers for each stage of the restoration process. We also cover the seasonal and regional patterns that affect homes along the Wasatch Front, the freeze-thaw cycles that stress older plumbing, the spring runoff that tests crawl space drainage, the dry summers that make wildfire smoke a real indoor air quality concern for Utah County residents. If a hazard shows up regularly in this area, we try to cover it before it shows up in your home.

Read whatever is useful. These posts are written to help you make good decisions about your property, whether you end up calling us or not.
