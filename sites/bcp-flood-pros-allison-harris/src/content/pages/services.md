From a burst pipe at 2 a.m. to a kitchen fire that leaves soot in every room, property damage rarely happens on a convenient schedule or in a tidy category. FIX Restoration handles the full range of damage recovery for homeowners, landlords, commercial property owners, and multi-family operators across Utah County and the greater Wasatch Front, from the first emergency call through the final coat of paint.

## Emergency restoration services

Water damage moves fast. Within hours, moisture behind drywall or under hardwood begins the conditions that lead to secondary damage; within days, that window closes in the wrong direction. FIX Restoration responds to water intrusion, burst pipes, appliance failures, sewage backups, and storm flooding, with industrial extraction and structural drying equipment designed to pull moisture from materials before permanent damage sets in. Fire and smoke losses require a different approach: smoke residue is acidic and continues etching surfaces long after flames are out, so fire damage response focuses on stopping that ongoing deterioration while securing the structure. Biohazard and trauma cleanup demands strict containment and disposal protocols that protect both occupants and crew. Storm damage, hail, wind, and the freeze-thaw cycles that are routine across Utah's elevation swings, often combines several of these categories at once, and FIX Restoration treats it that way rather than as separate line items.

## Reconstruction and renovation

Mitigation stops the damage. Reconstruction puts the building back. Once affected materials are removed and the structure is dry and stable, FIX Restoration's rebuild crews take over, reframing walls, replacing insulation, hanging and finishing drywall, restoring flooring, and returning kitchens and bathrooms to livable condition. Because the same company that documented the damage is also managing the rebuild, scope gaps and miscommunications between separate contractors are eliminated. FIX Restoration also takes on general contracting work outside of insurance claims, so if a loss reveals deferred maintenance or a homeowner wants to make improvements during the rebuild, that work can be folded into the same project.

## Specialty services

Some damage problems don't fit neatly into emergency response or full reconstruction. Mold inspection and remediation addresses fungal growth that may predate a loss event or develop quietly after one. Odor removal, whether from smoke, sewage, or other sources, uses targeted treatments rather than masking agents. Board-up and tarping services secure a structure immediately after a fire, break-in, or storm event to prevent weather intrusion and unauthorized entry while the broader recovery plan comes together.
