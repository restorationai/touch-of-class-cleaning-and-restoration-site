## Why Cedar Fort Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most homes in Cedar Fort are newer construction, built during the growth wave that pushed out from Eagle Mountain and Saratoga Springs over the past two decades. That sounds like good news, but newer builds in this area often rely on PEX supply lines run through uninsulated garage walls and crawl spaces that face real temperature swings between December and February. When nighttime temps drop hard and the heat in an attached garage isn't enough, those lines freeze and split. The rupture usually happens quietly, and by morning there can be several inches of standing water wicking into OSB subfloor and wall framing.

Beyond freeze events, Cedar Fort's position near the base of the Oquirrhs means drainage patterns during heavy rain or rapid snowmelt can overwhelm window wells and foundation drainage systems. Water that pools against a foundation here doesn't always move off quickly because of the clay content in the soil at mid-slope elevations. That trapped moisture finds the path of least resistance, which is often a hairline crack in a poured concrete foundation wall.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Cedar Fort

The first priority when we arrive is stopping the source and documenting what the water has already reached. We use thermal imaging cameras alongside moisture meters to map saturation that isn't visible at the surface. OSB subfloor, for example, can read relatively dry on the face while holding significant moisture at the core, and that hidden saturation is what leads to structural softening and mold colonization if it isn't addressed.

Once the scope is mapped, we extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable extraction units, then set drying equipment calibrated to the room volume and material types involved. In Cedar Fort's drier climate, ambient conditions actually help accelerate evaporation, but that also means moisture moves through materials faster and can migrate into wall cavities before it's captured. We monitor readings daily and adjust equipment placement until every affected material reaches its target moisture content. Nothing is signed off based on how things look; the numbers have to confirm it.
