## Local Note: Finished Basements and Vapor Barriers in Alpine Homes

Many of the larger homes built in Alpine during the 2000s were finished with vapor barriers behind the drywall on below-grade walls, which is good building practice but creates a specific drying challenge. Moisture that gets behind that barrier can read as dry at the drywall surface while the framing and insulation behind it remain saturated. If you are renting air movers and dehumidifiers after a basement water event, pay attention to wall surface temperatures and not just surface moisture readings. If the wall feels cold and the meter reads low but the space still smells damp after several days of equipment runtime, that is a signal that moisture is trapped behind the assembly and a more invasive drying approach may be needed.
