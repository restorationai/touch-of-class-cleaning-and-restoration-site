## What Biohazard Situations Look Like in Orem Properties

Orem's housing stock reflects several decades of growth along the I-15 corridor. Older homes, particularly those built in the 1960s and 1970s in established residential pockets, often have porous concrete block foundations, original hardwood subfloors, and HVAC systems that circulate air through the whole structure. When a biohazard event occurs in one of these homes, biological material can migrate into flooring seams, wall cavities, and ductwork faster than it would in a newer build with tighter construction. Apartment complexes near campus present a different challenge: shared ventilation, adjacent units, and property management chains that require careful coordination before work can begin.

Sharps and needle cleanup is a call we receive more often in higher-density rental areas, and it requires specific handling, puncture-resistant containers, chain-of-custody documentation, and disposal through a licensed medical waste carrier. Infectious material cleanup in any setting follows the same principle: nothing leaves the property in an ordinary trash bag.

## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Orem

Every job begins with a discreet arrival. We use unmarked vehicles when requested, and we do not discuss the nature of the work with neighbors or building staff beyond what is operationally necessary. Once on site, the process moves in a deliberate sequence:

1. **Assessment and containment**, we identify the affected area, establish a containment boundary, and put on appropriate personal protective equipment before anything else happens.
2. **Removal of affected materials**, porous materials that cannot be decontaminated are bagged, sealed, and removed for disposal through a licensed medical waste facility. This includes flooring, drywall, insulation, and soft goods as needed.
3. **Disinfection and treatment**, surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for bloodborne pathogens and other biological hazards. We follow established industry protocols for dwell time and surface contact.
4. **Documentation**, we photograph affected areas before and after, generate a detailed scope of work, and provide records that support an insurance claim if one is being filed.
5. **Clearance**, before we leave, the space is visually and odor-free. For situations involving significant contamination, we can coordinate independent verification testing if the property owner or insurer requires it.
