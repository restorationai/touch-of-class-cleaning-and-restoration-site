## Why Vineyard Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Issues

Vineyard's growth has been rapid, and the housing stock reflects it: most of the city's residential neighborhoods were built within the last fifteen years, which means newer construction methods but also a concentration of homes still under warranty claims and HOA oversight. Rapid framing and engineered lumber perform well under normal conditions, but when fire or impact damage compromises the building envelope, those materials can absorb moisture quickly if the opening isn't sealed within hours. Utah Lake's proximity also means humidity levels near the shoreline can be meaningfully higher than what you'd find further inland in Utah County, accelerating the window between "open structure" and "wet structure."

Winter ice storms and the occasional high-wind event off the Wasatch front are the most common triggers for emergency calls in this area. A cracked or missing section of roof decking left open overnight during a January storm can let in enough moisture to affect insulation, ceiling drywall, and framing before morning. Tarping done correctly, weighted and sealed at the edges rather than just draped, is what separates a dry interior from a secondary water claim.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Vineyard

When FIX Restoration arrives at a Vineyard property, the first step is a quick exterior assessment to identify every breach: broken windows, compromised doors, damaged roof sections, or gaps left by fire department ventilation cuts. Nothing gets boarded or tarped until we know the full scope, because a missed opening on the back side of a structure can undo everything sealed at the front.

For window and door openings, we use plywood cut to fit the frame and secured in a way that doesn't cause additional damage to the surrounding trim or siding. For roof damage, heavy-duty polyethylene tarps are anchored with wood battens screwed into undamaged decking, not just weighted with sandbags that can shift in wind. The goal is a seal that holds through the next storm cycle, not just the next 24 hours. We photograph every opening before and after, which matters when your insurance adjuster needs documentation of the pre-mitigation condition.

## Local Note

Vineyard's newer subdivisions were largely built on former agricultural land with clay-heavy soil that drains slowly. That matters for board-up and tarping work because roof runoff from a poorly anchored tarp doesn't dissipate quickly at grade, it pools against foundations. When we tarp a roof in this part of Utah County, we pay attention to where water will shed and make sure it's directed away from the structure's perimeter rather than toward it. It's a small detail that prevents a secondary problem from developing while the primary repairs are being scheduled.
