## Why Lehi's Growth Pattern Shapes Biohazard Response

Lehi's housing stock spans a wide range of eras and construction types. The older neighborhoods closer to Main Street and the original town center were built with different materials, thicker subfloor assemblies, older plumbing chases, and in some cases original hardwood over concrete slab, that absorb and retain biological contamination differently than the vinyl-plank-over-OSB construction common in the city's newer master-planned communities. That difference matters during remediation. Porous materials that have absorbed biological fluids cannot simply be cleaned; they must be removed and disposed of according to Utah Department of Environmental Quality guidelines for regulated medical waste. Knowing which materials in a given home behave which way is part of doing the job correctly.

Lehi's rapid densification also means a higher share of multi-unit properties, short-term rentals, and investor-owned homes than the city had a generation ago. Property managers in these situations often need documentation for insurance carriers and, in some cases, for Utah County health coordination, paperwork that a qualified remediation company generates as part of the standard scope of work.

## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Lehi

When you call FIX Restoration, the first conversation is private and practical. We ask enough to understand the scope, explain what the process involves, and schedule arrival at a time that works for you, including coordinating access with property managers or family members who may need to be elsewhere during the work.

On-site, the process follows a structured sequence:

- **Containment**, affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination with the rest of the property.
- **Removal of porous materials**, flooring, drywall, insulation, or soft goods that cannot be decontaminated are bagged and transported as regulated waste.
- **Disinfection**, hospital-grade EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected hard surfaces, with contact times verified rather than estimated.
- **Odor neutralization**, biological contamination produces persistent odor compounds that require more than surface treatment; we use thermal fogging or hydroxyl technology depending on the space.
- **Clearance documentation**, we provide written records of what was removed, what was treated, and the methods used, which your insurance carrier or property manager may require.
