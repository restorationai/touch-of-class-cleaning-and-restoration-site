## Why South Jordan Properties Are Prone to Water Intrusion

The valley floor beneath South Jordan is largely composed of fine-grained lacustrine soils, the remnant bed of ancient Lake Bonneville. These soils drain slowly, and after a heavy spring snowmelt or a summer monsoon surge off the Oquirrh Mountains, that moisture has nowhere to go quickly. Crawl spaces and slab-on-grade foundations are especially vulnerable: water wicks upward, vapor migrates through concrete, and by the time you notice a musty smell, the subfloor or framing has already been wet for longer than it looks.

South Jordan also grew rapidly through the 1990s and 2000s, which means a large share of the housing stock uses engineered lumber, OSB sheathing, and composite flooring materials. These materials are more moisture-sensitive than older dimensional lumber. OSB, in particular, begins to swell and delaminate within 24 to 48 hours of sustained saturation, so getting airflow established quickly is not optional.

## Equipment We Rent for Flood and Drying Work

FIX Restoration's rental inventory is the same equipment our own technicians use on professional jobs, not the consumer-grade units available at big-box stores.

- **High-capacity air movers (carpet drying fans):** Low-profile axial units that direct high-velocity air across wet flooring and under cabinet toe kicks, accelerating evaporation at the surface where it matters.
- **Commercial dehumidifiers:** Refrigerant and desiccant units sized for the cubic footage of your space, not a one-size-fits-all residential unit. Proper sizing is critical in Utah's climate, where indoor humidity can swing dramatically between seasons.
- **Flood pumps and water extractors:** Submersible pumps for standing water removal and truck-mount-style extractors for pulling moisture from carpet and pad before drying begins.
- **Moisture meters and thermal cameras:** Available as add-ons so you can track drying progress behind walls and under flooring without guessing.

When you pick up equipment, a FIX technician walks you through placement strategy for your specific floor plan. Putting five air movers in the wrong configuration can actually slow drying by creating dead zones.
