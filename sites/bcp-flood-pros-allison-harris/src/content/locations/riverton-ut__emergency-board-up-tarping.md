## Why Riverton Properties See Board-Up and Tarping Needs

Riverton's housing stock skews toward the 1990s and 2000s construction boom that filled in the farmland along Bangerter Highway and Mountain View Corridor. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Riverton

Every call starts with a quick phone assessment so the crew arrives with the right materials. The crew measures, cuts, and anchors panels to the structural framing, not just the finish trim, so the barrier holds through the wind events Riverton sees in late spring and early fall.

For roof tarping, the process follows a systematic approach: remove loose debris, inspect the decking for soft spots, lay a minimum six-mil polyethylene tarp overlapping the ridge by at least two feet, and weight or screw the perimeter so uplift cannot peel it back.
