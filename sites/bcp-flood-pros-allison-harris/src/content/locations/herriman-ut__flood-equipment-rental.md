## Why Herriman Homes Are Particularly Vulnerable to Moisture Problems

Herriman's rapid growth over the past two decades means the housing stock spans a wide range of construction eras and build quality. Subdivisions developed in the early 2000s often used engineered lumber floor systems and OSB sheathing, both of which absorb moisture faster than dimensional lumber and begin to swell and delaminate within 24 to 48 hours of sustained wetness. Newer builds closer to the Mountain View Corridor corridor tend to have finished basements with spray-foam insulation cavities that can trap moisture behind drywall long after the surface reads dry to the touch.

The local soil profile compounds the problem. Herriman's clay-dominant subgrade expands when wet and sheds water laterally rather than letting it percolate down. During heavy rain events or rapid snowmelt off the Oquirrhs, that lateral movement pushes water toward foundation walls and window wells. A sump pump that runs dry or a window well drain that clogs can put several inches of standing water into a finished lower level within an hour.

## The Equipment That Actually Moves the Needle

Not all drying equipment is created equal, and undersized gear is one of the most common reasons a DIY drying attempt fails. The equipment FIX Restoration puts in your hands is the same commercial inventory used on professional mitigation jobs.

- **Flood pumps and wet-vacs** pull standing water before any drying can begin. Submersible pumps handle deeper accumulations; high-powered wet-vac extraction wands reach water trapped under flooring and in carpet pad.
- **High-velocity air movers** are positioned in a daisy-chain pattern around the perimeter of the wet area, forcing warm air across wet surfaces and into wall cavities. Residential box fans move a fraction of the CFM these units produce.
- **Refrigerant dehumidifiers** pull the evaporated moisture out of the air and discharge it as condensate. In Herriman's summer months, ambient humidity is low enough that dehumidifiers work efficiently, but the sheer volume of moisture released from wet concrete and OSB still requires commercial-capacity units to keep pace.
- **Moisture meters and thermal cameras** let you confirm drying progress rather than guess. Renting these alongside the drying equipment means you know when the job is actually done.
