## Why Provo Properties Are Prone to Water Intrusion

Provo's position along the Wasatch Front creates a specific set of conditions that make water damage more common here than in drier parts of Utah County. The valley floor sits on lake-bed soils left behind by ancient Lake Bonneville, fine-grained sediments that drain slowly and hold moisture against foundation walls long after the surface looks dry. Homes built in the 1950s through 1970s, which make up a large share of Provo's residential neighborhoods, frequently have uncoated block foundations and minimal perimeter drainage.
