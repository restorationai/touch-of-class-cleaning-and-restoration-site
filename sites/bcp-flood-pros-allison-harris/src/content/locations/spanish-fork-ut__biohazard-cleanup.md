## Why Spanish Fork Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Spanish Fork's housing stock spans several decades of construction. The older ranch-style homes common in the city's more established areas often feature original hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath interior walls, and crawl spaces with limited ventilation. These materials are more porous than modern construction, which matters in biohazard situations: biological material can wick into wood grain and wall cavities faster than it does in newer drywall-and-concrete builds. Identifying the true extent of contamination requires more than a visual sweep, it requires testing and methodical material assessment.

At the same time, Spanish Fork continues to grow outward, with newer developments on the city's edges featuring open floor plans and engineered wood flooring. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Spanish Fork

Every job begins with a thorough assessment, not an assumption.
