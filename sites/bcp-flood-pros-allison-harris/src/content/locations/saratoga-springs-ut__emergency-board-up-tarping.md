## Why Saratoga Springs Properties Face Unique Exposure Risks

The rapid residential growth along the western shore of Utah Lake has produced a housing stock that skews newer, most neighborhoods were built out in the 2000s and 2010s, but newer construction in this corridor comes with its own vulnerabilities. Engineered lumber trusses and OSB sheathing are efficient to build with, but they absorb moisture quickly once a roof deck is breached. A tarp delay of even 24 to 48 hours after a storm puncture can allow OSB to swell and delaminate, turning a straightforward shingle repair into a full sheathing replacement.

The lake-effect microclimate also matters. Afternoon thunderstorms in summer can drop hail and driving rain with little warning, and the soil in low-lying areas near the lake shore drains slowly. That combination, fast precipitation, saturated ground, and open structural cavities, means standing water can migrate into wall assemblies through a broken window or displaced soffit faster than it would in drier inland communities.
