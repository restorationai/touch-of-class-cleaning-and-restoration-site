## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Herriman

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water, meaning it carries bacteria, viruses, and other pathogens that require more than extraction and drying. The process has a specific sequence, and skipping steps creates health risks that linger long after the water is gone.

The work starts with containment: sealing off the affected area to prevent cross-contamination to clean parts of the home. From there, the crew removes standing sewage and any porous materials that absorbed it. Drywall, carpet, and pad that have contacted raw sewage are typically non-salvageable and are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to Utah solid waste handling requirements.

Once the space is cleared, the sanitization phase begins. Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination. After disinfection, structural drying equipment, including air movers and dehumidifiers calibrated for the ambient conditions, runs until moisture readings in framing and concrete return to acceptable baselines. In Herriman's climate, where summer humidity is low but basement slabs can hold residual moisture for longer than expected, drying timelines require monitoring rather than guessing.
