## Why Herriman Properties See Water Damage Events

Harriman's rapid growth over the past two decades means the housing stock spans a wide range of construction vintages, from early 2000s tract homes to brand-new builds in expanding subdivisions closer to the mountain bench. Older plumbing connections in homes built during the first wave of Herriman's development can be more prone to supply-line fatigue, while newer construction sometimes features open-concept layouts where a single leak under a kitchen island can reach three or four adjoining rooms before anyone notices.

The soil composition here compounds the problem. The clay-dominant ground along much of the valley floor drains slowly, which means exterior water from irrigation overspray, snowmelt off the Oquirrhs, or a heavy July monsoon storm does not absorb quickly. Instead it pools against foundations and finds any gap in a window well, a door threshold, or a crack in a basement wall. Once inside, water on a concrete slab reads dry at the surface within a day or two, while moisture trapped beneath luxury vinyl plank or carpet padding continues to feed mold colonization for weeks.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Herriman

The first priority on any water cleanup call is stopping the source. If a pipe has burst and the shutoff is unclear, our crew locates the main before anything else. From there the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and monitoring in a sequence calibrated to what the materials actually need, not a fixed number of days.

Extraction equipment pulls standing water from hard floors and carpet simultaneously. Thermal imaging cameras help locate moisture that has wicked into wall cavities or under cabinetry without visible surface signs. Industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are then positioned based on the room layout and material types present. In Herriman homes with finished basements, which represent a large share of the housing stock, this often means staging equipment across multiple levels and monitoring readings daily until the structure reaches its dry standard.

Documentation runs alongside every step. Moisture readings, photos, and drying logs are compiled in a format that supports insurance claims and gives you a clear record of what was found and what was done.

## Insurance Coordination for Herriman Water Damage Claims

Most standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge, which covers the majority of the emergency water cleanup calls we receive. What they typically do not cover is gradual leakage, so the distinction between a burst pipe and a slow drip that went unnoticed for months matters to your adjuster.

FIX Restoration documents losses in a format that insurance carriers recognize. We photograph affected materials before any removal, log moisture readings with timestamps, and can communicate directly with your adjuster to answer scope questions. This reduces back-and-forth and helps move the claim forward without putting the documentation burden entirely on you during an already stressful situation.
