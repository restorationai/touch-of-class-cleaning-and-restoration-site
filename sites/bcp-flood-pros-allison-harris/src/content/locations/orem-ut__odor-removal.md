## Why Orem Properties Hold Odors Longer

Orem's housing stock spans several decades and building styles, from mid-century ranch homes near the older core of the city to newer construction on the east bench closer to the Wasatch foothills. Older homes often have less vapor-permeable insulation and more porous interior finishes that absorb odor-causing compounds deeply. When a fire, flood, or long-term pet issue saturates those materials, the smell can persist for months even after visible damage is cleaned up.

The region's low humidity also plays a counterintuitive role. Dry air slows the natural off-gassing process that would otherwise help dissipate odors over time, meaning smoke residue and biological odors tend to stay concentrated in porous materials rather than gradually releasing. At the same time, when Orem does get moisture from winter storms or spring runoff, sealed crawlspaces and basements can develop musty, mold-adjacent smells that spread through forced-air systems quickly.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Orem

Effective odor elimination follows a structured sequence. The first step is always identifying the source material: charred framing, saturated subfloor, contaminated ductwork, or residue embedded in soft goods. Treating the air without addressing the source is temporary at best.

Once the source is confirmed, we match the method to the odor type and the structure:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates the same porous pathways that smoke traveled during a fire, neutralizing odor compounds bonded to wall cavities, attic insulation, and subflooring.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules in occupied or sensitive spaces where ozone treatment is not appropriate, such as homes with plants, electronics, or pets that cannot be relocated.
- **Ozone treatment** is effective for severe, widespread odors in unoccupied structures, particularly after smoke or biohazard events. The space must be vacated and properly ventilated before re-entry.
- **HVAC and duct deodorization** is often a necessary final step in Orem homes with forced-air systems, since ductwork circulates odor-laden air throughout the entire structure if left untreated.

The method used, and sometimes the combination of methods, depends on what caused the odor, how long it has been present, and the construction of your specific home.

## Local Note

Orem's forced-air heating systems run hard from October through March, and that extended heating season creates a specific pattern worth knowing: smoke odors from a single fireplace incident or kitchen fire can be distributed through every room in the house within hours if the HVAC is running. By the time a homeowner notices the smell has spread, the ductwork, air handler, and return registers have all been coated. In those situations, deodorizing only the room where the fire occurred will not resolve the problem. A full duct inspection and treatment is almost always part of the solution for fire-related odors in Orem homes with central air.
