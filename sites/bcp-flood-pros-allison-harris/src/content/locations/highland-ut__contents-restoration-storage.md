## Why Highland Homes See Contents Damage the Way They Do

Most of Highland's residential development happened in waves from the 1980s through the 2000s, which means a lot of the housing stock features finished basements, open-concept main floors, and large square footage, all of which amplify the spread of a loss event. A water line failure in a finished basement doesn't just soak the concrete; it wicks into carpet padding, drywall, wood furniture legs, and stored boxes before most homeowners even notice. Fire and smoke from a kitchen or garage travel fast through open floor plans, leaving odor and soot on upholstered furniture, drapes, and clothing two or three rooms away from the source.

The elevation and climate add another layer. Highland's position along the western slope of the Wasatch means cold snaps arrive quickly and linger. Pipes in exterior walls and uninsulated garage spaces are especially vulnerable during January and February temperature swings. When those pipes let go, the volume of water released in a short time can overwhelm a room's contents before the water is even shut off.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Highland

The first step on any contents job is a room-by-room inventory, every item photographed, logged, and categorized by material type and condition. That documentation matters both for your insurance claim and for our own restoration workflow, since leather, electronics, textiles, and hardwood furniture each require different cleaning chemistry and drying conditions.

From there, salvageable contents are packed out and transported to our facility in American Fork for hands-on restoration. Smoke-affected fabrics go through ozone or hydroxyl treatment to neutralize odor at the molecular level rather than masking it. Hard surfaces are cleaned with pH-appropriate solutions matched to the specific residue, dry smoke from a fast-burning fire leaves a different deposit than wet smoke from smoldering materials, and treating them the same way produces poor results. Items that need time to dry or cure are held in our climate-controlled storage area, which keeps temperature and humidity stable regardless of what the weather is doing outside.

Once your home passes structural drying and any needed repairs, we coordinate return delivery and help you place items back in the right rooms. Nothing comes back until it's genuinely ready.

## Highland Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental losses, a burst pipe, an appliance overflow, fire damage, and contents restoration is typically a covered line item under personal property coverage. The inventory and photo documentation we produce at pack-out is exactly what adjusters need to process a contents claim, and we can share that file directly with your carrier.

If your policy includes an additional living expenses or loss-of-use provision, the storage period while your home is being repaired may also be reimbursable. We recommend confirming that with your adjuster early, and we're happy to answer questions about how our documentation process works with most major carriers.
