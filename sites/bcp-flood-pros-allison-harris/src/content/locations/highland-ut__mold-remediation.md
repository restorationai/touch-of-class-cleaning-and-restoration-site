## Why Highland Properties See Mold Issues

Highland's housing stock skews toward larger single-family homes built from the late 1980s through the 2000s, a construction era that relied heavily on OSB sheathing, paper-faced drywall, and tightly sealed building envelopes. Those materials hold moisture efficiently, which is a problem when a vapor barrier fails or a window flashing lets water track into a wall cavity. Tightly built homes also limit the incidental air exchange that would otherwise slow mold colonization.

The city's elevation and proximity to Utah Lake's regional drainage basin mean that spring snowmelt raises the water table across much of northern Utah County. Crawl spaces and slab-on-grade foundations in Highland can see ground moisture intrusion during March and April even when there's been no plumbing failure. Mold can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure, which means a wet crawl space that goes unnoticed over a long weekend already has a head start.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Highland

When FIX Restoration arrives at a Highland property, the first step is a thorough moisture mapping of the affected area, not just the visible growth, but the surrounding materials that may be holding moisture without showing surface discoloration yet. Thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters let the crew identify hidden saturation behind drywall or under flooring before containment is set.

Containment comes next. Negative air pressure barriers isolate the work zone so that disturbing mold colonies doesn't push spores into unaffected rooms. HEPA-filtered air scrubbers run continuously throughout the remediation. Affected porous materials, drywall, insulation, wood framing that has exceeded safe moisture thresholds, are removed and bagged for disposal per industry protocols. Non-porous surfaces are treated and dried to documented moisture levels before any reconstruction begins.

Final clearance matters. The job isn't finished when the visible mold is gone; it's finished when post-remediation air and surface testing confirms that spore counts have returned to normal background levels. FIX Restoration coordinates with independent industrial hygienists for that clearance testing when clients or their carriers require third-party verification.
