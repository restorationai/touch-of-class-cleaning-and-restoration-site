## Why Alpine Properties Hold Odors Longer

Alpine's elevation and climate work against you when odors take hold. At roughly 4,900 feet, the thinner, drier air actually carries volatile organic compounds differently than lower-elevation Front Range communities. More importantly, the area's cold winters mean homes stay sealed for months at a time. Forced-air heating systems circulate odor molecules through ductwork repeatedly, embedding them in insulation, carpet padding, and the porous surfaces of finished basements that are common in Alpine's larger single-family homes.

The housing stock here skews newer than much of Utah County, with many homes built in the 2000s and 2010s featuring engineered wood framing, spray-foam insulation, and low-VOC interior finishes. Those materials can absorb smoke and biological odors deeply, and they respond differently to heat-based treatments than older construction does. A thermal fogging approach that works well in a 1970s rambler in a neighboring city may need to be adjusted for a tightly sealed, energy-efficient Alpine home to avoid over-saturating the space.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Alpine

Effective odor elimination starts with finding every source, not just the obvious one. We begin with a thorough inspection that includes HVAC registers, crawl spaces, and any finished basement areas, because odor molecules settle into low spaces and get redistributed every time the furnace cycles.

From there, the method we choose depends on what caused the odor and what the structure can handle:

- **Thermal fogging** pushes a deodorizing fog into the same microscopic spaces the odor penetrated, neutralizing compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them. It works well for smoke odor removal after a kitchen fire or a fireplace backdraft.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing compounds continuously over time. It is safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces and is often the right call for pet odors or long-standing musty smells.
- **Ozone treatment** is highly effective for severe smoke odor in vacant structures, but it requires the space to be completely unoccupied during treatment and properly ventilated afterward. In Alpine's climate, timing ozone treatments around outdoor temperature matters for safe and effective ventilation.
- **Source removal and HEPA cleaning** precede every chemical or equipment treatment. No deodorization method works if the physical source, charred material, soiled carpet, or contaminated insulation, is still present.

After treatment, we verify results with post-treatment air quality checks rather than relying on the nose alone, which fatigues quickly in enclosed spaces.

## Local Note

Alpine's newer, tightly built homes often have whole-house ventilation systems, including heat recovery ventilators, that are separate from the main HVAC. When smoke or biological odors get into these systems, homeowners sometimes notice the smell returning days after a surface-level treatment because the HRV is pulling in residual odor from wall cavities or the attic space. We check these systems specifically during our inspection, because missing them is one of the most common reasons an odor problem seems to come back after a first attempt at remediation.
