Homes in the 84042 ZIP code range from newer construction in planned subdivisions to older ranch-style builds where plumbing runs through uninsulated crawl spaces, making freeze-and-burst events a recurring winter concern. When a pipe lets go or a roof takes on water, the contents of a finished basement or main-floor living area can be soaked within minutes. Smoke and soot from a kitchen or garage fire compound the problem: fabrics and porous materials trap odor molecules that ordinary cleaning cannot reach.

Because many Lindon neighborhoods are family-oriented and owner-occupied, homes tend to be furnished with items that carry real sentimental weight alongside replacement value. That combination makes professional contents restoration, rather than simple replacement, worth the effort.

## Our Contents Restoration Process in Lindon

The first step after a loss is a room-by-room contents inventory. Every item is photographed, catalogued by category and condition, and assigned a cleaning or storage path before anything leaves your home. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and gives you a clear record of what was recovered.

Pack-out uses padded moving materials and sealed containers sized to the item, not the nearest available box. Textiles go through specialized laundering that removes smoke residue and microbial contamination without damaging fibers. Hard surfaces, electronics, and furniture are cleaned using ultrasonic equipment or controlled wet-cleaning methods depending on the material. Documents and photographs receive freeze-drying or air-drying treatment to arrest further deterioration.

During restoration, your belongings are held in a climate-controlled storage facility, which is particularly important in Utah County where summer temperatures and low humidity can warp wood and crack leather if items are left in an uncontrolled environment. Once your home passes drying verification and any reconstruction is complete, we coordinate a scheduled return and place items back in their original rooms.

## Insurance Coordination for Lindon Property Owners

Most homeowner policies in Utah cover sudden and accidental water or fire losses, and contents restoration is typically a covered line item under personal property coverage. FIX Restoration documents every item in the inventory with photographs and condition notes formatted to meet adjuster review standards. If your policy includes additional living expense coverage, storage fees for your contents during the restoration period may be reimbursable. Ask your adjuster specifically about that line, and we can provide itemized storage invoices that match the format carriers expect.

## Local Note

Lindon's newer subdivisions often include finished basements with luxury vinyl plank or engineered hardwood flooring. When water intrudes, homeowners sometimes assume these floors are ruined and begin pulling them up before a restoration crew arrives. In many cases that is premature: engineered hardwood can be dried in place with the right desiccant equipment if the subfloor moisture content is addressed quickly. More relevant to contents, the storage areas and mechanical rooms common in these basements often hold holiday items, sporting equipment, and archived documents in cardboard boxes, which wick water rapidly. Prioritizing those storage areas during pack-out, before attention turns to furniture, frequently saves items that would otherwise be written off as total losses.
