## Our Biohazard Remediation Process in Pleasant Grove

Every job starts with a discreet arrival. Unmarked vehicles are available on request, and the crew works without unnecessary foot traffic or noise. The process follows a structured sequence:

**Containment first.** Affected areas are isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where appropriate, preventing cross-contamination to the rest of the property.

**Removal of affected materials.** Biohazardous waste, including sharps, infectious materials, and contaminated soft goods, is collected, packaged, and transported according to applicable state and federal regulations governing biological waste disposal in Utah.

**Surface treatment and verification.** Hard surfaces are treated with EPA-registered hospital-grade disinfectants.
