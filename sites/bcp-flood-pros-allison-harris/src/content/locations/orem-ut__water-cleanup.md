Utah County's freeze-thaw cycle is one of the more punishing in the Intermountain West. Orem's housing stock spans several decades, and older ranch-style homes built in the 1960s and 1970s often have galvanized steel supply lines that have narrowed with mineral buildup over time, making them more vulnerable to pressure spikes when temperatures swing.

Beyond plumbing failures, Orem's position at the base of the Wasatch Front means that heavy rain events, especially the atmospheric river storms that have become more frequent in recent years, can overwhelm residential drainage systems. Yards that slope toward the foundation rather than away from it channel runoff directly against basement walls. ## Our Water Cleanup Process in Orem

The first priority on any water cleanup job is stopping the source and documenting the extent of the loss before anything is moved or dried. That documentation matters for insurance claims and for understanding where water has actually traveled, not just where it's visible. Water follows the path of least resistance, and in a home with wood-framed floors over a crawl space, that often means the subfloor is saturated long before the surface flooring shows any sign of a problem.

Once the scope is mapped, standing water is extracted using truck-mounted and portable extraction units. After bulk water is removed, the drying phase begins with commercial-grade air movers and dehumidifiers positioned to create airflow across wet structural materials. Moisture readings are taken at multiple points, walls, subfloor, framing, and logged daily. Drying is not declared complete based on how a surface feels; it's complete when the readings return to established dry standards for the material type and the ambient conditions in the home.

## Orem Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowners policies in Utah cover sudden and accidental water losses, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, an ice dam that forces water under shingles. What they typically do not cover is gradual leakage that went unaddressed over time. That distinction matters because the way a loss is documented affects how a claim is received by the adjuster. ## Local Note

Orem's older neighborhoods, particularly the blocks of ranch homes built close to the ground on slab foundations, present a specific drying challenge that isn't obvious until you're on site. Slab construction means there's no crawl space beneath the floor, so when water saturates hardwood or engineered flooring laid directly over concrete, the concrete itself holds moisture and releases it slowly back into the flooring from below. Standard drying setups that work well in a wood-framed floor system can fall short here. Drying mats placed directly on the floor surface pull moisture upward through the flooring rather than relying on airflow alone, and they make a measurable difference in drying time on slab jobs. It's a detail that matters in Orem's housing stock specifically.
