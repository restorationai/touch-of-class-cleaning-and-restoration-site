## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Spanish Fork

The first priority after any storm event is stopping the water from moving deeper into the structure. FIX Restoration crews begin with a full exterior and interior assessment, checking the roof deck, attic cavity, wall cavities, and crawl space, because water that enters at the roofline rarely stays there. Moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras reveal saturation behind drywall and insulation that looks dry to the eye. Once the scope is mapped, emergency tarping or board-up secures the building envelope so no additional weather exposure compounds the loss while the repair plan is being finalized.

From there, the process moves into controlled drying: industrial air movers and dehumidifiers are positioned to create the airflow patterns that pull moisture out of framing and sheathing, not just surface materials. Debris removal, downed branches, displaced roofing material, broken glass, runs in parallel so the property is safe to work in and document for the insurance claim. Structural repairs, from roof decking replacement to interior drywall and paint, close out the job once drying logs confirm the structure has returned to acceptable moisture levels.
