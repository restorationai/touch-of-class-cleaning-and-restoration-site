## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Lindon

When we arrive on site, the first priority is a quick walk of the perimeter to identify every breach point, not just the obvious one. Broken window board-up follows a similar discipline: we check adjacent frames for cracked glazing that could fail under the next wind load.

For roof tarping, we use reinforced poly tarps rated for the weight and UV exposure common to Utah summers, secured with wood battens and fasteners that distribute load rather than concentrating it at a few anchor points.
