## Why Herriman Homes Develop Persistent Odors

Most of Herriman's residential neighborhoods were built after 2000, which means the housing stock is dominated by tight, energy-efficient construction. That's great for utility bills, but it works against you when an odor event happens. Reduced air exchange means smoke, pet dander, cooking residue, and post-water-damage mildew have fewer natural pathways out. The smell circulates through the HVAC system, embeds in spray-foam insulation cavities, and settles into the engineered wood products and LVP flooring common in homes built during the city's rapid growth period.

The Oquirrh foothills also produce temperature inversions during winter months that trap particulate matter close to the ground. If a fire loss or smoke event happens during an inversion, the odor compounds that settle into your home's surfaces are more concentrated than they would be on a clear, breezy day. That's not a detail most restoration companies account for when they're planning a deodorization scope.

## Our Deodorization Process, Calibrated to Herriman Conditions

Odor removal is not a single step. The process begins with identifying where odor molecules have actually migrated, not just where the smell is strongest. In Herriman's newer builds, that often means checking inside HVAC ducts, behind the vapor barrier on exterior walls, and under the floating floor systems that are standard in homes built here over the last two decades.

Once the source materials are identified and any saturated or charred content is removed, we deploy the appropriate deodorization method for the specific odor type and affected materials:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates porous surfaces the same way smoke did, neutralizing odor molecules at the molecular level rather than masking them. It works especially well in finished basements and carpeted living spaces common in Herriman's larger floor plans.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor compounds in the air and on surfaces. Because it's safe to run while occupants are present, it's often the right choice when a family needs to stay in the home during part of the process.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces where a high concentration of odor molecules has bonded to hard surfaces. We seal the space, run the equipment for a calculated dwell time, and then ventilate thoroughly before re-entry.

The method we use, or the combination of methods, depends on what the loss looks like, not on a fixed menu.
