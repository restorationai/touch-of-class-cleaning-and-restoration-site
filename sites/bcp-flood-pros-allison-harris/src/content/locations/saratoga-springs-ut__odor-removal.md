## Why Saratoga Springs Homes Hold Odors Longer

The geography matters. Saratoga Springs developed rapidly from the mid-2000s onward, which means the dominant housing stock is newer construction with tighter building envelopes, engineered lumber framing, and spray-foam or blown-in insulation. Tighter homes are more energy-efficient, but they also trap odor molecules with far less natural air exchange than older, leakier construction. A smoke event from a kitchen fire or a malfunctioning gas fireplace can saturate HVAC ductwork, engineered wood subfloor, and foam insulation in ways that surface cleaning cannot reach.

The proximity to Utah Lake adds humidity swings that are unusual for a high-desert climate. In late summer and early fall, lake-effect moisture pushes into the valley, raising indoor relative humidity enough to reactivate odors that seemed dormant. Pet urine odors are a common example: the urea salts crystallize when dry, then off-gas again when humidity climbs. That cycle repeats until the source material is treated at the molecular level, not just masked.

## Our Deodorization Process in Saratoga Springs

Effective odor elimination starts with identifying where the odor source is actually living, not just where it smells strongest. We inspect HVAC returns, subfloor cavities, wall cavities near exterior penetrations, and attic spaces, because odor migrates and settles in the lowest-pressure zones of a structure.

Depending on what we find, treatment may involve one or more of the following approaches:

- **Thermal fogging** pushes a deodorizing agent in a fine, heated mist that penetrates porous materials the same way the original odor did. It is especially effective for smoke odor removal after a fire or a smoking-related loss.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor-causing compounds at the molecular level. Hydroxyls are safe to run in occupied or partially occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner cannot vacate.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces and is one of the most aggressive tools available for heavy smoke or severe biological odors. We follow manufacturer and industry guidelines on concentration and dwell time, then thoroughly air out the space before re-occupancy.
- **Source removal and encapsulation** addresses materials that cannot be deodorized in place, including saturated insulation, contaminated subfloor sections, and heavily affected drywall.

For HVAC-distributed odors, we treat the duct system directly rather than assuming surface treatment will carry through the vents.

## Equipment and Methods Calibrated to Local Conditions

Saratoga Springs winters present a specific challenge: when outdoor temperatures drop into the teens and the inversion sets in, ventilating a home after ozone treatment becomes complicated. Running ozone in a sealed structure works well, but airing out the space afterward requires either waiting for a warmer window or using mechanical ventilation to flush the air. We plan treatment schedules around this, particularly for jobs in the colder months between November and February.

For smoke odor removal in homes with the spray-foam insulation common in post-2005 Saratoga Springs construction, thermal fogging is often the preferred first step because the fog penetrates the same micro-pathways the smoke used. Hydroxyl generators then run continuously to address residual off-gassing from engineered materials that absorbed smoke particles.
