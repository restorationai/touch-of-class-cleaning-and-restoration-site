## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Orem

Every job starts with a rapid exterior assessment, identifying all breach points, evaluating roof deck integrity before anyone walks it, and noting any utility hazards flagged by Rocky Mountain Power or Dominion Energy that may still be active after a fire. That step protects the crew and prevents compounding the loss.

For window and door board-up, we use minimum ½-inch OSB cut to fit and secured with structural screws rather than nails, which hold under lateral wind load and are easier to remove cleanly when reconstruction begins. Openings in masonry or stucco exteriors, common in Orem's mid-century and newer Mediterranean-style builds, get additional perimeter sealing to prevent water wicking behind the panel.

Roof tarping follows IICRC and industry best-practice guidelines: we anchor tarps over the ridge rather than stopping at the damage perimeter, run ballast boards along all edges, and use UV-resistant poly rated for extended outdoor exposure. On steeper-pitch roofs, which appear frequently in Orem's residential neighborhoods, we use fall-protection equipment and adjust anchor points accordingly. Photos and measurements are documented throughout for your insurance claim.
