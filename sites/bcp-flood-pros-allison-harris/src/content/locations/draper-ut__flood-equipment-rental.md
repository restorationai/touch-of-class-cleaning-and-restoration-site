## Equipment We Rent for Flood Drying in Draper

Professional drying is a system, not a single machine. Renting the right combination of equipment makes the difference between a space that reads dry on a meter and one that actually is dry through the full depth of the material.

**Air movers** create high-velocity airflow across wet surfaces, pulling moisture from carpet, pad, hardwood, and drywall into the air column where a dehumidifier can capture it. A single air mover pointed at a wet wall accomplishes far less than a calculated placement pattern that rotates airflow across every affected surface.

**LGR dehumidifiers** (low-grain refrigerant units) are the workhorses of structural drying. They pull significantly more moisture per day than a consumer-grade unit from a hardware store, and they continue working efficiently even as indoor humidity drops toward target levels, which a standard dehumidifier cannot do.

**Flood pumps and extraction equipment** handle standing water before drying begins. Trying to run air movers over standing water wastes time and equipment capacity.

**Moisture meters and thermal imaging** help you confirm that drying is actually progressing through the material, not just at the surface. We can walk you through basic meter use when you pick up equipment.

## Working with Insurance When You Rent Equipment

Many homeowners assume that renting and operating equipment themselves means insurance will not cover the cost. That is not always the case. Homeowners policies that cover the underlying water loss event often cover reasonable mitigation expenses, including equipment rental, when the rental is part of a documented effort to limit further damage. Keep your rental receipts, photograph the affected areas before and during drying, and log your daily moisture readings if possible.
