## Why Provo Properties See Mold Issues

The homes closest to Center Street and the blocks radiating out from downtown Provo tend to be mid-century construction or older, many with original plaster-and-lath walls, single-pane windows, and basement utility rooms that were never designed with modern HVAC in mind. That combination holds humidity against surfaces where it has nowhere to go. Add Utah Lake's proximity to the west side of the city and the seasonal irrigation that keeps Provo's residential lots green through August, and you have consistent ground moisture working against foundations and crawl spaces.

Provo also sits at roughly 4,500 feet elevation, which means heating systems run hard through a long winter. When warm interior air meets cold exterior walls, especially in homes with inadequate insulation, condensation forms on framing and sheathing inside the wall cavity. That's a slow, invisible mold incubator that only shows up when a baseboard starts to buckle or a musty odor becomes impossible to ignore.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Provo

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection, moisture meters, thermal imaging where wall access is limited, and a visual assessment of the HVAC system, since mold spores travel through ductwork and can colonize a second room before the first is even treated. Once the scope is mapped, the affected area is sealed with negative-air containment barriers to prevent cross-contamination to the rest of the home. That step matters especially in Provo's older two-story layouts, where open stairwells connect floors without natural air breaks.

After containment is established, compromised materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of per standard remediation protocol. Structural surfaces that can be cleaned rather than removed are treated with EPA-registered antimicrobial agents. Industrial air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously during the process to capture airborne spores. Before containment comes down, air quality is verified, if a homeowner or their insurance carrier requires third-party clearance testing, we coordinate with an independent industrial hygienist to complete that step before the job closes.
