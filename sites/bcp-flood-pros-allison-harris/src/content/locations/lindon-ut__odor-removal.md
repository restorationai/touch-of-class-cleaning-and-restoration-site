## Why Lindon Homes Hold Odors Differently

Much of Lindon's residential development happened in waves, from modest ranch-style homes built in the 1970s and 1980s to the larger two-story construction that spread through the area in the 2000s. Those older builds often have fiberglass batt insulation in wall cavities and original carpet tack strips that act like sponges for smoke and pet odors. The subfloor beneath that carpet is frequently OSB or older particleboard, both of which absorb volatile organic compounds deeply and release them slowly over weeks.

Utah County's dry summers and cold, still winters create a seasonal pattern worth knowing: during inversion season, windows stay closed for days at a time, concentrating any odor source indoors. A single smoke event, a slow sewer gas leak, or a water intrusion that goes unnoticed over a weekend can saturate soft goods and framing before anyone realizes the scope of the problem.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Lindon

Odor removal is not a single step. The source has to be addressed before any deodorization technology can hold.

We start with a physical inspection, tracing the odor to its origin rather than masking the symptom. Once the source material is removed or cleaned, we select the deodorization method matched to the loss type:

- **Thermal fogging** uses a petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing solution converted to a fine fog that penetrates porous surfaces the same way smoke molecules did. It is particularly effective after structure fires in Lindon homes where smoke has migrated into wall cavities and attic framing.
- **Hydroxyl generation** produces hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing molecules at a chemical level. Hydroxyl units can run continuously in occupied or semi-occupied spaces, which matters when a homeowner cannot vacate for an extended period.
- **Ozone treatment** is highly effective for severe smoke and biological odors but requires the space to be unoccupied during treatment and properly ventilated afterward. We schedule ozone jobs carefully in Lindon's colder months when airing out a home takes longer.
- **Enzyme and antimicrobial application** handles the biological side of pet, sewage, and decomposition odors at the surface and subsurface level before any fogging or gas-phase treatment begins.

For most residential jobs in Lindon, we use a layered approach: source removal, enzyme treatment where applicable, then either hydroxyl or thermal fogging depending on occupancy and severity.
