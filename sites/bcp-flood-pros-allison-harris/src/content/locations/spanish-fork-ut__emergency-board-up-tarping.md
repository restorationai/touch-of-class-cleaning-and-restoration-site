## Why Spanish Fork Properties Face Particular Exposure Risks

The geography here matters. Spanish Fork Canyon acts as a natural wind corridor, and when a storm system pushes through, gusts can arrive with little warning and significant force. ## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Spanish Fork

Every board-up or tarping job starts with a fast visual assessment of all breach points: windows, doors, garage openings, and any roof sections where decking or framing is exposed. For broken window board-up, we cut plywood to fit the rough opening, secure it with structural screws rather than staples, and tape the perimeter to reduce wind infiltration. For roof tarping, we use heavy-duty polyethylene tarps, typically 6-mil or heavier, anchored with lumber battens screwed into undamaged decking rather than simply weighted at the edges. A tarp that lifts in a canyon wind gust does nothing; the anchor method matters as much as the material.

For fire damage board-up, we also account for structural instability. If a wall or roof section is compromised beyond simple covering, we flag it clearly and communicate directly with the property owner and, when applicable, the insurance adjuster before proceeding. Documentation, photographs of every breach point before and after boarding, is part of every job because your carrier will need it.

## Local Note

One thing worth knowing about Spanish Fork specifically: the area around the historic downtown and the older streets near the city center includes homes with masonry construction, brick veneer and in some cases full brick or block walls, that behave differently after a fire or impact event than standard wood-frame construction. Masonry can look intact while mortar joints are heat-damaged and structurally weakened. When we board up a masonry-walled structure after fire damage, we avoid anchoring into compromised brick and instead use freestanding or tension-based securing methods where the wall integrity is in question. It is a small detail, but it prevents a board-up crew from making a damaged wall worse.
