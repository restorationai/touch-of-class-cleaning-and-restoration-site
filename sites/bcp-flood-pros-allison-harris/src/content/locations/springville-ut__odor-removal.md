## Why Springville Homes Hold Odors Longer

The housing stock here tells a story. A large share of Springville's single-family homes were built between the 1950s and the 1980s, and those construction eras leaned heavily on plaster-over-lath walls, original wood subfloors, and minimal vapor barriers in crawl spaces. Smoke, pet dander, mold off-gassing, and cooking odors don't just sit on the surface of those materials. They penetrate the substrate. When you run a fan and spray an air freshener, you move the problem around. The smell comes back the next time the furnace kicks on and heats those walls from the inside.

Springville also has a meaningful number of homes with swamp coolers rather than central AC. Evaporative coolers pull outside air through a wet pad and push it through the house, which is efficient in a dry climate but also means odors from one area of the home cycle through every room quickly. A single smoke event or a slow sewer leak can saturate a whole house before the source is even identified.

## Our Deodorization Process, Calibrated to Utah County Conditions

Effective odor removal starts with source identification, not equipment deployment. Before any treatment begins, the crew walks the property to locate the origin: char behind a wall cavity, urine-soaked subfloor padding, a slow sewer gas leak from a degraded wax ring. Treating the air without addressing the source is a temporary fix.

Once the source is contained or removed, the appropriate deodorization method is matched to the material and the severity:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates porous surfaces the same way smoke did, neutralizing odor-causing particles at the molecular level. It's particularly effective in older homes where smoke has reached into wall cavities and ceiling framing.
- **Hydroxyl generation** uses UV light to produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor compounds in the air and on surfaces. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl treatment can run while occupants and pets are present, which matters for families who can't easily vacate.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces with severe contamination. The property must be vacated during treatment and aired out completely before re-entry. This method is aggressive and effective for deeply embedded odors in confined spaces.
- **Thermal and HEPA air scrubbing** runs alongside other methods to capture particulates that carry odor, including soot, mold spores, and pet dander.

The method isn't chosen by default. It's chosen by what the home actually needs.
