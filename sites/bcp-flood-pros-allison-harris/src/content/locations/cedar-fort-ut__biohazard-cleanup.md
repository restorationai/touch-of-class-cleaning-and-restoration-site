## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Cedar Fort

Every biohazard call begins with a careful assessment. Before any removal begins, the affected area is secured and contained to prevent cross-contamination with the rest of the structure. This is especially relevant in Cedar Fort properties where open floor plans or connected outbuildings can allow air to circulate freely between spaces.

From there, the process follows a consistent sequence:

- **Containment and PPE setup**: The work area is isolated using physical barriers and negative air pressure where needed.
- **Removal of affected materials**: Porous materials that cannot be decontaminated to a safe standard are carefully removed and disposed of in accordance with Utah Department of Environmental Quality guidelines for biohazardous waste.
- **Disinfection and treatment**: All surfaces in the affected zone are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants appropriate for the specific biological hazard present.
- **Odor control**: Biological incidents often leave persistent odor even after visible material is removed. Odor treatment is part of the remediation, not an afterthought.
- **Clearance documentation**: When the work is complete, documentation is provided that can support an insurance claim or satisfy a property sale disclosure requirement.

The timeline varies depending on the scope and the materials involved, but most residential biohazard jobs in Cedar Fort are completed within one to two days.
