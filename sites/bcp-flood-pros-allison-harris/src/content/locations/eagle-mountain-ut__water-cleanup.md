## Why Eagle Mountain Properties See Water Damage Issues

Most of Eagle Mountain's housing stock was built after 2000, which means polybutylene plumbing is rare, but the trade-off is a high concentration of PEX and CPVC supply lines installed during the construction boom years. Those materials perform well, but connection fittings at manifolds and appliance hookups are a common failure point, especially when homes experience the temperature swings that come with Utah's high-desert elevation. A fitting that weeps slowly all winter can let go completely when spring temperatures fluctuate.

The city also expanded rapidly across terrain with clay-heavy subsoil that sheds water rather than absorbing it. During heavy rain or rapid snowmelt, that runoff finds its way toward foundations and window wells. Homes built on the lower-elevation pads in newer phases of development can see groundwater intrusion that looks minor at first but saturates the subfloor assembly from below, where it is invisible to a standard moisture meter reading taken at the surface.

Eagle Mountain's newer construction also means many homes have finished basements with spray-foam insulation in the rim joist area. That foam traps moisture against the framing if water gets behind it, and the problem does not show up until mold has already colonized the wood.

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Eagle Mountain

When FIX Restoration arrives on site, the first priority is stopping the source if it has not already been addressed. Eagle Mountain residents are served by Eagle Mountain City utilities, and the main shutoff is typically at the curb box near the street. If the interior shutoff has failed, knowing where the curb box is located saves critical minutes.

After the source is controlled, the crew performs a full moisture mapping of the structure using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters. Slab-on-grade construction, which dominates Eagle Mountain's newer neighborhoods, means water travels laterally under flooring rather than dropping to a basement, so mapping has to extend well beyond the visible wet area. Standing water is extracted using truck-mounted equipment, and structural drying begins with commercial-grade desiccant or refrigerant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers positioned to create a drying chamber around affected assemblies.

Documentation runs parallel to every step. Moisture readings, equipment placement logs, and daily drying records are compiled in a format that satisfies most major insurance carriers and supports the claim from the first call to final sign-off.

## Eagle Mountain Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner policies cover sudden and accidental water discharge, which is the category that covers burst pipes, failed appliance supply lines, and similar events. Slow leaks that developed over time are treated differently by adjusters, which is why the documentation FIX Restoration produces from the first hour on site matters. Photographs taken before extraction begins, moisture readings with timestamps, and a clear scope of affected materials give the carrier what it needs to process the claim without unnecessary back-and-forth.
