## Why Lehi Properties Hold Odors Differently

Lehi sits in Utah County where the semi-arid climate creates a specific odor challenge: low ambient humidity means porous materials like wood framing, OSB sheathing, and concrete block dry out and become especially absorbent. When smoke, sewage backup, or a pet accident introduces odor compounds into a dry, porous substrate, those molecules penetrate deeper than they would in a more humid climate. The result is a smell that returns days after surface cleaning because the source is still locked inside the material.

The newer subdivisions spreading across Lehi also tend toward open floor plans with high vaulted ceilings and minimal interior wall mass. That architecture circulates air efficiently under normal conditions, but after a fire or a long-term pet odor problem, the same open layout distributes odor molecules throughout the entire living space quickly. Treating only the room of origin rarely resolves the problem.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Lehi

Effective deodorization starts with identifying the odor source, not masking it. The process typically moves through these stages:

**Source removal**: Any material that is saturated with odor compounds beyond recovery, charred debris, soiled padding, or contaminated insulation, is removed before any deodorization equipment is deployed. Treating over a source just delays the problem.

**Thermal fogging**: A petroleum-based or water-based deodorizing fog is heated and dispersed through the space. The fog particles are small enough to follow the same pathways smoke took into wall cavities, subflooring, and ductwork, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than coating surfaces.

**Hydroxyl deodorization**: For occupied or semi-occupied spaces where strong chemical treatments are not practical, hydroxyl generators produce hydroxyl radicals that break down odor-causing VOCs without requiring occupants to vacate for extended periods. This is particularly useful in Lehi's newer attached townhome communities where neighboring units may be occupied.

**Ozone treatment**: In vacant structures with severe smoke or biological odors, ozone treatment can reach into cavities and porous materials that other methods cannot. Because ozone at treatment concentrations requires the space to be unoccupied, we coordinate timing carefully with homeowners and property managers.

**HVAC deodorization**: In Lehi's newer builds, forced-air systems move a high volume of air and can reintroduce odors long after surface treatment if the ductwork itself is not addressed. We treat supply and return lines as part of the standard scope when odor has been circulating through the system.
