## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Eagle Mountain

Every situation is different, and FIX Restoration does not approach a biohazard call with a checklist designed for a different type of loss. The process begins with a thorough assessment of the affected area, identifying all surfaces and materials that require attention, including those that are not immediately visible. Documentation is prepared throughout, both for the property owner's records and for insurance purposes.
