## Why South Jordan Properties See Repeated Storm Damage

The western edge of the Salt Lake Valley creates a geography that concentrates storm energy. Cold air draining off the Oquirrh Mountains to the west collides with warmer valley air, producing localized wind shear that can exceed what the broader metro experiences. South Jordan's newer master-planned subdivisions tend to feature large, open lots with limited mature tree canopy, which means wind has fewer natural breaks before it reaches rooflines and fences.

Hail is a particular concern here. The valley's elevation keeps summer convective storms energetic long enough to produce large hailstones, and the region sees multiple significant hail events most years. Concrete tile roofs common in newer South Jordan construction can crack or chip under impact without showing obvious surface damage, allowing water infiltration that only becomes visible weeks later as staining on interior ceilings. Composite shingles on older sections of the city fare differently: granule loss accelerates aging, and a storm that looks minor from the street can shave years off a roof's remaining life.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in South Jordan

The first priority after any storm loss is stopping additional water from entering the structure. That means emergency tarping or board-up before a second weather system can compound the damage. Once the envelope is secured, the assessment phase begins: a systematic inspection of the roof deck, attic space, exterior cladding, windows, and any below-grade areas where runoff may have entered.

Documentation happens at every stage. Photographs, moisture readings, and written scope notes are organized in a format that supports insurance claims from the start, not as an afterthought. Structural drying follows for any areas where water has penetrated, using commercial dehumidifiers and air movers calibrated to the interior conditions. Debris removal, including downed limbs and displaced roofing material, is handled as part of the restoration scope rather than as a separate trade call. Repairs move from structural to cosmetic: decking, sheathing, framing, then insulation, drywall, and finish work.

South Jordan's newer construction era is worth paying attention to after hail events.
