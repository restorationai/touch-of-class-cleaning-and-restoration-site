## Why Herriman Properties See Mold Issues

The housing stock here is almost entirely post-2000, which means engineered wood framing, OSB sheathing, and spray foam insulation are common. Those materials hold moisture differently than older lumber. OSB in particular can begin to support mold colonization within 24 to 48 hours of sustained wetting, and because it looks structurally intact long after it has been compromised, the damage is easy to miss on a casual inspection. Herriman also sits near the Oquirrh Mountain foothills, where drainage patterns push groundwater toward lower-lying subdivisions during spring snowmelt. Crawl spaces that were dry in August can see standing water by March, and that moisture wicks upward into subfloor assemblies before it ever shows up on a homeowner's radar.

HOA communities, which cover a large share of Herriman's residential neighborhoods, add another layer. Many associations require that exterior work, including any penetrations for ventilation or equipment staging, be approved before a crew starts. We factor that into scheduling so remediation is not delayed by a paperwork gap.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Herriman

Every project starts with a thorough moisture mapping inspection. We use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to find wet material behind surfaces, not just what is visible. Once the scope is clear, we establish containment using negative air pressure and heavy-duty poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run continuously throughout the work.

Affected materials are removed, bagged, and disposed of according to current Utah solid waste guidelines. Structural cavities are treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial solution, then dried to manufacturer-specified moisture content before any rebuild begins. Because Herriman's newer homes often use engineered lumber that cannot simply be dried and left in place once mold has colonized, replacement is frequently the right call rather than surface treatment alone. We document every step with photos and moisture readings, which matters when you are submitting a claim to your homeowner's insurance carrier.
