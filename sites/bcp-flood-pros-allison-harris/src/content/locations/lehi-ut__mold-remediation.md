## Why Lehi Properties See Mold Issues

Lehi straddles the transition between the older agricultural core of Utah County and one of the fastest-growing tech-corridor communities in the Mountain West. That mix matters for mold. Newer subdivisions built quickly during construction surges sometimes have minor framing defects, improperly dried lumber, gaps in vapor barriers, or HVAC ductwork that condenses moisture in crawl spaces during winter inversions. Older properties closer to the original Lehi townsite can have aging plumbing and less-forgiving building envelopes.

Utah Valley also experiences temperature inversions during winter months that trap cold, damp air at ground level. Crawl spaces and basement slab edges in homes along the valley floor are particularly vulnerable. When interior heating runs hard against a cold exterior wall or an uninsulated rim joist, condensation accumulates, and mold doesn't need much. It can begin colonizing porous materials within 24 to 48 hours of sustained moisture exposure.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lehi

Mold remediation isn't mold removal in the sense of simply wiping a surface. The process starts with identifying the moisture source, because any remediation that skips that step will fail within months. Our team inspects the affected area, uses moisture meters and thermal imaging to locate hidden saturation, and then contains the work zone with physical barriers and negative air pressure to prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms.

Contaminated porous materials, drywall, insulation, subflooring, are removed and bagged following established industry protocols. Structural surfaces that can be saved are HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Air scrubbers with HEPA filtration run throughout the project to capture airborne spores. Before any reconstruction begins, the area needs to read dry and clear, we don't close walls until the numbers support it.

In Lehi's newer construction, we frequently encounter spray-foam insulation that can mask moisture intrusion at roof penetrations or around window bucks. That material requires careful inspection because it doesn't wick moisture visibly the way fiberglass batts do.

## Lehi Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most homeowner's insurance policies cover mold remediation when it results from a sudden, accidental water loss, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, a roof leak from a storm. Mold that developed slowly from deferred maintenance is typically excluded, though the line between the two isn't always obvious. We document the affected area thoroughly with photographs, moisture readings, and written scope notes, which gives your adjuster what they need to evaluate the claim.

Lehi has a significant number of HOA-governed communities, particularly in the Traverse Mountain and newer western Lehi developments. ## Local Note

One pattern worth knowing in Lehi: many of the homes built during the rapid expansion of the mid-2000s through the 2010s used OSB (oriented strand board) sheathing rather than plywood on exterior walls and roof decks. OSB is more sensitive to moisture than plywood, it swells, delaminates, and holds water longer once it gets wet. In a mold remediation job where the exterior sheathing is involved, drying times and the decision about whether to remediate or replace the material can differ significantly from what a homeowner might expect based on older construction. We account for that when we scope the work.
