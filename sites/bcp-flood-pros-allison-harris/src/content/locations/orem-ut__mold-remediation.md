## Why Orem Properties See Mold Issues

The Wasatch Front's elevation and snowpack mean that spring thaw delivers a sustained moisture load to foundations and crawl spaces from roughly February through May. In Orem specifically, homes built in the 1960s through 1980s, a significant portion of the city's residential stock, often have pier-and-beam or shallow slab foundations with minimal vapor barriers. Crawl spaces in those homes can hold relative humidity well above 70% for weeks at a time, which is enough for mold colonies to establish within 24 to 48 hours of a water intrusion event.

Irrigation season adds another layer. Orem's pressurized irrigation system, which delivers secondary water for landscaping, runs from roughly April through October. Overspray, grade issues, and aging sprinkler heads near foundations are a consistent source of slow, chronic moisture intrusion, the kind that doesn't trigger an insurance claim but quietly feeds mold inside wall cavities and under flooring for months before anyone notices.

Homes near the base of the Wasatch foothills, where drainage naturally flows westward across the valley floor, can also experience elevated soil moisture after heavy snowmelt years, pushing humidity into basements and crawl spaces even without a plumbing failure.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Orem

Mold remediation is not the same as mold cleaning. Wiping visible growth with bleach addresses the surface but leaves the root structure, the mycelium, intact inside porous materials, and it does nothing about the moisture condition that allowed growth in the first place.

The process starts with a thorough assessment: moisture mapping with a calibrated meter, visual inspection of suspect cavities, and documentation of affected areas. Containment comes next, polyethylene barriers and negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers prevent spores from migrating to unaffected rooms during the work. Affected porous materials (drywall, insulation, wood framing with surface colonization) are removed and bagged for disposal. Non-porous surfaces are cleaned with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Structural wood with deeper penetration may be HEPA-vacuumed, sanded, and treated before encapsulation.

Drying and dehumidification run concurrently to bring the affected area to acceptable moisture levels before any reconstruction begins. Skipping that step, closing up a wall before the framing is genuinely dry, is how mold comes back.

## Orem Insurance & HOA Coordination

Whether mold remediation is covered depends on the cause of the moisture. Sudden and accidental water intrusion, a burst pipe, an appliance failure, is typically a covered peril under standard homeowners policies. Gradual leaks or deferred maintenance are usually excluded. FIX Restoration documents the loss thoroughly: moisture readings, photographs of affected materials, and a written scope of work that gives your adjuster what they need to process the claim.

If your property falls under an HOA, check your CC&Rs before work begins. Some Orem-area HOA developments require written notice before interior remediation work that involves structural material removal, and a small number require third-party clearance testing after remediation is complete. Knowing that requirement upfront prevents delays at closeout.

## Local Note

Orem's pressurized irrigation system is a genuine quality-of-life feature, but it creates a mold risk pattern that's easy to misread. Because secondary water doesn't show up on a water bill the way culinary water does, homeowners often don't notice that a sprinkler head has been soaking a foundation planter bed, or the sill plate behind it, for an entire irrigation season. If a mold assessment turns up unexplained moisture on an exterior wall between April and October, the irrigation system is one of the first things worth checking before assuming a plumbing failure.
