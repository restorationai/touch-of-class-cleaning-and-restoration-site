## Why Pleasant Grove Properties Face Particular Board-Up and Tarping Risks

The Wasatch Front climate creates a specific sequence of hazards that most other Utah communities don't see in the same combination. Winter inversions trap moisture at valley level, and when a warm front breaks through, rapid freeze-thaw cycles can exploit any gap in a compromised roof or broken window within hours. Homes in Pleasant Grove's older residential corridors, many built in the 1960s and 1970s with wood-frame construction and original single-pane windows, are especially vulnerable because those windows shatter rather than flex under impact, and the framing around them offers less resistance to wind-driven rain than modern construction.

Fire damage compounds the problem. A structure fire that appears contained to one room often leaves the attic or eave line open to the sky once firefighters ventilate the roof. Without a properly weighted and sealed tarp installed before the next weather event, smoke-damaged insulation becomes water-damaged insulation, and the remediation scope expands significantly.

## Our Emergency Board-Up and Tarping Process in Pleasant Grove

Every board-up and tarping job starts with a rapid exterior assessment, identifying every breach point, not just the obvious ones. A broken front window is easy to spot; a displaced ridge cap or a cracked soffit vent is not, and both can funnel water into wall cavities.

For window and door board-ups, we use exterior-grade plywood secured with structural screws rather than nails, which holds better against sustained wind load and is easier to remove cleanly when the rebuild begins. Panels are cut to overlap the rough opening and anchor into the surrounding framing, not just the trim.

Roof tarps are installed with batten boards across the peak and weighted edges rather than simply draped and stapled. On a Wasatch Front roof, a tarp that isn't mechanically secured will lift in the next canyon wind event, which can arrive with little warning in Pleasant Grove's geography. We size tarps to extend well past the damaged area so runoff channels away from the breach rather than pooling at its edges.
