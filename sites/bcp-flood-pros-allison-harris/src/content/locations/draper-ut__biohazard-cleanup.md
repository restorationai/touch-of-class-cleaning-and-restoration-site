## Why Draper Properties Present Unique Biohazard Considerations

Draper's growth pattern matters for biohazard remediation in ways that aren't immediately obvious. Homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s closer to the valley floor often have finished basements with carpet over concrete slab, a combination that makes thorough decontamination more involved than it looks on the surface. ## Our Biohazard Cleanup Process in Draper

Every job begins with a thorough assessment before any materials are touched. We identify the full extent of affected surfaces, including areas that may not be visible, and document conditions for insurance purposes. From there, the process follows a structured sequence: containment to prevent cross-contamination, removal of affected materials following regulated disposal protocols, treatment of all surfaces with EPA-registered disinfectants, and verification testing to confirm the space has been returned to a safe condition.

For blood cleanup, bodily fluid cleanup, and infectious material cleanup, proper personal protective equipment and containment are non-negotiable. Sharps and needle cleanup requires additional handling protocols to protect both our crew and anyone who enters the space afterward. We transport all biological waste to licensed disposal facilities in compliance with Utah Department of Environmental Quality requirements, not to municipal waste streams.

Throughout the process, we work quietly and without unnecessary foot traffic. Discretion is not an afterthought; it is built into how we schedule and staff these jobs.
