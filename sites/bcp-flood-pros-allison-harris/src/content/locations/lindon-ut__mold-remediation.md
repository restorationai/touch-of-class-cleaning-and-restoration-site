## Why Lindon Properties See Mold Issues

Lindon's position along the Wasatch Front means it shares Utah County's seasonal extremes: dry summers that crack foundation caulk and grout, followed by wet springs when snowmelt pushes groundwater close to slab edges. Homes built in the 1990s and early 2000s, which make up a significant portion of Lindon's housing stock, were often framed with OSB sheathing rather than plywood. OSB absorbs moisture more readily and holds it longer, which gives mold spores a reliable food source once any water intrusion occurs, whether from a slow roof leak, a failed window flashing, or a dripping supply line behind drywall.

Crawl spaces are a particular concern in this part of Utah County. Homes with vented crawl spaces can pull humid air in during spring thaw, and if the vapor barrier is torn, missing, or undersized, the subfloor framing above becomes a consistent mold habitat. Many homeowners in the area don't discover this until they're preparing to sell and a home inspector flags it.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Lindon

Remediation is not the same as cleaning. Wiping visible mold with bleach leaves the root structure, called hyphae, intact inside porous materials. The process starts with containment: sealing the affected area with polyethylene sheeting and establishing negative air pressure using HEPA-filtered air scrubbers so that disturbed spores can't migrate to clean rooms during work.

Once containment is in place, affected materials are assessed. Non-porous surfaces can be HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an EPA-registered antimicrobial agent. Porous materials like drywall, insulation, and OSB sheathing that have active mold growth typically need to be removed and bagged for disposal rather than treated in place. After removal, the structural framing is cleaned, treated, and dried to a moisture content that won't support regrowth. Final clearance involves a post-remediation inspection to confirm the area is ready for reconstruction.

Because Lindon's climate means mold is often tied to an ongoing moisture source, the remediation scope always includes identifying and documenting that source. Removing mold without addressing the water pathway that fed it is a short-term fix.

## Lindon Insurance Coordination

Mold remediation coverage varies significantly depending on how the loss is documented. Most homeowner policies in Utah cover mold remediation only when it results from a sudden and accidental covered peril, such as a burst pipe, rather than long-term seepage or maintenance neglect. The distinction matters, and the documentation your adjuster receives at the start of the claim often determines how that distinction gets applied.
