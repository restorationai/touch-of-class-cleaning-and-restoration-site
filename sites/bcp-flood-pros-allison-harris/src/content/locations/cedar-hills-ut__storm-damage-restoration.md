## Why Cedar Hills Properties See Storm Damage Differently

The geography here works against homeowners in a few specific ways. Cedar Hills sits at a higher elevation than much of Utah County, which means wind speeds during frontal storms are consistently stronger than what the valley records. That elevation also means heavier snowfall, and when a late-season storm drops wet, heavy snow on trees that have already leafed out, limb failures and full tree-overs are common. Roof decking, gutters, siding, and fence lines take the brunt of it.

The soil profile in this area is another factor. The bench soils along the Wasatch foothills drain quickly in some spots and pond badly in others, depending on how a lot was graded when the subdivision went in. After a heavy rain event, water that looks like it's running off a neighbor's yard can end up migrating toward a foundation or into a crawl space before a homeowner even notices the storm has passed. By the time the carpet feels soft or a musty smell develops, water has often been sitting under the subfloor for hours.

## Our Storm Damage Restoration Process in Cedar Hills

Every storm loss is different, but the sequence matters. When we arrive, the first priority is stopping ongoing intrusion: tarping compromised roofs, boarding openings left by fallen limbs or wind-driven debris, and identifying where water is still moving. We document everything with photos and written notes before a single piece of material is moved, because that documentation is what your insurance adjuster needs to process the claim accurately.

Once the structure is stabilized, we assess moisture using thermal imaging and moisture meters, not just visual inspection. Water behind stucco or inside wall cavities reads dry at the surface long after it has saturated the framing behind it. Drying equipment, air movers, and dehumidifiers go in based on what the readings actually show, not on a standard formula.

Debris removal follows a clear plan: tree limbs and roofing material come out first, then interior demolition of any materials that cannot be dried in place. We coordinate with your insurance carrier throughout, and we can work directly with most major carriers on billing so you are not managing two separate processes at once.
