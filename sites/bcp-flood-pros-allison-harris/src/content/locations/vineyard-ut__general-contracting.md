## Why Vineyard's Building Stock Creates Unique Renovation Demands

Most of Vineyard's residential neighborhoods were built during the 2000s and 2010s, which means homeowners are hitting the point where original finishes, fixtures, and mechanical systems are ready for a first major update. Kitchen and bathroom remodels in this market often reveal undersized ventilation, minimal insulation in interior walls, and plumbing rough-ins that weren't positioned with future flexibility in mind.

The proximity to Utah Lake also matters. Vineyard sits on lake-adjacent soil that can hold moisture longer than the drier benchland communities to the east.
