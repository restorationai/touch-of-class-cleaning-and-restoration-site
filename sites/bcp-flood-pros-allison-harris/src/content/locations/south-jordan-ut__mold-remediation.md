## Why South Jordan Properties See Mold Issues

Much of South Jordan's residential development happened in waves from the late 1990s through the 2010s, which means a large share of the housing stock uses OSB sheathing and paper-faced drywall, both of which absorb and hold moisture faster than older construction materials. Tract homes built quickly during those growth years sometimes have compressed framing cavities and minimal vapor barriers in crawl spaces, two conditions that let condensation accumulate behind walls without any visible sign at the surface.

The valley's clay-heavy soil compounds the problem. When irrigation season runs from spring through early fall, water that doesn't drain fast enough through compacted clay can wick upward along foundation walls. Finished basements and below-grade bonus rooms, common in South Jordan's larger floor plans, are particularly vulnerable to that slow, invisible intrusion. By the time a musty odor is noticeable in a bedroom or media room, mold has typically been active for weeks.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in South Jordan

Every remediation starts with a thorough inspection using moisture meters and thermal imaging, not just a visual walkthrough. Mold behind drywall reads dry at the surface while the paper backing and framing stay saturated, and skipping the instrument step means missing the actual boundary of the affected area.

Once the scope is mapped, the crew establishes containment using negative air pressure and heavy poly barriers to prevent spores from migrating into unaffected rooms during removal. Affected materials, drywall, insulation, and any compromised framing, are removed and bagged inside the containment zone. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously through the removal and treatment phases.

After physical removal, affected surfaces are treated and dried to measured moisture targets before any reconstruction begins. Closing a wall before the framing reaches an appropriate moisture content is one of the most common reasons mold returns within a year, and it's a step we don't shortcut regardless of schedule pressure.
