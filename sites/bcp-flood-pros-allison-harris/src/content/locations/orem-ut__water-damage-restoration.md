## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Orem

Every job starts with moisture mapping. Before any equipment is placed, technicians use thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters to trace where water has actually traveled, not just where it's visible. In Orem homes with finished basements, water behind drywall can read dry at the surface while the framing cavity behind it stays saturated. Finding those hidden pockets early prevents secondary damage and shortens total drying time.

Once the scope is documented, standing water is removed with truck-mounted extraction units. Structural drying follows using industrial air movers and refrigerant or desiccant dehumidifiers sized to the affected area. Readings are logged at each visit so the drying curve is trackable, both for your own peace of mind and for the insurance file. Materials that cannot be dried in place (saturated carpet pad, compromised drywall below the flood line) are removed and documented before disposal. The goal is a verified dry standard, not a visual one.
