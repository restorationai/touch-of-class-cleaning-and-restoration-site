## Why Riverton Properties Are Particularly Vulnerable to Water Intrusion

Riverton's elevation and position along the Jordan River corridor mean that seasonal runoff has somewhere to go, and that somewhere is often toward residential foundations. Much of the city's housing stock was built during the rapid growth of the 1990s and early 2000s, which means finished basements with engineered wood subfloors, laminate flooring, and framed interior walls that absorb standing water quickly. Unlike poured-slab construction common in warmer climates, these walk-out and daylight basements have multiple penetration points where water can enter: window wells, utility chases, and footer drains that back up when the ground is already saturated.

The clay-heavy soils throughout much of the Salt Lake Valley drain slowly after heavy precipitation, which means the pressure against foundation walls can persist for days after a storm passes. A portable sump pump running continuously for 48 to 72 hours is often the difference between a manageable cleanup and a structural drying project that stretches into weeks.

## Equipment We Rent for Flood and Drying Jobs in Riverton

The right equipment depends on what you are dealing with. A slow leak behind a washing machine calls for a different setup than a basement with several inches of standing water after a storm event.

For standing water, we rent submersible flood pumps capable of moving high volumes quickly, along with discharge hoses long enough to route water to a street drain or yard without damaging landscaping. Once the bulk water is out, the real drying work begins.

For structural drying, we stock:

- **Low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers** that pull significantly more moisture per day than consumer-grade units from a hardware store
- **High-velocity air movers** that create the airflow pattern needed to pull moisture out of wall cavities and subfloor assemblies, not just off the surface
- **Carpet drying fans** designed to work under lifted carpet and pad without requiring full removal in lower-saturation situations
- **Moisture meters and hygrometers** so you can track actual drying progress rather than guessing

Equipment is delivered clean, sanitized, and ready to run. We walk you through placement before we leave.

## The Drying Process: What to Expect When You Rent

Professional drying is not just running a dehumidifier in the middle of the room. The IICRC S500 standard, which guides how structural drying should be performed, calls for a specific relationship between airflow and dehumidification capacity based on the affected square footage and material types.

When you rent from FIX Restoration, a technician reviews the affected area with you, calculates the appropriate equipment load, and explains where each unit should be positioned. Air movers are typically placed at a low angle against walls to create a vortex effect that draws moisture out of wall cavities. Dehumidifiers are positioned to capture that moisture-laden air before it migrates to unaffected areas of the home.

We also advise on containment. In a finished Riverton basement, leaving doors open to the rest of the house during drying can raise the humidity throughout the entire structure, which slows the process and can create conditions that encourage mold growth in areas that were never wet.
