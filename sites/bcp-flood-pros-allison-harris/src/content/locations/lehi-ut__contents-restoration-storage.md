## Why Lehi Properties See Contents Damage the Way They Do

Lehi sits in a high-desert basin where temperature swings between seasons are dramatic. Winters push pipes toward freezing in crawl spaces and garage utility rooms, especially in the tract-built homes from the early 2000s that dominate much of the city's west side. Summers bring intense UV and occasional microburst storms that can drive water through window seals and roof penetrations faster than drainage systems handle it. That combination, freeze risk in winter, fast-moving storm water in summer, means contents damage here tends to cluster around two distinct events: slow, hidden leaks that go unnoticed for weeks, and sudden acute losses that soak an entire floor in hours.

Lehi's newer construction also features open-concept layouts with large great rooms. When smoke or water reaches one of those spaces, it affects a wide footprint of furniture, electronics, and textiles in a single event. Older homes near the city's historic core sometimes have plaster-and-lath interior walls that hold moisture longer than modern drywall, which can extend the window during which contents absorb odor and humidity if they aren't moved out promptly.

## Our Contents Restoration & Storage Process in Lehi

The process starts with a room-by-room inventory before anything is moved. Every item is photographed, condition-noted, and logged, a record that matters both for your insurance claim and for getting everything back to the right room when the job is done. Items that can be restored are separated from those that cannot; we don't make that call casually, because replacement costs often exceed cleaning costs for quality furniture and electronics.

Pack-out uses padded moving blankets and sealed crates to protect items during transport to our climate-controlled storage facility. Once there, textiles go through commercial laundering or dry-cleaning depending on fiber type. Hard surfaces, cabinetry, artwork frames, electronics housings, are cleaned with methods matched to the material. Smoke odor is addressed with ozone treatment or hydroxyl generation depending on what's stored alongside the affected items. Structural drying at the property can proceed without the added complication of working around furniture, which shortens the overall project timeline.

Return delivery is scheduled once the property is cleared for occupancy. Items go back to their documented locations, which matters more than it sounds, after a stressful loss, coming home to a room that looks like yours again is part of the recovery.
