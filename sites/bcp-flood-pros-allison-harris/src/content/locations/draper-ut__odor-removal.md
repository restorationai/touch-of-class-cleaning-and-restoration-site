## Why Draper Properties Experience Persistent Odors

Draper's climate is the starting point for understanding why odors linger. The low humidity that defines Utah's high desert means organic odor compounds dry into porous surfaces rather than evaporating. Smoke residue from a fireplace backup or kitchen fire bonds to drywall, carpet fiber, and ceiling texture in a way that feels permanent because, without the right treatment, it effectively is. Sewage and biohazard odors follow a similar pattern: the dry air pulls moisture out of the contaminated material quickly, concentrating the odor-causing compounds and locking them into the substrate.

Draper's newer construction also plays a role. Many homes built in the 2000s and 2010s use tight building envelopes designed for energy efficiency, which is great for utility bills but means that when an odor source enters the structure, there is very little natural air exchange to dilute it. Ductwork in these homes can circulate odor throughout every room within hours of a fire or sewage event.

## Our Odor Removal and Deodorization Process in Draper

Effective odor elimination is not a single step. The process starts with identifying every surface and cavity where odor compounds have migrated, which in a tightly sealed Draper home often includes wall cavities, attic insulation, and ductwork that a visual inspection alone will not reveal.

From there, the method depends on the odor source and the materials involved:

- **Thermal fogging** penetrates porous materials the same way smoke did, neutralizing odor compounds at the molecular level rather than masking them. It is particularly effective after fire and smoke events in Draper homes where residue has moved into wall cavities.
- **Hydroxyl deodorization** uses UV-generated hydroxyl radicals to break down odor molecules in the air and on surfaces. Unlike ozone, hydroxyl generators can run while occupants and pets are present in adjacent spaces, which matters for families managing a partial displacement.
- **Ozone treatment** is reserved for unoccupied spaces where a high-concentration application is appropriate. In Draper's dry climate, ozone disperses efficiently through a structure, making it effective for whole-home smoke odor removal when the property is vacant.
- **Source removal and content cleaning** precede any atmospheric treatment. No deodorization method works long-term if the contaminated material is still in place.
