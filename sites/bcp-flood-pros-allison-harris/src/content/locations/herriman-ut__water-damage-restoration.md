When water finds its way into a slab, a finished basement, or a laundry room on the upper floor of a two-story townhome, it moves fast and hides well. ## Why Herriman Properties Are Prone to Water Intrusion

The soils across much of Herriman's newer development corridors are expansive clays, the kind that swell when wet and shrink when dry. That cycle creates micro-gaps around foundation walls and slab penetrations, and those gaps become pathways for groundwater after a heavy storm or a sustained snowmelt event. The Wasatch Front's freeze-thaw cycle compounds the problem: water that seeps into a crack in late fall freezes, expands, and widens the crack before spring even arrives.

Herriman's housing stock skews newer, which means most homes have PEX or CPVC supply lines rather than copper. Those materials are generally reliable, but pressure spikes from the local water system, combined with improper installation in fast-built tracts, can cause pinhole failures or fitting separations inside walls. Because the walls are typically framed with OSB sheathing rather than older board sheathing, a slow leak behind drywall can saturate the OSB and begin supporting mold colonization within 48 to 72 hours of the initial failure.

## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Herriman

The first step on any job is source control. If a supply line is still running, we help you locate the shutoff and confirm the water is stopped before any drying equipment goes in. From there, the process follows a clear sequence.

**Moisture mapping.** We use thermal imaging cameras and calibrated moisture meters to trace exactly how far water has traveled behind walls, under flooring, and into subfloor assemblies. In Herriman's two-story floor plans, water from an upstairs bathroom can travel along floor joists and appear in a first-floor ceiling well away from the original failure point.

**Water extraction.** Standing water is removed with truck-mounted and portable extraction units. In finished basements, which are common in Herriman's single-family homes, we pay close attention to carpet pad and engineered hardwood, both of which hold water invisibly at the surface.

**Structural drying.** Industrial air movers and refrigerant dehumidifiers are placed according to a drying plan calibrated to the square footage, material types, and ambient conditions. Utah's low relative humidity is an asset here, but it does not eliminate the need for equipment. Drying is monitored with daily moisture readings until affected materials reach their dry standard.

**Documentation.** Every moisture reading, photograph, and equipment placement is logged. That documentation goes to your insurance adjuster and becomes the record of scope if any dispute arises.

## Herriman Insurance Coordination

Most standard homeowner policies in Utah cover sudden and accidental water damage, which includes burst pipes, appliance failures, and overflow events. Gradual leaks and maintenance-related failures are typically excluded. When you call us, we document the loss in a format that insurance adjusters recognize: moisture logs, photos with timestamps, equipment placement records, and a written scope of affected materials.

We bill major carriers directly and work with your adjuster through the claims process. If your HOA has a master policy that overlaps with your individual unit policy, we can help clarify which scope falls under which coverage before work begins.

## Local Note

Herriman's newer subdivisions frequently have shared utility easements running through rear yards and between attached garage walls. In townhome and paired-home configurations, a water loss in one unit can migrate through the shared wall cavity into the adjacent unit before either homeowner notices. If you are in an attached home and suspect water damage, it is worth notifying your neighbor early, because the drying scope on your side may not be complete if the shared wall is still wet from their side. We flag this at the moisture-mapping stage and communicate with both parties when the evidence points to shared-wall involvement.
