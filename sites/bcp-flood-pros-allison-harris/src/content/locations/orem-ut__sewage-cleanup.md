## Why Orem Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Orem sits in a stretch of Utah Valley where the water table can rise noticeably during spring snowmelt off the Wasatch Range. That seasonal pressure doesn't just affect surface drainage, it can force groundwater and sewer gas back through floor drains and low-lying fixtures, particularly in homes with older clay-tile lateral lines that have shifted or cracked over decades of freeze-thaw cycling. Many of Orem's established residential streets were developed in the 1950s through 1970s, and the sewer laterals installed then were never designed to handle the loads of expanded households or the soil movement that comes with Utah's temperature swings.

Municipal sewer main blockages are another common trigger. When the main line serving a block surcharges during a heavy storm event, the backflow has to go somewhere, and it typically finds the lowest drain in the nearest structure. Finished basements, which are common throughout Orem, are especially vulnerable because they sit below the street-level sewer elevation.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Orem

The first priority on any sewage call is stopping the source. If the backup is from a municipal line, that means coordinating with Orem City Public Works to confirm the main is clear before any interior work begins, otherwise cleaned areas can re-contaminate. Once the source is controlled, the process moves through defined stages:

**Containment and PPE setup**, Affected areas are isolated to prevent cross-contamination to unaffected rooms. Sewage is classified as Category 3 water (grossly contaminated), which means full personal protective equipment for every crew member and strict decontamination protocols before leaving the site.

**Extraction and removal**, Standing sewage and saturated porous materials (carpet, pad, drywall below the flood line, insulation) are removed. In Orem's older ranch-style homes, this often includes pulling up vinyl or linoleum flooring that has trapped contaminated water beneath it, a layer that looks dry at the surface but reads saturated on a moisture meter.

**Antimicrobial treatment**, All affected structural surfaces are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants. Concrete block foundations and unfinished crawl spaces, common in Orem's mid-century construction, require multiple applications because the porous surface holds pathogens differently than poured concrete.

**Drying and verification**, Commercial drying equipment runs until moisture readings confirm the structure is back to normal levels. Documentation is kept throughout for insurance purposes.

## Local Note

Orem has a significant share of homes built on concrete slab foundations, particularly in subdivisions developed during the 1960s and 1970s. When a sewer line fails beneath a slab, the sewage can migrate through the slab itself and wick upward into flooring adhesives and subfloor materials before anyone notices a problem at the surface. In these situations, thermal imaging and moisture mapping across the full slab footprint, not just the visibly affected area, is the only way to find the full extent of contamination. Skipping that step and cleaning only what's visible is how a sewage loss turns into a mold problem three weeks later.
