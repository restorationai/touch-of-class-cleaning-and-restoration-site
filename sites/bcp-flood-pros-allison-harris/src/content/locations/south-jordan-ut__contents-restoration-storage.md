## Why South Jordan Properties See Contents Losses

The Wasatch Front's climate puts South Jordan homes through a real stress cycle. Winters bring hard freezes that can catch supply lines in garages and crawl spaces off guard, especially in homes where the builder tucked plumbing close to an exterior wall to save interior square footage. Summers swing to the opposite extreme, with low humidity that causes wood furniture and cabinetine to dry out and check, which matters when you're deciding whether a piece is worth restoring or has already been compromised before the loss event.

South Jordan also has a significant share of newer construction, homes built in the last fifteen to twenty years with open floor plans and engineered wood flooring. Engineered wood responds to water differently than solid hardwood: it can delaminate within hours of saturation, which means the furniture and contents sitting on it are often in contact with moisture far longer than the homeowner realizes. By the time a loss is discovered, upholstered pieces have already begun to wick water from the subfloor.

## Our Contents Restoration and Storage Process in South Jordan

The first step on any contents job is a thorough inventory. Every item in the affected area is photographed, catalogued, and assessed before anything is moved. That documentation matters for your insurance claim and for your own peace of mind when items come back from our climate-controlled storage facility.

From there, items are sorted into three categories: restore, clean-and-return, or total loss. Textiles go through specialized laundering or dry-cleaning depending on fiber content and the type of contamination. Hard surfaces, furniture frames, and decorative items are cleaned using methods matched to the material, whether that is ultrasonic cleaning for small valuables and electronics components, or foam and upholstery cleaning for sofas and chairs. Documents and photographs get priority handling because paper is among the most time-sensitive materials in any loss.

While your contents are in storage, you have access to an itemized list so you know exactly where everything is. Nothing goes into a warehouse and disappears. When your home is ready, items are returned, placed, and inventoried out.

## South Jordan Insurance Coordination

Contents claims are often the most disputed part of a property loss because insurers and policyholders frequently disagree on whether an item is restorable or a total loss. The documentation we generate from the initial inventory, including photographs, condition notes, and restoration estimates, is formatted to support your adjuster's review process. We work with most major carriers and can communicate directly with your claims contact to reduce the back-and-forth that slows settlements down.

If your policy includes additional living expense coverage, your adjuster may also need documentation of storage costs during displacement. We provide that as part of our standard close-out package.
