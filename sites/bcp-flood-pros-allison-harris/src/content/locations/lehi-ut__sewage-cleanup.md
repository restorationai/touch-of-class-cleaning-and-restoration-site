## Why Lehi Properties See Sewage Backup Issues

Lehi sits in a zone where two very different infrastructure realities collide. Older neighborhoods closer to historic Main Street were built on sewer laterals that have been in the ground for decades, clay or Orangeburg pipe that cracks, roots into, and collapses with little warning. Meanwhile, the master-planned communities that have filled in around Thanksgiving Point and along Traverse Ridge were built quickly to meet demand, and some early-2000s subdivisions are now hitting the age where grease accumulation, root intrusion, and shifting Utah County soils start causing first-time backups.

Lehi also sits at the base of the Wasatch foothills, where spring snowmelt and occasional heavy monsoon-pattern storms in July and August can overwhelm municipal sewer capacity. When the city's main lines surcharge during a heavy rain event, the pressure reverses and finds the path of least resistance, usually a floor drain or a basement toilet. That's not a plumbing failure you can fix with a snake; it requires full sewage extraction, surface decontamination, and structural drying before the space is safe to occupy.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Lehi

Sewage is classified as Category 3 water, the most contaminated classification in the industry, and the cleanup process reflects that. The first step on any job is containment: isolating the affected area to prevent cross-contamination into clean living spaces, particularly important in Lehi's open-concept floor plans where finished basements flow directly into main living areas.

Once contained, the extraction phase removes all standing water and saturated material. Drywall, insulation, and flooring that has absorbed sewage contamination typically cannot be dried in place, it has to come out. After structural demo, every affected surface is treated with an EPA-registered disinfectant formulated for Category 3 losses. We then deploy commercial drying equipment, desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers, calibrated to Utah's semi-arid climate, where low ambient humidity can actually accelerate drying timelines compared to coastal markets, but also masks moisture that's still present inside wall cavities and subfloor assemblies. Moisture mapping with thermal imaging and pin meters guides every drying decision.

## Lehi Insurance Coordination for Sewage Losses

Sewage backup claims have a specific wrinkle that catches Lehi homeowners off guard: standard homeowners policies often exclude sewer backup unless you've added a separate rider.
