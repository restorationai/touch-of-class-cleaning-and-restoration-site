## Why Lindon Properties See Water Damage Differently

## Our Emergency Water Cleanup Process in Lindon

When FIX Restoration arrives at a Lindon property, the first step is a full moisture mapping of the affected area using thermal imaging and calibrated moisture meters, not a visual scan. Water follows the path of least resistance, and in Lindon's bench-grade homes that often means it has traveled further than the obvious wet spot suggests.

From there, the process moves through extraction, structural drying, and monitoring:

- **Extraction**: Truck-mounted and portable extraction units pull standing water from carpet, hardwood, and concrete. Speed here directly limits how far saturation spreads.
- **Drying equipment placement**: Industrial air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers are positioned based on the moisture map, not just placed near the obvious damage.
- **Daily monitoring**: Moisture readings are logged at each visit so drying progress is documented, which matters for insurance claims and for confirming the structure is genuinely dry before any rebuild begins.
- **Content protection**: Furniture, cabinetry, and personal items in the affected zone are moved or blocked to prevent secondary damage during the drying period.
