## Why Riverton Roofs Wear Out Faster Than the Warranty Suggests

The Wasatch Front climate is harder on roofing materials than most manufacturers' warranty language anticipates. Summer temperatures in Riverton regularly push attic spaces well past 150°F, accelerating asphalt shingle granule loss from the top down. Then winter arrives with snow loads that can linger for weeks on lower-slope sections, and the melt-refreeze cycle at the eave line forces water under shingles that were never designed to sit beneath standing ice. Add the alkaline soil and the occasional high-wind event that funnels through the Jordan Narrows corridor to the west, and you have a combination that shortens roof life faster than the regional average.

Homes built during Riverton's rapid growth periods often have roof decks that were installed quickly and may show early signs of sheathing deterioration beneath a surface that still looks intact from the street. When we inspect a roof here, we probe the deck, not just the surface layer.

## Our Roofing Installation and Replacement Process in Riverton

Every project begins with a full inspection that includes the deck, flashing, ridge, valleys, and penetrations. We document existing conditions with photographs before any material is removed, which matters both for your records and for any insurance coordination.

Once the old roofing is stripped, we inspect the sheathing for soft spots, delamination, or rot that is common in areas where ice dams have forced water beneath the surface over multiple seasons. Damaged decking is replaced before anything goes on top of it. We then install an ice-and-water shield along the eave line and in the valleys, a step that is not optional in a climate where Riverton regularly sees hard freezes from November through March. Synthetic underlayment goes over the field, followed by the finish material, whether that is architectural shingles, metal roofing, or another system suited to the slope and exposure of your specific home.

Flashing at chimneys, skylights, and wall transitions is sealed with materials rated for the temperature range this area sees. A finished roof that leaks at a flashing joint is not a finished roof.

## Local Note

Riverton's bench areas and the neighborhoods that step up toward the foothills tend to have steeper roof pitches than the flatter subdivisions closer to the valley floor. Steeper pitches shed snow more readily but create a different set of installation challenges, including safety rigging requirements and the need for experienced crews comfortable working on high-slope surfaces. They also concentrate runoff at the eave line more aggressively, which means gutter sizing and downspout placement deserve attention at the same time a roof is being replaced. It is a detail that is easy to overlook when the focus is on the roofing material itself, but getting it wrong means the new roof sheds water onto inadequate drainage and the cycle of damage starts again.
