## Our Board-Up and Tarping Process in Provo

The first thing we assess on arrival is the structural integrity of what we are anchoring to. Screwing plywood into a compromised header or nailing a tarp to rotted fascia creates a false sense of security that fails the next time the wind picks up. We inspect the framing around each breach point before any material goes up.

For window and door openings, we cut OSB or plywood to fit flush against the exterior framing, fasten it with structural screws rather than nails, and seal the perimeter to limit air and moisture infiltration. For roof damage, we use reinforced polyethylene tarps rated for UV and puncture resistance, secured with weighted battens and anchor lines rather than staples alone, a method that holds through the pressure differentials that canyon drafts create along the east bench.

We document every opening, every material used, and every fastener point with photographs before we leave. That documentation goes directly to your insurance adjuster and becomes part of the claim file.
