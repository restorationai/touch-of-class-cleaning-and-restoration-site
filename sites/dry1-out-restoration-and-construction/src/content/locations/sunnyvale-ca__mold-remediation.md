Mold remediation in Sunnyvale looks different depending on which decade your house was built. A slab-foundation Eichler in Cherry Chase with a flat roof and exposed-beam ceiling traps moisture in ways a 1990s tract home near Lakewood Village never will. If you're smelling that musty, slightly sweet odor in a hallway closet or seeing dark speckling creep along a bathroom ceiling, the growth pattern and the removal plan both depend on how the structure was built.

## Why Sunnyvale Properties See Mold Issues

Sunnyvale's marine-layer fog rolls in most mornings for much of the year, and it settles into low-slope and flat roofs, the kind that dominate the Eichler-style homes scattered through Cherry Chase, Ponderosa Park, and parts of the Heritage District. Those roofs were built with minimal attic ventilation by design, which was fine for the climate assumptions of the 1950s and 1960s but leaves little room for moisture to escape once a roof membrane starts to fail. Add in the radiant slab heating common to these homes, and a slow slab leak can wick moisture upward into drywall and baseboards for months before anyone notices a problem. Newer condos and townhomes near Murphy Avenue have a different issue: tighter building envelopes and less natural airflow mean a single unventilated bathroom or an undersized exhaust fan can push humidity past the point where mold colonizes within 48 to 72 hours of a hidden leak.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Sunnyvale

We start every job with a visual and moisture-mapping inspection, checking slab perimeters, attic decking, and any crawlspace access points separately, since Eichler-era homes often have no true crawlspace and the moisture story is told entirely at the slab edge. Once we've identified the source and the extent of colonization, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into HVAC returns or adjoining rooms. Removal of affected materials follows IICRC S520 guidance, with HEPA-filtered vacuuming and air scrubbing running throughout demolition and cleanup. Before we close out containment, we verify the space is within acceptable parameters using surface and air sampling protocols, then hand off to our reconstruction side for drywall, flooring, or cabinetry replacement if that's part of the scope.

## Sunnyvale Insurance & HOA Coordination

Many of the condo and townhome complexes built since the early 2000s, including several clusters near Lakewood Village, operate under HOAs that require documentation before common-wall or shared-roof mold work can proceed, particularly when the source is a shared plumbing stack or roof penetration. We photograph and log moisture readings at each stage so that documentation is ready whether it's going to your HOA board, your insurance adjuster, or both. Coverage for mold itself is often limited unless it's tied to a sudden, covered water loss, so we're upfront early about what's likely reimbursable versus what falls to the homeowner, rather than letting that conversation happen after the work is done.

One thing we watch for specifically in Sunnyvale's older Eichler tracts: the original built-in planter boxes some of these homes have along interior walls, a mid-century design feature, were often never fully waterproofed from the slab below.
