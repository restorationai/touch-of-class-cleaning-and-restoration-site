## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Harmony Grove

We start by identifying the water category and source, because a slab leak, a roof intrusion, and a septic backup all call for different extraction and drying plans. On slab-foundation homes we often pull baseboards and drill small weep holes rather than tearing out full sections of wall, which keeps repair costs down while still letting trapped moisture escape. Drying is set up and monitored per the IICRC S500 standard, with moisture readings taken at the slab, framing, and any affected cabinetry, since Harmony Grove's clay soil can keep ground-level moisture elevated longer than homes on sandier lots closer to the coast. For properties with crawlspaces or raised foundations, more typical on the older lots tucked into the hillsides, we also check for ventilation issues that can slow drying times even after the visible water is gone.
