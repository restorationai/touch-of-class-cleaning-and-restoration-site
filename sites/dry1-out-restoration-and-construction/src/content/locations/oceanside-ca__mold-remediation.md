## Our Mold Remediation Process in Oceanside

We start with a moisture assessment, checking not just where mold is visible but where humidity readings suggest it's about to become visible. Containment goes up next, typically negative-air barriers sealing off the affected room so spores don't migrate into the HVAC system or adjacent living space. From there it's physical removal of affected materials (drywall, insulation, sometimes subflooring depending on how far moisture traveled), HEPA vacuuming of surrounding surfaces, and antimicrobial treatment. Drying follows the IICRC S520 mold remediation standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space and monitored daily until moisture content reads normal. We photograph and document every stage for your insurance file before anything gets closed back up.
