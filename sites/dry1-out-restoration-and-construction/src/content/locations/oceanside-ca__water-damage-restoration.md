Oceanside's housing stock splits roughly into two eras, and each has its own failure point. ## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Oceanside

We start with moisture mapping, using thermal imaging and penetrating meters to find out exactly how far water has traveled past the visible wet spot, which matters most in slab homes where the leak source and the damage point are rarely in the same room. From there we extract standing water, remove anything saturated beyond drying (carpet pad, baseboard, sometimes a section of drywall), and set air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the structure rather than the room. Drying is tracked daily against the IICRC S500 standard, not by a fixed calendar, because a 1960s bungalow with plaster walls and a 2005 stucco build dry at different rates even with identical equipment running.
