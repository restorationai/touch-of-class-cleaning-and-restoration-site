## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Oakland

We start every job with moisture mapping, not a visual walkthrough, because plaster-and-lath walls common in pre-1950 Oakland construction can look dry on the surface while the lath behind them is still saturated. Extraction comes first, pulling standing water from floors and crawlspaces before it migrates further into subfloor or framing. From there we set up structural drying per the IICRC S500 standard, which in older homes usually means more air movers and longer drying cycles than a newer stucco-and-drywall build would need, since plaster releases trapped moisture slowly. We track readings daily and don't close out drying until the materials actually hit dry standard, not just until the carpet feels dry to the touch.
