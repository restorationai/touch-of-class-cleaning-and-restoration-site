## Why Temecula Properties See Sewage Backups

Much of Temecula sits on expansive clay soil that swells and contracts with the region's wet winters and dry summers. That seasonal movement puts steady pressure on PVC sewer laterals running under slab foundations, which is how most homes in Redhawk, Paloma del Sol, Harveston, and Temeku Hills were built. Over fifteen to thirty years, that pressure shows up as cracked joints, bellied pipe sections, and root intrusion where landscaping trees were planted close to the line. Outside the city's core sewer service area, particularly in parts of Wine Country, properties still run on septic systems, and a saturated or failing leach field can push effluent back into the house instead of the yard. Either way, the result is the same: wastewater inside living space that needs to be removed, not just mopped up.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Temecula

We start by identifying the source, because a slab leak behaves differently from a septic backup and the containment plan changes accordingly. Category 3 water (sewage) requires removal of porous materials it has contacted, carpet, pad, baseboards, and often the bottom few inches of drywall, since these materials can't be sanitized back to a safe condition. Once the affected materials are out, we extract standing wastewater, apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to framing and subfloor, and set up negative air pressure to keep contaminated air from migrating into the rest of the house. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard for structural drying, with daily moisture readings until the subfloor and framing read dry, not just the surface. In homes with slab foundations, that often means monitoring longer than a typical water loss because moisture trapped under a slab vapor barrier takes time to release.

## Temecula HOA and Insurance Coordination

Most of the communities we work in around Temecula, Redhawk, Harveston, Temeku Hills, and Paloma del Sol, are governed by active HOAs with architectural committees that want documentation before any demolition or drywall replacement begins.
