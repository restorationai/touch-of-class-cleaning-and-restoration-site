## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Sunnyvale

We start with board-up and a structural walk-through, checking exposed beams and roof decking for char depth before deciding what's salvageable versus what needs replacement. Soot removal on open-grain wood common to Eichler and ranch-style homes requires dry chemical sponging before any wet cleaning, since water-based cleaners can drive soot deeper into unsealed wood. ## Equipment & Methods We Use for Fire Damage

Structural drying after firefighting water follows the IICRC S500 standard, and our FSRT-trained techs handle soot classification first: wet smoke from a slow smoldering fire behaves differently on surfaces than the dry, powdery soot from a fast-burning fire, and the cleaning agent has to match.
