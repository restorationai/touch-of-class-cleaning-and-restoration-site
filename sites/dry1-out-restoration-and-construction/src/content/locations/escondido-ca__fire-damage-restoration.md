## Why Escondido Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Escondido sits far enough inland that Santa Ana wind conditions in late summer and fall bring low humidity and high heat, the same pattern that drives wildland fire risk along the edges of San Pasqual Valley and the hillside lots near Felicita. Away from the wildland interface, the risk looks different: homes in the Old Escondido Historic District were largely built in the early 1900s through the 1930s, with original knob-and-tube or early cloth-wrapped wiring still live behind plaster walls in some properties. That combination, older electrical systems plus decades of dry inland heat on aging insulation, is a common source of structural fires that start inside a wall cavity rather than in a kitchen or garage.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Escondido

We start with emergency board-up and roof tarping to stop weather and secondary damage, then move into soot and residue assessment that's calibrated to the materials underneath. Lime plaster, common in the historic district's older homes, holds soot differently than modern drywall; dry-sponge cleaning has to come before any wet treatment or the residue gets driven deeper into the surface instead of lifting off it. From there we clear structural char, run air scrubbers and ozone or hydroxyl treatment for smoke odor, and document every affected material and assembly for the insurance claim before reconstruction framing begins.

## Escondido Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most fire claims in Escondido move through the same carrier process we see across San Diego County: photo and moisture documentation, a scope of loss, and direct billing to the adjuster where the carrier allows it. Properties inside the Old Escondido Historic District carry one added layer: exterior repairs or replacement of visible siding, trim, or roofing material may need to match the home's historic character before the city signs off, so we flag those properties early and coordinate material selection with the homeowner and their adjuster rather than defaulting to standard modern materials.
