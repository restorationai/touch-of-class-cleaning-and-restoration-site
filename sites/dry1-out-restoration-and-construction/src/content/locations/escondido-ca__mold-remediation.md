## Why Escondido Properties See Mold Issues

Escondido's inland location means less marine layer than coastal San Diego but sharper overnight humidity swings, especially near irrigated groves in San Pasqual Valley and along the creek bottoms that feed into the valley floor. Older homes in the Old Escondido Historic District and the blocks around Downtown Grand Avenue were often built with crawlspace foundations and minimal vapor barriers, which trap ground moisture against subfloor framing. ## Our Mold Remediation Process in Escondido

We start with a moisture map of the affected area, using a combination of surface moisture meters and, where the damage is hidden behind drywall or under flooring, a thermal camera to find the full extent of the saturation before we cut into anything. From there, our IICRC AMRT-trained technicians set up physical containment, typically poly sheeting and negative air pressure, to keep spores from migrating into unaffected rooms while we remove and bag contaminated material. Hard surfaces get HEPA-vacuumed and treated with an antimicrobial, and we run air scrubbers through the containment zone until readings come back clean. For older Old Escondido homes with plaster or lath construction, drying takes longer than it would in a modern drywall room because plaster releases moisture slowly and unevenly, so we extend monitoring rather than pulling equipment on a fixed schedule.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Mold

Containment and negative air pressure do the heavy lifting on any job where we're removing mold-affected material, but the equipment mix changes by substrate. Crawlspace work under a 1940s Old Escondido home calls for low-profile air movers and a dehumidifier sized for a vented, dirt-floor space, while a stucco-sealed home near Felicita often needs a more aggressive dehumidification setup because the structure itself is holding humidity in. We document moisture content before and after with data logs, which also becomes the paperwork your insurance adjuster or HOA will want to see before a remediation file gets closed out.

## Escondido Insurance & HOA Coordination

Most Escondido-area homeowner policies draw a hard line between mold caused by a sudden, covered water event, like a burst supply line, and mold attributed to long-term neglect or ongoing moisture, which is often excluded.
