## Our Mold Remediation Process in Santa Cruz

We start with a moisture map of the affected area, using thermal imaging and a hygrometer to find where the source is still active versus where it's residual staining. Containment goes up next, typically negative-air chambers with HEPA filtration sealed off from living space, which matters more in the tighter floor plans found near UC Santa Cruz rental housing where HVAC returns often sit close to bedrooms. Removal of affected material follows IICRC S520 guidelines: porous materials like drywall and insulation are generally discarded, while structural framing is treated and dried in place when the growth hasn't compromised it. We finish with a HEPA vacuum pass and, when a homeowner or HOA requires it, we coordinate post-remediation air sampling with a third-party hygienist before signing off.
