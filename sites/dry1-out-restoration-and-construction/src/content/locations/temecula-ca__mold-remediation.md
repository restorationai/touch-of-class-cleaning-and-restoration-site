## Why Temecula Properties See Mold Issues

Temecula's climate looks dry on paper, triple-digit summer afternoons and cool, arid nights, but the valley sits in a pocket where Wine Country irrigation, residential landscaping, and seasonal fog off the Santa Margarita watershed keep humidity levels higher than the desert reputation suggests. Homes in Redhawk and Paloma del Sol, built mostly from the late 1980s through the early 2000s, use stucco exteriors and tile roofing systems that trap condensation in attic spaces when HVAC units cycle hard during summer. Older properties closer to Old Town Temecula sit nearer Murrieta Creek's floodplain, where ground moisture migrates into crawlspaces and slab foundations more readily. Slow leaks under slab, common in homes with original plumbing from that era, are one of the most frequent mold triggers we trace back during inspections in this area.

## Our Mold Remediation Process in Temecula

We start with source identification, because treating visible mold without stopping the moisture feeding it just buys a few months before it returns. Once the source is confirmed, whether that's a roof flashing failure, an HVAC condensation line, or a slab leak, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the rest of the home through the HVAC system. HEPA air scrubbers run continuously while affected materials, drywall, insulation, sometimes subfloor, are removed and bagged per California disposal guidelines. Surfaces are then treated with antimicrobial solution and dried using the same structural drying principles our IICRC ASD technicians apply on water jobs, since mold remediation only holds if the underlying material is brought back to a stable moisture content, not just visually dry.

HOA communities like Redhawk, Temeku Hills, and parts of Harveston have architectural review requirements that can affect exterior containment setups, trailer or equipment staging, and any work that touches shared walls or common-area irrigation lines.
