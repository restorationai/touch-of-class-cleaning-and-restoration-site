## Our Emergency Water Removal & Cleanup Process in San Diego

We start by identifying the water category, clean, gray, or black, because that determines how we handle extraction and what materials can be saved versus removed. Submersible pumps and truck-mount extractors pull standing water first, then we move to moisture mapping with penetrating meters to find out how far the water traveled under flooring or into wall cavities. In older Hillcrest and North Park homes with plaster or lath-and-plaster walls, that mapping step matters more: plaster holds moisture longer than drywall and can read deceptively dry at the surface while still saturated underneath. Drying follows the IICRC S500 standard, with air movers and dehumidifiers placed based on the moisture readings rather than a one-size layout, and we recheck daily until materials hit dry standard.
