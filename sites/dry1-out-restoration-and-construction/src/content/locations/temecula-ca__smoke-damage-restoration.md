## Our Smoke Damage Restoration Process in Temecula

We start with a walkthrough to identify whether the residue is wet smoke (slow-burning, oily, harder to remove) or dry smoke (fast-burning, powdery, easier to wipe but quick to spread into HVAC ductwork). From there we isolate the affected rooms, run HEPA air scrubbers to pull particulate out of circulation, and hand-clean or media-blast hard surfaces depending on the material. Soft goods, cabinetry, and upholstery get treated on-site when possible; items with deep odor penetration go to our contents facility for ultrasonic cleaning. Thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment follows to neutralize odor at the molecular level rather than masking it, and we test for odor return before we call the job closed.

## Equipment & Methods We Use for Smoke Residue Cleanup

Wildfire ash behaves differently than structure-fire soot. It's finer, it travels further into soffits and attic spaces, and it tends to settle on exterior surfaces like patio furniture and pool equipment as much as inside the home. We use HEPA-filtered vacuums and air scrubbers for the fine particulate, dry chemical sponges for surfaces where wet cleaning would smear residue, and ozone or hydroxyl generators for odor neutralization once visible soot is removed. For HVAC systems pulling smoke smell through the whole house, we inspect and clean the ductwork and change or treat the filtration before calling the air quality resolved.
