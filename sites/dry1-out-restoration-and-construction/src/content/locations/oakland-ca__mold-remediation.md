## Our Mold Remediation Process in Oakland

We start with a moisture and visual inspection to map where the colony is active and where it's likely spreading unseen, which matters more in plaster-and-lath construction than in newer drywall homes. Once we know the scope, we isolate the work area with physical containment and negative air pressure so spores don't migrate into the rest of the house while materials come out. Affected drywall, insulation, or plaster gets removed and bagged for disposal, exposed framing is cleaned and treated, and HEPA air scrubbers run through the containment zone to pull airborne spores out of circulation. Our technicians hold IICRC AMRT training specific to mold remediation, and we finish with a final moisture check before anything gets closed back up, because sealing damp framing behind new drywall just restarts the problem in a different spot.

## Oakland Insurance & HOA Coordination

Condo buildings near Jack London Square and the Grand Lake waterfront often carry HOA bylaws that specify who's responsible for mold originating inside a unit versus mold tied to a building envelope or shared plumbing issue, so we document moisture readings and photos in a way that holds up whether the claim goes through a homeowner's policy or the association's master policy. Most carriers want to see the source of moisture identified and corrected, not just the mold removed, since reappearance after a closed claim tends to raise questions. We put together that documentation as part of the job rather than as an afterthought, which keeps the claims conversation moving instead of stalling on missing paperwork.
