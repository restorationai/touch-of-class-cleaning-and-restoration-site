## Why Oakland Properties See Fire Damage Issues

Oakland's fire risk isn't uniform across the city, and that shapes how a loss plays out. In the hill neighborhoods around Montclair, wind-driven wildfire and ember intrusion remain a real concern given the terrain and vegetation that made the 1991 Oakland Hills fire so destructive; homes here often need exterior assessment for ember entry points around eaves, vents, and deck undersides even when the structural fire was contained quickly. Closer to the flats, in neighborhoods like Rockridge and Temescal, the culprit is more often aging electrical: knob-and-tube wiring, undersized panels, and decades-old PG&E service drops feeding houses that were never wired for modern appliance loads. Jack London Square's converted warehouses and live-work lofts introduce a different problem entirely: automatic sprinkler suppression that stops a fire fast but leaves behind a combined fire-and-water loss that needs both smoke remediation and structural drying worked together, not back to back.

## Our Fire Damage Restoration Process in Oakland

We start with a structural safety check, because a lot of Oakland's older housing stock, especially single-wall construction found in parts of Fruitvale and the flats near Lake Merritt, behaves differently under heat than modern framing. Charred studs can look stable and still be compromised. From there we document the loss for your carrier, board up or tarp exposed openings, and begin HEPA-filtered soot and smoke removal before porous materials like plaster, lath, and old hardwood finish absorb odor permanently. Smoke restoration on pre-WWII materials takes more patience than drywall jobs: plaster holds odor compounds longer, and thermal fogging or hydroxyl treatment often needs multiple passes to fully neutralize it. Once materials test clean, we move into rebuild coordination for anything beyond cosmetic repair.

We document room-by-room with photos and moisture or soot readings so your adjuster has what they need without a second site visit. ## Equipment & Methods We Use for Fire Damage

Soot removal starts with dry sponging and HEPA vacuuming on painted surfaces and upholstery before any wet cleaning, since introducing moisture too early can smear soot deeper into the material. For odor, we use thermal fogging and hydroxyl generators rather than masking agents, targeting the actual smoke particles trapped in drywall, insulation, and HVAC ductwork. Where fire and sprinkler water overlap, as it often does in converted Jack London Square spaces, we run structural drying equipment alongside odor treatment so one process doesn't undo the other.
