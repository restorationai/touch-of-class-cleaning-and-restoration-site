## Why Concord Sees Sewage Backups and Overflows

A lot of Concord's housing stock west of Ygnacio Valley Road went in during the 1950s and 60s, which means clay sewer laterals are still common between the house and the city main. Clay pipe cracks at the joints over time, and tree roots from mature street trees find their way in, especially in neighborhoods like Dana Estates where the landscaping has had seventy years to mature. Root intrusion slows the line gradually until a heavy household load, like a full load of laundry plus a shower, pushes it over the edge and sends wastewater back up through the lowest drain in the house. Properties on septic systems on the outskirts of the city see a different pattern: saturated leach fields after a wet winter, which forces an overflow at the tank or distribution box rather than inside the structure itself.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Concord

We start by identifying the source and shutting off the cause, whether that means isolating a fixture, calling in a plumber for the lateral, or confirming the septic tank needs pumping before we touch the interior. Everything in contact with raw sewage gets treated as Category 3 water, which under the IICRC S500 standard means porous materials like carpet pad, baseboard, and drywall below the flood line are typically removed rather than dried in place. We extract standing wastewater, remove and bag contaminated materials for proper disposal, and apply EPA-registered antimicrobial treatment to framing and subfloor before setting up containment and negative air where needed. ## Concord Insurance & HOA Coordination

Sewer backup coverage is usually a separate endorsement on a homeowner's policy rather than something included automatically, and Concord homeowners are sometimes surprised to learn their base policy excludes it.
