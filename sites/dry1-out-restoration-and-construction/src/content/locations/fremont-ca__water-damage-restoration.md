## Our Water Damage Restoration Process in Fremont

We start with extraction and a moisture map of the affected area, then calibrate drying to what we're actually dealing with. We monitor both with meters and thermal imaging and keep drying equipment in place until readings confirm the structure is dry, not just dry to the touch, following IICRC S500 drying guidelines throughout.
