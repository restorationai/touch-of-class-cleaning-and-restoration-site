Mold Solutionz 24/7 was founded in 2017 in Cerritos, California, with a specific focus on mold remediation and water damage — the kind of work that doesn't wait for convenient timing. Since then, the company has built its operation around one consistent reality: moisture problems in Southern California homes don't announce themselves, and when they do surface, the clock is already running.

## What we do

On any given day, that means arriving at a house where a slow slab leak finally made itself known through buckled hardwood, or walking a landlord through a bathroom wall that's been quietly growing mold behind the tile for months. It means picking up the phone at 2 a.m. when a water heater fails and a family is standing in a flooded garage. It means generating the moisture readings, photo documentation, and scope-of-work reports that insurance adjusters need to process a claim — so homeowners aren't left navigating that paperwork alone while their house is still wet.

The work is technical and it's also personal. People call during genuinely bad stretches of their year. Showing up prepared, explaining what's actually happening inside their walls, and moving quickly enough to prevent secondary damage — that's what the day-to-day looks like.

## Our certifications and licensure

The team holds IICRC Water Restoration Technician certification (WRT #70145645). The IICRC — the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification — is the body that sets the technical standards for how water damage is assessed, dried, and documented across the industry. Its S500 standard, for example, defines the drying protocols and equipment benchmarks that separate a proper remediation from one that leaves residual moisture behind a wall. Holding WRT certification means the technicians on your job have been trained and tested against those standards, not just on-the-job habits.

## Where we work

Mold Solutionz 24/7 is based in Cerritos and serves communities throughout the surrounding area of Los Angeles and Orange counties — including Norwalk, Lakewood, Downey, Long Beach, Anaheim, Buena Park, La Mirada, Bellflower, Artesia, and Paramount, among others. The housing stock across this corridor is a mix of postwar tract homes, mid-century ranch houses, and newer construction — each with its own typical failure points. Older homes in particular can carry decades of deferred maintenance behind walls and under subfloors, which is worth knowing before any remediation scope is finalized. If you're not sure whether your city falls within the service area, a quick call to (714) 409-4758 will confirm it.
