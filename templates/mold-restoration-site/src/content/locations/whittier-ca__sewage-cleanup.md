## Why Whittier Properties See Sewage Backup More Often Than Owners Expect

Whittier sits on a mix of clay-heavy soils that shift seasonally — expanding with winter rains, contracting in the dry summer heat. That ground movement stresses the older clay-tile and cast-iron lateral lines that still run beneath many homes in the Uptown Whittier and Mar Vista neighborhoods. A hairline crack in a 70-year-old clay pipe is all it takes: tree roots from the mature sycamores and liquid ambers lining residential streets find that crack, widen it, and eventually block the line entirely. The result is a sewage backup that surfaces at the lowest drain in the house — usually a floor drain, laundry tub, or first-floor toilet — without any warning.

## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Whittier

Sewage is classified as Category 3 — the most contaminated class of water loss — which means standard wet-vac extraction is only the beginning. Our IICRC WRT-certified technicians follow a structured protocol every time:

**1. Containment and safety setup.** We isolate the affected area with poly sheeting and establish negative air pressure where cross-contamination to living spaces is a risk. PPE is non-negotiable on Category 3 losses.

**2. Bulk extraction.** Truck-mounted extraction units pull standing sewage from flooring, subfloor cavities, and any accessible crawl space. In Whittier's older ranch-style and California bungalow homes, crawl spaces are often shallow and poorly ventilated — we use flexible hose extensions to reach areas a standard wand can't access.

**3. Contaminated material removal.** Porous materials that have absorbed sewage — drywall, insulation, certain flooring types — are removed, bagged, and disposed of per Los Angeles County health and waste guidelines. We photograph everything before removal for your insurance documentation.

**4. Antimicrobial treatment.** All hard surfaces, framing, and concrete are treated with EPA-registered disinfectants rated for Category 3 contamination. We pay particular attention to subfloor joists and sill plates in older homes, where sewage can wick into wood grain and harbor pathogens long after visible water is gone.

**5. Structural drying and clearance.** Commercial desiccant dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers run until moisture readings in walls and subfloors return to baseline. We verify with thermal imaging before equipment is pulled.
