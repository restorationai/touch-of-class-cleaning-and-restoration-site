## Our Sewage Cleanup and Sanitization Process in Fullerton

Every sewage call starts with containment, not extraction. Before any equipment rolls in, we assess which materials have been directly contacted by black water and which are at secondary risk from splash or vapor. In Fullerton's older homes, that often means hardwood subfloors, plaster-and-lath walls in bathrooms, and original tile set in sand-bed mortar — all of which absorb contaminated water differently than modern cement board or drywall.

Once the scope is mapped, the process moves in this sequence:

1. **Extraction** — truck-mounted and portable extractors remove standing sewage and saturated material. Solids are bagged and disposed of per Orange County Health Care Agency guidelines for biohazardous waste.
2. **Controlled demolition** — saturated drywall, insulation, and flooring below the flood line are removed and documented photographically for insurance purposes.
3. **Antimicrobial treatment** — EPA-registered disinfectants are applied to all affected structural surfaces, including subfloor, framing, and wall cavities. We do not skip this step on "dry" surfaces that may have received aerosol contamination.
4. **Drying and monitoring** — commercial dehumidifiers and air movers run until moisture readings in structural materials return to baseline. In Orange County's climate, ambient humidity is generally low, which aids drying — but enclosed crawl spaces under older Fullerton homes trap moisture and require targeted equipment placement.
5. **Clearance verification** — before any reconstruction begins, we confirm that no residual moisture or microbial activity remains in the structure.

Our team holds IICRC WRT certification (#70145645), and sewage jobs are handled under the same documentation protocols used for insurance billing.
