# Flood Fixers Continues Water and Flood Damage Restoration Services Across San Diego County

**San Diego, CA** — July 23, 2026 — Flood Fixers, a water and flood damage restoration company based in San Diego, is continuing to serve residential and commercial customers across 15 communities in the greater San Diego region, offering around-the-clock response to water-related emergencies.

The company provides a range of services that address water and flood damage at multiple stages, from initial mitigation through full reconstruction. Its service offerings include water damage restoration, flood damage restoration, burst pipe repair, basement flooding cleanup, reconstruction, and general contracting. Flood Fixers holds both IICRC Certified and EPA Lead-Safe Certified credentials, which apply to its restoration and contracting work.

Flood Fixers operates on a 24-hour, seven-day-a-week basis, meaning customers facing burst pipes, storm flooding, or other water emergencies can reach the company at any hour. The company can be contacted by phone at (855) 204-1124 or through its website at https://flood-fixers.com.

The company's current service area spans San Diego, Chula Vista, Oceanside, Escondido, Carlsbad, El Cajon, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, La Mesa, Santee, Poway, National City, Spring Valley, and Temecula. The coverage area extends from coastal communities in northern San Diego County south through the urban core and into inland cities, as well as north into Riverside County with service in Temecula.

"Water damage moves fast, and the communities we serve deserve a restoration company that is ready to respond at any time of day," said a spokesperson for Flood Fixers. "Our certifications and our full-service approach — from initial cleanup through reconstruction — mean customers work with one company from start to finish."
