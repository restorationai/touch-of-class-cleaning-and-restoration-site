# Crew Restoration & Construction Continues Damage Restoration and Construction Services Across Tri-State Region

**Sioux Falls, SD** — August 5, 2026 — Crew Restoration & Construction is maintaining its full range of damage restoration and general contracting services across a multi-state service area spanning South Dakota, Minnesota, and Iowa.

Founded in 2015 and based in Sioux Falls, the company offers eight service lines: water damage restoration, fire damage restoration, mold remediation, storm damage restoration, general contracting, roofing, sewage cleanup, and biohazard cleanup. The company holds IICRC certification and operates under license number 3814.

Crew Restoration & Construction carries a 4.8-star rating on Google, drawn from 488 customer reviews. The company serves more than 40 communities across the tri-state area, including Sioux Falls, Brookings, Yankton, Harrisburg, Brandon, and Tea in South Dakota; Worthington, Luverne, Pipestone, Slayton, Adrian, Edgerton, and Fulda in Minnesota; and Le Mars, Sioux Center, Orange City, Rock Valley, Rock Rapids, Sibley, George, Hawarden, Hull, Alton, Akron, Remsen, Hinton, Merrill, and Kingsley in Iowa, among others.

The company's coverage also extends to smaller South Dakota communities including Dell Rapids, Hartford, Canton, North Sioux City, Lennox, Flandreau, Parker, Salem, Crooks, Baltic, Garretson, Worthing, Volga, Beresford, Dakota Dunes, Elk Point, and Madison.

"We remain committed to serving homeowners and property owners across this region with professional, certified restoration work," said a spokesperson for Crew Restoration & Construction. "Our goal is to help people recover and rebuild after some of the most difficult situations they will face as property owners, and we take that responsibility seriously."
