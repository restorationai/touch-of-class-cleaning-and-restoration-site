# DISS Restoration Continues 24/7 Damage Restoration Service Across Mahoning Valley

**Youngstown, OH** — August 5, 2026 — DISS Restoration, an IICRC-certified restoration contractor based in Youngstown, Ohio, continues to provide residential and commercial damage restoration services to communities throughout the Mahoning Valley region.

The company offers a broad range of services, including water damage restoration, fire damage restoration, mold remediation, storm damage restoration, sewage cleanup, biohazard cleanup, contents restoration and general contracting. Founded in 2021, DISS Restoration operates around the clock, providing 24/7 availability to property owners facing time-sensitive damage situations.

DISS Restoration holds multiple industry certifications, including recognition as an IICRC Certified Firm. Individual technician credentials include the IICRC Water Restoration Technician (WRT), Fire and Smoke Restoration Technician (FSRT), Applied Microbial Remediation Technician (AMRT) and Applied Structural Drying (ASD) designations. The company is also EPA Lead-Safe Certified and OSHA trained.

The company serves nine communities across northeastern Ohio: Youngstown, Warren, Boardman, Austintown, Niles, Girard, Struthers, Canfield and Hubbard. Property owners in those areas can reach the company at any hour for emergency and non-emergency restoration needs.

"Our focus is on being ready when property owners need help most — whether that is the middle of the night after a pipe bursts or in the days following a fire," said a spokesperson for DISS Restoration. "Holding certifications across water, fire, mold and structural drying means our crews are trained to handle the full scope of what a property owner might face."
