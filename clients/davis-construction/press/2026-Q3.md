# Davis Construction Contractors Continues Full-Service Home Construction and Restoration Work Across Northern Alabama

**Madison, AL** — July 23, 2026 — Davis Construction Contractors, a family-owned construction and home-services company based in Madison, Alabama, continues to serve residential and commercial customers across the northern Alabama region with a broad range of construction, remodeling, and restoration services.

Founded in 2008, Davis Construction Contractors has built its business around a comprehensive service offering that covers projects from the ground up as well as targeted repairs and improvements. The company handles home remodeling, new construction, decks, pergolas and fences, roofing, siding and gutters, painting and trim, water damage restoration, and storm damage restoration. That combination of new-build capability and damage-response work allows the company to serve customers at multiple stages of a home's life cycle, from initial construction through long-term maintenance and recovery after weather events.

The company's service area spans three northern Alabama communities: Madison, Huntsville, and Athens. Each market presents its own mix of older housing stock in need of renovation and newer developments requiring construction services, and Davis Construction Contractors operates across all three.

As a family-owned business, Davis Construction Contractors says its operating approach prioritizes direct communication and accountability on every project. "We've been doing this work in northern Alabama since 2008, and we stay focused on making sure every customer feels like they're working with people who genuinely care about the outcome," said a spokesperson for Davis Construction Contractors. "Whether it's a full remodel, a new deck, or emergency storm damage work, we treat every job the same way."

Customers and property owners in Madison, Huntsville, and Athens who are planning construction projects, home improvements, or who have experienced water or storm damage can contact Davis Construction Contractors by phone or through the company's website to discuss project needs and scheduling.
