# Lakewood Restoration Firm Reregisters Under New Name to Better Reflect Round-the-Clock Services

**Lakewood, WA** — September 16, 2026 — The Lakewood-based property restoration company formerly known as Frontline Fire & Flood now operates under the registered trade name Frontline Fire & Flood - 24/7 Emergency Plumbing, Water and Fire Damage Restoration, the company announced today.

The new name is a registered doing-business-as designation for the same legal entity, Frontline Fire & Flood, which has been operating from its Lakewood location since 2014. Ownership, staff, phone number and service commitments remain unchanged under the rebranding.

The company provides water damage restoration, fire damage restoration, mold remediation, storm damage restoration, sewage cleanup, asbestos abatement, emergency board-up and tarping, and contents restoration and storage. It operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week and holds a Google rating of 4.9 based on 133 reviews. The firm carries certifications from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification, including designations as an IICRC Certified Firm and individual credentials in mold remediation (AMRT), water damage restoration (WRT), fire and smoke restoration (FSRT), and structural drying (ASD). The company is also EPA Lead-Safe Certified and OSHA Trained, and holds contractor license No. CCFRONTFF761CB.

Frontline Fire & Flood - 24/7 Emergency Plumbing, Water and Fire Damage Restoration serves residential and commercial customers across a broad area of western Washington, including Lakewood, Tacoma, Puyallup, University Place, Olympia, Lacey, Federal Way, Seattle, Bellevue, Renton, Kent, Auburn, Gig Harbor, Bremerton, Port Orchard, Centralia and more than a dozen additional communities in the region.

"The updated name tells customers exactly what to expect from us — around-the-clock availability for the kinds of emergencies that can't wait until morning," a spokesperson for Frontline Fire & Flood - 24/7 Emergency Plumbing, Water and Fire Damage Restoration said. "Nothing about the way we work has changed; we just wanted the name to make that clarity immediate."
