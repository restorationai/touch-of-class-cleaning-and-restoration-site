# National Restoration Construction Expands Water Damage Services to Gig Harbor, Bremerton, Port Orchard and University Place

**Federal Way, WA** — July 5, 2026 — National Restoration Construction LLC, a water damage and restoration contractor serving the greater Puget Sound region since 2004, has extended its service area to four additional communities across the Kitsap Peninsula and the greater Tacoma area: Gig Harbor, Bremerton, Port Orchard and University Place.

The company provides a range of residential and commercial restoration services, including water damage restoration, flood damage restoration, burst pipe repair, basement flooding cleanup, appliance leak cleanup, frozen pipe restoration, roof leak repair and sewage cleanup. National Restoration Construction operates around the clock, seven days a week, and holds certifications from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification (IICRC), the Better Business Bureau, and the Environmental Protection Agency, in addition to being recognized as a Lead-Safe Certified Firm and an ANSI Certified company. The company is licensed by the Washington State Department of Labor & Industries under license number NATIORC792M6.

On Google, the company carries a 4.9-star rating based on 53 reviews. Its established service area spans Federal Way, Seattle, Bellevue, Tacoma, Everett, Kent, Renton, Auburn, Kirkland and Redmond, in addition to the four communities added in the current expansion.

"We have been serving homeowners and businesses across the Puget Sound area for more than two decades, and expanding to Gig Harbor, Bremerton, Port Orchard and University Place allows us to bring that same level of certified, around-the-clock restoration service to communities that face the same water damage risks as our core markets," said a spokesperson for National Restoration Construction. "We are committed to being available when property owners need us most, regardless of the hour or the scope of the damage."

The expansion reflects continued demand for professional water damage response services in western Washington, where wet winters, aging infrastructure and proximity to Puget Sound can contribute to flooding, roof leaks and pipe failures.
