3 Basic Categories of Water Damage That We Work On

Category 1 (Clean Water)

This water originates from a clean source like a broken supply line or a toilet tank before the flush. While less hazardous initially, it demands swift action before it mixes with contaminants.

Category 2 (Grey Water)

This category includes water from sources like washing machine overflow or toilet bowls with urine but no feces. It carries physical, chemical, or biological contaminants and can cause illness if handled incorrectly.

Category 3 (Black Water)

This water is grossly unsanitary, coming from sewage, river floods, or standing water that has begun to support bacteria growth. Our teams use full biohazard protocols and PPE for safe cleanup.
