About the Business

Meridian Restoration Pros is committed to restoring homes and businesses after water, mold, fire, or storm damage. Our services include water damage restoration, flood cleanup, water extraction, structural drying, and professional sewage cleanup to control contamination and prevent structural issues. We also offer mold inspection and mold remediation to maintain healthy indoor environments. Our team provides reliable fire and smoke damage restoration along with complete storm damage cleanup. With advanced equipment and trained professionals, we respond quickly and work efficiently to return properties to safe, clean conditions.
