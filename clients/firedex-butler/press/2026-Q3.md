# FireDEX Butler Continues Restoration Services Across Western Pennsylvania Communities

**Cranberry Township, PA** — July 23, 2026 — FireDEX Butler, Inc. is maintaining its full range of property damage restoration services across nine communities in western Pennsylvania, offering around-the-clock response to residential and commercial customers facing water, fire, and storm emergencies.

The company, operating from its location at 9133 Marshall Rd in Cranberry Township, provides services that include water damage restoration, flood damage restoration, basement flooding cleanup, sewage cleanup, burst pipe repair, frozen pipe restoration, storm damage restoration, and fire damage restoration. FireDEX Butler is available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, giving property owners access to emergency assistance at any hour.

FireDEX Butler has been serving the region since 1981 and holds a Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor Registration through the Pennsylvania Attorney General's office under license number PA001913. The company's service area spans Cranberry Township, Wexford, Mars, Zelienople, Evans City, Seven Fields, Butler, Gibsonia, and Beaver Falls.

"We remain committed to being there for property owners in our region when they need help most," said a spokesperson for FireDEX Butler. "Whether it's a burst pipe in the middle of the night or fire damage that requires immediate attention, our team is ready to respond and help people get back to normal as quickly as possible."

Property damage from water intrusion, sewage backups, and severe weather can escalate quickly without prompt professional intervention. FireDEX Butler's service lineup is designed to address those situations from initial cleanup through the restoration process, covering both the immediate emergency response and the longer-term recovery work that follows.

Residents and business owners in the covered communities can reach FireDEX Butler at (724) 452-7400 or visit the company's website at https://firedex.net for more information about available services.
