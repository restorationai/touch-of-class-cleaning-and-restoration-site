# ProRestoration Services Continues Around-the-Clock Damage Restoration Across Kern County

**Bakersfield, CA** — July 23, 2026 — ProRestoration Services Inc. continues to provide water, flood, and fire damage restoration to residential and commercial property owners across Bakersfield and surrounding Kern County communities, with 24-hour availability seven days a week.
