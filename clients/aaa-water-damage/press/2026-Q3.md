# AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care Continues Around-the-Clock Service Across Oahu

**Honolulu, HI** — August 5, 2026 — AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care, operating under the legal name AAA Flood Service LLC, is maintaining its full range of water damage, mold, and carpet care services across 13 communities on Oahu, with 24-hour availability for residents and property owners facing emergency situations.

The company offers a broad scope of residential and commercial services, including water damage restoration, flood damage restoration, burst pipe repair, sewage cleanup, mold remediation, mold inspection testing, carpet cleaning, and upholstery cleaning. The combination of emergency remediation and routine cleaning services positions the company to address both urgent property damage and ongoing maintenance needs throughout the island.

AAA Water Damage Restoration & Carpet Care operates around the clock, seven days a week, providing continuous availability for time-sensitive situations such as burst pipes, flooding events, and sewage backups. Rapid access to professional restoration services can be a critical factor in limiting structural damage and reducing the risk of secondary issues such as mold growth.

The company's service area spans communities across the southern and eastern portions of Oahu as well as areas to the north and west, including Honolulu, Hawaii Kai, Kahala, Aina Haina, Kaimuki, Diamond Head, Kailua, Kaneohe, Pearl City, Aiea, Ewa Beach, Kapolei, and Mililani.
