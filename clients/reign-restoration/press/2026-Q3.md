# Reign Restoration Continues Damage Restoration and General Contracting Services Across North Texas and Dallas Metro Region

**Royse City, TX** — August 5, 2026 — Reign Restoration, a damage restoration and general contracting company based in Royse City, Texas, continues to serve residential and commercial property owners across a 17-city footprint spanning the Dallas metropolitan area and surrounding communities.

The company offers six core services: water damage restoration, fire damage restoration, mold remediation, storm damage restoration, general contracting, and contents restoration. Reign Restoration operates around the clock, providing 24/7 availability to property owners facing time-sensitive damage situations.

Reign Restoration holds several credentials from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification, including designation as an IICRC Certified Firm. Individual technician certifications include the IICRC WRT (Water), IICRC AMRT (Mold), and IICRC FSRT (Fire & Smoke). The company also holds mold remediation contractor license number MRC2276.

"Our focus is on being available when property owners need help most, whether that is the middle of the night after a pipe bursts or in the days following a severe storm," said a spokesperson for Reign Restoration. "We serve a wide corridor from Greenville and Terrell in the east through the core Dallas market and into Frisco and McKinney to the north, and we want every community in that area to know qualified help is close by."

Reign Restoration's service area covers Royse City, Rockwall, Fate, Rowlett, Wylie, Garland, Mesquite, Dallas, Richardson, Plano, Allen, McKinney, Frisco, Highland Park, University Park, Greenville, and Terrell. The company is headquartered at 6691 TX-276 STE C in Royse City and can be reached by phone at any hour.
