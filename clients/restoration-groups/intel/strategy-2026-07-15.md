## The strategy question: one site or many?

**ONE site. Not one site per GBP, not one per state.** Reasons:

1. **Authority is their binding constraint, not page count.** 91 referring
   domains across ~500 combined pages is why two programmatic sites rank for
   nothing. Splitting that authority across more domains multiplies the
   failure. Every competitor beating them organically (PDQ, PuroClean,
   Northeast Power Dry) runs city pages on ONE strong domain.
2. **GBPs don't need their own domains.** Each GBP links to its geo hub on the
   one domain (Kenilworth → homepage or /service-areas/kenilworth-nj/, Staten
   Island → /service-areas/staten-island-ny/). That's the pattern the winners
   use.
3. **They already ran the multi-site experiment** — two 200+ page WP sites,
   two brands, split signals. It produced Maps dominance (from reviews) and
   organic invisibility. Consolidation IS the fix, not more fragmentation.

**Consolidation plan:** build on therestorationgroup.com (our stack), 301 the
ENTIRE restorationgroups.com tree (209 URLs) AND the old therestorationgroup
WP tree (274 URLs) into it. Two redirect maps, not one. Their existing vendor
must stop publishing at cutover (ask who/what they pay — intel Q3 still open).

## Map-pack reality check (say this plainly Monday)

Map rankings are proximity-bound. Two offices = strong pack presence in a
~15-25 min radius around Kenilworth + Staten Island. No GBP = no map pack in
PA, period, no matter how many pages we build. The honest coverage stack:
- **Maps:** 2 GBPs today. A third real PA office (with real address + staff)
  is the only legitimate way to get PA pack presence. No virtual offices —
  suspension risk to the 256-review listing isn't worth it.
- **Organic + AI answers:** where town pages work at any distance — our lane.
- **Paid (LSA/Ads):** buys reach beyond the pack radius immediately. LSA needs
  the NJ HIC (have it now) + per-state licensing for PA/NY.

## Territory build-out: tranches, not a 500-page dump

NJ alone has 564 municipalities. Publishing them all onto a 91-RD domain at
once = thin-content index bloat (their current failure, repeated by us).

- **Tranche 1 (launch):** ~60-80 areas — Union/Essex/Middlesex/Bergen/Morris/
  Somerset/Passaic counties (county hubs + top towns by population/jobs),
  Staten Island + Brooklyn + Manhattan hubs. Full services × priority towns.
- **Tranche 2 (weeks 3-6):** next NJ county rings + Rockland/Westchester NY if
  jobs support it. Driven by geo-grid + GSC data, ~50 pages per tranche.
- **Tranche 3:** PA (Bucks/Montgomery/Philly) — organic play only until a PA
  office exists; pair with LSA/Ads for real PA lead flow.
- Every tranche: pages get real local content (our template), IndexNow ping,
  internal links from county hubs. Kill criteria: pages with zero impressions
  after 60-90 days get consolidated into county hubs, not left thin.

Ask Monday (intel Q7): **where do jobs actually come from today?** Build the
first tranche around revenue, not ambition.
