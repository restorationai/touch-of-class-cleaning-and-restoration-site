# The Restoration Group Continues Water and Fire Damage Services Across New Jersey and New York Metro Area

**Kenilworth, NJ** — July 23, 2026 — The Restoration Group, a Kenilworth-based damage restoration company, is continuing to serve residential and commercial property owners across more than 50 communities in New Jersey and the New York metropolitan area.

The company provides a range of restoration services, including water damage restoration, flood damage restoration, burst pipe repair, basement flooding cleanup, appliance leak cleanup, sewage cleanup, fire damage restoration, and smoke damage restoration. The Restoration Group operates around the clock and responds to service calls within 60 minutes, offering property owners immediate assistance when damage occurs.

The Restoration Group holds certification as an IICRC Certified Firm, credential number 210213, issued by the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification. The company was founded in 2021 and is based at 500 S 31st St in Kenilworth, N.J.

The company's service area spans communities throughout Union, Essex, Bergen, Hudson, Middlesex, Somerset, Passaic, and Morris counties in New Jersey, as well as portions of New York City, including Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Staten Island. Among the New Jersey communities served are Newark, Jersey City, Elizabeth, Westfield, Cranford, Summit, Montclair, Hoboken, Hackensack, Morristown, Paterson, and New Brunswick, along with dozens of additional municipalities.

"Property damage from water or fire can happen at any hour, and our goal is to be on site quickly so that homeowners and businesses can begin the recovery process as soon as possible," said a spokesperson for The Restoration Group. "We are committed to serving this region with the technical standards that IICRC certification requires."

Property owners seeking restoration services can reach The Restoration Group at (855) 650-7422 or visit the company's website at therestorationgroup.com.
