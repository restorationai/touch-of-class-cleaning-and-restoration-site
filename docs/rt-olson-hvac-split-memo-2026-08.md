# RT Olson: Plumbing vs HVAC Split — Recommendation (2026-08-18)

Context: another agency's audit (read aloud by Bobby on the 08-18 call; document itself went to
the getrestorationai.com inbox, not contact@ — forward for verification) proposes: a separate
rtohvac.com site, cutting the main site to a 16-page plumbing-only structure, and calls
city pages "old-school SEO." Bobby is conflicted and asked for our recommendation.

Business facts from the call: HVAC is a separate entity (partnership), separate technicians,
separate bank accounts. GBP and Facebook page are shared under "RT Olson Plumbing, Heating
and Air." Plumbing Google Ads converted poorly ($6k -> 24 conversions, landing-page problem).
HVAC Facebook ads run 9.5x ROAS with another vendor. Bobby's fear: losing 10+ years of
domain/GBP equity (site since 2014).

## Recommendation, in one line

Keep ONE organic site and ONE Google Business Profile. Let ads run to dedicated landing
pages (on a separate ads-only domain if the ads vendor prefers). Do NOT shrink the site to
16 pages, and do NOT change the GBP name right now.

## Reasoning

1. **The conversion problem is a landing-page problem, not a site-architecture problem.**
   His own numbers say so: ads dumped visitors onto a homepage where they had to navigate.
   The fix is single-intent landing pages per ad group (our SKAG landing-page system, or the
   ads vendor's own LPs). Splitting the organic site would not have fixed his $6k -> 24.
2. **A separate rtohvac.com as an ADS-ONLY property is fine and we support it.** PPC landing
   domains are disconnected from organic and from the GBP. If their team wants to run HVAC
   Google/Meta ads to rtohvac.com landing pages, no conflict with anything we run. It should
   be noindex and never linked from the GBP.
3. **Splitting the ORGANIC site would spend his strongest asset.** rtolsonplumbing.com has
   12 years of history and every link he has ever earned. A new rtohvac.com starts from zero
   authority, and the main site loses its HVAC relevance for nothing: plumbing pages and
   HVAC pages target disjoint queries, so they don't dilute each other. Google ranks pages,
   not sites, for these local terms.
4. **The "16 pages, embed the cities" claim is the one we can disprove with data.** Our
   city-and-service pages are individually written with real local substance (this is the
   methodology the whole fleet runs on, validated against ranking data). Collapsing 124
   targeted pages into 16 forfeits every suburb query he could own. Their point about tight
   navigation is right though, and already handled: services live under one Services hub,
   HVAC under its own section, plus the new hero lead form for conversion.
5. **One GBP, unchanged name.** Splitting the profile restarts reviews at zero (Bobby's own
   instinct said no). The name-change idea ("RT Olson Plumbers" via DBA) is a real ranking
   lever but carries live re-verification/suspension risk — we are managing exactly that
   suspension case for another client right now. Park it; revisit after launch once rankings
   from the new site settle. GBP name changes stay suggestion-only per standing policy.
6. **Sister-company links:** the plumbing site links Dry County on water-damage-adjacent
   pages (template supports it natively) once Dry County has its own domain.

## What we do this week

- Launch rtolsonplumbing.com (124 pages, hero form) — conversion + rankings first.
- Keep ac-repair / furnace-repair / indoor-air-quality pages (GBP lists HVAC categories;
  removing pages would break GBP-to-site parity).
- Offer: if Bobby wants Google Ads restarted later, we scaffold SKAG campaigns + dedicated
  LPs on the existing domain (or an ads-only domain) — the proven fix for his conversion
  numbers.
- Verify this memo against the actual audit document when forwarded.
