# Audit Stats Library (Vetted)

**Purpose:** This file is the ONLY permitted source of marketing/search statistics quoted in automated "free SEO audit" reports sent to water-damage-restoration business owners. Every entry below was verified against a primary or highly reputable source on **2026-07-05**. The report generator may ONLY quote the `claim` text from this file **verbatim** (including its hedging language, e.g. "as of Feb 2026"). Do not round differently, drop the date qualifier, or combine claims. If a stat is not in this file, it does not go in a report.

**Maintenance:** Re-verify entries at least quarterly. When a source publishes a newer figure, update the entry (claim + date + URL) rather than adding a duplicate. `confidence` = high (verified directly against the named source), medium (source confirmed but with caveats noted).

## Stats

- **claim:** ChatGPT now serves 900 million weekly active users, per OpenAI's announcement in February 2026 — up from 800 million just four months earlier.
- **source:** OpenAI announcement, as reported by TechCrunch (also Search Engine Land)
- **url:** https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users/
- **date:** Announced 2026-02-27
- **confidence:** high
- **notes:** OpenAI's most recent official WAU figure as of 2026-07-05. (Sensor Tower/Reuters separately reported ~1B monthly app users in June 2026, but that is a third-party estimate of a different metric — do not quote it.)

### ai_overviews_prevalence
- **claim:** By early 2026, Google was showing an AI Overview on roughly 48% of queries tracked by BrightEdge — up from about 30% a year earlier; other trackers using different keyword sets measure prevalence between roughly 16% and 48%.
- **source:** BrightEdge Generative Parser / Weekly AI Search Insights (range corroborated by Semrush and Conductor tracking)
- **url:** https://www.brightedge.com/resources/weekly-ai-search-insights
- **date:** Early 2026 (BrightEdge tracking, Feb 2025 – Feb 2026)
- **confidence:** medium
- **notes:** Prevalence varies heavily by keyword mix, device, and tracker. Always quote with the "queries tracked by BrightEdge" qualifier — never as "48% of all Google searches."

### local_search_visit_within_day
- **claim:** Google research found that 76% of people who search for something nearby on their smartphone visit a related business within a day (Google/Ipsos, 2016).
- **source:** Google / Ipsos, "I-Want-to-Go Moments: From Search to Store," Think with Google
- **url:** https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/consumer-insights/consumer-trends/mobile-location-searches-to-store-visit-data/
- **date:** 2016 (May 2016 research; still Google's most-cited local-intent figure)
- **confidence:** medium
- **notes:** Google migrated Think with Google content in 2025–26, so the original URL now redirects to business.google.com; the figure is Google's own and remains universally attributed to this 2016 study. Always keep the "(Google/Ipsos, 2016)" dating in the claim.

- **"46% of all Google searches are local" / "97% of people learn about local companies online"** — legacy stats with no traceable primary source (often mis-attributed to Google/HubSpot). Omitted.
- **ChatGPT referral conversion rate (7.1%)** — appeared only in aggregator roundups; primary study not located. Omitted.
- **Per-vertical AI Overview prevalence for home services / restoration** — no defensible public figure found as of 2026-07-05. Omitted.
