Santino (2026-09-17, Davis Construction / Robert-COC DNS thread): always CC
the business owner on any email exchanged with third-party associates,
agencies, or vendors regarding that client's account.

**Why:** the owner must see what outsiders are changing or requesting on
their infrastructure (DNS, email, ads, tracking); silent third-party threads
are how conflicting setups and surprises happen.

**How to apply:** before sending any reply to a vendor/associate (BDA, RGP,
COC-type contractors), add the owner's on-file email to CC (e.g. Heath Davis
davisheath09@gmail.com, Bob Olson bob@rtoplumbing.com). Owner emails live in
clients/{slug}/plan-input.json brand.email.
