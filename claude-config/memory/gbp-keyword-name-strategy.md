GBP keyword-name renames are a standard Rank AI service with three records
that must stay in sync:

1. **DB truth** (what the app renders): `marketing_gbp_suggestions`
   item_type `name` (+ gating `category` rows), ranked by confidence on the
   Profile Rename card. Seeded 2026-09-10 for: RT Olson (4), Pro
   Restoration (4 incl. triple-stack ceiling + Plumber category gate at
   0.81), All Pro (3), NaRestCo (1), California Restoration West (4).
2. **Reasoning record**: `rank-ai/docs/gbp-rename-candidates.md` — every
   candidate + why + market data. Update it whenever candidates change.
3. **Method**: skill `rank-ai-gbp-rename` — data-gathering steps, naming
   rules, seeding shape, sequence, landmines.

**House stance (Santino 2026-09-10): lead aggressive.** First choice =
`{Brand} - 24/7 Emergency {A} & {B}` wherever a DBA can back it;
conservative category-mirror names rank BELOW as fallbacks. The
triple-stack (`24/7 Emergency {A}, {B}, & {C}`) is a presentable ceiling
option with honest costs: per-term dilution, ~100-char field limit,
3-term report class, verbatim DBA required.

Key validated data (2026-09-09/10): DataForSEO search_volume == native
Keyword Planner exactly; CA emergency plumber 8-10k/mo, water heater
repair 9,900/mo; WA mold 1,900 > water 1,600; 2025 fire corridors keep
fire terms elevated for CRW. OneStop proof case: CSLB #951907 registered
under the full keyworded name.

Sequence is law: DBA filed, then citations/signage/site, then ONE manual
GBP change. Never auto-pushed. Category gate before any name term
(Plumber category before plumbing name words on [[allpro-prorestoration-site-state]]'s
Pro Restoration).
