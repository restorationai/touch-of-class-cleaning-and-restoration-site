Frequently asked questions

Who do you work with?

Owner-operated and growing home service businesses: restoration, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, electrical, remodeling, cleaning and similar trades.

Do I have to do anything?

Very little. You connect your accounts once and send us photos from the field when you can. We handle the rest and keep you updated.

Why do you ask me to connect my Facebook, Instagram or Google accounts?

So we can publish posts, keep your business information accurate and report results on your behalf. You approve each connection yourself, can see exactly what access is granted, and can disconnect at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Plans are month to month.
